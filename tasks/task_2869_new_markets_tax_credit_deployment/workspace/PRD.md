# New Markets Tax Credit Deployment API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A Community Development Entity (CDE) that wins New Markets Tax Credit allocation authority takes on an obligation that outlives every spreadsheet the deal team built to win it. The award commits the CDE to a named service area, to a minimum concentration of capital in severely distressed census tracts, and to community outcomes it must certify year after year. Capital then flows through a single-purpose investment fund, capitalised by a leverage lender and a tax-credit investor, into a qualified equity investment (QEI) whose seven-year compliance period begins the instant the cash is placed. From there the CDE must keep substantially all of the investment working in qualified low-income community investments (QLICIs), redeploy every dollar of returned principal inside a fixed window, and prove all of it to the certifying agency annually. A single missed test, a business that stops qualifying, or a decertification of the CDE itself converts a benefit into a clawback.

This document specifies the API that runs that lifecycle end to end. Today the work is done in a patchwork of loan-servicing spreadsheets, closing binders and side letters, and the failure mode is always the same: the compliance breach is discovered at the audit rather than at the moment the deal moved. The system replaces that with a single deterministic record of allocation authority, deployment, eligibility, community benefits and unwind, in which every threshold is evaluated as it is crossed, every derived counter is recomputed in the same request that changed its input, and every rejection names a machine-readable reason a compliance officer can act on.

Two design commitments shape everything that follows. First, the system is a **compliance measurement engine before it is a bookkeeping engine**: the ordering of its checks is itself a specification, because a request that is simultaneously ineligible and underfunded must report the eligibility failure so that the operator fixes the regulatory problem rather than the cash problem. Second, all business time is read from a **logical clock**, never from wall-clock time, so that a seven-year compliance period, a 365-day reinvestment deadline and a 90-day option window can all be exercised deterministically. Every business path in this specification is mounted under `/nmtc`.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to the API must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the capacity in which it acts. If either header is absent or empty, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the five role names below, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). If the headers are well-formed but the named role is not authorised for the target operation, the rejection is `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403).

The five roles are exactly `CertifyingAgency`, `CDESponsor`, `ComplianceReviewer`, `TaxCreditInvestor` and `QALICBSponsor`.

- **`CertifyingAgency`** is the federal certifying and allocating authority. It certifies and decertifies community development entities, announces allocation awards, registers census-tract data, and drives the logical clock. It never deploys capital, never runs a compliance test and never participates in an unwind. When `X-User-Role` is `CertifyingAgency`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server does not compare it to any entity's `id`.
- **`CDESponsor`** is the allocatee community development entity itself. It signs the allocation agreement, issues and voids qualified equity investments, deploys sub-allocations and collects their principal, redeploys returned capital, cures at-risk investments, binds community benefits agreements, files agency reports, and opens and settles unwinds. It is explicitly barred from running any compliance test. When `X-User-Role` is `CDESponsor`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `CDE`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `CDE` compare the resource's foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. Ownership reaches through an allocation award's `cde_id`, and transitively from a qualified equity investment through its award to that award's CDE, and from a sub-allocation through its investment and award to that same CDE. An acting `CDESponsor` that is not the owning CDE of the target chain is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403).
- **`ComplianceReviewer`** is the independent compliance function. It runs project-company eligibility tests, the annual substantially-all tests of a qualified equity investment, and the annual community-benefits certifications. It can move no money, issue nothing and sign nothing. When `X-User-Role` is `ComplianceReviewer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it — as `performed_by_user_id` on each eligibility test and on each annual compliance test — but never compares it to any entity's `id`.
- **`TaxCreditInvestor`** is the investor that capitalises the investment fund alongside the leverage lender and that holds the put option at unwind. When `X-User-Role` is `TaxCreditInvestor`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it as `investor_user_id` on the fund it forms and copies it to `put_holder_user_id` when the option window on a qualified equity investment opens; it is never required to equal any entity's `id`. Ownership checks for this role compare `X-User-ID` against the target fund's `investor_user_id`, or against an unwind's `put_holder_user_id`, and a mismatch is `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`.
- **`QALICBSponsor`** is the sponsor of the qualified active low-income community business. It registers the business, files its job reports, and holds the call option at unwind. When `X-User-Role` is `QALICBSponsor`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it as `sponsor_user_id` on the business it registers and as `filed_by_user_id` on each job report it files, but never compares it to any entity's `id`. Ownership checks for this role compare `X-User-ID` against the target business's `sponsor_user_id`, or against an unwind's `call_holder_user_id`; a mismatch is `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. Because the CDE names the call holder explicitly when it opens an unwind, the call holder need not be the sponsor of any particular business.

Every read operation under `/nmtc` — every single-resource read and every list — is available to all five roles. Write operations are role-exclusive as set out in §4.

**Segregation of duties.** The party that places capital can never be the party that measures it, and the separation goes one step further than role separation. The `ComplianceReviewer` user id recorded on the most recent eligibility test of any project company funded by a given qualified equity investment is barred from running that investment's annual compliance test. Such an attempt is rejected with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). Exercising the full compliance chain therefore requires two distinct `ComplianceReviewer` user identifiers.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

List operations are paginated through two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of items returned; it defaults to 10, has a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 100. `offset` is the number of items to skip and defaults to 0, with a minimum of 0. A `limit` outside the range 1 through 100 inclusive, a non-integer `limit`, a negative `offset` or a non-integer `offset` is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION` (HTTP 400).

Unless a specific list operation states another ordering, list results are sorted by `created_at` ascending. Where a domain ordering is more useful the deviation is stated with the operation in §4: eligibility tests are returned oldest `performed_at` first, annual compliance tests oldest `compliance_year` first, reinvestment obligations oldest `arose_at` first, and job reports oldest `period_start_at` first.

Every list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside the envelope's `meta` object. `total_count` is the number of items matching the filters before pagination is applied, and `has_more` is true when items remain beyond the returned page.

### 2.4. The Logical Clock and the Fixed 365-Day Year

Nothing in this system reads wall-clock time. Every server-stamped business instant — the issuance instant `issued_at` of a qualified equity investment, the placement instant `deployed_at` of a sub-allocation, the `performed_at` of an eligibility test or an annual compliance test, the `arose_at` of a reinvestment obligation, the `agreement_start_at` of a community benefits agreement, the `exercised_at` and `settled_at` of an unwind — is read from a single server-side logical clock. Before the clock has ever been set it reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z`, and it must never fall back to the host's real time. One administrative, test-driving capability moves it: the certifying agency supplies a target instant as `to_iso8601` and the server answers with the new setting as `current_utc`. The clock may be moved backwards as well as forwards, and moving it mutates no entity — only subsequent requests observe the new instant.

A "year" in this domain means exactly 365 days, that is 31,536,000 seconds. Leap days are not counted, so compliance anniversaries drift relative to calendar dates; this is intentional and is to be implemented literally. Compliance year *n* of a qualified equity investment spans the half-open interval that begins at the issuance instant advanced by 365 days multiplied by one less than *n*, and ends at the issuance instant advanced by 365 days multiplied by *n*. The lower bound is inclusive and the upper bound is exclusive, so consecutive compliance years tile the seven-year period without gaps and without overlap.

All timestamps, whether client-supplied or server-stamped, are ISO 8601 in UTC with exactly three fractional digits, for example `2026-09-01T00:00:00.000Z`.

### 2.5. Monetary Units, Percentages and Rounding

Every monetary field in this specification is an integer number of cents, and every field name that ends in `_cents` carries that meaning. No monetary quantity is ever represented as a floating-point number, and no money arithmetic is performed in floating point. Where a specification says an amount is at least one million dollars, the stored value is 100000000 cents.

Unless a specific calculation states otherwise, percentages are rounded to 2 decimal places using round-half-up, and cent amounts are rounded to whole cents using round-half-up. Rounding is applied only where a formula says it is applied: intermediate sums of integer cents stay exact, and a percentage is rounded once, at the end. Basis points are integers, and one basis point is one ten-thousandth, so converting a basis-point rate into cents means multiplying the cent base by the basis-point count and dividing by 10000.

### 2.6. Common Resource Properties

Primary resources — community development entities, allocation awards, project companies, investment funds, qualified equity investments, sub-allocations, reinvestment obligations, community benefits agreements and unwind transactions — all carry the same four system-managed properties:

- `id` (string): a server-minted UUID. It carries no type prefix of any kind.
- `created_at` (string): the ISO 8601 UTC instant, with three fractional digits, at which the row was created.
- `updated_at` (string): the ISO 8601 UTC instant of the row's most recent modification. Any mutation refreshes it, including a mutation that only changes a derived counter.
- `version` (integer): a counter that increments by exactly 1 on every mutation of that row, including mutations caused by a cascade from another resource rather than by a request aimed at this row.

Four record types are append-only observations rather than mutable resources, and they carry only `id` and `created_at` — both `updated_at` and `version` are absent from them entirely: census tracts, eligibility tests, annual compliance tests and job reports. A census tract in particular is immutable configuration: its statistics and its whole derived distress profile are computed once, when it is registered, and never revised.

### 2.7. Parallel State Dimensions and Cascading Consistency

Two resources in this system carry two independent state dimensions on the same row, and confusing them is the most common modelling error. A qualified equity investment has a `deployment_status` that tracks where its cash sits and a `compliance_status` that tracks how its testing has gone; the two evolve independently and reach terminal values independently. A voided investment stays `COMPLIANT` forever, and an investment whose compliance has been clawed back may still be sitting in `deployment_status` `DEPLOYING`. An allocation award likewise carries a `lifecycle_status` — the contractual dimension — and a fully derived `compliance_posture` that gates new activity. An `ACTIVE` award may be `RESTRICTED`, and a `RESTRICTED` award still accepts sub-allocations on investments it has already issued.

Consistency is immediate, not eventual. Whenever a request changes a fact that a derived value depends on, every dependent value is recomputed inside that same request, and every row so touched has its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` incremented by 1. This is what makes the cascades in §3 multi-hop: a failing eligibility test does not merely record a test result, it stops sub-allocations from qualifying, which changes the substantially-all measurement of each parent investment, which can move that investment to `AT_RISK`, which changes its award's at-risk count, which can move the award's posture from `UNRESTRICTED` to `RESTRICTED` and stop new issuance — all in the one request. Cascades are conditional: rows already in a terminal state that the cascade does not name are skipped, and skipping them means their `version` is not incremented.

Derived counters are never sticky. `compliance_posture` in particular is recomputed from scratch after every request that changes any investment's `compliance_status`, any agreement's `status`, or the CDE's `certification_status`, so curing an investment back to `COMPLIANT` can move its award from `RESTRICTED` back to `UNRESTRICTED` in the same request that cured it, with no operator action.

### 2.8. Path Resolution and Referential Lookup Order

When a path contains a resource identifier, the identifier is resolved before any body field is examined, and where a path contains more than one identifier they are resolved strictly left to right. The first identifier that resolves to nothing decides the response, and each resource has its own not-found code: `ERR_CDE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ALLOCATION_AWARD_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CENSUS_TRACT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_QALICB_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INVESTMENT_FUND_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_QEI_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_QLICI_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REINVESTMENT_OBLIGATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CBA_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_UNWIND_NOT_FOUND`, all HTTP 404.

Foreign keys supplied in a request body are resolved after path identifiers and in the order in which the operation declares its input fields, again reporting the first failure with the not-found code of the missing resource's own type. A tract named as another tract's adjoining low-income community is resolved the same way and reports `ERR_CENSUS_TRACT_NOT_FOUND`, and the sub-allocation named as the target of a reinvestment reports `ERR_QLICI_NOT_FOUND`.

### 2.9. Optimistic Concurrency on Allocation Authority

One write in this system is guarded by optimistic concurrency control, because one write is legitimately repeatable and therefore cannot be protected by a state precondition. An award may accept many qualified equity investments, so a second issuance breaks no state rule; what makes it wrong is that the client sized the investment against an allocation authority figure and a posture that another issuance, a void, a cascade from a failed eligibility test or an annual test has since changed.

Issuing a qualified equity investment therefore requires `expected_allocation_award_version` as a mandatory, non-optional input field carrying the `version` of the target allocation award as the client last observed it. If that value does not equal the award's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_AWARD_VERSION` (HTTP 412) and nothing is persisted. The client's remedy is to re-read the award, re-size the investment against the authority that is actually left, and retry.

No other operation in this specification takes a version field, and no other operation performs a version comparison.

### 2.10. The Order of Rejection

Validation is a strictly ordered ladder, not a set. Rules are evaluated in ascending priority, the first violated rule alone decides the response, and a rejected request persists nothing at all — no partial cascade, no counter movement, no version increment. The ordering itself is a domain commitment: this is a tax-compliance system, so regulatory eligibility outranks referential wiring, which outranks temporal windows, which outranks structural constraints, and money checks always run last. A request that both draws on exhausted allocation authority and targets an uncertified business reports the eligibility failure, never the funding failure.

The complete ladder, in evaluation order:

1. `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` are both present and non-empty, on every operation — otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. `X-User-Role` names one of the five declared roles, on every operation — otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
3. The acting role is authorised for the target operation, on every operation — otherwise `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED`.
4. On every write operation, every required input field is present, of its declared type, inside any declared range, and every enumerated field carries a declared value — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. Unknown fields, and attempts to set server-managed or derived fields from the body, are silently ignored rather than rejected.
5. On every list operation, `limit` and `offset` are valid — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION`.
6. Every path identifier resolves, left to right, on every operation that has one — otherwise the matching not-found code.
7. Every foreign key in the body resolves, in input-declaration order, on award announcement, census-tract registration, business registration, investment issuance, sub-allocation deployment and agreement creation — otherwise the matching not-found code.
8. The acting user owns the target resource, on every write acted on by a `CDESponsor`, a `TaxCreditInvestor` or a `QALICBSponsor` — otherwise `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`.
9. Segregation of duties holds on an annual compliance test — otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`.
10. `expected_allocation_award_version` equals the award's current `version` on investment issuance — otherwise `ERR_STALE_AWARD_VERSION`.
11. `certification_number` is unique across community development entities and `tract_code` is unique across census tracts — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_CERTIFICATION_NUMBER` or `ERR_DUPLICATE_TRACT_CODE`.
12. The CDE owning the target award is still `CERTIFIED`, on award announcement, allocation-agreement signing, investment issuance and sub-allocation deployment — otherwise `ERR_CDE_NOT_CERTIFIED`.
13. The target award's `lifecycle_status` is `ACTIVE`, on investment issuance, sub-allocation deployment, agreement creation and agency reporting — otherwise `ERR_AWARD_NOT_ACTIVE`.
14. The target award's `compliance_posture` permits the operation — `RESTRICTED` blocks new investment issuance, `FROZEN` blocks both investment issuance and sub-allocation deployment — otherwise `ERR_AWARD_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`.
15. The source state permits the requested transition, on every action operation — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.
16. The target investment's `compliance_status` is not `RECAPTURED`, on sub-allocation deployment, cure deployment, unwind opening and reinvestment — otherwise `ERR_QEI_RECAPTURED`.
17. An annual test does not skip ahead, on an annual compliance test — otherwise `ERR_TEST_SEQUENCE_INVALID`.
18. No existing annual test of the same investment already covers the requested window, on an annual compliance test — otherwise `ERR_TEST_WINDOW_OVERLAP`.
19. The requested reporting period does not overlap an existing period of the same agreement, on job-report filing — otherwise `ERR_REPORT_PERIOD_OVERLAP`.
20. The project company is not already inside another award's seven-year compliance period, on sub-allocation deployment — otherwise `ERR_QALICB_COMPLIANCE_PERIOD_CONFLICT`.
21. The target investment has no live unwind row, on unwind opening — otherwise `ERR_UNWIND_ALREADY_OPEN`.
22. The referenced census tract is eligible to host a sub-allocation, and a contiguity claim points at a genuine low-income community, on census-tract registration and business registration — otherwise `ERR_TRACT_NOT_LIC_ELIGIBLE`.
23. The target business's `eligibility_status` is `CERTIFIED`, on sub-allocation deployment — otherwise `ERR_QALICB_NOT_CERTIFIED`.
24. The target business's `eligibility_status` is not `DISQUALIFIED`, on sub-allocation deployment, agreement creation and cure deployment — otherwise `ERR_QALICB_DISQUALIFIED`.
25. The business's community benefits agreement is not `BREACHED`, on sub-allocation deployment — otherwise `ERR_CBA_BREACHED`.
26. The business already has an agreement bound to the funding investment's award, on sub-allocation deployment — otherwise `ERR_CBA_MISSING`.
27. The investment fund backing a new investment is `FUNDED`, on investment issuance — otherwise `ERR_FUND_NOT_FUNDED`.
28. The reinvestment is inside the obligation's deadline, on reinvestment — otherwise `ERR_REINVESTMENT_DEADLINE_PASSED`.
29. The logical clock is inside the investment's deployment window, on sub-allocation deployment — otherwise `ERR_DEPLOYMENT_WINDOW_CLOSED`.
30. The logical clock is inside the option window, on option exercise — otherwise `ERR_OPTION_WINDOW_CLOSED`.
31. The business's `state_code` lies inside the award's committed service area, on sub-allocation deployment — otherwise `ERR_OUTSIDE_SERVICE_AREA`.
32. The investment has completed all seven compliance years, on unwind opening — otherwise `ERR_COMPLIANCE_PERIOD_NOT_COMPLETE`.
33. A fund closing received both capital legs in full — otherwise `ERR_CAPITAL_STACK_SHORTFALL`.
34. The requested investment amount fits inside the award's remaining authority and the fund's available capital, on investment issuance — otherwise `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_ALLOCATION_AUTHORITY`.
35. The requested placement fits inside the investment's uninvested cash, on sub-allocation deployment and on reinvestment — otherwise `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_UNINVESTED_CASH`.
36. A repayment does not exceed the sub-allocation's outstanding principal — otherwise `ERR_REPAYMENT_EXCEEDS_PRINCIPAL`.
37. A reinvestment does not exceed what the obligation still owes — otherwise `ERR_REINVESTMENT_EXCEEDS_OBLIGATION`.

Two adjacencies in this ladder are deliberate and are worth stating twice. **Pair (a):** the posture gate at rung 14 sits above the fiscal authority check at rung 34, so an award that is both `RESTRICTED` and out of allocation authority reports `ERR_AWARD_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`, not `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_ALLOCATION_AUTHORITY`. **Pair (b):** the certification gate at rung 23 sits above the deployment-window check at rung 29, so a sub-allocation aimed at a business in `RECERTIFICATION_DUE` after the deployment deadline has passed reports `ERR_QALICB_NOT_CERTIFIED`, not `ERR_DEPLOYMENT_WINDOW_CLOSED`.

### 2.11. Two Conserved Ledgers

Two quantities in this system are conserved exactly, with no rounding ever applied to either, and every request that touches them must leave the invariant true.

**Allocation authority.** An award's `remaining_authority_cents` always equals its `allocation_amount_cents` less the sum of `qei_amount_cents` over every qualified equity investment of that award whose `deployment_status` is anything other than `VOIDED`. Issuance draws authority down and raises `issued_qei_count` by 1; voiding an investment returns exactly the same amount and lowers `issued_qei_count` by 1, with no drift and with no fee retained, so re-issuing the identical amount afterwards must succeed. An investment whose compliance has been clawed back still consumes authority: recapture is a credit clawback, not an authority release. Authority never goes below zero, because a request whose amount exceeds the remaining authority is refused outright and leaves the ledger untouched.

**Investment capital.** A qualified equity investment's `qei_amount_cents` always equals its `permitted_fees_cents` plus its `uninvested_cash_cents` plus the sum of `outstanding_principal_cents` over its sub-allocations whose `status` is `DEPLOYED`, `PARTIALLY_REPAID` or `NON_QUALIFYING`, plus principal that has been repaid and not yet redeployed. Capital only ever moves between two buckets. Deploying a sub-allocation moves principal out of uninvested cash and into outstanding principal. Repaying principal moves it back the other way and opens a reinvestment obligation for the returned amount. Redeploying under an obligation moves it out of uninvested cash and into outstanding principal again. A full deploy-then-repay round trip therefore returns `uninvested_cash_cents` to its exact pre-deployment value, and uninvested cash never goes below zero. Repayment never touches `permitted_fees_cents`, so the fee bucket is invariant for the whole life of the investment.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. The Certifying Agency, the Allocatee and the Allocation Award

Everything begins with certification. The certifying agency records a community development entity by supplying its `legal_name` (1 to 120 characters), the `certification_number` the agency has issued (1 to 40 characters, and unique across all entities — a repeat is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CERTIFICATION_NUMBER`), and the geographic scope it is certified to serve as `service_area_scope`, which is one of `NATIONAL`, `MULTI_STATE`, `STATEWIDE` or `LOCAL`. A new entity starts with `certification_status` `CERTIFIED`, with `decertified_at` and `decertification_reason` both null, and with `award_count` at 0. That counter tracks how many allocation awards name this entity, at any point in their lifecycle.

Announcing an allocation award is likewise the agency's act. The payload names the allocatee through `cde_id`, the allocation round through `award_year` (an integer from 2020 through 2035, and no entity may hold two awards for the same round — the pair of allocatee and round year must be unique), the authority granted as `allocation_amount_cents` (at least 100000000 cents, that is one million dollars), the states the award commits the entity to serve as `service_area_state_codes` (between 1 and 10 entries, each exactly two uppercase letters, with no duplicates), the community-targeting commitment as `min_severe_distress_share_pct` (a percentage from 0.00 through 100.00 with 2 decimal places), and the compliance-measurement convention as `substantially_all_method`, which is either `DIRECT_TRACING` or `AGGREGATE_BALANCE` and is fixed for the award's whole life. The award opens with `lifecycle_status` `ANNOUNCED` and `compliance_posture` `UNRESTRICTED`; `remaining_authority_cents` is initialised to the full `allocation_amount_cents`; `issued_qei_count`, `at_risk_qei_count`, `recaptured_qei_count` and `cba_breach_count` all start at 0, `severe_distress_share_pct` starts at 0.00, and `last_agency_report_year` starts null. Announcement also raises the allocatee's `award_count` by 1 and increments that entity's `version`. An award may only be announced to an entity that is still `CERTIFIED`; otherwise the answer is `ERR_CDE_NOT_CERTIFIED`.

An announced award grants nothing until the allocation agreement is executed. Signing it — an act reserved to the `CDESponsor` that *is* the allocatee, and refused with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` for any other entity — carries a single field, `signatory_name`, and moves the award from `ANNOUNCED` to `ACTIVE`, stamping `allocation_agreement_signed_at` from the logical clock and recording the signatory. The award's `compliance_posture` is re-derived in the same request. A second attempt to sign an award that is no longer `ANNOUNCED` is `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`. Until this signature exists, no qualified equity investment can be issued against the award at all: the agreement *is* the commitment on service area, targeted distress and community outcomes, and issuance without it would be a commitment-free draw on public authority.

The `compliance_posture` dimension is fully derived and is recomputed, from scratch, after every request that changes any qualified equity investment's `compliance_status`, any community benefits agreement's `status`, or the allocatee's `certification_status`. Its three clauses are evaluated top-down and the first matching clause wins. If the allocatee's `certification_status` is `DECERTIFIED`, or the award has at least one investment whose `compliance_status` is `RECAPTURED`, the posture is `FROZEN`, which bars both new investment issuance and new sub-allocation deployment. Otherwise, if the award has at least two investments whose `compliance_status` is `AT_RISK`, or at least one community benefits agreement in `BREACHED`, the posture is `RESTRICTED`, which bars new investment issuance while still permitting sub-allocations from investments already issued. Otherwise the posture is `UNRESTRICTED`. Because the derivation is stateless, curing an at-risk investment back to `COMPLIANT` lowers the at-risk count and can return the award from `RESTRICTED` to `UNRESTRICTED` inside the very request that cured it, with no operator action; the posture is never sticky. Note carefully that a shortfall against `min_severe_distress_share_pct` is *reported* but never restricting: it flags the annual agency report and refuses no request.

Annual reporting to the certifying agency is the allocatee's obligation and requires the award to be `ACTIVE`. The `CDESponsor` supplies a single `report_year`, and the server answers with a snapshot: the `allocation_award_id` reported on, the `report_year`, `issued_qei_count` as the count of the award's investments whose `deployment_status` is anything other than `VOIDED`, `deployed_principal_cents` as the sum of `outstanding_principal_cents` over the sub-allocations of those investments whose `status` is `DEPLOYED` or `PARTIALLY_REPAID`, `qualifying_qalicb_count` as the number of *distinct* project companies funded by those same sub-allocations, the freshly recomputed `severe_distress_share_pct`, a `severe_distress_shortfall` flag that is true exactly when that share sits strictly below the award's `min_severe_distress_share_pct`, and the award's derived `at_risk_qei_count`, `recaptured_qei_count` and `cba_breach_count` copied across. Filing sets `last_agency_report_year` to the reported year and increments the award's `version`.

Decertification is the agency's most consequential act, and it is the first of three recapture triggers. It applies only to an entity still in `CERTIFIED`, takes a `decertification_reason`, and moves the entity to `DECERTIFIED` while stamping `decertified_at` and copying the reason across. From there the cascade runs three hops in one request. **Hop one:** every award of that entity whose `lifecycle_status` is `ANNOUNCED` or `ACTIVE` has its posture re-derived, and because the allocatee is no longer certified the derivation returns `FROZEN`; an `ACTIVE` award whose `issued_qei_count` is 0, however, has nothing to claw back and instead moves `ACTIVE` to `TERMINATED` with its `remaining_authority_cents` zeroed. **Hop two:** because those awards became `FROZEN`, every investment on them whose `compliance_status` is `COMPLIANT` or `AT_RISK`, and whose `deployment_status` is neither `VOIDED` nor `UNWOUND`, moves to `RECAPTURED` with `recapture_trigger_type` set to `CDE_DECERTIFICATION` and `recapture_exposure_cents` computed as described in §3.8. **Hop three:** because those investments changed compliance state, each award's `recaptured_qei_count` and `at_risk_qei_count` are recomputed, and the at-risk count falls as rows leave `AT_RISK`. The cascade is conditional throughout: awards already `COMPLIANCE_PERIOD_CLOSED` or `TERMINATED` are skipped, and investments already `RECAPTURED`, `RELEASED`, `VOIDED` or `UNWOUND` are skipped. The entity's own `version` increments by 1, as does the `version` of every award and every investment actually touched. `DECERTIFIED` is terminal: nothing returns an entity to certification.

### 3.2. Mapping Distress: The Census Tract Register

Location eligibility is settled before any deal is structured, in an immutable register the agency maintains. A tract is registered with its `tract_code` (exactly 11 digits, and unique across the register — a repeat is `ERR_DUPLICATE_TRACT_CODE`), the `state_code` it sits in (exactly two uppercase letters), its `poverty_rate_pct` (0.00 through 100.00), its `mfi_ratio_pct` — median family income expressed as a percentage of the applicable area median, from 0.00 through 300.00 — and its `unemployment_ratio`, the tract unemployment rate divided by the national average, from 0.00 through 10.00. Each of these three statistics carries 2 decimal places. Optionally the payload may name an adjoining tract through `contiguous_lic_tract_id`, which points at another row of the same register; when omitted the field is null.

Registration computes the tract's entire distress profile once and forever. The statutory low-income-community test is a disjunction of two prongs: `is_low_income_community` is true when the `poverty_rate_pct` is at least 20.00, or when the `mfi_ratio_pct` is at most 80.00. Contiguity relief exists only for tracts that fail that test: `qualifies_by_contiguity` is true when the tract is not itself a low-income community, *and* an adjoining tract has been named through `contiguous_lic_tract_id`, *and* the tract's own `poverty_rate_pct` is at least 15.00. The named adjoining tract must itself have `is_low_income_community` true; a contiguity claim that points at a tract which does not qualify on its own statistics is refused with `ERR_TRACT_NOT_LIC_ELIGIBLE`. The gate that the rest of the system reads is `eligible_for_qlici`, which is true when the tract is a low-income community or qualifies by contiguity.

Targeted distress is measured twice over, once as a count and once as a score, because the two answer different questions. `severe_criteria_met_count` counts how many of three independent criteria the tract meets, and is therefore an integer from 0 through 3: the `poverty_rate_pct` being at least 30.00 counts as one, the `mfi_ratio_pct` being at most 60.00 counts as one, and the `unemployment_ratio` being at least 1.50 counts as one. The `distress_score` is a weighted blend of three normalised factors. The poverty factor is the `poverty_rate_pct` divided by 30.00, capped at 1. The income factor is the amount by which the `mfi_ratio_pct` falls short of 80.00 — floored at zero, so a tract at or above 80.00 contributes nothing — divided by 20.00 and capped at 1. The unemployment factor is the amount by which the `unemployment_ratio` exceeds 1.00 — again floored at zero — divided by 0.50 and capped at 1. Each factor is clamped into the range zero through one *before* it is weighted, never afterwards. The score is then 40 times the poverty factor plus 35 times the income factor plus 25 times the unemployment factor, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places, and finally capped at 100.00.

The `distress_tier` follows from both measures, evaluated in order, and it is where the two gates bite together. A tract that is not `eligible_for_qlici` is `INELIGIBLE` regardless of its statistics. Otherwise, a tract meeting at least 2 of the severe criteria *and* scoring at least 75.00 is `SEVERE`. Otherwise, a tract meeting at least 1 severe criterion *and* scoring at least 50.00 is `HIGH`. Everything else eligible is `MODERATE`. A tract can therefore score very high and still land in `MODERATE` when it meets zero severe criteria — a tract at poverty 28.00, income ratio 62.00 and unemployment ratio 1.40 scores 88.83 with a severe-criteria count of zero, and is `MODERATE`. Only the `SEVERE` tier counts toward an award's severe-distress share.

Because a tract is configuration rather than a live resource, it is never revised after registration; its adjacency edge remains readable as a filter on the register.

### 3.3. The Project Company and the Six-Prong Eligibility Test

A `QALICBSponsor` registers the qualified active low-income community business that will receive capital, naming the `census_tract_id` of its principal place of business, its `legal_name` (1 to 120 characters), the `state_code` of that principal place of business (exactly two uppercase letters), and its `naics_code` (exactly six digits). The `sponsor_user_id` is stamped from the acting `X-User-ID` and is never read from the body — any value supplied there is ignored. The tract named must be `eligible_for_qlici`; a business whose principal location does not qualify is refused with `ERR_TRACT_NOT_LIC_ELIGIBLE`. A new business starts with `eligibility_status` `PENDING`, `last_certified_at` and `latest_test_result` both null, `consecutive_failed_test_count` at 0, and `funding_qlici_count` at 0 — that counter tracks how many of its sub-allocations have a `status` other than `REPAID`.

Eligibility is established by a six-prong test that only a `ComplianceReviewer` may run, against the business named by `qalicb_id` in the path. The reviewer supplies five measured percentages and one location determination: `gross_income_lic_pct`, the share of gross income from the active conduct of business inside low-income communities, which must be at least 50.00; `services_lic_pct`, the share of services performed by employees inside low-income communities, at least 40.00; `tangible_property_lic_pct`, the share of tangible property located inside low-income communities, at least 40.00; `collectibles_pct`, the share of assets held as collectibles other than inventory, at most 5.00; `nonqualified_financial_property_pct`, the share held as nonqualified financial property, at most 5.00; and `location_prong_passed`, which mirrors the `eligible_for_qlici` determination of the business's tract as it stands at the instant of the test. All bounds are inclusive and compared at 2 decimal places: gross income of exactly 50.00 passes and 49.99 fails; collectibles of exactly 5.00 pass and 5.01 fails. Each test row records the `performed_by_user_id` of the reviewer and its `performed_at` instant from the logical clock.

Every prong is evaluated independently and all six must pass for the `result` to be `PASS`; otherwise it is `FAIL`. The `failed_prongs` array names every prong that failed, always in the fixed order `LOCATION`, `GROSS_INCOME`, `SERVICES`, `TANGIBLE_PROPERTY`, `COLLECTIBLES`, `NQFP`, whatever order the inputs arrived in, and it is an empty array on a pass. Only the percentages on this request decide the result; earlier tests influence nothing except the consecutive-failure counter.

A pass certifies the business. From `PENDING`, from `RECERTIFICATION_DUE`, from `FAILED`, or from `CERTIFIED` itself, a passing test lands the business in `CERTIFIED`, sets `last_certified_at` to the logical clock, sets `latest_test_result` to `PASS`, resets `consecutive_failed_test_count` to 0, and increments the business's `version`. A failure raises `consecutive_failed_test_count` by 1 and sets `latest_test_result` to `FAIL`; if the raised counter is still below 2 the business lands in `FAILED`, and once the raised counter reaches 2 the business lands in `DISQUALIFIED`. `DISQUALIFIED` is terminal — two consecutive failures bar the business permanently, and no test, cure or agreement returns it.

A failure is never confined to the test row, because outstanding capital in a business that has stopped qualifying stops counting. The **disqualification cascade** runs three hops. **Hop one:** every sub-allocation of this business whose `status` is `DEPLOYED` or `PARTIALLY_REPAID` moves to `NON_QUALIFYING` with `non_qualifying_since_at` stamped from the logical clock, and its principal leaves every substantially-all numerator. **Hop two:** because those rows stopped qualifying, each parent investment recomputes its `substantially_all_pct_direct`, and any such investment whose `compliance_status` is `COMPLIANT` and whose recomputed percentage falls below 85.00 moves to `AT_RISK` with `cure_deadline_at` set to the logical clock advanced by 180 days. **Hop three:** because those investments entered `AT_RISK`, each parent award's `at_risk_qei_count` rises and its `compliance_posture` is re-derived, so an award reaching two at-risk investments moves from `UNRESTRICTED` to `RESTRICTED` and stops accepting new issuance. The business's `version` increments, as does that of every sub-allocation, investment and award actually touched. When the failure is the second consecutive one and the business reaches `DISQUALIFIED`, the same three-hop cascade runs over any sub-allocation still in `DEPLOYED` or `PARTIALLY_REPAID`, and additionally every community benefits agreement of that business whose `status` is `ACTIVE` or `CURE_REQUIRED` moves to `BREACHED` with `breached_at` stamped, after which each parent award's `cba_breach_count` is recomputed and its posture re-derived.

Recovery is symmetrical and equally cascading. A passing test on a business that was in `FAILED` triggers the **requalification cascade**. **Hop one:** every sub-allocation of the business whose `status` is `NON_QUALIFYING` returns to the state its repayment history implies — to `REPAID` when its `outstanding_principal_cents` is 0, otherwise to `PARTIALLY_REPAID` when its `total_repaid_cents` is greater than 0, otherwise to `DEPLOYED` — and `non_qualifying_since_at` is cleared to null so the principal re-enters every substantially-all numerator. **Hop two:** because those rows qualify again, each parent investment recomputes its `substantially_all_pct_direct`, and an investment that is `AT_RISK`, whose recomputed percentage has reached at least 85.00, and whose `cure_deadline_at` has not yet been passed by the logical clock, returns to `COMPLIANT` with `cure_deadline_at` cleared to null. **Hop three:** because those investments left `AT_RISK`, each award's `at_risk_qei_count` is recomputed and its posture re-derived, so an award may return from `RESTRICTED` to `UNRESTRICTED`. Every row touched has its `version` incremented.

Every one of these interim recomputations uses the direct-tracing variant of the substantially-all measurement, whatever the award's `substantially_all_method` says. The aggregate-balance variant needs a closed measurement window and is computed only by an annual compliance test.

Certification also goes stale. A business in `CERTIFIED` that is funded by at least one sub-allocation of a tested investment whose `status` is `DEPLOYED` or `PARTIALLY_REPAID`, and whose `last_certified_at` lies strictly more than 365 days before the logical clock, is swept to `RECERTIFICATION_DUE` when that investment's annual compliance test runs. The comparison is strict: at exactly 365 days the certification is still fresh and the business is not swept. While a business sits in `RECERTIFICATION_DUE`, principal already outstanding keeps qualifying, but no new sub-allocation may be deployed to it — that attempt is `ERR_QALICB_NOT_CERTIFIED`, exactly as for a business still in `PENDING` or one in `FAILED`. A further passing test closes the cycle back to `CERTIFIED`.

### 3.4. Structuring the Transaction: The Fund, the Leverage Loan and the Equity Investment

A `TaxCreditInvestor` forms the single-purpose investment fund that will hold both legs of the capital stack. The fund is described by its `legal_name` (1 to 120 characters), the equity the investor commits as `equity_contribution_cents` (zero or more), the `leverage_lender_name` (1 to 120 characters), the committed loan principal as `leverage_loan_principal_cents` (zero or more), and the loan's interest rate as `leverage_loan_rate_bps`, an integer count of basis points from 0 through 5000. The `investor_user_id` is stamped from the acting `X-User-ID`, and every later ownership check for this role compares against that stored value. A newly formed fund is in `status` `FORMED`, with `equity_received_cents`, `leverage_received_cents`, `leverage_loan_outstanding_cents`, `capital_available_cents` and `deployed_to_qei_cents` all at 0 and `closed_at` null.

Financial closing is the same investor's act and is where committed money becomes received money. The request reports `equity_received_cents` and `leverage_received_cents`, and both must match their commitments exactly: the equity received must equal `equity_contribution_cents` and the leverage received must equal `leverage_loan_principal_cents`. A short leg on either side is refused with `ERR_CAPITAL_STACK_SHORTFALL`, and a fund that is no longer `FORMED` cannot be closed again — that is `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`. On success the fund moves `FORMED` to `FUNDED`, stamps `closed_at` from the logical clock, records both received amounts, sets `leverage_loan_outstanding_cents` equal to the leverage received, sets `capital_available_cents` to the equity received plus the leverage received less anything already deployed, and increments the fund's `version`.

Only then can the allocatee place capital. Issuing a qualified equity investment is a `CDESponsor` act naming the `allocation_award_id` whose authority it consumes, the `investment_fund_id` that makes the investment, the gross `qei_amount_cents` (at least 10000000 cents, that is one hundred thousand dollars), and `expected_allocation_award_version` as described in §2.9. The backing fund must be in `FUNDED` — its equity and leverage proceeds both received and closing run — or the request is refused with `ERR_FUND_NOT_FUNDED`. The award must be `ACTIVE`, its allocatee still `CERTIFIED`, and its posture neither `RESTRICTED` nor `FROZEN`. Finally, the gross amount must fit inside both the award's `remaining_authority_cents` and the fund's `capital_available_cents`; exceeding either is `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_ALLOCATION_AUTHORITY`, and the ledgers are left untouched.

Pricing the permitted reserves is a tiered calculation whose evaluation order is pinned. The tier is selected from the **gross** `qei_amount_cents`, never from a net or post-fee figure: a gross below 500000000 cents draws a `reserve_bps` of 500, a gross that is at least 500000000 but below 1500000000 draws 400, and a gross of 1500000000 or more draws 300. The tier bounds are half-open, so 499999999 sits at 500 basis points, 500000000 sits at 400, 1499999999 sits at 400, and 1500000000 sits at 300. The `permitted_fees_cents` are then the gross amount multiplied by the chosen basis points, divided by 10000, rounded half-up to whole cents in that single step and no other. The `net_deployable_cents` are the gross amount less those permitted fees, and `uninvested_cash_cents` opens at exactly that net figure. Deriving the tier from a net figure is the classic error and produces different money: a gross of 500000000 sits in the 400-basis-point tier and yields fees of 20000000 and a net of 480000000, where a net-derived tier would fall back to 500 basis points and wrongly yield fees of 25000000 and a net of 475000000. The permitted-fee bucket is never deployable into sub-allocations.

Issuance also fixes the investment's clocks and counters. `issued_at` is stamped from the logical clock and anchors every compliance-year window for the next seven years; `issued_by_user_id` records the acting sponsor; `deployment_deadline_at` is the issuance instant advanced by 365 days, and it is the last instant at which any sub-allocation may be placed from this investment. The investment opens with `deployment_status` `ISSUED` and `compliance_status` `COMPLIANT`, with `compliance_years_completed` at 0, `substantially_all_pct_direct` and `substantially_all_pct_aggregate` both at 0.00, `accrued_unpaid_fee_cents` at 0, `recapture_trigger_type` at `NONE`, `recapture_exposure_cents` at 0, and `cure_deadline_at` and `voided_at` both null. In the same request the award's `remaining_authority_cents` falls by the gross amount, its `issued_qei_count` rises by 1 and its `version` increments; the fund's `deployed_to_qei_cents` rises by the same amount, its `capital_available_cents` falls by it, the fund moves `FUNDED` to `QEI_MADE`, and its `version` increments.

An issuance can be unwound before any cash moves. Voiding an investment is the compensating action for issuing it: the `CDESponsor` supplies a `void_reason`, and the operation is permitted only while the `deployment_status` is `ISSUED`, no sub-allocation row exists against the investment, and `compliance_years_completed` is 0; anything else is `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`. The void stamps `voided_at`, zeroes `uninvested_cash_cents`, `permitted_fees_cents` and `net_deployable_cents`, returns the gross amount to the award's `remaining_authority_cents` exactly and lowers `issued_qei_count` by 1, and returns the same amount to the fund by lowering `deployed_to_qei_cents` and raising `capital_available_cents`. When the voided row was the only investment on that fund whose `deployment_status` was not `VOIDED`, the fund moves `QEI_MADE` back to `FUNDED` so a replacement investment can be made. The investment's, the award's and the fund's `version` all increment. No fee is retained anywhere, so re-issuing the identical amount immediately afterwards must succeed.

### 3.5. Deploying Sub-Allocations into the Community

Placing capital into a project is the deal's whole point, and it is the most heavily gated operation in the system. A `CDESponsor` deploys a qualified low-income community investment by naming the funding investment through `qei_id`, the receiving business through `qalicb_id`, the tranche through `loan_class` — `A` is the leveraged senior note and `B` is the equity-equivalent note — and the money through `principal_cents`, which must be at least 100000 cents. The new row stamps `deployed_at` from the logical clock, opens in `status` `DEPLOYED` with `outstanding_principal_cents` equal to `principal_cents`, `total_repaid_cents` at 0, `allocated_fee_cents` at 0, `non_qualifying_since_at` null, and `created_by_cure` false.

Before any of that is written, the request must clear the eligibility gates in the order §2.10 fixes. The receiving business must be in `eligibility_status` `CERTIFIED` — `PENDING`, `RECERTIFICATION_DUE` and `FAILED` all block new deployment with `ERR_QALICB_NOT_CERTIFIED`, and a business in `DISQUALIFIED` is barred permanently with `ERR_QALICB_DISQUALIFIED`. Its community benefits agreement must not be `BREACHED`, or the answer is `ERR_CBA_BREACHED`. There must in fact *be* an agreement binding that business to the same award as the funding investment; a business with no such agreement is refused with `ERR_CBA_MISSING`, which is what forces the community-outcome commitment to exist before the money moves rather than after.

Two structural gates then apply. A project company may not sit inside two awards' compliance periods at once: for every existing non-`VOIDED` investment from a *different* award that already funds this business, the interval that starts 30 days before that investment's `issued_at` and ends 30 days after that investment's `issued_at` advanced by 2555 days must not contain this investment's `issued_at`. The interval's lower bound is inclusive and its upper bound is exclusive, and sub-allocations funded from the *same* award are exempt from the check entirely; a collision is `ERR_QALICB_COMPLIANCE_PERIOD_CONFLICT`. Separately, the business's `state_code` must appear in the award's `service_area_state_codes`, or the deployment is refused with `ERR_OUTSIDE_SERVICE_AREA` — a check skipped altogether when the allocatee's `service_area_scope` is `NATIONAL`, since a nationally scoped entity has committed to no particular state list.

Timing and cash close the ladder. The logical clock must be at or before the investment's `deployment_deadline_at`, that is within 365 days of issuance; past that instant new principal cannot be placed and the answer is `ERR_DEPLOYMENT_WINDOW_CLOSED`. And the placement must fit: `principal_cents` may not exceed the investment's `uninvested_cash_cents`, or the request is refused with `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_UNINVESTED_CASH`.

Deployment drives the investment's cash-placement dimension. From `ISSUED`, a placement that leaves uninvested cash behind moves the investment to `DEPLOYING`, and one that consumes the last uninvested cent moves it straight to `FULLY_DEPLOYED`. From `DEPLOYING`, a further placement that still leaves a positive remainder keeps it in `DEPLOYING`, and one that brings the remainder to exactly zero moves it to `FULLY_DEPLOYED` with `uninvested_cash_cents` set to 0. In every case the placed principal leaves `uninvested_cash_cents` and appears as outstanding principal, exactly preserving the capital ledger of §2.11. The receiving business's `funding_qlici_count` rises by 1. The investment's `version`, the business's `version` and the award's `version` all increment.

Each deployment recomputes the investment's `substantially_all_pct_direct`, and this is the measurement the whole compliance regime turns on. Under the direct-tracing convention, the qualifying set is the investment's sub-allocations whose `status` is `DEPLOYED` or `PARTIALLY_REPAID`; rows in `NON_QUALIFYING` and rows in `REPAID` contribute nothing. The percentage is 100 multiplied by the sum of `outstanding_principal_cents` across that qualifying set, divided by the investment's gross `qei_amount_cents` less its `permitted_fees_cents`, and rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. Only the percentage is rounded; the underlying sums stay exact in integer cents. The compliance threshold is 85.00 and it is inclusive — exactly 85.00 passes, and 84.99 fails. A deployment that lifts a struggling investment back over that line can move its `compliance_status` from `AT_RISK` to `COMPLIANT`.

Deployment also moves the award's community-targeting measure. The `severe_distress_share_pct` is recomputed over currently qualifying deployments only: take every sub-allocation whose `status` is `DEPLOYED` or `PARTIALLY_REPAID` under every non-`VOIDED` investment of the award, sum their `outstanding_principal_cents`, and separately sum the same figure restricted to sub-allocations whose receiving business sits in a census tract whose `distress_tier` is `SEVERE`. The share is 100 multiplied by the severe sum, divided by the total sum, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places; when the total sum is 0 the share is 0.00. A severe outstanding balance of 60000000 cents against a total of 95000000 cents yields 63.16. This figure is reported to the agency and, when it falls below the award's `min_severe_distress_share_pct`, flags the annual report — but it refuses nothing and restricts nothing. The award's `compliance_posture` is re-derived in the same request.

### 3.6. Returned Capital: Repayment, the Reinvestment Obligation and Redeployment

Principal that comes back does not end the obligation; it restarts it. When a `CDESponsor` records a principal repayment on a sub-allocation, the request carries a single `amount_cents`, which must be greater than 0 and may not exceed that sub-allocation's `outstanding_principal_cents` — an excess is `ERR_REPAYMENT_EXCEEDS_PRINCIPAL`. Three hops follow in the one request.

**Hop one** moves the sub-allocation: `total_repaid_cents` rises by the amount, `outstanding_principal_cents` falls by it, and the row moves from `DEPLOYED` to `PARTIALLY_REPAID` when the amount is strictly less than the outstanding balance, or straight to `REPAID` when the amount equals it exactly, in which case `outstanding_principal_cents` becomes 0 and `total_repaid_cents` becomes the full original `principal_cents`. A further partial repayment on a row already in `PARTIALLY_REPAID` keeps it there, and a final one takes it to `REPAID`. `REPAID` is terminal, and full repayment is the compensating action for deployment: the investment's capital ledger is preserved exactly, with `uninvested_cash_cents` returning to its pre-deployment value.

**Hop two** returns the cash and opens the redeployment duty: the investment's `uninvested_cash_cents` rises by the repaid amount, and a reinvestment obligation row is created naming the investment through `qei_id` and the repaid sub-allocation through `source_qlici_id`, with `amount_cents` equal to the principal just returned, `reinvested_cents` at 0, `arose_at` stamped from the logical clock, `reinvest_by_at` set to that instant advanced by 365 days, `status` `OPEN`, and `satisfied_at` and `breached_at` both null. The investment's `deployment_status` moves to `REINVESTMENT_PENDING`, whether it was in `DEPLOYING` or in `FULLY_DEPLOYED` beforehand. Each further partial repayment opens a further obligation row for the newly returned amount.

**Hop three** re-measures compliance, because uninvested cash has risen while outstanding qualifying principal has fallen: `substantially_all_pct_direct` is recomputed, and when the recomputed value falls below 85.00 and the investment's `compliance_status` is `COMPLIANT`, the investment moves to `AT_RISK` with `cure_deadline_at` set to the logical clock advanced by 180 days, which in turn raises the award's `at_risk_qei_count` and re-derives its `compliance_posture`. The award's `severe_distress_share_pct` is recomputed as well. The sub-allocation's `version`, the investment's `version` and, where it was touched, the award's `version` all increment by 1.

Redeploying is the mirror image. A `CDESponsor` satisfies an obligation by naming the `target_qlici_id` that will receive the money and the `amount_cents` to move. The target must be a sub-allocation of the *same* investment as the obligation and must be in `status` `DEPLOYED` or `PARTIALLY_REPAID`. The obligation must still be `OPEN`, the logical clock must be at or before its `reinvest_by_at` — a later attempt is `ERR_REINVESTMENT_DEADLINE_PASSED` — the amount must fit inside the investment's `uninvested_cash_cents`, or the answer is `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_UNINVESTED_CASH`, and the obligation's `reinvested_cents` plus the new amount may not exceed its `amount_cents`, or the answer is `ERR_REINVESTMENT_EXCEEDS_OBLIGATION`. A recaptured investment accepts no reinvestment at all: that is `ERR_QEI_RECAPTURED`.

On success the obligation's `reinvested_cents` rises by the amount, the target sub-allocation's `principal_cents` *and* its `outstanding_principal_cents` both rise by the same amount, and the investment's `uninvested_cash_cents` falls by it. While the cumulative `reinvested_cents` remains strictly below the obligation's `amount_cents` the obligation stays `OPEN`; the moment the cumulative figure equals `amount_cents` exactly the obligation becomes `SATISFIED` with `satisfied_at` stamped from the logical clock. Once no obligation on the investment remains `OPEN`, the investment leaves `REINVESTMENT_PENDING`: it returns to `DEPLOYING` when uninvested cash remains greater than 0 after the reinvestment, and moves to `FULLY_DEPLOYED` when the reinvestment brought `uninvested_cash_cents` to exactly 0. In either case `substantially_all_pct_direct` is recomputed, and when it reaches at least 85.00 while the investment is `AT_RISK` and the logical clock is still at or before `cure_deadline_at`, the `compliance_status` returns to `COMPLIANT` with `cure_deadline_at` cleared to null and the award's at-risk count and posture re-derived. The obligation's, the sub-allocation's, the investment's and the award's `version` all increment.

A missed deadline is a different animal, because it is discovered rather than requested. The 365-day reinvestment deadline is never enforced on a read and never enforced by the passage of time alone: an obligation that is still `OPEN` when the logical clock has moved past its `reinvest_by_at` is flipped to `BREACHED`, with `breached_at` stamped, only when the investment's next annual compliance test runs. Those flips are counted into that test's `breached_obligation_count`, and any breach at all forces the test result to `FAIL` regardless of how healthy the governing percentage looks. `SATISFIED` and `BREACHED` are both terminal for an obligation.

### 3.7. Community Outcomes: Agreements, Job Reports and Annual Certification

Capital placed in a distressed tract is expected to produce jobs, and the commitment is written down before the money moves. A `CDESponsor` binds a project company to a community benefits agreement by naming the business through `qalicb_id` and the award whose community-outcome commitments it sits under through `allocation_award_id`, then setting the terms: `target_fte_count`, the committed number of qualifying full-time-equivalent jobs per certification year (at least 1); `wage_floor_cents_per_hour`, the minimum average hourly wage in cents for a reporting period to count toward that commitment (zero or more); `local_hire_pct_target`, the committed minimum share of headcount hired from the service area (0.00 through 100.00 with 2 decimal places); and `measurement_basis`, which is either `HEADCOUNT` or `PAYROLL_HOURS` and governs how achievement is measured for the agreement's whole life. The award must be `ACTIVE` and the business must not be `DISQUALIFIED`. A new agreement stamps `agreement_start_at` from the logical clock — the anchor for every certification-year window — opens in `status` `ACTIVE`, with `consecutive_cure_required_count` at 0, `latest_compliance_pct` and `latest_local_hire_pct` at 0.00, `last_certified_year` null and `breached_at` null.

The sponsor of the business files the evidence. Each job report covers one period, bounded by `period_start_at` and `period_end_at`, where the end instant must be strictly after the start instant, and reports the period's `headcount` (zero or more), the `qualifying_headcount` meeting the agreement's job-quality definition (zero or more, and never greater than `headcount`), the `total_hours` on payroll (zero or more), the `qualifying_hours` attributable to qualifying employees (zero or more, and never greater than `total_hours`), the `local_hires` drawn from the service area (zero or more, and never greater than `headcount`), and the period's `avg_hourly_wage_cents`. At filing time the server evaluates the wage gate once and freezes the answer in `wage_floor_met`, which is true exactly when `avg_hourly_wage_cents` is greater than or equal to the agreement's `wage_floor_cents_per_hour`. The report also records `filed_by_user_id` from the acting header and `filed_at` from the logical clock. Reporting periods are **closed** intervals and may not overlap any existing period of the same agreement: two reports that share even a single endpoint instant collide, there is no buffer between them, and the collision is `ERR_REPORT_PERIOD_OVERLAP`. An agreement that has reached `CLOSED` accepts no further reports, and that attempt is `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.

Certification is the reviewer's act. A `ComplianceReviewer` certifies one year of performance by supplying the `certification_year` being closed and the window bounds `window_start_at` and `window_end_at`. The measured set is every job report of the agreement that falls *entirely* inside the window — its `period_start_at` at or after the window start and its `period_end_at` at or before the window end — taken in ascending `period_start_at` order. Reports whose `wage_floor_met` is false are excluded from the job-quality numerator entirely, because a sponsor may not buy compliance by underpaying, but they are *not* excluded from the local-hire measure.

Achievement is computed under the agreement's own `measurement_basis`. Under `HEADCOUNT`, the `achieved_fte` is the sum of `qualifying_headcount` across the wage-passing reports, and is therefore a whole number of people. Under `PAYROLL_HOURS`, the `achieved_fte` is the sum of `qualifying_hours` across the wage-passing reports divided by 2080 hours — one full-time year — rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. The `compliance_pct` is then 100 multiplied by the `achieved_fte`, divided by the agreement's `target_fte_count`, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. The two bases genuinely disagree, and the disagreement is the point: a part-time-heavy project reports many hours across few heads and scores lower under `HEADCOUNT` than under `PAYROLL_HOURS` — against a target of 25, eighteen qualifying heads and 45000 qualifying hours produce 72.00 percent on the headcount basis and 86.52 percent on the payroll-hours basis.

The `local_hire_pct` spans **every** report in the window, wage-failing ones included: it is 100 multiplied by the sum of `local_hires` across all those reports, divided by the sum of their `headcount`, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places, and it is 0.00 when that total headcount is 0. The response also reports `report_count`, the number of reports inside the window, and `excluded_wage_failing_report_count`, how many of them were dropped from the job-quality numerator by the wage gate.

The `classification` then follows from both measures. When the `compliance_pct` is at least 100.00 *and* the `local_hire_pct` is at least the agreement's `local_hire_pct_target`, the classification is `CERTIFIED`. When the `compliance_pct` is at least 80.00 but below 100.00 and the local-hire measure still meets its target, the classification is `CURE_REQUIRED`. Anything else — a compliance percentage below 80.00, or a local-hire percentage short of target at any compliance level — is `SHORTFALL`. The floors are inclusive: exactly 100.00 is `CERTIFIED`, exactly 80.00 is `CURE_REQUIRED`, and 79.99 is `SHORTFALL`. A window containing no reports at all yields an `achieved_fte` of 0, a `compliance_pct` of 0.00, a `local_hire_pct` of 0.00 and therefore an immediate `SHORTFALL`. The response echoes the `community_benefits_agreement_id`, the `certification_year`, the governing `measurement_basis`, and the agreement's `resulting_status` after the classification has been applied.

Applying the classification drives the agreement's own state machine, and every path writes `latest_compliance_pct` and `latest_local_hire_pct` from the figures just computed. A `CURE_REQUIRED` classification against an `ACTIVE` agreement moves it to `CURE_REQUIRED` and raises `consecutive_cure_required_count` by 1. A `CERTIFIED` classification resets `consecutive_cure_required_count` to 0 and sets `last_certified_year` to the certified year, returning an agreement in `CURE_REQUIRED` to `ACTIVE` and leaving an `ACTIVE` agreement where it is — this is the intended cycle from `ACTIVE` to `CURE_REQUIRED` and back. A `SHORTFALL` moves the agreement to `BREACHED` with `breached_at` stamped from the logical clock. And a second consecutive cure year breaches too: a `CURE_REQUIRED` classification against an agreement already in `CURE_REQUIRED`, whose raised `consecutive_cure_required_count` reaches 2, moves it to `BREACHED` even though neither year alone was a shortfall. A breach recomputes the parent award's `cba_breach_count` and re-derives its `compliance_posture`, increments both the agreement's and the award's `version`, and from that moment on refuses any new sub-allocation to that business with `ERR_CBA_BREACHED`. An agreement can also be breached from outside, when its business is disqualified by a second consecutive failed eligibility test, and it can be closed from outside, when its parent award's compliance period closes at settlement. `BREACHED` and `CLOSED` are both terminal.

### 3.8. The Seven-Year Compliance Period: Annual Testing, Cure, Fees and Recapture

The heart of the regime is one test, run once per compliance year, by a `ComplianceReviewer` who is not the reviewer that last certified any business this investment funds — otherwise the request is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. The request names only the `compliance_year` being measured, an integer from 1 through 7. Years may not be skipped: the requested year must be no greater than the investment's `compliance_years_completed` plus one, and a request for a later year than the next one due is `ERR_TEST_SEQUENCE_INVALID`. A request naming a year that has *already* been tested passes that rung deliberately and is caught one rung lower by the window check: no two annual tests of the same investment may cover overlapping windows, and a repeat is `ERR_TEST_WINDOW_OVERLAP`.

The window is derived, never supplied. Its `window_start_at` is the investment's `issued_at` advanced by 365 days multiplied by one less than the requested year; its `window_end_at` is the `issued_at` advanced by 365 days multiplied by the requested year; and `window_days` is always 365 under the fixed-year convention. The lower bound is inclusive and the upper bound exclusive, so the seven windows tile the compliance period exactly.

Each test measures the substantially-all requirement **both** ways and stores both answers. The `measured_pct_direct` is the direct-tracing figure defined in §3.5 — point-in-time outstanding balances of qualifying sub-allocations over a denominator that nets out permitted fees. The `measured_pct_aggregate` is time-weighted: for each qualifying sub-allocation, count the whole days it was outstanding inside the window, clamped to begin at the later of the window start and the row's `deployed_at` and to end at the earlier of the window end and the logical clock, with a row that has stopped qualifying accruing no further days beyond its `non_qualifying_since_at`; multiply each row's `principal_cents` by its day count; sum those products; then take 100 multiplied by that sum, divided by the investment's gross `qei_amount_cents` multiplied by `window_days`, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. The two denominators differ deliberately — the direct variant nets out permitted fees and the aggregate variant does not — and because a sub-allocation deployed late in a window is heavily discounted, identical data can pass under direct tracing and fail under the aggregate basis. The qualifying set is the same for both: only sub-allocations whose `status` is `DEPLOYED` or `PARTIALLY_REPAID`, never `NON_QUALIFYING` and never `REPAID`.

Which figure decides is not the client's choice. The test copies the award's `substantially_all_method` into its own `governing_method`, and sets `governing_pct` to `measured_pct_direct` when that method is `DIRECT_TRACING` and to `measured_pct_aggregate` when it is `AGGREGATE_BALANCE`. The `result` is `PASS` when the `governing_pct` is at least 85.00 *and* the test's `breached_obligation_count` is 0; otherwise it is `FAIL`. Every interim recomputation elsewhere in the system — on deployment, on repayment, on reinvestment, and inside the eligibility cascades — uses direct tracing unconditionally, because the aggregate basis needs a closed window; the annual test is the only place the award's method is honoured and the only place `substantially_all_pct_aggregate` is ever written.

The test is also the system's epoch boundary, and it performs three sweeps. It flips every reinvestment obligation of the investment that is still `OPEN` past its `reinvest_by_at` to `BREACHED`, counting them in `breached_obligation_count`. It sweeps every business funded by a qualifying sub-allocation of this investment out of `CERTIFIED` and into `RECERTIFICATION_DUE` when that business's `last_certified_at` lies strictly more than 365 days before the logical clock, counting them in `recertification_swept_qalicb_count`. And it expires any unwind row of this investment still sitting in `OPTION_WINDOW_OPEN` once the logical clock has reached that row's `option_window_close_at`.

The year's CDE asset-management fee is allocated by the same request, on a waterfall whose rounding order is pinned. The `fee_allocation_total_cents` is the investment's gross `qei_amount_cents` multiplied by 50 basis points and divided by 10000, rounded half-up to whole cents. The targets are the investment's sub-allocations whose `status` is `DEPLOYED` or `PARTIALLY_REPAID`, ordered by `created_at` ascending and never by identifier; the basis is the sum of their `principal_cents`. If there are no targets, or the basis is 0, the entire fee accrues instead: `accrued_unpaid_fee_cents` on the investment rises by the whole `fee_allocation_total_cents` and no sub-allocation's `allocated_fee_cents` changes. Otherwise each target's share is the total fee multiplied by that target's `principal_cents`, divided by the basis, rounded half-up to whole cents — each share rounded first, individually. The residual is the total fee less the sum of those rounded shares, and it may be negative when the shares over-allocate or positive when they under-allocate. Each target's `allocated_fee_cents` rises by its own share, and the single residual is then pushed onto exactly one row: the target with the greatest `outstanding_principal_cents`, with ties broken by the earliest `created_at`. Rounding a running total instead of each share would produce different per-row values, so the order matters: a fee of 500000 over principals of 31666667, 31666667 and 31666666 (a basis of 95000000) gives three shares of 166667, summing to 500001, so the residual is negative one, the earliest-created largest row lands at 166666, and the final vector is 166666, 166667, 166667. Sub-allocations in `NON_QUALIFYING` or `REPAID` are excluded and their notional share is not redistributed to them.

The order in which the test updates the investment is pinned as well: `compliance_years_completed` is set to the tested `compliance_year` **before** any recapture arithmetic runs, so an exposure computed in the same request reads the year just completed and not the year before it. That counter simply counts the annual test rows recorded against the investment, whether they passed or failed.

On a passing test an investment sitting in `AT_RISK` returns to `COMPLIANT` and its `cure_deadline_at` is cleared to null. On a failing test an investment in `COMPLIANT` moves to `AT_RISK` with `cure_deadline_at` set to the logical clock advanced by 180 days. A failing test against an investment *already* in `AT_RISK` whose `cure_deadline_at` the logical clock has passed is the end of the road: the investment moves to `RECAPTURED`, the test's `recapture_triggered` is set true, and `recapture_trigger_type` is set to `SUBSTANTIALLY_ALL_FAILURE` — or to `REINVESTMENT_BREACH` when the same test's `breached_obligation_count` is greater than 0, since returned capital left idle past its window is a different sin from capital that never qualified. These are the second and third recapture triggers; the first is the decertification of the allocatee described in §3.1. In every case the award's `at_risk_qei_count` and `recaptured_qei_count` are recomputed, its `compliance_posture` re-derived, and both the investment's and the award's `version` incremented. `RECAPTURED` and `RELEASED` are the only terminal compliance states, and a recaptured investment is frozen against deployment, cures, reinvestment and unwind alike, all of which answer `ERR_QEI_RECAPTURED`.

Exposure is measured on a fixed statutory ladder rather than pro rata. The cumulative credit claimed after a given number of completed compliance years is expressed in basis points of the gross `qei_amount_cents`: 500 after one year, 1000 after two, 1500 after three, 2100 after four, 2700 after five, 3300 after six and 3900 after seven — the full 39 percent of the investment. With zero completed years the `recapture_exposure_cents` is 0. Otherwise the claimed amount is the gross `qei_amount_cents` multiplied by the ladder's basis points for the completed year count, divided by 10000 and rounded half-up. Interest is simple and never compounded, charged at 300 basis points per completed year on that cumulative claimed amount rather than year by year: the claimed amount multiplied by 300, multiplied by the number of completed years, divided by 10000, and rounded half-up as its own separate step. The `recapture_exposure_cents` is the claimed amount plus that interest — two independent roundings, never one combined multiplication. On a gross of 100000000 cents the ladder runs 5000000 plus 150000 equals 5150000 at year one; 15000000 plus 1350000 equals 16350000 at year three; 27000000 plus 4050000 equals 31050000 at year five; and 39000000 plus 8190000 equals 47190000 at year seven. A later year never retroactively reduces an exposure already recorded.

Between the warning and the clawback there is a cure. While an investment is `AT_RISK` and the logical clock is at or before its `cure_deadline_at`, the `CDESponsor` may deploy additional qualifying principal by naming the receiving business through `qalicb_id` and the money through `principal_cents`. A cure on an investment that is not `AT_RISK`, or one attempted after `cure_deadline_at`, is `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`; a cure aimed at a `DISQUALIFIED` business is `ERR_QALICB_DISQUALIFIED`; a cure that exceeds the investment's `uninvested_cash_cents` is `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_UNINVESTED_CASH`; and a cure on a recaptured investment is `ERR_QEI_RECAPTURED`. A successful cure creates a sub-allocation with `created_by_cure` true, `loan_class` `A` and `status` `DEPLOYED`, which is how a cure-created placement is told apart from an ordinary one when the investment's sub-allocations are listed. The placed principal leaves `uninvested_cash_cents`, `substantially_all_pct_direct` is recomputed under direct tracing, and when the recomputed percentage reaches at least 85.00 the investment returns to `COMPLIANT` with `cure_deadline_at` cleared; when it does not, the investment stays `AT_RISK` and the deadline stands. Either way the award's `at_risk_qei_count` and posture are recomputed and both `version` counters increment.

### 3.9. Unwind: The Option Window, Exercise and Settlement

An investment that has survived all seven compliance years is taken apart deliberately. The `CDESponsor` opens the option window, naming the sponsor who will hold the call through `call_holder_user_id` (1 to 64 characters). The investment must have `compliance_years_completed` equal to 7, or the request is refused with `ERR_COMPLIANCE_PERIOD_NOT_COMPLETE`, and it must have no live unwind row: an existing row in `OPTION_WINDOW_OPEN`, `PUT_EXERCISED`, `CALL_EXERCISED` or `SETTLED` blocks a new one with `ERR_UNWIND_ALREADY_OPEN`, and only a prior row in `EXPIRED` permits a fresh attempt. The put holder is never taken from the body: `put_holder_user_id` is copied from the `investor_user_id` of the investment's fund, and that identity alone may exercise the put.

Both options are priced once, at opening, and never re-priced. The `fund_residual_value_cents` is the investment's `uninvested_cash_cents`, plus the sum of `outstanding_principal_cents` across its sub-allocations whose `status` is `DEPLOYED`, `PARTIALLY_REPAID` or `NON_QUALIFYING`, less the fund's `leverage_loan_outstanding_cents`. A sub-allocation that has stopped qualifying is still a real receivable, so it counts here even though it is excluded from every substantially-all numerator. The `put_price_cents` is a flat nominal 100000 cents and is never scaled by anything. The `call_price_cents` is fair-market: take the residual value floored at zero, multiply by 250 basis points, divide by 10000 and round half-up; then subtract the investment's `accrued_unpaid_fee_cents`; then apply a floor of 100000 cents to whatever remains. The order is load-bearing — the basis-point multiplication is rounded before the accrued fee is subtracted, and the floor is applied last, so a large accrued fee can never drive the call price below the floor or negative. With a leverage balance of 35000000 cents: uninvested cash of 95000000 with no sub-allocations and an accrued fee of 500000 gives a residual of 60000000 and a call price of 1000000; uninvested cash of 15000000 with 80000000 outstanding and an accrued fee of 1000000 gives the same residual of 60000000 and a call price of 500000; uninvested cash of 0 with 95000000 outstanding and no accrued fee gives the same residual again and a call price of 1500000. The `option_window_open_at` is the investment's `issued_at` advanced by 2555 days — seven years of 365 days each — and the `option_window_close_at` is 90 days after that, so the window's lower bound is inclusive and its upper bound exclusive. The row opens in `status` `OPTION_WINDOW_OPEN` with `exercised_option_type`, `exercised_at`, `settlement_reference` and `settled_at` all null and `settlement_amount_cents` at 0.

Exercise is a fork with two mutually exclusive branches, and the branches do not merely differ in price. The request carries `option_type`, either `PUT` or `CALL`. A `PUT` may be exercised only by a `TaxCreditInvestor` whose `X-User-ID` equals `put_holder_user_id`; a `CALL` may be exercised only by a `QALICBSponsor` whose `X-User-ID` equals `call_holder_user_id`; a mismatch on either branch is `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. Both branches require the row to be in `OPTION_WINDOW_OPEN`, require the investment's `compliance_status` to be `COMPLIANT`, and require the logical clock to be at or after `option_window_open_at` and strictly before `option_window_close_at` — outside that window the answer is `ERR_OPTION_WINDOW_CLOSED`. Both branches stamp `exercised_at` from the logical clock and record `exercised_option_type`. On the put branch the row moves to `PUT_EXERCISED`, `settlement_amount_cents` is set to the `put_price_cents`, the investor's fund interest transfers at the nominal put price, the fund's `leverage_loan_outstanding_cents` is left untouched, and no fee sweep occurs — the investment's `accrued_unpaid_fee_cents` survives exercise intact. On the call branch the row moves to `CALL_EXERCISED`, `settlement_amount_cents` is set to the `call_price_cents`, and because the accrued CDE fee has already been netted inside that price, the investment's `accrued_unpaid_fee_cents` is swept to 0 in the same request and the investment's `version` increments alongside the unwind's. The two branches rejoin at settlement.

Settlement is the allocatee's act — the `CDESponsor` that owns the award holding the investment — and it carries a single `settlement_reference`. It is permitted only from `PUT_EXERCISED` or `CALL_EXERCISED`; a row still in `OPTION_WINDOW_OPEN`, or already `SETTLED` or `EXPIRED`, answers `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`. Settlement stamps `settled_at` from the logical clock, records the reference and moves the row to `SETTLED`. The consequences fan out across the whole structure in that one request. The investment's `deployment_status` moves to `UNWOUND` from either `FULLY_DEPLOYED` or `DEPLOYING`, and its `compliance_status` moves from `COMPLIANT` to `RELEASED` — the seven-year period closing with no clawback. Every sub-allocation of the investment whose `status` is `DEPLOYED`, `PARTIALLY_REPAID` or `NON_QUALIFYING` moves to `REPAID`, with `outstanding_principal_cents` set to 0 and `total_repaid_cents` set to its full `principal_cents`. The fund moves from `QEI_MADE` to `WOUND_UP`, with `leverage_loan_outstanding_cents` and `capital_available_cents` both set to 0, once every investment of that fund whose `deployment_status` is not `VOIDED` has reached `UNWOUND`. And the award bubbles up: when the settled investment was the last non-`VOIDED` investment on its award and every such investment now reads `UNWOUND`, the award moves from `ACTIVE` to `COMPLIANCE_PERIOD_CLOSED`, and every community benefits agreement under that award whose `status` is `ACTIVE` or `CURE_REQUIRED` moves to `CLOSED`, after which those agreements refuse further job reports and further certifications with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`. The unwind row's `version`, the investment's `version`, each sub-allocation's `version`, the fund's `version` and — where they were touched — the award's and each agreement's `version` all increment by 1.

An option window can also simply lapse. When an annual compliance test of the investment runs and finds an unwind row still in `OPTION_WINDOW_OPEN` with the logical clock at or after `option_window_close_at`, that row moves to `EXPIRED` and its `version` increments. Expiry is terminal for that row only: the investment keeps its `compliance_status` untouched, and a fresh unwind may then be opened on it and priced against the conditions then prevailing.

## 4. API Endpoint Definitions

Every path below is rooted at the `/nmtc` prefix, with the sole exception of the logical-clock control endpoint, which sits outside it. Creation endpoints that bring a brand-new record into being answer `201 Created`; every other successful call, including the action endpoints that advance a state machine, answer `200 OK`. Errors carry the status codes catalogued in §5: `400` for malformed input, `401` for a missing or unrecognised identity, `403` for a role or ownership refusal, `404` for an unresolvable path or body reference, `409` for a conflict with the stored state of the system, `412` for a stale version precondition, and `422` for a request that is well-formed and permitted but violates a business rule. ## 5. Error Code Catalogue

Every failed request returns the error envelope of §2.2 with one of the identifiers below in its `error_id`, and the ladder of §2.10 decides which identifier wins when a request violates more than one rule at once.

| Error Identifier | HTTP Status and Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` | 401 — the `X-User-ID` or `X-User-Role` header is missing or empty, or `X-User-Role` is not one of the five declared roles. |
| `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` | 403 — the acting role is not listed among the authorized roles of the target endpoint. |
| `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` | 403 — the acting `CDESponsor` does not own the award chain, the acting `TaxCreditInvestor` is not the fund's `investor_user_id` or the unwind's `put_holder_user_id`, or the acting `QALICBSponsor` is not the business's `sponsor_user_id` or the unwind's `call_holder_user_id`. |
| `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` | 403 — the `ComplianceReviewer` who performed the latest eligibility test of a business funded by this investment attempts to run that investment's annual compliance test. |
| `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` | 400 — a required field is missing, mistyped, out of range, or carries an undeclared enum value. |
| `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION` | 400 — `limit` falls outside 1 through 100, or `offset` is negative or not an integer. |
| `ERR_CDE_NOT_FOUND` | 404 — no community development entity exists with the given identifier. |
| `ERR_ALLOCATION_AWARD_NOT_FOUND` | 404 — no allocation award exists with the given identifier. |
| `ERR_CENSUS_TRACT_NOT_FOUND` | 404 — no census tract exists with the given identifier, including one named by `contiguous_lic_tract_id`. |
| `ERR_QALICB_NOT_FOUND` | 404 — no project company exists with the given identifier. |
| `ERR_INVESTMENT_FUND_NOT_FOUND` | 404 — no investment fund exists with the given identifier. |
| `ERR_QEI_NOT_FOUND` | 404 — no qualified equity investment exists with the given identifier. |
| `ERR_QLICI_NOT_FOUND` | 404 — no sub-allocation exists with the given identifier, including one named by `target_qlici_id` on a reinvestment. |
| `ERR_REINVESTMENT_OBLIGATION_NOT_FOUND` | 404 — no reinvestment obligation exists with the given identifier. |
| `ERR_CBA_NOT_FOUND` | 404 — no community benefits agreement exists with the given identifier. |
| `ERR_UNWIND_NOT_FOUND` | 404 — no unwind transaction exists with the given identifier. |
| `ERR_STALE_AWARD_VERSION` | 412 — the supplied `expected_allocation_award_version` does not equal the award's current `version` at issuance time. |
| `ERR_DUPLICATE_CERTIFICATION_NUMBER` | 409 — another community development entity already holds this `certification_number`. |
| `ERR_DUPLICATE_TRACT_CODE` | 409 — another census tract already holds this `tract_code`. |
| `ERR_CDE_NOT_CERTIFIED` | 409 — the community development entity owning the award has `certification_status` `DECERTIFIED`. |
| `ERR_AWARD_NOT_ACTIVE` | 409 — the award's `lifecycle_status` is `ANNOUNCED`, `COMPLIANCE_PERIOD_CLOSED` or `TERMINATED` where `ACTIVE` is required. |
| `ERR_AWARD_POSTURE_RESTRICTED` | 409 — the award's `compliance_posture` is `RESTRICTED`, which blocks investment issuance, or `FROZEN`, which blocks both investment issuance and sub-allocation deployment. |
| `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` | 409 — the requested action is not a declared transition from the resource's current state: re-signing an allocation agreement, re-closing a fund, voiding a deployed investment, curing a `COMPLIANT` investment or one past its `cure_deadline_at`, exercising or settling an unwind that is already settled, or reporting under a `CLOSED` agreement. |
| `ERR_QEI_RECAPTURED` | 409 — the target investment's `compliance_status` is `RECAPTURED`, which freezes deployment, cures, reinvestment and unwind alike. |
| `ERR_TEST_SEQUENCE_INVALID` | 409 — the requested `compliance_year` exceeds the investment's `compliance_years_completed` plus one, skipping a year, or falls outside 1 through 7. |
| `ERR_TEST_WINDOW_OVERLAP` | 409 — the requested annual-test window overlaps an existing test window of the same investment under the half-open convention, which is how a request naming an already-tested year is caught. |
| `ERR_REPORT_PERIOD_OVERLAP` | 409 — the requested job-report period overlaps an existing report period of the same agreement, where the intervals are closed and a single shared endpoint instant collides. |
| `ERR_QALICB_COMPLIANCE_PERIOD_CONFLICT` | 409 — the project company is already inside another award's seven-year compliance period, including the 30-day symmetric buffer; sub-allocations from the same award are exempt. |
| `ERR_UNWIND_ALREADY_OPEN` | 409 — the investment already carries an unwind transaction that is not `EXPIRED`. |
| `ERR_TRACT_NOT_LIC_ELIGIBLE` | 422 — the referenced census tract has `eligible_for_qlici` false, or a contiguity claim points at a tract that is not itself a low-income community. |
| `ERR_QALICB_NOT_CERTIFIED` | 422 — the target project company's `eligibility_status` is `PENDING`, `RECERTIFICATION_DUE` or `FAILED` where `CERTIFIED` is required. |
| `ERR_QALICB_DISQUALIFIED` | 422 — the target project company's `eligibility_status` is `DISQUALIFIED`, a permanent bar. |
| `ERR_CBA_BREACHED` | 422 — the target project company's community benefits agreement is `BREACHED`. |
| `ERR_CBA_MISSING` | 422 — the project company has no community benefits agreement bound to the funding investment's allocation award. |
| `ERR_FUND_NOT_FUNDED` | 422 — the investment fund backing a new qualified equity investment is not in `status` `FUNDED`. |

| `ERR_CAPITAL_STACK_SHORTFALL` | 422 — a fund closing reports equity or leverage proceeds short of the committed amounts. |
| `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_ALLOCATION_AUTHORITY` | 422 — the requested `qei_amount_cents` exceeds the award's `remaining_authority_cents` or the fund's `capital_available_cents`. |
