# Staffing Data Reporting & Verification API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Skilled nursing facilities are required to report every hour their staff actually works, quarter by quarter, in a form that a state survey agency can audit line by line against payroll documents — and those same hours, divided by the number of residents actually in the building, become the staffing star rating the public sees. The reporting chain today is brittle in exactly the places where it matters most. Hours arrive from three different origins (a facility's own payroll system, individual contractors, and staffing agencies that invoice rather than run payroll), each job title carries its own rule about how much of a worked hour counts as direct resident care, a single nurse may split a shift between bedside work and administrative duties, and the resident census the facility reports at midnight frequently disagrees with the independently derived MDS count. When any one of those inputs is wrong, the error does not stop at the spreadsheet: it propagates into a published rating, which propagates into a consumer decision, which propagates into an appeal.

This API is the system of record for that chain. It captures individual worked shifts with their split-role allocations, classifies each allocated fraction into direct-care and excluded time, reconciles facility-reported census against MDS census, rolls the results into a quarterly submission, runs a validation pass that raises fatal and advisory findings, enforces segregation of duties between the staffing office that prepares the quarter and the leadership that attests to it, and then hands the accepted quarter to a national publisher that computes hours per resident day, assigns star points on two independent nurse dimensions, applies a weekend staffing floor and a zero-RN-day override, and publishes a rating.

The system is deliberately unforgiving about provenance and timing. Every reported hour may be offered a payroll document as support, and a state auditor's verification of that support directly moves both the audit-readiness score and the audit-adjusted variant of the staffing measure. Every write is bounded by a correction window; amending an already-decided quarter costs money on an escalating schedule that is capped facility-wide. Discrepancy investigations put a facility into progressively more restrictive reporting postures, and a substantiated investigation retroactively revises a published measure in place. A facility that believes the rating is wrong has a bounded appeal window and, if it prevails, gets the measure suppressed, the investigation overturned, and its outstanding amendment surcharge waived. The requirements below specify that machinery precisely.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

This section defines the system-wide contracts: authentication, response shape, pagination, common resource properties, numeric precision, stale-version protection, and the cross-cutting reporting principles that every workflow in §3 depends on.

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to the API — reads included — must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, naming the acting identity, and `X-User-Role`, naming the capacity that identity is acting in. If either header is absent or empty, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`. If `X-User-Role` carries a string that is not one of the seven role names below, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNKNOWN_ROLE`. If the role is recognised but is not among the roles listed for the target endpoint in §4, the request is rejected with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE`.

The seven roles are `PlatformAdmin`, `StaffingCoordinator`, `FacilityAdministrator`, `VendorRepresentative`, `StateAuditor`, `CmsPublisher`, and `AppealsReviewer`.

- `PlatformAdmin` operates the reporting platform itself: it registers facilities, opens and closes reporting periods, defines job categories, and drives the logical clock. It may also register workers and vendors, suspend vendors, terminate workers, file payroll documents, open submissions, create vendor batches, and read anything.
- `StaffingCoordinator` is the facility staffing office. It captures hours, census days, and vendor batch acceptances, and runs the validation pass.
- `FacilityAdministrator` is facility leadership. It attests and submits the quarter, answers discrepancy information requests, and files rating appeals.
- `VendorRepresentative` is the staffing agency contact. It files, attests, and revokes agency hour batches.
- `StateAuditor` is the state survey agency. It accepts or rejects submissions, verifies payroll support on individual hours, waives findings, suspends vendors, and runs discrepancy investigations.
- `CmsPublisher` is the national rating publisher. It computes staffing measures from accepted submissions and publishes star ratings.
- `AppealsReviewer` is an independent reviewer that grants or denies rating appeals; it may not compute, publish, or audit measures.

**What `X-User-ID` means for each role.** When `X-User-Role` is `StaffingCoordinator`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Worker`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Worker` or a `Facility` compare the resource's facility linkage to that `Worker`'s own `facility_id`. When `X-User-Role` is `FacilityAdministrator`, `X-User-ID` is likewise the `id` of the acting `Worker`, with the same facility-scoped ownership comparison. When `X-User-Role` is `VendorRepresentative`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `StaffingVendor`; ownership checks compare the target batch's `vendor_id` (or the `vendor_id` supplied in the request body) to `X-User-ID`. When `X-User-Role` is `PlatformAdmin`, `StateAuditor`, `CmsPublisher`, or `AppealsReviewer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server records it where a transition writes it — as `accepted_by_user_id`, `verified_by_user_id`, `waived_by_user_id`, `opened_by_user_id`, `substantiated_by_user_id`, `computed_by_user_id`, or `decided_by_user_id` — but never compares it to any entity's `id`.

For the three entity-backed roles, an `X-User-ID` that does not resolve to an existing row of the backing entity is rejected with `ERR_IDENTITY_NOT_FOUND`. For the two `Worker`-backed roles there are two further identity gates. First, the acting `Worker`'s `authorized_roles` array must contain the acting role name — the array holds the literal strings `STAFFING_COORDINATOR` and `FACILITY_ADMINISTRATOR` — and if it does not, the request is rejected with `ERR_WORKER_ROLE_NOT_AUTHORIZED`. Second, the acting `Worker`'s `status` must be `ACTIVE`; a `TERMINATED` worker is rejected with `ERR_ACTOR_INACTIVE`. On any write, the acting `Worker`'s `facility_id` must equal the `facility_id` of the target resource, or of the target resource's parent submission when the target is nested beneath one; a mismatch is rejected with `ERR_FACILITY_SCOPE_VIOLATION`. For a `VendorRepresentative` acting on batch creation, batch submission, or batch revocation, an `X-User-ID` that does not match the batch's owning vendor is rejected with `ERR_VENDOR_SCOPE_VIOLATION`.

Two duties are separated by design and share a single rejection code, `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. The `Worker` who ran the validation pass may not be the `Worker` who attests and submits the quarter, and the `StateAuditor` identity that verified any hour of a submission may not be the identity that accepts that submission.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination and List Filtering

Every list endpoint is paginated through two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of rows returned; it defaults to 10, has a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 100. `offset` is the number of rows to skip and defaults to 0, with no upper bound. A `limit` that is not an integer in the range 1 through 100 inclusive, or an `offset` that is not an integer of zero or greater, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION`.

Unless a specific endpoint states otherwise, list results are ordered by `created_at` ascending. The `meta` object of every list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, and `has_more`, where `total_count` is the size of the filtered result set before the window is applied and `has_more` indicates whether rows remain beyond the window.

List endpoints also accept optional equality filters, all of which combine conjunctively with each other and with pagination. Facilities may be filtered by `state_code` and `posture`. Reporting periods may be filtered by `state_code`, `status`, and `period_code`. Job categories may be filtered by `direct_care_class`, `nurse_group`, and `code`. Workers may be filtered by `facility_id`, `primary_job_category_id`, `vendor_id`, `worker_type`, and `status`. Vendors may be filtered by `facility_id` and `status`. Agency batches may be filtered by `vendor_id`, `facility_id`, `reporting_period_id`, and `status`. Payroll documents may be filtered by `facility_id`, `worker_id`, and `support_type`. Quarterly submissions may be filtered by `facility_id`, `reporting_period_id`, `validated_by_worker_id`, `attested_by_worker_id`, and `status`. Hour entries may be filtered by `submission_id`, `facility_id`, `reporting_period_id`, `worker_id`, `job_category_id`, `vendor_submission_id`, `payroll_record_id`, `work_date`, `entry_status`, `verification_status`, and `source`. Census days may be filtered by `submission_id`, `facility_id`, `reporting_period_id`, `status`, and `census_date`. Staffing measures may be filtered by `submission_id`, `facility_id`, `reporting_period_id`, `publication_status`, `audit_status`, and `measure_variant`. Discrepancy cases may be filtered by `facility_id`, `submission_id`, `staffing_measure_id`, `trigger_reason`, and `status`. Rating appeals may be filtered by `staffing_measure_id`, `facility_id`, `discrepancy_case_id`, `filed_by_worker_id`, `grounds`, and `status`. The finding list scoped to one submission may be filtered by `code`, `severity`, and `status`.

### 2.4. Common Resource Properties and the Logical Clock

Every persisted resource carries a server-minted `id` formatted as a UUID with no type prefix, and a `created_at` timestamp rendered as an ISO 8601 UTC instant with three fractional digits. That rendering is canonical for every instant-valued field the API returns — `updated_at`, a `ReportingPeriod`'s `submission_deadline` and `correction_window_end`, a `QuarterlySubmission`'s `correction_deadline`, every `submitted_at` and `accepted_at`, a finding's `resolved_at`, a measure's `published_at` and `appeal_deadline`, an appeal's `decided_at`, and the `current_utc` the clock endpoint answers with — each of which is written with exactly three fractional digits and a trailing `Z`. An instant supplied in a request body is normalised into that form rather than echoed back verbatim, so a `correction_window_end` filed as `2026-12-31T23:59:59Z` reads back — and is copied into `correction_deadline` — as `2026-12-31T23:59:59.000Z`; date fields in `YYYY-MM-DD` form and time-of-day fields in `HH:MM` form are, by contrast, stored and returned exactly as supplied. Almost every resource additionally carries `updated_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp refreshed on every mutation of that resource, and `version`, an integer that begins at 1 on creation and increments by exactly one on every mutation — including mutations that only touch derived or rolled-up fields.

`PayrollRecord` is the single exception. A filed payroll document is immutable evidence: it carries only `id` and `created_at`. Both `updated_at` and `version` are absent from a `PayrollRecord` payload entirely.

All time-dependent behaviour in the system reads a **logical clock** rather than wall-clock time. Before any clock write, the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and it never falls back to real time. A `PlatformAdmin` repositions it by supplying a `to_iso8601` instant to `POST /_clock/set_current_utc`, which answers with `current_utc` set to the new instant. Every deadline comparison in this specification — submission deadlines, correction windows, appeal windows, and the late-day arithmetic of the amendment surcharge — reads that logical clock.

### 2.5. Numeric Precision and Rounding

The system uses three distinct precisions, and every one of them rounds half up.

- **Hour quantities** — `raw_hours`, `direct_care_hours`, `excluded_hours`, `rn_hours`, `lpn_hours`, `na_hours`, `paid_hours`, `reported_hours_total`, `accepted_hours_total`, `accepted_direct_care_hours`, and every submission-level hour rollup (`total_raw_hours`, `total_direct_care_hours`, `total_excluded_hours`, `total_rn_direct_care_hours`, `weekend_direct_care_hours`, `vendor_sourced_hours`) — carry exactly 2 decimal places.
- **Ratios and per-resident-day rates** — `variance_pct`, `hprd_reported`, `weekend_hprd_reported`, `total_nurse_hprd`, `rn_hprd`, `weekend_total_nurse_hprd`, and the three component ratios of the audit-readiness score — carry exactly 4 decimal places. The `audit_support_score` itself carries 2 decimal places.
- **Money** is held in whole cents as integers: `amendment_surcharge_cents`, `waived_surcharge_cents`, `outstanding_surcharge_cents`, and `surcharge_waived_cents` are never fractional.

Counts — `day_count`, `line_count`, `total_resident_days`, `resident_count`, `mds_resident_count`, `open_case_count`, `substantiated_case_count`, `open_fatal_finding_count`, `open_warning_finding_count`, `info_request_count`, `facility_response_count`, `amendment_index`, `allocation_revision`, `days_with_zero_rn_hours`, `total_nurse_points`, `rn_points`, `staffing_rating`, `latest_staffing_rating`, `certified_bed_count`, `cms_job_code`, and `submission_version` — are exact integers.

Two rounding orders are pinned and must not be rearranged, because rearranging them produces different numbers. First, when an hour entry's direct-care credit is computed from several allocation lines, **each line is rounded to 2 decimal places before the lines are summed**; summing unrounded line values and rounding once at the end is a different and incorrect result. Second, whenever an hour total feeds a division that produces a 4-decimal rate, **the hour total is rounded to 2 decimal places first and the quotient is then rounded to 4 decimal places**.

### 2.6. Stale-Version Protection on Reporting Writes

Three write endpoints are guarded by optimistic concurrency control, because each of them re-states a value that other writers can move underneath the client.

`POST /hour-entries` requires `expected_submission_version` as a non-optional body field carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `QuarterlySubmission`. If that value does not equal the submission's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_SUBMISSION_VERSION` and HTTP 412. A new hour entry legitimately changes the submission's rollup totals, its `hprd_reported`, and its validation posture, so a writer holding a stale view of the submission would be appending hours on top of a rollup it never saw.

`PUT /hour-entries/{id}/allocations` requires `expected_submission_version` on the same terms and rejects a mismatch with `ERR_STALE_SUBMISSION_VERSION` and HTTP 412. Replacing an entry's allocation set is a full re-statement of that entry's direct-care credit and rewrites the parent submission's rollup fields, so a replacement computed against a rollup the client never observed is refused.

`POST /rating-appeals` requires `expected_measure_version` as a non-optional body field carrying the client's last observed `version` of the `StaffingMeasure` being appealed. A mismatch is rejected with `ERR_STALE_MEASURE_VERSION` and HTTP 412. An audit revision can rewrite `staffing_rating` in place without minting a new measure, so an appeal filed on a stale view would be contesting a rating that no longer exists.

No other endpoint takes a version field. In particular, voiding an hour entry carries no version token at all, so it silently advances the parent submission's `version` — a client that voids an entry and then creates a new one must re-read the submission before supplying `expected_submission_version` again.

### 2.7. Reporting Posture: the Facility-Level Compliance Ladder

Each `Facility` carries a derived `posture` that is recomputed from scratch, never incrementally, every time a discrepancy investigation for that facility changes status, and also on the acceptance of a submission when that acceptance opens an investigation. The derivation counts the facility's `DiscrepancyCase` rows: `substantiated_case_count` is the number in status `SUBSTANTIATED`, and `open_case_count` is the number in status `OPEN`, `INFO_REQUESTED`, or `UNDER_REVIEW`. The ladder is then evaluated top to bottom, so a substantiated case dominates any number of merely open ones: `posture` is `SANCTIONED` when `substantiated_case_count` is at least 1; otherwise `AUDIT_HOLD` when `open_case_count` is at least 1; otherwise `REPORTING_OPEN`. A newly registered facility starts at `REPORTING_OPEN` with both counts at 0.

The same derivation refreshes two more facility-level figures. `outstanding_surcharge_cents` is the sum, over every `QuarterlySubmission` of the facility whose `status` is anything other than `VOID`, of that submission's `amendment_surcharge_cents` less its `waived_surcharge_cents`. And `latest_staffing_rating` is the `staffing_rating` of the facility's `StaffingMeasure` whose `publication_status` is `PUBLISHED`, choosing the candidate with the latest `published_at` and breaking ties by the latest `created_at`; when no published measure remains for the facility, `latest_staffing_rating` is null.

Posture is a gate, not merely a label. Moving from `REPORTING_OPEN` to `AUDIT_HOLD` happens when a case for the facility enters one of the three open statuses while no substantiated case exists, and it blocks attestation of the quarter with `ERR_FACILITY_UNDER_HOLD`. Moving from `AUDIT_HOLD` to `SANCTIONED` happens when a case reaches `SUBSTANTIATED`; that refreshes `substantiated_case_count`, reverts every `VALIDATED` submission of the facility back to `DRAFT` with a fatal `FACILITY_SANCTIONED` finding raised on each, and blocks the validation pass with `ERR_FACILITY_SANCTIONED`. Recovery runs the other way: when the last `SUBSTANTIATED` case is overturned, `substantiated_case_count` becomes 0 and the facility drops to `AUDIT_HOLD` if at least one case remains in an open status, or all the way back to `REPORTING_OPEN` if `open_case_count` is 0. And when the last case in an open status is dismissed while `substantiated_case_count` is already 0, `AUDIT_HOLD` returns to `REPORTING_OPEN`.

### 2.8. Reporting Periods, Deadlines, and the Correction Window

A `ReportingPeriod` fixes the calendar boundaries and the two deadlines that govern a quarter for one state. Its `submission_deadline` is the instant after which attestation is late and amendment surcharges begin to accrue late fees. Its `correction_window_end` is the harder stop: after that instant, no hour write, no census write, no attestation, and no amendment is accepted for the period. `correction_window_end` must be strictly after `submission_deadline`.

Each `QuarterlySubmission` copies `ReportingPeriod.correction_window_end` into its own `correction_deadline` at creation time, and every temporal guard on that submission compares the logical clock against that copy. A write attempted when the logical clock is strictly after `correction_deadline` is rejected with `ERR_CORRECTION_WINDOW_CLOSED`; a write at exactly `correction_deadline` is still accepted.

Separately, a period's own `status` — `OPEN` at creation, `CLOSED` afterwards — gates almost every reporting write. Any write against a period whose `status` is `CLOSED` is rejected with `ERR_PERIOD_CLOSED`.

### 2.9. Cascade Consistency: Revalidation, Rollups, and Locking

The submission is the consistency boundary of this system, and it is deliberately jealous about it. Any successful write that changes the hour set or the census set of a submission re-runs the submission's rollup aggregation, refreshes the affected `Worker`'s reported-hours figure where hours moved, increments the submission's `version` by exactly one, and — if the submission had already reached `VALIDATED` — reverts it to `DRAFT` so that the changed data must be validated again before it can be attested. This revert applies to hour creation, allocation replacement, hour voiding, census creation, census correction, census reconciliation, and agency batch acceptance and revocation alike. The `VALIDATED → DRAFT` revert is silent in the sense that the client does not request it; it is a consequence of touching the data.

The other automatic revert has the same shape but a different cause: when a facility's `posture` becomes `SANCTIONED` through the substantiation of a discrepancy case, every `VALIDATED` submission of that facility reverts to `DRAFT`, gains a new `ValidationFinding` with `code` `FACILITY_SANCTIONED`, `severity` `FATAL`, and `status` `OPEN`, has its `open_fatal_finding_count` refreshed, and has its `version` incremented by one. That cascade leaves the reverted submission's `validated_by_worker_id` and `attested_by_worker_id` exactly as they were — clearing them back to null is the business of a reopen (§3.7) and of nothing else.

At the other end of the lifecycle, `FINAL` and `VOID` are terminal. A `FINAL` submission is locked against all further writes; a `VOID` submission was never accepted and accepts nothing. Any write attempt against a submission in either terminal state is rejected with `ERR_SUBMISSION_STATE_INVALID`, including an attempt to waive one of its findings.

### 2.10. Nested Path Resolution

When a request path carries more than one identifier — as in `POST /submissions/{id}/findings/{finding_id}/waive` — the identifiers are resolved strictly left to right, and the first one that fails to resolve determines the rejection. A waive request naming a submission that does not exist is rejected with `ERR_SUBMISSION_NOT_FOUND` before the finding is ever looked up; only once the submission resolves is the finding looked up, and a finding that does not exist — or exists but does not belong to the named submission — is rejected with `ERR_FINDING_NOT_FOUND`. The same left-to-right discipline applies to every path in §4, and each entity has its own not-found code: `ERR_FACILITY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REPORTING_PERIOD_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_JOB_CATEGORY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_WORKER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_VENDOR_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_VENDOR_SUBMISSION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PAYROLL_RECORD_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_HOUR_ENTRY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CENSUS_DAY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SUBMISSION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FINDING_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MEASURE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CASE_NOT_FOUND`, and `ERR_APPEAL_NOT_FOUND`.

Foreign keys supplied in a request body are resolved with the same codes, and the resolution reaches inside arrays: every `job_category_id` named by an allocation line must resolve, and every `worker_id` and `job_category_id` named by a line of an agency batch must resolve, or the whole request is refused.

### 2.11. Order of Evaluation for Rejections

When a single request violates several rules at once, exactly one error is returned, and which one is fully determined. Checks are evaluated in the following order, and the first violation encountered wins.

1. Both `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` are present and non-empty — otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. `X-User-Role` names one of the seven declared roles — otherwise `ERR_UNKNOWN_ROLE`.
3. The acting role is listed for the target endpoint — otherwise `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE`.
4. For an entity-backed role — that is, on any endpoint called as `StaffingCoordinator`, `FacilityAdministrator`, or `VendorRepresentative` — `X-User-ID` resolves to an existing row of the backing entity — otherwise `ERR_IDENTITY_NOT_FOUND`.
5. On any endpoint called as `StaffingCoordinator` or `FacilityAdministrator`, the acting `Worker`'s `authorized_roles` contains the acting role name — otherwise `ERR_WORKER_ROLE_NOT_AUTHORIZED`.
6. On any endpoint called as `StaffingCoordinator` or `FacilityAdministrator`, the acting `Worker`'s `status` is `ACTIVE` — otherwise `ERR_ACTOR_INACTIVE`.
7. On any **write** endpoint called as `StaffingCoordinator` or `FacilityAdministrator`, the acting `Worker`'s `facility_id` matches the target's facility — otherwise `ERR_FACILITY_SCOPE_VIOLATION`.
8. On agency batch creation, batch submission, and batch revocation, a `VendorRepresentative`'s `X-User-ID` matches the batch's vendor — otherwise `ERR_VENDOR_SCOPE_VIOLATION`.
9. On attestation of a quarter, the acting `Worker`'s `id` differs from the submission's `validated_by_worker_id` — otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`.
10. On acceptance of a quarter, the acting `X-User-ID` differs from `verified_by_user_id` on every hour entry of that submission — otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`.
11. Every identifier in the request path resolves, left to right — otherwise the corresponding not-found code from §2.10.
12. Every foreign key in the request body resolves, including those nested inside allocation lines and agency batch lines — otherwise the corresponding not-found code.
13. Every required body field for the endpoint is present and of the declared type — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.
14. Every enum-typed body field carries a value from that field's declared set — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_ENUM_VALUE`.
15. On list endpoints, `limit` and `offset` are within their declared bounds — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION`.
16. Date fields match the `YYYY-MM-DD` shape, time-of-day fields match `HH:MM` in 24-hour form, and datetime fields are ISO 8601 UTC — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_DATE_FORMAT`.
17. The governing `ReportingPeriod` is `OPEN` — otherwise `ERR_PERIOD_CLOSED`. This applies to submission creation, every hour write (creation, allocation replacement, voiding), every census write (creation, correction, reconciliation), the validate, submit, accept, reject, and reopen operations on a submission, and the submit, accept, and revoke operations on an agency batch.
18. The target `QuarterlySubmission` is in a status the requested operation accepts — otherwise `ERR_SUBMISSION_STATE_INVALID`. Hour writes and census writes accept `DRAFT` or `VALIDATED`; the validation pass accepts `DRAFT`; attestation accepts `VALIDATED`; acceptance and rejection accept `SUBMITTED`; reopening accepts `ACCEPTED` or `REJECTED`; measure computation accepts `ACCEPTED`. The check also guards finding waiver and agency batch acceptance and revocation, where the terminal `FINAL` and `VOID` states are refused.
19. The target `HourEntry` has `entry_status` `ACTIVE` — otherwise `ERR_HOUR_ENTRY_VOIDED`. This guards allocation replacement, voiding, and verification.
20. When a verification records the outcome `SUPPORTED`, the target entry's `payroll_record_id` is not null — otherwise `ERR_SUPPORT_DOCUMENT_MISSING`.
21. The target `VendorHourSubmission` is in a status the requested operation accepts — `DRAFT` or `REJECTED` for batch submission, `SUBMITTED` for batch acceptance and rejection, `ACCEPTED` for revocation — otherwise `ERR_VENDOR_SUBMISSION_STATE_INVALID`.
22. The governing `StaffingVendor` has `status` `ACTIVE` — otherwise `ERR_VENDOR_SUSPENDED`. This guards batch creation, batch submission, and batch acceptance.
23. On census reconciliation, the target day's `mds_resident_count` is not null — otherwise `ERR_MDS_COUNT_MISSING`.
24. On appeal filing, granting, and denial, the referenced `StaffingMeasure` has `publication_status` `PUBLISHED` — otherwise `ERR_MEASURE_NOT_PUBLISHED`.
25. On measure computation and measure publication, no existing `StaffingMeasure` for the submission has `audit_status` `UNDER_AUDIT` — otherwise `ERR_MEASURE_UNDER_AUDIT`.
26. The target `DiscrepancyCase` is in a status the requested operation accepts — `OPEN` or `UNDER_REVIEW` for an information request, `INFO_REQUESTED` for a facility response, `INFO_REQUESTED` or `UNDER_REVIEW` for substantiation, `UNDER_REVIEW` for dismissal — otherwise `ERR_CASE_STATE_INVALID`.
27. On granting and denial, the target `RatingAppeal` has `status` `SUBMITTED` — otherwise `ERR_APPEAL_STATE_INVALID`.
28. On the validation pass and on measure computation, the submission has at least one `CensusDay` whose `status` is `REPORTED`, `RECONCILED`, or `DISPUTED` — otherwise `ERR_NO_CENSUS_DATA`.
29. On finding waiver, the target `ValidationFinding` has `status` `OPEN` — otherwise `ERR_FINDING_NOT_OPEN`.
30. On hour creation and allocation replacement, `expected_submission_version` equals the submission's current `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_SUBMISSION_VERSION`.
31. On appeal filing, `expected_measure_version` equals the measure's current `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_MEASURE_VERSION`.
32. On the validation pass and on measure publication, the owning facility's `posture` is not `SANCTIONED` — otherwise `ERR_FACILITY_SANCTIONED`.
33. On attestation, the owning facility's `posture` is exactly `REPORTING_OPEN` — otherwise `ERR_FACILITY_UNDER_HOLD`.
34. On attestation, the submission's `open_fatal_finding_count` is 0 — otherwise `ERR_FATAL_FINDINGS_OPEN`.
35. On substantiation, the target case's `facility_response_count` is at least 1 — otherwise `ERR_CASE_RESPONSE_REQUIRED`.
36. On hour creation and on agency batch acceptance, `work_date` falls within the submission's period from `start_date` to `end_date` inclusive — otherwise `ERR_WORK_DATE_OUTSIDE_PERIOD`.
37. On hour creation and on agency batch acceptance, `work_date` is on or after the referenced `Worker`'s `hire_date` — otherwise `ERR_WORK_DATE_BEFORE_HIRE`.
38. On hour creation and on agency batch creation, `shift_end` is strictly later than `shift_start` on the same `work_date` — otherwise `ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_INVALID`.
39. On hour creation and on agency batch acceptance, the new shift does not overlap another `ACTIVE` hour entry of the same worker on the same date — otherwise `ERR_SHIFT_OVERLAP`.
40. On period creation, the new period's date range does not intersect an existing period for the same state — otherwise `ERR_PERIOD_OVERLAP`.
41. On payroll document filing, the worker's overlapping documents stay within the daily paid-hours ceiling — otherwise `ERR_PAYROLL_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`.
42. The logical clock is at or before the submission's `correction_deadline` — otherwise `ERR_CORRECTION_WINDOW_CLOSED`. This guards hour creation, allocation replacement, hour voiding, census creation, census correction, census reconciliation, attestation, and reopening.
43. On appeal filing, the logical clock is at or before the referenced measure's `appeal_deadline` — otherwise `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`.
44. On hour creation and on agency batch acceptance, the referenced `Worker`'s `facility_id` equals the target submission's `facility_id` — otherwise `ERR_WORKER_FACILITY_MISMATCH`.
45. On agency batch creation and acceptance, every line's worker is an `AGENCY_CONTRACTOR` of that batch's vendor — otherwise `ERR_VENDOR_WORKER_MISMATCH`.
46. On hour creation, the offered payroll document belongs to the same worker and covers `work_date` — otherwise `ERR_PAYROLL_SUPPORT_MISMATCH`.
47. On discrepancy case creation, the named submission belongs to the named facility and, when a measure is named, that measure was computed from that submission — otherwise `ERR_CASE_SUBMISSION_MISMATCH`.
48. On appeal filing, a named contested case belongs to the same facility as the appealed measure — otherwise `ERR_APPEAL_CASE_MISMATCH`.
49. On hour creation, allocation replacement, and agency batch creation, the allocation set is non-empty and its percentages sum to exactly 100.00 — otherwise `ERR_ALLOCATION_SUM_INVALID`.
50. On hour creation and on agency batch acceptance, the computed shift length does not exceed the maximum single-shift length — otherwise `ERR_SHIFT_LENGTH_EXCEEDED`.
51. On hour creation and on agency batch acceptance, the worker's reported hours for that date stay within the daily reported-hours ceiling — otherwise `ERR_DAILY_HOURS_EXCEEDED`.
52. On submission creation, no non-`VOID` submission already exists for that facility and period — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_SUBMISSION`.
53. On census creation, `census_date` is not already used on that submission — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_CENSUS_DAY`.
54. On census creation, `census_date` falls within the submission's period inclusive — otherwise `ERR_CENSUS_DATE_OUTSIDE_PERIOD`.
55. On facility registration, `ccn` is unused — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_CCN`.
56. On period creation, `period_code` is unused for that state and `correction_window_end` is strictly after `submission_deadline` — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_PERIOD_CODE`.
57. On job category creation, `code` is unused and the credit percentage is consistent with the declared class — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_JOB_CATEGORY`.
58. On vendor registration, `tax_id` is unused at that facility — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_VENDOR_TAX_ID`.
59. On payroll document filing, `document_ref` is unused at that facility — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_DOCUMENT_REF`.
60. On worker registration, `vendor_id` is present exactly when the worker is an agency contractor — otherwise `ERR_VENDOR_LINKAGE_INVALID`.
61. On agency batch creation, `lines` has at least one element — otherwise `ERR_VENDOR_LINES_EMPTY`.
62. On measure computation, no `StaffingMeasure` with `publication_status` `PROVISIONAL` or `PUBLISHED` already exists for that submission — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_MEASURE`.
63. On appeal filing, no `RatingAppeal` with `status` `SUBMITTED` already exists for that measure — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_APPEAL`.
64. On reopening, the facility's outstanding surcharge balance plus the assessment this reopen would make stays within the facility-wide surcharge cap — otherwise `ERR_SURCHARGE_CAP_EXCEEDED`.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up the Reporting Universe

Before a single hour can be captured, three pieces of reference data must exist, and all three are the platform operator's responsibility.

A **facility** is identified to the outside world by its `ccn`, the CMS Certification Number, which is exactly 6 characters long and must be unique across every facility in the system; a collision is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CCN`. Registration also supplies the legal `name`, the `state_code` under which the facility reports (exactly two uppercase letters), and `certified_bed_count`, which must be at least 1. The five derived fields are seeded on registration: `posture` at `REPORTING_OPEN`, `open_case_count` at 0, `substantiated_case_count` at 0, `outstanding_surcharge_cents` at 0, and `latest_staffing_rating` at null.

A **reporting period** governs one quarter for one state. It is created with a `period_code` such as `2026Q2`, a `state_code`, an inclusive `start_date` and `end_date` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form (with `end_date` on or after `start_date`), a `submission_deadline`, and a `correction_window_end`. The system computes `day_count` as the inclusive number of calendar days from `start_date` through `end_date` — a period running from the first of April through the thirtieth of June has a `day_count` of 91, not 90 — and sets `status` to `OPEN`. Two uniqueness rules apply at once and share the code `ERR_DUPLICATE_PERIOD_CODE`: `period_code` must be unused for that `state_code`, and `correction_window_end` must be strictly after `submission_deadline`. Overlap is checked separately: the new period's closed-inclusive date range must not intersect the closed-inclusive range of any existing period for the same `state_code`, and sharing even a single boundary date counts as an intersection and is refused with `ERR_PERIOD_OVERLAP`.

Closing a period is how a quarter is put to bed. Closing moves the period's `status` from `OPEN` to `CLOSED` and sweeps every submission of that period in one pass: each `ACCEPTED` submission moves to `FINAL`, and each submission still sitting in `DRAFT`, `VALIDATED`, or `REJECTED` moves to `VOID`, since it was never accepted. Every submission touched by the sweep has its `version` incremented by one. A `FINAL` submission is locked against all further writes. Closing requires the period to be `OPEN`.

A **job category** carries the classification rules that turn a worked hour into a reported hour. It is defined with an internal `code` (unique across all job categories, with a collision refused by `ERR_DUPLICATE_JOB_CATEGORY`), a `cms_job_code` integer in the range 1 through 40 inclusive, a `direct_care_class` of `DIRECT_CARE`, `DIRECT_CARE_SPLIT`, `ADMINISTRATIVE`, or `NON_DIRECT_CARE`, a `nurse_group` of `RN`, `LPN`, `NA`, or `NONE`, and a `direct_care_credit_pct` between 0.00 and 1.00 inclusive carrying exactly 2 decimal places. That credit is the fraction of allocated hours the category earns as direct care, and it is constrained by the class: a category declared `ADMINISTRATIVE` or `NON_DIRECT_CARE` must carry a `direct_care_credit_pct` of exactly 0.00, and a non-zero credit on such a class is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_JOB_CATEGORY` — the same code that guards `code` uniqueness. The optional boolean `weekend_countable` declares whether hours in this category count toward weekend staffing metrics and defaults to `true` when omitted.

### 3.2. Workers, Vendors, and Payroll Evidence

Every hour in the system is attributed to a **worker** who belongs to exactly one facility. Registration supplies the owning `facility_id`, the `full_name` as it appears on payroll, a `worker_type` of `EMPLOYEE`, `AGENCY_CONTRACTOR`, or `INDIVIDUAL_CONTRACTOR`, a `primary_job_category_id` naming the worker's default job category, and a `hire_date` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form, which is the first date on which the worker may report hours. Two optional fields round out the payload. `vendor_id` links the worker to a staffing agency and is governed by a strict biconditional: it must be supplied exactly when `worker_type` is `AGENCY_CONTRACTOR`, and must be absent (defaulting to null) for the other two worker types; violating either direction is refused with `ERR_VENDOR_LINKAGE_INVALID`. `authorized_roles` is an array of role names the worker may act as, whose elements are drawn from `STAFFING_COORDINATOR` and `FACILITY_ADMINISTRATOR`, and which defaults to an empty array — meaning a worker registered without it cannot act at all. A new worker starts with `status` `ACTIVE` and `reported_hours_total` at 0.00. Terminating a worker moves `status` to `TERMINATED`, after which that identity is refused with `ERR_ACTOR_INACTIVE`.

A **staffing vendor** is the agency that supplies contract staff to a facility. It is registered with the receiving `facility_id`, the agency's legal `vendor_name`, its `tax_id` — unique within that `facility_id`, with a collision refused by `ERR_DUPLICATE_VENDOR_TAX_ID` — and `attestation_contact`, the email of the representative who attests submitted hours. A new vendor starts with `status` `ACTIVE` and `accepted_hours_total` at 0.00. Suspending a vendor moves its `status` from `ACTIVE` to `SUSPENDED`, which blocks new batch creation, batch submission, and batch acceptance with `ERR_VENDOR_SUSPENDED`.

**Payroll documents** are the audit substrate. A `PayrollRecord` is filed with the owning `facility_id`, the `worker_id` it covers, an inclusive `pay_period_start` and `pay_period_end` (with the end on or after the start), the `paid_hours` on the document (strictly greater than 0.00, carrying 2 decimal places), a `support_type` of `TIMECARD`, `PAYROLL_JOURNAL`, or `INVOICE`, and an immutable external `document_ref` that must be unique within that `facility_id`; a collision is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_DOCUMENT_REF`. Payroll documents are the one resource with no `updated_at` and no `version`, because a filed document is never edited.

Filing is constrained by an aggregate capacity rule that stops a facility from papering the same days twice. Take the payroll documents already on file for the same `worker_id` whose closed-inclusive pay period intersects the new document's closed-inclusive pay period. Add the `paid_hours` of all of them to the new document's `paid_hours`. Then form the union of all those intersecting pay periods together with the new one, and count the distinct calendar days in that union. The sum may not exceed 24.00 hours multiplied by that day count; exceeding it is refused with `ERR_PAYROLL_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. A single worker cannot be documented for more than a full day of paid time per calendar day, no matter how the pay periods are sliced.

### 3.3. Opening a Quarter and Capturing Worked Hours

A **quarterly submission** is the container for one facility's reported hours and census for one period. It is opened with just a `facility_id` and a `reporting_period_id`, and at most one submission whose `status` is anything other than `VOID` may exist for that pair — a second attempt is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_SUBMISSION`. On creation the submission enters `DRAFT`, sets `amendment_index` to 0, sets every hour and money rollup to its zero value, sets `hprd_reported` and `weekend_hprd_reported` and `audit_support_score` to null, and copies the period's `correction_window_end` into `correction_deadline`.

An **hour entry** records one worked shift. The staffing office supplies the `submission_id` the hour lands in, the `worker_id` who worked the shift, the `job_category_id` the shift is nominally reported under, the `work_date` as a calendar date, the `shift_start` and `shift_end` as times of day in 24-hour `HH:MM` form, the `allocations` array described in the next paragraph, the `expected_submission_version` token from §2.6, and optionally a `payroll_record_id` naming the payroll document offered as support (defaulting to null, which means the hour is reported without support). The entry copies `facility_id` and `reporting_period_id` down from its parent submission rather than accepting them, so an entry can never disagree with its container. Its `source` is set to `PAYROLL`, its `vendor_submission_id` stays null, its `entry_status` starts at `ACTIVE`, its `verification_status` starts at `UNVERIFIED`, its `verified_by_user_id` starts at null, and its `allocation_revision` starts at 0. The `version` of the parent submission is incremented and that new value is recorded on the entry as `submission_version`.

Several guards fire before an hour is admitted. The `work_date` must fall within the period's `start_date` through `end_date` inclusive (`ERR_WORK_DATE_OUTSIDE_PERIOD`) and must be on or after the worker's `hire_date` (`ERR_WORK_DATE_BEFORE_HIRE`). The referenced worker's `facility_id` must equal the submission's `facility_id` (`ERR_WORKER_FACILITY_MISMATCH`). When a `payroll_record_id` is offered, that document's `worker_id` must equal the entry's `worker_id` and the `work_date` must fall within the document's `pay_period_start` through `pay_period_end` inclusive; otherwise the hour is refused with `ERR_PAYROLL_SUPPORT_MISMATCH`. `shift_end` must be strictly later than `shift_start` on the same `work_date` (`ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_INVALID`). And no worker may be in two places at once: treating each shift as the half-open interval that includes its start and excludes its end, the new shift's interval must not intersect the interval of any other `ACTIVE` hour entry for the same `worker_id` on the same `work_date`. Touching endpoints do not intersect, so a shift ending at `15:00` and another starting at `15:00` are both accepted; a genuine intersection is refused with `ERR_SHIFT_OVERLAP`.

**Shift length and split-role allocation.** The shift length in hours, stored as `raw_hours`, is derived from the two times of day: convert each to a count of minutes since midnight by multiplying its hour part by 60 and adding its minute part, subtract the start from the end to get the elapsed minutes, divide by 60, and round half up to 2 decimal places. A single shift may not run longer than 16.00 hours; a longer one is refused with `ERR_SHIFT_LENGTH_EXCEEDED`. Separately, the sum of `raw_hours` across all `ACTIVE` hour entries for the same `worker_id` on the same `work_date`, counting the new entry, may not exceed 20.00 hours; exceeding that ceiling is refused with `ERR_DAILY_HOURS_EXCEEDED`.

The `allocations` array is how a split role is expressed. It must contain at least one element, each element an object carrying a `job_category_id` and a `percent` with exactly 2 decimal places, and the `percent` values must sum to exactly 100.00 — an empty array or a sum that misses 100.00 in either direction is refused with `ERR_ALLOCATION_SUM_INVALID`. The array's order is the pinned processing order: lines are processed exactly as supplied, first element first, and are never re-sorted. For each line in turn, resolve its `job_category_id` to a job category, read that category's `direct_care_credit_pct`, and compute that line's direct-care contribution as `raw_hours` multiplied by the line's `percent` divided by 100, multiplied by the credit fraction, **then rounded half up to 2 decimal places**. `direct_care_hours` is the sum of those already-rounded per-line values. Rounding each line before summing is not an optimisation detail; summing the unrounded line values and rounding the total once is a different and incorrect answer. A 7.50-hour shift split 45.00 / 27.50 / 27.50 across categories crediting 1.00, 0.60, and 0.00 yields 4.62 under the pinned per-line rounding and 4.61 under a single final rounding, and 4.62 is the required result.

Three nurse-group subtotals are cut from the same per-line values: `rn_hours` is the sum of the line contributions whose category has `nurse_group` `RN`, `lpn_hours` the sum for `LPN`, and `na_hours` the sum for `NA`. Lines whose category has `nurse_group` `NONE` contribute to none of the three, though they may still contribute to `direct_care_hours` if their credit fraction is non-zero. `excluded_hours` is `raw_hours` less `direct_care_hours`, rounded half up to 2 decimal places — this is where non-direct-care and administrative time lands. Finally, `is_weekend` is set to true when `work_date` falls on a Saturday or a Sunday and false otherwise.

The staffing office may re-state an entry's split at any time while the parent submission is in `DRAFT` or `VALIDATED` and the correction window is still open, by replacing the whole `allocations` array. Replacement re-runs the classification above from scratch on the stored `raw_hours`, increments `allocation_revision` by one, and re-records `submission_version` from the parent's new `version`. It carries `expected_submission_version` and rejects a stale token as described in §2.6. Replacement on an entry whose `entry_status` is already `VOID` is refused with `ERR_HOUR_ENTRY_VOIDED`.

An hour entry can be withdrawn rather than corrected. Voiding moves `entry_status` from `ACTIVE` to `VOID`, which is terminal, and requires the entry to be `ACTIVE`, the parent submission to be in `DRAFT` or `VALIDATED`, and the logical clock to be at or before the period's correction window end. The void removes the entry's hours from every submission rollup, refreshes the owning worker's `reported_hours_total`, reverts a `VALIDATED` parent submission to `DRAFT`, and increments the submission's `version` by one. Note that `entry_status` and `verification_status` are two independent dimensions on the same entry: voiding never touches the verification dimension, and verifying never touches the void dimension.

**How hours roll up.** Whenever the hour set or census set of a submission changes, the submission's aggregates are recomputed from scratch over two populations: the hour entries of that submission whose `entry_status` is `ACTIVE` (voided entries contribute to nothing), and the census days of that submission whose `status` is `REPORTED`, `RECONCILED`, or `DISPUTED` — all three count. From the hour population, `total_raw_hours` is the sum of `raw_hours`, `total_direct_care_hours` the sum of `direct_care_hours`, `total_excluded_hours` the sum of `excluded_hours`, and `total_rn_direct_care_hours` the sum of `rn_hours`, each rounded half up to 2 decimal places. `weekend_direct_care_hours` is the sum of `direct_care_hours` restricted to entries whose `is_weekend` is true, and `vendor_sourced_hours` is the sum of `direct_care_hours` restricted to entries whose `source` is `VENDOR_SUBMISSION`, both at 2 decimal places. From the census population, `total_resident_days` is the exact integer sum of `resident_count`, and the weekend resident-day total is that same sum restricted to days whose `is_weekend` is true. `weekend_hprd_reported` is null when the weekend resident-day total is 0, and otherwise `weekend_direct_care_hours` divided by that total, rounded half up to 4 decimal places. In the same pass, `reported_hours_total` is refreshed for every worker referenced by the active hour population, as the sum of `raw_hours` over that worker's active entries in the submission, rounded half up to 2 decimal places.

### 3.4. Census Reporting and MDS Reconciliation

Hours mean nothing without a denominator, and the denominator is the midnight resident census. A **census day** is reported against a submission with the `submission_id`, the `census_date` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form, the facility-reported `resident_count` (zero or greater), and optionally `mds_resident_count`, the independently derived count used for reconciliation, which defaults to null when not yet supplied. Like an hour entry, a census day copies `facility_id` and `reporting_period_id` down from its parent submission. `census_date` must be unique within the submission (`ERR_DUPLICATE_CENSUS_DAY`) and must fall within the period's `start_date` through `end_date` inclusive (`ERR_CENSUS_DATE_OUTSIDE_PERIOD`). On creation the day enters `status` `REPORTED`, `variance_pct` is null, `is_weekend` is computed from whether `census_date` falls on a Saturday or Sunday, and `submission_version` records the parent submission's `version` after the write.

Either count may be corrected while the parent submission is in `DRAFT` or `VALIDATED` and the correction window is open, by supplying a new `resident_count`, a new `mds_resident_count`, or both. A correction recomputes `variance_pct` whenever `mds_resident_count` is non-null after the change, re-runs the submission rollup, reverts a `VALIDATED` parent to `DRAFT`, and increments the submission's `version` by one, re-recording it on the day as `submission_version`.

**Reconciliation** is the act of comparing the two counts and deciding whether they agree closely enough. It requires the day to be in `REPORTED` or `DISPUTED`, requires `mds_resident_count` to be non-null (a null count is refused with `ERR_MDS_COUNT_MISSING`), and requires the parent submission to be in `DRAFT` or `VALIDATED`. The variance is computed as follows. When `mds_resident_count` is 0, `variance_pct` is 0.0000 if `resident_count` is also 0 and 1.0000 otherwise. Otherwise, `variance_pct` is the absolute difference between `resident_count` and `mds_resident_count`, divided by `mds_resident_count`, rounded half up to 4 decimal places.

The tolerance is a relative variance of 0.0500 — five percent — and the comparison is made against the already-rounded `variance_pct`, with the boundary itself counting as agreement. A `variance_pct` of 0.0500 or less moves the day to `RECONCILED`; anything strictly greater moves it to `DISPUTED`. A facility-reported count of 105 against an MDS count of 100 produces exactly 0.0500 and reconciles; 106 against 100 produces 0.0600 and disputes. Reconciliation is re-runnable and the two outcomes have different consequences. Moving to `RECONCILED` stores the recomputed `variance_pct` and resolves any `OPEN` finding with `code` `CENSUS_VARIANCE` whose `subject_ref` equals that `census_date`. Moving to `DISPUTED` stores `variance_pct` and creates a `ValidationFinding` with `code` `CENSUS_VARIANCE`, `severity` `WARNING`, `status` `OPEN`, and `subject_ref` equal to that `census_date`. Both outcomes revert a `VALIDATED` parent submission to `DRAFT` and increment the submission's `version` by one. Because the counts can be corrected, a `DISPUTED` day is not stuck: correcting a count and reconciling again moves `DISPUTED` to `RECONCILED` once the variance falls to the tolerance or below, and resolves the outstanding `CENSUS_VARIANCE` finding for that date. There is no terminal census status.

### 3.5. Agency Hour Batches from Staffing Vendors

Agency staff are not on the facility's payroll, so their hours arrive as a **batch** filed by the vendor rather than as individual entries typed by the staffing office. A batch names the supplying `vendor_id`, the receiving `facility_id`, the `reporting_period_id` the lines fall in, an `attested_by` string naming the vendor signatory who attests the batch, and a `lines` array with at least one element — an empty array is refused with `ERR_VENDOR_LINES_EMPTY`. Each line is an object carrying a `worker_id`, a `job_category_id`, a `work_date`, a `shift_start`, a `shift_end`, and its own `allocations` array in exactly the shape an individually captured hour uses. The batch records `line_count` as the number of elements in `lines`, enters `status` `DRAFT`, and starts with `accepted_direct_care_hours` at 0.00, `accepted_hour_entry_ids` as an empty array, `rejection_reason` null, and `submitted_at` null. At creation time each line's `shift_end` must be strictly later than its `shift_start` (`ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_INVALID`), each line's allocation percentages must sum to exactly 100.00 (`ERR_ALLOCATION_SUM_INVALID`), and every line's worker must have `worker_type` `AGENCY_CONTRACTOR` with a `vendor_id` equal to the batch's `vendor_id` — a line naming an employee, an individual contractor, or another agency's contractor is refused with `ERR_VENDOR_WORKER_MISMATCH`.

The vendor representative attests the batch by submitting it, which moves `status` from `DRAFT` to `SUBMITTED`, stamps `submitted_at` with the logical clock instant, and clears any `rejection_reason` left over from an earlier round. Submission requires the batch to be in `DRAFT` or `REJECTED`, the supplying vendor to be `ACTIVE`, and the governing period to be `OPEN`.

Acceptance is the staffing office's act, and it is where agency lines become reportable hours. It requires the batch to be `SUBMITTED`, the vendor to be `ACTIVE`, and a quarterly submission to exist for that batch's `facility_id` and `reporting_period_id` in `DRAFT` or `VALIDATED` status; the accepting request names that container through a `submission_id`. Acceptance moves the batch to `ACCEPTED` and synthesizes one hour entry per element of `lines`, in `lines` order, each classified by exactly the same shift-length and split-role arithmetic an individually captured hour goes through, each with `source` set to `VENDOR_SUBMISSION` and `vendor_submission_id` set to this batch. The ids of the synthesized entries are recorded in `accepted_hour_entry_ids` in `lines` order, `accepted_direct_care_hours` is set to the sum of their `direct_care_hours`, and the vendor's `accepted_hours_total` is refreshed as the sum of `accepted_direct_care_hours` over that vendor's `ACCEPTED` batches. The target submission's rollups and its as-submitted hours-per-resident-day figures are then recomputed, a `VALIDATED` target reverts to `DRAFT`, and the target submission's `version` is incremented by one — as is the batch's own `version`. Because acceptance creates hours, the same per-line guards that police individual capture apply here too: `work_date` inside the period, `work_date` on or after the worker's `hire_date`, the worker belonging to the submission's facility, the worker being an agency contractor of this vendor, no shift overlapping another active entry for that worker and date, no shift longer than 16.00 hours, and no worker exceeding 20.00 reported hours for a single date.

A batch the facility does not trust is rejected instead, which requires the batch to be `SUBMITTED`, records the supplied `rejection_reason`, and moves `status` to `REJECTED`. Rejection is not the end: a rejected batch may be corrected and re-submitted, which clears `rejection_reason`, re-stamps `submitted_at`, and returns the batch to `SUBMITTED`, closing the cycle `DRAFT → SUBMITTED → REJECTED → SUBMITTED`.

Finally, the vendor may withdraw a batch it has already had accepted, provided the target quarterly submission is still in `DRAFT` or `VALIDATED`. Revocation moves the batch from `ACCEPTED` to the terminal `REVOKED`, sets `entry_status` to `VOID` on every hour entry listed in `accepted_hour_entry_ids`, resets `accepted_direct_care_hours` to 0.00, refreshes the vendor's `accepted_hours_total`, recomputes the target submission's rollups, reverts a `VALIDATED` target to `DRAFT`, and increments both the batch's and the target submission's `version` by one.

### 3.6. The Validation Pass, Findings, and Attestation

**Validation** is the staffing office's dry run of the quarter, and it is re-runnable: every run recomputes findings from scratch. It requires the submission to be in `DRAFT`, the governing period to be `OPEN`, the facility's `posture` to be anything other than `SANCTIONED`, and the submission to have at least one census day in `REPORTED`, `RECONCILED`, or `DISPUTED` — a submission with no census data at all is refused with `ERR_NO_CENSUS_DATA`. A successful run refreshes every hour and census rollup, computes the as-submitted hours per resident day into `hprd_reported` and the weekend figure into `weekend_hprd_reported`, rebuilds the finding set, refreshes `open_fatal_finding_count` and `open_warning_finding_count`, records the acting worker's id as `validated_by_worker_id`, moves `status` from `DRAFT` to `VALIDATED`, and increments `version` by one.

**Hours per resident day** is the central published metric, and it is computed twice from the same submission under two different readings. Under the **as-submitted** reading, the numerator population is every hour entry of the submission whose `entry_status` is `ACTIVE`, regardless of `verification_status`, and each counted census day contributes its facility-reported `resident_count`. Under the **audit-adjusted** reading, the numerator population is narrowed to active entries whose `verification_status` is `SUPPORTED` — unverified and unsupported hours are dropped entirely — and each counted census day contributes its `mds_resident_count` when that is non-null and its `resident_count` otherwise. In both readings the census population is the days in `REPORTED`, `RECONCILED`, or `DISPUTED`.

Given a reading, three hour sums are formed and each is rounded half up to 2 decimal places: the total direct-care hours over the numerator population, the total `rn_hours` over that population, and the direct-care hours over that population restricted to entries whose `is_weekend` is true. Two denominators are formed as exact integer sums: the counted residents over all included census days, and the counted residents over the weekend days only. Then `total_nurse_hprd` is the total direct-care hours divided by the resident-day denominator, rounded half up to 4 decimal places, or null when that denominator is 0; `rn_hprd` is the RN hours divided by the same denominator on the same terms; and `weekend_total_nurse_hprd` is the weekend direct-care hours divided by the weekend denominator, rounded half up to 4 decimal places, or null when the weekend denominator is 0. A result is flagged implausible when `total_nurse_hprd` is non-null and strictly greater than 12.0000 hours per resident day. The validation pass writes the as-submitted `total_nurse_hprd` to `hprd_reported`, which is null when `total_resident_days` is 0.

Either reading can also be requested as a computed read that persists nothing. The read takes an optional `variant` query parameter; omitting it or passing `AS_SUBMITTED` selects the as-submitted reading and passing `AUDIT_ADJUSTED` selects the audit-adjusted one. The response carries the `submission_id`, the `variant` actually applied, `total_direct_care_hours`, `total_rn_hours`, `weekend_direct_care_hours`, `resident_days`, `weekend_resident_days`, `total_nurse_hprd`, `rn_hprd`, `weekend_total_nurse_hprd`, and the boolean `implausible`.

**The finding set.** A `ValidationFinding` records one problem with one submission. It carries a `code`, a `severity`, a `subject_ref` that is an opaque human-readable pointer to the offending subject — typically a `work_date` or `census_date` string, and never a foreign key — a human-readable `detail`, a `status`, a `waived_by_user_id`, and a `resolved_at`. `FATAL` findings block attestation; `WARNING` findings do not. Every finding begins at `status` `OPEN` with `waived_by_user_id` and `resolved_at` null. The validation pass raises exactly these five kinds:

- `CENSUS_INCOMPLETE`, `severity` `WARNING`, one finding per calendar date from the period's `start_date` through `end_date` inclusive for which the submission has no census day at all, with `subject_ref` set to that date.
- `CENSUS_VARIANCE`, `severity` `WARNING`, one finding per census day whose `status` is `DISPUTED`.
- `HPRD_IMPLAUSIBLE`, `severity` `FATAL`, a single finding with `subject_ref` set to the literal string `PERIOD`, raised when the as-submitted `hprd_reported` exceeds 12.0000.
- `MISSING_PAYROLL_SUPPORT`, `severity` `WARNING`, one finding per `ACTIVE` hour entry whose `payroll_record_id` is null.
- `ZERO_DIRECT_CARE_DAY`, `severity` `WARNING`, one finding per census day whose `census_date` has no `ACTIVE` hour entry carrying `direct_care_hours` greater than 0.00.

The same pass closes the loop in the other direction: every finding that was previously `OPEN` and whose condition no longer holds is moved to `status` `RESOLVED` with `resolved_at` stamped from the logical clock. Two further codes exist but are never raised by the validation pass itself: `UNSUPPORTED_HOURS`, raised as a `WARNING` when an auditor marks an hour unsupported, and `FACILITY_SANCTIONED`, raised as a `FATAL` by the sanction cascade of §2.7.

A state auditor may **waive** an open finding so that it stops counting, by supplying a `waiver_note`. Waiving requires the finding's `status` to be `OPEN` (`ERR_FINDING_NOT_OPEN`), moves `status` to `WAIVED`, records `waived_by_user_id` from the acting identity, stamps `resolved_at`, appends the `waiver_note` to the finding's `detail`, refreshes the submission's `open_fatal_finding_count` and `open_warning_finding_count`, and increments the submission's `version` by one. Waiving a `FATAL` finding is therefore the auditor's route to unblocking a stuck quarter.

**Attestation** moves the submission from `VALIDATED` to `SUBMITTED` and is reserved to facility leadership. It requires the submission to be `VALIDATED`, `open_fatal_finding_count` to be exactly 0 (`ERR_FATAL_FINDINGS_OPEN`), the facility's `posture` to be exactly `REPORTING_OPEN` — an `AUDIT_HOLD` facility is refused with `ERR_FACILITY_UNDER_HOLD` — the acting worker's id to differ from `validated_by_worker_id` (`ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`), and the logical clock to be at or before the period's correction window end. It records the acting worker's id as `attested_by_worker_id`, stamps `submitted_at` with the logical clock instant, and increments `version` by one. Attestation after the `submission_deadline` is still permitted while the correction window remains open; being late does not block the attestation, it only feeds the late-fee arithmetic of any later amendment.

### 3.7. State Review: Acceptance, Payroll Verification, Audit Readiness, and Amendments

A `SUBMITTED` quarter sits with the state survey agency, which may accept it or send it back. **Rejection** requires the submission to be `SUBMITTED`, records the supplied `rejection_reason` and the acting identity as `accepted_by_user_id`, moves `status` to `REJECTED`, and increments `version` by one.

**Acceptance** requires the submission to be `SUBMITTED` and requires the acting identity to differ from `verified_by_user_id` on every hour entry of that submission — an auditor who personally verified any hour of the quarter may not also sign off on the quarter, and an attempt to do so is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. Acceptance moves `status` to `ACCEPTED`, records `accepted_by_user_id` and stamps `accepted_at`, scores the quarter's audit readiness on a sampled basis and stores the result in `audit_support_score`, and increments `version` by one. When that score comes out below the risk floor of 70.00, the acceptance opens a discrepancy investigation on the spot: a `DiscrepancyCase` with `auto_opened` true, `trigger_reason` `AUDIT_SCORE_BELOW_FLOOR`, `opened_by_user_id` set to the literal string `SYSTEM`, and `status` `OPEN`. Opening that case immediately re-derives the facility's `posture`, `open_case_count`, and `substantiated_case_count`, which in practice moves a previously clean facility from `REPORTING_OPEN` to `AUDIT_HOLD` and thereby blocks its next attestation.

**Payroll verification** is how an auditor asserts, hour by hour, that the payroll record actually supports the reported time. The verification carries an `outcome` of either `SUPPORTED` or `UNSUPPORTED`. It requires the entry's `verification_status` to be `UNVERIFIED` or `UNSUPPORTED` — an already-`SUPPORTED` entry is not re-verifiable — and requires the entry's `entry_status` to be `ACTIVE` (`ERR_HOUR_ENTRY_VOIDED`). The two outcomes are not symmetric.

Recording `SUPPORTED` additionally requires the entry's `payroll_record_id` to be non-null; verifying an unsupported-by-construction hour as supported is refused with `ERR_SUPPORT_DOCUMENT_MISSING`. It moves `verification_status` to `SUPPORTED` from either `UNVERIFIED` or `UNSUPPORTED`, sets `verified_by_user_id` to the acting identity, resolves the `OPEN` `UNSUPPORTED_HOURS` finding previously raised for this entry, resolves any `OPEN` `MISSING_PAYROLL_SUPPORT` finding whose `subject_ref` names this entry's `work_date` and whose condition no longer holds, and refreshes the parent submission's `open_warning_finding_count`.

Recording `UNSUPPORTED` imposes no documentation precondition. It moves `verification_status` from `UNVERIFIED` to `UNSUPPORTED`, sets `verified_by_user_id` to the acting identity, creates a new `ValidationFinding` on the parent submission with `code` `UNSUPPORTED_HOURS`, `severity` `WARNING`, and `status` `OPEN`, and refreshes the parent submission's `open_warning_finding_count`. Neither outcome touches `entry_status`, and neither recomputes the submission's hour rollups — unsupported hours remain in the as-submitted totals and only disappear under the audit-adjusted reading.

**Audit readiness scoring** combines three ratios into a single score out of 100. Take the considered entry set for the chosen reading. `considered_hours` is the sum of `direct_care_hours` over that set, rounded half up to 2 decimal places, and `supported_hours` is the same sum restricted to entries whose `verification_status` is `SUPPORTED`. The support ratio is 1.0000 when `considered_hours` is 0.00, and otherwise `supported_hours` divided by `considered_hours` rounded half up to 4 decimal places. Next, take the entries in the considered set whose `source` is `VENDOR_SUBMISSION`; the vendor attestation ratio is 1.0000 when there are none, and otherwise the count of those whose originating batch has `status` `ACCEPTED` divided by the count of all of them, rounded half up to 4 decimal places. Third, take the submission's census days in `REPORTED`, `RECONCILED`, or `DISPUTED`; the census reconciled ratio is 0.0000 when there are none — a submission with no census days scores nothing on this dimension rather than a free full mark — and otherwise the count of those whose `status` is `RECONCILED` divided by the total count, rounded half up to 4 decimal places. Each of the three ratios is rounded to 4 decimal places before it is weighted. The score is then 100 multiplied by the sum of the support ratio weighted at 0.50, the vendor attestation ratio weighted at 0.30, and the census reconciled ratio weighted at 0.20, with that single product rounded half up to 2 decimal places. A submission is at risk when the score is strictly below 70.00, so a score of exactly 70.00 is not at risk.

The two readings differ only in the considered set. The **sampled** reading, which is the one acceptance uses, takes the first 5 `ACTIVE` hour entries of the submission ordered by `work_date` ascending with `created_at` ascending as the tie-break; when the submission has fewer than 5 active entries, the sample is all of them. The **full** reading takes every `ACTIVE` hour entry of the submission, with no truncation and therefore no dependence on ordering. Both readings are available as a computed read against a facility that persists nothing: it takes a `submission_id` and an optional `variant`, where omitting the variant or passing `SAMPLED` selects the sampled reading and passing `FULL` selects the full one, and answers with `submission_id`, the `variant` applied, `considered_entry_count`, `considered_hours`, `supported_hours`, `support_ratio`, `vendor_attestation_ratio`, `census_reconciled_ratio`, `score`, and `at_risk`.

**Amendments cost money.** Reopening a quarter for correction is available from either `ACCEPTED` or `REJECTED`, and in both cases it lands the submission back in `DRAFT`, closing the cycle `DRAFT → VALIDATED → SUBMITTED → ACCEPTED → DRAFT`. It requires the governing period to be `OPEN` and the logical clock to be at or before the period's correction window end, increments `amendment_index` by one, adds the assessed surcharge to `amendment_surcharge_cents`, refreshes the facility's `outstanding_surcharge_cents`, clears both `validated_by_worker_id` and `attested_by_worker_id` so the segregation-of-duties pairing must be re-established, and increments `version` by one.

The surcharge is assessed before the status change is applied, and its evaluation order is pinned. First, the amendment index the reopen would produce is the current `amendment_index` plus one. The base fee is 0 cents when the submission being reopened is in `REJECTED` — a quarter the state sent back is corrected free of charge — and otherwise is 25000 cents plus 2500 cents for each amendment beyond the first, that is, 2500 cents multiplied by one less than the prospective amendment index. Second, lateness is measured from the period's `submission_deadline`: take the whole seconds from that deadline to the current logical clock instant; if that is zero or negative there are no late days, and otherwise the late-day count is that number of seconds divided by 86400 and rounded **up** to the next whole number, so any fraction of a day past the deadline counts as a full late day. Third — and this is the pinned part — the late rate is selected from the **base fee**, not from the eventual total: the rate is 0.0300 when the base fee is at least 27500 cents, 0.0200 when it is at least 25000 cents, 0.0100 when it is at least 1 cent, and 0.0000 when the base fee is 0. Fourth, the late fee is the base fee multiplied by that rate multiplied by the late-day count, rounded half up to a whole number of cents, and the surcharge is the base fee plus the late fee. Because the tier is fixed from the base fee, the resulting surcharge may land in a numerically higher tier band than the rate applied to it; reading the tier off the final total is a different and incorrect result. A base fee of 25000 cents with 6 late days yields 28000 cents under the pinned order, where reading the tier off the total would yield 29500.

The cap is facility-wide, not per submission. If the facility's current `outstanding_surcharge_cents` plus the surcharge this reopen would assess exceeds 50000 cents, the reopen is refused with `ERR_SURCHARGE_CAP_EXCEEDED` and no state change of any kind is applied — no index increment, no fee accrual, no status move. Only when the sum stays within the cap does the assessment commit.

### 3.8. Public Staffing Measures and the Star Rating

An accepted quarter becomes a public measure when the national publisher computes one from it. Computation names the source `submission_id`, requires that submission's `status` to be `ACCEPTED`, requires it to have at least one counted census day, requires that no measure with `publication_status` `PROVISIONAL` or `PUBLISHED` already exists for that submission (`ERR_DUPLICATE_MEASURE`), and requires that no existing measure for that submission be frozen with `audit_status` `UNDER_AUDIT` (`ERR_MEASURE_UNDER_AUDIT`). The new `StaffingMeasure` copies `facility_id` and `reporting_period_id` from the source submission, sets `measure_variant` to `AS_SUBMITTED`, `publication_status` to `PROVISIONAL`, `audit_status` to `NOT_AUDITED`, `published_at` and `appeal_deadline` to null, `has_open_appeal` to false, and `computed_by_user_id` to the acting identity. It then computes `total_nurse_hprd`, `rn_hprd`, and `weekend_total_nurse_hprd` under the as-submitted reading described in §3.6, and derives its star points and rating.

**Star assignment runs in four steps, in order.** Step one is two independent tier lookups, every boundary of which is inclusive. `total_nurse_points` is 5 when `total_nurse_hprd` is at least 3.6000, otherwise 4 when it is at least 3.0000, otherwise 3 when it is at least 2.4000, otherwise 2 when it is at least 1.8000, and otherwise 1. `rn_points` is 5 when `rn_hprd` is at least 0.7500, otherwise 4 when it is at least 0.5500, otherwise 3 when it is at least 0.3500, otherwise 2 when it is at least 0.2000, and otherwise 1. A null hours-per-resident-day figure — which happens when the submission has no resident days at all — scores 1 point on that dimension. Step two combines the two: the weaker dimension governs, so the base star is the smaller of `total_nurse_points` and `rn_points`. Step three applies the weekend staffing floor to that combined star rather than to either tier separately: when `weekend_total_nurse_hprd` is null, or is strictly less than 2.4000, the star is reduced by one but never below 1; when it is exactly 2.4000 or greater, the star is unchanged. The floor is therefore exclusive — equalling it does not downgrade. Step four is the zero-RN-day override, applied last and unconditionally. `days_with_zero_rn_hours` is the count of the source submission's census days in `REPORTED`, `RECONCILED`, or `DISPUTED` whose `census_date` has no `ACTIVE` hour entry of that submission carrying `rn_hours` greater than 0.00. When that count reaches 4 or more, `staffing_rating` is forced to 1 regardless of everything computed above; otherwise `staffing_rating` is the star that survived step three. Every comparison in all four steps is made against the 4-decimal rounded hours-per-resident-day values.

`StaffingMeasure` carries two independent state dimensions, and neither one moves the other. `publication_status` is the public-facing dimension. **Publishing** moves it from `PROVISIONAL` to `PUBLISHED`; it requires `publication_status` to be `PROVISIONAL`, requires `audit_status` to be one of `NOT_AUDITED`, `AUDIT_CONFIRMED`, or `AUDIT_REVISED` (so a measure frozen `UNDER_AUDIT` cannot be published, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_MEASURE_UNDER_AUDIT`), and requires the facility's `posture` to be anything other than `SANCTIONED` (`ERR_FACILITY_SANCTIONED`). Publishing stamps `published_at` with the logical clock instant, sets `appeal_deadline` to `published_at` plus 30 calendar days, and refreshes the facility's `latest_staffing_rating`. From `PUBLISHED` the only exit is to the terminal `SUPPRESSED`, which is not a direct operation but a consequence of a granted appeal; suppression clears the facility's `latest_staffing_rating` when no other `PUBLISHED` measure remains for that facility, and sets `has_open_appeal` back to false.

`audit_status` is the second dimension, and every transition on it is a consequence of a discrepancy investigation rather than a request against the measure. It moves from `NOT_AUDITED` to `UNDER_AUDIT` when a case naming the measure enters `UNDER_REVIEW`; that freezes the measure against recomputation for the same submission. From `UNDER_AUDIT` it moves to `AUDIT_CONFIRMED` when the case is dismissed, which leaves `measure_variant`, `total_nurse_hprd`, `rn_hprd`, and `staffing_rating` exactly as they were. From `UNDER_AUDIT` it moves to `AUDIT_REVISED` when the case is substantiated, and that path rewrites the measure in place: `measure_variant` flips to `AUDIT_ADJUSTED`, `total_nurse_hprd`, `rn_hprd`, and `weekend_total_nurse_hprd` are recomputed under the audit-adjusted reading, `total_nurse_points`, `rn_points`, `days_with_zero_rn_hours`, and `staffing_rating` are re-derived from those new figures by the same four-step assignment, and the facility's `latest_staffing_rating` is refreshed if this measure's `publication_status` is `PUBLISHED`. Throughout all of this, `publication_status` is untouched by audit transitions — a published measure whose rating has been revised downward remains published, under a new number.

### 3.9. Discrepancy Investigations and Rating Appeals

An investigation is opened either automatically, by an acceptance whose audit-readiness score fell below the floor, or deliberately by a state auditor. A manually opened `DiscrepancyCase` names the `facility_id` under investigation, the `submission_id` whose data is disputed, a `trigger_reason` of `AUDIT_SCORE_BELOW_FLOOR`, `UNSUPPORTED_HOURS`, `CENSUS_MISMATCH`, `VENDOR_DATA_GAP`, or `PUBLIC_COMPLAINT`, an investigator `detail` narrative, and optionally a `staffing_measure_id` naming the measure the investigation affects, which defaults to null when no measure exists yet. Referential integrity is checked in one step: the named submission's `facility_id` must equal the case's `facility_id`, and when a measure is named its own `submission_id` must equal the case's `submission_id`; either mismatch is refused with `ERR_CASE_SUBMISSION_MISMATCH`. A manually opened case sets `status` `OPEN`, `auto_opened` false, and `opened_by_user_id` to the acting identity, and starts with `info_request_count` and `facility_response_count` at 0 and `last_request_note`, `last_response_note`, `substantiated_by_user_id`, and `resolution_note` all null. Creation immediately re-derives the facility's `posture` and `open_case_count`. An auto-opened case carries `auto_opened` true and the literal string `SYSTEM` in `opened_by_user_id`.

Evidence gathering is iterative. The auditor requests information by supplying a `request_note`; this is permitted from `OPEN` or `UNDER_REVIEW`, moves `status` to `INFO_REQUESTED`, records the note as `last_request_note`, and increments `info_request_count` by one. The facility answers by supplying a `response_note`; this is permitted only from `INFO_REQUESTED`, requires the acting worker's `facility_id` to equal the case's `facility_id`, moves `status` to `UNDER_REVIEW`, records the note as `last_response_note`, and increments `facility_response_count` by one. A response also has a cross-entity consequence: when the case names a `staffing_measure_id` and that measure's `audit_status` is `NOT_AUDITED`, the response moves that measure's `audit_status` to `UNDER_AUDIT`, freezing it. From `UNDER_REVIEW` the auditor may request information again, which returns `status` to `INFO_REQUESTED` and increments `info_request_count`, closing the cycle `INFO_REQUESTED → UNDER_REVIEW → INFO_REQUESTED` for as many evidence rounds as the investigation needs.

Two outcomes close an investigation, and they differ sharply in blast radius. **Dismissal** is permitted only from `UNDER_REVIEW`, records the supplied `resolution_note`, moves `status` to the terminal `DISMISSED`, moves any named measure's `audit_status` from `UNDER_AUDIT` to `AUDIT_CONFIRMED` with `staffing_rating` left alone, and re-derives the facility's `posture` and `open_case_count` — which drops an `AUDIT_HOLD` facility back to `REPORTING_OPEN` if that was its last open case and it has no substantiated one.

**Substantiation** is permitted from either `INFO_REQUESTED` or `UNDER_REVIEW`, but in both cases only after the facility has been heard at least once: the case's `facility_response_count` must be at least 1, and a substantiation attempted before any response is refused with `ERR_CASE_RESPONSE_REQUIRED`. This is exactly what allows substantiation from `INFO_REQUESTED` — a further evidence round may be opened after a response, and the facility's earlier answer still counts as having been heard. Substantiation records `substantiated_by_user_id` and the supplied `resolution_note`, moves `status` to `SUBSTANTIATED`, and then fires three cascades. First, when the case names a `staffing_measure_id`, that measure's `audit_status` moves from `UNDER_AUDIT` to `AUDIT_REVISED` with the full audit-adjusted recompute of §3.8, including the refresh of the facility's `latest_staffing_rating` when the revised measure is `PUBLISHED`. Second, the facility's `posture` is re-derived, which sets it to `SANCTIONED` and refreshes `substantiated_case_count` and `open_case_count`. Third, every `VALIDATED` submission of that facility reverts to `DRAFT`, gains a `FATAL` `FACILITY_SANCTIONED` finding, and has its `version` incremented by one.

`SUBSTANTIATED` is not terminal. The one exit is to `OVERTURNED`, which is a consequence of a granted appeal: it copies the appeal's `decision_note` into the case's `resolution_note` and re-derives the facility's `posture`, which clears `SANCTIONED` once no other substantiated case remains for that facility.

**Rating appeals.** A facility administrator contests a published rating by filing a `RatingAppeal` against the `staffing_measure_id`, with `grounds` of `DATA_ENTRY_ERROR`, `CENSUS_ERROR`, `VENDOR_DATA_ERROR`, or `MEASURE_CALCULATION_ERROR`, a `narrative` explaining the facility's position, the `expected_measure_version` token of §2.6, and optionally a `discrepancy_case_id` naming the case the appeal contests, defaulting to null when the appeal stands alone. Filing requires the referenced measure's `publication_status` to be `PUBLISHED` (`ERR_MEASURE_NOT_PUBLISHED`), requires the logical clock to be at or before that measure's `appeal_deadline` — the instant 30 calendar days after publication — with a later filing refused as `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`, requires that no appeal with `status` `SUBMITTED` already exist for that measure (`ERR_DUPLICATE_APPEAL`), and requires any named contested case to belong to the same facility as the appealed measure (`ERR_APPEAL_CASE_MISMATCH`). The appeal copies `facility_id` from the appealed measure, records the acting worker's id as `filed_by_worker_id`, enters `status` `SUBMITTED` with `decided_by_user_id`, `decision_note`, and `decided_at` null and `surcharge_waived_cents` at 0, sets the appealed measure's `has_open_appeal` to true, and increments that measure's `version` by one.

An independent reviewer then decides, and the two decisions are deliberately asymmetric. Both require the appeal's `status` to be `SUBMITTED` (`ERR_APPEAL_STATE_INVALID`) and the appealed measure's `publication_status` to still be `PUBLISHED`, and both record `decided_by_user_id`, the supplied `decision_note`, and `decided_at` from the logical clock.

**Granting** moves the appeal's `status` to the terminal `GRANTED` and unwinds four things. The appealed measure moves from `PUBLISHED` to `SUPPRESSED` and its `has_open_appeal` is cleared, with the facility's `latest_staffing_rating` refreshed — cleared to null when no other published measure remains. When the appeal names a `discrepancy_case_id` and that case's `status` is `SUBSTANTIATED`, the case moves to `OVERTURNED` and the facility's `posture` is re-derived. And the money is returned: `surcharge_waived_cents` on the appeal is set to the source submission's `amendment_surcharge_cents` less its `waived_surcharge_cents` — that is, the whole amount still outstanding on that submission — that amount is added to the submission's `waived_surcharge_cents`, and the facility's `outstanding_surcharge_cents` is refreshed accordingly.

**Denying** moves the appeal's `status` to the terminal `DENIED` and clears the appealed measure's `has_open_appeal`. Beyond the three decision fields and that flag, nothing changes: the measure keeps its `publication_status` and its `staffing_rating`, any contested case keeps its `status`, and the submission's `amendment_surcharge_cents` and `waived_surcharge_cents` are left exactly as they were.
