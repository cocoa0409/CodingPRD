# Textile & Apparel Labeling Compliance API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Apparel brands do not fail labeling law because they intend to deceive. They fail it because the label is printed months before the laboratory report lands, because the mill that spun the yarn quietly moved to another country, because the hangtag copywriter and the sewn-in-label author work from different spreadsheets, and because nobody can say afterwards which piece of evidence the printed string was supposed to rest on. By the time a customs officer at the port pulls a carton and finds no country-of-origin statement, the goods are already in the trade, the penalty exposure is real, and the brand has no defensible record of what it believed and when.

This document specifies the Textile & Apparel Labeling Compliance service: a system of record that refuses to let a label reach production until every statement printed on it is tied to a live piece of substantiating evidence. Fiber content is not "declared and trusted" — it is declared, benched at an accredited laboratory, and compared against the measured result under the strictest tolerance among the markets the style will be sold into. Country of origin is not a field someone types — it is computed twice, once by the substantial-transformation rule and once by the yarn-forward rule, from a supply graph the brand actually filed, and a compliance officer then pins which of the two legal methods applies. Care instructions are not copied from a similar garment — they are substantiated by a wash-performance run that tested the exact temperature the label prints. When any of that evidence is disproved or invalidated, the system does not warn: it withdraws the affected artifacts automatically, all the way down to the hangtags hanging off them.

The service also owns the consequences. Imported entries are marked-reviewed at the border, penalties are assessed against a ledger that follows contractual responsibility rather than whichever legal entity happened to file the entry, and a brand that accumulates enough exposure or enough critical audit findings loses the right to approve new labels at all. Independent auditors raise findings against mislabeled inventory, the brand dispositions every affected unit, and the auditor — never the party that performed the remediation — verifies it. Segregation of duties is not advisory here; it is enforced request by request.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

Every endpoint in this specification is mounted under the `/labeling` prefix, with a single administrative exception: the test-driving logical-clock endpoint sits at the top level under `/_clock`. All request and response bodies are JSON.

### 2.1. Operators, Roles and Access Control

Every request must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting operator, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role it acts in. If either header is absent or blank after trimming, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the seven role names defined below, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). This is the very first check the service performs, ahead of every other validation in this document. Once the caller is authenticated, the service compares the acting role against the list of roles authorized for the invoked endpoint in §4; a role that is not listed there is rejected with `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403).

The seven roles are `ComplianceAdmin`, `BrandAdmin`, `LabelingSpecialist`, `LabelingApprover`, `AccreditedLab`, `CustomsOfficer` and `Auditor`.

A `ComplianceAdmin` is the platform's own compliance operator. It owns the market-requirement registry, the brand registry, the logical clock and the legal pinning of an origin determination, and it may stand in for most operational roles across every brand — but it is deliberately barred from approving a label and from verifying an audit finding, so that no single role can both author compliance evidence and bless it. A `BrandAdmin` is the brand of record acting for itself. A `LabelingSpecialist` authors label artwork content and may not approve anything. A `LabelingApprover` is the only role that can bless a label for production, and it can neither author nor submit the artifact it approves. An `AccreditedLab` is the independent laboratory: it reserves benches and is the only role permitted to post measurement results, so no brand-side operator can write the evidence its own labels depend on. A `CustomsOfficer` is the border authority and the sole role that may decide a marking review; it cannot file entries, cannot file corrections and cannot touch labels. An `Auditor` is the independent compliance auditor and the sole role that may verify a remediated finding; it cannot author, approve or publish labels, cannot record the disposition on the very finding it will later verify, and cannot decide a marking review.

Identity semantics differ by role, and they matter because ownership checks depend on them. When `X-User-Role` is `BrandAdmin`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Brand` row; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Brand` compare the resource's brand-bearing field to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, for a `BrandAdmin` the brand chain must resolve to the acting brand id along all four routes the service knows: a style's `brand_id` directly, a material input's `style_id` resolved to its style's `brand_id`, a shipment's `importer_brand_id`, and an audit finding's `style_id` resolved to its style's `brand_id`. A mismatch on any of these is rejected with `ERR_BRAND_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (HTTP 403). This scope check guards exactly six write operations: style creation, fiber-declaration replacement, material-input append, hangtag creation, import-shipment filing and import-shipment correction.

For the other six roles — `ComplianceAdmin`, `LabelingSpecialist`, `LabelingApprover`, `AccreditedLab`, `CustomsOfficer` and `Auditor` — `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server may record it (for example, a `LabelingSpecialist`'s identifier is stored as `authored_by_user_id` on the label artifact it creates or last edited, and a `LabelingApprover`'s identifier is stored as `approved_by_user_id` on approval) but it never compares such an identifier to any entity's `id`.

The one authorization rule that reads a stored identifier is segregation of duties on label approval: the `X-User-ID` of the approving `LabelingApprover` must differ from the `authored_by_user_id` recorded on the artifact. If they are the same operator, the approval is rejected with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). Because this check needs the loaded row, it is evaluated after existence and state checks, but it remains an authorization decision rather than a business rule.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every list endpoint accepts two optional query parameters. `limit` controls page size: it defaults to 10 when omitted, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` controls the starting position: it defaults to 0 and its minimum accepted value is 0. A `limit` outside the range of 1 through 100 inclusive, or a negative `offset`, is a request-shape violation and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400).

List responses carry `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside `meta`. `total_count` is the number of rows matching the filters before the page window is applied, and `has_more` is true exactly when rows remain beyond the current window. Unless a specific list endpoint says otherwise, results are sorted by `created_at` ascending. Two list endpoints override that default: the market-requirement list is ordered by `market_code` ascending, and the supply-graph node list is ordered by `depth` ascending and then by `created_at` ascending within a depth.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and the Logical Clock

Every persisted resource carries a server-minted `id`, formatted as a UUID with no type prefix, and a `created_at` timestamp. Most resources additionally carry an `updated_at` timestamp that is refreshed on every mutation and an integer `version` that starts at 1 on creation and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation of that row — including mutations the row suffers as a side effect of an operation aimed at some other resource. The one exception is the market-requirement resource: a market regime is immutable once registered, so those rows carry only `id` and `created_at`, and neither `updated_at` nor `version` is ever emitted for them. There is no update endpoint for a market regime.

`created_at`, `updated_at` and the two envelope timestamps are ISO 8601 UTC instants rendered with three fractional digits. Every other datetime in this specification — whether supplied on input or emitted on output — is an ISO 8601 UTC instant rendered with second precision and a trailing `Z`, for example `2026-08-03T09:00:00Z`; no fractional-second digits are ever emitted or accepted on those fields. The fields governed by that rule are `specimen_received_at`, `results_due_at`, `valid_until`, `issued_at`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, `review_decided_at`, `redelivery_deadline_at`, `audit_window_from`, `audit_window_to`, `closed_at`, `disposition_recorded_at` and `verified_at`.

Temporal rules in this system never read the host's wall clock. Instead the service maintains a **logical clock** that governs test-evidence validity, redelivery windows, label effective windows, laboratory bench occupancy and audit windows. At server startup the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and until it is explicitly moved it must not fall back to real time. A `ComplianceAdmin` moves it by supplying a `current_utc` instant to the administrative clock endpoint, which echoes back the value it set; setting the clock mutates no stored row and triggers no recomputation of any derived field.

### 2.5. Optimistic Concurrency on Parent Rows

Three write operations are guarded by optimistic concurrency control, and in each case the version being checked belongs to the *parent* row whose state the write reasons about, not to the row being created.

`PUT /labeling/styles/{id}/fiber-declaration` requires a non-optional `expected_style_version` field carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `Style`. Replacing a whole fiber declaration is a legitimate supersede — a writer that submits the same replacement against an up-to-date style version always succeeds — so nothing else blocks it. A writer whose `expected_style_version` is behind the style's current `version` has therefore based its replacement on a declaration, a test outcome or an origin state that has since moved, and the replacement is rejected with `ERR_STALE_STYLE_VERSION` (HTTP 412) rather than silently discarding the newer facts.

`POST /labeling/origin-determinations` likewise requires a non-optional `expected_style_version` matching the target style's current `version`. Creating a determination legitimately supersedes the style's prior draft determination, so a create against an unchanged style would itself succeed. A create carrying an `expected_style_version` behind the style's current `version` was computed against a supply graph that has since gained or lost a node, and it is rejected with `ERR_STALE_STYLE_VERSION` (HTTP 412) so that the frozen candidate-country pair can never disagree with the graph it claims to describe.

`POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/hangtags` requires a non-optional `expected_parent_label_spec_version` matching the current `version` of the parent permanent label named in the path. A style carries at most one live hangtag per permanent label, and creating a new one legitimately supersedes the prior hangtag, so no state precondition blocks the create. A create carrying an `expected_parent_label_spec_version` behind the parent's current `version` was authored against parent content that has since been edited, re-approved or withdrawn, and it is rejected with `ERR_STALE_LABEL_VERSION` (HTTP 412).

No other endpoint in this specification accepts a version field, and none may be rejected for a version reason.

### 2.6. Path Resolution and Reference Eligibility

Identifiers embedded in a URL path are resolved strictly left to right, and the first one that does not resolve to a live row decides the 404. The per-kind codes are `ERR_BRAND_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STYLE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MATERIAL_INPUT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LAB_TEST_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ORIGIN_DETERMINATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LABEL_SPEC_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_IMPORT_SHIPMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_COMPLIANCE_AUDIT_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_AUDIT_FINDING_NOT_FOUND`, all HTTP 404.

Identifiers supplied in a request body are resolved after the path, in the field order the endpoint declares for its input list, and they produce the same per-kind 404 codes. A market code supplied anywhere — in a style's `target_market_codes`, in a label artifact's `market_codes`, or as a shipment's `entry_market_code` — must resolve to a registered market regime; if it does not, the request is rejected with `ERR_MARKET_REQUIREMENT_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404).

Existence is a weaker question than eligibility. A referenced row may exist and still be the wrong kind of thing or sit in the wrong state, in which case the rejection is `ERR_REFERENCE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (HTTP 422). The eligibility rules are: an `origin_determination_id` must name a determination belonging to the same style; a `care_test_id` must name a care-performance laboratory run of the same style whose state is `PASSED`; a `corrective_label_spec_id` must name a permanent label of the same style whose state is `PUBLISHED`, and on an audit-finding disposition it is required exactly when `units_relabeled` is greater than zero and must be omitted when `units_relabeled` is zero; and a `parent_label_spec_id` must name a permanent label, never another hangtag. This eligibility gate guards six operations: permanent-label creation, label editing, hangtag creation, label approval, audit-finding disposition and import-shipment correction.

### 2.7. The Rejection Ladder

Almost every write in this system is guarded by several checks at once, and a request can be defective in more than one way simultaneously. The service therefore evaluates checks in one fixed order and returns the error of the **earliest** applicable check — never the most obvious defect, and never the last one discovered. The ladder, from first evaluated to last, is:

1. Header authentication — `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`, on every endpoint.
2. Role authorization against the endpoint's role list — `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED`, on every endpoint.
3. Existence of every path identifier, left to right — the per-kind 404 codes, on every endpoint with a path parameter.
4. Brand scope for a `BrandAdmin` — `ERR_BRAND_SCOPE_VIOLATION`, on `POST /labeling/styles`, `PUT /labeling/styles/{id}/fiber-declaration`, `POST /labeling/styles/{id}/material-inputs`, `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/hangtags`, `POST /labeling/import-shipments` and `POST /labeling/import-shipments/{id}/correct`.
5. Existence of every identifier and market code in the body, in the endpoint's declared field order — the per-kind 404 codes and `ERR_MARKET_REQUIREMENT_NOT_FOUND`, on every endpoint that carries one.
6. Request shape — `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, on every endpoint. This covers a missing required field, an enum value that is not a declared member, a number outside its declared range, an array that is required to be non-empty but is empty, a `limit` outside 1 through 100 or a negative `offset`, a `method` that does not match the `test_type` it is reserved for, a `wash_temp_c` present on a fiber-content reservation or absent on a care-performance one, and a `results_due_at` that is not strictly after `specimen_received_at`.
7. Licensor coherence on brand registration — `ERR_LICENSOR_REQUIRED`, on `POST /labeling/brands`.
8. Optimistic concurrency — `ERR_STALE_STYLE_VERSION` or `ERR_STALE_LABEL_VERSION`, on the three operations named in §2.5.
9. State legality: the target row must sit in a state from which the requested transition is declared — `ERR_LABEL_STATE_INVALID`, `ERR_LAB_TEST_STATE_INVALID`, `ERR_ORIGIN_STATE_INVALID`, `ERR_SHIPMENT_STATE_INVALID`, `ERR_AUDIT_STATE_INVALID` or `ERR_FINDING_STATE_INVALID` (all HTTP 409), on every action endpoint that drives a state transition.
10. Segregation of duties — `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, on `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/approve`.
11. Fiber substantiation of the style — `ERR_FIBER_NOT_SUBSTANTIATED`, on permanent-label approval.
12. Origin issuance of the style — `ERR_ORIGIN_NOT_ISSUED`, on permanent-label approval.
13. Care-temperature substantiation — `ERR_CARE_NOT_SUBSTANTIATED`, on permanent-label approval.
14. Identity disclosure, then placement permanence — `ERR_IDENTITY_DISCLOSURE_MISSING` and `ERR_PLACEMENT_NOT_PERMANENT`, on permanent-label approval, in that internal order.
15. Marketing-claim support — `ERR_HANGTAG_CLAIM_UNSUPPORTED`, on `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/hangtags` and on `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/approve`.
16. Reference eligibility — `ERR_REFERENCE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, on the six operations named in §2.6.
17. Printed-content match — `ERR_LABEL_CONTENT_MISMATCH`, on permanent-label approval.
18. Evidence validity horizon — `ERR_TEST_EVIDENCE_EXPIRED`, on permanent-label approval.
19. Redelivery window — `ERR_REDELIVERY_WINDOW_EXPIRED`, on `POST /labeling/import-shipments/{id}/correct`.
20. Window ordering — `ERR_INVALID_EFFECTIVE_WINDOW`, on `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/publish` and `POST /labeling/compliance-audits`.
21. Language coverage — `ERR_LANGUAGE_REQUIREMENT_UNMET`, on permanent-label approval.
22. Market subset — `ERR_MARKET_NOT_TARGETED`, on `POST /labeling/label-specs`, `PATCH /labeling/label-specs/{id}` and `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/approve`.
23. Fiber percentage sum and fiber-name distinctness — `ERR_FIBER_PERCENT_SUM`, on `POST /labeling/styles` and `PUT /labeling/styles/{id}/fiber-declaration`.
24. Non-empty declaration on the style — `ERR_FIBER_DECLARATION_EMPTY`, on `POST /labeling/label-specs` and `POST /labeling/lab-tests`.
25. Supply-graph stage order — `ERR_MATERIAL_STAGE_ORDER`, on `POST /labeling/styles/{id}/material-inputs`.
26. Uniqueness — `ERR_DUPLICATE_STYLE_CODE` on `POST /labeling/styles`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_ENTRY_NUMBER` on `POST /labeling/import-shipments`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_MARKET_CODE` on `POST /labeling/market-requirements`, `ERR_TEST_ALREADY_RESERVED` on `POST /labeling/lab-tests` and `ERR_DUPLICATE_OPEN_FINDING` on `POST /labeling/compliance-audits/{id}/findings` (all HTTP 409).
27. Hangtag text consistency — `ERR_HANGTAG_INCONSISTENT`, on `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/hangtags` and `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/approve`.
28. Laboratory bench occupancy — `ERR_LAB_BENCH_CAPACITY`, on `POST /labeling/lab-tests` and `POST /labeling/lab-tests/{id}/retest`.
29. Published market-window occupancy — `ERR_LABEL_WINDOW_OVERLAP`, on `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/publish`.
30. Disposition completeness — `ERR_DISPOSITION_INCOMPLETE`, on `POST /labeling/audit-findings/{id}/disposition`.
31. Correction sizing, which requires `corrected_units` to be greater than zero and at most the entry's `quantity_units` — `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400), on `POST /labeling/import-shipments/{id}/correct`.
32. Origin-graph sufficiency — `ERR_ORIGIN_GRAPH_INSUFFICIENT`, on `POST /labeling/origin-determinations`.
33. Laboratory result coherence — `ERR_LAB_RESULT_INCOHERENT`, on `POST /labeling/lab-tests/{id}/results`.
34. Suspended brand posture — `ERR_BRAND_POSTURE_SUSPENDED`, on `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/approve` and `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/publish`.
35. Restricted brand posture — `ERR_BRAND_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`, on `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/approve`.
36. Durability adequacy — `ERR_DURABILITY_INSUFFICIENT`, on `POST /labeling/label-specs/{id}/approve`.

Two consequences of this ordering deserve emphasis, because they are counter-intuitive. First, a suspended or restricted brand posture is checked *late*, so an approval attempt against a badly authored label reports the content defect and not the posture — the posture codes surface only once every content gate has passed. Second, reference eligibility sits below the first four label-content gates, so a permanent label whose style is not yet substantiated reports `ERR_FIBER_NOT_SUBSTANTIATED` even when its pinned care test is also the wrong kind of row.

### 2.8. Measurement Precision, Rounding and Text Normalization

All monetary amounts are carried at exactly 2 decimal places, and every monetary rounding in this specification is round-half-up. Where a formula performs two roundings, the order of the roundings is normative and is stated with the formula: rounding an intermediate to cents and then rounding the scaled product is not the same number as scaling the unrounded intermediate and rounding once, and only the stated order is correct.

Fiber percentages, tolerance widths, dimensional change and colorfastness grades are all carried and compared at exactly one decimal place, with round-half-up where a rounding is called for. Marking-duty rates are carried at three decimal places. Weights are carried at one decimal place. Counts of units, cycles and findings are exact integers with no rounding anywhere.

Threshold comparisons are inclusive unless this document says otherwise. Two thresholds are deliberately strict rather than inclusive, and both are called out where they appear: the penalty-ledger suspension threshold and the ordering requirements on effective and audit windows.

Printed text is compared after normalization, and normalization means exactly three operations applied in this order: trim leading and trailing whitespace, collapse every internal run of whitespace to a single space, then convert to uppercase. Comparison after normalization is exact string equality, and casing is never locale-dependent.

### 2.9. Cascade Discipline and Version Propagation

This system withdraws artifacts rather than flagging them. When a piece of evidence a label rests on is disproved or invalidated, the label is pulled automatically, and so is anything hanging off that label. Every such cascade obeys three rules.

First, the reason is recorded, not inferred. A label artifact's `withdrawn_reason` holds either an operator-supplied note or exactly one of three cascade literals: `AUTO_FIBER_MISSTATED` when a fiber-content laboratory result disproved the declaration, `AUTO_ORIGIN_VOIDED` when a supply-graph change voided the pinned origin determination, and `AUTO_PARENT_WITHDRAWN` when the artifact was pulled because its parent permanent label was pulled.

Second, cascades are transitive but shallow in a specific way: a permanent label is withdrawn first, and then every hangtag whose `parent_label_spec_id` names it is withdrawn with the parent-withdrawn literal. The state sets differ between the two hops. A permanent label is caught by a cascade when its state is `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` or `PUBLISHED` — a draft permanent label is left alone, because it has not yet asserted anything. A hangtag is caught by a cascade when its state is `DRAFT`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` or `PUBLISHED`, so a hangtag is pulled even while it is still a draft.

Third, every row a cascade touches has its `version` incremented by 1 and its `updated_at` refreshed, exactly as if it had been mutated directly. That includes the originating row, every intermediate parent, and every cascaded child. Version bumps are the mechanism by which the optimistic-concurrency guards of §2.5 notice that the world moved underneath a client, so a cascade that forgot to bump a version would silently defeat them.

### 2.10. The Market Regime Registry

Nothing in this system can be evaluated without knowing which market a garment is sold into, so the market regime registry is the first thing a `ComplianceAdmin` populates. Each registered row governs one sales market, keyed by a `market_code` of 2 to 8 characters drawn from uppercase letters and the underscore — `US`, `CA`, `MX`, `EU_FR` — and unique across all rows; a second attempt at the same code is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_MARKET_CODE`.

A regime row states what that market demands. `required_languages` is a non-empty array of distinct two-letter lowercase ISO 639-1 codes, all of which must appear on a permanent label sold into the market. `fiber_tolerance_pp` is the allowed absolute deviation, in percentage points, between a declared fiber percentage and the laboratory-measured percentage; it ranges from 0.0 to 5.0 inclusive at one decimal place. `absolute_claim_tolerance_pp` replaces it whenever the declaration is an absolute claim, and ranges from 0.0 to 1.0 inclusive at one decimal place. `min_durability_cycles` is the minimum number of wash cycles for which the permanent label must remain legible in that market, and is a non-negative integer. `marking_duty_rate` is the ad valorem marking-duty rate applied to an entry found marking-deficient at that market's port, ranging from 0.0 to 0.5 inclusive at three decimal places. Two booleans complete the row: `requires_identity_disclosure`, defaulting to true, says whether the permanent label must disclose a registered identification number or the company legal name, and `requires_origin_statement`, also defaulting to true, says whether the permanent label must carry a country-of-origin statement.

Because a market regime is immutable, everything downstream may treat a resolved regime row as a stable fact. The registry list is readable by all seven roles and accepts an optional `market_code` filter alongside the usual page window; registration is a `ComplianceAdmin` privilege.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Trade Parties, Responsibility of Record, and Brand Posture

A `ComplianceAdmin` registers every trade party the platform knows. A party carries a `legal_name` of 1 to 120 characters that must be non-blank after trimming, and a `party_type` that is one of `BRAND_OWNER`, `PRIVATE_LABEL_RETAILER` or `LICENSEE`. Identity disclosure on a permanent label can be satisfied two ways, so a party may also carry an `rn_number` — a registered identification number matching the pattern of the two letters `RN` followed by exactly five digits. When `rn_number` is absent it defaults to null, and a null registered identification number means the label must display the company legal name instead.

Licensing is where responsibility gets interesting. A party whose `party_type` is `LICENSEE` prints somebody else's marks, and it must therefore name that somebody: `licensor_brand_id` points at the licensor party whose marks it prints, and it is required when `party_type` is `LICENSEE` and must be null for every other party type. Separately, `responsibility_mode` records which party is of record for label accuracy and for marking-penalty exposure, taking the value `SELF_OF_RECORD` or `LICENSOR_OF_RECORD`; choosing `LICENSOR_OF_RECORD` also requires a non-null `licensor_brand_id`. Registration is rejected with `ERR_LICENSOR_REQUIRED` when a licensee omits its licensor, when `LICENSOR_OF_RECORD` is chosen without a licensor, or when a licensor is supplied by a party that is not a licensee.

From those two inputs the service computes `responsible_brand_id`, the **brand of record**: it equals `licensor_brand_id` when `responsibility_mode` is `LICENSOR_OF_RECORD`, and it equals the row's own `id` when the mode is `SELF_OF_RECORD`. Everything fiscal and everything postural in this system is attributed through that resolution rather than to whichever entity happened to file a document. A private-label retailer that keeps `SELF_OF_RECORD` carries its own exposure; a licensee that elects `LICENSOR_OF_RECORD` moves its whole exposure onto the licensor's row.

Three computed fields track the consequences. `outstanding_penalty_usd` is the party's marking-penalty ledger of record, carried at 2 decimal places and never negative, and it starts at 0.00 because a freshly registered party owns no shipments. It is the sum, over every customs entry whose importing party resolves through the brand-of-record rule to this party and whose marking state is either `REDELIVERY_ORDERED` or `MARKING_CORRECTED`, of that entry's assessed penalty minus its mitigated amount, with the total rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. Entries still in `ENTRY_FILED` and entries found `MARKING_COMPLIANT` contribute nothing. The ledger window is all-time; there is no date filter, and because the sum is order-insensitive no tie-break is needed.

`critical_audit_finding_count` is the sum of the critical-finding counts of every compliance audit whose audited party resolves through the same brand-of-record rule to this party, and it starts at 0. And `posture` is the gate that both of the others feed, taking the value `OPEN`, `RESTRICTED` or `SUSPENDED` and starting at `OPEN`. It is derived in a fixed order: if the ledger is **strictly greater** than USD 25,000.00 the posture is `SUSPENDED`, so a ledger of exactly 25,000.00 is not suspended; otherwise, if there exists at least one compliance audit of this brand of record whose state is `IN_REVIEW` and whose critical-finding count is **at least** 3 — three critical findings is enough — the posture is `RESTRICTED`; otherwise it is `OPEN`. Note carefully that the restriction predicate reads the *audit's* state rather than the finding rows directly, which is exactly what makes a finding verification propagate finding to audit to brand rather than finding to brand.

Posture is recomputed on every operation whose narrative below says so: on a customs marking review, on a customs correction filing, when an audit takes its first finding and moves out of `OPEN`, when an audit's open-finding count reaches zero and it moves to `PENDING_CLOSURE`, when a reopened finding pulls the audit back to `IN_REVIEW`, when a finding is verified and closed, and when an audit is closed. A party may be read individually, and the party list can be filtered by `party_type`, by `licensor_brand_id` to find every licensee of one licensor through the self-referential reverse lookup, by `responsible_brand_id` to find every party whose responsibility of record resolves to a given party, and by `posture`.

### 3.2. Styles, Fiber Declarations, and the Printable Fiber String

A style is the unit of compliance work. It names its owning party through a `brand_id` in the creation payload, carries a brand-facing `style_code` of 1 to 32 characters that must be unique within that party — a repeat is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_STYLE_CODE` — and declares a `product_class` from `WOVEN_OUTERWEAR`, `KNIT_TOP`, `HOSIERY` and `HOME_TEXTILE`. It also declares `target_market_codes`, a non-empty array of distinct market codes each of which must resolve to a registered market regime, and a `net_weight_grams` figure greater than zero at one decimal place, which is the finished-garment net weight used as the denominator in yarn-forward weight reasoning.

The fiber declaration itself lives in `declared_fibers`, an array whose entries each hold a `fiber_generic_name` written in uppercase letters and spaces, a `declared_pct` at one decimal place, and an `ornamentation_exempt` boolean. Fiber names must be distinct within the array. The array may be omitted or supplied empty at style creation, in which case it defaults to an empty array and the style simply stands undeclared; but whenever it is non-empty, the declared percentages must sum to exactly 100.0 after each line is rounded to one decimal place. A non-empty array that misses 100.0, or one that declares the same fiber name twice, is rejected with `ERR_FIBER_PERCENT_SUM`.

Every style carries **two independent state dimensions on the same row**, and this independence is not decorative: nothing in one dimension reads or writes the other. `substantiation_state` is the fiber-content dimension and starts at `FIBER_DECLARED`, moving among `FIBER_DECLARED`, `FIBER_TEST_PENDING`, `FIBER_SUBSTANTIATED` and `FIBER_MISSTATED`. `origin_state` is the country-of-origin dimension and starts at `ORIGIN_UNDETERMINED`, moving among `ORIGIN_UNDETERMINED`, `ORIGIN_DRAFTED` and `ORIGIN_ISSUED`. Neither dimension has a terminal state; both are cyclic. What makes the pair observable is the computed `label_readiness` field, which reads `READY` when `substantiation_state` is `FIBER_SUBSTANTIATED` **and** `origin_state` is `ORIGIN_ISSUED`, and reads `BLOCKED` in every other combination. It defaults to `BLOCKED` on a new style and is recomputed by every operation that touches either dimension.

The other computed field on a style is `effective_fiber_content_text`, the canonical printable fiber string, which defaults to the empty string and is recomputed at style creation and on every replacement of the declaration. It is built as follows. If the declaration array is empty, the result is the empty string. Otherwise the lines are split into two groups: a line belongs to the **listed** group when its `ornamentation_exempt` flag is false and its `declared_pct` is at least 5.0 percent; every other line — one that is ornamentation-exempt, or one below 5.0 percent — belongs to the **bucketed** group. The listed lines are sorted by `declared_pct` descending, and lines with equal percentages are then ordered by `fiber_generic_name` ascending; insertion order and row identifiers never participate. Each listed line renders as its percentage at exactly one decimal place, then a percent sign, then a single space, then the fiber name. If the bucketed group is non-empty, one final segment is appended: the sum of the bucketed lines' declared percentages, rounded half-up to one decimal place, rendered at one decimal place, followed by a percent sign, a space, and the words `OTHER FIBER`. That segment is always last regardless of how large its percentage turns out to be. All segments are joined with a comma followed by a single space.

Replacing a style's declaration is a deliberate, version-guarded act performed by a `BrandAdmin` or a `ComplianceAdmin`, and it carries `declared_fibers` together with the `expected_style_version` guard described in §2.5. It is refused outright while any permanent label of that style sits in `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` or `PUBLISHED`, because those artifacts have already asserted the old declaration. The submitted array must be non-empty — an empty array here is a shape violation, not a sum violation — and its percentages must still sum to exactly 100.0. What happens next depends on which fiber state the style was in, and the three cases differ in exactly one respect, namely what becomes of the existing laboratory evidence.

From `FIBER_DECLARED` the replacement is an edit of a declaration that was never benched: the array is replaced, the printable string recomputed, the style stays at `FIBER_DECLARED`, `label_readiness` is recomputed and the style's `version` is incremented by 1. No laboratory row is touched at all, because nothing had been substantiated on this dimension yet. This is also the path taken by the first declaration written onto a style that was created without one.

From `FIBER_SUBSTANTIATED` the replacement invalidates real evidence: the array is replaced and the printable string recomputed, every fiber-content laboratory row of this style whose state is `PASSED` transitions to `SUPERSEDED`, the style drops from `FIBER_SUBSTANTIATED` to `FIBER_DECLARED`, `label_readiness` becomes `BLOCKED`, and the style's `version` is incremented by 1.

From `FIBER_MISSTATED` the replacement is a correction after a failure: the array is replaced and the printable string recomputed, the failed laboratory row is deliberately **left untouched in `FAILED`** so that the audit trail survives, the style returns from `FIBER_MISSTATED` to `FIBER_DECLARED`, and the style's `version` is incremented by 1.

A style may be read individually, showing both parallel dimensions and the computed fiber string. The per-party style list is ordered by `created_at` ascending and can be filtered by `substantiation_state`, by `origin_state` and by `label_readiness`.

### 3.3. The Supply Graph and Two Ways of Determining Origin

Origin is not asserted, it is derived — and to derive it the brand must first file the supply graph. A `BrandAdmin` or `ComplianceAdmin` appends nodes one at a time to a style's graph. Each node names the processing `stage` performed, drawn from `FIBER`, `YARN`, `FABRIC`, `CUT_AND_SEW` and `TRIM`; the `country_code` where that stage happened, written as exactly two uppercase letters; the `value_added_usd` contributed at that stage, at least 0.00 at 2 decimal places; the `weight_grams` of fiber passing through that stage, at least 0.0 at one decimal place; and an `is_ornamentation` boolean, defaulting to false, which marks decorative inputs that are excluded from origin reasoning entirely. A node may also name a `parent_input_id`, the upstream node it was made from; a null `parent_input_id` marks a graph root, and the node's `style_id` is taken from the style in the path.

The stages form a rank ladder, and the graph must respect it. `TRIM` ranks 0, `FIBER` ranks 1, `YARN` ranks 2, `FABRIC` ranks 3 and `CUT_AND_SEW` ranks 4. A supplied `parent_input_id` must reference a node of the *same* style whose stage rank is strictly less than the new node's stage rank, so yarn can be made from fiber but never from fabric; and a `TRIM` node must always be a root, meaning its `parent_input_id` must be null. Violations of either rule — a cross-style parent, a parent at an equal or higher rank, or a parented trim node — are rejected with `ERR_MATERIAL_STAGE_ORDER`. On success the node's `depth` is computed as 0 for a root and as its parent's depth plus 1 otherwise.

Appending a node has a consequence that surprises people, and it is intentional. Because an issued determination was computed from a frozen snapshot of the graph, changing the graph makes that determination a description of something that no longer exists. So appending a node runs a four-hop cascade, of which only the first hop is unconditional: hops two through four fire exactly when the style's `origin_state` stands at `ORIGIN_ISSUED` at the moment of the append, and an append onto a style at `ORIGIN_UNDETERMINED` or `ORIGIN_DRAFTED` therefore creates the node and leaves the origin dimension, every determination and every label artifact exactly where they were. The first hop creates the material-input row. The second hop, caused by the first, transitions the style's `ISSUED` origin determination to `VOID`. The third hop, caused by the second, withdraws every permanent label whose `origin_determination_id` is that voided determination and whose state is `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` or `PUBLISHED`, recording `withdrawn_reason` as `AUTO_ORIGIN_VOIDED`. The fourth hop, caused by the third, withdraws every hangtag whose `parent_label_spec_id` is one of the labels pulled in the third hop and whose state is `DRAFT`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` or `PUBLISHED`, recording `AUTO_PARENT_WITHDRAWN`. Alongside all that, the style's `origin_state` drops from `ORIGIN_ISSUED` to `ORIGIN_UNDETERMINED`, `label_readiness` becomes `BLOCKED`, `label_readiness` is recomputed, and the version of the voided determination and of every artifact the cascade touched is incremented by 1. The style's own `version` is incremented by exactly 1 — and its `updated_at` refreshed — on **every** successful append, whether or not there was an issued determination to void, and exactly once even when the full cascade ran; the bump belongs to hop one, not to the cascade. It has to, because §2.5's determination guard is there to catch a create computed against "a supply graph that has since gained or lost a node", and §2.9 makes the version bump the mechanism by which that guard notices the graph moved — a first draft is taken from `ORIGIN_UNDETERMINED`, where there is no issued determination and hence no cascade at all.

Individual nodes are readable, and the node list is ordered by `depth` ascending and then `created_at` ascending. Filtering by `style_id` returns every node of a style; filtering by `parent_input_id` returns the direct children of one node through the self-referential reverse lookup; `stage` and `country_code` filters are also available.

Drafting a determination is done by a `LabelingSpecialist` or a `ComplianceAdmin`, naming the `style_id` and supplying the `expected_style_version` guard of §2.5. It is refused with `ERR_ORIGIN_GRAPH_INSUFFICIENT` unless the style's graph has at least one non-ornamentation node at stage `YARN`, `FABRIC` or `CUT_AND_SEW` — the pool the first legal method needs — and at least one non-ornamentation node at stage `YARN` or `FIBER`, which is the pool the second method needs.

Both methods are computed at draft time and both results are frozen onto the new row, because the point of a determination is that a compliance officer can see what each legal theory would say before choosing one. The population for both is every material-input node of the style whose `is_ornamentation` flag is false, with no state filter of any kind because a supply node has no lifecycle; the count of that population is stored as `graph_node_count`. Trim nodes never enter either pool.

The **substantial-transformation** variant takes the non-ornamentation nodes whose stage is `YARN`, `FABRIC` or `CUT_AND_SEW`, groups them by `country_code`, and for each country sums the `value_added_usd` of its nodes at 2 decimal places. The country with the greatest summed value wins, and the result is stored as `st_country`. Ties are broken first in favour of the country whose highest stage rank within the group is greater, and then, if still tied, in favour of the alphabetically earlier country code. The ranking key is compared as an exact decimal figure and is never rounded.

The **yarn-forward** variant takes only the non-ornamentation nodes whose stage is `YARN`; if the style has no yarn node at all, and only then, it falls back to the nodes whose stage is `FIBER`. It groups that pool by `country_code` and for each country sums `weight_grams` at one decimal place. The country with the greatest summed weight wins and is stored as `yf_country`, with the same two tie-breaks — greater highest stage rank within the group first, then alphabetically earlier country code. The consequence of ranking by weight rather than value is that a country which spun the majority of the yarn wins even when it added almost no value, whereas the substantial-transformation variant hands the win to whoever performed the most valuable finishing.

The row also stores `divergent`, which is true exactly when `st_country` and `yf_country` are not the same country. A `determined_country` and a `method_pinned` are both null while the row is a `DRAFT`, because no legal theory has been chosen yet.

Drafting behaves differently depending on where the origin dimension already stands, and all three paths are legitimate. From `ORIGIN_UNDETERMINED` the style advances to `ORIGIN_DRAFTED`. From `ORIGIN_DRAFTED` the style self-transitions back to `ORIGIN_DRAFTED` and the determination that was already sitting in `DRAFT` transitions to `SUPERSEDED`, so at most one draft ever stands. From `ORIGIN_ISSUED` the style returns to `ORIGIN_DRAFTED` while the issued row **keeps standing** until the new row is itself issued; `label_readiness` returns to `BLOCKED`, which is what blocks any permanent-label approval while the re-draft is pending. In all three cases the style's `version` is incremented by 1 and the new determination is created in `DRAFT`.

Issuing is reserved to a `ComplianceAdmin`, who supplies the `method` to pin — either `SUBSTANTIAL_TRANSFORMATION` or `YARN_FORWARD`. The determination must be in `DRAFT`; an issue against any other state is rejected with `ERR_ORIGIN_STATE_INVALID`. On success the row records `method_pinned`, derives `determined_country` from the frozen pair — `st_country` under substantial transformation, `yf_country` under yarn-forward — stamps `issued_at` from the logical clock, and moves from `DRAFT` to `ISSUED`. Any other determination of the same style that was standing in `ISSUED` transitions to `SUPERSEDED`. The style's `origin_state` advances from `ORIGIN_DRAFTED` to `ORIGIN_ISSUED`, `label_readiness` is recomputed, and both the determination's `version` and the style's `version` are incremented by 1.

A superseded determination is a trap worth naming. Label artifacts that pinned it keep their pin — the system does not silently repoint them — so their `origin_text` continues to be checked against the superseded row's country and therefore no longer agrees with the style's current determination. Approval of a permanent label additionally requires that the determination it pins is the style's *current* issued row; a label pinning a determination that has since been superseded, or a style whose origin dimension is not at `ORIGIN_ISSUED` at all, is rejected with `ERR_ORIGIN_NOT_ISSUED`. Determinations are readable individually, showing both candidate countries and the divergence flag, and the per-style determination list is ordered by `created_at` ascending with optional `state` and `method_pinned` filters.

### 3.4. Laboratory Evidence: Benches, Tolerances and Wash Performance

Only an `AccreditedLab` — or a `ComplianceAdmin` standing in for one — may reserve a bench, and only an `AccreditedLab` may post measurements. That separation is the whole point: no brand-side operator can write the evidence its own labels depend on.

A reservation names the `style_id` whose specimen was submitted, a `test_type` of either `FIBER_CONTENT` or `CARE_PERFORMANCE`, a `lab_code` of 1 to 16 characters drawn from uppercase letters, digits and hyphens, and a `method` from `AATCC_20A`, `ISO_1833`, `AATCC_135` and `ISO_6330`. Method and test type must agree: `AATCC_20A` and `ISO_1833` are the fiber-content methods, `AATCC_135` and `ISO_6330` are the care-performance methods, and a mismatched pair is a request-shape violation. A reservation also fixes its window with `specimen_received_at`, the instant the specimen arrives, and `results_due_at`, the nominal instant bench work ends, which must be strictly after `specimen_received_at`. A care-performance reservation must carry a `wash_temp_c` naming the temperature to be substantiated, drawn from 20, 30, 40, 60 and 95 degrees Celsius; a fiber-content reservation must leave `wash_temp_c` null. A fiber-content reservation against a style whose declaration array is empty is rejected with `ERR_FIBER_DECLARATION_EMPTY`.

The bench itself is a scarce resource, and the resource is the pair of laboratory and method rather than the laboratory alone. A reservation occupies the interval running from `specimen_received_at` through `results_due_at` plus a 24-hour conditioning buffer, and that interval is **closed at both ends**, so an interval whose start instant equals another interval's end instant does collide. At most two reservations may overlap any instant on a given laboratory-and-method pair: if two other rows already in `BENCH_RESERVED` on that pair occupy an interval overlapping the requested one, the request is rejected with `ERR_LAB_BENCH_CAPACITY`. Separately, a style may hold at most one reservation per test type at a time; a second one while the first is still `BENCH_RESERVED` is rejected with `ERR_TEST_ALREADY_RESERVED`.

A successful reservation creates a laboratory row in state `BENCH_RESERVED` with `attempt_no` at 1. A fiber-content reservation also drives the style's substantiation dimension: from `FIBER_DECLARED` it advances to `FIBER_TEST_PENDING`, and from `FIBER_SUBSTANTIATED` it *also* advances to `FIBER_TEST_PENDING`, which is the re-verification of an already substantiated style. In that re-verification case the previously `PASSED` fiber-content row is **not** superseded yet — it is superseded only when the new row itself reaches `PASSED` — and already published labels are left alone, because nothing has been disproved. In both cases the reservation requires the style's declaration array to be non-empty, `label_readiness` is recomputed, and the style's `version` is incremented by 1. A care-performance reservation leaves both style dimensions untouched.

Posting results is where the evidence becomes real. The payload may carry `measured_fibers`, `dimensional_change_pct`, `colorfastness_grade`, `legibility_cycles` and `cycles_run`, and which of them are required depends on the test type. The row must be in `BENCH_RESERVED`; a posting against any other state is rejected with `ERR_LAB_TEST_STATE_INVALID`. For a fiber-content run, `measured_fibers` must be non-empty — each entry pairing a `fiber_generic_name` with a `measured_pct` at one decimal place — and the measured percentages must sum to a value within 99.0 through 101.0 inclusive; a sum outside that band is rejected with `ERR_LAB_RESULT_INCOHERENT`. For a care-performance run, all four of `cycles_run`, `dimensional_change_pct`, `colorfastness_grade` and `legibility_cycles` must be present. `cycles_run` counts the wash cycles actually run and must be non-negative; `dimensional_change_pct` is a signed dimensional change at one decimal place; `colorfastness_grade` is a grey-scale grade from 1.0 through 5.0 inclusive at one decimal place; and `legibility_cycles` is the number of wash cycles after which the sewn-in label was still legible, a non-negative integer that the durability gate later consumes.

**The fiber tolerance evaluation** decides a fiber-content verdict in three steps. First it picks the governing tolerance, and the strictest market wins. The relevant markets are exactly the style's `target_market_codes`. The declaration is treated as an **absolute claim** when, among the lines whose `ornamentation_exempt` flag is false, there is exactly one line and its `declared_pct` is 100.0 — the classic "100.0% COTTON" case. For an absolute claim the governing tolerance is the smallest `absolute_claim_tolerance_pp` across those markets, and because that figure is 0.0 in the United States market, any measured foreign fiber at all fails. Otherwise — two or more non-exempt lines, or a single non-exempt line below 100.0 — the governing tolerance is the smallest `fiber_tolerance_pp` across those markets.

Second, deviations are computed per fiber name over the **union** of the declared and measured name sets. Ornamentation-exempt declaration lines are ignored entirely on the declared side; every measured line enters on the measured side, including a fiber that the declaration never mentioned, which compares against a declared 0.0 and is therefore the usual way an absolute claim fails. For each name in the union the deviation is the absolute difference between the measured percentage for that name — 0.0 if the laboratory did not report it — and the declared percentage for that name — 0.0 if the non-exempt declaration does not list it. The largest of those deviations, rounded half-up to one decimal place, is stored on the row as `result_delta_pp_max`; that field stays null for a care-performance run and until results are posted.

Third, the comparison is inclusive: the verdict is a pass when `result_delta_pp_max` is less than or equal to the governing tolerance, so a deviation exactly equal to the tolerance passes.

**The care performance evaluation** is a simpler three-condition gate, and all three comparisons are inclusive. A care-performance run passes when `cycles_run` is at least 5, the absolute value of `dimensional_change_pct` is at most 3.0 percent, and `colorfastness_grade` is at least 3.5. Only the four result fields posted on that single row participate — no other laboratory row and no style field. Note that `legibility_cycles` is deliberately **not** part of the care verdict; it is consumed later, by the durability gate at label approval.

A passing run sets the row to `PASSED`, releases the bench occupancy interval, supersedes the previously `PASSED` row of the same style and test type if one existed, and stamps `valid_until`, the evidence validity horizon. That horizon is derived from `specimen_received_at` and nothing else, so it never depends on the instant at which the laboratory posted the result: a fiber-content row expires one year — 365 days — after its specimen was received, and a care-performance row expires two years — 730 days — after its specimen was received. A fiber-content pass drives the style's substantiation dimension from `FIBER_TEST_PENDING` to `FIBER_SUBSTANTIATED`, recomputes `label_readiness` and increments the style's `version` by 1. A care-performance pass leaves both style dimensions untouched; that evidence is consumed only when a label pinning the run is approved.

A failing run is where the cascade fires, and it runs in four hops. The first hop sets the laboratory row to `FAILED` and stores `result_delta_pp_max`. The second hop, caused by the first, sets the style's `substantiation_state` to `FIBER_MISSTATED` and its `label_readiness` to `BLOCKED`. The third hop, caused by the second, withdraws every permanent label of that style whose state is `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` or `PUBLISHED`, recording `withdrawn_reason` as `AUTO_FIBER_MISSTATED`, and a withdrawn published label stops occupying its market window. The fourth hop, caused by the third, withdraws every hangtag whose `parent_label_spec_id` names one of the labels pulled in the third hop and whose state is `DRAFT`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` or `PUBLISHED`, recording `AUTO_PARENT_WITHDRAWN`. The style's `version` and the `version` of every artifact touched in the third and fourth hops are each incremented by 1, and the bench occupancy is released. A failing care-performance run, by contrast, leaves both style dimensions untouched — but any label pinning that run can no longer be approved.

A failed specimen may be re-benched on the same row, which keeps the attempt history in one place. Retesting requires the row to be in `FAILED`; anything else is rejected with `ERR_LAB_TEST_STATE_INVALID`. The retest supplies a fresh `specimen_received_at` and `results_due_at` — again strictly ordered — and may optionally move the run to a different `lab_code` or `method`, or restate `wash_temp_c`. Bench capacity is re-checked against the new window under exactly the same two-overlap rule. On success the row returns to `BENCH_RESERVED`, `attempt_no` is incremented by 1, the occupancy interval is replaced, and `measured_fibers`, `dimensional_change_pct`, `colorfastness_grade`, `legibility_cycles`, `cycles_run`, `result_delta_pp_max` and `valid_until` are all cleared; `valid_until` is then recomputed from the new `specimen_received_at` when results next pass. A fiber-content retest drives the style's substantiation dimension from `FIBER_MISSTATED` back to `FIBER_TEST_PENDING` and increments the style's `version` by 1. Filing a brand-new reservation has the same effect on that dimension; the retest form is simply the one that preserves attempt history on a single row.

The fourth laboratory state, `SUPERSEDED`, is reached only by the system and only from `PASSED` — either because a newer run of the same style and test type reached `PASSED`, or because the style's declaration was replaced. It is terminal, it increments the row's `version` by 1, and its practical effect is that any label pinning that run through `care_test_id` can no longer clear the care-substantiation gate at approval. Laboratory rows are readable individually, showing measurements, the evaluation delta and the validity horizon; the per-style list is ordered by `created_at` ascending and can be filtered by `test_type`, `state`, `lab_code` and `method`.

### 3.5. Authoring a Permanent Label

A label artifact is the printed thing, and it comes in two kinds recorded in `artifact_type`: a `PERMANENT_LABEL` is the sewn-in label, and a `HANGTAG` is the swing ticket that must agree with it. The kind is never supplied by the caller — drafting a label directly yields a `PERMANENT_LABEL`, and hangtags come into existence only through the hangtag sub-resource described in §3.6.

A `LabelingSpecialist` (or a `ComplianceAdmin`) drafts a permanent label against a `style_id`, and the payload is the entire printed artifact. `market_codes` is a non-empty array of distinct market codes the artifact is printed for, and it must be a subset of the style's `target_market_codes` — a code outside that list is rejected with `ERR_MARKET_NOT_TARGETED`, both at drafting time and again at approval. `languages` is a non-empty array of distinct two-letter lowercase codes actually printed on the artifact. `placement_code` says where the artifact is affixed and is one of `NECK_CENTER_INSIDE`, `SIDE_SEAM_INSIDE`, `WAISTBAND_INSIDE` and `NOT_PERMANENT`, the last of which is legal only on a hangtag. `durability_cycles_rating` is the non-negative number of wash cycles the label stock is claimed to stay legible for.

The care instruction set is five fields. `wash_temp_c` is the printed wash temperature, drawn from 20, 30, 40, 60 and 95 degrees Celsius. `bleach_code` defaults to `NO_BLEACH` and may also be `NON_CHLORINE_ONLY` or `ANY_BLEACH`. `dry_code` defaults to `TUMBLE_LOW` and may also be `TUMBLE_NORMAL`, `LINE_DRY` or `FLAT_DRY`. `iron_code` defaults to `IRON_LOW` and may also be `IRON_MEDIUM` or `DO_NOT_IRON`. `dryclean_code` defaults to `DO_NOT_DRYCLEAN` and may also be `ANY_SOLVENT` or `PETROLEUM_ONLY`.

Three fields carry the compliance statements themselves. `identity_display_mode` is either `RN`, meaning the artifact discloses the registered identification number, or `COMPANY_NAME`, meaning it prints the legal name. `fiber_content_text` is the fiber content string of 1 to 200 characters exactly as it will be printed. `origin_text` is the country-of-origin statement of 1 to 64 characters exactly as it will be printed, whose canonical form is the words `Made in` followed by a space and the two-letter country code. `marketing_claims` defaults to an empty array and stays empty on a permanent label; it is a hangtag concern.

Finally, a permanent label pins its evidence. `origin_determination_id` names the determination whose country the label prints, and `care_test_id` names the care-performance laboratory run that substantiates its care code set. Both are required on a permanent label and both are null on a hangtag. Drafting is refused with `ERR_FIBER_DECLARATION_EMPTY` when the style's declaration array is empty, and refused with `ERR_REFERENCE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` when a pinned reference is the wrong kind or in the wrong state per §2.6.

A newly drafted permanent label lands in state `DRAFT` with `parent_label_spec_id` null, `consistency_state` at `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `approved_by_user_id`, `revision_note`, `withdrawn_reason`, `effective_from` and `effective_to` all null, and `authored_by_user_id` set to the acting operator's `X-User-ID`.

Every creation and every edit recomputes `content_hash`, the canonical content fingerprint that lets two artifacts printing the same words be recognised as identical. It is built by concatenating five segments separated by a single vertical-bar character, in this order: the normalized `fiber_content_text`; the normalized `origin_text`; the care code string; the identity string; and the artifact's `languages` sorted ascending and joined with a single comma between entries. The care code string is the `wash_temp_c` value rendered as a string, then a forward slash, then `bleach_code`, a forward slash, `dry_code`, a forward slash, `iron_code`, a forward slash, and `dryclean_code`. The identity string is the owning party's `rn_number` verbatim when `identity_display_mode` is `RN`, and the normalized `legal_name` of that party when the mode is `COMPANY_NAME`. Normalization is the trim-collapse-uppercase procedure of §2.8. The finished string is encoded as UTF-8, hashed with SHA-256, and stored as lowercase hexadecimal. Only fields of the artifact itself plus the registered identification number and legal name of the party that owns its style participate; for a hangtag the care code fields and `identity_display_mode` are inherited verbatim from the parent permanent label at creation time, which is why two artifacts printing the same text produce the same hash.

Editing an artifact is permitted while it is in `DRAFT` or in `REVISION_REQUESTED`, and any other state is rejected with `ERR_LABEL_STATE_INVALID`. Every content field of the artifact may be supplied, omitted fields are left untouched, and the market-subset rule is re-checked on the resulting set. A `DRAFT` artifact self-transitions and stays in `DRAFT`; a `REVISION_REQUESTED` artifact returns to `DRAFT` and its `revision_note` is cleared. In both cases `content_hash` is recomputed, `authored_by_user_id` is set to the acting operator's `X-User-ID` — which re-arms the segregation-of-duties check for the next approval attempt — a hangtag additionally has its `consistency_state` recomputed against its parent, and the artifact's `version` is incremented by 1.

Sending a draft to approval is deliberately cheap: submission runs no compliance gate at all, so that the full ladder is exercised in one place. The artifact must be in `DRAFT`, and it must — if it is a permanent label — already carry both a non-null `origin_determination_id` and a non-null `care_test_id`. On success it moves from `DRAFT` to `PENDING_APPROVAL` and its `version` is incremented by 1.

### 3.6. The Approval Gate, Publication, and Withdrawal

Approval is the single point at which everything this system knows is brought to bear, and only a `LabelingApprover` may perform it. The artifact must be in `PENDING_APPROVAL`; any other state is rejected with `ERR_LABEL_STATE_INVALID`. The acting operator's `X-User-ID` must differ from the recorded `authored_by_user_id`. Because the authoring cycle can repeat any number of laps — `PENDING_APPROVAL` to `REVISION_REQUESTED` to `DRAFT` and back to `PENDING_APPROVAL` — every lap re-runs the whole gate against whatever the style's two dimensions now say, not against what they said when the draft was first written.

For a permanent label the gate has, beyond the state and duty-segregation checks, six ordered content steps plus three evidence and posture checks. The evidence checks come first in ladder order. The style's `substantiation_state` must be `FIBER_SUBSTANTIATED`, or the approval is rejected with `ERR_FIBER_NOT_SUBSTANTIATED`. The style's `origin_state` must be `ORIGIN_ISSUED` and the pinned determination must be the style's current issued row, or the approval is rejected with `ERR_ORIGIN_NOT_ISSUED`. The pinned care-performance run must be a `PASSED` care-performance row of the same style whose `wash_temp_c` equals the artifact's own `wash_temp_c`; a mismatch means the printed care instruction was never wash-tested and is rejected with `ERR_CARE_NOT_SUBSTANTIATED`.

The six content steps are then evaluated **in exactly this order, and the first failure decides the error** — which is not the order a human would narrate them in, so it is worth reading twice.

Step one is identity disclosure. For every market on the artifact whose regime sets `requires_identity_disclosure` to true, the disclosure must actually be available: if `identity_display_mode` is `RN` and the party's `rn_number` is null, or if the mode is `COMPANY_NAME` and the party's `legal_name` is blank after trimming, the approval is rejected with `ERR_IDENTITY_DISCLOSURE_MISSING`. Step two is placement: a permanent label whose `placement_code` is `NOT_PERMANENT` is rejected with `ERR_PLACEMENT_NOT_PERMANENT`. Step three is the content match, and it compares the printed strings against the facts as they stand *at approval time* rather than as they stood when the draft was authored: the normalized `fiber_content_text` must equal the style's normalized `effective_fiber_content_text`, and the normalized `origin_text` must equal the words `MADE IN` followed by a single space and the `determined_country` of the pinned determination — read from the pinned row even if that row has since become superseded or void. Either mismatch is rejected with `ERR_LABEL_CONTENT_MISMATCH`. Step four is language coverage: the union of `required_languages` across the regimes of the artifact's `market_codes` must be a subset of the artifact's `languages`, and a shortfall is rejected with `ERR_LANGUAGE_REQUIREMENT_UNMET`. Step five re-checks the market subset and is rejected with `ERR_MARKET_NOT_TARGETED`. Step six is durability, rejected with `ERR_DURABILITY_INSUFFICIENT`.

**The durability adequacy check** works out a floor and compares the substantiated figure against it. The floor starts as the greatest `min_durability_cycles` among the regimes of exactly the artifact's `market_codes` — the label's own market set, not the style's full target list. That base is then scaled by a factor that depends on the style's `product_class`: woven outerwear scales by 1.00, a knit top by 1.25, hosiery by 1.50, and a home textile by 0.80. The scaled product is rounded **up** to the next whole cycle, so a base of 25 cycles on a knit top yields 25 multiplied by 1.25, which is 31.25, and therefore a floor of 32. The substantiated figure is the smaller of two numbers: the `legibility_cycles` reported by the pinned care-performance run, and the `durability_cycles_rating` the specialist claimed for the label stock. The check passes when the substantiated figure is greater than or equal to the floor, so a substantiated figure exactly equal to the floor passes.

Two further gates fire on approval. The pinned care test's `valid_until` must be at or after the logical clock — evidence whose horizon is strictly in the past is rejected with `ERR_TEST_EVIDENCE_EXPIRED`, and the same applies to the validity horizon of the style's passed fiber test. And the posture of the brand of record must be neither `SUSPENDED`, which is rejected with `ERR_BRAND_POSTURE_SUSPENDED`, nor `RESTRICTED`, which is rejected with `ERR_BRAND_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`; both sit near the very bottom of the ladder, with the suspension check evaluated before the restriction check.

When every gate clears, the artifact moves from `PENDING_APPROVAL` to `APPROVED`, `approved_by_user_id` is set to the acting operator's `X-User-ID`, the style's `label_readiness` is recomputed, and the `version` of both the artifact and the style is incremented by 1.

An approver who is not satisfied may instead send the artifact back. That requires the artifact to be in `PENDING_APPROVAL`, takes a `revision_note`, moves the artifact to `REVISION_REQUESTED`, stores the note, and increments its `version` by 1. The artifact re-enters the authoring cycle rather than terminating, and the note is cleared the next time the author edits it.

Publication puts an approved artifact into production for a half-open effective window. It requires state `APPROVED`, and it takes `effective_from` and `effective_to`, with `effective_to` strictly after `effective_from` — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_EFFECTIVE_WINDOW`. The brand of record's posture must not be `SUSPENDED`; note that a `RESTRICTED` posture blocks approval but still permits publication of an already approved artifact. The window is exclusive of its upper bound, and exactly one published artifact may occupy a given style-and-market pair at any instant: for every market this artifact shares with another published artifact of the same style, the half-open window from `effective_from` inclusive to `effective_to` exclusive must not intersect that row's window. Windows that merely touch — where a new `effective_from` equals an existing `effective_to` — are allowed; a true intersection is rejected with `ERR_LABEL_WINDOW_OVERLAP`. On success the artifact moves from `APPROVED` to `PUBLISHED`, stores both instants, begins occupying the style-and-market window for every listed market, recomputes the style's `label_readiness`, and increments both its own `version` and the style's `version` by 1.

Withdrawal is terminal and may be performed from `APPROVED` or from `PUBLISHED` by a `LabelingSpecialist`, a `LabelingApprover` or a `ComplianceAdmin`, with a supplied `withdrawn_reason`. Withdrawing from `PUBLISHED` also releases the style-and-market window occupancy for every listed market. Either way, every hangtag whose `parent_label_spec_id` names this artifact and whose state is `DRAFT`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` or `PUBLISHED` is withdrawn with `withdrawn_reason` set to `AUTO_PARENT_WITHDRAWN`; the style's `label_readiness` is recomputed; and the `version` of this row, of the style, and of every cascaded child is incremented by 1. Withdrawal from any other state — and any action at all on an artifact already in `WITHDRAWN` — is rejected with `ERR_LABEL_STATE_INVALID`.

Two paths into `WITHDRAWN` are never operator calls. A permanent label sitting in `PENDING_APPROVAL` is withdrawn by the system when its parent is pulled, when a fiber-content result disproves the declaration, or when a supply-graph change voids the pinned determination, and the recorded reason is the corresponding cascade literal `AUTO_FIBER_MISSTATED`, `AUTO_ORIGIN_VOIDED` or `AUTO_PARENT_WITHDRAWN`. A hangtag sitting in `DRAFT` is withdrawn by the system when its parent permanent label is withdrawn, with `withdrawn_reason` set to `AUTO_PARENT_WITHDRAWN`. Both paths increment the affected row's `version` by 1.

Artifacts are readable individually, showing state, pinned evidence references and the consistency verdict. The artifact list is ordered by `created_at` ascending and can be filtered by `style_id` for every artifact of a style, by `parent_label_spec_id` for the hangtags of one permanent label, by `origin_determination_id` or `care_test_id` for the artifacts pinned to a given piece of evidence, by `artifact_type`, by `state`, by `consistency_state`, and by `market_code`, which matches artifacts whose `market_codes` array contains the supplied value.

### 3.7. Hangtags, Marketing Claims, and Agreement with the Sewn-In Label

A hangtag is the marketing surface, and the classic compliance failure is a hangtag that says something the sewn-in label does not. This system therefore refuses to let a hangtag exist as an independent assertion: it hangs off exactly one permanent label, and it is checked against it.

A `LabelingSpecialist`, a `BrandAdmin` or a `ComplianceAdmin` creates a hangtag against a permanent label, supplying the version guard `expected_parent_label_spec_version` described in §2.5 together with `market_codes`, `languages`, `marketing_claims`, `fiber_content_text` and `origin_text`. Nothing is inherited implicitly on the compliance-bearing side — the fiber and origin strings are supplied and then checked — while the care code set and `identity_display_mode` are inherited verbatim from the parent at creation time so that the two artifacts fingerprint identically when they print the same words. The parent named in the path must itself be a permanent label; hanging a hangtag off another hangtag is rejected with `ERR_REFERENCE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`.

A created hangtag lands in `DRAFT` with `artifact_type` set to `HANGTAG`, `parent_label_spec_id` set to the parent's identifier, `style_id` copied from the parent, `placement_code` fixed to `NOT_PERMANENT`, and both `origin_determination_id` and `care_test_id` null. `authored_by_user_id` is set to the acting operator's `X-User-ID`, the consistency verdict is computed, and `content_hash` is computed after it. The parent permanent label's own `version` is incremented by 1 by the creation, which is precisely why the next hangtag create must carry the parent's new version.

**The consistency computation** produces the `consistency_state` field, which reads `NOT_APPLICABLE` on a permanent label and either `CONSISTENT` or `INCONSISTENT` on a hangtag. Exactly one parent row is consulted, resolved through `parent_label_spec_id`, along with that parent's style declaration and the care-performance run the parent pinned; sibling hangtags are never consulted. The verdict has two halves and both must hold.

The text half holds when the hangtag's normalized `fiber_content_text` equals the parent's normalized `fiber_content_text` **and** its normalized `origin_text` equals the parent's normalized `origin_text`. A divergence on either string makes the artifact inconsistent, and the operation is rejected with `ERR_HANGTAG_INCONSISTENT` — both when the hangtag is created and again when it is approved.

The claim half holds when every entry of `marketing_claims` — a set of distinct values drawn from `MADE_IN_USA`, `ORGANIC_COTTON` and `WRINKLE_FREE` — is supported by substantiated evidence. `MADE_IN_USA` is supported only when the parent's normalized `origin_text` is exactly the string `MADE IN US`. `ORGANIC_COTTON` is supported only when the style's declaration contains a line whose `fiber_generic_name` is exactly `ORGANIC COTTON` and whose `declared_pct` is at least 95.0 percent. `WRINKLE_FREE` is supported only when the care-performance run the parent pinned is in state `PASSED` and its `dimensional_change_pct` is at most 2.0 percent. Percentage comparisons here are made at one decimal place and are inclusive; string comparisons are exact after normalization. An unsupported claim is rejected with `ERR_HANGTAG_CLAIM_UNSUPPORTED`, on hangtag creation and on approval alike — and because claim support sits above text consistency on the ladder of §2.7, a hangtag that is wrong in both respects reports the unsupported claim rather than the text divergence.

A hangtag travels the same authoring cycle as a permanent label: it is edited while `DRAFT` or `REVISION_REQUESTED`, and every edit recomputes both its `consistency_state` and its `content_hash`. It is submitted, and at approval the only artifact-specific requirement beyond the shared checks is that its `consistency_state` reads `CONSISTENT`. It is withdrawn explicitly like any other artifact, and it is withdrawn implicitly whenever its parent goes — from `DRAFT`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` or `PUBLISHED` — with `AUTO_PARENT_WITHDRAWN` recorded as the reason.

### 3.8. Imported Goods at the Border: Marking Review, Penalties and Mitigation

A customs entry is filed by a `BrandAdmin` or a `ComplianceAdmin` and names the `importer_brand_id` of the importer of record, the `style_id` the entry covers, a customs `entry_number` of 1 to 24 characters that must be unique across all entries — a repeat is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_ENTRY_NUMBER` — a `quantity_units` count greater than zero, a `declared_value_usd` greater than 0.00 at 2 decimal places, and an `entry_market_code` naming the port of entry, which is what supplies the marking-duty rate later. A filed entry lands in `marking_state` `ENTRY_FILED` with every penalty figure at 0.00, `corrected_units` at 0, `corrective_label_spec_id` null, and `assessment_basis`, `posture_at_review`, `deficiency_code`, `review_decided_at` and `redelivery_deadline_at` all null.

Only a `CustomsOfficer` decides a marking review, and only from `ENTRY_FILED`; a review of any other state is rejected with `ERR_SHIPMENT_STATE_INVALID`. The review carries an `outcome` of either `COMPLIANT` or `DEFICIENT`, and a `deficiency_code` that must be absent on a compliant outcome and present on a deficient one, drawn from `MISSING_ORIGIN`, `WRONG_ORIGIN`, `MISSING_IDENTITY`, `NON_PERMANENT_PLACEMENT` and `LANGUAGE_DEFICIENT`. Either way `review_decided_at` is stamped from the logical clock. A compliant outcome moves the entry to the terminal state `MARKING_COMPLIANT` and leaves every penalty figure at 0.00.

A deficient outcome moves the entry to `REDELIVERY_ORDERED`, stores the `deficiency_code`, and assesses a penalty. **The order of the assessment steps is normative**, because the escalation multiplier is derived from a ledger that must not contain the penalty being assessed.

First, the posture of the brand of record is evaluated **excluding this entry from the ledger**, and the result is frozen onto the row as `posture_at_review`. This is what makes it impossible for an assessment to escalate itself. Second, the marking-duty rate is read from the regime of the entry's port — the `marking_duty_rate` of the `entry_market_code` regime — and a duty base is computed as `declared_value_usd` multiplied by that rate, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. Third, `penalty_ad_valorem_usd` is that rounded duty base multiplied by the escalation multiplier for the frozen posture, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places again. The multiplier is 1.00 for an `OPEN` posture, 1.50 for `RESTRICTED` and 2.00 for `SUSPENDED`. The two roundings are ordered and the order matters: round the duty to cents first, then round the escalated product. Multiplying the unrounded duty by the multiplier and rounding once yields a different number and is not the pinned semantics.

Fourth, the statutory alternative is computed. `penalty_statutory_usd` is the smaller of two figures: `quantity_units` multiplied by a per-unit penalty of USD 1.25, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places; and `declared_value_usd` multiplied by a statutory cap rate of 0.80, also rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. Fifth, the variant actually applied is selected and stored in `assessment_basis`: it is `STATUTORY_PER_UNIT` when the frozen `posture_at_review` is `SUSPENDED`, and `AD_VALOREM` otherwise. Sixth, `penalty_assessed_usd` is set to whichever of the two figures the basis names, and `penalty_mitigated_usd` is set to 0.00. The practical difference between the two variants is stark: on a high-value, low-count entry the escalated ad valorem charge sits far below the per-unit figure, while on a high-count, low-value entry the per-unit charge sits far above it.

Only after all of that does the entry enter the ledger, and the brand-of-record's `outstanding_penalty_usd` and `posture` are recomputed with this entry now included — which is how a single large assessment can drive a party to `SUSPENDED`. The row also derives `redelivery_deadline_at` as `review_decided_at` plus a redelivery window of 30 days. The entry's `version` and the party's `version` are each incremented by 1.

A correction is filed by a `BrandAdmin` or a `ComplianceAdmin` and only from `REDELIVERY_ORDERED`. It names a `corrective_label_spec_id`, which must resolve to a permanent label of the same style in state `PUBLISHED`, and a `corrected_units` count of the units actually re-marked, which must be greater than zero and at most the entry's `quantity_units`. The logical clock must be at or before `redelivery_deadline_at`; a correction filed after that instant has passed is rejected with `ERR_REDELIVERY_WINDOW_EXPIRED`.

**The mitigation computation** reads only this entry, and it treats `penalty_assessed_usd` as frozen by the marking review — it is never recomputed here. A correction ratio is formed as `corrected_units` divided by `quantity_units`, kept as an exact rational and never rounded. A correction counts as **prompt** when the logical clock is at or before `review_decided_at` plus 10 days. The mitigation rate is the full rate of 1.00 when the correction is both complete — `corrected_units` equal to `quantity_units` — and prompt; in every other case it is the partial rate of 0.50. Then `penalty_mitigated_usd` is the assessed penalty multiplied by that rate, multiplied by the correction ratio, with a **single** rounding half-up to 2 decimal places applied at the very end.

On success the entry moves to the terminal state `MARKING_CORRECTED`, stores `corrective_label_spec_id` and `corrected_units`, and the brand-of-record's `outstanding_penalty_usd` is recomputed — dropping by the mitigated amount — followed by its `posture`, which may fall back from `SUSPENDED`. The entry's `version` and the party's `version` are each incremented by 1.

An entry is readable individually, showing its pinned posture, both candidate penalty figures, the basis actually applied and the mitigation recorded. The entry list is ordered by `created_at` ascending and can be filtered by `importer_brand_id`, by `style_id`, by `corrective_label_spec_id` for entries re-marked against one published label, by `marking_state` and by `entry_market_code`.

### 3.9. Compliance Audits, Mislabeled Inventory, and Reading the Style Dossier

An audit is opened over one party by an `Auditor` or a `ComplianceAdmin`. It names the audited `brand_id`, a `scope` of `STYLE_DOSSIER`, `MARKET_LABELING` or `IMPORT_MARKING`, and a bounded audited period given by `audit_window_from` inclusive and `audit_window_to` exclusive, where the upper bound must be strictly after the lower bound — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_EFFECTIVE_WINDOW`. A new audit lands in state `OPEN` with `open_finding_count`, `critical_finding_count` and `units_in_scope` all at 0 and `closed_at` null; all three counters are derived from the child findings and are never supplied by the caller.

Findings are raised against a style inside an audit by an `Auditor` or a `ComplianceAdmin`, naming the `style_id` at fault, a `finding_code`, and a `units_affected` count greater than zero that represents the mislabeled units sitting in inventory. The finding codes are `FIBER_CONTENT_MISSTATED`, `ORIGIN_MISSTATED`, `CARE_UNSUBSTANTIATED`, `IDENTITY_MISSING`, `PLACEMENT_NON_PERMANENT` and `HANGTAG_INCONSISTENT`. An audit already in `CLOSED` cannot take a finding, and the attempt is rejected with `ERR_AUDIT_STATE_INVALID`. The same style may not carry two live findings of the same code inside one audit: a second one while an existing finding of that style and code sits in `OPEN` or `REMEDIATED` is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_OPEN_FINDING`.

**Severity is derived, never supplied.** The base severity comes from the finding code alone: `FIBER_CONTENT_MISSTATED` and `ORIGIN_MISSTATED` are `CRITICAL`; `CARE_UNSUBSTANTIATED`, `IDENTITY_MISSING` and `PLACEMENT_NON_PERMANENT` are `MAJOR`; and `HANGTAG_INCONSISTENT` is `MINOR`. That base then escalates by exactly one level when `units_affected` reaches 5,000 units, where escalation maps `MINOR` to `MAJOR`, `MAJOR` to `CRITICAL`, and `CRITICAL` to `CRITICAL`. The breakpoint is inclusive: 4,999 affected units does not escalate, 5,000 does. Only the finding's own code and affected quantity participate; sibling findings never affect its severity.

A new finding lands in state `OPEN` with `units_relabeled`, `units_destroyed` and `units_released` all 0, `units_unresolved` equal to `units_affected`, `corrective_label_spec_id` null, and `disposition_recorded_at`, `verified_at` and `reopen_reason` all null. The first finding on an audit also drives the audit from `OPEN` to `IN_REVIEW`, after which the audit's counters are re-derived and the brand-of-record's `posture` is recomputed; the audit's `version` is incremented by 1.

**The audit rollup** re-derives three numbers over the findings of exactly that audit, and the difference between them is the crux of the whole audit lifecycle. `open_finding_count` counts the findings whose state is `OPEN` **or** `REMEDIATED` — a remediated finding is still open for closure purposes, because nobody has verified it yet. `critical_finding_count` counts the subset of those whose `severity` is `CRITICAL`. `units_in_scope` sums `units_affected` over **every** finding of the audit with no state filter at all, which is what makes it invariant under disposition, verification and reopening. All three are order-insensitive counts and sums, so no tie-break is needed. The rollup then derives the audit's own state: an audit in `IN_REVIEW` whose open-finding count has reached 0 moves to `PENDING_CLOSURE`, and an audit in `PENDING_CLOSURE` whose open-finding count has risen above 0 falls back to `IN_REVIEW`. That fall-back is what makes the audit lifecycle a cycle rather than a one-way street.

Recording a disposition is the brand side's job, and it is deliberately **not** open to an `Auditor`: the role that verifies a remediation may not perform it. A `LabelingSpecialist` or `ComplianceAdmin` supplies `units_relabeled`, `units_destroyed` and `units_released`, each of which must be zero or greater, plus a `corrective_label_spec_id` when — and only when — `units_relabeled` is greater than zero, naming a published permanent label of the same style as the finding. Supplying the reference with a zero relabeled count, or omitting it with a positive one, is rejected with `ERR_REFERENCE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`.

**Affected units are a conserved quantity**, and `units_affected` is immutable after creation precisely so that they can be. `units_unresolved` is always `units_affected` less the sum of the relabeled, destroyed and released counts, and it may never be negative; after every request that touches a finding, the four buckets must still add back up to the affected total. A disposition requires that the three supplied counters sum to exactly `units_affected`, which drives `units_unresolved` to exactly 0; any other sum is rejected with `ERR_DISPOSITION_INCOMPLETE`. On success the finding stores the three counters, the corrective reference and a `disposition_recorded_at` stamped from the logical clock, and moves from `OPEN` to `REMEDIATED`. The audit's counters are re-derived — the finding is still counted, because remediated sits in the open set, so the audit does not yet move to `PENDING_CLOSURE` on this account alone unless it was the only remaining one — and the `version` of the finding and of the audit are each incremented by 1. A disposition against a finding that is not `OPEN` is rejected with `ERR_FINDING_STATE_INVALID`.

Verification is the `Auditor`'s exclusive act and the third link in a finding-to-audit-to-brand chain. It requires the finding to be in `REMEDIATED`. The first hop stamps `verified_at` from the logical clock and moves the finding to `CLOSED`. The second hop, caused by the first, re-derives the parent audit's counters, and when the open-finding count reaches 0 the audit moves from `IN_REVIEW` to `PENDING_CLOSURE` and its critical-finding count necessarily re-derives to 0. The third hop, caused by the second, recomputes the brand-of-record's `posture`: because no audit of that party is any longer in `IN_REVIEW` with at least 3 critical findings, a posture that was `RESTRICTED` for that reason drops back to `OPEN`. The `version` of the finding, of the audit and of the party are each incremented by 1.

A closed finding may be reopened by an `Auditor` or a `ComplianceAdmin` when the correction did not hold, supplying a `reopen_reason`. It requires the finding to be in `CLOSED` and the parent audit to be in any state other than `CLOSED`. The finding returns to `OPEN`, stores the reason, clears `verified_at`, `disposition_recorded_at` and `corrective_label_spec_id`, and resets `units_relabeled`, `units_destroyed` and `units_released` to 0 — which restores `units_unresolved` to the full `units_affected` and leaves the audit's `units_in_scope` byte-identical to its pre-disposition value. The parent audit is pulled from `PENDING_CLOSURE` back to `IN_REVIEW` and re-derives its counters, and the brand-of-record's `posture` is recomputed. The `version` of the finding, of the audit and of the party are each incremented by 1.

Closing an audit is terminal and is performed by an `Auditor` or a `ComplianceAdmin`. An audit that never took a finding may be closed directly from `OPEN`; an audit with findings may be closed only from `PENDING_CLOSURE`. A close requested while the audit is in `IN_REVIEW` — which means it still has open findings — is rejected with `ERR_AUDIT_STATE_INVALID`. Closure stamps `closed_at` from the logical clock, recomputes the audited party's `posture` so that a party restricted by this audit returns to `OPEN` once it closes, and increments the audit's `version` and the party's `version` by 1.

Audits are readable individually with their rolled-up counts, and the audit list is ordered by `created_at` ascending with optional `brand_id`, `state` and `scope` filters. Findings are readable individually with their severity, disposition buckets and unresolved remainder, and the finding list is ordered by `created_at` ascending with optional `audit_id`, `style_id`, `corrective_label_spec_id`, `state`, `severity` and `finding_code` filters.

Finally, a computed read gives the whole labeling posture of one style in a single response, which is what an auditor or an approver reaches for before touching anything. It returns the style's identifier as `style_id`, both parallel dimensions as `substantiation_state` and `origin_state`, the derived `label_readiness`, the canonical `effective_fiber_content_text`, and a `determined_country` that is null unless the style has an issued determination. It returns `required_durability_cycles`, the derived durability floor computed the same way as the approval gate's floor but over the style's whole `target_market_codes` list — the widest floor the style could ever have to meet — rather than over any one label's market set. It returns `substantiated_durability_cycles`, which is the `legibility_cycles` of the newest passed care-performance run of the style and reads 0 when the style has none. It returns `published_permanent_label_count`. And it returns `blocking_reasons`, the list of error identifiers for the four style-level gates that are not yet satisfied, listed in ladder order: `ERR_FIBER_NOT_SUBSTANTIATED` while `substantiation_state` is anything other than `FIBER_SUBSTANTIATED`, `ERR_ORIGIN_NOT_ISSUED` while `origin_state` is anything other than `ORIGIN_ISSUED`, `ERR_CARE_NOT_SUBSTANTIATED` while the style has no passed care-performance run, and `ERR_DURABILITY_INSUFFICIENT` while the substantiated cycles figure is strictly less than the required one. That list is empty exactly when all four hold. Label-specific gates — market subset, language coverage, placement and content match — are not evaluated here, because no label is named in the request. The read stores nothing.

## 4. Endpoint Definitions

Endpoints that bring a new resource into existence answer with HTTP 201 and return the created resource in `data`. Endpoints that modify an existing resource — every state action, every edit, and the administrative clock — answer with HTTP 200 and return the updated resource. Read endpoints answer with HTTP 200, returning a single object with `response_type` set to `"object"` or a list with `response_type` set to `"array"`. The table below is a routing summary only: request payloads, response shapes and side effects are specified by the narratives in §2 and §3, and must be reconstructed from there.
