# Nature Center Education API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

The nature center runs a small education department against an unusually wide set of simultaneous constraints. A single third-grade field trip has to sit on a standards-aligned program that has actually cleared curriculum review, arrive with enough adult chaperones to satisfy the ratio the program was written for, be led by a naturalist who is both cleared for employment and certified for that grade band, walk a trail segment whose habitat can absorb the group, and — if the program includes a live animal ambassador — respect a welfare ceiling on how many minutes that animal may spend in front of children this week. When it rains, the trip must fall back to a declared indoor alternate rather than being cancelled. When the school cannot afford the visit, a funder pool must underwrite part of the program fee and part of the bus, without either bucket quietly overdrawing the other.

Today these constraints live in spreadsheets, a shared calendar, and the institutional memory of two coordinators. The Nature Center Education API replaces that with a single system of record, mounted under `/nature-center`, that holds the program catalog, the school partnerships, the seasonal educator roster and its training pipeline, the permitted animal ambassadors and their care status, the trail and habitat inventory, the bookings themselves, the scholarship and transportation subsidy pools, the outreach equipment manifests, and the teacher evaluations that funders read at the end of the year.

Two product commitments shape almost every rule that follows. First, **no reservation may outlive the thing that authorizes it**: when a wildlife permit lapses, when an animal goes on a veterinary hold, when a trail closes for restoration, or when an educator leaves the roster, every confirmed booking that depended on that resource must fall back to an unconfirmed state and give back the capacity it was holding. Second, **the money must never drift**: every cent a funder pool shows as pending, committed or disbursed must be reconstructible from the applications that put it there, and reversing an award must undo exactly what granting it did.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Access Control, Roles and Identity Binding

Every request to the API — including reads — must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting user, and `X-User-Role`, declaring the capacity in which they act. If either header is missing or empty the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`. If `X-User-Role` carries a string that is not one of the seven roles below, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNKNOWN_ROLE`. If the role is recognised but is not among the roles authorised for the target operation, the request is rejected with `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED`.

The seven roles are `EducationDirector`, `CurriculumSpecialist`, `BookingCoordinator`, `StewardshipManager`, `GrantsOfficer`, `Naturalist`, and `TeacherContact`.

Five of the roles are operator roles whose identity is opaque to the data model:

- When `X-User-Role` is `EducationDirector`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server may record it (for example as `reviewed_by_user_id` on a `ProgramOffering` or on an `EvaluationReport`, or as `assessed_by_user_id` on a `TrainingRecord`) but never compares it to any entity's `id`.
- When `X-User-Role` is `CurriculumSpecialist`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server may record it (for example as `authored_by_user_id` on a `ProgramOffering` or as `enrolled_by_user_id` on a `TrainingRecord`) but never compares it to any entity's `id`.
- When `X-User-Role` is `BookingCoordinator`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. It may be recorded (for example as `submitted_by_user_id` on an `AidApplication` created by a coordinator) but is not compared to any entity's `id`.
- When `X-User-Role` is `StewardshipManager`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity and is not compared to any entity's `id`.
- When `X-User-Role` is `GrantsOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. It may be recorded as `reviewer_user_id` on an `AidApplication` but is not compared to any entity's `id`.

Two roles are entity-backed, and for those the header value is a foreign key:

- When `X-User-Role` is `Naturalist`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Educator`; ownership checks on any resource that references an `Educator` compare the resource's foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, a `Naturalist` may act on a booking only when that booking's `lead_educator_id` equals `X-User-ID`, and may read an educator record only when the educator `id` in the path equals `X-User-ID`; on the two animal-ambassador reads, which carry no educator reference at all, the check reduces to requiring that `X-User-ID` itself name an existing `Educator`. A violation is rejected with `ERR_NOT_ASSIGNED_EDUCATOR`.
- When `X-User-Role` is `TeacherContact`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `SchoolPartner`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `SchoolPartner` compare the resource's foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. A teacher may act only on bookings whose `school_id` equals `X-User-ID`, and only on aid applications and evaluation reports whose booking belongs to that school; on the three catalog reads, which carry no owning school at all, the check reduces to requiring that `X-User-ID` itself name an existing `SchoolPartner`. A violation is rejected with `ERR_SCHOOL_SCOPE_VIOLATION`.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits. It is transport metadata rather than a stored or business value, and it alone is generated from the host's real clock rather than from the logical program clock of §2.5; it therefore bears no relation to any entity's `created_at` or `updated_at`.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every list endpoint accepts two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of rows returned; it defaults to 10 when omitted, may not be lower than 1, and may not be higher than 100. `offset` skips a number of rows from the front of the result set; it defaults to 0 and may not be negative. A `limit` outside the range 1 through 100, or a negative `offset`, is a request-shape failure and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

Unless a specific endpoint says otherwise, list results are ordered by `created_at` ascending. Because `created_at` is stamped from the logical clock (§2.5), rows minted under one clock reading share an identical `created_at`, and the clock may even be moved backwards between two creates; the order a list actually returns is therefore the server's creation sequence — the row written first is returned first — which coincides with `created_at` ascending whenever the clock has only moved forward.

List responses carry `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside the `meta` object of the envelope, where `total_count` is the number of rows matching the filters before pagination is applied and `has_more` indicates whether rows remain beyond the current page.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and Version Semantics

Every resource the API mints carries an `id` that is a server-generated UUID. Identifiers carry no type prefix and no domain-flavoured shape — a booking id and a permit id are indistinguishable by inspection, and clients must not parse them.

Every resource also carries a `created_at` timestamp: an ISO 8601 UTC instant rendered with three fractional digits, stamped when the row is first written. It is read from the logical program clock of §2.5 as that clock stands at the moment of the write, never from real wall-clock time, so two rows created without moving the clock in between carry the identical `created_at`. Instants supplied by a client — a booking's `start_utc` and `end_utc`, a permit's `valid_from` and `valid_to`, an educator's `season_end_date` — are stored and echoed back as the literal string the client wrote rather than re-rendered to three fractional digits.

Most resources additionally carry `updated_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC instant with three fractional digits refreshed on every mutation, and `version`, an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increments by one on every mutation of that resource. `updated_at` is refreshed from that same logical clock, so a mutation performed without having moved the clock rewrites `updated_at` to the same rendered instant while `version` still advances; `updated_at` visibly moves only once the clock has been moved. A mutation includes changes to derived values: when a booking's confirmation raises a trail segment's `visitors_this_week`, that segment has been mutated, so its `updated_at` and `version` both move. The same is true when a permit's status change recomputes an animal ambassador's `welfare_posture`, and when an aid decision recomputes a scholarship fund's ledger counters. Every derived value in the system is recomputed from first principles at the close of each successful mutating request, so no counter is ever left stale; a recomputation that lands on the value the resource already held is not a mutation and moves neither field; and a resource's `version` advances at most once per request no matter how many of that request's side effects touch it.

There is exactly one exception in this system. A `StandardAlignment` is an immutable claim: once attached to an offering it is never edited. Standard alignments therefore carry only `id` and `created_at` — both `updated_at` and `version` are absent from a `StandardAlignment` payload. Every other entity in the system — school partners, program offerings, curriculum materials, educators, training records, permits, animal ambassadors, trail segments, bookings, scholarship funds, aid applications, equipment manifests and evaluation reports — carries the full set of `id`, `created_at`, `updated_at` and `version`.

### 2.5. The Logical Program Clock and the ISO Program Week

Nothing in this system reads real wall-clock time, the envelope's `api_request_timestamp` of §2.2 excepted. The server maintains a **logical clock**, and every deadline, expiry, weekly window and "is this in the past" test is evaluated against it, as is every `created_at`, `updated_at` and other server-stamped instant a resource carries. Before any call to `POST /_clock/set_current_utc`, the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`; that startup default is mandatory and the server never falls back to real time. An `EducationDirector` may move the clock by supplying `to_iso8601`, and the response reports the new instant as `current_utc`, echoed back as the literal string the client supplied. Moving the clock mutates no entity and runs no expiry sweep of its own — lazy expiry happens only inside the business operations that declare it.

Several counters in this system are scoped to an **ISO program week**. The week containing the logical clock is the half-open window that begins at Monday `00:00:00Z` and ends at the following Monday `00:00:00Z`: an instant is inside the window when it is at or after the week's start and strictly before the start plus seven days. An animal's weekly exposure counters, a trail segment's weekly visitor counter, and an educator's weekly delivery load are all computed over the bookings whose `start_utc` falls inside that window.

### 2.6. Path and Foreign-Key Resolution Order

When a path contains a resource identifier, that identifier is resolved before anything in the body is examined. When a path contains more than one identifier, they are resolved strictly left to right, and the first identifier that does not name an existing row determines the error returned. The thirteen not-found codes map one-to-one onto the thirteen addressable entities: `ERR_OFFERING_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MATERIAL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SCHOOL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EDUCATOR_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TRAINING_RECORD_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PERMIT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TRAIL_SEGMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_BOOKING_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FUND_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AID_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MANIFEST_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_EVALUATION_REPORT_NOT_FOUND`. All thirteen answer with HTTP 404.

Foreign keys carried in a request body are resolved after the path, and they too are resolved in a fixed order rather than all at once: `school_id` first, then `offering_id`, then `trail_segment_id`, then `lead_educator_id`, then `animal_id`, then `fund_id`, then `booking_id`. The first of these that fails to resolve determines the 404 code. Two further body references stand outside that sequence because no operation carries them alongside another: an animal registration's `permit_id`, which answers `ERR_PERMIT_NOT_FOUND`, and an offering creation's optional `rain_alternate_for_offering_id`, which answers `ERR_OFFERING_NOT_FOUND`. This ordering matters on the create-booking operation and on resource assignment, where two references can be wrong simultaneously and the client needs a deterministic answer.

### 2.7. Numeric Discipline: Cents, Minutes, Persons and Rounding

Money in this system is always an integer count of cents. There are no fractional cents anywhere in a stored value or a response body, and no monetary field is ever rendered as a string. Wherever a monetary computation produces a fraction, the result is rounded **half up** to a whole cent — and the point at which that rounding happens is pinned by each calculation rather than left to the implementer, because rounding a per-student price before multiplying gives a different answer from rounding the product.

Time and capacity are integers too. Durations, buffers, exposure ceilings and weekly loads are whole minutes; interval arithmetic is performed at whole-minute resolution. Headcounts, trail capacities and weekly visitor budgets are whole persons.

Two derived values are not money. A `learning_gain_index` is a number in the closed range 0.00 through 1.00 carrying exactly two decimal places, rounded half up once at the end of its computation. The funder rollup's `weighted_learning_gain_index` and `tier_1_student_share_pct` each carry exactly three decimal places, rounded half up, and both report `0.000` when the student denominator they divide by is zero.

Ratios supplied by clients keep their input precision: `title_i_pct` is a number from 0 through 1 inclusive, and `transport_sublimit_pct` is a number from 0 through 1 inclusive that defaults to `0.25` when the funder pool is opened without one.

### 2.8. Optimistic Concurrency on Bookings and Funds

Two operations in this system can be issued twice against the same parent without either attempt violating a business rule, which means no state precondition can protect the second writer from acting on a stale read. Both are therefore guarded by optimistic concurrency control on the parent's `version`.

The first is resource assignment on a booking. A request to `POST /nature-center/bookings/{id}/assign-resources` must carry `expected_booking_version`, which is not optional, holding the `version` of the target `Booking` as the client last observed it. Reassigning a lead educator while the booking is still `REQUESTED` is legitimate product behaviour, so nothing about the booking's state rejects a second assignment; what must be rejected is an assignment computed against a view of the booking that some other writer has already superseded. If `expected_booking_version` does not equal the booking's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_BOOKING_VERSION` and HTTP 412. A successful assignment increments `Booking.version` by 1, so a client that wants to assign twice must re-read between the two calls.

The second is aid application creation. A request to `POST /nature-center/aid-applications` must carry `expected_fund_version`, again not optional, holding the `version` of the target `ScholarshipFund` as the client last observed it. A second application against the same open pool is perfectly legal, so no state check rejects it; what must be rejected is an application sized against a stale reading of the pool's `pending_cents` and `available_cents`. If `expected_fund_version` does not equal the fund's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_FUND_VERSION` and HTTP 412. A successful create increments `ScholarshipFund.version` by 1.

No other endpoint in this system accepts a version field. Confirming, diverting, delivering, cancelling, recording a no-show, recording a payment, reviewing an aid application, changing a permit or animal or trail status, and every catalog and staffing operation are all issued without any expected-version input, and supplying one has no defined effect.

### 2.9. Validation Precedence

When a request violates more than one rule, the client must receive a deterministic answer. Checks are therefore evaluated in the fixed global order below, and the **first** check that fails determines the response. A later, more specific failure never masks an earlier one — a request from an unrecognised role against a non-existent booking answers `ERR_UNKNOWN_ROLE`, not `ERR_BOOKING_NOT_FOUND`.

1. Both `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` are present and non-empty — otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (401). Applies to every endpoint.
2. `X-User-Role` names one of the seven declared roles — otherwise `ERR_UNKNOWN_ROLE` (401). Applies to every endpoint.
3. The acting role is authorised for the target operation — otherwise `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (403). Applies to every endpoint.
4. The request body and query string conform to the operation's input shape: every required field present, every type correct, every enum value inside its declared set, every number inside its declared range, `limit` between 1 and 100 inclusive, and `offset` at least 0 — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400). Applies to every endpoint.
5. Interval fields are correctly ordered: a booking's `end_utc` is strictly after its `start_utc`, and a permit's `valid_to` is strictly after its `valid_from` — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_TIME_RANGE` (422). Applies to booking creation and permit creation.
6. Every identifier in the path resolves, left to right, to the corresponding not-found code (404) as described in §2.6. Applies to every endpoint with a path identifier.
7. Every foreign key in the body resolves, in the order given in §2.6, to the corresponding not-found code (404). Applies to booking creation, resource assignment, animal registration, aid application creation and program offering creation.
8. A `TeacherContact` is acting inside their own school's scope — otherwise `ERR_SCHOOL_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (403). This scope check guards eleven operations: creating a booking, cancelling a booking, opening an evaluation report on a booking, submitting an evaluation report, creating an aid application, reading one booking, listing a booking's evaluation reports, reading one aid application, listing program offerings, reading one program offering, and listing an offering's curriculum materials.
9. A `Naturalist` is acting on their own assignment — otherwise `ERR_NOT_ASSIGNED_EDUCATOR` (403). This check guards six operations: recording delivery of a booking, reading one booking, reading one educator, listing an educator's training records, listing animal ambassadors, and reading one animal ambassador.
10. On a catalog review decision, `X-User-ID` differs from the offering's `authored_by_user_id` — otherwise `ERR_SOD_REVIEW_SAME_ACTOR` (403).
11. On a training assessment, `X-User-ID` differs from the training record's `enrolled_by_user_id` — otherwise `ERR_SOD_ASSESS_SAME_ACTOR` (403).
12. On an evaluation review decision, `X-User-ID` differs from the report's `submitted_by_user_id` — otherwise `ERR_SOD_EVALUATION_SAME_ACTOR` (403).
13. Track-dependent training fields agree with the declared `track`, and a passing assessment carries a `score` — otherwise `ERR_TRACK_FIELD_MISMATCH` (422). Applies to enrolling an educator in a training track and to recording an assessment.
14. The target resource's own current state permits the requested transition — otherwise the state code for that entity (409): `ERR_INVALID_OFFERING_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_MATERIAL_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_EDUCATOR_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_TRAINING_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_PERMIT_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_ANIMAL_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_TRAIL_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_BOOKING_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_FUNDING_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_AID_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_MANIFEST_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_EVALUATION_STATE` or `ERR_INVALID_FUND_STATE`. Applies to every operation that drives a state transition.
15. The optimistic-concurrency check of §2.8: `expected_booking_version` equals `Booking.version` on resource assignment (`ERR_STALE_BOOKING_VERSION`, 412), and `expected_fund_version` equals `ScholarshipFund.version` on aid application creation (`ERR_STALE_FUND_VERSION`, 412).
16. The referenced program offering is `PUBLISHED` — otherwise `ERR_OFFERING_NOT_PUBLISHED` (409). Applies to booking creation and booking confirmation.
17. The referenced school partner is not `SUSPENDED` — otherwise `ERR_SCHOOL_SUSPENDED` (409). Applies to booking creation and booking confirmation.
18. The referenced scholarship fund is `OPEN` — otherwise `ERR_FUND_NOT_OPEN` (409). Applies to aid application creation and aid application review.
19. The permit covering the referenced animal ambassador is `ACTIVE` — otherwise `ERR_PERMIT_NOT_ACTIVE` (409). Applies to resource assignment and booking confirmation.
20. The referenced animal ambassador's care status is `AVAILABLE` — otherwise `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_AVAILABLE` (409). Applies to resource assignment and booking confirmation.
21. The referenced trail segment's status is `OPEN` — otherwise `ERR_TRAIL_NOT_OPEN` (409). Applies to resource assignment and booking confirmation.
22. The referenced educator's status is `CLEARED` — otherwise `ERR_EDUCATOR_NOT_CLEARED` (422). Applies to resource assignment and booking confirmation.
23. The referenced educator's `curriculum_certified_grade_bands` contains the booked offering's `grade_band`, evaluated after the lazy training expiry that resource assignment performs — confirmation runs no sweep of its own and reads the credentials exactly as the last assignment left them — otherwise `ERR_EDUCATOR_NOT_CERTIFIED_FOR_GRADE_BAND` (422). Applies to resource assignment and booking confirmation.
24. When both an educator and an animal are attached, the educator's `animal_handling_tier` is at least the animal's `handling_tier_required` under the ordering `NONE` below `TIER_1` below `TIER_2` — otherwise `ERR_HANDLING_TIER_INSUFFICIENT` (422). Applies to resource assignment and booking confirmation.
25. When the trail segment's `habitat_sensitivity` is `HIGH`, the booking's `student_count` plus `chaperone_count` is at most 25 persons — otherwise `ERR_HABITAT_SENSITIVITY_LIMIT` (422). Applies to resource assignment and booking confirmation.
26. Chaperone coverage is met — otherwise `ERR_CHAPERONE_RATIO_UNMET` (422). At confirmation this means `chaperone_count` is at least `required_chaperones`; at delivery it means `actual_chaperone_count` is at least the ratio requirement recomputed from `actual_student_count`. Applies to booking confirmation and booking delivery.
27. Confirming would leave the animal's projected weekly exposure strictly below its ceiling: `projected_exposure_minutes_7d` plus the offering's `duration_minutes` is strictly less than `weekly_exposure_cap_minutes` — otherwise `ERR_ANIMAL_WELFARE_CAP_REACHED` (422). Landing exactly on the ceiling is a rejection. Applies to booking confirmation.
28. When the offering's `public_contact_required` is true, the animal's `effective_contact_rule` is `SUPERVISED_TOUCH` — otherwise `ERR_PUBLIC_CONTACT_NOT_PERMITTED` (422). Applies to booking confirmation.
29. A new booking's `start_utc` is strictly after the logical clock — otherwise `ERR_BOOKING_START_IN_PAST` (409). Applies to booking creation.
30. When the assigned educator's `employment_type` is `SEASONAL_EDUCATOR`, the booking's `end_utc` is at or before that educator's `season_end_date` — otherwise `ERR_EDUCATOR_SEASON_ENDED` (409). Applies to resource assignment.
31. The candidate booking's buffered educator interval does not intersect the buffered interval of any other booking led by the same educator — otherwise `ERR_EDUCATOR_TIME_CONFLICT` (409). Applies to resource assignment.
32. The candidate booking's `start_utc`-to-`end_utc` window, treated as half-open, does not intersect the same window of another booking with `delivery_status` of `CONFIRMED`, `RAIN_DIVERTED` or `DELIVERED` that references the same animal ambassador — otherwise `ERR_ANIMAL_TIME_CONFLICT` (409). Applies to resource assignment.
33. No existing standard alignment on this offering already claims the same pairing of `standard_code` and `dimension` — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_STANDARD_ALIGNMENT` (422). Applies to attaching a standard alignment.
34. Standards coverage is sufficient — otherwise `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_STANDARD_ALIGNMENT` (422). Sending an offering into review requires `standard_alignment_count` of at least 1; publishing it requires at least 2. Applies to submitting an offering for review and to deciding a catalog review.
35. `materials_ready` is true — otherwise `ERR_MATERIALS_NOT_READY` (422). Applies to deciding a catalog review with a publish decision.
36. `lead_educator_id` is set; `trail_segment_id` is set when the offering's `requires_trail_segment` is true; `animal_id` is set when the offering's `requires_live_animal` is true — otherwise `ERR_REQUIRED_RESOURCE_MISSING` (422). Applies to booking confirmation.
37. An explicitly supplied `alternate_offering_id` satisfies the rain-plan eligibility filter — otherwise `ERR_RAIN_ALTERNATE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (422), which is also the answer when the identifier names no offering at all, since this reference is never resolved as a foreign key. Applies to rain diversion.
38. When `alternate_offering_id` is omitted, the rain-plan candidate pool is non-empty — otherwise `ERR_NO_RAIN_ALTERNATE` (409). Applies to rain diversion.
39. The booking has no aid application whose status is `SUBMITTED`, `APPROVED`, `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` or `DISBURSED` — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_AID_APPLICATION` (409). Applies to aid application creation.
40. The booking has no evaluation report whose status is `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `RETURNED` or `ACCEPTED` — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_EVALUATION` (409). Applies to opening an evaluation report.
41. Every code listed in `shortage_kit_codes` also appears in the manifest's `kit_codes` — otherwise `ERR_SHORTAGE_CODE_UNKNOWN` (422). Applies to advancing an equipment manifest.
42. No booking references the offering with a `delivery_status` of `REQUESTED`, `CONFIRMED` or `RAIN_DIVERTED` — otherwise `ERR_OFFERING_HAS_ACTIVE_BOOKINGS` (409). Applies to retiring an offering.
43. The fund's `pending_cents` is exactly 0 when the pool is being closed — otherwise `ERR_FUND_HAS_PENDING_APPLICATIONS` (409). Applies to changing a fund's status.
44. The training record's `attempt_count` is strictly below 3 — otherwise `ERR_TRAINING_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED` (422). Applies to re-enrolling a failed training record.
45. `student_count` plus `chaperone_count` is at most the offering's `max_group_size` — otherwise `ERR_GROUP_SIZE_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to booking creation and booking confirmation.
46. Aggregate concurrent persons over the trail occupancy window do not exceed the segment's `capacity_persons` — otherwise `ERR_TRAIL_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to booking confirmation.
47. The segment's `visitors_this_week` plus the candidate's `student_count` plus `chaperone_count` is at most `weekly_visitor_budget_persons` — otherwise `ERR_TRAIL_WEEKLY_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to booking confirmation.
48. The educator's `delivery_load_minutes_this_week`, recomputed with the candidate booking's own contribution excluded, plus the offering's `duration_minutes` is at most that educator's weekly cap; exceeding the cap strictly rejects — otherwise `ERR_EDUCATOR_WEEKLY_LOAD_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to resource assignment.
49. The school's `equity_tier` is not `NONE` — otherwise `ERR_NOT_AID_ELIGIBLE` (422). Applies to aid application creation.
50. Fund capacity is adequate — otherwise `ERR_FUND_INSUFFICIENT` (402). At creation this means `requested_scholarship_cents` plus `requested_transport_cents` is at most the fund's `available_cents`; at review with an approve decision it means the award waterfall yields an `awarded_scholarship_cents` plus `awarded_transport_cents` total strictly greater than zero. Applies to aid application creation and aid application review.
51. When transportation subsidy is requested, the remaining transport sublimit is strictly positive: the fund's `total_cents` multiplied by its `transport_sublimit_pct`, rounded half up to whole cents, minus `transport_awarded_cents`, must be greater than zero — otherwise `ERR_TRANSPORT_SUBLIMIT_EXCEEDED` (402). Applies to aid application creation and aid application review.
52. A payment's `amount_cents` is at most the booking's `net_due_cents` minus its `paid_cents` — otherwise `ERR_PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_BALANCE` (422). Applies to recording a payment.

### 2.10. Withdrawal Cascades and Counter Symmetry

The single most important consistency guarantee in this system is that a confirmed booking never holds capacity it is no longer entitled to. Whenever the authority behind an assigned resource is withdrawn, the server reverts every affected booking from `CONFIRMED` back to `REQUESTED`, clears the specific foreign keys that were withdrawn, records why on `resource_release_reason`, and — critically — reverses the counters that confirmation had incremented. `TrailSegment.visitors_this_week` is decreased by the booking's `student_count` plus `chaperone_count` when a trail segment was attached, and `AnimalAmbassador.projected_exposure_minutes_7d` is decreased by the offering's `duration_minutes` when an animal was attached. Each touched resource is a genuine mutation: `updated_at` is refreshed and `version` is incremented on the booking, on the trail segment and on the animal alike.

Two long cascades in particular must fire end to end rather than stopping at the first hop.

The first begins with a permit. Moving a permit from `ACTIVE` to `LAPSED` or to `SUSPENDED` recomputes every animal ambassador under that permit so that its `welfare_posture` becomes `BLOCKED` and its `effective_contact_rule` becomes `NO_PUBLIC_CONTACT`. Because those animals are now blocked, every booking that references them with `delivery_status` of `CONFIRMED` reverts to `REQUESTED` with both `animal_id` and `trail_segment_id` cleared and `resource_release_reason` set to `PERMIT_LAPSE`; and because those bookings left `CONFIRMED`, the trail visitor counter and the animal's projected exposure counter are both decremented. Revoking the permit outright goes further: every covered animal transitions to `RETIRED`, and the same booking reversion and counter release follow.

The second begins with a no-show. Recording a no-show increments the school's `no_show_strikes` and recomputes `SchoolPartner.status`. If the school reaches suspension, every other booking of that school still sitting at `REQUESTED` transitions to `CANCELLED` with `resource_release_reason` set to `SCHOOL_SUSPENSION`; and because those bookings are now cancelled, every aid application on them whose status is `APPROVED` or `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` transitions to `REVOKED`, the awarded amounts are reset to zero, the fund's `committed_cents`, `transport_awarded_cents` and `available_cents` are recomputed, and each affected booking's `aid_amount_cents` and `net_due_cents` fall back to 0.

The same reversion machinery is driven by three other withdrawals, each described in the workflow that owns it: an animal placed on a veterinary hold or retired, a trail segment closed for the season, and an educator deactivated off the roster.

### 2.11. Segregation of Duties

Three decisions in this system may not be made by the person who set them up, because each is a quality gate rather than a workflow step. A catalog review decision may not be recorded by the `X-User-ID` stored as the offering's `authored_by_user_id`. A training assessment may not be recorded by the `X-User-ID` stored as the record's `enrolled_by_user_id`. And an evaluation review decision may not be recorded by the `X-User-ID` stored as the report's `submitted_by_user_id`. Each violation carries its own code — `ERR_SOD_REVIEW_SAME_ACTOR`, `ERR_SOD_ASSESS_SAME_ACTOR` and `ERR_SOD_EVALUATION_SAME_ACTOR` respectively — and all three answer 403. Note that the evaluation segregation check binds only the accept branch of an evaluation review; returning a report to the teacher is not a sign-off and does not require a second actor, though the role restriction still applies.

### 2.12. The Two Parallel Dimensions of a Booking

A `Booking` runs two state machines at once, and they are genuinely independent.

`delivery_status` is the operational dimension: it begins at `REQUESTED` and moves among `CONFIRMED`, `RAIN_DIVERTED`, `DELIVERED`, `NO_SHOW` and `CANCELLED`, of which the last three are terminal. `funding_status` is the money dimension: it begins at `UNFUNDED` and moves among `AID_PENDING`, `AID_APPROVED`, `INVOICED`, `SETTLED` and `WRITTEN_OFF`, of which `SETTLED` and `WRITTEN_OFF` are terminal.

Neither dimension gates the other's ordinary progress. A booking may sit at `CONFIRMED` while its funding walks `UNFUNDED` to `AID_PENDING` to `AID_APPROVED`, and no funding transition ever changes `delivery_status`. The coupling runs in one direction only, and only at three specific moments: delivery drives funding to `INVOICED`, a no-show drives funding to `WRITTEN_OFF`, and a cancellation that revokes an award drives funding from `AID_APPROVED` back to `UNFUNDED`. When a funding-dimension precondition blocks an operation — recording a payment against a booking that is not `INVOICED`, for instance — the rejection is `ERR_INVALID_FUNDING_STATE`, distinct from the `ERR_INVALID_BOOKING_STATE` that a delivery-dimension violation produces.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Authoring and Publishing the Program Catalog

A `CurriculumSpecialist` starts every program in the catalog. Creating an offering requires a `title` for the printed catalog, a `grade_band` drawn from `K_2`, `G3_5`, `G6_8` and `G9_12`, a `delivery_mode` of `ONSITE_FIELD_TRIP`, `OUTREACH_AT_SCHOOL` or `VIRTUAL`, a `duration_minutes` of scheduled contact time between 30 and 480 inclusive, a `max_group_size` between 1 and 500 inclusive covering students and chaperones together, a `min_chaperone_ratio_students_per_adult` between 1 and 30 inclusive, and a `base_fee_cents_per_student` list price between 0 and 100000 cents inclusive. Three boolean switches are optional and each defaults to false: `requires_live_animal`, `requires_trail_segment` and `public_contact_required`. An offering may also name another offering through `rain_alternate_for_offering_id`, which declares this offering to be a rain-plan alternate for the offering named — the catalog is self-referential in exactly this one way. A fresh offering lands in `DRAFT` with `revision_round` at 0, `standard_alignment_count` at 0, `materials_ready` false, and `authored_by_user_id` stamped with the acting `X-User-ID`.

Standards coverage is added one claim at a time. Each claim carries the `standard_code` as printed in the framework, a `dimension` of `DISCIPLINARY_CORE_IDEA`, `SCIENCE_PRACTICE` or `CROSSCUTTING_CONCEPT`, and an optional `grade_band` that defaults to the parent offering's own `grade_band` when omitted. Each claim records the `offering_id` it belongs to. Two claims on the same offering may not share the same combination of `standard_code` and `dimension` — a repeat is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_STANDARD_ALIGNMENT`. A claim may be attached whatever state the parent offering is in, `RETIRED` included. Every accepted claim increments the offering's `standard_alignment_count` by 1. Standard alignments are the one immutable record type in the system, so they carry no `updated_at` and no `version`, and the alignment list can be paged like any other collection.

Teachers need something to do before and after the visit, which is what curriculum materials are for. A material is created against an offering with a `phase` of either `PRE_VISIT` or `POST_VISIT` and a `url_slug` naming the downloadable packet, and it records its own `offering_id`; creation is open whatever state the parent offering is in, and only publishing tests that state. Materials begin at `DRAFT` and may be published, moving `DRAFT` to `PUBLISHED`; publishing is only possible while the material is in `DRAFT` — otherwise the rejection is `ERR_INVALID_MATERIAL_STATE` — and only while the parent offering sits in `DRAFT`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `REVISION_REQUESTED` or `PUBLISHED`, a parent-state failure being reported as `ERR_INVALID_OFFERING_STATE`. Every publish recomputes the parent's `materials_ready`, which is true precisely when the offering has at least one `PRE_VISIT` material in `PUBLISHED` **and** at least one `POST_VISIT` material in `PUBLISHED`. Material lists can be filtered by `phase` and by `status`, whose full set is `DRAFT`, `PUBLISHED` and `ARCHIVED`.

Sending an offering into review advances `DRAFT` to `UNDER_REVIEW`, or `REVISION_REQUESTED` to `UNDER_REVIEW` — the revision loop `REVISION_REQUESTED → UNDER_REVIEW → REVISION_REQUESTED` is a supported cycle, not an error path. Either way the offering must already carry at least one standards claim, and an offering with `standard_alignment_count` of 0 is rejected with `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_STANDARD_ALIGNMENT`.

The `EducationDirector` decides the review by supplying a `decision` of either `PUBLISH` or `REQUEST_REVISION`, plus an optional `reviewer_note`. Both branches require the offering to be sitting at `UNDER_REVIEW` and both are barred to the author under §2.11. The `PUBLISH` branch carries three further requirements: `standard_alignment_count` must be at least 2 (a single claim is enough to enter review but not to ship, and a shortfall is again `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_STANDARD_ALIGNMENT`), and `materials_ready` must be true or the decision is rejected with `ERR_MATERIALS_NOT_READY`. On success the offering moves `UNDER_REVIEW → PUBLISHED`, `published_at` is stamped with the logical clock, and `reviewed_by_user_id` records the deciding `X-User-ID`. The `REQUEST_REVISION` branch moves the offering `UNDER_REVIEW → REVISION_REQUESTED`, increments `revision_round` by 1, records `reviewed_by_user_id`, and reverts **every** curriculum material of that offering currently in `PUBLISHED` back to `DRAFT` — which in turn recomputes `materials_ready` to false as soon as any material reverts. That reversion is deliberate: a revision round invalidates the packets teachers would have downloaded, so the specialist must republish them before the offering can ship again.

Retirement takes an offering out of the catalog and accepts an optional `reason`. An offering in `PUBLISHED` may be retired only when no booking references it with a `delivery_status` of `REQUESTED`, `CONFIRMED` or `RAIN_DIVERTED`; live demand blocks retirement with `ERR_OFFERING_HAS_ACTIVE_BOOKINGS`. An offering still in `DRAFT` may also be retired directly. Both routes behave identically downstream: the offering moves to `RETIRED`, every curriculum material of that offering whose status is `DRAFT` or `PUBLISHED` transitions to `ARCHIVED`, and `materials_ready` is recomputed to false. `RETIRED` is terminal, and an offering sitting in `UNDER_REVIEW` or `REVISION_REQUESTED` cannot be retired at all — such an attempt answers `ERR_INVALID_OFFERING_STATE`. Catalog browsing supports filters on `grade_band`, `delivery_mode`, `status` (`DRAFT`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `REVISION_REQUESTED`, `PUBLISHED`, `RETIRED`) and `rain_alternate_for_offering_id`, the last of which lets a coordinator enumerate the declared rain plans for a given program.

### 3.2. School Partners, Equity Tiers and Partnership Standing

Registering a school partner captures the `name` as it should appear on the booking confirmation, the `district_code` that funder outcome reports are keyed on, a `title_i_pct` between 0 and 1 inclusive giving the share of enrolled students qualifying for Title I support, and a `distance_miles` one-way road distance from the nature center that must be greater than 0 and at most 400. A teacher's `contact_email` is optional. New partners open at `ACTIVE` with `no_show_strikes` at 0 and `outstanding_balance_cents` at 0.

The aid eligibility tier is derived, never supplied. A school's `equity_tier` is `TIER_1` when `title_i_pct` is at least 0.75, `TIER_2` when it is at least 0.50 but below 0.75, `TIER_3` when it is at least 0.25 but below 0.50, and `NONE` below 0.25. That tier is the gate on the whole subsidy programme, and a school at `NONE` cannot apply for aid at all.

Partnership standing is likewise derived. `no_show_strikes` is the count of that school's bookings whose `delivery_status` is `NO_SHOW`; bookings in any other delivery state are not counted. Whenever a no-show is recorded the standing is recomputed: the school is `SUSPENDED` once it has accumulated two or more strikes, `PROBATION` at one strike, and `ACTIVE` otherwise. The first strike therefore drives `ACTIVE → PROBATION` — the strike total has reached one and is still below two — and the second drives `PROBATION → SUSPENDED`. Each transition refreshes the partner's `updated_at`. `SUSPENDED` is terminal: there is no path back, and suspension immediately triggers the cancellation-and-revocation cascade described in §2.10. A suspended school cannot have new bookings created for it and cannot have existing ones confirmed; both attempts answer `ERR_SCHOOL_SUSPENDED`.

What a school owes is tracked as `outstanding_balance_cents`: over the school's bookings whose `funding_status` is `INVOICED`, it is the sum of each booking's `net_due_cents` minus that booking's `paid_cents`. It is recomputed whenever a booking is invoiced at delivery and whenever a payment is recorded.

### 3.3. Staffing: The Hiring Pipeline, Training Tracks and Certification Decay

Every educator enters as an applicant. The `EducationDirector` registers a `full_name` and an `employment_type` of either `STAFF_NATURALIST` or `SEASONAL_EDUCATOR`, and may supply a `season_end_date` — mandatory in practice for seasonal hires, since it is the last instant a seasonal educator may be assigned to lead a booking, and optional for staff naturalists. A new educator sits at `APPLICANT` with `animal_handling_tier` of `NONE`, an empty `curriculum_certified_grade_bands` array, and `delivery_load_minutes_this_week` at 0.

The pipeline advances through a single hiring operation carrying an `action`. `START_ONBOARDING` moves `APPLICANT → ONBOARDING` and stamps `background_check_started_at` with the logical clock. `CLEAR` moves `ONBOARDING → CLEARED`, but only for an educator who already holds at least one training record on the `CURRICULUM` track with status `ASSESSED_PASS` — nobody reaches the roster without having passed curriculum training at least once, and a missing curriculum pass is refused with `ERR_INVALID_EDUCATOR_STATE` exactly as a wrong source state is. `DEACTIVATE` moves either `CLEARED → INACTIVE` or `ONBOARDING → INACTIVE`, and `INACTIVE` is terminal. The two deactivation branches are deliberately asymmetric in their consequences. Deactivating an educator who was only `ONBOARDING` has no downstream effect beyond the status change, because such an educator can never have been assigned to a booking. Deactivating a `CLEARED` educator, by contrast, strips them off the schedule: every booking led by that educator whose `delivery_status` is `REQUESTED` or `CONFIRMED` has its `lead_educator_id` cleared, any such booking sitting at `CONFIRMED` reverts `CONFIRMED → REQUESTED` with `resource_release_reason` set to `PERMIT_LAPSE` and the trail and exposure counters released per §2.10 — a booking that was already `REQUESTED` merely loses the educator and keeps whatever reason it was carrying — and the educator's `delivery_load_minutes_this_week` is recomputed to 0. Attempting a hiring action from a state that does not permit it answers `ERR_INVALID_EDUCATOR_STATE`.

Training is recorded per educator per track. Enrolment records the `educator_id`, a `track` of either `CURRICULUM` or `LIVE_ANIMAL_HANDLING`, and exactly one track-dependent field: a `grade_band` is required for a `CURRICULUM` enrolment and must be absent for a handling enrolment, while a `handling_tier` of `TIER_1` or `TIER_2` is required for a `LIVE_ANIMAL_HANDLING` enrolment and must be absent for a curriculum enrolment. Getting that pairing wrong is rejected with `ERR_TRACK_FIELD_MISMATCH`. A new record opens at `ENROLLED` with `attempt_count` at 0 and `enrolled_by_user_id` stamped from `X-User-ID`. Training lists can be filtered by `track` and by `status`, whose full set is `ENROLLED`, `ASSESSED_PASS`, `ASSESSED_FAIL` and `EXPIRED`.

Recording an assessment supplies an `outcome` of `PASS` or `FAIL` and, on a pass, a `score` between 0 and 100 inclusive; a `PASS` submitted without a `score` is another `ERR_TRACK_FIELD_MISMATCH`, while a `score` sent alongside a `FAIL` is permitted and is persisted on the record. The assessor must not be the enroller, per §2.11. A pass additionally requires the recorded `score` to be at least 70 — a `PASS` carrying a score below 70 cannot take the record into `ASSESSED_PASS` and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_TRAINING_STATE`, as is any assessment of a record that is not sitting at `ENROLLED`. Both outcomes increment `attempt_count` by 1 and stamp `assessed_by_user_id` with the deciding `X-User-ID`. The two outcome branches then diverge with equal specificity. `ENROLLED → ASSESSED_FAIL` does nothing further. `ENROLLED → ASSESSED_PASS` stamps `passed_at` with the logical clock, sets `expires_on` to that instant plus a validity window of one year — 365 days — and re-derives the educator's aggregate credentials: `curriculum_certified_grade_bands` is refreshed to the set of grade bands for which the educator holds a `CURRICULUM` record in `ASSESSED_PASS` when the track was curriculum, and `animal_handling_tier` is refreshed to the highest `handling_tier` across the educator's passing `LIVE_ANIMAL_HANDLING` records — `NONE` when there are none — when the track was handling.

A failed record can be retried, but not indefinitely. The `EducationDirector` may return `ASSESSED_FAIL → ENROLLED`, which clears the recorded `score`, provided `attempt_count` is still strictly below 3; a fourth attempt is refused with `ERR_TRAINING_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`. The cycle `ENROLLED → ASSESSED_FAIL → ENROLLED` is therefore bounded at three assessments.

Certifications decay, and the decay is evaluated lazily rather than swept. At the moment a coordinator attaches resources to a booking, every `ASSESSED_PASS` record belonging to the named educator whose `expires_on` is at or before the logical clock transitions `ASSESSED_PASS → EXPIRED`, and only then are `curriculum_certified_grade_bands` (with the lapsed band dropped) and `animal_handling_tier` (downgraded, possibly all the way to `NONE`) re-derived. `EXPIRED` is terminal. The consequence matters: an educator whose curriculum pass lapsed last week is still nominally `CLEARED`, but the grade-band check at assignment time will see the expired record and reject with `ERR_EDUCATOR_NOT_CERTIFIED_FOR_GRADE_BAND`.

Weekly workload is the other staffing constraint. `delivery_load_minutes_this_week` is the sum of the booked offerings' `duration_minutes` across every booking whose `lead_educator_id` is this educator and whose `delivery_status` is `REQUESTED`, `CONFIRMED`, `RAIN_DIVERTED` or `DELIVERED`, restricted to bookings whose `start_utc` falls inside the ISO program week containing the logical clock. Cancelled and no-show bookings contribute nothing. That running total is checked against a cap that depends on employment type: a seasonal educator may carry six hours of delivery per week — 360 minutes — while a staff naturalist may carry ten hours, or 600 minutes. The check is strict: the running total is recomputed with the candidate booking's own contribution excluded — so re-assigning resources on a booking the educator already leads never double-counts it — and adding the candidate offering's `duration_minutes` to that total must not exceed the cap, and a total that lands above it is rejected with `ERR_EDUCATOR_WEEKLY_LOAD_EXCEEDED` while a total landing exactly on the cap is allowed.

### 3.4. Permits, Animal Ambassadors and Welfare Posture

Nothing may be exhibited to a school group without a current permit behind it. A `StewardshipManager` records a permit with the issuing `agency` — `USFWS`, `STATE_DNR` or `USDA` — the `permit_number` as issued, and the `valid_from` and `valid_to` instants bounding the authorisation; `valid_to` must fall strictly after `valid_from` or the create is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_TIME_RANGE`. Permits open at `ACTIVE`, and their standing moves through `SUSPENDED`, `LAPSED` and `REVOKED` by way of a status-setting operation that takes the target `status` and an optional `note`. From `ACTIVE` the manager may go to `SUSPENDED`, to `LAPSED` or to `REVOKED`; from either `SUSPENDED` or `LAPSED` the permit may be restored to `ACTIVE`; `REVOKED` is terminal. Any other combination is refused with `ERR_INVALID_PERMIT_STATE`.

The consequences of a permit move are graded, and the three downward branches deserve equal attention. Moving to `SUSPENDED` recomputes every animal ambassador under that permit so that `welfare_posture` becomes `BLOCKED` and `effective_contact_rule` becomes `NO_PUBLIC_CONTACT`, and — because those animals are now blocked — carries on into the booking-release cascade of §2.10, reverting every `CONFIRMED` booking on a covered animal to `REQUESTED` with both `animal_id` and `trail_segment_id` cleared and `resource_release_reason` set to `PERMIT_LAPSE`. Moving to `LAPSED` does exactly the same. Moving to `REVOKED` is harsher still: every covered ambassador transitions to `RETIRED`, and every booking referencing one of those animals with `delivery_status` of `CONFIRMED` reverts `CONFIRMED → REQUESTED` with both `animal_id` and `trail_segment_id` cleared and `resource_release_reason` set to `PERMIT_LAPSE`. Restoring a permit to `ACTIVE` from either `SUSPENDED` or `LAPSED` recomputes `welfare_posture` and `effective_contact_rule` for every covered animal from current data — it does **not** re-confirm the bookings the lapse released, which must be re-confirmed deliberately.

An animal ambassador is registered under a permit with a `common_name` used in programme copy, a `species_code` as recorded on the permit, the `permit_id` authorising its programme use, a `handling_tier_required` of `TIER_1` or `TIER_2` naming the minimum educator handling credential needed to lead a programme with it, a baseline `contact_rule` of `NO_PUBLIC_CONTACT`, `VIEW_ONLY` or `SUPERVISED_TOUCH`, and a `weekly_exposure_cap_minutes` welfare ceiling greater than 0 and at most 1200. A new ambassador opens at care status `AVAILABLE` with `welfare_posture` of `OPEN`, `effective_contact_rule` equal to its `contact_rule`, and both exposure counters at 0. Ambassador lists may be filtered by `permit_id`, by care `status` and by `welfare_posture`.

Care status has four values — `AVAILABLE`, `RESTING`, `VET_HOLD` and `RETIRED` — and the keeper drives it with an explicit target `status` plus an optional `note`. `AVAILABLE → RESTING` and `RESTING → AVAILABLE` are routine rotation and simply recompute `welfare_posture` and `effective_contact_rule`. `VET_HOLD → AVAILABLE` likewise recomputes both derived values. `AVAILABLE → VET_HOLD` is the serious one: it forces `welfare_posture` to `BLOCKED` and `effective_contact_rule` to `NO_PUBLIC_CONTACT`, and every booking referencing that animal with `delivery_status` of `CONFIRMED` reverts `CONFIRMED → REQUESTED` with `animal_id` and `trail_segment_id` cleared and `resource_release_reason` set to `PERMIT_LAPSE`. Retirement is reachable from `AVAILABLE`, `RESTING` or `VET_HOLD`, forces `welfare_posture` to `BLOCKED` and `effective_contact_rule` to `NO_PUBLIC_CONTACT`, drives the very same booking reversion a veterinary hold does — `CONFIRMED → REQUESTED` with `animal_id` and `trail_segment_id` cleared and `resource_release_reason` set to `PERMIT_LAPSE` — and is terminal. A status change from a state that does not permit it answers `ERR_INVALID_ANIMAL_STATE`.

Welfare posture is where the animal-care policy actually bites, and it rests on two exposure counters computed over the ISO program week containing the logical clock. The **projected** counter, `projected_exposure_minutes_7d`, is forward-looking: it sums the booked offerings' `duration_minutes` over every booking that references this animal, whose `delivery_status` is `CONFIRMED` or `RAIN_DIVERTED`, and whose `start_utc` falls inside that week. The **realized** counter, `realized_exposure_minutes_7d`, is retrospective: it sums `actual_contact_minutes` over the bookings referencing this animal whose `delivery_status` is `DELIVERED` and whose `start_utc` falls inside the same week. Bookings in `REQUESTED`, `NO_SHOW` or `CANCELLED` contribute to neither. The two diverge whenever on-site contact ran shorter or longer than the scheduled duration, and that divergence is intentional — the realized figure is a welfare reporting number and is **never** used to gate anything.

Posture is derived from the projected counter alone, in this order. If the covering permit's status is anything other than `ACTIVE`, or the animal's own care status is `VET_HOLD` or `RETIRED`, then `welfare_posture` is `BLOCKED`. Otherwise, if `projected_exposure_minutes_7d` has reached or passed `weekly_exposure_cap_minutes`, the posture is `BLOCKED`. Otherwise, if the projected total has reached or passed sixty percent of the ceiling — that is, `weekly_exposure_cap_minutes` multiplied by 0.60 and rounded half up to whole minutes — the posture is `RESTRICTED`. Otherwise it is `OPEN`. From the posture the system derives `effective_contact_rule`: a `BLOCKED` animal is forced to `NO_PUBLIC_CONTACT`; a `RESTRICTED` animal whose baseline `contact_rule` is `SUPERVISED_TOUCH` is downgraded to `VIEW_ONLY`; in every other case the effective rule equals the baseline `contact_rule`. A restricted screech owl can therefore still be shown to a class, but the children may no longer touch it.

The confirm-time exposure gate is stricter than the posture threshold and is stated separately because the boundary is easy to get wrong. Confirmation is refused when the animal's `projected_exposure_minutes_7d` plus the booked offering's `duration_minutes` has reached or exceeded `weekly_exposure_cap_minutes`; landing exactly on the ceiling is a rejection, carrying `ERR_ANIMAL_WELFARE_CAP_REACHED`. Posture and contact rules are recomputed whenever a booking is confirmed, cancelled, marked no-show or delivered, whenever an animal's care status changes, and on every permit status transition.

### 3.5. Trails and Habitat Protection

A trail segment is registered with a `name`, a `capacity_persons` ceiling between 1 and 400 inclusive on how many people may occupy it at any single instant, a `habitat_sensitivity` classification of `LOW`, `MODERATE` or `HIGH`, a `weekly_visitor_budget_persons` resource-protection ceiling between 1 and 5000 inclusive on total persons routed through the segment in an ISO program week, and an optional `recovery_buffer_minutes` trailing recovery window between 0 and 240 inclusive that defaults to 30 minutes. Segments open at `OPEN` with `visitors_this_week` at 0.

Segment availability is a three-value machine: `OPEN`, `RESTORATION_REST` and `CLOSED`, driven by an explicit target `status` and an optional `note`. `OPEN → RESTORATION_REST` and `RESTORATION_REST → OPEN` are ordinary seasonal rest, with no downstream effect. `OPEN → CLOSED` is a withdrawal: every booking on that segment whose `delivery_status` is `CONFIRMED` reverts `CONFIRMED → REQUESTED` with `trail_segment_id` cleared and `resource_release_reason` set to `PERMIT_LAPSE`, and `visitors_this_week` is recomputed afterwards. `CLOSED → OPEN` restores availability without restoring those bookings. Any other transition is refused with `ERR_INVALID_TRAIL_STATE`, and a segment that is not `OPEN` cannot be attached to a booking or carry one through confirmation — that rejection is `ERR_TRAIL_NOT_OPEN`.

Three separate ceilings protect a segment, and they are checked independently.

The first is instantaneous crowding. Each booking occupies its segment over a **half-open** window that begins at the booking's `start_utc` and ends at its `end_utc` plus the segment's `recovery_buffer_minutes` — the window includes its start instant and excludes its end instant, and the buffer is appended only at the trailing edge. A booking contributes `student_count` plus `chaperone_count` persons over that window. Only bookings whose `delivery_status` is `CONFIRMED`, `RAIN_DIVERTED` or `DELIVERED` occupy anything; bookings in `REQUESTED`, `CANCELLED` or `NO_SHOW` occupy nothing. To evaluate a candidate, the server collects every other booking on the same segment in one of those three occupying states whose window intersects the candidate's window, adds the candidate's own persons to the sum of those overlappers' persons, and refuses confirmation with `ERR_TRAIL_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` when that aggregate exceeds `capacity_persons`. Note the two consequences of half-open windows: a group starting exactly at the instant another group's window ends — that is, at the earlier booking's `end_utc` plus `recovery_buffer_minutes` — does **not** overlap it, and the check aggregates across all overlappers rather than rejecting on the first one it finds.

The second is the weekly resource-protection budget. `visitors_this_week` is the sum of `student_count` plus `chaperone_count` over the bookings on this segment whose `delivery_status` is `CONFIRMED`, `RAIN_DIVERTED` or `DELIVERED` and whose `start_utc` falls inside the ISO program week containing the logical clock. Confirmation is refused with `ERR_TRAIL_WEEKLY_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` when that running total plus the candidate's own persons would exceed `weekly_visitor_budget_persons`.

The third is a per-booking habitat ceiling. On a segment whose `habitat_sensitivity` is `HIGH`, no single booking may route more than 25 persons — counting students and chaperones together — across it. Exceeding that is `ERR_HABITAT_SENSITIVITY_LIMIT`, and unlike the other two trail ceilings this one is enforced both when the segment is attached to a booking and again at confirmation.

### 3.6. Booking a Visit: Request, Resource Assignment and Confirmation

A booking is opened either by a `BookingCoordinator` at the desk or by a `TeacherContact` acting for their own school. The request names the `school_id` visiting or hosting, the `offering_id` being booked, the `start_utc` and `end_utc` bounding the scheduled programme, a `student_count` between 1 and 500 inclusive, and a `chaperone_count` between 0 and 200 inclusive. The interval must be properly ordered, with `end_utc` strictly after `start_utc`, or the create fails with `ERR_INVALID_TIME_RANGE`. The programme must already be `PUBLISHED` — a draft or retired offering cannot be booked, and the rejection is `ERR_OFFERING_NOT_PUBLISHED` — the school must not be `SUSPENDED`, the whole party of `student_count` plus `chaperone_count` must fit inside the offering's `max_group_size` or the create is refused with `ERR_GROUP_SIZE_EXCEEDED`, and `start_utc` must lie strictly after the logical clock or the create is refused with `ERR_BOOKING_START_IN_PAST`.

A newly created booking opens at `delivery_status` of `REQUESTED` and `funding_status` of `UNFUNDED`, with `trail_segment_id`, `lead_educator_id`, `animal_id` and `rain_diverted_to_offering_id` all unset and `resource_release_reason` null. Two derived numbers are computed immediately. `required_chaperones` is the `student_count` divided by the offering's `min_chaperone_ratio_students_per_adult`, rounded **up** to the next whole adult — a class of 61 students on a program written for one adult per 15 students needs 5 chaperones, not 4. And `provisional_gross_fee_cents` is computed by the provisional variant of the fee assessment described in §3.8, with `net_due_cents` initialised to that provisional figure since no aid has been awarded yet.

Attaching resources is a separate step, and it is the operation guarded by optimistic concurrency. Alongside the mandatory `expected_booking_version` described in §2.8, the coordinator may supply any combination of `lead_educator_id`, `trail_segment_id` and `animal_id`; only the fields actually supplied are updated — a field sent as `null` counts as not supplied, an empty string is `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, and there is no way to detach an already-attached resource through this operation — and every check below is evaluated against the merged set the booking would end up holding: the newly supplied reference where one was given, the reference already sitting on the booking otherwise. A booking may be reassigned repeatedly while it remains `REQUESTED`. Every successful assignment increments the booking's `version` by 1 and recomputes the named educator's `delivery_load_minutes_this_week`. It is also the moment the system performs the lazy training-expiry sweep described in §3.3, re-deriving the educator's `curriculum_certified_grade_bands` and `animal_handling_tier` before any credential check is evaluated against them.

Assignment then runs a battery of checks in the order fixed by §2.9. The permit behind a named animal must be `ACTIVE` (`ERR_PERMIT_NOT_ACTIVE`); the animal's own care status must be `AVAILABLE` (`ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_AVAILABLE`); a named trail segment must be `OPEN` (`ERR_TRAIL_NOT_OPEN`); the educator must be `CLEARED` (`ERR_EDUCATOR_NOT_CLEARED`); the educator's `curriculum_certified_grade_bands` must contain the booked offering's `grade_band` (`ERR_EDUCATOR_NOT_CERTIFIED_FOR_GRADE_BAND`); when both an educator and an animal are attached, the educator's `animal_handling_tier` must be at least the animal's `handling_tier_required`, where `NONE` ranks below `TIER_1` which ranks below `TIER_2` (`ERR_HANDLING_TIER_INSUFFICIENT`); and a `HIGH`-sensitivity segment must not be handed a party larger than 25 persons (`ERR_HABITAT_SENSITIVITY_LIMIT`).

Three checks belong to assignment alone. A seasonal educator may not be attached to a booking that would run past the end of their season: when `employment_type` is `SEASONAL_EDUCATOR`, the booking's `end_utc` must fall at or before that educator's `season_end_date`, or the assignment is rejected with `ERR_EDUCATOR_SEASON_ENDED` — and a seasonal educator carrying no `season_end_date` at all fails this check unconditionally. Educator double-booking is prevented over a **closed, symmetrically buffered** interval: each booking blocks its educator from the booking's `start_utc` minus a travel buffer through its `end_utc` plus the same buffer, inclusive at both ends, where the buffer is 45 minutes for an `ONSITE_FIELD_TRIP` or `VIRTUAL` programme and 60 minutes for an `OUTREACH_AT_SCHOOL` programme because the educator has to drive there and back. A conflict exists when any other booking — a different booking led by the same educator, in `REQUESTED`, `CONFIRMED`, `RAIN_DIVERTED` or `DELIVERED` — has a blocked interval that intersects the candidate's, and because these intervals are closed, two intervals that merely touch at an endpoint **do** collide. The rejection is `ERR_EDUCATOR_TIME_CONFLICT`. It is worth contrasting this with the trail rule of §3.5: the educator interval is closed and buffered on both sides so touching endpoints collide, while the trail interval is half-open and buffered only at the trailing edge so touching endpoints do not, and the trail additionally aggregates persons across every overlapper rather than rejecting on the first one. Finally, an animal may not be double-booked either: the candidate's `start_utc`-to-`end_utc` window, treated as half-open, must not intersect the same window of another booking referencing the same ambassador whose `delivery_status` is `CONFIRMED`, `RAIN_DIVERTED` or `DELIVERED`, or the assignment is refused with `ERR_ANIMAL_TIME_CONFLICT`. Weekly educator load, as described in §3.3, is checked here too.

Confirmation is what turns a request into a reservation, and it takes no input beyond the booking identity. It advances `REQUESTED → CONFIRMED` and requires the booking to be sitting at `REQUESTED`; anything else answers `ERR_INVALID_BOOKING_STATE`. Confirmation re-runs the offering-published, school-not-suspended and group-size checks, because any of the three may have changed since the booking was created. It then insists that the resources the programme actually needs are present: `lead_educator_id` must be set on every booking, `trail_segment_id` must be set when the offering's `requires_trail_segment` is true, and `animal_id` must be set when `requires_live_animal` is true — a gap answers `ERR_REQUIRED_RESOURCE_MISSING`. Every credential and status check from the assignment battery is re-evaluated at confirmation as well, so a permit that lapsed in the interim blocks the confirmation rather than being discovered on the day.

Confirmation adds four checks of its own. Chaperone coverage must be met — `chaperone_count` at least `required_chaperones`, or `ERR_CHAPERONE_RATIO_UNMET`. The animal welfare exposure gate of §3.4 must pass. The trail's instantaneous capacity and its weekly visitor budget must both accommodate the party, per §3.5. And when the offering's `public_contact_required` is true, the animal's `effective_contact_rule`, read as it stands before this confirmation's own exposure joins the projection, must be exactly `SUPERVISED_TOUCH`; a `VIEW_ONLY` or `NO_PUBLIC_CONTACT` effective rule — including one produced by a `RESTRICTED` posture downgrading an otherwise touchable animal — is rejected with `ERR_PUBLIC_CONTACT_NOT_PERMITTED`.

On success, confirmation commits capacity in three places at once: the booking moves to `CONFIRMED`, the attached trail segment's `visitors_this_week` increases by `student_count` plus `chaperone_count`, the attached animal's `projected_exposure_minutes_7d` increases by the offering's `duration_minutes`, and that animal's `welfare_posture` and `effective_contact_rule` are recomputed from the new projection — which means confirming one booking can push an ambassador from `OPEN` into `RESTRICTED` and thereby block the next booking that needs supervised touch. Coordinators can find work by filtering the booking list on `school_id`, `offering_id`, `trail_segment_id`, `lead_educator_id`, `animal_id`, `rain_diverted_to_offering_id`, `delivery_status` and `funding_status`.

### 3.7. Rain Plans, Delivery, No-Shows and Cancellations

New England weather is why the rain plan exists. A confirmed booking may be diverted to an indoor alternate, which advances `CONFIRMED → RAIN_DIVERTED`. The coordinator may name the substitute explicitly through `alternate_offering_id` and may record a free-text `reason`. When named explicitly, the substitute must satisfy the eligibility filter below or the request is rejected with `ERR_RAIN_ALTERNATE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`.

When `alternate_offering_id` is omitted, the server picks the substitute itself. The candidate pool is every program offering that declares itself a rain alternate for the booked programme through its `rain_alternate_for_offering_id`, whose `status` is `PUBLISHED`, whose `grade_band` equals the originally booked offering's `grade_band`, whose `requires_trail_segment` is false — a rain plan that still needs the trail is no rain plan — and whose `max_group_size` is at least the booking's `student_count` plus `chaperone_count`. The pool is ranked by `standard_alignment_count` descending, so the best-aligned substitute wins; ties on alignment count are broken by `base_fee_cents_per_student` ascending, so the cheaper substitute wins; and ties on both are broken by `created_at` ascending, so the longest-standing offering wins; a residual tie on `created_at` — routine, since offerings created under one logical-clock reading share it — is broken by creation sequence, so the offering written first still wins. If the pool is empty the diversion fails with `ERR_NO_RAIN_ALTERNATE`, the booking stays at `CONFIRMED`, and nothing at all is mutated.

A successful diversion records the chosen substitute on `rain_diverted_to_offering_id`, releases the trail — clearing `trail_segment_id` and decreasing that segment's `visitors_this_week` by `student_count` plus `chaperone_count` — and recomputes `provisional_gross_fee_cents` with the rain credit applied and `net_due_cents` from it. Note what diversion does **not** release: the animal ambassador stays attached and its projected exposure stays committed, because the ambassador comes indoors with the class.

Delivery closes the operational dimension. Either a `BookingCoordinator` or the assigned `Naturalist` records `actual_student_count`, `actual_chaperone_count`, and optionally `actual_contact_minutes` for the animal contact time that actually occurred; all three are integers of at least 0 carrying no upper bound, and an omitted `actual_contact_minutes` is stored as 0. Delivery is reachable from two states, and the branches rejoin: `CONFIRMED → DELIVERED` and `RAIN_DIVERTED → DELIVERED` are both valid, and both require the chaperone ratio to hold against the numbers that actually turned up — `actual_chaperone_count` must be at least `actual_student_count` divided by the offering's `min_chaperone_ratio_students_per_adult`, rounded up to the next whole adult, or the delivery is rejected with `ERR_CHAPERONE_RATIO_UNMET`. Both branches then compute `final_gross_fee_cents` through the final variant of the fee assessment, recompute `net_due_cents` from it, advance `funding_status` to `INVOICED` from whatever it held — `AID_PENDING` included, in which case the still-`SUBMITTED` application is left undecided and its hold stays on the pool — transition every aid application of this booking whose status is `APPROVED` or `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` to `DISBURSED`, and recompute the school's `outstanding_balance_cents`. They differ in exactly one respect, which the fee assessment handles automatically: a booking that carries a `rain_diverted_to_offering_id` has the rain credit applied to its final gross fee, and a booking delivered straight from `CONFIRMED` does not. On the animal side, delivery moves exposure from the projected book to the realized one: `realized_exposure_minutes_7d` increases by `actual_contact_minutes` and `projected_exposure_minutes_7d` decreases by the offering's `duration_minutes` when an animal was attached.

When the bus never arrives, the coordinator records a no-show with an optional `note`. This is reachable from `REQUESTED` and from `CONFIRMED` alike, and both branches set `delivery_status` to `NO_SHOW`, set `final_gross_fee_cents` and `net_due_cents` to the flat no-show fee of 7,500 cents, set `funding_status` to `WRITTEN_OFF`, set `resource_release_reason` to `NO_SHOW`, increment the school's `no_show_strikes` and recompute `SchoolPartner.status`. The branch out of `CONFIRMED` additionally has capacity to give back, so it decreases `TrailSegment.visitors_this_week` by `student_count` plus `chaperone_count` when a trail segment was attached and decreases `AnimalAmbassador.projected_exposure_minutes_7d` by the offering's `duration_minutes` when an animal was attached. If the recomputed standing suspends the school, the cascade of §2.10 continues into that school's other requested bookings. A no-show also handles awarded aid: from `AID_APPROVED` the funding dimension moves to `WRITTEN_OFF` and every `APPROVED` or `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` application on the booking is `REVOKED` with the fund's `committed_cents` and `available_cents` recomputed; from `UNFUNDED`, and equally from `AID_PENDING` with its `SUBMITTED` application left undecided, it simply moves to `WRITTEN_OFF` with `net_due_cents` set to the no-show fee.

Cancellation is available to the `BookingCoordinator` and to the school's own `TeacherContact`, with an optional `reason`, and like the no-show it has two source states treated with equal care. From `REQUESTED`, cancellation sets `delivery_status` to `CANCELLED` with `resource_release_reason` of `CANCELLED_BY_SCHOOL` — or `SCHOOL_SUSPENSION` when the cancellation was driven by the suspension cascade rather than by a request — and then revokes money: every aid application of this booking whose status is `APPROVED` or `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` transitions to `REVOKED`, the fund's `committed_cents` and `available_cents` are recomputed, and the booking's `aid_amount_cents` and `net_due_cents` fall to 0; an application still sitting at `SUBMITTED` is left untouched and the booking's `funding_status` stays at `AID_PENDING`. From `CONFIRMED`, cancellation does all of that and additionally releases the committed capacity: `visitors_this_week` decreases by `student_count` plus `chaperone_count` when a trail segment was attached and `projected_exposure_minutes_7d` decreases by the offering's `duration_minutes` when an animal was attached. `transport_subsidy_cents` likewise falls to 0. Cancellation is not available once a booking has been delivered, has been marked a no-show, or has already been cancelled — those attempts answer `ERR_INVALID_BOOKING_STATE`.

### 3.8. Fee Assessment, Scholarship Funds and the Award Waterfall

**Fee assessment** runs in six pinned steps, and it exists in two variants that differ only in which headcount they read. The **provisional** variant reads the planned `student_count` and is evaluated when a booking is created, when it is diverted to a rain alternate, and when an outreach equipment manifest is loaded; its result is stored in `provisional_gross_fee_cents`. The **final** variant reads the `actual_student_count` recorded on site and is evaluated at delivery; its result is stored in `final_gross_fee_cents`. Because the two headcounts can select different discount bands, the final figure is not simply a rescaling of the provisional one.

Step one establishes the billable headcount as the larger of the applicable headcount and a floor of ten billable students, so a class of six is priced as ten. Step two selects the volume discount from that billable count: no discount at all for 1 through 14 students, 5 percent for 15 through 29, 10 percent for 30 through 59, and 15 percent for 60 or more. Step three applies the discount **per student and rounds before multiplying**: the offering's `base_fee_cents_per_student` is multiplied by one minus the discount fraction, that per-student amount is rounded half up to a whole cent, and only then is it multiplied by the billable headcount to give the programme fee. The alternative order — multiplying first and rounding the product — is explicitly not used, and the two orders differ by cents on realistic inputs.

Step four adds travel, and only for outreach. When the booked offering's `delivery_mode` is `OUTREACH_AT_SCHOOL`, the travel fee is a flat base of 4,000 cents plus 175 cents for every mile of the school's one-way `distance_miles`, with the per-mile component multiplied by a vehicle factor, the whole sum rounded half up to a whole cent. The vehicle factor is 2.0 when an equipment manifest of this booking whose `status` is `LOADED`, `RETURNED` or `SHORTAGE_REPORTED` carries a `vehicle_class` of `BOX_TRUCK`, and 1.0 otherwise; manifests still sitting in `DRAFT` are excluded from that test, which is why loading a manifest re-prices the booking. For any other `delivery_mode` the travel fee is zero. Step five adds the programme fee and the travel fee to give the gross. Step six applies the rain credit: when the booking carries a non-null `rain_diverted_to_offering_id`, the gross is multiplied by one minus a 20 percent credit and rounded half up to a whole cent. The result of step six is what gets stored.

**Scholarship pools** are opened by a `GrantsOfficer` with a `funder_name`, a `fiscal_year` between 2020 and 2100 inclusive, a `total_cents` pool size greater than 0 and at most 100000000, and an optional `transport_sublimit_pct` between 0 and 1 inclusive that defaults to 0.25 and caps the share of the pool spendable on transportation. A new pool opens at `OPEN` with `pending_cents`, `committed_cents`, `disbursed_cents` and `transport_awarded_cents` all at 0 and `available_cents` equal to `total_cents`.

Those five counters are derived, not stored independently, and each is reconstructible from the pool's applications. `pending_cents` is the sum of `requested_scholarship_cents` plus `requested_transport_cents` over the pool's applications whose status is `SUBMITTED`. `committed_cents` is the sum of `awarded_scholarship_cents` plus `awarded_transport_cents` over those whose status is `APPROVED` or `PARTIALLY_APPROVED`. `disbursed_cents` is the same sum over those whose status is `DISBURSED`. `transport_awarded_cents` is the sum of `awarded_transport_cents` alone over those whose status is `APPROVED`, `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` or `DISBURSED`. And `available_cents` is `total_cents` less `pending_cents` less `committed_cents` less `disbursed_cents`. A pool's `status` moves `OPEN → FROZEN` and `FROZEN → OPEN` freely, and `OPEN → CLOSED` only when `pending_cents` is exactly 0 — an undecided application blocks closure with `ERR_FUND_HAS_PENDING_APPLICATIONS`. `CLOSED` is terminal, and any other status change answers `ERR_INVALID_FUND_STATE`.

**Applying for aid** is done by the school's `TeacherContact` or by a `BookingCoordinator` on their behalf. The application names the `booking_id` it would fund, the `fund_id` it draws on, a `requested_scholarship_cents` between 0 and 10000000 inclusive, a `requested_transport_cents` in the same range, and the `expected_fund_version` of §2.8. It is accepted only while the booking's `funding_status` is `UNFUNDED` — tested first and answered with `ERR_INVALID_FUNDING_STATE` — and its `delivery_status` is `REQUESTED` or `CONFIRMED`, tested second and answered with `ERR_INVALID_BOOKING_STATE`, only against a pool whose status is `OPEN` (`ERR_FUND_NOT_OPEN`), only for a school whose `equity_tier` is not `NONE` (`ERR_NOT_AID_ELIGIBLE`), and only when the booking has no other application sitting in `SUBMITTED`, `APPROVED`, `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` or `DISBURSED` (`ERR_DUPLICATE_AID_APPLICATION`). Two fiscal gates apply at creation: the two requested amounts together must not exceed the pool's `available_cents` (`ERR_FUND_INSUFFICIENT`), and a non-zero transport request requires a strictly positive remaining transport sublimit, computed as `total_cents` multiplied by `transport_sublimit_pct` and rounded half up to whole cents, less `transport_awarded_cents` (`ERR_TRANSPORT_SUBLIMIT_EXCEEDED`). A successful create opens the application at `SUBMITTED`, stamps `submitted_by_user_id`, advances the booking's `funding_status` from `UNFUNDED` to `AID_PENDING`, recomputes the pool's `pending_cents` and `available_cents`, and increments `ScholarshipFund.version` by 1.

**Deciding an application** is the `GrantsOfficer`'s exclusive act, taking a `decision` of `APPROVE` or `DENY` plus an optional `reviewer_note` that is persisted on the application as its `decision_note`, with `reviewer_user_id` stamped from `X-User-ID`. The application must be sitting at `SUBMITTED` — anything else is `ERR_INVALID_AID_STATE` — and the pool must be `OPEN`. A `DENY` transitions `SUBMITTED → DENIED`, leaves `awarded_scholarship_cents` and `awarded_transport_cents` at 0, returns the booking's `funding_status` from `AID_PENDING` to `UNFUNDED`, and recomputes the pool's `pending_cents` and `available_cents` so the hold is released. `DENIED` is terminal.

An `APPROVE` runs the award waterfall, which has nine pinned steps and two branch outcomes. Step one fixes the fee basis: the booking's `final_gross_fee_cents` when its `delivery_status` is already `DELIVERED`, and its `provisional_gross_fee_cents` otherwise. Step two reads the tier cap from the school's `equity_tier`: 100 percent of the fee basis for `TIER_1`, 70 percent for `TIER_2`, 40 percent for `TIER_3`, and zero for `NONE`. Step three computes the floor the school always pays out of pocket, at 150 cents per planned student — the booking's `student_count` multiplied by 150. Step four takes the scholarship ceiling as the smallest of three quantities: the `requested_scholarship_cents`, the fee basis multiplied by the tier cap fraction and rounded half up to whole cents, and the fee basis less the contribution floor with a lower bound of zero.

Step five computes the transport ceiling. The mileage entitlement is 260 cents per mile applied to **round-trip** distance — the school's `distance_miles` multiplied by two, then by 260, rounded half up to whole cents. The remaining sublimit is the pool's `total_cents` multiplied by its `transport_sublimit_pct`, rounded half up to whole cents, less `transport_awarded_cents`, with a lower bound of zero. The transport ceiling is the smallest of the `requested_transport_cents`, the mileage entitlement and the remaining sublimit. Step six establishes the waterfall budget as the pool's current `available_cents` plus this application's own `requested_scholarship_cents` plus its own `requested_transport_cents` — the application's own `SUBMITTED` hold is released back into the budget before it is spent, so an application never competes against itself.

Steps seven and eight fill two buckets in a fixed order. The scholarship bucket fills first: `awarded_scholarship_cents` is the smaller of the scholarship ceiling and the budget, and that award is then subtracted from the budget. The transport bucket takes whatever remains: `awarded_transport_cents` is the smaller of the transport ceiling and the reduced budget. Step nine chooses the outcome state: the application transitions `SUBMITTED → APPROVED` when `awarded_scholarship_cents` equals `requested_scholarship_cents` **and** `awarded_transport_cents` equals `requested_transport_cents`, and `SUBMITTED → PARTIALLY_APPROVED` when the total awarded is less than the total requested but still greater than zero. An award total of exactly zero is not stored at all — it is refused with `ERR_FUND_INSUFFICIENT` under the fiscal gate of §2.9. Rounding half up to whole cents is applied only where step four, step five and the sublimit ceiling explicitly call for it.

Either approval outcome advances the booking's `funding_status` from `AID_PENDING` to `AID_APPROVED` and recomputes the pool's `pending_cents`, `committed_cents`, `transport_awarded_cents` and `available_cents`. The booking's own money fields are recomputed too, and the split between them matters: `aid_amount_cents` is the sum of `awarded_scholarship_cents` over this booking's applications whose status is `APPROVED`, `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` or `DISBURSED`, while `transport_subsidy_cents` is the corresponding sum of `awarded_transport_cents`. Only the scholarship credit reduces what the school owes — the transportation subsidy is paid to the carrier and never touches the school's bill. Accordingly, `net_due_cents` is the `final_gross_fee_cents` less `aid_amount_cents` once the booking has been delivered and the `provisional_gross_fee_cents` less `aid_amount_cents` before that, in either case with a lower bound of zero.

Awarded aid moves on delivery and reverses on withdrawal, symmetrically. At delivery, every application on the booking in `APPROVED` or `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` transitions to `DISBURSED`, with the pool's `committed_cents` decreased and its `disbursed_cents` increased by the same awarded totals; `DISBURSED` is terminal. On a cancellation or a no-show, those same applications transition to `REVOKED` instead: `awarded_scholarship_cents` and `awarded_transport_cents` are reset to 0, the pool's `committed_cents`, `transport_awarded_cents` and `available_cents` are recomputed, and the booking's `aid_amount_cents` and `net_due_cents` are recomputed accordingly. Revocation is a true compensation — it reverses every ledger effect the approval had, leaving no drift — and `REVOKED` is terminal. A booking whose funding sat at `AID_APPROVED` when the cancellation revoked its award returns to `UNFUNDED` with `aid_amount_cents` at 0.

**Invoicing and settlement** close the money dimension. Delivery advances funding to `INVOICED` from either `UNFUNDED` — in which case `net_due_cents` is simply the `final_gross_fee_cents` — or from `AID_APPROVED`, in which case the awarded applications disburse as described and `net_due_cents` reflects the aid credit; the school's `outstanding_balance_cents` is recomputed either way. A `BookingCoordinator` then records payments by supplying an `amount_cents`, an integer of at least 0 with no ceiling of its own, which increases the booking's `paid_cents`. A payment may only be recorded against a booking whose `funding_status` is `INVOICED` — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_FUNDING_STATE` — and may not exceed the outstanding balance, that is `net_due_cents` less `paid_cents`, or it is refused with `ERR_PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_BALANCE`. Once the accumulated `paid_cents` reaches or exceeds `net_due_cents`, funding advances `INVOICED → SETTLED`, and the school's `outstanding_balance_cents` is recomputed. `SETTLED` is terminal, as is the `WRITTEN_OFF` state a no-show produces. Aid application lists can be filtered by `booking_id`, by `fund_id` and by `status`.

### 3.9. Outreach Equipment, Teacher Evaluation and Funder Outcome Reporting

Outreach programmes travel, so their kit is manifested. A `BookingCoordinator` opens a manifest against any booking, whatever the booked offering's `delivery_mode` and whatever the booking's `delivery_status`, with the `kit_codes` array of kits being loaded — which may be empty — and a `total_weight_kg` greater than 0 and at most 4000; the manifest records its own `booking_id` and opens at `DRAFT` with an empty `shortage_kit_codes` array. Manifests advance through an operation carrying an `action`. `LOAD` moves `DRAFT → LOADED` and is permitted only while the parent booking's `delivery_status` is `CONFIRMED`; any other parent state answers `ERR_INVALID_BOOKING_STATE`, checked after the manifest's own state. Loading is also the moment `vehicle_class` is decided: a manifest heavier than 250 kilograms requires a `BOX_TRUCK`, and one at or below that threshold travels in a `VAN` — the comparison is strictly greater-than, so a manifest weighing exactly 250 kilograms goes in a van. Because a box truck doubles the per-mile component of the outreach travel fee, loading recomputes the booking's `provisional_gross_fee_cents`.

`RETURN` closes the manifest, and which terminal state it reaches depends on what came back. When `shortage_kit_codes` is omitted or empty, the manifest moves `LOADED → RETURNED`. When `shortage_kit_codes` is non-empty, every code in it must also appear in the manifest's `kit_codes` — a code that was never loaded cannot come back missing, and the mismatch is rejected with `ERR_SHORTAGE_CODE_UNKNOWN` — and the manifest moves `LOADED → SHORTAGE_REPORTED` with the reported codes persisted on `shortage_kit_codes`. Both `RETURNED` and `SHORTAGE_REPORTED` are terminal, and an advance action from a state that does not permit it answers `ERR_INVALID_MANIFEST_STATE`. Manifest lists can be filtered by `status`.

Teacher feedback is the raw material of funder reporting. A `TeacherContact` opens an evaluation against their own booking — opening tests only school scope and the duplicate rule, never the booking's `delivery_status`, so a report may be drafted before the visit has been delivered and only submission insists on delivery — supplying three teacher ratings on a 1-to-5 scale — `teacher_score_standards_alignment`, `teacher_score_logistics` and `teacher_score_engagement` — plus a `student_pre_score_avg` and a `student_post_score_avg`, each a class average between 0 and 20 inclusive, and an optional free-text `teacher_comment`. The report records its `booking_id`, opens at `DRAFT` with `learning_gain_index` at 0, and only one evaluation may exist per booking: a booking already carrying a report in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `RETURNED` or `ACCEPTED` refuses a second with `ERR_DUPLICATE_EVALUATION`.

Submission advances `DRAFT → SUBMITTED` and requires the parent booking's `delivery_status` to be `DELIVERED`, an undelivered parent answering `ERR_INVALID_BOOKING_STATE` once the report's own state has passed; it stamps `submitted_by_user_id` from `X-User-ID` and computes `learning_gain_index`. The `EducationDirector` then decides the report with a `decision` of `ACCEPT` or `RETURN` plus an optional `reviewer_note`, stamping `reviewed_by_user_id`. `RETURN` moves `SUBMITTED → RETURNED`, after which the teacher may resubmit — `RETURNED → SUBMITTED` recomputes `learning_gain_index` from scratch, so the cycle `SUBMITTED → RETURNED → SUBMITTED` can legitimately produce a different index each time as the offering's standards coverage changes underneath it. `ACCEPT` moves `SUBMITTED → ACCEPTED`, requires an accepting `X-User-ID` different from `submitted_by_user_id` per §2.11, and is terminal; only an `ACCEPTED` report becomes eligible for the funder outcome rollup. A submit or review action from a state that does not permit it answers `ERR_INVALID_EVALUATION_STATE`, and evaluation lists can be filtered by `status`.

The **learning gain index** is computed in five pinned steps and lands as a number between 0.00 and 1.00 with two decimal places. Step one measures normalised student gain against the headroom left below the maximum assessment score of 20: when 20 less the `student_pre_score_avg` is zero or negative there is no headroom and the normalised gain is 0; otherwise the normalised gain is the `student_post_score_avg` less the `student_pre_score_avg`, divided by 20 less the `student_pre_score_avg`. That figure is then clamped into the closed range 0 through 1, so a class that scored worse afterwards contributes 0 rather than a negative number. Step two builds the teacher composite by weighting the three ratings — half the weight on `teacher_score_standards_alignment`, three tenths on `teacher_score_engagement` and two tenths on `teacher_score_logistics`, weights that sum to one — and dividing the weighted sum by 5 to normalise the 1-to-5 scale into a 0-to-1 fraction. Step three sets a delivery fidelity factor: 1.00 when the booking's `delivery_status` is `DELIVERED` and its `rain_diverted_to_offering_id` is null, 0.80 when the booking is `DELIVERED` but carries a rain alternate, and 0.00 in every other case — an undelivered booking scores zero no matter how good the ratings are. Step four applies a thin-alignment penalty keyed on the offering's standards coverage: the factor is 0.90 when the booked offering's `standard_alignment_count` is below 3 as it stands at submit time, and 1.00 otherwise. Step five combines everything: six tenths of the normalised gain plus four tenths of the teacher composite, that sum multiplied by the fidelity factor and then by the alignment factor, the product clamped into the closed range 0 through 1, and finally rounded half up to two decimal places — the single rounding in the whole computation.

The **funder outcome rollup** is what a `GrantsOfficer` or the `EducationDirector` reads at the end of a grant period. It reports the pool's `fund_id`, its `total_cents`, `committed_cents`, `disbursed_cents` and `available_cents` as they currently stand, plus four derived figures. `awarded_application_count` is the number of applications on this pool whose status is `APPROVED`, `PARTIALLY_APPROVED` or `DISBURSED`. The remaining three are computed over a narrower set: the evaluation reports whose status is `ACCEPTED`, whose booking has `delivery_status` of `DELIVERED`, and whose booking carries at least one aid application on this pool with status `DISBURSED`. Reports in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `RETURNED` are excluded, and a booking whose aid was approved but not yet delivered contributes to `awarded_application_count` while contributing nothing to the outcome figures. `students_served_count` is the sum of `actual_student_count` over the bookings of that narrower set. `weighted_learning_gain_index` is each qualifying report's `learning_gain_index` multiplied by its booking's `actual_student_count`, those products summed, divided by `students_served_count`, rounded half up to three decimal places — and reported as `0.000` when `students_served_count` is zero. `tier_1_student_share_pct` is the sum of `actual_student_count` over those qualifying bookings whose school's `equity_tier` is `TIER_1`, divided by `students_served_count`, rounded half up to three decimal places — likewise `0.000` when the denominator is zero.

Every other operation, including every state transition, every status change, every review decision, every payment and every read, answers `200 OK`.
