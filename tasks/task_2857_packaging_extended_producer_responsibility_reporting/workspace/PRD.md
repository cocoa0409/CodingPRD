# Packaging EPR Reporting API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Consumer brand owners that place packaged goods on the market are now obligated, state by state, to report the packaging they put into the waste stream and to pay a fee for it. The obligation is not uniform: each state defines the obligated party differently, sets its own small-producer thresholds, decides whether eco-modulation may raise a fee as well as lower it, and even pins the order in which money is rounded. A brand that sells in six states therefore faces six different arithmetic answers for the same shipment of the same product. Compliance teams today reconcile this in spreadsheets, and the reconciliation is wrong often enough that third-party verifiers routinely open correction cycles.

This document specifies the Packaging Extended Producer Responsibility Reporting service, an HTTP API mounted under `/epr` that carries a packaging obligation end to end: analysing which legal entity is the obligated party, collecting packaging material data at stock-keeping-unit granularity, registering producers into state programs, brokering membership in producer responsibility organisations and their published fee schedules, pricing reports through eco-modulated tiered rates, routing large reports through independent verification, and settling the result as an invoice. The service is the system of record for every number a regulator, a producer responsibility organisation and an independent verifier would otherwise dispute.

Two design commitments run through the whole specification and are worth stating before any detail. First, **every fee number is reproducible**: a fee is a pure function of the report's line data, the state program's policy triple, and the fee schedule that the producer's membership pinned at approval time — never of whatever schedule happens to be effective when someone presses the button. Second, **every aggregate is a conserved quantity**: year-to-date tonnage and assessed fee balances are recomputed from their defining sets rather than adjusted by deltas, so a forward transition paired with its compensating reversal restores the previous value exactly, with no drift.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the capacity in which that principal acts. Exactly four roles are recognised, spelled precisely as follows: `ProgramRegulator`, `ProducerComplianceOfficer`, `ProAdministrator`, and `IndependentVerifier`. If either header is absent, or if `X-User-Role` carries any value other than these four, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). Authentication is resolved before anything else about the request is inspected. Once the caller is authenticated, the service checks that the acting role is one of the roles authorised for the requested endpoint as listed in §4; a role acting outside that list is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403).

A `ProgramRegulator` is a state-agency operator. It owns state programs and their reporting periods, onboards producers and producer responsibility organisations, determines the obligated party, decides, suspends and reinstates registrations, assigns independent verifiers, and closes reports. When `X-User-Role` is `ProgramRegulator`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server may record it — for example as `assigned_by_user_id` on a verification engagement, which is written from the header of the regulator that created the engagement — but it never compares it to any entity's `id`.

An `IndependentVerifier` is a third-party assurance provider that performs fieldwork on an assigned engagement and issues the opinion that either verifies a report or opens its correction window; it never files or prices a report. When `X-User-Role` is `IndependentVerifier`, `X-User-ID` is likewise an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it as `verifier_user_id` on the engagement (supplied in the assignment request by the regulator) and as the comparison basis for the engagement-ownership rule, but it never resolves it to an entity row.

A `ProducerComplianceOfficer` acts for exactly one obligated producer: it maintains packaging data on stock keeping units, opens and submits state registrations, applies for membership in a producer responsibility organisation, files and corrects packaging reports, and models packaging redesigns. When `X-User-Role` is `ProducerComplianceOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Producer`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Producer` compare the resource's producer-reference field to `X-User-ID`. Because most producer-scoped resources reach the producer indirectly — a report line reaches it through its report, that report's registration and that registration's `producer_id` — the comparison always follows the reference chain to the owning producer rather than looking for a producer field on the immediate target.

A `ProAdministrator` acts for exactly one producer responsibility organisation: it authors and publishes fee schedules, decides membership applications, records membership fee payments, assesses report fees, and issues or voids invoices. When `X-User-Role` is `ProAdministrator`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Pro`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Pro` compare the resource's PRO-reference field to `X-User-ID`, again following the chain where the reference is indirect (an invoice reaches its PRO through the `membership_id` it names, and a fee assessment reaches its PRO through the registration's live membership). Any producer-scoped or PRO-scoped operation whose reachable owner differs from `X-User-ID` is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403). The parallel rule for verifiers — an engagement action performed by anyone other than the engagement's own `verifier_user_id` — is rejected with `ERR_NOT_ASSIGNED_VERIFIER` (HTTP 403).

Ownership is checked after authentication, role authorisation, request-shape validation and resource existence, and before any state or business rule. The full admission order is set out in §2.5.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.


### 2.3. Pagination

List endpoints are paginated through two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of items returned; when omitted it defaults to 10, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` is the number of items to skip and defaults to 0; negative offsets are not accepted. A `limit` below 1 or above 100, or a negative `offset`, is a request-shape failure and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400).

Unless a specific endpoint says otherwise, every list endpoint returns its items ordered by `created_at` ascending. List responses carry `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside the `meta` object of the envelope described in §2.2; `total_count` counts the rows matching the filters before the pagination window is applied, and `has_more` is true when rows remain beyond the current window.

Every list endpoint also accepts the filter parameters named in §3 alongside `limit` and `offset`. Filters are combined conjunctively, and an unknown enum value supplied to a filter is a request-shape failure rather than an empty result.

### 2.4. Common Resource Properties and the Logical Clock

All twelve persisted resource types — `Producer`, `Sku`, `StateProgram`, `ReportingPeriod`, `Pro`, `FeeSchedule`, `Registration`, `Membership`, `PackagingReport`, `ReportLine`, `VerificationEngagement` and `Invoice` — carry the same four system-managed properties:

- `id` (string): a server-minted UUID. It carries no type prefix and no embedded semantics; clients must treat it as opaque.
- `created_at` (string): an ISO 8601 timestamp in UTC with three fractional digits, for example `2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z`, recording when the row was first created.
- `updated_at` (string): an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, refreshed on every mutation of the row — including mutations that only rewrite a derived field such as a weight rollup, a posture or a fee subtotal.
- `version` (integer): a monotonically increasing counter that starts at 1 when the row is created and increases by exactly 1 on every mutation of that row. A cascade that touches five rows increments the `version` of all five.

Because a derived-field refresh is a mutation, a single request can legitimately advance the `version` of resources the client never named. §2.9 enumerates which cascades touch which rows.

Every deadline, window boundary and correction check in this specification is evaluated against a **logical UTC clock** held by the server, never against wall-clock time. Before any client sets it, the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and it never falls back to the host's real time. A `ProgramRegulator` may advance or rewind it by supplying a `current_utc` instant; doing so mutates no business row, increments no `version`, and — importantly — does not by itself recompute any compliance posture. A registration that has become delinquent purely by the passage of logical time therefore keeps its stored posture until the next operation that recomputes it.

### 2.5. Request Admission Order

Checks are applied in a fixed order, and the first failure is the response. Later checks never run, so a request that violates two rules always reports the earlier one. The order is:

1. Authentication — both headers present and `X-User-Role` one of the four declared roles, else `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. Role authorisation — the acting role appears in the endpoint's authorised list, else `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`.
3. Request shape — required body fields present, JSON types correct, enum values inside their declared ranges, integer quantities non-negative where the field requires it, `limit` within 1 to 100 and `offset` at least 0, ISO 8601 instants parseable, and monetary and weight fields expressed as integers or decimals inside their declared precision. Any failure is `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.
4. Path-parameter existence — resolved strictly left to right along the URL. The response names the first identifier in the path that resolves to nothing, using the type-specific code: `ERR_PRODUCER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SKU_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STATE_PROGRAM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REPORTING_PERIOD_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PRO_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REGISTRATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MEMBERSHIP_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REPORT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REPORT_LINE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ENGAGEMENT_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND`, each HTTP 404. A request for a reporting period under a state program that does not exist therefore reports `ERR_STATE_PROGRAM_NOT_FOUND`, not `ERR_REPORTING_PERIOD_NOT_FOUND`.
5. Body foreign-key existence — every identifier field in the body is resolved in the order the endpoint declares its input fields, using the same twelve type-specific 404 codes.
6. Ownership — the producer-scoped and PRO-scoped comparisons of §2.1, else `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`.
7. State — the target resource's own status permits the requested transition. Where a more specific state error exists it is used; otherwise the generic `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409) is returned.

Everything after step 7 — the specific state guards, the optimistic-concurrency checks, the business-safety, referential, constraint, temporal and fiscal rules — is ordered relative to itself in §2.10, and each individual rule is described in the §3 workflow it guards.

### 2.6. Weights, Money and Rounding Discipline

Two units of measure dominate the specification and each has a fixed precision. **Packaging weights in kilograms are carried at three decimal places**, and **component weights in grams at two decimal places**; in both cases the rounding method is round half up (a value exactly at the midpoint rounds away from zero). **Money is expressed in whole cents as integers** on every stored and returned field, with one deliberate exception: `ReportLine.line_fee_exact_cents` is carried at four decimal places so that the state-specific rounding order of §2.7 remains observable. Percentages used by eco-modulation are whole integers throughout. Revenue figures are whole US dollars.

Rounding is applied where this document says it is applied and nowhere else. In particular, per-stock-keeping-unit weight contributions are rounded to three decimals *before* being summed into a producer-level total, so the producer total is a sum of already-rounded values rather than a rounding of an exact sum. Within a fee assessment, by contrast, no intermediate rounding occurs between a component's eco-modulated exact amount and the line's exact total: the four-decimal figure absorbs the whole line.

### 2.7. Multi-State Variance: the Policy Triple

Each state program pins three policy choices that together make identical packaging data produce different compliance outcomes in different states. This triple is the single largest source of variance in the system and every fee number depends on all three.

`obligation_basis` selects which variant of the exemption analysis the state applies, and is either `ENTITY_LEVEL` or `GROUP_LEVEL`. `eco_mod_policy` is either `BONUS_MALUS`, in which eco-modulation both surcharges poorly recyclable packaging and discounts good packaging, or `DISCOUNT_ONLY`, in which every positive net percentage is clamped to zero so surcharges are waived while discounts survive. `fee_rounding_order` is either `PER_LINE_THEN_SUM` or `SUM_THEN_ROUND`, and pins where the rounding to whole cents happens when a report's lines are totalled.

The rounding order is genuinely observable rather than cosmetic. Consider a report with two lines whose exact amounts are 49.500 and 94.500 cents. Under `PER_LINE_THEN_SUM` each line is rounded first, giving 50 and 95, and the subtotal is 145 cents. Under `SUM_THEN_ROUND` the exact amounts are added to 144.000 and the subtotal is 144 cents. Because a line always stores its own rounded value regardless of the state's order, under `SUM_THEN_ROUND` the sum of the stored per-line values can exceed the report's stored subtotal by up to half a cent per line — that is, by up to half the report's line count. Invoices always bill the report's stored subtotal and never the sum of the line values; §3.8 describes the read-only breakdown that surfaces this reconciliation delta explicitly.

### 2.8. Version Pinning and Optimistic Concurrency

Two kinds of version handling coexist in this service and they must not be conflated.

**Version pinning** is a durability guarantee about pricing. When a producer responsibility organisation approves a membership application, the elected fee schedule's `version` at that instant is recorded on the membership as `fee_schedule_version_pinned`, and from then on every report priced under that membership is priced against the schedule the membership names — its rates, its brackets, its bands — regardless of any later mutation, supersession or republication elsewhere. Pricing does not re-check the pinned schedule's `status`, so a schedule that has since become `SUPERSEDED` still prices reports for the memberships that pinned it. What pricing does re-check is the schedule's *effective window* against the reporting period; see §3.8.

**Optimistic concurrency control** is a lost-update guard, and it applies to exactly two write operations, each keyed on a parent entity's `version`.

A fee-schedule publish — `POST /epr/fee-schedules/{id}/publish` — requires a non-optional `expected_pro_version` field in its body, carrying the client's last observed `version` of the owning `Pro`. If it does not equal the `Pro` row's current `version`, the publish is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PRO_VERSION` (HTTP 412). The guard exists because publication legitimately truncates the PRO's prior open-ended published schedule for the same state program, so a publish issued from a stale view of the PRO would silently supersede a schedule the client never saw.

A membership creation — `POST /epr/memberships` — requires a non-optional `expected_registration_version` field in its body, carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `Registration`. If it does not equal the registration's current `version`, the creation is rejected with `ERR_STALE_REGISTRATION_VERSION` (HTTP 412). The guard exists because a new membership legitimately terminates the registration's prior `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION` membership — a producer is allowed to switch PRO mid-year — so a create issued from a stale view of the registration would supersede coverage the client never observed.

No other endpoint takes a version field of any kind, and no other endpoint performs an optimistic-concurrency check. Clients read the current `Pro` version from a PRO fetch and the current registration version from a registration fetch.

### 2.9. Cascade Propagation and Conserved Aggregates

Compliance state is not stored independently on each row; several fields exist only as functions of other rows, and the service keeps them true at the end of every request. Consistency is immediate: a client that reads any affected resource after a mutating call sees the fully propagated result, never an intermediate one.

Four aggregates are maintained this way, and each is **recomputed from its defining set** rather than adjusted by a delta:

- `Producer.annual_packaging_weight_kg` is the producer-level tonnage rollup over its `ACTIVE` stock keeping units.
- `Sku.unit_packaging_weight_grams` is the per-unit weight rollup over the stock keeping unit's `ACTIVE` components.
- `Registration.reported_weight_kg_ytd` is the year-to-date tonnage rollup over the registration's reports whose submission dimension is inside the counted set.
- `Producer.assessed_fee_balance_cents` is the money rollup over the producer's invoices that are still `ISSUED`.

Recomputation-from-set rather than delta arithmetic is what makes the last two **conserved quantities**. Tonnage that enters the counted set on a submission and leaves it again when a verifier opens a correction restores the previous kilogram figure exactly; money that enters the balance when an invoice is issued and leaves it again when that invoice is voided restores the previous integer exactly. No rounding drift can accumulate across repeated issue-and-void or submit-and-correct cycles, and an implementation that adjusts these fields by deltas will diverge on the second cycle.

Cascades propagate through more than one hop, and each hop is a mutation in its own right — it refreshes `updated_at` and increments `version` on the row it touches. The three long cascades are the revenue-restatement cascade over a producer's registrations and their draft reports (§3.1), the packaging-revision cascade from a component set up through the producer's tonnage rollup and its determination-staleness flag (§3.2), and the six-hop correction cascade that a qualified or adverse verification opinion sets off across the report, its registration, its producer, its invoice and its membership (§3.7). Each is spelled out in full in the subsection named.

Derived fields are also reconciled in the other direction. `Registration.compliance_posture` is recomputed by ten distinct operations, and whenever it changes the registration's memberships are reconciled to match it: a posture of `AT_RISK` moves an `ACTIVE` membership to `PROBATION`, a posture of `COMPLIANT` moves a `PROBATION` membership back to `ACTIVE`, and a posture of `DELINQUENT` leaves membership status untouched. This reconciliation is a server-initiated transition; no client ever addresses a membership's probation directly.

### 2.10. Rule Evaluation Priority Beyond Admission

Where several business rules could all reject the same request, their relative order is fixed and observable. After the seven admission steps of §2.5, the remaining checks are evaluated in this order, and again the first failure is the response:

1. **Endpoint-specific state guards**, in this internal order: the reachable registration must be `ACTIVE` (`ERR_REGISTRATION_NOT_ACTIVE`); the named fee schedule must be `PUBLISHED` (`ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_NOT_PUBLISHED`); the report must be in `DRAFT` for line mutations (`ERR_REPORT_NOT_IN_DRAFT`); the report behind an engagement action must be `UNDER_VERIFICATION` (`ERR_REPORT_NOT_UNDER_VERIFICATION`).
2. **Optimistic concurrency**, first `ERR_STALE_PRO_VERSION` on a publish, then `ERR_STALE_REGISTRATION_VERSION` on a membership creation.
3. **Business-safety rules**, in this internal order: obligation determined (`ERR_OBLIGATION_NOT_DETERMINED`), determination current (`ERR_DETERMINATION_STALE`), producer actually obligated (`ERR_PRODUCER_NOT_OBLIGATED`), registration not exempt (`ERR_EXEMPT_REGISTRATION_CANNOT_REPORT`), segregation of duties (`ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`), verifier rotation (`ERR_VERIFIER_ROTATION_REQUIRED`), assigned-verifier identity (`ERR_NOT_ASSIGNED_VERIFIER`), verification completed where required (`ERR_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`).
4. **Referential agreement between named resources**, in this internal order: period-to-program (`ERR_PERIOD_PROGRAM_MISMATCH`), stock keeping unit to producer (`ERR_SKU_PRODUCER_MISMATCH`), fee schedule to PRO (`ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_PRO_MISMATCH`), fee schedule to state program (`ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_PROGRAM_MISMATCH`), membership to report registration (`ERR_MEMBERSHIP_REGISTRATION_MISMATCH`), material rate present for every priced material (`ERR_MATERIAL_RATE_MISSING`).
5. **Uniqueness and structural constraints**, in this internal order: duplicate registration (`ERR_DUPLICATE_REGISTRATION`), duplicate report for a period (`ERR_DUPLICATE_REPORT_FOR_PERIOD`), duplicate report line (`ERR_DUPLICATE_REPORT_LINE`), period window overlap (`ERR_PERIOD_WINDOW_OVERLAP`), fee-schedule effective-window overlap (`ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_EFFECTIVE_OVERLAP`), tier discontinuity (`ERR_FEE_TIER_DISCONTINUOUS`), schedule with no rates at publish (`ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_HAS_NO_RATES`), stock keeping unit with no components at activation (`ERR_SKU_HAS_NO_COMPONENTS`), producer with no active stock keeping units at determination (`ERR_PRODUCER_HAS_NO_ACTIVE_SKUS`), report with no lines at submission (`ERR_REPORT_HAS_NO_LINES`), engagement already open (`ERR_ENGAGEMENT_ALREADY_OPEN`), verifier capacity (`ERR_VERIFIER_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`), open reports blocking withdrawal (`ERR_REGISTRATION_HAS_OPEN_REPORTS`).
6. **Temporal rules**, in this internal order: fieldwork window validity (`ERR_INVALID_FIELDWORK_WINDOW`), registration deadline (`ERR_REGISTRATION_DEADLINE_PASSED`), submission window not yet open (`ERR_SUBMISSION_WINDOW_NOT_OPEN`), submission window closed (`ERR_SUBMISSION_WINDOW_CLOSED`), correction window expired (`ERR_CORRECTION_WINDOW_EXPIRED`), correction window still open (`ERR_CORRECTION_WINDOW_OPEN`), pinned schedule not effective for the period (`ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_NOT_EFFECTIVE_FOR_PERIOD`).
7. **Fiscal rules**, last of all, in this internal order: no live membership (`ERR_NO_ACTIVE_MEMBERSHIP`), membership not in a payable state (`ERR_MEMBERSHIP_NOT_ACTIVE`), payment amount mismatch (`ERR_MEMBERSHIP_FEE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`), membership fee still outstanding (`ERR_MEMBERSHIP_FEE_OUTSTANDING`), invoice not issued (`ERR_INVOICE_NOT_ISSUED`), findings note required (`ERR_FINDINGS_NOTE_REQUIRED`).

Two consequences are worth calling out because they are easy to get backwards. A report submission attempted by a producer whose determination has gone stale *and* whose membership fee is unpaid reports `ERR_DETERMINATION_STALE`, because business-safety precedes fiscal. A report submission attempted outside the submission window *and* without a live membership reports the temporal failure `ERR_SUBMISSION_WINDOW_NOT_OPEN` or `ERR_SUBMISSION_WINDOW_CLOSED`, again because fiscal rules run last.

### 2.11. Segregation of Duties and Verifier Independence

Independence of assurance is enforced structurally rather than by convention, and three distinct rules together produce it.

The first is **self-verification**: the acting verifier's `X-User-ID` must differ from the `submitted_by_user_id` recorded on the report under examination, and must also differ from the `assigned_by_user_id` recorded on the engagement. A producer cannot verify its own filing, and the regulator that commissioned the engagement cannot perform it. The rule is enforced both when the engagement is created and again when the opinion is issued, and either failure is `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 422).

The second is **rotation**: the verifier named on a new engagement must not be the verifier who issued the opinion on the immediately preceding `FINALIZED` report of the same registration. Refusal is `ERR_VERIFIER_ROTATION_REQUIRED` (HTTP 422).

The third is **capacity with a buffer**: a verifier may not hold more than one open engagement whose fieldwork window, widened by a two-day buffer at each end, overlaps the window being requested. Refusal is `ERR_VERIFIER_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 409). §3.7 gives the exact counting rule.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Establishing the Obligated Party

Every compliance obligation begins with a question of law rather than of packaging: which legal entity is the obligated party? A `ProgramRegulator` onboards each candidate by supplying its registered `legal_name`, a compliance `contact_email`, the three status flags that drive the classification — `brand_owner_flag`, `importer_of_record_flag` and `domestic_licensor_flag` — and its whole-dollar global gross revenue for the trailing fiscal year as `annual_gross_revenue_usd`, which must not be negative. An optional `parent_producer_id` may name another producer, marking this row as an affiliate of that parent; when it is omitted the field is null and the producer stands alone. Producers are created with `status` `DRAFT`, `obligated_party_role` `UNDETERMINED`, `annual_packaging_weight_kg` of 0, `determination_stale` false and `assessed_fee_balance_cents` of 0.

Determining the obligation is a separate regulator action that moves the producer from `DRAFT` to `OBLIGATION_DETERMINED`. It may only be performed while the producer is in `DRAFT` — a second determination on an already-determined producer is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE` — and it requires the producer to have at least one stock keeping unit whose `status` is `ACTIVE`, otherwise the request is refused with `ERR_PRODUCER_HAS_NO_ACTIVE_SKUS`. The classification itself is a single verdict written to `obligated_party_role`, and it is decided as an **ordered ladder in which the first matching clause wins and no later clause is evaluated even when its flag is also true**: if `brand_owner_flag` is true the role is `BRAND_OWNER`; otherwise if `importer_of_record_flag` is true the role is `IMPORTER`; otherwise if `domestic_licensor_flag` is true the role is `DOMESTIC_LICENSOR`; otherwise the role is `NOT_OBLIGATED`. A party that is simultaneously the brand owner and the importer of record is classified `BRAND_OWNER` and nothing else. The classification consults the producer row alone — affiliates play no part here, and enter the picture only in the exemption analysis of §3.5.

The same action refreshes the producer's tonnage rollup and clears `determination_stale` to false, then increments the producer's `version` by 1 and refreshes `updated_at`. The tonnage rollup is computed as follows. For each stock keeping unit of this producer whose `status` is `ACTIVE`, multiply its `units_sold_annual_estimate` by its `unit_packaging_weight_grams`, divide by one thousand to convert grams to kilograms, and round that per-unit contribution half up to three decimal places. Add those already-rounded contributions together, taking the units in ascending order of their own `created_at`, and round the total half up to three decimal places again. Stock keeping units still in `DRAFT` are excluded entirely, as are components that have been superseded. `unit_packaging_weight_grams` itself is the sum of the `unit_weight_grams` of the unit's `ACTIVE` components, rounded half up to two decimal places.

Restating revenue is the deliberate reverse edge that makes the producer lifecycle cyclic: a `ProgramRegulator` may take a producer from `OBLIGATION_DETERMINED` back to `DRAFT` by supplying a corrected `annual_gross_revenue_usd` together with a `reason`. The corrected figure is written, `obligated_party_role` is reset to `UNDETERMINED` and `determination_stale` is set to false. Because the producer has left `OBLIGATION_DETERMINED`, a three-hop cascade follows and every hop is mandatory. In the first hop, every registration of this producer whose `status` is `SUBMITTED` or `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED`, with `suspension_reason` set to `PRODUCER_REDETERMINATION_REQUIRED` and `exemption_status` reset to `UNDETERMINED`. In the second hop, because those registrations became `SUSPENDED`, every packaging report of each suspended registration whose submission dimension reads `DRAFT` moves to `BLOCKED`. In the third hop, again because those registrations became `SUSPENDED`, the compliance posture of each is recomputed and yields `DELINQUENT`. The `version` of the producer and of every mutated registration and report is incremented. Restating revenue while the producer is still `DRAFT` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.

Producer records are readable individually — the fetch returns the computed rollups alongside the input fields — and as a list. The list accepts `parent_producer_id` as a filter, which is precisely how a caller resolves the affiliate set that the group-level exemption variant depends on, plus `status` and `obligated_party_role`, all alongside `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.2. Packaging Material Data by Stock Keeping Unit

Packaging data is collected at the granularity of the stock keeping unit. A `ProducerComplianceOfficer` creates one by naming the owning `producer_id`, the item's `gtin` — its global trade item number, which must be unique within that producer — the consumer-facing `name`, the `product_category`, and the `units_sold_annual_estimate` used for the producer-level tonnage rollup. The category is one of `BEVERAGE`, `FOOD_DRY`, `HOUSEHOLD_CHEM`, `MEDICAL_DEVICE` or `PERSONAL_CARE`, and it is the input to the category-exemption test of §3.5. A newly created unit is in `DRAFT` with an empty `components` list, `unit_packaging_weight_grams` of 0 and `revision_count` of 0. The officer's `X-User-ID` must equal the named `producer_id`.

Components are appended one at a time. Each append supplies a `material_type` — one of `PLASTIC`, `PAPER`, `GLASS` or `METAL` — the component's `unit_weight_grams` at two decimal places, its `recyclability_class` from `READILY_RECYCLABLE`, `LIMITED` or `NOT_RECYCLABLE`, its post-consumer recycled content as an integer `pcr_content_pct` between 0 and 100 inclusive, and an `is_reusable` flag. The service assigns the next `component_seq` in sequence, sets the component's own `status` to `ACTIVE`, recomputes `unit_packaging_weight_grams` from the `ACTIVE` components, and increments the unit's `version`. Components are never deleted; the append is the only way to add one and the revision described below is the only way to retire one.

Activation is what makes a unit count. It moves the unit from `DRAFT` to `ACTIVE`, may only be performed while the unit is in `DRAFT`, requires at least one component whose own `status` is `ACTIVE` — otherwise `ERR_SKU_HAS_NO_COMPONENTS` — and requires the acting officer's `X-User-ID` to equal the unit's `producer_id`. Activation recomputes `unit_packaging_weight_grams`, then recomputes the owning producer's `annual_packaging_weight_kg`, and — this is the coupling that catches implementers out — sets the producer's `determination_stale` to true whenever that producer is in `OBLIGATION_DETERMINED`. Both the unit's `version` and the producer's `version` are incremented. Note that the producer's `status` does not revert: material-data drift never un-determines an obligation, it only marks the determination stale, and a stale determination blocks registration submission until a regulator runs the determination again.

Redesigning packaging is a wholesale replacement rather than an edit. A `ProducerComplianceOfficer` supplies a `components` array of replacement components together with a `reason`; the request must carry at least one replacement component, the unit must already be `ACTIVE`, and `X-User-ID` must equal the unit's `producer_id`. Four cascade hops follow and each is required. First, every element of `components` whose own `status` is `ACTIVE` moves to `SUPERSEDED`, and the replacements are appended with `status` `ACTIVE` and `component_seq` values continuing the existing sequence — so the array grows and the history of previous packaging remains readable. Second, because the active component set changed, `unit_packaging_weight_grams` is recomputed and `revision_count` is incremented by 1. Third, because that per-unit weight changed, the producer's `annual_packaging_weight_kg` is recomputed. Fourth, because that producer rollup changed, the producer's `determination_stale` is set to true whenever the producer is in `OBLIGATION_DETERMINED`. The `version` of both the unit and the producer is incremented. Crucially, weights already captured on submitted report lines are **not** retroactively altered: a line's `line_weight_kg` is recomputed only when the report that owns it is submitted or resubmitted.

Individual units are readable with their full component array and derived weight, and the list endpoint filters on `producer_id`, `status` and `product_category` alongside `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.3. State Programs, Reporting Periods and Their Windows

A `ProgramRegulator` registers each state's program with its two-letter `state_code`, which must be unique across programs and consist of two uppercase letters, together with the policy triple of §2.7 — `obligation_basis`, `eco_mod_policy` and `fee_rounding_order` — and four threshold values. `small_producer_revenue_threshold_usd` and `small_producer_tonnage_threshold_kg` are the **strict** upper bounds of the small-producer exemption and must both be greater than zero. `exempt_product_categories` is an array of product categories excluded from fee assessment in this state and may be empty. `verification_required_threshold_kg` must be greater than zero and is the tonnage at or above which a report needs a third-party opinion before it can be assessed as final. `registration_deadline` is an ISO 8601 UTC instant and is the last instant, inclusive, at which a registration may be submitted in that state. Programs are readable individually and as a list filtered by `state_code` and `obligation_basis`.

Reporting periods hang off a state program and are created by the regulator inside that program's path. Each carries a `period_label` such as `CA-2026-H2`, unique within its program, a data window given as `data_start` and `data_end` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form, a submission window given as the ISO 8601 UTC instants `submission_open` and `submission_deadline`, and a `correction_window_days` count of at least one whole day. The data window is **half-open**: `data_start` is inclusive and `data_end` is exclusive. Consequently a new period may not overlap an existing period of the same program, but two periods that merely touch — one ending exactly where the next begins — are allowed; an actual overlap is refused with `ERR_PERIOD_WINDOW_OVERLAP`. The submission window, by contrast, is closed at both ends: submissions are accepted from `submission_open` inclusive to `submission_deadline` inclusive. `correction_window_days` is not used at period creation at all; it is the number of whole days added to the instant of a qualified or adverse opinion to derive the report's correction deadline in §3.7.

Periods are readable both as a reverse list under their owning program — which accepts only `limit` and `offset` — and individually by their own identifier.

### 3.4. Producer Responsibility Organisations and Their Fee Schedules

A producer responsibility organisation is a thin record: a `ProgramRegulator` registers it with a `name`, which must be unique across organisations, and an operations `contact_email`. It is created at `version` 1, and that `version` matters far beyond bookkeeping — it is the anchor of the optimistic-concurrency guard on schedule publication described in §2.8, so clients read it from a fetch of the organisation and pin it into `expected_pro_version`. Organisations are readable individually and as a plain list taking only `limit` and `offset`.

Fee schedules are authored by a `ProAdministrator` for exactly one pairing of organisation and state program, named as `pro_id` and `state_program_id`. A schedule declares an inclusive `effective_from` date in `YYYY-MM-DD` form and either an inclusive `effective_to` date or null for an open-ended schedule; a flat annual `membership_fee_cents` that must not be negative; the two administrative brackets `admin_fee_bps_low` and `admin_fee_bps_high`, each an integer count of basis points between 0 and 2000 inclusive; and a `material_rates` array. Schedules are created in `DRAFT`.

The `material_rates` array is where tiered pricing lives. Each row names a `material_type` from `PLASTIC`, `PAPER`, `GLASS` and `METAL`, a `tier_min_kg` floor, a `tier_max_kg` ceiling that is null for the top band, and a `rate_cents_per_kg` price. The bands of any one material must form a contiguous half-open partition: sorted by `tier_min_kg`, the first band must start at 0, each band's `tier_max_kg` must equal the next band's `tier_min_kg`, and exactly the last band may have a null `tier_max_kg`. A material whose bands leave a gap, overlap, fail to start at zero, or end in zero or more than one open-ended band is refused at creation with `ERR_FEE_TIER_DISCONTINUOUS`. Because the bands are half-open, a basis figure exactly equal to a band's `tier_min_kg` selects **that** band rather than the one below it.

Effective windows of the same organisation-and-program pairing are kept strictly apart, and the rule is deliberately stricter than the one governing reporting periods. A closed window runs from `effective_from` to `effective_to` inclusive at both ends, and a new schedule may neither overlap nor even touch an existing window of the same pairing; a schedule whose `effective_to` is null is open-ended and therefore blocks every later window outright. Violations are refused with `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_EFFECTIVE_OVERLAP`. Schedules are readable individually with their rate bands, and as a list filtered by `pro_id`, `state_program_id` and `status`.

Publication is the transition from `DRAFT` to `PUBLISHED` and belongs to the owning organisation's administrator, whose `X-User-ID` must equal the schedule's `pro_id`. The schedule must still be `DRAFT`, its `material_rates` array must be non-empty — an empty list is refused with `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_HAS_NO_RATES` — and the request must carry `expected_pro_version` matching the organisation's current `version`, failing which the publish is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PRO_VERSION` at HTTP 412. On success the schedule's `status` becomes `PUBLISHED`, and a **truncation** follows: if another schedule of the same `pro_id` and `state_program_id` pairing is `PUBLISHED` with a null `effective_to`, that older row's `effective_to` is set to the new schedule's `effective_from` minus one day and its `status` moves to `SUPERSEDED`. The organisation's `version` is incremented by 1, as is the `version` of both schedules touched.

Truncation has a consequence that only surfaces much later. A membership that pinned the truncated schedule keeps pointing at it — `fee_schedule_version_pinned` is deliberately not refreshed — so pricing still uses the truncated row's rates. But the truncated row's effective window is now shorter, and if it no longer covers the data window of the period being priced, the assessment is refused with `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_NOT_EFFECTIVE_FOR_PERIOD`. A producer whose organisation republishes mid-year can therefore find its report unpriceable even though nothing about the report changed.

### 3.5. State Registration and the Exemption Analysis

A `ProducerComplianceOfficer` opens a registration by naming a `producer_id` and a `state_program_id`; `X-User-ID` must equal that `producer_id`. The producer must already have been through obligation determination — a producer still in `DRAFT` is refused with `ERR_OBLIGATION_NOT_DETERMINED` — and no other registration for the same producer-and-program pairing may exist in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`, failing which the request is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_REGISTRATION`. A registration in `REJECTED` or `WITHDRAWN` does not block a fresh one. New registrations are created in `DRAFT` with `exemption_status` `UNDETERMINED`, `exemption_basis_applied` `NONE`, `compliance_posture` `UNDETERMINED`, `assessed_group_revenue_usd` and `assessed_group_weight_kg` of 0, `reported_weight_kg_ytd` of 0, and a null `suspension_reason` and `submitted_at`.

Submitting the registration moves it `DRAFT` → `SUBMITTED` and is where the exemption analysis runs. Four guards apply before it: the acting officer's `X-User-ID` must equal the registration's `producer_id`; the producer must be in `OBLIGATION_DETERMINED`, else `ERR_OBLIGATION_NOT_DETERMINED`; the producer's `determination_stale` must be false, else `ERR_DETERMINATION_STALE` — this is what a component append followed by an activation, or a revenue restatement, costs the producer; the producer's `obligated_party_role` must not be `NOT_OBLIGATED`, else `ERR_PRODUCER_NOT_OBLIGATED`; and the logical clock must be at or before the state program's `registration_deadline`, else `ERR_REGISTRATION_DEADLINE_PASSED`. On success `submitted_at` is set to the logical clock and `version` is incremented.

The exemption analysis writes four fields — `exemption_status`, `exemption_basis_applied`, `assessed_group_revenue_usd` and `assessed_group_weight_kg` — and runs in two stages, the first of which short-circuits the second.

The first stage is the **category test**, and it is evaluated first. Gather the producer's stock keeping units whose `status` is `ACTIVE`. If that set is non-empty and every one of those units has a `product_category` that is a member of the state program's `exempt_product_categories`, then `exemption_status` becomes `EXEMPT_CATEGORY`, both `assessed_group_revenue_usd` and `assessed_group_weight_kg` are written as 0, `exemption_basis_applied` is copied from the state program's `obligation_basis`, and the second stage does not run. A producer with even one active unit outside the state's exempt list falls through to the second stage, and so does a producer with no active units at all.

The second stage is the **small-producer test**, and it compares an assessed revenue figure and an assessed weight figure against the state's two thresholds. Both comparisons are **strict**: the producer is exempt only when its assessed revenue is strictly less than `small_producer_revenue_threshold_usd` **and** its assessed weight is strictly less than `small_producer_tonnage_threshold_kg`. A figure exactly equal to either threshold is not exempt. When both strict comparisons hold, `exemption_status` becomes `EXEMPT_SMALL_PRODUCER`; otherwise it becomes `OBLIGATED`. Whichever branch is taken, the two figures actually compared are recorded as `assessed_group_revenue_usd` and `assessed_group_weight_kg`, and `exemption_basis_applied` is copied from the state program's `obligation_basis`.

Which figures are compared depends on the state's `obligation_basis`, and this is the deliberate multi-state divergence. Under `ENTITY_LEVEL`, the assessed revenue is the producer's own `annual_gross_revenue_usd` and the assessed weight is the producer's own `annual_packaging_weight_kg`; affiliates are ignored entirely, even when the producer's `parent_producer_id` is non-null. Under `GROUP_LEVEL`, the assessed figures cover the whole one-level group the producer belongs to. That group is the producer itself, together with every producer whose `parent_producer_id` names the producer, and — when the producer's own `parent_producer_id` is non-null — also that parent itself and every other producer sharing that same parent. The assessed revenue is the sum of `annual_gross_revenue_usd` across the group; the assessed weight is the sum of `annual_packaging_weight_kg` across the group, rounded half up to three decimal places. Group membership does not depend on the affiliates' own registration status: an affiliate that has never registered anywhere still contributes its revenue and its tonnage. The practical result is that one producer, on identical data, can be `EXEMPT_SMALL_PRODUCER` in an entity-level state and `OBLIGATED` in a group-level state at the same moment.

A `ProgramRegulator` then decides the submitted registration by supplying a `decision` of either `ACCEPT` or `REJECT`, with an optional `reason` that becomes mandatory for a rejection. The registration must be in `SUBMITTED`. `ACCEPT` moves it to `ACTIVE` and recomputes its `compliance_posture`, which for a freshly accepted registration with no report overdue and none awaiting correction yields `COMPLIANT`. `REJECT` moves it to `REJECTED`, sets `compliance_posture` to `UNDETERMINED`, and terminates every membership of the registration whose `status` is `PENDING`, `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION`, writing `REGISTRATION_REJECTED` into each membership's `decision_reason`. Both branches increment the `version` of every row touched. `REJECTED` and `WITHDRAWN` are terminal registration states.

Suspension and reinstatement form the registration's cycle. A regulator suspends an `ACTIVE` registration by supplying a `suspension_reason`, which is stored on the row; every report of that registration whose submission dimension reads `DRAFT` moves to `BLOCKED`, and the posture is recomputed and yields `DELINQUENT` for as long as the registration stays `SUSPENDED`. Reinstatement takes a `SUSPENDED` registration back to `ACTIVE` and takes no body at all, but it demands that the owning producer is again in `OBLIGATION_DETERMINED` with `determination_stale` false — otherwise it is refused with `ERR_DETERMINATION_STALE`, which is exactly what a suspension caused by a revenue restatement produces until the regulator re-determines the obligation. On success `suspension_reason` is cleared, every report of the registration whose submission dimension reads `BLOCKED` moves back to `DRAFT`, and the posture is recomputed; when the recomputed posture is `COMPLIANT`, every membership of the registration in `PROBATION` moves back to `ACTIVE`. Version numbers are incremented on all touched rows.

Withdrawal is the producer's own exit. A `ProducerComplianceOfficer` whose `X-User-ID` equals the registration's `producer_id` withdraws an `ACTIVE` registration with a `reason`, but only when no report of that registration sits in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_VERIFICATION` or `CORRECTION_REQUIRED`; any open report refuses the withdrawal with `ERR_REGISTRATION_HAS_OPEN_REPORTS`. Withdrawal moves the registration to `WITHDRAWN`, terminates every membership in `PENDING`, `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION` with `decision_reason` `REGISTRATION_WITHDRAWN`, and sets `compliance_posture` to `UNDETERMINED`.

The compliance posture itself is a four-valued verdict recomputed by ten operations: registration submission, decision, suspension, reinstatement and withdrawal; membership creation and membership decision; report submission, resubmission and close-out; and the issuing of a verification opinion. It is never recomputed by setting the logical clock alone. The verdict is decided as follows. A registration in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `REJECTED` or `WITHDRAWN` has posture `UNDETERMINED`. A registration in `SUSPENDED` has posture `DELINQUENT`. For a registration in `ACTIVE`, an ordered ladder applies and the first match wins: if any report of the registration sits in `DRAFT` or `BLOCKED` while its reporting period's `submission_deadline` is strictly earlier than the logical clock, the posture is `DELINQUENT`; otherwise if any report of the registration sits in `CORRECTION_REQUIRED`, the posture is `AT_RISK`; otherwise if any report of the registration has a fee dimension reading `VOIDED`, the posture is `AT_RISK`; otherwise the posture is `COMPLIANT`. After the posture is written, the registration's memberships are reconciled exactly as §2.9 describes.

Registrations are readable individually — the fetch surfaces the exemption verdict, the year-to-date tonnage and the posture — and as a list filtered by `producer_id`, `state_program_id`, `status`, `exemption_status` and `compliance_posture`.

### 3.6. Joining a Producer Responsibility Organisation

A registration discharges its fee obligation through a membership. A `ProducerComplianceOfficer` applies by naming the `registration_id`, the `pro_id` of the organisation being joined, and the `fee_schedule_id` of the schedule it elects, together with the `expected_registration_version` demanded by §2.8. The elected schedule must be `PUBLISHED`; a `DRAFT` or `SUPERSEDED` schedule is refused with `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_NOT_PUBLISHED`. The producer's obligation determination must still be current, so a producer with `determination_stale` true is refused with `ERR_DETERMINATION_STALE`. The elected schedule must belong to the organisation named in the same request — otherwise `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_PRO_MISMATCH` — and must have been published for the same state program as the registration — otherwise `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_PROGRAM_MISMATCH`. A registration version that no longer matches yields `ERR_STALE_REGISTRATION_VERSION` at HTTP 412.

The application is created in `PENDING` with `membership_fee_cents` of 0, `fee_payment_status` `OUTSTANDING`, a null `fee_schedule_version_pinned` and a null `decision_reason`. Creating it also **supersedes** whatever coverage the registration already had: any membership of that registration whose `status` is `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION` moves to `TERMINATED` with `decision_reason` set to `SUPERSEDED_BY_NEW_MEMBERSHIP`. The registration's `version` is incremented and its `compliance_posture` recomputed. This supersession is precisely why the operation is guarded by optimistic concurrency: without it, a client working from a stale registration view could cancel coverage it had never seen.

The organisation's administrator then decides the application with a `decision` of `APPROVE` or `REJECT` and an optional `reason` that becomes mandatory for a rejection; in both cases the acting `X-User-ID` must equal the membership's `pro_id` and the membership must still be `PENDING`. Approval additionally requires the elected schedule to be `PUBLISHED` at that moment — `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_NOT_PUBLISHED` otherwise — and the registration to be in `SUBMITTED` or `ACTIVE`, with any other registration status refused as `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. Approval moves the membership `PENDING` → `ACTIVE`, assesses the membership fee, sets `fee_payment_status` to `OUTSTANDING`, and pins `fee_schedule_version_pinned` to the elected schedule's `version` at that instant. Rejection moves the membership `PENDING` → `REJECTED` and writes the supplied text into `decision_reason`. Both branches recompute the registration's `compliance_posture` and increment the `version` of both the membership and the registration. `REJECTED` and `TERMINATED` are terminal membership states.

The membership fee is assessed once, at approval, from the producer's tonnage as it stands at that instant. Take the producer's `annual_packaging_weight_kg`, subtract a free allowance of 500 kilograms, divide the remainder by one thousand, and round the quotient **up** to the next whole number to obtain a count of surcharge units; if the result would be negative, the count is zero instead. The fee is the elected schedule's `membership_fee_cents` plus 500 dollars — that is, fifty thousand cents — for each surcharge unit. Because the rounding is upward, a producer at exactly 500 kilograms incurs no surcharge at all, while a producer at 500.001 kilograms incurs one full unit. The figure is written to `membership_fee_cents` on the membership and is never revisited: stock keeping units activated the following week do not retroactively change an approved membership's fee.

Payment is recorded by the organisation's administrator with an `amount_cents` figure that must equal the membership's assessed `membership_fee_cents` **exactly** — neither underpayment nor overpayment is accepted, and a mismatch is refused with `ERR_MEMBERSHIP_FEE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`. The membership must be in `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION`; a `PENDING`, `REJECTED` or `TERMINATED` membership has nothing assessed to pay and is refused with `ERR_MEMBERSHIP_NOT_ACTIVE`. On success `fee_payment_status` moves to `PAID` and the membership's `version` is incremented.

Between `ACTIVE` and `PROBATION` the membership cycles under server control only, driven by the registration's posture as described in §2.9 and §3.5. The distinction bites at report-filing time and it is asymmetric on purpose: while a membership is in `PROBATION`, a first submission of a report is refused with `ERR_MEMBERSHIP_NOT_ACTIVE`, but a resubmission of a report already in correction remains allowed — a producer under probation must be able to fix the very report that put it there. Memberships are readable individually, showing the assessed fee, the payment status and the pinned schedule version, and as a list filtered by `registration_id`, `pro_id`, `fee_schedule_id`, `status` and `fee_payment_status`.

### 3.7. Filing, Verifying and Correcting a Packaging Report

A packaging report carries **two independent state dimensions** on one row. `submission_status` tracks filing and assurance and runs over `DRAFT`, `BLOCKED`, `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_VERIFICATION`, `VERIFIED`, `CORRECTION_REQUIRED`, `FINALIZED` and `REJECTED`, with `FINALIZED` and `REJECTED` terminal. `fee_status` tracks money and runs over `UNASSESSED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `FINAL`, `INVOICED` and `VOIDED`. The two advance independently: pricing the report moves only the fee dimension and never touches the submission dimension, while submitting it moves only the submission dimension. Reports begin at `DRAFT` and `UNASSESSED`.

A `ProducerComplianceOfficer` opens a report by naming a `registration_id` and a `reporting_period_id`. The registration must be `ACTIVE`, else `ERR_REGISTRATION_NOT_ACTIVE`. The registration must not be exempt: an `exemption_status` of `EXEMPT_SMALL_PRODUCER` or `EXEMPT_CATEGORY` is refused with `ERR_EXEMPT_REGISTRATION_CANNOT_REPORT`. The named period must belong to the same state program as the registration, otherwise `ERR_PERIOD_PROGRAM_MISMATCH`. And no report may already exist for the same registration-and-period pairing in any submission state other than `REJECTED`, otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_REPORT_FOR_PERIOD` — a rejected report is the one case that leaves the pairing free for a fresh attempt.

Lines are appended to a `DRAFT` report one stock keeping unit at a time, each naming a `sku_id` and the `units_sold` inside the period's data window for that unit. The owning report must be in `DRAFT`; a line mutation against a report in any other submission state, including `BLOCKED`, is refused with `ERR_REPORT_NOT_IN_DRAFT`. The registration reached from the report must be `ACTIVE`, else `ERR_REGISTRATION_NOT_ACTIVE`. The named unit must belong to the same producer as the report's registration, else `ERR_SKU_PRODUCER_MISMATCH`. At most one line may exist per report-and-unit pairing, so a repeat is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_REPORT_LINE`. Two derived fields are computed at once. `line_weight_kg` is obtained by taking each `ACTIVE` component of the named unit, multiplying the line's `units_sold` by that component's `unit_weight_grams`, dividing by one thousand, rounding half up to three decimal places, and summing those per-component contributions. `fee_exempt` is set to true exactly when the unit's `product_category` is a member of the state program's `exempt_product_categories` — note that this is a per-line judgement, whereas the category exemption of §3.5 requires *every* active unit to be exempt. New lines start with `line_fee_exact_cents` and `line_fee_cents` at 0 and an empty `eco_mod_detail`. Appending a line recomputes the report's `total_weight_kg`, `fee_basis_weight_kg` and `line_count` and increments the report's `version`. `total_weight_kg` is the sum of `line_weight_kg` across **all** lines including the fee-exempt ones, at three decimal places; `fee_basis_weight_kg` is the same sum restricted to lines whose `fee_exempt` is false. A line may also be removed, but only while the owning report is still in `DRAFT` — a removal aimed at a report in any other submission state is refused with `ERR_REPORT_NOT_IN_DRAFT`, exactly as an append would be. Removal deletes the line, recomputes the same three fields and increments the report's `version`; the response carries no body. Lines are readable individually with their eco-modulation audit detail, and as a list filtered by `packaging_report_id`, `sku_id` and `fee_exempt`.

Submission is the busiest guarded operation in the service. The report must be in `DRAFT`; the acting officer's `X-User-ID` must equal the registration's `producer_id`; the registration must be `ACTIVE`, else `ERR_REGISTRATION_NOT_ACTIVE`; the registration's `exemption_status` must be `OBLIGATED`, else `ERR_EXEMPT_REGISTRATION_CANNOT_REPORT`; the producer's `determination_stale` must be false, else `ERR_DETERMINATION_STALE`; the report must carry at least one line, else `ERR_REPORT_HAS_NO_LINES`; the logical clock must be at or after the period's `submission_open`, else `ERR_SUBMISSION_WINDOW_NOT_OPEN`, and at or before its `submission_deadline`, else `ERR_SUBMISSION_WINDOW_CLOSED`; the registration must have a membership in `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION`, and with none at all the refusal is `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_MEMBERSHIP` at HTTP 402 while a membership merely sitting in `PROBATION` gives `ERR_MEMBERSHIP_NOT_ACTIVE`; and that membership's `fee_payment_status` must read `PAID`, else `ERR_MEMBERSHIP_FEE_OUTSTANDING` at HTTP 402.

On success the submission dimension moves `DRAFT` → `SUBMITTED` and five things are recomputed in order: every line's `line_weight_kg`, then the report's `total_weight_kg`, `fee_basis_weight_kg` and `line_count`, and then `verification_required`, which is set to true exactly when `total_weight_kg` is greater than or equal to the state program's `verification_required_threshold_kg`. `submitted_by_user_id` is set to the acting `X-User-ID` and the report's submission instant is recorded from the logical clock. The registration's `reported_weight_kg_ytd` is then recomputed and therefore rises by this report's `total_weight_kg`; that field is the sum, at three decimal places, of `total_weight_kg` over the registration's reports whose submission dimension is `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_VERIFICATION`, `VERIFIED` or `FINALIZED`, taken in ascending order of report `created_at` and counted across all reporting periods. Reports in `DRAFT`, `BLOCKED`, `CORRECTION_REQUIRED` or `REJECTED` are outside that counted set. Finally the registration's `compliance_posture` is recomputed, and the `version` of the report, of every one of its lines and of the registration is incremented.

Assigning an independent verifier is a `ProgramRegulator` action taken against a `SUBMITTED` report. It names the `packaging_report_id`, the `verifier_user_id` of the verifier being engaged, and the fieldwork window as `fieldwork_start` and `fieldwork_end` dates in `YYYY-MM-DD` form; the window is closed at both ends and `fieldwork_end` must be strictly later than `fieldwork_start`, else `ERR_INVALID_FIELDWORK_WINDOW`. Segregation of duties applies as described in §2.11, so a `verifier_user_id` equal to the report's `submitted_by_user_id`, or equal to the acting regulator's own identifier, is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. Rotation applies too: if the named verifier issued the opinion on the registration's immediately preceding `FINALIZED` report, the assignment is refused with `ERR_VERIFIER_ROTATION_REQUIRED`. Only one engagement may be open per report, so an existing engagement of the same report in `ASSIGNED` or `IN_PROGRESS` refuses the request with `ERR_ENGAGEMENT_ALREADY_OPEN`. Capacity is then counted: over all engagements of the same `verifier_user_id` whose `status` is `ASSIGNED` or `IN_PROGRESS`, count those whose fieldwork window — widened by two days before `fieldwork_start` and two days after `fieldwork_end` — intersects the window being requested; that count must be strictly less than two, otherwise `ERR_VERIFIER_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. The engagement is created in `ASSIGNED` with `assigned_by_user_id` taken from the acting regulator's header, `round_index` set to the report's `correction_round` plus one, a null `opinion`, a null `findings_note` and a null opinion instant; the report's submission dimension moves `SUBMITTED` → `UNDER_VERIFICATION` and its `version` is incremented.

The assigned verifier then starts fieldwork, moving the engagement `ASSIGNED` → `IN_PROGRESS`. Only the engagement's own `verifier_user_id` may do so — anyone else is refused `ERR_NOT_ASSIGNED_VERIFIER` — and the report behind the engagement must be `UNDER_VERIFICATION`, else `ERR_REPORT_NOT_UNDER_VERIFICATION`. Issuing the opinion moves the engagement `IN_PROGRESS` → `OPINION_ISSUED`, again only for the assigned verifier — anyone else is refused `ERR_NOT_ASSIGNED_VERIFIER` — and again only while the report behind the engagement is `UNDER_VERIFICATION`, any other report position being refused with `ERR_REPORT_NOT_UNDER_VERIFICATION`. The body carries an `opinion` of `UNQUALIFIED`, `QUALIFIED` or `ADVERSE`, plus a `findings_note` that is mandatory and must be non-empty whenever the opinion is `QUALIFIED` or `ADVERSE`; a missing note in those two cases is refused with `ERR_FINDINGS_NOTE_REQUIRED`. The opinion and the logical-clock instant `opinion_issued_at` are written, the engagement's `version` is incremented, and `OPINION_ISSUED` is terminal. Engagements are readable individually and as a list filtered by `packaging_report_id`, `verifier_user_id`, `status` and `opinion` — the last two being what a caller uses to reconstruct the rotation and capacity positions.

An `UNQUALIFIED` opinion is the quiet branch: the report's submission dimension moves `UNDER_VERIFICATION` → `VERIFIED`, its `version` is incremented, the report becomes eligible for final assessment, and nothing else in the system changes.

A `QUALIFIED` or an `ADVERSE` opinion is the loud branch: the report's submission dimension moves `UNDER_VERIFICATION` → `CORRECTION_REQUIRED` and a six-hop cascade runs, every hop of which is required. First, `correction_due_at` is set to the opinion instant plus the reporting period's `correction_window_days` whole days. Second, because the report has left the counted set, the registration's `reported_weight_kg_ytd` is recomputed and therefore falls by this report's `total_weight_kg`. Third, because the report is no longer a valid fee basis, its fee dimension moves to `VOIDED` if it currently reads `PROVISIONAL`, `FINAL` or `INVOICED`; and if an invoice of this report is still `ISSUED`, that invoice moves to `VOID` with `void_reason` set to `REPORT_CORRECTION_REQUIRED`. Fourth, because that invoice was voided, the producer's `assessed_fee_balance_cents` is recomputed and therefore falls by that invoice's `total_cents`. Fifth, because a report of this registration is now in `CORRECTION_REQUIRED`, the registration's `compliance_posture` is recomputed and yields `AT_RISK`. Sixth, because the posture became `AT_RISK`, the registration's membership in `ACTIVE` moves to `PROBATION`. The `version` of the report, the registration, the producer, the invoice and the membership are all incremented.

Resubmission closes the correction cycle, taking the report `CORRECTION_REQUIRED` → `SUBMITTED`. It is the producer's own action and takes no body. The report must be in `CORRECTION_REQUIRED`; the acting `X-User-ID` must equal the registration's `producer_id`; the logical clock must be at or before `correction_due_at`, else `ERR_CORRECTION_WINDOW_EXPIRED`; the registration must be `ACTIVE`, else `ERR_REGISTRATION_NOT_ACTIVE`; a membership of the registration in either `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION` must exist with `fee_payment_status` `PAID`, failing which the fiscal refusals `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_MEMBERSHIP` or `ERR_MEMBERSHIP_FEE_OUTSTANDING` apply; and the report must still carry at least one line, else `ERR_REPORT_HAS_NO_LINES`. On success `correction_round` is incremented by 1, `correction_due_at` is cleared, every line's `line_weight_kg` is recomputed and then `total_weight_kg`, `fee_basis_weight_kg` and `verification_required` follow, the fee dimension moves `VOIDED` → `UNASSESSED` (or stays `UNASSESSED` if it was never priced) with `fee_subtotal_cents`, `tier_basis_kg` and `fee_variant_applied` cleared back to their defaults, the registration's `reported_weight_kg_ytd` is recomputed and therefore rises again by the new `total_weight_kg`, `submitted_by_user_id` is set to the acting header, and the `compliance_posture` is recomputed — when it returns `COMPLIANT`, the registration's `PROBATION` membership moves back to `ACTIVE`. Versions on the report, its lines, the registration and the membership are incremented. Because a corrected report can be verified a second time, and because the engagement's `round_index` records which correction round it belongs to, the rotation rule of §2.11 is what keeps the second opinion independent of the first.

Close-out is a `ProgramRegulator` action carrying a `decision` of `FINALIZE` or `REJECT` with an optional `reason`. `FINALIZE` is permitted from `SUBMITTED` or from `VERIFIED`. From `SUBMITTED` it additionally demands that `verification_required` is false — a report at or above the state's verification threshold that has not been verified is refused with `ERR_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED` — while from `VERIFIED` no such demand applies because verification has already happened. Both `FINALIZE` paths demand that the fee dimension already reads `INVOICED`, otherwise the close is refused with `ERR_INVOICE_NOT_ISSUED`; a report cannot be finalised before it has been billed. `FINALIZE` moves the submission dimension to `FINALIZED`. `REJECT` is permitted only from `CORRECTION_REQUIRED`, and only once the correction window has run out: the logical clock must be strictly after `correction_due_at`, and a rejection attempted while the window is still open is refused with `ERR_CORRECTION_WINDOW_OPEN`. `REJECT` moves the submission dimension to `REJECTED` and clears `correction_due_at`. Both decisions recompute the registration's `compliance_posture`, and because a rejected report is neither overdue nor awaiting correction, the posture typically returns to `COMPLIANT` and any `PROBATION` membership of the registration moves back to `ACTIVE`. Versions are incremented on every touched row.

Reports are readable individually — the fetch exposes both dimensions, the weight rollups, the tier basis and the fee subtotal — and as a list filtered by `registration_id`, `reporting_period_id`, `submission_status` and `fee_status`.

### 3.8. Pricing the Report: Eco-Modulation and the Two Assessment Variants

Pricing is the organisation administrator's job and it moves only the fee dimension. The request carries a single field, `variant`, whose value is either `PROVISIONAL` or `FINAL`, and the two produce genuinely different money from identical line data.

Before any arithmetic, the assessment resolves the priced context. The registration behind the report is read, and through it the state program. The membership is then selected by an explicit eligibility filter: the memberships of that registration whose `status` is `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION`. At most one such row exists, because creating a membership terminates the prior one; should that invariant ever be broken, the tie is broken by taking the latest `created_at` and, if those are equal, the highest `version`. An empty candidate set refuses the assessment with `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_MEMBERSHIP` at HTTP 402. The acting `X-User-ID` must equal that membership's `pro_id`, else `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. Rates then come from the schedule that membership names in `fee_schedule_id` — the schedule pinned at approval — and its `status` is deliberately **not** re-checked here, so a `SUPERSEDED` schedule still prices reports for the memberships that pinned it. What is checked is the pinned schedule's effective window against the reporting period: `effective_from` must be on or before the period's `data_start`, and either `effective_to` is null or `data_start` falls on or before `effective_to`. A pinned schedule whose window has been truncated past the period's start refuses the assessment with `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_NOT_EFFECTIVE_FOR_PERIOD`.

Each variant is admitted from a different report position. `PROVISIONAL` is admitted only while the fee dimension reads `UNASSESSED` and the submission dimension reads `SUBMITTED` or `UNDER_VERIFICATION`; any other submission position is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. `FINAL` is admitted from a fee dimension of `UNASSESSED` or `PROVISIONAL`, and requires the submission dimension to read `VERIFIED`, or to read `SUBMITTED` with `verification_required` false; any other combination is refused with `ERR_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`. Pricing `FINAL` over an existing `PROVISIONAL` result overwrites `tier_basis_kg`, `fee_subtotal_cents` and every per-line fee field rather than adding to them.

The **tier basis** is the one input the two variants disagree about, and it is what makes the same lines cost different amounts. Under `PROVISIONAL`, `tier_basis_kg` is the registration's `reported_weight_kg_ytd` minus this report's own `total_weight_kg`, rounded half up to three decimal places — that is, the tonnage the producer had already reported when this report arrived, **excluding** the report being priced. Under `FINAL`, `tier_basis_kg` is the registration's `reported_weight_kg_ytd` unchanged — the year-to-date tonnage **including** the report being priced. Whenever the report's own weight carries the running total across a band boundary, the two variants select different rate bands and produce different subtotals from byte-identical line data.

The tier is selected **once per material for the whole report** and applied to every kilogram of that material: this is average-rate pricing, not marginal per-band pricing. For a given component's `material_type`, the applicable rate row is the single element of the pinned schedule's `material_rates` for that material whose `tier_min_kg` is less than or equal to `tier_basis_kg` and whose `tier_max_kg` is either null or strictly greater than `tier_basis_kg`. If the pinned schedule holds no row at all for a material that appears in an `ACTIVE` component of a non-exempt line, the assessment is refused with `ERR_MATERIAL_RATE_MISSING`.

Lines are then priced in ascending order of their own `created_at`. A line whose `fee_exempt` is true is skipped entirely: its `line_fee_exact_cents` and `line_fee_cents` are both set to 0 and its `eco_mod_detail` is left empty. For every other line, each `ACTIVE` component of the line's stock keeping unit is priced in ascending `component_seq` order. The component's weight contribution, `comp_weight_kg`, is the line's `units_sold` multiplied by that component's `unit_weight_grams`, divided by one thousand, rounded half up to three decimal places.

**Eco-modulation** then adjusts the money for that component, and its evaluation order is pinned because the order is observable. First the surcharge: `malus_pct` is 0 when `recyclability_class` is `READILY_RECYCLABLE`, 20 when it is `LIMITED`, and 60 when it is `NOT_RECYCLABLE`. Next the recycled-content bonus: divide `pcr_content_pct` by ten, discard the fractional part to get a whole number of complete ten-point steps, multiply by five percentage points, and cap the result at 25 percentage points. Then the reuse bonus: 15 percentage points when `is_reusable` is true and nothing otherwise. The two bonuses are added together to give `bonus_pct`, and the net adjustment `net_pct` is the surcharge minus that combined bonus. `net_pct` is then clamped into the closed range from minus 30 to plus 60 — no component may earn more than a 30 percent discount and none may attract more than a 60 percent surcharge. Only after the clamp does the state's `eco_mod_policy` apply: under `DISCOUNT_ONLY`, any `net_pct` above zero is replaced by zero, so surcharges are waived while discounts survive intact; under `BONUS_MALUS` the clamped value stands as it is. Finally the money base is multiplied: the component's exact contribution, `comp_fee_exact_cents`, is `comp_weight_kg` multiplied by the selected row's `rate_cents_per_kg`, multiplied by one plus `net_pct` divided by one hundred. Capping the recycled-content bonus **before** the reuse bonus is added is what allows a fully recyclable, fully recycled, reusable component to reach a raw net of minus 40 and be clamped up to minus 30, while ensuring no component ever passes below that floor.

Every component priced this way contributes one audit row to the line's `eco_mod_detail`, recording the component's `material_type`, its `comp_weight_kg`, the `rate_cents_per_kg` actually applied, the `malus_pct`, the `bonus_pct`, the resulting `net_pct` and the `comp_fee_exact_cents`. The component contributions are summed with **no intermediate rounding**, and the line's total is written to `line_fee_exact_cents` rounded half up to four decimal places. `line_fee_cents` is then that four-decimal figure rounded half up to a whole cent, and it is **always** stored regardless of the state's rounding order.

The report's `fee_subtotal_cents` is derived from the lines by the state program's `fee_rounding_order`, taking the lines in ascending `created_at` order. Under `PER_LINE_THEN_SUM`, each line's `line_fee_exact_cents` is rounded half up to a whole cent first and those whole cents are added. Under `SUM_THEN_ROUND`, the four-decimal exact values are added first and the single total is rounded half up to a whole cent. §2.7 works the arithmetic through and explains why the second order can leave the sum of the stored per-line values above the report's subtotal; invoices always bill the report's subtotal.

A successful assessment writes `tier_basis_kg`, `fee_subtotal_cents` and `fee_variant_applied` on the report, writes every per-line fee field, moves the fee dimension to `PROVISIONAL` or `FINAL` as requested, leaves the submission dimension untouched, and increments the `version` of the report and of every line.

The last assessment is also readable in expanded form. A read-only breakdown returns the `tier_basis_kg` used, the `fee_variant_applied`, the `fee_rounding_order_applied` for that state, each line's exact and rounded values, and the reconciliation delta between the sum of the stored per-line values and the report's stored subtotal. It writes nothing, increments no `version`, and is available to all four roles.

### 3.9. Invoicing, Balance Conservation and Redesign Modelling

An invoice bills exactly one finally-assessed report. The organisation's administrator names the `packaging_report_id` and the `membership_id` whose pinned schedule supplies the administrative brackets; the report's fee dimension must read `FINAL`, and the named membership must cover the same registration as the report, otherwise the request is refused with `ERR_MEMBERSHIP_REGISTRATION_MISMATCH`.

The administrative fee is computed in three pinned steps, and the order matters because it resolves a dependency that only looks circular. Step one copies the report's `fee_subtotal_cents` onto the invoice. Step two selects the bracket **from that subtotal and never from the invoice total**: `admin_fee_bps_applied` takes the pinned schedule's `admin_fee_bps_low` when `fee_subtotal_cents` is strictly below 400 dollars — that is, strictly below forty thousand cents — and its `admin_fee_bps_high` otherwise. Step three computes the money: `admin_fee_cents` is `fee_subtotal_cents` multiplied by `admin_fee_bps_applied`, divided by ten thousand, rounded half up to a whole cent, and `total_cents` is `fee_subtotal_cents` plus `admin_fee_cents`. An operator would naturally call `total_cents` "the invoice amount" and would naturally read the schedule's two rates as low and high brackets "of the invoice" — but the bracket is keyed on the pre-administrative subtotal and is **never** re-derived after `total_cents` is known, even in the case where adding `admin_fee_cents` carries `total_cents` across the forty-thousand-cent break.

The invoice is created in `ISSUED` with a null `void_reason`, the report's fee dimension moves `FINAL` → `INVOICED`, and the producer's `assessed_fee_balance_cents` is recomputed and therefore rises by this invoice's `total_cents`. That balance is the sum of `total_cents` over every invoice still in `ISSUED` whose report's registration belongs to this producer, added in ascending invoice `created_at` order and using integer arithmetic only; voided invoices are outside the set. The `version` of the report and of the producer is incremented.

Voiding is the compensating operation. The organisation's administrator supplies a `void_reason`; the invoice must still be `ISSUED`, and a second void on an already-voided invoice is refused with `ERR_INVOICE_NOT_ISSUED`. Ownership applies: the acting `X-User-ID` must equal the `pro_id` reached through the invoice's membership, else `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. On success the invoice moves `ISSUED` → `VOID` with the reason recorded, the report's fee dimension moves `INVOICED` → `FINAL`, and the producer's `assessed_fee_balance_cents` is recomputed and therefore falls by that invoice's `total_cents`, restoring the exact pre-issue integer. The report's `fee_subtotal_cents` and every per-line fee field are left untouched, so the report remains priced and can be re-invoiced. Versions on the invoice, the report and the producer are incremented. `VOID` is terminal for an invoice. Note the asymmetry with the correction cascade of §3.7: an operator-initiated void returns the fee dimension to `FINAL`, whereas a void that happens as part of the correction cascade drives the fee dimension to `VOIDED` instead. Invoices are readable individually with their bracket, administrative fee and total, and as a list filtered by `packaging_report_id`, `membership_id` and `status`.

Redesign modelling closes the loop back to packaging design and is the only pricing capability that writes nothing. A `ProducerComplianceOfficer` or a `ProgramRegulator` asks what a candidate packaging specification would cost, naming the `state_program_id` whose eco-modulation policy should apply, the `fee_schedule_id` whose rate bands should be used — and here the schedule's `status` is irrelevant, so a `DRAFT` schedule may be modelled — the `tier_basis_kg` at which to price, a `units_basis` of either `BASELINE_UNITS` or `PROJECTED_UNITS`, an optional `projected_units` count, and the `candidate_components` array describing the redesigned packaging.

The unit count comes from the basis. Under `BASELINE_UNITS` the projection uses the stock keeping unit's own `units_sold_annual_estimate` and any `projected_units` in the body is ignored outright. Under `PROJECTED_UNITS` the projection uses `projected_units`, which is required in that mode; the same candidate component set therefore yields a proportionally different delta. Two component sets are then priced at the **same** unit count and the **same** tier so that the delta isolates the redesign rather than a volume or tier change: the baseline set is the stock keeping unit's current `ACTIVE` components, and the projected set is `candidate_components`. For each set, every component's weight is the unit count multiplied by that component's `unit_weight_grams`, divided by one thousand, rounded half up to three decimal places; that weight is priced through the very same eco-modulation described in §3.8, at the rate row selected for the component's material from the supplied `tier_basis_kg`, under the named state program's `eco_mod_policy`; and the component contributions are summed and rounded half up to a whole cent. The response reports `projected_units`, `projected_unit_weight_grams`, `projected_line_weight_kg`, `baseline_line_fee_cents`, `projected_line_fee_cents` and their difference as `fee_delta_cents` — the projected side minus the baseline side, so a saving is negative — together with per-material eco-modulation detail. Nothing is persisted: the stock keeping unit, its `revision_count` and the producer's tonnage rollup are all unchanged, and calling the capability twice with the same body returns byte-identical output.
