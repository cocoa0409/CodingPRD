# Water Rights Transfer & Adjudication Support API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A state water resources department administers a finite, over-appropriated resource through paperwork: certificates that record a priority date, annual reports that prove the water was actually put to beneficial use, engineering studies that translate a century of irrigation into a transferable volume, and decrees that finally settle who is senior to whom. Today that work is distributed across spreadsheets, filing cabinets and the institutional memory of individual staff engineers. The consequence is not merely administrative friction — it is double-allocation. When two transfer applications escrow volume against the same right, when a lease commits credit that a decree has already severed, or when an examiner recommends more acre-feet than a claimant ever claimed, the department discovers the error only after the water has been delivered.

This service is the department's system of record for the entire transfer and adjudication pipeline. It documents rights and their points of diversion, maintains an append-only ledger of annual beneficial use, quantifies historical consumptive use under two competing engineering methods, escrows and settles the volume moved by change-of-use and point-of-diversion applications, adjudicates protests through negotiation or hearing, imposes and prices mitigation conditions, assesses abandonment and forfeiture risk, runs a basin water bank with seasonal lease assignments, and tracks general-stream adjudication claims from filing through decree. Two quantities are conserved absolutely: the acre-feet recorded against a water right, and the credit recorded against a water bank account. Every forward operation in this service has a compensating operation, and the pair must leave both identities exactly true.

The design priority is defensibility rather than throughput. Volumes are computed by pinned evaluation orders so that two engineers reading the same records reach the same number to the cent. Dispositions are structurally separated from the technical work they rely on, and separated again at the level of the individual operator: the person who certified a consumptive-use study may never be the person who signs the transfer decision that rests on it. Time is a supplied value rather than a wall-clock reading, so that a filing deadline, a compliance due date and a lease delivery window can all be reasoned about deterministically.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

Every business collection in this service is a root-level peer resource. There is no single owning aggregate root: water rights, transfer applications, protests, approval conditions, water bank accounts, lease assignments, decree dockets and adjudication claims are all addressed directly, and only beneficial-use records, consumptive-use studies, points of diversion, injury analyses and an application's approval-condition listing are reached through a parent path.

### 2.1. Operator Identity and Role-Based Access Control

Every request must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting user, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role that user is acting in. If either header is absent or blank, or if `X-User-Role` carries a value outside the five declared roles, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). If the headers are well formed but the named role is not authorized for the endpoint being called, the rejection is `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403). These two checks are the first and second things this service ever evaluates, in that order.

The five roles are `Claimant`, `WaterRightsAnalyst`, `HearingOfficer`, `DecisionAuthority` and `BasinAdjudicator`. They form a deliberate chain of custody: the claimant is the party of record, the analyst performs technical documentation and quantification, the hearing officer adjudicates protests, the decision authority signs the disposition, and the basin adjudicator owns the decree docket and administrative sanctions.

When `X-User-Role` is `Claimant`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Claimant` row; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Claimant` compare the resource's claimant reference to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, right, point-of-diversion, beneficial-use-record, consumptive-use-study, application, injury-analysis and approval-condition operations compare against the `claimant_id` of the governing `WaterRight`; water-bank-account and lease operations compare against the `claimant_id` of the `WaterBankAccount`; protest operations compare against the protest's `protestant_claimant_id`; adjudication-claim operations compare against the `claimant_id` of the claimed right; and reading a single `Claimant` compares against that row's own `id`. The check is indifferent to whether the operation reads or writes: it applies to every operation that names one specific resource in its path, and to a listing nested beneath a named parent right or application, where the parent's owner is what is compared. It does not apply to the top-level collection listings, which are neither ownership-filtered nor ownership-refused — a `Claimant` calling one of those sees every row matching the supplied query filters, whoever owns it. A `DecreeDocket` references no claimant at all, so nothing addressed directly on a docket is ownership-scoped. A mismatch is `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403). Lodging an objection against another party's claim is deliberately outside this rule — an objector must *not* own the objected claim, and that inverse check carries its own error code.

When `X-User-Role` is `WaterRightsAnalyst`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it where a transition writes it — as `reported_by_user_id` on a `BeneficialUseRecord`, as `certified_by_user_id` on an `HcuStudy`, as `prepared_by_user_id` on an `InjuryAnalysis`, or as `imposed_by_user_id` on an `ApprovalCondition` — but never compares it to any entity's `id`. The same holds for `HearingOfficer`, whose identifier is recorded as `resolved_by_user_id` on a resolved `Protest`; for `DecisionAuthority`, whose identifier is recorded as `decided_by_user_id` on a decided `TransferApplication` and as `imposed_by_user_id` on the conditions that disposition creates; and for `BasinAdjudicator`, whose identifier is likewise opaque and is recorded only where one of those same audit fields applies. None of these four roles is ownership-scoped, and none of their identifiers is ever resolved against a `Claimant` row.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

List endpoints accept `limit` and `offset` as query parameters. `limit` defaults to 10 when omitted, with a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 100; `offset` defaults to 0 and may not be negative. A `limit` outside the inclusive range of 1 through 100, or a negative `offset`, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400). Every list response reports `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside `meta`.

Unless a specific listing states otherwise, list results are ordered by `created_at` ascending. Several collections deliberately depart from that default because their business ordering differs: water rights and transfer applications are listed newest first, by `created_at` descending with `id` ascending used purely as a display-stability tie-break and never as a business discriminator; beneficial-use records are ordered by `use_year` ascending then `created_at` ascending; lease assignments are ordered by `lease_start_date` ascending then `created_at` ascending; and adjudication claims are ordered by `claimed_priority_date` ascending then `created_at` ascending, which is the same seniority ordering used to assign rank inside a decree. No listing in this service ever orders by `id`.

### 2.4. The Logical Clock as the Sole Time Source

Nothing in this service reads wall-clock time. A logical current UTC instant is maintained by the service and is the only time source consulted by any temporal validation or calculation — protest deadlines, claim filing deadlines, compliance due dates, lease delivery windows, forfeiture look-back windows and the recording of `certified_at` all read it and nothing else. At service startup the logical clock is mandatorily initialised to `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, so a service that has never been told otherwise behaves as though the current instant were that value. Any of the five roles may move the clock, supplying a `current_utc` instant; the clock may be moved forward or backward freely, and the response echoes the `current_utc` instant now in effect. Setting the clock touches no entity row and bumps no version.

### 2.5. Common Resource Properties and the Append-Only Evidence Ledger

Every persisted resource carries an `id` that is a server-minted UUID with no type prefix, and a `created_at` timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC form with three fractional digits. Almost every resource additionally carries an `updated_at` timestamp in the same format, refreshed on every mutation, and a `version` integer that begins at 1 on creation and increments by exactly 1 on each mutation of that row. Clients never supply `id`, `created_at`, `updated_at` or `version`.

`BeneficialUseRecord` is the single exception. Annual beneficial-use reports are immutable evidence: there is no update and no delete operation for them, and a record carries only `id` and `created_at` — it has no `updated_at` field and no `version` field at all. Everything else in the service — `Claimant`, `WaterRight`, `PointOfDiversion`, `HcuStudy`, `TransferApplication`, `InjuryAnalysis`, `Protest`, `ApprovalCondition`, `WaterBankAccount`, `LeaseAssignment`, `DecreeDocket` and `AdjudicationClaim` — carries the full complement of four common properties.

### 2.6. Path Resolution and Nested Sub-Resource Ownership

Path parameters are resolved strictly left to right, and the error returned belongs to the leftmost segment that fails to resolve. A request naming a right that does not exist fails with `ERR_WATER_RIGHT_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404) before anything nested beneath that right is examined. The parallel existence codes are `ERR_CLAIMANT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_POINT_OF_DIVERSION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_HCU_STUDY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TRANSFER_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INJURY_ANALYSIS_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PROTEST_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_APPROVAL_CONDITION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_WATER_BANK_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LEASE_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DECREE_DOCKET_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_ADJUDICATION_CLAIM_NOT_FOUND`, each at HTTP 404.

A sub-resource identifier must additionally resolve to a row whose parent reference matches the parent named earlier in the path. A consumptive-use study addressed beneath a right that does not own it is treated as missing rather than as a permission failure, and the same treatment applies to approval-condition and adjudication-claim sub-paths. Foreign keys supplied in a request body are resolved with the same rigour: `claimant_id`, `water_right_id`, `hcu_study_id`, `proposed_point_of_diversion_id`, `point_of_diversion_id`, `water_bank_account_id`, `decree_docket_id`, `transfer_application_id` and `protestant_claimant_id` must each name an existing row, and a body foreign key that does not resolve yields the corresponding not-found code at HTTP 404.

### 2.7. Stale-Version Protection on Ledger-Rewriting Writes

Three write operations rewrite a ledger that the caller must have read in order to decide the write was sensible. Each therefore requires the client to declare which revision of the parent it observed, as a non-optional request field, and each rejects the write when that declaration no longer matches the parent's stored `version`.

`POST /hcu-studies/{id}/certify` requires `expected_water_right_version` carrying the client's last observed `version` of the parent `WaterRight`. If it does not equal the right's current `version`, the certification is rejected with `ERR_STALE_RIGHT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Certification legitimately supersedes whatever study was previously certified and overwrites the right's certified volume, so a second certification is a valid product operation rather than an invalid transition — the stale-version check is the only thing that prevents a client from replacing a certification it never saw.

`POST /transfer-applications/{id}/submit` likewise requires `expected_water_right_version` matching the parent `WaterRight`'s current `version`, rejecting a mismatch with `ERR_STALE_RIGHT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Several applications may legitimately be pending against one right at once, so a repeat submission is a valid operation on identical state; the version check is what prevents a client from escrowing volume against a transferable figure that an earlier escrow already consumed. The resubmission of a revised application carries and enforces the same field under the same error code.

`POST /water-bank-accounts/{id}/deposits` requires `expected_water_bank_account_version` matching the parent `WaterBankAccount`'s current `version`, rejecting a mismatch with `ERR_STALE_ACCOUNT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Repeated deposits are legitimate on identical state, so the version check is the only thing that stops a client committing a deposit decision made against a credit ledger it never saw.

No other endpoint in this service accepts a version field. Every other write proceeds on the state it finds.

### 2.8. Volumetric Precision and Rounding Discipline

All rounding in this service is round-half-up. Acre-foot volumes, dollar amounts and credit balances are carried at 2 decimal places unless a specific calculation pins a wider intermediate precision, and where a calculation does pin one, the intermediate width is part of the contract rather than an implementation detail: a value rounded to 3 decimal places before being summed produces a different final cent than the same value summed raw. Surcharge rates are stored at 3 decimal places. Ratios internal to the forfeiture assessment are carried at 4 decimal places and diversion rates are summed at 3 decimal places. Day counts are always whole days, truncated downward, computed in UTC.

Boundary inclusivity is stated explicitly wherever a threshold appears in this document, because the department's rules are frequently inclusive on one side only. Two habits recur: a budget or capacity gate accepts exact equality, while a deadline gate treats the deadline instant itself as already past.

### 2.9. Two Conserved Ledgers

The first conserved quantity lives on each water right. A right's certified volume is partitioned at all times into exactly three buckets, and the identity `certified_hcu_af` equals `transferable_af` plus `encumbered_af` plus `transferred_away_af` must hold exactly at 2 decimal places after every operation. `encumbered_af` is the sum of the requested volume of every transfer application on the right that is currently pending — that is, in `SUBMITTED`, `PROTEST_PERIOD`, `UNDER_PROTEST`, `REVISION_REQUESTED` or `READY_FOR_DECISION` — plus the bank hold, which is the total volume deposited into water bank accounts from this right minus the volume already consumed by this right's leases that have reached `COMPLETED` or `CANCELLED`, each of those two sums rounded to 2 decimal places before they are added and the total rounded again to 2 decimal places. `transferred_away_af` is the sum of the requested volume of every application on the right that reached `APPROVED` or `APPROVED_WITH_CONDITIONS`, plus the consumed volume of this right's `COMPLETED` or `CANCELLED` leases, rounded to 2 decimal places. `transferable_af` is `certified_hcu_af` less `encumbered_af` less `transferred_away_af`, rounded to 2 decimal places, and may never be negative — a derivation that would drive it below zero reports 0.00 instead. `available_credit_af` is floored the same way. Every forward action and its compensating action must leave the identity true: submit against withdraw, submit against denial, deposit against lease cancellation.

The second conserved quantity lives on each water bank account, where the identity `deposited_credit_af` equals `available_credit_af` plus `committed_credit_af` plus `leased_out_af` must likewise hold exactly at 2 decimal places. `committed_credit_af` is the summed requested volume of this account's leases in `RESERVED` or `SUSPENDED`; `leased_out_af` is the summed requested volume of its `ACTIVE` leases; `deposited_credit_af` is the total volume deposited into the account minus the consumed volume of its `COMPLETED` or `CANCELLED` leases; and `available_credit_af` is `deposited_credit_af` less `committed_credit_af` less `leased_out_af`, never negative. Each of those four figures is rounded to 2 decimal places. `suspended_lease_count` counts this account's leases currently in `SUSPENDED`. Creating a lease moves credit from available to committed; cancelling a `RESERVED` lease moves it back, and must restore not merely the identity but each individual term to its prior value.

A `FORFEITED` water right is exempt from the first aggregation entirely: all four of its volume figures are pinned to 0.00, which satisfies the identity trivially.

### 2.10. Cascade Discipline and Derived Status

Several operations in this service reach several entities deep. Where they do, every link is mandatory and the document describes each link in the workflow that fires it. On every row a cascade touches, `updated_at` is refreshed and `version` is incremented by 1 — including rows the caller never named, such as a lease suspended because a condition on a distant application was violated.

A water right's `status` is never set directly by a ledger operation. It is derived, and the precedence is pinned and evaluated top to bottom with the first match winning: a right already `FORFEITED` stays `FORFEITED`, because forfeiture is terminal and sticky; otherwise a right already `SUSPENDED` stays `SUSPENDED`, because reinstatement is the only route out; otherwise a right that has never been activated reads `UNADJUDICATED`; otherwise a right whose `transferred_away_af` is strictly greater than 0.00 reads `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED`; otherwise a right whose `encumbered_af` is strictly greater than 0.00 reads `ENCUMBERED`; otherwise it reads `ACTIVE`. Because `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED` outranks `ENCUMBERED`, a right holding both a completed transfer and a fresh escrow reads `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED`.

A water bank account's `posture` is derived in the same style, and its precedence is equally pinned: `FROZEN` dominates `RESTRICTED`, which dominates `OPEN`. The account reads `FROZEN` when the holding claimant's `standing_status` is `BARRED` or when `suspended_lease_count` is at least 1. Failing that, it reads `RESTRICTED` when the holding claimant's `standing_status` is `DELINQUENT` or when `available_credit_af` is strictly below 5.00 acre-feet. Otherwise it reads `OPEN`. The credit floor is strict: an available credit of exactly 5.00 acre-feet is not `RESTRICTED`, while 4.99 is; and a single suspended lease is already enough for `FROZEN`. Posture is recomputed whenever the account's credit ledger is recomputed, whenever a claimant's standing is administratively set, and as the final link of both the condition-violation cascade and the fee-payment cascade.

A decree docket's `phase` is normally advanced deliberately, but it also bubbles up from its children: when the last outstanding claim on a docket is decreed, the docket advances itself.

### 2.11. Validation Precedence

Validations are evaluated in one strict ascending priority order across the whole service. The first violated rule wins, its error is returned, and no later rule is evaluated — so a request that breaks three rules at once produces exactly one, fully determined error code. The ladder from the top is: header presence and role membership (`ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`, then `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED`); path-parameter existence, then nested-parent ownership of a sub-resource identifier; claimant ownership of the target resource (`ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`); request-body well-formedness (`ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`); body foreign-key existence; then the domain-specific referential, uniqueness, concurrency, state, safety, temporal, constraint and fiscal families in that sequence.

Within that ordering, three consequences are worth stating outright because they decide real cases. Body well-formedness outranks every business rule but is itself outranked by ownership, so a `Claimant` poking at another party's resource with a malformed body receives `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` rather than `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. The concurrency checks sit above every state and business check, so a stale version declaration surfaces before the service considers whether the transition was even legal. And the safety family sits above the fiscal family, so a claimant who is not in good standing is rejected for standing before the service ever consults the volume budget.

### 2.12. Segregation of Duties

Segregation is enforced twice over. Structurally, the role lists in §4 keep preparatory work away from dispositive power: an analyst can neither adjudicate a protest nor decide an application, a hearing officer never renders a final disposition, a decision authority is barred from every preparatory step, and the basin adjudicator — despite being the department's administrative backstop for documentation, banking and leasing — cannot adjudicate protests, cannot decide transfers and cannot file a protest.

Per-user, the check is sharper. The operator identifier issuing a transfer disposition must differ from the `certified_by_user_id` recorded on the consumptive-use study the application relies on, and must differ from the `resolved_by_user_id` recorded on every protest filed against that application. Either collision is `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). Because these identifiers are opaque, the same human acting first as an analyst and later as a decision authority is caught by the identifier comparison even though the role changed.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Claimants of Record and the Documentation of a Right

Everything in the department's files hangs off a party of record. Registering a claimant — a capability held by analysts and by the basin adjudicator — takes a `legal_name` of 1 to 160 non-blank characters, a `contact_email` that must contain an at-sign because it is the notice address to which protests and decisions are served, and an `entity_type` drawn from `INDIVIDUAL`, `IRRIGATION_DISTRICT`, `MUNICIPALITY` and `TRIBE`. The entity class drives no logic and exists for reporting. A newly registered claimant begins with `standing_status` set to `IN_GOOD_STANDING`, an `outstanding_fee_balance_usd` of 0.00 and an `open_application_count` of 0.

Both of those counters are derived and neither is ever supplied by a client. `outstanding_fee_balance_usd` is the sum, carried at 2 decimal places, of the assessed total fee of every approval condition whose transfer application sits on a water right belonging to this claimant and whose fiscal dimension still reads `OUTSTANDING`. `open_application_count` is the number of transfer applications on this claimant's rights whose status is one of `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `PROTEST_PERIOD`, `UNDER_PROTEST`, `REVISION_REQUESTED` or `READY_FOR_DECISION`.

Standing is otherwise administrative. Only the basin adjudicator may set `standing_status` directly, supplying one of `IN_GOOD_STANDING`, `DELINQUENT` or `BARRED`. `BARRED` is a sanction that blocks protest filing and fee payment outright; `DELINQUENT` is normally reached automatically the moment a mitigation fee falls due and is cleared automatically once the outstanding balance returns to exactly 0.00. Setting standing bumps the claimant's `version` and forces every water bank account of that claimant to recompute its posture, since a barred holder freezes an account and a delinquent holder restricts it.

Documenting a right is likewise analyst and adjudicator work. The payload names the `claimant_id` of the holder of record, a `right_number` of 1 to 40 characters that must be unique across every right in the service — a collision is `ERR_DUPLICATE_RIGHT_NUMBER` (HTTP 409) — the `priority_date` established by first beneficial use, a `source_stream_code` of 1 to 24 characters naming the stream or aquifer diverted from, a `purpose_of_use` drawn from `IRRIGATION`, `MUNICIPAL`, `STOCKWATER`, `INDUSTRIAL` and `INSTREAM_FLOW`, a `place_of_use_acres` figure of zero or more, an `authorized_diversion_af` face-value annual limit strictly greater than zero, an `authorized_rate_cfs` instantaneous rate strictly greater than zero, and an optional `bank_enrolled` flag that defaults to false. Earlier priority dates are senior; the priority date is the primary sort key for adjudication ranking and never changes as a side effect of anything. A new right lands in `UNADJUDICATED` with all four volume buckets at 0.00, so nothing is transferable until a consumptive-use study has been certified, and its forfeiture assessment is run immediately so that `forfeiture_risk_score` and `forfeiture_risk_band` are populated from birth.

Rights can be listed with optional filters on `claimant_id`, on `status`, and on `forfeiture_risk_band`, alongside `limit` and `offset`. Retrieving a single right returns its full four-bucket ledger together with its risk figures.

A headgate is registered beneath its right as a point of diversion. The payload carries a `legal_description` of 1 to 120 characters giving the quarter-quarter location, a `stream_reach_code` of 1 to 24 characters, a `max_rate_cfs` strictly greater than zero recording the physical capacity of the diversion works, and a `stream_miles_from_headwater` figure of zero or more. That river-mile figure matters later: the absolute difference between the existing and the proposed headgate's values is what an analyst measures when attenuating an instantaneous-depletion injury calculation for distance. A new headgate always starts in `PROPOSED`; it reaches `AUTHORIZED` either through the right's activation or as a side effect of an approved point-of-diversion change, and it reaches the terminal `RETIRED` only when a newly authorized headgate supersedes it. Points of diversion beneath a right can be listed with an optional `status` filter.

Annual beneficial use is reported into an append-only evidence ledger, and claimants may file their own reports alongside analysts and the adjudicator. Each record names a `use_year` between 1850 and 2100, a `reported_diversion_af` figure of zero or more recording the acre-feet actually diverted and applied, an `irrigated_acres` figure of zero or more recording the acreage actually farmed, and a `verification_source` drawn from `METER`, `AERIAL_IMAGERY`, `AFFIDAVIT` and `NONE`. Neither figure is narrowed on the way in: unlike the volumes that §2.8's 2-decimal default governs, `reported_diversion_af` and `irrigated_acres` are stored and echoed exactly as supplied, at whatever precision the reporter sent, because §3.3 re-rounds the reported figure itself at 3 decimal places downstream. The service stamps `reported_by_user_id` with the calling operator identifier. Only one record may exist per right per calendar year; a second attempt at the same year is `ERR_DUPLICATE_USE_YEAR` (HTTP 409). Appending a record re-derives the parent right's forfeiture assessment and bumps the right's `version`. Records beneath a right can be listed with optional `use_year_from` and `use_year_to` bounds.

The evidentiary distinction carried by `verification_source` is the hinge of two separate rules and the two rules read it differently. Only `METER` and `AERIAL_IMAGERY` rows are admissible in a consumptive-use quantification: `AFFIDAVIT` and `NONE` rows are worthless for computing how much water may be moved. But all four values participate in the forfeiture look-back, because an affidavit is still evidence that the water was used, even if it is not evidence of how much.

### 3.2. Perfecting a Right, Abandonment Risk, and Forfeiture

A right is perfected by an analyst or the adjudicator, moving it `UNADJUDICATED → ACTIVE`. Perfection is reachable only from `UNADJUDICATED`: a right in any other derived status, forfeited and suspended rights included, is `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409), and that state gate is evaluated before the evidentiary one below, so a second perfection of an already-active right reports the state error rather than anything about its evidence. The substantive precondition is evidentiary: at least one beneficial-use record on the right must carry a `verification_source` of `METER` or `AERIAL_IMAGERY`. Affidavit-only evidence cannot perfect a right, and an attempt on such a right is `ERR_NO_VERIFIED_USE_EVIDENCE` (HTTP 422). Perfecting the right also authorizes its infrastructure: every point of diversion on the right still in `PROPOSED` moves to `AUTHORIZED`, each such headgate's `version` bumped. The forfeiture assessment is re-run, refreshing `forfeiture_risk_score`, `forfeiture_risk_band` and `consecutive_nonuse_years`, and the right's own `version` is bumped.

Abandonment risk is scored on demand, and the projection stored on the right's row — `forfeiture_risk_score`, `forfeiture_risk_band` and `consecutive_nonuse_years` — is re-stamped by every operation that recomputes that right's volume ledger, which covers every event that could move the score: appending a beneficial-use record, perfecting a right, reinstating a right, and any movement of the right into or out of `SUSPENDED`. A plain read is deliberately not such an operation: reading a right, singly or inside a listing, re-derives its volume ledger and its derived status but reports the stored projection exactly as the last of those operations left it, so advancing the logical clock alone never moves a stored score. The dedicated assessment view described below always recomputes as of the logical clock without persisting anything, which is what makes the two readings differ. The scoring window is a closed ten-year band ending with the year *before* the calendar year of the logical clock, taken in UTC; the current calendar year is deliberately excluded because its reporting year is not yet complete. Within that window, a year counts as a used year when a record exists for it whose `reported_diversion_af` is at least 0.50 acre-feet. Every other year in the window is a non-use year, and that emphatically includes years for which no record exists at all. `consecutive_nonuse_years` is the length of the longest unbroken run of non-use years inside the window.

The score itself is a weighted sum of four terms less one credit. Take the records inside the window and let their count be the denominator, floored at one so that a right with no records at all is still scoreable. The unverified ratio is the proportion of those records whose `verification_source` is `AFFIDAVIT` or `NONE`, rounded to 4 decimal places. The mean reported diversion is the average of `reported_diversion_af` across those records, rounded to 3 decimal places. Utilization is that mean divided by the right's `authorized_diversion_af`, rounded to 4 decimal places and then capped at 1.0 so that over-diversion never reads as negative underuse. The raw score is then: nine points for each consecutive non-use year, plus twenty-five points multiplied by the unverified ratio, plus thirty points multiplied by one minus utilization, plus a flat twelve-point penalty when the right's `status` is currently `SUSPENDED`, less a flat ten-point credit when any record in the window carries a `verification_source` of `METER`. That raw figure is clamped into the range zero through one hundred and rounded to 2 decimal places.

Bands are inclusive at the bottom: a score of 70.00 or above reads `HIGH`, a score from 40.00 up to but not including 70.00 reads `ELEVATED`, and anything below reads `LOW`. A score of exactly 40.00 is `ELEVATED` and 39.99 is `LOW`. A dedicated read-only assessment view returns the whole breakdown as of the logical clock: `water_right_id`, the `lookback_start_year` and `lookback_end_year` bounding the window (the latter being the year before the clock's calendar year), the `nonuse_year_count` of non-use years in the window, `consecutive_nonuse_years`, the `unverified_component` (the unverified ratio multiplied by twenty-five, at 2 decimal places), the `underuse_component` (one minus utilization, multiplied by thirty, at 2 decimal places), the `nonuse_component` (consecutive non-use years multiplied by nine), the `suspended_penalty` of either zero or twelve, the `meter_credit` of either zero or ten, the resulting `forfeiture_risk_score` and `forfeiture_risk_band`, and a `forfeiture_eligible` boolean.

Statutory forfeiture eligibility is that boolean, and it is inclusive: exactly five consecutive non-use years is eligible, four is not. Declaring forfeiture — analyst or adjudicator work, accompanied by a non-blank `finding_note` — is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409) when the right already reads `FORFEITED`, and with `ERR_FORFEITURE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (HTTP 422) when `consecutive_nonuse_years` falls short of five; the sticky-state check is evaluated first. Eligibility is the only other gate, so a right in `UNADJUDICATED`, `SUSPENDED`, `ACTIVE`, `ENCUMBERED` or `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED` may all be forfeited once the non-use run is long enough.

When it does succeed, the declaration is the deepest cascade in the service and `FORFEITED` is terminal and sticky. The first link zeroes all four volume buckets — `certified_hcu_af`, `encumbered_af`, `transferred_away_af` and `transferable_af` all become 0.00 — and bumps the right's `version`. The second link reaches the right's dependents: every transfer application on the right in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `PROTEST_PERIOD`, `UNDER_PROTEST`, `REVISION_REQUESTED` or `READY_FOR_DECISION` moves to `DENIED` with its `escrowed_af` released to 0.00, any still-unresolved protests on those applications moving to `DISMISSED` and any `DRAFT` injury analysis moving to `VOIDED`, with `pending_review_count` decremented accordingly; and every lease assignment on the right in `RESERVED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` moves to `CANCELLED`, settled by the lease settlement calculation with the unused portion returned to its account's available credit. The third link reaches the conditions of those newly denied applications: every condition whose compliance dimension reads `IMPOSED` moves to `RELEASED`, and every condition whose fiscal dimension reads `OUTSTANDING` moves to `REVERSED`, decreasing the claimant's `outstanding_fee_balance_usd` by each reversed total fee. Exactly as after a voluntary payment, if that balance reaches precisely 0.00 while the claimant is `DELINQUENT`, standing returns to `IN_GOOD_STANDING`. "Affected" is exact here, because this cascade reaches a bounded set of parties: the right's own holder, but only if at least one condition actually had a fee reversed, plus the holder of every account that funded one of the leases just cancelled. Each claimant so reached has its standing re-evaluated and its `version` bumped, and every water bank account belonging to any of them then re-runs its credit ledger and its posture and bumps its `version`. Where the cascade reversed no fee and cancelled no lease it reaches no claimant at all: not one account row is touched, and the holder's derived counters are re-derived in place without its `version` moving.

Reinstatement is the other direction, and it closes the cycle `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`. An analyst or the adjudicator supplies a non-blank `reinstatement_note`. Reinstatement is reachable only from `SUSPENDED`: a right in any other derived status is `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409), that state gate being evaluated after the note is validated and before either of the two conditions below. Two conditions must then hold: no approval condition on any application of this right may still read `VIOLATED`, and `consecutive_nonuse_years` must be strictly below five. Failure on either count is `ERR_VIOLATION_UNRESOLVED` (HTTP 409). On success the right leaves `SUSPENDED` and its status is re-derived — which means it may well land on `ENCUMBERED` or `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED` rather than `ACTIVE`, depending on what its ledger holds — the forfeiture score is recomputed without the twelve-point suspension penalty, and the right's `version` is bumped. What reinstatement pointedly does *not* do is touch a single lease or bank account row: leases suspended by the condition-violation cascade are not auto-resumed, and each requires its own separate resumption decision.

### 3.3. Quantifying Historical Consumptive Use

Nothing may be transferred out of a right until the department has quantified what the right has historically consumed, as distinct from what its certificate nominally authorizes. That quantification is a consumptive-use study, and only a `WaterRightsAnalyst` may run one — it is the single operation the basin adjudicator is barred from despite otherwise sharing every right-documentation capability.

A study declares a `method` of either `DIVERSION_BASED` or `CIR_BASED` and a study period given as `study_period_start_year` and `study_period_end_year`, both inclusive calendar years, with the end year at least equal to the start year. An end year earlier than the start year is `ERR_INVALID_WINDOW` (HTTP 422). The quantification runs synchronously at creation, so the response already carries the finished figures, and the study is stored in `DRAFT` — advisory only, moving no volume bucket on the right.

The admissible record set is narrower than the forfeiture window. A beneficial-use record qualifies when it belongs to the study's right, its `use_year` falls within the study period with both bounds inclusive, its `verification_source` is `METER` or `AERIAL_IMAGERY`, and its `reported_diversion_af` is strictly greater than zero. Rows resting on affidavit or on no evidence at all are excluded, and so are rows reporting zero diversion, even though both kinds still count toward the forfeiture look-back. Qualifying rows are taken in ascending `use_year` order, which is a total order because only one record may exist per right per year, so no tie-break is needed. `qualifying_year_count` is how many rows survive that filter. When none do, the request is rejected with `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_USE_HISTORY` (HTTP 422) — an empty study period is refused, never quantified as zero acre-feet.

The volume is then built in five pinned steps. First, a per-year consumptive use is computed for each qualifying row and each of those per-year values is rounded to 3 decimal places *before* anything is summed. Under `DIVERSION_BASED` the per-year value is that year's `reported_diversion_af` multiplied by a ditch efficiency of 0.65, which reads the metered or imaged diversion volume and discounts it for conveyance losses; `irrigated_acres` is ignored entirely. Under `CIR_BASED` the per-year value is that year's `irrigated_acres` multiplied by a crop irrigation requirement of 2.10 acre-feet per acre, which reads the requirement of the acreage actually farmed and ignores `reported_diversion_af` beyond having used it as an admissibility filter. For identical records the two methods return materially different volumes, so which method gets certified determines how much water the right may ever move.

Second, the already-rounded per-year values are summed, that sum is divided by `qualifying_year_count`, and the quotient is rounded to 3 decimal places to give `average_annual_cu_af`. The order here is pinned and consequential: rounding each year first and then averaging is the specified reading, and averaging the raw values with a single rounding at the end yields a different final cent. Third, a face-value cap is computed as the right's `authorized_diversion_af` multiplied by 0.65, rounded to 3 decimal places, and the smaller of the average and that cap is carried forward. Fourth, a short-history haircut applies when `qualifying_year_count` is strictly fewer than three years, in which case `short_history_haircut_applied` is set true and the carried value is multiplied by 0.85; the comparison is strict, so exactly three qualifying years takes no haircut while two years does. Fifth and last, the result is rounded once to 2 decimal places to give `transferable_cu_af`.

Studies beneath a right can be listed with an optional `status` filter, and a single study can be read directly to inspect its certification identity.

Certification is what makes a study bind. An analyst or the adjudicator certifies a `DRAFT` study, declaring `expected_water_right_version` as described in §2.7. Three things must hold: the parent right's `status` must be one of `ACTIVE`, `ENCUMBERED`, `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED` or `SUSPENDED` — a right that has been forfeited can never back a certification, and a violation of this gate is `ERR_RIGHT_NOT_TRANSFERABLE` (HTTP 409); `qualifying_year_count` must be at least 1; and the declared version must match. Certifying anything that is not in `DRAFT` is `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409).

On success the study moves `DRAFT → CERTIFIED`, `certified_by_user_id` is stamped with the calling operator identifier and `certified_at` with the logical clock, and the study's `version` is bumped. Whatever study was previously `CERTIFIED` on the same right, if any, moves to the terminal `SUPERSEDED` with its own `version` bumped — a right therefore has at most one certified study at any instant. The right's `certified_hcu_af` is overwritten with the newly certified study's `transferable_cu_af`, `transferable_af` is recomputed as certified volume less encumbered less transferred-away so that the conserved identity of §2.9 still holds, the right's `status` is re-derived by the pinned precedence, and the right's `version` is bumped. The recorded certifier identity is what later bars that individual from signing dispositions on applications built atop this study.

### 3.4. Drafting, Submitting and Re-scoping a Transfer Application

An application is drafted against a certified quantification by the claimant, an analyst or the adjudicator. Its payload names the `water_right_id` of the source right, the `hcu_study_id` of the quantification it relies on, an `application_type` drawn from `CHANGE_OF_USE`, `POINT_OF_DIVERSION_CHANGE`, `PLACE_OF_USE_CHANGE` and `COMBINED`, and a `requested_af` figure strictly greater than zero at 2 decimal places recording how much historical consumptive use the applicant asks to move.

Three further fields describe the requested change, and all three are declared optional at the transport level because which of them is mandatory depends entirely on the type. `proposed_point_of_diversion_id` must be present exactly when the type is `POINT_OF_DIVERSION_CHANGE` or `COMBINED` and must be absent otherwise; `proposed_purpose_of_use`, drawn from the same five-value set as a right's authorized purpose, must be present exactly when the type is `CHANGE_OF_USE` or `COMBINED`; and `proposed_place_of_use_acres`, a figure of zero or more, must be present exactly when the type is `PLACE_OF_USE_CHANGE` or `COMBINED`. Presence where absence is required is as much a failure as the reverse, and either direction is `ERR_APPLICATION_SCOPE_INCOMPLETE` (HTTP 422). Separately, the referenced study must belong to the same right the application names, and a proposed headgate, when supplied, must belong to that same right; either mismatch is `ERR_STUDY_RIGHT_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422). Scope coherence is checked before the study-ownership check.

A drafted application sits in `DRAFT`. No volume is escrowed, `escrowed_af` reads 0.00, and no bucket on the right has moved. Applications can be listed with optional filters on `water_right_id`, `hcu_study_id`, `proposed_point_of_diversion_id` and `status`, plus `limit` and `offset`; a single application can be read to inspect its escrow, protest counters and decision identity.

Submission is where volume actually moves, and it is the clearest illustration of the priority ladder at work. The submitter — claimant, analyst or adjudicator — supplies only `expected_water_right_version`. Five gates apply, and their relative order is fixed: the declared version must match the right's current `version`, or the submission is `ERR_STALE_RIGHT_VERSION` (HTTP 412); the application must be in `DRAFT` and the relied-upon study must be in `CERTIFIED`, since a `DRAFT` or `SUPERSEDED` study cannot support a submission and either failure is `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409); the right's `status` must be `ACTIVE`, `ENCUMBERED` or `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED`, with `SUSPENDED` and `FORFEITED` both yielding `ERR_RIGHT_NOT_TRANSFERABLE` (HTTP 409); the acting claimant's `standing_status` must be `IN_GOOD_STANDING`, with `DELINQUENT` or `BARRED` yielding `ERR_CLAIMANT_NOT_IN_GOOD_STANDING` (HTTP 403); and only then is the volume budget consulted, requiring `requested_af` to be no greater than the right's `transferable_af`, with equality accepted and any excess rejected as `ERR_TRANSFERABLE_VOLUME_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 422). A delinquent claimant asking for more water than the right holds therefore learns about the standing problem, not the volume problem. When the caller is acting as a `Claimant`, `X-User-ID` must equal the `claimant_id` of the application's right.

A successful submission moves the application `DRAFT → SUBMITTED` and sets `escrowed_af` to `requested_af`. On the right, `encumbered_af` increases by that same amount and `transferable_af` decreases by it, conserving the ledger identity; the right's status is re-derived, which is what carries an otherwise idle right `ACTIVE → ENCUMBERED`; the claimant's `open_application_count` is recomputed; and both the application's and the right's `version` are bumped. An application's `escrowed_af` equals its `requested_af` for exactly as long as its status is one of `SUBMITTED`, `PROTEST_PERIOD`, `UNDER_PROTEST`, `REVISION_REQUESTED` or `READY_FOR_DECISION`, and reads 0.00 in every other status.

Resubmission closes the revision leg described in §3.6 and moves the application `REVISION_REQUESTED → SUBMITTED`. It carries a fresh `requested_af` together with `expected_water_right_version`, and it is available to the claimant, an analyst or the adjudicator. The escrow is swapped atomically rather than released and re-taken, which changes the budget arithmetic: the new `requested_af` must be no greater than the right's `transferable_af` plus the application's existing `escrowed_af`, because the existing escrow is being exchanged rather than double-counted. Equality is accepted; excess is `ERR_TRANSFERABLE_VOLUME_EXCEEDED`. `revision_round` must be strictly below the cap of 2 or the request is `ERR_REVISION_LIMIT_REACHED` (HTTP 409), and the standing, right-status and stale-version gates all apply exactly as they do on first submission. On success the right's `encumbered_af` first decreases by the old escrow and then increases by the new requested volume, `transferable_af` is recomputed, the right's status is re-derived, `escrowed_af` becomes the new requested volume, `revision_round` is incremented by 1, and the application's, the right's and the recomputed analysis's versions are all bumped.

Withdrawal is the applicant's escape hatch and is reachable from `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `PROTEST_PERIOD`, `UNDER_PROTEST` and `REVISION_REQUESTED`, accompanied by a `withdrawal_reason`. When acting as a `Claimant` the caller must own the right behind the application. The application reaches the terminal `WITHDRAWN`; `escrowed_af` is released to 0.00 and the released volume moves from the right's `encumbered_af` back into its `transferable_af`, conserving the ledger, with the right's status re-derived and its `version` bumped. Withdrawal from `DRAFT` is the one case where there is no escrow to release and no ledger movement occurs at all. Every protest on the application still in `FILED`, `IN_NEGOTIATION` or `IN_HEARING` moves to the terminal `DISMISSED` with its `version` bumped and `unresolved_protest_count` recomputed to 0; any `DRAFT` injury analysis moves to the terminal `VOIDED` with its `version` bumped and `pending_review_count` recomputed; and the claimant's `open_application_count` is recomputed.

### 3.5. Injury Analysis and the Publication of the Protest Window

Before the public may respond to an application, the department must state on the record how much injury the proposed move would cause to other appropriators. That statement is an injury analysis, prepared by an analyst, a hearing officer or the adjudicator. It names its `method` — either `INSTANTANEOUS_DEPLETION` or `SEASONAL_AVERAGE` — a `pod_move_miles` figure of zero or more at 2 decimal places giving the absolute river-mile separation between the existing and proposed headgates as the analyst measures it, and an optional `return_flow_credit_af` of zero or more at 2 decimal places that defaults to 0.00 when omitted. The service stamps `prepared_by_user_id` with the calling operator identifier.

Under `INSTANTANEOUS_DEPLETION`, a distance factor is computed as one minus fifteen thousandths for each river mile of the move, rounded to 4 decimal places, and then floored at 0.55 by taking whichever of the two is larger. Gross depletion is the application's `requested_af` multiplied by a depletion factor of 0.92 and then by that distance factor, rounded to 2 decimal places. Moving the diversion far downstream therefore materially reduces computed injury, but only down to the floor. Under `SEASONAL_AVERAGE` there is no distance attenuation whatsoever — `pod_move_miles` is read from the payload, stored, and then ignored by the arithmetic — and gross depletion is simply `requested_af` multiplied by a flat seasonal factor of 0.78, rounded to 2 decimal places. Because one model attenuates and the other does not, `SEASONAL_AVERAGE` can produce either a higher or a lower net injury than `INSTANTANEOUS_DEPLETION` depending on the distance; the two cross over at a move of 15.22 river miles.

`offset_af` is not an input. It is the sum, rounded to 2 decimal places, of the stipulated offset of every protest on this application that has reached `RESOLVED_STIPULATED` on the negotiation track; hearing-track resolutions contribute nothing to it. `net_injury_af` is then gross depletion less `offset_af` less `return_flow_credit_af`, floored at 0.00 by taking whichever of that difference and zero is larger, and rounded to 2 decimal places. The two credits are independent and both are subtracted.

At most one analysis may be pending per application. Preparing a new one moves any existing `DRAFT` analysis on the same application to the terminal `VOIDED` with its `version` bumped, and seeds the new analysis's `offset_af` from the offsets already stipulated, so re-running an analysis in the middle of a negotiation does not silently discard concessions the protestants already obtained. `pending_review_count` on the right is the number of `DRAFT` injury analyses whose application sits on that right; it therefore rises by one for a first analysis and nets to zero when one draft replaces another, and the right's `version` is bumped either way. Analyses on an application can be listed with an optional `status` filter, and a single analysis can be read to inspect its gross-depletion, offset and net-injury components.

Publication opens the protest window and is available to analysts and the adjudicator, moving the application `SUBMITTED → PROTEST_PERIOD`. It requires an injury analysis to already exist so that protestants have something to respond to; an application with neither a `DRAFT` nor a `FINALIZED` analysis is rejected with `ERR_INJURY_ANALYSIS_MISSING` (HTTP 422). The window length is not caller-supplied. `protest_deadline` is stamped from the logical clock at thirty days out on a first publication, when `revision_round` is 0, and at fifteen days out on every republication after a revision, when `revision_round` is 1 or more — a revised application gets a materially shorter window because the basin has already had its first look.

An unprotested window is closed by an analyst, a hearing officer or the adjudicator, moving the application `PROTEST_PERIOD → READY_FOR_DECISION`. Two conditions govern: the logical clock must have reached `protest_deadline` — attempting it earlier is `ERR_WINDOW_STILL_OPEN` (HTTP 409) — and `unresolved_protest_count` must be 0. This route exists only from `PROTEST_PERIOD`. The very first protest filing already carries the application into `UNDER_PROTEST`, and a protested application leaves `UNDER_PROTEST` only when its last protest clears or when a revision is requested, so attempting to close the window on an `UNDER_PROTEST` application is `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. On success the application's `DRAFT` injury analysis moves to the terminal `FINALIZED`, freezing `gross_depletion_af`, `offset_af` and `net_injury_af` at their current values, with the analysis's `version` bumped; `pending_review_count` on the right drops by one because a draft analysis left the pending set; and the application's and right's versions are bumped.

### 3.6. Protest Adjudication, the Revision Cycle, and the Clearance Cascade

Filing a protest is the one operation reserved exclusively to the `Claimant` role — agency staff cannot file one on a claimant's behalf. The payload names the `transfer_application_id` under protest, the `protestant_claimant_id` of the protesting party, a `claimed_injury_af` figure strictly greater than zero, and a `basis` drawn from `INJURY_TO_SENIOR_RIGHT`, `PUBLIC_INTEREST`, `ENLARGEMENT` and `NO_BENEFICIAL_USE`. `X-User-ID` must equal the `protestant_claimant_id` supplied. The named application must itself be open to protest — its `status` must read `PROTEST_PERIOD` or `UNDER_PROTEST`, and any other status, a `REVISION_REQUESTED` application whose window was cleared included, is `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409); this gate is evaluated ahead of the protestant's standing and ahead of all three gates below, so it, and not the window error, answers an application that was never published. The protestant's `standing_status` must not be `BARRED`, though a merely `DELINQUENT` protestant may still file; a barred protestant is `ERR_CLAIMANT_NOT_IN_GOOD_STANDING`.

Three further gates guard the filing, and their relative order is what decides ambiguous cases. The logical clock must be strictly before `protest_deadline`; filing at exactly the deadline instant is late and yields `ERR_PROTEST_WINDOW_CLOSED` (HTTP 422). The `protestant_claimant_id` must differ from the `claimant_id` of the right behind the protested application, since a claimant cannot protest their own transfer, and a self-filing is `ERR_SELF_PROTEST_FORBIDDEN` (HTTP 422). And no existing protest on the application may already carry the same `protestant_claimant_id`, regardless of that earlier protest's status, with a repeat being `ERR_DUPLICATE_PROTEST` (HTTP 409). The temporal gate outranks the duplicate gate: a protestant who files a second protest *after* the deadline has passed breaks both rules at once and receives the window error, not the duplicate error.

A new protest lands in `FILED` with `track` unset and `stipulated_offset_af` at 0.00. The filing itself drives the application: the first protest moves it `PROTEST_PERIOD → UNDER_PROTEST`, and each additional protestant leaves it at `UNDER_PROTEST → UNDER_PROTEST` while raising `unresolved_protest_count`, which counts this application's protests in `FILED`, `IN_NEGOTIATION` or `IN_HEARING`. Protests can be listed with optional filters on `transfer_application_id`, `protestant_claimant_id`, `status` and `track`, plus `limit` and `offset`; a single protest can be read to inspect its track, stipulated offset and resolver identity.

Routing is the fork, and it belongs exclusively to the `HearingOfficer`. Supplying a `track` of `NEGOTIATION` moves the protest `FILED → IN_NEGOTIATION`; supplying `HEARING` moves it `FILED → IN_HEARING`. The only state gate is the protest's own: it must read `FILED`, and anything else is `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409). The parent application's status is deliberately not re-examined here, so a protest carried across a revision leg remains routable while its application sits in `REVISION_REQUESTED`. Either way the chosen `track` is recorded, the protest's `version` is bumped, and no counter moves — the protest is still unresolved, so `unresolved_protest_count` is unchanged. The two tracks reach the same resolved state by entirely different means, which is the point of the fork.

Resolution is likewise the hearing officer's alone, and exactly three combinations of track and `outcome` are admissible. Every resolution, whichever combination it is, carries a non-blank `resolution_note`, and that note is recorded on the protest in all three cases. The `outcome` and the `resolution_note` are checked as body well-formedness before the protest's own status is consulted and before the track-and-outcome pairing is judged, so a call missing either field is `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` even against a protest that was never routed. A `NEGOTIATION` protest with `outcome` `STIPULATE` requires `stipulated_offset_af`, which must be strictly greater than 0.00 and no greater than the protest's `claimed_injury_af`, with equality accepted; a value at or below zero or above the claim is `ERR_OFFSET_EXCEEDS_CLAIM` (HTTP 422). A `HEARING` protest with `outcome` `STIPULATE` requires a `condition_type` and accepts an optional `compliance_due_date` only alongside it, stored as supplied and left null when omitted. A `HEARING` protest with `outcome` `DISMISS` needs nothing beyond that note. The fourth combination — negotiation plus dismissal — is not a declared transition and is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. Every resolution records `resolved_by_user_id` from the calling operator identifier, and that record thereafter bars that individual from signing the disposition on this application.

The two stipulation branches carry deliberately divergent side-effect sets. On the negotiation branch the protest moves `IN_NEGOTIATION → RESOLVED_STIPULATED`, the `stipulated_offset_af` is recorded, and the application's `DRAFT` injury analysis recomputes `offset_af` as the sum of stipulated offsets across all negotiation-track resolved protests and recomputes `net_injury_af` accordingly — a volumetric concession, and no condition is created. On the hearing branch the protest moves `IN_HEARING → RESOLVED_STIPULATED`, `stipulated_offset_af` stays at 0.00 because the hearing branch grants no volumetric offset at all and therefore leaves `net_injury_af` untouched, and instead one approval condition is created on the application with `source` set to `HEARING_RESOLUTION`, `status` `IMPOSED` and `required_af` equal to the protest's `claimed_injury_af`. When that condition's `condition_type` is `MITIGATION_PAYMENT` it is priced as described in §3.8, enters `payment_status` `OUTSTANDING`, and increases the claimant's `outstanding_fee_balance_usd` — which in turn drives the claimant to `DELINQUENT` and every one of that claimant's bank accounts to `RESTRICTED`. The dismissal branch moves the protest `IN_HEARING → DISMISSED` with neither an offset nor a condition. All three outcomes decrement `unresolved_protest_count`.

A protestant may also abandon its own protest from `FILED`, `IN_NEGOTIATION` or `IN_HEARING`, supplying a `withdrawal_reason`; the adjudicator may do the same on a claimant's behalf. The protest reaches the terminal `WITHDRAWN` with no offset and no condition, and `unresolved_protest_count` is decremented.

Whichever route clears the last protest — a stipulation on either track, a dismissal, or a withdrawal — the same clearance cascade fires when `unresolved_protest_count` reaches exactly 0. The application auto-transitions `UNDER_PROTEST → READY_FOR_DECISION`; its `DRAFT` injury analysis moves to the terminal `FINALIZED`, freezing `gross_depletion_af`, `offset_af` and `net_injury_af`; `pending_review_count` on the right is decremented; and the versions of the protest, the application, the analysis and the right are all bumped. This cascade fires regardless of whether `protest_deadline` has passed — clearing every protest is by itself sufficient to close the negotiation phase.

The alternative to clearing protests is sending the application back to be re-scoped, which only a `HearingOfficer` may do and which requires `revision_round` to be strictly below the cap of 2 and at least one unresolved protest to exist. A `revision_note` accompanies the request. The application moves `UNDER_PROTEST → REVISION_REQUESTED` and `protest_deadline` is cleared to null so the republished application receives a fresh, shorter window. Two things deliberately do not happen: the escrow is not released — it stays in place across the revision and is swapped atomically on resubmission, so no ledger bucket on the right moves and the right's `version` is not bumped — and the outstanding protests are held exactly where they are, so `unresolved_protest_count` does not move either. Exceeding the cap is `ERR_REVISION_LIMIT_REACHED`. This closes the cycle `SUBMITTED → PROTEST_PERIOD → UNDER_PROTEST → REVISION_REQUESTED → SUBMITTED`, which may run at most twice.

### 3.7. The Disposition and Its Three Divergent Branches

Only a `DecisionAuthority` may render a disposition, and it is that role's sole mutating capability — no other role may approve, condition, or deny a transfer. The application must be in `READY_FOR_DECISION`. The payload carries a `disposition` of `APPROVE`, `APPROVE_WITH_CONDITIONS` or `DENY`, a non-blank `decision_note` recording the rationale, and a `conditions` array required exactly when the disposition is `APPROVE_WITH_CONDITIONS` and rejected for the other two. Each entry in that array is an object of `condition_type`, `required_af` and `compliance_due_date`.

Four checks precede every branch. A `FINALIZED` injury analysis must exist on the application, absent which the disposition is `ERR_INJURY_ANALYSIS_MISSING`. The deciding operator identifier must differ from the `certified_by_user_id` of the application's study and from the `resolved_by_user_id` of every protest on it, per §2.12, with either collision yielding `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. The application's `requested_af` must be no greater than the right's `encumbered_af`, since the disposition can only convert volume that is actually in escrow. And on both approving branches an injury test applies, which is where the branches begin to diverge.

**Unconditional approval** requires the `FINALIZED` analysis's `net_injury_af` to be strictly below the materiality threshold of 0.01 acre-feet. A residual injury of exactly 0.01 blocks it and yields `ERR_UNMITIGATED_INJURY` (HTTP 422) — such an application must be carried by conditions instead. On success the application moves `READY_FOR_DECISION → APPROVED`, `escrowed_af` is set to 0.00, and on the right the escrowed volume moves out of `encumbered_af` and into `transferred_away_af` so that the conserved identity holds across the move. The right's status is re-derived, which lands it on `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED`. No approval condition is created and no fee is assessed whatsoever — that absence is precisely what distinguishes this branch from the conditional one, and `mitigation_fee_total_usd` stays at 0.00.

**Conditional approval** requires a non-empty `conditions` array whose summed `required_af`, taken only over entries whose `condition_type` is `MITIGATION_PAYMENT` or `DRY_YEAR_FORBEARANCE`, is at least the `FINALIZED` analysis's `net_injury_af`. Equality is sufficient; a shortfall is `ERR_MITIGATION_INSUFFICIENT` (HTTP 422). Note that measurement-device and return-flow-maintenance conditions, while perfectly valid conditions, contribute nothing to that mitigation sum. On success the application moves `READY_FOR_DECISION → APPROVED_WITH_CONDITIONS` and performs exactly the same volume-ledger movement as unconditional approval, plus a substantial set of effects the other branch does not: one approval condition row is created per array entry with `source` set to `DECISION`, `status` `IMPOSED` and `imposed_by_user_id` set to the calling operator identifier; each `MITIGATION_PAYMENT` condition is priced and enters `payment_status` `OUTSTANDING` while every other condition type enters `payment_status` `NOT_APPLICABLE`; the application's `mitigation_fee_total_usd` is set to the sum of the priced total fees, which is the sum of `total_fee_usd` over this application's conditions whose fiscal dimension reads `OUTSTANDING` or `PAID`; the claimant's `outstanding_fee_balance_usd` increases by that same sum and, when the balance rises above 0.00 while the claimant is `IN_GOOD_STANDING`, `standing_status` moves to `DELINQUENT`; and every water bank account of that claimant recomputes its posture, a delinquent holder driving each one to `RESTRICTED`.

**Denial** is the compensating action for submission and restores the right's ledger to its exact pre-submission values. The application moves `READY_FOR_DECISION → DENIED`, `escrowed_af` is set to 0.00, and the released volume moves out of `encumbered_af` and back into `transferable_af`. The right's status is re-derived, the claimant's `open_application_count` is recomputed, no headgate status changes and no conditions are created, so `mitigation_fee_total_usd` remains 0.00.

Both approving branches share one further effect that denial does not. When the `application_type` is `POINT_OF_DIVERSION_CHANGE` or `COMBINED`, the application's `proposed_point_of_diversion_id` names a headgate that moves `PROPOSED → AUTHORIZED`, and every *other* headgate on the same right currently in `AUTHORIZED` moves to the terminal `RETIRED`. Existing lease assignments already referencing a newly retired headgate keep their status untouched, but no new lease may ever reference a `RETIRED` headgate. All three dispositions set `decided_by_user_id` to the calling operator identifier and `decision_note` to the supplied text, recompute the claimant's `open_application_count`, and bump the application's `version`, the right's `version` and the `version` of every other row they touch. `pending_review_count` on the right does not move under any disposition, because the analysis was already `FINALIZED` before the application became decidable.

### 3.8. Approval Conditions: Two Independent Dimensions

Approval conditions are never created directly by any caller. They arrive only from a conditional disposition, in which case `source` reads `DECISION`, or from a hearing-track protest resolution, in which case `source` reads `HEARING_RESOLUTION`. Each condition names a `condition_type` from `DRY_YEAR_FORBEARANCE`, `MEASUREMENT_DEVICE`, `RETURN_FLOW_MAINTENANCE` and `MITIGATION_PAYMENT`, a `required_af` volume of zero or more (defaulting to 0) that the applicant is obliged to forbear, replace or mitigate, and an optional `compliance_due_date`. The conditions riding on an application can be listed with optional filters on `status` and `payment_status`; a single condition can be read to inspect both of its dimensions together with the full fee breakdown.

That pair of dimensions is the structural point. `status` is the compliance dimension and `payment_status` is the fiscal dimension, and the two evolve independently through separate operations. A condition begins at `IMPOSED` on the compliance axis. On the fiscal axis it begins at `NOT_APPLICABLE`, and it moves to `OUTSTANDING` at the moment of creation only when its `condition_type` is `MITIGATION_PAYMENT` and its `required_af` is strictly greater than zero. A condition left on `NOT_APPLICABLE` carries a zeroed fee block rather than an absent one: `base_fee_usd` 0.00, `surcharge_pct` 0.000, `admin_surcharge_usd` 0.00 and `total_fee_usd` 0.00, all four present in its representation and none of them ever revised, since no operation on either axis re-prices a condition after creation.

Pricing a mitigation payment runs in a pinned order, because the surcharge tier depends on the fee and the surcharge is a percentage of the fee. The circularity resolves in exactly one direction. First, `base_fee_usd` is `required_af` multiplied by a base rate of 125.00 dollars per acre-foot, rounded to 2 decimal places. Second, the tier is selected from that pre-surcharge subtotal and never from the final total: a `base_fee_usd` strictly below 5,000.00 dollars takes a `surcharge_pct` of 0.040, a `base_fee_usd` from 5,000.00 up to but not including 20,000.00 takes 0.070, and 20,000.00 or above takes 0.095. The boundaries are half-open upward, so a base fee of exactly 5,000.00 sits in the middle tier and exactly 20,000.00 sits in the top tier. `surcharge_pct` is stored at 3 decimal places. Third, `admin_surcharge_usd` is `base_fee_usd` multiplied by `surcharge_pct`, rounded to 2 decimal places. Fourth, `total_fee_usd` is `base_fee_usd` plus `admin_surcharge_usd`, rounded to 2 decimal places. Only the single condition being created contributes to its own price; no other condition on the application enters the arithmetic.

Paying is available to the claimant that owns the right behind the application, and to the adjudicator on that claimant's behalf; the payer's `standing_status` must not be `BARRED`. Payment is always in full — partial payment is not modelled — and it fires a four-link cascade. The condition's `payment_status` moves `OUTSTANDING → PAID` and its `version` is bumped, while `status` stays exactly where it was, because paying does not satisfy a condition. The claimant's `outstanding_fee_balance_usd` decreases by `total_fee_usd` and the claimant's `version` is bumped. When that balance reaches precisely 0.00 and `standing_status` reads `DELINQUENT`, standing returns to `IN_GOOD_STANDING`; a residual balance of 0.01 leaves the claimant `DELINQUENT`. Finally every water bank account of that claimant recomputes its posture and bumps its `version`, so a `RESTRICTED` account clears only when the standing is genuinely restored and no other restriction trigger — thin available credit — still applies.

Signing off compliance is available to the same two roles and carries `compliance_evidence`. The cross-dimension guard bites here: `payment_status` must be `NOT_APPLICABLE` or `PAID`, so an outstanding mitigation fee blocks compliance sign-off and yields `ERR_CONDITION_FEE_OUTSTANDING` (HTTP 409). The compliance dimension cannot terminate while the fiscal dimension is still owed. Sign-off is accepted from two starting states. From `IMPOSED` it is ordinary sign-off, moving the condition `IMPOSED → SATISFIED`, which is terminal, bumping the condition's `version` and touching no other row. From `VIOLATED` it is the cure leg — late compliance after field enforcement already recorded a violation — moving the condition `VIOLATED → SATISFIED` and bumping its `version`. `VIOLATED` is deliberately not terminal for exactly this reason. Curing does not itself lift anything: the right stays `SUSPENDED`, since suspension is sticky in the status derivation, and its suspended leases stay `SUSPENDED` until each is separately resumed. What curing does is clear the reinstatement gate, and curing every violated condition on a right is the only way reinstatement becomes reachable at all.

Recording non-compliance belongs exclusively to the `WaterRightsAnalyst` as field enforcement staff, accompanied by a `violation_note`. When the condition carries a `compliance_due_date`, the logical clock must have reached it; recording a violation earlier is `ERR_WINDOW_STILL_OPEN`. A condition with no due date may be violated at any time. This is the second four-link cascade and it reaches from a single condition all the way to a bank account's posture. The condition moves `IMPOSED → VIOLATED` and its `version` is bumped. The water right behind the application moves to `SUSPENDED` — reachable from `ACTIVE`, from `ENCUMBERED` or from `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED` — its forfeiture score is recomputed with the twelve-point suspension penalty now applying, and its `version` is bumped. Where the right sits in none of those three statuses — most often because an earlier violation on a sibling condition already suspended it — the violation still stands and both remaining links still fire, but the right's own row is neither re-statused nor bumped. Every lease assignment on that right currently in `ACTIVE` moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED`, its `suspension_count` is incremented, and on its funding account the lease's `requested_af` moves out of `leased_out_af` and back into `committed_credit_af`, leaving `available_credit_af` untouched so the credit identity is conserved. And each affected account increments `suspended_lease_count` and recomputes its posture, which lands on `FROZEN` because a single suspended lease is sufficient and `FROZEN` dominates both `RESTRICTED` and `OPEN`.

The forfeiture cascade of §3.2 supplies the two remaining terminal states on these axes. On the compliance axis a condition of a force-denied application moves `IMPOSED → RELEASED`, which is terminal; on the fiscal axis, whenever such a condition was `OUTSTANDING`, it moves `OUTSTANDING → REVERSED`, which is likewise terminal, and the claimant's `outstanding_fee_balance_usd` decreases by that condition's `total_fee_usd` with the claimant's bank postures recomputed. The two axes therefore terminate separately and in different vocabularies: `SATISFIED` or `RELEASED` on compliance, `PAID` or `REVERSED` on fiscal.

### 3.9. Water Banking, Delivery Leases, and Two Different Interval Geometries

A water bank account is the second of the two conserved ledgers. Opening one is administrative work reserved to the `WaterRightsAnalyst` and the `BasinAdjudicator`; it names a `claimant_id` as account holder and a `basin_code` of 1 to 24 characters, and it comes into existence with all four credit buckets at 0.00, `suspended_lease_count` at 0 and `posture` `OPEN`, which satisfies the credit identity of §2.9 trivially. Accounts can be listed by all five roles with optional filters on `claimant_id`, `basin_code` and `posture`, and a single account can be read to inspect the four-bucket ledger together with its derived posture.

Depositing is how transferable volume crosses from the right ledger into the credit ledger. The claimant that holds the account, the analyst and the adjudicator may deposit; the payload names the source `water_right_id`, a `deposit_af` volume and the `expected_water_bank_account_version` demanded by §2.7. There is no standalone deposit entity — the volume is recorded directly on both ledgers, and the deposited volume attributable to a source right is exactly what §2.9 calls that right's bank hold. Three business gates apply in ascending order after the ownership, body-shape and existence gates: the source right's status must be `ACTIVE`, `ENCUMBERED` or `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED`, otherwise `ERR_RIGHT_NOT_TRANSFERABLE` (409); the right's `bank_enrolled` flag must be set, otherwise `ERR_RIGHT_NOT_BANK_ENROLLED` (422); and `deposit_af` must be no greater than the right's `transferable_af` as the ledger stands at the moment of the call, with equality accepted, otherwise `ERR_TRANSFERABLE_VOLUME_EXCEEDED`. Note that enrolment is checked *after* the status gate, so a suspended but bank-enrolled right reports non-transferability rather than non-enrolment. On success `deposited_credit_af` and `available_credit_af` both increase by `deposit_af`, which conserves the credit identity because the deposit lands in the unencumbered bucket; the posture is recomputed, so a deposit can lift an account off the low-credit `RESTRICTED` trigger; and on the source right the volume moves out of `transferable_af` and into `encumbered_af`, conserving the right identity and re-deriving the status from `ACTIVE` to `ENCUMBERED`. Both rows bump their `version`.

Reserving a lease commits credit to a named lessee at a named headgate over a named window. The claimant, the analyst and the adjudicator may reserve. The payload carries `water_bank_account_id`, `water_right_id`, `point_of_diversion_id`, a `lessee_name` of 1 to 160 characters, `lease_start_date`, a `lease_end_date` strictly after it, a `requested_af` strictly greater than zero at 2 decimal places, a `diversion_rate_cfs` strictly greater than zero, a `rental_rate_usd_per_af` of zero or more that is carried for reporting only and enters no calculation anywhere in the system, and a `valuation_method` of `PRO_RATA_DAILY` or `SEASON_BLOCK` that determines nothing until the lease is terminated early. The lease is created in `RESERVED`, `committed_credit_af` increases and `available_credit_af` decreases by `requested_af` — the credit identity is conserved because the total is merely repartitioned — and the posture is recomputed, since committing credit can drop `available_credit_af` below the 5.00 acre-foot floor and force `RESTRICTED`. Leases can be listed by all five roles with optional filters on `water_bank_account_id`, `water_right_id`, `point_of_diversion_id` and `status`, and a single lease can be read to inspect its settlement figures and suspension history.

Reservation runs the longest gate ladder in the system, and its ordering is load-bearing. The named headgate must belong to the named right, otherwise `ERR_POD_RIGHT_MISMATCH` (422). The right's status must be `ACTIVE`, `ENCUMBERED` or `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED`, otherwise `ERR_RIGHT_NOT_TRANSFERABLE`. The account holder's `standing_status` must read `IN_GOOD_STANDING` — reserving a lease is held to the same strict standing bar as submitting a transfer application, so `DELINQUENT` blocks it just as `BARRED` does — otherwise `ERR_CLAIMANT_NOT_IN_GOOD_STANDING` (403). The window must be well-formed, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_WINDOW` (422). Then come the two interval rules, then the headgate-authorization rule, and only last the credit-budget rule — so a window collision, priced at priority 41, is reported in preference to insufficient credit, priced at priority 46, even when both are true of the same request.

The two interval rules are deliberately not the same shape, and they act on disjoint populations, so both geometries are independently reachable from a single request. The **same-right, different-headgate** rule enforces that a right's water is delivered at only one headgate at a time. It examines every non-terminal lease — `RESERVED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`, never `COMPLETED` or `CANCELLED` — that sits on the same `water_right_id` but a *different* `point_of_diversion_id`. Each such lease projects a fallow exclusion window running from 7 whole days before its `lease_start_date` to 7 whole days after its `lease_end_date`, computed in UTC. The collision test is closed on both ends: the candidate collides when its `lease_start_date` is at or before the exclusion window's end *and* its `lease_end_date` is at or after the exclusion window's start. Touching endpoints therefore do collide — a candidate starting exactly 7 days after an existing lease's end instant still collides, and a gap of 7 days and one second is the smallest separation that is accepted. A collision is `ERR_LEASE_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (409).

The **same-headgate** rule enforces a physical capacity limit instead of an exclusivity rule, and it treats the window as half-open. It examines every non-terminal lease at the same `point_of_diversion_id`, and considers a lease overlapping only when it shares a positive-length intersection with the candidate: the existing lease's `lease_start_date` is strictly before the candidate's `lease_end_date` and its `lease_end_date` is strictly after the candidate's `lease_start_date`. Touching endpoints do *not* overlap here, so back-to-back leases at one headgate are always permitted. The gate is a conservative aggregate rather than a per-instant maximum: the candidate's `diversion_rate_cfs` plus the `diversion_rate_cfs` of every overlapping lease, summed and rounded to 3 decimal places, must be no greater than the headgate's `max_rate_cfs`, and equality is accepted. Exceeding it is `ERR_POD_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (409). The contrast is the point: two fully overlapping windows at one headgate pass whenever their aggregate rate fits, while the very same two windows at two headgates of the same right collide even when separated by a few days. Finally the headgate's own `status` must be `AUTHORIZED` — `PROPOSED` and `RETIRED` headgates take no new leases — otherwise `ERR_POD_NOT_AUTHORIZED` (409); and `requested_af` must be no greater than the account's `available_credit_af`, equality accepted, otherwise `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_BANK_CREDIT` (422).

Delivery begins when the claimant holding the account, the analyst or the adjudicator starts a `RESERVED` lease, moving it `RESERVED → ACTIVE`. Three gates apply beyond ownership. The funding account's `posture` must be `OPEN`, so an account that is `RESTRICTED` by a delinquent holder or thin credit, or `FROZEN` by a suspended lease, cannot begin new deliveries; the failure is `ERR_ACCOUNT_POSTURE_BLOCKED` (409). The headgate must still be `AUTHORIZED`, which is a genuine possibility of failure because a transfer approval can retire a headgate after the lease was reserved. And the logical clock must be at or after `lease_start_date` and strictly before `lease_end_date`, otherwise `ERR_LEASE_WINDOW_TIMING` (422). On success `committed_credit_af` decreases and `leased_out_af` increases by `requested_af` while `available_credit_af` is untouched, so the credit identity is conserved by repartition alone; the posture is recomputed.

An `ACTIVE` lease is driven to `SUSPENDED` only by the system, as the third link of the condition-violation cascade of §3.8. Resumption is a separate, deliberately per-lease act by the analyst or the adjudicator: reinstating the right does not auto-resume anything. It requires the leased right to be back in service — status `ACTIVE`, `ENCUMBERED` or `PARTIALLY_TRANSFERRED`, otherwise `ERR_RIGHT_NOT_TRANSFERABLE` — and the logical clock still inside the delivery window on the same at-or-after-start, strictly-before-end test, otherwise `ERR_LEASE_WINDOW_TIMING`. A lease whose window elapsed while it was suspended can therefore never be resumed. Resumption moves the lease `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`, closing the cycle `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`, moves `requested_af` out of `committed_credit_af` and back into `leased_out_af`, decrements `suspended_lease_count`, and recomputes the posture — so `FROZEN` clears only when that count reaches zero and no other trigger still applies.

Closing out a lease that ran to term is analyst or adjudicator work and requires the logical clock to have reached `lease_end_date`, otherwise `ERR_LEASE_WINDOW_TIMING`. The lease moves `ACTIVE → COMPLETED`, which is terminal, with `used_af` set to the whole `requested_af` and `settlement_credit_af` set to 0.00 by definition. On the account both `leased_out_af` and `deposited_credit_af` decrease by `requested_af`, because the water was actually delivered and permanently leaves the bank, and the identity is conserved because both sides of it shrink together. On the source right the used volume moves out of `encumbered_af` and into `transferred_away_af`.

Early termination is available to the claimant holding the account, the analyst and the adjudicator, carries a `cancellation_reason`, and is reachable from `RESERVED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`. Cancelling from `ACTIVE` additionally requires the logical clock to be strictly before `lease_end_date`. From `RESERVED` it is the exact compensating action for reservation: `used_af` is 0.00 regardless of `valuation_method` because delivery never began, `settlement_credit_af` is the whole `requested_af`, and the credit moves out of `committed_credit_af` and back into `available_credit_af`, restoring every individual term of the credit ledger to its precise pre-reservation value. From `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` the settlement calculation splits the volume, and only the settlement portion returns to available credit: from `ACTIVE` the credit comes out of `leased_out_af`, from `SUSPENDED` it comes out of `committed_credit_af` and `suspended_lease_count` is decremented. In both cases `available_credit_af` increases by `settlement_credit_af` and `deposited_credit_af` decreases by `used_af`. Whenever `used_af` is greater than zero the used volume also moves from the source right's `encumbered_af` to its `transferred_away_af`. The posture is recomputed and both the lease and the account bump their `version`. The source right bumps its own only where that movement actually happened: a cancellation from `RESERVED` settles at a `used_af` of 0.00, leaves every one of the right's four buckets and its derived status exactly as they were, and therefore leaves the right's row entirely untouched — no refreshed `updated_at` and no `version` increment.

The settlement split is where the two valuation methods diverge, and its rounding is pinned in two steps. The term length is the count of whole days in the half-open window from `lease_start_date` up to but not including `lease_end_date`. The elapsed length is the count of whole days from `lease_start_date` to the earlier of the logical clock and `lease_end_date`, floored to whole days and never negative. Under `PRO_RATA_DAILY` the charged days are exactly the elapsed days, so an early termination returns the unconsumed fraction of the volume down to the day. Under `SEASON_BLOCK` any started block is charged in full: the elapsed days are divided by 30 and rounded *up* to the next whole block, zero elapsed days yielding zero blocks, and the charged days are that block count times 30, capped at the term length so a partly elapsed final block can never charge beyond the whole term. `used_af` is then `requested_af` multiplied by the charged days and divided by the term days, rounded to 3 decimal places and then that result rounded again to 2 decimal places — the two-step rounding is deliberate and both steps are round-half-up. `settlement_credit_af` is a single 2-decimal-place subtraction of `used_af` from `requested_af`. Because the block method rounds elapsed days upward first, it returns strictly less credit than the daily method for the same termination instant whenever the elapsed days are not an exact multiple of 30, and the two methods therefore disagree on the acre-feet returned to `available_credit_af` for otherwise identical leases. The forfeiture cascade of §3.2 force-cancels a right's non-terminal leases through this same settlement path, from `RESERVED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` alike.

### 3.10. Basin Adjudication: Dockets, Claims, Objections, and Decrees

General stream adjudication is the `BasinAdjudicator`'s exclusive province at the docket level. Opening a docket names a `basin_code` of 1 to 24 characters, a `docket_number` unique across all dockets — a collision is `ERR_DUPLICATE_DOCKET_NUMBER` (409) — and a `claim_filing_deadline` that is an *exclusive* cut-off. The docket opens in phase `OPEN_FOR_CLAIMS` with `claim_count`, `decreed_claim_count` and `total_decreed_af` all zeroed, and any of the five roles may read it to inspect its phase and rolled-up counters.

Phase advance is requested by the adjudicator with an explicit `target_phase` of `EXAMINATION`, `OBJECTION_PERIOD`, `DECREE_ENTERED` or `CLOSED`, and every advance is a compound gate over the docket's children. Advancing to `EXAMINATION` requires two things at once: the logical clock must have reached `claim_filing_deadline`, a temporal failure reported as `ERR_WINDOW_STILL_OPEN` (409), and the docket must hold at least one claim, a child-population failure reported as `ERR_DOCKET_CHILDREN_NOT_READY` (422). Because the temporal rule sits at a lower priority number than the child gate, a docket that is both early and empty reports the still-open window. A docket with zero claims can never leave `OPEN_FOR_CLAIMS` at all. Advancing to `OBJECTION_PERIOD` requires every claim on the docket to have left `FILED`, meaning each one reads `EXAMINED`, `OBJECTED` or `DECREED`. Advancing to `DECREE_ENTERED` requires every claim to read `DECREED`. Advancing to `CLOSED` requires only that the docket currently reads `DECREE_ENTERED` and imposes no further child gate. A `target_phase` that is not the docket's immediate successor is `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (409), and `CLOSED` is terminal. Reaching `EXAMINATION` closes filing permanently; reaching `DECREE_ENTERED` freezes `total_decreed_af`; reaching `CLOSED` makes the docket read-only.

Filing a claim is open to the claimant asserting it, the analyst and the adjudicator. The payload names a `decree_docket_id`, the `water_right_id` asserted, a `claimed_priority_date` that may legitimately differ from the right's own recorded `priority_date`, and a `claimed_af` strictly greater than zero at 2 decimal places. Three business gates apply in ascending order. First, only one claim may exist for a given docket-and-right pair, a second attempt yielding `ERR_DUPLICATE_CLAIM` (409). Second, the docket must be in `OPEN_FOR_CLAIMS`, otherwise `ERR_DOCKET_PHASE_INVALID` (409). Third, the logical clock must be strictly before `claim_filing_deadline`, so filing at exactly the deadline instant is late and yields `ERR_CLAIM_FILING_CLOSED` (422). That order matters in two ways: re-filing an existing claim into a docket that has already advanced past filing reports the duplicate rather than the phase, and filing a fresh claim into an advanced docket reports the phase rather than the lateness. Filing recomputes the docket's `claim_count` and bumps the docket's `version`. Claims can be listed by all five roles with optional filters on `decree_docket_id`, `water_right_id`, `filing_status` and `verification_status`, and a single claim can be read to inspect both dimensions together with its decreed volume and seniority rank.

A claim carries two independent status dimensions from birth and they advance through separate operations in either order. `filing_status` is the procedural dimension, starting at `FILED` and running `FILED → EXAMINED → DECREED` with an optional detour through `OBJECTED`. `verification_status` is the evidentiary dimension, starting at `UNVERIFIED`. Neither operation on one axis ever touches the other axis, and no docket counter moves on a verification.

Examination is adjudicator-only and valid only while the docket reads `EXAMINATION`. It records an `examiner_recommended_af` and an `examination_note`, and the recommendation must be strictly greater than zero and no greater than `claimed_af`, with equality accepted. The two ends of that bound carry different identities here, unlike the offset bound of §3.6: a recommendation above the claim is the domain refusal `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_EXCEEDS_CLAIM` (422), while a recommendation at or below zero fails as body well-formedness and is `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400) — so `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_EXCEEDS_CLAIM` is reserved strictly for the upper end. The claim moves `FILED → EXAMINED` and `verification_status` is left untouched.

Verification is field work performed by the analyst or the adjudicator, in any docket phase whatsoever, and carries a `verification_outcome` of `FIELD_VERIFIED` or `DISPUTED` plus a non-blank `field_note`. Reaching `FIELD_VERIFIED` requires the claim's right to hold at least one beneficial-use record whose `verification_source` is `METER` or `AERIAL_IMAGERY` — the same physical-evidence standard that governs the first activation of a right in §3.1 — and the failure is `ERR_NO_VERIFIED_USE_EVIDENCE` (422). Recording `DISPUTED` needs no evidence at all. Both `UNVERIFIED → FIELD_VERIFIED` and `UNVERIFIED → DISPUTED` are available, and `DISPUTED → FIELD_VERIFIED` is the cycle on this axis, so a disputed claim can later be re-verified once the evidence arrives. This axis has no terminal state.

Objecting is available to any claimant other than the one behind the claim, and to the adjudicator, while the docket reads `OBJECTION_PERIOD`. It carries a non-blank `objection_basis` and `objection_note`, each 1 to 240 characters. The operator identifier must differ from the `claimant_id` of the water right named by the claim, otherwise `ERR_SELF_OBJECTION_FORBIDDEN` (422). The first objection moves the claim `EXAMINED → OBJECTED`; every further objection is a self-loop that leaves the claim at `OBJECTED` and only increments `objection_count`, which is what drives the haircut below. There is no cap on the number of objections.

Entering the decree is adjudicator-only, valid only while the docket reads `OBJECTION_PERIOD`, and reachable from `EXAMINED` or from `OBJECTED`. It carries a `decree_note`. Its distinguishing feature is a guard on the *other* dimension: `verification_status` must read `FIELD_VERIFIED`, otherwise `ERR_CLAIM_NOT_VERIFIED` (409). An examined but unverified claim, and a disputed one, are both undecreeable, which is exactly why the two axes must be advanced independently before a decree can be entered.

The decreed volume runs a waterfall whose evaluation order is pinned. First the volume is floored at the lesser of `claimed_af` and `examiner_recommended_af`. Second, when `objection_count` is at least 1, that figure is multiplied by 0.90 and rounded to 3 decimal places. Third, when the absolute difference between the claim's `claimed_priority_date` and the right's recorded `priority_date` is more than 366 whole days, the running figure is multiplied by 0.90 again and rounded to 3 decimal places; the tolerance test is strict, so a discrepancy of exactly 366 days is within tolerance and 367 days is not. Fourth, `decreed_af` is the running figure rounded once to 2 decimal places. Because the haircuts compound multiplicatively and each intermediate is rounded to 3 decimal places before the next is applied, a claim carrying both an objection and a priority mismatch retains 0.81 of its floored volume rather than losing 0.20 of it, and the single final 2-decimal-place rounding is applied only once, at the end.

Entering a decree moves the claim to `DECREED`, which is terminal on the procedural axis, and then re-ranks seniority across the whole docket. `priority_rank` is recomputed for *every* decreed claim on the docket, not merely the new one, as a 1-based rank ordered by `claimed_priority_date` ascending and tie-broken by `created_at` ascending, never by `id`; each re-ranked claim bumps its own `version`. A claim's rank is null until it is decreed, so a late-filed but senior claim can push every junior claim's rank down when it is finally decreed. The docket's `decreed_claim_count` and `total_decreed_af` — the sum of `decreed_af` over that docket's decreed claims, at 2 decimal places — are recomputed and the docket bumps its `version`. Finally the bubble-up fires: when `decreed_claim_count` becomes equal to `claim_count`, the docket auto-advances `OBJECTION_PERIOD → DECREE_ENTERED` and `total_decreed_af` is frozen, without any separate phase-advance request. That auto-advance is the compound counterpart of the manual advance described above, and both reach the same phase by the same child condition.

## 4. Endpoint Definitions

Every path below is rooted directly at the API's base URL — there is no version prefix and no service segment. Every path placeholder is literally `{id}` and names the resource of the collection segment immediately preceding it. Endpoints that bring a new resource into existence return HTTP 201; every other successful call, including every state transition, every recomputation and the logical-clock setter, returns HTTP 200. Every mutating endpoint bumps the mutated row's `version`, with the single exception of the append-only beneficial-use record, which carries no `version` field at all. In the Authorized Role(s) column, "All roles" means all five of `Claimant`, `WaterRightsAnalyst`, `HearingOfficer`, `DecisionAuthority` and `BasinAdjudicator`.
