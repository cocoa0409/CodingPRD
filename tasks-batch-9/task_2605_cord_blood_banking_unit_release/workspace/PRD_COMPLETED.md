# Cord Blood Banking & Unit Release API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A cord blood bank sits at an unusual intersection: it is a clinical laboratory, a cryogenic warehouse, a regulated tissue registry and a consumer subscription business, and a single physical object — one bag of umbilical cord blood, collected once and never reproducible — travels through all four. This document specifies the API that governs that object end to end: the enrollment of an expectant parent and the informed consent that authorizes everything downstream, dispatch of a collection kit to the delivery suite, the collection itself under hard volume and time-to-processing limits, laboratory receipt, volume reduction, cell-count and viability testing against program-specific acceptance thresholds, cryopreservation into a numbered tank slot, and finally either publication on a public registry or custody inside a privately billed family storage account.

The operational pain the system is built to remove is not data entry — it is the silent divergence between what a clinician believes about a unit and what is true. A maternal serology panel can seroconvert weeks after donation. A tank can drift above its alarm threshold at three in the morning. A family can stop paying. A transplant centre can hold a unit for a patient who never comes to transplant, while a second centre with a better-matched patient is told the unit is unavailable. Each of these events must propagate deterministically to every artifact that depends on it — the unit's eligibility, its registry listing, the holds placed against it, the invoices billed for it — and each must be reversible in exactly the cases where the triggering condition is itself reversed. Every cascade and every compensating reversal in this specification is therefore mandatory behaviour, not an implementation nicety.

Two further constraints shape the whole design. First, release of a unit for transplant is an irreversible clinical act, so it is gated by segregation of duties: the technologist who measured the unit may not judge its eligibility, and the medical director who judged its eligibility may not authorize its release. Second, every deadline, expiry, validity window and delinquency clock in the system reads a server-side **logical clock** rather than wall-clock time, so that the entire behaviour of the bank is reproducible. All resources are mounted under the `/cbb` prefix, with the sole exception of the logical-clock test-driving endpoint.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, declaring the capacity in which it acts. If either header is absent — and a header that is present but empty or whitespace-only counts as absent — or if `X-User-Role` is not exactly one of the ten role names declared below, the comparison being case-sensitive so that `platformadmin` is not a role, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). If the headers are well formed but the declared role does not appear in the addressed endpoint's authorized-role set, the request is rejected with `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403). Role membership is checked before any body parsing, any path lookup and any business rule.

The ten roles are `IntakeCoordinator`, `ParentGuardian`, `CollectionSite`, `ProcessingTechnologist`, `MedicalDirector`, `RegistryOperator`, `TransplantCenter`, `BillingAdministrator`, `FacilitiesEngineer` and `PlatformAdmin`.

- `IntakeCoordinator` — bank-side intake staff who register expectant parents, capture or amend consent on their behalf, dispatch collection kits and file maternal panels.
- `ParentGuardian` — the enrolling parent, acting on their own enrollment only.
- `CollectionSite` — delivery-suite staff who record the collection event at birth; they cannot receive, process or judge a unit.
- `ProcessingTechnologist` — laboratory staff who receive or reject collections, run volume reduction, record quality tests and cryopreserve units; they never determine eligibility and never release.
- `MedicalDirector` — the medical authority who determines eligibility, authorizes release, disposes units, amends maternal panels and adjudicates excursion dispositions.
- `RegistryOperator` — the public-registry desk, which publishes eligible public-donation units and suspends or resumes their listings; it touches neither private billing nor medical decisions.
- `TransplantCenter` — an external transplant centre that searches inventory and places, confirms, declines or cancels holds for its own patients.
- `BillingAdministrator` — the private-storage billing desk, which opens accounts, issues invoices and posts payments.
- `FacilitiesEngineer` — the cryogenic facilities engineer, who commissions tanks, posts temperature readings and acknowledges alarms; resolving an excursion is a shared authority with the medical director.
- `PlatformAdmin` — the operator of record, authorized on every business endpoint plus the nightly batch and the logical-clock endpoint, and still bound by every state, safety, fiscal and segregation-of-duties rule that binds anyone else.

**Identity semantics.** When `X-User-Role` is `ParentGuardian`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Enrollment`; ownership checks on any resource that references an `Enrollment` compare the resource's foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, a `ParentGuardian` may address only its own enrollment, its own `StorageAccount` and the enrollment-scoped and account-scoped collections reachable from them, and any attempt to address another enrollment's data is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403). The enumeration of guarded operations is exhaustive rather than illustrative: this ownership check applies to exactly these ten — fetching an enrollment, listing an enrollment's storage accounts, listing an enrollment's collection kits, listing an enrollment's maternal screenings, signing consent, opening a consent amendment, withdrawing the enrollment, fetching a storage account, listing a storage account's invoices, and posting a payment against an invoice — and to no others. The remaining `ParentGuardian`-authorized reads deliberately carry no ownership filter and are answered for any caller whose headers clear the authentication and role rungs, whatever enrollment the addressed row belongs to: fetching a collection kit by id, fetching a maternal screening by id, fetching a storage invoice by id, and listing a storage account's units.

When `X-User-Role` is any of `IntakeCoordinator`, `CollectionSite`, `ProcessingTechnologist`, `MedicalDirector`, `RegistryOperator`, `TransplantCenter`, `BillingAdministrator`, `FacilitiesEngineer` or `PlatformAdmin`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server may record it — as `operator_user_id` on a `ProcessingRun`, as `performed_by_user_id` on a `QualityTest`, as `eligibility_determined_by_user_id` on a `CordBloodUnit`, as `acknowledged_by_user_id` on a `TemperatureExcursion`, and as `requesting_center_user_id` on a `Reservation` — but it is never compared to any entity's `id`. One opaque identity carries a further ownership rule: a `TransplantCenter` may address only a `Reservation` whose `requesting_center_user_id` equals its `X-User-ID`, and a mismatch is likewise `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. That check governs five operations: fetching a reservation, fetching a reservation's release summary, confirming a reservation, declining a reservation and cancelling a reservation. It does not govern the paginated `/cbb/reservations` collection: that list is not ownership-filtered, so a `TransplantCenter` reading it sees every centre's holds and may narrow the set only through the declared query filters.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request: the literal prefix `req_` followed by exactly twenty characters drawn from the uppercase letters and the digits, as the example shows.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

List endpoints accept two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of elements returned; it defaults to 10, may not be below 1 and may not exceed 100. `offset` is the number of elements to skip and defaults to 0. A `limit` or `offset` that is out of range, is not an integer, or is supplied with an empty value is rejected with `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED` (HTTP 422); neither is ever silently clamped, and both are validated before any other query parameter on the same request. Unless a specific endpoint declares a different order, every list is sorted by `created_at` ascending, with creation sequence — the order in which the rows were written — as the tie-break whenever two rows share a `created_at`, which is the ordinary case under a logical clock that only advances when a test drives it; a row's `id` is never an ordering key. The one deliberate exception to the default order is the ranked unit search, whose ordering is defined by its own ranking expression and whose pagination is applied only after ranking.

The `meta` object of a list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more`. `total_count` counts every element that satisfies the request's filters before the pagination window is applied, and `has_more` is true exactly when elements remain beyond the current window. An empty result set is a successful 200 response with an empty `data` array and a `total_count` of 0 — never an error — and an `offset` past the end of the result set is likewise a successful 200 with an empty `data` array, the requested `offset` echoed back, the true `total_count` and `has_more` false. Filters are conjunctive: a request supplying several narrows to the rows that satisfy all of them. A filter that names an enumerated axis rejects a value outside that enumeration with `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED` (HTTP 422) rather than returning an empty page; a filter supplied with an empty value is treated as absent and applied not at all; and a query parameter the addressed endpoint does not declare is ignored without error.

### 2.4. Common Resource Properties, Registry Codes and the Logical Clock

Every resource in the system carries a server-minted `id`: a UUID string with no type prefix of any kind. Clients never supply an `id`. Alongside it, most resources carry a standard audit triple:

- `created_at` (string) — an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, recording when the row was first written.
- `updated_at` (string) — an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp in the same format, refreshed on every mutation of the row, including mutations the row's owner never requested (a cascade, a nightly-run advance, a recomputation of a derived field).
- `version` (integer) — a counter that starts at 1 on creation and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation of the row. Recomputing a derived value such as `balance_cents`, `occupied_slots` or `quality_acceptance_score` is a mutation for this purpose. The three reconciled figures qualify that statement: the reconciliations of `balance_cents`, `occupied_slots` and `cumulative_excursion_minutes_30d` each compare the recomputed figure against the one already stored and rewrite the row only when the two differ, so a reconciliation that lands on the value already held is not a mutation and leaves that row's `updated_at` and `version` untouched.

The `QualityTest` resource is the single exception: a test record is immutable once written, so it carries only `id` and `created_at`. It has no `updated_at` field and no `version` field, and neither field appears in its representation.

`CordBloodUnit` additionally carries a human-facing label, `unit_registry_code`, which is the literal prefix `CBU-` followed by the unit's creation sequence number zero-padded to six digits. The sequence is 1-based and ordered by `created_at` ascending across all units in the bank, so the first unit ever minted is `CBU-000001`. The code is never derived from the unit's `id`.

Every deadline, expiry, validity window, buffered interval and delinquency clock in this specification reads a single server-side logical clock, never the host's wall clock. Before the logical clock has been set even once, it reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and it must not fall back to wall-clock time under any circumstance. An administrative test-driving capability sets the clock to a supplied `current_utc` instant and echoes it back; setting the clock mutates no entity and fires no cascade — the next business call or nightly batch is what observes the new time.

### 2.5. Reverse Reachability and Left-to-Right Path Resolution

Every entity in this system is a peer collection mounted directly under `/cbb`. Parentage is expressed only through foreign-key fields carried in request bodies — never through nested creation paths — so no resource is ever created by posting into another resource's sub-path. Nested `GET` paths exist for exactly one purpose: to make every foreign key reverse-reachable, so that a client holding a parent can discover its children. They create nothing and mutate nothing.

Two entities can only be discovered this way, and implementers should treat those channels as load-bearing. A `CordBloodUnit` is never created by a direct request; it is synthesized when a collection is received at the laboratory, and the only way to find it is to list the units of that collection. A `TemperatureExcursion` is likewise never created by a direct request; it is opened by a temperature reading that breaches the alarm threshold, and the only way to find it is to list the excursions of that tank.

When a path carries an identifier, the identifier is resolved before any body field is examined. Where a path carries more than one identifier, resolution proceeds strictly left to right and the first unresolvable segment determines the error. Each entity has its own 404 code — `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STORAGE_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_KIT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_COLLECTION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PROCESSING_RUN_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_QUALITY_TEST_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SCREENING_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TANK_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EXCURSION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LISTING_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_RESERVATION_NOT_FOUND` — and the same catalogue is reused when a foreign key supplied in a request body fails to resolve. A body reference to a non-existent enrollment is `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_FOUND`, a body reference to a non-existent unit is `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND`, and so on for every reference the system accepts: `enrollment_id`, `kit_id`, `collection_id`, `unit_id`, `tank_id`, `account_id`, `reservation_id` and `confirmatory_test_id`.

### 2.6. Cascade Discipline, Compensation and Deterministic Ordering

A great many operations in this system mutate more than one row. When they do, the whole cascade is part of the operation's contract: every secondary row named in §3 must be advanced, its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` incremented, in the same request. Three disciplines govern this.

**Determinism.** Whenever a cascade walks a set of rows, it walks them in a pinned order so the outcome is reproducible. Units within a tank are processed by `created_at` ascending; registry listings of a unit by `created_at` ascending; reservations of a unit by `created_at` ascending; invoices in the nightly batch by `due_date` ascending with `created_at` ascending as the tie-break; accounts in the nightly batch by `created_at` ascending. A row's `id` is never used as an ordering key anywhere in this system.

**Compensation.** Several cascades exist in matched pairs, where the reversing operation must restore exactly the state the forward operation destroyed — no more and no less. Re-signing consent after an amendment returns held kits and consent-suspended listings to where they were. Resolving a temperature excursion with a benign disposition returns quarantined units and quarantine-suspended listings to where they were, but deliberately does **not** revive the reservations that quarantine cancelled. Cancelling a confirmed hold returns the unit and its listing to exactly their pre-confirmation values. Paying an account's balance down to zero reverses the delinquency ladder for every unit not yet disposed. Where a compensation is deliberately partial, §3 says so explicitly.

**Conservation.** A tank's `occupied_slots` is not a counter that is nudged up and down; it is always the count of units whose `tank_id` is that tank and whose `availability_status` is neither `RELEASED` nor `DISPOSED`. A unit that is `QUARANTINED` or `PENDING_ABANDONMENT` still physically occupies its slot and is still counted. After every mutation — including every compensating one — the invariant must hold that `occupied_slots` equals that count, and that `capacity_slots` minus `occupied_slots` is the number of free slots offered to the next cryopreservation.

### 2.7. Optimistic Concurrency on the Cord Blood Unit

Two operations attach a new record to a `CordBloodUnit` on the strength of what the client believes about that unit — its counts, its tier, its eligibility, the holds already standing against it. Both therefore require the client to prove that its read of the unit is current.

Placing a hold on a unit requires `expected_unit_version` in the request body, carrying the client's last observed `version` of that unit. Publishing a registry listing for a unit likewise requires `expected_unit_version` in its request body. In both cases the field is mandatory, not optional. If the supplied value does not equal the unit's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION` (HTTP 412), and nothing is written.

The intent differs slightly between the two. A hold does not itself change a unit's `availability_status`, and two holds with disjoint windows may legitimately coexist on the same `LISTED` unit; the version check is what catches the case where another writer has already attached a hold, or a listing, that this client never observed. For publication, a relisting after a withdrawn listing is a legitimate act on its own terms; the version check exists so that a publication built on a stale read of the unit's counts, tier or eligibility can never reach a registry. No other endpoint in this system accepts a version field, and no other endpoint performs a version check.

### 2.8. Numeric Precision, Cent Arithmetic and Rounding Order

All rounding in this system is **round half up**, and the number of decimal places is fixed per quantity rather than global. Volumes in millilitres are carried to 1 decimal place. Percentages — recovery, volume reduction, viability-derived figures — are carried to 2 decimal places. Cell doses are carried to 2 decimal places. The unit acceptance score is carried to 2 decimal places. Elapsed transit hours are carried to 2 decimal places. All money is carried as an integer number of cents; there are no fractional cents anywhere in the system, and no monetary field is ever expressed in dollars.

Two rounding-order rules are pinned and must not be reorganized, because they change results:

- **Score composition rounds once, at the end.** The three weighted components of a unit's acceptance score, and their weighted sum, are never rounded. Only the final score is rounded, exactly once, to 2 decimal places.
- **Invoice lines round individually, then sum.** Each invoice line is rounded to whole cents before it participates in the total, and the total is never re-rounded afterwards. Summing unrounded lines and rounding the sum is a different — and wrong — answer that can differ by a cent.

A third rule concerns gates rather than storage: where a threshold comparison and a persisted display value derive from the same quantity, the **gate reads the unrounded quantity** and the persisted field is the rounded one. The collection-to-receipt window is the canonical case: the acceptance decision is made on the exact elapsed time, while `hours_to_receipt` is the rounded record of it.

### 2.9. The Order of Adjudication

Many operations in this bank are guarded by several checks at once, and which error a caller sees is part of the contract. Checks are therefore evaluated in a single fixed order, and the **first** check that fails determines both the error identifier and the HTTP status; no later check may mask an earlier one, and no earlier check may be skipped because a later one would also fail. The ladder, from first-evaluated to last-evaluated, is:

1. **Authentication** — both headers present and `X-User-Role` one of the ten declared roles, on every endpoint. Otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (401).
2. **Role authorization** — the caller's role appears in the addressed endpoint's authorized-role set, on every endpoint. Otherwise `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (403).
3. **Parent-guardian ownership** — a `ParentGuardian` addresses only its own enrollment and rows reachable from it, on the ten operations enumerated in §2.1. Otherwise `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (403). How this rung interacts with rung 6 depends on whether the check needs a lookup, and the difference is deliberate. On the seven enrollment-addressed operations the check compares the path's enrollment identifier straight to `X-User-ID` and reads nothing, so a `ParentGuardian` naming an enrollment that is not its own is refused with the 403 here even when no such enrollment exists at all; only when the identifier does equal its own does the request proceed to rung 6 and, if nothing is stored under it, answer `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_FOUND`. The account-addressed and invoice-addressed forms must instead load the row to discover which enrollment owns it, so they can only fire on a row that resolves: an unresolvable storage-account or invoice identifier passes this rung silently and is answered by rung 6 with `ERR_STORAGE_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND`.
4. **Transplant-centre ownership** — a `TransplantCenter` addresses only a reservation whose `requesting_center_user_id` is its own, on the five operations enumerated in §2.1. Otherwise `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (403). This check also has to load the reservation to read that field, so it behaves like the account-addressed form above: an unresolvable reservation identifier passes this rung and is answered by rung 6 with `ERR_RESERVATION_NOT_FOUND`.
5. **Request shape** — on every write endpoint: the body parses, every required field is present, every enum value is a declared member, every numeric field lies inside its declared range and every timestamp is RFC 3339 UTC. Otherwise `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED` (422). Date-valued body fields are the exception to that last clause: `expected_delivery_date`, `prepaid_through_date`, `billing_period_start`, `billing_period_end` and `intended_transplant_date` each carry a calendar date rather than an instant, so a bare `YYYY-MM-DD` is the expected form and is accepted, while a full RFC 3339 UTC instant is accepted too and simply truncated to its date; anything else is `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED`. Either form is normalized to midnight UTC, and from then on the value is stored, compared and echoed in the same ISO 8601 UTC form as any timestamp — so `2026-08-20` reads back as `2026-08-20T00:00:00.000Z`, and the date fields the server derives from these (an invoice's `due_date`, an account's `paid_through_date`) carry that same form. Five further shape conventions hold on every write endpoint. An absent request body and an empty request body are both read as the empty JSON object, so an endpoint with no required fields accepts either; conversely a body that is not a JSON object — malformed JSON, a JSON array, a bare scalar, or a JSON payload sent under a non-JSON `Content-Type` — is `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED` (422), never HTTP 400 and never HTTP 415. A field present with an explicit `null` is treated exactly as though it were absent, so a required field set to `null` is `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED` while an optional one set to `null` falls back to its declared default. Types are checked strictly rather than coerced: a boolean field rejects `0`, `1` and `"true"`; a numeric field rejects booleans and numeric strings; and a field declared as an integer rejects a non-integer JSON number such as `1.5`. Body fields the endpoint does not declare are ignored rather than rejected, including a client-supplied `id`, which never influences the server-minted one. And an acceptable RFC 3339 UTC timestamp may be written with a lowercase `t` or `z`, with a space in place of the `T`, with or without fractional digits, with the zone designator omitted altogether (which is read as UTC), or with a numeric `±HH:MM` or `±HHMM` offset that is converted to UTC before storage; a value that names no real calendar instant — `2026-13-01T00:00:00Z`, a bare `YYYY-MM-DD` where an instant is expected, an epoch-seconds number, or any non-string — is `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED`.
6. **Path existence** — every identifier in the path resolves, left to right, on every endpoint that has one. Otherwise the entity-specific 404 from §2.5.
7. **Body foreign-key existence** — every foreign key supplied in a body resolves. This applies to opening a storage account, dispatching a collection kit, recording a collection, filing a maternal screening, opening a processing run, recording a quality test, cryopreserving a unit, publishing a registry listing, issuing a storage invoice and placing a reservation. Otherwise the entity-specific 404 from §2.5.
8. **Unit version currency** — `expected_unit_version` equals the addressed unit's current `version`, on reservation creation and registry-listing creation. Otherwise `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION` (412).
9. **State admissibility** — the target row is in a state from which the requested transition is declared, on every action endpoint. This rung covers every axis in the system: enrollment lifecycle and consent, kit, collection, unit processing and availability, processing run, excursion, listing, invoice and reservation. The error is the one belonging to the entity whose state was wrong — `ERR_INVALID_ENROLLMENT_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_UNIT_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_RESERVATION_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_KIT_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_COLLECTION_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_EXCURSION_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_RUN_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_INVOICE_STATE` or `ERR_INVALID_LISTING_STATE` — each at HTTP 409.
10. **Segregation of duties** — on eligibility determination and on release, per §2.10. Otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (403).
11. **Consent currency** — on kit dispatch, collection recording, registry publication and cryopreservation, per §2.11. Otherwise `ERR_CONSENT_NOT_CURRENT` (409).
12. **Amendment budget** — on opening a consent amendment, the enrollment's `consent_amendment_count` is strictly below three. Otherwise `ERR_CONSENT_AMENDMENT_LIMIT` (409).
13. **Maternal panel present and unexpired** — on eligibility determination, registry publication and release, the enrollment has at least one `MaternalScreening` in status `COMPLETED` whose `valid_until` is not in the past. Otherwise `ERR_MATERNAL_SCREENING_INCOMPLETE` (409).
14. **Maternal panel not reactive** — on those same three operations, the governing completed screening's `overall_result` is not `REACTIVE`. Otherwise `ERR_MATERNAL_SCREENING_REACTIVE` (422).
15. **Sterility negative** — on eligibility determination, cryopreservation and registry publication, the unit's post-processing test recorded `sterility_result` of `NEGATIVE`. Otherwise `ERR_STERILITY_POSITIVE` (422).
16. **Acceptance test present** — on eligibility determination and cryopreservation, the unit has at least one quality test whose `test_kind` is `POST_PROCESSING`. Otherwise `ERR_NO_QUALITY_TEST` (409).
17. **Confirmatory test linked** — on release, the reservation's `confirmatory_test_id` is not null and names a passing confirmatory test. Otherwise `ERR_CONFIRMATORY_TEST_REQUIRED` (409).
18. **Unit eligibility** — on reservation creation, registry publication and release, the unit's `eligibility_status` is `ELIGIBLE`. Otherwise `ERR_UNIT_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (409).
19. **Release dose floor** — on release, `post_thaw_dose_x1e7_per_kg` is at least 2.50. Otherwise `ERR_DOSE_BELOW_MINIMUM` (422).
20. **Public listing thresholds** — on registry publication, the unit's post-processing counts clear the public floors of 900 million total nucleated cells, 1500 thousand CD34+ cells and 85.0 percent viability. Otherwise `ERR_QUALITY_BELOW_PUBLIC_THRESHOLD` (422).
21. **Program-type fit** — on registry publication the unit must be `PUBLIC_DONATION`; on opening a storage account, and on issuing an invoice against that account, the enrollment must be `PRIVATE_FAMILY`. Otherwise `ERR_PROGRAM_TYPE_MISMATCH` (422).
22. **One account per enrollment** — on opening a storage account, the enrollment does not already own one. Otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_STORAGE_ACCOUNT` (409).
23. **One live listing per unit** — on registry publication, the unit has no listing in `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`. Otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_LISTING` (409).
24. **Kit shelf life** — on recording a collection and on receiving it, the kit's `expires_at` is not in the past. Otherwise `ERR_KIT_EXPIRED` (409).
25. **Processing window** — on receipt, the exact elapsed hours from `collected_at` to the logical clock do not exceed 48.0. Otherwise `ERR_PROCESSING_WINDOW_EXCEEDED` (422).
26. **Transport temperature** — on receipt, `transport_temp_c` lies inside the closed range from 2.0 to 30.0 degrees Celsius. Otherwise `ERR_TRANSPORT_TEMP_OUT_OF_RANGE` (422).
27. **Tank capacity** — on cryopreservation, the target tank's `occupied_slots` is strictly below its `capacity_slots`. Otherwise `ERR_TANK_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (409).
28. **Tank operability** — on cryopreservation the target tank's `status` must be `ONLINE`, and on release the tank holding the unit must be `ONLINE`; `ALARM`, `MAINTENANCE` and `OFFLINE` all block both. Otherwise `ERR_TANK_NOT_OPERATIONAL` (409).
29. **Maintenance window** — on cryopreservation, `freeze_at` does not fall inside the tank's maintenance window, which is closed at its start and open at its end. Otherwise `ERR_MAINTENANCE_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (409).
30. **Rolling excursion budget** — on cryopreservation, the tank's `cumulative_excursion_minutes_30d` does not exceed 120 minutes; the comparison is strictly greater, so a tank sitting at exactly 120 still accepts units. Otherwise `ERR_EXCURSION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (409).
31. **Hold window separation** — on reservation creation, the requested window widened by 24 hours on each side does not overlap an incumbent hold on the same unit. Otherwise `ERR_HOLD_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (409).
32. **Confirmation window** — on confirming a hold, the logical clock is no later than 72 hours after the reservation's `created_at`. Otherwise `ERR_CONFIRMATION_WINDOW_EXPIRED` (409).
33. **Account standing** — on reservation creation and registry publication, the unit's storage account is not in `DELINQUENT`, `ABANDONMENT_NOTICE` or `ABANDONED`. Otherwise `ERR_ACCOUNT_DELINQUENT` (402).
34. **Zero balance at release** — on release, the unit's storage account has a `balance_cents` of exactly 0. Otherwise `ERR_OUTSTANDING_BALANCE` (402).
35. **Payment size** — on posting a payment, the supplied `amount_cents` does not exceed the invoice's residual, that residual being `amount_due_cents` minus `amount_paid_cents`. Otherwise `ERR_PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_BALANCE` (422).

The practical consequence is worth spelling out, because it is easy to get backwards. On a release attempt where the unit is ineligible **and** the account is delinquent **and** the dose is short, the caller sees the eligibility failure, because rung 18 precedes rungs 19 and 34. On a cryopreservation attempt into a full tank that is also in `ALARM`, the caller sees `ERR_TANK_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`, because rung 27 precedes rung 28. And because segregation of duties sits at rung 10, a director who is disqualified from determining a unit's eligibility learns that immediately, rather than having the answer masked by a missing maternal panel at rung 13.

### 2.10. Segregation of Clinical Duties

Two clinical judgements about a unit must be made by different people from the people who produced the evidence for them. First, the `MedicalDirector` who determines a unit's eligibility must not be the user recorded in `performed_by_user_id` on that unit's `POST_PROCESSING` quality test — the technologist who measured the unit may not also judge it. Second, the `MedicalDirector` who authorizes a release must not be the user recorded in `eligibility_determined_by_user_id` on the unit — the director who judged the unit may not also ship it. Both comparisons are on `X-User-ID`, both are absolute (there is no override flag, no supervisory bypass, and `PlatformAdmin` is not exempt), and both fail with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` at HTTP 403.

### 2.11. Consent as a Standing Precondition

Informed consent in this system is not a one-time checkbox; it is a live property of the enrollment that can lapse and be restored. `consent_status` is a cyclic axis independent of the enrollment's operational `lifecycle_status`, and four downstream operations require it to be currently `CONSENTED`: dispatching a collection kit, recording a collection, publishing a registry listing and cryopreserving a unit. In addition, the enrollment's `consent_version` must equal the enrollment's current `consent_version` — that is, the work must be built on the signature that is in force now, not on a superseded one. An in-flight amendment leaves the enrollment in `AMENDMENT_PENDING` and therefore blocks all four, and a revoked consent blocks them permanently. The rejection in every case is `ERR_CONSENT_NOT_CURRENT` at HTTP 409.

### 2.12. Two Custody Models: Public Donation and Private Family

`program_type` is chosen once, at enrollment, is immutable thereafter, and is the single switch that decides how a unit is owned, judged and paid for. It is copied onto the `CordBloodUnit` at the moment the unit is minted, and thereafter the unit's own copy governs.

A `PUBLIC_DONATION` unit is donated to the community. It is never billed, has no storage account, is judged against the strict public acceptance tier, and reaches inventory only by being published on a registry — cryopreserving it leaves it unavailable until a registry operator lists it. A `PRIVATE_FAMILY` unit remains the family's property. It is billed to a `StorageAccount` opened against the enrollment, is judged against the more permissive private acceptance tier, and enters inventory the moment it is cryopreserved. Exactly one storage account may exist per enrollment, only a `PRIVATE_FAMILY` enrollment may have one, and invoices may only be issued against accounts of such enrollments; a mismatch anywhere along that chain is `ERR_PROGRAM_TYPE_MISMATCH`. The consent flag `research_use_authorized` records, at the enrollment level, whether a unit that fails clinical acceptance may nonetheless be diverted to research, and defaults to false; `hla_typing_consent` records whether the family authorizes the HLA typing that public registry publication requires, and likewise defaults to false.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Enrollment, Consent and the Amendment Cycle

Everything in the bank begins with an **Enrollment**, registered by an `IntakeCoordinator` on behalf of an expectant parent. Registration supplies the custody model in `program_type` (either `PRIVATE_FAMILY` or `PUBLIC_DONATION`, immutable once written), the consenting parent's `primary_parent_name` of between 1 and 120 characters, the `expected_delivery_date` that will later anchor the maternal-screening window, and the `collection_site_code` of between 1 and 20 characters identifying the hospital that will perform the collection. Two consent flags may be supplied at registration and both default to false when omitted: `research_use_authorized` and `hla_typing_consent`. A freshly registered enrollment has `lifecycle_status` of `CONSENT_PENDING`, `consent_status` of `PENDING`, a `consent_version` of 0 and a `consent_amendment_count` of 0, and its `consent_signed_at` and `withdrawal_reason_code` are both null. Coordinators browsing the enrollment population may narrow the list by `lifecycle_status`, by `consent_status`, by `program_type` or by `collection_site_code`.

The enrollment carries **two independent state axes** and confusing them is the single most common modelling error here. `lifecycle_status` tracks operational progress through `CONSENT_PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, `KIT_DISPATCHED`, `COLLECTED` and the two terminal values `CLOSED` and `WITHDRAWN`. `consent_status` tracks the currency of the consent document through `PENDING`, `CONSENTED`, `AMENDMENT_PENDING` and the terminal `REVOKED`. The second axis is cyclic: a consent may be amended and re-signed repeatedly without the operational axis moving at all.

Signing consent — an action available to the `ParentGuardian` themselves, to an `IntakeCoordinator` acting on their behalf, or to a `PlatformAdmin` — requires a `consent_document_hash` identifying the signed instrument — a value the server validates for shape but does not persist, the enrollment carrying no field for it — and may optionally restate `research_use_authorized` and `hla_typing_consent`, each of which, when omitted or sent as an explicit `null`, leaves the enrollment's stored flag exactly as it was rather than resetting it to false. Every signature advances `consent_status` to `CONSENTED`, increments `consent_version` by exactly 1, and stamps `consent_signed_at` with the logical clock. Only the **first** signature also moves the operational axis, `CONSENT_PENDING → ACTIVE`; later re-signatures leave `lifecycle_status` exactly where it was. Signature is admissible from `consent_status` of `PENDING` and from `AMENDMENT_PENDING`, and is refused with `ERR_INVALID_ENROLLMENT_STATE` when the enrollment's `lifecycle_status` has already reached `CLOSED` or `WITHDRAWN`.

Consent amendments exist because families change their minds about research use and HLA typing after the fact. Opening one carries an `amendment_reason_code` — likewise validated for shape and not persisted, the enrollment carrying no field for it — and drives the consent axis `CONSENTED → AMENDMENT_PENDING`, incrementing `consent_amendment_count` by 1. A family may traverse this loop at most three times: when `consent_amendment_count` has already reached 3, a further amendment is refused with `ERR_CONSENT_AMENDMENT_LIMIT`. Attempting to amend a consent that has never been signed — one still sitting at `PENDING` — is not an amendment-budget failure but a state failure, `ERR_INVALID_ENROLLMENT_STATE`.

While an amendment is open, the bank must stop relying on the superseded signature, and two cascades enforce that. Every collection kit of the enrollment in status `DISPATCHED` moves to `HELD` and records a `hold_reason_code` of `CONSENT_AMENDMENT_PENDING`. Every registry listing belonging to the enrollment's units whose `listing_status` is `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED` with a `suspension_reason_code` of `CONSENT_AMENDMENT_PENDING`, because a public listing asserts current consent. The re-signature that closes the loop, `AMENDMENT_PENDING → CONSENTED`, compensates both exactly: every `HELD` kit of the enrollment whose `expires_at` has not yet passed returns to `DISPATCHED` and has its `hold_reason_code` cleared to null, and every listing that was suspended specifically for `CONSENT_AMENDMENT_PENDING` returns to `ACTIVE` with its `suspension_reason_code` cleared to null. A `HELD` kit whose shelf life lapsed during the amendment is deliberately not revived — it will be swept to `EXPIRED` by the nightly batch instead.

Withdrawal is the parent's exit. It records a `withdrawal_reason_code`, drives `lifecycle_status` to `WITHDRAWN` from `CONSENT_PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, `KIT_DISPATCHED` or `COLLECTED`, and simultaneously drives `consent_status` to `REVOKED` from `PENDING`, `CONSENTED` or `AMENDMENT_PENDING`. It is admissible only while `lifecycle_status` is not already `CLOSED` or `WITHDRAWN`, and from `COLLECTED` it carries one extra guard: no unit of the enrollment may have an `availability_status` of `RESERVED` or `RELEASED`, because a unit already promised to, or shipped to, a patient cannot be recalled; that guard reports `ERR_INVALID_UNIT_STATE`, the error of the unit whose state blocks the exit, and it is evaluated only after the enrollment's own lifecycle rung has passed. The compensating reach of withdrawal grows with how far the enrollment had progressed. Withdrawing from `CONSENT_PENDING` revokes consent and nothing else. Withdrawing from `ACTIVE` or `KIT_DISPATCHED` additionally returns every kit of the enrollment in `DISPATCHED` or `HELD` to `RETURNED` with `hold_reason_code` cleared, and voids every storage invoice of the enrollment's account whose status is `ISSUED`, `PAST_DUE` or `DELINQUENT`, after which the account's `balance_cents` is recomputed. Withdrawing from `COLLECTED` does all of that and, in addition, disposes inventory: every unit of the enrollment whose `availability_status` is neither `RELEASED` nor `DISPOSED` moves to `DISPOSED` with a `disposition_reason` of `CONSENT_REVOKED`, a `disposed_at` stamped from the logical clock, a `processing_status` forced to `DISCARDED` and any `quarantine_reason_code` cleared to null; because those units left inventory, the `occupied_slots` of every tank that held one is recomputed; every registry listing of those units in `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` moves to `WITHDRAWN`; every hold standing against those units in `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` is cancelled with a `cancellation_reason_code` of `UNIT_DISPOSED`; and the invoice void and balance recomputation happen as above.

The enrollment's own quiet ending is `COLLECTED → CLOSED`, which no client requests directly. It fires as a cascade the moment every unit reachable from the enrollment has an `availability_status` in `DISPOSED` or `RELEASED` — whether that last unit left by transplant release or by disposal. At least one unit must exist for the cascade to fire at all: an enrollment that reached `COLLECTED` but never minted a unit, because its collection was rejected, is never closed this way and simply stays `COLLECTED`. The close in turn drives the enrollment's `StorageAccount`, if it has one, to `CLOSED`, and only from `ACTIVE` or `ABANDONED` — an account still standing at `PAST_DUE`, `DELINQUENT` or `ABANDONMENT_NOTICE` is left on its rung.

### 3.2. Kit Dispatch, Collection at Delivery and Laboratory Receipt

Once an enrollment is `ACTIVE` and its consent is current, an `IntakeCoordinator` dispatches a **CollectionKit** to the delivery suite by supplying the `enrollment_id`, the manufacturing `kit_lot_code` of between 1 and 30 characters, and the outbound `courier_tracking_code` of between 1 and 40 characters. Dispatch stamps `dispatched_at` from the logical clock and computes `expires_at` as `dispatched_at` plus a shelf life of 120 days; the kit begins in `DISPATCHED`, and the enrollment advances `ACTIVE → KIT_DISPATCHED`. Only one kit may be outstanding at a time: dispatch is refused while the enrollment already has a kit in `DISPATCHED` or `HELD`, with `ERR_INVALID_KIT_STATE`. The enrollment's own axis is examined before the kit population, so the ordinary second-dispatch attempt — where the enrollment has itself already advanced to `KIT_DISPATCHED` — is refused with `ERR_INVALID_ENROLLMENT_STATE` rather than the kit error, and consent currency is checked after both. A kit's status ranges over `DISPATCHED`, `HELD`, and the three terminal values `USED`, `EXPIRED` and `RETURNED`, and `hold_reason_code` is populated only while the kit is `HELD`.

At delivery, `CollectionSite` staff record the **Collection** against the kit. The payload names the `kit_id`, the instant of collection in `collected_at`, the gross `collection_volume_ml` (strictly greater than zero, carried to 1 decimal place), the `anticoagulant_volume_ml` of citrate-phosphate-dextrose present within that gross figure (at least zero and strictly less than the gross volume), and the `delivery_mode`, which is either `VAGINAL` or `CESAREAN` and is recorded for registry reporting. The system immediately derives `net_volume_ml` as the gross collection volume minus the anticoagulant volume, rounded half up to 1 decimal place — this net figure, not the gross one, is what the laboratory will later reduce. Recording a collection consumes the kit (`DISPATCHED → USED`) and advances the enrollment `KIT_DISPATCHED → COLLECTED`. The kit must be in `DISPATCHED` at that moment: a kit sitting in `HELD` because of an open amendment, or already `USED`, `EXPIRED` or `RETURNED`, yields `ERR_INVALID_KIT_STATE`. The kit's axis is examined before the enrollment's, which must itself read `KIT_DISPATCHED` or the request is refused with `ERR_INVALID_ENROLLMENT_STATE`. Separately, a kit whose `expires_at` already lies in the past yields `ERR_KIT_EXPIRED`, a rung below both of those. A collection begins life in `COLLECTED` and its `rejection_reason_code` is null, as are its `transport_temp_c`, `received_at` and `hours_to_receipt`, all three of which only the receipt step fills in.

Receipt at the processing laboratory is a `ProcessingTechnologist` action carrying only `transport_temp_c`, the shipper's temperature on arrival, recorded to 1 decimal place. Receipt is the system's hardest time gate, and it is evaluated on exact arithmetic: the elapsed time from `collected_at` to the logical clock, expressed in hours as an exact value, must not exceed 48.0 hours, and a shipment arriving at exactly 48.0 hours is accepted while 48.01 hours is not. Exceeding the window is `ERR_PROCESSING_WINDOW_EXCEEDED`. The same elapsed figure, rounded half up to 2 decimal places, is persisted as `hours_to_receipt`; the acceptance decision never reads that rounded value. Receipt also demands that the kit be `USED`, that the kit's `expires_at` not have passed, and that `transport_temp_c` lie within the closed range from 2.0 to 30.0 degrees Celsius — a shipper at exactly 2.0 or exactly 30.0 is acceptable, anything outside is `ERR_TRANSPORT_TEMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`.

A successful receipt advances the collection `COLLECTED → RECEIVED`, stamps `received_at` from the logical clock, and **mints the CordBloodUnit** — the one and only way a unit ever comes into existence, exactly one per collection. The new unit records its `collection_id`, starts with a `processing_status` of `RECEIVED`, an `availability_status` of `UNAVAILABLE` and an `eligibility_status` of `PENDING`, copies `program_type` from the owning enrollment, receives its `unit_registry_code` from the bank-wide creation sequence, and resolves its `storage_account_id` by walking from its collection to that collection's kit, from the kit to the kit's enrollment, and from the enrollment to the storage account whose `enrollment_id` matches; the field stays null for a `PUBLIC_DONATION` unit and for a `PRIVATE_FAMILY` enrollment that has not yet opened an account. That walk happens once, at mint, and is never revisited: opening a storage account afterwards does not backfill the units already minted, so such a unit stays unbilled — no invoice reaches it and no settlement is ever posted for it. Its `tank_id` and `slot_code` are null until cryopreservation, and `cryopreserved_at`, `quarantine_reason_code`, `disposition_reason`, `disposed_at`, `eligibility_reason_code`, `eligibility_determined_by_user_id`, `post_processing_volume_ml`, `post_processing_tnc_millions`, `post_processing_cd34_thousands`, `post_processing_viability_pct` and `quality_acceptance_score` are all null at this point — the whole characterization block arrives later, from the run and the acceptance test.

Not every collection survives. A technologist or a `MedicalDirector` may reject a collection that has not yet been received, supplying a `reason_code` and an optional `note`. The reason code selects the terminal state deterministically: `LATE` drives the collection to `REJECTED_LATE`, `TEMP` to `REJECTED_TEMP`, `VOLUME` to `REJECTED_VOLUME`, and `CONTAMINATION` to `DISCARDED`. In every case the supplied code is persisted in `rejection_reason_code` and **no unit is created**. Rejection requires the collection to still be in `COLLECTED`; attempting to reject or receive a collection that has already left that state is `ERR_INVALID_COLLECTION_STATE`.

### 3.3. Volume Reduction and the Acceptance Test

A unit sitting in `processing_status` of `RECEIVED` is raw: too voluminous to freeze and not yet characterized. A `ProcessingTechnologist` opens a **ProcessingRun** against it, naming the `unit_id`, the reduction `method` (either `HES_SEDIMENTATION` or `AUTOMATED_SEPARATION`) and the operator's pre-reduction count in `pre_tnc_millions`, which must be strictly greater than zero. Opening a run is admissible only when the unit has no other run in `IN_PROGRESS`, only when the unit's `availability_status` is not `DISPOSED`, `RELEASED` or `QUARANTINED`, and only while its `processing_status` is exactly `RECEIVED`. The live-run test comes first of the three and is reported as `ERR_INVALID_RUN_STATE`, so a unit carrying a run in `IN_PROGRESS` sees that error even when its own axes would also refuse it; both unit-axis failures are `ERR_INVALID_UNIT_STATE`, with the availability axis examined before the processing axis. The new run starts in `IN_PROGRESS`, stamps `started_at` from the logical clock, records the acting technologist's `X-User-ID` in `operator_user_id`, and **freezes** `pre_volume_ml` from the net volume of the unit's collection at the moment of run creation. That frozen figure is never re-read afterwards: if the collection's volumes were somehow to change, the run's arithmetic would still use the value captured at creation. The run's `post_volume_ml`, `post_tnc_millions`, `tnc_recovery_pct`, `volume_reduction_pct` and `completed_at` are all null until the run is closed. Opening the run drives the unit `RECEIVED → PROCESSING`.

Closing the run supplies an `outcome` of either `COMPLETED` or `FAILED`, together with `post_volume_ml` (strictly greater than zero, 1 decimal place) and `post_tnc_millions` (strictly greater than zero). The run must be in `IN_PROGRESS`; otherwise `ERR_INVALID_RUN_STATE`. **Both** outcomes stamp `completed_at` from the logical clock and compute both recovery metrics — this symmetry matters, because a failed run is still a measured run. `tnc_recovery_pct` is the post-reduction count divided by the pre-reduction count, multiplied by 100, rounded half up to 2 decimal places. `volume_reduction_pct` is the frozen pre-reduction volume minus the post-reduction volume, divided by the frozen pre-reduction volume, multiplied by 100, and likewise rounded half up to 2 decimal places.

The two outcomes then diverge on the unit. `IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED` advances the unit `PROCESSING → VOLUME_REDUCED` and copies the run's post-reduction volume onto the unit as `post_processing_volume_ml`. `IN_PROGRESS → FAILED` reverts the unit `PROCESSING → RECEIVED` and sets the unit's `post_processing_volume_ml` back to null, precisely so that a second run may be opened on a unit whose first reduction went wrong.

Characterization happens through **QualityTest** records, which a technologist writes and which are immutable thereafter. Every test names its `unit_id`, its `test_kind`, the measured `tnc_millions` and `cd34_thousands` (both **integers** strictly greater than zero, as are a processing run's `pre_tnc_millions` and `post_tnc_millions`, so that a non-integer JSON number such as `1.5` in any of the four is `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED`), the 7-AAD `viability_pct` between 0.0 and 100.0 at 1 decimal place, the tested `volume_ml` (strictly greater than zero, 1 decimal place) and the `sterility_result`, which is one of `NEGATIVE`, `POSITIVE` or `PENDING`. A `reservation_id` may additionally be supplied, and is meaningful only for a confirmatory test; it is resolved against the reservation catalogue like any body foreign key — an unknown one being `ERR_RESERVATION_NOT_FOUND` — but it is not itself a field of the test record and never appears in the test's representation, the link being recorded on the reservation instead. No unit-axis rung guards a test at all: one may be recorded whatever the unit's `processing_status` and whatever its `availability_status`, a disposed unit included. Recording a test stamps `performed_at` from the logical clock and records the technologist's `X-User-ID` in `performed_by_user_id` — the anchor that later disqualifies that person from judging the unit. Each test computes its own verdict into `outcome`, either `PASS` or `FAIL`, along with `failure_reason_codes`, the list of threshold codes that failed sorted in ascending order and empty whenever the outcome is `PASS`.

There are two kinds of test and they are judged by different rules. A `POST_PROCESSING` test is the acceptance test on the reduced unit, and at most one may exist per unit; a second attempt while one already exists is refused with `ERR_INVALID_UNIT_STATE`, adjudicated once the body's foreign keys have resolved. Its thresholds depend on the unit's custody tier. For a `PUBLIC_DONATION` unit the floors are 900 million total nucleated cells, 1500 thousand CD34+ cells and 85.0 percent viability; for a `PRIVATE_FAMILY` unit they are 400 million total nucleated cells, 500 thousand CD34+ cells and 75.0 percent viability. The code `STERILITY` is recorded whenever `sterility_result` is anything other than `NEGATIVE`; `TNC` whenever the measured count is below the tier's cell-count floor; `CD34` whenever the measured CD34+ figure is below the tier's CD34+ floor; and `VIABILITY` whenever the measured viability is below the tier's viability floor. Each comparison is strict, so a measurement sitting exactly on a floor passes it.

A `CONFIRMATORY` test is the attached-segment thaw test performed for a transplant reservation, and it is judged against fixed thresholds rather than tier ones. First a recovery figure is derived: the confirmatory test's measured count divided by the unit's stored `post_processing_tnc_millions`, multiplied by 100, rounded half up to 2 decimal places. That figure feeds the verdict and the post-thaw dose but is not itself persisted on the test record, which carries no recovery field of its own; it reaches clients only as the `confirmatory_recovery_pct` of the release summary in §3.7, recomputed there from the same two counts. The code `STERILITY` is recorded only when `sterility_result` is `POSITIVE` — a still-`PENDING` culture does not fail a confirmatory test, which is the one place the two kinds treat sterility differently. `VIABILITY` is recorded when the measured viability is below 70.0 percent, and `RECOVERY` when the derived recovery figure is below 60.0 percent. When a confirmatory test names a reservation and its own outcome is `PASS`, it links itself to that reservation as the reservation's `confirmatory_test_id` and the reservation's post-thaw dose is computed and persisted. That linkage reads the named reservation without regard to its `status`, and it additionally needs the unit to carry a `post_processing_tnc_millions` to divide by: where the unit has none, no recovery figure exists at all, the `RECOVERY` code therefore cannot be recorded, and the test neither links itself nor computes any dose even when its outcome is `PASS`. Linking writes only `confirmatory_test_id` and `post_thaw_dose_x1e7_per_kg` on the reservation; its `dose_variant_applied` stays `PRE_CRYO` until the release itself flips it.

Writing a `POST_PROCESSING` test also republishes the unit's summary counts — `post_processing_tnc_millions`, `post_processing_cd34_thousands` and `post_processing_viability_pct` are copied from that single test — and recomputes the unit's `quality_acceptance_score`, a figure between 0.00 and 100.00 that condenses the whole characterization into one number. The score is built in a pinned order from five inputs and one tier selection, where the tier is again chosen by the unit's `program_type`. For `PUBLIC_DONATION` the score tier targets 1500 million total nucleated cells and 3000 thousand CD34+ cells, uses a viability floor of 85.0 percent, and treats an acceptable post-processing volume as running from a minimum of 24.0 to a maximum of 26.0 millilitres. For `PRIVATE_FAMILY` the score tier targets 900 million total nucleated cells and 1500 thousand CD34+ cells, uses a viability floor of 75.0 percent, and accepts post-processing volumes from a minimum of 20.0 to a maximum of 30.0 millilitres. Given those, the composition is:

- The cell-count component is the smaller of 1.0 and the unit's `post_processing_tnc_millions` divided by the tier's cell-count target.
- The CD34+ component is the smaller of 1.0 and the unit's `post_processing_cd34_thousands` divided by the tier's CD34+ target.
- The viability component is the larger of 0.0 and the quantity formed by subtracting the tier's viability floor from the unit's `post_processing_viability_pct` and dividing that difference by 100.0 minus the same floor.
- A volume penalty of 0.10 applies when the unit's `post_processing_volume_ml` is below the tier's volume minimum or above the tier's volume maximum, and is 0.0 otherwise. A volume sitting exactly on either boundary incurs no penalty, and so does an absent one: when the unit carries no `post_processing_volume_ml` at all — an acceptance test written before any run completed, or after a failed run reset the field to null — the penalty is 0.0 rather than the out-of-band 0.10.
- A sterility gate takes the value 0.0 when the `POST_PROCESSING` test's `sterility_result` is anything other than `NEGATIVE`, and 1.0 when it is `NEGATIVE`.
- The weighted sum is 0.45 times the cell-count component plus 0.35 times the CD34+ component plus 0.20 times the viability component.
- The score is the sterility gate multiplied by the larger of 0.0 and the weighted sum minus the volume penalty, that product multiplied by 100, and the result rounded half up to 2 decimal places.

Rounding order here is not negotiable: the three components and the weighted sum are never rounded, and the single rounding happens once, at the very end. A positive sterility culture therefore zeroes the score outright rather than merely penalizing it. Confirmatory tests never enter this score — only the unit's single `POST_PROCESSING` test does, together with the post-processing volume that the completed run published. The score is derived at that one moment and never again: a later processing run that republishes `post_processing_volume_ml` with a different figure, or resets it to null on failure, does not re-derive the score, so the stored value keeps whatever volume was in force when the acceptance test was written.

The score is meaningful against a tier minimum: 70.00 for a `PUBLIC_DONATION` unit and 55.00 for a `PRIVATE_FAMILY` unit, in both cases cleared by a score at least equal to the minimum.

### 3.4. The Maternal Panel and Eligibility Determination

Cord blood carries the mother's infectious-disease risk, so no unit may be judged without a maternal panel. A **MaternalScreening** is filed by a `MedicalDirector` or an `IntakeCoordinator` against an `enrollment_id`, recording the instant the maternal blood was drawn in `collected_at` and the serology results themselves: `hiv_result` for HIV-1/2, `hbv_result` for hepatitis B, `hcv_result` for hepatitis C, `htlv_result` for HTLV-I/II, `syphilis_result` for the treponemal marker, and optionally `cmv_result` for cytomegalovirus. Each marker takes one of `NON_REACTIVE`, `REACTIVE`, `INDETERMINATE` or `PENDING`. A boolean `nat_confirmed` records whether nucleic-acid testing has confirmed the serology panel and is false until stated otherwise; an omitted `cmv_result` is stored as null rather than defaulted to any marker value. Filing carries no state rung at all: a panel may be filed against an enrollment whatever its `lifecycle_status` and whatever its `consent_status`, one already `WITHDRAWN` or `CLOSED` included.

Only five of the six markers govern anything. `hiv_result`, `hbv_result`, `hcv_result`, `htlv_result` and `syphilis_result` are the **governing markers**; `cmv_result` is informational and never changes the panel's verdict. Filing or amending a panel derives its `status` and `overall_result` in a fixed order. If any governing marker is still `PENDING`, the panel's `status` is `PENDING` and `overall_result` is null. Otherwise, if `collected_at` falls outside the window running from 7 days before the enrollment's `expected_delivery_date` to 7 days after it — a window whose endpoints are both inclusive — the panel's `status` is `INVALID`, `overall_result` is null, and the record can never govern eligibility for the rest of its life. Otherwise the panel's `status` is `COMPLETED` and `overall_result` is derived by precedence: `REACTIVE` if any governing marker is `REACTIVE`; failing that, `INDETERMINATE` if any governing marker is `INDETERMINATE`; failing that, `NON_REACTIVE`. Alongside the verdict, `reactive_marker_codes` is populated with the names of the governing markers whose result is `REACTIVE`, sorted in ascending order and empty when none are. Finally, `valid_until` is set to `collected_at` plus 180 days — the panel's shelf life, roughly six months of clinical currency.

Where an enrollment has several panels, exactly one **governs**: of the panels in status `COMPLETED`, ordered by `created_at` ascending, the last one wins. Panels in `PENDING`, `INVALID` or `EXPIRED` are excluded from that selection entirely, which means a newer `INVALID` panel never displaces an older `COMPLETED` one.

Amending a panel is a `MedicalDirector` privilege and exists because confirmatory nucleic-acid results arrive days after the serology. An amendment may restate any of `hiv_result`, `hbv_result`, `hcv_result`, `htlv_result`, `syphilis_result`, `cmv_result` or `nat_confirmed`, and the panel's verdict is re-derived from scratch by the rules above. A `PENDING` panel may thereby reach `COMPLETED` (when the amendment leaves no governing marker `PENDING` and the draw date is inside the window) or `INVALID` (when the draw date is outside it). A panel already in `COMPLETED` stays in `COMPLETED` — a self-transition that recomputes `overall_result` and `reactive_marker_codes` in place — and this is precisely the post-donation seroconversion case. Only those two statuses admit re-derivation of the verdict: amending a panel that already sits in `INVALID` or `EXPIRED` is accepted and does rewrite the markers, but that panel's `status` and `overall_result` are frozen exactly as they stood, so such a record can never be rehabilitated into a governing one. Its `reactive_marker_codes` is nevertheless re-derived from the amended markers, and its `valid_until` recomputed to the same figure it already held, since `collected_at` is not amendable. An amendment that restates nothing at all is likewise accepted and still counts as a mutation of the row.

When that recomputation turns the **governing** panel `REACTIVE`, a three-level cascade fires on everything the panel authorized. Every unit of the enrollment whose `eligibility_status` was `ELIGIBLE` moves `ELIGIBLE → INELIGIBLE` with an `eligibility_reason_code` of `MATERNAL_REACTIVE`. Every registry listing of those units whose `listing_status` is `ACTIVE` or `MATCHED` moves to `SUSPENDED` with a `suspension_reason_code` of `MATERNAL_REACTIVE`. Every hold on those units in `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` is cancelled with a `cancellation_reason_code` of `MATERNAL_REACTIVE`. One thing deliberately does **not** happen: the unit's `availability_status` is left untouched, so a hold that had already reached `CONFIRMED` stays `CONFIRMED` and the unit stays `RESERVED`. That is intentional — the reactive panel is caught at release time by the maternal check rather than by silently unwinding a confirmed clinical commitment, and the release attempt fails with `ERR_MATERNAL_SCREENING_REACTIVE`.

**Eligibility determination** is the `MedicalDirector`'s formal judgement on a unit and may carry an optional `decision_note`. It is admissible only when the unit's `processing_status` is `VOLUME_REDUCED` or `CRYOPRESERVED` **and** its `eligibility_status` is still `PENDING` — a unit already judged `ELIGIBLE`, `INELIGIBLE` or `INDETERMINATE` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_UNIT_STATE`, exactly as an inadmissible processing status is, the processing axis being examined before the eligibility axis so there is no second determination and no re-judgement of a unit whose eligibility a cascade has since revised. It further requires a governing completed panel, exactly one `POST_PROCESSING` quality test on the unit, and a `sterility_result` of `NEGATIVE` on that test. It also requires that the determining director's `X-User-ID` differ from the test's `performed_by_user_id`; the rejection order among all of these is the ladder in §2.9. When the operation is admissible, the outcome is one of three, and the `eligibility_status` axis starts at `PENDING` for every unit:

- If the governing panel's `overall_result` is `INDETERMINATE`, the unit moves `PENDING → INDETERMINATE` with an `eligibility_reason_code` of `MATERNAL_INDETERMINATE`.
- Otherwise, if the unit's `quality_acceptance_score` is at least its program tier's accept minimum — 70.00 for `PUBLIC_DONATION`, 55.00 for `PRIVATE_FAMILY` — the unit moves `PENDING → ELIGIBLE` with an `eligibility_reason_code` of `ACCEPTED`.
- Otherwise the unit moves `PENDING → INELIGIBLE` with an `eligibility_reason_code` of `SCORE_BELOW_TIER_MINIMUM`.

In all three cases the determining director's `X-User-ID` is written to `eligibility_determined_by_user_id`, which is what later bars that same person from authorizing the release. The determination is not a resource of its own: nothing is minted, the response is the updated unit row itself, and the endpoint therefore answers HTTP 200.

Eligibility can also be revoked without anyone asking. Beyond the `ELIGIBLE → INELIGIBLE` move driven by a newly reactive panel, an `ELIGIBLE` unit moves `ELIGIBLE → INDETERMINATE` with an `eligibility_reason_code` of `SCREENING_EXPIRED` when the nightly batch expires the enrollment's governing panel — that is, when the logical clock passes the panel's `valid_until` and the panel moves `COMPLETED → EXPIRED`. Every `ACTIVE` listing of such a unit simultaneously moves to `SUSPENDED` with a `suspension_reason_code` of `SCREENING_EXPIRED`.

### 3.5. Cryogenic Custody: Tanks, Slots, Alarms and Excursions

A `FacilitiesEngineer` commissions a **StorageTank** with a globally unique `tank_code` of between 1 and 20 characters, a `tank_class` of either `STANDARD` or `PREMIUM`, a `capacity_slots` count of at least 1, and optionally a scheduled maintenance window given as `maintenance_window_start` and `maintenance_window_end`. The window is inclusive at its start and exclusive at its end, both endpoints may be null when no maintenance is scheduled, and when both are present the end must be strictly later than the start. A `tank_code` that duplicates a tank already commissioned, and an end that is not strictly later than its start, are both refused at the request-shape rung with `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED` (422) — the uniqueness test is a shape test here, not a state one, and it runs before any row is written. Supplying exactly one of the two endpoints is accepted and simply leaves the tank with no effective window: every window comparison in the system — the cryopreservation conflict check on `freeze_at` and the nightly maintenance sweep alike — requires both endpoints to be present and is skipped entirely otherwise. A new tank begins in `status` of `ONLINE`, with `occupied_slots` at 0 and `cumulative_excursion_minutes_30d` at 0; `last_temperature_c` and `last_reading_at` are null until the first reading arrives. `PREMIUM` class is not merely cosmetic: it attracts a surcharge on every storage invoice line, as §3.8 describes. A tank's status ranges over `ONLINE`, `ALARM`, `MAINTENANCE` and the terminal `OFFLINE`, and only an `ONLINE` tank accepts new cryopreservation.

**Cryopreservation** is the technologist action that puts a characterized unit into physical storage. It names the destination `tank_id`, the physical `slot_code` inside that tank, and the `freeze_at` instant that will be recorded as the unit's `cryopreserved_at`. It drives the unit `VOLUME_REDUCED → CRYOPRESERVED`, requires the unit's `availability_status` to read `UNAVAILABLE` at that moment — any other value on either axis being `ERR_INVALID_UNIT_STATE`, with the processing axis examined before the availability one — and is guarded by five further conditions beyond consent currency: the unit must have exactly one `POST_PROCESSING` quality test (absence is `ERR_NO_QUALITY_TEST`) whose `sterility_result` is `NEGATIVE` (otherwise `ERR_STERILITY_POSITIVE`); the destination tank's `status` must be `ONLINE`, so that `ALARM`, `MAINTENANCE` and `OFFLINE` tanks each yield `ERR_TANK_NOT_OPERATIONAL`; the tank's `occupied_slots` must be strictly below its `capacity_slots`, and a tank already at capacity yields `ERR_TANK_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`; the tank's `cumulative_excursion_minutes_30d` must not exceed 120 minutes, with the comparison strictly greater so that a tank sitting at exactly 120 minutes still admits units and one at 121 yields `ERR_EXCURSION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`; and `freeze_at` must not fall inside the tank's maintenance window, where a `freeze_at` exactly equal to `maintenance_window_start` is inside the window and refused with `ERR_MAINTENANCE_WINDOW_CONFLICT` while a `freeze_at` exactly equal to `maintenance_window_end` is outside it and accepted.

A successful cryopreservation persists `tank_id`, `slot_code` and `cryopreserved_at`, then sets the unit's availability by custody model: a `PRIVATE_FAMILY` unit moves `UNAVAILABLE → PRIVATE_STORED` and is immediately part of billable inventory, while a `PUBLIC_DONATION` unit stays `UNAVAILABLE` and becomes `LISTED` only when a registry operator publishes it. Because the unit now occupies a slot, the tank's `occupied_slots` is recomputed as the count of its units that are neither `RELEASED` nor `DISPOSED`. The same request also re-reconciles that tank's `cumulative_excursion_minutes_30d`; under the rule of §2.4 each of those two reconciliations rewrites the tank only if the figure it recomputes differs from the one already stored, so a cryopreservation into a tank with no resolved excursions in the window bumps the tank's `version` once, for the occupancy alone.

Temperature surveillance runs through readings that an engineer posts against a tank, each carrying a `reading_c` and the `reading_at` instant it was taken. Every reading refreshes `last_temperature_c` and `last_reading_at` from the supplied values, and no state rung guards the posting: a reading is accepted against a tank in any status, `OFFLINE` included. A reading is not itself a persisted entity — nothing is stored for it beyond those two tank fields and whatever excursion it opens — so the operation creates no addressable row, answers HTTP 200 rather than 201, and returns the updated **tank** as its `data` rather than any excursion it may have opened. A reading strictly warmer than −150.0 degrees Celsius is an alarm condition: the tank moves `ONLINE → ALARM` (or `MAINTENANCE → ALARM`, if the tank happened to be in its maintenance window; only those two statuses move, so a tank sitting in any other status — terminally `OFFLINE` after a total loss, for one — keeps the status it holds while the excursion is opened and the cascade below still fires in full), and a **TemperatureExcursion** is opened in status `OPEN` with `detected_at` set to the reading's `reading_at` and `peak_temperature_c` set to the reading's `reading_c`, its `acknowledged_at`, `acknowledged_by_user_id`, `resolved_at`, `duration_minutes` and `disposition` all null. A reading of exactly −150.0 does not open an excursion, and neither does a reading below it. Only one excursion may be live per tank, so an alarm reading is ignored for excursion-opening purposes while that tank already has an excursion in `OPEN`, `ACKNOWLEDGED` or `ESCALATED`. While an excursion remains unresolved, `peak_temperature_c` tracks the warmest reading observed.

Opening an excursion fires the deepest cascade in the system, four links long: reading, then tank, then unit, then listing, then the holds on that listing. Every unit in the alarmed tank whose `availability_status` is `PRIVATE_STORED` or `LISTED` moves to `QUARANTINED` with a `quarantine_reason_code` of `EXCURSION_OPEN`, processed in `created_at` ascending order. For each such unit, its registry listing in `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED` with a `suspension_reason_code` of `UNIT_QUARANTINED`, again in `created_at` ascending order. And for each listing thereby suspended, every hold on that unit in `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` is cancelled with a `cancellation_reason_code` of `LISTING_SUSPENDED`, once more in `created_at` ascending order. That third link hangs off the second rather than off the unit, so a quarantined unit that carries no `ACTIVE` listing — the ordinary `PRIVATE_STORED` case — keeps its pending holds standing untouched. Quarantined units keep occupying their slots throughout, so the tank's `occupied_slots` does not change.

Alarm response has its own lifecycle. An engineer acknowledges an excursion — optionally with an `acknowledgement_note` — which moves it `OPEN → ACKNOWLEDGED` or `ESCALATED → ACKNOWLEDGED`, stamping `acknowledged_at` from the logical clock and `acknowledged_by_user_id` from the acting engineer's `X-User-ID`. Acknowledgement writes nothing outside the excursion row: the tank stays in `ALARM` and its units stay `QUARANTINED`, because only resolution moves either. An excursion left unacknowledged for more than 30 minutes past its `detected_at` is escalated `OPEN → ESCALATED` by the nightly batch; escalation changes nothing but the row's audit fields, and an escalated excursion may still be acknowledged afterwards. Acknowledging an excursion that is neither `OPEN` nor `ESCALATED`, or resolving one that is neither `ACKNOWLEDGED` nor `ESCALATED`, is `ERR_INVALID_EXCURSION_STATE`.

Resolution is the joint authority of a `MedicalDirector` and a `FacilitiesEngineer`, and carries the engineering judgement in `disposition` together with the final `peak_temperature_c`. Both fields are required, and the supplied `peak_temperature_c` overwrites whatever peak the readings had tracked — even when the figure supplied is cooler than the warmest reading observed, the resolver's number being taken as the record of the event. It stamps `resolved_at` from the logical clock and computes `duration_minutes` as the whole minutes from `detected_at` to `resolved_at`, discarding any partial minute; a `resolved_at` that is not later than `detected_at` — which the logical clock makes reachable — yields exactly 0 rather than a negative figure. The `disposition` then forks the outcome sharply, and both branches are equally specified. A `disposition` of `NO_IMPACT` or `REQUALIFY` moves the excursion to `RESOLVED`, returns the tank `ALARM → ONLINE`, and compensates the quarantine cascade: each quarantined unit of that tank returns to `PRIVATE_STORED` if it is `PRIVATE_FAMILY`, or `LISTED` if it is `PUBLIC_DONATION` and it has a listing suspended for `UNIT_QUARANTINED`, or `UNAVAILABLE` if it is `PUBLIC_DONATION` and carries no such listing — the state such a unit sat in before any registry operator published it — with `quarantine_reason_code` cleared to null in all three cases and units walked in `created_at` ascending order; each such listing returns from `SUSPENDED` to `ACTIVE` with `suspension_reason_code` cleared to null. The compensation stops there: the holds that quarantine cancelled are **not** revived. A `disposition` of `TOTAL_LOSS` moves the excursion to `CLOSED_LOSS`, takes the tank `ALARM → OFFLINE`, leaves every quarantined unit `QUARANTINED`, and moves every `SUSPENDED` listing of those units to `WITHDRAWN`. Either way, the tank's `cumulative_excursion_minutes_30d` is recomputed once resolution is recorded, and either way a tank that is not in `ALARM` when its excursion is resolved keeps whatever status it already holds — the `→ ONLINE` and `→ OFFLINE` moves fire only out of `ALARM`, while the unit-level and listing-level halves of each branch run regardless.

That rolling figure is the sum of `duration_minutes` over the tank's excursions whose `status` is `RESOLVED` or `CLOSED_LOSS` and whose `detected_at` falls inside the window running from 30 days before the logical clock to the logical clock, with both endpoints included. Because a tank may hold at most one live excursion at a time, those intervals cannot overlap and the merge is a plain sum; rows are visited in `detected_at` ascending order with `created_at` ascending as the tie-break. Tanks may also be browsed by `status` or `tank_class`.

Independently of alarms, the nightly batch walks tanks into and out of maintenance. A tank in `ONLINE` moves `ONLINE → MAINTENANCE` when the logical clock is at or after `maintenance_window_start` and strictly before `maintenance_window_end`. A tank in `MAINTENANCE` moves `MAINTENANCE → ONLINE` when the logical clock is strictly before `maintenance_window_start` or at or after `maintenance_window_end`. Neither transition has any effect beyond the tank's own audit fields.

### 3.6. Publication to the Public Registry

A public-donation unit becomes findable by transplant centres only once a `RegistryOperator` publishes it. Publication creates a **RegistryListing** naming the `unit_id`, the `registry_code` of the registry it is published to (between 1 and 20 characters), the three published allele-pair strings `hla_a`, `hla_b` and `hla_drb1` (each between 1 and 20 characters), and the unit's current `version` in `expected_unit_version`. There is no HLA arithmetic anywhere in this system: the three allele strings are published verbatim and never parsed or compared server-side.

Publication carries the longest precondition set of any create operation, and every one of the following must hold. The unit's `program_type` must be `PUBLIC_DONATION`; a private family unit is refused with `ERR_PROGRAM_TYPE_MISMATCH`. Its `processing_status` must be `CRYOPRESERVED` and its `availability_status` must be `UNAVAILABLE`, a wrong value on either axis being `ERR_INVALID_UNIT_STATE`. Its `eligibility_status` must be `ELIGIBLE`, and anything else is `ERR_UNIT_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. The owning enrollment's `consent_status` must be `CONSENTED` and its `hla_typing_consent` must be true — publishing HLA data without HLA-typing consent is `ERR_CONSENT_NOT_CURRENT`. The unit's post-processing figures must clear the public listing floor: `post_processing_tnc_millions` at least 900, `post_processing_cd34_thousands` at least 1500, `post_processing_viability_pct` at least 85.0, and `quality_acceptance_score` at least 70.00, each boundary inclusive, with any shortfall reported as `ERR_QUALITY_BELOW_PUBLIC_THRESHOLD`. Note that this floor is the same triple the `PUBLIC_TIER` quality test uses, so a unit may be eligible on its own merits and still be too weak for the public registry. The unit must have no existing listing whose `listing_status` is `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`, and a second attempt while such a listing lives is `ERR_DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_LISTING`. Finally, `expected_unit_version` must equal the unit's current `version`. The narrative order above is not the adjudication order — §2.9 alone governs that — so the version rung in fact fires first of all, the two unit-state axes next, and the program-type rung only after consent, the maternal panel, sterility, eligibility and the quality floors. The consequence worth naming is that a `PRIVATE_FAMILY` unit which has already entered `PRIVATE_STORED` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_UNIT_STATE` at the availability axis rather than with `ERR_PROGRAM_TYPE_MISMATCH`, and only a private unit still sitting at `UNAVAILABLE` ever reaches the custody rung.

A successful publication stamps `listed_at` from the logical clock, sets `listing_status` to `ACTIVE` with `suspension_reason_code` null, moves the unit `UNAVAILABLE → LISTED`, increments the unit's `version` by one, and freezes a `potency_grade` on the listing row. The grade is a strict threshold ladder over the unit's post-processing counts at the moment of publication: grade `A` when `post_processing_tnc_millions` is at least 1500 **and** `post_processing_cd34_thousands` is at least 3000; otherwise grade `B` when `post_processing_tnc_millions` is at least 1200 **and** `post_processing_cd34_thousands` is at least 2000; otherwise grade `C`. Both boundary comparisons are inclusive, and both counts must clear a tier for that tier to apply, so a unit with 1600 TNC and 2500 CD34 grades `B`, not `A`. The grade is written once and is never recomputed for the life of the listing, even if the unit is later re-tested, suspended and resumed.

A listing lives in one of four states — `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, `MATCHED` and the terminal `WITHDRAWN` — and whenever it is `SUSPENDED` a `suspension_reason_code` records why, drawn from `UNIT_QUARANTINED`, `CONSENT_AMENDMENT_PENDING`, `MATERNAL_REACTIVE`, `SCREENING_EXPIRED` and `REGISTRY_HOLD`. The reason code is not decoration: it determines who may lift the suspension. `REGISTRY_HOLD` is the registry desk's own hold and only the registry desk clears it; the other four are consequences of cascades and are lifted only by their own compensating cascade.

The registry desk suspends an `ACTIVE` listing — optionally recording a `suspension_note` — when HLA re-typing or a registry audit requires the unit off the market. The listing moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason_code` set to `REGISTRY_HOLD`, the unit moves `LISTED → UNAVAILABLE` with its `version` incremented by one, and every hold on that unit in `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` is cancelled with a `cancellation_reason_code` of `LISTING_SUSPENDED`. The unit stays frozen in its slot throughout, so the tank's `occupied_slots` is untouched. This is the one situation in which a fresh publication attempt on the same unit is adjudicated by the unit-state rung rather than the duplicate-listing rung: the unit is `UNAVAILABLE` rather than `LISTED`, so the duplicate-listing check on a `SUSPENDED` listing is what refuses it.

Resuming is the exact compensation. A `SUSPENDED` listing moves `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE` with `suspension_reason_code` cleared to null, and the unit moves `UNAVAILABLE → LISTED` with its `version` incremented by one. The `potency_grade` is deliberately not recomputed. Resuming is admissible only when `suspension_reason_code` is exactly `REGISTRY_HOLD` and the unit's `eligibility_status` is still `ELIGIBLE`; a listing suspended for `UNIT_QUARANTINED`, `CONSENT_AMENDMENT_PENDING`, `MATERNAL_REACTIVE` or `SCREENING_EXPIRED`, and a listing that is not `SUSPENDED` at all, are both refused with `ERR_INVALID_LISTING_STATE`. A unit that is no longer `ELIGIBLE` is instead refused with `ERR_UNIT_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, evaluated after that listing-state pair, so a `REGISTRY_HOLD` listing over an ineligible unit reports the eligibility error while a quarantine-suspended listing over the same unit reports the listing error. Resume declares no body fields of its own, so an absent, empty or populated body is equally acceptable. The cancelled holds are never revived by a resume.

The remaining listing transitions are all consequences of events described elsewhere and are listed here for completeness. A listing moves `ACTIVE → MATCHED` when a hold on its unit is confirmed, and back `MATCHED → ACTIVE` when that confirmed hold is cancelled. It moves `MATCHED → WITHDRAWN` when the confirmed hold is released for transplant, and `ACTIVE → WITHDRAWN` or `SUSPENDED → WITHDRAWN` when its unit is disposed, when the enrollment is withdrawn, or when a temperature excursion is resolved as a total loss. It moves `MATCHED → SUSPENDED` with a `suspension_reason_code` of `MATERNAL_REACTIVE` when the governing maternal panel is amended to reactive. Cascaded suspensions with reason `UNIT_QUARANTINED` or `CONSENT_AMENDMENT_PENDING` are the only cascaded suspensions that can return to `ACTIVE`, and they do so through excursion resolution and re-consent respectively. A unit's whole listing history, including `WITHDRAWN` rows, remains readable in `created_at` ascending order.

### 3.7. Search, Holds, Confirmation and Release for Transplant

A transplant centre finds inventory through a ranked search that takes a `program_scope` of either `PUBLIC` or `PRIVATE_DIRECTED`, the recipient's `patient_weight_kg`, and optionally a `required_hla_match_count` and a `min_dose_x1e7_per_kg` floor. The candidate set is defined by explicit predicates: `eligibility_status` equal to `ELIGIBLE`; `processing_status` equal to `CRYOPRESERVED`; the holding tank's `status` equal to `ONLINE`. A `program_scope` of `PUBLIC` additionally requires `availability_status` equal to `LISTED` and the existence of a registry listing in `ACTIVE`; a `program_scope` of `PRIVATE_DIRECTED` instead requires `availability_status` equal to `PRIVATE_STORED`. When `min_dose_x1e7_per_kg` is supplied, a unit qualifies only when its pre-freeze dose for the supplied `patient_weight_kg` is at least that floor. The `required_hla_match_count` parameter is a request-side declaration only — no unit field corresponds to it and no HLA matching is performed — so it filters nothing server-side; it is nonetheless shape-checked like every other query parameter, and must be a number inside the closed range 0 to 6, a value outside that range being `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED` (422) even though the parameter never narrows the candidate set. `program_scope` and `patient_weight_kg` are both required, a missing one being `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED`, and `patient_weight_kg` must be strictly positive while `min_dose_x1e7_per_kg`, when supplied, may not be negative.

Qualifying units are ranked by that pre-freeze dose in descending order, with `post_processing_cd34_thousands` descending as the first tie-break, `created_at` ascending as the second and creation sequence ascending as the third and final one; `id` is never consulted as a tie-break. Pagination is applied after ranking. Each result row projects `unit_id`, `unit_registry_code`, `program_type`, `potency_grade`, `post_processing_tnc_millions`, `post_processing_cd34_thousands`, `post_processing_viability_pct`, `pre_cryo_dose_x1e7_per_kg` and the `tank_code` of the holding tank. The projected `potency_grade` is read off the unit's registry listings rather than the unit: it is the frozen grade of the unit's most recent listing in `ACTIVE`, falling back to the grade of its most recent listing of any status when none is active, and it is `null` when the unit has never been listed at all — the ordinary case for a `PRIVATE_DIRECTED` result. When nothing qualifies, the response is HTTP 200 with an empty array and a `total_count` of 0 — never an error.

**The two dose variants.** A unit's cell dose per kilogram of recipient weight is computed two ways from the same unit counts and the same `patient_weight_kg`, and the two legitimately produce different numbers. The `PRE_CRYO` variant is `post_processing_tnc_millions` divided by ten times `patient_weight_kg`, rounded half up to two decimals. It treats the pre-freeze count as fully recoverable, ignoring both thaw viability and TNC recovery, so it is always at least the `POST_THAW` value for the same inputs. The `POST_THAW` variant is computed from the confirmatory test in exactly three rounded steps, in exactly this order: first `confirmatory_recovery_pct` is the confirmatory test's `tnc_millions` divided by the unit's `post_processing_tnc_millions`, multiplied by 100, rounded half up to two decimals; then an effective count is the unit's `post_processing_tnc_millions` multiplied by the confirmatory `viability_pct` divided by 100 and by that already-rounded recovery percentage divided by 100, itself rounded half up to two decimals **before** any division; then the dose is that effective count divided by ten times `patient_weight_kg`, rounded half up to two decimals. Which variant gates which operation is pinned and never negotiable: search ranking and reservation creation use `PRE_CRYO` only; the release gate uses `POST_THAW` only. A unit can therefore clear the pre-freeze screen at reservation time and still be refused at release.

**Placing a hold.** A `TransplantCenter` places a **Reservation** on a unit, supplying `unit_id`, its own `requesting_center_name` (between 1 and 120 characters), the `patient_weight_kg` (positive, to one decimal place), the declared `patient_hla_match_count` (an integer from 0 to 6 inclusive), the hold window as `hold_from` and `hold_until` where `hold_until` must be strictly later than `hold_from`, and the unit's current `version` in `expected_unit_version`. The row is created in `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY`, `requesting_center_user_id` is set from the acting centre's `X-User-ID`, `pre_cryo_dose_x1e7_per_kg` is computed and `dose_variant_applied` is set to `PRE_CRYO`, and the unit's `version` is incremented by one. The row's `superseded_by_reservation_id`, `confirmatory_test_id`, `intended_transplant_date`, `post_thaw_dose_x1e7_per_kg`, `settlement_total_cents`, `released_at` and `cancellation_reason_code` are all null at creation.

Hold windows on one unit are exclusive, and they are exclusive with a logistics margin. Each incumbent hold on the same unit in status `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` or `CONFIRMED` occupies a closed interval from its `hold_from` to its `hold_until`, and that interval is widened by 24 hours on **both** sides before comparison. The new hold conflicts when its `hold_from` is strictly earlier than the incumbent's `hold_until` plus 24 hours **and** the incumbent's `hold_from` is strictly earlier than the new `hold_until` plus 24 hours; a conflict is `ERR_HOLD_WINDOW_CONFLICT`. Because both comparisons are strict, a gap of exactly 24 hours between two windows touches the buffered edge without crossing it and is accepted, while a gap of 23 hours 59 minutes 59 seconds is rejected. Incumbents are examined in `hold_from` ascending order with `created_at` ascending as the tie-break; the first conflict decides, though the reported error is identical whichever incumbent is found. Holds are browsable by `unit_id`, by `superseded_by_reservation_id`, by `confirmatory_test_id` and by `status`.

**The confirmation fork.** From `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` a hold takes one of three outward edges, and the two voluntary branches have deliberately asymmetric consequences that must both be implemented exactly.

Confirming is branch A. The centre supplies the `intended_transplant_date` and must act within 72 hours of the hold's `created_at`; the boundary is inclusive, so an attempt at exactly 72 hours succeeds and one past it is `ERR_CONFIRMATION_WINDOW_EXPIRED`. Confirmation further requires that the acting `X-User-ID` equal `requesting_center_user_id`, that the unit's `availability_status` be `LISTED` or `PRIVATE_STORED`, and that its `eligibility_status` be `ELIGIBLE`. A hold no longer sitting in `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` is `ERR_INVALID_RESERVATION_STATE`, an inadmissible unit availability — `QUARANTINED` or `PENDING_ABANDONMENT`, say — is `ERR_INVALID_UNIT_STATE`, and an ineligible unit is `ERR_UNIT_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; the three are adjudicated in exactly that order, and all three ahead of the 72-hour window, so a stale hold on a quarantined unit never reports the window error. On success the hold moves `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY → CONFIRMED`, `intended_transplant_date` is persisted, the unit moves `LISTED → RESERVED` or `PRIVATE_STORED → RESERVED`, the unit's registry listing in `ACTIVE` moves to `MATCHED`, and every **sibling** hold on the same unit still in `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` is moved to `DECLINED` with a `cancellation_reason_code` of `SUPERSEDED_BY_CONFIRMED_HOLD` and with `superseded_by_reservation_id` set to the confirming hold's `id`.

Declining is branch B, and it is the mirror image in scope: the centre supplies a `reason_code` of between 1 and 60 characters, the hold moves `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY → DECLINED`, `cancellation_reason_code` is set to that `reason_code`, and **nothing else in the system changes**. The unit's `availability_status` is not touched, its registry listing is not touched, no sibling hold is affected, and the tank's `occupied_slots` is unchanged. Declining requires only that the acting `X-User-ID` equal `requesting_center_user_id` and that the hold still be `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY`; anything else is `ERR_INVALID_RESERVATION_STATE`.

The third outward edge is involuntary: the nightly batch moves a hold `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY → EXPIRED` once the logical clock passes 72 hours after its `created_at`, recording a `cancellation_reason_code` of `CONFIRM_WINDOW_LAPSED`. A hold may also be cancelled out of `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` by cascade, with a `cancellation_reason_code` of `LISTING_SUSPENDED`, `MATERNAL_REACTIVE` or `UNIT_DISPOSED`, or declined by the delinquency cascade with `ACCOUNT_DELINQUENT`.

**Cancelling a confirmed hold** is the compensating edge for branch A. The centre supplies a `reason_code`, again of between 1 and 60 characters; the hold moves `CONFIRMED → CANCELLED`, the unit returns from `RESERVED` to `LISTED` if its `program_type` is `PUBLIC_DONATION` or to `PRIVATE_STORED` if it is `PRIVATE_FAMILY`, and the unit's `MATCHED` listing returns to `ACTIVE`. The tank's `occupied_slots` is provably identical before and after, since the unit never left its slot. Cancellation likewise requires ownership and a current status of `CONFIRMED`. A confirmed hold is also cancelled by cascade with a `cancellation_reason_code` of `UNIT_DISPOSED` when its unit is disposed.

**Release for transplant** is the irreversible act, performed by a `MedicalDirector` with an optional `release_note`, and it is gated on eight conditions, all of which must hold. The hold's `status` must be `CONFIRMED`. Its `confirmatory_test_id` must be non-null and that confirmatory test's `outcome` must be `PASS`; absence is `ERR_CONFIRMATORY_TEST_REQUIRED`. The hold's `post_thaw_dose_x1e7_per_kg` must be at least 2.50, inclusive at the boundary, and a shortfall is `ERR_DOSE_BELOW_MINIMUM`. The unit's `eligibility_status` must be `ELIGIBLE`. The enrollment's governing panel — the latest `COMPLETED` maternal screening — must have an `overall_result` of exactly `NON_REACTIVE`, so that both `REACTIVE` and `INDETERMINATE` block the release — but they block it under different codes: a `REACTIVE` panel is refused with `ERR_MATERNAL_SCREENING_REACTIVE`, while an `INDETERMINATE` reading, or any other value that is not `NON_REACTIVE`, is refused with `ERR_MATERNAL_SCREENING_INCOMPLETE`, the same code an absent or expired panel earns one rung earlier. The holding tank's `status` must be `ONLINE`, and an `ALARM`, `MAINTENANCE` or `OFFLINE` tank is `ERR_TANK_NOT_OPERATIONAL`. The releasing director's `X-User-ID` must differ from the unit's `eligibility_determined_by_user_id`, and a match is `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. And where the unit has a `storage_account_id`, that account's `status` must not be `DELINQUENT`, `ABANDONMENT_NOTICE` or `ABANDONED`, and its `balance_cents` must be exactly 0; because an account only reaches those three statuses while it carries a positive residual, a release refused on either ground reports `ERR_OUTSTANDING_BALANCE`, which is the fiscal rung scoped to release. The delinquency rung `ERR_ACCOUNT_DELINQUENT` is what refuses hold placement and registry publication, as §2.9 sets out.

A successful release moves the hold `CONFIRMED → RELEASED`, stamps `released_at` from the logical clock, sets `dose_variant_applied` to `POST_THAW`, and computes and persists `settlement_total_cents` on the hold while mirroring the same figure to the account's `last_settlement_total_cents`. Where the unit carries no `storage_account_id` — the ordinary public-donation case — the waterfall does not run at all and the hold's `settlement_total_cents` stays null. The unit moves `RESERVED → RELEASED` with `disposition_reason` set to `TRANSPLANT_RELEASED` and `disposed_at` stamped from the logical clock; its `processing_status` is deliberately left unchanged, because the unit ships still frozen. The unit's `MATCHED` listing moves to `WITHDRAWN`, the tank's `occupied_slots` is recomputed downward because the unit has left inventory, and if this was the enrollment's last live unit the enrollment moves to `CLOSED`, which in turn closes its storage account.

Because the release gate is long and its failure is expensive, a read-only pre-flight summary of a hold is available and evaluating it mutates nothing. It reports `reservation_id`, `unit_id`, both `pre_cryo_dose_x1e7_per_kg` and `post_thaw_dose_x1e7_per_kg`, the `confirmatory_recovery_pct` behind the post-thaw figure, a boolean `dose_gate_passed`, a boolean `confirmatory_test_present`, the governing `maternal_result`, the holding `tank_status`, the `account_balance_cents`, the `projected_settlement_total_cents` the release would post, and a `blocking_error_id` naming the first gate that would refuse the release under the §2.9 order — null when no gate would refuse it. Its null conventions are fixed rather than incidental: `confirmatory_recovery_pct` is null whenever the hold names no confirmatory test or the unit carries no `post_processing_tnc_millions` to divide by, and is otherwise recomputed from those two counts; `dose_gate_passed` is false whenever no post-thaw dose has been computed yet; `maternal_result` and `tank_status` are null when the enrollment has no governing completed panel and when the unit holds no `tank_id` respectively; and where the unit has no storage account at all, `account_balance_cents` reads 0 while `projected_settlement_total_cents` reads null, because the waterfall does not run.

### 3.8. Private Storage Billing and the Delinquency Ladder

Only a private family enrollment is billed. A `BillingAdministrator` opens a **StorageAccount** against an `enrollment_id`, recording a `billing_contact_email` that must contain the character `@`, a `plan_term_years` that must be exactly 1, 5 or 18, and a `prepaid_through_date` stating how far storage is already paid for. The enrollment must have `program_type` equal to `PRIVATE_FAMILY`, and a public-donation enrollment is refused with `ERR_PROGRAM_TYPE_MISMATCH`; the enrollment must not already own an account, and a second attempt is `ERR_DUPLICATE_STORAGE_ACCOUNT`. A new account begins with `status` of `ACTIVE`, `balance_cents` of 0, `lifetime_paid_cents` of 0, `paid_through_date` seeded from `prepaid_through_date`, and `last_settlement_total_cents` and `delinquency_stage_entered_at` both null. Opening an account is guarded by nothing else: neither the enrollment's `lifecycle_status` nor its `consent_status` is consulted, so an account may be opened against an enrollment that is still `CONSENT_PENDING` and against one already `WITHDRAWN`. The `plan_term_years` value is what later selects the settlement variant: a term of 1 year selects anniversary accrual, and a term of 5 or 18 years selects prorated termination.

An account's `status` walks a five-rung ladder plus a terminal close: `ACTIVE`, `PAST_DUE`, `DELINQUENT`, `ABANDONMENT_NOTICE`, the terminal `ABANDONED`, and the terminal `CLOSED`. Every advance along it stamps `delinquency_stage_entered_at` from the logical clock, and every cure clears that field back to null. Two further money fields track history: `lifetime_paid_cents` is the monotonic sum of every payment ever accepted on the account and is never reduced by a void or a write-off, while `last_settlement_total_cents` holds the settlement figure of the most recent unit that left inventory on this account and is null until the first such settlement.

The account's `balance_cents` is not a running total but a reconciliation, recomputed after every invoice mutation — issue, payment, delinquency advance, void, write-off — and at every settlement. It is the sum, over the account's invoices whose `status` is `ISSUED`, `PAST_DUE` or `DELINQUENT`, of `amount_due_cents` minus `amount_paid_cents`. Invoices in `PAID`, `VOID` or `WRITTEN_OFF` are excluded entirely, no billing-period window applies, and an empty outstanding set yields exactly 0. Because the reconciliation is a sum it is order-insensitive and needs no tie-break.

**Issuing an invoice.** A **StorageInvoice** names its `account_id`, the inclusive `billing_period_start`, the exclusive `billing_period_end` which must be strictly later than the start, and the billable `prorated_days` count which must be between 1 and 365 inclusive. The invoice opens in `status` of `ISSUED` with `due_date` set to `billing_period_start` plus 30 days, `amount_paid_cents` at 0 and `stage_advanced_at` null until a nightly advance stamps it. No account-status rung guards issuance: an invoice may be issued against an account standing at any rung of the delinquency ladder, a `CLOSED` or `ABANDONED` one included, provided only that the owning enrollment resolves and is `PRIVATE_FAMILY`. Its money is computed line by line, and the rounding order is pinned: **each line is rounded to whole cents with round-half-up before the lines are summed, and the sum is never re-rounded.**

- `base_line_cents` is 18000 cents multiplied by `prorated_days` divided by 365, rounded half up to whole cents.
- `surcharge_line_cents` is `base_line_cents` — the **already-rounded** base line — multiplied by 0.075 and rounded half up, but only when the single unit billed to this account sits in a tank whose `tank_class` is `PREMIUM`; where more than one unit is billed to the account its units are walked in `created_at` ascending order and one `PREMIUM` tank among them is enough to attract the surcharge. It is 0 otherwise, and a `STANDARD` class is assumed when no unit has been cryopreserved yet — a unit whose `tank_id` is still null contributes nothing to the test.
- `admin_line_cents` is 18000 cents multiplied by 0.0125, rounded half up.
- `late_fee_line_cents` is 0 at issue and grows only by nightly delinquency advances.
- `amount_due_cents` is the plain sum of those four lines.

Rounding each line before the sum, rather than summing exact values and rounding once, changes the answer: at a `prorated_days` of 100 on a `PREMIUM` tank the two orders differ by one cent, and this specification mandates round-then-sum.

**Paying an invoice.** A payment carries an `amount_cents` — a strictly positive **integer** number of cents, so that `0`, a negative figure and a non-integer such as `12.5` are each `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED` — and a `payment_reference` of at least one character, which the server validates and then discards, the invoice carrying no field for it. The amount must not exceed the invoice's residual — `amount_due_cents` minus `amount_paid_cents` — and an overpayment is refused with `ERR_PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_BALANCE`, which means partial payments are permitted but the invoice can never be paid beyond its face value. An accepted payment adds `amount_cents` to the invoice's `amount_paid_cents` and to the account's `lifetime_paid_cents`, then triggers reconciliation of `balance_cents`. When the payment brings `amount_paid_cents` exactly up to `amount_due_cents`, the invoice moves to the terminal `PAID` from whichever of `ISSUED`, `PAST_DUE` or `DELINQUENT` it was in, and the account's `paid_through_date` is advanced to the later of its current value and the invoice's `billing_period_end` — never backwards. Paying an invoice that is already `PAID`, `WRITTEN_OFF` or `VOID` is `ERR_INVALID_INVOICE_STATE`, evaluated before the residual test so that a second full payment reports the state error rather than `ERR_PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_BALANCE`. The response body is the updated invoice, not the account, even though the account is mutated in the same request.

Payment is also the cure for the whole ladder. Whenever a payment drives the account's `balance_cents` to 0, an account in `PAST_DUE`, `DELINQUENT` or `ABANDONMENT_NOTICE` returns to `ACTIVE` with `delinquency_stage_entered_at` cleared to null; from `DELINQUENT` and from `ABANDONMENT_NOTICE` the cure additionally returns every unit of that account whose `availability_status` is `PENDING_ABANDONMENT` to `PRIVATE_STORED`. The cure from `PAST_DUE` has no unit-level effect, because no unit was flagged at that rung. An `ABANDONED` account is terminal and cannot be cured.

**The nightly ladder.** Delinquency advances only inside the nightly batch, and the order of work is pinned so the outcome is reproducible. First the batch walks the invoices whose `status` is `ISSUED` or `PAST_DUE` and which still carry a positive residual, ordered by `due_date` ascending with `created_at` ascending as the tie-break — never by `id`. For each such invoice it counts the whole calendar days from the date of `due_date` to the date of the logical clock, which is 0 while the clock is at or before the due date. An `ISSUED` invoice advances to `PAST_DUE` once that count reaches 30; a `PAST_DUE` invoice advances to `DELINQUENT` once it reaches 90. Each boundary is inclusive, and **at most one stage advance per invoice per run**, so an invoice 95 days past due that is still `ISSUED` reaches `PAST_DUE` tonight and `DELINQUENT` only on the next run. Every advance adds a late fee to `late_fee_line_cents` equal to the invoice's residual at that moment multiplied by 0.015 and rounded half up, recomputes `amount_due_cents` from the four lines, and stamps `stage_advanced_at` from the logical clock. Advancing requires that `amount_paid_cents` still be strictly below `amount_due_cents`.

The batch then walks the accounts in `created_at` ascending order, advancing each by at most one rung per run. An `ACTIVE` account moves to `PAST_DUE` when one of its invoices reached `PAST_DUE` in this run. A `PAST_DUE` account moves to `DELINQUENT` when one of its invoices reached `DELINQUENT` in this run, and that advance carries the second link of the delinquency cascade: every unit of the account whose `availability_status` is `PRIVATE_STORED` moves to `PENDING_ABANDONMENT`, and every hold on such a unit in `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` is declined with a `cancellation_reason_code` of `ACCOUNT_DELINQUENT`. A `DELINQUENT` account moves to `ABANDONMENT_NOTICE` when the days-past-due of its oldest `DELINQUENT` invoice reaches 180, and an `ABANDONMENT_NOTICE` account moves to the terminal `ABANDONED` when that same figure reaches 365. Each of those advances stamps `delinquency_stage_entered_at`, and `balance_cents` is reconciled after each.

Two other paths take an invoice out of the outstanding set without payment. Disposal of a unit for non-payment moves every remaining invoice of the account in `ISSUED`, `PAST_DUE` or `DELINQUENT` to the terminal `WRITTEN_OFF` — the whole outstanding set that `balance_cents` reconciles over, not the `DELINQUENT` rows alone — and is admissible only while the account is in `ABANDONMENT_NOTICE` or `ABANDONED`. Withdrawal of the enrollment moves every invoice in `ISSUED`, `PAST_DUE` or `DELINQUENT` to the terminal `VOID`. Both recompute `balance_cents`, and neither reduces `lifetime_paid_cents`. An account moves to the terminal `CLOSED` — from `ACTIVE` or from `ABANDONED` — as a cascade once every unit billed to it has reached `RELEASED` or `DISPOSED`.

### 3.9. Disposal, Settlement and the Nightly Batch

A `MedicalDirector` disposes of a unit with a `reason` and an optional `note`. The `reason` becomes the unit's `disposition_reason` and is one of `ABANDONED_NONPAYMENT` for a unit lost to non-payment, `QUALITY_FAILURE` for a unit that cannot meet its tier, `MATERNAL_INELIGIBLE` for a unit condemned by the maternal panel, `CONSENT_REVOKED` for a unit destroyed on withdrawal of consent, `EXCURSION_LOSS` for a unit lost to a temperature event, and `TRANSPLANT_RELEASED`, which is reserved for the release path and is set by the system rather than supplied here. Disposal is admissible from any `availability_status` except `RELEASED`, `DISPOSED` and `RESERVED`, so a unit under a confirmed hold must have that hold cancelled first; a `reason` of `ABANDONED_NONPAYMENT` additionally requires the account to be in `ABANDONMENT_NOTICE` or `ABANDONED`. That extra guard is reported as `ERR_INVALID_UNIT_STATE`, the same code the availability rung uses and not a fiscal code, it is evaluated only after that availability rung, and it likewise refuses a unit that carries no storage account at all — a public-donation unit can never be disposed for non-payment.

Disposal is terminal on every axis of the unit and fires the widest cascade in the system. The unit's `processing_status` is set to `DISCARDED`, its `availability_status` to `DISPOSED`, its `disposition_reason` to the supplied `reason` and its `disposed_at` from the logical clock; any `quarantine_reason_code` it still carried is cleared to null in the same write, since a disposed unit is no longer under quarantine, while its `eligibility_status` and `eligibility_reason_code` are left exactly as they stood. The tank's `occupied_slots` is recomputed, releasing the slot. Every listing of the unit whose `listing_status` is `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` or `MATCHED` moves to `WITHDRAWN`. Every hold on the unit in `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` or `CONFIRMED` moves to `CANCELLED` with a `cancellation_reason_code` of `UNIT_DISPOSED`. Where the unit has a `storage_account_id`, the settlement described next is computed and posted to `last_settlement_total_cents` and `balance_cents` is reconciled; when the `reason` was `ABANDONED_NONPAYMENT` the account also moves to `CLOSED` and its remaining `ISSUED`, `PAST_DUE` and `DELINQUENT` invoices move to `WRITTEN_OFF`. Finally, if this was the enrollment's last live unit, the enrollment moves to `CLOSED`.

**The settlement waterfall** runs on release and on the director's disposal described above, and only when the unit has a `storage_account_id`. The consent-revocation disposal that withdrawal performs in §3.1 is deliberately outside it: that path voids the account's outstanding invoices instead and posts no settlement figure at all, so `last_settlement_total_cents` is left exactly as it was. It is a five-step evaluation whose order is pinned, which is what makes the disposition fee — a tier over the settlement — well defined rather than circular.

1. The outstanding **balance** is the account's reconciled `balance_cents` over its `ISSUED`, `PAST_DUE` and `DELINQUENT` invoices, computed before any fee is applied.
2. The **refund** is the storage fee assessment. First, unused days are the whole calendar days in the half-open interval from the date of the logical clock up to but excluding the date of `paid_through_date`, and are 0 when `paid_through_date` is at or before the logical clock. Then the variant is selected by the account's `plan_term_years`: anniversary accrual (`ANNIVERSARY_ACCRUAL`), when `plan_term_years` is 1, refunds nothing at all, however early in the period the unit leaves inventory; prorated termination (`PRORATED_TERMINATION`), when `plan_term_years` is 5 or 18, refunds 18000 cents multiplied by those unused days divided by 365, rounded half up to whole cents. No invoice row participates in this step — only `paid_through_date` and `plan_term_years` do.
3. The **pre-fee subtotal** is the balance minus the refund, and it may be zero or negative when the refund exceeds what is owed.
4. The **disposition fee** is a tier keyed on that pre-fee subtotal and never on the final total: a subtotal at or below 0 attracts 2500 cents, a subtotal from 1 to 9999 cents inclusive attracts 5000 cents, and a subtotal of 10000 cents or more attracts 7500 cents.
5. The **settlement total** is the pre-fee subtotal plus that disposition fee.

The result is written to the account's `last_settlement_total_cents`, and when the trigger was a release it is also mirrored to the hold's `settlement_total_cents`. All arithmetic is in whole cents, and the tier boundaries are inclusive exactly as written.

**The nightly batch** is the single scheduled operation and is available to the platform operator alone. It reads the logical clock and performs seven families of work: expiring kits, expiring maternal panels, expiring unconfirmed holds, escalating unacknowledged excursions, moving tanks into and out of their maintenance windows, advancing the invoice and account delinquency ladder with its late fees, and flagging the units of newly delinquent accounts. The families execute in exactly that order — kit expiry, then panel expiry, then hold lapse, then excursion escalation, then the maintenance sweep, then the invoice ladder, and last the account ladder, which carries the unit flagging inside it rather than as a pass of its own — and the sequence is load-bearing rather than cosmetic, because the account ladder consumes the very set of invoices the invoice ladder advanced earlier in the same run. Concretely: a kit in `DISPATCHED` or `HELD` whose `expires_at` has passed moves to `EXPIRED`; a maternal panel in `COMPLETED` whose `valid_until` has passed moves to `EXPIRED`, taking its `ELIGIBLE` units to `INDETERMINATE` with reason `SCREENING_EXPIRED` and their `ACTIVE` listings to `SUSPENDED` with the same reason; a hold in `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` past its 72-hour window moves to `EXPIRED` with reason `CONFIRM_WINDOW_LAPSED`; an excursion in `OPEN` unacknowledged for more than 30 minutes moves to `ESCALATED`; tanks enter and leave `MAINTENANCE` by comparing the clock to their window; invoices and accounts advance as §3.8 sets out; and `PRIVATE_STORED` units of a newly `DELINQUENT` account move to `PENDING_ABANDONMENT` with their `PENDING_CONFIRMATORY` holds declined for `ACCOUNT_DELINQUENT`.

Every row advances at most one stage per run, and every family walks its rows in the pinned order of §2.6 so that two runs over the same data produce the same result; the families §2.6 does not name — kits, panels, holds, excursions and tanks — are each walked by `created_at` ascending with creation sequence as the tie-break. Every expiry comparison the batch makes is strict: a kit whose `expires_at`, a panel whose `valid_until`, or a hold whose 72-hour deadline falls exactly on the logical clock survives the run untouched, and only a clock strictly later than the stamp expires it. The run reports what it did as ten integer counters: `kits_expired`, `screenings_expired`, `reservations_expired`, `excursions_escalated`, `tanks_entered_maintenance`, `tanks_exited_maintenance`, `invoices_advanced_past_due`, `invoices_advanced_delinquent`, `accounts_advanced` and `units_flagged_pending_abandonment`. A run over a system with nothing due returns all ten counters at 0, and running the batch twice with no clock movement in between leaves the second run's counters at 0 as well.

The logical clock itself is set through a dedicated test-driving endpoint that takes a `current_utc` value and returns it. The endpoint is the one route outside the `/cbb` prefix and belongs to the platform operator alone; `current_utc` is required and must be a string holding an RFC 3339 UTC instant, so a missing field, a non-string such as a number, and an unparseable string are each `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED`. The clock retains the parsed, normalized UTC instant, but the 200 response echoes the client's own string back **verbatim** — an offset form such as `2026-09-01T05:00:00+05:00` or a zero-fraction form such as `2026-09-01T00:00:00Z` is returned exactly as written rather than re-serialized into the three-fractional-digit form of §2.7, so this one field is the sole place a timestamp escapes without normalization. Setting the clock mutates no entity and fires no cascade; nothing happens until the next business call or nightly run reads the new value.

## 4. Endpoint Definitions

Every endpoint below is authenticated by the `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` headers described in §2.1 and returns the envelope of §2.2. An operation that brings a new resource row into existence answers with HTTP 201 — this includes receiving a collection, which creates the cord blood unit and answers 201 even though the addressed resource is the collection. Every other successful operation, including all reads, all state transitions, all action endpoints and the nightly batch, answers with HTTP 200. List endpoints accept `limit` and `offset` as described in §2.3. The free-text explanatory fields the write endpoints accept — the `note` on a collection rejection and on a unit disposal, the `decision_note` on an eligibility determination, the `suspension_note` on a listing suspension, the `acknowledgement_note` on an excursion acknowledgement and the `release_note` on a release for transplant — are each optional, each validated for shape and then **discarded**: no entity carries a field for any of them and none is ever echoed in a response. Each accepts the empty string as well as any non-empty text, each is bounded at 500 characters with the single exception of the collection-rejection `note`, which carries no upper bound, and supplying a non-string is `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED` in every case.

Two routing fallbacks sit outside the §2.9 ladder because they are decided by path matching before authentication runs, and they therefore answer even a request that carries no headers at all. A request whose path matches no rule declared in the table below is `ERR_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404) with no entity-specific code, since no entity was addressed; a request to a declared path using a method that path does not declare is `ERR_METHOD_NOT_ALLOWED` (HTTP 405). A trailing slash is insignificant in path matching, so `/cbb/enrollments/` and `/cbb/enrollments` address the same rule. Both fallbacks return the standard error envelope of §2.2. Beyond them, no endpoint in this system ever answers a 5xx status: any failure that does not map to a catalogued condition is reported as `ERR_VALIDATION_FAILED` (HTTP 422).
