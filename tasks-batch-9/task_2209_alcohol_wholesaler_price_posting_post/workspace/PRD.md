# Alcohol Price Posting API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

In control states and licence states alike, an alcohol wholesaler may not simply sell at whatever price it likes. Each month it must file a price schedule with the state regulator, brand by brand, container size by container size, package configuration by package configuration. Once that schedule is certified it is *posted*, and a statutory quiet period — the **post-and-hold window** — opens during which competing wholesalers may amend their own posted prices downward to match what they have just seen. Only after the window has closed and the calendar has turned do the prices actually take effect. Everything a wholesaler subsequently charges a retailer must reconcile to those posted prices, and every deviation is a penalty-bearing event.

This document specifies the Alcohol Price Posting API: the system of record for a state price posting program, the wholesalers licensed under it, the brands they are appointed to distribute, the monthly price schedules they file, the discount and combination-sale instruments they attach to posted items, the competitive amendments and clerical corrections that move posted prices after certification, the retailer invoices that must reconcile to those prices, and the compliance audits that convert off-posted-price sales into assessed monetary penalties. The system's hardest requirements are not CRUD requirements — they are ordering requirements. A filing is late or on time by a single millisecond. A hold window is open up to but not including its final instant. A penalty escalates by a tier that must be read *before* the current audit's own findings are folded into the violation history. Getting these boundaries wrong does not produce a slightly different number; it produces a legally wrong number.

Two audiences share the API. Licensee staff — filing agents and certifying officers — prepare, file, certify and invoice, and they are strictly segregated: the person who files a schedule may never be the person who certifies it. State regulators adjudicate registrations, accept or reject amendments, approve or deny corrections, drive the monthly posting run that makes prices effective, and conduct audits. Because every temporal rule in the system is testable only if time is controllable, the server never reads the real wall clock: it maintains a logical UTC clock that regulators advance explicitly.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

All endpoints are mounted at the root of the service; there is no shared API prefix.

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request — without exception, including every read — must carry two HTTP headers. `X-User-Role` names the capacity in which the caller is acting, and `X-User-ID` identifies the acting user. The system recognises exactly three role names, spelled precisely as follows: `FilingAgent`, `CertifyingOfficer`, and `ComplianceOfficer`.

If `X-User-Role` is absent, or carries a string that is not one of those three names, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). The same rejection applies when the named role is entity-backed and `X-User-ID` is absent. Authentication is resolved before anything else about the request is examined — before the URL path, before the body, before any business rule.

Two of the three roles are **entity-backed**, meaning the header identity resolves to a persisted record:

- When `X-User-Role` is `FilingAgent`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `LicenseeUser`. That record must exist, its `is_active` flag must be `true`, and its `user_role` must be either `FILING_AGENT` or `DUAL_ROLE`. Ownership checks on any resource that references a `Wholesaler` compare the resource's owning wholesaler to the `wholesaler_id` recorded on the acting `LicenseeUser`.
- When `X-User-Role` is `CertifyingOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `LicenseeUser`. That record must exist, its `is_active` flag must be `true`, and its `user_role` must be either `CERTIFYING_OFFICER` or `DUAL_ROLE`. Ownership checks work identically: the resource's owning wholesaler is compared to the acting user's `wholesaler_id`.

A `user_role` of `DUAL_ROLE` therefore satisfies both `FilingAgent` and `CertifyingOfficer` claims. A `LicenseeUser` whose `is_active` is `false` is never authorised for anything, whatever its `user_role`.

The third role is **opaque**. When `X-User-Role` is `ComplianceOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque regulator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server does not resolve it against any record and never compares it to any entity's `id`; presence of the header is not required for this role to authenticate, and no `LicenseeUser` lookup is performed. Consequently no wholesaler-ownership restriction ever narrows a `ComplianceOfficer`: the regulator sees and acts across every wholesaler in the program.

When the role authenticates but is not permitted to invoke the endpoint, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403). The same code and status are used when an entity-backed role's `X-User-ID` fails to resolve to an active `LicenseeUser` whose `user_role` matches the claimed role. When the role is permitted for the endpoint but the specific resource belongs to a different wholesaler, the rejection is the distinct `ERR_FORBIDDEN_WHOLESALER_SCOPE` (HTTP 403) — see §2.11.

Every read endpoint listed in §4 is available to all three roles. Write endpoints are partitioned: `FilingAgent` exclusively performs brand registration filing, schedule creation, schedule item creation, schedule submission, schedule withdrawal, discount tier creation, combination sale declaration, hold amendment filing, error correction filing, retailer invoice creation, invoice line creation and invoice validation. `CertifyingOfficer` exclusively performs schedule certification. `ComplianceOfficer` exclusively performs logical clock control, state program registration, wholesaler registration, licence status changes, licensee user registration, brand approval and rejection, returning a schedule for correction, hold amendment acceptance, rejection and revocation, error correction approval and denial, audit creation, collection, assessment, closure and voiding, and the posting run.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination, Ordering and Filtering of Collections

Every collection endpoint accepts two pagination query parameters. `limit` is the maximum number of records to return; when omitted it defaults to 10, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` is the number of records to skip before the page begins; when omitted it defaults to 0 and its minimum accepted value is 0. A `limit` below 1 or above 100, a negative `offset`, or a non-integer value for either, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400).

Unless a subsection states otherwise, every list endpoint returns its records sorted by `created_at` in ascending order, and the pagination window is applied after that sort. List responses carry `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` in the `meta` object, where `total_count` is the number of records matching the filters before the pagination window is applied and `has_more` is `true` exactly when the sum of `offset` and the number of records returned is less than `total_count`.

All other query parameters on list endpoints are filters. Every filter is an exact-equality match against the named stored field, an omitted filter imposes no restriction, and when several filters are supplied they are combined conjunctively — a record must satisfy all of them to appear. An enum-typed filter carrying a value that is not a declared member of that enumeration is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. The filters available on each collection are: `state_code` on state programs; `state_program_id` and `license_status` on wholesalers; `wholesaler_id` and `user_role` on licensee users; `wholesaler_id`, `registration_status` and `beverage_class` on brands; `wholesaler_id`, `state_program_id`, `posting_month`, `status`, `filed_by_user_id`, `certified_by_user_id` and `superseded_by_schedule_id` on price schedules; `schedule_id`, `brand_id`, `supersedes_item_id` and `status` on price schedule items; `item_id` and `status` on quantity discount tiers; `schedule_id` and `status` on combination sales; `amending_item_id`, `reference_item_id` and `status` on hold amendments; `item_id` and `status` on error corrections; `wholesaler_id`, `audit_id`, `validation_status` and `audit_status` on retailer invoices; `invoice_id` and `item_id` on invoice lines; `wholesaler_id`, `state_program_id`, `audit_period_month` and `status` on compliance audits; and `audit_id`, `invoice_line_id` and `finding_type` on audit findings.

### 2.4. The Logical Posting Clock

Because every substantive rule in this domain is a deadline, a window or an effective date, the server does not read the machine's wall clock at any point. Instead it maintains a **logical current UTC instant**. At start-up that instant is `2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z`. Every timestamp the server stamps — `created_at`, `updated_at`, `filed_at`, `certified_at`, an audit finding's `created_at`, a posting run's `run_at` — and every temporal comparison it performs — deadline tests, hold-window containment, correction-window containment, posted-price as-of resolution, combination-window cooldown — reads this logical clock and nothing else.

A `ComplianceOfficer` moves the clock by supplying `to_iso8601`, an ISO 8601 instant, to the clock-setting capability. The clock is set to exactly that instant; no entity in the system is mutated as a side effect, no `version` anywhere is incremented, and the response body reports the new position of the clock as `current_utc`, a string. The clock may be moved forwards or backwards. Wherever this document says "the current server time", it means the logical clock's present value.

All datetime values accepted as input and all datetime values emitted in responses are ISO 8601 UTC instants with three fractional-second digits and a trailing `Z`. A datetime input that is not a parseable ISO 8601 instant is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400).

### 2.5. Record Identity, Revision Counters and the Audit Trail

Every persisted record carries an `id`, a string holding a server-minted UUID with no type prefix and no client influence, and a `created_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC instant with three fractional digits recording when the record was first written.

Records of type `StateProgram`, `Wholesaler`, `LicenseeUser`, `Brand`, `PriceSchedule`, `PriceScheduleItem`, `QuantityDiscountTier`, `CombinationSale`, `HoldAmendment`, `ErrorCorrection`, `RetailerInvoice`, `InvoiceLine` and `ComplianceAudit` additionally carry `updated_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC instant in the same format, and `version`, an integer. A newly created record has `version` equal to 1 and `updated_at` equal to `created_at`. Thereafter the governing rule is uniform: **whenever a request changes any stored field of a record, that record's `updated_at` is refreshed to the current server time and its `version` is incremented by exactly 1** — once per request, no matter how many of its fields changed in that request. Conversely, a record whose stored fields are not changed by a request keeps its `updated_at` and its `version` untouched. Where later subsections say "and increments the wholesaler's `version`", they are pointing out a cross-entity instance of this same rule, not a second increment.

`AuditFinding` is the one exception. A finding is immutable once recorded, so it carries only `id`, `created_at` and its payload fields; it has no `updated_at` field and no `version` field at all, and neither key appears in an audit finding response body.

### 2.6. Money, Percentages and Rounding

Every monetary quantity in this system is an integer count of **cents**, never a decimal currency amount, and every field whose name ends in `_cents` is an integer in the JSON body. All arithmetic that produces a stored monetary value ends in whole cents.

The default rounding method is **round half up** — a fractional remainder of exactly one half rounds away from zero to the next whole cent. Round half up is used for a quantity discount tier's derived case price, for an invoice's handling surcharge, for a combination sale's minimum allowed price, for each audit finding's penalty component, and for the final escalated audit penalty. There is exactly one place where the rounding differs, and it is deliberate: the promotional allowance cap on an invoice line is computed with **floor** (truncation towards zero), never with round half up. Whenever a rule rounds several components and then sums them, it rounds each component first and sums the rounded values; summing raw products and rounding once at the end is a different figure and is never the rule.

Percentage-valued fields — `max_quantity_discount_pct`, `max_promotional_allowance_pct`, `off_posted_penalty_rate_pct`, `discount_pct`, `variance_pct`, `tier_multiplier` — are decimal fractions, not percentage points: a value of `0.15` means fifteen percent. They are stored and returned as JSON numbers preserving the precision supplied or computed, and comparisons against them are performed unrounded.

### 2.7. Window Arithmetic and Boundary Inclusivity

Four different windows govern this domain, and each has its own inclusivity, which must be honoured to the millisecond.

- The **posting deadline** is an instant, and it is inclusive. A filing that lands exactly on the deadline instant is on time; only a filing strictly after it is late.
- The **post-and-hold window** is the half-open interval that begins at the schedule's `certified_at`, inclusive, and ends at its `hold_window_ends_at`, exclusive. The window is open when the current server time is at or after `certified_at` and strictly before `hold_window_ends_at`. A current server time exactly equal to `hold_window_ends_at` means the window is closed.
- The **correction window** ends at an exclusive instant computed from the schedule's `certified_at`. It is open while the current server time is strictly before that end instant; a current server time exactly equal to it means the window has closed.
- A **combination sale promotional window** runs from `promo_starts_at`, inclusive, to `promo_ends_at`, exclusive.
- A **brand registration interval** is, by contrast, a closed interval: both `registration_effective_from` and `registration_expires_on` are inside it, and an instant equal to either endpoint is contained in the interval.

Wherever this document expresses a window length in days, the conversion is exact and fixed: one day is 86,400 seconds. A three-day hold window is 259,200 seconds after `certified_at`; a fifteen-day correction window is 1,296,000 seconds after `certified_at`; a seven-day cooldown buffer is 604,800 seconds.

### 2.8. Cascade Discipline Across Schedules, Items, Tiers and Combinations

The posting hierarchy is deep in behaviour even though it is flat in storage: a schedule owns items, an item owns discount tiers, a schedule owns combination sales whose members are its items, and an item's price is the input to both. When a price moves, everything derived from it moves with it in the same request — the system never leaves a derived figure stale for a later sweep to fix.

Concretely: whenever a `PriceScheduleItem`'s `case_price_cents` changes, every `ACTIVE` `QuantityDiscountTier` of that item has its `discounted_case_price_cents` recomputed, and every affected `CombinationSale` has its `floor_price_cents` and `min_allowed_price_cents` recomputed. A combination whose already-offered `combined_case_price_cents` no longer reaches its recomputed `min_allowed_price_cents` is moved to `VOID`; a combination previously `VOID` whose `combined_case_price_cents` again reaches the recomputed `min_allowed_price_cents` is returned to `ACTIVE`. The two directions are not symmetric in scope, and the asymmetry is intentional: accepting an amendment recomputes only the combinations currently in `ACTIVE` status (and may void them), whereas revoking an amendment recomputes every combination containing the item regardless of its current status (and may therefore resurrect one).

Rollup counters are maintained by the same immediate discipline. A schedule's `item_count` tracks the items attached to it, a schedule's `correction_count` tracks approved corrections across all of its items, an item's `amendment_count` tracks accepted-and-not-revoked amendments, an item's `correction_count` tracks approved corrections applied to it, an invoice's `line_count` tracks its lines, an audit's `finding_count` tracks its findings, and a wholesaler's `late_filing_count`, `frivolous_amendment_count`, `off_posted_violation_count` and `open_audit_count` track their respective events. Each of these is written in the same request as the event that changes it.

Ordering of cascades within a single request is: primary entity first, then its children, then the cross-aggregate parents (wholesaler penalty buckets, schedule status rollups). Every record whose stored fields the cascade touches gets the `updated_at` and `version` treatment of §2.5.

### 2.9. The Four Penalty Buckets and Total Exposure

A `Wholesaler` accumulates monetary exposure in four independent buckets, each an integer count of cents that starts at 0 on registration: `late_filing_penalty_accrued_cents` for filings made after the posting deadline, `correction_penalty_accrued_cents` for repeat clerical corrections, `amendment_penalty_accrued_cents` for frivolous hold amendments, and `assessed_penalty_cents` for penalties charged through compliance audits. The stored field `penalty_exposure_cents` is always the plain sum of those four buckets, recomputed and rewritten in the same request as any accrual or reversal that touched any of them.

Three of the four buckets are reversible, and reversal is exact rather than approximate. Withdrawing a late-filed schedule subtracts the very same late filing penalty amount that submission added and decrements `late_filing_count`. Voiding an assessed audit subtracts the audit's `penalty_assessed_cents` from `assessed_penalty_cents` and its `finding_count` from `off_posted_violation_count`. Reversal never floors at zero implicitly — the reversals are defined to undo exactly what the corresponding accrual added, so a correctly implemented ledger cannot go negative.

### 2.10. The Order of Rejection

When a request violates more than one rule, exactly one error is returned, and which one is not a matter of taste. Validation proceeds in the following strict ascending order; the **first** rule violated determines the response, and no later rule is evaluated at all.

1. **Authentication.** `X-User-Role` is present and names one of the three known roles, and for an entity-backed role `X-User-ID` is present. Otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (401). Applies to every endpoint.
2. **Role authorisation.** The named role is authorised for this endpoint, and for an entity-backed role `X-User-ID` resolves to a `LicenseeUser` whose `is_active` is `true` and whose `user_role` matches the claimed role (`DUAL_ROLE` matching both). Otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (403). Applies to every endpoint.
3. **Path existence.** Every identifier appearing in the URL path exists, checked left to right, and the first identifier that does not resolve determines the error. The error is the not-found code of that identifier's entity type (404). Applies to every endpoint with a path parameter. Path existence is resolved before the request body is examined at all.
4. **Body shape.** Every required body field is present; every field has its declared type; every enum field carries a declared member; every numeric field falls inside its declared range; every datetime field is a parseable ISO 8601 instant; and every interval field pair is ordered so the end strictly follows the start (`registration_expires_on` strictly after `registration_effective_from`; `promo_ends_at` strictly after `promo_starts_at`). Otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400). Applies to every endpoint with a request body. Array-length degeneracy is *not* a shape violation — a `member_item_ids` array with fewer than two distinct entries passes this gate and is caught at priority 19.
5. **Body foreign keys.** Every foreign key supplied in the request body references an existing record; the error is the not-found code of the referenced entity type (404). Applies to every endpoint whose body carries a foreign key. Body foreign keys are resolved after body shape and before ownership.
6. **Wholesaler scope.** For an entity-backed role, the target resource belongs to the acting `LicenseeUser`'s wholesaler. Otherwise `ERR_FORBIDDEN_WHOLESALER_SCOPE` (403). Applies to the thirteen write endpoints enumerated in §2.11.
7. **Optimistic concurrency.** The supplied expected parent version equals the parent's stored `version`; a mismatch yields `ERR_STALE_SCHEDULE_VERSION` on `POST /hold-amendments` and `ERR_STALE_WHOLESALER_VERSION` on `POST /compliance-audits`, both HTTP 412. Concurrency is checked after ownership and before state.
8. **State admissibility.** The target entity is in a state from which the requested transition is defined. In addition: on `POST /hold-amendments` the amending item's `status` must be `POSTED`; on `POST /error-corrections` the item's `status` must be `POSTED` or `EFFECTIVE`; on `POST /invoice-lines` the invoice's `audit_status` must be `NOT_AUDITED`; and on `POST /retailer-invoices/{id}/validate` the invoice must carry at least one line and its `audit_status` must be `NOT_AUDITED`. Otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (409). Applies to every transition endpoint plus `POST /hold-amendments`, `POST /error-corrections` and `POST /invoice-lines`. State admissibility outranks every business rule below it — an amendment filed against an item that has already gone `EFFECTIVE` is reported as `ERR_INVALID_STATE` even though its post-and-hold window has also closed, because priority 8 is reached before priority 12.
9. **Licence standing.** The governing wholesaler's `license_status` is `ACTIVE`. Otherwise `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE` (409). Applies to exactly two endpoints: `POST /price-schedules/{id}/submit` and `POST /retailer-invoices/{id}/validate`. A suspended licensee may neither file nor invoice.
10. **Brand registration prerequisite.** Every brand referenced by an item of the schedule being submitted has `registration_status` `APPROVED` and a registration interval containing the schedule's `effective_date`. Otherwise `ERR_BRAND_NOT_REGISTERED` (409). Applies to `POST /price-schedules/{id}/submit`.
11. **Self-reference prohibition.** The reference item of a hold amendment belongs to a wholesaler other than the amending item's wholesaler. Otherwise `ERR_SELF_REFERENCE_NOT_ALLOWED` (409). Applies to `POST /hold-amendments`.
12. **Hold window open.** The amending item's schedule is inside its post-and-hold window. Otherwise `ERR_HOLD_WINDOW_CLOSED` (409). Applies to two endpoints: `POST /hold-amendments` and `POST /hold-amendments/{id}/accept`.
13. **Interval conflict.** Brand registration intervals for the same wholesaler, brand name and beverage class must not overlap, yielding `ERR_BRAND_REGISTRATION_OVERLAP` on `POST /brands`; combination sale promotional windows for the same wholesaler must be separated by at least the program's cooldown buffer, yielding `ERR_COMBINATION_COOLDOWN_VIOLATION` on `POST /combination-sales`. Both HTTP 409.
14. **Uniqueness.** A schedule may not carry two items with the same brand, size and package, yielding `ERR_DUPLICATE_SCHEDULE_ITEM` on `POST /price-schedule-items`; a wholesaler may not hold two non-withdrawn schedules for the same posting month, yielding `ERR_DUPLICATE_POSTING_MONTH` on `POST /price-schedules`; a wholesaler may not hold two non-void audits for the same period month, yielding `ERR_DUPLICATE_AUDIT_PERIOD` on `POST /compliance-audits`. All HTTP 409. Uniqueness is enforced after interval conflicts.
15. **Instrument permitted by the state.** Quantity discount tiers require the governing program's `quantity_discount_allowed` to be `true`, otherwise `ERR_QUANTITY_DISCOUNT_PROHIBITED` on `POST /quantity-discount-tiers`; combination sales require `combination_sale_allowed` to be `true`, otherwise `ERR_COMBINATION_SALE_PROHIBITED` on `POST /combination-sales`. Both HTTP 422.
16. **Segregation of duties.** The certifying user is not the user who filed the schedule. Otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (409). Applies to `POST /price-schedules/{id}/certify`.
17. **Fiscal relations.** Declared prices satisfy their arithmetic relations. In order of the endpoint they guard: an amendment must be a reduction (`ERR_AMENDMENT_NOT_A_REDUCTION`) and must not fall below its reference (`ERR_AMENDMENT_BELOW_REFERENCE`) on `POST /hold-amendments`, the reduction test being evaluated before the reference test; a schedule item's unit price multiplied by its bottles per case must be at least its case price (`ERR_CASE_PRICE_EXCEEDS_UNIT_AGGREGATE`) on `POST /price-schedule-items`; a combination price must be at or above the minimum allowed price (`ERR_COMBINATION_BELOW_FLOOR`) on `POST /combination-sales`; a discount percentage must not exceed the state cap (`ERR_DISCOUNT_EXCEEDS_STATE_CAP`) on `POST /quantity-discount-tiers`; and a correction's variance must not exceed the clerical limit (`ERR_CORRECTION_EXCEEDS_CLERICAL_LIMIT`) on `POST /error-corrections`. All HTTP 422. Fiscal relations are checked after every structural and state rule above them.
18. **Correction quota.** The schedule has not already reached the program's `max_corrections_per_schedule` approved corrections. Otherwise `ERR_CORRECTION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to `POST /error-corrections/{id}/approve`.
19. **Cardinality.** A schedule being submitted has at least one item (`ERR_SCHEDULE_HAS_NO_ITEMS` on `POST /price-schedules/{id}/submit`); an invoice line has a non-zero total quantity (`ERR_ZERO_QUANTITY_LINE` on `POST /invoice-lines`); a combination sale names at least two distinct member items (`ERR_COMBINATION_TOO_FEW_MEMBERS`) and every named member belongs to the declared schedule (`ERR_ITEM_NOT_ON_SAME_SCHEDULE`), both on `POST /combination-sales`, the member-count test evaluated before the membership test. All HTTP 422.
20. **Correction window open.** The item's correction window is still open. Otherwise `ERR_CORRECTION_WINDOW_CLOSED` (409). Applies to `POST /error-corrections`. Note the consequence of ordering: a correction whose variance also exceeds the clerical limit is reported as `ERR_CORRECTION_EXCEEDS_CLERICAL_LIMIT`, because priority 17 is reached before priority 20.
21. **Hold window containment within the month.** The post-and-hold window computed at certification ends strictly before the schedule's `effective_date`. Otherwise `ERR_HOLD_WINDOW_OVERRUNS_EFFECTIVE` (409). Applies to `POST /price-schedules/{id}/certify`. Certification so late that the hold window would still be open when prices take effect is not permitted.
22. **Auditable material exists.** At least one `FLAGGED` invoice of the audited wholesaler falls inside the audit period month. Otherwise `ERR_NO_FLAGGED_INVOICES` (409). Applies to `POST /compliance-audits/{id}/collect`. An audit cannot be collected when there is nothing to find.

A worked consequence of this table: submitting a schedule that has no items, whose wholesaler is `SUSPENDED`, is rejected with `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`, not `ERR_SCHEDULE_HAS_NO_ITEMS`, because licence standing sits at priority 9 and cardinality at priority 19. Likewise a hold amendment filed by a wholesaler against its own posted item, outside the hold window and at a raised price, is rejected with `ERR_SELF_REFERENCE_NOT_ALLOWED`.

### 2.11. Path Resolution and Wholesaler Ownership

Every write and read endpoint that names a resource in its path does so with a single `id` segment. That identifier is resolved before the body is parsed, and the not-found code that is returned identifies the entity type of the segment: `ERR_STATE_PROGRAM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_WHOLESALER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LICENSEE_USER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_BRAND_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PRICE_SCHEDULE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PRICE_SCHEDULE_ITEM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_QUANTITY_DISCOUNT_TIER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_COMBINATION_SALE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_HOLD_AMENDMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ERROR_CORRECTION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_RETAILER_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INVOICE_LINE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_COMPLIANCE_AUDIT_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_AUDIT_FINDING_NOT_FOUND`, each HTTP 404. The same codes are reused when a foreign key inside a request body fails to resolve at priority 5 — for instance a `state_program_id` that names nothing yields `ERR_STATE_PROGRAM_NOT_FOUND`, a `brand_id` that names nothing yields `ERR_BRAND_NOT_FOUND`, and a member of `member_item_ids` that names nothing yields `ERR_PRICE_SCHEDULE_ITEM_NOT_FOUND`.

`ERR_FORBIDDEN_WHOLESALER_SCOPE` guards exactly thirteen write endpoints, and for each the owning wholesaler is determined as follows. On `POST /brands`, `POST /price-schedules` and `POST /retailer-invoices` the owner is the `wholesaler_id` carried in the body. On `POST /price-schedule-items`, `POST /combination-sales`, `POST /price-schedules/{id}/submit`, `POST /price-schedules/{id}/certify` and `POST /price-schedules/{id}/withdraw` the owner is the wholesaler of the target schedule. On `POST /quantity-discount-tiers`, `POST /hold-amendments` and `POST /error-corrections` the owner is the wholesaler of the schedule that owns the referenced item — for a hold amendment, specifically the schedule of the **amending** item, never the reference item. On `POST /invoice-lines` and `POST /retailer-invoices/{id}/validate` the owner is the wholesaler of the target invoice. Because `ComplianceOfficer` is opaque, none of these checks constrain the regulator; they apply only when the acting role is `FilingAgent` or `CertifyingOfficer`.

### 2.12. Optimistic Concurrency Control

Two creation endpoints are guarded by optimistic concurrency because both derive their behaviour from a parent whose state may have moved under the client's feet. No other endpoint in this API accepts or requires a version field of any kind.

`POST /hold-amendments` requires `expected_schedule_version`, a non-optional integer input carrying the client's last observed `version` of the `PriceSchedule` that owns the amending item. If that value does not equal the schedule's currently stored `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_SCHEDULE_VERSION` (HTTP 412) and no `HoldAmendment` is created. The rationale is competitive fairness: an amendment must be drafted against the schedule as the competitor actually read it, so an amendment written against an outdated view of the schedule is rejected outright rather than layered on top of a change the filer never saw. A successful create increments that schedule's `version` by 1 — which is precisely what invalidates every other client's previously read version.

`POST /compliance-audits` requires `expected_wholesaler_version`, a non-optional integer input carrying the client's last observed `version` of the `Wholesaler` to be audited. If that value does not equal the wholesaler's currently stored `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_WHOLESALER_VERSION` (HTTP 412) and no `ComplianceAudit` is created. The rationale is that an audit's escalation tier is a function of the wholesaler's violation history, so opening an audit against a stale view of the wholesaler would fix the wrong tier. A successful create increments `Wholesaler.version` by 1, because `open_audit_count` is incremented in the same request.

Both checks sit at priority 7 of §2.10: after path existence, body shape, body foreign keys and ownership, and before any state or business rule.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up a Program, its Licensees and its Filing Staff

Everything in the system hangs from a **state price posting program**, the regulator's configuration record for one jurisdiction. A `ComplianceOfficer` registers one by supplying a `state_code` — a two-letter code that must be unique across all programs — and a `program_name` for display. Every other attribute of a program is optional on input and falls back to a documented default, and it is these defaults that quietly set the tone of the whole system: `posting_deadline_day` defaults to the 10th of the posting month and must be an integer from 1 through 28 inclusive; `hold_window_days` defaults to 3 and must be at least 1; `correction_window_days` defaults to 15 and must be at least 1; `combo_cooldown_days` defaults to 7 and must be at least 0; `quantity_discount_allowed` and `combination_sale_allowed` both default to `true`; `max_quantity_discount_pct` defaults to `0.15` and must fall between 0 and 1 inclusive; `max_promotional_allowance_pct` defaults to `0.05` and must fall between 0 and 1 inclusive; `depletion_allowance_per_case_cents` defaults to 150 cents per case and must be at least 0; `late_filing_penalty_cents` defaults to 250,000 cents — two thousand five hundred dollars per late filing — and must be at least 0; `repeat_correction_penalty_cents` defaults to 100,000 cents and must be at least 0; `frivolous_amendment_penalty_cents` defaults to 75,000 cents and must be at least 0; `off_posted_penalty_rate_pct` defaults to `0.40` and must fall between 0 and 1 inclusive; and `max_corrections_per_schedule` defaults to 2 and must be at least 1.

A **wholesaler** is registered by a `ComplianceOfficer` with a `license_number` that must be unique across all wholesalers, a `legal_name`, and the `state_program_id` naming the program it files under. Its `license_status` is optional on input and defaults to `ACTIVE`; the only other permitted value is `SUSPENDED`. The regulator may later move a wholesaler between those two values through a dedicated licence-standing capability that takes a `license_status` in the body and returns the updated wholesaler with its `version` advanced by 1. All twelve of a wholesaler's derived numbers — the four penalty buckets, `penalty_exposure_cents`, `late_filing_count`, `frivolous_amendment_count`, `off_posted_violation_count` and `open_audit_count` — start at 0 and are never set directly by any client.

The people who act for a wholesaler are **licensee users**, registered by a `ComplianceOfficer` with the `wholesaler_id` they act for, a `full_name`, and a `user_role` that is required and must be one of `FILING_AGENT`, `CERTIFYING_OFFICER` or `DUAL_ROLE`. The optional `is_active` flag defaults to `true`; setting it to `false` at registration creates a user who can never authenticate. This is the record behind the `X-User-ID` header for the two entity-backed roles, and it is also the record that makes segregation of duties enforceable: because a schedule remembers which licensee user filed it, the system can later refuse to let that same person certify it.

### 3.2. Brand Registration as a Posting Prerequisite

No price may be posted for a brand the wholesaler is not registered to distribute, so brand registration is the first thing a `FilingAgent` does. Filing a registration supplies the `wholesaler_id`, the `brand_name` as it will appear on the price schedule, the `supplier_name` of the supplier or importer that appointed the wholesaler, a `beverage_class` that must be `SPIRITS`, `WINE` or `MALT`, and the closed interval `registration_effective_from` through `registration_expires_on`, where the expiry instant must be strictly after the effective instant. The new registration begins in `PENDING` with `rejection_reason` unset.

Registration intervals cannot overlap for a given combination of wholesaler, brand name and beverage class. Because these are closed intervals, two registrations conflict when the requested `registration_effective_from` is at or before an existing registration's `registration_expires_on` **and** that existing registration's `registration_effective_from` is at or before the requested `registration_expires_on` — intervals that merely touch at an endpoint do overlap. Only registrations that have not been rejected count as existing occupants of the timeline: registrations in `PENDING` and `APPROVED` block, and a registration in `REJECTED` does not. A conflict is refused with `ERR_BRAND_REGISTRATION_OVERLAP`.

The regulator disposes of a pending registration in one of two ways. Approval moves `registration_status` from `PENDING` to `APPROVED` and advances the brand's `version` by 1. Rejection moves it from `PENDING` to `REJECTED`, records the supplied `rejection_reason`, and likewise advances the brand's `version` by 1.

The prerequisite bites at submission time rather than at item-creation time. When a schedule is filed, every brand behind every one of its items must be in `APPROVED` status, and each such brand's closed registration interval must contain the schedule's `effective_date` — meaning `registration_effective_from` is at or before `effective_date` and `registration_expires_on` is at or after it. A brand that is still `PENDING`, a brand that was `REJECTED`, or an approved brand whose registration expires before the month the prices would take effect all produce the same refusal, `ERR_BRAND_NOT_REGISTERED`.

### 3.3. Assembling and Filing the Monthly Price Schedule

A **price schedule** is one wholesaler's filing for one posting month. A `FilingAgent` opens one by supplying the `wholesaler_id`, the `state_program_id`, and a `posting_month` written as four digits, a hyphen, and two digits — the `YYYY-MM` form. A schedule always begins in `DRAFT`, with `filed_at`, `filed_by_user_id`, `certified_at`, `certified_by_user_id`, `hold_window_ends_at`, `return_reason` and `superseded_by_schedule_id` all unset, with `is_late` `false`, and with `return_count`, `correction_count` and `item_count` all 0.

The one derived value computed at creation is `effective_date`, and it embodies the central temporal fact of the program: **a schedule posted in one month takes effect at the first instant of the following month.** Parse `posting_month` into a year and a month number. If the month number is 12, the effective year is the year plus one and the effective month is 1; otherwise the effective year is the same year and the effective month is the month number plus one. The `effective_date` is then midnight UTC on the first day of that effective year and month, written with three fractional digits — always exactly `00:00:00.000Z`. A `posting_month` of `2026-09` therefore yields an `effective_date` of `2026-10-01T00:00:00.000Z`, and a `posting_month` of `2026-12` yields `2027-01-01T00:00:00.000Z`.

A wholesaler may not hold two live schedules for the same posting month: if it already has a schedule for that `posting_month` in any status other than `WITHDRAWN`, the create is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_POSTING_MONTH`. Withdrawing a schedule therefore frees the month for a fresh filing.

Into a schedule the filing agent adds **price schedule items**, one per priced brand-size-package combination. Each item carries the `schedule_id` it belongs to, the `brand_id` being priced, a `size_ml` container size in millilitres of at least 1, a `bottles_per_case` count of at least 1, a `case_price_cents` of at least 1 and a `unit_price_cents` of at least 1. The pricing relation the state cares about is that a case must never cost more than buying the same bottles individually: `unit_price_cents` multiplied by `bottles_per_case` must be at least `case_price_cents`, and a violation is refused with `ERR_CASE_PRICE_EXCEEDS_UNIT_AGGREGATE`. Within one schedule the triple of brand, `size_ml` and `bottles_per_case` must be unique; a repeat is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_SCHEDULE_ITEM`. Adding an item increments the schedule's `item_count` by 1 and advances the schedule's `version` by 1. Item creation imposes no state requirement on the parent schedule — the schedule's `status` is not consulted when a line is added.

A new item begins in `DRAFT` with `certified_case_price_cents`, `certified_unit_price_cents` and `supersedes_item_id` unset and with `amendment_count` and `correction_count` both 0. Items remain `DRAFT` right through submission; it is certification, not filing, that posts them.

Filing the schedule is the `DRAFT → SUBMITTED` transition, performed by a `FilingAgent` of the schedule's own wholesaler. Four preconditions guard it, in the order given in §2.10: the wholesaler's `license_status` must be `ACTIVE` or the filing is refused with `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`; every item's brand must be approved and its registration interval must contain the schedule's `effective_date` or the filing is refused with `ERR_BRAND_NOT_REGISTERED`; and the schedule must carry at least one item or the filing is refused with `ERR_SCHEDULE_HAS_NO_ITEMS`. Filing sets `filed_at` to the current server time, sets `filed_by_user_id` to the acting licensee user, and advances the schedule's `version` by 1. On a resubmission both `filed_at` and `filed_by_user_id` are overwritten, so they always describe the most recent submission.

Filing also decides lateness, and the boundary is exact. The program's `posting_deadline_day`, zero-padded to two digits, names a day inside the posting month itself; the **deadline instant** is the last millisecond of that day, that is the posting month's year and month, the deadline day, and a time of `23:59:59.999Z`. For a `posting_month` of `2026-09` with a `posting_deadline_day` of 10, the deadline instant is `2026-09-10T23:59:59.999Z`. `is_late` is set to `true` when `filed_at` is strictly after the deadline instant and to `false` otherwise; a `filed_at` exactly equal to the deadline instant is on time.

The consequences of lateness reach across into the wholesaler. When `is_late` comes out `true`, the wholesaler's `late_filing_count` is incremented by 1, the program's `late_filing_penalty_cents` is added to the wholesaler's `late_filing_penalty_accrued_cents`, `penalty_exposure_cents` is recomputed as the sum of the four buckets, and the wholesaler's `version` is advanced by 1. When `is_late` comes out `false`, none of this happens: no counter moves, no penalty accrues, and the wholesaler's `version` and `updated_at` are left exactly as they were. Both branches matter, and they are the difference between a wholesaler record that a client's cached `version` still matches and one it does not.

A `ComplianceOfficer` who finds a filed schedule deficient sends it back: the `SUBMITTED → DRAFT` transition requires the schedule to be in `SUBMITTED` (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`), records the supplied `return_reason`, increments `return_count` by 1, and advances the schedule's `version` by 1. Returning a schedule does **not** reverse any late filing penalty already accrued. The consequence is deliberate and cumulative: a schedule that is returned and then filed late again accrues the late filing penalty a second time and increments `late_filing_count` a second time, because each submission is evaluated afresh against the deadline instant.

The filing agent's escape hatch is withdrawal, the `SUBMITTED → WITHDRAWN` transition, available only while the schedule is in `SUBMITTED` (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`). Withdrawal moves every one of the schedule's items to `WITHDRAWN` regardless of the status each item was in, and advances the schedule's `version` by 1. If `is_late` is `true`, withdrawal reverses the accrual exactly: the wholesaler's `late_filing_count` is decremented by 1, the program's `late_filing_penalty_cents` is subtracted from `late_filing_penalty_accrued_cents`, `penalty_exposure_cents` is recomputed, and the wholesaler's `version` is advanced by 1. If `is_late` is `false` there is nothing to reverse, and the wholesaler record is not touched at all. `WITHDRAWN` is terminal for a schedule.

### 3.4. Certification, the Post-and-Hold Window and Segregation of Duties

Certification is the moment prices become public and the statutory quiet period begins. It is the `SUBMITTED → POSTED` transition and it belongs exclusively to a `CertifyingOfficer`. Three preconditions guard it and they are evaluated in this order: the acting licensee user must belong to the schedule's wholesaler, or the request is refused with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_WHOLESALER_SCOPE`; the acting licensee user must not be the user recorded in the schedule's `filed_by_user_id`, or the request is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`; and the post-and-hold window that certification would open must end strictly before the schedule's `effective_date`, or the request is refused with `ERR_HOLD_WINDOW_OVERRUNS_EFFECTIVE`. Note that a person holding `DUAL_ROLE` can legitimately file and certify — but not the same schedule, because the segregation test compares licensee user identities, not capacities. The schedule must of course be in `SUBMITTED`; otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE` is returned at priority 8, ahead of all three.

Certification stamps `certified_at` with the current server time, records the acting user in `certified_by_user_id`, and computes `hold_window_ends_at` as `certified_at` advanced by the program's `hold_window_days` multiplied by 86,400 seconds. With the default three-day window, a schedule certified at `2026-09-20T12:00:00.000Z` has a `hold_window_ends_at` of `2026-09-23T12:00:00.000Z`. That end instant must fall strictly before `effective_date`; a `hold_window_ends_at` exactly equal to `effective_date` fails the test and produces `ERR_HOLD_WINDOW_OVERRUNS_EFFECTIVE`.

Certification also posts the items. Every item of the schedule that is in `DRAFT` moves to `POSTED`, and for each such item the system snapshots the prices as filed: `case_price_cents` is copied into `certified_case_price_cents` and `unit_price_cents` into `certified_unit_price_cents`. Those two snapshot fields never change again. They exist so that the state can always ask what the wholesaler originally attested to, independently of every amendment and correction that follows. The schedule's own `version` is advanced by 1.

While the window is open — from `certified_at` inclusive up to but not including `hold_window_ends_at` — competitors may amend, as described in §3.6. Once the current server time reaches `hold_window_ends_at`, the window is shut, and the prices simply wait for the calendar.

### 3.5. Quantity Discount Tiers and Combination Sales

Two promotional instruments may be attached to posted prices, and each state decides independently whether it tolerates them at all.

A **quantity discount tier** says that a retailer buying enough cases pays less per case. A `FilingAgent` attaches one by naming the `item_id` it discounts, a `min_cases` threshold of at least 2, and a `discount_pct` that must be strictly greater than 0 and at most 1. If the governing program has `quantity_discount_allowed` set to `false`, the tier is refused with `ERR_QUANTITY_DISCOUNT_PROHIBITED`; if `discount_pct` is strictly greater than the program's `max_quantity_discount_pct`, it is refused with `ERR_DISCOUNT_EXCEEDS_STATE_CAP` — a `discount_pct` exactly equal to the cap is permitted. A new tier is `ACTIVE`; the only other status is `INACTIVE`, which the posting run assigns when the tier's item is superseded, and which no client sets directly.

The tier's own price is derived, never supplied: `discounted_case_price_cents` is the item's `case_price_cents` multiplied by the quantity one minus `discount_pct`, rounded half up to whole cents. An item priced at 120,000 cents per case with a `discount_pct` of `0.125` yields a `discounted_case_price_cents` of 105,000. This derivation is re-run, for every `ACTIVE` tier of the item, whenever that item's `case_price_cents` changes — which in practice means whenever a hold amendment is accepted, whenever an accepted amendment is revoked, and whenever an error correction is approved.

A **combination sale** offers one case of each of several items for a single bundled price. A `FilingAgent` declares one by naming the `schedule_id` whose items form the combination, a `member_item_ids` array of `PriceScheduleItem` identifiers, the promotional window `promo_starts_at` (inclusive) through `promo_ends_at` (exclusive, and required to be strictly after the start), and the `combined_case_price_cents` being offered, which must be at least 1. If the governing program has `combination_sale_allowed` set to `false`, the declaration is refused with `ERR_COMBINATION_SALE_PROHIBITED`. The array must name at least two **distinct** item identifiers — fewer distinct entries than that is refused with `ERR_COMBINATION_TOO_FEW_MEMBERS` — and every named member must belong to the declared schedule, a violation being refused with `ERR_ITEM_NOT_ON_SAME_SCHEDULE`.

The floor a combination may not breach is derived in two steps. First, `floor_price_cents` is the plain sum of `case_price_cents` across every member item. Second, a discount band is selected **from that pre-discount floor**: a floor below 50,000 cents (five hundred dollars) permits no discount at all, a band percentage of `0.00`; a floor of at least 50,000 cents but below 150,000 cents permits five percent, a band percentage of `0.05`; and a floor of 150,000 cents or more permits ten percent, a band percentage of `0.10`. `min_allowed_price_cents` is then `floor_price_cents` multiplied by the quantity one minus the band's percentage, rounded half up to whole cents. Keying the band on the pre-discount floor is load-bearing: a `floor_price_cents` of exactly 150,000 lands in the ten percent band and yields a `min_allowed_price_cents` of 135,000; keying the band on the discounted value would have selected the five percent band and produced 142,500, which is not the rule. A `combined_case_price_cents` strictly below the computed `min_allowed_price_cents` is refused with `ERR_COMBINATION_BELOW_FLOOR`; a price exactly equal to it is permitted. A new combination is `ACTIVE`; the only other status is `VOID`.

Combination sales are also rationed in time. A state that permits them still forbids a wholesaler from running them back to back, and the buffer is the program's `combo_cooldown_days` converted to seconds at 86,400 seconds per day. Each promotional window is treated as half-open — start inclusive, end exclusive — and each is then widened forward by the buffer. A requested window conflicts with an existing `ACTIVE` combination sale of the same wholesaler when **both** of these hold: the requested `promo_starts_at` is strictly before that existing window's `promo_ends_at` advanced by the buffer, and that existing window's `promo_starts_at` is strictly before the requested `promo_ends_at` advanced by the buffer. Both comparisons are strict, so a requested window beginning exactly at an existing window's end plus the buffer does not conflict. With the default seven-day cooldown and an existing window running from `2026-10-01T00:00:00.000Z` up to `2026-10-10T00:00:00.000Z`, a requested window starting `2026-10-16T00:00:00.000Z` conflicts because it falls before `2026-10-17T00:00:00.000Z`, while a requested window starting exactly `2026-10-17T00:00:00.000Z` does not. A conflict is refused with `ERR_COMBINATION_COOLDOWN_VIOLATION`. Combinations in `VOID` status are not occupants of the timeline and never cause a cooldown conflict.

### 3.6. Competitive Amendments and Clerical Corrections

Two mechanisms move a posted price after certification, and they exist for entirely different reasons.

**Hold amendments** are the competitive mechanism, and the whole point of post-and-hold. A `FilingAgent` who sees a rival's posted price may lower its own to match, by filing an amendment that names the `amending_item_id` — its own posted item whose price would change — the `reference_item_id` — the competitor's posted item being matched — a `new_case_price_cents` of at least 1, a `new_unit_price_cents` of at least 1, and the `expected_schedule_version` demanded by §2.12. The amending item's `status` must be `POSTED`; anything else is `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. Because the mechanism exists to match a *competitor*, the reference item must belong to a different wholesaler than the amending item; a wholesaler naming its own item is refused with `ERR_SELF_REFERENCE_NOT_ALLOWED`. The amending item's schedule must still be inside its post-and-hold window at the current server time, or the filing is refused with `ERR_HOLD_WINDOW_CLOSED`.

Amendment prices are admissible in only one direction. The amendment must be a genuine reduction: `new_case_price_cents` must be strictly below the amending item's current `case_price_cents`, and a value equal to or above it is refused with `ERR_AMENDMENT_NOT_A_REDUCTION`. And it must not undercut the price being matched: `new_case_price_cents` must be at or above the reference item's current `case_price_cents`, and a value below it is refused with `ERR_AMENDMENT_BELOW_REFERENCE` — matching the reference exactly is permitted. Filing a valid amendment creates a `HoldAmendment` in `PENDING` status with `prior_case_price_cents`, `prior_unit_price_cents`, `rejection_reason` and `revocation_reason` all unset, and advances the amending item's schedule `version` by 1.

Acceptance by a `ComplianceOfficer` is the `PENDING → ACCEPTED` transition. It requires the amendment to be in `PENDING` (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`) and the amending item's schedule to still be inside its post-and-hold window (otherwise `ERR_HOLD_WINDOW_CLOSED`) — the window is re-tested at acceptance time, so an amendment filed inside the window but adjudicated after it closed cannot be accepted. Acceptance first captures the amending item's current prices into the amendment's `prior_case_price_cents` and `prior_unit_price_cents`, then writes `new_case_price_cents` into the item's `case_price_cents` and `new_unit_price_cents` into the item's `unit_price_cents`, then increments the item's `amendment_count` by 1, then recomputes `discounted_case_price_cents` on every `ACTIVE` quantity discount tier of that item, then recomputes `floor_price_cents` and `min_allowed_price_cents` on every `ACTIVE` combination sale that contains the item and moves to `VOID` any such combination whose `combined_case_price_cents` now falls strictly below its recomputed `min_allowed_price_cents`, and finally advances the item's `version` by 1.

Rejection is the `PENDING → REJECTED` transition, requiring `PENDING` status (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`). It records the supplied `rejection_reason` and leaves the amending item's prices, its `amendment_count`, its tiers and its combinations entirely unchanged. What it does move is the filer's record: the amending wholesaler's `frivolous_amendment_count` is incremented by 1, and when the resulting count reaches 3 or more the program's `frivolous_amendment_penalty_cents` is added to that wholesaler's `amendment_penalty_accrued_cents`. `penalty_exposure_cents` is then recomputed and the wholesaler's `version` is advanced by 1 on every rejection, whether or not the penalty threshold was crossed, because the count itself always changes. The threshold is "reaches 3 or more", not "equals 3": the third rejection accrues the penalty and so does every rejection after it.

Revocation is the `ACCEPTED → REVOKED` transition, requiring `ACCEPTED` status (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`) and taking a `revocation_reason`. It restores the amending item's `case_price_cents` from `prior_case_price_cents` and its `unit_price_cents` from `prior_unit_price_cents`, decrements the item's `amendment_count` by 1, recomputes `discounted_case_price_cents` on every `ACTIVE` quantity discount tier of the item, and recomputes `floor_price_cents` and `min_allowed_price_cents` on **every** combination sale containing the item — not merely the `ACTIVE` ones — returning to `ACTIVE` any combination currently in `VOID` whose `combined_case_price_cents` is at or above the recomputed `min_allowed_price_cents`. The item's `version` is advanced by 1. Both `REJECTED` and `REVOKED` are terminal for an amendment; there is no path back to `PENDING`.

**Error corrections** are the clerical mechanism. A `FilingAgent` files one against a certified item by naming the `item_id`, the `corrected_case_price_cents` of at least 1, the `corrected_unit_price_cents` of at least 1, and a free-text `reason` narrating the clerical error. The item's `status` must be `POSTED` or `EFFECTIVE`; anything else is `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. Filing captures the item's current prices into the correction's `prior_case_price_cents` and `prior_unit_price_cents` and creates the correction in `SUBMITTED` status with `denial_reason` unset.

A correction must actually look like a typo, and that is measured by `variance_pct`: the absolute difference between `corrected_case_price_cents` and `prior_case_price_cents`, divided by `prior_case_price_cents`, left as an unrounded decimal fraction. The clerical limit is one tenth. A `variance_pct` strictly greater than `0.10` is not a clerical error but a rewrite, and is refused with `ERR_CORRECTION_EXCEEDS_CLERICAL_LIMIT`; a `variance_pct` exactly equal to `0.10` is accepted. Filing must also fall inside the program's correction window, which runs from the item's schedule `certified_at` up to but not including `certified_at` advanced by `correction_window_days` multiplied by 86,400 seconds — with the default fifteen days, 1,296,000 seconds after certification. A current server time at or after that end instant refuses the filing with `ERR_CORRECTION_WINDOW_CLOSED`. Because the clerical-limit test sits above the window test in §2.10, a correction that is both too large and too late is reported as `ERR_CORRECTION_EXCEEDS_CLERICAL_LIMIT`.

Approval by a `ComplianceOfficer` is the `SUBMITTED → APPROVED` transition. It requires the correction to be in `SUBMITTED` (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`) and requires the item's schedule to have strictly fewer approved corrections than the program's `max_corrections_per_schedule`; at or above that number the approval is refused with `ERR_CORRECTION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`. Approval writes `corrected_case_price_cents` into the item's `case_price_cents` and `corrected_unit_price_cents` into the item's `unit_price_cents`, increments the item's `correction_count` by 1 and the owning schedule's `correction_count` by 1, recomputes `discounted_case_price_cents` on every `ACTIVE` quantity discount tier of the item, and advances the item's `version` by 1. When the schedule's resulting `correction_count` reaches 2, the program's `repeat_correction_penalty_cents` is added to the wholesaler's `correction_penalty_accrued_cents`, `penalty_exposure_cents` is recomputed and the wholesaler's `version` is advanced by 1 — the first correction on a schedule is free, the second costs the wholesaler money.

Denial is the `SUBMITTED → DENIED` transition, requiring `SUBMITTED` status (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`). It records the supplied `denial_reason`, leaves the item's prices, counters and tiers untouched, and advances the correction's own `version` by 1. Both `APPROVED` and `DENIED` are terminal.

### 3.7. Taking Prices Effective: The Posting Run and Posted-Price Resolution

A posted schedule does not become effective by itself. A `ComplianceOfficer` drives a **posting run** for one program, naming the `state_program_id` to sweep. The run considers every schedule of that program whose `status` is `POSTED` and whose two temporal gates have both passed: the schedule's `effective_date` must be at or before the current server time, and its `hold_window_ends_at` must be at or before the current server time. Both comparisons are inclusive, so a schedule whose `effective_date` equals the clock's present value is eligible. Eligible schedules are processed in ascending `effective_date`, ties broken by ascending `certified_at`, remaining ties broken by ascending `created_at`, so that supersession chains resolve deterministically.

For each eligible schedule the run performs the `POSTED → EFFECTIVE` transition: every item of that schedule in `POSTED` status moves to `EFFECTIVE`, and the schedule itself moves to `EFFECTIVE` with its `version` advanced by 1. Then, for each newly effective item, the run looks for the prior item in `EFFECTIVE` status carrying the same posting key and, when it finds one, moves that older item to `SUPERSEDED` and records the older item's identifier in the newer item's `supersedes_item_id`. Every quantity discount tier of a superseded item that was `ACTIVE` moves to `INACTIVE` — the old tiers stop being usable for invoice pricing the instant their item is displaced. Finally, when every item of an earlier schedule has become `SUPERSEDED`, that earlier schedule undergoes the `EFFECTIVE → SUPERSEDED` transition: its `superseded_by_schedule_id` is set to the schedule that displaced it and its `version` is advanced by 1. A schedule with even one item not yet superseded stays `EFFECTIVE`. `SUPERSEDED` is terminal for a schedule.

The run's response reports the `state_program_id` it swept, the `run_at` instant it executed at (the current server time), a `processed_schedule_count` integer counting the schedules it moved from `POSTED` to `EFFECTIVE`, an `effective_schedule_ids` array of string identifiers listing exactly those schedules, and a `superseded_schedule_ids` array of string identifiers listing the schedules it moved to `SUPERSEDED`. Both arrays are ordered by the schedules' `created_at` ascending. A run that finds nothing eligible succeeds with a `processed_schedule_count` of 0 and two empty arrays.

The **posting key** is the identity under which the state tracks a price over time, and it has six components: the owning wholesaler, the state program, the brand's `brand_name`, the brand's `beverage_class`, the item's `size_ml`, and the item's `bottles_per_case`. Two items match on the posting key when all six agree. Note what is *not* in the key — the brand's identifier itself is not, so a re-registered brand with the same name and beverage class continues the same posted-price lineage.

Resolving what price was posted for a key as of some instant is a distinct capability, exposed per item and used internally by invoice validation. The candidate set is every price schedule item whose posting key matches the subject item's posting key, whose `status` is `EFFECTIVE` or `SUPERSEDED`, and whose owning schedule's `effective_date` is at or before the as-of instant. Items still in `DRAFT`, `POSTED` or `WITHDRAWN` are never candidates. If the candidate set is empty, no posted price exists for that key as of that instant. Otherwise the resolved item is the candidate with the greatest owning-schedule `effective_date`; ties are broken by the greatest owning-schedule `certified_at`; any remaining tie is broken by the earliest `created_at` — that is, ascending.

Resolution comes in two variants that read the identical candidate set and differ only in which price fields they report. The **`as_amended`** variant, which applies when the `variant` query parameter is omitted or set to `as_amended`, reports the resolved item's live `case_price_cents` and `unit_price_cents` — reflecting every accepted hold amendment and every approved error correction. The **`as_certified`** variant, which applies when `variant` is set to `as_certified`, reports the resolved item's `certified_case_price_cents` and `certified_unit_price_cents` snapshot taken at certification, ignoring every later amendment and correction. No rounding is performed in either variant; both figures are already whole cents.

The per-item resolution capability accepts an optional `as_of` instant, defaulting to the current server time when omitted, and the optional `variant`. It answers with the subject `item_id`, the `variant` actually applied, the `as_of` instant actually used, the `resolved_item_id` identifying the candidate that won, and the `posted_case_price_cents` and `posted_unit_price_cents` integers. When the candidate set is empty, `resolved_item_id`, `posted_case_price_cents` and `posted_unit_price_cents` are all reported as null. A `variant` value that is neither `as_amended` nor `as_certified`, and an `as_of` that is not a parseable ISO 8601 instant, are both rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

### 3.8. Retailer Invoicing and Off-Posted-Price Detection

Everything above exists so that this section can be checked. A `FilingAgent` opens a **retailer invoice** by naming the `wholesaler_id` that issued it, the `retailer_license_number` of the retailer being billed, and the `invoice_date` — the instant that will be used as the as-of date when posted prices are resolved for every line. A new invoice has `validation_status` `DRAFT` and `audit_status` `NOT_AUDITED`, an unset `audit_id`, and `line_count`, `subtotal_cents`, `surcharge_cents`, `total_cents`, `total_allowance_cents`, `off_posted_line_count` and `total_variance_cents` all 0. Note that the two status dimensions are independent: `validation_status` moves among `DRAFT`, `VALIDATED` and `FLAGGED` and tracks what validation found, while `audit_status` moves among `NOT_AUDITED`, `UNDER_AUDIT` and `ASSESSED` and tracks the regulator's audit custody of the document.

Lines are added by the same filing agent, each naming the `invoice_id` it belongs to and the `item_id` the retailer was charged for. Quantities are optional and both default to 0: `cases_quantity` counts full cases and must be at least 0, `bottles_quantity` counts loose bottles and must be at least 0. The two prices actually charged are required: `case_price_charged_cents` and `unit_price_charged_cents`, each at least 0 — zero is permitted here, precisely so that a give-away can be detected as an off-posted sale rather than rejected as malformed. Two allowances are optional and default to 0: `promotional_allowance_cents` and `depletion_allowance_cents`, each at least 0. A line whose `cases_quantity` and `bottles_quantity` are *both* zero is degenerate and is refused with `ERR_ZERO_QUANTITY_LINE`. A line may only be added while the invoice's `audit_status` is `NOT_AUDITED`; once the regulator has taken custody, the document is frozen and the attempt is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. Adding a line increments the invoice's `line_count` by 1 and advances the invoice's `version` by 1. Every computed field of a new line — `gross_cents`, `quantity_discount_cents`, `allowed_promotional_cents`, `excess_promotional_cents`, `allowed_depletion_cents`, `excess_depletion_cents`, `net_price_cents`, `posted_gross_cents`, `variance_cents`, `finding_variance_cents` — starts at 0, and `is_off_posted` starts `false`; nothing is priced until validation runs.

Validation is the workhorse. It is invoked by a `FilingAgent` on the invoice's own wholesaler, and it requires that wholesaler's `license_status` to be `ACTIVE` (otherwise `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`), that the invoice carry at least one line, and that the invoice's `audit_status` be `NOT_AUDITED` (either failure being `ERR_INVALID_STATE`). Validation is repeatable: an invoice may be validated from `DRAFT`, and re-validated from `VALIDATED` or from `FLAGGED`, as often as the filer likes for as long as the regulator has not taken custody. Every invocation reprices every line from scratch against the *currently* resolved posted prices, recomputes every invoice rollup, and advances the invoice's `version` by 1.

**Pricing one line.** The `gross_cents` is `cases_quantity` multiplied by `case_price_charged_cents`, plus `bottles_quantity` multiplied by `unit_price_charged_cents`.

The quantity discount is next, and it is measured against the *posted* price, not the charged price. If the governing program — the program the invoicing wholesaler files under — has `quantity_discount_allowed` set to `false`, then `quantity_discount_cents` is 0 and no tier is consulted at all. Otherwise the eligible tiers are every `ACTIVE` quantity discount tier of the line's item whose `min_cases` is at or below the line's `cases_quantity`. If no tier is eligible, `quantity_discount_cents` is 0. If some are, the selected tier is the one with the greatest `min_cases`; ties are broken by the greatest `discount_pct`; any remaining tie is broken by the earliest `created_at` — ascending. The `quantity_discount_cents` is then `cases_quantity` multiplied by the difference between the item's own `case_price_cents` and the selected tier's `discounted_case_price_cents`. Loose bottles never attract a quantity discount.

The two allowances are capped independently, and their rounding differs. The promotional cap is `gross_cents` multiplied by the program's `max_promotional_allowance_pct`, **floored** to whole cents — truncated, never rounded half up. The `allowed_promotional_cents` is the smaller of the line's `promotional_allowance_cents` and that cap, and `excess_promotional_cents` is the line's `promotional_allowance_cents` minus `allowed_promotional_cents`. The depletion cap is `cases_quantity` multiplied by the program's `depletion_allowance_per_case_cents` — with the default of 150 cents per case, a dollar fifty per case, and nothing at all allowed for loose bottles. The `allowed_depletion_cents` is the smaller of the line's `depletion_allowance_cents` and that cap, and `excess_depletion_cents` is the line's `depletion_allowance_cents` minus `allowed_depletion_cents`.

The `net_price_cents` is then `gross_cents`, less `quantity_discount_cents`, less `allowed_promotional_cents`, less `allowed_depletion_cents`. The excess portions are deliberately *not* deducted — money the wholesaler was not entitled to give away does not reduce what the retailer owes; it becomes exposure.

**Detecting an off-posted line.** With the line priced, the system resolves the posted price for the line's item as of the invoice's `invoice_date`, using the `as_amended` variant of §3.7.

If no posted price exists for that key as of that instant, the line was sold on an unposted brand: `posted_gross_cents` is 0, `variance_cents` is 0, `finding_variance_cents` is the entire `gross_cents`, and `is_off_posted` is `true`. The whole charge is exposure, and the condition is classified `UNPOSTED_BRAND`.

If a posted price does exist, `posted_gross_cents` is `cases_quantity` multiplied by the resolved posted case price plus `bottles_quantity` multiplied by the resolved posted unit price. The signed `variance_cents` is `posted_gross_cents` minus `gross_cents`, so a positive `variance_cents` means the retailer was undercharged relative to the posted price and a negative one means overcharged. The `finding_variance_cents` is the absolute value of `variance_cents`, plus `excess_promotional_cents`, plus `excess_depletion_cents` — a line can therefore be exposed for charging exactly the posted price while handing over an over-cap allowance. Finally, `is_off_posted` is `true` exactly when `finding_variance_cents` is not zero, and `false` when it is zero.

**Rolling up the invoice.** The `subtotal_cents` is the sum of `net_price_cents` across every line of the invoice, each line already reduced to whole cents before the summation. The state's handling surcharge is then chosen from a band keyed on `subtotal_cents` **before** any surcharge is added: a subtotal below 500,000 cents (five thousand dollars) attracts no surcharge at all, a rate of `0.000`; a subtotal of at least 500,000 cents but below 2,000,000 cents (twenty thousand dollars) attracts one half of one percent, a rate of `0.005`; a subtotal of 2,000,000 cents or more attracts one percent, a rate of `0.010`. The `surcharge_cents` is `subtotal_cents` multiplied by the band's rate, rounded half up to whole cents, performed exactly once; and `total_cents` is `subtotal_cents` plus `surcharge_cents`.

Three exposure rollups accompany the money. The `total_allowance_cents` is the sum, across every line, of `allowed_promotional_cents` plus `allowed_depletion_cents` — allowed portions only, never the excesses. The `off_posted_line_count` is the number of lines whose `is_off_posted` is `true`. The `total_variance_cents` is the sum of `finding_variance_cents` over just those lines whose `is_off_posted` is `true`.

The invoice's `validation_status` follows directly: `FLAGGED` when `off_posted_line_count` is greater than 0, and `VALIDATED` when it is 0. That gives six defined validation transitions, and they are symmetric. From `DRAFT` a clean invoice goes `DRAFT → VALIDATED` and a dirty one goes `DRAFT → FLAGGED`. A revalidated clean invoice goes `VALIDATED → VALIDATED`, and a revalidated dirty one goes `VALIDATED → FLAGGED`. A previously flagged invoice that now prices clean goes `FLAGGED → VALIDATED`, and in that one branch the system additionally clears `off_posted_line_count` and `total_variance_cents` to 0 explicitly, so no residue of the earlier finding survives; a previously flagged invoice that still prices dirty goes `FLAGGED → FLAGGED`. All six branches reprice every line and recompute every rollup, and all six advance the invoice's `version` by 1. The invoice has no terminal validation state — it can always be revalidated while its `audit_status` remains `NOT_AUDITED`.

### 3.9. Compliance Audits, Penalty Assessment and Exposure Reporting

A `ComplianceOfficer` opens a **compliance audit** by naming the `wholesaler_id` under audit, the `state_program_id` whose penalty rate will govern the assessment, the `audit_period_month` in `YYYY-MM` form, and the `expected_wholesaler_version` demanded by §2.12. A wholesaler may not be under two live audits for the same month: an existing audit for that `audit_period_month` in any status other than `VOID` refuses the create with `ERR_DUPLICATE_AUDIT_PERIOD`. The new audit begins in `OPEN` with `finding_count`, `total_variance_cents`, `base_penalty_cents`, `penalty_assessed_cents` and `prior_violation_count` all 0, `tier_multiplier` at `1.0`, and `void_reason` unset. Opening an audit increments the audited wholesaler's `open_audit_count` by 1 and advances that wholesaler's `version` by 1.

**Collection** is the `OPEN → COLLECTED` transition. It requires the audit to be in `OPEN` (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`) and requires at least one invoice of the audited wholesaler whose `validation_status` is `FLAGGED` and whose `invoice_date` falls inside the `audit_period_month` — that is, whose UTC year and month equal the audited month; when there is none, the collection is refused with `ERR_NO_FLAGGED_INVOICES`. Every such flagged invoice is collected: its `audit_status` moves to `UNDER_AUDIT` and its `audit_id` is stamped with this audit's identifier, which also freezes it against further line additions and further validation.

Collection then records one immutable **audit finding** per off-posted line of every collected invoice. Each finding carries an `audit_id` pointing at this audit, an `invoice_line_id` pointing at the line that produced it, a `finding_type` classifying the condition, a `variance_cents` equal to that line's `finding_variance_cents`, and a `penalty_component_cents`. The `finding_type` is chosen by the first matching clause of this sequence: `UNPOSTED_BRAND` when no posted price could be resolved for the line's item as of the invoice date; otherwise `OFF_POSTED_UNDERCHARGE` when the line's `variance_cents` is greater than 0; otherwise `OFF_POSTED_OVERCHARGE` when the line's `variance_cents` is less than 0; otherwise `EXCESS_PROMOTIONAL_ALLOWANCE` when the line's `excess_promotional_cents` is greater than 0; otherwise `EXCESS_DEPLETION_ALLOWANCE`. Findings are recorded in the order of their invoices' `created_at` ascending and, within an invoice, their lines' `created_at` ascending.

Each finding's `penalty_component_cents` is that finding's `variance_cents` multiplied by the program's `off_posted_penalty_rate_pct` — with the default rate, two fifths of the variance — rounded half up to whole cents. The audit's `base_penalty_cents` is the sum of those already-rounded per-finding components. Rounding each finding before summing is the rule; summing the raw products and rounding once at the end produces a different figure and is not permitted. Collection also sets the audit's `finding_count` to the number of findings recorded and its `total_variance_cents` to the sum of their `variance_cents`, and advances the audit's `version` by 1.

**Assessment** is the `COLLECTED → ASSESSED` transition, requiring `COLLECTED` status (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`). Its first act is the one most easily got wrong: the audit captures `prior_violation_count` from the wholesaler's `off_posted_violation_count` **as it stands before this audit's own `finding_count` is added to it**. The escalation tier is read from that captured figure: a `prior_violation_count` of 2 or fewer yields a `tier_multiplier` of `1.00`; a `prior_violation_count` of at least 3 and at most 9 yields `1.50`; a `prior_violation_count` of 10 or more yields `2.00`. The `penalty_assessed_cents` is then `base_penalty_cents` multiplied by `tier_multiplier`, rounded half up to whole cents — a second rounding, applied after the per-finding rounding that produced `base_penalty_cents`.

Assessment then reaches across into the wholesaler and the invoices. The audit's `penalty_assessed_cents` is added to the wholesaler's `assessed_penalty_cents`, the audit's `finding_count` is added to the wholesaler's `off_posted_violation_count`, `penalty_exposure_cents` is recomputed as the sum of the four buckets, and the wholesaler's `version` is advanced by 1. Every invoice this audit collected has its `audit_status` moved from `UNDER_AUDIT` to `ASSESSED`. The audit's own `version` is advanced by 1.

Two dispositions close out an assessed audit, and they are mirror images. **Closing** is the `ASSESSED → CLOSED` transition, requiring `ASSESSED` status (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`); it changes nothing but the status and advances the audit's `version` by 1. Collected invoices remain `ASSESSED`, the wholesaler's totals stand, and `CLOSED` is terminal. **Voiding** is the `ASSESSED → VOID` transition, likewise requiring `ASSESSED` status (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`), and it unwinds the assessment exactly: it records the supplied `void_reason`, subtracts the audit's `penalty_assessed_cents` from the wholesaler's `assessed_penalty_cents`, subtracts the audit's `finding_count` from the wholesaler's `off_posted_violation_count`, recomputes `penalty_exposure_cents`, advances the wholesaler's `version` by 1, returns every invoice the audit collected to `audit_status` `NOT_AUDITED` and clears each such invoice's `audit_id` back to unset, and advances the audit's `version` by 1. A voided audit releases its invoices for revalidation and frees the month for a fresh audit; `VOID` is terminal. Note that voiding does not delete the audit's findings — they remain readable, and their `variance_cents` and `penalty_component_cents` stand as a record of what the regulator had computed.

**Exposure reporting** closes the loop. A wholesaler's total exposure may be read under either of two variants. Both begin with the same accrued figure: `late_filing_penalty_accrued_cents` plus `correction_penalty_accrued_cents` plus `amendment_penalty_accrued_cents` plus `assessed_penalty_cents`. The **`assessed_exposure`** variant, which applies when the `variant` query parameter is omitted or set to `assessed_exposure`, reports that accrued figure as the exposure and nothing more; the stored `penalty_exposure_cents` on the wholesaler always equals this variant. The **`provisional_exposure`** variant, which applies when `variant` is set to `provisional_exposure`, additionally projects the penalties that are still in flight: for every compliance audit of that wholesaler whose `status` is `OPEN` or `COLLECTED`, and for every audit finding of each such audit, the finding's `variance_cents` is multiplied by the governing program's `off_posted_penalty_rate_pct` and rounded half up to whole cents, and those rounded amounts are summed into the provisional figure. No tier escalation whatsoever is applied to the provisional part — the multiplier is treated as `1.00` even for a wholesaler whose history would otherwise escalate it. The exposure under this variant is the accrued figure plus the provisional figure, and it is therefore always greater than or equal to the exposure under `assessed_exposure`. The response reports the `wholesaler_id`, the `variant` actually applied, the `accrued_cents`, the `provisional_cents` (which is 0 under `assessed_exposure`) and the resulting `exposure_cents`, all three amounts being integers. A `variant` value that is neither `assessed_exposure` nor `provisional_exposure` is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.
