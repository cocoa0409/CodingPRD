# Neurodiagnostic EMU API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

An epilepsy monitoring unit (EMU) exists to provoke and record the very events it spends the rest of the year preventing. A patient is admitted, their anti-seizure medication is deliberately reduced step by step, and continuous video-EEG runs until enough electrographic events have been captured to localize the seizure onset zone. Every hour of that stay is contested: the payer funds a finite number of monitored days, the unit owns a finite number of beds, a technologist can only watch so many concurrent acquisitions, and the recording itself consumes storage at a rate that scales with channel count and modality. The clinical question — *stay longer to capture one more event, or discharge now* — is therefore never purely clinical. It is a negotiation between capture yield, medication-reduction risk, payer authorization and disk headroom.

This document specifies the Neurodiagnostic EMU API, the system of record for that negotiation. Mounted under `/neurodiagnostics`, it governs unit and bed inventory, technologist coverage windows, payer authorization requests and their day ledger, admissions and their medication-reduction protocols, video-EEG acquisition in resumable segments, seizure-detection alerting with nurse bedside response timeliness, spike and seizure marking, interpretation turnaround against a reading-window service level, data residency and export packaging for surgical evaluation, retention sweeps, and the multidisciplinary surgical candidacy conference. Five operator populations share the workflow — the coordinator who runs the unit, the technologist who acquires, the nurse who responds at the bedside, the epileptologist who interprets and decides, and the external payer reviewer who funds — and the system enforces a segregation of duties between them rather than trusting convention.

Two engineering constraints shape everything that follows. First, the server keeps a **logical clock** rather than reading wall time: every deadline, coverage window, turnover buffer, retention expiry and elapsed-day figure is measured against that clock, which tests advance explicitly. Second, several of the system's most consequential attributes are **derived and reconciled immediately** — an admission's capture adequacy, a unit's storage pressure and gating posture, an authorization's day ledger — so a single adjudication or export build can ripple across four entities in one request. Implementations must treat those ripples as part of the contract, not as eventual housekeeping.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Operators, Headers and Role Authority

Every request, without exception, carries two headers: `X-User-ID` identifying the acting operator and `X-User-Role` declaring the capacity in which they act. If either header is absent, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the five declared roles, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). The five roles, spelled exactly as the system recognizes them, are `UnitCoordinator`, `Technologist`, `Nurse`, `Epileptologist` and `PayerReviewer`. Once authentication succeeds, authority is checked against the operation being attempted: a role that is not listed among the authorized roles for the target endpoint in §4 is rejected with `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403).

No role in this system is backed by a persisted entity, and that is a deliberate design decision worth stating field by field. When `X-User-Role` is `UnitCoordinator`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server may record it in audit narrative but never compares it to any entity's `id`. The same holds for `Technologist`: `X-User-ID` is opaque, and although a coverage window carries a `technologist_user_id` supplied at roster time, that value is an opaque operator string too and is never compared to any entity's `id`. When `X-User-Role` is `Nurse`, `X-User-ID` is opaque and is recorded as `nurse_user_id` on the safety intervention the nurse documents, again without any identity lookup. When `X-User-Role` is `Epileptologist`, `X-User-ID` is opaque and is recorded in four places — `prelim_author_user_id` and `final_author_user_id` on a review report, `presenter_user_id` on a surgical conference case, and `voter_user_id` on a conference ballot — and in each of those places it is compared only against *another recorded operator string*, never against an entity `id`. When `X-User-Role` is `PayerReviewer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque external-reviewer identifier that is not persisted on any entity and is not compared to anything.

Two segregation-of-duties rules are built on those recorded operator strings, and both are enforced by string comparison of `X-User-ID`. The epileptologist who issued a study's preliminary interpretation may not issue its final interpretation; an attempt is rejected with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). And the epileptologist who presented a surgical conference case may still cast a ballot, but that ballot is recorded with `counted` set to false and is excluded from every tally.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every list endpoint accepts two optional query parameters. `limit` caps the number of records returned; it defaults to 10 when omitted, may not be smaller than 1, and may not be larger than 100. `offset` is the number of records to skip before the page begins; it defaults to 0 and has no upper bound beyond the size of the collection.

Unless a specific endpoint states otherwise, list results are ordered by their creation timestamp in ascending order, oldest first. The response envelope's `meta` object for a list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more`, where `total_count` is the size of the full filtered collection and `has_more` indicates whether records remain beyond the current page.

### 2.4. The Logical Clock

The server does not read wall-clock time. It maintains a logical UTC clock whose value at process startup is exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and which must never fall back to the host's real time. Every computed instant in the system — admission timestamps, segment boundaries, alert detection, adjudication, report issuance, package build and delivery, retention expiry — is read from that clock, and every window test (technologist coverage, bed turnover, reading-window overlap, retention eligibility) is evaluated against it.

The clock is advanced through the administrative test-driving endpoint `POST /_clock/set_current_utc`, which accepts a single field `to_iso8601` carrying the target instant and responds with `{ "current_utc": "..." }`. All five roles may drive the clock. The clock is monotonic: a request whose `to_iso8601` is earlier than the current logical value is rejected with `ERR_CLOCK_MONOTONIC` (HTTP 422). Setting the clock to its current value is not a backwards move.

### 2.5. Common Record Properties

Records in this system carry a common preamble. `id` is a server-minted UUID with no type prefix and no client control over its value. `created_at` is an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits recording when the record was first persisted. `updated_at` is an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp in the same format, refreshed on every mutation of the record — including mutations that only touch derived fields such as a unit's `storage_pressure_pct` or an admission's `capture_adequacy_index`. `version` is an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation of that record, including server-initiated cascade mutations that no client requested directly.

Two record types are append-only evidence rather than mutable state, and they carry a deliberately reduced preamble: a **SafetyIntervention** and a **ConferenceVote** each expose only `id` and `created_at`. Both `updated_at` and `version` are absent from those two record types entirely — they are never updated after creation, so there is nothing for those fields to describe. Every other record type in the system (monitoring units, beds, technologist shifts, patients, authorization requests, admissions, taper plans, studies, seizure alerts, event markings, review reports, export packages and surgical conference cases) carries the full set of four.

### 2.6. Rejection Precedence: The Validation Ladder

When a single request violates more than one rule, exactly one error is returned, and which one is not a matter of implementation convenience. Checks are evaluated in the following fixed order, and the first violation encountered ends the request. Nothing later in the ladder may mask something earlier in it.

1. **Authentication.** Both headers present and `X-User-Role` a declared role, otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (401). Applies to every endpoint.
2. **Authorization.** The acting role appears in the target endpoint's authorized-role list, otherwise `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (403). Applies to every endpoint.
3. **Request shape.** The body and query string parse, required fields are present, every enum value is a member of its declared set, and every number respects its declared range, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400). Applies to every endpoint.
4. **Path identifiers.** Every identifier embedded in the URL path resolves to an existing record, validated left to right, otherwise the 404 matching the first unresolvable segment: `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_BED_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SHIFT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PATIENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ADMISSION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TAPER_PLAN_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STUDY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ALERT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MARKING_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REPORT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EXPORT_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_CASE_NOT_FOUND`. Applies to every endpoint carrying a path identifier.
5. **Body foreign keys.** Every foreign key supplied in the request body resolves, validated in the order the endpoint declares its input fields, otherwise the corresponding 404 from the set `ERR_PATIENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_BED_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SHIFT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ADMISSION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STUDY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ALERT_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_EXPORT_NOT_FOUND`. Applies to every creating endpoint that takes foreign keys in the body.
6. **Declared transition.** The target record is in a state from which the requested transition is declared, otherwise the state error belonging to that record type: `ERR_INVALID_AUTHORIZATION_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_ADMISSION_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_TAPER_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_STUDY_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_DATA_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_ALERT_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_MARKING_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_REPORT_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_EXPORT_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_CASE_STATE` or `ERR_INVALID_SHIFT_STATE`, each HTTP 409. Applies to every state-transition endpoint.
7. **Optimistic concurrency.** Where a version expectation is required, it matches, otherwise `ERR_STALE_ADMISSION_VERSION` or `ERR_STALE_STUDY_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/extension-decisions` and `POST /neurodiagnostics/exports` only. See §2.7.
8. **Reporting segregation of duties.** The `X-User-ID` issuing a final interpretation differs from the report's `prelim_author_user_id`, otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (403). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/reports/{id}/issue-final`.
9. **Taper safety interlock.** No seizure alert on any study of the admission stands in `CONFIRMED_CLINICAL`, otherwise `ERR_TAPER_BLOCKED_BY_OPEN_EVENT` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/taper-plans/{id}/advance-step`.
10. **Documented bedside response.** A clinical adjudication, and the closing of an alert that stands in `CONFIRMED_CLINICAL`, require at least one safety intervention on that alert, otherwise `ERR_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/alerts/{id}/adjudicate` and `POST /neurodiagnostics/alerts/{id}/close`.
11. **Technologist coverage.** The named coverage window is in state `ACTIVE` and the logical clock lies inside its half-open window, otherwise `ERR_NO_TECH_COVERAGE` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/studies/{id}/start` and `POST /neurodiagnostics/studies/{id}/resume`.
12. **Concurrent-acquisition capacity.** The coverage window's `assigned_study_count` is strictly below its `max_concurrent_studies`, otherwise `ERR_TECH_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/studies/{id}/start`.
13. **Setting agreement.** When the funding authorization stands in `APPROVED`, the admission's `setting` equals the authorization's `authorized_setting` at admit time; and a study's `modality` equals its admission's `setting` at start time. Otherwise `ERR_SETTING_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422). An authorization that is not `APPROVED` has a null `authorized_setting`, so this rule stays silent for it and rung 19 catches the request instead. Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/admit` and `POST /neurodiagnostics/studies/{id}/start`.
14. **Bed availability.** The requested bed is available — it has no `current_admission_id`, and either its `released_at` is null or the logical clock has reached its `turnover_clear_at` — otherwise `ERR_BED_OCCUPANCY_CONFLICT` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/admit`.
15. **Bed presence rules.** An `INPATIENT_VEEG` admission supplies a bed, otherwise `ERR_BED_REQUIRED_FOR_INPATIENT` (422); an `AMBULATORY_VEEG` admission supplies none, otherwise `ERR_BED_NOT_ALLOWED_FOR_AMBULATORY` (422). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/admit`.
16. **Final interpretation prerequisite.** The study has a review report in `FINAL_ISSUED` before an export package may be created against it, otherwise `ERR_REPORT_NOT_FINAL` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/exports`.
17. **Marking closure prerequisite.** No event marking of the study is still `PROPOSED` when the final interpretation is issued, otherwise `ERR_MARKING_NOT_ADJUDICATED` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/reports/{id}/issue-final`.
18. **Delivery prerequisite.** The referenced export package stands in `DELIVERED` before a conference case may be presented, otherwise `ERR_EXPORT_NOT_DELIVERED` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/conference-cases/{id}/present`.
19. **Approved funding.** The funding authorization stands in `APPROVED` before an admission may be admitted against it, otherwise `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_NOT_APPROVED` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/admit`.
20. **Sufficient authorized days.** The authorization's `days_available` is at least the number of days being reserved, otherwise `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_DAYS_EXHAUSTED` (HTTP 402). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/admit`.
21. **Storage lock.** The unit's `posture` is not `STORAGE_LOCKED`, otherwise `ERR_UNIT_STORAGE_LOCKED` (409). Applies to four operations: `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/admit`, `POST /neurodiagnostics/studies/{id}/start`, `POST /neurodiagnostics/studies/{id}/resume` and `POST /neurodiagnostics/exports/{id}/build`.
22. **Quota headroom.** The unit's `storage_used_gb` plus the package volume about to be allocated does not exceed the unit's `storage_quota_gb`, otherwise `ERR_STORAGE_QUOTA_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/exports/{id}/build`.
23. **Round caps.** An authorization's `resubmission_count` is below 2 when resubmitting, otherwise `ERR_RESUBMISSION_LIMIT`; a report's `addendum_count` is below 2 when opening an addendum, otherwise `ERR_ADDENDUM_LIMIT`; a case's `defer_round` is below 2 when re-presenting, otherwise `ERR_DEFER_LIMIT`. All three are HTTP 409, and they apply respectively to `POST /neurodiagnostics/authorizations/{id}/submit`, `POST /neurodiagnostics/reports/{id}/open-addendum` and `POST /neurodiagnostics/conference-cases/{id}/present`.
24. **One ballot per round.** The voting `X-User-ID` has not already cast a ballot on this case in the current `defer_round`, otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_VOTE` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/conference-cases/{id}/votes`.
25. **Quorum.** The case's `quorum_met` is true, otherwise `ERR_QUORUM_NOT_MET` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/conference-cases/{id}/close-vote`.
26. **Yield basis.** The admission has a non-zero `weighted_event_count` and a non-zero `elapsed_monitored_days`, otherwise `ERR_NO_YIELD_BASIS` (422). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/extension-decisions`.
27. **Target not already met.** The admission has not already met its capture target when an extension is requested, otherwise `ERR_TARGET_ALREADY_MET` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/extension-decisions`.
28. **Marking inside the recording.** An event marking's `onset_at` falls inside a closed acquisition segment of its study, otherwise `ERR_MARKING_OUTSIDE_STUDY_WINDOW` (422). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/markings`.
29. **Nothing left running.** No study of the admission is in `PREPARING`, `ACQUIRING` or `PAUSED` at discharge, otherwise `ERR_OPEN_STUDY_ON_DISCHARGE` (409); and no study is still assigned to a coverage window being closed, otherwise `ERR_SHIFT_HAS_ACTIVE_STUDIES` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/discharge` and `POST /neurodiagnostics/technologist-shifts/{id}/close` respectively.
30. **Temporal sanity.** A coverage window's `ends_at` is strictly after its `starts_at`, otherwise `ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_INVALID` (422); and the logical clock never moves backwards, otherwise `ERR_CLOCK_MONOTONIC` (422). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/technologist-shifts` and `POST /_clock/set_current_utc` respectively.
31. **Decided-case protection.** An export package referenced by a conference case that has reached `CANDIDATE` or `NON_CANDIDATE` may not be revoked, otherwise `ERR_EXPORT_IN_DECIDED_CASE` (409). Applies to `POST /neurodiagnostics/exports/{id}/revoke`.

### 2.7. Optimistic Concurrency on Extension Decisions and Export Creation

Two write operations in this system act on numbers the client must have *already observed*, and both are protected by an explicit version expectation. Neither can be protected by a state precondition, because in both cases repeating the operation is legitimate product behaviour.

A request to `POST /neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/extension-decisions` must carry `expected_admission_version` — a non-optional integer field holding the `version` of the target admission as the client last read it. If that value does not equal the admission's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_ADMISSION_VERSION` (HTTP 412). An admission may legitimately be extended more than once, so no state check can substitute for this guard; its purpose is to force the deciding epileptologist to prove that the capture and risk figures they reasoned over are still current, because any alert adjudication, marking retraction or prior extension has already recomputed `capture_adequacy_index`, `projected_daily_yield` and the authorization day ledger.

A request to `POST /neurodiagnostics/exports` must carry `expected_study_version` — a non-optional integer field holding the `version` of the referenced study as the client last read it. If that value does not equal the study's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_STUDY_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Multiple packages per study are legitimate, since a later package may supersede an earlier one, so again no state check covers this; the guard forces the requester to have observed the study's current `stored_gb` and `data_state`, which together determine the package volume and the quota check that follows at build time.

No other endpoint in this system takes a version field of any kind. Where a request does not appear in the two rules above, the server performs the mutation without any concurrency check.

### 2.8. Numeric Precision, Rounding and Truncation Discipline

Three distinct numeric conventions coexist, and mixing them produces wrong answers that no state machine will catch.

Storage volumes and percentages — a study's `stored_gb`, a package's `package_gb`, a unit's `storage_used_gb` and `storage_pressure_pct`, and a unit's declared `storage_quota_gb` — carry exactly 2 decimal places and are rounded half up. Interpretation turnaround figures, `turnaround_calendar_hours` and `turnaround_coverage_hours`, likewise carry 2 decimal places rounded half up. Capture figures — `weight`, `weighted_event_count`, `capture_adequacy_index`, `capture_adequacy_index_prospective`, `projected_daily_yield`, `deficit_weighted` and `elapsed_monitored_days` — carry exactly 3 decimal places and are rounded half up.

Where the specification says a quantity is measured in whole minutes, the fractional part is discarded rather than rounded: segment durations contributing to `acquired_minutes`, and an alert's `latency_minutes`, are both truncated down to the whole minute. Day counts in the authorization ledger are integers, and the elapsed days spent at discharge are rounded *up* to the next whole day. In the length-of-stay computation, the days implied by the remaining deficit are rounded up and the days permitted by the risk budget are rounded down; the intermediate daily risk figure carries 1 decimal place. Vote tallies and quorum figures are plain integer counts.

One rounding rule deserves separate emphasis because the order of operations is observable in the result: a study's stored volume is rounded per closed segment **and again after each accumulation**, never accumulated raw and rounded once at the end. §3.5 states the procedure step by step.

### 2.9. Parallel State Dimensions, Cascades and Compensations

Three record types in this system carry two state fields that advance **independently of one another**, and neither dimension may be inferred from the other. An admission carries `clinical_state` (the bedside lifecycle: `SCHEDULED`, `ADMITTED`, `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET`, `DISCHARGED`, beginning at `SCHEDULED`) alongside `authorization_state` (the payer-day posture: `AUTH_PENDING`, `AUTH_ACTIVE`, `AUTH_EXHAUSTED`, `AUTH_CLOSED`, beginning at `AUTH_PENDING`); a fully settled stay is `DISCHARGED` on the first dimension and `AUTH_CLOSED` on the second. A study carries `acquisition_state` (`PREPARING`, `ACQUIRING`, `PAUSED`, `COMPLETED`, beginning at `PREPARING`) alongside `data_state` (`ONLINE`, `EXPORT_HELD`, `ARCHIVED`, `PURGED`, beginning at `ONLINE`), and one data-residency transition is explicitly cross-guarded: archiving requires the acquisition dimension to have reached `COMPLETED`. A seizure alert carries `triage_state` (`DETECTED`, `CONFIRMED_CLINICAL`, `CONFIRMED_SUBCLINICAL`, `REJECTED_ARTIFACT`, `CLOSED`, beginning at `DETECTED`) alongside `response_state` (`AWAITING_RESPONSE`, `RESPONDED_ON_TIME`, `RESPONDED_LATE`, beginning at `AWAITING_RESPONSE`), the triage dimension forking on adjudication and rejoining at `CLOSED`.

Server-initiated transitions are first-class. When an operation on one record satisfies or violates the criteria for another record's state, the server transitions that other record in the same request, without waiting for a client call against it. Each such reconciliation is a real mutation of the affected record: it refreshes that record's `updated_at` and increments its `version` by 1, exactly as a client-driven change would. This is what makes the cascades in §3 observable — an epileptologist adjudicating an alert may, in a single request, move event markings, recompute five admission figures, move the admission's clinical dimension and complete a taper plan, bumping four records' versions.

Cascades run in both directions. Where a forward cascade grants something (capture credit banked, target met, taper completed, storage allocated, packages blocked), a corresponding compensating cascade withdraws it exactly (marking retracted, target un-met, taper returned to active, storage released, packages returned to the queue). A compensation restores the prior value precisely rather than approximately: revoking an export package subtracts back the same `package_gb` it added.

### 2.10. Path Resolution Order for Nested Reads

Several read and write paths address a child through its parent — for example `/neurodiagnostics/units/{id}/beds`, `/neurodiagnostics/patients/{id}/authorizations`, `/neurodiagnostics/admissions/{id}/taper-plans`, `/neurodiagnostics/studies/{id}/alerts`, `/neurodiagnostics/studies/{id}/reports` and `/neurodiagnostics/conference-cases/{id}/votes`. For any such path, identifiers are resolved strictly left to right, and the request fails on the *first* segment that does not resolve, carrying that segment's own 404 error. A request for the alerts of a study that does not exist therefore returns `ERR_STUDY_NOT_FOUND`, never an empty list and never an alert-level error. The same precedence applies to writes: the parent's existence is settled before any child-level or business-level rule is evaluated.

### 2.11. Storage Pressure and the Unit Gating Posture

A monitoring unit is not merely an inventory record; it is the gate through which acquisitions and exports must pass. Its `storage_used_gb` is recomputed, as a figure rounded half up to 2 decimal places, as the sum of `stored_gb` over every study of the unit whose `data_state` is `ONLINE` or `EXPORT_HELD`, plus the sum of `package_gb` over every export package on those same studies whose `status` is `BUILT` or `DELIVERED`. Studies whose data has moved to `ARCHIVED` or `PURGED` are excluded, and packages in `QUEUED`, `BLOCKED`, `REVOKED` or `PURGED` are excluded. From that figure, `storage_pressure_pct` is the used volume divided by the unit's `storage_quota_gb`, multiplied by 100, rounded half up to 2 decimal places.

The unit's `posture` is then derived, and the precedence between its two constraints is pinned: **storage lock outranks coverage constraint.** If `storage_pressure_pct` has reached 90.00 percent or more, the posture is `STORAGE_LOCKED`. Otherwise, if the unit's `active_study_count` is greater than zero and is greater than or equal to its `coverage_capacity`, the posture is `COVERAGE_CONSTRAINED`. Otherwise the posture is `OPEN`, which is also the value a newly created unit carries. Here `active_study_count` is the number of studies of the unit whose `acquisition_state` is `ACQUIRING`, and `coverage_capacity` is the sum of `max_concurrent_studies` over the unit's coverage windows whose `state` is `ACTIVE` and whose half-open window — inclusive of `starts_at`, exclusive of `ends_at` — contains the current logical clock.

This derivation is not a nightly job. It is re-evaluated on every study segment close, every study archive, every retention purge, every export build, revoke and purge, every coverage-window activation and closure, and every study start, pause, resume and completion. A unit also maintains `beds_available`, the count of its beds whose computed `status` is `AVAILABLE`, `late_response_count`, the number of alerts on its studies whose `response_state` is `RESPONDED_LATE`, and `retention_sweep_index`, which counts completed retention sweeps and increases by 1 with each one.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up the Unit: Beds and Technologist Coverage

A coordinator brings a monitoring unit into existence by naming it and declaring its capacity. The creation payload carries a `name` for display, a `bed_count` of at least 1 recording the unit's declared inpatient bed count, and a `storage_quota_gb` — a positive volume carried to 2 decimal places — against which all storage pressure is measured. Note that `bed_count` is an informational declaration only; it is the counterpart to, and is not reconciled against, the bed records actually created. A newly created unit starts with `storage_used_gb` at 0, `storage_pressure_pct` at 0, `posture` at `OPEN` and `retention_sweep_index` at 0.

Beds are then created one at a time, each carrying the `unit_id` of the owning unit and a `label` that is human-readable and unique within that unit. Creating a bed re-derives the owning unit's `beds_available`. A bed's `status` is never set by a client — it is computed. A bed reads `OCCUPIED` while an admission whose `clinical_state` is `ADMITTED` or `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET` holds it, and while that is true the bed's `current_admission_id` points back at that admission. A bed reads `TURNOVER` when its `released_at` is set and the logical clock has not yet reached its `turnover_clear_at`. A bed reads `AVAILABLE` when no admission occupies it and either `released_at` is still null — meaning the bed has never been occupied — or the clock has reached `turnover_clear_at`. The turnover buffer itself is a fixed hour and a half: `turnover_clear_at` is 90 minutes after `released_at`, and it is null whenever `released_at` is null.

Technologist coverage is rostered as windows. Each window is created against a `unit_id` with a `technologist_user_id` naming the rostered operator, a `starts_at` that is the inclusive beginning of coverage, an `ends_at` that is the exclusive end, and a `max_concurrent_studies` of at least 1 declaring how many studies may sit against this window in `ACQUIRING` or `PAUSED` at once. A window whose `ends_at` is not strictly after its `starts_at` is refused with `ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_INVALID`. A new window opens in `SCHEDULED` with `assigned_study_count` at 0.

Coverage is switched on and off by the coordinator. Activating a window moves `state` from `SCHEDULED` to `ACTIVE`, and because the unit's `coverage_capacity` sums the capacities of active windows containing the clock, activation immediately re-derives both `coverage_capacity` and `posture`. Closing a window moves `state` from `ACTIVE` to `CLOSED` and likewise re-derives `coverage_capacity` and `posture` — but only if the window carries nothing: a window whose `assigned_study_count` is greater than zero is refused with `ERR_SHIFT_HAS_ACTIVE_STUDIES`. Activating a window that is not `SCHEDULED`, closing one that is not `ACTIVE`, or naming a `CLOSED` window when starting acquisition, are all rejected with `ERR_INVALID_SHIFT_STATE`. `CLOSED` is terminal; there is no reopening.

### 3.2. Payer Authorization and the Monitored-Day Ledger

Patients are registered by the coordinator with an `mrn` that is unique across all patients, a `display_name`, and a `risk_tier` of `LOW`, `MODERATE` or `HIGH`. That tier is not decoration: it sets the base term in both the nurse-response deadline and the length-of-stay risk budget described later.

Funding is negotiated before anyone is admitted. The coordinator drafts an authorization request against a `patient_id`, declaring the `requested_setting` the unit is asking the payer to cover — either `INPATIENT_VEEG` or `AMBULATORY_VEEG` — and a `requested_days` of at least 1. The request opens in `DRAFT` with its whole day ledger at zero: `authorized_days`, `days_reserved`, `days_consumed` and `days_available` all 0, `resubmission_count` 0, and `payer_note` null.

Submission moves the request from `DRAFT` to `SUBMITTED` and, on that first submission, explicitly clears `payer_note` back to null. The payer reviewer then records a decision carrying an `outcome` of `APPROVE`, `PEND` or `DENY`, optionally with `payer_note` free text. A `PEND` outcome moves `SUBMITTED` to `PENDED` and stores the supplied `payer_note`, returning the request to the unit for one more attempt; resubmitting a `PENDED` request moves it back to `SUBMITTED` and increments `resubmission_count` by 1, and a resubmission attempted when `resubmission_count` has already reached 2 is refused with `ERR_RESUBMISSION_LIMIT`. An `APPROVE` outcome requires the decision payload to carry both an `authorized_days` of at least 1 and an `authorized_setting`; either one missing is an `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. On approval, the request moves to `APPROVED`, stores `authorized_setting` and `authorized_days` from the payload, stores `payer_note`, and initializes the ledger with `days_available` equal to `authorized_days`, `days_reserved` at 0 and `days_consumed` at 0. A `DENY` outcome moves the request to `DENIED`, stores `payer_note`, and leaves `authorized_days` at 0. `APPROVED` and `DENIED` are both terminal. Submitting anything that is neither `DRAFT` nor `PENDED`, or recording a payer decision against anything that is not `SUBMITTED`, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_AUTHORIZATION_STATE`. Every one of these transitions increments the authorization request's own `version` by 1.

The four ledger figures obey a conserved equality that must hold after **every** mutation, without exception: `authorized_days` equals `days_consumed` plus `days_reserved` plus `days_available`. Three events move days between those buckets. Admitting a patient adds that admission's `planned_days` to `days_reserved` and subtracts the same number from `days_available`. A granted length-of-stay extension adds the granted days to `days_reserved` and subtracts them from `days_available`. Discharge settles: the days actually spent are the lesser of `days_reserved` and the elapsed monitored days rounded up to the next whole day; that spent figure is added to `days_consumed`, the remainder of `days_reserved` beyond the spent figure returns to `days_available`, and `days_reserved` is then set to 0. Only an authorization in `APPROVED` can fund a stay at all — requests in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `PENDED` or `DENIED` carry `authorized_days` of 0 and cannot fund anything. A `PayerReviewer` may read the patient record, that patient's authorization requests, the authorization itself and the admissions that justify it, but holds no other write authority anywhere in the system.

### 3.3. Admission, Bed Occupancy and Discharge Settlement

Scheduling an admission is a bookkeeping act, not a clinical one. The coordinator supplies the `patient_id` of the monitored patient, the `authorization_id` of the authorization funding the stay, the `unit_id` of the unit doing the monitoring, the `setting` being requested (`INPATIENT_VEEG` or `AMBULATORY_VEEG`), a `scheduled_admit_at` planned instant, a `target_event_count` of at least 1 stating how many weighted events the epileptologist wants captured before discharge, and a `planned_days` of at least 1 to be reserved against the authorization. The new record opens with `clinical_state` at `SCHEDULED` and `authorization_state` at `AUTH_PENDING`, and — this matters — **no authorization days are reserved at scheduling time**. Its `admitted_at`, `discharged_at`, `planned_discharge_at`, `bed_id`, `last_extension_outcome` and `disposition` are all null, and its capture figures, `extension_count` and `last_extension_granted_days` are all 0.

Admitting the patient is where the constraints bite, and the order in which they bite is fixed. The admit payload carries an optional `bed_id`. The server checks, in this sequence: that `clinical_state` is `SCHEDULED`, rejecting otherwise with `ERR_INVALID_ADMISSION_STATE`; that when the authorization stands in `APPROVED` the admission's `setting` equals its `authorized_setting`, rejecting otherwise with `ERR_SETTING_MISMATCH`; that a supplied bed for an inpatient stay is available in the sense of §2.11 and §3.1, rejecting otherwise with `ERR_BED_OCCUPANCY_CONFLICT`; that an `INPATIENT_VEEG` stay supplied a `bed_id` at all and an `AMBULATORY_VEEG` stay supplied none, rejecting otherwise with `ERR_BED_REQUIRED_FOR_INPATIENT` or `ERR_BED_NOT_ALLOWED_FOR_AMBULATORY` respectively; that the authorization stands in `APPROVED`, rejecting otherwise with `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_NOT_APPROVED`; that the authorization's `days_available` is at least `planned_days`, rejecting otherwise with `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_DAYS_EXHAUSTED`; and finally that the unit's `posture` is not `STORAGE_LOCKED`, rejecting otherwise with `ERR_UNIT_STORAGE_LOCKED`. Note deliberately that the setting-agreement check precedes the approved-funding check, so an approved authorization for the wrong setting surfaces as a setting mismatch, while an unapproved authorization surfaces as an approval failure.

On success, admission does six things at once. It stamps `admitted_at` from the logical clock. For an inpatient stay it stores the supplied bed on `bed_id`, whereupon that bed reads `OCCUPIED` and its `current_admission_id` points back at this admission. It sets `planned_discharge_at` to 24 hours per planned day after `admitted_at` — that is, `admitted_at` plus `planned_days` multiplied by a full day. It moves `authorization_state` from `AUTH_PENDING` to `AUTH_ACTIVE`. It reserves the days on the funding ledger, adding `planned_days` to `days_reserved` and subtracting the same from `days_available` so the conserved equality still holds. And it increments the admission's `version` by 1. From then on, `elapsed_monitored_days` reads the days between `admitted_at` and the current logical clock, to 3 decimal places, and reads 0 for a stay still `SCHEDULED`.

Discharge is the coordinator's closing act and carries a `disposition` of `ROUTINE`, `SURGICAL_EVALUATION` or `TERMINATED_EARLY`. It is permitted from `ADMITTED` and equally from `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET`, and the two paths perform identical settlement — there is no reduced treatment for the target-met path. In both cases the server first refuses if any study of the admission is still in `PREPARING`, `ACQUIRING` or `PAUSED`, with `ERR_OPEN_STUDY_ON_DISCHARGE`; discharging from any other clinical state is `ERR_INVALID_ADMISSION_STATE`. On success it stamps `discharged_at` from the clock and freezes `elapsed_monitored_days` at that instant, so the figure stops moving thereafter. It stores the supplied `disposition`. It settles the day ledger exactly as §3.2 describes, spending the lesser of `days_reserved` and the elapsed monitored days rounded up to a whole day, returning the remainder to `days_available` and zeroing `days_reserved`. It moves `authorization_state` to `AUTH_CLOSED`, whether that dimension was standing at `AUTH_ACTIVE` or had already fallen to `AUTH_EXHAUSTED`. For an inpatient stay it releases the bed: the bed's `current_admission_id` is cleared, its `released_at` is set to `discharged_at`, its `turnover_clear_at` is set to 90 minutes after that, its `status` therefore reads `TURNOVER`, and the admission's own `bed_id` is cleared. Every taper plan of the admission still in `ACTIVE` or `HELD` is driven to `ABORTED`. And the admission's `version` increments by 1.

One authorization-dimension transition happens without any client acting on the admission at all. When an extension decision leaves the funding authorization's `days_available` at zero, or when the decision's outcome is a denial for want of authorized days, the server moves `authorization_state` from `AUTH_ACTIVE` to `AUTH_EXHAUSTED`, recomputes `capture_adequacy_index_prospective` — whose inputs include `days_available` — and increments the admission's `version` by 1. A stay may sit in `AUTH_EXHAUSTED` while remaining clinically `ADMITTED`; the two dimensions do not constrain each other, and discharge closes the funding dimension from either value.

Admissions can be listed with filters on `patient_id`, `authorization_id`, `unit_id`, `bed_id`, `clinical_state` and `authorization_state`, and a dedicated computed read exposes the two capture-adequacy variants side by side for the length-of-stay conversation, returning `adequacy_retrospective`, `adequacy_prospective`, `weighted_event_count`, `projected_daily_yield`, `deficit_weighted` and `elapsed_monitored_days` in one object.

### 3.4. The Medication-Reduction Protocol

Provoking events is the point of the stay, and the instrument is a staged medication reduction. An epileptologist creates a taper plan against an `admission_id` with a `total_steps` count of at least 1. The plan opens in `ACTIVE` with `current_step` at 0 and `hold_reason` null. An admission may carry more than one plan over its life, and the plans of an admission are listable.

Advancing a step is the plan's self-loop: the plan stays `ACTIVE` and `current_step` increases by 1. Three conditions guard the advance. The plan must be `ACTIVE` — advancing a plan that is not, holding a plan that is not `ACTIVE`, or resuming a plan that is not `HELD`, are all `ERR_INVALID_TAPER_STATE`. The admission's `clinical_state` must be `ADMITTED`, and not merely non-terminal: advancing against a `SCHEDULED`, `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET` or `DISCHARGED` admission is `ERR_INVALID_ADMISSION_STATE`. And no seizure alert on any study of that admission may be standing in `CONFIRMED_CLINICAL` — a confirmed clinical event that has been adjudicated but not yet closed is an open safety question, and advancing the taper while one stands is refused with `ERR_TAPER_BLOCKED_BY_OPEN_EVENT`. Because an `ACTIVE` plan always has at least one step left by construction — reaching `total_steps` moves the plan out of `ACTIVE` — the advance never needs to guard against overshoot: `current_step` is capped at `total_steps`, and the step at which `current_step` reaches `total_steps` moves the plan to `COMPLETED` in the same request. Each advance also raises the risk terms used by later evaluations, tightening subsequent alert-response deadlines and shrinking the risk budget available to later extension decisions, and increments the plan's `version` by 1.

Holding is the safety valve. A hold moves an `ACTIVE` plan to `HELD` and stores the supplied `hold_reason`; resuming moves a `HELD` plan back to `ACTIVE`, and like the advance it requires the admission's `clinical_state` to be `ADMITTED`, failing with `ERR_INVALID_ADMISSION_STATE` otherwise. A resume does not clear `hold_reason` — that field remains populated as the record of why the plan was ever held, and is null only for a plan that has never been held. Both transitions increment the plan's `version`.

The remaining taper transitions belong to the server, not to any client. A plan in `ACTIVE` moves to `COMPLETED` when its `current_step` reaches `total_steps`, and a plan in either `ACTIVE` or `HELD` moves to `COMPLETED` when the admission reaches `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET` — the target having been met, there is nothing left to provoke. Reaching `COMPLETED` freezes `current_step` where it stands. The compensating path runs the other way: if a marking retraction later drags the admission's capture index back below its target and the admission returns from `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET` to `ADMITTED`, every plan of that admission standing in `COMPLETED` with a `current_step` still short of `total_steps` returns to `ACTIVE`, ready to resume reducing. A plan that had legitimately finished its steps stays `COMPLETED`. Finally, discharge drives every plan still in `ACTIVE` or `HELD` to `ABORTED`, which is terminal. All of these server-driven moves increment the plan's `version` by 1 apiece.

### 3.5. Video-EEG Acquisition, Segments and Electrode Maintenance

A technologist creates a study against an `admission_id`, declaring a `channel_count` of at least 8 and a `modality` of `INPATIENT_VEEG` or `AMBULATORY_VEEG`. The study opens with `acquisition_state` at `PREPARING` and `data_state` at `ONLINE`, with `acquired_minutes`, `stored_gb`, `closed_segment_count` and `impedance_fail_count` at 0 and `shift_id`, `current_segment_started_at`, `completed_at`, `last_impedance_kohm` and `export_hold_reason` all null.

Beginning acquisition moves `acquisition_state` from `PREPARING` to `ACQUIRING`, and the request names the covering window through a `shift_id` in its payload. Six conditions must hold, and each has its own rejection: the study must be `PREPARING` (`ERR_INVALID_STUDY_STATE`); the admission's `clinical_state` must be `ADMITTED` (`ERR_INVALID_ADMISSION_STATE`); the study's `modality` must equal the admission's `setting` (`ERR_SETTING_MISMATCH`); the named window's `state` must be `ACTIVE` with the logical clock inside its window, at or after `starts_at` and strictly before `ends_at` (`ERR_NO_TECH_COVERAGE`); that window's `assigned_study_count` must be strictly less than its `max_concurrent_studies` (`ERR_TECH_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`); and the unit's `posture` must not be `STORAGE_LOCKED` (`ERR_UNIT_STORAGE_LOCKED`). On success the study pins the named window on its own `shift_id` and keeps it for the rest of its life, opens its first segment by stamping `current_segment_started_at` from the clock, increments the window's `assigned_study_count`, increments the unit's `active_study_count` and re-derives the unit's `posture` — which may now read `COVERAGE_CONSTRAINED` — and increments the study's `version` by 1.

Pausing closes the open segment. The study must be `ACQUIRING`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STUDY_STATE`. The pause payload carries a mandatory `reason` and two optional maintenance fields, `maintenance_kind` and `impedance_kohm`. On success, the elapsed time from `current_segment_started_at` to now, truncated down to whole minutes, is added to `acquired_minutes`; `closed_segment_count` increases by 1; `current_segment_started_at` is cleared to null; `stored_gb` is recomputed by the procedure below; the unit's `storage_used_gb`, `storage_pressure_pct` and `posture` are re-derived; the unit's `active_study_count` is decremented; and the study's `version` increments by 1. When the pause carries a `maintenance_kind` of `ELECTRODE_REGEL`, the accompanying `impedance_kohm` is recorded on the study's `last_impedance_kohm`, and if that reading exceeds the electrode limit of 10.00 kilohms, `impedance_fail_count` increases by 1. A study that has never had an impedance recorded carries a null `last_impedance_kohm`.

Resuming re-opens acquisition. The study must be `PAUSED`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STUDY_STATE`; the pinned coverage window must again be `ACTIVE` with the clock inside its half-open window, otherwise `ERR_NO_TECH_COVERAGE`; and the unit must not be `STORAGE_LOCKED`, otherwise `ERR_UNIT_STORAGE_LOCKED`. A resume opens a new segment by stamping `current_segment_started_at` from the clock, increments the unit's `active_study_count`, re-derives the unit's `posture`, and increments the study's `version` by 1. Note the asymmetry with the start: a resume does not re-check concurrent capacity, and it does not re-pin `shift_id`.

Completion ends the recording, and its two entry paths differ in a way the implementer must respect. Completing from `ACQUIRING` closes the currently open segment and recomputes `acquired_minutes`, `closed_segment_count` and `stored_gb`, stamps `completed_at` from the clock, decrements both the window's `assigned_study_count` and the unit's `active_study_count` and re-derives the unit's `posture`, re-derives the unit's `storage_used_gb` and `storage_pressure_pct`, creates exactly one review report for the study in `PRELIM_DRAFT`, and increments the study's `version` by 1. Completing from `PAUSED` has no open segment to close, so `acquired_minutes` and `stored_gb` are left exactly as they stand; it stamps `completed_at`, decrements the window's `assigned_study_count`, creates the same single review report in `PRELIM_DRAFT`, and increments the study's `version` by 1. Completing a study that is already `COMPLETED` is `ERR_INVALID_STUDY_STATE`. The report created by completion is not returned by the completion response — the only way to discover it is to list the reports of that study.

The storage volume of a study is derived from its closed segments alone, and the order of rounding is part of the contract because it changes the answer. First compute the study's per-minute rate: 0.0004 GB per channel-minute, multiplied by the study's `channel_count`, multiplied by the modality factor, which is 2.5 for `INPATIENT_VEEG` and 1.0 for `AMBULATORY_VEEG`. Then begin a running total at zero and walk the study's closed segments in ascending order of segment start, breaking ties by ascending creation timestamp. For each segment, take its duration truncated down to whole minutes, multiply by the per-minute rate, and round that product half up to 2 decimal places to get that segment's volume; then add the segment volume to the running total and round the new running total half up to 2 decimal places before moving on. The final running total is `stored_gb`. Rounding each segment and re-rounding after each accumulation is required; summing raw segment volumes and rounding once at the end yields a different figure and is wrong. The segment currently open, if any, contributes nothing until it closes.

Studies may be listed with filters on `admission_id`, `shift_id`, `acquisition_state` and `data_state`.

### 3.6. Seizure Detection, Bedside Response and Triage Adjudication

An alert may be raised on a study by a technologist, a nurse or an epileptologist, and its payload carries only a `source` of `ALGORITHM`, `TECHNOLOGIST` or `PATIENT_PUSH_BUTTON`. On creation the server stamps `detected_at` from the logical clock, opens `triage_state` at `DETECTED` and `response_state` at `AWAITING_RESPONSE`, leaves `responded_at`, `latency_minutes` and `adjudicated_at` null, and — critically — freezes the nurse response deadline on `response_deadline_minutes` at that instant. The deadline is never recomputed afterwards, no matter how the patient's taper or the unit's posture subsequently change.

The deadline is built from four inputs that interact through a clamp. Start from the base allowance for the patient's `risk_tier`: 10 minutes for `LOW`, 6 minutes for `MODERATE`, 3 minutes for `HIGH` — the higher the clinical risk, the tighter the base. Subtract one minute for each reduction step already taken, that is, subtract the `current_step` of the admission's taper plan, since deeper medication reduction demands a faster bedside response; a taper plan in `ACTIVE`, `HELD` or `COMPLETED` supplies that step, `ABORTED` plans are ignored, and when the admission has no taper plan at all the step reads 0. Add 2 minutes when the unit's `posture` at detection time is `COVERAGE_CONSTRAINED`, and nothing otherwise. Add 1 minute when the UTC hour of `detected_at` is 22 or later, or 5 or earlier — the overnight allowance. Then clamp the sum: take the lesser of the sum and a ceiling of 15 minutes, and then take the greater of that result and a floor of 2 minutes. The clamped whole number is `response_deadline_minutes`.

A nurse documents the bedside response as a safety intervention against the alert, supplying a non-empty `interventions` list whose members are drawn from `POSITIONING`, `SUCTION`, `OXYGEN`, `SEIZURE_TESTING`, `VITALS`, `RESCUE_MEDICATION` and `PHYSICIAN_NOTIFIED`, plus two booleans: `injury_observed`, recording whether injury was seen during the event, and `rescue_med_given`, recording whether a rescue medication was administered. The server stamps `nurse_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID` and `recorded_at` from the logical clock. Interventions are append-only evidence: more than one may be documented per alert, they are listable, and they are never edited. The study must be in `ACQUIRING` or `PAUSED` for an intervention to be recorded; otherwise the request is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STUDY_STATE`.

The *first* intervention on an alert is what settles the response dimension. It stamps the alert's `responded_at` from the clock and computes `latency_minutes` as the elapsed minutes from `detected_at` to `responded_at`, truncated down. If that latency is less than or equal to `response_deadline_minutes` — equality counts as on time — `response_state` moves from `AWAITING_RESPONSE` to `RESPONDED_ON_TIME`. If the latency is strictly greater, `response_state` moves to `RESPONDED_LATE` and, additionally, the unit's `late_response_count` increases by 1. Either way the alert's `version` increments by 1. Later interventions on the same alert are recorded as evidence but do not move the response dimension or restamp the latency.

Adjudication is the epileptologist's fork on the triage dimension, and the payload carries a `classification` of `CLINICAL`, `SUBCLINICAL` or `ARTIFACT`. The alert must be standing in `DETECTED`; adjudicating one that is not, or closing one that is still `DETECTED` or already `CLOSED`, is `ERR_INVALID_ALERT_STATE`. The three branches carry deliberately different weight.

A `CLINICAL` classification moves `triage_state` to `CONFIRMED_CLINICAL` — but only if at least one safety intervention already exists on that alert, otherwise the adjudication is refused with `ERR_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED`. A clinical event that nobody attended is not an adjudicable finding. On success it stamps `adjudicated_at` from the clock and runs the capture cascade in four links: first, every event marking pointing at this alert whose `status` is `PROPOSED` moves to `ADJUDICATED`; second, that marking transition recomputes the admission's `weighted_event_count`, `capture_adequacy_index`, `capture_adequacy_index_prospective`, `projected_daily_yield` and `deficit_weighted`; third, if `capture_adequacy_index` has reached 1.000 or more, the admission's `clinical_state` moves from `ADMITTED` to `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET`; and fourth, that admission transition drives every taper plan of the admission in `ACTIVE` or `HELD` to `COMPLETED`. The alert's own `version` increments by 1, as does each record the cascade touched.

A `SUBCLINICAL` classification moves `triage_state` to `CONFIRMED_SUBCLINICAL` and stamps `adjudicated_at`. On this branch **no safety intervention is required** — an electrographic-only event has no bedside correlate to attend. Every event marking pointing at this alert whose `status` is `PROPOSED` still moves to `ADJUDICATED`, which recomputes the same five admission capture figures and may in turn drive the admission to `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET` and complete its taper plans exactly as the clinical branch does; subclinical events carry real, if smaller, capture weight. The alert's `version` increments by 1.

An `ARTIFACT` classification moves `triage_state` to `REJECTED_ARTIFACT` and stamps `adjudicated_at`. Its cascade runs the opposite way: every event marking pointing at this alert whose `status` is `PROPOSED` moves to `RETRACTED` with `retraction_reason` set to the literal `ALERT_REJECTED_AS_ARTIFACT`, and the admission's capture figures are recomputed and consequently do not increase. The alert's `version` increments by 1.

Closing an alert is the join point where all three triage branches converge on `CLOSED`. Closing from `CONFIRMED_CLINICAL` is permitted only when the response dimension has left `AWAITING_RESPONSE` — a confirmed clinical event may not be closed with the bedside response still outstanding, and the refusal is `ERR_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED`. Closing from `CONFIRMED_SUBCLINICAL` carries no bedside-response requirement at all, and closing from `REJECTED_ARTIFACT` likewise carries none, having granted no capture credit in the first place. Each close increments the alert's `version` by 1. The close from `CONFIRMED_CLINICAL` has one further consequence worth stating plainly: because the taper interlock keys on alerts standing in `CONFIRMED_CLINICAL`, closing such an alert is what unblocks taper advancement for that admission.

### 3.7. Event Marking, Capture Adequacy and the Length-of-Stay Decision

Markings are the currency of capture. Either a technologist or an epileptologist creates one against a `study_id` with an `onset_at` electrographic onset instant and an `event_class` of `CLINICAL_SEIZURE`, `SUBCLINICAL_SEIZURE`, `SPIKE_BURST` or `ARTIFACT`. Two payload fields are optional: `duration_seconds`, the event duration in seconds, which must be at least 1 and defaults to 60 when omitted; and `alert_id`, pointing at the alert this marking substantiates, which is null for a review-time marking that no alert produced. The `onset_at` must fall inside a closed acquisition segment of the study, otherwise the marking is refused with `ERR_MARKING_OUTSIDE_STUDY_WINDOW`.

At creation, two derived values are frozen onto the marking. `taper_step_at_onset` captures the `current_step` of the admission's taper plan at that moment, reading 0 when the admission has no taper plan. And `weight` is computed as the class weight of the `event_class` — 1.0 for `CLINICAL_SEIZURE`, 0.5 for `SUBCLINICAL_SEIZURE`, and 0 for both `SPIKE_BURST` and `ARTIFACT` — multiplied by a provoked-event discount of 0.75 when `taper_step_at_onset` is 2 or greater, or by 1.0 when it is below 2, with the product rounded half up to 3 decimal places. The reasoning is clinical: an event provoked by two or more reduction steps is worth less as evidence of the patient's habitual seizures than one that arose earlier. Spike bursts and artifacts weigh nothing at any taper depth. A new marking opens with `status` at `PROPOSED`, `adjudicated_at` and `retraction_reason` null, and it accrues no capture credit yet.

Adjudicating a marking moves `status` from `PROPOSED` to `ADJUDICATED` and banks its weight. The marking must be `PROPOSED`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_MARKING_STATE`, and the study's `data_state` must not be `PURGED`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_DATA_STATE`. On success it stamps `adjudicated_at` from the clock, recomputes the admission's `weighted_event_count`, `capture_adequacy_index`, `capture_adequacy_index_prospective`, `projected_daily_yield` and `deficit_weighted`, may thereby drive the admission from `ADMITTED` to `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET`, and increments the marking's `version` by 1. The same transition also occurs as the cascade from alert adjudication described in §3.6.

Retraction runs the compensation. A marking in `ADJUDICATED` moves to `RETRACTED`, storing the supplied `retraction_reason`, recomputing the admission's `weighted_event_count` and both capture-adequacy variants downward, possibly driving the admission from `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET` back to `ADMITTED` — which in turn returns that admission's `COMPLETED` taper plans whose `current_step` is short of `total_steps` to `ACTIVE` — and incrementing the marking's `version` by 1. A marking still in `PROPOSED` may also be retracted directly, storing `retraction_reason` and incrementing `version`, but the admission's capture figures are left unchanged because a `PROPOSED` marking never contributed weight in the first place. Retracting a marking that is already `RETRACTED` is `ERR_INVALID_MARKING_STATE`; `RETRACTED` is terminal. Markings are listable with filters on `study_id`, `alert_id` and `status`.

The admission's capture figures are all recomputed from one input set: the event markings whose study belongs to this admission, whose `status` is `ADJUDICATED`, and whose `event_class` is `CLINICAL_SEIZURE` or `SUBCLINICAL_SEIZURE`. `PROPOSED` and `RETRACTED` markings are excluded outright, and spike-burst and artifact markings carry zero class weight so they never contribute. From that set: `weighted_event_count` is the sum of the members' `weight` values, rounded half up to 3 decimal places. `deficit_weighted` is the greater of zero and the admission's `target_event_count` minus `weighted_event_count`, to 3 decimal places. `elapsed_monitored_days` is the number of days from `admitted_at` to the current logical clock while the stay is not discharged, and from `admitted_at` to `discharged_at` once it is, to 3 decimal places. `projected_daily_yield` is 0 when `elapsed_monitored_days` is 0, and otherwise `weighted_event_count` divided by `elapsed_monitored_days`, to 3 decimal places.

Two adequacy figures are then derived from the same numbers, and only one of them ever gates anything. The retrospective figure stored on `capture_adequacy_index` is the lesser of 1.000 and `weighted_event_count` divided by `target_event_count`, rounded half up to 3 decimal places; it counts only weight already banked by adjudicated markings, it is clamped at 1.000, and it is **the only figure the `ADMITTED` to `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET` transition reads** — the transition fires when it reaches 1.000 or more, and the reverse transition fires when it falls below 1.000. The prospective figure stored on `capture_adequacy_index_prospective` is `weighted_event_count` plus the product of `projected_daily_yield` and the authorization's `days_available`, all divided by `target_event_count`, rounded half up to 3 decimal places; it adds the yield still expected from the authorized days that remain, it is **not** clamped, so it may exceed 1.000, and it is advisory only — it is shown to the epileptologist weighing length of stay and never gates a transition. Whenever `days_available` is greater than zero and `projected_daily_yield` is greater than zero, the prospective figure is strictly larger than the retrospective one for identical marking data.

The length-of-stay extension decision is the epileptologist's judgement call, made computable. It is guarded by the version expectation of §2.7, and before any arithmetic runs, two refusals apply: an admission already standing in `CAPTURE_TARGET_MET` is refused with `ERR_TARGET_ALREADY_MET`, since its deficit is zero and no extension is warranted, and a `DISCHARGED` admission is refused with `ERR_INVALID_ADMISSION_STATE`. The evaluation then proceeds in seven pinned steps against the state as it stands *before* this call, applying its grant exactly once at the end — which is how the apparent circularity (the risk budget limits the days granted, the days granted move the planned discharge, the elapsed days fed the yield) is resolved.

**Step one** reads the admission's `elapsed_monitored_days` as it stands. **Step two** reads `deficit_weighted`. **Step three** reads `projected_daily_yield`; if the admission's `weighted_event_count` is zero or the elapsed monitored days figure is zero, the yield has no basis and the request is refused with `ERR_NO_YIELD_BASIS`. **Step four** computes the days the deficit implies: `deficit_weighted` divided by `projected_daily_yield`, rounded **up** to a whole number of days, never negative. **Step five** computes a daily risk figure carried to 1 decimal place: the base risk for the patient's `risk_tier` — 2 for `LOW`, 3 for `MODERATE`, 5 for `HIGH` — plus 1.5 for each reduction step already taken, using the taper plan's **current** step and never a projected one, plus a further 2 when the unit's `posture` is `COVERAGE_CONSTRAINED` and nothing otherwise. The plan supplying that step is one in `ACTIVE`, `HELD` or `COMPLETED`; `ABORTED` plans are excluded and an admission with no plan reads a step of 0. **Step six** turns a per-stay risk allowance of 24 units into a day budget: 24 divided by the daily risk figure, rounded **down** to a whole number of days. **Step seven** grants the smallest of three ceilings — the days the deficit implies, the days the risk budget permits, and the authorization's `days_available`. If that smallest figure is zero or negative, no days are granted, and the outcome distinguishes the reason: it is a risk refusal when the risk budget itself came out at zero or below, and otherwise it is an authorized-day refusal. If the figure is positive, the outcome is a grant.

The decision is always recorded, whether or not it granted anything. `extension_count` increases by 1, `last_extension_granted_days` is set to the days granted — 0 for any refusal — and `last_extension_outcome` records which of the four declared outcomes applied: `GRANTED`, `DENIED_RISK`, `DENIED_AUTH_DAYS` or `DENIED_TARGET_MET`. On a grant, the granted days are reserved on the funding ledger, adding to `days_reserved` and subtracting from `days_available`, and `planned_discharge_at` is pushed out by 24 hours per granted day, so that the planned discharge always reflects `admitted_at` plus the initially planned days plus every day granted since. `capture_adequacy_index_prospective` is recomputed, since `days_available` has moved. If the decision leaves `days_available` at zero, or if the outcome was the authorized-day refusal, the admission's `authorization_state` moves from `AUTH_ACTIVE` to `AUTH_EXHAUSTED` as described in §3.3. The admission's `version` increments by 1, which is precisely why the next extension decision needs a freshly observed version.

### 3.8. Interpretation, Addenda and Turnaround Governance

Interpretation begins with a record nobody created by hand. When a study's acquisition completes, the server synthesizes exactly one review report for it in `PRELIM_DRAFT`, carrying the `study_id` of the recording being interpreted; because that creation returns the study rather than the report, listing the study's reports is the only channel through which the report becomes discoverable. A fresh report has `localization`, `prelim_issued_at`, `final_issued_at`, `prelim_author_user_id`, `final_author_user_id`, `turnaround_calendar_hours`, `turnaround_coverage_hours` and `addendum_reason` all null, `addendum_count` at 0 and `sla_breached` at false.

Issuing the preliminary interpretation moves `status` from `PRELIM_DRAFT` to `PRELIM_ISSUED`, and the payload carries the `localization` text describing the seizure-onset localization. The report must be in `PRELIM_DRAFT` (or, as below, `ADDENDUM_OPEN`), otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REPORT_STATE`, and the study's `acquisition_state` must be `COMPLETED` so that its `completed_at` is available, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STUDY_STATE`. On that first issuance the server stamps `prelim_issued_at` from the logical clock and `prelim_author_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`, computes both turnaround figures, and sets `sla_breached`.

Turnaround is measured between exactly two instants: the study's `completed_at` and the report's `prelim_issued_at`, both logical-clock values, and no other report enters the computation. The calendar figure on `turnaround_calendar_hours` counts every elapsed hour between those two instants, nights and non-reading hours included, rounded half up to 2 decimal places. The coverage figure on `turnaround_coverage_hours` counts only the minutes that fall inside the unit's daily reading window, which runs from 08:00:00 UTC up to but not including 18:00:00 UTC. To compute it, walk each UTC calendar day from the date of the study completion through the date of the preliminary issuance inclusive; for each such day, take that day's reading window, take the portion of the interval between the two instants that overlaps that window — treating a non-overlap as zero rather than as a negative amount — and add the overlap's minutes to a running total. The coverage figure is that total divided by 60, rounded half up to 2 decimal places. Because nights and weekends are excluded, the coverage figure is strictly smaller than the calendar figure whenever the interval spans a night. `sla_breached` is true if and only if `turnaround_coverage_hours` is **strictly greater** than the eight-hour preliminary reading service level; a coverage figure of exactly 8 is not a breach. The coverage figure is the only one that drives `sla_breached`; the calendar figure exists for external reporting of wall-clock delay and gates nothing.

An addendum reopens the preliminary interpretation. Opening one moves `status` from `PRELIM_ISSUED` to `ADDENDUM_OPEN`, increments `addendum_count` by 1 and stores the supplied `addendum_reason`; the report must be `PRELIM_ISSUED`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REPORT_STATE`, and a report whose `addendum_count` has already reached 2 is refused with `ERR_ADDENDUM_LIMIT`. Re-issuing from `ADDENDUM_OPEN` returns `status` to `PRELIM_ISSUED` and overwrites `localization` from the payload, but it deliberately **leaves `prelim_issued_at`, `turnaround_calendar_hours`, `turnaround_coverage_hours` and `sla_breached` at their original values** — an addendum revises the clinical content, not the historical record of how promptly the first read was delivered. `addendum_reason` is null only while `addendum_count` is 0.

Issuing the final interpretation moves `status` from `PRELIM_ISSUED` to `FINAL_ISSUED`, which is terminal, and its payload also carries `localization`. Three conditions guard it. The report must be `PRELIM_ISSUED`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REPORT_STATE` — a report sitting in `ADDENDUM_OPEN` must be re-issued as preliminary first. The acting `X-User-ID` must differ from `prelim_author_user_id`, otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`: two epileptologists must have looked at the recording. And no event marking of the study may still be `PROPOSED`, otherwise `ERR_MARKING_NOT_ADJUDICATED` — the interpretation cannot be final while findings remain unadjudicated. On success the server stamps `final_issued_at` from the clock, stamps `final_author_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`, stores `localization`, increments the report's `version` by 1, and thereby unblocks export packaging for that study.

### 3.9. Data Residency, Export Packaging, Retention and Surgical Candidacy

The data-residency dimension of a study moves independently of its acquisition. Placing an export hold moves `data_state` from `ONLINE` to `EXPORT_HELD` and stores the supplied `hold_reason` on the study's `export_hold_reason`; the study must be `ONLINE`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_DATA_STATE`. The hold cascades: every export package of that study standing in `QUEUED` moves to `BLOCKED` with `blocked_reason` set to the literal `STUDY_EXPORT_HELD`. Releasing the hold moves `data_state` back from `EXPORT_HELD` to `ONLINE` — the study must be `EXPORT_HELD`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_DATA_STATE` — and every package of that study standing in `BLOCKED` whose `blocked_reason` is `STUDY_EXPORT_HELD` returns to `QUEUED`. Note that a held study's volume still counts toward the unit's storage figure; only archiving relieves that.

Archiving is the storage-relief move, and it is the one cross-dimension guarded transition in the study machine: `data_state` moves from `ONLINE` to `ARCHIVED` only when `acquisition_state` has reached `COMPLETED`. A study whose `data_state` is not `ONLINE` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_DATA_STATE`; a study whose acquisition has not completed is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STUDY_STATE`. Archiving removes that study's `stored_gb` from the unit's `storage_used_gb`, re-derives `storage_pressure_pct` and `posture`, and — when the posture consequently leaves `STORAGE_LOCKED` — returns every export package of that unit standing in `BLOCKED` with `blocked_reason` equal to the literal `UNIT_STORAGE_LOCKED` to `QUEUED`. The study's `version` increments by 1. The last data-residency step, `ARCHIVED` to `PURGED`, belongs to the retention sweep and requires both that the study's data be `ARCHIVED` and that its admission be `DISCHARGED`.

Export packages carry recorded EEG out to a surgical centre. Creating one names the `study_id` being exported, a boolean `include_video` stating whether synchronized video travels with it, the version expectation `expected_study_version` of §2.7, and optionally a `supersedes_export_id` pointing at the package this one replaces — null for a first-generation package. The study must already have a review report in `FINAL_ISSUED`, otherwise the create is refused with `ERR_REPORT_NOT_FINAL`. A new package opens in `QUEUED` with `package_gb` at 0 and `built_at`, `delivered_at`, `recipient_center`, `retention_expires_at`, `blocked_reason` and `revocation_reason` all null. The create also increments the referenced study's `version` by 1, which is exactly why a client queuing two packages against one study must re-read the study between them.

Building a package is where storage is actually allocated, and it is the densest cascade in the system. Four conditions apply: the package must be `QUEUED`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_EXPORT_STATE`; the study's `data_state` must be `ONLINE`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_DATA_STATE`; the unit's `posture` must not be `STORAGE_LOCKED` — equivalently its `storage_pressure_pct` must be below 90.00 percent — otherwise `ERR_UNIT_STORAGE_LOCKED`; and the unit's `storage_used_gb` plus the volume about to be allocated must not exceed its `storage_quota_gb`, otherwise `ERR_STORAGE_QUOTA_EXCEEDED`. The volume itself is a two-branch figure: when `include_video` is true, `package_gb` equals the study's `stored_gb` at build time exactly; when it is false, `package_gb` is the study's `stored_gb` divided by the study's modality factor — 2.5 for `INPATIENT_VEEG`, 1.0 for `AMBULATORY_VEEG` — rounded half up to 2 decimal places, since stripping video from an inpatient recording strips most of its bulk while an ambulatory recording carries no video premium to strip. On success the cascade runs in four links: first, `package_gb` is set from that computation, `built_at` is stamped from the clock, and `retention_expires_at` is set to 30 days after `built_at`, that being the first instant from which a sweep may purge the package; second, the package volume is added to the unit's `storage_used_gb`, which re-derives `storage_pressure_pct`; third, if that pressure has reached 90.00 percent or more the unit's `posture` becomes `STORAGE_LOCKED`; and fourth, because the posture is now locked, every *other* export package of that unit standing in `QUEUED` moves to `BLOCKED` with `blocked_reason` set to `UNIT_STORAGE_LOCKED`. The package's `version` increments by 1, as does that of every record the cascade touched.

Blocking and unblocking are entirely server-driven. A package in `QUEUED` moves to `BLOCKED` whenever the unit's posture becomes `STORAGE_LOCKED` or the study enters `EXPORT_HELD`, with `blocked_reason` recording which of the two literals — `UNIT_STORAGE_LOCKED` or `STUDY_EXPORT_HELD` — caused it. A package in `BLOCKED` returns to `QUEUED` when the condition named by its own `blocked_reason` no longer holds: the posture leaving `STORAGE_LOCKED` through a retention sweep, an archive or a revocation releases storage-locked packages, and the study leaving `EXPORT_HELD` releases hold-blocked packages. Returning to `QUEUED` clears `blocked_reason`. Each of these moves increments the package's `version` by 1. A blocked package is never auto-built; it must be built explicitly once it is queued again.

Delivery moves a package from `BUILT` to `DELIVERED`, stamping `delivered_at` from the clock and storing the supplied `recipient_center` naming the surgical centre; a package that is not `BUILT` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_EXPORT_STATE`. Delivery is what unblocks the surgical conference. Revocation is the compensating action and is permitted from `BUILT` and from `DELIVERED` alike; anything else is `ERR_INVALID_EXPORT_STATE`. It stores the supplied `revocation_reason`, subtracts `package_gb` from the unit's `storage_used_gb` — restoring the pre-build figure exactly, not approximately — re-derives `storage_pressure_pct` and `posture`, and where the posture leaves `STORAGE_LOCKED` returns packages blocked by the storage lock to `QUEUED`. `package_gb` itself is retained on the revoked package as the historical record of what was once allocated. Revoking a delivered package carries one extra guard: if any surgical conference case referencing that package has reached `CANDIDATE` or `NON_CANDIDATE`, the revocation is refused with `ERR_EXPORT_IN_DECIDED_CASE`, because the decision was made on that data. Both `REVOKED` and `PURGED` are terminal.

The retention sweep is the coordinator's ops-run checkpoint over a whole unit, and it does four things in one pass. It increments the unit's `retention_sweep_index` by 1. It purges every export package of the unit whose `status` is `BUILT` or `DELIVERED` and whose `retention_expires_at` has been reached by the logical clock, moving it to `PURGED` and subtracting its `package_gb` from the unit's `storage_used_gb`; both entry paths into `PURGED` release storage identically. It purges every study of the unit whose `data_state` is `ARCHIVED` and whose admission is `DISCHARGED`, moving that study's data to `PURGED`. Then it re-derives `storage_used_gb`, `storage_pressure_pct` and `posture`, and where the posture leaves `STORAGE_LOCKED` it returns packages blocked with `blocked_reason` of `UNIT_STORAGE_LOCKED` to `QUEUED`. The sweep increments the unit's own `version` by 1, along with the version of every package and study it moved. Packages may be listed with filters on `study_id`, `supersedes_export_id` and `status`.

Surgical candidacy is decided by a multidisciplinary conference over one delivered package. An epileptologist opens a case naming the `admission_id` whose candidacy is in question and the `export_id` of the package the conference will review; the case opens in `DRAFT` with `presenter_user_id` and `decision_at` null, `defer_round` at 0, every tally — `eligible_vote_count`, `candidate_votes`, `non_candidate_votes`, `defer_votes` and `distinct_specialty_count` — at 0, and `quorum_met` false. Presenting the case moves `status` from `DRAFT` to `PRESENTED` and stamps `presenter_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`; the referenced package must stand in `DELIVERED`, otherwise `ERR_EXPORT_NOT_DELIVERED`, and a case that is neither `DRAFT` nor `DEFERRED` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_CASE_STATE`.

Ballots are cast against a presented case. Each ballot records the `case_id` of the case it was cast on, taken from the case addressed in the request rather than from the payload, and each carries a `specialty` of `EPILEPTOLOGY`, `NEUROSURGERY`, `NEUROPSYCHOLOGY` or `NEURORADIOLOGY`, a `vote` of `CANDIDATE`, `NON_CANDIDATE` or `DEFER`, and an optional free-text `rationale`. The server stamps `voter_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`, `round_index` from the case's `defer_round` at the instant the ballot was cast, and `counted` — which is false when the voter is the case's own `presenter_user_id` and true otherwise, so that the presenter may speak but not decide. An operator who has already cast a ballot on this case in the current `defer_round` is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_VOTE`; voting against a case that is not `PRESENTED` is `ERR_INVALID_CASE_STATE`. Ballots are append-only and listable across all rounds, and each new ballot recomputes the case tallies.

The tally is drawn only from ballots on this case whose `counted` is true and whose `round_index` equals the case's current `defer_round`; the presenter's uncounted ballot and every ballot from an earlier round are excluded. From that set, `eligible_vote_count` is the number of ballots, `candidate_votes`, `non_candidate_votes` and `defer_votes` are the counts of each `vote` value, and `distinct_specialty_count` is the number of distinct `specialty` values present. `quorum_met` is true if and only if `eligible_vote_count` is at least 3 **and** `distinct_specialty_count` is at least 2 — three voices spanning at least two disciplines.

Closing the round resolves the outcome in a pinned order that must be followed exactly, because two of its branches would otherwise be reachable by a different rule. First, if `quorum_met` is false the close is refused with `ERR_QUORUM_NOT_MET`. Second, if `defer_votes` is greater than or equal to `candidate_votes` **and** greater than or equal to `non_candidate_votes`, the outcome is a deferral — a deferral plurality wins ties against both other options. Third, otherwise, if `candidate_votes` is strictly greater than `non_candidate_votes`, the outcome is candidacy. Fourth, otherwise, if `non_candidate_votes` is strictly greater than `candidate_votes`, the outcome is non-candidacy. Fifth, otherwise — the remaining case being an exact tie between candidate and non-candidate ballots with both exceeding the defer count — the outcome is again a deferral.

A candidacy or non-candidacy outcome moves `status` to `CANDIDATE` or `NON_CANDIDATE` respectively and stamps `decision_at` from the logical clock; both are terminal. A deferral outcome moves `status` to `DEFERRED`, increments `defer_round` by 1, and leaves `decision_at` null. Critically, a deferral **retains the tallies of the round just closed** — `eligible_vote_count`, `candidate_votes`, `non_candidate_votes`, `defer_votes`, `distinct_specialty_count` and `quorum_met` all keep the values that decided the deferral, so the record of why the case was deferred survives. Those figures are reset only by the next presentation. Re-presenting a `DEFERRED` case moves it back to `PRESENTED` and resets the per-round tallies to the new round, and a case whose `defer_round` has already reached 2 is refused with `ERR_DEFER_LIMIT`. Closing a case that is not `PRESENTED` is `ERR_INVALID_CASE_STATE`. Every conference-case transition increments the case's `version` by 1. Cases may be listed with filters on `admission_id`, `export_id` and `status`.
