# Draft & Combine Operations API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Every spring the league office compresses a year of talent evaluation into a handful of weeks: amateur players declare their intent to enter the draft, a scouting committee rules on who is actually eligible, several hundred invitees are routed through a single facility for medical examinations, on-field testing and club interviews inside one compressed week, thirty-two front offices negotiate for draft position, and a live selection clock allocates the entire incoming player class in two days. The Draft & Combine Operations API is the system of record for that sequence. It is mounted under `/draft-ops`, and it owns the authoritative answer to questions that today are settled by phone calls and spreadsheets: whose declaration counts, who may read a prospect's medical file, which club owns overall pick 47 at this instant, and how much rookie cap a club has left.

The product problem is not workflow — it is arbitration. A club that reads a prospect's record at 10:00 and offers a contract at 10:05 must not be allowed to silently overwrite an offer made in between. A prospect who revokes medical consent must have that revocation reach every club that holds a share, every evaluation derived from it, and every outstanding contract offer that depended on the club's ability to verify the file. A forfeited pick must simultaneously drop out of the trade market, reduce its owner's negotiating capital, and void the trades that owner has open. Because the league operates under public scrutiny and collective-bargaining obligations, none of these outcomes may depend on request timing, on database row order, or on an operator's judgement: identical inputs must always produce identical, explainable results.

Three design commitments follow from that. First, every ordering rule in the system is expressed over declared business fields plus creation timestamps — never over internal identifiers — so a draft order or a compensatory award list can be recomputed and audited from published inputs alone. Second, all time-sensitive behaviour reads a server-controlled logical clock rather than wall-clock time, so declaration deadlines, combine-week windows, selection-clock expiries and consent lapses are reproducible. Third, the system distinguishes *authoritative* figures, which gate decisions, from *advisory* figures, which are published to front offices for negotiation but never enforced; the two are computed by deliberately different methods and are expected to disagree.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

This section defines the contract-level rules that hold across every endpoint. Where a rule below refers to a business concept, §3 supplies the domain narrative; where §3 describes a workflow, the guards and formats defined here still apply.

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request — including reads — must carry two headers. `X-User-ID` identifies the acting principal and `X-User-Role` declares the capacity in which it is acting. If either header is absent, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the six role names below, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). If the headers are well formed but the declared role is not among the roles authorized for the target operation, the request is rejected with `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403).

The six roles are `LeagueDraftAdmin`, `ClubGM`, `ClubMedicalDirector`, `CombineMedicalExaminer`, `EligibilityCommittee`, and `ProspectAgent`.

- `LeagueDraftAdmin` is the league-office operator that creates cycles, drives phase changes, runs the lottery and the draft order, administers the selection clock, decides medical record shares and verifies compensatory claims. When `X-User-Role` is `LeagueDraftAdmin`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server never compares it to any entity's `id`.
- `ClubGM` is a club general manager. When `X-User-Role` is `ClubGM`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Club`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Club` compare the resource's club foreign key to `X-User-ID`.
- `ClubMedicalDirector` is club medical staff cleared to handle restricted evaluations. When `X-User-Role` is `ClubMedicalDirector`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Club`, and the same club-ownership comparison applies.
- `CombineMedicalExaminer` is a league-contracted examiner. When `X-User-Role` is `CombineMedicalExaminer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it as `filed_by_user_id` on the `MedicalEvaluation` the examiner files, but does not compare it to any entity's `id`.
- `EligibilityCommittee` is a league eligibility committee member. When `X-User-Role` is `EligibilityCommittee`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it as `opened_by_user_id` or `decided_by_user_id` on an `EligibilityWaiver`, and compares those two recorded values to each other for separation-of-duties purposes, but never compares them to any entity's `id`.
- `ProspectAgent` is the certified agent acting for one prospect. When `X-User-Role` is `ProspectAgent`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the represented `Prospect`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Prospect` compare the resource's prospect foreign key to `X-User-ID`. The agent's identifier is also recorded as `submitted_by_user_id` when a waiver is filed.

For the three entity-backed roles, acting on a resource that belongs to a different `Club` or a different `Prospect` is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403). A share requester's declared capacity is additionally persisted on the share as `requested_by_role`, whose only permitted values are `ClubGM` and `ClubMedicalDirector`, alongside the requester's identifier in `requested_by_user_id`.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

List endpoints accept two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of rows returned; it defaults to 10, may not be smaller than 1, and may not be larger than 100. `offset` is the number of rows to skip; it defaults to 0 and may not be negative. A `limit` outside 1 through 100, or a negative `offset`, is a malformed request and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400).

Unless a specific list endpoint declares a different order, list results are sorted by `created_at` ascending. The endpoints that declare their own order are: the cycle's club list, ordered by `draft_order_index` ascending and then `created_at` ascending; the combine-booking list, ordered by `starts_at` ascending and then `created_at` ascending; the draft-pick list, ordered by `overall_number` ascending; and the compensatory-claim list, ordered by `claim_score` descending and then `created_at` ascending.

Every list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, and `has_more` inside the `meta` object of the envelope. `total_count` reports the number of rows matching the filters before pagination is applied, and `has_more` is true when rows remain beyond the current page.

### 2.4. Deterministic Guard Ordering

Most endpoints in this system are guarded by more than one rule, and clients depend on knowing *which* rejection they will see when several rules are simultaneously violated. Guards are therefore evaluated in a single fixed order, top to bottom; the first guard that fails determines the response, and no later guard runs. The order is:

1. Authentication — both headers present and `X-User-Role` one of the six declared roles, else `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (401). Applies to every endpoint.
2. Role authorization — the acting role appears in the target endpoint's authorized list, else `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (403). Applies to every endpoint.
3. Request shape — body and query parameters parse; every required field is present; every enum value is a member of its declared set; every number lies inside its declared range; `ends_at` is strictly after `starts_at`; `limit` is between 1 and 100 inclusive; `offset` is zero or greater. Otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400). Applies to every endpoint.
4. Path existence — every resource named by a path parameter exists, checked left to right (see §2.9), else `ERR_RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND` (404). Applies to every endpoint that has path parameters.
5. Body reference existence — every foreign key supplied in the request body resolves to an existing row, checked in the order the fields are declared in the endpoint's input list, else `ERR_REFERENCED_ENTITY_NOT_FOUND` (404). Applies to every endpoint that accepts foreign-key fields.
6. Resource ownership — for the entity-backed roles, `X-User-ID` equals the resource's owning foreign key: `ClubGM` and `ClubMedicalDirector` must match the `Club`, and `ProspectAgent` must match the `Prospect`. Otherwise `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (403). Applies to every club-scoped and prospect-scoped mutation.
7. Cycle phase — the governing `DraftCycle` is in a `status` that permits the operation, else `ERR_CYCLE_PHASE_INVALID` (409). Declarations are permitted only while the cycle is `DECLARATION_OPEN`; combine bookings only while it is `COMBINE_WEEK`; the lottery and the draft-order finalize only while it is `ORDER_PENDING`; selections and clock actions only while it is `DRAFT_LIVE`; pick trades only while it is `ORDER_FINAL` or `DRAFT_LIVE`; undrafted-free-agent contracts only while it is `UDFA_WINDOW`. Applies to every cycle-scoped mutation.
8. Cycle posture — the cycle's `posture` is not `FROZEN`, else `ERR_CYCLE_FROZEN` (409). Applies to every cycle-scoped mutation.
9. Transition legality — the supplied action corresponds to a declared transition out of the target resource's current state, else `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` (409). Applies to every action and status endpoint.
10. Parent version freshness — the supplied `expected_<parent>_version` equals the parent's current `version`, else `ERR_STALE_PARENT_VERSION` (412). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/player-contracts` and `POST /draft-ops/medical-record-shares`.
11. Consent — the prospect's `consent_status` is `GRANTED`, `consent_expires_at` is strictly after the current logical instant, and the requested `scope` does not exceed `consent_scope`, else `ERR_CONSENT_NOT_GRANTED` (403). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/medical-record-shares` and `POST /draft-ops/medical-record-shares/{id}/decision`.
12. Evaluation disclosability — the target evaluation's `disclosure_state` is `DISCLOSABLE`, else `ERR_EVALUATION_SEALED` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/medical-record-shares`.
13. Confidentiality tier — an evaluation whose `confidentiality_tier` is `RESTRICTED` may only be requested or granted for a `ClubMedicalDirector`; a `ClubGM` request against a `RESTRICTED` evaluation is refused with `ERR_CONFIDENTIALITY_TIER_VIOLATION` (403). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/medical-record-shares` and `POST /draft-ops/medical-record-shares/{id}/decision`.
14. Separation of duties — the acting `X-User-ID` differs from the user who performed the conflicting earlier step, else `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (403). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/eligibility-waivers/{id}/review`.
15. Trade parties — `proposing_club_id` differs from `receiving_club_id` and the acting club is a party to the trade, else `ERR_SELF_TRADE_FORBIDDEN` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades` and `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades/{id}/counter`.
16. Declaration deadline — the current logical instant is at or before the cycle's `declaration_deadline_at`, unless the prospect's `late_declaration_waived` is true, else `ERR_DECLARATION_DEADLINE_PASSED` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/prospects/{id}/declaration`.
17. Combine-week containment — `starts_at` is at or after `combine_week_start_at` and `ends_at` is at or before `combine_week_end_at`, else `ERR_OUTSIDE_COMBINE_WEEK` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/combine-bookings`.
18. Running clock — the current logical instant is strictly before the pick's `deadline_at` for any action that assumes a running clock, else `ERR_CLOCK_ALREADY_EXPIRED` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/draft-picks/{id}/selections` and `POST /draft-ops/draft-picks/{id}/clock-action`.
19. Undrafted-window openness — the current logical instant is strictly before `udfa_window_close_at` for undrafted-free-agent offers and signings, else `ERR_UDFA_WINDOW_CLOSED` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/player-contracts` and `POST /draft-ops/player-contracts/{id}/status`.
20. Cycle cohesion — every referenced `Club`, `Prospect`, `DraftPick` and `CompensatoryClaim` belongs to the same `DraftCycle` as the operation, else `ERR_CROSS_CYCLE_REFERENCE` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades`, `POST /draft-ops/medical-record-shares`, `POST /draft-ops/player-contracts`, `POST /draft-ops/combine-bookings` and `POST /draft-ops/compensatory-claims`.
21. Booking resource shape — a `MEDICAL_EXAM` booking supplies exactly `medical_station_id`; a `CLUB_INTERVIEW` booking supplies exactly `interview_room_id` and `club_id`; an `ON_FIELD_TEST` booking supplies none of the three. Otherwise `ERR_BOOKING_RESOURCE_MISMATCH` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/combine-bookings`.
22. Evaluation source — the referenced `CombineBooking` has `booking_type` `MEDICAL_EXAM`, `status` `COMPLETED`, and a `prospect_id` equal to the supplied `prospect_id`, else `ERR_BOOKING_NOT_MEDICAL_COMPLETED` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/medical-evaluations`.
23. Pick ownership — every pick in `offered_pick_ids` is owned by `proposing_club_id`, every pick in `requested_pick_ids` is owned by `receiving_club_id`, and all of them are `PENDING`, else `ERR_PICK_NOT_OWNED_BY_CLUB` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades`, `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades/{id}/counter` and `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades/{id}/accept`.
24. Contract-to-pick coherence — for `DRAFTED` origin, `draft_pick_id` is present, that pick's `status` is `USED`, its `selected_prospect_id` equals the supplied `prospect_id` and its `owning_club_id` equals the supplied `club_id`; for `UDFA` origin, `draft_pick_id` is absent and `udfa_signing_bonus_cents` is present. Otherwise `ERR_CONTRACT_PICK_MISMATCH` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/player-contracts`.
25. Selectability — the prospect being selected has `eligibility_status` `ELIGIBLE`, a `medical_clearance_status` that is not `FAILED`, `declaration_status` `DECLARED`, and `drafted_pick_overall_number` equal to 0, else `ERR_PROSPECT_NOT_SELECTABLE` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/draft-picks/{id}/selections`.
26. Lottery sequence validity — every value in `drawn_combination_sequence` lies between 1 and the total number of assigned lottery combinations inclusive, and the sequence names at least as many distinct owning clubs as there are lottery slots to fill, else `ERR_LOTTERY_SEQUENCE_INVALID` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/draft-cycles/{id}/lottery`.
27. Club code uniqueness — `club_code` is unique among the clubs sharing a `draft_cycle_id`, else `ERR_CLUB_CODE_DUPLICATE` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/clubs`.
28. Declaration idempotence — the prospect's `declaration_status` is `UNDECLARED` when the action is `DECLARE`, else `ERR_ALREADY_DECLARED` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/prospects/{id}/declaration`.
29. Waiver information rounds — `info_request_count` is below the cap of two information requests when the action is `REQUEST_INFO`, else `ERR_WAIVER_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/eligibility-waivers/{id}/review`.
30. Negotiation rounds — `round_index` is below the cap of three negotiation rounds when the action is a counter, else `ERR_TRADE_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades/{id}/counter`.
31. Out-of-order authorization — a `PENDING` pick used while another pick holds the clock has `out_of_order_authorized` true and the request carries `out_of_order_ack` true, else `ERR_OUT_OF_ORDER_NOT_AUTHORIZED` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/draft-picks/{id}/selections`.
32. Station availability — the new interval, inflated by the station's `turnaround_buffer_minutes` on both sides, does not overlap the half-open interval of any booking already on that station in `SCHEDULED` or `CHECKED_IN`, else `ERR_STATION_SLOT_CONFLICT` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/combine-bookings`.
33. Room capacity — the number of `SCHEDULED` or `CHECKED_IN` bookings on the interview room whose half-open intervals overlap the new interval is strictly less than the room's `concurrent_capacity`; two intervals that merely touch, with a gap of zero, do not overlap. Otherwise `ERR_ROOM_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/combine-bookings`.
34. Prospect itinerary — the new interval, widened by ten minutes of travel allowance on both sides, does not overlap any other booking of the same prospect in `SCHEDULED` or `CHECKED_IN`, else `ERR_PROSPECT_ITINERARY_CONFLICT` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/combine-bookings`.
35. Selector pool — the combine invitation selector's candidate pool is non-empty, else `ERR_NO_ELIGIBLE_PROSPECTS` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/draft-cycles/{id}/combine-invitations/select`.
36. Trade authorization — neither party club's `trade_authorization_tier` is `SUSPENDED`, else `ERR_TRADE_AUTHORIZATION_SUSPENDED` (403). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades`, `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades/{id}/counter` and `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades/{id}/accept`.
37. Trade balance — `imbalance_ratio` is at most the tolerance of 0.15, inclusive at the boundary, else `ERR_TRADE_VALUE_IMBALANCED` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/pick-trades/{id}/accept`.
38. Timeout inventory — the owning club's `timeouts_remaining` is greater than zero when the action is `TIMEOUT`, else `ERR_NO_TIMEOUTS_REMAINING` (409). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/draft-picks/{id}/clock-action`.
39. Rookie pool — signing the contract keeps the club's signed first-year charges at or below `rookie_pool_total_cents`, else `ERR_ROOKIE_POOL_EXCEEDED` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/player-contracts/{id}/status`.
40. Undrafted pool — the requested `udfa_signing_bonus_cents` is at most the club's `udfa_pool_remaining_cents`, else `ERR_UDFA_POOL_EXHAUSTED` (422). Applies to `POST /draft-ops/player-contracts`.

Two consequences of this ordering deserve emphasis. Because cycle phase (rule 7) precedes cycle posture (rule 8), an operation attempted in the wrong phase reports `ERR_CYCLE_PHASE_INVALID` even when the cycle is also frozen. And because transition legality (rule 9) precedes the version check (rule 10), an action that is not a legal transition reports `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` regardless of whether the supplied version is also stale.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock and Temporal Windows

Nothing in this system reads wall-clock time. All deadline, window and expiry comparisons read a server-controlled **logical clock**, and a `LeagueDraftAdmin` moves that clock by submitting a target instant as `to_iso8601` to `POST /_clock/set_current_utc`, which answers with the resulting `current_utc`. Before any such call the logical clock reads exactly the server startup default `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`; it never falls back to real time under any circumstance.

Advancing the clock is not a passive act. Each advance also sweeps the medical-sharing ledger: every `MedicalRecordShare` in `GRANTED` whose `expires_at` is at or before the new instant transitions to `EXPIRED`, its `effective_access` becomes `NONE`, and its evaluation's `active_share_count` is decremented by one.

Temporal comparisons are performed at millisecond resolution, and their boundary behaviour is fixed and asymmetric by design. A selection clock is treated as expired when the current instant is at or after `deadline_at`, while a timeout may only be spent while the current instant is strictly before `deadline_at` — so at the exact boundary instant the pick is expired and no timeout is possible. A declaration is accepted when the current instant is at or before `declaration_deadline_at`. A share's access ends at `expires_at` exclusive: access exists only while the current instant is strictly before it. An undrafted-free-agent offer or signing requires the current instant to be strictly before `udfa_window_close_at`, while the cycle may only be closed once the current instant has reached or passed that same instant.

### 2.6. Record Identity, Audit Fields and Version Semantics

Every resource in this system — `DraftCycle`, `Club`, `Prospect`, `EligibilityWaiver`, `MedicalStation`, `InterviewRoom`, `CombineBooking`, `MedicalEvaluation`, `MedicalRecordShare`, `DraftPick`, `PickTrade`, `CompensatoryClaim` and `PlayerContract` — carries the same four system-managed properties:

- `id` — a server-minted UUID, with no type prefix and no client-supplied component.
- `created_at` — an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, recorded when the row is created.
- `updated_at` — an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, refreshed on every mutation of the row.
- `version` — an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increases by exactly one on every mutation of the row.

There are no exceptions in this domain: every entity listed above carries all four properties. A recomputation of a derived field counts as a mutation. When a selection recomputes a club's `draft_capital_points`, when a signing recomputes `rookie_pool_remaining_cents`, or when a granted share raises an evaluation's `active_share_count`, the affected row's `updated_at` is refreshed and its `version` is incremented — even though no client asked for that row to change. This is what makes the version-freshness rule of §2.8 meaningful.

### 2.7. Points, Cents and Rounding Discipline

Three numeric families coexist in this system, each with its own fixed precision, and every rounding step uses the half-up convention.

Money is always expressed in **whole cents** as an integer. There are no fractional cents anywhere in a stored value or in a response. Contract arithmetic rounds half up to whole cents at each step the formula names, and the order of those steps is part of the specification rather than an implementation detail; §3.8 spells the order out.

**Trade chart points** carry one decimal place, rounded half up. This applies to a pick's `chart_value_points`, to a club's `draft_capital_points`, to a cycle's `pick_inventory_chart_points`, and to both the authoritative `chart_balance_points` and the advisory `surplus_balance_points` on a trade. The trade `imbalance_ratio` is the one exception in this family: it carries four decimal places, rounded half up.

**Scores and shares** carry the precision their source field declares. A `CompensatoryClaim`'s `claim_score` and `advisory_snap_adjusted_score` carry two decimal places, rounded half up, and the award bands they feed are inclusive at their lower bounds. A prospect's `scouting_grade` is compared at two decimal places; `snap_pct` is a two-decimal share between 0.00 and 1.00 inclusive; a club's `prior_season_win_pct` and `strength_of_schedule` are three-decimal shares between 0.000 and 1.000 inclusive and are compared at three decimal places. Clock arithmetic is in whole minutes, though the comparisons that use its results run at millisecond resolution.

### 2.8. Stale-Parent Rejection on Shares and Contract Offers

Two create operations in this system legitimately *supersede* an earlier row, and for exactly that reason both require the client to prove it has seen the parent's latest state.

`POST /draft-ops/player-contracts` requires `expected_prospect_version` as a non-optional input field, carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `Prospect`. If that number does not equal the prospect's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PARENT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Because a club's new offer lapses that club's prior outstanding offer for the same prospect, this check is the only deterministic protection against a writer holding an outdated view of the prospect quietly discarding an offer it never observed. A successful offer increments `Prospect.version` by one, so a client that wishes to make a second offer must re-read the prospect first.

`POST /draft-ops/medical-record-shares` requires `expected_medical_evaluation_version` as a non-optional input field, carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `MedicalEvaluation`. If that number does not equal the evaluation's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PARENT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). A newer request for the same evaluation and club supersedes the prior `REQUESTED` share, so the freshness check prevents a requester with an outdated view of the evaluation's disclosure state from superseding a request it never observed. A successful share request increments `MedicalEvaluation.version` by one.

No other endpoint takes a version field. Endpoints outside these two are processed without any parent-version comparison, and supplying an unexpected version field to them has no effect on the outcome beyond ordinary request-shape validation.

### 2.9. Left-to-Right Path Resolution and Cycle Cohesion

When a path names more than one resource — for example `/draft-ops/prospects/{id}/eligibility-waivers` — the identifiers are resolved strictly left to right. The first identifier that does not resolve produces `ERR_RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404) immediately, and no identifier to its right is examined. A waiver list requested under a prospect that does not exist therefore reports a missing prospect, never a missing waiver collection.

Foreign keys supplied in a request body are resolved after all path parameters, in the order the endpoint declares its input fields, and an unresolvable one produces `ERR_REFERENCED_ENTITY_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404). Existence alone is not sufficient: once every reference resolves, the system additionally verifies that all of them live in the same `DraftCycle` as the operation. A trade that names a pick from another cycle, a share whose club belongs to another cycle, a booking whose prospect belongs to another cycle, a contract whose pick belongs to another cycle, or a compensatory claim whose club belongs to another cycle is rejected with `ERR_CROSS_CYCLE_REFERENCE` (HTTP 422). Cycles are hermetic: no resource may ever bridge two of them.

### 2.10. Separation of Duties and Confidentiality Tiering

The league operates two independent controls that restrict *who* may act, over and above ordinary role permissions.

The first is separation of duties on eligibility waivers. Three roles in the waiver story must be three different people: the agent that filed the request may not open review of it, and whoever opened review may not be the one who grants or denies it. Both violations report `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). The system enforces this by comparing the acting `X-User-ID` against `submitted_by_user_id` when review is opened, and against `opened_by_user_id` when a decision is recorded. Reopening review after an information request overwrites `opened_by_user_id` with the new reviewer, so a committee can legitimately hand a file to a colleague between rounds and the colleague may then decide it.

The second is confidentiality tiering on medical evaluations. An evaluation whose `confidentiality_tier` is `RESTRICTED` is readable only by club medical staff: only a `ClubMedicalDirector` may request a share against it, and a share against it may only be granted while the recorded `requested_by_role` is `ClubMedicalDirector`. A `ClubGM` attempting either step against a `RESTRICTED` evaluation is refused with `ERR_CONFIDENTIALITY_TIER_VIOLATION` (HTTP 403). Evaluations at the `STANDARD` tier are open to both club roles.

### 2.11. Cascade and Recomputation Discipline

Many operations in this domain mutate more than the row the client addressed, and the secondary mutations are part of the contract rather than a convenience. Three families recur.

**Downward cancellation.** When a prospect leaves the pipeline — by withdrawing a declaration, by being ruled ineligible from an unreviewed posture, or by having an accepted combine invitation withdrawn — every one of that prospect's bookings still in `SCHEDULED` is moved to `CANCELLED`, releasing the station or room interval it held so that other prospects may book it.

**Upward recomputation.** Derived aggregates are recomputed at the moment their inputs change, never lazily. A pick changing owner, status or chart value recomputes the owning club's `draft_capital_points` and re-derives its `trade_authorization_tier`, and recomputes the cycle's `pick_inventory_chart_points` and `pick_inventory_count`. A contract reaching or leaving `SIGNED` recomputes the club's `rookie_pool_remaining_cents`. A share entering or leaving `GRANTED` recomputes its evaluation's `active_share_count` and may flip the evaluation's `disclosure_state`. Registering or withdrawing a club recomputes the cycle's `club_count`.

**Compensating reversal.** Two operations are designed to be exact inverses of an earlier one, and the invariants they preserve are testable. Rescinding an accepted trade restores every traded pick's `owning_club_id` to its pre-acceptance owner, so a cycle's `pick_inventory_chart_points` is numerically identical before the acceptance and after the rescission. Voiding a signed contract restores the club's `rookie_pool_remaining_cents` to its pre-signing value, so that at every instant a club's `rookie_pool_remaining_cents` plus the sum of first-year charges on its signed contracts equals its `rookie_pool_total_cents`.

Cascaded transitions are real transitions: they refresh `updated_at` and increment `version` on every row they touch, and they fire without the client issuing a request against those rows.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up a Cycle: Registration, Facilities and the Phase Ladder

A `LeagueDraftAdmin` opens the operating year by creating a **draft cycle**, the container every other resource in this system ultimately belongs to. Creation takes the `season_year` the cycle governs, which must be an integer from 2000 through 2100 inclusive, and the four instants that shape the calendar: `declaration_deadline_at`, after which a prospect may no longer declare without relief; `combine_week_start_at`, the inclusive start of the compressed booking window; `combine_week_end_at`, the exclusive end of that window, which must fall strictly after `combine_week_start_at`; and `draft_start_at`, the earliest instant the selection clock may be started for overall pick one, which must fall strictly after `combine_week_end_at`. Two optional inputs shape the draft itself. `rounds_count` declares how many rounds the cycle awards, an integer from 1 through 7 inclusive that defaults to 7. `compressed_final_day` is a boolean that defaults to false and, when true, shortens the clock in the later rounds as §3.7 describes. A newly created cycle has `status` `SETUP`, `posture` `OPEN`, `club_count` zero, and `lottery_completed` false.

The cycle's `status` is the phase ladder, and it takes exactly one of ten values: `SETUP`, `DECLARATION_OPEN`, `COMBINE_WEEK`, `ORDER_PENDING`, `ORDER_FINAL`, `DRAFT_LIVE`, `DRAFT_SUSPENDED`, `DRAFT_COMPLETE`, `UDFA_WINDOW`, `CLOSED`. Only a `LeagueDraftAdmin` moves it, by naming the desired next phase in `target_status`; a requested phase that is not a declared transition out of the current one is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`. Each phase change increments the cycle's `version` by one.

The `posture` field is not set by anyone — it is derived from `status` on every read, and takes one of three values. `posture` is `FROZEN` when `status` is `DRAFT_SUSPENDED` or `CLOSED`; it is `RESTRICTED` when `status` is `DRAFT_LIVE`, `DRAFT_COMPLETE` or `UDFA_WINDOW`; and it is `OPEN` in every other phase. A `FROZEN` posture is the hard stop: every selection, trade and clock action is refused with `ERR_CYCLE_FROZEN` while it holds.

Walking the ladder from the top: `SETUP` advances to `DECLARATION_OPEN` only once `club_count` is at least 2, at which point prospects may begin declaring. `DECLARATION_OPEN` advances to `COMBINE_WEEK` only once at least one prospect in the cycle has reached `declaration_status` `DECLARED`; from that moment the declaration endpoint stops accepting new declarations and combine bookings may be created. `COMBINE_WEEK` advances to `ORDER_PENDING` only once no booking belonging to a prospect of this cycle remains in `SCHEDULED` or `CHECKED_IN` — every booking must have reached `COMPLETED`, `NO_SHOW` or `CANCELLED` first. That advance carries a deliberate consequence: any prospect still sitting at `eligibility_status` `UNREVIEWED` is left `UNREVIEWED`, and because only an `ELIGIBLE` prospect may be selected, such a prospect is now unselectable for the remainder of the cycle. `ORDER_PENDING` is where the lottery and the draft-order mint happen (§3.5), and it advances to `ORDER_FINAL` through the finalize operation rather than a phase request.

`ORDER_FINAL` advances to `DRAFT_LIVE` only once the current logical instant has reached or passed `draft_start_at`; the posture becomes `RESTRICTED`, and no pick is on the clock until the clock is first advanced. From `DRAFT_LIVE` the admin may suspend the draft, moving to `DRAFT_SUSPENDED` with no precondition at all; the posture becomes `FROZEN`. Resuming moves `DRAFT_SUSPENDED` back to `DRAFT_LIVE`, again with no precondition; the posture returns to `RESTRICTED` and the pick that held the clock keeps its existing `deadline_at` untouched — a suspension does not buy the club on the clock any extra time. This pair may repeat any number of times.

The tail of the ladder is partly automatic. `DRAFT_LIVE` becomes `DRAFT_COMPLETE` on its own, during a clock advance, once every pick in the cycle sits in `USED`, `PASSED`, `FORFEITED` or `LAPSED`; that transition converts every remaining `PASSED` pick to `LAPSED` and sets `current_overall_number` to 0. `DRAFT_COMPLETE` advances to `UDFA_WINDOW` on admin request with no precondition, which freezes each registered club's undrafted-signing budget and opens undrafted contracts for offer (§3.8). `UDFA_WINDOW` advances to `CLOSED` only once the current logical instant has reached or passed `udfa_window_close_at`; every contract still in `OFFERED` becomes `LAPSED` and the posture becomes `FROZEN` permanently. `CLOSED` is terminal.

Two of the cycle's remaining derived fields describe the pick market rather than the calendar. `pick_inventory_count` is the number of picks belonging to the cycle whose `status` is anything other than `FORFEITED`, and `pick_inventory_chart_points` is the sum of `chart_value_points` over that same set, carried to one decimal place. `current_overall_number` is the `overall_number` of whichever pick currently holds the clock, and 0 when none does. `club_count` is the number of clubs with this cycle's `draft_cycle_id` whose `registration_status` is `REGISTERED` — withdrawn clubs never count. `order_finalized_at` is null until the draft order is minted; `udfa_window_close_at` is likewise null until then, and `lottery_slot_club_ids` is an empty list until the lottery runs.

A club joins a cycle when the admin registers it with a payload carrying the cycle's `draft_cycle_id`, a `club_code` of exactly three uppercase letters that must be unique among the clubs of that cycle (a repeat is refused with `ERR_CLUB_CODE_DUPLICATE`), a human-readable `market_name`, and the prior-season seeding inputs that will drive the draft order: `prior_season_win_pct` and `strength_of_schedule`, each a three-decimal share from 0.000 through 1.000 inclusive, and `head_to_head_rank`, the league-published integer rank of 1 or greater. The payload also carries `rookie_pool_total_cents`, the club's whole-cent rookie cap allowance, which may not be negative. Four inputs are optional: `registration_status`, either `REGISTERED` or `WITHDRAWN` and defaulting to `REGISTERED`; `playoff_qualified`, defaulting to false; `playoff_exit_round`, an integer from 0 through 4 inclusive that defaults to 0 and stays 0 for clubs that missed the postseason; and `lottery_combinations`, a non-negative integer count of lottery chances that defaults to 0 and is 0 for playoff clubs. Registering a club recomputes the cycle's `club_count` and seeds the club's `timeouts_remaining` at two selection-clock timeouts for the whole cycle.

The rest of a club's fields are derived. `forfeited_round_numbers` is the ascending list of round numbers whose picks the league has stripped from the club. `pre_lottery_rank` and `draft_order_index` are set by the lottery (§3.5). `rookie_pool_remaining_cents`, `draft_capital_points`, `trade_authorization_tier`, `udfa_pool_total_cents` and `udfa_pool_remaining_cents` are all recomputed from their inputs as §3.6 and §3.8 describe.

Prospects are registered by the admin too, with `draft_cycle_id`, the player's legal `full_name`, a `position_group` drawn from `QB`, `RB`, `WR`, `TE`, `OL`, `DL`, `LB`, `DB` and `ST`, an amateur institution `school_code`, `seasons_played` as an integer from 0 through 6 inclusive, a `scouting_grade` from 0.00 through 100.00 inclusive at two decimal places, and `position_group_scarcity_rank`, the league's ascending scarcity rank for that position group, an integer of 1 or greater. Registration sets all five of the prospect's parallel state dimensions to their initial values — `declaration_status` `UNDECLARED`, `eligibility_status` `UNREVIEWED`, `invitation_status` `NOT_INVITED`, `consent_status` `NONE`, `signing_status` `UNSIGNED` — along with `medical_clearance_status` `PENDING`, `declared_at` null, `late_declaration_waived` false, `early_entry_waived` false, `consent_scope` null, `consent_expires_at` null and `drafted_pick_overall_number` 0. These dimensions advance independently of one another; a prospect can be `DECLARED` and `UNREVIEWED` and `INVITED` and `NONE` and `UNSIGNED` all at once.

Facility inventory is registered outside any cycle, because the same building serves every year. A **medical station** carries a globally unique `station_code`, an `exam_type` of `ORTHO`, `CARDIAC`, `IMAGING` or `NEURO`, a `turnaround_buffer_minutes` from 0 through 60 inclusive that defaults to 15 and represents the sanitation and turnaround time that must separate consecutive examinations, and a `status` of `AVAILABLE` or `OFFLINE` that defaults to `AVAILABLE`. An **interview room** carries a globally unique `room_code`, a `concurrent_capacity` from 1 through 8 inclusive that defaults to 2 and caps how many overlapping bookings the room can hold at any instant, and the same `AVAILABLE` or `OFFLINE` `status` defaulting to `AVAILABLE`. Both are read-only after registration for the purposes of this specification; only bookings move against them.

The cycle's own list views are filterable. The club list accepts an optional `registration_status` filter alongside `limit` and `offset`. The prospect list accepts optional `declaration_status`, `eligibility_status` and `invitation_status` filters alongside `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.2. Declaration, Eligibility Determination, and the Waiver Review Loop

Everything downstream depends on the declaration dimension, and only a `ProspectAgent` may move it, by submitting an `action` of `DECLARE` or `WITHDRAW` against the prospect it represents.

`DECLARE` advances `declaration_status` from `UNDECLARED` to `DECLARED` and stamps `declared_at` with the current logical instant. It requires the cycle to be `DECLARATION_OPEN`, and it requires either that the current logical instant is at or before the cycle's `declaration_deadline_at`, or that the prospect's `late_declaration_waived` flag is already true. A late attempt without that relief is refused with `ERR_DECLARATION_DEADLINE_PASSED`; an attempt against a prospect whose `declaration_status` is anything other than `UNDECLARED` is refused with `ERR_ALREADY_DECLARED`.

`WITHDRAW` moves `declaration_status` from `DECLARED` to `WITHDRAWN`. It is permitted only while the cycle is `DECLARATION_OPEN` or `COMBINE_WEEK`, and only while the prospect's `signing_status` is `UNSIGNED` — a player who already has a contract in flight cannot walk away through this door. Withdrawal is intentionally destructive: it forces `eligibility_status` to `INELIGIBLE`, and it cancels every one of the prospect's bookings still in `SCHEDULED`.

The eligibility dimension belongs to the `EligibilityCommittee`, which submits an `action` of `DECLARE_ELIGIBLE` or `DECLARE_INELIGIBLE`. `DECLARE_ELIGIBLE` advances `eligibility_status` from `UNREVIEWED` to `ELIGIBLE`, and requires two things: the prospect's `declaration_status` is `DECLARED`, and the prospect has completed at least three amateur `seasons_played` — or, failing the seasons test, carries an `early_entry_waived` flag that is already true. A prospect that reaches `ELIGIBLE` becomes selectable in the draft as long as its `medical_clearance_status` is not `FAILED`.

`DECLARE_INELIGIBLE` has two distinct source states, and their rules differ. From `UNREVIEWED`, it requires the prospect's `declaration_status` to be either `DECLARED` or `WITHDRAWN`, and it has two side effects: an `invitation_status` sitting at `INVITED` or `ACCEPTED` is forced to `WITHDRAWN`, and every booking of that prospect still in `SCHEDULED` is cancelled. From `ELIGIBLE`, it requires only that the prospect's `drafted_pick_overall_number` is 0 — a player who has already been selected cannot be retroactively declared ineligible — and it has exactly one side effect: an `invitation_status` sitting at `INVITED` or `ACCEPTED` is forced to `WITHDRAWN`. Bookings are not cancelled on the `ELIGIBLE` to `INELIGIBLE` path, because by the time a prospect has been ruled eligible the combine week is behind it. `INELIGIBLE` is terminal on this dimension.

Relief from either the deadline or the seasons requirement comes through an **eligibility waiver**, which a `ProspectAgent` files against the prospect it represents. The filing carries a `waiver_type` of `LATE_DECLARATION`, `EARLY_ENTRY` or `MEDICAL_HARDSHIP`, and a written `rationale`. The new waiver starts at `status` `SUBMITTED` with `info_request_count` 0, `opened_by_user_id` null, `decided_by_user_id` null and `decided_at` null, and the filer's identifier is recorded as `submitted_by_user_id`. The waiver list for a prospect accepts optional `status` and `waiver_type` filters together with `limit` and `offset`.

Review is a bounded loop driven by a single review operation that takes an `action` and, on the terminal branches, a `decision_note`. Its states are `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `INFO_REQUESTED`, `GRANTED`, `DENIED` and `WITHDRAWN`, the last three terminal.

- `OPEN_REVIEW` from `SUBMITTED` advances the waiver to `UNDER_REVIEW`. It requires the acting committee member's identifier to differ from `submitted_by_user_id`, else `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, and it records the acting identifier as `opened_by_user_id`.
- `REQUEST_INFO` from `UNDER_REVIEW` moves the waiver to `INFO_REQUESTED` and increments `info_request_count` by one. It requires `info_request_count` to be below two; a third information request is refused with `ERR_WAIVER_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`.
- `OPEN_REVIEW` from `INFO_REQUESTED` returns the waiver to `UNDER_REVIEW` with no precondition, and records the acting identifier as `opened_by_user_id` — overwriting whatever was there. The loop from `UNDER_REVIEW` through `INFO_REQUESTED` and back may therefore run at most twice before the committee must decide.
- `GRANT` from `UNDER_REVIEW` moves the waiver to `GRANTED`. It requires the acting identifier to differ from `opened_by_user_id`, else `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, and it requires `decision_note` to be present. It sets `decided_by_user_id` to the acting identifier and `decided_at` to the current logical instant. It also grants the relief itself: a `LATE_DECLARATION` waiver sets the prospect's `late_declaration_waived` to true, and an `EARLY_ENTRY` waiver sets the prospect's `early_entry_waived` to true. A `MEDICAL_HARDSHIP` waiver sets neither flag; it is recorded for the committee's own purposes and changes no prospect field.
- `DENY` from `UNDER_REVIEW` moves the waiver to `DENIED` under exactly the same two preconditions — the acting identifier differs from `opened_by_user_id`, and `decision_note` is present — and sets `decided_by_user_id` and `decided_at`. It grants no relief and touches no prospect field.
- `WITHDRAW` from `SUBMITTED` moves the waiver to `WITHDRAWN`. This is the one branch reserved to the `ProspectAgent`, and it requires the acting identifier to equal the `id` of the prospect named by the waiver's `prospect_id`. It has no side effects.

### 3.3. Combine Invitations and Scheduling the Compressed Week

The invitation dimension has four moves, and they are split between the league and the agent.

Inviting advances `invitation_status` from `NOT_INVITED` to `INVITED` and is performed by a `LeagueDraftAdmin` with an `action` of `INVITE`. It requires the cycle to be `DECLARATION_OPEN` or `COMBINE_WEEK`, the prospect's `eligibility_status` to be anything other than `INELIGIBLE`, and the prospect's `declaration_status` to be `DECLARED`. It has no side effects beyond the status change itself.

Accepting advances `INVITED` to `ACCEPTED` and belongs to the `ProspectAgent` via an `action` of `ACCEPT`; it too requires the cycle to be `DECLARATION_OPEN` or `COMBINE_WEEK`, and from that point bookings may be created for the prospect. Declining advances `INVITED` to `DECLINED`, also by the agent, with no precondition at all; its side effect is that the prospect's `medical_clearance_status` becomes `UNVERIFIED`, because a player who never attends cannot be examined. Withdrawing an invitation advances `ACCEPTED` to `WITHDRAWN` and is reserved to the admin, again with no precondition; it cancels every booking of that prospect still in `SCHEDULED` and sets `medical_clearance_status` to `UNVERIFIED`.

Because hand-inviting several hundred players is impractical, the league also runs a **bulk selector** that invites the best available prospects in one pass. The caller supplies `target_count`, which may not exceed 320. The selector first assembles its pool: every prospect of this cycle whose `declaration_status` is `DECLARED`, whose `eligibility_status` is anything other than `INELIGIBLE`, and whose `invitation_status` is still `NOT_INVITED`. Prospects already at `INVITED`, `ACCEPTED`, `DECLINED` or `WITHDRAWN` are never re-selected. The pool is then ordered by `scouting_grade` descending; ties on grade compared at two decimal places are broken by `position_group_scarcity_rank` ascending, so that scarcer position groups are favoured; ties on both are broken by `created_at` ascending. The first `target_count` prospects in that order have their `invitation_status` set to `INVITED`. If the pool is empty the whole call is refused with `ERR_NO_ELIGIBLE_PROSPECTS`. If the pool holds at least one prospect but fewer than `target_count`, the entire pool is invited and no error is raised. The response reports `invited_count`, which is the number actually invited, and `invited_prospect_ids`, the list of prospects it touched.

Scheduling the week itself is an admin operation, because the league controls the facility. A **combine booking** names the `prospect_id` being scheduled, the `booking_type` reserved — `MEDICAL_EXAM`, `ON_FIELD_TEST` or `CLUB_INTERVIEW` — and the half-open interval it occupies, with `starts_at` inclusive and `ends_at` exclusive; `ends_at` must fall strictly after `starts_at`. Which resource fields accompany the booking is dictated entirely by `booking_type`: a `MEDICAL_EXAM` supplies `medical_station_id` and neither of the other two; a `CLUB_INTERVIEW` supplies both `interview_room_id` and `club_id` and no station; an `ON_FIELD_TEST` supplies none of the three. Any other combination is refused with `ERR_BOOKING_RESOURCE_MISMATCH`. The interval must sit wholly inside the cycle's compressed week: `starts_at` at or after `combine_week_start_at` and `ends_at` at or before `combine_week_end_at`, else `ERR_OUTSIDE_COMBINE_WEEK`. The server computes `duration_minutes` as the whole number of minutes between `starts_at` and `ends_at`.

Three conflict rules then run, in the order stated in §2.4. A medical station admits one examination at a time, and the sanitation window counts: the proposed interval is widened by the station's `turnaround_buffer_minutes` at both ends, and if that widened interval overlaps the half-open interval of any booking already on that station in `SCHEDULED` or `CHECKED_IN`, the request is refused with `ERR_STATION_SLOT_CONFLICT`. With the default buffer of a quarter of an hour, two examinations at the same station must therefore be separated by at least fifteen clear minutes. An interview room admits several clubs at once, but not without limit: the system counts the bookings on that room in `SCHEDULED` or `CHECKED_IN` whose half-open intervals overlap the proposed one, and that count must be strictly less than the room's `concurrent_capacity`, else `ERR_ROOM_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. Two intervals that merely touch — one ending at the exact instant the next begins — do not overlap, so a zero gap is legal in a room. Finally, the prospect must physically be able to make it: the proposed interval is widened by ten minutes of travel allowance at both ends, and if that widened interval overlaps any other booking of the same prospect in `SCHEDULED` or `CHECKED_IN`, the request is refused with `ERR_PROSPECT_ITINERARY_CONFLICT`. Only bookings in `SCHEDULED` or `CHECKED_IN` occupy a resource; those in `COMPLETED`, `NO_SHOW` or `CANCELLED` have released their interval and never block anything.

A booking is created in `SCHEDULED` and is moved by the admin with a status `action`. `CHECK_IN` advances `SCHEDULED` to `CHECKED_IN`, and requires both that the cycle is `COMBINE_WEEK` and that the current logical instant has reached the point half an hour before `starts_at` — a prospect may present up to thirty minutes early, no earlier. `COMPLETE` advances `CHECKED_IN` to `COMPLETED` with no further precondition; the resource interval is released, and for a `MEDICAL_EXAM` booking a medical evaluation may now be filed against it. `MARK_NO_SHOW` moves either `SCHEDULED` or `CHECKED_IN` to `NO_SHOW`, and on both paths requires that the current logical instant has reached or passed `ends_at`; on both paths, if the booking's `booking_type` is `MEDICAL_EXAM`, the prospect's `medical_clearance_status` becomes `UNVERIFIED`. `CANCEL` moves `SCHEDULED` to `CANCELLED`, with no precondition, and releases the interval so it no longer blocks new bookings; the same transition also fires as a cascade whenever a prospect withdraws or is ruled ineligible from an unreviewed posture, or has an accepted invitation withdrawn. `COMPLETED`, `NO_SHOW` and `CANCELLED` are all terminal.

The booking list is filterable by `prospect_id`, `medical_station_id`, `interview_room_id`, `club_id`, `booking_type` and `status`, with `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.4. Medical Findings, Consent, and Record Sharing

Only a `CombineMedicalExaminer` files a **medical evaluation**, and only against a booking that actually happened. The filing names the `combine_booking_id` it documents and the `prospect_id` it concerns, and carries a coded `findings_code` summary plus a `medical_grade` of `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_NOTE`, `FAIL_RECHECK` or `RED_FLAG`. The referenced booking must have `booking_type` `MEDICAL_EXAM`, `status` `COMPLETED`, and a `prospect_id` equal to the one supplied; any deviation is refused with `ERR_BOOKING_NOT_MEDICAL_COMPLETED`. The examiner's identifier is recorded as `filed_by_user_id`.

Two derived fields follow immediately from the grade. `confidentiality_tier` is `RESTRICTED` when `medical_grade` is `FAIL_RECHECK` or `RED_FLAG`, and `STANDARD` otherwise — that is, for `PASS` and `PASS_WITH_NOTE`. `disclosure_state` starts at `DISCLOSABLE` and `active_share_count` starts at 0. The grade also drives the prospect's clearance dimension, and the mapping is exhaustive: a grade of `PASS` or `PASS_WITH_NOTE` advances `medical_clearance_status` from `PENDING` to `CLEARED`; a grade of `RED_FLAG` advances it from `PENDING` to `FAILED`, after which any attempt to select that prospect is refused with `ERR_PROSPECT_NOT_SELECTABLE`; and a grade of `FAIL_RECHECK` leaves `medical_clearance_status` where it stands, because a recheck is neither a clearance nor a disqualification. The `UNVERIFIED` value on this dimension never comes from an evaluation at all — it is what a missed, declined or unverifiable examination leaves behind.

Evaluations are listable with optional `prospect_id`, `combine_booking_id` and `medical_grade` filters, plus `limit` and `offset`.

No club sees any of this without the player's permission. The consent dimension is the prospect's own, driven by a `ProspectAgent` with an `action` of `GRANT` or `REVOKE`, and it is genuinely cyclic — consent may be granted, revoked and granted again without bound. Granting from `NONE` advances `consent_status` to `GRANTED` and requires the prospect's `declaration_status` to be `DECLARED` and the supplied `consent_expires_at` to fall strictly after the current logical instant; it sets `consent_scope`, which is either `SUMMARY` or `FULL` and names the widest disclosure the consent permits, and it sets `consent_expires_at`. Granting again from `REVOKED` advances `consent_status` back to `GRANTED` under the single requirement that the supplied `consent_expires_at` falls strictly after the current logical instant, and likewise sets `consent_scope` and `consent_expires_at`. Critically, a renewed consent does not reinstate anything: shares that were previously revoked stay revoked, and a club that wants access again must request it again. Both `consent_scope` and `consent_expires_at` are null whenever `consent_status` is not `GRANTED`.

Revocation is the widest cascade in the system, and it runs in five distinct steps. Moving `consent_status` from `GRANTED` to `REVOKED` requires no precondition, and then: every `MedicalRecordShare` in `GRANTED` that belongs to any evaluation of this prospect becomes `REVOKED`; each affected evaluation recomputes its `active_share_count` and, on reaching 0, has its `disclosure_state` set to `SEALED`; if that leaves every one of the prospect's evaluations `SEALED`, the prospect's `medical_clearance_status` becomes `UNVERIFIED`, because no club can any longer verify the file; because the clearance has left `CLEARED`, every `PlayerContract` for this prospect still in `OFFERED` becomes `LAPSED` and the prospect's `signing_status` returns to `UNSIGNED`; and finally `consent_scope` and `consent_expires_at` are cleared to null.

A **medical record share** is a club's request for access. Either a `ClubGM` or a `ClubMedicalDirector` may file one, naming the `medical_evaluation_id` sought, the `club_id` requesting, the `scope` requested as `SUMMARY` or `FULL`, and `expected_medical_evaluation_version` as described in §2.8. The share is created in `REQUESTED`, the requester's identifier is stored as `requested_by_user_id` and the requester's declared capacity as `requested_by_role`, and any earlier share for the same `medical_evaluation_id` and the same `club_id` still sitting in `REQUESTED` is immediately marked `SUPERSEDED`. A request against an evaluation whose `disclosure_state` is `SEALED` is refused with `ERR_EVALUATION_SEALED`, and a `ClubGM` request against a `RESTRICTED` evaluation is refused with `ERR_CONFIDENTIALITY_TIER_VIOLATION`. Filing a share increments the evaluation's `version`.

Only a `LeagueDraftAdmin` decides a share, with an `action` of `GRANT`, `DENY` or `REVOKE` and an optional `denial_reason`. `GRANT` advances `REQUESTED` to `GRANTED` and has three preconditions: the prospect's `consent_status` is `GRANTED`, the target evaluation is still `DISCLOSABLE`, and the share's `scope` does not exceed the prospect's `consent_scope` — a `FULL` share against a `SUMMARY` consent is refused with `ERR_CONSENT_NOT_GRANTED`, while a `SUMMARY` share against a `FULL` consent is fine. Granting stamps `granted_at` with the current logical instant, computes `expires_at`, and increments the evaluation's `active_share_count` by one. `DENY` advances `REQUESTED` to `DENIED` and requires `denial_reason` to be present; it has no side effects. `REVOKE` advances `GRANTED` to `REVOKED`, requires `denial_reason` to be present when it is invoked directly rather than reached by the consent cascade, decrements the evaluation's `active_share_count` by one, and — when that count reaches 0 — sets the evaluation's `disclosure_state` to `SEALED`. A share may also reach `EXPIRED` from `GRANTED`, but never by request: that transition is driven by the logical clock sweep of §2.5 whenever `expires_at` is at or before the new instant, and it likewise decrements `active_share_count` and drives `effective_access` to `NONE`. `DENIED`, `REVOKED`, `EXPIRED` and `SUPERSEDED` are all terminal.

The access window itself is computed at the moment of granting, and it is the *earliest* of three independent caps: the cycle's `combine_week_end_at` plus seventy-five days, the cycle's `draft_start_at` plus three days, and the prospect's own `consent_expires_at`. Whichever of those three instants comes first becomes the share's `expires_at`; the tightest cap always wins, so a consent that lapses a week after the combine ends overrides both league caps. The prospect chain used to find those inputs runs from the share to its `MedicalEvaluation`, from there to the `CombineBooking` it documents, and from there to the `Prospect` and that prospect's cycle. `expires_at` is null until the share is granted, as is `granted_at`. The share's `effective_access` reports what the club can actually read right now: it equals the share's `scope` — `SUMMARY` or `FULL` — while `status` is `GRANTED` and the current logical instant is strictly before `expires_at`, and it is `NONE` in every other case, including at the exact `expires_at` instant.

Shares are listable with optional `medical_evaluation_id`, `club_id`, `status` and `scope` filters, plus `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.5. Building the Draft Order: Lottery, Seeding, Forfeits and Compensatory Awards

Once the combine week has closed and the cycle sits at `ORDER_PENDING`, the league runs the lottery. This is a self-transition — the cycle stays at `ORDER_PENDING` while its derived order fields change — and it requires that `lottery_completed` is still false and that `club_count` is at least 2. The caller supplies `drawn_combination_sequence`, the ordered list of combination values the league drew on stage. Every value in that list must lie between 1 and the total number of combinations assigned across the eligible pool, inclusive, and the list must name enough distinct clubs to fill every lottery slot; a value out of range or a sequence that cannot fill the slots is refused with `ERR_LOTTERY_SEQUENCE_INVALID`. Running the lottery sets `lottery_completed` to true, sets `lottery_slot_club_ids`, and sets `pre_lottery_rank` and `draft_order_index` on every registered club.

The ranking runs in four steps, in this order.

**Step one — seed the non-playoff pool.** The pool is every club of this cycle whose `registration_status` is `REGISTERED` and whose `playoff_qualified` is false. Order that pool by `prior_season_win_pct` ascending, so the worst record seeds first; break ties by `strength_of_schedule` ascending; break remaining ties by `head_to_head_rank` ascending; break any remaining tie by `created_at` ascending. Assign `pre_lottery_rank` as the 1-based position in that order. Playoff clubs receive `pre_lottery_rank` 0. A club whose `registration_status` is `WITHDRAWN` is excluded from both pools entirely and receives `draft_order_index` 0.

**Step two — lay out the combination ranges.** Walk the non-playoff pool in ascending `pre_lottery_rank` order with a cursor that starts at 1. Each club takes a contiguous block of combination numbers beginning at the cursor and running through the cursor plus that club's `lottery_combinations` minus one; the cursor then advances by that club's `lottery_combinations`. The blocks are disjoint and contiguous, and the total number of assigned combinations is the cursor's final value minus one — equivalently, the sum of `lottery_combinations` across the pool.

**Step three — resolve the drawn sequence into slots.** The number of lottery slots to fill is the smaller of four and the number of clubs in the non-playoff pool. Walk `drawn_combination_sequence` in the order the caller supplied it. For each value, find the one club whose block contains it. If that club already holds a slot, skip the value and move on — a duplicate draw awards nothing. Otherwise append that club to the slot list. Stop as soon as the slot list is full. The cycle's `lottery_slot_club_ids` is then the list of those clubs' identifiers, in slot order, so the first entry won the first slot.

**Step four — build the final order.** Start with the slot winners in slot order. Then append every remaining non-playoff club in ascending `pre_lottery_rank` order, skipping those already placed. Then append the playoff clubs — every club of this cycle whose `registration_status` is `REGISTERED` and whose `playoff_qualified` is true — ordered by `playoff_exit_round` ascending, then by `prior_season_win_pct` ascending, then by `strength_of_schedule` ascending, then by `created_at` ascending. Every playoff club therefore drafts after every non-playoff club, no matter how poor its record. Each club's `draft_order_index` is its 1-based position in that combined order. Ranks are whole integers, the two percentage inputs are compared at three decimal places, and a club's `id` is never consulted as an ordering input — the order is fully reproducible from published business fields plus creation timestamps.

With the order resolved, the admin finalizes it, which advances the cycle from `ORDER_PENDING` to `ORDER_FINAL`. Finalizing requires `lottery_completed` to be true and `order_finalized_at` to still be null — the mint runs exactly once. It mints one pick per club per round, `rounds_count` multiplied by `club_count` rows in total, each created in `PENDING`. A pick's `original_club_id` records the club whose slot produced it and never changes thereafter; its `owning_club_id` records the current owner and does change when trades are accepted. `round_number` is the round, `pick_in_round` is the 1-based position inside the round taken directly from the owning club's `draft_order_index`, and `overall_number` is the 1-based position across the whole draft. `draft_cycle_id` on a pick is minted here and is never client-supplied. Both `is_compensatory` and `compensatory_sequence` default to false and 0 for these regular picks, and `selected_prospect_id`, `selection_source`, `pass_reason`, `on_clock_started_at` and `deadline_at` all start null, with `timeouts_used` at 0 and `out_of_order_authorized` false.

Forfeits are honoured at mint time. For each club, the picks whose `round_number` appears in that club's `forfeited_round_numbers` are not minted in `PENDING`; they are minted directly in `FORFEITED` with `chart_value_points` forced to 0.0. Finalizing also stamps `order_finalized_at` with the current logical instant and computes `udfa_window_close_at` as seventy-two hours after `draft_start_at`. The response reports `created_pick_count`, `compensatory_pick_count` and `first_overall_pick_id`.

A pick may also be forfeited later, by a `LeagueDraftAdmin` submitting a `FORFEIT` clock action against a `PENDING` pick while the cycle is `ORDER_FINAL` or `DRAFT_LIVE`. That single action has five effects: the pick's `chart_value_points` is set to 0.0; its `round_number` is appended to the `forfeited_round_numbers` of the club named by `original_club_id`; the owning club's `draft_capital_points` and `trade_authorization_tier` are recomputed; every `PickTrade` of the owning club still in `PROPOSED` or `COUNTERED` becomes `VOIDED_BY_CAPITAL` if that recomputation drops the tier to `SUSPENDED`; and the cycle's `pick_inventory_chart_points` and `pick_inventory_count` are recomputed. `FORFEITED` is terminal — a forfeited pick can never be reinstated, selected or traded.

Compensatory picks reward clubs that lost more talent than they acquired. A `ClubGM` submits a **compensatory claim** naming the `draft_cycle_id`, the `club_id` claiming or being charged, a `claim_type` of `DEPARTURE` or `ARRIVAL`, the `player_name` whose movement is claimed, the `departed_apy_cents` average per-year value of the movement in whole cents (which may not be negative), the `snap_pct` prior-season participation share as a two-decimal value from 0.00 through 1.00 inclusive, and an optional `postseason_honor` of `NONE`, `ALL_CONFERENCE` or `ALL_LEAGUE` that defaults to `NONE`. Departure claims earn credit; arrival claims cancel departures one for one. A claim is created in `SUBMITTED`, and both score variants and the `qualifies` flag are computed immediately as a preview.

The **authoritative score** is built as follows. The base figure is `departed_apy_cents` divided by one million — that is, the movement's average annual value expressed in dollars — held as an exact quotient without intermediate rounding. A banded snap factor is then chosen from the prospect's participation share: the factor is 1.00 when `snap_pct` is at least 0.65; otherwise 0.85 when `snap_pct` is at least 0.45; otherwise 0.70 when `snap_pct` is at least 0.25; otherwise 0.50. An honours bonus is added as a flat term: 40 when `postseason_honor` is `ALL_LEAGUE`, 20 when it is `ALL_CONFERENCE`, and 0 when it is `NONE`. The `claim_score` is the base figure multiplied by the banded snap factor, plus the honours bonus, rounded half up to two decimal places. The `qualifies` flag is true when `claim_score` is at least 250.00, inclusive at that boundary. Because the factor is banded, every participation share inside a band scores identically.

The **advisory score** answers a different question and is never enforced. `advisory_snap_adjusted_score` is the same base figure multiplied by the *raw* `snap_pct` rather than by the band, plus the same honours bonus, rounded half up to two decimal places. For any participation share strictly inside a band the two figures diverge, and a claim can sit below 250.00 on the advisory figure while authoritatively qualifying. Clubs receive the advisory figure as a what-if number for their own planning; it decides nothing.

The admin verifies or rejects each claim before the award pass runs, with an `action` of `VERIFY` or `REJECT`. `VERIFY` advances `SUBMITTED` to `VERIFIED` and is permitted only while the cycle is `COMBINE_WEEK` or `ORDER_PENDING`; it freezes both `claim_score` and `advisory_snap_adjusted_score` at their current values. `REJECT` advances `SUBMITTED` to `REJECTED` with no precondition and no side effect. Only claims in `VERIFIED` enter the award pass; claims still in `SUBMITTED` and claims in `REJECTED` are excluded.

The award pass itself runs once, inside the finalize operation, and it is per club. For each club, the qualifying departures are its `VERIFIED` `DEPARTURE` claims with `qualifies` true, and the qualifying arrivals are its `VERIFIED` `ARRIVAL` claims with `qualifies` true; the net entitlement is the count of qualifying departures minus the count of qualifying arrivals, floored at zero. The qualifying departures are ranked by `claim_score` descending, then by `created_at` ascending, and the number actually awarded is the smallest of three figures: the net entitlement, the per-club cap of four awards, and the league-wide cap of eight awards less whatever the pass has already granted. Because clubs are processed against a single league-wide ordering that uses the same key — highest `claim_score` first, earliest `created_at` as the tiebreak — the league cap is consumed by the highest-scoring claims in the league, not by whichever club happened to be considered first.

Each awarded claim advances from `VERIFIED` to `AWARDED`, and its round is banded from the authoritative score, inclusive at each lower bound: `awarded_round` is 3 when `claim_score` is at least 1200, otherwise 4 when it is at least 700, otherwise 5 when it is at least 450, otherwise 6 when it is at least 300, and otherwise 7. `awarded_sequence` is the 1-based order among the compensatory awards landing in that same round. Each award mints one further `DraftPick` with `is_compensatory` true and `compensatory_sequence` equal to the award's `awarded_sequence`, appended after every regular pick at the end of the awarded round, so compensatory picks sit at the tail of their round in `overall_number` order. Every remaining `VERIFIED` claim — one that does not qualify, one whose club or the league has already exhausted its cap, or one cancelled out by an arrival — advances from `VERIFIED` to `REJECTED_CAP`, with `awarded_round` and `awarded_sequence` left at 0. `REJECTED`, `AWARDED` and `REJECTED_CAP` are all terminal.

Claims are listable with optional `draft_cycle_id`, `club_id`, `claim_type` and `status` filters, plus `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.6. Trading Picks: Chart Valuation, Negotiation Rounds and Conditional Obligations

Trading is only possible once the order exists, so a trade may be proposed, countered or accepted only while the cycle is `ORDER_FINAL` or `DRAFT_LIVE`. Every pick carries a **chart value** minted at finalize time: `chart_value_points` is the round's chart base multiplied by a decay factor of 0.94 raised to the power of `pick_in_round` minus one, rounded half up to one decimal place. The chart bases are 1000.0 for round one, 420.0 for round two, 250.0 for round three, 150.0 for round four, 90.0 for round five, 50.0 for round six and 25.0 for round seven. A `FORFEITED` pick's `chart_value_points` is forced to 0.0 regardless of where it sits in the chart.

Chart value aggregates into a club's negotiating capital. `draft_capital_points` is the sum of `chart_value_points` over the picks whose `owning_club_id` is that club and whose `status` is `PENDING` or `ON_CLOCK`, carried to one decimal place; picks that are `USED`, `PASSED`, `FORFEITED` or `LAPSED` contribute nothing. From that figure the club's `trade_authorization_tier` is derived on every read: it is `FULL` when `draft_capital_points` is at least 1200.0, `LIMITED` when it is at least 900.0 but below 1200.0, and `SUSPENDED` when it is below 900.0. A club at `SUSPENDED` is out of the market: proposing, countering or accepting with either party in that tier is refused with `ERR_TRADE_AUTHORIZATION_SUSPENDED`.

A `ClubGM` opens a negotiation by naming the `draft_cycle_id`, the `proposing_club_id` (which must be the acting club), the `receiving_club_id` counterparty, the `offered_pick_ids` it is sending, and the `requested_pick_ids` it is asking for. The two clubs must differ and the acting club must be a party to the trade, else `ERR_SELF_TRADE_FORBIDDEN`. Every pick in `offered_pick_ids` must currently be owned by `proposing_club_id`, every pick in `requested_pick_ids` must currently be owned by `receiving_club_id`, and all of them must be `PENDING`; a pick that is `FORFEITED`, `USED`, `PASSED`, `LAPSED` or on the clock may not appear on either side, and any violation is refused with `ERR_PICK_NOT_OWNED_BY_CLUB`. A new proposal is created in `PROPOSED` with `round_index` 1 and `last_actor_club_id` set to `proposing_club_id`, which means the proposing club may not be the next to act.

Two valuations are computed on every proposal and every counter, and they answer different questions. The **authoritative chart valuation** treats the picks as pure positional currency and ignores contract economics entirely. Let the incoming total be the sum of `chart_value_points` across `requested_pick_ids` and the outgoing total be the same sum across `offered_pick_ids`. Then `chart_balance_points` is the incoming total minus the outgoing total, rounded half up to one decimal place, stated from the proposing club's point of view — a positive figure means the proposing club is gaining chart value. The `imbalance_ratio` is the absolute value of `chart_balance_points` divided by the largest of the incoming total, the outgoing total and 1.0, rounded half up to four decimal places; the 1.0 floor keeps the ratio well defined when both sides are worthless. Acceptance is allowed only when `imbalance_ratio` is at most 0.15, inclusive at that boundary; a wider gap is refused with `ERR_TRADE_VALUE_IMBALANCED`. This is the only variant that gates anything.

The **advisory market-surplus valuation** values each pick by the gap between what the market pays for that round's talent and what the pick's slotted rookie contract will cost. For one pick, the surplus is the round's positional market annual value in cents minus that exact pick's `slot_total_value_cents`, that difference divided by one million cents, rounded half up to one decimal place. The positional market annual values are 2,200,000,000 cents for round one, 1,400,000,000 for round two, 900,000,000 for round three, 700,000,000 for round four, 560,000,000 for round five, 480,000,000 for round six and 420,000,000 for round seven. Then `surplus_balance_points` is the sum of the per-pick surpluses across `requested_pick_ids` minus the same sum across `offered_pick_ids`, rounded half up to one decimal place. Because slotted cost falls far faster across the rounds than market price does, this figure can carry the opposite sign from `chart_balance_points` for the very same package — a trade the chart calls a loss can be a surplus gain. It is published to front offices as guidance and it never gates acceptance.

Countering replaces the package and consumes a negotiation round. The counterparty supplies fresh `offered_pick_ids` and `requested_pick_ids`; the trade moves from `PROPOSED` to `COUNTERED`, or from `COUNTERED` back to `COUNTERED` on a subsequent round. Both paths require that the acting club is not the current `last_actor_club_id` — clubs must alternate — and that `round_index` is below three, else `ERR_TRADE_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`. The first counter, out of `PROPOSED`, additionally requires that the acting club's `trade_authorization_tier` is not `SUSPENDED`. Every counter increments `round_index` by one, replaces `offered_pick_ids` and `requested_pick_ids` with the new package, recomputes `chart_balance_points`, `imbalance_ratio` and `surplus_balance_points`, and sets `last_actor_club_id` to the acting club.

Acceptance is available from both `PROPOSED` and `COUNTERED`, with identical preconditions and identical effects on both paths. The five preconditions are: the acting club is not the current `last_actor_club_id`; the cycle's `posture` is not `FROZEN`; neither club's `trade_authorization_tier` is `SUSPENDED`; `imbalance_ratio` is at most 0.15; and every offered and requested pick is still `PENDING` and still owned by the side that declared it. On success the trade moves to `ACCEPTED`, `owning_club_id` is swapped on every offered and every requested pick, and `draft_capital_points` and `trade_authorization_tier` are recomputed for both clubs. Acceptance deliberately leaves the conditional dimension untouched, because that dimension was already set when the trade was proposed.

Three terminations exist, each taking an `action` and, for the league's own, a `reason`. `REJECT` moves `PROPOSED` to `REJECTED` or `COUNTERED` to `REJECTED`, and on both paths requires that the acting club is not the current `last_actor_club_id` — you reject the other side's offer, not your own. `WITHDRAW` moves `PROPOSED` to `WITHDRAWN` and requires the opposite: the acting club *is* the current `last_actor_club_id`, because withdrawing is retracting your own outstanding offer. `RESCIND` is reserved to a `LeagueDraftAdmin`, moves `ACCEPTED` to `RESCINDED`, and requires that every traded pick is still `PENDING` and that `reason` is present. Rescission is a true compensating action: it restores `owning_club_id` on every traded pick to its pre-acceptance owner, recomputes `draft_capital_points` and `trade_authorization_tier` for both clubs, and sets `conditional_status` to `VOID` if it was `OPEN`. Because nothing else changes, the cycle's `pick_inventory_chart_points` reads identically before the acceptance and after the rescission.

A trade may also die without anyone acting on it. When a forfeit drops either party club's `trade_authorization_tier` to `SUSPENDED`, every trade of that club still in `PROPOSED` moves to `VOIDED_BY_CAPITAL`, and so does every trade of that club still in `COUNTERED`; on both paths, if the trade's `conditional_status` was `OPEN`, it becomes `VOID`. `REJECTED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `VOIDED_BY_CAPITAL` and `RESCINDED` are all terminal on the negotiation dimension.

The conditional dimension runs in parallel with the negotiation dimension and is entirely independent of it. A proposal may attach a conditional future pick by supplying `conditional_round_number`, an integer from 1 through 7 inclusive, together with `conditional_condition_code`, the event that must occur for the pick to convey, drawn from `PLAYOFF_BERTH`, `SNAP_THRESHOLD` and `PRO_BOWL_SELECTION`. From the code the server derives `conditional_probability_tier`: `LIKELY` for `PLAYOFF_BERTH`, `POSSIBLE` for `SNAP_THRESHOLD`, and `REMOTE` for `PRO_BOWL_SELECTION`. When a condition is attached, `conditional_status` is set to `OPEN` at proposal time; when none is attached, `conditional_status` stays at its default of `NONE`, `conditional_round_number` and `conditional_condition_code` are null, and `conditional_conveyed_overall_number` stays 0.

Only a `LeagueDraftAdmin` resolves a condition, supplying a boolean `condition_satisfied`, and only on a trade whose negotiation dimension has reached `ACCEPTED`. When `condition_satisfied` is true, the outcome depends on the probability tier — and the split is deliberate, because remote conditions are settled in the following year's cycle rather than this one. For a tier of `LIKELY` or `POSSIBLE`, `conditional_status` moves from `OPEN` to `MET_CONVEYED` and `conditional_conveyed_overall_number` is set to the highest `overall_number` among the trade's traded picks. For a tier of `REMOTE`, `conditional_status` moves from `OPEN` to `DEFERRED` instead: the conditional pick rolls forward to the following cycle and `conditional_conveyed_overall_number` stays 0. When `condition_satisfied` is false, `conditional_status` moves from `OPEN` to `UNMET_EXTINGUISHED` with no side effects, whatever the tier. `MET_CONVEYED`, `UNMET_EXTINGUISHED`, `DEFERRED` and `VOID` are all terminal on this dimension.

Trades are listable with optional `draft_cycle_id`, `proposing_club_id`, `receiving_club_id`, `last_actor_club_id`, `status` and `conditional_status` filters, plus `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.7. Running the Selection Clock

Once the cycle is `DRAFT_LIVE`, the league drives the draft forward through a repeatable **clock advance** checkpoint. Advancing does two jobs. If no pick currently holds the clock, it puts the lowest-numbered `PENDING` pick on the clock. If a pick holds the clock and that clock has run out, it passes that pick and moves the clock along — repeatedly, until it reaches a pick whose clock is still running or runs out of picks.

Putting a pick on the clock moves it from `PENDING` to `ON_CLOCK`. Three conditions must hold: the cycle is `DRAFT_LIVE`, no other pick in the cycle is already `ON_CLOCK`, and this pick has the lowest `overall_number` among the cycle's `PENDING` picks. The transition stamps `on_clock_started_at`, computes `deadline_at`, and updates the cycle's `current_overall_number` to the pick's `overall_number`.

The deadline is computed from the round. The allotted minutes are 10 for round one, 7 for round two, 5 for rounds three, four, five and six, and 4 for round seven. If the cycle's `compressed_final_day` is true and the pick's `round_number` is 5 or higher, one minute is subtracted from that allotment — so on a compressed final day rounds five and six run to four minutes and round seven to three. Then `deadline_at` is `on_clock_started_at` plus that allotment, plus two minutes for each timeout already recorded in the pick's `timeouts_used`. A pick's clock is expired when the current logical instant is at or after `deadline_at`, and a timeout may only be spent while the current logical instant is strictly before `deadline_at`.

When the clock advance finds an expired pick, that pick moves from `ON_CLOCK` to `PASSED` with `pass_reason` set to `EXPIRED`, and the `PENDING` pick with the next-lowest `overall_number` is put on the clock — but with `on_clock_started_at` set to the *expired pick's* `deadline_at`, not to the current instant. That is what makes the cascade work: because each successor's clock starts where its predecessor's ended, a single advance call issued long after the draft stalled will pass every pick whose notional window has already elapsed, one after another, until it reaches a pick whose recomputed `deadline_at` still lies ahead of the current instant. The response reports `expired_pick_id`, `on_clock_pick_id`, `passed_count` and `lapsed_count`.

If the advance finds that no pick in the cycle remains `PENDING` or `ON_CLOCK` — every pick sits in `USED`, `PASSED`, `FORFEITED` or `LAPSED` — the cycle itself moves from `DRAFT_LIVE` to `DRAFT_COMPLETE`. That transition converts every pick still in `PASSED` to `LAPSED` and sets `current_overall_number` to 0. Lapsing is not merely bookkeeping: a lapsed pick's `slot_first_year_cap_charge_cents` becomes part of the owning club's undrafted-signing credit base, as §3.8 describes. `USED`, `FORFEITED` and `LAPSED` are terminal pick states.

Four clock actions are available, split between the club that owns the pick and the league.

A `ClubGM` may **pass** the pick it holds on the clock, moving it from `ON_CLOCK` to `PASSED` with `pass_reason` set to `VOLUNTARY`; the cycle must be `DRAFT_LIVE` and the current logical instant must be strictly before `deadline_at`, else `ERR_CLOCK_ALREADY_EXPIRED`. Passing immediately puts the next `PENDING` pick on the clock with `on_clock_started_at` set to the current instant. A passed pick is not lost — its club may still use it at any time before the draft completes.

A `ClubGM` may also **take a timeout**, a self-transition that leaves the pick `ON_CLOCK`. It requires the cycle to be `DRAFT_LIVE`, the current logical instant to be strictly before `deadline_at`, and the owning club's `timeouts_remaining` to be greater than zero, else `ERR_NO_TIMEOUTS_REMAINING`. Each timeout extends `deadline_at` by two minutes, increments the pick's `timeouts_used` by one, and decrements the club's `timeouts_remaining` by one. Every club starts each cycle with two timeouts for the entire draft, so a club that spends both on its first-round pick has none for the rest of the weekend.

A `LeagueDraftAdmin` may **forfeit** a `PENDING` pick, as §3.5 details, and may **authorize an out-of-order selection**. The latter is a self-transition that leaves the pick `PENDING`; it requires the cycle to be `DRAFT_LIVE` and the pick's `out_of_order_authorized` to still be false, and it sets `out_of_order_authorized` to true.

Selections are made by the `ClubGM` that owns the pick, naming the `prospect_id` it is taking and, where relevant, an `out_of_order_ack` boolean. Whatever the path, the prospect must be selectable: `eligibility_status` `ELIGIBLE`, `medical_clearance_status` anything other than `FAILED`, `declaration_status` `DECLARED`, and `drafted_pick_overall_number` still 0 so that the prospect is undrafted. A prospect failing any of those is refused with `ERR_PROSPECT_NOT_SELECTABLE`. Three source states admit a selection, and they differ in their preconditions and in the `selection_source` they record.

- From `ON_CLOCK` the pick moves to `USED` with `selection_source` `ON_TIME`. This requires the cycle to be `DRAFT_LIVE`, the current logical instant to be strictly before `deadline_at`, the pick's `owning_club_id` to equal the acting club, and the prospect to be selectable. It sets `selected_prospect_id`, sets the prospect's `drafted_pick_overall_number` to this pick's `overall_number`, recomputes the club's `draft_capital_points` and `trade_authorization_tier`, and puts the next `PENDING` pick on the clock with `on_clock_started_at` set to the current instant.
- From `PASSED` the pick moves to `USED` with `selection_source` `AFTER_PASS`. This requires the cycle to be `DRAFT_LIVE`, the pick's `owning_club_id` to equal the acting club, and the prospect to be selectable — there is no deadline test, because a passed pick has no running clock. It sets `selected_prospect_id`, sets the prospect's `drafted_pick_overall_number`, and clears `pass_reason`. It does not disturb whichever pick currently holds the clock.
- From `PENDING` the pick moves to `USED` with `selection_source` `OUT_OF_ORDER`. This requires the cycle to be `DRAFT_LIVE`, the pick's `out_of_order_authorized` to be true, another pick to currently hold the clock, and the request to carry `out_of_order_ack` true; a `PENDING` pick used while another pick holds the clock without both the league authorization and the acknowledgement is refused with `ERR_OUT_OF_ORDER_NOT_AUTHORIZED`. The pick that holds the clock keeps its `deadline_at` entirely unchanged — jumping the queue steals no time from the club on the clock.

Picks are listable with optional `draft_cycle_id`, `owning_club_id`, `original_club_id`, `selected_prospect_id`, `round_number` and `status` filters, plus `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.8. Contract Slotting, the Rookie Pool and the Undrafted Free Agent Window

Rookie compensation in this league is not negotiated — it is slotted by pick position. A `ClubGM` offers a contract by naming the `prospect_id` being signed, the `club_id` signing, the contract `origin` of `DRAFTED` or `UDFA`, and `expected_prospect_version` as §2.8 requires. A `DRAFTED` offer must additionally name the `draft_pick_id` it is slotted to, and that pick's `status` must be `USED`, its `selected_prospect_id` must equal the supplied `prospect_id`, and its `owning_club_id` must equal the supplied `club_id`. A `UDFA` offer must omit `draft_pick_id` entirely and must instead supply `udfa_signing_bonus_cents`, the bonus the club is offering. Any mismatch on either path is refused with `ERR_CONTRACT_PICK_MISMATCH`.

The slotted value is derived as follows, and the order of the rounding steps is part of the specification.

The year-one base for a pick position starts from the round's base amount and steps down by the round's per-slot decrement for each position past the first: it is the round base minus the round's decrement multiplied by `pick_in_round` minus one. The round bases in whole cents are 120,000,000 for round one, 100,000,000 for round two, 92,000,000 for round three, 88,000,000 for round four, 85,000,000 for round five, 82,000,000 for round six and 80,000,000 for round seven. The per-slot decrements in whole cents are 2,000,000 for round one, 800,000 for round two, 400,000 for round three, 200,000 for round four, 120,000 for round five, 80,000 for round six and 40,000 for round seven. The year-one figure is then the larger of that computed base and the league minimum of 79,500,000 cents, so deep picks never fall below the minimum. A `UDFA` contract has no pick slot at all, and so uses the league minimum of 79,500,000 cents as its year-one figure directly.

Contract length is fixed by origin: `contract_years` is 4 for a `DRAFTED` contract and 3 for a `UDFA` contract. The per-year base values escalate at 4.25 percent, and each year is rounded before the next is derived. Concretely: the first year's stored value is the year-one figure rounded half up to whole cents; each subsequent year's stored value is the *previous year's already-rounded* value multiplied by 1.0425, rounded half up to whole cents; repeat until there are `contract_years` values. `total_value_cents` is the sum of those per-year rounded values. Rounding per year before summing, rather than escalating in full precision and rounding once, is deliberate and the two approaches do not agree.

The signing bonus depends on origin. For a `DRAFTED` contract, `signing_bonus_cents` is `total_value_cents` multiplied by the round's bonus share, rounded half up to whole cents; the bonus shares are 0.60 for round one, 0.40 for round two, 0.25 for round three, 0.15 for round four, 0.10 for round five, 0.08 for round six and 0.05 for round seven. For a `UDFA` contract, `signing_bonus_cents` is exactly the client-supplied `udfa_signing_bonus_cents`, with no derivation.

The cap figures use a pinned proration order. First compute the per-year bonus share as `signing_bonus_cents` divided by `contract_years`, rounded half up to whole cents. Then `first_year_cap_charge_cents` is the first year's stored base value plus that per-year bonus share. And `total_cap_commitment_cents` is `total_value_cents` plus the per-year bonus share multiplied by `contract_years`. It must not be computed as `total_value_cents` plus `signing_bonus_cents`: because the per-year share is rounded before being multiplied back up, the pinned order can differ from that shortcut by as much as one cent per contract year.

The same slotting arithmetic runs at draft-order finalize time for every minted pick, populating four read-only fields on the pick from that pick's own position and the four-year drafted length: `slot_total_value_cents`, `slot_signing_bonus_cents`, `slot_first_year_cap_charge_cents` and `slot_total_cap_commitment_cents`. These are the figures the advisory surplus valuation of §3.6 reads, and the last of them is also what a lapsed pick contributes to the undrafted credit base.

A new offer is created in `OFFERED`, advances the prospect's `signing_status` from `UNSIGNED` to `OFFER_OUT`, and increments `Prospect.version` by one. If the same club already has an `OFFERED` contract outstanding for the same prospect, that earlier contract is moved to `LAPSED` — this is precisely the supersession the version check of §2.8 protects. `contract_years`, `total_value_cents`, `signing_bonus_cents`, `first_year_cap_charge_cents` and `total_cap_commitment_cents` are all computed at offer time; `signed_at` stays null while the contract is `OFFERED`.

The contract lifecycle has four states — `OFFERED`, `SIGNED`, `VOIDED` and `LAPSED`, the last two terminal — and two actions. **Signing** is the `ProspectAgent`'s move alone: the acting identifier must equal the contract's `prospect_id`, the cycle's `posture` must not be `FROZEN`, the contract must currently be `OFFERED`, and for a `UDFA` contract the current logical instant must be strictly before `udfa_window_close_at`, else `ERR_UDFA_WINDOW_CLOSED`. Signing moves the contract from `OFFERED` to `SIGNED`, stamps `signed_at` with the current logical instant, advances the prospect's `signing_status` from `OFFER_OUT` to `SIGNED`, and recomputes the club's `rookie_pool_remaining_cents`.

**Voiding** is the signing club's move: the acting club must equal the contract's `club_id`. From `OFFERED` it moves the contract to `VOIDED` and returns the prospect's `signing_status` to `UNSIGNED`. From `SIGNED` it also moves the contract to `VOIDED` and returns `signing_status` to `UNSIGNED`, and additionally restores the club's `rookie_pool_remaining_cents` to its pre-signing value. A contract also reaches `LAPSED` without anyone acting on it, in three circumstances: a newer `OFFERED` contract arrives from the same club for the same prospect; the prospect revokes medical consent in a way that leaves the file unverifiable; or the cycle moves from `UDFA_WINDOW` to `CLOSED`. On every lapse the prospect's `signing_status` returns to `UNSIGNED` unless the prospect already holds a `SIGNED` contract.

The rookie pool is a simple, exact-integer ledger, recomputed on every contract status change and on every read of the club. `rookie_pool_remaining_cents` is `rookie_pool_total_cents` minus the sum of `first_year_cap_charge_cents` over that club's contracts whose `status` is `SIGNED`; contracts in `OFFERED`, `VOIDED` or `LAPSED` are excluded from that sum entirely. The gate applies at signing time: if the club's signed first-year charges *after* this signing would exceed `rookie_pool_total_cents`, the signing is refused with `ERR_ROOKIE_POOL_EXCEEDED`. Because the void path restores the figure exactly, a club's `rookie_pool_remaining_cents` plus its signed first-year charges always equals `rookie_pool_total_cents`, no matter how many sign-and-void sequences it runs.

The undrafted free agent window opens when the cycle moves from `DRAFT_COMPLETE` to `UDFA_WINDOW`, and that transition computes and freezes an undrafted budget for every `REGISTERED` club in four steps.

**Step one — the base pool.** The base is a flat league allowance of 40,000,000 cents plus a ten percent rollover of whatever rookie cap the club has left: take the club's `rookie_pool_remaining_cents` as read at the instant of the transition, multiply it by 0.10, round half up to whole cents, and add that to the flat allowance.

**Step two — the credit rate tier.** The rate is keyed on the base pool figure from step one, and on nothing else. The rate is 0.25 when the base pool is at least 120,000,000 cents; otherwise 0.40 when the base pool is at least 60,000,000 cents; otherwise 0.55. Note that the rate falls as the base pool rises — the credit is a subsidy for clubs with little rollover. This tier must be selected before the credit is added, never from the post-credit total; reversing the two steps changes the tier and therefore the answer.

**Step three — the lapsed-pick credit.** Sum `slot_first_year_cap_charge_cents` over the picks whose `owning_club_id` is this club and whose `status` is `LAPSED`; picks in `USED`, `PASSED`, `PENDING` or `FORFEITED` contribute nothing. Multiply that sum by the credit rate from step two, round half up to whole cents, and add it to the base pool. The result is `udfa_pool_total_cents`, which is 0 for every club before the draft completes and is frozen at this transition.

**Step four — the waterfall.** As undrafted contracts are offered, the pool is drawn down in `created_at` ascending order across the club's `UDFA`-origin contracts whose `status` is `OFFERED` or `SIGNED`. Starting from `udfa_pool_total_cents`, each such contract's `signing_bonus_cents` is subtracted in turn; if a contract's bonus exceeds what remains at its turn, the offer is refused with `ERR_UDFA_POOL_EXHAUSTED`. Whatever survives the walk is the club's `udfa_pool_remaining_cents`. Contracts in `VOIDED` or `LAPSED` release their draw and no longer consume the pool.

The window closes at `udfa_window_close_at`, which the finalize operation set to seventy-two hours after `draft_start_at`. Contracts are listable with optional `prospect_id`, `club_id`, `draft_pick_id`, `origin` and `status` filters, plus `limit` and `offset`.
