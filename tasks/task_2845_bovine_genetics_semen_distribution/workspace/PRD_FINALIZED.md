# Bovine Genetics & Semen Distribution API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A cattle genetics company lives or dies by the traceability of a single straw of semen. Between the moment a promising young bull is spotted in a breeder's barn and the moment one of his doses is thawed over a cow in another hemisphere, the business must record an acquisition contract, a quarantine period, a battery of disease assays, a booked collection window on an approved bench, a laboratory quality verdict, a cryopreserved lot with a per-dose carrying value, a country-specific export certificate signed by a veterinarian who did not run the tests, a shipment into a distributor's liquid-nitrogen tank, and finally an insemination report that closes the invoice. Today that chain lives in six disconnected spreadsheets, and the company cannot answer the two questions its auditors and its accountants ask most often: *which bull was this straw cut from, and what is it worth today?*

This document specifies the API that replaces those spreadsheets. It is mounted under `/genetics`. The system is deliberately built around two hard invariants. The first is **dose conservation**: every physical straw ever cryopreserved is accounted for in exactly one of five buckets on its lot, and no operation may create or destroy a dose without moving it out of another bucket. The second is **valuation reversibility**: when a bull's genetic proof is updated, the entire straw inventory carrying his genetics is revalued in one cascade, and if that proof is later withdrawn the inventory returns to exactly the figure it carried before — to the cent.

Around those invariants sits a compliance regime. Regulatory approval attaches to facilities, health currency attaches to bulls, and export eligibility attaches to the intersection of a bull's test history and a destination country's rule set. Because certification is the point where money and public health meet, two segregation-of-duties barriers are enforced in the data model itself: the laboratory technician who judged an ejaculate may not be the one who releases the straws cut from it, and the veterinarian who recorded a bull's disease results may not be the one who signs his export paperwork. Time itself is a first-class, server-controlled input, so that expiry, isolation and liquid-nitrogen decay are all reproducible rather than dependent on wall-clock drift.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to this API carries two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the capability set that principal is exercising. There are seven roles, spelled exactly as follows: `ProgramDirector`, `FacilityManager`, `StaffVeterinarian`, `LabTechnician`, `ExportCoordinator`, `Distributor`, and `BreedingAdvisor`.

The `ProgramDirector` is the genetics programme owner: sire acquisition and retirement, filing and publishing genomic evaluations, rolling proofs back, the commercial gates on consignment approval, rejection and dispatch, and ownership of the logical clock. A `FacilityManager` runs exactly one collection facility's bench — booking slots, cancelling them, and recording collections performed. The `StaffVeterinarian` is the herd-health authority: ordering and resulting health tests, clearing bulls for collection, suspending and reinstating them, and signing export certificates. A `LabTechnician` works in the processing laboratory: quality-evaluating ejaculates, cutting straw lots, and releasing or condemning them. The `ExportCoordinator` handles regulatory affairs — maintaining destination rule sets, drafting certificates and submitting them to authorities — but may never sign one, because issuance belongs to a veterinarian. A `Distributor` is a field distribution partner that receives consignments into its own tanks, services them with liquid nitrogen, reports usage, returns unused stock and registers customer herds. A `BreedingAdvisor` is the customer-facing programme adviser who asks the selector engine for mating recommendations and records the customer's decision, with no authority over inventory, health or certification.

Role identity is not uniform, and the difference matters for every ownership check in the system:

- When `X-User-Role` is `FacilityManager`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Facility`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Facility` compare the resource's facility foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, a `FacilityManager` may only address a `CollectionBooking` whose `facility_id` equals its own `X-User-ID`, may only create a booking whose body `facility_id` equals its own `X-User-ID`, and may only house a sire into the facility its `X-User-ID` identifies. A violation is rejected with `ERR_FACILITY_SCOPE` (HTTP 403), and that check governs booking creation, booking completion, booking cancellation and facility approval-status changes.
- When `X-User-Role` is `Distributor`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Distributor`; ownership checks compare the addressed resource's `distributor_id` to `X-User-ID`. For a `ConsignmentTransfer` this is the transfer's own `distributor_id`; for a `Tank` it is the tank's `distributor_id`; for a `CustomerHerd` it is the herd's `distributor_id`; and for a `MatingRecommendation` it is the `distributor_id` of the herd the recommendation belongs to. Any mismatch on a `Distributor`-role call under consignment transfers, tanks or customer herds is rejected with `ERR_DISTRIBUTOR_SCOPE` (HTTP 403).
- When `X-User-Role` is `ProgramDirector`, `StaffVeterinarian`, `LabTechnician`, `ExportCoordinator` or `BreedingAdvisor`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server may record it — as `recorded_by_user_id` on a `HealthTest`, as `evaluated_by_user_id` on an `Ejaculate`, as `released_by_user_id` on a `StrawLot`, as `issued_by_user_id` on an `ExportCertificate`, or as `approved_by_user_id` on a `ConsignmentTransfer` — and later compares it against other recorded operator identifiers for the segregation-of-duties barriers, but it is never compared to any entity's `id`.

If `X-User-Role` is missing, or names a role that is not authorised for the addressed operation, the request fails with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403). Four operations additionally require `X-User-ID` to be present and non-empty, because each one stamps an operator identity that a later barrier will read: recording a health-test result, recording an ejaculate quality verdict, releasing a straw lot, and issuing an export certificate. On those four, an absent or empty `X-User-ID` yields `ERR_USER_ID_REQUIRED` (HTTP 400).

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every list endpoint accepts two optional query parameters, `limit` and `offset`. `limit` defaults to 10 when omitted, may not be smaller than 1, and may not be larger than 100. `offset` defaults to 0 and may not be negative. A value outside those bounds is a request-shape error, not a silently clamped value, and is rejected with `ERR_VALIDATION` (HTTP 400).

List results are ordered by `created_at` ascending unless the endpoint's own narrative states otherwise. The `meta` object of a list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more`: `total_count` is the number of rows matching the filters before the page window is applied, and `has_more` is true when rows remain beyond the current window.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Audit Stamps and Record Revisions

Every resource in this domain is identified by `id`, a server-minted UUID carrying no type prefix — a facility id and a sire id are indistinguishable by shape, and clients must never construct one. Clients also never supply `id`; it is assigned at creation.

All fourteen resource types carry the same audit triple. `created_at` is the ISO 8601 UTC instant the row was first persisted, rendered with three fractional digits. `updated_at` is the ISO 8601 UTC instant of the row's most recent mutation, likewise with three fractional digits, and it is refreshed on every mutation including mutations that arrive as a side effect of an operation addressed at a different resource. `version` is an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation of that row. Because several operations in this domain mutate rows the caller never named — a proof publication touches straw lots, a facility suspension touches sires and bookings — the `version` of a row a client is holding can advance without that client issuing any request, which is precisely why the two version-pinned writes described in §2.7 exist.

Timestamps that the system derives rather than accepts — `isolation_ends_at`, `valid_until`, `resulted_at`, `completed_at`, `collected_at`, `qc_decided_at`, `published_at`, `issued_at`, `expires_at`, `received_at`, `decided_at`, `last_service_at` and `next_service_due_at` — all use the same ISO 8601 UTC form with three fractional digits, and all are read from the logical clock rather than from wall-clock time.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock and the Epoch Sweep

Nothing in this domain reads the host machine's wall clock. Instead the server maintains a single **logical clock** whose startup default is exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`; before any clock call has ever been made, every temporal rule reads that instant, and the server never falls back to real time. A `ProgramDirector` moves the clock forward by supplying a `current_utc` instant. The clock is strictly monotonic: a `current_utc` at or before the stored clock is refused with `ERR_CLOCK_REWIND` (HTTP 422).

Advancing the clock is not a passive act. Each successful move runs an **epoch sweep** in a fixed order. First, every `HealthTest` whose `result` is `NEGATIVE` and whose `valid_until` is at or before the new clock moves to `EXPIRED`. Second, every sire that thereby loses coverage of at least one of the four base required assays, and whose `collection_status` is `ELIGIBLE`, moves to `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `REQUIRED_TEST_EXPIRED`. Third, each of those suspended sires' `SCHEDULED` bookings whose `start_at` lies after the new clock become `VOIDED`. Fourth, every `Tank` re-derives its `ln2_level_pct`, its `next_service_due_at` and its `tank_status` from the liquid-nitrogen decay curve. The response body reports what the sweep touched, as `current_utc` alongside four id arrays — `expired_health_test_ids`, `suspended_sire_ids`, `voided_booking_ids` — plus `service_due_tank_ids` and `out_of_service_tank_ids` naming the tanks that landed in each of those two postures.

### 2.6. Nested Path Resolution and Foreign-Key Integrity

Several resources are addressed through a parent path — a bull's health tests and genomic evaluations hang off the bull, a straw lot's consignment transfers hang off the lot, a herd's mating recommendations hang off the herd. Path segments are resolved strictly left to right: if the parent id names no stored row, the request fails with `ERR_RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404) without the child id ever being examined. The same code covers every entity type; there is no per-type variant.

Foreign keys supplied in a request body are resolved after the path and before any business rule. `facility_id`, `sire_id`, `dam_sire_id`, `ejaculate_id`, `straw_lot_id`, `distributor_id`, `tank_id`, `export_certificate_id`, `country_requirement_id`, `base_sire_id`, `retest_of_test_id`, `supersedes_evaluation_id` and every individual entry of `excluded_sire_ids` must each name a stored row; any that does not yields `ERR_RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404).

### 2.7. Version Pinning on Proof Filing and Dose Requests

Two write operations in this system are legitimate on their own terms yet depend on a snapshot the client read earlier, so both require the client to pin the parent revision it saw.

Filing a genomic evaluation against a bull requires `expected_sire_version` in the request body. This is not optional. Its value must equal the target `Sire` row's current `version`; if any other writer has advanced that `version` since the client last read the bull, the creation is rejected with `ERR_STALE_SIRE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). The check exists because filing a draft proof is a legitimate supersede operation — once published it displaces the bull's current proof and revalues his entire straw inventory — so a second filing derived from an outdated view of the bull would otherwise be accepted on its own terms. Pinning `version` makes it deterministic which view of the bull the draft was derived from, including the parent average pinned at creation.

Requesting a consignment of doses against a straw lot requires `expected_straw_lot_version` in the request body, likewise non-optional. Its value must equal the target `StrawLot` row's current `version`; a mismatch is rejected with `ERR_STALE_STRAW_LOT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). A dose request is legitimate whenever the lot is `RELEASED`, so the rejection cannot be derived from any state precondition; pinning `version` fixes the dose-ledger and valuation snapshot the request was sized against. A proof publication that revalued the lot in between is exactly the kind of intervening write this rejection catches.

No other endpoint in this API accepts a version field. Requests to operations outside these two must not be expected to carry one.

### 2.8. Precision, Rounding Order and Monetary Discipline

All money in this domain is carried as whole integer cents in fields whose names end in `_cents`; no fractional cent is ever stored or returned. Where a monetary quantity is derived by division or by a percentage, the result is rounded half up to a whole number of cents.

Three rounding orders are pinned, and getting them wrong produces figures that differ by cents from the specification. First, a bull's genetic sub-indices are rounded to two decimal places *before* the composite is blended from them, and the composite is then rounded to two decimal places again — the composite is never computed from unrounded sub-indices. Second, a lot's carrying value is rounded to whole cents *per straw* first, and any lot-level figure is that already-rounded per-straw value multiplied by a dose count; summing an unrounded total and rounding at the end is wrong here. Third, a consignment's volume rebate is keyed on the pre-rebate subtotal and rounded to whole cents at that single step only, with every other step of the settlement exact integer arithmetic.

Two-decimal, round-half-up precision governs `production_index`, `health_index`, `parent_average_index`, `composite_index`, the published index snapshots on a bull, and the inbreeding percentage. Four-decimal, round-half-up precision governs the expected conception rate, which is expressed as a fraction between 0 and 1 rather than a percentage. One-decimal, round-half-up precision governs a tank's liquid-nitrogen level, whose elapsed-day input is always floored and never rounded. Dose counts are integers throughout: fractional doses do not exist, and a theoretical dose yield is floored.

### 2.9. The Base Health-Currency Doctrine

Four disease assays form the **base required test set** for every bull in the collection programme: `BLUETONGUE`, `IBR`, `BVD` and `TRICHOMONIASIS`. A bull's health is *current* when, for each of those four types, there exists at least one `HealthTest` row for that bull whose `result` is `NEGATIVE` and whose `valid_until` is strictly later than the logical clock. The comparison is strictly greater-than, so a test whose `valid_until` equals the clock exactly is already lapsed and satisfies nothing. Rows in `PENDING`, `POSITIVE`, `INCONCLUSIVE` or `EXPIRED` never satisfy a requirement, whatever their type. When several `NEGATIVE` and unlapsed rows exist for the same type, any one of them satisfies that type — the test is existential, so no ordering or tie-break is needed.

Health currency gates four distinct operations, and each rejection carries `ERR_HEALTH_TESTS_NOT_CURRENT` (HTTP 422): clearing a bull out of isolation, reinstating a suspended bull to `ELIGIBLE`, creating a collection booking for him, and approving a consignment drawn from a lot cut from his semen. The predicate is also re-evaluated on every clock move, which is how a lapse turns into a suspension without any operator acting.

The six assay types the system understands are `BLUETONGUE`, `IBR`, `BVD`, `TRICHOMONIASIS`, `EBL` and `BRUCELLOSIS`. The last two are never part of the base set, but a destination country may demand either or both.

### 2.10. Rule Evaluation Order

Because many operations are guarded by several rules at once, the order in which they are evaluated is part of the contract: a request that violates two rules must fail on the earlier one. Rules are evaluated in the following bands, earliest first.

First, request shape. Every non-optional field in the operation's input list must be present and non-null; unknown body keys are ignored rather than rejected. Then every supplied field must parse to its declared type, and every enum-typed field must be a member of its declared set — `breed`, `product_line`, `test_type`, `result`, `requested_product_line` and `valuation_basis` are all closed sets. Then numeric, string and datetime bounds: cents fields at least 0, capacities at least 1, `straw_count` at least 1, `volume_ml` greater than 0, `concentration_m_per_ml` greater than 0, percentages within 0 to 100, `isolation_days` at least 0, `doses` at least 1, `limit` within 1 to 100, `offset` at least 0, every `country_code` exactly two uppercase letters, and every instant a valid ISO 8601 UTC timestamp — a bare calendar date carrying no time component is not one, and is refused in this band rather than read as midnight, whereas the two calendar-date inputs of this domain, `birth_date` and `sample_date`, are `YYYY-MM-DD` dates rather than instants and are parsed as such. All three shape bands report `ERR_VALIDATION` (HTTP 400).

Second, authorisation: the role check (`ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`), then the operator-identity requirement on the four stamped operations (`ERR_USER_ID_REQUIRED`), then the distributor scope check (`ERR_DISTRIBUTOR_SCOPE`), then the facility scope check (`ERR_FACILITY_SCOPE`).

Third, existence: path ids left to right, then body foreign keys (`ERR_RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND`).

Fourth, uniqueness: the code-collision family (`ERR_DUPLICATE_CODE`), then the outstanding-pending-test rule (`ERR_DUPLICATE_PENDING_TEST`).

Fifth, version pinning. `expected_sire_version` and `expected_straw_lot_version` are compared after existence and uniqueness but **before any state precondition**, so a stale version on a request that is also illegal on state grounds still returns 412 rather than 409.

Sixth, state preconditions — the source-state checks on sires, facilities, bookings, ejaculates, lots, evaluations, certificates, transfers and tanks, all HTTP 409.

Seventh, segregation of duties (`ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, `ERR_CERTIFIER_TESTED_SIRE`), both HTTP 403.

Eighth, business safety and regulatory rules; ninth, referential rules; tenth, temporal rules; eleventh, capacity and constraint rules — all HTTP 422.

Last of all, and deliberately last, the fiscal rule: the distributor credit-limit projection (`ERR_CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`). Any safety, referential, temporal or constraint violation outranks it, so a consignment that is both health-blocked and over the credit limit reports the health failure.

Three orderings inside those bands are called out explicitly because they are easy to get backwards. A consignment request against a lot that is not `RELEASED` reports `ERR_LOT_NOT_RELEASED` even when the named tank also belongs to the wrong distributor, because the lot-state check outranks the tank-ownership check. A booking at a facility where the bull is not housed reports `ERR_SIRE_NOT_AT_FACILITY` even when the requested window also violates the rest buffer, because the referential check outranks the temporal one. And a consignment approval blocked on health currency reports `ERR_HEALTH_TESTS_NOT_CURRENT` even when the projected exposure also breaks the credit limit.

### 2.11. Cascade Discipline and Compensating Actions

Many operations here mutate rows other than the one addressed, and the specification of each such operation includes its full cascade, not merely its primary effect. Three families recur.

**Suspension cascades** run downhill from a facility to its resident bulls and from a bull to his bookings and certificates. Suspending a facility suspends the eligible bulls housed there, and their suspension in turn voids their future bookings — two hops from one call. A bull's suspension, from any of its four causes, voids his future `SCHEDULED` bookings and revokes his `ISSUED` export certificates.

**Valuation cascades** run from a published proof through straw lots to consignments and then to distributor exposure — four hops, each of which is described where publication is described. Their compensating action, proof rollback, walks the same four hops in reverse and is required to land on exactly the pre-publication figure.

**Ledger movements** are always transfers between buckets on one lot, never creations or destructions. The identity `straw_count` equals `doses_available` plus `doses_reserved` plus `doses_consigned` plus `doses_used` plus `doses_destroyed` holds after every mutation without exception, and `straw_count` itself is immutable after the lot is created.

Reinstatement is never automatic where the specification does not say so. Returning a facility to `APPROVED` does not restore the bulls it suspended or the bookings it voided; each bull must be reinstated by its own collection-status change.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Registering a Bull and Wiring the Pedigree Graph

A `ProgramDirector` brings a bull into the system by registering him with a breed-association `registration_code` of 1 to 32 characters, a `breed` drawn from `HOLSTEIN`, `JERSEY` or `ANGUS`, a calendar `birth_date` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form, and the cash `acquisition_cost_cents` paid for the animal. The registration code must be unique across every bull ever registered; a collision is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CODE`. Two optional parentage fields wire the new animal into the pedigree: `sire_id` names his genetic father and `dam_sire_id` names his maternal grandsire — the dam's own sire. Either may be omitted or null, and a bull with both null is a pedigree root.

Not every registered bull is destined for the barn. A purely ancestral animal, registered only so that his descendants' pedigrees resolve, is created the same way with `acquisition_cost_cents` of 0 and simply never contracted. That is possible because a bull carries **two independent status dimensions on the same row**, neither of which is a function of the other. `acquisition_status` tracks commercial ownership and starts at `PROSPECT`, walking `PROSPECT → CONTRACTED → RESIDENT → RETIRED`, with `RETIRED` terminal. `collection_status` tracks health and collection eligibility and starts at `NOT_HOUSED`, moving through `ISOLATION`, `ELIGIBLE` and `SUSPENDED` to the terminal `WITHDRAWN`. A `PROSPECT` bull may be referenced as a pedigree parent and may even carry a published proof while never entering a facility.

At registration the server derives `pedigree_depth` from the supplied parents: it is 0 when both `sire_id` and `dam_sire_id` are null, and otherwise 1 plus the largest `pedigree_depth` among the non-null parents. The published-proof snapshot fields — `published_production_index`, `published_health_index` and `published_composite_index` — are initialised to 0.00, `published_evaluation_effective_at` and the derived proof timestamps to null, and both `progeny_daughter_count` and `collection_count` to 0. `collection_count` thereafter tracks the number of this bull's bookings that have reached `COMPLETED`.

Pedigree integrity is enforced at every write that touches an edge. Neither `sire_id` nor `dam_sire_id` may equal the bull's own `id`, and no edge may close a cycle in the pedigree graph; either fault is refused with `ERR_PEDIGREE_CYCLE`. The same rule is re-checked when a parentage correction is filed and again whenever the inbreeding calculation traverses the graph, so a cycle can never be reached by any route.

After DNA verification a parentage correction may be filed as registry work rather than as a state transition: the amendment rewrites `sire_id` and/or `dam_sire_id`, where a supplied null clears an edge and an omitted field leaves it untouched. `pedigree_depth` is re-derived and `version` increments by 1, but neither status dimension changes, no published index field moves, and no straw lot is revalued. Critically, evaluations already on file are **not** recomputed — a parent average is pinned at the moment its evaluation is created, so only evaluations filed after the correction see the amended graph. Future inbreeding traversals, by contrast, always use the current graph.

Bulls are discoverable as a list filtered by `breed`, `acquisition_status`, `collection_status` and `housing_facility_id`, and by pedigree edge: filtering on `sire_id` reaches a father's progeny and filtering on `dam_sire_id` reaches a maternal grandsire's, so the graph is navigable in both directions.

### 3.2. From Contract to Bench: Acquisition, Housing and Isolation

Signing the acquisition contract advances the animal `PROSPECT → CONTRACTED` on the acquisition dimension only, storing the negotiated `contract_price_cents` on the row; `collection_status` is untouched and remains `NOT_HOUSED`. Each of the three acquisition moves demands its own source state — contracting requires `PROSPECT`, housing requires `CONTRACTED`, retiring requires `RESIDENT` — so a repeated call fails on the second attempt with `ERR_SIRE_ACQUISITION_STATE`.

Housing is the one operation that moves both dimensions in a single call: `CONTRACTED → RESIDENT` on the acquisition dimension and `NOT_HOUSED → ISOLATION` on the collection dimension. The caller supplies the destination `facility_id` and the number of `isolation_days` the animal will serve. The server stores `housing_facility_id`, retains the supplied figure as `isolation_days_applied` — which a destination country will later interrogate — and computes `isolation_ends_at` as the logical clock plus `isolation_days` multiplied by 86400 seconds. The target facility's `resident_sire_count` is then recomputed as the number of bulls whose `housing_facility_id` is that facility and whose `acquisition_status` is `RESIDENT`, and the bull's `version` increments by 1 with `updated_at` refreshed. Housing may be performed by a `ProgramDirector` or by the `FacilityManager` of the receiving facility.

Two facility conditions gate housing. The facility's `approval_status` must be `APPROVED`; a suspended facility can neither house a bull nor accept a new booking nor back an export submission, and each such attempt reports `ERR_FACILITY_SUSPENDED`. And the facility must have room: housing may not push `resident_sire_count` above the facility's `housing_capacity`. Every resident already there is individually legal — it is the newcomer that breaks the cap — and the refusal is `ERR_FACILITY_AT_CAPACITY`.

Commercial retirement, recorded with a `retirement_reason`, advances `RESIDENT → RETIRED`. It also advances `collection_status` to `WITHDRAWN` on the same row, voids every `SCHEDULED` booking naming this bull with `cancel_reason` set to `SIRE_SUSPENDED`, recomputes the `resident_sire_count` of the facility named by `housing_facility_id`, and increments the bull's `version` by 1. The `housing_facility_id` itself is retained for audit rather than cleared, and existing straw inventory cut from this bull stays sellable.

### 3.3. Disease Testing and the Collection-Eligibility Cycle

A `StaffVeterinarian` orders a health test against a bull by naming the `facility_id` where the sample was drawn, the `test_type` assayed, the calendar `sample_date` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form on which the sample was taken, and the `validity_days` a negative finding will remain current for. The row is created with `result` at `PENDING` and `valid_until` null. `sample_date` is stored and returned in that same `YYYY-MM-DD` form, and wherever a rule measures it against the logical clock or adds days to it — the future-date refusal below, the `valid_until` a `NEGATIVE` result earns, and the test-age check at certificate issuance — it is read as UTC midnight of that day. A `sample_date` later than the logical clock is refused with `ERR_SAMPLE_DATE_FUTURE`, and a bull may not carry two `PENDING` tests of the same `test_type` at once — the outstanding one must be resulted first, or the order is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_PENDING_TEST`.

When an earlier assay came back ambiguous, the follow-up sample is filed with `retest_of_test_id` pointing at the row it re-runs. That target must belong to the same bull, be of the same `test_type`, and carry a `result` of either `POSITIVE` or `INCONCLUSIVE`; anything else — another bull's test, another type, or a test that is still `PENDING`, `NEGATIVE` or `EXPIRED` — is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_RETEST_TARGET`. The bands of §2.10 decide which refusal a bad target actually earns: because the outstanding-pending rule sits in band 4 and the retest-target rule in the referential band, naming this bull's own outstanding `PENDING` test of the requested `test_type` as the target answers `ERR_DUPLICATE_PENDING_TEST` instead, that uniqueness refusal firing on the mere existence of the outstanding row whatever the order names as its target. A `PENDING` target therefore reaches `ERR_INVALID_RETEST_TARGET` only when it is another bull's row or a row of another `test_type`. A first-line test simply omits the field. Tests are listed and filtered by `sire_id`, `facility_id`, `test_type`, `result` and `retest_of_test_id`, the last of which reaches every retest descended from a given ambiguous result.

Recording the laboratory verdict on a `PENDING` row is where the branching happens, and the three branches have deliberately different consequences. All three stamp `resulted_at` from the logical clock and store the acting `X-User-ID` as `recorded_by_user_id`, a stamp that will later bar that individual from signing this bull's export paperwork. A `PENDING → NEGATIVE` result additionally computes `valid_until` as `sample_date` plus `validity_days` multiplied by 86400 seconds. A `PENDING → POSITIVE` result leaves `valid_until` null and, when the assayed `test_type` belongs to the base required set and the bull's `collection_status` is `ELIGIBLE`, drives him to `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `POSITIVE_TEST_RESULT`; that suspension then voids his `SCHEDULED` bookings whose `start_at` is at or after the logical clock with `cancel_reason` `SIRE_SUSPENDED`, revokes his `ISSUED` export certificates with `revocation_reason` `SIRE_SUSPENDED`, and increments the bull's `version` by 1. A `PENDING → INCONCLUSIVE` result also leaves `valid_until` null, leaves the bull's `collection_status` entirely untouched, satisfies no country's requirement, and unlocks the filing of a retest against this row. The fourth transition, `NEGATIVE → EXPIRED`, is never operator-driven: it fires during the epoch sweep when the logical clock reaches or passes `valid_until`, and when the lapsing row was the last current cover for a base required type it drags the bull from `ELIGIBLE` to `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` `REQUIRED_TEST_EXPIRED`. `POSITIVE`, `INCONCLUSIVE` and `EXPIRED` are all terminal for a test row.

Clearing a bull out of quarantine is the veterinarian's `ISOLATION → ELIGIBLE` move, optionally annotated with a `clearance_note`. It has three preconditions: the bull's `collection_status` is `ISOLATION`, the logical clock has reached `isolation_ends_at` — the boundary is inclusive, so clearance exactly at that instant succeeds, and anything earlier is `ERR_ISOLATION_INCOMPLETE` — and his health is current across the four base assays, failing which the answer is `ERR_HEALTH_TESTS_NOT_CURRENT`. On success `suspension_reason` is cleared to null and `version` increments by 1.

Thereafter the collection dimension is a cycle that may repeat without bound. A veterinarian, or a `ProgramDirector`, may move the bull manually by supplying a target `collection_status` and an optional `suspension_reason`. `ELIGIBLE → SUSPENDED` records `suspension_reason` `MANUAL`, voids the bull's `SCHEDULED` bookings whose `start_at` is at or after the logical clock with `cancel_reason` `SIRE_SUSPENDED`, revokes his `ISSUED` certificates with `revocation_reason` `SIRE_SUSPENDED`, and bumps `version`. `SUSPENDED → ELIGIBLE` is the reinstatement, and it re-checks the whole world afresh: the bull's `acquisition_status` must be `RESIDENT`, the facility named by `housing_facility_id` must be `APPROVED`, and all four base assays must again be covered by a `NEGATIVE` test whose `valid_until` is strictly after the clock. Reinstatement clears `suspension_reason` and bumps `version`. A move to `WITHDRAWN` is terminal on the collection dimension and leaves `acquisition_status` alone. Any move the dimension does not permit — clearance outside `ISOLATION`, suspension outside `ELIGIBLE`, reinstatement outside `SUSPENDED`, or any move out of `WITHDRAWN` — is refused with `ERR_SIRE_COLLECTION_STATE`.

Suspension therefore arrives from four distinct causes, and each writes its own token into `suspension_reason`: `MANUAL` from the veterinarian's own hand, `POSITIVE_TEST_RESULT` from a positive base assay, `REQUIRED_TEST_EXPIRED` from the clock lapsing a required test, and `FACILITY_SUSPENDED` from the bull's facility losing its approval. Whatever the cause, the reinstatement precondition is the same full re-check.

### 3.4. Facilities, the Collection Bench and the Capture of an Ejaculate

A collection facility is registered by a `ProgramDirector` with a human-readable `code` of 1 to 32 characters that must be unique across all facilities, a two-uppercase-letter `country_code` naming the country the site physically sits in, a `housing_capacity` of at least 1 bounding how many `RESIDENT` bulls may live there, and a `bench_capacity` of at least 1 bounding how many `SCHEDULED` collection windows may overlap one another there. An optional `approved_export_countries` array — empty when omitted — lists the two-letter destination codes this site is cleared to export semen to, and is consulted whenever an export certificate is submitted. A new facility opens with `approval_status` at `APPROVED`, `suspension_reason` null and `resident_sire_count` at 0.

Regulatory approval is a cycle the director drives by supplying a target `approval_status` and, when suspending, a free-text `suspension_reason`. The `APPROVED → SUSPENDED` move stores that reason and then runs a two-hop cascade: every bull whose `housing_facility_id` is this facility and whose `collection_status` is `ELIGIBLE` becomes `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` `FACILITY_SUSPENDED`, and — caused by that first hop — each of those bulls' `SCHEDULED` bookings whose `start_at` is at or after the logical clock become `VOIDED` with `cancel_reason` `FACILITY_SUSPENDED`. The facility's own `version` increments by 1 and `updated_at` refreshes. The reverse move, `SUSPENDED → APPROVED`, clears `suspension_reason` and bumps `version`, and does nothing else: it restores neither the suspended bulls nor the voided bookings, each of which needs its own explicit reinstatement. Facility reads expose the derived `resident_sire_count` alongside the stored fields.

Booking the bench is the `FacilityManager`'s work. A booking names the `sire_id` to be collected, the `facility_id` owning the bench, and a half-open window from an inclusive `start_at` to an exclusive `end_at`, and is created with `status` `SCHEDULED`, `cancel_reason`, `completed_at` and `collected_by_ref` all null. Because the window is half-open, a booking that starts at exactly the instant another ends does not overlap it. Six rules guard creation, and the ordering matters. The bull's `collection_status` must be `ELIGIBLE`, or the answer is `ERR_SIRE_COLLECTION_STATE`. The facility's `approval_status` must be `APPROVED`, or `ERR_FACILITY_SUSPENDED`. His health must be current across the four base assays, or `ERR_HEALTH_TESTS_NOT_CURRENT`. He must actually live there — his `housing_facility_id` must equal the booking's `facility_id` — or `ERR_SIRE_NOT_AT_FACILITY`, and that referential check is evaluated *before* the rest-buffer rule, so a booking that is at the wrong site and inside the rest window reports the wrong-site error. The window itself must be well-formed and future-dated, with `start_at` strictly before `end_at` and `start_at` at or after the logical clock, or `ERR_INVALID_WINDOW`.

The rest buffer protects the animal. A new window's `start_at` must be at least 48 hours after the `end_at` of the bull's most recent `SCHEDULED` or `COMPLETED` booking, and at least 48 hours before the `start_at` of his next one. Both boundaries are closed, so a gap of exactly 48 hours is permitted; anything tighter is `ERR_SIRE_REST_BUFFER`. Bookings already in `CANCELLED` or `VOIDED` are invisible to this rule.

Bench capacity is the facility-side counterpart. Count the `SCHEDULED` bookings at that facility whose half-open windows overlap the requested one; that count plus one must not exceed the facility's `bench_capacity`, or the request is refused with `ERR_BENCH_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. Adjacency is explicitly not overlap: a previous booking whose `end_at` equals the new `start_at` does not count. `CANCELLED` and `VOIDED` bookings are likewise excluded from the overlap count, which is precisely why cancelling a booking frees the slot.

A booking has three possible endings and they are not interchangeable. `SCHEDULED → CANCELLED` is operator-initiated: the manager or a director supplies a free-text `cancel_reason`, the row mints nothing, and the freed slot returns to both the rest-buffer and bench-overlap calculations. `SCHEDULED → VOIDED` is produced only by the system, as a cascade from a bull suspension or a facility suspension, and it records a machine token — `SIRE_SUSPENDED` or `FACILITY_SUSPENDED` — in `cancel_reason` rather than operator text; it too frees the slot, and it only ever touches bookings whose `start_at` is at or after the clock. Either way, a booking that is no longer `SCHEDULED` accepts no further transition and reports `ERR_BOOKING_STATE`.

`SCHEDULED → COMPLETED` is where semen enters the system. The acting `FacilityManager`'s `X-User-ID` must equal the booking's `facility_id`, the logical clock must have reached the booking's `start_at` — earlier attempts are `ERR_BOOKING_NOT_STARTED` — the bull must still be `ELIGIBLE` and the facility still `APPROVED`. The completion payload carries the raw `volume_ml` collected, the sperm `concentration_m_per_ml` in millions of cells per millilitre, the fresh pre-freeze `motility_pct`, and an optional free-text `collected_by_ref` naming the collector. The booking records `completed_at` from the logical clock and stores `collected_by_ref`, the bull's `collection_count` increments by 1, and exactly one **ejaculate** row is minted.

That ejaculate is not returned in the completion response — it is discovered only by listing ejaculates filtered by `booking_id`, which alongside `sire_id` and `qc_status` is the sole discovery channel for a cascade-created row. The new ejaculate copies `booking_id` and `sire_id` from the booking, carries `volume_ml`, `concentration_m_per_ml`, `motility_pct` and `collected_by_ref` from the completion payload, stamps `collected_at` from the logical clock, and enters `qc_status` `PENDING_QC` with `post_thaw_motility_pct`, `morphology_normal_pct`, `evaluated_by_user_id` and `qc_decided_at` all null. Its `total_doses_theoretical` is the volume in millilitres multiplied by the concentration in millions per millilitre, divided by the 15 million sperm cells that make up one insemination dose, with the quotient floored to a whole number of doses. That figure is frozen on the row and becomes the hard ceiling on every straw ever cut from this collection.

### 3.5. Laboratory Quality Evaluation, Straw Processing and the Dose Ledger

Post-thaw quality evaluation is the `LabTechnician`'s first gate, and the ejaculate must be `PENDING_QC` for it — anything else is `ERR_EJACULATE_STATE`. The technician submits a `verdict` of either `ACCEPTED` or `REJECTED` together with the measured `post_thaw_motility_pct` and `morphology_normal_pct`. Both branches store those two measurements, stamp `qc_decided_at` from the logical clock, and record the acting `X-User-ID` as `evaluated_by_user_id`. An `ACCEPTED` verdict, however, is only permitted when `post_thaw_motility_pct` is at least 35.0 and `morphology_normal_pct` is at least 70.0; a verdict of `ACCEPTED` accompanied by sub-threshold figures is refused outright with `ERR_QC_BELOW_THRESHOLD` rather than silently downgraded to a rejection. `PENDING_QC → ACCEPTED` unlocks straw processing. `PENDING_QC → REJECTED` is terminal and no lot may ever be cut from that collection.

Cutting a lot requires the parent ejaculate to be `ACCEPTED` — a `PENDING_QC` or `REJECTED` parent yields `ERR_EJACULATE_STATE`. The technician declares the `product_line` the semen was processed into (`CONVENTIONAL`, `SEXED` or `EMBRYO`), the `straw_count` of doses cryopreserved, and the total laboratory `processing_cost_cents` assigned to the lot, which must be at least 1. Sorted-sperm purity is conditional on the product line: a `SEXED` lot must supply `sexing_purity_pct` within 50.0 to 99.9, while a `CONVENTIONAL` or `EMBRYO` lot must omit it entirely — a value supplied on a non-sexed lot is an error rather than an ignored key, and either fault reports `ERR_PRODUCT_LINE_FIELDS`. The dose ceiling is aggregate, not per-lot: the sum of `straw_count` across every lot already cut from this ejaculate, including the row being created, must not exceed the ejaculate's `total_doses_theoretical`, or the request is refused with `ERR_DOSE_CAP_EXCEEDED`.

A new lot copies `sire_id` from its ejaculate — that is the "by sire" axis of the inventory — records the `ejaculate_id` it was cut from, and enters `distribution_status` `QUARANTINE`. Its five-bucket dose ledger opens with `doses_available` equal to `straw_count` and `doses_reserved`, `doses_consigned`, `doses_used` and `doses_destroyed` all at 0. Its carrying value is struck on the **standard-cost** basis: `unit_book_value_cents` is the lot's `processing_cost_cents` divided by its `straw_count`, rounded half up to a whole number of cents *per straw*, with `valuation_basis` set to `STANDARD_COST` and `pre_revaluation_unit_value_cents` left null. Lots are listed and filtered by `sire_id`, `ejaculate_id`, `product_line` and `distribution_status`.

The quarantine gate then branches. Releasing a lot for distribution takes an optional `release_note` and requires `distribution_status` to be `QUARANTINE`; a release or rejection attempted on any other status is `ERR_LOT_STATE`. Release is also the first segregation-of-duties barrier: the releasing `X-User-ID` must differ from the `evaluated_by_user_id` recorded on the lot's parent ejaculate, and the same individual attempting both roles is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). On success the lot moves `QUARANTINE → RELEASED`, stores `released_by_user_id`, increments `version` by 1 and refreshes `updated_at`, and thereby unlocks three downstream capabilities: consignment requests, export certification and selection by the mating-recommendation engine. Its value also migrates from the quarantine bucket of the plant valuation into the released bucket.

Condemning a lot is the sibling branch. It takes a `rejected_reason`, requires `QUARANTINE`, and moves the whole shelf ledger in one step: `doses_destroyed` increases by the current `doses_available` and `doses_available` is then set to 0, leaving `straw_count` untouched as the conserved total. Every `ISSUED` export certificate covering this lot becomes `REVOKED` with `revocation_reason` `LOT_REJECTED`. The lot also drops out of the plant inventory aggregate entirely, and its `version` increments by 1 with `updated_at` refreshed.

One further lot transition is never operator-driven. `RELEASED → DEPLETED` fires automatically after any ledger mutation that leaves `doses_available`, `doses_reserved` and `doses_consigned` all at 0 — the doses have all been inseminated or written off. A depleted lot leaves the plant inventory aggregate, and its `version` increments by 1 with `updated_at` refreshed. `REJECTED` and `DEPLETED` are both terminal.

The plant's straw inventory value is available as a computed read for a `ProgramDirector` or `LabTechnician`. It reports `plant_inventory_value_cents`, the sum over every lot whose `distribution_status` is `QUARANTINE` or `RELEASED` of that lot's `unit_book_value_cents` multiplied by the sum of its `doses_available`, `doses_reserved` and `doses_consigned`. Doses in `doses_used` or `doses_destroyed` are consumed and contribute nothing, and lots in `REJECTED` or `DEPLETED` are excluded from the source set altogether even though their stored unit value survives. `quarantine_value_cents` is that same sum restricted to lots in `QUARANTINE`, `released_value_cents` the same sum restricted to lots in `RELEASED`, and `lot_count` the number of rows in the source set. Because every term is already a product of integers, no rounding occurs at aggregation time, and because the aggregate is a pure sum the order of traversal is irrelevant.

### 3.6. Genomic Evaluation, Proof Publication and Inventory Revaluation

A genomic evaluation is a bull's scored genetic proof, filed by a `ProgramDirector` against the bull named in the path and scored **immediately and irrevocably at creation**. The payload carries the predicted transmitting abilities for milk, fat and protein yield as `milk_kg`, `fat_kg` and `protein_kg`; the `somatic_cell_score`, where a lower figure is healthier; the daughter pregnancy-rate deviation `fertility_pct` in percentage points; the productive-life deviation `longevity_months` in months; the `reliability_pct` of the evaluation, which must be at least 0 and at most 99; the `progeny_daughter_count` of milking daughters contributing progeny data, which must be at least 0; the `effective_at` instant at which this proof becomes official once published; and the `expected_sire_version` described in §2.7. The row is created with `status` `DRAFT`, `supersedes_evaluation_id` and `published_at` null, and `revaluation_delta_cents` at 0. Filing also increments the bull's own `version`, but touches none of his published index fields — those move only on publication.

Scoring proceeds in six pinned steps. First, the two raw trait aggregates. The production aggregate is 0.02 multiplied by `milk_kg`, plus 0.60 multiplied by `fat_kg`, plus 0.80 multiplied by `protein_kg`. The health aggregate is 8.0 multiplied by the quantity (3.00 minus `somatic_cell_score`) multiplied by 100, plus 6.0 multiplied by `fertility_pct`, plus 1.5 multiplied by `longevity_months`. Second, the reliability shrink factor: take `reliability_pct` divided by 100, then clamp it to a floor of 0.30 and a ceiling of 0.95 — values below the floor become 0.30 and values above the ceiling become 0.95. Third, and this is the pinned rounding order, `production_index` is the production aggregate multiplied by the shrink factor rounded half up to two decimal places, and `health_index` is the health aggregate multiplied by the shrink factor rounded half up to two decimal places; the composite is later computed from these **already-rounded** sub-indices, never from the unrounded products.

Fourth, the variant boundary. When `progeny_daughter_count` is at least 50, `evaluation_basis` is `PROGENY_BLENDED`; when it is 49 or fewer, `evaluation_basis` is `GENOMIC_ONLY`. The boundary is inclusive at 50. The two branches then set `parent_average_weight` differently and with equal specificity. Under `GENOMIC_ONLY`, `parent_average_weight` is fixed at 0.50, so half the composite is inherited from the pedigree — the appropriate treatment for a young bull with no usable progeny data. Under `PROGENY_BLENDED`, `parent_average_weight` is 0.50 minus 0.004 multiplied by the smaller of `progeny_daughter_count` and 100, with the result floored at 0.10 — that is, if the subtraction yields anything below 0.10 the weight is 0.10. The daughter count is therefore capped at 100 for weighting purposes, and beyond that cap the weight rests at its computed value of 0.10. Because the weight decays with daughter count, identical trait inputs produce a different composite under the two bases whenever the parent average differs from the trait-only blend.

Fifth, the parent average. `parent_average_index` is the sum of the currently published composite index of the bull named by his `sire_id` and the currently published composite index of the bull named by his `dam_sire_id`, divided by 2, rounded half up to two decimal places. The divisor is always 2 and is never reduced: a parent whose id is null, or who has no published proof, contributes 0.00 rather than being skipped. Only a parent's `PUBLISHED` evaluation feeds this figure — rows in `DRAFT`, `SUPERSEDED` or `ROLLED_BACK` contribute nothing — and the value is **pinned at creation**, so later pedigree corrections and later parent publications never move it.

Sixth, the blend. `composite_index` is one minus `parent_average_weight`, multiplied by the quantity (0.60 multiplied by `production_index` plus 0.40 multiplied by `health_index`), plus `parent_average_weight` multiplied by `parent_average_index`, with the whole result rounded half up to two decimal places. `evaluation_basis`, `parent_average_weight`, `parent_average_index`, `production_index`, `health_index` and `composite_index` are all frozen on the row at creation and never recomputed.

Publication is the director's `DRAFT → PUBLISHED` move, optionally annotated with a `publication_note`; the same operation reinstates a `SUPERSEDED` row, and an evaluation that is neither `DRAFT` nor `SUPERSEDED` reports `ERR_EVALUATION_STATE`. When the bull already carries a published proof, the candidate's `effective_at` must be strictly later than the bull's `published_evaluation_effective_at`; proofs may not be back-dated over the incumbent. On success `published_at` is stamped from the logical clock, `supersedes_evaluation_id` is set to the id of the bull's previously published evaluation — null when this is his first — and that previously published row moves `PUBLISHED → SUPERSEDED`, stopping its contribution to the bull's published snapshot.

What follows is a four-hop value cascade, and every hop is part of the contract. **Hop 1** refreshes the bull's `published_production_index`, `published_health_index`, `published_composite_index`, `published_evaluation_effective_at` and `progeny_daughter_count` from this evaluation, and increments the bull's `version` by 1. **Hop 2**, caused by hop 1, revalues stock: every lot whose `sire_id` is this bull and whose `distribution_status` is `QUARANTINE` or `RELEASED` first snapshots its current `unit_book_value_cents` into `pre_revaluation_unit_value_cents`, then recomputes `unit_book_value_cents` on the market basis and sets `valuation_basis` to `MARKET_REVALUED`. Lots already in `REJECTED` or `DEPLETED` are never revalued and keep whatever unit value they carried when they left the set. **Hop 3**, caused by hop 2, re-derives `carried_value_cents` on every consignment against a revalued lot, as that transfer's `doses_on_hand` multiplied by the lot's new `unit_book_value_cents`. **Hop 4**, caused by hop 3, re-derives `consignment_value_cents` on every distributor referenced by those transfers. Finally the evaluation's own `revaluation_delta_cents` records the plant inventory value immediately after hop 2 minus the plant inventory value immediately before it — a figure that is 0 for as long as the row remains a draft.

The market basis works from genetic merit rather than laboratory cost. Take 45 cents per index point, multiply by the bull's `published_composite_index` floored at 0 — a negative published composite contributes 0 rather than a negative value — and multiply by the product-line multiplier: 1.00 for `CONVENTIONAL`, 1.85 for `SEXED` and 3.40 for `EMBRYO`. For a `SEXED` lot only, multiply the running figure by the lot's `sexing_purity_pct` divided by the reference purity of 90.0. Round that result half up to whole cents, then take the larger of it and the market floor of 800 cents, and store the outcome as `unit_book_value_cents`. The same physical lot therefore carries two different unit values before and after a proof update, and the difference flows onward into `carried_value_cents` and into each distributor's `consignment_value_cents`.

Rollback is publication's exact compensating action. A director withdraws a `PUBLISHED` proof with a `rollback_reason`; the row becomes `ROLLED_BACK`, which is terminal, and rolling back anything not currently `PUBLISHED` reports `ERR_EVALUATION_STATE`. Compensating hop 1 reinstates the row named by `supersedes_evaluation_id` from `SUPERSEDED` back to `PUBLISHED` and refreshes the bull's published snapshot from it; when `supersedes_evaluation_id` is null instead, `published_production_index`, `published_health_index` and `published_composite_index` all reset to 0.00, `published_evaluation_effective_at` resets to null and `progeny_daughter_count` resets to 0. Either way the bull's `version` increments by 1. Compensating hop 2 restores every lot revalued by the withdrawn publication: `unit_book_value_cents` is set back to `pre_revaluation_unit_value_cents` verbatim, `valuation_basis` is set back to the basis that stored value came from, and `pre_revaluation_unit_value_cents` is cleared to null. Compensating hop 3 re-derives `carried_value_cents` on every `RECEIVED` consignment against those lots from the restored unit value, and compensating hop 4 re-derives `consignment_value_cents` on every affected distributor. The invariant that binds the pair is exact: after a rollback the plant inventory value returns to precisely the figure it carried before the corresponding publication, to the cent.

The `SUPERSEDED → PUBLISHED` reinstatement is thus a system move, not an operator one: it fires when the evaluation that displaced a row is itself rolled back, which the server recognises because the rolled-back row's `supersedes_evaluation_id` names the row being reinstated. The reinstated proof becomes the bull's official evaluation again and repopulates his published snapshot. Because that cycle can repeat, a single evaluation row may leave `PUBLISHED` and return to it any number of times. Evaluations are listed and filtered by `sire_id`, by `status`, and by `supersedes_evaluation_id`, the last of which walks the supersession chain.

### 3.7. Destination Rule Sets and Export Certification

Before anything can be exported, the destination's import regime must be on file. A `ProgramDirector` or `ExportCoordinator` declares one country's rule set with a two-uppercase-letter `country_code` that must be unique across all rule sets — a collision reports `ERR_DUPLICATE_CODE` — a non-empty `required_test_types` array drawn from the six assay types the system understands, a `max_test_age_days` of at least 1 bounding how old each required test's `sample_date` may be at issuance, a `min_isolation_days` of at least 0 the exporting bull must have served, and two booleans, `sexed_semen_allowed` and `embryo_allowed`, saying whether sorted-semen and embryo product lines may be certified for that destination at all.

An `ExportCoordinator` drafts a certificate binding exactly three things: the `sire_id` whose health status the document attests, the `straw_lot_id` it covers — one certificate covers one lot — and the `country_requirement_id` naming the destination rule set to apply. The lot and the bull must agree: the lot named by `straw_lot_id` must have the same `sire_id` as the certificate, or the draft is refused with `ERR_LOT_SIRE_MISMATCH`. On creation the row enters `status` `DRAFT` with `submission_round` at 0, `rejection_reason`, `issued_by_user_id`, `issued_at`, `expires_at` and `revocation_reason` all null, and `destination_country_code` copied verbatim from the referenced rule set's `country_code`.

Submitting the certificate to the destination authority, optionally annotated with a `submission_note`, is where the destination's whole rule set is enforced at once. The certificate must be in `DRAFT` or in `REJECTED`; any other source state is `ERR_CERTIFICATE_STATE`. Its `submission_round` must be strictly below 3 at the moment of submission, because a certificate may enter `SUBMITTED` at most three times; a fourth attempt is refused with `ERR_RESUBMISSION_LIMIT`. The facility that housed the bull must still be `APPROVED`, failing which `ERR_FACILITY_SUSPENDED`, and that facility's `approved_export_countries` must contain the certificate's `destination_country_code`, failing which `ERR_DESTINATION_NOT_APPROVED`. The lot's `product_line` must be permitted by the destination's flags — a `SEXED` lot requires `sexed_semen_allowed` to be true and an `EMBRYO` lot requires `embryo_allowed` to be true, while a `CONVENTIONAL` lot is gated by neither — and a violation reports `ERR_PRODUCT_NOT_PERMITTED`. Finally the quarantine actually served must be long enough for the destination: the bull's `isolation_days_applied` must be at least the destination's `min_isolation_days`, with the boundary inclusive so that exactly the minimum passes, and a shortfall reports `ERR_ISOLATION_TOO_SHORT`. On success `submission_round` increments by 1 and any prior `rejection_reason` is cleared.

The destination authority's refusal is recorded as `SUBMITTED → REJECTED` with a `rejection_reason`, and it deliberately leaves `submission_round` unchanged — the counter records submissions, not rejections. The coordinator may correct the paperwork and resubmit while rounds remain, so the `SUBMITTED → REJECTED → SUBMITTED` cycle can run through three submissions; a third rejection leaves `submission_round` at 3 and the next submission attempt is refused.

Signing is the `StaffVeterinarian`'s `SUBMITTED → ISSUED` move, optionally annotated with an `issue_note`, and it is the strictest gate in the system. The certificate must be `SUBMITTED`. The covered lot's `distribution_status` must be `RELEASED`, or the signature is refused with `ERR_LOT_NOT_RELEASED`. Then, for every `test_type` in the destination's `required_test_types`, there must exist a `HealthTest` for the certificate's `sire_id` of that type whose `result` is `NEGATIVE`, whose `valid_until` is strictly later than the logical clock, and whose age — the logical clock minus its `sample_date` — is no greater than the destination's `max_test_age_days` multiplied by 86400 seconds. If any required type has no such row, issuance fails with `ERR_COUNTRY_TEST_MISSING`. Where a type has more than one satisfying row, the server picks the one with the latest `valid_until`, breaking a tie on the earliest `created_at`, so that the outcome is deterministic.

The second segregation-of-duties barrier bites here: the signing `X-User-ID` must not appear as the `recorded_by_user_id` on any `HealthTest` belonging to the certificate's bull. A veterinarian who resulted this bull's assays may not sign his paperwork, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_CERTIFIER_TESTED_SIRE` (HTTP 403). On success the row stores `issued_by_user_id` from the acting header and `issued_at` from the logical clock, and sets `expires_at` to the **earliest** `valid_until` among the chosen satisfying tests — the certificate's validity window is the intersection of the individual test windows, so it lapses with the first of them. Issuance is what unlocks cross-border dispatch of the covered lot.

Revocation ends the certificate's life. A `ProgramDirector` or `StaffVeterinarian` may revoke an `ISSUED` certificate manually with a `revocation_reason`, and `ISSUED → REVOKED` is terminal; revoking anything not `ISSUED` is `ERR_CERTIFICATE_STATE`. The same transition also fires automatically from two upstream events, each writing its own machine token into `revocation_reason`: `SIRE_SUSPENDED` when the bull's `collection_status` becomes `SUSPENDED` from any of the four causes, and `LOT_REJECTED` when the covered lot is condemned. Once a certificate is revoked, any consignment still sitting in `REQUESTED` or `APPROVED` that names it can no longer be dispatched. Certificates are listed and filtered by `sire_id`, `straw_lot_id`, `country_requirement_id` and `status`.

### 3.8. The Distributor Network: Tanks, Liquid Nitrogen, Consignment and Settlement

A distribution partner is registered by a `ProgramDirector` with its legal `name`, the two-uppercase-letter `country_code` it operates in, and a `credit_limit_cents` of at least 0 bounding the exposure the plant will carry against it. An optional `consignment_enabled` flag defaults to true and acts as a hard feature switch. The plant itself sits in the country `US`; whenever a partner's `country_code` differs from `US`, every dispatch to that partner requires an issued export certificate, and when it equals `US` the shipment is domestic. Three derived exposure fields open at 0: `consignment_value_cents`, `outstanding_balance_cents` and `cumulative_doses_used`.

Tanks are the physical shelf in the field, registered by a `ProgramDirector` or by the partner itself. A tank carries the manufacturer `serial`, unique across all tanks and refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CODE` on collision; the `distributor_id` of the partner holding it; a `capacity_straws` of at least 1; an optional `service_interval_days` defaulting to 60; and two optional field-service annotations, `assigned_technician_name` and `technician_cert_expiry_at`, the instant that technician's certification lapses, both null when the tank is unassigned. An optional `initial_fill_pct` defaults to 95.0. At creation `last_service_at` is stamped from the logical clock, `last_service_fill_pct` is set to the initial fill, `service_count` is 0 and `tank_status` is `IN_SERVICE`.

Liquid nitrogen boils off on a step curve. Count `elapsed_days` as the logical clock minus `last_service_at`, divided by 86400 seconds and floored to whole days — partial days never count. The current `ln2_level_pct` is then `last_service_fill_pct` minus 2.0 percentage points for each elapsed whole day, with a floor of 0: the level never goes negative. It is reported to one decimal place, rounded half up. Alongside it, `next_service_due_at` is `last_service_at` plus `service_interval_days` multiplied by 86400 seconds. From those two figures `tank_status` follows deterministically: when `ln2_level_pct` is exactly 0 the tank is `OUT_OF_SERVICE`; otherwise, when `ln2_level_pct` is at or below 25.0 — the boundary is inclusive — or the logical clock has reached `next_service_due_at`, the tank is `SERVICE_DUE`; in every other case it is `IN_SERVICE`. All three figures are re-derived for every tank on each clock move and on every tank read, so a tank's posture is always a function of the current clock rather than of a stored decision.

That gives the tank a service cycle. `IN_SERVICE → SERVICE_DUE` fires on a clock move when the recomputed level has fallen to or below 25.0 while still above 0, or when the clock has reached `next_service_due_at`; the tank is not yet unusable, but because dispatch demands `IN_SERVICE`, approved consignments aimed at it stall until it is filled. `SERVICE_DUE → OUT_OF_SERVICE` fires on a clock move when the recomputed level reaches exactly 0, and the tank can then receive nothing at all. `IN_SERVICE → OUT_OF_SERVICE` is the same terminal drop reached in one hop: a single large clock jump can take the level from healthy to 0 and skip `SERVICE_DUE` entirely. Refilling closes the cycle from either direction. The holding partner records a top-up by supplying `fill_pct`, which must be strictly greater than the tank's currently recomputed `ln2_level_pct` — you cannot "fill" a tank to a level at or below where it already sits — and the acting `X-User-ID` must equal the tank's `distributor_id`. A service sets `last_service_at` to the logical clock and `last_service_fill_pct` to the supplied `fill_pct`, restarting the decay curve from that origin; recomputes `next_service_due_at` as the logical clock plus `service_interval_days` multiplied by 86400 seconds; re-derives `tank_status`, which returns to `IN_SERVICE` once the new fill clears 25.0; and increments `service_count` by 1. That increment is billable: it raises the service fee of every consignment sitting in this tank when it is eventually settled. Tanks are listed and filtered by `distributor_id` and `tank_status`, and a tank read also exposes `straws_on_hand`, the sum of the still-outstanding doses across the tank's `IN_TRANSIT` and `RECEIVED` consignments.

A consignment request is raised against a released lot by a `ProgramDirector` or by the receiving partner. The body names the consignee `distributor_id`, the destination `tank_id`, the number of `doses` wanted (at least 1), the `unit_price_cents` per dose agreed for settlement (at least 1), an optional `export_certificate_id` authorising a cross-border shipment — null for a domestic one — and the `expected_straw_lot_version` of §2.7. The lot's `distribution_status` must be `RELEASED`: `QUARANTINE`, `REJECTED` and `DEPLETED` lots cannot be consigned, and the refusal is `ERR_LOT_NOT_RELEASED`, evaluated **before** the tank-ownership rule so a request that is wrong on both counts reports the lot-state error. The destination tank must belong to the named consignee — its `distributor_id` must equal the body's `distributor_id` — or `ERR_TANK_NOT_OWNED_BY_DISTRIBUTOR`. The requested `doses` must not exceed the lot's `doses_available`, or `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_DOSES`. When a certificate is cited, it must genuinely cover this shipment: its `sire_id` must equal the lot's `sire_id`, its `straw_lot_id` must equal the lot being shipped, and its `status` must be `ISSUED`; a cross-border shipment that cites no certificate, or cites one failing any of those tests, reports `ERR_CERTIFICATE_NOT_APPLICABLE`. And the destination country must be approved for the lot's origin site: the certificate's `destination_country_code`, or for a domestic shipment the consignee's `country_code`, must appear in the `approved_export_countries` of the facility that hosted the booking behind this lot's ejaculate, with `US` always permitted; otherwise `ERR_DESTINATION_NOT_APPROVED`. A successful request lands in `status` `REQUESTED` with `approved_by_user_id`, `rejection_reason` and `tank_service_count_at_dispatch` null, `doses_used` and `doses_returned` at 0, and no dose bucket moved.

Approval is the `ProgramDirector`'s commercial gate and the first ledger movement, and it may carry an optional `approval_note`. The transfer must be `REQUESTED` — any other source state is `ERR_TRANSFER_STATE` — and the lot must still be `RELEASED`. The bull behind the lot must have current health across all four base assays, or `ERR_HEALTH_TESTS_NOT_CURRENT`. The consignee's `consignment_enabled` must be true, or `ERR_CONSIGNMENT_DISABLED` — a false flag blocks approval whatever the partner's exposure. The destination tank must have room: its `straws_on_hand` plus the requested `doses` must not exceed its `capacity_straws`, and a breach reports `ERR_TANK_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. The lot must still hold the stock: `doses_available` must be at least `doses`, or `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_DOSES`. Where the transfer cites an `export_certificate_id`, that certificate must still cover the shipment at approval time — `status` `ISSUED`, its `sire_id` equal to the lot's `sire_id` and its `straw_lot_id` equal to the lot being shipped — and a certificate that has since been revoked or never applied reports `ERR_CERTIFICATE_NOT_APPLICABLE`. Last of all comes the credit gate. The partner's current exposure is `consignment_value_cents` plus `outstanding_balance_cents`; the projection adds the requested `doses` multiplied by the lot's `unit_book_value_cents`, and approval is permitted only when that projected figure is at most `credit_limit_cents`. The comparison is inclusive, so a projection landing exactly on the limit is approved, and a projection above it is refused with `ERR_CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`. On success `doses` move out of the lot's `doses_available` and into its `doses_reserved`, `approved_by_user_id` is stamped from the acting header, and the lot's `version` increments by 1. Refusing a request instead records a `rejection_reason` and moves `REQUESTED → REJECTED`, which is terminal and moves no dose at all, because the doses were never reserved.

The two exposure aggregates deserve their own statement, because their status filters are exact. `consignment_value_cents` is the sum, over the partner's consignments whose `status` is `RECEIVED`, of each transfer's `doses_on_hand` multiplied by its lot's `unit_book_value_cents`. `outstanding_balance_cents` is the sum of `settlement_total_cents` over the partner's consignments whose `status` is `RECONCILED`, and `cumulative_doses_used` is the sum of `doses_used` over those same reconciled rows. Consignments in `REQUESTED`, `APPROVED`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `REJECTED` or `RETURNED` contribute nothing to any of the three.

Dispatch, optionally annotated with a `dispatch_note`, moves `APPROVED → IN_TRANSIT`. The tank's `tank_status` must be `IN_SERVICE`; a `SERVICE_DUE` tank stalls the shipment until it is filled, and an `OUT_OF_SERVICE` tank reports `ERR_TANK_OUT_OF_SERVICE`. When the consignee's `country_code` differs from `US`, `export_certificate_id` must be non-null, that certificate's `status` must be `ISSUED`, its `straw_lot_id` must equal the transfer's `straw_lot_id` and its `destination_country_code` must equal the consignee's `country_code`; otherwise the shipment is refused with `ERR_CERTIFICATE_NOT_APPLICABLE`. Dispatch snapshots `tank_service_count_at_dispatch` from the tank's current `service_count` — the baseline for the eventual service fee — re-derives the tank's `straws_on_hand`, since the doses now count against tank capacity, and re-checks that capacity, reporting `ERR_TANK_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` if the tank would be overfilled. No dose moves: they stay reserved.

Arrival is the partner's own confirmation, optionally annotated with a `receipt_note`, and requires the acting `X-User-ID` to equal the transfer's `distributor_id`. One gate stands between the source-state check and the ledger movement: the destination tank's recomputed `tank_status` must not be `OUT_OF_SERVICE`, since such a tank can receive nothing at all, and an arrival into one is refused with `ERR_TANK_OUT_OF_SERVICE` while the transfer stays `IN_TRANSIT` and no dose moves. A `SERVICE_DUE` tank, by contrast, still accepts the arrival — only the empty tank blocks it. `IN_TRANSIT → RECEIVED` moves `doses` out of the lot's `doses_reserved` and into `doses_consigned`, stamps `received_at` from the logical clock, sets `carried_value_cents` to the transfer's `doses_on_hand` multiplied by the lot's `unit_book_value_cents`, re-derives the partner's `consignment_value_cents`, and increments the lot's `version` by 1. `doses_on_hand` is always the transfer's `doses` minus its `doses_used` minus its `doses_returned`, and `carried_value_cents` is that product only while the transfer is `RECEIVED` — in every other status it is 0. Stock physically sits with the partner at this point but is still owned by the plant.

Reconciliation closes the commercial loop. The partner reports how many doses were inseminated as `doses_used` and, optionally, how many came back as `doses_returned`, which defaults to 0. The transfer must be `RECEIVED` and the acting `X-User-ID` must equal its `distributor_id`. The two figures must account for the whole consignment: `doses_used` plus `doses_returned` must equal the transfer's `doses`, and a sum that exceeds or falls short of that total is refused with `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_DOSES`. On success `RECEIVED → RECONCILED` stores both figures, and the ledger moves in three parts: the lot's `doses_consigned` falls by the transfer's `doses`, its `doses_used` rises by the reported `doses_used`, and its `doses_available` rises by the reported `doses_returned` — a conserving three-way move that leaves `straw_count` untouched. `carried_value_cents` is set to 0 because `doses_on_hand` is now 0, the partner's `consignment_value_cents`, `outstanding_balance_cents` and `cumulative_doses_used` are all re-derived, the lot's `version` increments by 1, and a lot whose `doses_available`, `doses_reserved` and `doses_consigned` have all reached 0 moves `RELEASED → DEPLETED`.

The settlement figures are then struck in a pinned order, because the rebate tier is keyed on a subtotal that already includes the service fee and evaluating it anywhere else yields different cents. Step one: `gross_cents` is the reported `doses_used` multiplied by the transfer's `unit_price_cents` — returned doses are never billed and never enter the settlement. Step two: `service_fee_cents` is 120 dollars, that is 12000 cents, for each liquid-nitrogen service performed on the destination tank since dispatch, computed as the tank's current `service_count` minus the transfer's `tank_service_count_at_dispatch`; services performed before dispatch are not billed to this consignment. Step three: the pre-rebate subtotal is `gross_cents` plus `service_fee_cents`. Step four: the volume rebate percentage is taken from the highest tier whose minimum subtotal is at or below that pre-rebate subtotal, where the tiers are 0 percent from a subtotal of 0, 3 percent from a subtotal of 500000 cents, and 6 percent from a subtotal of 1500000 cents, each boundary inclusive. Step five: `rebate_cents` is `gross_cents` multiplied by that percentage, rounded half up to whole cents — the rebate applies to the goods only and never to the service fee. Step six: `settlement_total_cents` is the pre-rebate subtotal minus `rebate_cents`. Every step but the fifth is exact integer arithmetic. All four figures — `gross_cents`, `service_fee_cents`, `rebate_cents` and `settlement_total_cents` — are null before reconciliation.

Returning a consignment unused is the compensating action for the whole approve-dispatch-receive sequence. It takes a `return_reason`, requires the transfer to be `RECEIVED`, requires the acting `X-User-ID` to equal its `distributor_id`, and requires the transfer's `doses_used` to be exactly 0 — a consignment with any insemination reported against it must be reconciled instead. `RECEIVED → RETURNED` sets `doses_returned` to the full `doses`, moves those doses out of the lot's `doses_consigned` and back into `doses_available`, zeroes `carried_value_cents`, re-derives the partner's `consignment_value_cents`, and increments the lot's `version` by 1. `settlement_total_cents` stays null: nothing is billed. The invariant is exact — after an approval followed by a return of the same consignment, the lot's `doses_available` is precisely what it was immediately before the approval, every other bucket is unchanged, and `straw_count` is untouched. `REJECTED`, `RECONCILED` and `RETURNED` are all terminal for a consignment; any transition attempted from the wrong source state — approving or rejecting outside `REQUESTED`, dispatching outside `APPROVED`, receiving outside `IN_TRANSIT`, or reporting usage or returning outside `RECEIVED` — reports `ERR_TRANSFER_STATE`. Consignments are listed and filtered by `straw_lot_id`, `distributor_id`, `tank_id`, `export_certificate_id` and `status`.

### 3.9. Breeding Advisory: Mating Recommendations for Customer Herds

A customer herd is registered under a distribution partner by a `BreedingAdvisor` or by the partner itself. It carries a `herd_name`, the `distributor_id` servicing it, the two-uppercase-letter `country_code` it sits in, a `target_product_line` recording which product line the herd normally buys, and a `max_inbreeding_pct` ceiling — greater than 0 and at most 25 — defaulting to 6.25 when omitted. An optional `base_sire_id` names the herd's incumbent herd sire, who is excluded from every recommendation made for this herd; it is null when the herd has none. `recommendation_count` opens at 0 and thereafter tracks the number of this herd's recommendations sitting in `PROPOSED` or `ACCEPTED`. Herds are listed and filtered by `distributor_id` and `base_sire_id`.

Asking for a recommendation is the advisor's core operation, and the striking thing about it is that **the caller never names a bull** — the server selects one. The request carries the customer-side `dam_ref` identifying the cow to be bred, an optional `dam_sire_id` naming that cow's own sire (the maternal side of the inbreeding computation, whose absence means no pedigree overlap is possible), the `requested_product_line` for this mating, the `doses_requested` the customer wants committed if the recommendation is accepted, and an optional `excluded_sire_ids` array of bulls the customer refuses, which defaults to empty and is removed from the pool before ranking.

The eligibility filter is an explicit conjunction. A bull enters the candidate pool only when his `acquisition_status` is `RESIDENT`; his `collection_status` is `ELIGIBLE`; his `published_evaluation_effective_at` is not null, meaning he actually has a published proof; his id is not the herd's `base_sire_id`; his id is not among `excluded_sire_ids`; his inbreeding coefficient against the supplied `dam_sire_id` is at most the herd's `max_inbreeding_pct`, the boundary being inclusive; and there exists at least one straw lot of his whose `distribution_status` is `RELEASED`, whose `product_line` equals the `requested_product_line`, and whose `doses_available` is at least `doses_requested`. Bulls in `PROSPECT`, `CONTRACTED` or `RETIRED`, and bulls whose collection dimension is anything other than `ELIGIBLE`, never enter the pool even when they own releasable stock. The number of survivors is recorded on the recommendation as `candidate_pool_size`. When that count is 0 — whether because of residency, collection status, a missing published proof, missing releasable stock of the requested line, the exclusion list, base-sire identity or an inbreeding figure above the herd's ceiling — the request is refused with `ERR_NO_ELIGIBLE_SIRE` and no recommendation row is created at all.

Ranking is by highest `published_composite_index` first. Ties are broken by lower `inbreeding_pct` first, and a remaining tie by earlier `created_at` on the bull. A bull's `id` is never a decision input. The winner is stored as `recommended_sire_id`. Within that bull's eligible lots the server then picks the one with the most stock — highest `doses_available` first — breaking a tie on the earlier `created_at` of the lot, and stores it as `straw_lot_id`.

The inbreeding coefficient is Wright's coefficient, truncated at 3 generations on each side. When `dam_sire_id` is null the answer is 0.00 outright. Otherwise the server walks the pedigree upward from the candidate bull, following both the `sire_id` and `dam_sire_id` edges, at most 3 edges deep, and records for each ancestor reached the **shortest** generation distance from the candidate, counting the candidate himself as distance 0; where several paths reach the same ancestor, that shortest distance is the one that counts, which is what makes the sum order-independent. It performs the same bounded walk from the bull named by `dam_sire_id`, and additionally records that bull himself at distance 0 on the maternal side, because the dam's sire lies on the maternal path. For every ancestor appearing in both maps, take the candidate-side distance, take the maternal-side distance plus one — the extra step accounts for the dam standing between the cow and her own sire — add those two together, add one more, and contribute one half raised to that power. Sum the contributions across all shared ancestors, multiply by 100, and round half up to two decimal places to obtain `inbreeding_pct`. Pedigree membership does not depend on proof status: bulls with no published evaluation still participate in the walk. As a worked reference point, when the candidate's own `sire_id` is the same bull as the cow's `dam_sire_id`, the candidate-side distance is 1 and the maternal-side figure is 1, giving 12.50 percent.

The expected conception rate follows from the chosen pairing. Start from the base rate for the `requested_product_line`: 0.60 for `CONVENTIONAL`, 0.48 for `SEXED`, 0.42 for `EMBRYO`. Multiply by one plus 0.0004 for each point of the recommended bull's `published_composite_index`, where a negative published composite is treated as 0. Multiply by the selected lot's parent ejaculate's `post_thaw_motility_pct` divided by 100 — the post-thaw figure recorded at quality evaluation, not the fresh `motility_pct` taken at collection. Multiply by one minus 0.012 for each percentage point of the computed `inbreeding_pct`. Clamp the product to a floor of 0.2000 and a ceiling of 0.7500, and only then round half up to four decimal places, storing the result as `expected_conception_rate`, a fraction between 0 and 1 rather than a percentage.

A new recommendation enters `status` `PROPOSED` with `recommended_sire_id`, `straw_lot_id`, `candidate_pool_size`, `inbreeding_pct` and `expected_conception_rate` all derived and frozen, `doses_committed` at 0 and `decided_at` null; the herd's `recommendation_count` increments, and no dose moves yet. Recommendations are listed and filtered by `customer_herd_id`, `recommended_sire_id`, `straw_lot_id`, `dam_sire_id` and `status`.

The customer's answer arrives as a `decision` of either `ACCEPTED` or `WITHDRAWN`, recorded by the advisor or by the partner, and the two branches are symmetric in specification and opposite in effect. `PROPOSED → ACCEPTED` requires the recommendation to still be `PROPOSED`, the selected lot to still be `RELEASED`, and that lot's `doses_available` to be at least `doses_requested`, failing which `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_DOSES`. It sets `doses_committed` to `doses_requested`, stamps `decided_at` from the logical clock, moves `doses_requested` out of the lot's `doses_available` and into its `doses_reserved`, and increments the lot's `version` by 1. `PROPOSED → WITHDRAWN` likewise requires the recommendation to be `PROPOSED`, stamps `decided_at` from the logical clock, leaves `doses_committed` at 0, touches the lot ledger not at all, and decrements the herd's `recommendation_count`. Both target states are terminal.
