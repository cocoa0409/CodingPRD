# Motor Vehicle Dealer Board API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A state motor vehicle dealer board is one of the few regulators that must simultaneously run a licensing bureau, a franchise registry and a quasi-judicial tribunal out of the same case file. The same dealership that is waiting on a facility inspection may also be the incumbent protesting a franchisor's proposal to open a new point four kilometres down the highway, the claimant in a warranty reimbursement rate dispute, and the respondent in a consumer complaint whose restitution order is about to fall delinquent. Today those four workstreams live in four disconnected systems, and the board's staff reconcile them by hand — which is how a dealer whose licence lapsed on Tuesday still gets a hearing convened on Wednesday, and how a franchisor's amended notice quietly moots a protest nobody withdrew.

This document specifies the Motor Vehicle Dealer Board API, a single service mounted under `/dealer-board` that holds the whole regulatory record: dealer and salesperson licensure, franchise filings and the statutory notice periods that govern them, incumbent protests with good-cause hearing standards and market-analysis evidence, warranty labor rate arbitration between dealers and franchisors, and consumer complaint enforcement with sanctions, disciplinary points and a restitution ledger. Every determination the board makes is derived, never typed in: fee amounts, deficiency codes, market penetration shortfalls, good-cause weightings, hearing cost splits and point totals are all recomputed by the server from the record in front of it.

Two design commitments run through the whole specification and should be read as constraints rather than aspirations. First, **time is explicit**. The service keeps a logical clock that only an administrator may advance, and every statutory deadline — notice periods, protest windows, renewal windows, evidence cut-offs, appeal windows, the rolling 730-day disciplinary window — is measured against that clock rather than against wall-clock time. Advancing the clock also runs an operational sweep that expires licences, suspends delinquent dealers, ages disciplinary points out of the window and makes lapsed appeal windows final. Second, **the board's separations of function are enforced in code**. Licensing staff issue deficiency notices but never decide a dealer application; the hearing clerk owns the docket but never decides a protest; the investigator who takes a complaint may never be the operator who resolves it.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Board Users and Role-Based Access Control

Every request must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting party or operator, and `X-User-Role`, naming the capacity in which that party acts. If either header is missing or empty, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the six declared roles, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). If the headers are well-formed but the named role is not authorised for the endpoint being called, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403).

The six roles are `Dealer`, `Manufacturer`, `LicensingAnalyst`, `HearingClerk`, `ComplaintInvestigator` and `BoardAdmin`.

Two of those roles are **entity-backed**, meaning the header value identifies a persisted row and ownership is checked against it:

- When `X-User-Role` is `Dealer`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Dealer` row. Ownership checks on any resource that references a dealership compare that resource's dealer-bearing foreign key to `X-User-ID`: the `dealer_id` of a licence application, the `sponsoring_dealer_id` of a salesperson, the `protestant_dealer_id` of a protest, the `dealer_id` of a warranty rate dispute, and the `dealer_id` of a consumer complaint whose restitution is being paid.
- When `X-User-Role` is `Manufacturer`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Manufacturer` row. A franchisor may act only on a franchise filing whose `manufacturer_id` equals `X-User-ID`, only as the respondent on a warranty rate dispute whose `manufacturer_id` equals `X-User-ID`, and only on a protest whose protested filing names it as the franchisor.

A write attempt by an entity-backed caller against a resource owned by a different dealership or franchisor is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403). This ownership scoping applies to write operations whose authorised roles include `Dealer` or `Manufacturer`; read operations are never owner-scoped, so any authenticated role may read any record it is authorised to read.

The remaining four roles are **opaque**: `X-User-ID` is an operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity, and the server never compares it to any row's `id`.

- When `X-User-Role` is `LicensingAnalyst`, `X-User-ID` identifies board licensing staff. An analyst issues deficiency notices on dealer applications and decides salesperson licences, and may never decide a dealer application.
- When `X-User-Role` is `HearingClerk`, `X-User-ID` identifies the board's hearing clerk, who dockets and convenes protest hearings and may never decide one.
- When `X-User-Role` is `ComplaintInvestigator`, `X-User-ID` identifies a board enforcement investigator. This is the one opaque identifier the server persists: opening an investigation records the caller's `X-User-ID` as `investigator_user_id` on the complaint, and that stored value is the comparand for the board's separation of function. If the `X-User-ID` presented when a complaint is resolved equals the `investigator_user_id` stamped on it, the resolution is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403).
- When `X-User-Role` is `BoardAdmin`, `X-User-ID` identifies a board administrator/adjudicator. Administrators create reference data, decide dealer applications, protests and rate disputes, resolve complaints, let franchise filings take effect and drive the logical clock.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every collection endpoint accepts two optional query parameters. `limit` controls page size: it defaults to 10, may be no smaller than 1 and no larger than 100. `offset` controls the starting position: it defaults to 0 and may be no smaller than 0. A value outside those bounds is a malformed request rather than a value to be silently clamped.

List results are ordered by `created_at` ascending. The `meta` block of a list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` (the number of rows matching the filters before the page window is applied) and `has_more` (true when rows remain beyond the current window).

### 2.4. Common Record Properties and the Append-Only Evidence Logs

Every record the service persists carries a server-minted `id`, a UUID with no type prefix of any kind, and a `created_at` timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC notation with three fractional digits.

Most records additionally carry `updated_at`, in the same timestamp format, and `version`, an integer that starts at 1 when the record is created and increases by exactly 1 on every mutation of that record. `Manufacturer`, `HearingRoom`, `Dealer`, `LicenseApplication`, `Salesperson`, `FranchiseFiling`, `Protest`, `WarrantyRateDispute` and `ConsumerComplaint` all behave this way: whenever the server writes to one of them — including writes that only touch a derived counter — it refreshes `updated_at` and increments `version`.

The two evidence logs are deliberately different. A market study and an evidence submission are tenders into a quasi-judicial record: once filed they are never edited, never superseded and never re-scored. `MarketStudy` and `EvidenceSubmission` rows therefore expose only `id` and `created_at`. Both `updated_at` and `version` are absent from those two resources entirely, and no operation in this specification mutates a row of either kind after it is created.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock and the Operational Sweep

The service does not read wall-clock time for any business determination. It keeps a single logical clock whose startup value is exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z` and which never falls back to the host's real time. An administrator advances it by supplying a `to_iso8601` instant; the clock only ever moves forward, so an instant that is not strictly later than the current value is refused with `ERR_CLOCK_NOT_MONOTONIC`. Wherever this document says "the clock date", it means the UTC calendar date of the current logical clock instant; "the clock instant" means the full instant.

Advancing the clock is also the service's only batch job. In the same request the server performs an operational sweep that, in order of collection and always processing each collection in `created_at` ascending order so the resulting counters are deterministic:

- expires dealer licences whose `license_expires_on` is earlier than the new clock date;
- suspends any dealer that owes restitution on a sanctioned complaint whose payment deadline has passed;
- expires salesperson licences whose own `license_expires_on` is earlier than the new clock date, and rolls back salesperson discipline standing as disciplinary points age out of the rolling window;
- makes a decided protest unappealable once the new clock date is later than 30 days after the decision date.

The response to a clock advance reports the resulting `current_utc` together with four counters describing what the sweep touched: `swept_dealer_count`, `swept_salesperson_count`, `swept_protest_count` and `swept_complaint_count`.

### 2.6. Money, Percentages and Rounding Discipline

All monetary amounts in this system are integer counts of cents. There are no fractional cents anywhere in the model, and no amount is ever stored or returned as a decimal string. Wherever a monetary computation produces a fraction of a cent, the result is rounded **half up** to a whole cent — a value exactly on the half-cent boundary rounds away from zero.

Percentages and coordinate distances are decimal numbers carried to exactly two decimal places, again rounded half up. Where a calculation names an intermediate rounding step, that step is load-bearing: rounding earlier or later than specified produces a different answer, and the specified order governs.

Labor hours on a repair order line are the one quantity carried to one decimal place. Point totals and page counts are integers and are never rounded because they are never divided.

### 2.7. Parallel State Dimensions and Cascade Propagation

Two entities in this system carry **two independent state dimensions on one row**, and the distinction matters constantly.

A `Dealer` has a licence dimension, `license_status`, drawn from `PROSPECTIVE`, `LICENSED`, `SUSPENDED`, `EXPIRED`, `REVOKED` and `CANCELLED`, and a franchise dimension, `franchise_status`, drawn from `NONE`, `FRANCHISED`, `TERMINATION_PENDING` and `TERMINATED`. A new dealership begins at `PROSPECTIVE` and `NONE`. `REVOKED` and `CANCELLED` are terminal for the licence dimension. The two dimensions do not gate each other: a dealership may be `SUSPENDED` while still `FRANCHISED`, and it may be `LICENSED` while its franchise is `TERMINATION_PENDING`. The licence dimension responds to licence applications, complaint sanctions, restitution payments and the clock sweep; the franchise dimension responds only to franchise filings. Alongside the licence dimension the dealer carries `suspension_reason`, which is `NONE` whenever `license_status` is anything other than `SUSPENDED` and otherwise records why — either `DISCIPLINARY_POINTS` or `RESTITUTION_DELINQUENT`.

A `Salesperson` likewise has a licence dimension, `license_status`, drawn from `PENDING`, `LICENSED`, `RENEWAL_PENDING`, `DENIED`, `SUSPENDED_BY_SPONSOR` and `EXPIRED`, and a discipline dimension, `discipline_status`, drawn from `CLEAR`, `PROBATION`, `SUSPENDED_DISCIPLINARY` and `REINSTATED`. A new applicant begins at `PENDING` and `CLEAR`; `DENIED` is terminal. The discipline dimension is advanced only by complaint resolutions and rolled back only by the clock sweep as points age out. Neither dimension gates a transition of the other — a salesperson on `PROBATION` keeps selling — but the discipline dimension does independently bar a renewal approval.

Cascades in this system are **transactional and multi-link**: a single request may move a dealer, its sponsored salespersons, its open rate disputes and its open applications, and every link must land in the same request. Whenever a cascade touches a record that carries `version`, that record's `version` increments and its `updated_at` refreshes, and derived counters on the affected parents (`licensed_salesperson_count`, `open_dispute_count`, `active_filing_count`, `active_protest_count`, `booked_hearing_count`, `restitution_outstanding_cents`, `disciplinary_points`) are recomputed from the rows that survive the cascade rather than adjusted by guesswork.

### 2.8. Stale-Version Guards on Filings and Hearing Rooms

Two write operations in this system act on a parent record that another party is entitled to move underneath them, and both are guarded by an explicit optimistic-concurrency comparand. In each case the guard field is a required input, not an optional one: omitting it is a malformed request.

**Protesting a franchise filing** requires `expected_franchise_filing_version`, carrying the `version` of the `FranchiseFiling` as the protestant last observed it. A franchisor may legitimately amend a served notice, which re-stamps the proposed effective date and restarts the 30-day protest window and advances the filing's `version`; a protest computed against superseded notice terms would otherwise be docketed against terms that no longer exist. If `expected_franchise_filing_version` does not equal the filing's current `version`, the protest is refused with `ERR_STALE_FILING_VERSION` (HTTP 412).

**Docketing a protest hearing into a room** requires `expected_hearing_room_version`, carrying the `version` of the `HearingRoom` as the clerk last observed it on the room's booking sheet. Every booking, every release of a slot and every change to the room's daily capacity advances `HearingRoom.version`, so a clerk holding an older value was reading a booking sheet that no longer describes the room's occupancy. If `expected_hearing_room_version` does not equal the room's current `version`, the scheduling request is refused with `ERR_STALE_ROOM_VERSION` (HTTP 412).

No other write operation in this specification accepts a version field, and none applies a version check.

### 2.9. Path Resolution for Nested Records

Path identifiers are resolved strictly left to right, and the first identifier that does not resolve determines the error. A protest's market-study and evidence-submission collections are the only nested paths in the service; for those, the protest identifier is resolved first, so a request naming a protest that does not exist is refused with `ERR_PROTEST_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404) before any body field is examined.

Each entity has its own 404: `ERR_DEALER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MANUFACTURER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_HEARING_ROOM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SALESPERSON_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FILING_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PROTEST_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DISPUTE_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_FOUND`. The same catalogue serves foreign keys carried in a request body: after the path resolves, body foreign keys are resolved in the order the operation declares its inputs, so on a create that names both a dealership and a franchisor the dealership is checked first.

### 2.10. The Order in Which a Request Is Rejected

Many operations in this service are guarded by several checks at once, and a request that violates more than one must fail with a predictable error. Checks are therefore evaluated in a fixed order, and the first violated check determines the response. The stages are:

1. **Authentication** — headers present and the role recognised (`ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`).
2. **Role authorisation** — the caller's role is listed on the endpoint (`ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`).
3. **Ownership** — an entity-backed caller owns the resource being written (`ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`).
4. **Separation of function** — a complaint resolution is not attempted by its own investigator (`ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`).
5. **Request validity** — the body parses; every required field is present; every enum value is a declared member; every number lies inside its declared range; every date and datetime is ISO-8601; every repair order line carries `labor_charge_cents`, `labor_hours` and `repair_type`; `excluded_line_indexes` entries are unique and within range; `comparable_market_shares_pct` is present and non-empty when the study's `basis` is `COMPARABLE_MARKET`. Computed and system-maintained fields supplied by a client are ignored rather than honoured, never echoed back as given. Any violation here is `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400).
6. **Path existence** — every identifier in the path resolves, left to right (the 404 catalogue).
7. **Body foreign-key existence** — every foreign key in the body resolves, in the order the operation declares its inputs (the 404 catalogue).
8. **Stale-version guards** — `ERR_STALE_FILING_VERSION` and `ERR_STALE_ROOM_VERSION` (HTTP 412).
9. **State gates** — the target record is in a state that admits the requested action: `ERR_INVALID_APPLICATION_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_SALESPERSON_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_FILING_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_PROTEST_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_DISPUTE_STATE`, `ERR_INVALID_COMPLAINT_STATE` (all HTTP 409).
10. **Party-standing safety checks**, in this order: franchisor registration standing (`ERR_MANUFACTURER_REGISTRATION_LAPSED`), then application deficiencies (`ERR_APPLICATION_HAS_DEFICIENCIES`), then sponsor licensure (`ERR_SPONSOR_NOT_LICENSED`), then the two discipline bars — the undisclosed-history bar (`ERR_UNDISCLOSED_DISCIPLINE`) and the salesperson renewal bar (`ERR_SALESPERSON_DISCIPLINE_BAR`) — and finally the acting dealer's own licence standing (`ERR_DEALER_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`).
11. **Referential consistency**, in this order: line-make agreement (`ERR_LINE_MAKE_MISMATCH`), filing-type field consistency (`ERR_FILING_FIELDS_INCONSISTENT`), protestant standing (`ERR_NOT_INCUMBENT_DEALER`), relevant-market radius (`ERR_OUTSIDE_RELEVANT_MARKET`), tendering-party agreement (`ERR_STUDY_PARTY_MISMATCH`), and salesperson sponsorship agreement (`ERR_SALESPERSON_DEALER_MISMATCH`).
12. **Temporal checks**, in this order: notice period length (`ERR_NOTICE_PERIOD_TOO_SHORT`), protest window still open (`ERR_PROTEST_WINDOW_CLOSED`), protest window already closed (`ERR_PROTEST_WINDOW_OPEN`), effective date arrived (`ERR_EFFECTIVE_DATE_NOT_REACHED`), statutory hearing window (`ERR_HEARING_OUTSIDE_WINDOW`), hearing instant arrived (`ERR_HEARING_NOT_REACHED`), evidence cut-off (`ERR_EVIDENCE_DEADLINE_PASSED`), appeal window (`ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`), franchisor response window (`ERR_RESPONSE_WINDOW_CLOSED`), renewal window open (`ERR_RENEWAL_WINDOW_NOT_OPEN`), renewal grace not expired (`ERR_RENEWAL_GRACE_EXPIRED`), term contiguity (`ERR_TERM_NOT_CONTIGUOUS`) and clock monotonicity (`ERR_CLOCK_NOT_MONOTONIC`).
13. **Capacity, uniqueness and cycle-limit checks**, in this order: buffered room collision (`ERR_HEARING_ROOM_CONFLICT`), room day capacity (`ERR_ROOM_DAY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`), duplicate live protest (`ERR_DUPLICATE_PROTEST`), duplicate exhibit label (`ERR_DUPLICATE_EXHIBIT_LABEL`), amendment cap (`ERR_AMENDMENT_LIMIT_REACHED`), resubmission cap (`ERR_RESUBMISSION_LIMIT_REACHED`), active protests blocking a finalisation (`ERR_ACTIVE_PROTEST_BLOCKS_FINALIZE`), a pending appeal freezing a filing (`ERR_APPEAL_PENDING`), reference-data uniqueness (`ERR_DUPLICATE_LINE_MAKE` and `ERR_DUPLICATE_ROOM_CODE`), an already-open application of the same type (`ERR_OPEN_APPLICATION_EXISTS`) and an already-open dispute for the same pair (`ERR_OPEN_DISPUTE_EXISTS`).
14. **Fiscal and evidentiary sufficiency**, last of all, in this order: repair-order sample size (`ERR_INSUFFICIENT_RO_SAMPLE`), the declared rate exceeding the posted rate (`ERR_RATE_NOT_ABOVE_POSTED`), a loss figure being present for a sanction (`ERR_LOSS_REQUIRED_FOR_SANCTION`), sanction-type consistency (`ERR_SANCTION_TYPE_NOT_ALLOWED`), restitution overpayment (`ERR_RESTITUTION_OVERPAYMENT`), the existence of a probative protestant study (`ERR_NO_PROBATIVE_STUDY`) and the good-cause standard itself (`ERR_GOOD_CAUSE_NOT_ESTABLISHED`).

Two consequences of this ordering are worth spelling out. A filing that is `PROTEST_PENDING` passes the state gate for finalisation and is then rejected by the active-protest check, so `ERR_INVALID_FILING_STATE` and `ERR_ACTIVE_PROTEST_BLOCKS_FINALIZE` remain distinguishable. And because ownership is evaluated before request validity, a dealership acting on another dealership's record is told it is not the owner rather than being told its body is malformed.

### 2.11. Conserved Quantities: the Restitution Ledger and the Room-Slot Ledger

Two quantities in this system are conserved, and the invariants are stated here once so they can be relied on everywhere.

The **restitution ledger** conserves, after every single write: for each sanctioned complaint, the ordered restitution equals the amount already paid plus that complaint's remaining contribution to its dealership's outstanding balance. A dealership's `restitution_outstanding_cents` is the sum, over that dealership's complaints, of the remaining balance on each complaint still in `SANCTIONED` — a complaint that has reached `CLOSED_SATISFIED` contributes zero, and a complaint that reached `CLOSED_UNSUBSTANTIATED` never enters the ledger at all. A payment moves value from one side of the invariant to the other and never changes the sum; because no rounding is ever applied to a payment, no drift can accumulate across a run of partial payments. An overpayment is refused outright rather than clipped to the balance, precisely so the invariant cannot be broken.

The **room-slot ledger** conserves a hearing room's `booked_hearing_count` as exactly the number of protests that both name that room and hold a slot in it. A protest holds a slot when it has a hearing start instant recorded and its status is one of `SCHEDULED`, `HEARD`, `SUSTAINED`, `DENIED`, `APPEALED` or `FINAL`. Protests in `FILED`, `WITHDRAWN` or `DISMISSED` hold nothing, so they neither collide with a new booking nor consume a day's capacity. Releasing a slot decrements the count by exactly one and frees both the time interval and the day slot, so a room whose slot has just been released can be rebooked at the very same instant by a different protest.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Board Reference Data and the Dealership Record

Before any regulatory workflow can begin, an administrator seeds the board's reference data. A **franchisor** — the manufacturer or distributor of a vehicle line — is registered with its `legal_name`, the `line_make` it distributes in the state, the `home_office_state` of its head office, and the `posted_warranty_labor_rate_cents` it currently posts to its dealers. The `line_make` is an uppercase A-to-Z token between 2 and 20 characters (for example `AURORA`) and must be unique across all franchisors; a repeat is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_LINE_MAKE`. `home_office_state` is exactly two uppercase letters. The posted rate is a whole number of cents per hour, no lower than 1,000 and no higher than 100,000. Registration standing may be supplied as `state_registration_status`, which is either `REGISTERED` or `LAPSED` and defaults to `REGISTERED` when omitted. A franchisor record also exposes two derived counters the board reads constantly: `open_dispute_count`, the number of that franchisor's warranty rate disputes still in `FILED` or `UNDER_REVIEW`, and `active_filing_count`, the number of its franchise filings still in `NOTICE_SERVED` or `PROTEST_PENDING`. Both start at 0. Franchisors may be listed with filters on registration standing and line make.

A **hearing room** is created with a `room_code` unique across rooms and between 2 and 12 characters — a duplicate is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_ROOM_CODE` — and a `seating_capacity` of between 10 and 400 physical seats. Two optional settings govern the docket. `max_hearings_per_day` is the greatest number of protest hearings the room may host on one UTC calendar date; it accepts 1 through 4 and defaults to 2. `buffer_minutes` is the mandatory changeover time that must separate two hearings in that room; it accepts 0 through 240 and defaults to 60. The room also carries `booked_hearing_count`, which starts at 0 and is the conserved quantity described in §2.11.

A **dealership** record is opened by an administrator before any licence is applied for, with the applicant's `legal_name`, its `dealer_type` — either `NEW_MOTOR_VEHICLE` or `USED_ONLY` — and its `county_tier`, the population tier of the county containing its established place of business, which is one of `TIER_1`, `TIER_2` or `TIER_3`. The county tier is not cosmetic: it is the multiplier on every licence base fee the dealership will ever pay, at one and a quarter times the base in a `TIER_1` county, exactly the base in `TIER_2`, and four fifths of the base in `TIER_3`. The established place of business is given as a point on the board's planar market grid: `site_x_km` and `site_y_km`, each between -500 and 500 kilometres inclusive and carried to at most two decimal places. A `line_make` may be supplied for the line the dealership sells new — again uppercase A-to-Z, up to 20 characters — and is the empty string for a `USED_ONLY` dealership. Every new record begins with `license_status` at `PROSPECTIVE`, `franchise_status` at `NONE`, `suspension_reason` at `NONE`, `license_effective_on` and `license_expires_on` unset, `disciplinary_points` and `restitution_outstanding_cents` and `licensed_salesperson_count` at 0, and `approved_warranty_labor_rate_cents` unset until the board approves a warranty rate for it. Dealerships may be listed with filters on `license_status`, `franchise_status`, `county_tier` and `line_make`.

### 3.2. Dealer Licensure: the Revision Loop and the Board's Decision

A licence action opens as a **draft application** naming its `dealer_id` and an `application_type` — `INITIAL`, `RENEWAL`, `CHANGE_OF_OWNERSHIP` or `RELOCATION`. A dealership may open its own applications and an administrator may open one on a dealership's behalf, but a dealership may never hold two live applications of the same type: if another application of that same `application_type` is already in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `DEFICIENT`, the request is refused with `ERR_OPEN_APPLICATION_EXISTS`.

The draft carries the statutory particulars of the site. `facility_sqft` is the enclosed showroom and office square footage (0 to 500,000); `display_spaces` counts off-street vehicle display spaces (0 to 2,000); `service_bays` counts bays available for warranty and repair work (0 to 200); `bond_amount_cents` is the surety bond posted with the application and `insurance_liability_cents` the garage liability per-occurrence limit, both non-negative integers in cents; `zoning_certificate_status` records the municipal zoning certificate's standing as `APPROVED`, `CONDITIONAL`, `PENDING` or `DENIED`; and `line_make_count` is the number of distinct line makes to be franchised at the site, from 0 through 12, and is 0 for a used-only applicant. Three fields are conditional on the action requested: `requested_term_start_on`, the first day of the requested licence term, matters for a renewal; `relocation_site_x_km` and `relocation_site_y_km` give the proposed new coordinates and are required for a `RELOCATION` and ignored otherwise; and `new_owner_name` names the transferee and is required for a `CHANGE_OF_OWNERSHIP` and ignored otherwise. A freshly created application sits at `DRAFT` with `deficiency_codes` empty, `deficiency_count` at 0, `resubmission_count` at 0, and `submitted_at`, `decided_at`, `decision_outcome`, `fee_quoted_cents`, `fee_total_cents`, `fee_delta_cents` and `deficiency_note` all unset.

**Submitting** a draft moves the application `DRAFT → SUBMITTED` and is the dealership's own act; it is refused with `ERR_INVALID_APPLICATION_STATE` unless the application is in `DRAFT`, and a dealership whose `license_status` is `REVOKED` or `CANCELLED` may not submit at all. Submission stamps `submitted_at` with the current clock instant and runs the statutory sufficiency scan.

The **sufficiency scan** compares the application's own particulars against the minimums for its dealership's `dealer_type` and produces `deficiency_codes`, a list of unmet-requirement codes sorted in ascending lexicographic order — never in the order the checks happen to run — together with `deficiency_count`, which is simply the number of entries in that list. A new-motor-vehicle applicant must show at least 8,000 square feet of enclosed facility, at least 10 display spaces, at least 3 service bays, a bond of at least 5,000,000 cents (fifty thousand dollars) and garage liability of at least 100,000,000 cents (one million dollars). A used-only applicant must show at least 2,000 square feet, at least 3 display spaces, no service bays at all, a bond of at least 2,500,000 cents and garage liability of at least 50,000,000 cents. Falling below the facility minimum records `FACILITY_TOO_SMALL`; below the display minimum, `DISPLAY_SPACES_INSUFFICIENT`; below the bay minimum, `SERVICE_BAYS_INSUFFICIENT`; below the bond minimum, `BOND_INSUFFICIENT`; below the insurance minimum, `INSURANCE_INSUFFICIENT`. Zoning is satisfactory when `zoning_certificate_status` is `APPROVED`, and also when it is `CONDITIONAL` provided the `application_type` is `RENEWAL` or `RELOCATION`; in every other case the scan records `ZONING_NOT_APPROVED`. Every comparison is an integer comparison, and an application still in `DRAFT` is never scanned.

Submission also freezes a quoted price. **The licence fee** is assembled in a fixed order, and the order is load-bearing because the county multiplier scales the base fee alone. First, the base fee is selected by action: 120,000 cents for an `INITIAL` licence, 90,000 cents for a `RENEWAL`, 150,000 cents for a `CHANGE_OF_OWNERSHIP` and 75,000 cents for a `RELOCATION`. That base is multiplied by the dealership's county multiplier from §3.1 and the product is rounded half up to a whole cent; nothing else in the calculation is multiplied by the tier. Second, a line-make surcharge of 25,000 cents is levied for each line make named in `line_make_count`, with the surcharge as a whole capped at 100,000 cents. Third, a facility credit rewards a site above the 8,000-square-foot baseline: take the application's `facility_sqft` less 8,000, treat any shortfall as zero, divide the excess by 1,000 and discard the fraction, award 4,000 cents for each whole thousand square feet so counted, and cap the credit at 30,000 cents. Fourth, a late-renewal penalty applies only to a `RENEWAL` and only when the dealership already has a `license_expires_on` on file: count the whole days from that prior expiry to the reference date, treat a negative count as zero, charge 5,000 cents per late day, and cap the penalty at 50,000 cents. The fee is then the tiered base plus the surcharge, less the credit, plus the penalty, and never below zero. Note that the surcharge, the credit and the penalty are all flat cent amounts applied after the multiplier and each is capped independently of the others.

That single arithmetic is evaluated twice against two different reference dates, and the difference is the whole point. On submission and on every resubmission the reference date is the clock date at that moment, so the penalty reflects the delinquency as of filing, and the result is written to `fee_quoted_cents`. At decision time the reference date is the decision date, so any additional delinquent days that accrued while the board deliberated are billed; that result is written to `fee_total_cents`, and `fee_delta_cents` is `fee_total_cents` less `fee_quoted_cents`. Every other term is identical between the two evaluations, so on identical data a decision landing later than the submission yields a strictly larger total whenever the penalty cap has not yet been reached. A voided application is never re-priced.

Where the scan finds at least one deficiency, a `LicensingAnalyst` may return the application for correction, moving it `SUBMITTED → DEFICIENT` and storing the analyst's narrative as `deficiency_note`. The analyst may only do this from `SUBMITTED` and only when `deficiency_count` is at least 1; a `BoardAdmin` may not issue deficiency notices at all. The dealership then corrects and **resubmits**, moving the application `DEFICIENT → SUBMITTED` again. A resubmission overwrites only the facility, bond, insurance and zoning particulars actually supplied — `facility_sqft`, `display_spaces`, `service_bays`, `bond_amount_cents`, `insurance_liability_cents`, `zoning_certificate_status` and `line_make_count` are each optional on a resubmission and unmentioned ones keep their values — then increments `resubmission_count` by 1, re-stamps `submitted_at` from the clock, and re-runs both the sufficiency scan and the quoted-fee evaluation at the new clock position. The revision loop is bounded at two resubmissions: a third attempt, made when `resubmission_count` already stands at 2, is refused with `ERR_RESUBMISSION_LIMIT_REACHED`.

A renewal is fenced in by three date rules, each of which also governs a salesperson renewal. It may not be submitted earlier than 60 days before the current expiry (`ERR_RENEWAL_WINDOW_NOT_OPEN`), nor later than 30 days after it (`ERR_RENEWAL_GRACE_EXPIRED`). And a renewal's `requested_term_start_on` must be exactly the day after the licensee's current `license_expires_on`, so consecutive terms abut precisely and never overlap; a start date that does not line up is refused with `ERR_TERM_NOT_CONTIGUOUS`, both at submission and again at decision.

The **board's decision** is an administrator's act and is a genuine fork: the two halves have different side effects and must be implemented separately. It is taken by supplying an `outcome`, and a `denial_reason_code` where the outcome denies the application. An approval is permitted only from `SUBMITTED`; a denial is permitted from either `SUBMITTED` or `DEFICIENT`. Both halves stamp `decided_at`, set `decision_outcome` to `APPROVED` or `DENIED`, and recompute `fee_total_cents` and `fee_delta_cents`.

On the approving half, three further conditions apply before anything is written. The recomputed `deficiency_count` must be 0 — the statutory minimums are not waivable by the board, and an approval attempted on a deficient application is refused with `ERR_APPLICATION_HAS_DEFICIENCIES`. The dealership's `license_status` must not be `SUSPENDED`, `REVOKED` or `CANCELLED`. And the dealership's `restitution_outstanding_cents` must be exactly 0. Where those hold, the approval advances the dealership's licence dimension to `LICENSED` from either `PROSPECTIVE` or `EXPIRED` and writes the term dates: for anything other than a renewal, `license_effective_on` is the decision date; for a renewal it is the application's `requested_term_start_on`, which gives an `EXPIRED → LICENSED` renewal retroactive continuity with the term that just lapsed. Either way the dealer term runs 730 days, so `license_expires_on` is 729 days after `license_effective_on` and is the last day of the term inclusive. On the same approval the franchise dimension advances from `NONE` to `FRANCHISED` when the `dealer_type` is `NEW_MOTOR_VEHICLE`, and stays at `NONE` for a `USED_ONLY` dealership — the franchise dimension moving here independently of the licence dimension, exactly as §2.7 describes. A `RELOCATION` approval additionally overwrites the dealership's `site_x_km` and `site_y_km` with the application's `relocation_site_x_km` and `relocation_site_y_km`; a `CHANGE_OF_OWNERSHIP` approval overwrites the dealership's `legal_name` with `new_owner_name`. The dealership's `version` increments by 1.

On the denying half, `denial_reason_code` must be present, and both of the dealership's state dimensions are left completely untouched — that is the divergent half of the fork, and no term date, coordinate or legal name is written.

One further terminal state exists for an application, reached only by cascade. When a dealership is revoked in the course of a complaint resolution, every application it holds in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `DEFICIENT` moves to `VOID_SPONSOR_CANCELLED`. No fee is finalised for a voided application: its `fee_total_cents` stays unset. `APPROVED`, `DENIED` and `VOID_SPONSOR_CANCELLED` are all terminal. Applications may be read individually or listed with filters on `dealer_id`, `status` and `application_type`.

### 3.3. Salesperson Licensure, Renewal and the Discipline Dimension

Only a licensed dealership may put a salesperson forward. Sponsoring an applicant requires a `sponsoring_dealer_id`, the applicant's `full_name`, and `disclosed_prior_discipline` — the applicant's own boolean disclosure of prior disciplinary action in any jurisdiction. The sponsoring dealership's `license_status` must be `LICENSED` at that moment; a `SUSPENDED`, `EXPIRED`, `REVOKED` or `CANCELLED` dealership may not sponsor anybody, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_DEALER_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`. A new applicant starts with `license_status` at `PENDING`, `discipline_status` at `CLEAR`, `disciplinary_points` at 0, `renewal_count` at 0, `waiver_granted` false, and `license_effective_on`, `license_expires_on` and `denial_reason_code` all unset.

A `LicensingAnalyst` then **decides** the licence by supplying an `outcome`, optionally a `waiver_granted` flag, and a `denial_reason_code` where the decision denies. The same act serves both the initial grant and the completion of a renewal, so it is admitted only from `PENDING` or `RENEWAL_PENDING`; anywhere else it is refused with `ERR_INVALID_SALESPERSON_STATE`. Every approval, initial or renewal, requires the sponsoring dealership's `license_status` to be `LICENSED`; a sponsor that has fallen out of `LICENSED` bars the approval with `ERR_SPONSOR_NOT_LICENSED` unless the analyst records `waiver_granted` as true. An initial approval carries one further gate: an applicant who declared `disclosed_prior_discipline` as false while already carrying more than zero rolling `disciplinary_points` may only be licensed if `waiver_granted` is true, and otherwise draws `ERR_UNDISCLOSED_DISCIPLINE`. An applicant who disclosed truthfully, or who carries no points at all, needs no waiver.

An approval from `PENDING` moves the licence dimension `PENDING → LICENSED`, sets `license_effective_on` to the clock date and `license_expires_on` to 364 days later — a salesperson term is 365 days, with the expiry date the last day of the term inclusive — and increments the sponsoring dealership's `licensed_salesperson_count`. A denial from `PENDING` moves it `PENDING → DENIED`, stamps no term dates at all, and is terminal.

**Renewal** is the dealership's act and cycles the licence dimension without bound. Requesting a renewal moves the salesperson `LICENSED → RENEWAL_PENDING` and requires the clock date to fall inside the renewal window: on or after the day 60 days before `license_expires_on`, and on or before the day 30 days after it. Too early draws `ERR_RENEWAL_WINDOW_NOT_OPEN`; too late draws `ERR_RENEWAL_GRACE_EXPIRED`. A salesperson carrying 9 or more rolling `disciplinary_points` may not renew at all, and the request is refused with `ERR_SALESPERSON_DISCIPLINE_BAR`. Entering `RENEWAL_PENDING` decrements the sponsoring dealership's `licensed_salesperson_count` — the licence is momentarily not in force — and leaves `discipline_status` completely untouched, because the two dimensions are orthogonal. An approving decision from `RENEWAL_PENDING` moves the licence dimension back to `LICENSED`, likewise requires fewer than 9 rolling points on pain of `ERR_SALESPERSON_DISCIPLINE_BAR`, sets `license_effective_on` to the day after the previous `license_expires_on` so consecutive terms abut exactly, sets the new `license_expires_on` to 364 days after that, and increments both `renewal_count` and the sponsoring dealership's `licensed_salesperson_count`. A denying decision from `RENEWAL_PENDING` is terminal at `DENIED` and leaves `licensed_salesperson_count` alone, since it was already decremented when the renewal was requested.

Four movements of the licence dimension are not requested by anybody but follow from what happens to the sponsor. Whenever a sponsoring dealership leaves `LICENSED` — by disciplinary suspension, by restitution-delinquency suspension, by revocation, by licence expiry or by franchise cancellation — each of its salespersons in `LICENSED` or `RENEWAL_PENDING` moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_SPONSOR` and the dealership's `licensed_salesperson_count` is decremented accordingly. When the dealership is later reinstated to `LICENSED`, each of its salespersons sitting in `SUSPENDED_BY_SPONSOR` returns to `LICENSED` if its own `license_expires_on` is on or after the current clock date, and otherwise moves to `EXPIRED`; the count is recomputed either way, and no count changes on the move to `EXPIRED` because it was already decremented at suspension. Independently, the clock sweep moves a salesperson from `LICENSED` to `EXPIRED` whenever its own `license_expires_on` falls earlier than the new clock date, decrementing the sponsor's count, and moves a `SUSPENDED_BY_SPONSOR` salesperson to `EXPIRED` on the same condition without any count change.

The discipline dimension runs on a **rolling point total** computed entirely separately from the dealership's. A salesperson's `disciplinary_points` is the sum of the points assessed by every complaint that names that salesperson and stands in either `SANCTIONED` or `CLOSED_SATISFIED`, counting only complaints whose sanction date falls on or after the day exactly 730 days before the reference date. The lower bound is inclusive: a sanction dated exactly 730 days back still counts, one dated 731 days back does not. Decay is a step, not a slope — a sanction carries its full weight until its 730th day and none from the 731st onward — and the roll-off is observed only when a write or a clock sweep recomputes the window. This total is what the renewal bar reads, and the sponsoring dealership's own point total has no bearing on it.

Resolving a complaint that names a salesperson recomputes that total and then moves the discipline dimension. At 6 or more points but fewer than 9, a `CLEAR` salesperson moves to `PROBATION`. At 9 or more points, a salesperson on `PROBATION` moves to `SUSPENDED_DISCIPLINARY`, and a salesperson still `CLEAR` moves straight to `SUSPENDED_DISCIPLINARY` as well — a single `SUSPENSION_RECOMMENDED` sanction skips probation entirely. None of these movements touches `license_status`: a salesperson on `PROBATION` keeps selling, and the discipline dimension alone is what blocks a renewal approval. Rollback happens only on the clock sweep, as points age out: once the recomputed total falls below 9 a salesperson in `SUSPENDED_DISCIPLINARY` moves to `REINSTATED`, and once it falls below 6 a salesperson on `PROBATION` returns to `CLEAR`. Again `license_status` is untouched in both cases. Salespersons may be read individually or listed with filters on `sponsoring_dealer_id`, `license_status` and `discipline_status`.

### 3.4. Franchise Filings: Notice, Amendment and Taking Effect

A franchisor opens a **franchise filing** as a draft, naming its own `manufacturer_id`, the `filing_type` it proposes — `TERMINATION`, `RELOCATION` or `ADDITIONAL_POINT` — and the `proposed_effective_on` date on which it asks the action to take effect. The remaining inputs are conditional on the type, and getting them wrong is `ERR_FILING_FIELDS_INCONSISTENT`: a `TERMINATION` and a `RELOCATION` must both name the `affected_dealer_id` whose franchise is at issue, a `RELOCATION` and an `ADDITIONAL_POINT` must both carry the proposed destination as `site_x_km` and `site_y_km`, an `ADDITIONAL_POINT` must omit `affected_dealer_id` entirely, and `termination_cause_code` — the statutory cause cited — is present for a `TERMINATION` and for nothing else. Where an affected dealership is named, its `line_make` must match the franchisor's; a mismatch is refused with `ERR_LINE_MAKE_MISMATCH`. On creation the server copies the franchisor's `line_make` onto the filing as the line make whose representation is at issue, and the filing opens at `DRAFT` with `amendment_count` and `active_protest_count` at 0 and `notice_served_on`, `notice_days_provided` and `protest_window_ends_on` all unset.

**Serving statutory notice** moves the filing `DRAFT → NOTICE_SERVED` and is what opens the protest window. Only a franchisor whose `state_registration_status` is `REGISTERED` may serve; a lapsed franchisor is refused with `ERR_MANUFACTURER_REGISTRATION_LAPSED`. The notice period must reach the statutory minimum, measured as the whole days from the clock date to `proposed_effective_on`: at least 60 days for a `TERMINATION` or a `RELOCATION`, at least 30 days for an `ADDITIONAL_POINT`. A shorter period is refused with `ERR_NOTICE_PERIOD_TOO_SHORT`. Service stamps `notice_served_on` with the clock date, records `notice_days_provided` as the whole days from `notice_served_on` to `proposed_effective_on`, and sets `protest_window_ends_on` to 30 days after `notice_served_on` — the last day, inclusive, on which an incumbent may protest. It then recomputes the franchisor's `active_filing_count` and increments the filing's `version`. On a `TERMINATION`, service also moves the affected dealership's franchise dimension `FRANCHISED → TERMINATION_PENDING` and increments that dealership's `version`; the licence dimension is deliberately left alone, because the dealership keeps selling throughout the notice period.

A franchisor may **amend** a served notice, and the amendment loop is the reason the filing carries a version comparand at all. An amendment is admitted from `NOTICE_SERVED`, where it returns the filing to `NOTICE_SERVED`, and from `PROTEST_PENDING`, where it also returns the filing to `NOTICE_SERVED`. It requires a lapse-free franchisor, a new `proposed_effective_on` that again satisfies the type's notice minimum measured from the current clock date, optional replacement `site_x_km` and `site_y_km` coordinates, and a filing whose `amendment_count` is still below 2 — a third amendment is refused with `ERR_AMENDMENT_LIMIT_REACHED`. From `PROTEST_PENDING` an amendment is additionally barred once the record has moved on: if any protest against the filing stands in `HEARD`, `SUSTAINED`, `DENIED`, `APPEALED` or `FINAL`, the filing may no longer be amended and the request is refused with `ERR_INVALID_FILING_STATE`. The amendment increments `amendment_count` by 1, re-stamps `notice_served_on` to the clock date, recomputes `notice_days_provided`, and restarts the protest window by setting `protest_window_ends_on` to 30 days after the clock date. It overwrites `proposed_effective_on`, and overwrites the site coordinates when they were supplied. The filing's `version` increments by 1.

Amending from `PROTEST_PENDING` also runs a cascade, and every link matters. Each protest against that filing standing in `FILED` or `SCHEDULED` moves to `WITHDRAWN`. For each of those withdrawn protests the docket is released: `hearing_room_id`, `hearing_start_at`, `hearing_end_at` and `hearing_session_count` are all cleared, and for each hearing room so affected the room's `booked_hearing_count` is recomputed and its `version` increments by 1. The filing's `active_protest_count` is then recomputed to 0. What an amendment does **not** do is restore a dealership's franchise standing: a `TERMINATION_PENDING` dealership stays `TERMINATION_PENDING` while the notice period simply restarts around it.

**Finalising** a filing is an administrator's act and moves it to `EFFECTIVE`. It is admitted from `NOTICE_SERVED` and from `PROTEST_RESOLVED`; a filing in `DRAFT`, `EFFECTIVE` or `BARRED` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_FILING_STATE`. Four further conditions apply. The protest window must already have closed, meaning the clock date is strictly later than `protest_window_ends_on`, or the request draws `ERR_PROTEST_WINDOW_OPEN`. The proposed effective date must have arrived, meaning the clock date is on or after `proposed_effective_on`, or the request draws `ERR_EFFECTIVE_DATE_NOT_REACHED`. The filing's `active_protest_count` must be exactly 0, or the request draws `ERR_ACTIVE_PROTEST_BLOCKS_FINALIZE` — which is how a filing still sitting in `PROTEST_PENDING` is turned away, since it clears the state gate and then fails this one. And no protest against the filing may stand in `APPEALED`: a pending court appeal freezes the filing, and the attempt draws `ERR_APPEAL_PENDING`.

Finalisation's dispositions depend on the type, and the `TERMINATION` case is the longest cascade in the system. On a `TERMINATION` the affected dealership's franchise dimension moves `TERMINATION_PENDING → TERMINATED` and, for a `NEW_MOTOR_VEHICLE` dealership, its licence dimension moves `LICENSED → CANCELLED` in the same request. Because the franchise no longer exists, every warranty rate dispute naming that dealership and still standing in `FILED` or `UNDER_REVIEW` moves to `DISMISSED_MOOT`, and because those disputes are no longer open, `open_dispute_count` is recomputed for each franchisor whose dispute was mooted. In the same request each of that dealership's salespersons in `LICENSED` or `RENEWAL_PENDING` moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_SPONSOR`, and the dealership's `version` increments by 1. On a `RELOCATION`, finalisation overwrites the affected dealership's `site_x_km` and `site_y_km` with the filing's coordinates. On an `ADDITIONAL_POINT`, no dealership row is touched at all. In every case the franchisor's `active_filing_count` is recomputed and the filing's `version` increments by 1. `EFFECTIVE` and `BARRED` are the filing's terminal states. Filings may be read individually or listed with filters on `manufacturer_id`, `affected_dealer_id`, `filing_type` and `status`.

### 3.5. Protesting a Filing: Standing and the Relevant Market

An incumbent dealership protests a served filing by lodging a protest that names the `franchise_filing_id` under attack, its own `protestant_dealer_id`, the statutory `grounds_code` it asserts — `INADEQUATE_REPRESENTATION`, `NO_GOOD_CAUSE_FOR_TERMINATION` or `MARKET_ENCROACHMENT` — and the `expected_franchise_filing_version` comparand described in §2.8. The protestant must itself be a dealership in good standing: a protestant whose `license_status` is not `LICENSED` is turned away with `ERR_DEALER_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`.

The filing must be open to protest, which means standing in `NOTICE_SERVED` or `PROTEST_PENDING`; a filing still in `DRAFT`, or already in `PROTEST_RESOLVED`, `EFFECTIVE` or `BARRED`, is refused with `ERR_INVALID_FILING_STATE`. The 30-day window must still be running: the clock date must be on or before the filing's `protest_window_ends_on`, and a later filing draws `ERR_PROTEST_WINDOW_CLOSED`. The protestant's own `line_make` must match the line make carried on the protested filing, or the protest draws `ERR_LINE_MAKE_MISMATCH`. And a protestant may hold only one live protest per filing: if it already has a protest against that same filing standing in `FILED`, `SCHEDULED`, `HEARD`, `SUSTAINED`, `DENIED` or `APPEALED`, the second is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_PROTEST`.

**Standing** turns on the filing type. Against a `TERMINATION`, the only party with standing is the dealership being terminated, so the protestant must be the filing's own `affected_dealer_id`. Against a `RELOCATION` or an `ADDITIONAL_POINT`, standing belongs to a *different* dealership — the incumbent whose territory is at issue — and that dealership's `franchise_status` must be either `FRANCHISED` or `TERMINATION_PENDING`. A party that fits neither description is refused with `ERR_NOT_INCUMBENT_DEALER`.

For a `RELOCATION` or an `ADDITIONAL_POINT` the server then computes the **relevant-market distance**. Take the difference between the filing's proposed `site_x_km` and the protestant dealership's own `site_x_km`, and the difference between the filing's proposed `site_y_km` and the protestant's `site_y_km`; the distance is the square root of the sum of the two squared differences, rounded half up to two decimal places, and is recorded on the protest as `distance_to_site_km`. Standing requires the proposed site to fall inside or exactly on a relevant-market radius of 24.00 kilometres, so a computed distance of exactly 24.00 confers standing and anything greater is refused with `ERR_OUTSIDE_RELEVANT_MARKET`. Against a `TERMINATION`, standing does not rest on geography at all and `distance_to_site_km` stays unset.

A protest that survives all of that is created at `FILED` with `filed_on` stamped as the clock date, `hearing_room_id` and all four docket fields unset, `accepted_evidence_pages` and `late_evidence_count` at 0, and `good_cause_score`, `assessment_total_cents`, `protestant_share_cents`, `manufacturer_share_cents`, `decided_on`, `findings_note`, `appeal_filed_on`, `appellant_party` and `court_docket_number` all unset. Its `good_cause_threshold` is fixed by the type of filing it attacks: 40.00 where the filing is a `TERMINATION`, because the franchisor bears the burden of showing cause there, and 60.00 in every other case. The first protest against a filing moves that filing `NOTICE_SERVED → PROTEST_PENDING`; every protest, first or not, causes the filing's `active_protest_count` to be recomputed and its `version` to increment by 1. That counter is the number of protests against the filing standing in `FILED`, `SCHEDULED`, `HEARD` or `SUSTAINED`. A protest in `APPEALED` is deliberately excluded from it, because a pending court appeal is caught by the dedicated appeal guard described in §3.4 rather than by this count. Protests may be read individually or listed with filters on `franchise_filing_id`, `protestant_dealer_id`, `hearing_room_id` and `status`.

### 3.6. The Hearing Docket: Scheduling, Exhibits and Convening

The `HearingClerk` owns the docket. **Docketing** a protest moves it `FILED → SCHEDULED` and requires the `hearing_room_id` of the room, the `hearing_start_at` and `hearing_end_at` instants in UTC, a `hearing_session_count` between 1 and 3 giving the number of billable days of testimony reserved, and the `expected_hearing_room_version` comparand of §2.8.

Three timing rules govern the request. The hearing must fall inside the statutory hearing window, which runs from the day 30 days after `filed_on` to the day 180 days after `filed_on`, both bounds inclusive; and `hearing_end_at` must be strictly later than `hearing_start_at`. A violation of either is `ERR_HEARING_OUTSIDE_WINDOW`. Next, the requested interval must not collide with any other slot-holding hearing in the same room once the room's changeover buffer is applied on both sides. Treat each booking as a half-open interval running from its start instant up to but not including its end instant. Two bookings collide when the new booking's start is earlier than the existing booking's end extended forward by the room's `buffer_minutes`, and the existing booking's start is earlier than the new booking's end extended forward by the same buffer. Because both comparisons are strict, a gap exactly equal to the buffer is permitted, while genuinely back-to-back bookings collide. A collision is refused with `ERR_HEARING_ROOM_CONFLICT`. Finally, the room must have day capacity: counting only slot-holding hearings whose start instant falls on the same UTC calendar date as the requested start, the existing count must be strictly fewer than the room's `max_hearings_per_day`, or the request draws `ERR_ROOM_DAY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`.

Docketing stores all four docket fields on the protest, derives `evidence_deadline_at` as exactly 7 days before `hearing_start_at`, increments the room's `booked_hearing_count` by 1 and increments the room's `version` by 1.

Either party may then tender **exhibits** into the protest's append-only exhibit log, which is admitted while the protest stands in `FILED` or `SCHEDULED` and refused with `ERR_INVALID_PROTEST_STATE` otherwise. A tender names the `submitting_party` as `PROTESTANT` or `MANUFACTURER`, an `exhibit_label` of 1 to 20 characters as marked in the record, a `page_count` from 1 to 5,000, and optionally `leave_granted`, the presiding officer's leave to file out of time, which defaults to false and is ignored whenever the filing is timely. The declared party must match the caller's actual side of the protest — a `PROTESTANT` tender must come from the protest's protestant dealership and a `MANUFACTURER` tender from the protested filing's franchisor — and a mismatch is refused with `ERR_STUDY_PARTY_MISMATCH`. A protestant tendering evidence must itself still be `LICENSED`, on pain of `ERR_DEALER_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`. Within one protest, an `exhibit_label` must be unique for a given `submitting_party`, so the two sides may each mark their own Exhibit A but neither may mark two; a repeat draws `ERR_DUPLICATE_EXHIBIT_LABEL`.

The server stamps `received_at` with the clock instant and classifies the tender in its `acceptance_status`. An exhibit received on or before `evidence_deadline_at` takes the `acceptance_status` `ACCEPTED` — evidence submitted as of right. An exhibit received after that deadline takes `LATE_ACCEPTED` only when `leave_granted` is true *and* the tender still precedes `hearing_start_at`; the boolean `is_late` records exactly that outcome. Anything else — after the deadline without leave, or at or after the hearing start instant — is refused with `ERR_EVIDENCE_DEADLINE_PASSED` and no row is stored. Each accepted tender causes the protest's `accepted_evidence_pages` to be recomputed as the sum of `page_count` across its `ACCEPTED` and `LATE_ACCEPTED` exhibits, and its `late_evidence_count` to be recomputed as the number of its `LATE_ACCEPTED` exhibits. Refused tenders are never billed. Exhibit logs may be listed per protest with a filter on `submitting_party`.

**Convening** the docketed hearing is the clerk's last act on it, and it is a fork with two entirely different consequences. It is admitted only from `SCHEDULED`, and only once the clock instant has reached `hearing_start_at`; an early attempt draws `ERR_HEARING_NOT_REACHED`. Which branch runs is decided by the protestant's own licence standing at that instant.

Where the protestant dealership's `license_status` is still `LICENSED`, the protest moves `SCHEDULED → HEARD`. That branch requires at least one probative market study tendered by the `PROTESTANT` to be on file, or the hearing cannot proceed and the request is refused with `ERR_NO_PROBATIVE_STUDY`. Reaching `HEARD` freezes `accepted_evidence_pages` and `late_evidence_count` as they then stand, and computes `good_cause_score` together with `good_cause_threshold`. The room's `booked_hearing_count` is unchanged, because a heard protest still occupies its slot.

Where the protestant has instead lost standing — its `license_status` is `SUSPENDED`, `EXPIRED`, `REVOKED` or `CANCELLED` — the protest moves `SCHEDULED → DISMISSED` and gives up the room. That branch clears `hearing_room_id`, `hearing_start_at`, `hearing_end_at` and `hearing_session_count`, decrements the room's `booked_hearing_count` by exactly 1 and increments the room's `version` by 1, which is what frees both the interval and the day slot for another protest. No `good_cause_score` is computed on this branch and no cost assessment is ever levied. And where the dismissed protest was the last one still live against its filing, the filing moves `PROTEST_PENDING → PROTEST_RESOLVED` in the same request. `DISMISSED` is terminal, as are `WITHDRAWN`, `APPEALED` and `FINAL`.

### 3.7. Market Studies, the Good-Cause Standard and the Board's Protest Decision

A market study is the evidentiary heart of a protest. Either party may tender one into the protest's append-only study log — each study records the `protest_id` of the protest it is evidence in — while the protest stands in `FILED` or `SCHEDULED`, and each tender declares its `submitted_by_party` as `PROTESTANT` or `MANUFACTURER`. As with exhibits, a `PROTESTANT` study must be posted by the protest's protestant dealership and a `MANUFACTURER` study by the protested filing's franchisor, and a mismatch is refused with `ERR_STUDY_PARTY_MISMATCH`. A study is never edited, superseded or re-scored once filed.

Every study reports the relevant market area it studied: `total_market_registrations`, the new-vehicle registrations of all line makes in that area over the study year, and `line_make_registrations`, the registrations of the filing's own line make over the same year, which may not exceed the total. Both are non-negative integers. The study also tenders `permanency_investment_cents`, the permanency-of-investment figure attributed to the protestant's dealership, a non-negative integer in cents; `consumer_access_drive_minutes`, the drive time the study attributes to consumers reaching an authorised outlet, from 0 to 600 minutes; and `market_growth_pct`, the projected annual growth of the market area in percent, from -50.00 to 100.00 carried to two decimals.

The `basis` the party elects then decides how expected penetration is derived, and the two bases are genuinely different instruments. Under `STATEWIDE_AVERAGE` — the statutory default basis — the study must supply `statewide_line_make_share_pct`, the statewide share of the line make in percent between 0.00 and 100.00 to two decimals, and expected penetration is that figure verbatim; any `comparable_market_shares_pct` supplied alongside it is ignored. Under `COMPARABLE_MARKET` the study must instead supply `comparable_market_shares_pct`, a non-empty array of line-make shares in percent for the comparable markets the party chose, and expected penetration is the unweighted mean of those shares — their sum divided by how many there are — rounded half up to two decimals; any `statewide_line_make_share_pct` supplied alongside it is ignored. On the same registration counts the two bases therefore produce different shortfalls, and so a different score.

Three figures are computed and stored on each study as it is filed. Where `total_market_registrations` is zero there is no divisor, so `actual_penetration_pct` is 0.00 and `is_probative` is false; otherwise `actual_penetration_pct` is 100 multiplied by `line_make_registrations`, divided by `total_market_registrations`, rounded half up to two decimals, and `is_probative` is true. `expected_penetration_pct` is derived from the basis as described above. And `shortfall_pts` is `expected_penetration_pct` less `actual_penetration_pct`, rounded half up to two decimals, with any negative result raised to 0.00 — these are percentage points, not ratios. A non-probative study is stored but is excluded from good-cause scoring entirely, which is what stops a party from displacing its own controlling study with a zero-divisor filing.

**Good cause** is a weighted score over the studies on file. For each party the server first selects that party's *controlling* study among its probative studies only, ranking candidates by `created_at` descending, then by `shortfall_pts` descending, then by `total_market_registrations` descending; the row identifier is never a ranking key. The protestant's controlling study is required — its absence is the `ERR_NO_PROBATIVE_STUDY` condition from §3.6 — while the franchisor's controlling study is optional.

The net shortfall is the protestant's controlling `shortfall_pts` less the franchisor's controlling `shortfall_pts`, rounded half up to two decimals and never allowed below 0.00; where the franchisor has tendered no probative study, the amount subtracted is 0.00. Four components are then derived, each rounded half up to two decimals and each capped **before** any weight is applied:

- The **inadequacy component** is the net shortfall multiplied by 8, capped at 100.00.
- The **investment component** is the protestant controlling study's `permanency_investment_cents` divided by 500,000 — one point for every five thousand dollars of permanency investment — capped at 100.00.
- The **access component** is neutral at exactly 50.00 whenever the franchisor has no controlling study. Where it has one, take the franchisor study's `consumer_access_drive_minutes` less the protestant study's `consumer_access_drive_minutes`, multiply that difference by 4, add the neutral 50.00, round half up to two decimals, and then clamp the result to the range 0.00 through 100.00 inclusive.
- The **growth component** is the protestant controlling study's `market_growth_pct` multiplied by 5, capped at 100.00.

The `good_cause_score` is then four tenths of the inadequacy component, plus one quarter of the investment component, plus one fifth of the access component, plus fifteen hundredths of the growth component — weights that sum to exactly 1.00 — with the weighted sum rounded half up to two decimals once, at the very end. Good cause is established when `good_cause_score` is greater than or equal to `good_cause_threshold`, inclusive at the threshold itself.

The whole weighting is readable at any time, not only after a hearing, through a computed read on the protest that reports `controlling_protestant_study_id`, `controlling_manufacturer_study_id`, `net_shortfall_pts`, `inadequacy_component`, `investment_component`, `access_component`, `growth_component`, `good_cause_score`, `good_cause_threshold` and the boolean `good_cause_established`.

The **board's decision** on a heard protest is an administrator's act, admitted only from `HEARD`, and taken by supplying an `outcome` and optionally a `findings_note` recording the board's narrative. Both branches stamp `decided_on` with the clock date and levy the hearing cost assessment. Sustaining the protest — moving it `HEARD → SUSTAINED` — is permitted only when `good_cause_score` is greater than or equal to `good_cause_threshold`; otherwise the attempt is refused with `ERR_GOOD_CAUSE_NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Denying it moves the protest `HEARD → DENIED` and carries no such gate.

The **cost assessment** is built in a fixed order. Room time is 45,000 cents for each session reserved, so the session component is 45,000 multiplied by the protest's `hearing_session_count`. Transcription is 350 cents per page, so the transcript component is 350 multiplied by the protest's frozen `accepted_evidence_pages`. Those two amounts together are the subtotal. A late-filing surcharge is levied only when the protest's `late_evidence_count` is greater than zero, and its rate is chosen from the **subtotal** — never from the post-surcharge total — by taking the first matching band in ascending order: a subtotal of 100,000 cents or less attracts five percent, a subtotal above that but no more than 250,000 cents attracts eight percent, and any larger subtotal attracts twelve percent. The surcharge is the subtotal multiplied by that rate, rounded half up to a whole cent, and is zero when no exhibit came in late. `assessment_total_cents` is the subtotal plus the surcharge.

The two parties then split that total, and the arithmetic is asymmetric on purpose. The **prevailing** party's share is 30 percent of `assessment_total_cents`, rounded half up to a whole cent; the losing party absorbs the remainder, computed as `assessment_total_cents` less the prevailing share, so that `protestant_share_cents` and `manufacturer_share_cents` always re-sum to `assessment_total_cents` exactly with no rounding drift. Where the protest is sustained the franchisor has lost, so `protestant_share_cents` is the rounded prevailing share and `manufacturer_share_cents` is the remainder. Where the protest is denied the protestant has lost, so `protestant_share_cents` is the remainder and `manufacturer_share_cents` is the rounded prevailing share.

A sustained protest bars the filing. Where the sustained protest is the only protest against that filing not already in `WITHDRAWN` or `DISMISSED`, the filing moves `PROTEST_PENDING → BARRED` and may never be finalised, and the franchisor's `active_filing_count` is recomputed. Where the barred filing was a `TERMINATION`, the affected dealership's franchise dimension is restored `TERMINATION_PENDING → FRANCHISED`, because a filing that may never take effect cannot terminate anybody, and that dealership's `version` increments by 1. A denied protest instead lets the filing proceed: once no protest against it stands in `FILED`, `SCHEDULED`, `HEARD`, `SUSTAINED` or `APPEALED`, the filing moves `PROTEST_PENDING → PROTEST_RESOLVED` and its `active_protest_count` is recomputed to 0.

Either side may **notice an appeal** to the circuit court from `SUSTAINED` or from `DENIED`, supplying the `court_docket_number` the court assigned. The appeal must be noticed within 30 days of `decided_on`, meaning the clock date is on or before the day 30 days after the decision; later is refused with `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`. Noticing an appeal moves the protest to `APPEALED`, stamps `appeal_filed_on` with the clock date, records `court_docket_number`, and derives `appellant_party` from the caller's role: `MANUFACTURER` where the franchisor appealed, `PROTESTANT` where the protestant dealership did. While a protest sits in `APPEALED` the underlying filing is frozen against finalisation, as §3.4 describes.

If nobody appeals, the disposition hardens by itself. On a clock advance, any protest standing in `SUSTAINED` or `DENIED` whose `decided_on` is more than 30 days behind the new clock date moves to `FINAL`: the appeal window has lapsed and the disposition is unappealable, and where the protest was denied the filing may now be finalised.

### 3.8. Warranty Reimbursement Rate Disputes

A franchised dealership that believes its franchisor's posted warranty labor rate is below the retail rate it actually charges may bring a rate dispute, naming its own `dealer_id`, the `manufacturer_id` of the franchisor whose posted rate it challenges, and `repair_order_lines`, a sample of its own retail repair orders. The dealership must be `LICENSED` (`ERR_DEALER_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`), the franchisor must be `REGISTERED` (`ERR_MANUFACTURER_REGISTRATION_LAPSED`), and that particular dealership-and-franchisor pair must not already have a dispute standing in `FILED` or `UNDER_REVIEW` (`ERR_OPEN_DISPUTE_EXISTS`).

`repair_order_lines` holds between 1 and 100 elements and their order is preserved, because the position of each line is the index the franchisor will later cite. Each element carries `labor_charge_cents`, a non-negative integer; `labor_hours`, a number strictly greater than zero carried to at most one decimal place; and `repair_type`, one of `CUSTOMER_PAID`, `WARRANTY` or `INTERNAL`. Only a `CUSTOMER_PAID` line whose `labor_hours` is at least 0.5 counts as a **qualifying** line — warranty work, internal work and sub-half-hour lines are excluded from every computation in this section, whether or not the franchisor ever names them. `qualifying_line_count` is the number of qualifying lines across the whole sample and is frozen at filing; the franchisor's later exclusions never shrink it, so the sample-sufficiency test is a filing-time property. A sample carrying fewer than 5 qualifying lines is refused with `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_RO_SAMPLE`.

The dealership's **declared rate** is a per-order mean, and the order of rounding is the whole point of it: divide each qualifying line's `labor_charge_cents` by its `labor_hours`, round each of those per-order rates half up to a whole cent *first*, then average the rounded per-order rates by summing them and dividing by how many there are, rounding that average half up to a whole cent. The result is written to `declared_rate_cents`. Because every order carries equal weight regardless of how many hours it represents, a short high-charge order pulls this mean upward. The declared rate must strictly exceed the franchisor's `posted_warranty_labor_rate_cents` — a dealership may not litigate down to a rate it already receives — and a declared rate at or below the posted rate is refused with `ERR_RATE_NOT_ABOVE_POSTED`. A surviving dispute is created at `FILED`, with `filed_on` stamped as the clock date, `counter_rate_cents`, `spread_pct`, `manufacturer_response_type`, `approved_rate_cents`, `effective_on`, `decided_on` and `denial_reason_code` all unset, and `excluded_line_indexes` empty; the franchisor's `open_dispute_count` is recomputed.

The franchisor must **answer** within 30 days of `filed_on`, or the answer is refused with `ERR_RESPONSE_WINDOW_CLOSED`; the answer is admitted only from `FILED` and only from a `REGISTERED` franchisor, and moves the dispute `FILED → UNDER_REVIEW`. It supplies `manufacturer_response_type` as either `ACCEPT` or `REBUT`, and where it rebuts it may supply `excluded_line_indexes`, zero-based positions into `repair_order_lines` that the franchisor asks the board to disregard. Those indexes must be unique and within range, and they are meaningful only alongside `REBUT`.

On a `REBUT` the server computes the franchisor's **counter rate**, which is the same data pooled the opposite way round: over the lines that both qualify and whose index is not among `excluded_line_indexes`, sum all the `labor_charge_cents` and sum all the `labor_hours`, divide the first sum by the second *once*, and round half up to a whole cent only at the very end. Written to `counter_rate_cents`, this weights each order by its labor hours, so on identical data it lands at a materially different figure from the declared rate — that divergence is the entire subject matter of the dispute. The server also records `spread_pct`: 100 multiplied by the difference between `declared_rate_cents` and `counter_rate_cents`, divided by `counter_rate_cents`, rounded half up to two decimals. On an `ACCEPT`, both `counter_rate_cents` and `spread_pct` stay unset and the board may only approve the declared rate. Either way the franchisor's `open_dispute_count` is recomputed.

The **board's disposition** is an administrator's act taken by supplying an `outcome` and, on a denial, a `denial_reason_code`. An approval is admitted only from `UNDER_REVIEW` and requires all of: `declared_rate_cents` strictly above the franchisor's `posted_warranty_labor_rate_cents`, a `qualifying_line_count` of at least 5, and a dealership whose `license_status` is `LICENSED` and whose `franchise_status` is either `FRANCHISED` or `TERMINATION_PENDING`. The rate the board approves depends on how close the two computations came: where the franchisor accepted the sample, or where `spread_pct` is 5.00 or less, `approved_rate_cents` is the declared rate outright; otherwise the board splits the difference, and `approved_rate_cents` is the sum of `declared_rate_cents` and `counter_rate_cents` divided by two, rounded half up to a whole cent. The approval stamps `decided_on` with the clock date, sets `effective_on` to 30 days after `decided_on`, overwrites the dealership's `approved_warranty_labor_rate_cents` with the approved figure and increments that dealership's `version` by 1.

A denial is admitted from either `FILED` or `UNDER_REVIEW`, records `denial_reason_code` and `decided_on`, and leaves the dealership's `approved_warranty_labor_rate_cents` exactly as it was. Both dispositions recompute the franchisor's `open_dispute_count`. `APPROVED`, `DENIED` and `DISMISSED_MOOT` are the dispute's terminal states, the last of which is reached only through the termination cascade of §3.4. Disputes may be read individually or listed with filters on `dealer_id`, `manufacturer_id` and `status`.

### 3.9. Consumer Complaints, Sanctions and the Restitution Ledger

A `ComplaintInvestigator` intakes a consumer complaint naming the `dealer_id` complained of, the `complainant_name` of the consumer, the statutory `allegation_code`, and `alleged_loss_cents`, the consumer's claimed loss as a non-negative integer in cents. A `salesperson_id` may be named where an individual is implicated, and is left unset for a dealership-only complaint; where one is named, that salesperson must be sponsored by the very dealership complained of, or the intake is refused with `ERR_SALESPERSON_DEALER_MISMATCH`. The `allegation_code` is one of `ODOMETER_MISREPRESENTATION`, `FAILURE_TO_TRANSFER_TITLE`, `ADVERTISING_VIOLATION`, `DEPOSIT_RETENTION` or `UNLICENSED_SALESPERSON`, and it fixes the civil penalty base. A new complaint opens at `RECEIVED` with `points_assessed` at 0, `restitution_paid_cents` at 0 and `investigator_user_id`, `findings_code`, `substantiated`, `sanction_type`, `civil_penalty_cents`, `restitution_ordered_cents`, `restitution_due_on` and `resolved_at` all unset.

**Opening the investigation** moves the complaint `RECEIVED → UNDER_INVESTIGATION`, records the investigation's `findings_code`, and stamps `investigator_user_id` with the calling investigator's `X-User-ID` — the comparand that will later exclude that operator from resolving the file. An investigation may not be opened against a dealership whose `license_status` is already `CANCELLED`.

**Resolution** is an administrator's act, admitted only from `UNDER_INVESTIGATION` and only from an `X-User-ID` other than the stamped `investigator_user_id`. It supplies `substantiated` and, where the complaint is substantiated, a `sanction_type` of `WARNING`, `CIVIL_PENALTY`, `PROBATION` or `SUSPENSION_RECOMMENDED`. `sanction_type` must be present exactly when `substantiated` is true and absent when it is false; either mismatch is refused with `ERR_SANCTION_TYPE_NOT_ALLOWED`. A substantiated resolution additionally requires a `findings_code` other than `NO_VIOLATION_FOUND`, and an `alleged_loss_cents` strictly greater than zero so that the restitution ledger is never opened with a zero balance — a zero loss draws `ERR_LOSS_REQUIRED_FOR_SANCTION`.

The two halves of the resolution fork diverge completely. A resolution that does **not** substantiate the complaint moves it `UNDER_INVESTIGATION → CLOSED_UNSUBSTANTIATED`, stamps `resolved_at` with the clock instant, and stops there: `points_assessed` stays 0, `civil_penalty_cents`, `restitution_ordered_cents` and `restitution_due_on` all stay unset, and the dealership's `disciplinary_points` and `restitution_outstanding_cents` are left exactly as they were. An unsubstantiated complaint contributes nothing to any point total, at any age, ever.

A resolution that **does** substantiate the complaint moves it `UNDER_INVESTIGATION → SANCTIONED` and computes the sanction. `points_assessed` is fixed by the sanction chosen: a `WARNING` carries 0 points, a `CIVIL_PENALTY` carries 3, a `PROBATION` carries 6 and a `SUSPENSION_RECOMMENDED` carries 9. `restitution_ordered_cents` is the consumer's `alleged_loss_cents` capped at 1,500,000 cents (fifteen thousand dollars). The civil penalty is 0 for a `WARNING`; for every other sanction it is the allegation's base amount plus five percent of the restitution ordered, that loss-linked component rounded half up to a whole cent, with the sum then capped at 500,000 cents. The allegation bases are 200,000 cents for `ODOMETER_MISREPRESENTATION`, 100,000 cents for `FAILURE_TO_TRANSFER_TITLE`, 50,000 cents for `ADVERTISING_VIOLATION`, 75,000 cents for `DEPOSIT_RETENTION` and 150,000 cents for `UNLICENSED_SALESPERSON`. Note the ordering: the restitution cap binds before the penalty is computed, so a capped loss also caps the loss-linked half of the penalty, and both caps apply after the addition. `restitution_due_on` is set to 30 days after the resolution date and `restitution_paid_cents` is left at 0.

The sanction then recomputes the dealership's **rolling point total**. A dealership's `disciplinary_points` is the sum of `points_assessed` across every one of its complaints standing in `SANCTIONED` or `CLOSED_SATISFIED` whose sanction date falls on or after the day exactly 730 days before the reference date; the lower bound is inclusive, so a sanction dated exactly 730 days back still counts and one dated 731 days back does not. A complaint's sanction date is the date part of its `resolved_at` where it has one, and otherwise the date 30 days before its `restitution_due_on`. Settling restitution does not retire the points it carried — a `CLOSED_SATISFIED` complaint keeps contributing until it ages out.

Two thresholds then act on the licence dimension, and both cascade. Where the recomputed total reaches 12 but stays below 18, the dealership moves `LICENSED → SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `DISCIPLINARY_POINTS`; each of its salespersons in `LICENSED` or `RENEWAL_PENDING` moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_SPONSOR`; and because none remain in force, `licensed_salesperson_count` is recomputed to 0. Where the recomputed total reaches 18, the dealership moves `LICENSED → REVOKED` with `suspension_reason` left at `NONE`; each of its salespersons in `LICENSED` or `RENEWAL_PENDING` again moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_SPONSOR`; and every one of its licence applications standing in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `DEFICIENT` moves to `VOID_SPONSOR_CANCELLED`. Either way the dealership's `version` increments by 1. Where the complaint named a salesperson, that salesperson's own `disciplinary_points` and `discipline_status` are recomputed in the same request, as §3.3 describes.

**Restitution** is paid by the dealership, in full or in instalments, by supplying an `amount_cents`, and is admitted only while the complaint stands in `SANCTIONED`. A payment that would push `restitution_paid_cents` above `restitution_ordered_cents` is refused outright with `ERR_RESTITUTION_OVERPAYMENT` rather than being clipped to the balance. A payment that leaves a balance outstanding keeps the complaint in `SANCTIONED`, increases `restitution_paid_cents` by the amount paid and decreases the dealership's `restitution_outstanding_cents` by exactly that same amount, conserving the ledger invariant of §2.11. A payment that settles the balance exactly moves the complaint `SANCTIONED → CLOSED_SATISFIED`, sets `restitution_paid_cents` equal to `restitution_ordered_cents`, stamps `resolved_at` with the clock instant, and recomputes the dealership's `restitution_outstanding_cents` so that this complaint's contribution drops to 0.

Settling the last outstanding cent is also the compensating reversal for a delinquency suspension. Where the dealership was `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` `RESTITUTION_DELINQUENT` and no other complaint of that dealership remains delinquent, its licence dimension is restored `SUSPENDED → LICENSED` with `suspension_reason` returned to `NONE`. That restoration additionally requires the dealership's `restitution_outstanding_cents` to be 0, its recomputed `disciplinary_points` to be below 12, and its `license_expires_on` to be on or after the current clock date. On restoration, each of its salespersons sitting in `SUSPENDED_BY_SPONSOR` returns to `LICENSED` where its own `license_expires_on` is on or after the clock date and otherwise moves to `EXPIRED`, `licensed_salesperson_count` is recomputed, and the dealership's `version` increments by 1.

The delinquency itself is discovered by the clock. On a clock advance, where a dealership has an unsettled sanctioned complaint whose `restitution_due_on` falls strictly earlier than the new clock date and whose `restitution_paid_cents` is still below its `restitution_ordered_cents`, that dealership moves `LICENSED → SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `RESTITUTION_DELINQUENT`, its salespersons in `LICENSED` or `RENEWAL_PENDING` move to `SUSPENDED_BY_SPONSOR`, `licensed_salesperson_count` is recomputed and the dealership's `version` increments by 1. The inequality is strict: a payment deadline falling exactly on the new clock date is not yet delinquent. The complaint itself stays in `SANCTIONED` throughout — `SANCTIONED` is an absorbing but non-terminal state, held until the ledger is settled — and dealerships are swept in `created_at` ascending order so the sweep's counters are deterministic. `CLOSED_UNSUBSTANTIATED` and `CLOSED_SATISFIED` are the complaint's terminal states. Complaints may be read individually or listed with filters on `dealer_id`, `salesperson_id`, `status` and `allegation_code`.
