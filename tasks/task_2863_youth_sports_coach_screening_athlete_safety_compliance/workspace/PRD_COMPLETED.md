# Youth Sports Safety Compliance API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A national governing body does not coach anyone. It charters the clubs that do, and it answers for them — to parents, to insurers, to state licensing agencies, and increasingly to legislators who now require documented proof that every adult standing beside a minor athlete was screened, trained, and monitored. Today that proof lives in a filing cabinet at each club: a spreadsheet of background-check receipts, a stack of course-completion certificates, a folder of incident letters. When a complaint arrives, nobody can say within a working week whether the named coach was currently screened on the date in question, whether the club had trained him before his first practice, or whether a prior allegation should already have kept him off the field.

This document specifies the SafeSport compliance platform that replaces those filing cabinets with a single system of record, mounted under `/safesport`. The platform carries the whole chain of custody for adult eligibility: registering coaches and volunteers, scoring each one into a background-screening tier, pricing and ordering the screen against the club's prepaid escrow, receiving the vendor's raw return, adjudicating it against the disqualifying criteria, assigning and tracking abuse-prevention training, and only then permitting the individual to be placed in front of athletes. It also carries the consequences: incident intake under the mandatory-reporting obligation, routing to the independent investigating body, temporary and permanent restrictions on individuals under investigation, travel and lodging controls that keep adults and minor athletes out of shared rooms, electronic-communication authorization, periodic compliance audits of the clubs themselves, and sanction of the organizations that fail them.

Two design commitments run through every requirement below. First, **safety outranks money and convenience**: when several rules would refuse the same request, the platform reports the safety refusal, even when a fiscal or capacity refusal would also have applied. Second, **eligibility is derived, never asserted**: no caller may set a member's contact eligibility, a club's compliance posture, or an audit's score directly. Those values are recomputed by the platform from screening currency, training currency, active restrictions, and open audit findings every time an input to them changes, so a member who lapses at midnight is refused at 00:00 without anyone filing a form.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Actors, Identity Headers and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to every endpoint carries two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role that principal acts in. If either header is absent or empty — a value consisting only of whitespace counts as empty — or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the six roles defined below, the request is refused with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401) before anything else about the request is examined. If the headers are well formed but the named role is not authorized for the endpoint being called, the refusal is `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403).

The six roles are `NgbAdmin`, `ClubAdministrator`, `ScreeningVendor`, `SafeSportOfficer`, `ComplianceAuditor`, and `Member`.

`NgbAdmin` is national governing body staff. The role charters clubs, publishes abuse-prevention courses, registers investigating bodies, voids screening orders, and repositions the platform's logical clock; it carries no club-scope restriction and may read everything. When `X-User-Role` is `NgbAdmin`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity — the server may record it (for example as `reporter_user_id` on a case that an operator files) but never compares it to any entity's `id`.

`ClubAdministrator` administers exactly one chartered club. When `X-User-Role` is `ClubAdministrator`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Club`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Club` compare the resource's club foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, the addressed resource must belong to the administrator's own club, whether the linkage is direct (a club, a member's chartering club, a coach assignment's club, a room stay's club, an audit's club) or indirect (the chartering club of the member behind a screening order, a training assignment, or an interim measure, and the audited club behind an audit finding — a finding is scoped by the club its own audit examines rather than by the club that charters the member it names, which is also how an organization-level finding carrying no `member_id` is scoped). A mismatch is refused with `ERR_CLUB_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (HTTP 403), and the same code covers a body reference — not merely a path segment — that names another club's resource.

`ScreeningVendor` is the external background-screening provider. Its single capability is returning the raw disposition of a screen it was asked to run; it never adjudicates that disposition and never sees the disqualifying-criteria decision. When `X-User-Role` is `ScreeningVendor`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity, recorded at most for audit and never compared to an entity's `id`.

`SafeSportOfficer` is a governing-body safety officer: adjudicating returned screens, taking incident intake, routing cases to the investigating body, imposing and lifting temporary measures, and converting a measure to permanent. When `X-User-Role` is `SafeSportOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server does record it — as `reporter_user_id` on a case the officer files, as `imposed_by_user_id` on a measure the officer imposes, and as `converted_by_user_id` on a measure the officer converts — and later steps compare the acting `X-User-ID` against those recorded strings, but never against any entity's `id`.

`ComplianceAuditor` is the independent auditor of member organizations: opening audits, writing findings, finalizing the compliance score, rejecting or waiving remediation, and sanctioning noncompliant clubs. The role never touches screening, cases, or measures. When `X-User-Role` is `ComplianceAuditor`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity and is not compared to any entity's `id`.

`Member` is a registered coach or volunteer acting on their own behalf. When `X-User-Role` is `Member`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Member`; ownership checks compare the resource's member foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. A `Member` caller may start and complete only their own training assignments, and may read only their own member record and its sub-resources; addressing another member's resource is refused with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`. Filing an incident report is the one exception to the ownership rule: a `Member` may file a report naming any individual, so the reported member is deliberately not required to be the caller.

Two responsibilities are split across principals rather than roles. A `SafeSportOfficer` may not convert an interim measure that the same `X-User-ID` imposed, and may not close a case that the same `X-User-ID` filed; both refusals are `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). Separately, the two parties to an audit finding hold different halves of the decision authority: a `ClubAdministrator` may submit remediation evidence but may not reject or waive, and a `ComplianceAuditor` may reject or waive but may not submit remediation on the club's behalf; the mismatch is `ERR_DECISION_ROLE_MISMATCH` (HTTP 403).

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_` followed by exactly twenty characters drawn from the uppercase letters `A` through `Z` and the digits `0` through `9`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, read from real wall-clock time rather than from the logical clock of §2.5, so it does not move when that clock is repositioned.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every list endpoint accepts two optional query parameters. `limit` defaults to 10, must be at least 1, and must not exceed 100. `offset` defaults to 0 and must be at least 0. A `limit` outside 1 through 100, or a negative `offset`, is a request-shape failure and is refused with `ERR_VALIDATION` (HTTP 422), and so is a value for either parameter that is not a base-ten integer; a parameter supplied as an empty string is treated as absent and falls back to its default.

List responses carry `limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, and `has_more` inside `meta`, where `total_count` is the number of rows matching the filters before the page window is applied and `has_more` is true exactly when rows remain beyond the current window. Unless a specific list endpoint states otherwise, results are ordered by `created_at` ascending; because the logical clock stands still between repositionings, many rows share an identical `created_at`, and such ties are broken by creation sequence — the order in which the rows were actually created — while a list documented as descending is the exact reverse of that same ascending order. Four lists deviate deliberately: the audit list scoped to a single club is ordered by `created_at` descending so that the newest audit reads first; and all three room-stay lists — the one scoped to a single club, the one scoped to a single member, and the flat governing-body-wide collection alike — are ordered by `check_in` ascending and then by `created_at` ascending as a tie-break. All three room-stay lists fall back to creation sequence as their final tie-break when `check_in` and `created_at` are both identical.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and the `version` Counter

Every resource in this platform — clubs, members, screening orders, training courses, training assignments, coach assignments, investigating bodies, cases, interim measures, club audits, audit findings, and room stays — carries the same four common properties. `id` is a server-minted UUID with no type prefix and is never accepted from a client. `created_at` is the ISO 8601 UTC instant of creation with three fractional digits, read from the logical clock of §2.5 rather than from wall-clock time. `updated_at` is the ISO 8601 UTC instant of the most recent mutation of that row, likewise with three fractional digits, and equals `created_at` on a freshly created row. `version` is an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increases by exactly 1 on every mutation of that row, whether the mutation came from a direct client request or from a cascade triggered elsewhere. No entity in this platform is exempt: `updated_at` and `version` are present on all twelve resource types. The one exception to the `version` rule is the clock-driven lapse of §2.5, which refreshes `updated_at` alone; §2.10 names the remaining cases in which a row's stored fields move without either counter advancing, all of them re-derivations of computed values plus the two club fields the audit workflow writes directly.

Any field the specification below describes as derived, computed, snapshotted, or stamped is written by the server only. A request body that attempts to supply such a field is refused with `ERR_VALIDATION`, and that same refusal covers any key whatsoever that the called endpoint's input schema does not name — server-written or simply unrecognised; the client's only levers are the input fields named in the workflow narratives of §3. A key present with an explicit JSON `null` is treated exactly as though it had been omitted: a required field so supplied is refused as missing, and an optional one falls back to its documented default.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock and Lazily Evaluated Lapses

All expiry, lapse, window, and cooling-buffer arithmetic reads a **logical clock** rather than wall-clock time. On server start the clock stands at exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and until it is repositioned every rule that consults the clock sees that instant. An `NgbAdmin` may reposition the clock to any instant by supplying `to_iso8601`; the response reports the resulting `current_utc`. Day arithmetic anywhere in this document adds exact multiples of 24 hours, and every instant the platform emits is ISO 8601 UTC with three fractional digits. On the way in, an instant-valued input field accepts any ISO 8601 spelling of the same moment: a trailing `Z`, a numeric UTC offset which is normalized to UTC, any number of fractional digits or none at all, no zone designator at all which is read as UTC, or a bare `YYYY-MM-DD` calendar date which is read as midnight UTC. Year arithmetic — used only by the seven-year retention horizons of §3.5 — is not a multiple of 365 days but a calendar-year offset that keeps the same month and day of month, a 29 February source date landing on 28 February when the target year is not a leap year.

Four transitions are driven by the clock rather than by a caller, and all four are **evaluated lazily**: the platform applies them at the moment a read or a mutation next touches the affected row, so a client that repositions the clock and then reads sees the post-lapse state without any intervening request. A `CLEARED` screening becomes `EXPIRED` once the clock reaches or passes the member's `screening_expires_at` — the boundary is inclusive, so at exactly that instant the screen is already `EXPIRED`. An `ACTIVE` membership becomes `LAPSED` once the clock reaches or passes the member's `eligible_through`, again inclusively. An `ASSIGNED` training assignment becomes `EXPIRED` once the clock reaches or passes its `due_at`; an assignment already `IN_PROGRESS` is never expired by the clock. An `ACTIVE` coach assignment becomes `EXPIRED` once the clock reaches or passes its `season_end`, which is the exclusive end of the season interval. Whenever one of these lapses fires, the member's contact eligibility and the affected clubs' derived counters are recomputed by the same rules that govern a caller-driven change. A lapse refreshes the lapsing row's `updated_at` from the logical clock but does not advance its `version`, and the pure recomputation of derived values that follows a lapse changes neither. The evaluation is a sweep rather than a per-row check: every row in the platform is examined once at the start of every authenticated and authorized request, before that request's own handler runs, so a read of one resource also settles a pending lapse standing over an unrelated one.

### 2.6. Version Pinning on Escrow and Eligibility Writes

Three write operations act on a snapshot of a parent record that the client has already read, and each therefore requires the client to pin the parent's `version` in the request body. The field is non-optional on those three operations, and it is not accepted on any other endpoint.

Ordering a background screen requires `expected_club_version`, carrying the `version` of the chartering `Club` that the caller last observed; the pinned value must itself be a non-negative integer, so a negative or non-integer value is a request-shape failure refused with `ERR_VALIDATION` before any comparison is attempted. If it does not equal the club's current `version`, the request is refused with `ERR_STALE_CLUB_VERSION` (HTTP 412): another screening order, a void, or an escrow funding has already moved the balance the caller budgeted against, so the debit would be computed from a stale escrow view.

Activating a pending coach placement requires `expected_member_version`, carrying the `version` of the `Member` the caller last observed. If it does not equal the member's current `version`, the request is refused with `ERR_STALE_MEMBER_VERSION` (HTTP 412) — the activation would pin an eligibility view that a training completion, a renewal, or a screening adjudication has since rewritten.

Imposing an interim measure likewise requires `expected_member_version` against the `Member` named in the measure, and a mismatch with the member's current `version` is refused with `ERR_STALE_MEMBER_VERSION` (HTTP 412). A second measure of a different kind against an unchanged member is legitimate and would succeed, so this version check is the only guard against restricting a member record the caller has not actually observed.

### 2.7. Precision, Rounding and Monetary Representation

Money is represented exclusively as integer **cents** — `escrow_balance_cents`, `total_funded_cents`, `initial_escrow_cents`, `amount_cents`, `gross_subtotal_cents`, `discount_cents`, `expedite_cents`, `charged_cents`, `fine_cents`, and `sanction_fine_cents` are all whole numbers of cents, and no cent is ever represented as a fraction. Wherever a percentage or multiplier produces a fractional cent, the result is rounded half up to a whole cent, and the document below states exactly where each rounding occurs; a total is never rounded a second time after its rounded components have been summed.

Compliance scores are decimal numbers carried to exactly two decimal places and rounded half up, and they are capped at 100.00; on the wire a score is emitted as a JSON number rather than a decimal string, so a score of 100.00 appears as `100.0` and one of 0.00 as `0.0`, and a score not yet computed is emitted as `null`. Risk points and every counter in the system are integers. All threshold comparisons named in this document are stated with their inclusivity in words, and the stated inclusivity is normative: a comparison described as "reaches or exceeds" admits the boundary value, while one described as "strictly above" or "strictly before" does not.

### 2.8. The Order in Which a Request Is Refused

Because many operations are guarded by several rules at once, the platform evaluates its checks in one fixed order and reports **the first failure only**. The order encodes the governing body's priorities: identity, then authority, then existence, then scope, then request shape, then referenced existence, then uniqueness, then version pinning, then resource state, then club posture, then the athlete-safety rules, then temporal windows, then routing and capacity constraints, and last of all money. This is why an under-funded club that also names a disqualified member hears about the disqualification (an athlete-safety refusal) rather than the escrow shortfall (a fiscal refusal), and why safety refusals are never masked by capacity refusals.

The full order is:

1. Both `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` are present and non-empty and the role is one of the six declared roles — otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. The acting role is authorized for the called endpoint — otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`.
3. Every path-templated resource id resolves to an existing row, validated left to right, so an outer segment's 404 is reported before an inner one's — `ERR_CLUB_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SCREENING_ORDER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TRAINING_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_COACH_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INVESTIGATING_BODY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CASE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INTERIM_MEASURE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CLUB_AUDIT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AUDIT_FINDING_NOT_FOUND`, or `ERR_ROOM_STAY_NOT_FOUND` as appropriate.
4. A `ClubAdministrator`'s `X-User-ID` matches the owning club of the addressed resource — otherwise `ERR_CLUB_SCOPE_VIOLATION`.
5. A `Member` caller acts only on their own resources — otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`.
6. The request body conforms to the endpoint's input schema: required fields present, JSON types correct, enum values members of their declared set, string lengths within bounds, `limit` between 1 and 100, `offset` at least 0, `season_end` strictly after `season_start`, `check_out` strictly after `check_in`, `audit_period_end` strictly after `audit_period_start`, `effective_to` at or after `effective_from`, `jurisdiction_codes` non-empty and free of duplicates, and a room stay's `member_id` and `minor_athlete_age` pairing consistent with its `occupant_kind` — otherwise `ERR_VALIDATION`.
7. Every id or natural key named in the body resolves — a `club_id`, a `member_id`, an `investigating_body_id`, each entry of `sampled_member_ids`, and a `course_code` against a published course — otherwise `ERR_CLUB_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INVESTIGATING_BODY_NOT_FOUND`, or `ERR_TRAINING_COURSE_NOT_FOUND`.
8. Cross-aggregate referential rules hold: an interim measure's `member_id` equals the case's `reported_member_id`, every entry of an audit's `sampled_member_ids` is a member of the audited club, a finding's `member_id`, when supplied, is one of that audit's sampled members, and a `club_id` or a `member_id` supplied in the *body* belongs to the acting `ClubAdministrator`. That last rule sits here rather than at step 4 because it is tested only once every reference in the body has resolved: a `ClubAdministrator` who names a nonexistent club or member hears the 404 of step 7, and one who names another club's member on a request that also names an unpublished `course_code` hears `ERR_TRAINING_COURSE_NOT_FOUND` first. Any of these failing is refused with `ERR_CLUB_SCOPE_VIOLATION`.
9. Uniqueness holds: a member's `email` is unique across the entire governing body compared case-insensitively (`ERR_DUPLICATE_MEMBER_EMAIL`), and a course's `code` is unique across all courses (`ERR_DUPLICATE_COURSE_CODE`).
10. The pinned parent version matches — `expected_club_version` against the club's `version`, `expected_member_version` against the member's `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_CLUB_VERSION` or `ERR_STALE_MEMBER_VERSION`.
11. The addressed resource is in a state from which the requested transition exists — otherwise `ERR_SCREENING_ORDER_INVALID_STATE`, `ERR_TRAINING_ASSIGNMENT_INVALID_STATE`, `ERR_COACH_ASSIGNMENT_INVALID_STATE`, `ERR_CASE_INVALID_STATE`, `ERR_INTERIM_MEASURE_INVALID_STATE`, `ERR_AUDIT_INVALID_STATE`, `ERR_FINDING_INVALID_STATE`, `ERR_ROOM_STAY_INVALID_STATE`, or `ERR_MEMBER_INVALID_STATE`.
12. The governing club's `compliance_posture` permits the operation — otherwise `ERR_CLUB_POSTURE_FROZEN`.
13. The subject member's `screening_status` is not `DISQUALIFIED` — otherwise `ERR_MEMBER_DISQUALIFIED`.
14. The subject member is under no permanent restriction, and under no active restriction when the operation would place them in contact with athletes — otherwise `ERR_CONTACT_NOT_PERMITTED`.
15. No overlapping booked stay in the same room slot has a different `occupant_kind` — otherwise `ERR_MINOR_ADULT_LODGING_PROHIBITED`.
16. The electronic-communication policy is satisfied — otherwise `ERR_ELECTRONIC_COMM_NOT_PERMITTED`.
17. The subject member's `screening_status` is `CLEARED` — otherwise `ERR_SCREENING_NOT_CURRENT`.
18. The subject member's `training_status` is `CURRENT` — otherwise `ERR_TRAINING_NOT_CURRENT`.
19. The requested restriction window clears every active measure of the same kind including its cooling buffer — otherwise `ERR_MEASURE_WINDOW_CONFLICT`.
20. The renewal window is open — otherwise `ERR_RENEWAL_WINDOW_NOT_OPEN`.
21. The case's `allegation_category` falls within the target body's `scope_categories` — otherwise `ERR_BODY_SCOPE_MISMATCH`.
22. The target body's `intake_status` is `ACCEPTING` — otherwise `ERR_BODY_INTAKE_PAUSED`.
23. The target body's `open_case_count` is strictly below its `max_open_cases` — otherwise `ERR_BODY_CASELOAD_EXCEEDED`.
24. The room slot has capacity for one more occupant — otherwise `ERR_ROOM_OCCUPANCY_CONFLICT`.
25. Segregation of duties holds on measure conversion and case closure — otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`.
26. The submitted `decision` value is one the acting role may record — otherwise `ERR_DECISION_ROLE_MISMATCH`.
27. The club's `escrow_balance_cents` covers the computed charge — otherwise `ERR_ESCROW_INSUFFICIENT`.

### 2.9. Club Compliance Posture as a Gate Over Child Operations

Each club carries a derived `compliance_posture` with exactly three values — `OPEN`, `RESTRICTED`, and `FROZEN` — and that value gates what the club may do. The posture is recomputed by the platform, never set by a caller, and it is re-derived inside every operation that touches the club's findings, its sanction standing, or the eligibility of a member holding a placement there. The derivation is evaluated top to bottom and the first matching clause wins:

- The posture is `FROZEN` when the club's `sanction_status` is either `SUSPENDED` or `TERMINATED`, or when its `critical_open_finding_count` reaches or exceeds one. A single open finding of `CRITICAL` severity anywhere in the club's audit history is therefore enough to freeze it.
- Otherwise the posture is `RESTRICTED` when the club's `open_finding_count` reaches or exceeds three, or when its `restricted_member_count` reaches or is above one.
- Otherwise the posture is `OPEN`.

`open_finding_count` on the club counts every finding in status `OPEN` across every audit of that club, regardless of what status those audits themselves are in; `critical_open_finding_count` is the same count narrowed to findings whose `severity` is `CRITICAL`. `restricted_member_count` counts the distinct members who hold a coach placement at this club in status `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` and whose `contact_eligibility` is `RESTRICTED` or `PROHIBITED`. Placements still in status `PENDING` are deliberately excluded from that count, because nobody in a pending placement is standing in front of athletes yet; the consequence is that a member still working through screening or training does not by itself restrict their club, and the attempt to activate that member surfaces the screening-currency or training-currency refusal rather than the club-posture refusal. `active_assignment_count` on the club counts the placements there in status `ACTIVE`, and the identically named counter on the member counts that member's own `ACTIVE` placements.

A `FROZEN` posture blocks six operations at the club: registering a new member, ordering a screen, creating a coach placement, activating a coach placement, booking a travel room stay, and renewing a membership. A `RESTRICTED` posture blocks a strict subset — coach-placement activation and membership renewal — while still allowing screening orders, training work, and room-stay bookings to proceed. In every blocked case the refusal is `ERR_CLUB_POSTURE_FROZEN` (HTTP 409). A club also carries `sanction_status` with the values `NONE`, `PROBATION`, `SUSPENDED`, and `TERMINATED`, and `compliance_score` holding the compliance score of the club's most recently finalized audit to two decimal places, null until the first audit is finalized.

### 2.10. Cascade Semantics and Hierarchical Consistency

Several operations in this domain mutate more than one row. When they do, every row whose own stored fields the operation rewrites has its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` increased by one, exactly as if the row had been written directly, and every derived counter that depends on the changed rows is recomputed inside the same request — this platform has no deferred or eventually consistent recomputation. A caller who reads a club immediately after disqualifying one of its coaches sees the club's counters and posture already reduced. Recomputation is not itself a mutation, though: a row that changes only because a derived value was re-derived over it keeps both its `version` and its `updated_at`. Writing an audit finding therefore advances the finding's `version` while the audit whose four finding counters moved and the club whose finding counters and `compliance_posture` moved both keep theirs, and a member row whose `training_status`, `contact_eligibility`, `active_assignment_count`, or `records_retained_through` is re-derived in passing is likewise unchanged in `version` and `updated_at`. The club row extends the same treatment to two fields written to it directly by the audit workflow: an audit finalization writes the club's `compliance_score` and an audit closure writes its `sanction_status`, and neither advances the club's `version`.

Two cascades in this domain run four hops deep and cross four entity types each. The first begins when a restriction is imposed on an individual under investigation: hop one creates the measure, hop two rewrites the member's eligibility and possibly the membership dimension, hop three moves that member's coach placements, and hop four re-derives the counters and posture of every club that lost a placement. The second begins when a routed case closes as substantiated: hop one closes the case and releases the investigating body's caseload, hop two makes every active measure on the reported member permanent, hop three revokes the member and prohibits contact, and hop four terminates the member's placements and re-derives each affected club. §3.7 and §3.8 give both cascades hop by hop.

Compensating actions exist for the reversible cascades and are required to leave no drift behind. Voiding a screening order returns exactly the cents it debited. Lifting an interim measure restores exactly the placements its imposition suspended and reverses the counters its imposition moved. Rejecting a submitted remediation moves the finding back to the status it came from and restores the counters. In each case the state after the compensating action is indistinguishable from the state before the forward action, except for the audit trail and the accumulated `version` numbers.

### 2.11. Nested Resource Retrieval and Reference Resolution

Several paths nest one resource under another — a member's screening orders under the member, a case's interim measures under the case, an audit's findings under the audit, a club's room stays and audits under the club. Path parameters are always resolved **left to right**, and the first segment that fails to resolve determines the reported error. A request naming a nonexistent club and, beneath it, a nonexistent room stay reports the club's 404, never the room stay's. When a nested path exists purely to establish parentage, the parent id is taken from the path and never from the body: a screening order's `member_id`, an interim measure's `case_id`, an audit finding's `audit_id`, and a room stay's `club_id` are all filled in by the server from the URL, and a body that also carries them is rejected as a shape violation.

Flat collections with foreign-key filters exist alongside those nested paths so that every relationship declared in this system is reverse-reachable: screening orders can be listed by `member_id`, training assignments by `member_id` and `course_code`, coach placements by `member_id` and by `club_id`, cases by `club_id`, `reported_member_id`, and `investigating_body_id`, interim measures by `case_id` and `member_id`, audits by `club_id` and by `score_variant`, findings by `audit_id`, `member_id`, and `severity`, and room stays by `club_id`, `member_id`, `trip_label`, and `room_number`. Every one of these list endpoints additionally accepts a `status` filter matching that resource's own state field, and the member lists accept `screening_status`, `membership_status`, `contact_eligibility`, and `role_type`. Every filter is an exact-equality match and a filter supplied as an empty string is treated as absent. Where the filtered field is enum-valued — every `status` filter, plus `severity`, `score_variant`, `screening_status`, `membership_status`, `contact_eligibility`, and `role_type` — a value outside that enum is refused with `ERR_VALIDATION`; filters naming an id or a free-text value are not validated and simply match no row when nothing carries the supplied value. None of these flat collections is narrowed by the caller's own club: where a `ClubAdministrator` is among the authorized roles — the coach-placement list, the interim-measure list, and the audit-finding list — the response spans every club in the governing body, and it is the caller's own `club_id`, `member_id`, or `audit_id` filter, not the platform, that restricts it. Club scope bites on a single-resource read rather than on any list, where the addressed row's owning club is compared to the acting `X-User-ID`, and on a write that carries a `club_id` or a `member_id` in its body, where the comparison runs at step 8 of §2.8.

### 2.12. Conserved Quantities: Screening Escrow and the Finding Partition

Two invariants hold after every single request, and any implementation that can violate either one is incorrect.

The **escrow conservation identity** governs money. For every club, the club's current `escrow_balance_cents` plus the sum of `charged_cents` over every screening order belonging to a member of that club whose status is `ORDERED`, `RESULT_RETURNED`, or `ADJUDICATED` equals that club's `total_funded_cents`. Only two actions move the quantity: ordering a screen debits the balance, and voiding an order credits precisely the same number of cents back. A voided order keeps its `charged_cents` on the row for the audit trail but drops out of the sum because status `VOIDED` is excluded, so the identity survives the refund exactly. All arithmetic here is exact integer cents with no rounding, which is why an order-then-void round trip leaves zero drift.

The **finding partition identity** governs counts. For every audit, `finding_total_count` equals the sum of `open_finding_count`, `remediated_finding_count`, and `waived_finding_count`, and also equals the number of findings actually written against that audit, irrespective of their `severity`, their `member_id`, or whether `fine_cents` has been stamped. Writing a finding raises the total and the open count together. Submitting remediation moves one unit from the open count to the remediated count; rejecting that remediation moves the same unit back; waiving moves one unit from the open count to the waived count. The conserved quantity is the cardinality of the finding set, not money.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Chartering a Club, Funding the Screening Escrow and Registering Coaches

A club enters the system when governing-body staff charter it. The charter record carries the organization's legal `name` (1 to 120 characters, required) and the `state_code` where it is chartered, which is exactly two uppercase letters. Two further inputs shape everything that follows. `minor_athlete_program` declares whether the organization runs programs serving minor athletes; it defaults to true, and it gates exactly one rule in the platform — the electronic-communication policy's outright prohibition on private direct messaging in §3.6. The room-slot rules of the travel-and-lodging policy in §3.7 are deliberately *not* gated on it: the adult/minor separation rule and the two-occupant capacity rule are enforced against every club's bookings whatever this flag says. `roster_size_attested` is the roster headcount the organization self-attests at charter time, a non-negative integer that later serves as the denominator of one of the two compliance-score variants — a club that inflates its attestation drives its own future score down. Optionally the charter may seed the club's screening escrow with `initial_escrow_cents`; when omitted the seed is zero. A freshly chartered club has `escrow_balance_cents` and `total_funded_cents` both equal to the seeded amount, `sanction_status` of `NONE`, and `compliance_posture` of `OPEN`, with `compliance_score` null and all four of its finding and placement counters at zero.

Screening is prepaid. Adding money to the escrow is the only action in the platform that raises `total_funded_cents`, a lifetime total that never decreases: a caller supplies `amount_cents`, a positive whole number of cents, and both `escrow_balance_cents` and `total_funded_cents` rise by that amount while the club's `version` advances by one. Governing-body staff and the club's own administrator may both fund the escrow; nobody else may.

Registering a coach or volunteer is where compliance actually begins. The registration payload names the chartering `club_id`, the individual's `full_name` (1 to 120 characters), an `email` that must contain an at-sign and must be unique across the entire governing body when compared case-insensitively, the `role_type` held at the club — one of `HEAD_COACH`, `ASSISTANT_COACH`, `OFFICIAL`, `CHAPERONE`, or `VOLUNTEER` — the boolean `has_minor_athlete_contact` declaring whether the role involves direct contact with minor athletes, a `date_of_birth` as an ISO 8601 calendar date in year-month-day form (registrants are adults, and the date of birth is what later lets the lodging policy classify an occupant as an adult member), and `jurisdiction_codes`, a list of 1 to 6 two-uppercase-letter state codes, free of duplicates, that the background screen must cover. Registration is blocked at a club whose posture is `FROZEN`, and a duplicate email is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_MEMBER_EMAIL`.

A newly registered member starts at `screening_status` of `UNSCREENED` on the screening dimension and `membership_status` of `PENDING` on the membership dimension, with `training_status` of `NEVER` and `contact_eligibility` of `RESTRICTED`, and with `screening_expires_at`, `eligible_through`, `records_retained_through`, and `last_renewed_at` all null and `active_assignment_count` zero. These two status dimensions live on the same member row and advance on entirely different triggers; neither is a function of the other. The screening dimension moves through `UNSCREENED`, `SCREENING_ORDERED`, `FLAGGED`, `CLEARED`, `EXPIRED`, and the terminal `DISQUALIFIED`. The membership dimension moves through `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, `LAPSED`, and the terminal `REVOKED`. Registration also re-derives the chartering club's posture, since the new member counts toward the club's population.

Registration immediately scores the individual's screening risk, and that score is recomputed on exactly two later occasions: when an adjudication records a non-disqualifying record, and when a membership is renewed. The score, `risk_points`, is an integer from 0 to 100 built from four additive components. The role component contributes 40 points for a `HEAD_COACH`, 30 for an `ASSISTANT_COACH`, 25 for a `CHAPERONE`, 20 for an `OFFICIAL`, and 10 for a `VOLUNTEER`. The contact component contributes 25 points when `has_minor_athlete_contact` is true and nothing when it is false. The jurisdiction component contributes 5 points for each jurisdiction beyond the first — that is, five points multiplied by the number of entries in `jurisdiction_codes` less one, never taken below zero — and is capped at 15 points, so a screen covering four or more states contributes the same 15 as one covering six. The history component counts two lifetime exposures with no time window at all: each of the member's own screening orders that reached status `ADJUDICATED` with a `disposition` of `NON_DISQUALIFYING_RECORD` contributes 12 points (orders still `ORDERED`, still `RESULT_RETURNED`, or `VOIDED` contribute nothing), and each audit finding written against this member that currently stands in status `OPEN` contributes 6 points (findings that are `REMEDIATED` or `WAIVED` contribute nothing), across every audit of every club; the sum of those two contributions is then capped at 30 points. Adding the four components and capping the result at 100 yields `risk_points`.

The score selects a screening tier and writes it to `required_tier`: a score that reaches or exceeds 75 demands `TIER_3`, otherwise a score that reaches or exceeds 45 demands `TIER_2`, otherwise `TIER_1`. Both boundaries are inclusive, so a score of exactly 75 is `TIER_3` and a score of exactly 45 is `TIER_2`. Because the history component is lifetime, a member who accumulates prior non-disqualifying records or open findings can be lifted into a higher tier over time, and a tier uplift widens the set of courses the member must hold — which is how a previously eligible member can be pushed back to needing training.

### 3.2. Pricing and Ordering a Background Screen

The club's own administrator, and no other role, orders the background screen. The request body is deliberately small: an optional `expedited` flag defaulting to false, and the mandatory `expected_club_version` described in §2.6. Everything else about the order is snapshotted by the server. Before the order is written, six conditions must hold: the chartering club's posture must not be `FROZEN`; the member's screening dimension must currently be `UNSCREENED`, `CLEARED`, or `EXPIRED`, so an order attempted while the member sits in `SCREENING_ORDERED` or `FLAGGED` is refused with `ERR_MEMBER_INVALID_STATE`; the member must not be `DISQUALIFIED`, which is a terminal state from which no new screen may ever be ordered — a `DISQUALIFIED` member is deliberately carried *past* that state check so the refusal actually reported is `ERR_MEMBER_DISQUALIFIED` at position 13 of §2.8 rather than `ERR_MEMBER_INVALID_STATE` at position 11; no other screening order for that member may currently be in status `ORDERED` or `RESULT_RETURNED`, since a second in-flight screen would double-debit the escrow; the pinned club version must match; and the club's `escrow_balance_cents` must be at least the charge the platform is about to compute, failing which the order is refused with `ERR_ESCROW_INSUFFICIENT`. The escrow check is the very last gate in the entire order of refusal, so a request that is both unfunded and unsafe always reports the safety problem.

Two values are frozen onto the order at creation and never revisited. `tier` is a snapshot of the member's `required_tier` at order time, and `jurisdiction_count` is a snapshot of how many entries the member's `jurisdiction_codes` held at order time. Snapshotting matters: a later tier uplift on the member never retroactively reprices an order already in flight.

The charge is computed in eight pinned steps, and the order in which they are evaluated is normative because two plausible alternative orderings produce different cents. Step one takes the tier base fee: 2,500 cents for `TIER_1`, 4,800 cents for `TIER_2`, and 9,200 cents for `TIER_3`. Step two computes the multi-jurisdiction surcharge as 1,100 cents for each jurisdiction beyond the first — that is, 1,100 multiplied by the order's `jurisdiction_count` less one, never taken below zero. Step three adds the base to the surcharge and records the result as `gross_subtotal_cents`. Step four selects the volume-discount rate **from that pre-discount gross subtotal**: 10 percent when the gross subtotal reaches or exceeds 12,000 cents, otherwise 5 percent when it reaches or exceeds 8,000 cents, otherwise no discount at all. Step five multiplies the gross subtotal by the selected rate and rounds the product half up to a whole cent, recording it as `discount_cents`. Step six subtracts the discount from the gross subtotal to give the net subtotal. Step seven, only when `expedited` is true, takes 35 percent **of that post-discount net subtotal**, rounds half up to a whole cent, and records it as `expedite_cents`; when `expedited` is false, `expedite_cents` is zero. Step eight adds the net subtotal and the expedite fee to give `charged_cents`. Rounding happens exactly twice, at step five and step seven, and `charged_cents` itself is never rounded again. Note in particular that the discount band is chosen before any expedite fee exists, and the expedite percentage is taken after the discount has been deducted — keying the discount on a post-expedite total, or applying the expedite percentage to the gross subtotal, would both yield a different total.

A successful order is written in status `ORDERED` with all four money fields stamped, the club's `escrow_balance_cents` is debited by `charged_cents`, the club's `version` advances by one, and the member's screening dimension moves `UNSCREENED → SCREENING_ORDERED` (or `CLEARED → SCREENING_ORDERED`, or `EXPIRED → SCREENING_ORDERED`, all three governed by the identical preconditions and side effects). The member's `contact_eligibility` is re-derived — a member in `SCREENING_ORDERED` is never `ELIGIBLE` — and the member's `version` advances by one. Finally the posture of every club where this member holds a placement in status `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, or `SUSPENDED` is re-derived, which is how a re-screen at one club can restrict a second club that borrowed the same coach.

Re-screening before expiry is expected practice, and the `CLEARED → SCREENING_ORDERED` transition is what makes it possible. When a currently cleared member is re-screened, `screening_expires_at` is deliberately retained on the member until the new order is adjudicated; the member becomes `RESTRICTED` purely because the screening dimension has left `CLEARED`, not because the old expiry was erased. Together with the `EXPIRED → SCREENING_ORDERED` transition this closes the recurring `CLEARED → SCREENING_ORDERED → CLEARED` re-screening cycle.

### 3.3. Vendor Returns, Adjudication and Disqualification

The screening vendor's only capability is posting back what its search found. The vendor supplies `vendor_disposition`, which is either `NO_RECORD` or `RECORD_FOUND`, against an order that must currently be in status `ORDERED`; a post against an order in any other status is refused with `ERR_SCREENING_ORDER_INVALID_STATE`. The order moves `ORDERED → RESULT_RETURNED`, `returned_at` is stamped with the logical clock, and the member's `version` advances by one. The vendor's return by itself decides nothing about eligibility: `RECORD_FOUND` moves the member's screening dimension `SCREENING_ORDERED → FLAGGED`, which is at best `RESTRICTED` and never `ELIGIBLE`, while `NO_RECORD` leaves the member in `SCREENING_ORDERED` and the member reaches `CLEARED` later without ever visiting `FLAGGED`.

Adjudication belongs exclusively to a `SafeSportOfficer`, who supplies a `disposition` and a written `rationale` of 1 to 4,000 characters; the `rationale` is mandatory on the way in but is not persisted on the order and appears nowhere in the order's response body. The order must be in status `RESULT_RETURNED`. Three dispositions exist. `CLEAR` is permitted only when the vendor returned `NO_RECORD` and the member is still `SCREENING_ORDERED`; asserting `CLEAR` over a `RECORD_FOUND` return is not a legitimate adjudication and is refused as an invalid state for that order. `NON_DISQUALIFYING_RECORD` is the disposition for a record that was found but does not disqualify, and it is the only route out of `FLAGGED` toward eligibility; it is keyed on the member's screening dimension alone — it is accepted from either `SCREENING_ORDERED` or `FLAGGED` and the vendor's own return is not consulted at all, so an officer may legitimately record a non-disqualifying record over a `NO_RECORD` return and the member takes the resulting 12 history points. `DISQUALIFYING_RECORD` is available only over a `RECORD_FOUND` return and only from `FLAGGED`, and it ends the member's participation permanently.

On the two clearing dispositions the order moves `RESULT_RETURNED → ADJUDICATED`, `adjudicated_at` is stamped with the clock, and the order's `expires_at` is set to `adjudicated_at` plus 730 days — the two-year screening validity period. The member's screening dimension moves to `CLEARED` (from `SCREENING_ORDERED` on a `CLEAR`, from either `SCREENING_ORDERED` or `FLAGGED` on a `NON_DISQUALIFYING_RECORD`), and `screening_expires_at` on the member is set from the order's `expires_at`. When the disposition was `NON_DISQUALIFYING_RECORD` the risk score is re-run, so `risk_points` and `required_tier` are uplifted by the newly recorded history point. The member's forward eligibility horizon `eligible_through` is then re-stamped from the audit-snapshot projection of §3.5, `records_retained_through` is re-derived, and `contact_eligibility` is re-derived. If the member was still `PENDING` on the membership dimension and now derives to `ELIGIBLE`, the membership dimension auto-advances `PENDING → ACTIVE` as a bubble-up side effect, which also stamps `eligible_through`. The member's `version` advances by one, and posture is re-derived for the chartering club and for every club where the member holds a placement in status `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, or `SUSPENDED`.

Disqualification is the harshest cascade in the platform and it runs without discretion. On `DISQUALIFYING_RECORD` the order becomes `ADJUDICATED` with `adjudicated_at` stamped and `expires_at` left null, the member's screening dimension moves `FLAGGED → DISQUALIFIED` with `screening_expires_at` cleared to null, and `contact_eligibility` is set to `PROHIBITED`. The membership dimension then moves to `REVOKED` unconditionally — from `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, or `LAPSED` alike. Every coach placement that member holds in status `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, or `SUSPENDED` moves to `TERMINATED` with `suspended_reason` recorded as `SCREENING_DISQUALIFICATION`. For each club that lost an active placement, `active_assignment_count` is decremented, `restricted_member_count` is re-derived, and `compliance_posture` is re-derived. The member's `version` advances by one. `DISQUALIFIED` is terminal on the screening dimension and `REVOKED` is terminal on the membership dimension: no later action returns the individual to service.

Governing-body staff, and only they, may void an order that has gone wrong — a duplicate submission, a wrong jurisdiction list, a vendor engaged in error. The caller supplies a free-text `reason`, which is retained as `void_reason`. A void is available only while the order is in status `ORDERED` or `RESULT_RETURNED` — an order already `ADJUDICATED` or already `VOIDED` cannot be voided again — and only when the member holds no other order that reached `ADJUDICATED` with a `disposition` of `CLEAR` or `NON_DISQUALIFYING_RECORD`, so a void can never silently strip a member of a clearance that a different order legitimately conferred. The order moves to `VOIDED`, the club's `escrow_balance_cents` is credited with exactly the order's `charged_cents` and the club's `version` advances by one, and the member's screening dimension reverts to `UNSCREENED` — from `SCREENING_ORDERED` or from `FLAGGED` — with `contact_eligibility` re-derived and the member's `version` advanced by one. A void never rewrites `screening_expires_at`: voiding the re-screen of a formerly cleared member leaves that member's old expiry instant standing on the row while the screening dimension reads `UNSCREENED`, and because the clock-driven expiry of §2.5 fires only over a `CLEARED` screen, the retained instant has no further effect. The void is the exact compensating counterpart of the order, which is what keeps the escrow conservation identity of §2.12 intact.

A clearance does not last forever. Once the logical clock reaches or passes a cleared member's `screening_expires_at`, the screening dimension moves `CLEARED → EXPIRED` the next time any read or mutation touches that member, and `contact_eligibility` falls to at best `RESTRICTED`. Expiry deliberately does **not** terminate existing placements — the coach already on the field is not thrown off mid-season by a lapsed background check — but it does block the activation of any new placement, and it blocks renewal, both with `ERR_SCREENING_NOT_CURRENT`.

### 3.4. Abuse-Prevention Training and the Derivation of Contact Eligibility

Courses are published centrally by governing-body staff. A course carries a `code` that is its natural key — 3 to 20 characters of uppercase letters, digits, and hyphens, globally unique, with a collision refused as `ERR_DUPLICATE_COURSE_CODE` — a human-readable `title` of 1 to 200 characters, a `validity_days` figure between 1 and 3,650 stating how long a completion of that course stays current, a `delivery_mode` of either `ONLINE` or `IN_PERSON`, a `required_for_tiers` list naming which screening tiers must hold this course before athlete contact (a non-empty duplicate-free subset of `TIER_1`, `TIER_2`, and `TIER_3`), and a `pass_score` between 0 and 100 that defaults to 80. Widening a published course's `required_for_tiers` changes which members are considered covered on the next evaluation, so the catalogue is a live input to eligibility rather than static reference data.

Assigning a course to a member names the `member_id`, the `course_code` — which must equal the `code` of a course that actually exists, else `ERR_TRAINING_COURSE_NOT_FOUND` — and a `due_at` deadline. The two references resolve in a fixed order, `member_id` first and `course_code` second, and both resolve before the body-scope rule of §2.8 step 8 is applied, so a `ClubAdministrator` naming another club's member together with an unpublished course hears `ERR_TRAINING_COURSE_NOT_FOUND` rather than the scope violation. Governing-body staff and the club's administrator may both assign. The assignment is created in status `ASSIGNED` with `assigned_at` stamped from the logical clock, and `started_at`, `completed_at`, `score`, and `expires_at` all null. `due_at` is not constrained to lie in the future: an assignment whose deadline the clock has already passed is still created `ASSIGNED` and is reported as `ASSIGNED` in the creation response, and only the lapse sweep of §2.5 on the next request moves it to `EXPIRED`. An assignment may not be created against a member whose screening dimension is `DISQUALIFIED`; no other member state and no club posture blocks an assignment.

The member themself, or their club administrator acting for them, starts the course; when the caller's role is `Member` the acting `X-User-ID` must equal the assignment's own `member_id`, otherwise the request is refused with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`. Starting requires status `ASSIGNED` and moves the assignment `ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS` with `started_at` stamped. Starting has one further consequence that matters for planning: only a started assignment counts as in flight for the renewal-forward projection of §3.5, whose projected completion instant is the assignment's `due_at`. Starting advances the assignment's own `version` by one and nothing else: the member row is not rewritten, so its `version` and `updated_at` stand exactly where they were.

Completion requires status `IN_PROGRESS` and a `score` between 0 and 100 that reaches or exceeds the referenced course's `pass_score`; a score below the pass mark is not a completion and is refused with `ERR_TRAINING_ASSIGNMENT_INVALID_STATE` rather than `ERR_VALIDATION`, tested after the `IN_PROGRESS` requirement, while a `score` outside 0 through 100 is a request-shape failure refused with `ERR_VALIDATION` ahead of both. The assignment moves `IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED`, stamping `completed_at` from the clock, the submitted `score`, and `expires_at` as `completed_at` plus that course's `validity_days`. Completion then re-derives `training_status`, re-runs the audit-snapshot projection to re-stamp `eligible_through`, re-derives `contact_eligibility` and `records_retained_through`, bubbles the membership dimension `PENDING → ACTIVE` when the member's screening dimension is already `CLEARED` and nothing else blocks, advances the member's `version` by one, and re-derives `restricted_member_count` and `compliance_posture` for the chartering club and for every club where the member holds a placement in status `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, or `SUSPENDED`.

An assignment left untouched decays. Once the logical clock reaches or passes `due_at` while the assignment is still `ASSIGNED`, it moves `ASSIGNED → EXPIRED` on the next read or mutation that touches it, and an `EXPIRED` assignment is excluded from both projection variants entirely. An assignment already `IN_PROGRESS` is never expired by the clock — the deadline bites only on work never begun. Attempting to start an assignment that is not `ASSIGNED`, or to complete one that is already `COMPLETED` or `EXPIRED`, is refused with `ERR_TRAINING_ASSIGNMENT_INVALID_STATE`.

`training_status` is derived, never supplied. The platform first computes the member's training expiry under the audit-snapshot view — the earliest expiry among the courses required for the member's current `required_tier`, counting only `COMPLETED` assignments, exactly as §3.5 defines it. If that expiry is null — either because at least one required course has no qualifying completion at all, or because the required course set is itself empty, no published course naming the member's current `required_tier` — `training_status` is `NEVER`. Otherwise, if the logical clock has reached or passed that expiry, `training_status` is `LAPSED` — the boundary is inclusive, so at exactly the expiry instant the training is already lapsed. Otherwise `training_status` is `CURRENT`.

`contact_eligibility` is derived from three independent sources — the screening dimension, training currency, and any restriction in force — and its three clauses are evaluated top to bottom with the first match winning. The value is `PROHIBITED` when the member's screening dimension is `DISQUALIFIED`, or when any interim measure against the member stands in status `PERMANENT`. Otherwise the value is `RESTRICTED` when any interim measure against the member stands in status `ACTIVE`, or when the screening dimension is `UNSCREENED`, `SCREENING_ORDERED`, `FLAGGED`, or `EXPIRED`, or when `training_status` is `NEVER` or `LAPSED`. Otherwise the value is `ELIGIBLE`. Measures in status `LIFTED` are ignored entirely by this derivation, and assignments in status `ASSIGNED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, or `EXPIRED` are ignored by `training_status`. The derivation runs inside every transition whose effects touch either of those two member fields.

One derived consequence deserves stating on its own, because two different workflows depend on it. The membership dimension advances `PENDING → ACTIVE` by itself, with no caller asking for it, and it does so on exactly two occasions: inside a screening adjudication and inside a training completion. All four of the following must hold at that moment — the member's `membership_status` is still `PENDING`, the `screening_status` is `CLEARED`, the `training_status` is `CURRENT`, and no interim measure against that member stands in status `ACTIVE` or `PERMANENT`. When the bubble-up fires it sets `membership_status` to `ACTIVE` and stamps `eligible_through` from the audit-snapshot projection. When any of the four fails, the member simply stays `PENDING` and the operation that triggered the check still succeeds.

### 3.5. Eligibility Projection, Membership Renewal and Records Retention

Clubs plan seasons months ahead, so the platform exposes a computed read that projects how long a given member will remain eligible. The read accepts one optional query parameter, `variant`, whose two accepted values are `AUDIT_SNAPSHOT` and `RENEWAL_FORWARD`; when omitted the variant is `AUDIT_SNAPSHOT`. The response reports the `variant` actually applied, the resulting `projected_eligible_through`, the two horizons it was built from as `screening_expiry` and `training_expiry`, the member's current `screening_status`, `training_status`, `membership_status`, `contact_eligibility`, `required_tier`, and `risk_points`, the retention horizon `records_retained_through`, and a `blocking_reasons` array listing the conditions that currently stand between this member and athlete contact. That array draws on a fixed six-token vocabulary and emits every applicable token exactly once, always in this order: `SCREENING_DISQUALIFIED` when the screening dimension is `DISQUALIFIED`; `PERMANENT_MEASURE` when any interim measure against the member stands in status `PERMANENT`; `ACTIVE_INTERIM_MEASURE` when any stands in status `ACTIVE`; `SCREENING_NOT_CURRENT` when the screening dimension is neither `CLEARED` nor `DISQUALIFIED` — the token is deliberately suppressed on a disqualification, which `SCREENING_DISQUALIFIED` already reports; `TRAINING_NOT_CURRENT` when `training_status` is anything other than `CURRENT`; and `MEMBERSHIP_NOT_ACTIVE` when `membership_status` is anything other than `ACTIVE`. The array is empty exactly when none of those six conditions holds, and it is a plain report of the derived member row rather than an independent evaluation — nothing in it is gated on the requested `variant`.

The projection is built in three stages. First, the **required course set** is every published course whose `required_for_tiers` contains the member's *current* `required_tier`. Because the tier can be uplifted by history, widening the required set, a tier uplift can turn a previously non-null projection into null. Second, the **screening horizon** is the latest `expires_at` among the member's own screening orders that reached status `ADJUDICATED` with a `disposition` of `CLEAR` or `NON_DISQUALIFYING_RECORD`; when two such orders carry the identical `expires_at`, the one with the earlier `created_at` is the row selected, and when the member has no such order at all the screening horizon is null. Third, the **training horizon** is computed per required course and then combined. For a single course, the platform takes every assignment of that member for that `course_code` whose status is one of the variant's candidate statuses, computes for each a candidate expiry — `completed_at` plus the course's `validity_days` for an assignment already `COMPLETED`, or `due_at` plus the course's `validity_days` for an assignment merely projected to complete — and keeps the latest of those candidate expiries, breaking a tie on identical expiries in favour of the earlier `created_at`. If a required course has no candidate assignment at all, its per-course expiry is null. The training horizon is null if any required course yields null, and it is null in the same way when the required course set is *empty*: a member whose current `required_tier` is named by no published course has a null training horizon rather than an unbounded one, so a platform with no course published at all leaves every member's projection null and every member's `training_status` at `NEVER`. Otherwise the training horizon is the *earliest* of the per-course expiries, because the member stops being covered as soon as the first required course lapses.

`projected_eligible_through` is then null if either the screening horizon or the training horizon is null, and otherwise the earlier of the two. The two variants differ in exactly one respect — which assignment statuses count as candidates. Under `AUDIT_SNAPSHOT` the candidate set is `COMPLETED` alone: an in-flight course contributes nothing, so a member whose only completion for a required course is old projects an earlier horizon, or none at all. Under `RENEWAL_FORWARD` the candidate set is `COMPLETED` together with `IN_PROGRESS`: an in-progress assignment is projected to complete at its `due_at` and therefore contributes `due_at` plus `validity_days`, which can move the answer years later than the audit-snapshot answer over exactly the same rows. Assignments in status `ASSIGNED` are excluded by both variants — only a started course counts as in flight — and `EXPIRED` assignments are excluded by both as well. `AUDIT_SNAPSHOT` is the variant the platform itself always uses: it is the view stamped onto the member's `eligible_through` on every adjudication, every training completion, and every renewal, and it is the view every audit compliance evaluation reads. `RENEWAL_FORWARD` exists purely as a planning read.

Membership is a subscription and it must be renewed. The club's own administrator renews it, supplying a single input, the boolean `attestation_signed`, which must be true. Renewal is available from `membership_status` of `LAPSED`, closing the `ACTIVE → LAPSED → ACTIVE` cycle, and also from `ACTIVE` as an early renewal that leaves the status `ACTIVE` — the self-loop is what makes the renewal window observable at all. A renewal attempted from `PENDING`, `SUSPENDED`, or `REVOKED` has no transition and is refused with `ERR_MEMBER_INVALID_STATE`. Four further gates apply, in the fixed order of §2.8: the chartering club's `compliance_posture` must be `OPEN`, so both a `RESTRICTED` and a `FROZEN` club are barred from renewing anybody; the member's `screening_status` must be `CLEARED`, else `ERR_SCREENING_NOT_CURRENT`; the member's `training_status` must be `CURRENT`, else `ERR_TRAINING_NOT_CURRENT`; and the renewal window must be open. The window opens 90 days before the member's `eligible_through`: the logical clock must have reached or passed `eligible_through` minus 90 days. A renewal attempted while the clock is still strictly before that instant, or one whose `attestation_signed` is false, is refused with `ERR_RENEWAL_WINDOW_NOT_OPEN` — and because the window check sits below the state check in the order of refusal, a still-`ACTIVE` member renewed too early reports the window error rather than an invalid-state error. A member who is `DISQUALIFIED`, or who is under any permanent restriction, is refused earlier still.

A successful renewal sets `membership_status` to `ACTIVE` and `last_renewed_at` to the logical clock, re-runs the risk score so `risk_points` and `required_tier` reflect any history accumulated since registration, re-runs the audit-snapshot projection and re-stamps `eligible_through`, re-derives `records_retained_through` and `contact_eligibility`, and advances the member's `version` by one.

Records retention answers the inquiry obligation. `records_retained_through` is the later of two horizons, each seven years long: the member's `eligible_through` plus seven years, and the latest `closed_at` among the cases naming this member as `reported_member_id` — in any closed status, administrative, substantiated, and unsubstantiated alike — plus seven years. A horizon whose own input is absent is simply dropped from the comparison rather than nulling the whole result, so a member with no `eligible_through` but one closed case still retains records through that closure plus seven years. It is re-derived on every adjudication, every training completion, every renewal, and every case closure, and it is reported alongside the projection so that a single read answers a records inquiry about an individual. Until a member has an `eligible_through` and no case has ever closed against them, the horizon is null.

### 3.6. Team Placement, Activation and Electronic Communication

A coach placement is the record that puts a specific individual on a specific team at a specific club for a specific season, and it is the many-to-many join of this domain: one member may hold placements at several clubs, and the `club_id` on a placement may legitimately differ from the chartering club recorded on the member. The club administrator creating the placement supplies the `member_id`, the `club_id`, the `team_name`, and the season interval as `season_start` and `season_end`, where `season_end` must be strictly after `season_start`. The interval is half-open: the placement covers `season_start` inclusively and ends before `season_end` exclusively. The two body references resolve in a fixed order, `member_id` first and `club_id` second, so a request naming neither correctly reports `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_FOUND`, and the body-scope rule of §2.8 step 8 is applied to the `club_id` only once both have resolved. Creation is blocked at a `FROZEN` club — a `RESTRICTED` club may still create placements — blocked for a `DISQUALIFIED` member, and blocked for a member under any permanent restriction with `ERR_CONTACT_NOT_PERMITTED`; a merely `ACTIVE` restriction does not block creation, because a pending placement puts nobody in front of an athlete. A new placement is written in status `PENDING`, with `activated_at`, `eligibility_snapshot_version`, and `suspended_reason` all null, `electronic_comm_authorized` false, and both `electronic_comm_channel_kind` and `parent_copy_required` null — those two fields carry no initial default and stay null until an authorization is actually recorded — and the club's `restricted_member_count` and `compliance_posture` are re-derived. Nothing forbids two placements of the same member on the same team or over overlapping seasons at the same club; the placement carries no uniqueness rule at all.

Nothing about a `PENDING` placement puts anyone in front of athletes, which is why the derived `restricted_member_count` deliberately ignores pending placements. Activation is the gate. The club administrator activates the placement supplying only `expected_member_version`, and six conditions must hold — evaluated in the fixed order of §2.8 rather than in the order they happen to be listed here, so a stale pin is reported ahead of a placement that is not `PENDING`: the placement must be in status `PENDING`, else `ERR_COACH_ASSIGNMENT_INVALID_STATE`; the pinned member version must match, else `ERR_STALE_MEMBER_VERSION`; the placement's club must be at posture `OPEN`, so both `RESTRICTED` and `FROZEN` block activation; the member must not be `DISQUALIFIED`; the member must be under no permanent and no active restriction, else `ERR_CONTACT_NOT_PERMITTED`; and the member's `screening_status` must be `CLEARED` and `training_status` must be `CURRENT`, reported as `ERR_SCREENING_NOT_CURRENT` and `ERR_TRAINING_NOT_CURRENT` respectively. The restriction gate at position 14 of the order of refusal is measure-based only: a member whose `contact_eligibility` is merely `RESTRICTED` because screening or training currency is missing passes that gate and is refused later, by the screening-currency or training-currency check, which is what makes those two errors observable at all. Activation consults neither the season interval nor the membership dimension: a placement whose `season_end` the clock has already passed activates normally, and the lapse sweep of §2.5 then moves it `ACTIVE → EXPIRED` on the very next request.

Activation moves the placement `PENDING → ACTIVE`, stamps `activated_at` from the clock, and pins `eligibility_snapshot_version` to the member's `version` as it stood when the request arrived — which is to say to the very value the caller pinned in `expected_member_version`, read before this activation's own increment, so the stamped snapshot always reads one less than the member's `version` once the request has completed — a durable record of exactly which eligibility view the club approved. It increments the club's `active_assignment_count` and the member's `active_assignment_count`, advances the member's `version` by one, and re-derives the placement club's `compliance_posture`.

Electronic communication with minor athletes is authorized per placement, not per club, and it is authorized separately from activation. The club administrator supplies a `channel_kind` — one of `TEAM_PLATFORM`, `GROUP_EMAIL`, `GROUP_SMS`, or `PRIVATE_DIRECT_MESSAGE` — and the boolean `parent_copy_required` declaring whether a parent or guardian must be copied on every message. Four conditions must all hold, and any failure is reported as `ERR_ELECTRONIC_COMM_NOT_PERMITTED`: the placement must be in status `ACTIVE`; the member's `contact_eligibility` must be `ELIGIBLE`; whenever the governing club's `minor_athlete_program` is true, `channel_kind` must be anything other than `PRIVATE_DIRECT_MESSAGE`, because one-to-one private messaging with minor athletes is prohibited outright; and whenever the member's `has_minor_athlete_contact` is true, `parent_copy_required` must be true. One status is carved out of the first of those four: a `TERMINATED` placement is refused at position 11 of §2.8 with `ERR_COACH_ASSIGNMENT_INVALID_STATE`, so the `ERR_ELECTRONIC_COMM_NOT_PERMITTED` reading of the not-`ACTIVE` condition covers `PENDING`, `SUSPENDED`, and `EXPIRED` only. A member under any permanent or active restriction is refused between the two, with `ERR_CONTACT_NOT_PERMITTED` — after the `TERMINATED` check and before every remaining electronic-communication condition. On success the placement records `electronic_comm_authorized` as true along with the supplied `electronic_comm_channel_kind` and `parent_copy_required`. The authorization may be re-recorded any number of times while the placement is still `ACTIVE`, each success overwriting both fields, and once recorded it is never withdrawn by the platform: a placement afterwards suspended, expired, or terminated carries its `electronic_comm_authorized`, `electronic_comm_channel_kind`, and `parent_copy_required` forward unchanged.

Placements end in three ways. A season simply runs out: once the logical clock reaches or passes `season_end`, an `ACTIVE` placement moves `ACTIVE → EXPIRED` on the next touch, the club's and the member's `active_assignment_count` are both decremented, and the club's posture is re-derived — `season_end` being exclusive means that at exactly that instant the placement is already `EXPIRED`. A placement is suspended by a restriction and can come back: an `ACTIVE` placement moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` with `suspended_reason` recorded as `INTERIM_MEASURE` when its member is restricted, decrementing both active counters, incrementing the club's `restricted_member_count`, and re-deriving the club's posture; and it returns `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE` with `suspended_reason` cleared to null once the member has no remaining measure in status `ACTIVE` or `PERMANENT` and both `screening_status` is `CLEARED` and `training_status` is `CURRENT`, restoring both counters and re-deriving the club's `restricted_member_count` and posture. Or a placement is terminated for good: `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, and `SUSPENDED` placements all move to `TERMINATED` when their member's membership dimension reaches `REVOKED` or when their club is sanctioned, and when a measure against its member becomes permanent, every placement of that member in `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, or `SUSPENDED` moves to `TERMINATED` with `suspended_reason` recorded as `PERMANENT_MEASURE` — the reason is stamped on all three source statuses, not on `SUSPENDED` alone, exactly as `SCREENING_DISQUALIFICATION` is stamped on all three by a disqualification and `CLUB_SANCTION` on the `PENDING` and `ACTIVE` ones by a club sanction. Termination from `ACTIVE` decrements both `active_assignment_count` counters; termination from `PENDING` does not, because a pending placement was never counted. `EXPIRED` and `TERMINATED` are both terminal, and any trigger against a `TERMINATED` placement is refused with `ERR_COACH_ASSIGNMENT_INVALID_STATE`.

### 3.7. Travel, Lodging and Room-Slot Safety

Travel is where the minor-athlete abuse-prevention policies bite hardest, and the platform models it at the level of a single bed. A booking records one occupant in one room on one trip. The occupant is polymorphic: `occupant_kind` is either `ADULT_MEMBER` or `MINOR_ATHLETE`, and the two kinds carry mutually exclusive identity fields. When `occupant_kind` is `ADULT_MEMBER`, `member_id` must be set and `minor_athlete_age` must be null. When `occupant_kind` is `MINOR_ATHLETE`, `member_id` must be null and `minor_athlete_age` must be set — minor athletes are not registered members of the governing body, so only their age is recorded, and it must be between 5 and 17 inclusive. An inconsistent pairing is a shape violation refused with `ERR_VALIDATION`.

The rest of the booking describes the slot and the stay. `trip_label` (1 to 60 characters) identifies the trip and `room_number` (1 to 12 characters) identifies the room at the lodging property; together with the travelling club, those two values form the **room slot** that all lodging safety rules are evaluated within. `check_in` and `check_out` bound the occupancy interval, with `check_out` strictly after `check_in`, and the interval is half-open — the stay occupies `check_in` inclusively and releases the bed before `check_out`. Adjacency is therefore explicitly legal: a new stay whose `check_in` falls exactly on an existing stay's `check_out` does not overlap it, and two coaches may hand a room over on the same instant.

Bookings are made by the club administrator against a club that is not `FROZEN`, for a member who is not `DISQUALIFIED` and who is under no permanent or active restriction. The club scope of §2.1 is checked against the travelling club named in the path only: the occupant named by `member_id` need do no more than resolve to some member of the governing body — it is deliberately *not* required to be chartered by the travelling club, so a club may lawfully lodge a coach it borrowed from another club, and a `member_id` matching no member at all is refused with `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_FOUND` rather than a scope violation. Two overlap rules then run against the stays already in the same slot, and only stays in status `BOOKED` participate — a `CANCELLED` stay never conflicts with anything. The platform first collects the neighbouring stays: those in the same slot, in status `BOOKED`, whose interval intersects the requested one, where intersection means the requested `check_in` falls strictly before the neighbour's `check_out` and the neighbour's `check_in` falls strictly before the requested `check_out`. **The safety rule is evaluated first.** If any neighbour has a different `occupant_kind` from the requested booking, the request is refused with `ERR_MINOR_ADULT_LODGING_PROHIBITED`: an adult member and a minor athlete may never share a room slot across overlapping time, in either direction. **The capacity rule is evaluated second.** If the number of neighbours plus the requested booking would exceed two occupants, the request is refused with `ERR_ROOM_OCCUPANCY_CONFLICT`. Because safety strictly precedes capacity, a request that trips both rules always reports the lodging-safety refusal. A successful booking is written in status `BOOKED` with `cancel_reason` null.

Cancellation releases the bed. The club administrator supplies a `reason` of 1 to 1,000 characters, which is retained as `cancel_reason`, and the stay moves `BOOKED → CANCELLED`; from that moment its interval no longer participates in either overlap rule, so the freed slot can be rebooked immediately. A stay that is already `CANCELLED` cannot be cancelled again, and the second attempt is refused with `ERR_ROOM_STAY_INVALID_STATE`.

### 3.8. Incident Reporting, Case Routing, Interim Measures and Closure

Investigating bodies are registered centrally before any case can be routed to them. A body carries its `name` of 1 to 160 characters, a `scope_categories` list naming the allegation categories it accepts — a non-empty duplicate-free subset of `SEXUAL_MISCONDUCT`, `PHYSICAL_ABUSE`, `EMOTIONAL_MISCONDUCT`, `POLICY_VIOLATION`, and `FAILURE_TO_REPORT` — an `intake_status` of either `ACCEPTING` or `PAUSED` that defaults to `ACCEPTING`, and a `max_open_cases` ceiling of at least one. Its `open_case_count` starts at zero and is maintained by the platform as the number of cases currently sitting in status `ROUTED` against that body.

Filing a report is deliberately open to three roles — a `SafeSportOfficer`, a `ClubAdministrator`, or a `Member` — because a mandatory-reporting regime that only officers could use would fail its purpose. The report names the `club_id` where the conduct is alleged to have occurred, the `reported_member_id` of the coach or volunteer named, the `allegation_category` drawn from the same five-value set as a body's scope, the `reporter_kind` identifying who filed (`MANDATORY_REPORTER`, `PARENT`, `ATHLETE`, or `ANONYMOUS`), a `narrative` of 20 to 4,000 characters, and `occurred_at`, the instant the alleged conduct took place. The `club_id` resolves before the `reported_member_id`, and the body-scope rule of §2.8 step 8 is applied to the `club_id` only once both have resolved. Filing is gated on nothing else whatsoever: neither the club's `compliance_posture` nor the reported member's screening, membership, or restriction state stands in the way, so a report may be filed against a `DISQUALIFIED` or already-`REVOKED` member at a `FROZEN` club — and the same latitude governs imposing an interim measure, which consults no state of the member beyond an already-`PERMANENT` restriction. The case is created in status `INTAKE_REVIEW` with `intake_received_at` stamped from the logical clock, `investigating_body_id` null, and `reporter_user_id` set from the acting `X-User-ID` so that the reporting obligation leaves an audit trail. The platform then classifies `report_timeliness`: the value is `TIMELY` when the elapsed time from `occurred_at` to `intake_received_at` is at most 24 hours, and `LATE` otherwise — the boundary is inclusive, so a report received exactly 24 hours after the conduct is still `TIMELY`. `occurred_at` is not constrained to fall at or before the intake instant either, so a report whose alleged conduct is dated in the future classifies as `TIMELY`. A `LATE` report filed by a `MANDATORY_REPORTER` is precisely the exposure that an auditor later writes up as a `FAILURE_TO_REPORT` finding against the club.

From `INTAKE_REVIEW` a case forks two ways, and the two branches have deliberately disjoint side effects. **Branch one is routing.** An officer routes the case by naming an `investigating_body_id`, and three checks run in order: the case's `allegation_category` must appear in that body's `scope_categories`, else `ERR_BODY_SCOPE_MISMATCH`; the body's `intake_status` must be `ACCEPTING`, else `ERR_BODY_INTAKE_PAUSED`; and the body's `open_case_count` must be strictly below its `max_open_cases`, else `ERR_BODY_CASELOAD_EXCEEDED`. Routing moves the case `INTAKE_REVIEW → ROUTED`, stamps `investigating_body_id` and `routed_at` from the clock, and increments both the body's `open_case_count` and its `version` by one. Routing a case that is not in `INTAKE_REVIEW` is refused with `ERR_CASE_INVALID_STATE`. **Branch two is administrative closure.** An officer closes an unrouted case with a `closure_reason` and no `outcome` at all; the case moves `INTAKE_REVIEW → CLOSED_ADMINISTRATIVE` with `closed_at` and the supplied `closure_reason` stamped. `NO_JURISDICTION` and `DUPLICATE_REPORT` are the reasons this branch exists for, but the `closure_reason` enum is not partitioned by branch: all three of its values are accepted on either branch, and the presence or absence of `outcome` is the only thing the case's status actually keys on. Administrative closure touches nothing else: no investigating body's `open_case_count` moves, because the case was never routed, and any interim measure imposed during intake survives the closure untouched. The reported member is not rewritten either — its `records_retained_through` does pick the new closure up, because that horizon is re-derived over every member on every request, but the member's own `version` and `updated_at` stand still, unlike on either closure from `ROUTED`.

Interim measures are the platform's answer to the individual under investigation. An officer imposes one against an open case, supplying the `member_id` — which must equal the case's `reported_member_id`, else `ERR_CLUB_SCOPE_VIOLATION` — the `measure_kind`, which is one of `NO_CONTACT_DIRECTIVE`, `TEMPORARY_SUSPENSION`, or `SUPERVISED_CONTACT_ONLY`, the inclusive `effective_from` instant, an optional `effective_to` inclusive end (omitting it means the restriction is open-ended, and when present it must be at or after `effective_from`), and the `expected_member_version` of §2.6. Measures may be imposed while the case is in `INTAKE_REVIEW` or `ROUTED`; an attempt against a case in any closed status is refused with `ERR_CASE_INVALID_STATE`.

Before a new measure is written, the platform checks it against the member's existing restrictions of **the same kind**, and this window arithmetic is deliberately stricter than the lodging arithmetic. Only measures for the same member with the same `measure_kind` in status `ACTIVE` participate; `LIFTED` measures are ignored, and a `PERMANENT` measure has already blocked the request earlier at the restriction gate. Each participating measure occupies its interval from `effective_from` through `effective_to` **plus a trailing 14-day cooling buffer**, and a null `effective_to` extends that occupation indefinitely into the future. The requested window likewise extends indefinitely when its own `effective_to` is null. A conflict exists when the requested `effective_from` falls at or before the existing measure's buffered end **and** the existing measure's `effective_from` falls at or before the requested end. These endpoints are closed, unlike the room-slot rule: a requested `effective_from` landing exactly on an existing measure's buffered end **is** a conflict. Any conflict is refused with `ERR_MEASURE_WINDOW_CONFLICT`. Note that a second measure of a *different* kind against an otherwise unchanged member is legitimate and succeeds, which is exactly why version pinning is the only other guard on this operation.

Imposing a measure begins the four-hop restriction cascade. Hop one creates the measure in status `ACTIVE` with `permanence` of `TEMPORARY` and `imposed_by_user_id` set from the acting `X-User-ID`, leaving `converted_by_user_id`, `lifted_at`, `lift_reason`, and `rationale` null. Hop two rewrites the member: `contact_eligibility` becomes `RESTRICTED`, and when the `measure_kind` is `TEMPORARY_SUSPENSION` the membership dimension moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED`. Hop three moves every coach placement that member holds in status `ACTIVE` to `SUSPENDED` with `suspended_reason` recorded as `INTERIM_MEASURE`. Hop four re-derives every club that lost a placement: `active_assignment_count` is decremented, `restricted_member_count` is incremented, and `compliance_posture` is re-derived — which is how one officer's action at one club can restrict a second club that shares the coach. The member's `version` advances by one.

Lifting a measure is the exact compensating counterpart. An officer supplies a `reason` of 1 to 1,000 characters, retained as `lift_reason`, against a measure in status `ACTIVE` whose case is still in `INTAKE_REVIEW` or `ROUTED`; a measure that is not `ACTIVE` is refused with `ERR_INTERIM_MEASURE_INVALID_STATE`, and when the measure is ineligible and its case is closed at the same time that refusal is the one reported, ahead of `ERR_CASE_INVALID_STATE`. The measure moves `ACTIVE → LIFTED` with `lifted_at` stamped from the clock. Everything that follows is all-or-nothing on a single clearance test taken over the member as a whole: no measure in status `ACTIVE` or `PERMANENT` remains against them, `screening_status` is `CLEARED`, and `training_status` is `CURRENT`. When the test passes, the membership dimension returns `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`, `contact_eligibility` becomes `ELIGIBLE`, every placement of that member in status `SUSPENDED` whose `suspended_reason` is `INTERIM_MEASURE` returns to `ACTIVE` with `suspended_reason` cleared, both `active_assignment_count` counters are restored, and every affected club's `restricted_member_count` and `compliance_posture` are re-derived. When the test fails — a second measure still stands, or the member's screening or training has lapsed in the meantime — nothing is restored at all: the placements stay `SUSPENDED`, the membership dimension stays where it is, and only `contact_eligibility` and the other derived member values are recomputed. The membership restoration reaches exactly one source status: a membership sitting in `LAPSED` is left `LAPSED`, because only `SUSPENDED` is restored. Neither this path nor the unsubstantiated closure re-stamps `eligible_through`, so the horizon standing on the member from its last adjudication, completion, or renewal continues to govern. The member's `version` advances by one, and by design the round trip leaves no drift in any club's `active_assignment_count`.

Converting a measure to permanent is the segregation-of-duties step. The officer supplies a written `rationale`; the measure must be in status `ACTIVE`, the case must be in status `ROUTED`, and the acting `X-User-ID` must differ from the measure's `imposed_by_user_id` — the officer who imposed a restriction may not be the officer who makes it permanent, and an attempt by the same principal is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. On success the measure moves `ACTIVE → PERMANENT` with `permanence` set to `PERMANENT`, `converted_by_user_id` set from the acting `X-User-ID`, and the supplied `rationale` retained on the measure row and reported in its response. The member's `contact_eligibility` becomes `PROHIBITED` and the membership dimension moves to `REVOKED`, from `ACTIVE`, from `SUSPENDED`, or from `LAPSED` alike. Every placement of that member in status `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, or `SUSPENDED` moves to `TERMINATED`, and every affected club has `active_assignment_count`, `restricted_member_count`, and `compliance_posture` re-derived. The member's `version` advances by one. Both `LIFTED` and `PERMANENT` are terminal for a measure.

Closing a routed case is the second four-hop cascade, and its two branches are mirror images of one another. The officer supplies a `closure_reason` — `INVESTIGATION_COMPLETE` is the reason this branch exists for, though as on the administrative branch any of the three values is accepted — together with an `outcome`; the `outcome` is required when closing from `ROUTED` and forbidden when closing from `INTAKE_REVIEW`. That pairing is keyed on the case's own status rather than on the body alone, so it is a state-keyed transition rule evaluated at position 11 of §2.8 and not a request-shape rule: a violation in either direction — an `outcome` supplied on a closure from `INTAKE_REVIEW`, or an `outcome` omitted on a closure from `ROUTED` — is refused with `ERR_CASE_INVALID_STATE`, not with `ERR_VALIDATION`. In both branches the acting `X-User-ID` must differ from the case's `reporter_user_id`, else `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, and closing a case already in any closed status is refused with `ERR_CASE_INVALID_STATE`, which is reported ahead of the pairing rule.

On an `outcome` of `SUBSTANTIATED` the case moves `ROUTED → CLOSED_SUBSTANTIATED`: hop one stamps `closed_at` from the clock and decrements the investigating body's `open_case_count` by one; hop two flips every measure against the reported member that stands in status `ACTIVE` to status `PERMANENT` with `permanence` of `PERMANENT`, leaving `converted_by_user_id` and `rationale` null — this closure is a consequence of the outcome rather than an act of conversion by a named officer, so unlike the explicit convert-permanent step it stamps neither field, and the segregation rule of that step does not carry over either: the officer closing the case may well be the principal who imposed the measures, provided only that they did not file the report; hop three, because a permanent measure now exists, sets that member's `contact_eligibility` to `PROHIBITED` and moves the membership dimension to `REVOKED`; hop four terminates every placement of that member in status `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, or `SUSPENDED` and re-derives `active_assignment_count`, `restricted_member_count`, and `compliance_posture` for each affected club. The member's `records_retained_through` is re-derived from the closure instant and the member's `version` advances by one.

On an `outcome` of `UNSUBSTANTIATED` the case moves `ROUTED → CLOSED_UNSUBSTANTIATED` and every effect is the inverse: `closed_at` is stamped and the body's `open_case_count` is decremented by one exactly as on the substantiated branch, but every measure against the reported member in status `ACTIVE` moves to `LIFTED` with `lifted_at` stamped from the clock and `lift_reason` recorded as `CASE_UNSUBSTANTIATED`. The member's `contact_eligibility` is re-derived, and the same all-or-nothing restoration as the lift then runs over the same clearance test: only when no measure in status `ACTIVE` or `PERMANENT` remains and both `screening_status` is `CLEARED` and `training_status` is `CURRENT` does the membership dimension return `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE` — and only from `SUSPENDED`, never from `LAPSED` — and only then does every placement of that member in status `SUSPENDED` whose `suspended_reason` is `INTERIM_MEASURE` return to `ACTIVE`, restoring each club's `active_assignment_count`; when the test fails those placements stay `SUSPENDED`. `restricted_member_count` and `compliance_posture` are re-derived for every affected club either way, `records_retained_through` is re-derived from the closure instant exactly as on the substantiated branch, `eligible_through` is not re-stamped, and the member's `version` advances by one. `CLOSED_SUBSTANTIATED`, `CLOSED_UNSUBSTANTIATED`, and `CLOSED_ADMINISTRATIVE` are all terminal.

### 3.9. Club Compliance Audits, Findings and Sanctions

Auditing the clubs is the governing body's own accountability loop, and it belongs entirely to the `ComplianceAuditor` role. Opening an audit names the `club_id` under examination, the audited period as `audit_period_start` and `audit_period_end` (the end strictly after the start, with the start inclusive and the end exclusive), the `score_variant` that will be used at finalize time, and `sampled_member_ids`, the explicit list of members drawn into the sample. The sample list holds between zero and fifty entries with no duplicates, and every entry must be a member of the audited club — a foreign member in the sample is refused with `ERR_CLUB_SCOPE_VIOLATION`, and an id that matches no member at all with `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_FOUND`. An empty sample is explicitly legal. The audit is created in status `SAMPLED`, with `sampled_member_ids` stored and echoed in exactly the order the caller supplied — the list is never sorted or otherwise canonicalised, so the response reproduces the request's sequence entry for entry — `sampled_member_count` set to the number of entries supplied, `compliant_member_count`, `denominator_count`, `compliance_score`, and `sanction_fine_cents` all null, all four finding counters at zero, and `finalized_at`, `closed_at`, and `resolution` null.

While the audit is still `SAMPLED` the auditor writes findings against it. A finding carries a `finding_code` drawn from the catalogue of compliance defects — `SCREENING_LAPSED`, `TRAINING_LAPSED`, `UNSCREENED_CONTACT`, `LODGING_POLICY_BREACH`, `ELECTRONIC_COMM_BREACH`, `FAILURE_TO_REPORT`, or `RECORDS_INCOMPLETE` — a `severity` of `MINOR`, `MAJOR`, or `CRITICAL`, an optional `member_id` naming the individual the finding is written against (omitted for an organization-level finding, and when supplied it must be one of that audit's sampled members, else `ERR_CLUB_SCOPE_VIOLATION`), and an optional `is_repeat` flag defaulting to false that records whether the same `finding_code` was already sanctioned in a prior audit of this club. The finding is created in status `OPEN` with `fine_cents`, `evidence_note`, and `decision_note` null; the audit's `finding_total_count` and `open_finding_count` both rise by one, preserving the finding partition identity; and the club's `open_finding_count`, `critical_open_finding_count`, and `compliance_posture` are re-derived, which is how a single `CRITICAL` finding freezes a club the moment it is written. Writing a finding against an audit that is not `SAMPLED` is refused with `ERR_AUDIT_INVALID_STATE`.

Finalizing the audit computes the score. Finalize is available only from `SAMPLED` and moves the audit `SAMPLED → FINDINGS_ISSUED` with `finalized_at` stamped from the clock. The numerator, `compliant_member_count`, is the number of the audit's own sampled members who satisfy all four conditions at the moment of finalization — not at sampling time — namely a `screening_status` of `CLEARED`, a `training_status` of `CURRENT`, a `membership_status` of `ACTIVE`, and no interim measure against them in status `ACTIVE` or `PERMANENT`. The denominator, `denominator_count`, is chosen by the audit's `score_variant`. Under `SELF_ATTESTED` it is the club's `roster_size_attested`, the figure the club itself supplied at charter time, so an inflated attestation drives the score down for exactly the same sample. Under `REGISTRY_VERIFIED` it is the live number of coach placements at that club in status `PENDING` or `ACTIVE`; placements that are `SUSPENDED`, `EXPIRED`, or `TERMINATED` are excluded, which is what makes a sanctioned club's registry denominator shrink. For identical sampled members and identical compliance the two variants yield different scores whenever the attested roster differs from the registry count.

`compliance_score` itself is then resolved in three ordered clauses. When the audit's `sampled_member_count` is zero the score is exactly 100.00 — an audit that sampled nobody found nothing wrong with anybody. Otherwise, when `denominator_count` is zero the score is exactly 0.00. Otherwise the score is one hundred multiplied by the numerator and divided by the denominator, rounded half up to two decimal places, and capped at 100.00. Finalize copies the resulting score onto the club's own `compliance_score` field and re-derives the club's `open_finding_count`, `critical_open_finding_count`, and `compliance_posture`.

Between finalization and closure the two parties argue about the findings, and each may only take their own half of the decision. A decision carries a `decision` value and a `note` of 1 to 4,000 characters, and the audit must be in status `FINDINGS_ISSUED` throughout; when the finding's own status and the audit's status are both wrong the finding's is the one reported, so `ERR_FINDING_INVALID_STATE` precedes `ERR_AUDIT_INVALID_STATE`. A `ClubAdministrator` submits `REMEDIATION_SUBMITTED` against a finding in status `OPEN`: the finding moves `OPEN → REMEDIATED`, the supplied `note` is retained as `evidence_note`, the audit's `open_finding_count` falls by one while `remediated_finding_count` rises by one, and the club's finding counters and posture are re-derived. A `ComplianceAuditor` submits `REMEDIATION_REJECTED` against a finding in status `REMEDIATED`: the finding moves `REMEDIATED → OPEN`, the `note` is retained as `decision_note`, the `evidence_note` the club had submitted is deliberately left standing rather than cleared so the rejected remediation survives in the audit trail, `open_finding_count` rises by one while `remediated_finding_count` falls by one, and the club's counters and posture are re-derived — the compensating action that closes the `OPEN → REMEDIATED → OPEN` cycle, which a club and an auditor may traverse any number of times. A `ComplianceAuditor` may instead submit `WAIVED` against a finding in status `OPEN`: the finding moves `OPEN → WAIVED`, the `note` is retained as `decision_note`, `open_finding_count` falls by one while `waived_finding_count` rises by one, the finding is thereafter excluded from any fine, and the club's counters and posture are re-derived. `WAIVED` is terminal. A role submitting a decision reserved to the other party is refused with `ERR_DECISION_ROLE_MISMATCH`, and a decision against a finding in the wrong status — remediation or waiver on a finding that is not `OPEN`, rejection on one that is not `REMEDIATED`, or any decision on a `WAIVED` finding — is refused with `ERR_FINDING_INVALID_STATE`.

Closure is the auditor's verdict and it is available only from `FINDINGS_ISSUED`; an attempt from any other status is refused with `ERR_AUDIT_INVALID_STATE`. The auditor supplies a `resolution` of either `CLOSE_COMPLIANT` or `SANCTION`. A clean closure requires both that the audit's `compliance_score` reaches or exceeds the passing score of 90.00 and that its `open_finding_count` is exactly zero; the audit moves `FINDINGS_ISSUED → CLOSED_COMPLIANT` with `closed_at` stamped, `sanction_fine_cents` set to zero, the club's `sanction_status` reset to `NONE`, and the club's `compliance_posture` re-derived. A sanction closure is the branch available when the `compliance_score` is strictly below 90.00 or the `open_finding_count` is at least one; a `SANCTION` demanded over an audit that is both passing and free of open findings has no branch to take and is refused with `ERR_AUDIT_INVALID_STATE`, the same code a premature clean closure draws.

The sanction prices the fine per finding, and the rounding order is normative. First the platform computes the shortfall against the passing score: the passing score of 90.00 minus the audit's `compliance_score`, floored at 0.00, and then the shortfall factor as one plus 0.012 multiplied by those shortfall points — so an audit twenty points short carries a factor of 1.24. Then, for each finding on this audit still in status `OPEN` at close time, the raw amount is a base of 25,000 cents multiplied by the severity multiplier for that finding's `severity` (0.20 for `MINOR`, 0.55 for `MAJOR`, and 1.00 for `CRITICAL`), multiplied again by 1.375 when the finding's `is_repeat` is true and by 1.000 when it is false, and multiplied finally by the shortfall factor. **Each finding's raw amount is rounded half up to a whole cent and stored as that finding's own `fine_cents` before any summation happens.** The audit's `sanction_fine_cents` is then the exact integer sum of those already-rounded per-finding amounts, accumulated in ascending `created_at` order of the findings. Summing the raw amounts and rounding once at the end is a different number whenever several findings each carry a fractional part below half a cent that together exceed half a cent, and it is not the specified behaviour. Findings in status `REMEDIATED` or `WAIVED` take no part and keep `fine_cents` null.

The sanction branch then moves the audit `FINDINGS_ISSUED → CLOSED_SANCTIONED` with `closed_at` stamped, sets the club's `sanction_status` to `SUSPENDED`, and consequently forces the club's `compliance_posture` to `FROZEN` — a suspended club is frozen by the posture derivation of §2.9, not by a separate flag. Every coach placement at that club in status `PENDING` or `ACTIVE` moves to `TERMINATED` with `suspended_reason` recorded as `CLUB_SANCTION`, and the club's `active_assignment_count` and `restricted_member_count` are re-derived. From that point the club can neither register a member, order a screen, create or activate a placement, book a room stay, nor renew anybody: the whole child surface is closed until the sanction is resolved. `CLOSED_COMPLIANT` and `CLOSED_SANCTIONED` are both terminal.
