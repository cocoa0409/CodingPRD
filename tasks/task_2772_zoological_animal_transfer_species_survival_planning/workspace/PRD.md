# Zoological Transfer & Species Survival Planning API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Accredited zoological institutions do not own their most valuable animals in any ordinary sense. A snow leopard held in one collection is simultaneously a line in a coordinating body's studbook, a founder-representation figure in a genetic analysis, a slot in someone's quarantine building, and a term in a negotiated contract that may reserve its offspring for an institution three thousand kilometres away. Today those four views live in four places: a spreadsheet kept by the studbook keeper, an email thread between two registrars, a veterinarian's paper certificate file, and the memory of whoever last walked the quarantine corridor. When a transfer falls through — a positive tuberculosis result, a lapsed permit, a temperature excursion in the cargo hold, an aggressive introduction to the resident group — nobody can say with confidence which of the other three views must now be rolled back.

This document specifies the API that replaces those four views with one. It carries a population management plan from the coordinating body's genetic and demographic analysis, through pairing and holding recommendations, into a multi-round transfer negotiation covering ownership, breeding rights and offspring disposition, then through pre-shipment testing, permit issuance, crate conditioning, temperature-controlled shipping, receiving quarantine and behavioural introduction, and finally into studbook submissions filed back to the international species database. Every step is a state transition with named preconditions, every rejection has a stable machine-readable identifier, and every financial or demographic figure is computed by a rule stated here rather than negotiated per institution.

Two properties shape the design more than any other. First, **compensation is a first-class requirement**: an agreement that is terminated after execution must leave the transfer-credit ledger, the animal's custody, the recommendation's lifecycle and the programme's plan cycle exactly as it found them, which is why so much of §3 is written as cascades and their reversals. Second, **the coordinating body and the institutions are adversarial parties in a cooperative process**: the party that put terms on the table may not accept them, the veterinarian who drew the blood may not sign the certificate, and the veterinarian who signed the certificate may not clear the quarantine. Those separations are enforced by the server, not by convention.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Acting Identities and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to every endpoint must carry two headers: `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role`. If either header is absent or present but empty, the request is refused with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401) before anything else about the request is examined — including whether the target resource exists.

There are exactly four roles, and `X-User-Role` must spell one of them exactly: `AccreditationAdmin`, `ProgramCoordinator`, `Registrar`, `Veterinarian`. A value outside that set of four, or a value inside the set that is not listed among the authorized roles for the endpoint being called, is refused with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403). The authorized roles for each endpoint are given in the §4 table; every read endpoint in the system admits all four roles.

The meaning of `X-User-ID` depends on the role, and this binding is what ties an acting identity to the resources it may touch:

- When `X-User-Role` is `AccreditationAdmin`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier for staff of the accrediting association. It is not tied to any persisted entity, the server never compares it to any entity's `id`, and it may be recorded for audit but is never used for an ownership check. An `AccreditationAdmin` maintains the register of institutions and species programmes, decides every permit application, and drives the logical clock.
- When `X-User-Role` is `ProgramCoordinator`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `SpeciesProgram` — the coordinating body for that one programme. Ownership checks on any resource that references a `SpeciesProgram` compare the resource's programme foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`: a coordinator may publish only its own plan, may raise recommendations only under its own `species_program_id`, and may review only submissions filed into its own studbook.
- When `X-User-Role` is `Registrar`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Institution`. Ownership checks on any resource that references an `Institution` compare the resource's institution foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`: a registrar accessions animals only into its own collection, files studbook submissions only under its own `institution_id`, and acts on a transfer agreement only when its `X-User-ID` is one of that agreement's two parties.
- When `X-User-Role` is `Veterinarian`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Veterinarian`. Ownership checks compare veterinarian foreign-key fields on a resource to `X-User-ID`, and scope checks additionally read the acting veterinarian's own `institution_id` to decide which institution's resources are in reach.

Two distinct rejections express identity failures beyond the header checks. When a `Registrar` acts on an institution other than the one its `X-User-ID` names, or a `ProgramCoordinator` acts on a programme other than the one its `X-User-ID` names, the answer is `ERR_ACTOR_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (HTTP 403). When a registrar acts on a transfer agreement to which its institution is neither the sending nor the receiving party — or acts on the side of that agreement that is not its own — the answer is `ERR_NOT_A_PARTY_TO_AGREEMENT` (HTTP 403). A veterinarian reaching for a resource owned by an institution other than its employer is refused with `ERR_VET_INSTITUTION_MISMATCH` (HTTP 403).

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every list endpoint accepts two optional query parameters. `limit` controls page size: it defaults to 10 when omitted, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` controls the starting position: it defaults to 0 when omitted and its minimum accepted value is 0, with no upper bound. A `limit` below 1 or above 100, or a negative `offset`, is a schema failure and is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400).

Collections are returned in ascending `created_at` order — oldest record first — unless an individual endpoint's description in §4 states otherwise, and none do. When a list endpoint responds, the `meta` object carries `limit` (the effective page size for this response), `offset` (the effective starting position), `total_count` (the number of records matching the filters before pagination is applied) and `has_more` (true when records remain beyond this page). Single-resource reads and writes omit those four fields from `meta`.

Filter query parameters combine conjunctively: a request naming both a programme and a status returns only records matching both. An unrecognised enum value supplied to a filter is a schema failure rather than an empty result.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Audit Fields and the Logical Clock

Every resource carries a server-minted `id` in UUID form. Identifiers never carry a type prefix, and clients never supply them on create.

Most resources also carry three audit fields. `created_at` and `updated_at` are ISO 8601 UTC timestamps rendered with exactly three fractional-second digits; `created_at` is stamped once when the record is written and never changes, and `updated_at` is refreshed on every mutation of that record, including mutations a record suffers as a cascade from an operation aimed at a sibling. `version` is an integer that starts at 1 on create and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation of that record, again including cascaded mutations. There is one exception in this system: a `PreShipmentTest` record is immutable by construction — it has no update operation, and it carries only `id` and `created_at`. Both `updated_at` and `version` are absent from a pre-shipment test payload entirely.

All time in this system comes from a server-side logical clock rather than wall-clock time. Before any client sets it, the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and it never falls back to real time. An `AccreditationAdmin` may move it by calling the test-driving endpoint `POST /_clock/set_current_utc` with a `current_utc` instant; moving the clock mutates no entity and triggers no lazy transition by itself, so a recommendation whose expiry date has passed is still recorded as `PROPOSED` until something reads it. Every deadline, expiry, animal age, quarantine day count, sanitation buffer and validity window described in §3 is evaluated against this clock. Where a rule speaks of a calendar date rather than an instant, the date is the UTC calendar date of the clock's current instant.

### 2.5. Path Resolution and Referential Validation Order

Collections in this API are flat: `Animal`, `TransferAgreement`, `ComplianceDocument`, `PreShipmentTest`, `QuarantineOccupancy`, `ShipmentBooking`, `IntroductionProtocol`, `StudbookSubmission`, `PopulationRecommendation`, `Institution`, `Veterinarian` and `SpeciesProgram` each live directly under the root, and relationships between them are carried by foreign-key fields in request bodies rather than by nesting in the URL. The path variables that do appear — `{institutionId}`, `{speciesProgramId}`, `{animalId}`, `{recommendationId}`, `{agreementId}`, `{documentId}`, `{testId}`, `{occupancyId}`, `{bookingId}`, `{protocolId}` and `{submissionId}` — each name exactly one resource.

Existence is validated in a fixed order. Resources named in the path are resolved first, left to right, and only then are resources named in the request body resolved, in the order their fields are declared on the target entity. Each miss produces the 404 that names the missing kind: `ERR_SPECIES_PROGRAM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INSTITUTION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_VETERINARIAN_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DOCUMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TEST_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_OCCUPANCY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_BOOKING_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PROTOCOL_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_SUBMISSION_NOT_FOUND`. A pedigree pointer or a proposed mate counts as a named animal: a `sire_id`, `dam_id` or `paired_animal_id` that resolves to nothing is `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_FOUND`.

### 2.6. Numeric Precision and Rounding

Unless a rule in §3 says otherwise, every monetary-style and score-style quantity is stored and returned with two decimal places, and every rounding operation in this system rounds half away from zero — the value 0.125 rounded to two places is 0.13, and 2.345 rounded to two places is 2.35. Three quantities are the exceptions and carry four decimal places: a recommendation's `kinship_coefficient`, its `projected_offspring_inbreeding`, and its `demographic_need_index`. Day counts, session counts, slot counts, negotiation round counts, observation counts and excursion minutes are whole integers.

Where a formula rounds an intermediate value, **the rounding order is part of the specification, not an implementation detail**. Two calculations in §3 are deliberately constructed so that rounding each component before summing and rounding once at the end give different answers, and the specified order is the one that governs. Comparisons against thresholds always use the rounded, stored value, never an unrounded intermediate.

### 2.7. Optimistic Concurrency on Supersede-Bearing Writes

Two write operations in this system silently destroy work that a client may not have observed, and both are therefore guarded by an optimistic-concurrency check against a parent record's `version`.

Creating a `PopulationRecommendation` supersedes any `PROPOSED` recommendation already standing for the same subject animal in the same plan cycle. A writer whose view of the animal predates that standing recommendation would otherwise overwrite a decision it never saw. Accordingly, a `POST /population-recommendations` request must carry `expected_subject_animal_version`, a non-optional integer holding the client's last observed `version` of the subject `Animal`. If that value does not equal the subject animal's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_ANIMAL_VERSION` (HTTP 412), and the writer must re-read the animal — whose `pending_recommendation_id` now names the standing recommendation — before retrying.

Creating a `ShipmentBooking` supersedes a `RESERVED` booking already held against the same agreement, moving that earlier booking to `ABORTED` and releasing its carrier interval; a writer holding a stale view of the agreement would cancel transport arrangements it never observed. Accordingly, a `POST /shipment-bookings` request must carry `expected_transfer_agreement_version`, a non-optional integer holding the client's last observed `version` of the parent `TransferAgreement`. If that value does not equal the agreement's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_AGREEMENT_VERSION` (HTTP 412).

No other endpoint in this system takes a version field, and none of them may require one.

### 2.8. Cascade Integrity and Compensation Discipline

Several operations here mutate three, four or eight records at once, and the specification treats those secondary mutations as being just as binding as the primary one. Three disciplines apply throughout.

**Derived counters are recomputed, never incremented.** An institution's `occupied_slots` and `reserved_slots`, a programme's `current_population_count` and `open_recommendation_count`, and an animal's `crate_training_sessions_count` are all recomputed from scratch on every write that could change them, so a compensating transition cannot leave arithmetic drift behind. Two system-wide invariants follow and must hold after every single write: summing `occupied_slots` and `reserved_slots` across all institutions equals the number of animals whose `location_status` is anything other than `DISPOSED`, and summing `transfer_credit_balance` across all institutions equals exactly 0.00.

**A parent's state may be a function of its children's.** A species programme's `plan_status` is not set by any client while the plan is live: it closes and reopens as its recommendations enter and leave the open set. Whenever a recommendation of the programme's current cycle leaves the set of `PROPOSED` and `ENDORSED` recommendations, the programme's `open_recommendation_count` is recomputed, and a `PUBLISHED` programme whose count has reached zero — provided at least one recommendation has ever existed in the current cycle — moves to `CYCLE_CLOSED` with `cycle_closed_on` stamped from the logical clock. Whenever a compensating transition returns a recommendation to the open set so that the count rises above zero, a `CYCLE_CLOSED` programme returns to `PUBLISHED` and `cycle_closed_on` is cleared to null.

**Every cascaded write is a real write.** A record touched as a link in a cascade has its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` incremented exactly as if it had been the direct target of a request. This matters in practice: it is the mechanism by which one operation invalidates another party's optimistic-concurrency token, which is the intended serialization point between concurrent writers.

### 2.9. Rejection Precedence

When one request violates several rules at once, exactly one error is returned, chosen by a fixed precedence ladder. Reading the ladder top to bottom, the first violated rule wins:

1. **Authentication** — headers present and non-empty, else `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. **Authorization** — the role is one of the four and is authorized for this endpoint, else `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`.
3. **Schema** — required fields present; enum values in range; numbers within their stated bounds; dates and instants well-formed ISO 8601; `scheduled_end_date` strictly after `scheduled_start_date`; `arrival_utc` strictly after `departure_utc`; `temp_max_c` strictly greater than `temp_min_c`; `limit` between 1 and 100 inclusive and `offset` at least 0. Any failure is `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.
4. **Existence** — path resources left to right, then body foreign keys in field order, as described in §2.5.
5. **Actor scope and issuing authority** — `ERR_ACTOR_SCOPE_VIOLATION`, `ERR_NOT_A_PARTY_TO_AGREEMENT`, `ERR_VET_INSTITUTION_MISMATCH` and `ERR_WRONG_ISSUING_AUTHORITY` all sit at this rung and are mutually exclusive in practice, since each guards a different set of operations.
6. **Segregation of duties and turn-taking** — `ERR_NOT_COUNTERPARTY_TURN` and `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. These are evaluated after basic actor scope: a stranger to the agreement hears `ERR_NOT_A_PARTY_TO_AGREEMENT`, while a genuine party acting out of turn hears `ERR_NOT_COUNTERPARTY_TURN`.
7. **Optimistic concurrency** — `ERR_STALE_ANIMAL_VERSION` and `ERR_STALE_AGREEMENT_VERSION`. Note the position carefully: the stale-version check precedes every state and business check on the two endpoints that carry it, so a client whose token is stale hears about the token even when its payload would also have failed a later rule.
8. **State preconditions** — the whole family of 409 conflicts: `ERR_PROGRAM_PLAN_NOT_PUBLISHED`, `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_ALREADY_ENDORSED`, `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_NOT_ENDORSED`, `ERR_AGREEMENT_INVALID_STATE`, `ERR_DOCUMENT_ALREADY_DECIDED`, `ERR_BOOKING_INVALID_STATE`, `ERR_QUARANTINE_NOT_CLEARED`, `ERR_PROTOCOL_STAGE_INVALID`, `ERR_SUBMISSION_ALREADY_REVIEWED`, `ERR_ANIMAL_LOCATION_CONFLICT` and `ERR_ANIMAL_HAS_ACTIVE_AGREEMENT`.
9. **Accreditation and veterinary safety** — `ERR_INSTITUTION_NOT_ACCREDITED`, `ERR_INTERNATIONAL_TRANSFER_NOT_PERMITTED`, `ERR_DISPOSITION_POLICY_MISSING`, `ERR_VET_LICENSE_INVALID`, `ERR_VET_NOT_ACCREDITED_FOR_SCOPE` and `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_SURPLUS_APPROVED`.
10. **Test results, then panel completeness** — `ERR_POSITIVE_TEST_RESULT` is evaluated *before* `ERR_TEST_PANEL_INCOMPLETE`, so an agreement holding one positive result among an otherwise incomplete panel reports the positive result.
11. **Crate conditioning** — `ERR_CRATE_TRAINING_INCOMPLETE` first, then `ERR_CRATE_TRAINING_STALE`.
12. **Paperwork at confirmation** — `ERR_HEALTH_CERTIFICATE_MISSING`, then `ERR_PERMIT_NOT_ISSUED`, then `ERR_QUARANTINE_NOT_SCHEDULED`, then `ERR_HEALTH_CERTIFICATE_EXPIRED`. This is the exact order in which a shipment confirmation walks its gates.
13. **Lapsed directives and genetics** — `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_EXPIRED` sits alongside `ERR_HEALTH_CERTIFICATE_EXPIRED` on this rung, and is followed by `ERR_INBREEDING_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` and `ERR_INVALID_PAIRING`.
14. **Uniqueness** — `ERR_DUPLICATE_ANIMAL_LOCAL_ID`, then, one rung lower, `ERR_QUARANTINE_ROOM_CONFLICT`, `ERR_CARRIER_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` and `ERR_DUPLICATE_STUDBOOK_SUBMISSION`.
15. **Cyclic-process caps and minimum durations** — `ERR_NEGOTIATION_ROUND_LIMIT` and `ERR_QUARANTINE_MINIMUM_NOT_MET`.
16. **Capacity and custody** — last of all, `ERR_HOLDING_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` and `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_HELD_BY_SENDER`.

Two consequences are worth stating outright. On a shipment confirmation, crate conditioning is checked before any paperwork, so an animal with four logged sessions and no certificate at all reports the crate problem. On an agreement execution, the receiving institution's free-slot check is the very last gate, so an execution that would breach capacity *and* names a suspended receiver reports the accreditation problem instead.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. The Institutional Register and the Programme Plan Cycle

Nothing else in this system can be created until the accrediting association has registered the participants. Staff of the association register an accredited zoological institution by supplying its `name`, its regulatory `jurisdiction_code`, its accreditation standing, the `accreditation_expires_on` calendar date on which the current accreditation term ends, its `holding_capacity_slots` and its `disposition_policy_on_file` flag. A jurisdiction code is written as a two-letter country code, a hyphen, and a two- or three-character subdivision code — `US-CA`, `US-TX`, `CA-ON` — and the characters before the hyphen are the country code that later decides whether a transfer is domestic or international. `holding_capacity_slots` is a whole number of at least 0, meaning an institution may legitimately be registered with no room at all. `accreditation_status` defaults to `ACCREDITED` and takes one of `ACCREDITED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `SUSPENDED` or `TERMINATED`; `disposition_policy_on_file` defaults to false and is true only when a written policy for surplus animals is on file with the association. A freshly registered institution starts with `occupied_slots` at 0, `reserved_slots` at 0, `transfer_credit_balance` at 0.00 and `compliance_points_balance` at 0.00.

Association staff may afterwards adjust an institution's `accreditation_status`, `accreditation_expires_on`, `holding_capacity_slots` or `disposition_policy_on_file`; every one of those four is optional in the request and only the fields actually supplied change. The four computed fields — the two slot counts, the credit balance and the compliance-points balance — are never writable by any client, and each such adjustment increments the institution's `version` by 1.

Accreditation standing moves along a narrow, mostly one-way path. From `ACCREDITED` an institution may be moved to `PROVISIONAL`, and any institution that is not already `TERMINATED` may be placed on provisional standing. A `PROVISIONAL` institution may be restored to `ACCREDITED`, and restoration explicitly does **not** reset `compliance_points_balance` — the points that caused the demotion remain on the record. An institution that is either `ACCREDITED` or `PROVISIONAL` may be moved to `SUSPENDED`, and only a `SUSPENDED` institution may be moved onward to `TERMINATED`, which is terminal. Suspension cascades onto nothing: existing agreements are left exactly where they stand, and suspension acts purely as a forward guard that blocks acceptance and execution from that point on. While an institution is `PROVISIONAL`, executions of internationally scoped transfers into it are blocked even though domestic ones are not. A `TERMINATED` institution may no longer be named as a party on a new transfer agreement. Institutions can be listed with filters on `jurisdiction_code` and `accreditation_status`.

A population management programme is likewise created by association staff, one per managed taxon. The creator supplies the `taxon_name` (a scientific name such as *Panthera uncia*), the `program_type`, the `target_population_size` and the `studbook_keeper_name` of record at the coordinating body. `program_type` is one of `SSP_GREEN`, `SSP_YELLOW`, `SSP_RED` or `STUDBOOK_ONLY` and describes the coordinating body's management tier; `target_population_size` is a whole number of at least 1. A new programme opens with `plan_status` at `DRAFT`, `current_plan_cycle` at 1, `current_population_count` at 0, `open_recommendation_count` at 0 and `cycle_closed_on` null. Programmes can be listed with filters on `program_type` and `plan_status`.

Publication is the coordinating body's own act, and it is the gate that lets recommendations exist. The `ProgramCoordinator` whose `X-User-ID` is the programme's `id` publishes the plan, moving `plan_status` from `DRAFT` to `PUBLISHED`, optionally recording a `publication_note`; the programme's `target_population_size` must be at least 1 for the publication to stand. A first publication out of `DRAFT` leaves `current_plan_cycle` at 1. The same act performed on a programme that has reached `CYCLE_CLOSED` reopens it as the *next* cycle: `plan_status` returns to `PUBLISHED`, `current_plan_cycle` increases by 1, and `cycle_closed_on` is cleared to null. Either way the programme's `current_population_count` and `open_recommendation_count` are recomputed and its `version` increments by 1. Raising a recommendation against a programme whose `plan_status` is `DRAFT` or `CYCLE_CLOSED` is refused with `ERR_PROGRAM_PLAN_NOT_PUBLISHED` (HTTP 409).

Each institution's registrar registers its own attending veterinarians. Registration takes the employing `institution_id` — which must equal the registrar's own `X-User-ID`, or the request is refused with `ERR_ACTOR_SCOPE_VIOLATION` — plus the `full_name` that will be printed on certificates, the `license_number`, the `license_jurisdiction` (same two-part format as an institution's jurisdiction code), the `license_status` and the `usda_accreditation_category`. `license_status` is one of `ACTIVE`, `LAPSED` or `REVOKED` and defaults to `ACTIVE`; only an `ACTIVE` licence may sign a certificate or clear a quarantine. `usda_accreditation_category` is one of `NONE`, `CATEGORY_I` or `CATEGORY_II` and defaults to `NONE`; `CATEGORY_II` is the federal accreditation category required to sign a health certificate for an internationally scoped transfer. Both values are fixed at registration — there is no update operation for a veterinarian — and they are the values every later document decision, test record and quarantine clearance reads. Veterinarians can be listed with filters on `institution_id`, `license_status` and `usda_accreditation_category`.

### 3.2. Accessioning Animals into the Managed Population

A registrar accessions an animal into its own collection by naming the `species_program_id` the animal belongs to, the `holding_institution_id` physically holding it — which must equal the registrar's `X-User-ID` — the `owning_institution_id` that is the legal owner of record, an institution-local accession identifier `local_id`, the animal's `sex`, its `birth_date` as a calendar date, and its `founder_representation_pct`. Pedigree pointers `sire_id` and `dam_id` are optional and default to null, which is how a wild-caught founder is recorded. `sex` is one of `MALE`, `FEMALE` or `UNDETERMINED`. `founder_representation_pct` is the percentage of the managed gene pool traceable to this animal's founder lineage; it defaults to 0.0 and must be between 0.0 and 100.0 inclusive. The holder and the owner diverge routinely — that divergence is exactly what a breeding loan produces — so the two fields are independent inputs rather than one derived from the other. If another animal at the same holding institution already carries the submitted `local_id`, the accession is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_ANIMAL_LOCAL_ID` (HTTP 422); the uniqueness constraint is scoped to the pair of holder and local identifier, so two institutions may each use the accession number "M-014".

A newly accessioned animal opens with `location_status` at `HELD`, `genetic_designation` at `UNEVALUATED`, `pending_recommendation_id` null, `crate_training_sessions_count` at 0 and `last_crate_session_on` null. The accession recomputes the programme's `current_population_count` and the holder's `occupied_slots`.

An animal's state is genuinely two-dimensional, and the two dimensions advance independently of one another. **Physical custody** is tracked by `location_status`, which takes one of `HELD`, `RESERVED`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `IN_QUARANTINE`, `AWAITING_INTRODUCTION`, `INTRODUCING` or `DISPOSED`, with `DISPOSED` terminal. **Breeding management** is tracked by `genetic_designation`, which takes one of `UNEVALUATED`, `BREED_RECOMMENDED`, `HOLD_NO_BREED` or `SURPLUS_APPROVED`. An endorsement that sets a breeding designation leaves custody untouched; a shipment that moves custody leaves the breeding designation untouched. Only the abort of a behavioural introduction touches both at once, and §3.8 spells that out.

Slot accounting reads the custody dimension and nothing else. An institution's `occupied_slots` is the number of animals whose `holding_institution_id` is that institution and whose `location_status` is one of `HELD`, `IN_QUARANTINE`, `AWAITING_INTRODUCTION` or `INTRODUCING`. Its `reserved_slots` is the number of animals whose `location_status` is `RESERVED` or `IN_TRANSIT` and whose live `EXECUTED` agreement names that institution as the receiving party. Every animal that is not `DISPOSED` is therefore counted exactly once system-wide: while it sits at home it occupies a slot at its holder; from the moment its transfer executes until the moment it lands, it reserves a slot at its destination and occupies none at its sender; once it is in the destination's quarantine it occupies a slot there. `DISPOSED` animals occupy nothing and reserve nothing. A programme's `current_population_count`, by contrast, counts every animal of that programme whose `location_status` is anything other than `DISPOSED`, regardless of where it happens to be standing.

The `pending_recommendation_id` field points at the newest `PROPOSED` or `ENDORSED` recommendation whose subject is this animal, and is null when there is none. It is the field a client re-reads after an optimistic-concurrency rejection, because it names the standing decision the client had not seen. Animals can be listed with filters on `species_program_id`, `holding_institution_id`, `owning_institution_id`, `sire_id`, `dam_id`, `pending_recommendation_id`, `location_status` and `genetic_designation`, which together make it possible to ask for, say, every `BREED_RECOMMENDED` animal a given institution currently holds.

### 3.3. Genetic and Demographic Analysis: Raising a Recommendation

The coordinating body of a published programme raises directives against it. A recommendation names the `species_program_id` it belongs to — which must equal the coordinator's own `X-User-ID` — a `recommendation_type`, the `subject_animal_id` the directive is about, an `analysis_mode`, a `priority_rank` and an `expires_on` calendar date after which the directive may no longer be endorsed. `recommendation_type` is one of `BREED_WITH`, `HOLD`, `TRANSFER_OUT` or `SURPLUS_DISPOSITION`. `priority_rank` is the coordinating body's priority ordinal, a whole number of at least 1 where 1 is the highest priority. Three fields are conditionally required: `paired_animal_id` names the proposed mate and is required for a `BREED_WITH` directive and must be null for every other type, while `sending_institution_id` and `receiving_institution_id` name the institutions expected to send and receive the animal and are required for `BREED_WITH` and `TRANSFER_OUT` directives, defaulting to null otherwise. The recommendation is stamped with `plan_cycle` copied from the programme's `current_plan_cycle` at creation, and opens with `status` at `PROPOSED`. A recommendation's `status` runs over `PROPOSED`, `ENDORSED`, `DECLINED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `FULFILLED` and `EXPIRED`, of which `DECLINED`, `SUPERSEDED` and `EXPIRED` are terminal.

Structural validity of the pairing is checked before the recommendation is written. A `BREED_WITH` directive must name a `paired_animal_id` that is not the subject itself, both animals must belong to the same `species_program_id`, neither may be `DISPOSED`, and their `sex` values must be `MALE` and `FEMALE` in some order — two `FEMALE` animals, or a `FEMALE` and an `UNDETERMINED`, are not a pairing. A `HOLD` or `SURPLUS_DISPOSITION` directive must name no paired animal at all. Any of these failures is refused with `ERR_INVALID_PAIRING` (HTTP 422).

Raising a new directive for an animal legitimately displaces the old one. Any recommendation for the same `subject_animal_id` in the same `plan_cycle` that is still `PROPOSED` moves to `SUPERSEDED`, and the subject animal's `pending_recommendation_id` is repointed at the newer recommendation. If the standing recommendation for that animal and cycle has already reached `ENDORSED` or `FULFILLED`, however, it cannot be displaced and the create is refused with `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_ALREADY_ENDORSED` (HTTP 409). Because the create rewrites the animal, it increments the subject `Animal`'s `version` by 1 — and because it is exactly this silent displacement that the concurrency guard of §2.7 protects, the request must carry `expected_subject_animal_version`. The programme's `open_recommendation_count` is recomputed afterwards, and a programme that had reached `CYCLE_CLOSED` returns to `PUBLISHED` when the count rises above zero.

**Pedigree kinship.** Before the row is persisted, the server computes the kinship between the subject and the paired animal from the two animals' `sire_id` and `dam_id` ancestry; three generations of pointers are sufficient, because those two fields are the only pedigree the system stores. A null parent pointer never matches another null parent pointer — two founders with no recorded sire are not thereby siblings. Six relationship classes are examined, each contributing a candidate coefficient:

- The two animals share both a non-null sire and a non-null dam: they are **full siblings**, contributing 0.2500.
- Otherwise, exactly one of the two comparisons holds — either they share a non-null sire while their dams differ, or they share a non-null dam while their sires differ: they are **half siblings**, contributing 0.1250.
- The paired animal is the subject's recorded sire or dam, or the subject is the paired animal's recorded sire or dam: **parent and offspring**, contributing 0.2500.
- The paired animal is one of the subject's four grandparents (the sire and dam of the subject's sire and of the subject's dam), or the subject is one of the paired animal's grandparents: **grandparent and grandoffspring**, contributing 0.1250.
- The paired animal is a full or half sibling of one of the subject's parents, or the subject is a full or half sibling of one of the paired animal's parents: **aunt or uncle with niece or nephew**, contributing 0.1250.
- One of the subject's parents and one of the paired animal's parents are full or half siblings of each other: **first cousins**, contributing 0.0625.

The pedigree coefficient is the largest candidate contributed by any class that applies; when no class applies at all it is 0.0000. That value, rounded half up to four decimal places, is stored as `kinship_coefficient`. Animals whose `location_status` is `DISPOSED` still count as ancestors — pedigree is history, not inventory.

**Projected offspring inbreeding.** The `projected_offspring_inbreeding` figure is the inbreeding coefficient the pairing's offspring would carry, and how it is derived depends on `analysis_mode`, which is one of `PEDIGREE_ONLY` or `PEDIGREE_PLUS_MOLECULAR`. Under `PEDIGREE_ONLY` it equals the pedigree coefficient exactly, rounded half up to four decimal places; a `molecular_similarity_pct` submitted anyway is stored on the record but contributes nothing, so an unrelated pair scores 0.0000 and lands on `RECOMMENDED` every time. Under `PEDIGREE_PLUS_MOLECULAR` the marker panel is required: `molecular_similarity_pct` must be supplied, must lie between 0.0 and 100.0 inclusive, and is read as the band-sharing percentage between the two animals' marker profiles. The projected figure is then four tenths of that percentage expressed as a proportion of one — the percentage divided by 100 — plus six tenths of the pedigree coefficient, with the sum rounded half up to four decimal places. The molecular weighting is deliberately heavy enough to matter on its own: a pair with no recorded relationship whatsoever, whose pedigree coefficient is 0.0000, is pushed all the way to `NOT_RECOMMENDED` once its band sharing reaches 31.25 percent.

**Verdict.** The `pairing_verdict` is derived from the rounded `projected_offspring_inbreeding`, never from an unrounded intermediate, and takes one of `RECOMMENDED`, `CONDITIONAL`, `NOT_RECOMMENDED` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. For any recommendation whose type is not `BREED_WITH` the verdict is `NOT_APPLICABLE`, and both `kinship_coefficient` and `projected_offspring_inbreeding` are null on the record. For a `BREED_WITH` directive, the programme's tolerance for inbreeding is one eighth — a projected coefficient of 0.125 — and both bands are closed at their lower edge: a projected figure that has reached 0.125 or higher is `NOT_RECOMMENDED`, a figure that has reached half of that tolerance, 0.0625, but has not reached 0.125 is `CONDITIONAL`, and anything below 0.0625 is `RECOMMENDED`. A pair of full siblings, at 0.2500, is thus always `NOT_RECOMMENDED`; a pair of first cousins, at exactly 0.0625, is `CONDITIONAL` rather than `RECOMMENDED`.

**Demographic need.** Once the kinship coefficient is known, the same create computes a `demographic_need_index` — a weighted score between 0 and 1 combining how far the programme is from its target, how genetically scarce the subject animal's lineage is, and how much room the pairing leaves under the inbreeding ceiling. Three factors are each computed and rounded first. The *population gap* is the programme's `target_population_size` less its live `current_population_count`, divided by the `target_population_size`, rounded half up to six decimal places and then confined to the range 0.0 through 1.0, so a programme already at or over its target contributes 0.0 rather than a negative number. *Founder scarcity* is 1 less the subject animal's `founder_representation_pct` divided by 100, rounded half up to four decimal places, so an animal representing none of the gene pool scores 1.0 and one representing all of it scores 0.0. *Kinship relief* is 1 less the `kinship_coefficient` divided by the 0.125 inbreeding tolerance, rounded half up to four decimal places and confined to the range 0.0 through 1.0; where no kinship coefficient applies because the directive is not a `BREED_WITH`, the coefficient is treated as 0.0000 and the relief factor is therefore 1.0. Those three rounded factors are combined as one half of the population gap plus three tenths of founder scarcity plus two tenths of kinship relief — weights that are fixed and sum to 1 — and the composite is then multiplied by the programme's management-tier weight: 0.6 for `SSP_GREEN`, 0.8 for `SSP_YELLOW`, 1.0 for `SSP_RED` and 0.3 for `STUDBOOK_ONLY`. The product, rounded half up to four decimal places, is the `demographic_need_index`. Because the tier weight scales the whole composite after the weighted sum, a `STUDBOOK_ONLY` programme can never out-score an `SSP_RED` programme on identical demographics.

The `genetic_value_score` follows from it directly: it is the *rounded* `demographic_need_index` multiplied by 100, rounded half up to two decimal places. That figure — not the index itself — is the genetic input to the transfer credit valuation of §3.5, and it is frozen on the recommendation at creation, so a later change in the programme's population cannot re-value a transfer already priced against it. Recommendations can be listed with filters on `species_program_id`, `subject_animal_id`, `paired_animal_id`, `sending_institution_id`, `receiving_institution_id`, `recommendation_type`, `pairing_verdict`, `status` and `plan_cycle`.

### 3.4. Endorsement, Breeding Designations and Lapsed Directives

A directive from the coordinating body has no effect on an animal until the institution responsible for that animal endorses it. Endorsement is a registrar's act, and which registrar is entitled to perform it depends on the directive: for a `BREED_WITH` or `TRANSFER_OUT` directive, the acting `X-User-ID` must equal the recommendation's `sending_institution_id`; for a `HOLD` or `SURPLUS_DISPOSITION` directive, it must equal the subject animal's current `holding_institution_id`. A registrar reaching for someone else's directive is refused with `ERR_ACTOR_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (HTTP 403). The endorsing registrar may attach an optional `endorsement_note`.

Only a `PROPOSED` recommendation can be endorsed; an attempt against one that has already been endorsed, fulfilled, declined, superseded or expired is refused with `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_NOT_ENDORSED` (HTTP 409). Two further gates then apply in order. **Expiry first:** if the logical clock's calendar date is later than `expires_on`, the directive has lapsed. The lapse is recorded rather than merely reported — the recommendation's `status` becomes `EXPIRED` and the subject animal's `pending_recommendation_id` is cleared to null, both persisted, and only then does the request itself fail with `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_EXPIRED` (HTTP 422). A client that reads the recommendation immediately afterwards therefore observes `EXPIRED`, not `PROPOSED`. Endorsement on the `expires_on` date itself is still in time; only a date strictly after it lapses. **Genetics second:** a `BREED_WITH` directive whose `pairing_verdict` is `NOT_RECOMMENDED` may never be endorsed, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_INBREEDING_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 422). A `CONDITIONAL` verdict, by contrast, is endorsable exactly like a `RECOMMENDED` one.

A successful endorsement moves `status` from `PROPOSED` to `ENDORSED` and writes the breeding designations the directive implies, each of which is a transition on the animal's `genetic_designation` dimension and leaves `location_status` alone:

- A `BREED_WITH` directive sets **both** the subject animal's and the paired animal's `genetic_designation` to `BREED_RECOMMENDED`, moving each from `UNEVALUATED`.
- A `HOLD` directive sets the subject animal's `genetic_designation` to `HOLD_NO_BREED`, moving it from `UNEVALUATED`.
- A `SURPLUS_DISPOSITION` directive sets the subject animal's `genetic_designation` to `SURPLUS_APPROVED`, reachable either from `UNEVALUATED` or from `HOLD_NO_BREED`, and it is this designation alone that unlocks disposition of the animal.
- A `TRANSFER_OUT` directive changes no breeding designation; it exists to authorise a transfer agreement, nothing more.

Every animal whose designation actually changed has its `version` incremented by 1, and the recommendation's own `version` increments by 1. Later in the animal's life the designation may move again without any coordinating-body involvement: aborting a behavioural introduction drives a `BREED_RECOMMENDED` animal to `HOLD_NO_BREED`, as §3.8 describes.

An endorsed directive is not yet a completed one. When the transfer agreement founded on it executes, the recommendation moves from `ENDORSED` to `FULFILLED` and the animal's `pending_recommendation_id` is cleared; if that agreement is later terminated, the recommendation is pulled back from `FULFILLED` to `ENDORSED` and `pending_recommendation_id` is repointed at it, because the directive is once again outstanding. Both movements change the programme's open-recommendation set and therefore its `plan_status`, exactly as §2.8 requires.

### 3.5. Negotiating the Transfer Agreement

A transfer agreement is the contract that turns a directive into a movement, and it is the only entity in this system that both parties write to. The sending institution's registrar opens it in `DRAFT` by naming the `recommendation_id` of the `ENDORSED` directive it implements — a directive in any other status is refused with `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_NOT_ENDORSED` (HTTP 409) — the `animal_id` being transferred, which must be that directive's subject animal, the `sending_institution_id`, which must equal the opening registrar's own `X-User-ID`, and the `receiving_institution_id`. Four commercial terms are set at the same time: `ownership_disposition`, one of `OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER`, `BREEDING_LOAN` or `LOAN_WITH_RETAINED_OWNERSHIP`; `offspring_disposition_rule`, one of `FIRST_TO_SENDER`, `ALTERNATING`, `SPLIT_50_50` or `ALL_TO_RECEIVER`; `breeding_rights` granted to the receiver, one of `FULL`, `RESTRICTED_NO_BREED` or `CONTRACEPTION_REQUIRED`; and `transport_cost_share_pct`, the share of transport cost borne by the receiving institution, between 0.0 and 100.0 inclusive. A new agreement opens with `status` at `DRAFT`, `round_number` at 0, `last_action_institution_id` null, and both `provisional_credit_points` and `final_credit_points` null. An agreement's `status` runs over `DRAFT`, `OFFERED`, `COUNTERED`, `ACCEPTED`, `EXECUTED`, `COMPLETED` and `TERMINATED`, of which `COMPLETED` and `TERMINATED` are terminal.

Three gates guard the opening. The animal must be `HELD`, or the request is refused with `ERR_ANIMAL_LOCATION_CONFLICT` (HTTP 409); no other agreement naming the same animal may still be in `DRAFT`, `OFFERED`, `COUNTERED`, `ACCEPTED` or `EXECUTED`, or the request is refused with `ERR_ANIMAL_HAS_ACTIVE_AGREEMENT` (HTTP 409); and both parties must hold live accreditation — `ACCREDITED` or `PROVISIONAL` — or the request is refused with `ERR_INSTITUTION_NOT_ACCREDITED` (HTTP 422). The animal's `holding_institution_id` must additionally be the sending institution named on the agreement, and if it is not, the answer is `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_HELD_BY_SENDER` (HTTP 422).

**Scope is derived once and frozen.** At creation the server compares the two institutions' `jurisdiction_code` values to fill `transfer_scope`, which takes one of `INTRA_JURISDICTION`, `INTERSTATE` or `INTERNATIONAL`. When the two codes are identical the scope is `INTRA_JURISDICTION`. Otherwise the two country codes — the two characters before the hyphen in each code — are compared: when they differ the scope is `INTERNATIONAL`, and when they match while the subdivisions differ the scope is `INTERSTATE`. So `US-CA` to `US-CA` is intra-jurisdiction, `US-CA` to `US-TX` is interstate, and `US-CA` to `CA-ON` is international. The derived scope is frozen for the agreement's whole life: a later edit to either institution's `jurisdiction_code` does not re-derive it, so the document and test panels the agreement must satisfy are stable from the day it opens.

That derived scope selects two panels that the rest of the workflow reads. An `INTRA_JURISDICTION` transfer needs no compliance documents at all and a single-assay test panel of `TB`. An `INTERSTATE` transfer needs an `INTERSTATE_CVI` and a `STATE_IMPORT_PERMIT`, and a test panel of `TB` and `BRUCELLA`. An `INTERNATIONAL` transfer needs an `INTERSTATE_CVI`, a `CITES_EXPORT_PERMIT` and a `CITES_IMPORT_PERMIT`, and the full four-assay panel of `TB`, `BRUCELLA`, `HERPESVIRUS` and `FECAL_PARASITE`. A `USDA_TRANSPORT_PERMIT` is never required by any scope; it is accepted as an optional extra and is not looked for at confirmation.

**Negotiation alternates, and it is bounded.** Putting terms on the table advances the agreement from `DRAFT` to `OFFERED`, and only the sending institution may make that opening move; from `COUNTERED` the same act returns the agreement to `OFFERED` and either party may make it. Answering a standing offer with revised terms advances the agreement from `OFFERED` to `COUNTERED`. Both acts may carry any of `ownership_disposition`, `offspring_disposition_rule`, `breeding_rights` and `transport_cost_share_pct` — all four optional — and whichever of them is supplied overwrites the corresponding field on the agreement, so the terms of record are always the terms of the most recent action. Both acts increase `round_number` by exactly 1 and set `last_action_institution_id` to the acting `X-User-ID`. The opening offer therefore takes `round_number` from 0 to 1.

Turn-taking is enforced on every negotiation act other than the opening offer: the acting institution must not be the agreement's current `last_action_institution_id`. A party that tries to counter, re-offer or accept its own standing terms is refused with `ERR_NOT_COUNTERPARTY_TURN` (HTTP 403). An institution that is party to neither side hears `ERR_NOT_A_PARTY_TO_AGREEMENT` (HTTP 403) instead, and so does a receiving institution that tries to make the opening offer out of `DRAFT`, which is reserved to the sender. Attempting a negotiation act from a status that does not admit it — countering a `DRAFT` agreement, offering on an `ACCEPTED` one — is refused with `ERR_AGREEMENT_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409).

The offer-and-counter cycle would otherwise run forever, so the number of rounds is capped: an agreement may accumulate at most six offer-or-counter actions in total. An offer or counter attempted when `round_number` has already reached six is refused with `ERR_NEGOTIATION_ROUND_LIMIT` (HTTP 422). This cap is the only termination guarantee the negotiation loop has.

Acceptance closes the loop. The party that did **not** act last accepts the terms standing on the table, moving the agreement from `OFFERED` or from `COUNTERED` to `ACCEPTED`, optionally recording an `acceptance_note`. Two further conditions hold at acceptance: the receiving institution's `accreditation_status` must be `ACCREDITED` or `PROVISIONAL`, else `ERR_INSTITUTION_NOT_ACCREDITED`, and the founding recommendation must still be `ENDORSED`, else `ERR_RECOMMENDATION_NOT_ENDORSED`. Acceptance is not a negotiation round: it leaves `round_number` untouched and leaves `last_action_institution_id` pointing at whoever put the accepted terms on the table. The terms standing at acceptance are the terms of record from then on, and no further offer or counter is possible. Agreements can be listed with filters on `recommendation_id`, `animal_id`, `sending_institution_id`, `receiving_institution_id`, `last_action_institution_id`, `transfer_scope` and `status`.

### 3.6. Execution: Credit Valuation, Capacity and the Plan-Cycle Cascade

Execution is the moment the transfer becomes real, and it is the single most consequential write in the system. Only the sending institution's registrar may execute, and only from `ACCEPTED`; an optional `execution_note` may be recorded. Six substantive gates are checked. The founding recommendation must still be `ENDORSED` (`ERR_RECOMMENDATION_NOT_ENDORSED`). The animal must be `HELD` (`ERR_ANIMAL_LOCATION_CONFLICT`) and must be physically held by the sending institution named on the agreement (`ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_HELD_BY_SENDER`). The receiving institution must hold live accreditation, `ACCREDITED` or `PROVISIONAL` (`ERR_INSTITUTION_NOT_ACCREDITED`). Where `transfer_scope` is `INTERNATIONAL`, the receiver must be fully `ACCREDITED` — provisional standing suffices only for domestic scopes — and a provisional receiver on an international transfer is refused with `ERR_INTERNATIONAL_TRANSFER_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 422). Where `ownership_disposition` is `OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER`, the receiver's `disposition_policy_on_file` must be true, else `ERR_DISPOSITION_POLICY_MISSING` (HTTP 422). Finally, the receiver must have a free holding slot: its `occupied_slots` plus its `reserved_slots` plus one for this animal must not exceed its `holding_capacity_slots`, or the execution is refused with `ERR_HOLDING_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 422). An institution with three occupied and one reserved against a capacity of four is full, and the fifth animal cannot be executed in.

**Transfer credit valuation.** Transfers in this network are priced in transfer credit points rather than currency, and the same three-component valuation runs twice in an agreement's life: provisionally at execution, and finally at delivery. The valuation date is the logical clock date of the triggering request. From it the server derives the animal's age in whole elapsed anniversaries of its `birth_date`: the year count increments on the birthday itself, so an animal born on 10 May 2019 is 7 years old on 10 May 2026 and still 6 years old on 9 May 2026.

Three components are then computed, and **each is rounded half up to two decimal places before any of them are added together**:

- The **genetic component** is the founding recommendation's `genetic_value_score` multiplied by one half.
- The **husbandry component** is the animal's age in whole years multiplied by 1.125 points per year.
- The **transport component** is a transport base of 12.5 points multiplied by the agreement's `transport_cost_share_pct` expressed as a proportion of one — that is, the percentage divided by 100.

Those three rounded values are summed to a subtotal, the subtotal is multiplied by the ownership multiplier for the agreement's `ownership_disposition` — 1.00 for `OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER`, 0.50 for `BREEDING_LOAN`, 0.65 for `LOAN_WITH_RETAINED_OWNERSHIP` — and that product is rounded half up to two decimal places again. The rounding order is part of the rule, not an optimisation: the husbandry rate of 1.125 and the transport base of 12.5 are chosen precisely so that summing the unrounded components and rounding once at the end diverges by 0.01 from the specified order on ordinary inputs.

At execution the valuation stops there and the result is stored as `provisional_credit_points`. No transport penalty is applied, because no temperature record exists yet.

**The ledger.** Transfer credit is kept as a signed double-entry ledger in each institution's `transfer_credit_balance`, and only the two parties to an agreement are ever touched. Execution adds `provisional_credit_points` to the sending institution's balance and subtracts the same amount from the receiving institution's balance — the sender is credited for giving up the animal, the receiver debited for gaining it. Balances are stored signed to two decimal places and may legitimately be negative. Because every posting is double-entry, the system-wide sum of all balances is 0.00 at rest and remains 0.00 after execution, after the delivery re-valuation of §3.7 and after any compensating termination.

**The four-link cascade.** Beyond the ledger, execution runs a chain of four consequences, each caused by the previous one:

1. The animal's `location_status` moves from `HELD` to `RESERVED`. The sending institution's `occupied_slots` therefore drops by one and the receiving institution's `reserved_slots` rises by one, and both institutions' slot counts are recomputed.
2. Because the animal has left `HELD`, the directive it was moving under is satisfied: the founding recommendation moves from `ENDORSED` to `FULFILLED`, and the animal's `pending_recommendation_id` is cleared to null.
3. Because that recommendation has left the open set, the programme's `open_recommendation_count` is recomputed.
4. Because the recount reached zero while the programme was `PUBLISHED` — and at least one recommendation exists in the current cycle — the programme's `plan_status` moves to `CYCLE_CLOSED` and `cycle_closed_on` is stamped with the logical clock instant.

Alongside the cascade, `status` becomes `EXECUTED` and `executed_at` is stamped from the logical clock; where `ownership_disposition` is `OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER`, the animal's `owning_institution_id` is moved to the receiving institution, while under either loan form the legal owner stays with the sender. The agreement's `version` and the animal's `version` each increment by 1.

### 3.7. Clearing the Animal for Shipment: Testing, Paperwork, Crating and Transport

Nothing may leave the sending institution until the veterinary and regulatory record is complete, and the sequence in which that record is assembled is itself specified. Testing comes first, because certificates are issued against test results; paperwork second; crate conditioning and carrier arrangements third.

**Pre-shipment testing.** An attending veterinarian of the sending institution records each diagnostic result against the agreement by naming the `transfer_agreement_id`, the `animal_id` sampled — which must be the animal the agreement covers — the `collecting_veterinarian_id`, which must equal the acting veterinarian's own `X-User-ID`, the `test_code` performed, the `collected_on` calendar date, the `result`, and the `lab_reference` accession the laboratory assigned. `test_code` is one of `TB`, `BRUCELLA`, `HERPESVIRUS` or `FECAL_PARASITE`; `result` is one of `NEGATIVE`, `POSITIVE` or `INCONCLUSIVE`. The acting veterinarian must be employed by an institution that is a party to the agreement, and specifically must belong to the institution that owns the sampling — the sending institution — or the record is refused with `ERR_VET_INSTITUTION_MISMATCH` (HTTP 403); a veterinarian from an unrelated institution hears `ERR_NOT_A_PARTY_TO_AGREEMENT` instead. The veterinarian's `license_status` must be `ACTIVE`, and a `LAPSED` or `REVOKED` licence is refused with `ERR_VET_LICENSE_INVALID` (HTTP 422). Tests may be recorded only against an agreement whose status admits them — `ACCEPTED` or `EXECUTED` — and otherwise the answer is `ERR_AGREEMENT_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409). A test record is immutable: there is no operation that edits one, and it is the one entity in the system that carries neither `updated_at` nor `version`.

A positive result is not merely recorded, it invalidates work already done. Recording a `POSITIVE` result for an agreement drives every `ISSUED` health certificate of that agreement from `ISSUED` to `VOIDED` with its `decision_note` set to `VOIDED_BY_POSITIVE_TEST`, and drives every `RESERVED` or `CONFIRMED` shipment booking of that agreement to `ABORTED`, releasing each aborted booking's carrier interval. An `INCONCLUSIVE` result does not trigger that cascade, though it will block a later certificate just as firmly. Tests can be listed with filters on `transfer_agreement_id`, `animal_id`, `collecting_veterinarian_id`, `test_code` and `result`.

**Opening a compliance document.** The sending institution's registrar opens each certificate or permit application in `DRAFT` against the agreement. The `document_type` is one of `INTERSTATE_CVI`, `STATE_IMPORT_PERMIT`, `CITES_EXPORT_PERMIT`, `CITES_IMPORT_PERMIT` or `USDA_TRANSPORT_PERMIT`, and the type is polymorphic: it selects both who may decide the document and which fields it carries. An `INTERSTATE_CVI` requires an `issuing_veterinarian_id` naming the veterinarian who will sign it and an `exam_date` giving the calendar date of the pre-shipment physical examination, and must leave `issuing_authority_name` null. Every permit type is the mirror image: it requires an `issuing_authority_name` naming the government authority on the permit, and must leave both `issuing_veterinarian_id` and `exam_date` null. A document opens with `status` at `DRAFT` and with `document_number`, `valid_from`, `valid_until` and `decision_note` all null, and may be opened only while the agreement is `ACCEPTED` or `EXECUTED`. A document's `status` runs over `DRAFT`, `ISSUED`, `DENIED` and `VOIDED`, of which `DENIED` and `VOIDED` are terminal.

**Deciding it.** A decision carries an `outcome` of either `ISSUE` or `DENY`, and optionally a `document_number`, a `valid_until` and a `decision_note`. The document must still be `DRAFT`; a second decision on an already `ISSUED`, `DENIED` or `VOIDED` document is refused with `ERR_DOCUMENT_ALREADY_DECIDED` (HTTP 409). Authority is split strictly by document kind and this rule governs both outcomes equally: an `INTERSTATE_CVI` may be decided only by a `Veterinarian`, and only by the very veterinarian the document names in `issuing_veterinarian_id`, while every permit type may be decided only by an `AccreditationAdmin`. A permit decision attempted by a veterinarian, or a certificate decision attempted by association staff, is refused with `ERR_WRONG_ISSUING_AUTHORITY` (HTTP 403).

For a health certificate, four further conditions guard the signature. The signing veterinarian must be employed by the sending institution, else `ERR_VET_INSTITUTION_MISMATCH`. Duties are segregated: the veterinarian who collected any pre-shipment test for this agreement may not be the veterinarian who signs its certificate, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). The signing veterinarian's `license_status` must be `ACTIVE` and their `license_jurisdiction` must match the sending institution's `jurisdiction_code`, else `ERR_VET_LICENSE_INVALID`. And where the agreement's `transfer_scope` is `INTERNATIONAL`, the signing veterinarian's `usda_accreditation_category` must be `CATEGORY_II`; anything less is refused with `ERR_VET_NOT_ACCREDITED_FOR_SCOPE` (HTTP 422).

The test panel is then examined, and the two panel rejections are ordered. First, if any test in the panel required by the agreement's `transfer_scope` has a `result` of `POSITIVE` or `INCONCLUSIVE`, the certificate is refused with `ERR_POSITIVE_TEST_RESULT` (HTTP 422). Only afterwards is completeness assessed: the panel must contain one test for each `test_code` the scope requires, and each of those tests must have been collected within the sixty days running up to the examination — the interval from `collected_on` to `exam_date` must be at least zero and at most 60 whole days, so a sample drawn *after* the examination date does not count toward the panel at all, and neither does one drawn 61 days before it. A missing code, a stale sample, or an agreement with no recorded tests whatsoever is refused with `ERR_TEST_PANEL_INCOMPLETE` (HTTP 422); the empty-panel case fails here rather than trivially passing an empty-set test.

An `ISSUE` outcome sets `status` to `ISSUED` and copies the supplied `document_number` onto the record, then fills the validity window. For an `INTERSTATE_CVI`, `valid_from` is the `exam_date` and `valid_until` is the thirtieth day after the `exam_date`; for every permit type, `valid_from` is the logical clock's calendar date at the decision and `valid_until` is the date supplied in the decision payload. `valid_until` is inclusive — a certificate is still good on its `valid_until` date. A `DENY` outcome sets `status` to `DENIED` and records the supplied `decision_note`, and assigns no `document_number`, no `valid_from` and no `valid_until` at all. Either way the document's `version` increments by 1. An issued document is never moved into a separate expired state; lapse is evaluated against the logical clock when a shipment is confirmed, and the document's `status` stays `ISSUED` throughout. Documents can be listed with filters on `transfer_agreement_id`, `issuing_veterinarian_id`, `document_type` and `status`.

**Crate conditioning.** Independently of the paperwork, the holding institution's registrar logs crate-conditioning sessions against the animal, each session naming a `session_date`, a `trainer_name` and a `duration_minutes`. The registrar must be the animal's holder. Each logged session recomputes two fields on the animal: `crate_training_sessions_count` is the total number of sessions ever recorded for that animal, and `last_crate_session_on` is the latest `session_date` among them — the latest *date*, which need not belong to the most recently created record, so back-dating a session does not move the recency marker forward. Sessions are never expired or pruned, and the animal's `version` increments by 1 on each. Because the count and the recency marker are independent, an animal can satisfy one gate and fail the other.

**Booking transport.** Once the agreement is `EXECUTED`, either party's registrar books temperature-controlled carriage by naming the `transfer_agreement_id`, the `carrier_code` of the air-cargo carrier, the `crate_serial` of the IATA-compliant crate the animal was conditioned to, the scheduled `departure_utc` and `arrival_utc` instants, and the contracted temperature envelope as `temp_min_c` and `temp_max_c` in Celsius. `arrival_utc` must be strictly after `departure_utc` and `temp_max_c` strictly greater than `temp_min_c`. Booking against an agreement that is not `EXECUTED` is refused with `ERR_AGREEMENT_INVALID_STATE`. A new booking opens with `status` at `RESERVED`, `temperature_samples` null because no journey has happened yet, `thermal_excursion_minutes` at 0, and `departed_at` and `delivered_at` null. A booking's `status` runs over `RESERVED`, `CONFIRMED`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `DELIVERED` and `ABORTED`, of which `DELIVERED` and `ABORTED` are terminal.

Creating a booking supersedes transport already arranged: any `RESERVED` booking against the same agreement moves to `ABORTED` and releases its carrier interval, and the agreement's `version` increments by 1. That displacement is why the create carries `expected_transfer_agreement_version`, as §2.7 sets out.

Carrier capacity is the constraint that governs how many journeys one carrier may be flying at once. A carrier may have at most two live journeys overlapping any given window; the third is refused with `ERR_CARRIER_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 422). A journey counts as live while its booking is `RESERVED`, `CONFIRMED` or `IN_TRANSIT`, across every institution and every agreement — `DELIVERED` and `ABORTED` bookings release their interval and are ignored. Two journeys overlap when the existing booking's `departure_utc` is at or before the new booking's `arrival_utc` **and** the new booking's `departure_utc` is at or before the existing booking's `arrival_utc`. The comparison is closed at both ends and evaluated to the second, so two journeys that merely touch — one arriving at the exact instant the next departs — do count as overlapping. This is deliberately the opposite convention from the quarantine-room rule of §3.8, and the two must not share one implementation. Bookings can be listed with filters on `transfer_agreement_id`, `carrier_code` and `status`.

**Confirming, departing, arriving.** Confirmation is the sending registrar's act and moves the booking from `RESERVED` to `CONFIRMED`, optionally recording a `confirmation_note`. It walks six gates in a fixed order, and the first failure is the one reported: at least five recorded crate-conditioning sessions (`ERR_CRATE_TRAINING_INCOMPLETE`); a most recent session no more than fourteen days — two weeks — before the booking's departure date, measured from `last_crate_session_on` to the `departure_utc` date (`ERR_CRATE_TRAINING_STALE`); an `ISSUED` `INTERSTATE_CVI` for the agreement, which is required at confirmation whatever the transfer's scope (`ERR_HEALTH_CERTIFICATE_MISSING`); an `ISSUED` document for every permit type in the panel the agreement's `transfer_scope` requires, which for an `INTRA_JURISDICTION` transfer is an empty panel and therefore always satisfied (`ERR_PERMIT_NOT_ISSUED`); a `SCHEDULED` quarantine occupancy waiting at the receiving institution for this agreement (`ERR_QUARANTINE_NOT_SCHEDULED`); and a certificate whose `valid_until` is on or after the booking's arrival date (`ERR_HEALTH_CERTIFICATE_EXPIRED`). Note which end of the journey that last gate reads: expiry is measured against the `arrival_utc` date, not the departure date, so a certificate that is valid at take-off but lapses in flight is refused.

Departure is likewise the sending registrar's act. It moves the booking from `CONFIRMED` to `IN_TRANSIT`, stamps `departed_at` from the logical clock, optionally records a `departure_note`, and moves the animal's `location_status` from `RESERVED` to `IN_TRANSIT`; the animal must be `RESERVED` for it to be possible. The receiving institution keeps its reserved slot throughout the flight and no occupied slot is charged at either party. A transition attempted from the wrong booking status — confirming a `CONFIRMED` booking, departing a `RESERVED` one, delivering one that never departed — is refused with `ERR_BOOKING_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409).

Arrival is the **receiving** registrar's act, and it is the one transport operation the sender may not perform. The delivery request carries `temperature_samples`, the ordered array of Celsius readings taken through the journey at half-hourly spacing. From them the server computes `thermal_excursion_minutes`: it counts the readings that fall strictly outside the contracted envelope and multiplies that count by the thirty-minute sampling interval. The envelope is inclusive at both ends — a reading exactly equal to `temp_min_c` or exactly equal to `temp_max_c` is inside it and contributes nothing — and an empty array yields zero minutes. Four readings out of envelope therefore record 120 minutes of excursion.

Delivery then re-values the transfer. The same three-component valuation of §3.6 runs again, but with two differences that give this variant its name. It is valued at the **delivery** date rather than the execution date, so an animal that crossed a birthday in transit gains a whole husbandry year and its component grows by 1.13 points once rounded. And a thermal penalty is subtracted: `thermal_excursion_minutes` multiplied by 0.05 points per minute, rounded half up to two decimal places, is taken off the credit points, and the result — rounded half up to two decimal places and floored at 0.00, never negative — is stored as `final_credit_points`. The ledger is then **adjusted by the difference rather than posted afresh**: the amount by which `final_credit_points` exceeds `provisional_credit_points` is added to the sending institution's `transfer_credit_balance` and subtracted from the receiving institution's, so a downward re-valuation moves credit back the other way and the system-wide sum stays 0.00.

Delivery also stamps `status` as `DELIVERED` and `delivered_at` from the logical clock, moves the animal's `holding_institution_id` to the receiving institution, activates the agreement's `SCHEDULED` quarantine occupancy as §3.8 describes, moves the animal's `location_status` from `IN_TRANSIT` to `IN_QUARANTINE`, and recomputes both institutions' `occupied_slots` and `reserved_slots` — the receiver's reserved count falls by one and its occupied count rises by one. The booking's `version`, the agreement's `version` and the animal's `version` each increment by 1.

### 3.8. Arrival: Quarantine and Behavioural Introduction

**Reserving the room.** Before a shipment can be confirmed, the receiving institution's registrar must have a quarantine room waiting. Reserving one names the `institution_id` whose quarantine building will be used, the `transfer_agreement_id` the occupancy serves, the `animal_id` to be quarantined, the `quarantine_room_code` identifying the room within that institution, and the interval as `scheduled_start_date` and `scheduled_end_date`. The interval is inclusive at its start and exclusive at its end, and `scheduled_end_date` must be strictly after `scheduled_start_date`. The occupied resource is the pair of institution and room code, not the room code alone, so two institutions may both operate a room called "Q3". An occupancy opens with `phase` at `SCHEDULED`, `isolation_class` at `STANDARD`, `required_days` at 30, and `actual_start_date`, `cleared_on` and `clearing_veterinarian_id` all null. The `phase` runs over `SCHEDULED`, `ACTIVE`, `CLEARED`, `FAILED` and `CANCELLED`, of which the last three are terminal.

Room availability is checked against every other occupancy of the same institution-and-room pair whose phase is `SCHEDULED`, `ACTIVE`, `CLEARED` or `FAILED`, whatever transfer agreement that occupancy belongs to. Each such occupancy blocks a window that begins on its own `scheduled_start_date` and runs up to — but does not include — its effective end plus a post-occupancy sanitation buffer. The effective end is the occupancy's `cleared_on` date when it reached `CLEARED`, and its `scheduled_end_date` in every other case. **The buffer is conditional on how the room was released:** a room freed by a `FAILED` occupancy is blocked for a further fourteen days, whereas a room freed in any other way is blocked for a further three days. If the requested interval — again treated as beginning at `scheduled_start_date` and stopping short of `scheduled_end_date` — overlaps any blocked window, the reservation is refused with `ERR_QUARANTINE_ROOM_CONFLICT` (HTTP 422). Because both intervals stop short of their end dates, a new occupancy may legitimately start on the exact day a previous blocked window ends. And because the buffer is phase-conditional, the identical requested dates are accepted after a clean occupancy and refused after a failed one. A `CANCELLED` occupancy blocks nothing at all and carries no sanitation buffer, so the interval it once held may be reused immediately.

**Activation.** A scheduled occupancy is activated not by a client but by the arrival of the shipment it serves: when a booking of the same agreement is delivered, the occupancy's `phase` becomes `ACTIVE` and `actual_start_date` is stamped with the logical clock's date **at delivery** — not with the `scheduled_start_date` the registrar had planned for. Activation also decides how long the animal must stay. The threshold is strict: where the delivering booking's `thermal_excursion_minutes` is greater than 90 minutes, the `isolation_class` becomes `ENHANCED` and `required_days` becomes 45; at exactly 90 minutes of excursion, and anywhere below, the class stays `STANDARD` and `required_days` stays 30. One further half-hour sampling interval past the ninety-minute mark therefore adds fifteen days of isolation. The animal moves from `IN_TRANSIT` to `IN_QUARANTINE` at the same moment.

**Clearance.** Releasing the animal is a veterinary act performed by a veterinarian of the *receiving* institution — the occupancy's own institution — and it is the second half of the segregation-of-duties rule: the veterinarian who signed the agreement's health certificate may not clear its quarantine, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. The occupancy must be `ACTIVE`, and a clearance attempted on one that is `SCHEDULED`, already `CLEARED`, `FAILED` or `CANCELLED` is refused with `ERR_QUARANTINE_NOT_CLEARED` (HTTP 409). The acting veterinarian's `institution_id` must equal the occupancy's `institution_id`, else `ERR_VET_INSTITUTION_MISMATCH`, and their `license_status` must be `ACTIVE`, else `ERR_VET_LICENSE_INVALID`. The duration gate is inclusive: the whole days elapsed from `actual_start_date` to the logical clock's date must be at least `required_days`, so serving exactly thirty days on a `STANDARD` occupancy is enough while twenty-nine days is not, and a premature clearance is refused with `ERR_QUARANTINE_MINIMUM_NOT_MET` (HTTP 422). A successful clearance, which may carry an optional `clearance_note`, sets `phase` to `CLEARED`, stamps `cleared_on` with the logical clock's date, records `clearing_veterinarian_id` as the acting `X-User-ID`, and moves the animal from `IN_QUARANTINE` to `AWAITING_INTRODUCTION`, incrementing both the occupancy's `version` and the animal's `version` by 1.

**Failure.** The same veterinarian population may instead fail the quarantine, supplying a `failure_reason`; the same phase, institution and licence conditions apply. Failure sets `phase` to `FAILED`, records `clearing_veterinarian_id`, and leaves `cleared_on` null — a failed occupancy has no clearance date. It then unwinds the transfer: the parent agreement moves from `EXECUTED` to `TERMINATED` with `termination_reason` set to `QUARANTINE_FAILED`, which runs the entire compensating reversal chain described in §3.9, and the animal moves from `IN_QUARANTINE` back to `HELD` with its `holding_institution_id` returned to the sending institution — physically it stays where it is, under veterinary hold, but the register puts it back with its sender. Finally, as noted above, every later occupancy of that same room at that same institution is blocked for fourteen days rather than three. The occupancy's `version` increments by 1. Occupancies can be listed with filters on `institution_id`, `transfer_agreement_id`, `animal_id`, `clearing_veterinarian_id`, `quarantine_room_code`, `phase` and `isolation_class`.

**Opening an introduction.** An animal that has cleared quarantine is introduced to the resident social group at its new home. The receiving institution's registrar opens the protocol by naming the `transfer_agreement_id`, the `animal_id`, the `institution_id` running the protocol — which must equal the registrar's own `X-User-ID` — and the `resident_group_code` of the group the animal will join. The animal must be `AWAITING_INTRODUCTION`, else `ERR_ANIMAL_LOCATION_CONFLICT`; the agreement's quarantine occupancy must have reached `CLEARED`, and opening a protocol before it did is refused with `ERR_QUARANTINE_NOT_CLEARED`. The protocol opens with `stage` at `HOWDY`, `sessions_at_stage` at 0, `regression_count` at 0, `aggression_score_total` at 0.00, `observation_count` at 0 and `started_on` stamped with the logical clock's date, and it moves the animal from `AWAITING_INTRODUCTION` to `INTRODUCING`, incrementing the animal's `version` by 1. The `stage` runs over `HOWDY`, `PARTIAL_CONTACT`, `FULL_CONTACT`, `INTEGRATED` and `ABORTED`, of which `INTEGRATED` and `ABORTED` are terminal.

**Observations and the aggression accumulator.** Each behavioural observation records an `aggression_level`, an `observer_name` and an `observed_on` date, and each one may hold, advance, regress or abort the protocol. An observation recorded against a protocol already at `INTEGRATED` or `ABORTED` is refused with `ERR_PROTOCOL_STAGE_INVALID` (HTTP 409). Observations are processed in the order they were created. Each carries points by level: a `CALM` observation is worth 0 points, a `MINOR_AGGRESSION` observation 2 points, and a `SEVERE_AGGRESSION` observation 5 points.

Four counters track the protocol, and they differ in what resets them. `observation_count` is every observation ever recorded for the protocol and is never reset. `aggression_score_total` is the sum of points over every observation ever recorded and is likewise never reset; it is stored to two decimal places for envelope consistency although its value is always a whole number. `sessions_at_stage` counts only the observations recorded since the current stage was entered. And a **stage aggression accumulator** — the sum of points over the observations recorded since the current stage was entered, this one included — governs regression; both it and `sessions_at_stage` are reset to 0 on every single stage change, whether that change was an advance, a regression or an abort.

**Regression is evaluated before advancement**, and this ordering is decisive: when one observation would satisfy both conditions, the regression is what happens. If the stage accumulator has reached 6 points or more, the protocol regresses one stage — `FULL_CONTACT` back to `PARTIAL_CONTACT`, or `PARTIAL_CONTACT` back to `HOWDY` — incrementing `regression_count` by 1 and resetting both stage counters. Two special cases attach to that rule. At `HOWDY` there is no stage below to regress into, so an accumulator reaching 6 points at the first stage aborts the protocol outright, with no regard for how many regressions have been used. And at `PARTIAL_CONTACT` or `FULL_CONTACT`, a regression that would take `regression_count` past two is not performed: the protocol aborts instead, and `regression_count` is left standing at two. Two regressions are therefore survivable and the third is fatal. Note also that a single `SEVERE_AGGRESSION` observation is worth 5 points and does not on its own reach the six-point threshold, while three `MINOR_AGGRESSION` observations at one stage total 6 and do.

Advancement is considered only when the accumulator is still below 6 points, and then requires two things at once: `sessions_at_stage`, counting this observation, must have reached at least 3, and the last two observations recorded at this stage — this one included — must both be `CALM`. When both hold, the protocol advances one stage, `HOWDY` to `PARTIAL_CONTACT`, `PARTIAL_CONTACT` to `FULL_CONTACT`, or `FULL_CONTACT` to `INTEGRATED`, and both stage counters reset. When neither regression nor advancement applies, the protocol simply stays where it is and the observation is recorded. Because the accumulator resets on every stage change, the same sequence of aggression levels can land the protocol on different stages depending on where the resets fell.

**Integration completes the transfer.** Advancing out of `FULL_CONTACT` reaches `INTEGRATED`, returns the animal's `location_status` from `INTRODUCING` to `HELD` — now at its new institution, occupying a slot there — and moves the parent agreement from `EXECUTED` to `COMPLETED`. The ledger is left exactly as the delivery re-valuation left it: completion posts nothing.

**An abort runs a four-link cascade** of its own, and every link fires whichever of the three abort paths was taken:

1. The protocol's `stage` becomes `ABORTED`.
2. The animal's `location_status` returns from `INTRODUCING` to `HELD`, and its `genetic_designation` moves from `BREED_RECOMMENDED` to `HOLD_NO_BREED` — an animal that cannot be introduced to a group is not a breeding candidate.
3. Because the designation became `HOLD_NO_BREED`, every `BREED_WITH` recommendation still `PROPOSED` or `ENDORSED` that names this animal as either its `subject_animal_id` or its `paired_animal_id` moves to `DECLINED`, and where the animal's `pending_recommendation_id` pointed at one of those declined recommendations it is cleared to null.
4. Because those recommendations left the open set, the programme's `open_recommendation_count` is recomputed, and a `PUBLISHED` programme whose count has reached zero moves to `CYCLE_CLOSED` with `cycle_closed_on` stamped.

The protocol's `version` increments by 1 on every recorded observation, whether or not the stage changed. Protocols can be listed with filters on `transfer_agreement_id`, `animal_id`, `institution_id`, `resident_group_code` and `stage`.

### 3.9. Winding Down: Termination, Studbook Filing and Surplus Disposition

**Termination and compensation.** Either party's registrar may terminate an agreement, supplying a `reason` that is recorded verbatim as `termination_reason`. Termination is available from `OFFERED`, `COUNTERED`, `ACCEPTED` and `EXECUTED`; a `DRAFT` agreement that has never been offered, and one already `COMPLETED` or `TERMINATED`, cannot be terminated and the attempt is refused with `ERR_AGREEMENT_INVALID_STATE`. An institution that is party to neither side hears `ERR_NOT_A_PARTY_TO_AGREEMENT`. One further precondition applies to the registrar-initiated form: the animal's `location_status` must be either `HELD` or `RESERVED`, so an agreement whose animal is already in the air, in quarantine or mid-introduction cannot be unwound by a registrar, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_ANIMAL_LOCATION_CONFLICT`. The agreement's `version` increments by 1 in every case.

What termination compensates depends on how far the agreement had got, and the three cases are distinct. Terminating from `OFFERED` or from `COUNTERED` does nothing beyond setting `status` to `TERMINATED` and recording the reason — a negotiation that never closed has produced no side effects to unwind. Terminating from `ACCEPTED` additionally voids the paperwork: every compliance document of the agreement that is `DRAFT` or `ISSUED` moves to `VOIDED` with its `decision_note` set to `VOIDED_BY_TERMINATION`. Terminating from `EXECUTED` runs the full compensating chain, and every link of it is required:

- **The ledger is reversed at its recorded net value.** The net posted amount is `final_credit_points` where that field is not null, and `provisional_credit_points` otherwise. That net is subtracted from the sending institution's `transfer_credit_balance` and added back to the receiving institution's, returning both balances to their pre-execution values. The reversal never recomputes the valuation, so neither a birthday nor a temperature record falling between execution and termination can leave residue behind.
- Where `ownership_disposition` is `OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER`, the animal's `owning_institution_id` returns to the sending institution.
- An animal standing at `RESERVED` returns to `HELD`, and both institutions' slot counts are recomputed.
- The founding recommendation is pulled back from `FULFILLED` to `ENDORSED`, and the animal's `pending_recommendation_id` is repointed at it.
- Because that recommendation has re-entered the open set, the programme's `open_recommendation_count` is recomputed, and a programme sitting at `CYCLE_CLOSED` reopens to `PUBLISHED` with `cycle_closed_on` cleared to null.
- Every shipment booking of the agreement that is `RESERVED` or `CONFIRMED` moves to `ABORTED`, releasing its carrier interval.
- Every quarantine occupancy of the agreement that is `SCHEDULED` moves to `CANCELLED`, releasing its room interval with no sanitation buffer.
- Every compliance document of the agreement that is `DRAFT` or `ISSUED` moves to `VOIDED`.

The same chain runs when quarantine failure terminates the agreement rather than a registrar, with two differences: the `termination_reason` recorded is `QUARANTINE_FAILED`, and the animal-location precondition does not apply, since by definition the animal is in quarantine at that moment.

**Studbook filing.** Each institution reports its own events into the international species database. A registrar files a submission naming the `species_program_id` whose studbook receives it, the `institution_id` filing it — which must equal the registrar's `X-User-ID` — the `submission_type`, and the `reporting_period_end` calendar date that closes the reporting period. The optional `transfer_agreement_id` names the transfer being reported and is null for a `BIRTH` submission. `submission_type` is one of `ACQUISITION`, `DISPOSITION`, `BIRTH` or `TRANSFER`. The submission is stamped with `submitted_on` from the logical clock's date, opens with `status` at `SUBMITTED` and `review_note` null, and its `status` runs over `SUBMITTED`, `ACCEPTED` and `REJECTED`, the latter two terminal. Duplicate filings are refused: if a submission already exists for the same combination of programme, institution, `reporting_period_end` and `submission_type` and that existing submission is `SUBMITTED` or `ACCEPTED`, the new filing is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_STUDBOOK_SUBMISSION` (HTTP 422). A `REJECTED` predecessor does not block a replacement — that is precisely the point of rejecting one.

Lateness is assessed on filing, and the order of operations is part of the rule. The filing deadline is the thirtieth day after `reporting_period_end`, a one-month grace period. `days_late` is the whole days by which `submitted_on` falls after that deadline, or 0 when the filing was on time or early — it is never negative. `base_points` is `days_late` multiplied by half a point per day, rounded half up to two decimal places, and then **capped at 20 points**; the cap is applied before anything else happens, not after. The `surcharge_multiplier` is then selected **from `base_points` alone and never re-evaluated afterwards**: four tenths where `base_points` has reached 15 or more, one quarter where it has reached 8 but not 15, one tenth where it has reached 3 but not 8, and zero below 3. `surcharge_points` is `base_points` multiplied by that multiplier, rounded half up to two decimal places, and `total_compliance_points` is `base_points` plus `surcharge_points`, rounded half up to two decimal places. Re-deriving the multiplier from the total, or applying the cap after the surcharge instead of before it, puts ordinary inputs in a higher tier and yields a different figure. A filing forty days past the deadline therefore earns 20.00 base points after the cap, a four-tenths surcharge of 8.00 points, and 28.00 total compliance points. None of those four figures is posted anywhere at filing time.

**Review posts the points, and the points can cost an institution its accreditation.** The programme's `ProgramCoordinator` — the acting `X-User-ID` must equal the submission's `species_program_id` — reviews the filing with an `outcome` of either `ACCEPT` or `REJECT` and an optional `review_note`. The submission must still be `SUBMITTED`, else `ERR_SUBMISSION_ALREADY_REVIEWED` (HTTP 409). **Both outcomes post identically**: whether accepted or rejected, the submission's `total_compliance_points` are added to the filing institution's `compliance_points_balance`, because a rejected submission is still a filed submission and its lateness is still charged. The balance accumulates across every reviewed submission and is never decayed or reset — not even by a later restoration to full accreditation. Submissions still sitting at `SUBMITTED` contribute nothing, which is why the demotion below fires at review time rather than at filing time. If the posting leaves an institution that is currently `ACCREDITED` with a `compliance_points_balance` that has reached 21.00 points or more, its `accreditation_status` is demoted to `PROVISIONAL` automatically; the boundary is exact, so a balance of 21.00 demotes and a balance of 20.99 does not. Review increments the submission's `version` by 1 and the institution's `version` by 1. Submissions can be listed with filters on `species_program_id`, `institution_id`, `transfer_agreement_id`, `submission_type` and `status`.

**Surplus disposition.** The last thing that can happen to a managed animal is its disposition out of the population, and the accreditation standards make that deliberately hard. The holding institution's registrar disposes of an animal by supplying a `disposition_method` and an optional `disposition_note`. Five conditions must hold. The animal must be `HELD` (`ERR_ANIMAL_LOCATION_CONFLICT`). Its `genetic_designation` must be `SURPLUS_APPROVED`, which only an endorsed `SURPLUS_DISPOSITION` recommendation can establish, and any other designation is refused with `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_SURPLUS_APPROVED` (HTTP 422). The acting registrar must be the animal's holder (`ERR_ACTOR_SCOPE_VIOLATION`). The holding institution must have `disposition_policy_on_file` set to true, else `ERR_DISPOSITION_POLICY_MISSING`. And no transfer agreement naming that animal may still be in `DRAFT`, `OFFERED`, `COUNTERED`, `ACCEPTED` or `EXECUTED`, else `ERR_ANIMAL_HAS_ACTIVE_AGREEMENT`. On success the animal's `location_status` becomes `DISPOSED`, which is terminal; the programme's `current_population_count` and `open_recommendation_count` are recomputed as the animal drops out of the managed population, the former holder's `occupied_slots` is recomputed downward, and the animal's `version` increments by 1. A `DISPOSED` animal still appears in other animals' pedigrees, because ancestry is history rather than inventory.

## 4. Endpoint Definitions

Every endpoint below is mounted at the root of the API, so the path shown is the complete path. Creation endpoints — those that bring a new resource into existence — return HTTP 201 on success; every other successful call, including all reads, all lists, all state transitions and the logical-clock setter, returns HTTP 200. Recording a crate-training session, recording a pre-shipment test and recording a behavioural observation are creations and return 201 even though the crate-training session and the observation are not independently addressable resources. Every successful response carries the standard envelope of §2.2, with the affected resource in `data`; for the state-transition endpoints that resource is the parent whose state changed.
