# Contractor Licensing Board API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A state contractors licensing board sits at an uncomfortable intersection. On one side stand consumers who paid a contractor and got a half-finished deck, a mis-wired panel, or nothing at all. On the other stand tens of thousands of licensed contracting entities whose livelihoods depend on the board applying its own statutes evenly, predictably, and on the record. Between them sit five distinct board offices — the registrar, the licensing desk, enforcement field staff, the administrative-law bench, and the recovery-fund office — each of which the legislature has deliberately kept out of the others' decisions. The system described here is the case-management backbone that lets those offices work the same files without ever quietly doing each other's jobs.

This API governs the full arc of a contractor's regulatory life. It begins with classification by trade and the examination, journey-level experience and financial-responsibility gates that precede licensure. It continues through bond and insurance verification with continuous coverage monitoring, designation of the qualifying individual of record, and the entity-relationship changes that follow when that person leaves. When a consumer complains, the API screens for jurisdiction, attempts mediation, escalates to investigation, issues citations with a graded civil penalty, calendars disciplinary hearings before an officer who did not build the case, and grades the resulting sanction against a points ladder. Where a consumer has been harmed and the contractor will not make it right, the API adjudicates a claim against the consumer recovery fund, disburses it pro rata when the appropriation cannot cover every approved claim, suspends the licence until the money is repaid, and lifts that suspension the moment repayment is complete. Renewal, continuing education and reinstatement after suspension close the loop.

Two design commitments shape almost every rule that follows. First, **every date and instant the system stamps comes from a settable logical clock, never from wall-clock time** — statutes of limitation, appeal windows, judgment ripening periods, coverage intervals and grace periods are all reasoned about deterministically. Second, **a licence carries two independent state dimensions at once**: what the board has done to it (`status`) and whether it currently satisfies every continuing obligation (`compliance_posture`). Confusing the two is the single most common way an implementation of this specification goes wrong, and much of §2 and §3 exists to keep them apart.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

Every business resource in this system is mounted under the `/licensing` prefix. This section fixes the contracts that hold across all of them: authentication, envelope shape, pagination, identity and timestamp formats, arithmetic discipline, cascade semantics, concurrency tokens, and the order in which validations fire.

### 2.1. Board Actors and Role-Based Access Control

Every request other than the logical-clock control endpoint must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, naming the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, declaring the capacity in which that principal is acting. If either header is absent or present-but-empty, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401) before any other consideration. If both headers are present but the declared role is not among the roles the called endpoint admits, the request is rejected with `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403).

Seven roles exist, spelled exactly as follows.

- `BoardAdmin` — the board registrar. Owns reference data: trade classifications and fiscal-year fund appropriations are created by this role alone. Registers parties, calendars hearings alongside the bench, runs the compliance sweep, and drives the logical clock. Never adjudicates anything.
- `LicensingAnalyst` — the licensing desk. Decides applications, verifies and cancels coverage instruments, designates and withdraws qualifiers, reinstates suspended licences, screens complaints, records mediation outcomes, and defaults unappealed citations to final. Does not investigate, does not preside, does not touch the fund.
- `Investigator` — enforcement field staff. Opens complaint-driven and sting cases, closes them with a severity finding, issues citations, and may remand a complaint for a second mediation attempt. Barred from presiding over a hearing on a case it built.
- `HearingOfficer` — administrative-law officer. Presides over appealed citations and renders the disposition that grades the sanction ladder.
- `FundAdjudicator` — the recovery-fund office. Adjudicates and disburses consumer claims against the pool. Deliberately separate from both the licensing desk and enforcement, so that the office grading the violation is not the office spending the fund.
- `Licensee` — the contracting entity itself. Drives its own applications, banks continuing education, renews, repays recovery-fund money, appeals citations and complies with them. May never decide, screen, verify, investigate, preside or adjudicate.
- `Consumer` — the complaining consumer. Files and withdraws complaints and files recovery-fund claims. Cannot screen, mediate, investigate or adjudicate.

The five board-side roles — `BoardAdmin`, `LicensingAnalyst`, `Investigator`, `HearingOfficer` and `FundAdjudicator` — carry **opaque** identities. When `X-User-Role` is any one of those five, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server may record it (for example as `assigned_investigator_user_id` on an `Investigation` when an `Investigator` opens a case, or as the `presiding_officer_user_id` a hearing is calendared against) but it never compares such a value to any entity's `id`.

The two outside-party roles are entity-backed. When `X-User-Role` is `Licensee`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Licensee` row; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Licensee` compare the resource's foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. When `X-User-Role` is `Consumer`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Consumer` row; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Consumer` compare the resource's foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. Concretely: a `Licensee` actor may act only on licence applications, licences and citations that trace back to its own `Licensee` row, and a `Consumer` actor may act only on complaints whose `consumer_id` equals its own `Consumer` row. Any breach of either rule is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403). Ownership is checked on application submission, cure and withdrawal; on licence renewal, continuing-education banking and recovery repayment; on citation appeal and compliance; and on complaint withdrawal.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

List endpoints are paginated with two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of rows returned; it defaults to 10 when omitted, may not be smaller than 1, and may not exceed 100. `offset` is the number of rows to skip and defaults to 0; it may not be negative. A `limit` outside the one-to-one-hundred band, a negative `offset`, or a non-integer value for either is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

Unless a specific endpoint says otherwise, list results are ordered by `created_at` ascending — oldest first. This ordering is not cosmetic: the recovery-fund pro-rata distribution and the absorption order of repayment credits both depend on it, as §3.9 explains.

Every list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside the `meta` object of the envelope.

### 2.4. Common Resource Properties and the Logical Clock

Almost every resource in this system — trade classifications, fund accounts, licensees, qualifying individuals, licence applications, licences, coverage records, qualifier designations, consumers, complaints, investigations, citations, disciplinary hearings and recovery-fund claims — carries the same four system-managed properties:

- `id` (string): a server-minted UUID. It carries no type prefix of any kind.
- `created_at` (string): an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, e.g. `2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z`, recording when the row was first written.
- `updated_at` (string): an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp in the same format, refreshed on every mutation of the row — including mutations that only touch computed fields such as a licence's `compliance_posture` or a licensee's `unpaid_penalty_cents`.
- `version` (integer): a counter that increments by one on every mutation of the row. It is the token the optimistic-concurrency rules of §2.9 are stated against.

The examination record is the single exception. An `ExamResult` is an immutable event-log row hanging off a qualifying individual, and it carries **only** `id` and `created_at`. It has no `updated_at` and no `version`, and nothing in the system ever rewrites one; a mis-recorded sitting is corrected by recording another sitting, not by editing the first.

Every timestamp and every date the server stamps is read from a **logical clock**, never from the host's wall clock. Before any client sets it, that clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`. An administrative test-driving capability moves it to a supplied `current_utc` instant and mutates no entity; every subsequent read of the clock returns that instant. Creation timestamps, licence issuance dates, application submission timestamps, hearing decision timestamps, disbursement dates and the date stamped by a compliance sweep all derive from it. Where this document speaks of "the clock date" it means the calendar date, in UTC, of the current logical-clock instant; where it speaks of "the clock timestamp" it means the full instant.

Two date shapes appear in payloads and are validated separately. Plain calendar dates — `taken_on`, `expires_on`, `issued_on`, `effective_date`, `expiration_date`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, `contract_date`, `work_completed_on`, `correction_due_date`, and their siblings — are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings. Instants — `scheduled_start_utc`, `scheduled_end_utc`, `submitted_at`, `decided_at` — are RFC 3339 UTC datetimes. Anything that fails to parse in its declared shape is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

### 2.5. Money, Percentages and Arithmetic Discipline

All monetary amounts in this system are **integer cents**, and every field naming an amount ends in `_cents`. They must be transmitted as JSON integers, never as decimal strings; a money field arriving as `"45000"` rather than `45000` is a schema violation and draws `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, as does a negative amount. There are no floating-point money values anywhere in the API surface.

Rates and multipliers, by contrast, are fractional and live only inside calculations — they are never stored or returned. Where a fractional rate is applied to a cent amount, the product is rounded **half up to whole cents**. Two rules govern where that rounding happens, and they differ between calculations by design:

- The renewal fee is assembled in three ordered stages and is rounded half up to whole cents **independently at the end of each stage** that introduces a fraction. It is never computed as one long fractional expression rounded once at the end. §3.6 states the stages.
- The citation civil penalty multiplies every one of its factors together first, rounds the fully multiplied product half up to whole cents **exactly once**, and only then clamps it to the statutory ceiling. §3.8 states the factors.

Where a calculation floors rather than rounds — the education-credit ceiling in the experience calculation, and the per-claim share in the recovery-fund pro-rata distribution — this document says "rounded down to a whole number" explicitly. Two percentage-shaped inputs exist and both are plain numbers, not cents: `ownership_pct` on a qualifier designation, which must fall between 0 and 100 inclusive, and `ce_hours` / `ce_hours_completed`, which count continuing-education hours and may carry a fraction. `hazard_tier` must be exactly 1, 2 or 3, and an examination `score` must fall between 0 and 100 inclusive; violations of any of these draw `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

### 2.6. The Two Dimensions of a Licence

A `License` row runs two state machines at once, and neither drives the other.

The **licensure dimension** is `status`, one of `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, `EXPIRED` or `REVOKED`. It records what the board has done to the licence. A licence is minted `ACTIVE`; `REVOKED` is terminal and a revoked licence accepts no action whatsoever. When a licence sits in `SUSPENDED`, `suspension_reason` names why — `RECOVERY_FUND_PAYOUT`, `BOND_LAPSE` or `DISCIPLINARY` — and `suspension_until` gives the ISO date before which reinstatement is barred. A suspension conditioned on repayment or on restoring coverage is indefinite and leaves `suspension_until` null; only a disciplinary suspension carries a date. When a licence is not suspended, both fields are null.

The **compliance dimension** is `compliance_posture`, one of `COMPLIANT`, `CONDITIONAL` or `NON_COMPLIANT`. It records whether the licence currently satisfies its continuing obligations, and it is fully derived — never supplied by a client, never set directly by any request. It is recomputed and restamped on **every** write that can move one of its inputs: coverage verification, coverage rejection, coverage cancellation and coverage lapse; qualifier designation and qualifier withdrawal; citation finalization, hearing decision and citation compliance payment; recovery-fund disbursement and recovery repayment; licence issuance; renewal; reinstatement; revocation; and the ops-run compliance sweep.

The recomputation reads five things: the licence's coverage records (their `coverage_type`, `status`, `effective_date` and `expiration_date`), the licence's own `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents`, `qualifying_individual_id` and `status`, the citations standing against the licence (their `status`, `civil_penalty_cents` and `penalty_paid_cents`), the `sanction_tier` of the most recent decided disciplinary hearing on a citation of the licence, and the clock date. From those it derives three intermediate facts. **Live bond** means at least one coverage record on the licence has `coverage_type` of `BOND`, `status` of `VERIFIED`, and a coverage period that contains the clock date — that is, its `effective_date` is on or before the clock date and its `expiration_date` is strictly after it. **Live general liability** is the same test with `coverage_type` of `GENERAL_LIABILITY`. **Unpaid penalty exposure** is the sum, over every citation on the licence whose `status` is `FINAL`, `AFFIRMED` or `MODIFIED`, of that citation's `civil_penalty_cents` less its `penalty_paid_cents`. **Probation** holds when the most recently decided hearing on a citation of this licence produced a `sanction_tier` of `PROBATION` and the licence's `status` is `ACTIVE`.

The posture is then selected by a strictly ordered three-way test. The licence is `NON_COMPLIANT` if **any** of the following is true: it has no live bond; its `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents` is greater than zero; its `qualifying_individual_id` is null; or its `status` is `REVOKED`. Otherwise, the licence is `CONDITIONAL` if **any** of the following is true: its unpaid penalty exposure is greater than zero; it has no live general liability; or probation holds. Otherwise the licence is `COMPLIANT`. Because the `NON_COMPLIANT` clause is evaluated first, a `CONDITIONAL` trigger can never soften a licence that independently qualifies as `NON_COMPLIANT`. Worth internalising: a freshly minted licence has no verified bond yet and is therefore `NON_COMPLIANT` from birth, even though its `status` is `ACTIVE`; and withdrawing a licence's only qualifier drops it straight to `NON_COMPLIANT` without touching `status` at all.

### 2.7. Coverage Periods, Designation Windows and Boundary Inclusivity

Date boundaries in this domain are not uniform, and getting them wrong is the difference between a suspended contractor and a compliant one. Three conventions apply and must be kept distinct.

A **coverage period** on a coverage record is half-open: `[effective_date, expiration_date)`. The coverage exists on `effective_date` and on every date after it up to but **not including** `expiration_date`. A bond whose `expiration_date` is `2026-02-01` does not cover `2026-02-01`. Two consequences follow. First, a record beginning exactly on another's `expiration_date` does not overlap it, so such a pair may both be `VERIFIED`. Second, two half-open periods that meet at a boundary are *adjacent* and leave no uncovered day between them.

A **designation window** on a qualifier assignment is closed at both ends: `[effective_from, effective_to]`. The designation occupies the qualifier on `effective_from` and on `effective_to` itself. A null `effective_to` means an open-ended designation that occupies every date on or after `effective_from`.

A **hearing block** is half-open: `[scheduled_start_utc, scheduled_end_utc)`. A hearing beginning at the exact instant another ends does not conflict with it. The block must be non-empty: `scheduled_end_utc` strictly after `scheduled_start_utc`, or the request draws `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

Beyond these, schema validation itself enforces a handful of ordering rules on submitted dates: `expiration_date` must be strictly after `effective_date`; `effective_to`, when supplied, must be on or after `effective_from`; and `scheduled_end_utc` must be strictly after `scheduled_start_utc`. Each failure surfaces as `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

### 2.8. Cascade Discipline and Hierarchical Consistency

Many single requests in this system move more than one row. When they do, the cascade is not optional, not asynchronous, and not partial: every downstream effect lands inside the same request, and every row it touches has its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` incremented, exactly as if it had been written directly. Callers observing a resource after such a request see the fully settled world.

The dominant cascade chain runs licence → qualifier designations → qualifying individuals. Whenever a `License` enters `SUSPENDED` from `ACTIVE`, every qualifier designation on that licence whose `status` is `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE`, and because each of those designations has left `ACTIVE`, the `active_qualification_count` of its qualifying individual falls by one. When a licence returns from `SUSPENDED` to `ACTIVE` — whether by completing recovery repayment or by analyst reinstatement — every designation on it sitting in `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE` returns to `ACTIVE` provided its window has not closed (that is, its `effective_to` is null or is on or after the clock date), and each such return raises that qualifier's `active_qualification_count` by one. When a licence enters `REVOKED`, every designation on it in either `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE` moves to `TERMINATED_BY_REVOCATION` with its `effective_to` rewritten to the clock date, and `License.qualifying_individual_id` is cleared to null; a designation coming from `ACTIVE` releases a qualification slot (its person's `active_qualification_count` falls by one), while a designation coming from `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE` does not, because that slot was already released when the licence was suspended. Both terminal designation states, `WITHDRAWN` and `TERMINATED_BY_REVOCATION`, are final.

Licence expiry is the deliberate exception to all of this. When a compliance sweep expires an `ACTIVE` licence whose term has run, the licence's `compliance_posture` is recomputed but its qualifier designations are left completely untouched — the qualifier of record survives a lapsed term, because a lapse is a paperwork failure rather than a disqualification.

### 2.9. Presenting a Version: Optimistic Concurrency

Three write operations require the client to present the `version` of a *parent* row it last observed. These are the only three; every other endpoint in the system takes no version field whatsoever, and supplying one elsewhere is meaningless.

**Designating a qualifier.** A `POST /licensing/qualifier-assignments` request must carry `expected_license_version`, holding the client's last observed `version` of the target `License`. If it does not equal that licence's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_LICENSE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). This guard exists because a new designation legitimately supersedes the licence's prior designation of the same `relationship_type`, so no state precondition could ever stop the write; the stale-version check is the only thing preventing a writer from silently superseding a designation it never saw.

**Crediting a recovery repayment.** A `POST /licensing/licenses/{id}/recovery-repayments` request must carry `expected_license_version`, holding the client's last observed `version` of that `License`. A value that does not equal the licence's current `version` is rejected with `ERR_STALE_LICENSE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Partial repayments are legitimately repeatable, so a credit computed against a stale view of `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents` would silently over- or under-pay the fund.

**Disbursing an approved claim.** A `POST /licensing/recovery-fund-claims/{id}/pay` request must carry `expected_fund_account_version`, holding the client's last observed `version` of the `RecoveryFundAccount` the claim is charged to. A value that does not equal that account's current `version` is rejected with `ERR_STALE_FUND_ACCOUNT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Approval encumbers a licence's aggregate room but moves no cash, so a disbursement against any other claim changes the very `balance_cents` this payment's pro-rata factor is computed from — and the payment is otherwise perfectly legal, meaning no state precondition would catch it.

In all three cases the version field is a **required** input, not an optional hint. Both `POST /licensing/qualifier-assignments` and `POST /licensing/licenses/{id}/recovery-repayments` increment the target `License.version` by one when they succeed, and `POST /licensing/recovery-fund-claims/{id}/pay` increments the `RecoveryFundAccount.version` by one, so a client performing a run of writes must re-read the parent between each.

### 2.10. Segregation of Duties

The legislature separated the board's offices, and the API enforces that separation structurally rather than by convention. Three named checks implement it.

The investigator who built a case may not preside over its hearing: when a hearing is calendared, the supplied `presiding_officer_user_id` must differ from the `assigned_investigator_user_id` recorded on the `Investigation` behind the citation, or the request is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION` (HTTP 403). Only the officer the hearing was calendared against may render its decision: an actor whose `X-User-ID` differs from the hearing's `presiding_officer_user_id` is refused with `ERR_NOT_PRESIDING_OFFICER` (HTTP 403). And only the investigator who opened a case may close it or issue its citation: an actor whose `X-User-ID` differs from the case's `assigned_investigator_user_id` is refused with `ERR_NOT_ASSIGNED_INVESTIGATOR` (HTTP 403). The fund office is a fourth wall: no `LicensingAnalyst`, `Investigator` or `HearingOfficer` may adjudicate or disburse a claim, and no `FundAdjudicator` may decide an application, verify coverage, close a case or preside over a hearing.

### 2.11. Validation Ordering and Path Resolution

Validations fire in a fixed order, and the **first** rule whose condition is violated determines the response — later checks never mask earlier ones and are never reported. The bands, in ascending order of evaluation, are: request plumbing (authentication, role, schema, existence, ownership, pagination), then optimistic concurrency, then state, then business safety, then business referential integrity, then business temporal windows, then business constraints, and finally business fiscal rules. Within request plumbing the sequence is authentication, then role, then request-body schema, then existence of the row named by the `{id}` path parameter, then existence of every foreign key carried in the body, then ownership, then pagination arguments.

Two consequences of the ordering deserve emphasis, because they are easy to get backwards. First, the concurrency band sits *above* the state band: a request carrying a stale `expected_license_version` against a licence that is also in the wrong state reports the stale-version error, not the state error. Second, the safety band sits above the referential, temporal, constraint and fiscal bands, so — for example — a hearing decision rendered by the wrong officer reports `ERR_NOT_PRESIDING_OFFICER` even if the hearing has also not yet convened.

Existence checks resolve **left to right**. When a path names a parent and then a child — a qualifying individual and then its examination records, for instance — the parent is resolved first, and a missing parent produces the parent's own not-found error before the child is ever looked for. The same precedence applies to foreign keys in a request body: the entity a missing identifier names determines which not-found error is returned. `ERR_TRADE_CLASSIFICATION_NOT_FOUND` covers an unresolvable `classification_id`; `ERR_RECOVERY_FUND_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND` an unresolvable `fund_account_id`; `ERR_LICENSEE_NOT_FOUND` an unresolvable `licensee_id`; `ERR_QUALIFYING_INDIVIDUAL_NOT_FOUND` an unresolvable `qualifying_individual_id`; `ERR_CONSUMER_NOT_FOUND` an unresolvable `consumer_id`; `ERR_LICENSE_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND` a missing application; `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_FOUND` an unresolvable licence identifier in any position, including `license_id`, `subject_license_id` and `predecessor_license_id`; `ERR_COVERAGE_RECORD_NOT_FOUND` a missing coverage record; `ERR_QUALIFIER_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_FOUND` a missing designation; `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_FOUND` an unresolvable complaint identifier including `complaint_id`; `ERR_INVESTIGATION_NOT_FOUND` an unresolvable investigation identifier including `investigation_id`; `ERR_CITATION_NOT_FOUND` an unresolvable citation identifier including `citation_id`; `ERR_DISCIPLINARY_HEARING_NOT_FOUND` a missing hearing; and `ERR_RECOVERY_FUND_CLAIM_NOT_FOUND` a missing claim. All fourteen carry HTTP 404.

Schema validation, which fires before any of them, rejects a missing required field, a value of the wrong JSON type (including money sent as a string), an unrecognised enum member, an unparseable date or datetime, and each of the ordering and range rules named in §2.5 and §2.7. Every one of those failures reports `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` with HTTP 400.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Statutory Reference Data: Trades and the Fund Appropriation

Nothing in this system can be licensed until the board has described the trade being licensed. A `BoardAdmin` establishes each **trade classification** by supplying its statutory `code` — a unique identifier of between two and eight characters, such as `C-10` — together with the trade `name` that consumers recognise, such as Electrical. The classification is where every trade-specific threshold lives, and it is read on nearly every subsequent operation. Its `hazard_tier` is the public-hazard grading, restricted to 1, 2 or 3, and it does triple duty: it sizes the bond, it grades the civil penalty, and it colours the sanction. Its `min_experience_months` is the journey-level experience an offered qualifier must be able to show; its `min_net_worth_cents` is the financial-responsibility floor an applicant must declare; its `required_bond_amount_cents` is the minimum face amount the contractor bond must carry; its `required_ce_hours` is the continuing-education obligation per renewal term; and its `renewal_base_fee_cents` is the base renewal fee before any surcharge or fund assessment is layered on.

One further classification-level switch shapes complaint handling. The boolean `mediation_required` defaults to `true`, and when it is true an accepted complaint against a live licence in that trade is routed to a mediation attempt before enforcement ever sees it. Set it to `false` and complaints in that trade go straight to investigation. This flag is read from the *subject licence's* classification, not from anything the screening analyst types.

The consumer recovery fund is appropriated year by year. A `BoardAdmin` opens a **fund account** for a `fiscal_year` — unique across accounts — with an `initial_balance_cents` naming the appropriated pool size. That figure is immutable after creation; nothing in the system ever rewrites it. Two statutory ceilings are fixed at the same moment: `per_claim_cap_cents`, the maximum award any single claim may receive, and `per_license_aggregate_cap_cents`, the maximum total that may ever be charged against one licence over its lifetime. The live `balance_cents` is fully derived and is initialised to the appropriated amount: it is the `initial_balance_cents`, less the sum of `paid_amount_cents` over every claim charged to this account whose `status` is `PAID` or `REIMBURSED`, plus the sum of `repaid_amount_cents` over every claim charged to this account. Money that goes out lowers it; money a licensee reimburses raises it back.

### 3.2. Registering the Parties and Proving Examination Currency

Three kinds of party appear in board files, and each is registered before it can act. A **contracting entity** is registered with its `legal_name`, its `entity_type` — one of `SOLE_PROPRIETOR`, `PARTNERSHIP`, `CORPORATION` or `LLC` — and its board-of-record `contact_email`. The identifier the board returns for that row is precisely the value the entity later sends as `X-User-ID`. Two derived obligation totals hang off it, both initialised to zero and both restated whenever their inputs move. `unpaid_penalty_cents` is the sum, over every citation whose licence belongs to this entity and whose `status` is `FINAL`, `AFFIRMED` or `MODIFIED`, of that citation's `civil_penalty_cents` less its `penalty_paid_cents`. `outstanding_recovery_payout_cents` is the sum of `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents` over every licence held by this entity. Together they let a registrar see, in one read, what a contractor still owes the board and the fund.

A **qualifying individual** is the natural person who stands behind a licence. Registration takes a `full_name` and the `verified_experience_months` the board has confirmed at journey level. A derived `active_qualification_count`, initialised to zero, tracks how many designations that person currently holds in `ACTIVE` status; it is the number the concurrent-qualification cap is measured against, and §2.8 describes the cascade rules that move it.

Examination sittings are recorded against a qualifying individual as immutable event-log rows. Each sitting names its `exam_type` — either `TRADE` or `LAW_AND_BUSINESS` — and carries the raw scaled `score` between 0 and 100 inclusive plus the `taken_on` date the exam was sat. A `TRADE` sitting must also carry the `trade_code` naming the classification code it covers; a `LAW_AND_BUSINESS` sitting must omit `trade_code` entirely, since the law-and-business examination is trade-agnostic. Each row also records the `qualifying_individual_id` of the person who sat it, taken from the request path. Two values are derived at write time and never revised. `passed` is true when the `score` is at least 70; a score of exactly 70 passes. `expires_on` is the `taken_on` date advanced by forty-eight months — four years — and a passed examination stops satisfying any eligibility requirement once the clock date is strictly past that `expires_on`. On the expiry date itself the examination is still current. A board actor may filter a person's examination history by `exam_type` when reviewing it.

A **consumer** is registered with a `full_name`, a `contact_email` and a two-letter `mailing_state` code. As with contracting entities, the identifier returned for the consumer row is the value that consumer sends as `X-User-ID`.

### 3.3. From Application to Licence

A licence application opens in `DRAFT`. It is filed either by the contracting entity itself or by a `LicensingAnalyst` on its behalf, and it names the `licensee_id` applying, the `classification_id` being applied for, the `qualifying_individual_id` offered as qualifier, the `application_type`, and the `declared_net_worth_cents` offered against the financial-responsibility test. The `application_type` is one of `ORIGINAL` (a first licence in a trade), `ADDITIONAL_CLASSIFICATION` (a further trade for an already-licensed entity), or `REINSTATEMENT` (replacing a licence the board took away or let lapse). An optional `education_credential` names the credential offered as partial experience substitution; it is one of `NONE`, `APPRENTICESHIP`, `TWO_YEAR_DEGREE` or `FOUR_YEAR_DEGREE`, and it defaults to `NONE` when omitted. An optional `predecessor_license_id` names the licence being replaced. On creation the file lands in `DRAFT` with `deficiency_round_count` at zero and `credited_experience_months` left null until the first submission computes it.

Two gates apply at filing. A contracting entity may not hold two applications for the same `classification_id` in a non-terminal status — that is, while another application of theirs for that trade sits in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `DEFICIENT` — and a second such filing is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_APPLICATION`. And the `predecessor_license_id` field is tightly coupled to `application_type`: a `REINSTATEMENT` filing must supply it, and the licence it names must belong to the same `licensee_id`, carry the same `classification_id`, and sit in either `REVOKED` or `EXPIRED` status; every other `application_type` must omit it altogether. A predecessor that is `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`, one belonging to a different entity or a different trade, a `REINSTATEMENT` with no predecessor named, or a non-reinstatement that supplies one, all draw `ERR_PREDECESSOR_LICENSE_INVALID`. This same predecessor test is re-run at submission time.

**Submitting** moves the application `DRAFT → SUBMITTED` and is reserved to the contracting entity that owns it. It runs the full eligibility battery, and the checks fire in the order §2.11 fixes: the application's own state first, then the examination requirement, then the predecessor coupling, then the experience test, then the financial-responsibility test.

The examination requirement is two-pronged and both prongs must hold. The offered qualifier must have a `LAW_AND_BUSINESS` sitting that `passed` and whose `expires_on` is on or after the clock date, **and** a `TRADE` sitting that `passed`, whose `trade_code` equals the applied-for classification's `code`, and whose `expires_on` is on or after the clock date. A missing sitting, a failed sitting, a trade sitting for the wrong code, or a sitting whose validity has run out all draw `ERR_EXAM_REQUIREMENT_UNMET`. That same requirement is re-checked when the analyst approves, because forty-eight months can elapse between filing and decision.

The experience test compares **credited experience** against the classification's `min_experience_months`. Credited experience is derived in four steps and stamped onto `credited_experience_months`. Begin with the qualifier's `verified_experience_months`, clipped so it never exceeds the statutory experience ceiling of ninety-six months — eight years — and call that the base. Next, look up the raw credit the offered `education_credential` is worth: an `APPRENTICESHIP` is worth twelve months, a `TWO_YEAR_DEGREE` eighteen months, a `FOUR_YEAR_DEGREE` thirty-six months, and `NONE` is worth nothing. Then compute the ceiling on educational substitution, which is half of the classification's `min_experience_months`, rounded down to a whole number of months; a credential may never substitute for more than half the statutory requirement no matter how impressive it is. The allowed educational credit is the smaller of the raw credit and that ceiling. Finally, credited experience is the base plus the allowed educational credit, again clipped to the ninety-six-month ceiling. The applicant is eligible when credited experience is greater than or equal to `min_experience_months`, so exact equality passes; falling short draws `ERR_EXPERIENCE_INSUFFICIENT`. Worth noting the shape of this: a classification demanding forty-eight months caps educational substitution at twenty-four, so a four-year degree worth thirty-six months is clipped to twenty-four, and an applicant with twenty-four verified months exactly reaches forty-eight and passes while one with twenty-three reaches forty-seven and does not.

The financial-responsibility test is direct: `declared_net_worth_cents` must be at least the classification's `min_net_worth_cents`, or the submission is refused with `ERR_NET_WORTH_INSUFFICIENT`. A `REINSTATEMENT` submission additionally re-confirms that its `predecessor_license_id` names a licence sitting in `REVOKED` or `EXPIRED`.

On a successful submission the system recomputes `credited_experience_months` from the current inputs, stamps `submitted_at` with the logical-clock timestamp, and clears any lingering `deficiency_reason` back to null.

**The analyst's decision** is recorded against a `SUBMITTED` application by supplying an `outcome`, optionally accompanied by a `deficiency_reason` narrative or a `denial_reason` narrative. Three outcomes exist and they behave quite differently.

An outcome of `DEFICIENT` opens a cure round: the application moves `SUBMITTED → DEFICIENT`, `deficiency_round_count` rises by one, and the supplied `deficiency_reason` is stored as the last deficiency narrative. This is available only while `deficiency_round_count` is 0 or 1 — that is, while fewer than two findings have already issued. **The third deficiency finding does not produce another cure round.** When a deficiency outcome arrives on an application whose `deficiency_round_count` already stands at 2, the very same request body instead drives the application `SUBMITTED → DENIED`: `deficiency_round_count` is set to 3, the supplied `deficiency_reason` is still stored, and `denial_reason` is stamped automatically as `EXCESSIVE_DEFICIENCY_ROUNDS`. Three strikes is a statutory ceiling, not an analyst's discretion.

An outcome of `DENIED` drives `SUBMITTED → DENIED` directly and stores the analyst's supplied `denial_reason`. It has no cross-entity effect.

An outcome of `APPROVED` drives `SUBMITTED → APPROVED` and is the most consequential write in the system, because it mints a licence through a four-hop cascade. Before any of it runs, two preconditions are re-verified: both required examination sittings must still satisfy their validity at decision time, and the offered qualifier must currently occupy fewer than two concurrent designations covering the clock date. That second check counts the designations with `status` of `ACTIVE` whose window contains the clock date, and a qualifier already at the two-designation ceiling causes `ERR_QUALIFIER_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`.

The cascade then runs as follows. **First**, a new licence row is created in `ACTIVE` status, with `licensee_id`, `classification_id` and `predecessor_license_id` copied from the application, `application_id` pointing back at the application that minted it, `issued_on` set to the clock date, `expires_on` set to `issued_on` advanced by twenty-four months — a two-year term — a freshly minted unique `license_number`, and `ce_hours_completed` at zero. **Second**, *because* a licence now exists with no qualifier of record, a designation is created for it: it names the new licence and the application's `qualifying_individual_id`, takes a `relationship_type` of `RESPONSIBLE_MANAGING_EMPLOYEE`, an `ownership_pct` of 0, an `effective_from` equal to the licence's `issued_on`, a null `effective_to` (open-ended), and `ACTIVE` status — and the licence's `qualifying_individual_id` is pointed at that person. **Third**, *because* that designation entered `ACTIVE`, the qualifying individual's `active_qualification_count` rises by one. **Fourth**, *because* the new licence has no verified bond covering its `issued_on`, the posture recomputation of §2.6 stamps the licence's `compliance_posture` as `NON_COMPLIANT`. A brand-new licence is thus simultaneously `ACTIVE` and `NON_COMPLIANT`, and it will stay that way until a bond is verified against it.

**Curing** a `DEFICIENT` application returns it `DEFICIENT → SUBMITTED`, closing the cure cycle, and is again reserved to the owning contracting entity. The cure request may optionally replace any of three values: `declared_net_worth_cents`, `education_credential`, or the offered `qualifying_individual_id`. Whichever are supplied replace the stored values; whichever are omitted are left alone. Every eligibility precondition that guards a fresh submission is then re-evaluated against the cured field values — the examination requirement, the experience test and the financial-responsibility test alike, with the same error codes — and on success `credited_experience_months` is recomputed and `submitted_at` is restamped from the clock.

**Withdrawal** is available to the owning contracting entity from `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `DEFICIENT`, moving the application to `WITHDRAWN` with no cross-entity effect. `APPROVED`, `DENIED` and `WITHDRAWN` are all terminal; an application in any of them accepts no further action. Attempting an action from the wrong state — submitting an already-`SUBMITTED` application, curing one that is not `DEFICIENT`, deciding a `WITHDRAWN` one, or withdrawing a decided one — draws `ERR_APPLICATION_STATE_INVALID`. Board staff may retrieve applications individually or list them filtered by `licensee_id`, `classification_id`, `qualifying_individual_id`, `predecessor_license_id` or `status`.

### 3.4. The Qualifier of Record: Designation, Capacity and Departure

Every licence must have a natural person standing behind it, and the board tracks that relationship as a **qualifier designation**. The approval cascade of §3.3 creates the first one automatically, but entity-relationship changes are a fact of contracting life — the responsible managing employee resigns, an owner steps into the role, a person is promoted — and a `LicensingAnalyst` records each such change by designating a new qualifier.

A designation names the `license_id` being qualified and the `qualifying_individual_id` being designated, and it declares the statutory `relationship_type`: either `RESPONSIBLE_MANAGING_OFFICER` or `RESPONSIBLE_MANAGING_EMPLOYEE`. It carries an `effective_from` date on which the designation begins and an optional `effective_to` on which it ends; omitting `effective_to` creates an open-ended designation. The optional `ownership_pct` records the percentage of the contracting entity the qualifier owns and defaults to 0. Because designating a qualifier legitimately supersedes an existing one, the request must also present `expected_license_version` as described in §2.9 — without it there would be nothing at all to stop a writer from displacing a designation it had never observed.

The gates on a new designation fire in this sequence. The presented licence version is checked first, as §2.11 requires of the whole concurrency band. Then the licence's own state: a `REVOKED` licence accepts no designation, and any licence-state violation reports `ERR_LICENSE_STATE_INVALID`. Then the **concurrent-qualification cap**: a single person may hold at most two live designations covering any given date, so the system counts the designations already held by the offered person whose `status` is `ACTIVE` and whose window — from `effective_from` through `effective_to` inclusive, or open-ended when `effective_to` is null — contains the new designation's `effective_from`. If that count has already reached two, the designation is refused with `ERR_QUALIFIER_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. Then the **ownership floor**: a `RESPONSIBLE_MANAGING_OFFICER` must own at least ten percent of the entity, so an `ownership_pct` below 10 with that relationship type draws `ERR_OWNERSHIP_PCT_INSUFFICIENT`; a `RESPONSIBLE_MANAGING_EMPLOYEE` carries no ownership requirement. Finally the **window containment**: `effective_from` must be on or after the licence's `issued_on`, and `effective_to`, when supplied, must be on or before the licence's `expires_on`; a window escaping either boundary draws `ERR_ASSIGNMENT_EFFECTIVE_DATE_INVALID`.

A successful designation does five things. It increments the licence's `version` by one. It creates the designation in `ACTIVE` status. It raises the designee's `active_qualification_count` by one. It points the licence's `qualifying_individual_id` at the designee. And — this is the supersession — any designation already sitting `ACTIVE` on that licence **with the same `relationship_type`** is moved to `WITHDRAWN`, its `withdrawn_on` and its `effective_to` both rewritten to the clock date, and the superseded person's `active_qualification_count` falls by one. A designation of a *different* relationship type is left standing; a licence may carry one officer and one employee simultaneously. The licence's `compliance_posture` is then recomputed.

**Withdrawing** a designation is the other half of the relationship-change story, and it is a `LicensingAnalyst` action that supplies the `withdrawn_on` date. Only a designation in `ACTIVE` may be withdrawn — one already `WITHDRAWN`, `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE` or `TERMINATED_BY_REVOCATION` draws `ERR_ASSIGNMENT_STATE_INVALID` — and the supplied `withdrawn_on` must be on or after the designation's own `effective_from`. The withdrawal stores `withdrawn_on`, rewrites `effective_to` to that same date, and lowers the person's `active_qualification_count` by one. If the licence's `qualifying_individual_id` named the withdrawn person, that field is cleared to null and the licence's `compliance_posture` is recomputed — which, per §2.6, lands it at `NON_COMPLIANT`, because a licence with no qualifier of record is by definition out of compliance. A licence in that condition cannot renew until a replacement is designated. Designations may be read individually or listed filtered by `license_id`, `qualifying_individual_id` or `status`.

### 3.5. Bond and Insurance: Verification, Cancellation and Continuous Monitoring

Financial protection for consumers rests on instruments the board verifies with the carrier rather than taking on trust. A coverage record is filed against a licence by either the contracting entity or a `LicensingAnalyst`, naming the `license_id` it protects, its `coverage_type` — `BOND`, `GENERAL_LIABILITY` or `WORKERS_COMP` — the `carrier_name` of the surety or insurer of record, the carrier's `policy_number`, the `amount_cents` giving the bond's face amount or the policy's per-occurrence limit, and the half-open period bounded by `effective_date` and `expiration_date`. The record lands in `PENDING_VERIFICATION`, and — importantly — **filing an instrument does not improve a licence's compliance posture**. Until an analyst confirms it with the carrier it counts for nothing. A revoked licence accepts no filing at all.

**Verification** is exclusively a `LicensingAnalyst` action and carries an `outcome` of either `VERIFY` or `REJECT`, with an optional `rejection_reason` narrative. Only a record in `PENDING_VERIFICATION` may be verified or rejected; anything else draws `ERR_COVERAGE_STATE_INVALID`. On the `VERIFY` path two substantive tests apply, in this order. First, **non-overlap**: no other coverage record on the same licence with the same `coverage_type` and `status` of `VERIFIED` may overlap the candidate's half-open `[effective_date, expiration_date)` period. Because the periods are half-open, a record starting exactly on another's `expiration_date` does *not* overlap and may be verified; a genuine overlap draws `ERR_COVERAGE_PERIOD_OVERLAP`. Second, and only for a `BOND`, the **amount floor**: `amount_cents` must be at least the `required_bond_amount_cents` of the licence's own trade classification, or the verification is refused with `ERR_BOND_AMOUNT_INSUFFICIENT`. A bond that clears both tests moves `PENDING_VERIFICATION → VERIFIED`, its `verified_on` is stamped with the clock date, and the licence's `compliance_posture` is recomputed — typically lifting a newly minted licence out of `NON_COMPLIANT`. A `REJECT` outcome moves the record `PENDING_VERIFICATION → REJECTED` and also triggers a posture recomputation, which normally leaves the posture where it was unless the rejected instrument was the licence's only candidate.

**Cancellation** records a mid-term carrier cancellation and is the sharpest edge in the coverage story. A `LicensingAnalyst` supplies a `cancellation_date`, which must be on or after the record's `effective_date`; only a record in `VERIFIED` may be cancelled. The consequences run in four hops. **First**, `cancellation_date` is stored and the record's `expiration_date` is rewritten to that same date, so — the period being half-open — the record no longer covers `cancellation_date` or any date after it, and the record's `status` becomes `CANCELLED`. **Second**, *because* the record's period has been truncated, the licence's `compliance_posture` is recomputed; if the cancelled instrument was the licence's only bond covering the clock date the posture becomes `NON_COMPLIANT`, and in that case the licence itself moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `BOND_LAPSE` and `suspension_until` left null, because the suspension lasts until coverage is restored rather than for a fixed period. **Third**, *because* the licence has entered `SUSPENDED`, every designation on it in `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE`. **Fourth**, *because* those designations have left `ACTIVE`, each affected qualifying individual's `active_qualification_count` falls by one. `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED` and `LAPSED` are all terminal for a coverage record.

Continuous monitoring is what makes coverage lapses visible without a client asking. An ops-run checkpoint, available to `BoardAdmin` and `LicensingAnalyst`, sweeps the whole estate and does three things in sequence. It moves every coverage record from `VERIFIED → LAPSED` whose `expiration_date` has arrived — that is, where the clock date is on or after `expiration_date`, since coverage does not exist on the expiration date itself. It moves every licence from `ACTIVE → EXPIRED` whose term has run, meaning the clock date is strictly past `expires_on`, recomputing that licence's `compliance_posture` while deliberately leaving its qualifier designations untouched. And it moves every licence from `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` of `BOND_LAPSE` that has been left with no verified bond covering the clock date, cascading into that licence's `ACTIVE` designations and each affected qualifier's `active_qualification_count` exactly as a cancellation does. The checkpoint reports what it did as `lapsed_coverage_count`, `expired_license_count`, `suspended_license_count` and the `swept_on` date it ran against. Every posture it touches is restamped.

The board also cares whether coverage was *continuous*, not merely present today, and that question is answered by a **bond continuity** measurement expressed in uncovered days. The measurement window opens on the licence's `last_renewed_on` date, or — before any renewal has happened — on its `issued_on` date, and closes on the clock date; both ends are inclusive. Every bond record on the licence whose `status` is `VERIFIED`, `LAPSED` or `CANCELLED` contributes its half-open `[effective_date, expiration_date)` period; a cancelled record contributes its *rewritten* expiration, that is, its `cancellation_date`. Records still in `PENDING_VERIFICATION` or in `REJECTED` contribute nothing, because coverage that was never confirmed never existed — but a record that lapsed *does* count, since coverage that existed and then ran out was real coverage while it lasted. Those contributed periods are then merged: two periods that overlap merge, and — because they are half-open — two periods that merely meet at a boundary are adjacent and also merge into one continuous period with no gap between them. The measurement is finally the count of calendar dates in the window for which no merged period covers the date. Whole days only; there are no partial days. To see the boundary behaviour concretely: a bond running to `2026-02-01` and a successor beginning on `2026-02-01` merge seamlessly, whereas a successor beginning on `2026-02-02` leaves `2026-02-01` uncovered and the measurement returns one day.

### 3.6. Continuing Education, Renewal and Reinstatement

A licence term runs twenty-four months, and staying licensed means banking continuing-education hours and renewing before the term's grace period closes.

**Banking hours** is available to the contracting entity and to a `LicensingAnalyst`, and each credit supplies the `ce_hours` earned, the `completed_on` date the coursework finished, and the `provider_name` that delivered it. Hours are added to the licence's `ce_hours_completed`, which counts hours banked toward the *current* term only. Two constraints apply: `ce_hours` must be strictly greater than zero, and the resulting cumulative `ce_hours_completed` must not exceed the forty-hour term ceiling. A non-positive credit or one that would push the running total past forty hours is refused with `ERR_CE_HOURS_CEILING_EXCEEDED`. Hours may be banked while the licence is `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` or `EXPIRED` — a suspended contractor working off a reinstatement condition needs to be able to do exactly this — but never against a `REVOKED` licence.

**Renewal** is driven by the contracting entity itself and is defined from `ACTIVE` and from `EXPIRED`; a `SUSPENDED` licence cannot renew, and any state violation reports `ERR_LICENSE_STATE_INVALID`. Four business gates then fire, in this order. First, bond continuity: the uncovered-day measurement of §3.5 must come to zero over the expiring term, and any gap at all refuses the renewal with `ERR_COVERAGE_CONTINUITY_GAP`. Second, present compliance: the licence's `compliance_posture` must not be `NON_COMPLIANT`, and a non-compliant licence draws `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_COMPLIANT` — a `CONDITIONAL` posture, by contrast, renews perfectly well. Third, the renewal window: the clock date must be no later than the licence's `expires_on` advanced by ninety days of delinquent grace, so the ninetieth day still renews and the ninety-first does not, drawing `ERR_RENEWAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`. Fourth, continuing education: `ce_hours_completed` must be at least the classification's `required_ce_hours` **less the four-hour grace band**, so a shortfall of up to four hours is survivable — billable, as the fee calculation below shows, but survivable — while a larger shortfall is fatal and draws `ERR_CE_HOURS_INSUFFICIENT`.

The renewal fee is assembled in three ordered stages, and **the order is normative** because the assessment tier is read off the stage-two subtotal rather than off the finished total. Stage one begins with the classification's `renewal_base_fee_cents` and adds a continuing-education shortfall charge of 50 dollars — 5,000 cents — for each whole hour of shortfall, where the shortfall is the classification's `required_ce_hours` less the licence's `ce_hours_completed`, treated as zero when the licensee is at or above requirement. Stage two adds a delinquency surcharge, but only when the renewal is delinquent: when the clock date is strictly past the licence's `expires_on`, half of the stage-one subtotal is added, rounded half up to whole cents; when the renewal is timely, nothing is added. Stage three applies the recovery-fund assessment, and the rate depends on how large the stage-two subtotal is: a stage-two subtotal of at least 1,000 dollars — 100,000 cents — is assessed at fifteen percent, and anything below that threshold at ten percent. The assessment is that rate applied to the stage-two subtotal, rounded half up to whole cents, and added to it. The result is the fee, returned as `renewal_fee_cents` and stored on the licence as `last_renewal_fee_cents`. The trap to avoid is choosing the assessment rate from the finished total or re-tiering after the assessment has been added; the rate is fixed by the stage-two subtotal and never revisited. To make the composition concrete: a base fee of 45,000 cents against an eight-hour requirement with four hours banked, renewed thirty-two days after expiry, gives a stage one of 45,000 plus four times 5,000, or 65,000; a stage two of 65,000 plus half of 65,000, or 97,500; a rate of ten percent because 97,500 falls below 100,000; and a fee of 97,500 plus 9,750, or 107,250. Renewed timely instead, the same licence pays 65,000 plus 6,500, or 71,500.

The new term's `expires_on` depends on whether the renewal was timely or delinquent, **and that branch is keyed on the clock relative to `expires_on`, never on the licence's `status`.** A licence whose term has run but which no compliance sweep has yet flipped to `EXPIRED` still renews on the delinquent branch. When the renewal is timely — the clock date on or before `expires_on` — the new `expires_on` is the *previous* `expires_on` advanced by twenty-four months, anchored on the old expiry rather than on the clock, and no delinquency surcharge is assessed. When the renewal is delinquent — the clock date strictly past `expires_on` — the new `expires_on` is the clock date advanced by twenty-four months and the surcharge is assessed. Either way, `last_renewed_on` is set to the clock date, `renewal_count` rises by one, and `ce_hours_completed` is reset to zero for the fresh term. A renewal from `EXPIRED` returns the licence to `ACTIVE`; a renewal from `ACTIVE` is a self-loop that leaves the licensure dimension where it was. Renewal never touches the compliance dimension directly, though the posture is recomputed afterwards like any other mutation.

**Reinstatement** is a `LicensingAnalyst` action defined only from `SUSPENDED`, and it is deliberately harder than renewal. Reinstating a licence that is not suspended draws `ERR_LICENSE_STATE_INVALID`. Four gates then fire in order. First, live bond coverage: a coverage record with `coverage_type` of `BOND`, `status` of `VERIFIED`, and a period containing the clock date must exist, or the request draws `ERR_BOND_COVERAGE_MISSING`. Second, outstanding obligations: every citation on the licence whose `status` is `FINAL`, `AFFIRMED` or `MODIFIED` must be fully paid — the sum of `civil_penalty_cents` less `penalty_paid_cents` across them must come to zero — **and** the licence's `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents` must be zero; any money still owed draws `ERR_OUTSTANDING_OBLIGATIONS`. Third, the suspension period: when `suspension_until` is non-null the clock date must be on or after it, and an early attempt draws `ERR_SUSPENSION_PERIOD_ACTIVE`; an indefinite suspension with a null `suspension_until` imposes no waiting period at all, because its condition is the money and the coverage rather than the calendar. Fourth, continuing education: **reinstatement demands the classification's full `required_ce_hours` with no grace band whatsoever** — the four-hour indulgence renewal offers does not exist here — and a shortfall draws `ERR_CE_HOURS_INSUFFICIENT`.

A successful reinstatement moves the licence `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`, clears both `suspension_reason` and `suspension_until` to null, resets `ce_hours_completed` to zero, returns every designation on the licence sitting in `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE` to `ACTIVE` — each such return raising that qualifier's `active_qualification_count` by one — and recomputes the licence's `compliance_posture`. Licences may be read individually, exposing both state dimensions, the recovery-fund ledger fields, the continuing-education bank, the term dates and the `version` that serves as the concurrency token; or listed filtered by `licensee_id`, `classification_id`, `application_id`, `qualifying_individual_id`, the self-referential `predecessor_license_id`, `status` or `compliance_posture`.

### 3.7. Complaint Intake, Jurisdiction Screening and Mediation

A complaint enters the board's world in `RECEIVED`, filed either by the aggrieved consumer or by a `LicensingAnalyst` taking the story over the counter. Intake names the `consumer_id` of the complaining party, the `alleged_loss_cents` claimed as out-of-pocket damage, the `contract_date` on which the consumer signed with the accused party, and a `description` narrating the allegation. The accused party is identified one of two ways: by supplying the `license_id` of the licence complained about, or — for an unlicensed-practice allegation, where there is no licence to name — by supplying the `subject_business_name` under which the accused party trades. `subject_business_name` is required precisely when `license_id` is omitted. An optional `work_completed_on` records the date work stopped or was abandoned; when it is omitted, the `contract_date` stands in for it wherever limitation arithmetic needs a reference date. On intake `mediation_round_count` is zero, `settlement_amount_cents` is zero, and `jurisdiction_basis` is null until the file is screened.

**Screening** is the analyst's gatekeeping act, recorded as a `jurisdiction_finding` of either `ACCEPTED` or `REJECTED` with an optional `rejection_reason`. It is defined only from `RECEIVED`; screening an already-screened complaint draws `ERR_COMPLAINT_STATE_INVALID`. A `REJECTED` finding requires a `rejection_reason` and drives the complaint `RECEIVED → DISMISSED_NO_JURISDICTION`, storing that narrative as `dismissal_reason` and leaving `jurisdiction_basis` null — the board never reached the merits, so it never characterised the basis.

An `ACCEPTED` finding must clear three jurisdictional gates, and they fire in this order. First the **statute of limitations**: the clock date must be no later than the reference date — the `work_completed_on` when it exists, otherwise the `contract_date` — advanced by forty-eight months, which is four years. Accepting later draws `ERR_STATUTE_OF_LIMITATIONS_EXPIRED`. Second, and only when the complaint names a licence, the **licensure precedence** test: the `contract_date` must be on or after that licence's `issued_on`, because conduct predating licensure is outside the board's jurisdiction over the licensee; a contract signed before the licence issued draws `ERR_CONTRACT_PREDATES_LICENSE`. Third the **jurisdictional minimum**: `alleged_loss_cents` must be at least 500 dollars — 50,000 cents — and a smaller pleaded loss draws `ERR_LOSS_BELOW_JURISDICTIONAL_MINIMUM`.

An accepted complaint then forks, and **the fork is decided entirely by the accused party's licensure situation, never by anything in the request body.** The two branches carry genuinely different side-effect sets, so both need stating fully.

The **mediation branch** is taken when the complaint names a `license_id`, that licence's `status` is `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` or `EXPIRED`, and the licence's trade classification has `mediation_required` set to `true`. The complaint moves `RECEIVED → MEDIATION`, its `jurisdiction_basis` is stamped `LICENSED_SUBJECT`, its `mediation_round_count` is set to 1, and its `mediation_deadline` is set to the clock date advanced by thirty days — the window in which the current mediation attempt must produce something.

The **direct-enforcement branch** is taken when the complaint names no licence at all, or the licence it names is `REVOKED`, or that licence's classification has `mediation_required` set to `false`. The complaint moves `RECEIVED → INVESTIGATION`. Its `jurisdiction_basis` is stamped `UNLICENSED_PRACTICE` when no `license_id` was supplied, and `LICENSED_SUBJECT` otherwise. Critically, `mediation_round_count` stays at zero and `mediation_deadline` stays null: **this branch never opens a mediation window**, which matters later because the remand rule counts rounds.

**Recording a mediation outcome** is available to a `LicensingAnalyst` on a complaint in `MEDIATION`; anything else draws `ERR_COMPLAINT_STATE_INVALID`. The `outcome` is either `SETTLED` or `FAILED`, with an optional `settlement_amount_cents`. A `SETTLED` outcome requires a `settlement_amount_cents` of at least one cent and moves the complaint `MEDIATION → CLOSED_RESOLVED`, storing that figure as the cash the consumer actually received. A `FAILED` outcome moves the complaint `MEDIATION → INVESTIGATION` and clears `mediation_deadline` to null; it stores `settlement_amount_cents` when the request supplies one, recording a *partial* settlement that failed to resolve the whole grievance, and leaves the stored figure unchanged when it does not. Either way that stored figure matters later: it offsets any recovery-fund award on the same complaint, as §3.9 explains.

**Remanding** a complaint back for a second mediation attempt is an `Investigator` action supplying a `remand_reason`, defined only from `INVESTIGATION`. Two conditions guard it, both reported as `ERR_MEDIATION_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`: the complaint's `mediation_round_count` must be strictly below two, and no citation may yet exist on any investigation of the complaint. Enforcement that has already reached the citation stage does not go back to the table. A remand raises `mediation_round_count` by one, sets `mediation_deadline` to the clock date advanced by thirty days, and leaves every investigation on the complaint that is still `OPEN` open — the case is parked, not closed. Because the direct-enforcement branch never spent a round, a complaint that arrived at investigation without mediating can be remanded twice; one that mediated first can be remanded once.

**Withdrawal** belongs to the consumer alone and is defined from `RECEIVED`, `MEDIATION` or `INVESTIGATION`, moving the complaint to `WITHDRAWN`. Withdrawing from `MEDIATION` additionally clears `mediation_deadline` to null. Withdrawing at any stage leaves any `OPEN` investigation on the complaint standing: **enforcement is not consumer-controlled**, and a consumer who settles privately or loses interest does not thereby extinguish the board's own case. Withdrawing an already-closed complaint draws `ERR_COMPLAINT_STATE_INVALID`. The five closing states — `CLOSED_RESOLVED`, `CLOSED_UNFOUNDED`, `CLOSED_SANCTIONED`, `DISMISSED_NO_JURISDICTION` and `WITHDRAWN` — are all terminal. Complaints may be read individually or listed filtered by `consumer_id`, `license_id`, `status` or `jurisdiction_basis`.

### 3.8. Enforcement: Investigation, Citation and the Sanction Ladder

**Opening a case.** An `Investigator` opens an enforcement case by declaring its `origin`, either `COMPLAINT` or `STING`. A complaint-driven case names the `complaint_id` it arises from and must not be opened unless that complaint sits in `INVESTIGATION`; a sting case — the board's own operation against an unlicensed operator — must not name a `complaint_id` at all. Either way the target may be identified by `subject_license_id` when it holds a licence, and `subject_business_name` is required whenever `subject_license_id` is omitted. An `origin` of `COMPLAINT` without a `complaint_id`, an `origin` of `STING` with one, or a nameless unlicensed target all draw `ERR_INVESTIGATION_ORIGIN_MISMATCH`. The case opens in `OPEN` with `opened_on` set to the clock date and `assigned_investigator_user_id` recorded from the opening actor's `X-User-ID`. That recorded identifier is the segregation-of-duties key: the same investigator, and only the same investigator, may close the case and issue its citation.

**Closing a case.** The assigned investigator supplies a `finding` of either `SUBSTANTIATED` or `UNSUBSTANTIATED` together with an `evidence_summary` closure narrative; a `SUBSTANTIATED` finding must also supply a `finding_severity` of `MINOR`, `SERIOUS` or `EGREGIOUS`. Only an `OPEN` case may be closed, and anything else draws `ERR_INVESTIGATION_STATE_INVALID`; an actor other than the assigned investigator draws `ERR_NOT_ASSIGNED_INVESTIGATOR`. A `SUBSTANTIATED` closure additionally requires, when the origin was `COMPLAINT`, that the linked complaint still sit in `INVESTIGATION`. It stores `finding_severity`, `evidence_summary` and `closed_on`, and it deliberately leaves the linked complaint in `INVESTIGATION` awaiting a citation. An `UNSUBSTANTIATED` closure stores `evidence_summary` and `closed_on`, leaves `finding_severity` null, and cascades: when the origin was `COMPLAINT` and the linked complaint sits in `INVESTIGATION`, that complaint moves `INVESTIGATION → CLOSED_UNFOUNDED`. Both `SUBSTANTIATED` and `UNSUBSTANTIATED` are terminal for the case. Cases may be read individually or listed filtered by `complaint_id`, `subject_license_id`, `origin` or `status`.

**Issuing a citation.** A citation is raised on a substantiated case by supplying its `investigation_id`, the `classification_id` whose hazard tier will grade the penalty and the sanction, a statutory `violation_code` such as `B-7109`, and a `correction_due_date` by which the order of correction must be performed. Three gates fire in order. The case must be `SUBSTANTIATED` — a case still `OPEN` or closed `UNSUBSTANTIATED` draws `ERR_INVESTIGATION_NOT_SUBSTANTIATED`. The actor must be the case's assigned investigator, or `ERR_NOT_ASSIGNED_INVESTIGATOR`. And when the case names a licensed subject, the supplied `classification_id` must equal that licence's own `classification_id`, or the issuance draws `ERR_CLASSIFICATION_MISMATCH` — the board cannot cite an electrician under a plumbing classification to reach a harsher hazard tier.

Issuance lands the citation in `ISSUED` with `issued_on` set to the clock date and `appeal_deadline` set to `issued_on` advanced by fifteen days. The citation's `license_id` and its `severity` are copied from the case — `severity` taking the case's `finding_severity` — and two further values are computed. `subject_type` is `LICENSED` when a `license_id` was copied and `UNLICENSED` when none was: an unlicensed operator both doubles the penalty base and adds sanction points. `prior_final_citation_count` is the number of *other* citations against the same `license_id` whose `status` is `FINAL`, `AFFIRMED`, `MODIFIED` or `SATISFIED` — note that paying a citation off does not erase it from the recidivism count. The `civil_penalty_cents` is then assessed, `penalty_variant` is stamped `INITIAL_ASSESSMENT`, `penalty_paid_cents` starts at zero, and the linked complaint moves `INVESTIGATION → CITATION_ISSUED`.

The **civil penalty** is a product of five factors, multiplied together in full before any rounding. The **base** is set by `severity`: 500 dollars for `MINOR`, 2,500 dollars for `SERIOUS`, and 7,500 dollars for `EGREGIOUS` — expressed as cents, 50,000, 250,000 and 750,000 respectively. The **subject multiplier** is one for a `LICENSED` subject and two for an `UNLICENSED` one. The **hazard multiplier** is one plus a quarter for each tier above the first, so a `hazard_tier` of 1 gives one, a tier of 2 gives one and a quarter, and a tier of 3 gives one and a half. The **recidivism multiplier** is one plus a fifth for each prior final citation counted, with the count itself capped at three, so a licence with three or more priors reaches one and three fifths and goes no higher. The **mitigation multiplier** is one — no credit — except in the single circumstance described below, where it becomes three quarters, a twenty-five percent credit. Those five factors are multiplied together, the product is rounded half up to whole cents **exactly once**, and the result is finally clamped down to the statutory ceiling of 15,000 dollars, or 1,500,000 cents. Multiply everything first, round once, clamp last.

The penalty is computed under one of two variants, and knowing which one is running matters. The `INITIAL_ASSESSMENT` variant runs at issuance: it evaluates `prior_final_citation_count` as of the issuance moment, and its mitigation multiplier is always one, because no order of correction can possibly have been verified before the citation that orders it exists. The `POST_HEARING_RECALCULATION` variant runs when a hearing affirms the citation: it **re-evaluates** `prior_final_citation_count` as of the decision moment — so citations that became final while the appeal was pending now count against the appellant — and it earns the twenty-five percent mitigation credit when the deciding request supplies a `correction_verified_on` date and that date is on or before the citation's `correction_due_date`. To see both at work: a `SERIOUS`, `LICENSED` citation in a tier-3 trade with no priors assesses 250,000 times one times one and a half, or 375,000 cents; the same citation recalculated after two of the licensee's other citations went final during the appeal, with no correction filed, assesses 250,000 times one times one and a half times one and two fifths, or 525,000; and if the officer also verified the correction as performed on time it becomes 250,000 times one times one and a half times one and two fifths times three quarters, or 393,750. An `EGREGIOUS`, `UNLICENSED` citation in a tier-3 trade with no priors computes 750,000 times two times one and a half, or 2,250,000, and is clamped down to 1,500,000.

**The fork after issuance.** A citation goes one of two ways, and the two branches behave very differently. On the **appeal branch**, the contracting entity that holds the licence files an appeal with its `appeal_grounds`, moving the citation `ISSUED → APPEAL_REQUESTED`. The appeal must be filed while the clock date is on or before the `appeal_deadline`; filing later draws `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`. Appealing leaves `civil_penalty_cents` at its `INITIAL_ASSESSMENT` value, records the grounds in the citation's audit trail, and — importantly — the penalty is not yet collectable. Only the licensee named on the citation's licence may appeal it.

On the **default-final branch**, a `LicensingAnalyst` defaults an unappealed citation to `FINAL` once the appeal window has closed. This requires the clock date to be strictly past the `appeal_deadline`; attempting it while the window is still open — including on the deadline date itself — draws `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_OPEN`. Defaulting performs **no penalty recalculation and grades no sanction ladder**: the `INITIAL_ASSESSMENT` figure simply becomes collectable, the licensee's `unpaid_penalty_cents` rises by that citation's `civil_penalty_cents` less its `penalty_paid_cents`, the complaint behind the citation's case moves `CITATION_ISSUED → CLOSED_SANCTIONED`, and the licence's `compliance_posture` is recomputed — which drops it to at least `CONDITIONAL`, since unpaid penalty exposure now exists. Both branches are addressed only from `ISSUED`; appealing or finalizing anything else draws `ERR_CITATION_STATE_INVALID`.

**Calendaring the hearing.** An appealed citation is put before the bench by a `BoardAdmin` or a `HearingOfficer`, naming the `citation_id`, the `presiding_officer_user_id` who will preside, and the `scheduled_start_utc` and `scheduled_end_utc` bounding the hearing block. The citation must sit in `APPEAL_REQUESTED`, or the request draws `ERR_CITATION_STATE_INVALID`. Then the segregation-of-duties check of §2.10 fires, refusing a presiding officer who is the case's assigned investigator with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`. Then the calendar check: no other hearing in `SCHEDULED` status for the same presiding officer may overlap the half-open block, though a hearing beginning at the exact instant another ends is fine; a genuine overlap draws `ERR_HEARING_SLOT_CONFLICT`. Calendaring creates the hearing in `SCHEDULED` and moves the citation `APPEAL_REQUESTED → HEARING_SCHEDULED`. Hearings may be read individually or listed filtered by `citation_id`, `presiding_officer_user_id` or `status`.

**Deciding the appeal.** Only the hearing's own presiding officer may render the disposition, supplying a `decision` of `AFFIRM`, `MODIFY` or `VACATE` together with a `findings_summary`, plus optionally a `modified_penalty_cents` and a `correction_verified_on`. The gates fire in order: the hearing must be `SCHEDULED` (otherwise `ERR_HEARING_STATE_INVALID`) and its citation must be `HEARING_SCHEDULED`; the actor's `X-User-ID` must equal `presiding_officer_user_id` (otherwise `ERR_NOT_PRESIDING_OFFICER`); the clock timestamp must be at or after `scheduled_start_utc`, since a hearing cannot be decided before it convenes (otherwise `ERR_HEARING_NOT_CONVENED`); and the penalty-substitution rules must hold — `modified_penalty_cents` must be supplied for a `MODIFY` decision, must be omitted for `AFFIRM` and `VACATE`, and must never exceed the statutory ceiling of 1,500,000 cents, with any violation reporting `ERR_PENALTY_EXCEEDS_STATUTORY_MAX`. The decision stores `decision` and stamps `decided_at` from the clock, moving the hearing `SCHEDULED → DECIDED`.

The three dispositions then diverge, and each is spelled out fully. An **`AFFIRM`** moves the citation `HEARING_SCHEDULED → AFFIRMED`: `civil_penalty_cents` is overwritten by the `POST_HEARING_RECALCULATION` variant described above, `penalty_variant` becomes `POST_HEARING_RECALCULATION`, a supplied `correction_verified_on` is stamped onto the citation's `correction_completed_on`, the licensee's `unpaid_penalty_cents` rises by the recalculated `civil_penalty_cents` less `penalty_paid_cents`, and the sanction ladder is graded. A **`MODIFY`** moves the citation `HEARING_SCHEDULED → MODIFIED`: `civil_penalty_cents` is set to the officer's `modified_penalty_cents` outright with no recalculation, `penalty_variant` becomes `POST_HEARING_RECALCULATION`, the licensee's `unpaid_penalty_cents` rises by the modified `civil_penalty_cents` less `penalty_paid_cents`, and the sanction ladder is graded in exactly the same way as on an affirmance. A **`VACATE`** moves the citation `HEARING_SCHEDULED → VACATED`: `civil_penalty_cents` is set to zero, `penalty_variant` still becomes `POST_HEARING_RECALCULATION`, nothing is added to the licensee's `unpaid_penalty_cents`, no sanction whatsoever is applied to the licence, and both `sanction_points` and `sanction_tier` stay null. `VACATED` and `SATISFIED` are the terminal citation states.

The complaint behind the citation follows the disposition: on `AFFIRM` or `MODIFY` it moves `CITATION_ISSUED → CLOSED_SANCTIONED`, and on `VACATE` it moves `CITATION_ISSUED → CLOSED_UNFOUNDED`.

**The sanction ladder** is graded only on `AFFIRM` and `MODIFY`, and it is scored in whole points with no fractions anywhere. The `severity` contributes one point for `MINOR`, three for `SERIOUS`, and six for `EGREGIOUS`. Recidivism contributes two points for each prior final citation counted, with the count capped at three, so at most six points. The licence's `compliance_posture` contributes nothing when `COMPLIANT`, one point when `CONDITIONAL`, and two points when `NON_COMPLIANT` — and contributes nothing at all when the subject is unlicensed and there is no licence to read. An `UNLICENSED` `subject_type` contributes three points. And a licence carrying recovery-fund debt — that is, whose `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents` is greater than zero — contributes a further two points. **The posture is read as it stood immediately before this decision's own cascades**, so the penalty this very decision affirms can never feed back into its own sanction tier. The total is stamped as `sanction_points`, and the `sanction_tier` is then read off the ladder: `LETTER_OF_REPRIMAND` at two points or fewer, `PROBATION` from three through five, `SUSPENSION_30` from six through eight, `SUSPENSION_90` from nine through eleven, and `REVOCATION` at twelve points or more. Adjacent rungs are one point apart, so an egregious violation with one prior against a `CONDITIONAL` licence scores six plus two plus one, or nine, and lands on `SUSPENSION_90`, while the same violation against a `COMPLIANT` licence scores eight and lands on `SUSPENSION_30`.

The tier is then applied to the licence, and the four outcomes differ. `LETTER_OF_REPRIMAND` leaves the licence's `status` untouched and only restamps `compliance_posture`. `PROBATION` likewise leaves `status` untouched and only restamps `compliance_posture`, where it acts as a `CONDITIONAL` trigger — so a licence that independently qualifies as `NON_COMPLIANT` for want of a bond or for unrepaid fund money stays `NON_COMPLIANT`, per the ordering in §2.6. `SUSPENSION_30` and `SUSPENSION_90` move the licence into `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` of `DISCIPLINARY` and `suspension_until` set to the clock date advanced by thirty or ninety days respectively; this transition is defined from `ACTIVE` and from `EXPIRED`. `REVOCATION` moves the licence into `REVOKED` — defined from `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` and `EXPIRED` — clearing `suspension_reason` and `suspension_until` to null, and cascading exactly as §2.8 describes: every designation on the licence in `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE` moves to `TERMINATED_BY_REVOCATION`, `License.qualifying_individual_id` is cleared, each qualifier that held an `ACTIVE` designation has its `active_qualification_count` lowered by one, and the posture is recomputed to `NON_COMPLIANT`. A suspension arising from a disciplinary tier likewise cascades its `ACTIVE` designations to `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE`.

**Paying up.** A citation is discharged by its licensee filing `penalty_payment_cents` together with the `corrected_on` date the order of correction was performed. This is defined from `FINAL`, from `AFFIRMED` and from `MODIFIED` — all three behave identically — and moves the citation to `SATISFIED`. The payment must be exact: `penalty_payment_cents` must equal `civil_penalty_cents` less `penalty_paid_cents` to the cent, and both partial payment and overpayment are refused with `ERR_PAYMENT_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`. On success `penalty_paid_cents` is brought up to `civil_penalty_cents`, `correction_completed_on` is set to the supplied `corrected_on`, the licensee's `unpaid_penalty_cents` falls by the amount remitted, and the licence's `compliance_posture` is recomputed — returning to `COMPLIANT` when nothing else is outstanding. Citations may be read individually, exposing the penalty, the `penalty_variant`, the prior-citation count and the appeal deadline; or listed filtered by `investigation_id`, `license_id`, `classification_id` or `status`.

### 3.9. The Consumer Recovery Fund

The recovery fund is the board's last resort for a consumer whom a licensed contractor has harmed and will not make whole. It is the most cross-cutting object in the system: a single claim reaches across a consumer, a complaint, a licence and a fiscal-year appropriation, and paying it changes all four.

**Filing a claim.** A claim is filed by the harmed consumer or by a `FundAdjudicator` on their behalf. It names the `fund_account_id` of the appropriation it is charged against, the `complaint_id` of the underlying adjudicated complaint, the `basis` on which the fund is reached, and the `claimed_amount_cents` the consumer asks the fund to cover. The `basis` is either `FINAL_CITATION` — the board itself found the violation — or `COURT_JUDGMENT`, where the consumer has an unsatisfied civil judgment. A `COURT_JUDGMENT` claim must additionally carry the `judgment_amount_cents` of that unsatisfied judgment, the `judgment_docket_number` referencing the court file, and the `judgment_entered_on` date judgment was entered; a `FINAL_CITATION` claim must omit all three. Supplying an incomplete judgment set on a judgment claim, or supplying any judgment field on a citation claim, draws `ERR_JUDGMENT_FIELDS_MISMATCH`.

Four gates guard filing and they fire in this order. First, **the fund does not reach unlicensed operators**: a claim may only be filed on a complaint whose `license_id` is non-null, and a claim on an unlicensed-practice complaint is refused with `ERR_UNLICENSED_SUBJECT_NOT_CLAIMABLE`. Second, **one live claim per complaint**: at most one claim per complaint may exist in any status other than `DENIED`, so a second filing while one stands draws `ERR_DUPLICATE_CLAIM` — though a consumer whose claim was denied may file afresh. Third, the judgment-field coupling just described. Fourth, **the pleaded-loss ceiling**: `claimed_amount_cents` must be at least one cent and no greater than the complaint's `alleged_loss_cents`, because the fund cannot be asked for more than the loss the consumer pleaded; a violation draws `ERR_CLAIM_EXCEEDS_ALLEGED_LOSS`.

A filed claim lands in `FILED` with `consumer_id` and `license_id` copied from the complaint, `paid_amount_cents` and `repaid_amount_cents` at zero, and `estimated_award_cents` stamped from the **preliminary estimate**. That estimate is deliberately crude and is never revised afterwards: it is the smaller of the `claimed_amount_cents` and the appropriation's `per_claim_cap_cents`, and nothing else. It ignores any mediation settlement already in the consumer's hands, ignores the judgment amount, and ignores how much aggregate room the licence has left. It exists to set expectations, not to bind the adjudicator.

**Adjudicating a claim.** A `FundAdjudicator` records an `outcome` of `APPROVE` or `DENY`, with an optional `denial_reason`, against a claim in `FILED`; anything else draws `ERR_CLAIM_STATE_INVALID`. Two substantive gates then fire in order. The **basis must be established**: a `FINAL_CITATION` claim may only be approved when its complaint sits in `CLOSED_SANCTIONED`, and a `COURT_JUDGMENT` claim may only be approved when its complaint sits in `MEDIATION`, `INVESTIGATION`, `CITATION_ISSUED` or `CLOSED_SANCTIONED` — a complaint that was dismissed for want of jurisdiction, withdrawn, resolved in mediation or closed as unfounded supports no claim at all, and the attempt draws `ERR_CLAIM_BASIS_NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Then, for a `COURT_JUDGMENT` claim, the **ripening period**: the clock date must be on or after `judgment_entered_on` advanced by ninety days, because the judgment must have gone ninety days unsatisfied before the fund is reachable. Day ninety itself is ripe; day eighty-nine is not, and draws `ERR_JUDGMENT_NOT_RIPE`.

The **final award** is a genuinely different computation from the preliminary estimate, and it runs in four steps. Begin with the gross: for a `COURT_JUDGMENT` claim it is the smaller of `claimed_amount_cents` and `judgment_amount_cents`; for a `FINAL_CITATION` claim it is simply `claimed_amount_cents`. Next, subtract the complaint's `settlement_amount_cents` — the cash the consumer already collected in mediation — flooring the result at zero, since the fund never pays a negative amount. Then clip that net figure down to the appropriation's `per_claim_cap_cents`. Finally, clip it again to the licence's remaining aggregate room, which is the appropriation's `per_license_aggregate_cap_cents` less the licence's `recovery_encumbered_cents`, again floored at zero. The smaller of the per-claim figure and the remaining room is the award. Note what the award is **not** limited by: the appropriation's `balance_cents`. Approving a claim encumbers the licence's aggregate room but moves no cash, so the pool may legitimately be oversubscribed and the shortfall is resolved at disbursement time.

An `APPROVE` whose computation yields at least one cent moves the claim `FILED → APPROVED`, stamps `awarded_amount_cents` with the award and `adjudicated_on` with the clock date, and recomputes the licence's `recovery_encumbered_cents` — the sum of `awarded_amount_cents` over every claim on that licence whose `status` is `APPROVED`, `PAID` or `REIMBURSED` — so the award immediately counts against the aggregate cap even though no money has moved. An `APPROVE` whose computation yields exactly zero, meaning the aggregate cap has been exhausted, does **not** approve: the claim moves `FILED → DENIED` with `awarded_amount_cents` at zero, `adjudicated_on` stamped, and `denial_reason` set automatically to `AGGREGATE_CAP_EXHAUSTED`. An explicit `DENY` moves the claim `FILED → DENIED` with `awarded_amount_cents` at zero, `adjudicated_on` stamped, and `denial_reason` taken from the request. To see the divergence between the two variants concretely: against a per-claim cap of 1,500,000 cents and a per-licence aggregate cap of 2,000,000, a claim for 1,200,000 on a `FINAL_CITATION` basis whose complaint settled in mediation for 300,000, against a licence with nothing encumbered, is estimated at 1,200,000 but awarded 900,000 — the divergence is exactly the settlement. A second claim on the same licence for 1,500,000 with no settlement then faces 1,100,000 of remaining room and is awarded 1,100,000.

**Disbursing.** Disbursement is a `FundAdjudicator` action on a claim in `APPROVED` — anything else draws `ERR_CLAIM_STATE_INVALID` — and it must present `expected_fund_account_version` as §2.9 requires. The appropriation must have at least one cent of `balance_cents` to disburse from, or the request is refused with `ERR_FUND_BALANCE_EXHAUSTED`. The concurrency check fires first, the claim-state check second, and the balance check last.

What follows is the widest cascade in the system, and it runs in four hops. **First**, the pro-rata distribution runs over the **cohort**: every claim on the *same licence* as the target claim whose `status` is `APPROVED`, ordered by `created_at` ascending. Sum their `awarded_amount_cents` to get the total awarded. If the appropriation's `balance_cents` is at least that total, every cohort member's `paid_amount_cents` is set to its own `awarded_amount_cents` in full. Otherwise each member's `paid_amount_cents` is its `awarded_amount_cents` multiplied by the `balance_cents` and divided by the total awarded, computed in integer arithmetic and rounded down to a whole cent; the cents left over — the `balance_cents` less the sum of those floored shares — are then distributed one cent at a time to cohort members in `created_at` ascending order until none remain. That residual order is by creation time, never by identifier and never by claim size. Every cohort member then moves `APPROVED → PAID` with `paid_on` set to the clock date, **including sibling claims that were not the request's target**. **Second**, *because* cash has moved, the appropriation's `balance_cents` falls by the total disbursed while the licence's `recovery_paid_to_date_cents` and `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents` both rise by that same total, and the licensee's `outstanding_recovery_payout_cents` rises with them. **Third**, *because* `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents` is now greater than zero, the licence moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` of `RECOVERY_FUND_PAYOUT` and `suspension_until` left null — the suspension is indefinite and lasts until the money is repaid — and its `compliance_posture` becomes `NON_COMPLIANT`. A licence already `SUSPENDED` for some other reason keeps its earlier `suspension_reason`. **Fourth**, *because* the licence has entered `SUSPENDED`, every designation on it in `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE` and each affected qualifier's `active_qualification_count` falls by one. To make the arithmetic concrete: with 900,000 cents in the pool and a cohort of two claims awarded 700,000 and 400,000, the total awarded of 1,100,000 exceeds the balance, so the first claim's floored share is 572,727 and the second's is 327,272; the one leftover cent goes to the earlier-created claim, giving 572,728 and 327,272; the pool falls to zero and the licence carries 900,000 of repayment debt.

**Repaying the fund.** The suspended contracting entity repays by crediting `repayment_amount_cents` against the licence, presenting `expected_license_version` as §2.9 requires. Only the licensee that holds the licence may do this. The action is defined against a licence in `SUSPENDED`, and against an `ACTIVE` licence that still carries a non-zero `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents`; a `REVOKED` licence accepts nothing. The credit must be at least one cent and no greater than the licence's current `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents`, or it is refused with `ERR_REPAYMENT_EXCEEDS_OUTSTANDING` — the fund refuses to be overpaid.

A successful credit increments the licence's `version` by one and is absorbed by the licence's paid claims **oldest first, in `created_at` ascending order**: each claim's `repaid_amount_cents` rises until it reaches that claim's `paid_amount_cents`, at which point the claim moves `PAID → REIMBURSED` and the remainder of the credit rolls on to the next claim. The appropriation's `balance_cents` rises by the credited amount, the licence's `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents` falls by it, and the licensee's `outstanding_recovery_payout_cents` follows. When the credit brings `outstanding_recovery_repayment_cents` to exactly zero **and** the licence's `suspension_reason` is `RECOVERY_FUND_PAYOUT`, the payout suspension lifts of its own accord: the licence moves `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`, both `suspension_reason` and `suspension_until` are cleared to null, every designation in `SUSPENDED_BY_LICENSE` returns to `ACTIVE` with each such return raising its qualifier's `active_qualification_count` by one, and the `compliance_posture` is recomputed. No analyst action is needed; full repayment is self-executing. Partial repayments simply lower the outstanding figure and leave the suspension in place, which is exactly why the stale-version guard on this endpoint matters.

`DENIED` and `REIMBURSED` are the terminal claim states. Claims may be read individually — exposing `estimated_award_cents`, `awarded_amount_cents`, `paid_amount_cents`, `repaid_amount_cents`, `adjudicated_on`, `paid_on` and any `denial_reason` — or listed filtered by `fund_account_id`, `complaint_id`, `consumer_id`, `license_id` or `status`. The appropriation itself may be read to see its live `balance_cents` and the `version` that serves as the disbursement concurrency token.
