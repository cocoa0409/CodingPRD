# Airline Live Animal Travel Acceptance API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Carrying live animals is the least forgiving product an airline sells. A missing rabies certificate, a container two centimetres too short, a forecast high that crossed an embargo threshold overnight, a connection that shrank below the welfare minimum because an inbound leg slipped forty minutes — each of these is a reason to refuse carriage, and each of them is discovered by a different person, at a different counter, at a different hour. Today those judgements live in laminated binders, station-local spreadsheets and the institutional memory of senior acceptance agents. The result is inconsistency: two animals with identical paperwork are treated differently at two stations, and the airline learns about the discrepancy only when a regulator asks why an animal died in a hold.

This document specifies the Live Animal Travel Acceptance service: a single system of record for the airline's combined passenger-cabin, checked-baggage and cargo animal programme. It carries the programme configuration (species acceptance classes, breed restrictions, approved container models, station kennel pools, seasonal temperature embargoes and per-destination import paperwork rules), the commercial and welfare record of every booked animal (reservations, animal records, per-leg routing slots, health documents, kennel holds and welfare handling events), and the regulatory record of everything that goes wrong (injury, loss and death incidents with their disclosure clocks and adjudicated severity scores). Every acceptance decision the service makes is reproducible from stored data, and every refusal names a specific machine-readable reason.

Two design commitments shape the whole specification. The first is **segregation of duties**: the agent who books an animal may not be the agent who accepts it at the counter, the officer who reports an incident may not be the officer who investigates it, and the investigator may not be the officer who closes it. The second is **derived-field honesty**: booked counters, kennel occupancy pools, chargeable weights, fee totals, discount tiers and severity scores are all computed by pinned algorithms with pinned evaluation order, never supplied by a client, and they are re-derived on every write that could invalidate them. All endpoints described here are mounted at the service root.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Operators, Headers and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to the service — including every read — must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the operator making the call, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role the operator is acting in. If either header is absent or present but empty, the request is rejected with `ERR_MISSING_AUTH_HEADER` (HTTP 401). If `X-User-Role` carries any value other than the five role names below, the request is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_ROLE` (HTTP 401). If the role is recognised but is not among the roles authorised for the endpoint being called, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403).

The five roles are:

- **`ProgramAdmin`** — owns programme configuration: species profiles, breed restrictions, destination import requirements, container models, stations, temperature embargo rules, flight schedules and the logical clock.
- **`ReservationsAgent`** — books reservations, adds animals, reserves routing slots on flight legs, submits and resubmits health documents, and confirms, cancels or releases what it booked.
- **`AcceptanceAgent`** — screens animals, marks them travel-ready or denies them, accepts and offloads them at the counter, appends welfare checks, provisions kennel bays, runs irregular-operations care sweeps, and departs and arrives flight legs.
- **`VeterinaryReviewer`** — takes health documents under review and verifies or rejects them, and runs the per-reservation destination compliance sweep.
- **`ComplianceOfficer`** — investigates, discloses and closes animal incidents.

All five roles have an **opaque** identity. When `X-User-Role` is `ProgramAdmin`, `ReservationsAgent`, `AcceptanceAgent`, `VeterinaryReviewer` or `ComplianceOfficer`, the `X-User-ID` header is an opaque operator identifier that is not tied to any persisted entity: the server never resolves it to a row and never compares it to any entity's `id`. The server does, however, record it where the specification says so, and later compares recorded values to each other. Specifically, `Reservation.booked_by_agent_id` records the `X-User-ID` of the `ReservationsAgent` that opened the reservation; `WelfareCheck.performed_by_user_id` records the `X-User-ID` of the `AcceptanceAgent` that appended the check; `HealthDocument.reviewed_by_user_id` records the `X-User-ID` of the reviewer that last moved a document out of `UNDER_REVIEW`; and `AnimalIncident.reported_by_user_id`, `AnimalIncident.investigated_by_user_id` and `AnimalIncident.closed_by_user_id` record the `X-User-ID` values of the operators who reported, investigated and closed an incident. Those recorded values, and only those, drive the segregation-of-duties rules in §2.11.

Every `GET` endpoint in the service is readable by all five roles. Write authority is otherwise disjoint, with two deliberate exceptions: amending an animal record is shared between `ReservationsAgent` and `AcceptanceAgent`, and reporting an animal incident is shared between `AcceptanceAgent` and `ComplianceOfficer`. The computed charge summary read on a reservation is available to `ProgramAdmin`, `ReservationsAgent`, `AcceptanceAgent` and `ComplianceOfficer`, but not to `VeterinaryReviewer`.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.
- Three transport-level failures answer in the same envelope with identifiers outside the §5 catalogue: a path that matches no route answers `ERR_NOT_FOUND` (404), a path that matches a route but not the method answers `ERR_METHOD_NOT_ALLOWED` (405), and an unexpected server fault answers `ERR_INTERNAL` (500).

### 2.3. Pagination and List Filters

List endpoints accept two query parameters. `limit` is the maximum number of rows to return; when omitted it defaults to 10, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` is the number of rows to skip; when omitted it defaults to 0 and its minimum accepted value is 0. A `limit` that is not an integer in the range 1 through 100 inclusive, or an `offset` that is not an integer of at least 0, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION` (HTTP 400).

Unless a specific endpoint says otherwise, every list endpoint returns rows sorted by `created_at` in ascending order, ties among rows sharing a `created_at` being broken by the order in which the service wrote them. List responses carry `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside the envelope's `meta` object, where `total_count` is the number of rows matching the filters before pagination is applied and `has_more` is true when rows remain beyond the current page.

Collections also accept filters, all of them optional and all of them exact-match. Species profiles, stations, breed restrictions of one species and embargo rules of one station can be filtered by `status`; embargo rules of one station additionally by `direction`; destination requirements of one species by `destination_country_code` and `status`; container models by `cabin_eligible`; flight legs by `origin_station_id`, `destination_station_id`, `governing_embargo_rule_id` and `status`; reservations by `booking_status` and by `compliance_status`; animal records by `reservation_id`, `species_profile_id`, `container_model_id` and `status`; segment assignments by `animal_record_id`, `flight_leg_id` and `status`; the welfare checks of one segment by `check_type`; the health documents of one animal by `document_type` and `status`; kennel allocations by `station_id`, `animal_record_id` and `status`; and animal incidents by `animal_record_id`, `segment_assignment_id` and `status`. Every string-typed filter is compared verbatim against the stored value, the only boolean filter — `cabin_eligible` — accepts `true`, `false`, `1` and `0` case-insensitively and answers an empty page with `total_count` 0 for any other spelling rather than an error, and a query parameter a collection does not list is ignored rather than rejected.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and Version Semantics

Every resource the service persists carries an `id`, which is a server-minted UUID string with no type prefix, and a `created_at`, which is an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits recording when the row was first written. Clients never supply either field. `created_at` and `updated_at` are read from server wall-clock time, not from the logical clock of §2.7.

Most resources additionally carry `updated_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp refreshed on every mutation of the row, and `version`, an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increments by exactly one on every mutation of the row. This applies to species profiles, breed restrictions, container models, stations, temperature embargo rules, destination requirements, flight legs, reservations, animal records, segment assignments, health documents, kennel allocations and animal incidents. Recomputing a derived field counts as a mutation: when a cascade rewrites a leg's booked counters, or re-derives a reservation's money fields, that row's `updated_at` is refreshed and its `version` is incremented. Mutation is counted **per request, not per write**: however many times one request touches the same row — a leg whose counters are rewritten twice by a fan-out, a reservation whose money fields are reassembled after each of several released slots — that row's `version` advances by exactly one for the whole request, and a request that assigns a field the value it already held does not count as a mutation of that row at all.

Instants a client supplies — `scheduled_departure_utc`, `scheduled_arrival_utc`, `issued_at`, `expires_at`, `occupancy_start_utc`, `occupancy_end_utc`, `discovered_at_utc` and the delay and restore instants — are stored and echoed back **verbatim**, in exactly the spelling the client sent, without normalisation. Any ISO 8601 form Python's `datetime.fromisoformat` accepts is accepted, a trailing `Z` is honoured, and an instant carrying no zone designator is interpreted as UTC. Instants the server itself derives from the logical clock — `current_utc`, `WelfareCheck.recorded_at_utc`, `AnimalIncident.disclosure_deadline_utc` and `AnimalIncident.disclosed_at_utc` — are rendered as `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ` with the fractional part omitted entirely when the millisecond component is zero, and as three fractional digits otherwise.

Welfare checks are the single exception. A `WelfareCheck` is an append-only event: it carries only `id` and `created_at`. It has **no** `updated_at` field and **no** `version` field, and it is never updated after it is written.

### 2.5. Nested Resource Retrieval and Identifier Resolution Order

Several endpoints address a child through its parent — the breed restrictions of a species profile, the destination requirements of a species profile, the embargo rules of a station, the health documents of an animal record, the welfare checks of a segment assignment. When a path carries more than one identifier, the service resolves them strictly left to right and fails on the **first** identifier that does not resolve, returning HTTP 404 with the error identifier belonging to that resource class: `ERR_SPECIES_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_BREED_RESTRICTION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CONTAINER_MODEL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EMBARGO_RULE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DESTINATION_REQUIREMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FLIGHT_LEG_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_RESERVATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SEGMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DOCUMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ALLOCATION_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_INCIDENT_NOT_FOUND`.

The same discipline governs foreign keys supplied inside a request body: they are resolved in the order the endpoint declares its input fields, and the first one that fails to resolve produces that resource class's 404 error. So a segment-assignment create that names a non-existent animal record and a non-existent flight leg reports `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_FOUND`, because `animal_record_id` is declared before `flight_leg_id`.

### 2.6. Validation Precedence

When a single request violates several rules at once, the service reports the violation that comes first in the ordered ladder below. The ladder is normative: text order is evaluation order, and a rule lower down may never mask a rule higher up. Nothing is written to any row until every applicable check has passed.

1. Both authentication headers present and non-empty — otherwise `ERR_MISSING_AUTH_HEADER` (401). Applies to every endpoint.
2. `X-User-Role` is one of `ProgramAdmin`, `ReservationsAgent`, `AcceptanceAgent`, `VeterinaryReviewer`, `ComplianceOfficer` — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_ROLE` (401). Every endpoint.
3. The acting role appears in the called endpoint's authorised role list — otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (403). Every endpoint.
4. The request body parses as JSON, every required input field is present, every field has its declared type, every enum-typed field carries a declared member, every declared numeric bound holds, and no unknown field is supplied — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400). Applies to every `POST` and `PUT` endpoint. An absent or whitespace-only body is treated as the empty JSON object `{}`, so an endpoint whose every input is optional accepts a bodyless call while an endpoint that declares no inputs at all rejects any supplied field; a body that is valid JSON but not an object is rejected; and an explicit `null` is accepted only for the fields this document describes as nullable (`reason`, `rejection_reason`, `notes`, `max_weight_kg`, `measured_weight_kg`, `segment_assignment_id` and `regulator_reference`). The fields that must be JSON integers rather than merely numbers are `min_age_weeks`, `age_weeks`, `ventilated_side_count`, `kennel_bay_count`, `max_age_days_at_arrival`, the three per-routing animal limits, `sequence_index`, `bay_count`, `feeding_interval_minutes`, `expected_flight_leg_version` and `expected_station_version`; every weight, dimension, percentage and temperature is any JSON number, and a JSON boolean is never accepted where a number is declared.
5. Pagination parameters are in range — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION` (400). Applies to every list endpoint.
6. Every path identifier resolves, left to right — otherwise the matching 404 from §2.5. Applies to every endpoint carrying a path parameter.
7. Every foreign key in the request body resolves, in declared field order — otherwise the matching 404 from §2.5. Applies to `POST /breed-restrictions`, `POST /destination-requirements`, `POST /temperature-embargo-rules`, `POST /flight-legs`, `POST /animal-records`, `POST /segment-assignments`, `POST /health-documents`, `POST /kennel-allocations`, `POST /animal-incidents`, `POST /welfare-checks` and `POST /irops-care-sweeps`.
8. Segregation of duties — otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (403). Applies to exactly three operations: `POST /segment-assignments/{id}/accept`, `POST /animal-incidents/{id}/investigate` and `POST /animal-incidents/{id}/close`.
9. `expected_flight_leg_version` equals the target leg's current `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_FLIGHT_LEG_VERSION` (412). Applies to `POST /segment-assignments`.
10. `expected_station_version` equals the target station's current `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_STATION_VERSION` (412). Applies to `POST /kennel-allocations`.
11. The flight leg's `status` permits the requested operation — otherwise `ERR_FLIGHT_LEG_INVALID_STATE` (409). Applies to `POST /flight-legs/{id}/delay`, `POST /flight-legs/{id}/restore-schedule`, `POST /flight-legs/{id}/cancel`, `POST /flight-legs/{id}/depart`, `POST /flight-legs/{id}/arrive`, `PUT /flight-legs/{id}/forecast` and `POST /segment-assignments`.
12. The reservation's `booking_status` permits the requested operation — otherwise `ERR_RESERVATION_INVALID_STATE` (409). The permitted sets are `PENDING`, `HOLD_PARTIAL` or `REBOOK_REQUIRED` for confirmation; `PENDING`, `CONFIRMED`, `HOLD_PARTIAL` or `REBOOK_REQUIRED` for cancellation; `CONFIRMED` or `HOLD_PARTIAL` for segment acceptance; and `PENDING`, `CONFIRMED`, `HOLD_PARTIAL` or `IN_TRANSIT` for an irregular-operations care sweep. Applies to `POST /reservations/{id}/confirm`, `POST /reservations/{id}/cancel`, `POST /segment-assignments/{id}/accept` and `POST /irops-care-sweeps`.

The permitted sets are `DRAFT` or `TRAVEL_READY` for screening; `SCREENED` for marking travel-ready; `DRAFT` or `SCREENED` for denial; `DRAFT`, `SCREENED` or `TRAVEL_READY` for reserving a routing slot; `TRAVEL_READY` for segment acceptance; `DRAFT`, `SCREENED` or `TRAVEL_READY` for submitting a health document; `SCREENED`, `TRAVEL_READY` or `IN_TRANSIT` for reserving kennel bays; and `TRAVEL_READY` for every non-`DENIED` animal of a reservation being confirmed. ### 2.7. The Logical Clock

Document expiry, welfare-check timestamps, kennel occupancy windows and regulatory disclosure timing are all read from a **logical clock** held by the server, never from wall-clock time. Before any client sets it, the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and it never falls back to wall-clock time. A `ProgramAdmin` may advance it by supplying a `to_iso8601` instant; both the clock write endpoint and the clock read endpoint answer HTTP 200 with a `data` object whose single member is `current_utc`, carrying the value the clock holds after the call. The clock is monotonic: a `to_iso8601` earlier than the current value is rejected with `ERR_CLOCK_NOT_MONOTONIC`, while an instant equal to the current value is accepted and leaves the clock unchanged.

### 2.8. Money, Minor Currency Units and Rounding

All monetary amounts in the service — `segment_fee_amount`, `subtotal_amount`, `irops_fee_total`, `loyalty_discount_amount`, `refunded_amount` and `net_payable_amount` — are integers in minor currency units (for a currency with two decimal subunits, an amount of `12500` means 125.00). They are never expressed as decimals and never rounded a second time in the response layer. The one rate-typed money-adjacent field, `loyalty_tier_pct`, is a plain number carrying the selected discount rate.

Wherever a rounding step is specified, the method is **round half up**: a value whose fractional part is exactly one half rounds away from zero to the next whole unit. Two rounding disciplines matter and must not be confused. Per-segment travel fees are rounded to whole minor units **before** they are summed into a reservation subtotal; summing unrounded segment fees and rounding once at the reservation level is explicitly not the rule and produces a different total. Within the reservation charge assembly, by contrast, there is exactly **one** rounding point, at the loyalty discount step. Chargeable weight is not rounded but **ceiled** to whole kilograms, and container requirement thresholds are likewise ceiled to whole units. Severity scores are whole integers.

### 2.9. Optimistic Concurrency on Contended Pools

Two resources in this service are finite pools whose derived counters gate acceptance decisions, and writes against them are protected by optimistic concurrency control.

Reserving a routing slot — `POST /segment-assignments` — requires the non-optional input field `expected_flight_leg_version`, carrying the `version` of the target `FlightLeg` as the client last observed it. Every acceptance, release or offload on that leg advances the leg's booked counters and its `version`, so a client holding an older `version` made its routing-limit decision against a superseded booked count. If `expected_flight_leg_version` does not equal the leg's current `version`, the create is refused with `ERR_STALE_FLIGHT_LEG_VERSION` (HTTP 412) rather than being allowed to oversubscribe the leg.

Reserving holding bays — `POST /kennel-allocations` — requires the non-optional input field `expected_station_version`, carrying the `version` of the target `Station` as the client last observed it. The station's `reserved_bay_count` is the pool the aggregate-capacity check reads, and every allocation create or release advances it together with the station's `version`. If `expected_station_version` does not equal the station's current `version`, the create is refused with `ERR_STALE_STATION_VERSION` (HTTP 412).

Both version fields are integers of at least 1, a value outside that shape being an `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` of ladder step 4 rather than a stale-version refusal. No other endpoint in the service accepts a version field, and no other endpoint performs a version check. Endpoints not listed above are processed without any optimistic-concurrency gate.

### 2.10. Derived Counters and Cascade Reconciliation

The service holds a number of fields that are functions of their children and are never accepted from a client: `SpeciesProfile.breed_restriction_count`, `Station.reserved_bay_count`, `FlightLeg.block_minutes`, `FlightLeg.booked_cabin_count`, `FlightLeg.booked_checked_count`, `FlightLeg.booked_cargo_count`, `FlightLeg.posture`, `FlightLeg.governing_embargo_rule_id`, `Reservation.animal_count`, the six reservation money fields, `AnimalRecord.chargeable_weight_kg`, `AnimalRecord.container_compliance_state`, `AnimalRecord.deficiency_codes`, `SegmentAssignment.connection_minutes`, `SegmentAssignment.required_welfare_check_count`, `SegmentAssignment.completed_welfare_check_count`, `SegmentAssignment.segment_fee_amount`, `SegmentAssignment.fee_variant`, and the incident severity and disclosure fields.

`breed_restriction_count` is the number of breed restriction rows whose `species_profile_id` names the profile and whose `status` is `ACTIVE`. `reserved_bay_count` is the sum of `bay_count` over the station's kennel allocations whose `status` is `RESERVED` or `OCCUPIED`. Each `booked_<routing>_count` is the number of segment assignments on the leg whose `routing_type` matches and whose `status` is `RESERVED` or `ACCEPTED`. `animal_count` is the number of animal records on the reservation whose `status` is anything other than `DENIED`. `completed_welfare_check_count` is the number of welfare check rows whose `segment_assignment_id` names the segment.

Because these counters gate acceptance decisions, the service re-derives them **immediately** and in the same request as the write that invalidated them, and it propagates the consequences up and down the hierarchy: a released segment decrements its leg's booked counter, which re-derives that leg's `posture`, and also credits its owning reservation's `refunded_amount` and re-derives that reservation's `booking_status` and money fields. Each of those rows is a separate mutation with its own `updated_at` refresh and `version` increment, counted once per row per request under the rule of §2.4. When a cascade fans out across several legs and reservations, every touched row is reconciled, not just the one the client named.

### 2.11. Segregation of Duties

Three operations are barred to the operator who performed the preceding step, and all three report `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403) when the bar is breached. Accepting a segment at the counter requires that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from the `booked_by_agent_id` recorded on the owning reservation. Opening an incident investigation requires that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from the incident's `reported_by_user_id`. Closing an incident requires that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from the incident's `investigated_by_user_id`. Note that nothing bars the reporter from closing an incident someone else investigated, and nothing bars the investigator from filing the disclosure.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Programme Configuration: Species, Breeds and Import Paperwork

Everything starts with a **species profile**, registered by a `ProgramAdmin`. The profile is keyed by `species_code` — uppercase letters A through Z and the underscore character only, between 2 and 24 characters long, and unique across the whole programme, a duplicate being refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_SPECIES_CODE`. It carries a `common_name` for paperwork, an `acceptance_class` that is the programme-wide ceiling on how the species may travel (`ACCEPTED_ALL_ROUTINGS`, `CARGO_ONLY` or `EMBARGOED_SPECIES`), a `max_cabin_weight_kg` giving the combined animal-plus-container gross weight ceiling for cabin carriage (at least 0 and at most 50), a `min_age_weeks` of at least 0 below which no animal of the species is accepted at all, and a `requires_veterinary_review` flag which, when true, means every health document of the species must be verified by a `VeterinaryReviewer`; it defaults to false. Its `breed_restriction_count` starts at 0 and is maintained by the service. Its `status` is `ACTIVE` or `RETIRED`, may optionally be supplied on the create call, and defaults to `ACTIVE` when it is not; a `ProgramAdmin` may flip it through `PUT /species-profiles/{id}/status`, which increments the profile's `version` by one even when the supplied `status` equals the one already held and cascades to nothing else, and retiring a species blocks both adding new animals of that species and screening existing ones, each attempt being refused with `ERR_SPECIES_RETIRED`.

A **breed restriction** narrows a species. A `ProgramAdmin` attaches one by supplying the `species_profile_id` of the species it narrows, a `breed_name` between 1 and 60 characters, a `restriction_type`, and — only in one case — a `max_weight_kg`. There are four restriction types. `SNUB_NOSED` marks brachycephalic breeds, which carry a fee surcharge outside the cabin but are not refused. `BANNED` refuses the breed outright at screening with `ERR_BREED_BANNED`. `CARGO_ONLY` narrows the breed to cargo carriage. `WEIGHT_CAPPED` imposes a gross-weight ceiling, and for exactly this type `max_weight_kg` must be supplied; for the other three it must be absent, and a violation of either half of that rule — or a lowercased `breed_name` that duplicates an existing `ACTIVE` restriction of the same species — is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_BREED_RESTRICTION`. `max_weight_kg` otherwise defaults to null, and when supplied must be strictly greater than 0. The duplicate-name half of that rule is evaluated before the `max_weight_kg` pairing half, but since both report `ERR_DUPLICATE_BREED_RESTRICTION` the order is not observable. A restriction's `status` is `ACTIVE` or `WITHDRAWN`, is **not** accepted as a create input — a supplied `status` is an unknown field and so an `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` — and is always `ACTIVE` on the freshly created row; only `ACTIVE` rows participate in any acceptance decision. Creating a restriction increments the parent species profile's `breed_restriction_count` and its `version` by one; withdrawing or reinstating one through `PUT /breed-restrictions/{id}/status` recomputes `breed_restriction_count` and again increments both the restriction's and the species profile's `version` by one, even when the supplied `status` equals the one already held. Breed matching is always case-insensitive: a restriction's `breed_name` matches an animal when the two lowercase to the same string.

Import paperwork is expressed as **destination requirements**, also owned by `ProgramAdmin`. Each row names the `species_profile_id` it applies to, the `destination_country_code` enforcing it as exactly two uppercase letters, the `document_type` demanded (`HEALTH_CERTIFICATE`, `RABIES_VACCINATION`, `IMPORT_PERMIT` or `PARASITE_TREATMENT`), and a `max_age_days_at_arrival` of at least 1, which is the maximum number of whole days permitted between a document's issue instant and the scheduled arrival of the animal's final leg. The `mandatory` flag defaults to true and means a `VERIFIED` document of that type is required before acceptance; a requirement with `mandatory` false is advisory and gates nothing. Its `status` is `ACTIVE` or `SUPERSEDED`, is not accepted as a create input and is always `ACTIVE` on the freshly created row, and only `ACTIVE` rows are enforced; no endpoint in the service moves a destination requirement to `SUPERSEDED`, so the row is immutable once written.

### 3.2. Approved Containers, Stations and Seasonal Embargo Rules

A **container model** is a physical kennel design the programme has approved. A `ProgramAdmin` registers it with a `model_code` between 2 and 24 characters and unique across all models — a duplicate is `ERR_DUPLICATE_CONTAINER_MODEL` — together with its usable interior dimensions in centimetres (`interior_length_cm`, `interior_width_cm` and `interior_height_cm`, each strictly greater than 0), its `tare_weight_kg` of at least 0, which is the empty-container weight added to the animal's weight to form gross weight, its `ventilation_area_pct` between 0 and 100 inclusive expressing ventilated openings as a percentage of total wall area, and its `ventilated_side_count` between 0 and 4 giving how many sides carry openings. Two booleans record routing suitability: `wheels_removable` is true when wheels are absent or removable, which cargo carriage requires, and `cabin_eligible` is true when the model fits the under-seat envelope.

A **station** is an airport in the programme, registered with an `iata_code` of exactly three uppercase letters and unique across all stations — a duplicate is `ERR_DUPLICATE_STATION_CODE` — a `country_code` of exactly two uppercase letters, and a `kennel_bay_count` of at least 0 giving the total holding bays available for animal holds. Its `reserved_bay_count` starts at 0 and is maintained by the service. Its `status` is `ACCEPTING` or `SUSPENDED`, may optionally be supplied on the create call, and defaults to `ACCEPTING` when it is not; a `ProgramAdmin` may suspend or resume live animal acceptance, and each such call increments the station's `version` by one even when the supplied `status` equals the one already held, and cascades to nothing — suspending a station neither releases the routing slots already reserved on its legs nor the kennel bays already held there. While either the origin or the destination station of a leg is `SUSPENDED`, neither a routing slot on that leg nor a kennel hold at that station may be created, both being refused with `ERR_STATION_SUSPENDED`.

Temperature embargoes are configured per station as **temperature embargo rules**. A rule names its `station_id`, a `direction` of `ORIGIN` or `DESTINATION` selecting whether it applies when the station is the leg's departure or arrival point, a season expressed as `season_start_month_day` and `season_end_month_day`, each a month-and-day string of the form two digits, hyphen, two digits with a month of 01 through 12 and a day of 01 through 31, a `forecast_high_limit_c` and a `forecast_low_limit_c` in Celsius, and an `applies_to_routings` array with at least one member drawn from `CABIN`, `CHECKED` and `CARGO` naming the routings the embargo suppresses. Both season endpoints are inclusive, and a window whose end month-day sorts before its start month-day wraps across the turn of the year — a season from `11-15` to `03-01` contains `12-24` and `01-09` as well as `11-15` and `03-01`. A rule's `status` is `ACTIVE` or `SUPERSEDED`, is not accepted as a create input and is always `ACTIVE` on the freshly created row, and only `ACTIVE` rows participate in embargo determination; superseding or reactivating a rule, like creating one, increments the owning station's `version` by one — and, for the status call, the rule's own `version` too, even when the supplied `status` equals the one already held. Neither creating nor restatusing a rule re-runs embargo determination on any existing flight leg: legs already written keep the `governing_embargo_rule_id` and `posture` they last derived until one of the four leg-level events listed in §3.3 re-derives them.

### 3.3. Flight Legs: Scheduling, Embargo Determination and Posture

A **flight leg** is one scheduled hop that can carry animals. A `ProgramAdmin` creates it with a `flight_number`, the `origin_station_id` and `destination_station_id` it connects, a `scheduled_departure_utc` and a `scheduled_arrival_utc` that must be strictly after the departure instant (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_TIME_RANGE`), the `forecast_high_c` and `forecast_low_c` temperatures used for embargo determination, and three independent capacity ceilings — `cabin_animal_limit`, `checked_animal_limit` and `cargo_animal_limit`, each at least 0 — capping how many animals may hold a slot in each carriage mode. On creation the service computes `block_minutes` as the number of whole minutes between the scheduled departure and the scheduled arrival, initialises `booked_cabin_count`, `booked_checked_count` and `booked_cargo_count` to 0, runs embargo determination and then derives `posture`. The leg's own `status` starts at `SCHEDULED` and moves among `SCHEDULED`, `DELAYED`, `CANCELLED`, `DEPARTED` and `ARRIVED`, with `CANCELLED` and `ARRIVED` terminal.

**Embargo determination** selects at most one governing rule for the leg, and it re-runs on leg creation, on a forecast update, on a delay and on a schedule restore. The service first forms the month-and-day of the leg's `scheduled_departure_utc` in the same two-digit month, hyphen, two-digit day form the rules use. A candidate rule is one whose `status` is `ACTIVE`, whose season window contains that month-and-day, whose direction and station match the leg — that is, either its `direction` is `ORIGIN` and its `station_id` is the leg's `origin_station_id`, or its `direction` is `DESTINATION` and its `station_id` is the leg's `destination_station_id` — and whose temperature test trips: the leg's `forecast_high_c` is greater than or equal to the rule's `forecast_high_limit_c`, or the leg's `forecast_low_c` is less than or equal to the rule's `forecast_low_limit_c`. A forecast exactly equal to either limit trips the rule. Rules whose `status` is `SUPERSEDED` are never candidates. If no rule is a candidate, `governing_embargo_rule_id` is set to null. Otherwise the candidates are ordered by `forecast_high_limit_c` ascending and, among rules sharing the same high limit, by `created_at` ascending and then by the order in which the service wrote them — never by `id` — and the first rule in that order becomes the leg's `governing_embargo_rule_id`. Posture derivation then runs.

**Posture derivation** produces `FlightLeg.posture`, the single gate every child operation reads. It is a first-match-wins ladder in exactly this order. If the leg's `status` is `CANCELLED`, the posture is `CLOSED`. Otherwise, if `governing_embargo_rule_id` is not null, the posture is `EMBARGOED`. Otherwise, if the leg's `status` is `DEPARTED` or `ARRIVED`, the posture is `CLOSED`. Otherwise, if for any one of the three routings the leg's limit for that routing is greater than 0 and the booked count for that routing, multiplied by ten, is greater than or equal to that limit multiplied by nine, the posture is `RESTRICTED`. Otherwise the posture is `OPEN`. The nine-tenths load test is deliberately written as an integer cross-multiplication so no floating-point boundary ambiguity can arise: a leg whose cabin limit is 10 with 9 cabin animals booked is `RESTRICTED`, while the same leg with 8 booked is `OPEN`. Posture is re-derived on every write that can change the leg's `status`, its `governing_embargo_rule_id` or any booked counter — leg creation, forecast update, delay, schedule restore, cancellation, departure, arrival, and every routing-slot create, release or offload. Because the embargo branch sits above the departed/arrived branch, an embargoed leg that has departed still reports `EMBARGOED`, and because the cancelled branch sits above the embargo branch, a cancelled leg always reports `CLOSED`.

Updating a leg's forecast is a `ProgramAdmin` action supplying a fresh `forecast_high_c` and `forecast_low_c`, permitted only while the leg's `status` is `SCHEDULED` or `DELAYED` and otherwise refused with `ERR_FLIGHT_LEG_INVALID_STATE`. It re-runs embargo determination and posture derivation and increments the leg's `version` by one, unconditionally, whether or not the new forecast changes any derived field. It does **not** recompute any downstream slot's `connection_minutes` or `required_welfare_check_count`, because it moves neither scheduled instant. If the posture becomes `EMBARGOED`, the service immediately releases every routing slot on the leg that is still `RESERVED` and whose `routing_type` is contained in the governing rule's `applies_to_routings` — slots in a routing the rule does not suppress are left alone. Each release decrements the corresponding booked counter, which re-derives the posture, and drives the owning reservation to `HOLD_PARTIAL` or `REBOOK_REQUIRED` with `refunded_amount` incremented by the released slot's `segment_fee_amount` and the reservation charge assembly re-run.

Delaying a leg is the second cascade origin, and the recompute it triggers — not the delay itself — is what releases downstream work. A `ProgramAdmin` supplies a `new_departure_utc` and a `new_arrival_utc`. The leg must currently be `SCHEDULED`, the new departure must be strictly later than the leg's current `scheduled_departure_utc` (otherwise `ERR_SCHEDULE_NOT_MONOTONIC`) and the new arrival must be strictly after the new departure (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_TIME_RANGE`). The leg moves `SCHEDULED → DELAYED`, both scheduled instants are overwritten with the supplied values and `block_minutes` is recomputed. Then, in the first cascade hop, every routing slot that is `RESERVED` and that immediately follows a slot on this leg for the same animal — that is, whose `sequence_index` is one greater than that of a slot on this leg belonging to the same animal record — has its `connection_minutes` and `required_welfare_check_count` recomputed by the connection welfare window. In the second hop, every such slot whose recomputed `connection_minutes` has fallen below the connection minimum for its own `routing_type`, or has risen above the connection maximum, moves `RESERVED → RELEASED`, decrementing the booked counter for its routing on its own leg — which is generally a different leg from the delayed one — and re-deriving that leg's posture; a slot whose recomputed `connection_minutes` came out null, because the preceding slot of that animal is no longer live, is left alone. In the third hop, every reservation that owned a slot released in the second hop has its `booking_status` recomputed to `HOLD_PARTIAL` if it still retains at least one slot in `RESERVED` or `ACCEPTED` and to `REBOOK_REQUIRED` otherwise, has its `refunded_amount` incremented by each released slot's `segment_fee_amount`, and has the charge assembly re-run. The delayed leg's own `version` increments by one.

A delayed leg can be brought back. Restoring the schedule requires the leg to be `DELAYED` and a `restored_departure_utc` strictly earlier than the leg's current `scheduled_departure_utc` — a restore that does not move the departure earlier is `ERR_SCHEDULE_NOT_MONOTONIC` — with a `restored_arrival_utc` strictly after it. The leg moves `DELAYED → SCHEDULED`, both instants and `block_minutes` are restored to the supplied values, `connection_minutes` and `required_welfare_check_count` are recomputed for downstream slots in `RESERVED` or `ACCEPTED`, embargo determination and posture derivation re-run, and the leg's `version` increments by one. Slots that were already released during the delay are **not** restored; the `SCHEDULED → DELAYED → SCHEDULED` loop recovers the schedule, not the bookings.

Cancelling a leg is permitted from `SCHEDULED` or from `DELAYED`, both edges producing an identical cascade, with an optional `reason`. The leg's `status` becomes `CANCELLED` and its `posture` is set to `CLOSED`. Every routing slot on the leg that is `RESERVED` moves to `RELEASED` and every slot that is `ACCEPTED` moves to `OFFLOADED`, with `booked_cabin_count`, `booked_checked_count` and `booked_cargo_count` decremented accordingly; each animal that had an accepted slot returns `IN_TRANSIT → TRAVEL_READY`. Every affected reservation then has its `booking_status` recomputed to `HOLD_PARTIAL` or `REBOOK_REQUIRED` on the same retained-slot test as above — subject to that rule's own source-state guard in §3.5, so a reservation already `IN_TRANSIT` keeps `IN_TRANSIT` and is never driven to `INTERRUPTED` by a leg cancellation — has `refunded_amount` incremented by each released or offloaded slot's `segment_fee_amount`, and has the charge assembly re-run. The leg's `version` increments by one, unconditionally, as it also does on a delay, a restore, a departure and an arrival. Any attempt to delay, restore, cancel, depart, arrive, re-forecast a leg, or reserve a slot on it, when the leg's `status` does not permit that operation, is refused with `ERR_FLIGHT_LEG_INVALID_STATE`.

### 3.4. Opening a Booking: Reservations, Animals and Screening

A `ReservationsAgent` opens a **reservation** with a `passenger_name` identifying the travelling passenger or shipper of record, a `contact_email` to which hold and rebooking notices are sent, checked only for being a string of at least three characters and never parsed as an address, a `routing_type` of `CABIN`, `CHECKED` or `CARGO` that fixes the carriage mode for every animal on the booking, and an optional `is_service_animal_booking` flag defaulting to false which marks the booking as governed by service animal policy rather than pet policy. On creation the service stamps `booked_by_agent_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`, sets `booking_status` to `PENDING` and `compliance_status` to `UNVERIFIED`, sets `animal_count` to 0, and initialises `subtotal_amount`, `irops_fee_total`, `loyalty_tier_pct`, `loyalty_discount_amount`, `refunded_amount` and `net_payable_amount` all to 0.

A reservation carries **two independent state dimensions on the same row**. The commercial dimension, `booking_status`, ranges over `PENDING`, `CONFIRMED`, `HOLD_PARTIAL`, `REBOOK_REQUIRED`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `INTERRUPTED`, `COMPLETED` and `CANCELLED`, with `COMPLETED` and `CANCELLED` terminal. The documentation dimension, `compliance_status`, ranges over `UNVERIFIED`, `IN_REVIEW`, `VERIFIED`, `DEFICIENT` and `EXPIRED`. The two evolve independently: every commercial transition described in this section leaves `compliance_status` untouched, and every compliance transition in §3.6 leaves `booking_status` untouched. The commercial edges *read* the compliance dimension — confirmation demands `VERIFIED` — but they never write it.

Each **animal record** is added to a reservation by a `ReservationsAgent`, who supplies the `reservation_id` of the owning booking, the `species_profile_id` of the species, the `container_model_id` of the container the animal will travel in, an `animal_name`, a declared `breed_name`, an optional `animal_kind` of `PET` or `SERVICE_ANIMAL` defaulting to `PET`, a `declared_weight_kg` strictly greater than 0, a `height_at_shoulder_cm` and a `length_nose_to_tail_cm` each strictly greater than 0 giving the standing shoulder height and the nose-to-tail-base length, an `age_weeks` of at least 0, and an optional `behavior_attestation_on_file` flag defaulting to false. The animal is written with `status` `DRAFT` and `container_compliance_state` `UNEVALUATED`, its `measured_weight_kg` null, its `chargeable_weight_kg` 0 and its `deficiency_codes` an empty list; the reservation's `animal_count` is recomputed and the reservation's `version` increments by one. Adding an animal of a retired species is refused with `ERR_SPECIES_RETIRED`. The owning reservation's `booking_status` gates nothing here: an animal may be added to a reservation in any `booking_status`, including `CANCELLED` and `COMPLETED`, and doing so runs no charge assembly.

An animal record can be amended, by either a `ReservationsAgent` or an `AcceptanceAgent`, supplying any subset of `container_model_id`, `declared_weight_kg`, `measured_weight_kg` and `behavior_attestation_on_file`. This is how the measured weight reaches the record before counter acceptance, and how a rejected container is swapped. The amendment is permitted from **every** animal `status`, terminal ones included, and supplying `measured_weight_kg` as an explicit null clears a measured weight already on file. When the amendment changes `container_model_id` or `declared_weight_kg` to a value different from the one already stored, the animal's `container_compliance_state` resets to `UNEVALUATED` and its `deficiency_codes` are cleared, which means the animal must be screened again before it can be marked travel-ready; re-supplying the value already stored resets neither. The amendment recomputes no money and no weight of its own: `chargeable_weight_kg`, every slot's `segment_fee_amount` and `fee_variant`, and the reservation's charge assembly are all left exactly as they stand until the next screening or acceptance rewrites them. The animal's `version` increments by one, unconditionally, even when the body is empty or supplies only values the row already held.

An animal's `status` runs `DRAFT`, `SCREENED`, `TRAVEL_READY`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `DELIVERED`, `DENIED` and `DECEASED`, with `DELIVERED`, `DENIED` and `DECEASED` terminal. **Screening**, performed by an `AcceptanceAgent`, moves the animal to `SCREENED` from either `DRAFT` or `TRAVEL_READY`; the second of those is the re-screening loop `SCREENED → TRAVEL_READY → SCREENED` that a container or weight change forces. Screening is where the acceptance-criteria gates live, and they are evaluated in the ladder order of §2.6: the species profile's `status` must be `ACTIVE` (`ERR_SPECIES_RETIRED`), its `acceptance_class` must not be `EMBARGOED_SPECIES` (`ERR_SPECIES_NOT_ACCEPTED`), no `ACTIVE` breed restriction of `restriction_type` `BANNED` may match the animal's `breed_name` case-insensitively (`ERR_BREED_BANNED`), the animal's `age_weeks` must be at least the profile's `min_age_weeks` (`ERR_ANIMAL_UNDERAGE`), the routing must be permitted for the breed, and any `WEIGHT_CAPPED` cap must be respected. The routing gate reads both levels of configuration: when the species profile's `acceptance_class` is `CARGO_ONLY`, or when an `ACTIVE` breed restriction of `restriction_type` `CARGO_ONLY` matches the breed, the owning reservation's `routing_type` must be `CARGO`, and a cabin or checked booking for such an animal is refused with `ERR_ROUTING_NOT_PERMITTED_FOR_BREED`. The weight-cap gate compares the animal's gross weight — the weight basis in force plus the container model's `tare_weight_kg` — against the matching `WEIGHT_CAPPED` restriction's `max_weight_kg`, and a gross weight above that ceiling is refused with `ERR_BREED_WEIGHT_CAP_EXCEEDED`. That same cap is re-checked at counter acceptance, when the measured weight replaces the declared one.

When the gates pass, screening does three things in sequence. It runs the container compliance evaluation, overwriting `container_compliance_state` and `deficiency_codes`. It runs the travel fee in its provisional variant, overwriting `chargeable_weight_kg` and refreshing `segment_fee_amount` and `fee_variant` on every routing slot of the animal that is still `RESERVED`. And it re-runs the reservation charge assembly, refreshing `subtotal_amount`, `loyalty_tier_pct`, `loyalty_discount_amount` and `net_payable_amount` on the owning reservation. The animal's `version` increments by one.

**Container compliance evaluation** is a constraint-propagation pass that compares the chosen container model against the animal's dimensions and the reservation's routing. Four requirement thresholds are derived first. The required interior height in centimetres is the animal's `height_at_shoulder_cm` plus eight centimetres of standing clearance, ceiled to a whole centimetre. The required interior length in centimetres is the animal's `length_nose_to_tail_cm` multiplied by one and fifteen hundredths, ceiled to a whole centimetre. The required ventilation percentage is eight, plus the animal's `chargeable_weight_kg` divided by five and ceiled to a whole number — so a twelve-kilogram chargeable weight requires eleven percent and a fifteen-kilogram chargeable weight also requires eleven percent, while sixteen kilograms requires twelve. The required number of ventilated sides is two when `chargeable_weight_kg` is at most twenty, three when it is above twenty but at most thirty-two, and four above thirty-two. The gross and chargeable weights used here are the step-one values of the travel fee for the variant in force at screening time, which is the declared weight.

The evaluation then appends deficiency codes to `deficiency_codes` in **exactly this order**, skipping any test that does not fail. If the container model's `interior_height_cm` is strictly less than the required height, it appends `HEIGHT`. If its `interior_length_cm` is strictly less than the required length, it appends `LENGTH`. If its `ventilation_area_pct` is strictly less than the required ventilation percentage, it appends `VENT_PCT`. If its `ventilated_side_count` is strictly less than the required number of ventilated sides, it appends `VENT_SIDES`. If the reservation's `routing_type` is `CABIN` and the animal's gross weight is strictly greater than the species profile's `max_cabin_weight_kg`, it appends `GROSS_WEIGHT`. If the routing is `CABIN` and the container model's `cabin_eligible` is false, it appends `CABIN_DIM`. If the routing is `CARGO` and the container model's `wheels_removable` is false, it appends `WHEELS`. All four dimensional comparisons are strict, so a container that exactly meets a requirement passes it. When the resulting list is empty the animal's `container_compliance_state` becomes `COMPLIANT`; when it holds at least one code the state becomes `DEFICIENT`. Note that `interior_width_cm` constrains no deficiency code directly — it enters the picture only through the volumetric weight in the fee calculation.

A screened animal is promoted by an `AcceptanceAgent` on the `SCREENED → TRAVEL_READY` edge. Four gates apply, and they fire in this order. The animal's `container_compliance_state` must be `COMPLIANT`, and a `DEFICIENT` or `UNEVALUATED` state is refused with `ERR_CONTAINER_NON_COMPLIANT`. Then policy forks on `animal_kind`: when the animal is a `SERVICE_ANIMAL`, its `behavior_attestation_on_file` must be true, a false attestation being refused with `ERR_BEHAVIOR_ATTESTATION_MISSING`, and the import-document gate does not apply; when the animal is a `PET`, the attestation flag is ignored entirely and instead every `ACTIVE` destination requirement with `mandatory` true for the animal's species and destination country must be backed by a health document in `VERIFIED` status, a missing one being refused with `ERR_REQUIRED_DOCUMENT_MISSING` and a too-old one with `ERR_DOCUMENT_STALE_FOR_DESTINATION`. The destination country and the arrival instant this gate reads are those of the destination station and `scheduled_arrival_utc` of the leg under the animal's highest-`sequence_index` non-`RELEASED` slot — the same final-slot basis the compliance sweep of §3.6 uses — so an animal holding no live slot at all has no requirement to satisfy and passes this gate vacuously. Last of the four, and therefore after the container and paperwork gates rather than before them, the animal must already hold at least one routing slot in `RESERVED` at `sequence_index` 1, an animal without one being refused with `ERR_ANIMAL_INVALID_STATE` even though it is the animal's slots and not its `status` that fall short. Service animal policy therefore substitutes the behaviour attestation for the paperwork gate on this edge, and nothing else. Promotion recomputes the reservation's `animal_count` — which the `SCREENED → TRAVEL_READY` move never actually changes, so the reservation's `version` does not advance — and increments the animal's `version` by one.

An `AcceptanceAgent` may instead **deny** carriage, from `DRAFT` or from `SCREENED`, with an optional `reason`. The animal's `status` becomes `DENIED`, every routing slot of the animal that is `RESERVED` moves to `RELEASED` with the owning leg's counter for that routing decremented and that leg's posture re-derived, and the reservation's `animal_count` and charge assembly are recomputed. The animal's `version` increments by one. Any screen, promote, deny or downstream operation attempted from a status that does not permit it is refused with `ERR_ANIMAL_INVALID_STATE`.

**Confirming** a reservation is a `ReservationsAgent` action available from `PENDING`, from `HOLD_PARTIAL` and from `REBOOK_REQUIRED`, and the preconditions differ meaningfully between the three. From `PENDING`, the reservation's `compliance_status` must be `VERIFIED`, its `animal_count` must be at least 1, every animal whose `status` is not `DENIED` must be `TRAVEL_READY`, and every such animal must hold at least one routing slot in `RESERVED`. From `REBOOK_REQUIRED`, the `compliance_status` must be `VERIFIED` and every non-`DENIED` animal must hold at least one `RESERVED` slot on a leg whose `posture` is `OPEN` or `RESTRICTED` — an embargoed or closed leg does not satisfy the rebooking, which is what closes the `CONFIRMED → REBOOK_REQUIRED → CONFIRMED` loop without letting a booking re-confirm onto a leg that cannot carry it. From `HOLD_PARTIAL`, the `compliance_status` must be `VERIFIED` and every non-`DENIED` animal must hold a **contiguous** chain of `RESERVED` slots beginning at `sequence_index` 1, so a partially-held itinerary with a gap in the middle cannot be re-confirmed. The `animal_count` of at least 1 and the per-animal slot tests are demanded on all three edges, not on `PENDING` alone, and each of them — an empty booking, an animal with no `RESERVED` slot, an animal with no `RESERVED` slot on an open leg, an animal whose `RESERVED` chain is not contiguous from 1 — is reported as `ERR_RESERVATION_INVALID_STATE` (409), evaluated after both the per-animal `TRAVEL_READY` test and the `compliance_status` test. In all three cases the reservation moves to `CONFIRMED`, the charge assembly re-runs and the reservation's `version` increments by one; a `compliance_status` other than `VERIFIED` is refused with `ERR_COMPLIANCE_NOT_VERIFIED`, and a `booking_status` outside the permitted set with `ERR_RESERVATION_INVALID_STATE`.

**Cancelling** a reservation is permitted from `PENDING`, `CONFIRMED`, `HOLD_PARTIAL` or `REBOOK_REQUIRED`, with an optional `reason`, and every one of those edges behaves identically. The `booking_status` becomes `CANCELLED`. Every routing slot of every animal on the reservation that is `RESERVED` moves to `RELEASED`, with the owning leg's booked counter decremented and its posture re-derived. Every kennel allocation whose animal belongs to this reservation and whose `status` is `RESERVED` or `OCCUPIED` moves to `RELEASED`, decrementing that station's `reserved_bay_count` by the allocation's `bay_count`. The reservation's `refunded_amount` is incremented by half of its `subtotal_amount`, rounded half up. The charge assembly then re-runs, so the money identity still holds after the cancellation. The reservation's `version` increments by one.

### 3.5. Reserving Routing Slots: Limits, Sequencing and Connection Welfare Windows

A **routing slot** — a segment assignment — is the reservation of one carriage position for one animal on one flight leg. A `ReservationsAgent` creates one by supplying the `animal_record_id` of the animal, the `flight_leg_id` of the leg, the `routing_type` occupied, a `sequence_index` of at least 1 giving the one-based position of this slot in the animal's itinerary, and `expected_flight_leg_version`. The slot is written with `status` `RESERVED`; the slot lifecycle runs `RESERVED`, `ACCEPTED`, `COMPLETED`, `OFFLOADED` and `RELEASED`, with the last three terminal along with `COMPLETED`.

Creation is the most heavily guarded write in the service, and its checks fire in ladder order. The leg's `status` must be `SCHEDULED` or `DELAYED` to take a new slot, a `CANCELLED`, `DEPARTED` or `ARRIVED` leg being refused with `ERR_FLIGHT_LEG_INVALID_STATE`, and the animal's `status` must be `DRAFT`, `SCREENED` or `TRAVEL_READY` (`ERR_ANIMAL_INVALID_STATE`). The `expected_flight_leg_version` gate of §2.9 fires before any business check, the leg-status and animal-status gates included. The owning reservation's `booking_status` gates nothing here. The leg must not be embargoed for this routing: when the leg's `posture` is `EMBARGOED` and the governing rule's `applies_to_routings` contains the requested `routing_type`, the create is refused with `ERR_LEG_EMBARGOED`; an embargoed leg whose governing rule suppresses only cargo still accepts a cabin slot. Both the leg's origin and destination stations must be `ACCEPTING` (`ERR_STATION_SUSPENDED`). The requested `routing_type` must equal the owning reservation's `routing_type`, since a booking travels in one mode throughout, and a mismatch is `ERR_ROUTING_MISMATCH`. The `sequence_index` must be 1 for the animal's first slot and otherwise exactly one greater than the animal's current highest non-`RELEASED` `sequence_index`, and must not already be taken by a non-`RELEASED` slot; a skipped or duplicated position is `ERR_SEGMENT_SEQUENCE_INVALID`. The animal must not already hold another non-`RELEASED` slot whose leg's interval from `scheduled_departure_utc` to `scheduled_arrival_utc` intersects the requested leg's interval, where both intervals are **closed** so that merely touching endpoints already count as an intersection; an overlap is `ERR_ANIMAL_ITINERARY_OVERLAP`. Finally the routing limit is checked: the leg's booked count for the requested routing, plus one, must be at most the leg's corresponding limit, and a full routing is refused with `ERR_ROUTING_LIMIT_REACHED`.

Once the slot is written, the service increments the leg's booked counter for that routing by one and re-derives the leg's posture, runs the **connection welfare window** to write `connection_minutes` and `required_welfare_check_count`, runs the travel fee to write `segment_fee_amount` and `fee_variant`, re-runs the reservation charge assembly, and increments the leg's `version` by one.

The connection welfare window turns the gap between two consecutive legs into a welfare obligation. For a slot at `sequence_index` 1 there is no preceding leg, so `connection_minutes` is null and `required_welfare_check_count` is 0. For any later slot, the service finds the slot of the same animal whose `sequence_index` is one less and whose `status` is not `RELEASED`, and sets `connection_minutes` to the number of whole minutes from that preceding leg's `scheduled_arrival_utc` to this leg's `scheduled_departure_utc`, discarding any fractional minute — floored, so a negative gap floors away from zero. When no such preceding slot exists, because the one that used to occupy `sequence_index` minus one has since been released, the later slot is treated exactly like a first slot: `connection_minutes` is null, `required_welfare_check_count` is 0, and both connection bounds below are skipped rather than failed. That gap must be at least the connection minimum for the slot's own `routing_type` — one hour for `CABIN`, one and a half hours for `CHECKED`, and two hours for `CARGO`, since an animal travelling in the hold needs longer to be moved between aircraft — and a connection exactly equal to its minimum is accepted, while anything shorter is refused with `ERR_CONNECTION_BELOW_MINIMUM`. The gap must also not exceed twelve hours; a longer layover is refused with `ERR_CONNECTION_EXCEEDS_MAXIMUM`. `required_welfare_check_count` is then one, plus the `connection_minutes` divided by four hours with any remainder discarded — so a ninety-minute connection requires one check, a four-hour connection requires two, a seven-hour connection requires two, and an eight-hour connection requires three; a negative `connection_minutes`, which only a delay recompute can produce, is clamped to zero for this term and so yields one. The same computation re-runs whenever a delay or a schedule restore moves either leg of a connection.

A `ReservationsAgent` may **release** a slot that is `RESERVED`, with an optional `reason`; the same `RESERVED → RELEASED` edge is also driven by system cascades from embargo determination, leg delay, leg cancellation, animal denial and reservation cancellation. The slot's `status` becomes `RELEASED`, the owning leg's booked counter for its routing is decremented by one and the leg's posture is re-derived, the owning reservation's `refunded_amount` is incremented by the slot's `segment_fee_amount`, the reservation's `booking_status` is recomputed to `HOLD_PARTIAL` or `REBOOK_REQUIRED`, the charge assembly re-runs, and the leg's `version` increments by one. Two of those cascades suppress exactly two of those consequences: in the animal-denial cascade of §3.4 and in the reservation-cancellation cascade of §3.4 the per-slot `refunded_amount` credit and the `booking_status` recomputation are both skipped — each of those workflows carries its own money and status rules — while the release itself, the booked-counter decrement, the posture re-derivation, the charge-assembly re-run and the leg's `version` increment happen exactly as described here. A release or offload attempted against a slot whose `status` does not permit it is refused with `ERR_SEGMENT_INVALID_STATE`.

The recomputation of `booking_status` after any release is a single rule — the one rule every cascade in this specification invokes wherever it says a release, or a leg cancellation, drives or recomputes the owning reservation's `booking_status` to `HOLD_PARTIAL` or `REBOOK_REQUIRED` — and it is what produces the reservation's hold states. The rule runs only while the reservation's `booking_status` is `PENDING`, `CONFIRMED`, `HOLD_PARTIAL` or `REBOOK_REQUIRED`; a reservation in any other `booking_status` — `IN_TRANSIT`, `INTERRUPTED`, `COMPLETED` or `CANCELLED` — keeps the `booking_status` it already has. When at least one slot of the reservation moved `RESERVED → RELEASED` in the current request and the reservation still retains at least one slot in `RESERVED` or `ACCEPTED`, the reservation moves to `HOLD_PARTIAL`: the `CONFIRMED → HOLD_PARTIAL` edge, and equally `PENDING → HOLD_PARTIAL` and `REBOOK_REQUIRED → HOLD_PARTIAL` from those two source states. When no slot of the reservation remains in `RESERVED` or `ACCEPTED`, it moves to `REBOOK_REQUIRED` — directly from `CONFIRMED` or from `PENDING`, or from `HOLD_PARTIAL` when the last remaining reserved slot of an already-held reservation is released. In every one of those system edges the released slot's `segment_fee_amount` is added to `refunded_amount` and the charge assembly re-runs. Refunds are cumulative and are never reversed: re-confirming a rebooked reservation does not claw back what was already refunded.

### 3.6. Health Documentation and the Compliance Dimension

A `ReservationsAgent` submits a **health document** for an animal by supplying the `animal_record_id` it certifies, the `document_type` presented, the `issuing_country_code` of the issuing authority as exactly two uppercase letters, an `issued_at` instant and an `expires_at` instant that must be strictly after the issue instant (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_TIME_RANGE`). Submission is permitted while the animal's `status` is `DRAFT`, `SCREENED` or `TRAVEL_READY`. The document is written with `status` `SUBMITTED`, `revision_count` 0 and `reviewed_by_user_id` null. A document's lifecycle runs `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `VERIFIED`, `REJECTED` and `EXPIRED`, with `VERIFIED` and `EXPIRED` terminal.

A `VeterinaryReviewer` takes a `SUBMITTED` document **under review**, which is permitted only while the animal's `status` is not `DENIED`, `DECEASED` or `DELIVERED`; the document-status gate is evaluated first, so a document that is not `SUBMITTED` reports `ERR_DOCUMENT_INVALID_STATE` even when the animal is also terminal, and only a `SUBMITTED` document on a terminal animal reports `ERR_ANIMAL_INVALID_STATE`. The document moves `SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW`, its `version` increments by one, and the owning reservation's `compliance_status` is set to `IN_REVIEW` when it is currently `UNVERIFIED`, `DEFICIENT` or `EXPIRED` — the `UNVERIFIED → IN_REVIEW`, `DEFICIENT → IN_REVIEW` and `EXPIRED → IN_REVIEW` edges of the compliance dimension, each of which leaves `booking_status` untouched. Starting a review on a resubmitted document is how a deficient booking re-enters review, and starting one on a replacement document is how an expired booking does.

**Verifying** a document under review has two possible outcomes decided by the logical clock, and both return HTTP 200 with the resulting `status` on the document distinguishing them. Before either, the document's `issuing_country_code` must equal the `country_code` of the origin station of the animal's `sequence_index` 1 slot, that slot being the animal's non-`RELEASED` one at that index; a mismatch is refused with `ERR_DOCUMENT_ISSUER_MISMATCH`, while an animal that holds no live `sequence_index` 1 slot has nothing to compare against and skips the gate entirely rather than failing it. Because only a `VeterinaryReviewer` may call the endpoint at all, `requires_veterinary_review` imposes no further test here. Verification writes only the document; it never moves the owning reservation's `compliance_status`, which the sweep below is the sole route to `VERIFIED`. If the document's `expires_at` is strictly after the logical clock, the document moves `UNDER_REVIEW → VERIFIED`. If its `expires_at` is at or before the logical clock, the document moves `UNDER_REVIEW → EXPIRED` instead, which is terminal, and a replacement must be submitted. In both outcomes `reviewed_by_user_id` is stamped with the acting `X-User-ID` and the document's `version` increments by one.

**Rejecting** a document under review, with an optional `rejection_reason`, moves it `UNDER_REVIEW → REJECTED`, stamps `reviewed_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`, and increments the document's `version` by one. It also drives two other rows: the owning reservation's `compliance_status` is set to `DEFICIENT` from whatever value it held, `UNVERIFIED` and `EXPIRED` included — chiefly the `IN_REVIEW → DEFICIENT` and `VERIFIED → DEFICIENT` edges, all of which leave `booking_status` untouched, and none of which advances the reservation's `version` when `DEFICIENT` is already the value on the row — and the owning animal returns `TRAVEL_READY → SCREENED` if it was `TRAVEL_READY`, so a booking that had cleared its paperwork gate must clear it again.

A `ReservationsAgent` **resubmits** a `REJECTED` document with a fresh `issued_at` and `expires_at`. The document's `revision_count` must be strictly less than three; a fourth attempt is refused with `ERR_DOC_REVISION_LIMIT_REACHED`. The document moves `REJECTED → SUBMITTED`, `revision_count` increments by one, both instants are overwritten with the supplied values under the same strict ordering rule, `reviewed_by_user_id` is cleared to null, and the document's `version` increments by one. The `SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW → REJECTED → SUBMITTED` loop is therefore capped at three revisions.

The **compliance sweep** is a `VeterinaryReviewer` action on a whole reservation, and it is the only way the reservation reaches `VERIFIED`. It reads no precondition at all: it is available from every `compliance_status`, `UNVERIFIED` and `DEFICIENT` as much as `IN_REVIEW`, `VERIFIED` and `EXPIRED`, and from every `booking_status`. For every animal on the reservation whose `status` is not `DENIED`, the sweep finds every `ACTIVE` destination requirement with `mandatory` true whose `species_profile_id` matches the animal's species and whose `destination_country_code` matches the country of the destination station of the animal's highest-`sequence_index` slot, and demands that a health document of that `document_type` exists in `VERIFIED` status whose `issued_at` is at most `max_age_days_at_arrival` whole days before that slot's leg `scheduled_arrival_utc`, the whole-day count being the elapsed seconds from issue to arrival divided by 86 400 with any remainder discarded, compared inclusively. An animal that holds no non-`RELEASED` slot has no final leg and therefore contributes no demand; a reservation carrying no non-`DENIED` animal, or none whose species and destination raise a mandatory requirement, has an empty demand set and so sweeps straight to `VERIFIED`. The sweep has three possible results, and its two document tests are applied set-wide in ladder order: every demand across every animal is checked for the mere existence of a `VERIFIED` document first, and only once all of them are satisfied is any of them checked for freshness. If any demanded document is absent, the request fails with `ERR_REQUIRED_DOCUMENT_MISSING` and **nothing at all is written** — the operation is atomic all-or-nothing, and no field on the reservation, the animals or the documents changes. If a demanded document exists in `VERIFIED` status but was issued too long before arrival, the request fails with `ERR_DOCUMENT_STALE_FOR_DESTINATION`, again writing nothing. If every demanded document exists and is young enough, the sweep then examines expiry over exactly the set of documents that satisfied the freshness test — where several `VERIFIED` documents of one demanded type are young enough, all of them are in that backing set, and a `VERIFIED` document too old for the destination is excluded from it and so is never expired by the sweep: if every one of them has `expires_at` strictly after the logical clock, the reservation's `compliance_status` becomes `VERIFIED`; if at least one has `expires_at` at or before the logical clock, each such document moves `VERIFIED → EXPIRED` and the reservation's `compliance_status` becomes `EXPIRED`. Both of those outcomes return HTTP 200 with the reservation's `compliance_status` distinguishing them, and both leave `booking_status` untouched. The `VERIFIED → EXPIRED` compliance edge is exactly this case reached from an already-verified reservation after the logical clock has advanced past a backing document's `expires_at`. Every sweep increments the reservation's `version` by one.

### 3.7. Counter Acceptance, Kennel Holding and Flight Operations

**Acceptance at the counter** is the moment the animal and its container are physically checked in, and it is barred to the agent who sold the booking: an `AcceptanceAgent` whose `X-User-ID` equals the owning reservation's `booked_by_agent_id` is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. Beyond that, a slot moves `RESERVED → ACCEPTED` only when the leg's `status` is `SCHEDULED` or `DELAYED`, the animal's `status` is `TRAVEL_READY`, the owning reservation's `booking_status` is `CONFIRMED` or `HOLD_PARTIAL`, the slot's own `status` is `RESERVED` (otherwise `ERR_SEGMENT_INVALID_STATE`), the reservation's `compliance_status` is `VERIFIED` (otherwise `ERR_COMPLIANCE_NOT_VERIFIED`), the animal's `container_compliance_state` is `COMPLIANT` (otherwise `ERR_CONTAINER_NON_COMPLIANT`), and the animal's `measured_weight_kg` is present (otherwise `ERR_MEASURED_WEIGHT_REQUIRED`). Acceptance also re-runs the two weight gates against the measured weight rather than the declared one: a matching `WEIGHT_CAPPED` breed restriction's `max_weight_kg` must still be respected (`ERR_BREED_WEIGHT_CAP_EXCEEDED`), and on `CABIN` routing the gross weight must be at most the species profile's `max_cabin_weight_kg` (`ERR_CABIN_WEIGHT_EXCEEDED`); both are simply skipped when no measured weight is on file, so that the missing-weight refusal wins on that path. Posture is read only through the embargo test: an embargo that suppresses this routing blocks acceptance with `ERR_LEG_EMBARGOED`, while a leg whose `posture` is `EMBARGOED` under a rule that leaves this routing alone accepts normally, and a `RESTRICTED` posture is no obstacle. The leg-`status` gate is the one gate that fires **last** rather than in its ladder position: it is evaluated after segregation of duties, after the reservation, animal and slot status gates, after both weight gates, after the container gate, after the embargo gate, after the measured-weight gate and after the compliance gate, so a `CANCELLED`, `DEPARTED` or `ARRIVED` leg reports `ERR_FLIGHT_LEG_INVALID_STATE` only when nothing else is wrong. On success the slot's `status` becomes `ACCEPTED`, the travel fee runs in its final variant over **this slot only**, `AnimalRecord.chargeable_weight_kg` is recomputed from `measured_weight_kg`, the charge assembly re-runs, and the slot's `version` increments by one.

**Kennel provisioning** covers animals held on the ground between legs or during disruption. An `AcceptanceAgent` reserves bays by supplying the `station_id` providing them, the `animal_record_id` to be held, a `bay_count` of at least 1, an `occupancy_start_utc` and an `occupancy_end_utc` strictly after it (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_TIME_RANGE`), a `feeding_interval_minutes` between 60 and 720 inclusive giving the maximum minutes permitted between feedings while the animal is held, and `expected_station_version`. The animal's `status` must be `SCREENED`, `TRAVEL_READY` or `IN_TRANSIT`, and the station must be `ACCEPTING`. The occupancy interval is half-open: the start instant is inclusive and the end instant is exclusive.

Capacity is enforced by a **peak-occupancy projection** rather than a simple current-count comparison, because every allocation reserves its bays for a sanitation window of forty-five minutes after its stated end. The effective end of any allocation is therefore its `occupancy_end_utc` plus forty-five minutes. An existing allocation at the same station whose `status` is `RESERVED` or `OCCUPIED` overlaps the candidate when its `occupancy_start_utc` is strictly before the candidate's effective end **and** its own effective end is strictly after the candidate's `occupancy_start_utc`. The projected peak is the candidate's `bay_count` plus the sum of `bay_count` over all overlapping allocations, and the create is accepted only when that projected peak is at most the station's `kennel_bay_count`; exact equality is accepted, and anything greater is refused with `ERR_KENNEL_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. Because both interval comparisons are strict, an allocation starting exactly at another allocation's effective end does not overlap it. Allocations whose `status` is `RELEASED` are excluded from the projection entirely and free their bays immediately, without a sanitation window. On success the station's `reserved_bay_count` becomes the sum of `bay_count` over its `RESERVED` and `OCCUPIED` allocations — which is to say it grows by the new allocation's `bay_count` — and the station's `version` increments by one.

An allocation's `status` runs `RESERVED`, `OCCUPIED` and `RELEASED`, the last terminal. An `AcceptanceAgent` moves it `RESERVED → OCCUPIED` once the logical clock has reached or passed its `occupancy_start_utc` and while the animal's `status` is `TRAVEL_READY` or `IN_TRANSIT`; the three gates fire in that order, a status other than `RESERVED` and a window that has not yet opened both reporting `ERR_ALLOCATION_INVALID_STATE` and an animal outside those two statuses reporting `ERR_ANIMAL_INVALID_STATE`. Occupying changes no station counter, because the allocation was already consuming its `bay_count` bays while merely reserved, and it increments the allocation's `version` by one but not the station's. Releasing is permitted from `RESERVED` and from `OCCUPIED`, with an optional `reason`, and both edges behave identically: the allocation moves to `RELEASED`, the station's `reserved_bay_count` is decremented by the allocation's `bay_count`, and the station's `version` increments by one. Releases are also driven as system cascades from a death incident and from reservation cancellation. An occupy or release against a status that does not permit it is refused with `ERR_ALLOCATION_INVALID_STATE`.

**Welfare handling during transfers** is recorded as append-only welfare checks. An `AcceptanceAgent` appends one by supplying the `segment_assignment_id` of the slot during whose transfer the action was performed, a `check_type` of `WATER`, `FEED`, `VISUAL` or `SANITATION`, an optional `outcome` of `NORMAL` or `CONCERN` defaulting to `NORMAL`, and an optional free-text `notes` defaulting to null. The slot's `status` must be `RESERVED` or `ACCEPTED`; a `COMPLETED`, `OFFLOADED` or `RELEASED` slot takes no further check and is refused with `ERR_SEGMENT_INVALID_STATE`. Nothing else gates the append — not the leg's status, not the animal's, not the reservation's, and not the logical clock's position relative to the leg's schedule. The service stamps `recorded_at_utc` from the logical clock and `performed_by_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`, increments the slot's `completed_welfare_check_count`, and increments the slot's `version` by one; the append answers HTTP 201 with the welfare check itself in `data`. A welfare check is never updated after it is written.

**Departing a leg** is an `AcceptanceAgent` action available from `SCHEDULED` and from `DELAYED`, with identical preconditions and side effects on both edges. The leg's `posture` must not be `EMBARGOED` (`ERR_LEG_EMBARGOED`), every slot on the leg in `ACCEPTED` must have a `completed_welfare_check_count` of at least its `required_welfare_check_count` (`ERR_WELFARE_CHECKS_INCOMPLETE`), and only then may no slot on the leg remain in `RESERVED` (`ERR_LEG_HAS_UNACCEPTED_SEGMENTS`) — the welfare gate sits above the unaccepted-slot gate, as the ladder of §2.6 has it, so a leg carrying both an under-checked accepted slot and a still-reserved slot reports the welfare failure. The leg's own transition is thus gated on the aggregate state of its slots. On success the leg moves to `DEPARTED`, every animal holding an `ACCEPTED` slot on the leg moves `TRAVEL_READY → IN_TRANSIT` (an animal in any other status is left as it stands), every reservation owning such an animal moves to `IN_TRANSIT` from `CONFIRMED` **or** from `HOLD_PARTIAL` and is left alone from any other `booking_status`, posture derivation writes `CLOSED`, and the leg's `version` increments by one.

**Arriving a leg** requires the leg's `status` to be `DEPARTED` and moves it to `ARRIVED`. Every slot on the leg in `ACCEPTED` moves to `COMPLETED`. For each of those slots whose `sequence_index` equals the maximum `sequence_index` among the animal's non-`RELEASED` slots — that is, the animal's final leg — the animal moves `IN_TRANSIT → DELIVERED`. The `IN_TRANSIT → DELIVERED` move applies only to an animal actually in `IN_TRANSIT`; and "final leg" is judged against the animal's non-`RELEASED` slots as they stand before this leg's slots are marked `COMPLETED`. Then the completion bubbles up: a reservation whose `booking_status` is `IN_TRANSIT`, which carries at least one animal whose `status` is other than `DENIED`, and every one of whose such animals is `DELIVERED`, moves `IN_TRANSIT → COMPLETED`, and the charge assembly runs one final time for exactly those reservations that made the move. For an animal that is *not* on its final slot, no kennel allocation is created automatically; a hold for the onward connection must be provisioned as a separate act. The leg's `version` increments by one.

### 3.8. Irregular Operations, Charge Assembly and the Money Identity

The **travel fee** prices one animal on one leg, and it exists in two variants that differ in exactly one input. The provisional variant, `PROVISIONAL_AT_RESERVATION`, runs at screening time before the animal is physically weighed and takes its weight basis from `declared_weight_kg`; it rewrites every slot of the animal that is `RESERVED` and stamps `fee_variant` as `PROVISIONAL_AT_RESERVATION` on each. The final variant, `FINAL_AT_ACCEPTANCE`, runs when the animal is on the scale at the counter and takes its weight basis from `measured_weight_kg`; it rewrites only the slot being accepted and stamps that slot's `fee_variant` as `FINAL_AT_ACCEPTANCE`. A multi-leg itinerary can therefore legitimately carry one finalised slot and one still-provisional slot at the same moment, and the reservation's `subtotal_amount` is the mixed-variant sum of both.

Given the weight basis, the fee is built in four pinned steps. First, the gross weight in kilograms is the weight basis plus the container model's `tare_weight_kg`, and the volumetric weight in kilograms is the product of the container model's `interior_length_cm`, `interior_width_cm` and `interior_height_cm` divided by six thousand. The animal's `chargeable_weight_kg` is the greater of the gross and volumetric weights, ceiled to a whole kilogram. Second, a non-linear weight component is derived from that chargeable weight with a free allowance and two marginal rates: the first ten kilograms are free, so a chargeable weight of ten kilograms or less contributes a weight component of zero; above ten kilograms, every kilogram from the eleventh up to and including the thirty-second is charged at 450 minor units, and every kilogram beyond the thirty-second is charged at 700 minor units. Concretely, the weight component is the lesser of the chargeable weight and thirty-two, minus ten, multiplied by 450, plus the excess of the chargeable weight over thirty-two where that excess is treated as zero when the chargeable weight is at or below thirty-two, multiplied by 700. Third, the segment fee starts as the base fee for the slot's `routing_type` — 12500 minor units for `CABIN`, 20000 for `CHECKED`, 27500 for `CARGO` — plus that weight component, and then two multipliers are applied **in this order**. If the animal's breed is snub-nosed and the slot's `routing_type` is not `CABIN`, the fee is multiplied by one and fifteen hundredths and rounded half up to whole minor units. If the leg's `block_minutes` is strictly greater than eight hours and the animal's `animal_kind` is `PET`, the resulting fee is multiplied by one and two tenths and rounded half up to whole minor units; a `SERVICE_ANIMAL` never attracts the long-haul multiplier. A breed counts as snub-nosed when an `ACTIVE` breed restriction exists whose `species_profile_id` equals the animal's `species_profile_id`, whose `restriction_type` is `SNUB_NOSED`, and whose lowercased `breed_name` equals the animal's lowercased `breed_name`. The result is written to the slot's `segment_fee_amount` as an integer in minor units. Fourth — and this is the rounding order that must not be inverted — the reservation's segment total is the sum of the already-rounded `segment_fee_amount` values across every slot of the reservation in any status. Slots in `RELEASED` or `OFFLOADED` are not re-priced and keep the last `segment_fee_amount` they computed, precisely so that the refund arithmetic has an amount to work with.

**Irregular-operations care** is charged by an `AcceptanceAgent` naming a `flight_leg_id` that has been disrupted. The sweep first assembles the affected set — every reservation owning a slot on that leg in `RESERVED` or `ACCEPTED`, together with the distinct animals contributing those slots — and then gates in a pinned order: every reservation in that set must have a `booking_status` of `PENDING`, `CONFIRMED`, `HOLD_PARTIAL` or `IN_TRANSIT` (`ERR_RESERVATION_INVALID_STATE`), and only afterwards must the leg's `status` be `SCHEDULED`, `DELAYED` or `DEPARTED` (`ERR_FLIGHT_LEG_INVALID_STATE`). Because the reservation gate is evaluated first, a `CANCELLED` leg still carrying an ineligible reservation reports the reservation failure, while the leg gate is checked even when the affected set is empty. For every reservation in the set, the reservation's `irops_fee_total` is incremented by 3500 minor units for each such animal, the charge assembly re-runs — which may push the reservation across a loyalty tier boundary — and the reservation's `version` increments by one. The sweep is deliberately **repeatable**: calling it again for the same leg charges again, because a second disruption incurs a second round of care. A sweep of an eligible leg that owns no `RESERVED` or `ACCEPTED` slot writes nothing at all and still succeeds, reporting an `affected_animal_count` and a `total_fee_charged` of zero. The sweep persists no resource of its own — it only rewrites the reservations it charges — so it answers HTTP 200, not 201, with a computed body carrying the `flight_leg_id` swept, the `affected_animal_count`, the `irops_fee_per_animal` applied and the `total_fee_charged` across all reservations.

**Charge assembly** reconciles the reservation's money fields, and it re-runs on every write that changes a slot's fee, releases or offloads a slot, appends an irregular-operations care fee, or refunds the booking — screening, slot creation, slot acceptance, slot release, slot offload, an irregular-operations care sweep, reservation confirmation, reservation cancellation, leg cancellation, leg delay and a forecast update. Its evaluation order is pinned and normative, because the dependency looks circular: the discount rate is keyed on the subtotal, and the subtotal includes the irregular-operations fees that can themselves push the subtotal across a tier boundary. No step may be re-entered after a later step has run.

1. `subtotal_amount` is the sum of `segment_fee_amount` over **every** slot belonging to **every** animal record of the reservation, in **any** status — released and offloaded slots stay in this gross sum and are offset through `refunded_amount` rather than removed, which is what keeps `net_payable_amount` from going negative — plus the reservation's `irops_fee_total`.
2. `loyalty_tier_pct` is one tenth when `subtotal_amount` is at least 100000 minor units, otherwise one twentieth when `subtotal_amount` is at least 65000 minor units, otherwise zero. Tier selection reads `subtotal_amount` alone and never the post-refund base. The stored field renders as the JSON number `0.1`, `0.05` or `0` respectively — the zero tier as a plain integer, the two discount tiers as those two decimal fractions.
3. `loyalty_discount_amount` is `subtotal_amount` minus `refunded_amount`, multiplied by `loyalty_tier_pct`, rounded half up to whole minor units. This is the single rounding point in the whole assembly.
4. `net_payable_amount` is `subtotal_amount` minus `refunded_amount` minus `loyalty_discount_amount`.

After every write, the identity `subtotal_amount` equals `net_payable_amount` plus `loyalty_discount_amount` plus `refunded_amount` must hold. `refunded_amount` and `irops_fee_total` are accumulators on the reservation row: they are incremented by the events that create them and are never recomputed from scratch, and `refunded_amount` is never reversed. A computed charge-summary read is available on any reservation and returns its `reservation_id`, `subtotal_amount`, `irops_fee_total`, `loyalty_tier_pct`, `loyalty_discount_amount`, `refunded_amount`, `net_payable_amount` and a boolean `invariant_holds` reporting whether that identity currently balances.

**Offloading** an `ACCEPTED` slot is an `AcceptanceAgent` action with an optional `reason`, also driven as a system cascade from leg cancellation and from a death incident. The slot moves `ACCEPTED → OFFLOADED`, the owning leg's booked counter for its routing is decremented by one and its posture re-derived, the reservation's `refunded_amount` is incremented by the slot's `segment_fee_amount`, the reservation's `booking_status` is set to `INTERRUPTED` when it was `IN_TRANSIT`, the charge assembly re-runs, and the leg's `version` increments by one. A slot that has already `COMPLETED` on arrival cannot be offloaded.

### 3.9. Incidents, Regulatory Disclosure and Investigation Close-out

An **animal incident** is opened by an `AcceptanceAgent` or a `ComplianceOfficer` naming the `animal_record_id` involved, optionally the `segment_assignment_id` of the slot during which the event was discovered — left null when the discovery happened on the ground — an `incident_type` of `INJURY`, `LOSS` or `DEATH`, and the `discovered_at_utc` instant that starts the regulatory disclosure clock. When a `segment_assignment_id` is supplied it must belong to the supplied `animal_record_id`, and a mismatch is refused with `ERR_SEGMENT_ANIMAL_MISMATCH`. The incident is written with `status` `REPORTED`, `reported_by_user_id` stamped from the acting `X-User-ID`, `veterinary_finding` null, `investigated_by_user_id` and `closed_by_user_id` null, `adjudicated_severity_score` null, `disclosed_at_utc` null and `disclosure_on_time` null. Its lifecycle runs `REPORTED`, `INVESTIGATING`, `REGULATOR_DISCLOSED` and `CLOSED`, with `CLOSED` terminal.

Reporting immediately computes a preliminary severity score and a disclosure obligation. The **severity score** is a weighted sum in both of its variants. The base weight comes from the `incident_type`: 40 for `INJURY`, 70 for `LOSS`, 85 for `DEATH`. An in-transit adder of 10 applies when a `segment_assignment_id` was supplied, and is zero otherwise. A welfare-gap term also applies only when a `segment_assignment_id` was supplied: the gap is that slot's `required_welfare_check_count` minus its `completed_welfare_check_count`, treated as zero when the completed count already meets or exceeds the required count, and the welfare component is that gap multiplied by 6, capped at 12. The raw score is the base weight plus the in-transit adder plus the welfare component. In the `PRELIMINARY` variant, run at report time before any veterinary attribution exists, `preliminary_severity_score` is simply the raw score capped at 100, with no attribution factor and no late-disclosure penalty.

The **disclosure obligation** is then determined. `disclosure_required` is true when the `incident_type` is `LOSS` or `DEATH`, or when the `preliminary_severity_score` is at least 60 — a preliminary score of exactly 60 makes disclosure mandatory. The base window depends on the type: twenty-four hours for a `DEATH`, forty-eight hours for a `LOSS`, and one hundred and sixty-eight hours for an `INJURY`. That window is **halved** when the animal's itinerary is international, meaning at least one of its slots whose `status` is not `RELEASED` sits on a leg whose origin station's `country_code` differs from its destination station's `country_code`. `disclosure_deadline_utc` is then the `discovered_at_utc` instant plus that number of whole hours when disclosure is required, and null when it is not.

Reporting also cascades into the operational record for the two severe types. For an `incident_type` of `DEATH` the animal moves to `DECEASED` — from `TRAVEL_READY` when the death occurs before departure and from `IN_TRANSIT` when it occurs in flight, both edges producing an identical three-hop cascade. Hop one sets the animal's `status` to `DECEASED`. Hop two moves every slot of the animal that is `RESERVED` to `RELEASED` and every slot that is `ACCEPTED` to `OFFLOADED`, each decrementing the booked counter for its routing on its owning leg and re-deriving that leg's posture. Hop three moves every kennel allocation for the animal whose `status` is `RESERVED` or `OCCUPIED` to `RELEASED`, decrementing that station's `reserved_bay_count` by the allocation's `bay_count`, sets the owning reservation's `booking_status` to `INTERRUPTED`, increments its `refunded_amount` by the `segment_fee_amount` of every slot released or offloaded in hop two, and re-runs the charge assembly. For an `incident_type` of `LOSS` the animal moves to `DENIED` instead, with the same slot and allocation cascade; the reservation likewise becomes `INTERRUPTED` when it was `IN_TRANSIT`, and because a `DENIED` animal no longer counts, the reservation's `animal_count` is recomputed as part of the same cascade and can fall to zero. An `INJURY` triggers no operational cascade. No animal status gates reporting at all: an incident may be reported against an animal in any status, including one already `DECEASED` or `DENIED`, and the death or loss cascade simply re-runs over whatever slots and allocations remain live.

The investigation is a `ComplianceOfficer` workflow with a fork and a join. Opening the investigation moves the incident `REPORTED → INVESTIGATING` and stamps `investigated_by_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`; the officer who reported the incident may not open it, and such an attempt is `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. From `INVESTIGATING` there are two branches. **Branch A** applies to incidents whose `disclosure_required` is true: the officer files the regulatory disclosure, optionally recording a `regulator_reference`, and the incident moves `INVESTIGATING → REGULATOR_DISCLOSED` with `disclosed_at_utc` stamped from the logical clock and `disclosure_on_time` set to true when `disclosed_at_utc` is at or before `disclosure_deadline_utc` and false otherwise. Filing a disclosure for an incident whose `disclosure_required` is false is refused with `ERR_DISCLOSURE_NOT_REQUIRED`. **Branch B** applies to incidents whose `disclosure_required` is false: those close directly out of `INVESTIGATING`, writing no disclosure fields at all. Attempting to close an incident straight out of `INVESTIGATING` while its `disclosure_required` is true and no disclosure has been filed is refused with `ERR_DISCLOSURE_PENDING`.

Both branches **join** at `CLOSED`. Closing is permitted from `INVESTIGATING` and from `REGULATOR_DISCLOSED`, requires a `veterinary_finding` in the request body naming the adjudicated cause attribution as `CARRIER_ATTRIBUTABLE`, `PRE_EXISTING_CONDITION` or `INDETERMINATE`, and is barred to the officer whose `X-User-ID` equals the incident's `investigated_by_user_id`, that attempt being `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. Closing stamps `closed_by_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`, sets the `status` to `CLOSED`, increments the incident's `version` by one, and runs the severity score in its `ADJUDICATED` variant to write `adjudicated_severity_score`. That variant recomputes the raw score from the same formula at close time rather than reusing the number captured at report time, reading the linked slot's `required_welfare_check_count` and `completed_welfare_check_count` as they stand at the close — so welfare checks appended after the report shrink the gap term and lower the adjudicated score below what the preliminary score implied, and a slot whose row has since vanished contributes a zero gap. It multiplies that raw score by the attribution factor for the supplied `veterinary_finding` — one for `CARRIER_ATTRIBUTABLE`, nine tenths for `INDETERMINATE`, eight tenths for `PRE_EXISTING_CONDITION` — and rounds that product half up to a whole integer. It then adds a late-disclosure penalty of 20 whenever `disclosure_required` is true and `disclosure_on_time` is anything other than true — false, and equally the null it still holds if the close somehow reached the join without a disclosure ever being filed — and finally caps the result at 100. The rounding happens before the penalty is added and before the cap is applied. The two branches therefore differ in exactly one respect at the join: an incident that reached `CLOSED` through `REGULATOR_DISCLOSED` after its deadline carries the late-disclosure penalty term in its adjudicated score, while one that closed directly out of `INVESTIGATING` never can, because its `disclosure_required` was false. An investigate, disclose or close attempted from a status that does not permit it is refused with `ERR_INCIDENT_INVALID_STATE`.
