# Online Program Management Partnership API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Universities that want to stand up online degree programs rarely do it alone. They contract an **online program management (OPM) partner** that fronts the marketing money, builds the recruitment funnel, and often co-designs the courseware — and in exchange takes a slice of tuition for a decade or so. The arrangement is commercially attractive and legally delicate at the same time: the revenue split has to be computed to the cent across thousands of student-terms, the university cannot outsource the parts of the operation that accreditors and regulators treat as inherently institutional, and the incentive-compensation ban means the partner may not simply pay its recruiters per head enrolled.

This API is the system of record for those arrangements. It carries a partnership from a drafted contract through provost execution, tracks the individual online programs launched underneath it, records who owns each course's academic content, splits every student-support domain between the two organisations, screens enrollment-counselor pay plans against the incentive ban, meters marketing spend against a fixed envelope, recognises tuition revenue period by period, and settles the partner's share on a disputable, recalculable remittance statement. When the relationship ends, it enforces the teach-out obligation: every enrolled student must be able to finish, somebody has to pay for that, and the assets the partner holds have to change hands on agreed terms.

Two properties shape the whole design. First, money is conserved: cash collected, marketing budget consumed, and teach-out liability allocated are each governed by an identity that must hold exactly after every write, so a compensating entry restores the prior position with no residual drift. Second, nothing important happens on one actor's signature alone — the officer who cuts a statement may not approve it, the auditor who cleared a counselor's plan may not clear it again, and the partner's own account lead can only ever touch its own partnership's records.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

Everything in this section applies across the entire API surface. Where §3 describes a workflow, the rules here are still in force.

### 2.1. Actors, Authentication Headers and Partnership Scoping

Every request must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, naming the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the capacity in which it acts. If either header is absent, empty or consists only of whitespace, the request fails with `ERR_MISSING_AUTH_HEADERS`. Six roles exist and no others are accepted; a value outside this set fails with `ERR_UNKNOWN_ROLE`:

- `PlatformAdmin` — operator of the shared OPM administration platform. Registers partnerships and controls the logical clock.
- `UniversityProvost` — the university's academic officer. Owns program approval, the service-scope split, academic progress recording, statement approval, and teach-out plan drafting.
- `UniversityBursar` — the university's financial officer. Owns tuition collection, withdrawals, statement authoring, dispute resolution and remittance.
- `FacultyChair` — the faculty owner of academic content, and the only role that may approve, revise or retire course content.
- `ComplianceAuditor` — the independent reviewer. Screens incentive compensation plans and certifies teach-out plans, and is held to segregation of duties across consecutive reviews of the same counselor.
- `PartnerAccountLead` — the OPM partner's account lead.

When the role named on a request is a recognised role but is not among the roles authorised for the endpoint being called (see the table in §4), the request fails with `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED`. Several endpoints multiplex several business actions behind one call; for those, the role must additionally be permitted for the specific action value supplied, and a role-permitted caller invoking an action reserved for someone else is rejected with `ERR_ACTION_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (see §2.5 for the exact action-to-role map).

Identity semantics differ by role, and this distinction matters for ownership checks. When `X-User-Role` is `PartnerAccountLead`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Partnership`: the partner's identity *is* the partnership it manages. Ownership checks on any resource that references a `Partnership` therefore compare that resource's partnership linkage to `X-User-ID` — directly for a `Program`, an `EnrollmentCounselor` or a `RemittanceStatement`, and by one hop through the owning program for a `Course`, a `Lead`, an `Enrollment`, a `ServiceScopeAssignment`, a `MarketingSpendEntry` or a `TeachOutPlan`. A `PartnerAccountLead` acting on a resource governed by any other partnership is rejected with `ERR_PARTNERSHIP_SCOPE_VIOLATION`, and this applies to every endpoint whose authorised-role list includes `PartnerAccountLead`, reads included. On list endpoints the rule takes the form of a filter rather than a rejection: a `PartnerAccountLead` listing programs, counselors or remittance statements is silently narrowed to its own partnership, and an explicit `partnership_id` query filter naming a different partnership is rejected with `ERR_PARTNERSHIP_SCOPE_VIOLATION`. The course, lead and enrollment lists narrow the same way through the owning program, a `program_id` filter naming a program of another partnership is rejected the same way, and a `program_id` that names no program at all simply yields an empty page.

When `X-User-Role` is `PlatformAdmin`, `UniversityProvost`, `UniversityBursar`, `FacultyChair` or `ComplianceAuditor`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it where the workflow demands an audit trail — as `authored_by_user_id`, `approved_by_user_id` or `resolved_by_user_id` on a settlement statement, as `last_review_user_id` on a counselor, as `certified_by_user_id` on a teach-out plan — but never compares it to any entity's `id`.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_` followed by twenty upper-case alphanumeric characters.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, read from the logical clock of §2.4 rather than from wall-clock time.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination and List Ordering

List endpoints are paginated by two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of records returned; when it is omitted it defaults to 10, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` is the number of records to skip and defaults to 0. Every list response reports `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside the envelope's `meta` object, where `total_count` counts the records matching the request's filters before pagination is applied and `has_more` is true exactly when records remain beyond the returned page. Both parameters must arrive as bare integer literals: a non-integer value, a `limit` outside 1 through 100 or a negative `offset` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, as is any enum-valued filter carrying an undeclared member, while query parameters an endpoint does not declare are ignored rather than rejected.

Unless a specific endpoint says otherwise, list results are ordered by `created_at` ascending. The append-only ledgers — the bursar tuition ledger of an enrollment, the media ledger of a program — and the teach-out plan list of a program all follow the same ascending creation order, which is what makes them readable as a chronological journal. Because `created_at` is minted from the logical clock, records created inside one clock instant share a timestamp exactly; such ties are broken by the server's own insertion order, so the journal reading stays stable.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and the Logical Clock

Every resource is addressed by an `id` that the server mints as a UUID with no type prefix, and stamps with a `created_at` timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC form with three fractional digits. Most resources additionally carry an `updated_at` timestamp, refreshed on every mutation including mutations the server performs on its own initiative, and a `version` integer that starts at 1 on creation and increments by one on every mutation. One request advances a given entity's `version` at most once however many times that request touches it, and a derived-field recomputation whose result equals the value already stored is not a mutation at all: it refreshes neither `updated_at` nor `version`. `Partnership`, `Program`, `Course`, `ServiceScopeAssignment`, `EnrollmentCounselor`, `Lead`, `Enrollment`, `RemittanceStatement` and `TeachOutPlan` all carry the full set.

Two resources are deliberately leaner. A tuition ledger entry and a marketing ledger entry are append-only facts, never amended after the fact, and therefore carry only `id` and `created_at`: they have no `updated_at` field and no `version` field at all, and neither field should appear in their response payloads.

A `Partnership`'s `version` is the coordination point for the whole aggregate, so its advance is deliberately narrow. It becomes 1 at registration and increments by one on each contract action, on each terms amendment, on each `Program` creation under that partnership, and on each settlement statement creation. Recomputation of the partnership's derived ledger totals caused by a deeper child write — a tuition posting, a marketing entry, a remittance — refreshes `updated_at` on the partnership without advancing `version`, so a client holding a version number is only invalidated by writes that genuinely restate the contract or the settlement history.

All temporal reasoning in this system reads a **logical clock** rather than wall-clock time. Before the clock is ever set it reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and it never falls back to real time. A `PlatformAdmin`-only test-driving capability replaces the clock by supplying a `current_utc` instant — any ISO 8601 timestamp is accepted, whether it ends in `Z`, carries an explicit offset, or names no zone at all, in which case UTC is assumed — and the response reports both the new `current_utc` and the `previous_utc` it displaced, each rendered in UTC with three fractional digits; no entity is mutated by the call. Launch windows, contract expiry, settlement-period elapsing, withdrawal day counts, teach-out notice periods and every `..._at` system timestamp are all evaluated against this clock.

### 2.5. Request Screening Order

Multiple checks frequently apply to one call, and the order in which they run is part of the contract: a request that violates several rules must fail with the error belonging to the *earliest* applicable check. The ladder below is evaluated strictly top to bottom.

1. Both `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` are present and non-empty, on every endpoint — otherwise `ERR_MISSING_AUTH_HEADERS` (401).
2. `X-User-Role` names one of the six declared roles, on every endpoint — otherwise `ERR_UNKNOWN_ROLE` (401).
3. The named role appears in the called endpoint's authorised-role list, on every endpoint — otherwise `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (403).
4. The request body carries every required field of the endpoint and no unknown fields; every value matches its declared type; every enum-valued field carries a declared member; every integer field expressed in cents is zero or greater; every date field parses as an ISO date; and the paired date fields are ordered — `period_start` no later than `period_end`, `launch_window_start` strictly earlier than `launch_window_end`, and `term_start_date` no later than `term_end_date`, the performance run's `window_start` and `window_end` deliberately *not* forming such a pair, so an inverted window is accepted and simply matches no leads. Several fields are required only for one particular action value and are screened here too: `wind_down_effective_date` on the contract action `WIND_DOWN`, `faculty_approver_name` on the course action `APPROVE`, `reversal_note` on a `CREDIT_MEMO` marketing entry, `hours_to_first_contact` on the lead advance `CONTACT`, `disqualification_reason` on `DISQUALIFY`, `credits_completed` on `RECORD_PROGRESS`, `withdrawal_date` on `WITHDRAW`, `dispute_reason` on the statement action `DISPUTE`, `remittance_reference` on `REMIT`, and `asset_transfer_confirmed` on teach-out certification; for the string-valued members of that list an empty string counts as absent. Any breach yields `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400).
5. On the five multi-action endpoints — partnership contract actions, program lifecycle actions, course design actions, settlement statement actions, and enrollment academic actions — the acting role is permitted for the specific action value supplied, otherwise `ERR_ACTION_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (403). The map is: among contract actions, `SUBMIT_FOR_REVIEW` is `PartnerAccountLead`-only while `REQUEST_CHANGES`, `EXECUTE`, `WIND_DOWN` and `CLOSE_OUT` are `UniversityProvost`-only; among program lifecycle actions, `SUBMIT` and `LAUNCH` are `PartnerAccountLead`-only while `REQUEST_REVISION`, `APPROVE`, `REJECT`, `SUSPEND`, `RESUME`, `BEGIN_TEACH_OUT` and `CLOSE` are `UniversityProvost`-only; among course design actions, `START_DESIGN` and `SUBMIT_FOR_FACULTY_REVIEW` are `PartnerAccountLead`-only while `REQUEST_REVISION`, `APPROVE` and `RETIRE` are `FacultyChair`-only; among statement actions, `APPROVE` is `UniversityProvost`-only, `DISPUTE` is `PartnerAccountLead`-only, and `RESOLVE`, `REMIT` and `RECALCULATE` are `UniversityBursar`-only; among academic actions, `WITHDRAW` is `UniversityBursar`-only while `RECORD_PROGRESS` and `COMPLETE` are `UniversityProvost`-only.
6. The `Partnership` named by a path identifier or by a `partnership_id` in the body exists — checked on every partnership-scoped endpoint, on program creation and on counselor onboarding — otherwise `ERR_PARTNERSHIP_NOT_FOUND` (404).
7. The `Program` named by a path identifier or by a `program_id` in the body exists — checked on every program-scoped endpoint and on lead creation — otherwise `ERR_PROGRAM_NOT_FOUND` (404).
8. The `Course` named by the path identifier exists — checked on the single-course read, the course amendment and the course design actions — otherwise `ERR_COURSE_NOT_FOUND` (404).
9. When `prerequisite_course_id` is supplied, it names an existing `Course` belonging to the same `Program` — checked on course creation and course amendment — otherwise `ERR_PREREQUISITE_COURSE_NOT_FOUND` (404).
10. The `EnrollmentCounselor` named by the path identifier or by a `counselor_id` in the body exists — checked on every counselor-scoped endpoint and on lead creation — otherwise `ERR_COUNSELOR_NOT_FOUND` (404).
11. The `Lead` named by the path identifier exists — checked on the single-lead read, the funnel advance and the enroll conversion — otherwise `ERR_LEAD_NOT_FOUND` (404).
12. The `Enrollment` named by the path identifier exists — checked on every enrollment-scoped endpoint — otherwise `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_FOUND` (404).
13. The `RemittanceStatement` named by the path identifier exists — checked on the single-statement read and the statement actions — otherwise `ERR_STATEMENT_NOT_FOUND` (404).
14. The `TeachOutPlan` named by the path identifier exists — checked on certification — otherwise `ERR_TEACH_OUT_PLAN_NOT_FOUND` (404).
15. When the acting role is `PartnerAccountLead`, `X-User-ID` equals the `id` of the partnership governing the target resource, resolved as described in §2.1 — otherwise `ERR_PARTNERSHIP_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (403).
16. Segregation of duties holds: the `X-User-ID` approving a settlement statement differs from that statement's `authored_by_user_id`, and the `X-User-ID` deciding a counselor compliance review differs from that counselor's `last_review_user_id` whenever a prior review exists. Breaches yield `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (403). This applies to the statement-action endpoint when the action is `APPROVE`, and to the counselor compliance-review endpoint for all of its decisions.
17. `expected_partnership_version` equals the partnership's current `version`, on the terms amendment and on statement creation — otherwise `ERR_STALE_PARTNERSHIP_VERSION` (412). See §2.6.
18. The partnership's `status` permits the requested contract action or child write — otherwise `ERR_PARTNERSHIP_INVALID_STATE` (409). Concretely: contract actions must match a legal transition of the partnership lifecycle; `Program` creation, counselor compensation-plan filing, marketing spend posting and statement creation all require `status` to be `EXECUTED`; a terms amendment is accepted only while `status` is `DRAFT`, `UNDER_REVIEW` or `EXECUTED`. Among program lifecycle actions only `SUBMIT`, `LAUNCH` and `RESUME` require `EXECUTED`; counselor onboarding, compliance review, performance runs, course writes, service-scope writes, lead and enrollment writes, teach-out drafting and certification, and every statement action after creation inspect no partnership status at all.
19. The program's `status` permits the requested lifecycle action or child write — otherwise `ERR_PROGRAM_INVALID_STATE` (409). Concretely: lifecycle actions must match a legal transition; `Course` creation and `ServiceScopeAssignment` creation require `status` to be `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `REVISION_REQUESTED`; `Lead` creation requires `LAUNCHED`; `TeachOutPlan` creation requires `TEACH_OUT`. The check also guards lead enrollment and the `APPLY` advance, both of which require the lead's program to be `LAUNCHED`; the `CONTACT` and `DISQUALIFY` advances carry no program-state gate at all.
20. The course's `design_status` permits the requested design action, including the prerequisite gate on `SUBMIT_FOR_FACULTY_REVIEW` (the course named by `prerequisite_course_id` must itself be in `FACULTY_REVIEW` or `APPROVED`) and the dependent-course gate on `RETIRE`; and a course amendment is accepted only while `design_status` is `NOT_STARTED`, `IN_DESIGN` or `DESIGN_SUSPENDED`. Breaches yield `ERR_COURSE_INVALID_DESIGN_STATE` (409).
21. The counselor's `incentive_eligibility` permits the requested compliance decision — otherwise `ERR_COUNSELOR_INVALID_REVIEW_STATE` (409).
22. The lead's `status` permits the requested advance action or enrollment — otherwise `ERR_LEAD_INVALID_STATE` (409).
23. The enrollment's `status` permits the requested academic action or tuition posting; a tuition ledger entry may only be posted while the enrollment is `ENROLLED` or `ACTIVE`. Breaches yield `ERR_ENROLLMENT_INVALID_STATE` (409).
24. The statement's `status` permits the requested action, and `requires_recalculation` is false when the action is `APPROVE` — otherwise `ERR_STATEMENT_INVALID_STATE` (409).
25. The teach-out plan's `status` is `DRAFT` when certification is attempted — otherwise `ERR_TEACH_OUT_PLAN_INVALID_STATE` (409).
26. The filed compensation plan is permitted under the incentive-compensation ban, on the compensation-plan endpoint — otherwise `ERR_INCENTIVE_COMP_PROHIBITED` (422). See §3.6 for the permitted and prohibited shapes.
27. The counselor involved is not in `PROHIBITED_PLAN_ON_FILE`, on lead creation and on lead enrollment — otherwise `ERR_COUNSELOR_INCENTIVE_SUSPENDED` (409).
28. Faculty ownership of academic content is satisfied when a course is approved — otherwise `ERR_FACULTY_IP_OWNERSHIP_VIOLATION` (422). Applies to the course design-action endpoint when the action is `APPROVE`.
29. A non-delegable service domain is not assigned to the partner, on service-scope assignment creation — otherwise `ERR_NON_DELEGABLE_SERVICE_DOMAIN` (422).
30. `student_notification_date` precedes `teach_out_end_date` by at least the minimum notice period, on teach-out certification — otherwise `ERR_TEACH_OUT_NOTICE_TOO_SHORT` (422).
31. Teach-out obligations are discharged: a program may be closed only with a `CERTIFIED` teach-out plan in place and no remaining active enrollments, and a partnership may be closed out only when every one of its programs has settled into `CLOSED`, `REJECTED` or `DRAFT` and no settlement statement is still open. Breaches yield `ERR_TEACH_OUT_OBLIGATIONS_UNMET` (409). Applies to the program lifecycle action `CLOSE` and the contract action `CLOSE_OUT`.
32. The `service_domain` is not already assigned for this program, on service-scope assignment creation — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_SERVICE_DOMAIN` (409).
33. The prerequisite edge does not create a cycle in the program's course prerequisite graph, on course creation and course amendment — otherwise `ERR_PREREQUISITE_CYCLE` (422).
34. Every domain listed in `required_service_domains` has a service-scope assignment, on the program lifecycle action `APPROVE` — otherwise `ERR_SERVICE_SCOPE_INCOMPLETE` (409).
35. Every launch-required course of the program is `APPROVED`, on the program lifecycle action `APPROVE` — otherwise `ERR_COURSE_DESIGN_INCOMPLETE` (409).
36. The program has at least one course, on the program lifecycle action `SUBMIT` — otherwise `ERR_NO_COURSES_DEFINED` (409).
37. The logical clock date is no later than the partnership's `expiry_date`, on the program lifecycle actions `LAUNCH` and `RESUME`, on lead creation, on lead enrollment and on statement creation — otherwise `ERR_CONTRACT_TERM_EXPIRED` (409).
38. The logical clock date lies inside the program's launch window, on the program lifecycle action `LAUNCH` — otherwise `ERR_OUTSIDE_LAUNCH_WINDOW` (422).
39. No conflicting exclusive-CIP launch exists at the same university, on the program lifecycle action `LAUNCH` — otherwise `ERR_CIP_EXCLUSIVITY_CONFLICT` (409). See §3.5.
40. The requested settlement period does not overlap an existing statement period of the same partnership, on statement creation — otherwise `ERR_STATEMENT_PERIOD_OVERLAP` (409).
41. `period_end` is strictly earlier than the logical clock date, on statement creation — otherwise `ERR_PERIOD_NOT_ELAPSED` (422).
42. The screening demand score clears the submission threshold, on the program lifecycle action `SUBMIT`, evaluated after any revised inputs supplied on the action have been applied — otherwise `ERR_DEMAND_SCORE_BELOW_SUBMIT_THRESHOLD` (422).
43. The confirmed demand score clears the approval threshold, on the program lifecycle action `APPROVE` — otherwise `ERR_DEMAND_SCORE_BELOW_APPROVAL_THRESHOLD` (422).
44. The program's `total_enrollment_count` is strictly below its `enrollment_cap` before the enrollment is created, on lead enrollment — otherwise `ERR_ENROLLMENT_CAP_REACHED` (409).
45. A spend entry's `amount_cents` is no greater than the partnership's `marketing_budget_remaining_cents`, on marketing ledger posting — otherwise `ERR_MARKETING_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` (422).
46. The fee schedule and the contract structure agree, on partnership registration, on the terms amendment and on the contract action `SUBMIT_FOR_REVIEW` — otherwise `ERR_FEE_STRUCTURE_MISMATCH` (422).
47. `variable_component_cents` does not exceed one fifth of `annual_base_cents` on the filed plan, on the compensation-plan endpoint — otherwise `ERR_COMP_EXCEEDS_APPROVED_ENVELOPE` (422).
48. The statement's `net_payable_cents` is greater than zero, on the statement action `REMIT` — otherwise `ERR_NOTHING_TO_REMIT` (409).
49. A refund entry's `amount_cents` is no greater than the enrollment's collected amount less its already-refunded amount, on tuition ledger posting — otherwise `ERR_REFUND_EXCEEDS_COLLECTED` (422).

Because existence checks (items 6 through 14) run in this order, a nested request fails on the leftmost missing identifier in its path: a service-scope assignment posted under a program that does not exist reports `ERR_PROGRAM_NOT_FOUND` and never reaches the domain-duplication check, and a lead advance addressed to a lead whose program has vanished reports the program error first.

### 2.6. Optimistic Concurrency on the Partnership Record

Two write paths restate the partnership's economics, and both are guarded by optimistic concurrency control on the partnership's `version`.

`PATCH /partnerships/{id}/terms` requires `expected_partnership_version` as a non-optional input field carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `Partnership`. If the supplied value does not equal the partnership's current `version`, the amendment is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PARTNERSHIP_VERSION` and HTTP 412. Term amendments are legitimately repeatable — a second amendment observing the same partnership `status` would itself be a valid request — so state alone cannot distinguish a correct amendment from one computed against superseded tiers, a superseded marketing envelope or a superseded recognition method. The version check is the only deterministic guard.

`POST /partnerships/{id}/remittance-statements` likewise requires `expected_partnership_version` as a non-optional input field carrying the client's last observed `version` of the parent `Partnership`, and a value that does not equal the partnership's current `version` is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PARTNERSHIP_VERSION` and HTTP 412. A statement legitimately supersedes the partner's prior view of period economics, so a second create carrying a stale version cannot be told apart from a correct one by state alone; the check on the partnership's `version` is what makes the stale writer fail deterministically rather than silently overwrite a period view it never observed.

No other endpoint takes a version field. Course amendments, lifecycle actions, ledger postings, statement actions and teach-out certification are all screened by state and business rules only, and must reject a request body that carries `expected_partnership_version` as an unknown field.

### 2.7. Money, Rounding and the Two Conserved Ledgers

Every monetary quantity in the system is an integer number of cents; there are no fractional-cent amounts anywhere in stored state or in responses. Rates and percentages — revenue-share rates, refund percentages, holdback percentages, conversion and breach ratios — are decimal numbers.

Rounding is never left to the implementation's default. Wherever a computed money figure is derived by multiplication or division, the result is rounded **half up** to a whole cent, with three deliberate exceptions that round differently or not at all: the marketing recoupment cap is taken with a **floor**, the partner's teach-out obligation cap is taken with a **floor**, and the cash and marketing conservation identities are exact integer arithmetic in which no rounding participates. A program's demand score is rounded half up to 2 decimal places. A counselor's `conversion_rate` and the SLA breach ratio derived alongside it are rounded half up to 4 decimal places.

Two quantities are conserved and must reconcile exactly after every single write.

**The cash position (INV-1).** For one enrollment, `collected_cents` is the sum of that enrollment's tuition ledger entries whose `entry_type` is `PAYMENT`, and `refunded_cents` is the sum of those whose `entry_type` is `REFUND`. For the partnership, `collected_tuition_cents` is the sum of `amount_cents` over every `PAYMENT` entry of every enrollment of every program of the partnership, less the sum of `amount_cents` over every `REFUND` entry of the same set. Both directions are always included; the ledger is append-only and no state filter applies. The consequence is a compensating-action guarantee: a `PAYMENT` of some amount followed by a `REFUND` of the same amount on the same enrollment must leave `collected_tuition_cents` at exactly its pre-payment value, and must leave the enrollment's collected-minus-refunded difference at exactly its pre-payment value. No intermediate cascade — the enrollment flipping to `ACTIVE`, a statement being staled, a counselor clawback accruing — may leave any drift behind.

**The marketing envelope (INV-2).** For one program, `marketing_spend_net_cents` is the sum of `amount_cents` over that program's media ledger entries whose `entry_type` is `SPEND`, less the sum over those whose `entry_type` is `CREDIT_MEMO`. Summing that net figure over every program of the partnership gives the partnership's posted net spend. `marketing_budget_remaining_cents` is `marketing_budget_cents` less that posted net once the partnership has an `executed_date`, and is held at 0 before then, and `unrecouped_marketing_spend_cents` is that posted net less the sum of `marketing_recoupment_cents` over the partnership's statements whose `status` is `REMITTED`. The identity that the budget equals the remaining budget plus posted net spend holds after every write, and a `SPEND` of some amount followed by a `CREDIT_MEMO` of the same amount on the same program restores `marketing_budget_remaining_cents` to exactly `marketing_budget_cents` and restores the program's `marketing_spend_net_cents` to its pre-spend value.

### 2.8. Derived Fields, Cascade Discipline and Hierarchical Consistency

A large share of this system's state is derived rather than supplied. Enrollment counters on a program, pipeline and performance counters on a counselor, cash and recognition totals on a partnership, and every settlement figure on a statement are recomputed by the server as the underlying facts change — recomputed from the underlying facts in full rather than incrementally adjusted, so that a partnership's `recognized_revenue_cents` is the sum of `recognized_to_date_cents` over every enrollment of every one of its programs, its `remitted_to_partner_cents` the sum of `net_payable_cents` over its `REMITTED` statements, and its `holdback_balance_cents` the sum of `holdback_cents` over those same statements less the sum of `holdback_applied_cents` over the `CERTIFIED` teach-out plans of its programs — and each recomputation is a mutation of the entity that owns the field: it refreshes that entity's `updated_at`, and — subject to the narrow partnership-`version` rule in §2.4 — advances the owning entity's `version`.

Two multi-hop cascades exist, and their hop structure is normative because a later hop is triggered by an earlier hop's effect rather than by the original request.

**Cascade A** fires when a partnership is wound down. Its first hop transitions every program of that partnership whose `status` is `LAUNCHED` or `SUSPENDED` into `TEACH_OUT`, processing those programs in ascending `created_at` order and stamping each one's `teach_out_started_at` with the logical clock instant. Its second hop is caused by the first, not by the contract action: each program entering `TEACH_OUT` disqualifies its own leads in `NEW`, `CONTACTED` or `APPLIED` with `disqualification_reason` set to `PROGRAM_TEACH_OUT`, and suspends its own courses whose `design_status` is `IN_DESIGN` or `FACULTY_REVIEW` into `DESIGN_SUSPENDED`. Its third hop is caused by the second: every counselor whose leads were disqualified in hop two recomputes `open_pipeline_count`, and any counselor whose recomputed count is zero moves `performance_status` to `INACTIVE_PENDING_REVIEW` while `incentive_eligibility` is left exactly as it was.

**Cascade B** fires when an enrollment is withdrawn, and is described with its arithmetic in §3.8. Its shape is: hop one creates the compensating refund ledger entry and reconciles the cash position; hop two, caused by that refund entry rather than by the withdraw action, accrues the owning counselor's clawback; hop three, caused by that accrual, may suspend the counselor's incentive eligibility. Separately, the withdrawal stales the settlement statements whose closed periods contain the enrollment's term start.

Where an entity carries two independent state dimensions — as an enrollment counselor does — no transition on one dimension may read or write the other. A performance run never touches incentive eligibility, and a compliance decision never touches performance status. The cascades above respect this rule explicitly.

### 2.9. Segregation of Duties and Non-Delegable Responsibilities

The governance model is one of segregated duties rather than a simple permission hierarchy, and two of its rules are enforced against recorded identities rather than against roles.

A settlement statement is authored by a `UniversityBursar`, whose `X-User-ID` is captured as `authored_by_user_id`. Approval is a `UniversityProvost` act, and the approving `X-User-ID` must differ from `authored_by_user_id`; a match is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` even though the approver holds the correct role. The approving identity is recorded as `approved_by_user_id`, and the identity that resolves a dispute is recorded as `resolved_by_user_id`.

A counselor compliance review is a `ComplianceAuditor` act, and the deciding `X-User-ID` is captured as `last_review_user_id`. Once a first review exists, a second review of the same counselor by the same identity is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`: consecutive reviews of one counselor must be decided by different auditors.

Certain operational domains are non-delegable regardless of what the parties negotiate. `COURSE_DESIGN`, `FACULTY_TRAINING` and `ACCESSIBILITY_ACCOMMODATIONS` must always be performed by the university; assigning any of them to the partner is refused with `ERR_NON_DELEGABLE_SERVICE_DOMAIN`. Academic content approval is reserved to `FacultyChair` alone — the partner may draft and submit courseware but may never sign off on it.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Contracting the Partnership

A `PlatformAdmin` opens the relationship by registering a partnership. The registration payload names both parties — `university_name` and `partner_org_name` — and identifies the institution by its `university_ipeds_id`, a string of exactly six numeric characters that doubles as the exclusivity scope key described in §3.5. The economic model is fixed at registration by `contract_structure`, which is either `REVENUE_SHARE` or `FEE_FOR_SERVICE`, and the term is fixed by `term_months`, a whole number of months no fewer than 12 and no more than 120, measured from the `effective_date` supplied as an ISO calendar date. The server derives `expiry_date` as the day one day before the date that falls `term_months` months after `effective_date` — month arithmetic clamps the day of month to the last day of the target month, so 31 January plus one month is 28 or 29 February — and recomputes it whenever either `effective_date` or `term_months` changes. `revenue_recognition_method` — `PERIOD_STRAIGHT_LINE` or `MILESTONE_CHRONOLOGICAL` — declares which recognition variant the partnership's statements apply by default, and `marketing_budget_cents` sets the total media envelope the partner may consume across all of the partnership's programs.

Four inputs are optional at registration and take documented defaults. `revenue_share_tiers` is an ordered array of marginal tiers, each an object of `through_cents` and `partner_rate`, where the final tier carries `through_cents` as null to mean unbounded; when omitted, the partnership takes the standard ladder of 57.5 percent of the partner's share up to $250,000 of contract-year recognised revenue, 48.5 percent from there up to $750,000, and 39.5 percent above that. `refund_schedule` is an ordered array of withdrawal bands, each an object of `through_day` and `refund_pct`, and when omitted takes the standard schedule of a full refund through day 7, half through day 21, a quarter through day 35, and nothing thereafter — the final band again carrying `through_day` as null. `exclusivity_cip_codes` defaults to an empty array. `bundled_services_exception_claimed` defaults to false and declares whether the partnership claims the bundled-services exception that permits counselor salary adjustments not based solely on enrollment counts. `fee_schedule_monthly_cents`, the flat monthly service fee owed to the partner, defaults to 0. When either array is supplied it must be non-empty and every element must carry exactly its two declared keys, with `partner_rate` and `refund_pct` numbers between 0 and 1 inclusive and `through_cents` or `through_day` either a non-negative integer or null; a breach is `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. Beyond that the server does not police an array's internal coherence: it neither requires the elements to be sorted nor insists that the last of them be the unbounded null-bounded one. `exclusivity_cip_codes` entries are likewise taken as arbitrary strings, without the discipline-code pattern check a program's own `cip_code` must pass.

The fee schedule and the contract structure must agree at all times: a `fee_schedule_monthly_cents` greater than zero is only legal when `contract_structure` is `FEE_FOR_SERVICE`, and a partnership on `REVENUE_SHARE` must hold `fee_schedule_monthly_cents` at exactly 0. A registration or amendment that violates this is refused with `ERR_FEE_STRUCTURE_MISMATCH`, and the same coherence is re-checked when the contract is submitted for review.

A newly registered partnership is `DRAFT` with `version` 1. Its `marketing_budget_remaining_cents` is initialised to 0 rather than to the budget: no media money is available until the contract is executed, and it stays at 0 for exactly as long as `executed_date` is null, so a terms amendment restating `marketing_budget_cents` before execution still leaves the remaining envelope at 0 and only `EXECUTE` opens it. `change_request_count` starts at 0, `executed_date` and `wind_down_effective_date` are both null, and every computed ledger field — `collected_tuition_cents`, `recognized_revenue_cents`, `contract_year_recognized_cents`, `remitted_to_partner_cents`, `holdback_balance_cents`, `unrecouped_marketing_spend_cents` — starts at zero.

The contract then moves along a five-state path with one deliberate cycle. The `PartnerAccountLead` submits the draft for review with `SUBMIT_FOR_REVIEW`, taking `DRAFT → UNDER_REVIEW`; this is legal only while the partnership is `DRAFT`, only when a `FEE_FOR_SERVICE` partnership has a `fee_schedule_monthly_cents` strictly greater than 0 and a `REVENUE_SHARE` partnership has it at exactly 0, and it recomputes `expiry_date` and increments the partnership's `version`. A `UniversityProvost` reviewing the submission may push it back with `REQUEST_CHANGES`, taking `UNDER_REVIEW → DRAFT`, adding one to `change_request_count` and incrementing `version`; this loop may repeat any number of times, and `change_request_count` is the record of how many times it did. Alternatively the provost signs with `EXECUTE`, taking `UNDER_REVIEW → EXECUTED`: `executed_date` is set to the `executed_date` supplied on the action if one was supplied and otherwise to the logical clock's date, `marketing_budget_remaining_cents` is opened up to equal `marketing_budget_cents`, and `version` increments. Later, the provost may begin the exit with `WIND_DOWN` (`EXECUTED → WIND_DOWN`) or finish it with `CLOSE_OUT` (`WIND_DOWN → CLOSED_OUT`); both are described in §3.9. `CLOSED_OUT` is terminal. An action that does not match a legal transition out of the partnership's current `status` is refused with `ERR_PARTNERSHIP_INVALID_STATE`. Alongside `action`, the contract-action payload accepts an optional `change_request_note` for a pushed-back review, an optional `executed_date`, an optional `wind_down_reason`, and a `wind_down_effective_date` that is mandatory whenever `action` is `WIND_DOWN` and refused as `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` when omitted. `change_request_note` and `wind_down_reason` are accepted for the sake of the audit conversation but are neither stored on the partnership nor echoed in any response.

Amending terms is a partner-initiated act, permitted while the partnership is `DRAFT`, `UNDER_REVIEW` or `EXECUTED` and refused with `ERR_PARTNERSHIP_INVALID_STATE` in `WIND_DOWN` or `CLOSED_OUT`. Beyond the mandatory `expected_partnership_version` of §2.6, the amendment may carry any of `term_months`, `revenue_share_tiers`, `marketing_budget_cents`, `refund_schedule`, `revenue_recognition_method` and `fee_schedule_monthly_cents`. A change to `term_months` recomputes `expiry_date`; a change to `marketing_budget_cents` recomputes `marketing_budget_remaining_cents` against the posted net spend of §2.7, though that recomputation still yields 0 while `executed_date` is null; and the amendment increments the partnership's `version` by one.

A consolidated position read is available to every role except `FacultyChair`: it reports `collected_tuition_cents`, `recognized_revenue_cents`, `remitted_to_partner_cents`, `holdback_balance_cents`, `unrecouped_marketing_spend_cents`, `marketing_budget_remaining_cents`, an `open_statement_count` of the partnership's statements that have not reached `REMITTED` and are not absent from the settlement pipeline — that is, statements in `CALCULATED`, `UNIVERSITY_APPROVED`, `DISPUTED`, `RESOLVED_ADJUSTED` or `STALE` — and a `program_count` of the partnership's programs. Partnership lists may be narrowed by `status` and by `university_ipeds_id`.

### 3.2. Program Selection, Demand Screening and the Review Cycle

Under an `EXECUTED` partnership, the `PartnerAccountLead` proposes individual programs; attempting this under a partnership in any other `status` is refused with `ERR_PARTNERSHIP_INVALID_STATE`. A proposal names its parent with `partnership_id`, carries a marketed `program_name`, a `cip_code` in the six-character `NN.NNNN` discipline-code form, a `delivery_modality` of `ASYNCHRONOUS`, `SYNCHRONOUS` or `HYBRID`, and a `credential_level` of `CERTIFICATE`, `BACHELORS`, `MASTERS` or `DOCTORATE`. Its economics are `tuition_per_credit_cents`, the list tuition per credit hour, and `credits_to_complete`, the total credits the credential requires. Its capacity is `enrollment_cap`, the maximum number of enrollments in `ENROLLED`, `ACTIVE` or `COMPLETED` the program will accept. The optional `required_service_domains` array names the domains that must each be assigned before approval, and defaults to `MARKETING`, `LEAD_GENERATION`, `ENROLLMENT_COUNSELING`, `COURSE_DESIGN`, `STUDENT_ADVISING` and `ACCESSIBILITY_ACCOMMODATIONS`; when supplied explicitly every member must be a declared service domain, but the array may be empty and may repeat a domain. Finally, `launch_window_start` and `launch_window_end` bound the dates on which the program may go live, and the window is half-open: the start date is included and the end date is not. Creating a program increments the parent partnership's `version`. Creation is not screened against the partnership's `expiry_date`: only launch, resumption, lead work and statement creation are.

Five inputs feed the market-demand analysis. `projected_annual_enrollments` is the partner's own projection of new enrollments per year; `competitor_program_count` is the number of competing online programs in the same discipline code; `regional_wage_premium_pct` is the percentage wage premium credential-holders command in the region; `employer_partner_count` is the number of committed employer partners; and `prior_year_inquiry_volume` is the observed inquiry volume for the discipline in the prior year, which only the confirmed variant of the score consults.

**The demand score.** Four weighted components are summed and then scaled once by a modality factor. The enrollment component takes the effective projection divided by a projection ceiling of 400 annual enrollments, capped at 1, and multiplies it by a weight of 40. The competition component takes 1 less the `competitor_program_count` divided by a competitor ceiling of 30, floored at 0 so a hyper-competitive discipline contributes nothing rather than a negative amount, and multiplies it by a weight of 25. The wage component takes `regional_wage_premium_pct` divided by a wage-premium ceiling of 20 percentage points, capped at 1, and multiplies it by a weight of 20. The employer component takes `employer_partner_count` divided by an employer ceiling of 15, capped at 1, and multiplies it by a weight of 15. Those four component values are added to a raw score, and the raw score is multiplied by the modality factor — 1.00 for `ASYNCHRONOUS`, 0.92 for `SYNCHRONOUS`, 0.96 for `HYBRID` — exactly once, on the summed raw score and never per component. The product is rounded half up to 2 decimal places.

The two variants differ in one operand only. The **screening** variant takes the effective projection to be `projected_annual_enrollments` itself, accepting the partner's unverified number at face value, and writes its result to `demand_score_screening`. The **confirmed** variant takes the effective projection to be the smaller of `projected_annual_enrollments` and the observed funnel converted at a yield of one inquiry in ten — that is, `prior_year_inquiry_volume` multiplied by 0.10 and rounded half up to a whole number — and writes its result to `demand_score_confirmed`. Because the confirmed projection can only be lower or equal, identical program data yields a confirmed score at or below the screening score, and strictly below it whenever last year's inquiry funnel cannot support this year's projection. Both scores are computed at program creation. The screening score is recomputed on every submission, and the confirmed score is recomputed at the moment approval is evaluated.

**The review cycle.** A program starts `DRAFT` and reaches `SUBMITTED` through the partner's `SUBMIT` action, which requires the program to be `DRAFT`, the parent partnership to be `EXECUTED`, at least one course to exist for the program — otherwise `ERR_NO_COURSES_DEFINED` — and the recomputed `demand_score_screening` to be at least 45.00. A score below that threshold is refused with `ERR_DEMAND_SCORE_BELOW_SUBMIT_THRESHOLD`; the comparison is inclusive, so exactly 45.00 passes. A `UniversityProvost` may return the submission with `REQUEST_REVISION`, taking `SUBMITTED → REVISION_REQUESTED` and adding one to the program's `revision_round`; the payload may carry a `revision_note`. From `REVISION_REQUESTED` the partner resubmits with `SUBMIT` again, and this time the action may carry `revised_projected_annual_enrollments` and `revised_prior_year_inquiry_volume`: when supplied, these replace `projected_annual_enrollments` and `prior_year_inquiry_volume` on the program before the screening score is recomputed and re-tested against 45.00 — but the replacement and the new `demand_score_screening` are written through only when the test passes, so a submission refused with `ERR_DEMAND_SCORE_BELOW_SUBMIT_THRESHOLD` leaves the program's stored inputs and stored score exactly as they were. The `SUBMITTED → REVISION_REQUESTED → SUBMITTED` loop may repeat indefinitely, with `revision_round` counting the completed loops.

Approval is the provost's `SUBMITTED → APPROVED` act, and it is the strictest gate in the system. It requires the program to be `SUBMITTED`; every domain named in `required_service_domains` to have a service-scope assignment, failing which the request is refused with `ERR_SERVICE_SCOPE_INCOMPLETE`; every course of the program whose `required_for_launch` is true to have reached `APPROVED` design status, failing which the refusal is `ERR_COURSE_DESIGN_INCOMPLETE`; and the freshly recomputed `demand_score_confirmed` to be at least 62.00, failing which the refusal is `ERR_DEMAND_SCORE_BELOW_APPROVAL_THRESHOLD`. That comparison is inclusive too: exactly 62.00 passes. As with submission, the recomputed `demand_score_confirmed` is written onto the program only when approval succeeds; a refusal leaves the stored score untouched. These checks are evaluated in the order in which §2.5 lists them, so an incomplete service-scope split surfaces before an unfinished course design, which in turn surfaces before a short confirmed score. Alternatively the provost may end the proposal with `REJECT`, taking `SUBMITTED → REJECTED`; `REJECTED` is terminal and no further transition is possible from it. Any lifecycle action that does not match a legal transition out of the program's current `status` is refused with `ERR_PROGRAM_INVALID_STATE`. Only `SUBMIT`, `LAUNCH` and `RESUME` additionally require the parent partnership to be `EXECUTED`; the provost's own actions — `REQUEST_REVISION`, `APPROVE`, `REJECT`, `SUSPEND`, `BEGIN_TEACH_OUT` and `CLOSE` — read no partnership status, which is what lets a program be closed while its partnership already sits in `WIND_DOWN`. The `revision_note` and `teach_out_reason` a lifecycle action may carry are accepted but are not stored on the program and appear in no response.

Alongside the derived scores, a program carries counters the rest of the system maintains: `active_enrollment_count` counts its enrollments in `ENROLLED` or `ACTIVE`; `total_enrollment_count` counts those in `ENROLLED`, `ACTIVE` or `COMPLETED` and is the figure compared against `enrollment_cap`; `recognized_revenue_cents` accumulates recognised revenue across the program's enrollments; and `marketing_spend_net_cents` and `blended_cac_cents` are described in §3.7. All three counters start at zero, as does `marketing_spend_net_cents`, while `blended_cac_cents` starts null. Programs may be listed filtered by `partnership_id`, by `status` and by `cip_code`, each compared as an exact literal.

### 3.3. Academic Content and Service-Scope Responsibilities

**Courses.** While a program is still `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `REVISION_REQUESTED`, either the `PartnerAccountLead` or the `FacultyChair` may add courses to it; attempting to add a course to a program in any later state is refused with `ERR_PROGRAM_INVALID_STATE`. A course is created with a `course_code` that is expected to be unique within its program though the server does not enforce that uniqueness, a `title`, a whole number of `credits`, a `content_owner` of `UNIVERSITY`, `PARTNER` or `JOINT`, and a `design_cost_cents` recording the one-time design cost the partner bears. Two optional flags shape its role: `required_for_launch` defaults to true and marks the course as one that must reach approved design status before the program may launch, and `ip_license_grant_back` defaults to false and records whether the university receives a perpetual grant-back licence when the partner or both parties hold the copyright. An optional `prerequisite_course_id` declares a self-referential prerequisite edge to another course of the same program, and is null when the course has no prerequisite. A course's `program_id` links it to its program.

The prerequisite graph is validated on every write that touches it — course creation and course amendment alike. A supplied `prerequisite_course_id` must name an existing course of the same program, otherwise `ERR_PREREQUISITE_COURSE_NOT_FOUND`; and the resulting edge must not create a cycle when the edges are followed transitively, otherwise `ERR_PREREQUISITE_CYCLE`. A course pointing at itself counts as a cycle. Detection walks the chain forward from the proposed prerequisite: the edge is a cycle exactly when that walk arrives back at the course being written. Amendment is permitted only while the course's `design_status` is `NOT_STARTED`, `IN_DESIGN` or `DESIGN_SUSPENDED`, and refused with `ERR_COURSE_INVALID_DESIGN_STATE` otherwise; it may revise `title`, `credits`, `design_cost_cents`, `required_for_launch`, `ip_license_grant_back` and `prerequisite_course_id`. Amendments matter downstream: the `design_cost_cents` and `credits` in force at teach-out certification are the amended values, and they are what the asset-value and remaining-credit figures of §3.9 read.

Course design runs through six states with a review cycle in the middle. A new course sits at `NOT_STARTED`. The partner starts work with `START_DESIGN`, taking `NOT_STARTED → IN_DESIGN`, which is legal only while the parent program is `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `REVISION_REQUESTED`, `APPROVED` or `LAUNCHED` — a parent program in any other state is refused with `ERR_COURSE_INVALID_DESIGN_STATE` rather than with the program error, because the gate belongs to the course design ladder of §2.5 item 20. The partner then hands the content to faculty with `SUBMIT_FOR_FACULTY_REVIEW`, taking `IN_DESIGN → FACULTY_REVIEW`; this requires either that the course has no `prerequisite_course_id`, or that the course its `prerequisite_course_id` names has itself reached `FACULTY_REVIEW` or `APPROVED`, so content cannot be reviewed ahead of the material it builds on. The `FacultyChair` may send it back with `REQUEST_REVISION`, taking `FACULTY_REVIEW → IN_DESIGN` and adding one to the course's `revision_round`; the payload may carry a `revision_note`, which is accepted without being stored on the course, and the `IN_DESIGN → FACULTY_REVIEW → IN_DESIGN` loop may repeat.

Approval is the faculty chair's act alone. `FACULTY_REVIEW → APPROVED` requires a non-empty `faculty_approver_name` supplied on the action, screened as `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` before the course is even looked up — the faculty member of record, recorded onto the course and null until then — and it requires the content-ownership rule to hold: a course may be approved when its `content_owner` is `UNIVERSITY`, or when its `content_owner` is `PARTNER` or `JOINT` *and* its `ip_license_grant_back` is true. A partner-owned or jointly-owned course without the grant-back licence is refused with `ERR_FACULTY_IP_OWNERSHIP_VIOLATION`, because the university would otherwise lose the ability to keep teaching its own curriculum after the partner leaves. Finally the chair may take `APPROVED → RETIRED`, but only when no other course of the same program still points its `prerequisite_course_id` at this course while sitting in `NOT_STARTED`, `IN_DESIGN` or `FACULTY_REVIEW` — retiring the foundation out from under unfinished dependents is refused with `ERR_COURSE_INVALID_DESIGN_STATE`. Both `RETIRED` and `DESIGN_SUSPENDED` are terminal design states. `DESIGN_SUSPENDED` is never requested by a client: it is reached only when the parent program enters `TEACH_OUT`, which drags every course then sitting in `IN_DESIGN` or `FACULTY_REVIEW` into `DESIGN_SUSPENDED`. Courses may be listed filtered by `program_id`, by `prerequisite_course_id` and by `design_status`, the first two matched as exact identifiers.

**Service-scope assignments.** Splitting responsibility for student support is the `UniversityProvost`'s prerogative, exercised one domain at a time while the program is still `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `REVISION_REQUESTED`. Each assignment names a `service_domain` drawn from `MARKETING`, `LEAD_GENERATION`, `ENROLLMENT_COUNSELING`, `COURSE_DESIGN`, `FACULTY_TRAINING`, `TECH_PLATFORM`, `STUDENT_ADVISING`, `TUTORING`, `RETENTION_OUTREACH`, `CAREER_SERVICES`, `ACCESSIBILITY_ACCOMMODATIONS` and `FIELD_PLACEMENT`, and a `responsible_party` of `UNIVERSITY` or `PARTNER`. Two optional inputs complete the row: `sla_response_hours`, the contractual first-response service level in hours, which is any integer of zero or more and defaults to 24, and `cost_bearer`, which defaults to `PARTNER` and may be either `UNIVERSITY` or `PARTNER` — so a domain the university performs may still be paid for by the partner, and vice versa. Each assignment records the `program_id` whose scope it splits.

A domain may appear at most once per program; a second assignment of the same `service_domain` is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_SERVICE_DOMAIN`. And three domains may never be delegated: `COURSE_DESIGN`, `FACULTY_TRAINING` and `ACCESSIBILITY_ACCOMMODATIONS` must carry `responsible_party` equal to `UNIVERSITY`, and an attempt to hand any of them to `PARTNER` is refused with `ERR_NON_DELEGABLE_SERVICE_DOMAIN` — even though the same partnership may legitimately make the partner the `cost_bearer` for them. The `ENROLLMENT_COUNSELING` assignment carries particular weight beyond the approval gate: its `sla_response_hours` is the yardstick against which counselor first-contact speed is judged in §3.6.

### 3.4. Going to Market: Launch, Exclusivity, Suspension

An `APPROVED` program is not yet live. The `PartnerAccountLead` takes it to `LAUNCHED` with `LAUNCH`, and four conditions must hold simultaneously. The program must be `APPROVED` and its parent partnership must still be `EXECUTED`. The logical clock's date must fall inside the half-open launch window — on or after `launch_window_start` and strictly before `launch_window_end` — with a date outside that interval refused as `ERR_OUTSIDE_LAUNCH_WINDOW`. The logical clock's date must be no later than the partnership's `expiry_date`, since a program cannot open under an expired contract; a clock date past expiry is refused as `ERR_CONTRACT_TERM_EXPIRED`. And the discipline must be clear of an exclusivity conflict.

**CIP exclusivity.** The partner's launch exclusivity is scoped to a university, not to a contract. When a program's `cip_code` appears in the `exclusivity_cip_codes` of either partnership involved, no other program may already hold that same discipline code at the same institution over an overlapping window: specifically, a launch is blocked when some other program in `LAUNCHED`, `SUSPENDED` or `TEACH_OUT`, belonging to any partnership sharing this partnership's `university_ipeds_id`, carries the same `cip_code` and has a half-open launch window that overlaps this program's own half-open window. Because both windows are half-open, two windows that merely touch — one's `launch_window_end` equal to the other's `launch_window_start` — do **not** overlap, and both may launch. A genuine overlap is refused with `ERR_CIP_EXCLUSIVITY_CONFLICT`. On a successful launch the program's `launched_at` is stamped with the logical clock instant.

A live program can be paused and restarted. The `UniversityProvost` takes `LAUNCHED → SUSPENDED` with `SUSPEND`, and while a program is `SUSPENDED` no new lead may be created for it. `RESUME` takes `SUSPENDED → LAUNCHED`, requires the parent partnership to still be `EXECUTED` and the logical clock date to be no later than the partnership's `expiry_date`, and reopens lead creation. This pause-and-restart loop may repeat as often as the parties need.

From either `LAUNCHED` or `SUSPENDED`, the provost may begin winding the program down with `BEGIN_TEACH_OUT`, optionally carrying a `teach_out_reason`. Both edges land in `TEACH_OUT`, stamp `teach_out_started_at` with the logical clock instant, and fire the identical cascade: every lead of the program in `NEW`, `CONTACTED` or `APPLIED` moves to `DISQUALIFIED` with `disqualification_reason` set to `PROGRAM_TEACH_OUT`; every course of the program in `IN_DESIGN` or `FACULTY_REVIEW` moves to `DESIGN_SUSPENDED`; and every counselor whose leads were disqualified recomputes `open_pipeline_count`, with any counselor whose count reaches zero moving `performance_status` to `INACTIVE_PENDING_REVIEW` while `incentive_eligibility` is untouched. Closing the program out of `TEACH_OUT` is covered in §3.9.

### 3.5. Enrollment Counselors: Compensation Compliance and Performance Standards

Counselors are onboarded by the `PartnerAccountLead` against a partnership named by `partnership_id`, with a `full_name`, an `employer` of `UNIVERSITY` or `PARTNER`, and a `monthly_base_salary_cents` recording base pay at onboarding before any plan is filed. Onboarding reads no partnership status: a counselor may be created under a partnership still in `DRAFT` or already winding down, and only the compensation filing that follows insists on `EXECUTED`. A new counselor has no plan: `comp_plan_type` is `NONE_ON_FILE`, `annual_base_cents` and `variable_component_cents` are 0, `variable_basis` is `NONE`, and both state dimensions sit at their initial values — `performance_status` at `NOT_RATED` and `incentive_eligibility` at `PENDING_COMPLIANCE_REVIEW`. Every performance counter — `conversion_rate` at 0.0, `open_pipeline_count`, `rated_lead_count`, `enrolled_lead_count`, `sla_breach_count`, `clawback_pending_cents` — starts at zero, and `last_review_user_id` is null until the first review is decided.

A counselor carries **two independent state dimensions on one row**, and their independence is normative. The performance dimension moves among `NOT_RATED`, `MEETS_STANDARD`, `WATCH`, `PROBATION` and `INACTIVE_PENDING_REVIEW`. The incentive-compliance dimension moves among `PENDING_COMPLIANCE_REVIEW`, `ELIGIBLE`, `SUSPENDED` and `PROHIBITED_PLAN_ON_FILE`. No transition on either dimension reads or writes the other: a performance run leaves `incentive_eligibility` exactly as it found it, a compliance decision leaves `performance_status` exactly as it found it, and the teach-out cascade that drives a counselor to `INACTIVE_PENDING_REVIEW` leaves the incentive dimension alone. Counselor lists may be filtered by `partnership_id`, by `performance_status` and by `incentive_eligibility`.

**Filing a compensation plan.** The `PartnerAccountLead` files or replaces a counselor's plan while the parent partnership is `EXECUTED`, supplying a `comp_plan_type`, an `annual_base_cents`, and optionally a `variable_component_cents` and a `variable_basis`, which fall back to 0 and `NONE` respectively when omitted. The filing replaces all four fields on the counselor and returns the incentive dimension to `PENDING_COMPLIANCE_REVIEW` from `ELIGIBLE`, `SUSPENDED` or `PROHIBITED_PLAN_ON_FILE` alike — filing a new plan always re-opens review — while leaving `performance_status` untouched. This is the incentive dimension's cycle edge and it may be traversed repeatedly.

The plan is screened against the incentive-compensation ban before it is accepted. So that prohibited shapes are expressible and therefore rejectable, `comp_plan_type` accepts two request-only members, `PER_ENROLLMENT_COMMISSION` and `PER_APPLICATION_BOUNTY`, and `variable_basis` accepts two request-only members, `ENROLLMENT_COUNT` and `APPLICATION_COUNT`; none of the four is ever persisted. The screen works as follows. A `variable_basis` of `ENROLLMENT_COUNT` or `APPLICATION_COUNT` is always prohibited, and a `comp_plan_type` of `PER_ENROLLMENT_COMMISSION` or `PER_APPLICATION_BOUNTY` is always prohibited: paying per head enrolled or per application filed is exactly what the ban forbids. A `comp_plan_type` of `SALARY_PLUS_QUALITATIVE` paired with a `variable_basis` of `QUALITATIVE_MULTI_FACTOR` is permitted only when the parent partnership's `bundled_services_exception_claimed` is true, since the qualitative multi-factor shape relies on the bundled-services exception; without the claim it is refused. It is that pairing alone that needs the claim: `SALARY_PLUS_QUALITATIVE` filed with a `variable_basis` of `NONE` or `GRADUATION_COUNT` is permitted whatever the partnership claims. A `comp_plan_type` of `FIXED_SALARY` or `GRADUATION_MILESTONE` is always permitted, the latter paired with the `GRADUATION_COUNT` basis. Any prohibited shape is refused with `ERR_INCENTIVE_COMP_PROHIBITED`.

A second fiscal limit applies to plans that clear the ban: `variable_component_cents` may not exceed 20 percent of `annual_base_cents` — one fifth of declared annual base pay is the whole variable envelope — and a plan that exceeds it is refused with `ERR_COMP_EXCEEDS_APPROVED_ENVELOPE`. The ban screen runs before the envelope check, so a plan that is both prohibited in shape and oversized in variable pay reports `ERR_INCENTIVE_COMP_PROHIBITED`. The declared `variable_component_cents` is also the base against which clawback accrues in §3.8.

**Compliance review.** A `ComplianceAuditor` decides a filed plan by supplying a `decision` and an optional `reviewer_note`; the note is accepted without being stored on the counselor. A `decision` of `APPROVE` takes `PENDING_COMPLIANCE_REVIEW → ELIGIBLE`, and requires the counselor's `comp_plan_type` not to be `NONE_ON_FILE` — there is nothing to approve before a plan exists — and, when a prior review exists, requires the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from the recorded `last_review_user_id`. A `decision` of `REJECT_PLAN` takes `PENDING_COMPLIANCE_REVIEW → PROHIBITED_PLAN_ON_FILE`, after which no lead may be created naming that counselor. A `decision` of `SUSPEND` takes either `PENDING_COMPLIANCE_REVIEW` or `ELIGIBLE` to `SUSPENDED`. Every decision records the acting `X-User-ID` as `last_review_user_id`. A decision the current `incentive_eligibility` does not permit is refused with `ERR_COUNSELOR_INVALID_REVIEW_STATE`; a repeat decision by the identity that decided the previous review is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`.

**Performance runs.** Either the `PartnerAccountLead` or the `UniversityProvost` may run performance evaluation across every counselor of a partnership over a closed date window bounded by `window_start` and `window_end`. The window is inclusive on both ends and is interpreted at day granularity in UTC: a lead counts as inside the window when its `created_at` falls at or after midnight at the start of `window_start` and at or before the last millisecond of `window_end`. Counselors whose `performance_status` is `INACTIVE_PENDING_REVIEW` are excluded from the run entirely and keep their status untouched.

For each included counselor the run recomputes three window counters and one ratio. `rated_lead_count` counts the counselor's leads in `CONTACTED`, `APPLIED`, `ENROLLED` or `DISQUALIFIED` whose `created_at` falls in the window. `enrolled_lead_count` counts, over the same window, those in `ENROLLED`. `sla_breach_count` counts, over the same window, the leads whose `hours_to_first_contact` is recorded and exceeds the `sla_response_hours` of the `ENROLLMENT_COUNSELING` service-scope assignment of that lead's program — so the yardstick is per-program and comes from the split agreed in §3.3, and a lead never contacted at all contributes no breach because its `hours_to_first_contact` is still null. A lead whose program carries no `ENROLLMENT_COUNSELING` assignment at all also contributes no breach, there being no yardstick for it to exceed.

Rating then proceeds as follows. When `rated_lead_count` is fewer than 4, there is too little signal to judge: `conversion_rate` is set to 0.0000 and `performance_status` is set to `NOT_RATED`. Otherwise `conversion_rate` is `enrolled_lead_count` divided by `rated_lead_count`, rounded half up to 4 decimal places, and a base rating is chosen from it — `MEETS_STANDARD` when the rate is at least 0.2500, otherwise `WATCH` when the rate is at least 0.1500, otherwise `PROBATION`. Both thresholds are inclusive. A separate breach ratio is then computed as `sla_breach_count` divided by `rated_lead_count`, rounded half up to 4 decimal places; when that ratio is strictly greater than 0.3000 — the comparison is exclusive, so a ratio of exactly 0.3000 triggers nothing — the counselor is moved one step down the ladder from the base rating, `MEETS_STANDARD` becoming `WATCH` and `WATCH` becoming `PROBATION`, with `PROBATION` absorbing the downgrade and staying where it is. Otherwise the base rating stands.

This makes the performance dimension cyclic: a counselor may be downgraded from `MEETS_STANDARD` to `WATCH` or `PROBATION` on one run, recover to `MEETS_STANDARD` on the next, and be downgraded again on a later one. A run reports back an `evaluated_count` of the counselors it included, a `rated_count` of those that had at least 4 rated leads and therefore received a real rating, a `downgraded_count` of those whose breach ratio applied the one-step downgrade — counted whenever the ratio clears the threshold, the `PROBATION` case where the step has nowhere left to go included — and a `run_completed_at` stamped from the logical clock. The run mutates only the counselors it evaluated: it neither refreshes the parent partnership nor advances its `version`.

### 3.6. Lead Generation, Marketing Spend and Conversion to Enrollment

**Marketing.** The partner posts media entries against a program's ledger, one entry at a time, while the parent partnership is `EXECUTED`. An entry names its `program_id`, an `entry_type` of `SPEND` or `CREDIT_MEMO`, a `channel` of `PAID_SEARCH`, `PAID_SOCIAL`, `DISPLAY`, `EVENTS` or `LIST_PURCHASE`, an `amount_cents` of zero or more whose direction is carried entirely by `entry_type` — the sign never lives in the number, and only a negative value is rejected — and a `spend_date` on which the amount is recognised in the marketing ledger. A `reversal_note` is a free-text explanation and is required, as a non-empty string, on a `CREDIT_MEMO` entry; that requirement is screened before the program is looked up. Ledger entries are never amended or deleted.

A `SPEND` entry must fit inside the envelope: its `amount_cents` may not exceed the partnership's current `marketing_budget_remaining_cents`, and an entry that would overspend is refused with `ERR_MARKETING_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`. A `CREDIT_MEMO` faces no such ceiling, since it returns money to the envelope. Every posting recomputes the program's `marketing_spend_net_cents` and `blended_cac_cents`, and the partnership's `marketing_budget_remaining_cents` and `unrecouped_marketing_spend_cents`, exactly as §2.7 requires.

**Blended acquisition cost.** A program's `blended_cac_cents` is its `marketing_spend_net_cents` divided by its `total_enrollment_count`, rounded half up to whole cents. When `total_enrollment_count` is zero the value is null — never 0 — because dividing spend by no enrollments has no meaning, and the distinction between "no students yet" and "free students" must survive into the response. The figure is recomputed on every media posting and again each time a lead converts into an enrollment.

**The funnel.** With a program `LAUNCHED`, the `PartnerAccountLead` creates leads. A lead names the `program_id` it inquired about, the `counselor_id` of the counselor who owns it, a `source_channel` of `PAID_SEARCH`, `PAID_SOCIAL`, `ORGANIC`, `EMPLOYER_REFERRAL` or `LIST_PURCHASE`, and a `prospect_external_id` that is the partner-side prospect identifier and is expected to be unique within the program, though the server does not enforce that uniqueness. The named counselor need only exist: the server does not require it to belong to the same partnership as the program. Creation is refused with `ERR_PROGRAM_INVALID_STATE` when the program is not `LAUNCHED` — including while it is `SUSPENDED` — with `ERR_CONTRACT_TERM_EXPIRED` when the logical clock date is past the partnership's `expiry_date`, and with `ERR_COUNSELOR_INCENTIVE_SUSPENDED` when the named counselor's `incentive_eligibility` is `PROHIBITED_PLAN_ON_FILE`. A new lead is `NEW`, and creation recomputes the owning counselor's `open_pipeline_count`, which counts that counselor's leads in `NEW`, `CONTACTED` or `APPLIED`.

The partner advances a lead through the funnel. `CONTACT` takes `NEW → CONTACTED` and requires `hours_to_first_contact` — the hours between lead creation and first counselor contact — to be supplied on the action, where it is recorded onto the lead; it is null until then. Neither `CONTACT` nor `DISQUALIFY` inspects the program's status, so either may still be applied to a lead of a `SUSPENDED` or `TEACH_OUT` program. `APPLY` takes `CONTACTED → APPLIED`, requires the lead's program to still be `LAUNCHED`, and stamps `qualified_at` with the logical clock instant. `DISQUALIFY` takes a lead in `NEW`, `CONTACTED` or `APPLIED` to `DISQUALIFIED` and requires a `disqualification_reason` of `NOT_QUALIFIED`, `UNREACHABLE`, `WITHDREW_INTEREST` or `PROGRAM_TEACH_OUT` to be supplied; the same terminal state is also reached automatically by the teach-out cascade, which supplies `PROGRAM_TEACH_OUT` itself. Both `ENROLLED` and `DISQUALIFIED` are terminal. Every advance recomputes the counselor's `open_pipeline_count`. An action the lead's `status` does not permit is refused with `ERR_LEAD_INVALID_STATE`. Leads may be listed filtered by `program_id`, by `counselor_id` and by `status`.

**Conversion.** Turning an `APPLIED` lead into a student is a `UniversityBursar` act, and it is the point where the partner's funnel becomes the university's revenue. Conversion requires the lead to be `APPLIED`, its program to be `LAUNCHED`, the logical clock date to be no later than the partnership's `expiry_date`, the program's `total_enrollment_count` to be strictly less than its `enrollment_cap` — a program already at capacity is refused with `ERR_ENROLLMENT_CAP_REACHED` — and the owning counselor's `incentive_eligibility` not to be `PROHIBITED_PLAN_ON_FILE`, refused otherwise with `ERR_COUNSELOR_INCENTIVE_SUSPENDED`.

The conversion payload carries the university's own `student_external_id` for the student, the `credits_enrolled` registered for this term — any integer of zero or more — and the term boundaries `term_start_date` and `term_end_date`. The lead moves `APPLIED → ENROLLED`, and an enrollment is created in `ENROLLED` that records its `program_id` and the `lead_id` it converted from. Two money fields are derived immediately: `tuition_per_credit_snapshot_cents` copies the program's `tuition_per_credit_cents` as it stands at conversion, so later list-price changes never restate revenue already booked, and `tuition_charged_cents` is `credits_enrolled` multiplied by that snapshot. `credits_completed` starts at 0, `collected_cents` and `refunded_cents` and `recognized_to_date_cents` start at 0, and `withdrawal_date`, `refund_pct_applied` and `days_elapsed_at_withdrawal` are all null. The program's `active_enrollment_count` and `total_enrollment_count` each increase by one, the program's `blended_cac_cents` is recomputed against the new denominator, and the counselor's `open_pipeline_count` is recomputed. Enrollments may be listed filtered by `program_id`, by `lead_id` and by `status`.

### 3.7. Tuition Collection, Academic Progress and Withdrawal

The `UniversityBursar` posts cash to an enrollment's ledger while the enrollment is `ENROLLED` or `ACTIVE`; posting to a `COMPLETED` or `WITHDRAWN` enrollment is refused with `ERR_ENROLLMENT_INVALID_STATE`. Each entry names its `enrollment_id`, an `entry_type` of `PAYMENT` or `REFUND`, an `amount_cents` of zero or more whose direction is carried by `entry_type` — a zero entry is accepted and simply moves nothing — a `posted_date` on which it posts to the bursar ledger, and an optional free-text `reference_note`. Entries carry a `generated_by` marker that the server sets: `BURSAR_POST` for an entry a bursar posts by hand, and `WITHDRAWAL_REFUND` for the compensating entry the withdrawal cascade creates on its own. A manual `REFUND` may not exceed what remains collected — its `amount_cents` must be no greater than the enrollment's `collected_cents` less its `refunded_cents` — and an over-refund is refused with `ERR_REFUND_EXCEEDS_COLLECTED`.

Each posting recomputes the enrollment's `collected_cents` and `refunded_cents` and the partnership's `collected_tuition_cents`, per the conservation rule of §2.7. The very first `PAYMENT` entry has a further effect: it takes the enrollment `ENROLLED → ACTIVE`, because a student who has paid something is academically live. That transition is a system event rather than a client action, and it fires exactly when the enrollment is `ENROLLED` and `collected_cents` is greater than zero after the posting.

Academic progress is the `UniversityProvost`'s to record. `RECORD_PROGRESS` is a self-loop on `ACTIVE`: it requires a `credits_completed` value that must be at least 0 and no greater than the enrollment's `credits_enrolled` — a value above `credits_enrolled` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, and that comparison runs after the enrollment-state gate, so a `RECORD_PROGRESS` aimed at an enrollment that is not `ACTIVE` reports `ERR_ENROLLMENT_INVALID_STATE` first — writes it onto the enrollment, and leaves `status` at `ACTIVE`. It may be exercised repeatedly, each time changing the milestone recognition base of §3.8 without changing state. `COMPLETE` takes `ACTIVE → COMPLETED` and requires `credits_completed` to equal `credits_enrolled` exactly, failing which the refusal is `ERR_ENROLLMENT_INVALID_STATE` — a student cannot graduate a term they have not finished — and reduces the program's `active_enrollment_count` by one. Note that `total_enrollment_count` is unaffected, since a completed student still counts against the cap.

**Withdrawal and Cascade B.** `WITHDRAW` is a `UniversityBursar` act on an enrollment in `ENROLLED` or `ACTIVE`, carrying a `withdrawal_date` that must be no earlier than the enrollment's `term_start_date` — an earlier date is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, checked after the enrollment-state gate — plus an optional `withdrawal_reason`, which is accepted without being stored on the enrollment. Both `COMPLETED` and `WITHDRAWN` are terminal enrollment states.

The refund is computed from the partnership's refund schedule as it stands at the moment of withdrawal. First, `days_elapsed_at_withdrawal` is the whole number of days from `term_start_date` to `withdrawal_date` counting the first day, so a withdrawal on the term's opening day is day 1. Next, `refund_pct_applied` is the `refund_pct` of the **first** band of `refund_schedule`, in the schedule's declared order, whose `through_day` is either null or greater than or equal to `days_elapsed_at_withdrawal`; should no band match at all — a schedule whose every `through_day` is bounded and short of the elapsed count — the applied percentage is 0. The comparison is inclusive on `through_day`, which is why under the standard schedule a day-7 withdrawal takes the full-refund band while a day-8 withdrawal takes the half band. The refund amount is then the smaller of two figures: what is actually refundable, being `collected_cents` less `refunded_cents`, and what is contractually owed, being `tuition_charged_cents` multiplied by `refund_pct_applied` and rounded half up to whole cents.

Cascade B then runs in three causally ordered hops, plus one statement effect.

- **Hop 1** creates a tuition ledger entry with `entry_type` of `REFUND` and `generated_by` of `WITHDRAWAL_REFUND` for that refund amount — carrying the `withdrawal_date` as its `posted_date` and a null `reference_note`, and suppressed altogether when the computed amount is zero, an empty entry being nothing but clutter in the journal — and recomputes the enrollment's `refunded_cents`, the program's `active_enrollment_count` (down by one, since the student is no longer active) and the partnership's `collected_tuition_cents`.
- **Hop 2** is caused by hop one's refund entry rather than by the withdraw action itself, and reaches the counselor who owned the converting lead. When `days_elapsed_at_withdrawal` is no greater than 45 — the clawback window, a month and a half into the term — that counselor's `clawback_pending_cents` increases by its `variable_component_cents` multiplied by the enrollment's `refund_pct_applied`, rounded half up to whole cents. A withdrawal on day 46 or later accrues nothing.
- **Hop 3** is caused by hop two's accrual. When the counselor's `clawback_pending_cents` has reached 50,000 cents — five hundred dollars of accumulated clawback — and its `incentive_eligibility` is `ELIGIBLE`, that dimension moves to `SUSPENDED`. The comparison is inclusive: exactly 50,000 cents trips it. `performance_status` is left unchanged, once again demonstrating the dimensions' independence. A counselor already `SUSPENDED`, `PROHIBITED_PLAN_ON_FILE` or `PENDING_COMPLIANCE_REVIEW` is not moved by this hop.

Independently of those hops, the withdrawal invalidates settlement work already done. Every statement of the partnership whose `status` is `CALCULATED` or `UNIVERSITY_APPROVED`, and whose fully closed period contains the enrollment's `term_start_date`, moves to `STALE` with `requires_recalculation` set to true. A statement already `REMITTED`, `DISPUTED` or `RESOLVED_ADJUSTED` is not staled by this rule.

### 3.8. Revenue Recognition, Partner Share and Remittance Settlement

Settlement is where the partnership's economics are actually realised, and it is the most tightly ordered computation in the system.

**Cutting a statement.** A `UniversityBursar` cuts a statement for a partnership, supplying `period_start` and `period_end` — the settlement period is a fully closed interval including both endpoints — together with the mandatory `expected_partnership_version` of §2.6. The partnership must be `EXECUTED`, the logical clock date must be no later than its `expiry_date`, and `period_end` must fall strictly before the logical clock date: a period that has not fully elapsed cannot be settled, and an attempt to do so is refused with `ERR_PERIOD_NOT_ELAPSED` — though the overlap screen of §2.5 item 40 runs ahead of this one, so a request that is both overlapping and unelapsed reports `ERR_STATEMENT_PERIOD_OVERLAP`. The requested period must also not overlap any existing statement of the same partnership in `CALCULATED`, `UNIVERSITY_APPROVED`, `DISPUTED`, `RESOLVED_ADJUSTED`, `STALE` or `REMITTED` — that is, in any status at all. Because periods are fully closed, two periods that merely touch, one's `period_end` equal to the other's `period_start`, **do** overlap, and the second is refused with `ERR_STATEMENT_PERIOD_OVERLAP`. A new statement is created in `CALCULATED`, records the acting `X-User-ID` as `authored_by_user_id`, sets `partnership_id`, initialises `adjustment_delta_cents` to 0 and `requires_recalculation` to false, leaves `approved_by_user_id`, `resolved_by_user_id`, `dispute_reason`, `remittance_reference` and `remitted_at` null, and increments the partnership's `version` by one. Statements may be listed filtered by `partnership_id` and `status`.

**Step 1 — recognised revenue.** The statement's `recognition_method_applied` records which recognition variant produced its figures, inherited from the partnership's `revenue_recognition_method` unless a dispute resolution overrides it. Recognition works one **line** per in-scope enrollment. In scope are the enrollments belonging to programs of this partnership whose `status` is `ENROLLED`, `ACTIVE`, `COMPLETED` or `WITHDRAWN` and whose `term_start_date` is no later than the statement's `period_end`. Lines are ordered by the enrollment's `created_at` ascending, with ties broken by `student_external_id` ascending, and that order is pinned: the share computation below walks the lines in exactly this sequence. The statement's `line_count` records how many lines entered the computation.

Under the `PERIOD_STRAIGHT_LINE` variant, revenue accrues by elapsed calendar days of the term. The term's length in days is the number of days from `term_start_date` to `term_end_date` counting both endpoints. For any given date, the elapsed count is zero when that date falls before `term_start_date`, and otherwise is the number of days from `term_start_date` to that date counting the first day, capped at the term length so a term cannot over-recognise past its end. The cumulative recognised amount as of a date is `tuition_charged_cents` multiplied by that elapsed count, divided by the term length, rounded half up to whole cents. The line's amount is then the cumulative amount as of `period_end` minus the cumulative amount as of the day immediately before `period_start`. This cumulative-minus-prior form is mandatory: computing a period slice directly would let successive periods drift away from the cumulative curve. So a 30-day slice of a 106-day term recognises thirty one-hundred-and-sixths of tuition regardless of how far the student has actually got.

Under the `MILESTONE_CHRONOLOGICAL` variant, revenue accrues by academic progress instead. The cumulative recognised amount is `tuition_charged_cents` multiplied by `credits_completed`, divided by `credits_enrolled`, rounded half up to whole cents, and the line's amount is that cumulative figure minus the enrollment's `recognized_to_date_cents`. An enrollment registered for zero credits takes a cumulative figure of 0 rather than raising an arithmetic error. Identical enrollment data therefore produces a different period figure under the two variants whenever elapsed time and academic progress disagree.

The statement's `recognized_revenue_cents` is the sum of the line amounts, and each line contributes to the enrollment's `recognized_to_date_cents`, the program's `recognized_revenue_cents` and the partnership's `recognized_revenue_cents` and `contract_year_recognized_cents`. The last of those accumulates recognition attributable to statement periods whose `period_start` falls inside the current contract year — the twelve-month window that begins on the partnership's `effective_date`, repeats on each anniversary, and is selected by the logical clock's date — and it is what positions the marginal tier walk below.

**Recompute semantics.** When a statement is recomputed, whether by dispute resolution or by recalculation, the statement's own previously recorded line contributions are first **removed** from the enrollments' `recognized_to_date_cents` and from the partnership's `contract_year_recognized_cents`, so that the recompute sees exactly the baseline the original computation saw. Without this reversal, a milestone recompute of an already-recognised period would net against itself and produce a near-zero figure.

**Step 2 — the partner's gross share.** Under `FEE_FOR_SERVICE`, `share_gross_cents` is 0 and the tier walk is skipped entirely. Under `REVENUE_SHARE`, the walk proceeds line by line in the pinned order, with a running tier position that starts at the partnership's `contract_year_recognized_cents` as it stood before this statement — concretely, the sum of `recognized_revenue_cents` over the partnership's *other* statements whose `period_start` falls in the contract year containing this statement's own `period_start` — and advances as revenue is consumed — so a single line may straddle two marginal tiers, and successive lines see successively higher tier positions.

For each line, the amount remaining to allocate starts as the line's amount, and an exact-decimal accumulator for the line's share starts at zero. The tiers of `revenue_share_tiers` are visited in their declared order. A tier's room is its `through_cents` minus the running position, except that the final unbounded tier — the one whose `through_cents` is null — always has room for whatever remains. A tier whose room is zero or negative is already exhausted and is skipped. Otherwise the amount taken from that tier is the smaller of the remaining line amount and the tier's room; the taken amount multiplied by that tier's `partner_rate` is added to the line's accumulator; the taken amount is subtracted from the remaining line amount and added to the running position; and the walk stops for this line once nothing remains. Two edge shapes fall out of that mechanic rather than out of any separate rule: a schedule with no unbounded final tier simply leaves unshared whatever the bounded tiers cannot absorb, and a line whose amount is negative — which a milestone recompute can produce — is walked identically, taking the whole negative amount against the first tier with room and moving the running position backwards by it. A line amount of exactly zero is skipped and leaves the running position alone.

The rounding order here is pinned and consequential: **each line's accumulated share is rounded half up to whole cents, and the rounded integers are then summed** into `share_gross_cents`. Summing exact line shares and rounding once at the end is forbidden. The two orders differ by whole cents — two lines of 187,500 and 62,500 cents at a rate of 0.575 give 107,813 plus 35,938, so 143,751 cents per-line, against 143,750 cents if summed first and rounded once.

**Steps 3 through 7 — the settlement waterfall.** The remaining figures are computed in a strictly pinned order in which every step reads only the steps above it.

- Step 3 derives a recoupment ceiling as `share_gross_cents` multiplied by 0.15 and taken with a **floor**, not rounded half up: the cap never rounds up in the partner's favour. At most fifteen percent of the gross share may be swept to marketing recovery in any one period.
- Step 4 sets `marketing_recoupment_cents` to the smaller of the partnership's `unrecouped_marketing_spend_cents` and that ceiling.
- Step 5 sets `subtotal_cents` to `share_gross_cents` minus `marketing_recoupment_cents`.
- Step 6 selects `holdback_pct` from `subtotal_cents`: 0.10 when the subtotal is at least $100,000, and 0.05 otherwise — the comparison is inclusive at the trigger. `holdback_cents` is then `subtotal_cents` multiplied by `holdback_pct`, rounded half up to whole cents. The holdback exists to fund the teach-out obligations of §3.9.
- Step 7 sets `fee_for_service_cents` to the partnership's `fee_schedule_monthly_cents` when `contract_structure` is `FEE_FOR_SERVICE` and to 0 otherwise, and sets `net_payable_cents` to `subtotal_cents` minus `holdback_cents` plus `fee_for_service_cents`.

The apparent circularity between recoupment and holdback is resolved precisely by this order: the recoupment ceiling is a function of `share_gross_cents` and never of `subtotal_cents`, while the holdback tier is a function of `subtotal_cents` and therefore of the position *after* recoupment. A larger recoupment can consequently push a statement down into the lower holdback tier. Evaluating the holdback before the recoupment, or capping the recoupment on `subtotal_cents`, is forbidden.

**The settlement lifecycle.** A `CALCULATED` statement forks. On the straight branch, a `UniversityProvost` approves it, taking `CALCULATED → UNIVERSITY_APPROVED`; this requires `requires_recalculation` to be false and the approving `X-User-ID` to differ from `authored_by_user_id`, records `approved_by_user_id`, and leaves `adjustment_delta_cents` at 0. On the dispute branch, the `PartnerAccountLead` disputes it with a mandatory `dispute_reason`, taking `CALCULATED → DISPUTED`; the reason is recorded on the statement. A `UniversityBursar` then resolves the dispute, taking `DISPUTED → RESOLVED_ADJUSTED`: the resolution may carry a `recognition_method_override`, which replaces `recognition_method_applied` when supplied and leaves it unchanged when not, and an optional `adjustment_note`. Resolution re-runs all three settlement computations — so `recognized_revenue_cents`, `share_gross_cents`, `marketing_recoupment_cents`, `subtotal_cents`, `holdback_pct`, `holdback_cents`, `fee_for_service_cents` and `net_payable_cents` are all recomputed under the recompute semantics above — pins `adjustment_delta_cents` to the new `net_payable_cents` minus the pre-resolution `net_payable_cents`, and records the acting `X-User-ID` as `resolved_by_user_id`. Resolution leaves `approved_by_user_id` exactly as it stands — only recalculation clears it — and the optional `adjustment_note` is accepted without being stored. The two branches then **join**: a `RESOLVED_ADJUSTED` statement is approved by a `UniversityProvost` exactly as a `CALCULATED` one is, taking `RESOLVED_ADJUSTED → UNIVERSITY_APPROVED`, subject to the same segregation-of-duties check against `authored_by_user_id` and recording the same `approved_by_user_id`.

From the join there is one exit. A `UniversityBursar` remits with a mandatory `remittance_reference`, taking `UNIVERSITY_APPROVED → REMITTED`, which requires `net_payable_cents` to be strictly greater than zero — remitting nothing, or a negative balance, is refused with `ERR_NOTHING_TO_REMIT`. Remittance stamps `remitted_at` with the logical clock instant, adds `net_payable_cents` to the partnership's `remitted_to_partner_cents`, adds `holdback_cents` to the partnership's `holdback_balance_cents`, and subtracts `marketing_recoupment_cents` from the partnership's `unrecouped_marketing_spend_cents`. `REMITTED` is terminal.

Cutting across all of that is the staling cycle. A withdrawal in a closed period drives a `CALCULATED` or `UNIVERSITY_APPROVED` statement to `STALE` with `requires_recalculation` true, as §3.7 describes. A `UniversityBursar` brings it back with recalculation, taking `STALE → CALCULATED`: every computed money field is recomputed against current data under the recompute semantics, `requires_recalculation` returns to false, and `approved_by_user_id` is cleared to null so that a previously granted approval cannot survive a restatement. This `CALCULATED → STALE → CALCULATED` loop may repeat as later withdrawals land. An action the statement's `status` does not permit — or an approval attempted while `requires_recalculation` is true — is refused with `ERR_STATEMENT_INVALID_STATE`.

### 3.9. Contract Exit: Teach-Out, Asset Transfer and Close-Out

Exit is the obligation the whole holdback mechanism exists to fund: students already enrolled must be able to finish, and someone must pay for teaching them out.

**Winding down the partnership.** A `UniversityProvost` begins the exit from an `EXECUTED` partnership with `WIND_DOWN`, supplying a mandatory `wind_down_effective_date` that must be on or after the logical clock's date — an earlier date is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, screened after the partnership-state gate — and optionally a `wind_down_reason`. The supplied date is recorded on the partnership. The action then fires Cascade A exactly as §2.8 specifies: launched and suspended programs are dragged into `TEACH_OUT` in ascending `created_at` order with `teach_out_started_at` stamped, their open leads are disqualified with `PROGRAM_TEACH_OUT`, their in-flight course designs are suspended, and counselors left with an empty pipeline move to `INACTIVE_PENDING_REVIEW` with their incentive dimension untouched. The partnership's `version` increments.

**Drafting a teach-out plan.** For a program in `TEACH_OUT` — and only then — the `UniversityProvost` drafts a plan naming the `program_id` being taught out, a `teach_out_end_date` by which every remaining student must be able to complete, a `student_notification_date` on which affected students are told, an `asset_transfer_scope` of `NONE`, `COURSE_CONTENT_ONLY`, `COURSE_CONTENT_AND_LEAD_DATA` or `FULL_PLATFORM`, and an optional free-text `partner_obligation_note`. Drafting snapshots the situation: `affected_enrollment_count` is the number of the program's enrollments in `ENROLLED` or `ACTIVE` at plan creation, `remaining_credit_count` is the sum over those affected enrollments of `credits_enrolled` less `credits_completed`, and `teach_out_cost_cents` is that remaining credit count multiplied by a teach-out unit cost of $180 per credit. The plan is created in `DRAFT`, with `holdback_applied_cents`, `partner_obligation_cents`, `university_residual_cents` and `transferred_asset_value_cents` not yet allocated and therefore reported as 0 rather than null, and with `certified_by_user_id` and `certified_at` null. Creating a newer plan for the same program moves any existing `DRAFT` plan to `SUPERSEDED`, from which no further transition is possible; a plan already `CERTIFIED` is left untouched by that sweep, so a program drafted and certified twice ends up holding more than one `CERTIFIED` plan, and the close gate below is satisfied by any one of them.

**Certification and the liability waterfall.** Certification is the `ComplianceAuditor`'s act, taking `DRAFT → CERTIFIED`. It requires the plan to be `DRAFT`, the parent program to be in `TEACH_OUT` — a program that has moved on is refused with `ERR_PROGRAM_INVALID_STATE`, checked after the plan's own state and before the notice period — and `asset_transfer_confirmed` to be present and true on the request — a request that supplies false fails the request screen with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` — and it may carry an optional `certification_note`, accepted without being stored. It also requires adequate student notice: `student_notification_date` must precede `teach_out_end_date` by at least 90 whole days, and a shorter notice is refused with `ERR_TEACH_OUT_NOTICE_TOO_SHORT`.

On certification the teach-out liability is allocated into three buckets, filled strictly in this order and summing exactly to `teach_out_cost_cents`.

- `holdback_applied_cents` is the smaller of the partnership's `holdback_balance_cents` and `teach_out_cost_cents`, floored at 0 should that balance itself have gone negative — the money already withheld from remittances is consumed first.
- What remains after the holdback pool is the cost minus `holdback_applied_cents`. `partner_obligation_cents` is the smaller of that remainder and a cap of `teach_out_cost_cents` multiplied by 0.40 taken with a **floor**, not rounded half up: the partner's cash exposure is capped at forty percent of the total teach-out cost no matter how large the residual is.
- `university_residual_cents` is `teach_out_cost_cents` less `holdback_applied_cents` less `partner_obligation_cents` — whatever the university absorbs after the pool and the capped partner obligation. The three buckets conserve the cost exactly.

The asset value transferring at exit is computed alongside. When `asset_transfer_scope` is `NONE`, `transferred_asset_value_cents` is 0. Otherwise it is the sum of `design_cost_cents` over the program's courses whose `design_status` is `APPROVED`, `DESIGN_SUSPENDED` or `RETIRED`, multiplied by the scope factor and rounded half up to whole cents — half the book value for `COURSE_CONTENT_ONLY`, three quarters for `COURSE_CONTENT_AND_LEAD_DATA`, and the whole of it for `FULL_PLATFORM`. Courses still in `NOT_STARTED`, `IN_DESIGN` or `FACULTY_REVIEW` contribute nothing, because unfinished content has no transferable book value.

Certification then subtracts `holdback_applied_cents` from the partnership's `holdback_balance_cents`, releasing that money against the obligation it was withheld for, and records the acting `X-User-ID` as `certified_by_user_id` together with a `certified_at` stamped from the logical clock. `CERTIFIED` is a terminal plan state, as is `SUPERSEDED`. Certifying a plan that is not `DRAFT` is refused with `ERR_TEACH_OUT_PLAN_INVALID_STATE`.

**Closing a program.** With a certified plan in place, the `UniversityProvost` takes `TEACH_OUT → CLOSED`, which requires the program to be in `TEACH_OUT`, a plan with `status` `CERTIFIED` to exist for it, and `active_enrollment_count` to be exactly zero — no student may be left mid-stream. A close attempted without a certified plan, or with students still active, is refused with `ERR_TEACH_OUT_OBLIGATIONS_UNMET`. Success stamps `closed_at` with the logical clock instant. `CLOSED` is terminal, as is `REJECTED`.

**Closing out the partnership.** Finally the provost takes `WIND_DOWN → CLOSED_OUT`. Two conditions must hold: every program of the partnership must have settled into `CLOSED`, `REJECTED` or `DRAFT` — a program still `SUBMITTED`, `REVISION_REQUESTED`, `APPROVED`, `LAUNCHED`, `SUSPENDED` or `TEACH_OUT` blocks the close-out — and no statement of the partnership may still be open, meaning none may remain in `CALCULATED`, `UNIVERSITY_APPROVED`, `DISPUTED`, `RESOLVED_ADJUSTED` or `STALE`. Statements in `REMITTED` do not block, since they are settled. Either failure is refused with `ERR_TEACH_OUT_OBLIGATIONS_UNMET`. The close-out increments the partnership's `version`, and `CLOSED_OUT` is the terminal state of the whole arrangement.
