# Minor League Affiliation & Player Development API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A major league organization does not develop players in one place. It develops them across a chain of licensed minor league clubs and international academy complexes, each bound to the parent club by a Player Development License that dictates facility standards, staffing floors, housing and meal provision, travel class, and the split of reimbursable expense between the affiliate and the club. Every player movement inside that chain — a promotion from High-A to Double-A, a demotion to the complex, a rehabilitation assignment overlaying a full-season roster spot, the signing of an international amateur into a Dominican academy — is simultaneously a development decision, a roster-capacity decision, a medical decision, a payroll decision, and a filing obligation to the league office. Today these five concerns live in five disconnected spreadsheets, and the organization discovers a violation only when the league rejects a transaction report or an inspector downgrades a clubhouse.

This document specifies the API that consolidates them. The service is the authoritative record of affiliation agreements and their expense settlements, of league facility inspections and the remediation cycles they open, of coaching and support-staff allocation with curriculum consistency across affiliates, of player assignment across competition levels with workload governance and promotion criteria, of domestic reserve list and option year accounting, of medical clearance gating for assignment, rehab and return-to-play, of international bonus pool allocation with overage taxation, and of the league transaction reports every movement produces. The system's guiding stance is that no development decision may be recorded unless the compliance, capacity, medical and fiscal preconditions attached to it all hold at the instant of the write — the service refuses rather than reconciles later.

The API is a JSON-over-HTTP service whose business resources are peer collections mounted at the server root. Because deadlines, expiries, rolling workload windows and date intervals pervade the domain, the server reads a logical clock rather than wall-clock time, and that clock is settable so that a season's worth of development can be exercised deterministically.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

This section fixes the system-wide contract: authentication, the response envelope, pagination, identity and audit fields, the logical clock, validation ordering, concurrency control, rounding discipline, and the cross-cutting gates that every affiliate-scoped operation passes through.

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request must carry two headers. `X-User-ID` identifies the acting user and `X-User-Role` declares the capacity in which they act. If either header is absent or present but empty, the request is rejected with `ERR_MISSING_AUTH_HEADERS` (HTTP 401) before any other processing occurs. If `X-User-Role` carries a value outside the five recognized roles, the request is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_ROLE` (HTTP 401).

The five roles are `FarmDirector`, `MedicalDirector`, `ComplianceOfficer`, `AffiliateOperator`, and `InternationalScout`. Their remits are as follows. A `FarmDirector` runs the development pipeline: registering players and staff members, moving players between affiliates and levels, allocating coaching staff, recording workload outings and driving development plans. A `MedicalDirector` owns medical clearance for assignment, rehab start and return-to-play, together with the workload ceilings those clearances impose. A `ComplianceOfficer` certifies affiliation agreements and their expense settlements, files league facility inspections and their dispositions, opens and administers international bonus pools, and certifies league transaction filings. An `AffiliateOperator` acts on behalf of exactly one minor league club: it drafts and submits that club's own agreement and submits remediation for that club's own inspections. An `InternationalScout` signs international amateur players and submits their bonuses against the organization's signing pool for compliance approval, and never approves its own submissions.

Role gating operates at two levels. First, the acting role must appear on the endpoint being called; if it does not, the response is `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403). Second, on every disposition endpoint the requested `action` value must itself be permitted for the acting role, and a role that is authorized on the endpoint but not for the requested action receives the same `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403). Concretely: `SUBMIT` and `SUBMIT_REMEDIATION` are `AffiliateOperator`-only; `ACTIVATE`, `REJECT`, `SUSPEND`, `REINSTATE`, `TERMINATE`, `SETTLE`, `CLOSE`, `REOPEN`, `WAIVE`, `REQUEST_REVISION`, `APPROVE`, `VOID`, `FILE`, `LEAGUE_ACCEPT`, `LEAGUE_REJECT` and `REFILE` are `ComplianceOfficer`-only; and `ISSUE`, `DENY` and `REVOKE` are `MedicalDirector`-only. The assignment, staff-allocation and development-plan actions a `FarmDirector` drives (`ACTIVATE`, `COMPLETE`, `REVOKE`, `SUSPEND`, `RESUME`, `RECALL`, `END`, `RECORD_PROGRESS`, `RESUBMIT`, `MARK_ACHIEVED`) belong to that role on their respective resources.

The meaning of `X-User-ID` depends on the role. When `X-User-Role` is `AffiliateOperator`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Affiliate`; ownership checks on any resource that references an `Affiliate` compare the resource's affiliate foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. An `AffiliateOperator` may therefore only draft or dispose of an agreement whose `affiliate_id` equals its own `X-User-ID`, only dispose of a facility inspection whose `affiliate_id` equals its own `X-User-ID`, and only file agreements against the affiliate it identifies; any attempt to reach across to another club's agreement, inspection or allocation is rejected with `ERR_AFFILIATE_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (HTTP 403). When `X-User-Role` is `FarmDirector`, `MedicalDirector`, `ComplianceOfficer` or `InternationalScout`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it where a transition writes an actor field — `submitted_by_user_id` on an agreement, `waiver_granted_by_user_id` on a waived inspection, `requested_by_user_id` and `issued_by_user_id` on a medical clearance, `initiated_by_user_id` on an assignment, `approved_by_user_id` on an approved signing, and `certified_by_user_id` on a filed transaction report — but it never compares that value to any entity's `id`. Two of those recorded identifiers are compared against each other rather than against an entity: a league filing may not be certified by the same operator identifier that initiated the movement, and that collision yields `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403).

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

---

### 2.3. Pagination

List endpoints are paginated through two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of items returned; it defaults to 10, may not be lower than 1, and may not exceed 100. `offset` is the number of items to skip and defaults to 0. Every list endpoint that accepts these parameters also accepts the domain filters described alongside it in Section 4.

Unless a specific endpoint says otherwise, list results are sorted by the resource's creation timestamp, `created_at`, in ascending order. List responses populate four pagination fields inside the envelope's `meta` object: `limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, and `has_more`.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Audit Fields and Mutation Semantics

Every resource the API mints carries an `id`, which is a server-generated UUID string with no type prefix or other decoration; clients never supply it. Every resource also carries `created_at`, an ISO 8601 timestamp in UTC with exactly three fractional-second digits (for example `2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z`), fixed at the instant of creation and never changed afterwards.

Most resources additionally carry `updated_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits refreshed on every mutation, and `version`, an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increments by exactly one on every mutation of that resource. `Affiliate`, `AffiliationAgreement`, `FacilityStandardReview`, `StaffMember`, `StaffAllocation`, `Player`, `PlayerAssignment`, `DevelopmentPlan`, `MedicalClearance`, `OptionYearRecord`, `SigningPool`, `InternationalSigning` and `TransactionReport` all carry the full quartet of `id`, `created_at`, `updated_at` and `version`. The workload outing is the single exception: a workload log is an append-only event that is never edited after it is written, so it carries only `id` and `created_at` — it has neither an `updated_at` field nor a `version` field, and neither field appears in its response payload.

A mutation for these purposes means any change to any stored or derived field on the resource, including changes the server makes on its own initiative. When a cascade drives a player's roster state, or a recomputation rewrites an affiliate's roster counters, or a settlement writes an obligation figure, that is a mutation of the affected resource: its `updated_at` is refreshed and its `version` is incremented by one, exactly as though a client had written it directly. A client that observes a `version` of 4 on an affiliate, and then activates a player assignment at that affiliate, must expect the affiliate's `version` to have advanced.

Attempts to set a computed or system-managed field from a request body are ignored rather than rejected, as are unknown fields; the server simply drops them and proceeds with the fields it recognizes.

### 2.5. The Logical League Clock

Every deadline, expiry, rolling window and interval comparison in this system reads a single server-side logical UTC clock rather than real wall-clock time. Before any client sets it, the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and the server must never fall back to wall-clock time — a fresh deployment that has received no clock call behaves as though it were the first of August 2026.

An operating capability available to all five roles sets that clock forward or backward to a supplied instant. The request carries a single field, `to_iso8601`, holding the target instant, and the response body is an inline object of the shape `{ current_utc: <the new logical instant> }` rendered as an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits. Setting the clock is not a passive act. Because a great deal of derived state is a function of the current instant, the server re-evaluates, for every non-terminal row affected: whether a facility inspection is overdue (`is_overdue`), whether a medical clearance has lapsed (`is_expired`), and whether a pending transaction report has gone late (`is_late`, along with the `late_fee_cents` that lateness implies). It also recomputes every player's rolling workload figures and promotion readiness, because the rolling window moves with the clock, and it re-evaluates each affiliate's facility and posture derivation.

All calendar dates in this system — `term_start_date`, `term_end_date`, `review_date`, `remediation_due_date`, `effective_start`, `effective_end`, `signed_date`, `effective_date`, `end_date`, `log_date`, `certification_expires_on`, `expires_on` and `filing_deadline` — are strings in `YYYY-MM-DD` form and are compared as calendar dates in UTC. Datetime fields such as `remediation_submitted_at`, `charged_at` and `filed_at` are ISO 8601 UTC instants taken from the logical clock at the moment the action ran. Where a rule compares a date to "the clock date", it means the calendar date component of the logical clock instant in UTC.

### 2.6. Validation Order

When a single request violates several rules, exactly one error is returned, and which one is determined by a fixed evaluation order rather than by the order in which the reader happens to encounter the rules in this document. Checks are evaluated in the ascending order below, and the first violated check short-circuits every later one:

1. Both `X-User-Role` and `X-User-ID` are present and non-empty, otherwise `ERR_MISSING_AUTH_HEADERS` (401).
2. `X-User-Role` names one of the five declared roles, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_ROLE` (401).
3. The acting role appears on the target endpoint, otherwise `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (403).
4. For an `AffiliateOperator`, `X-User-ID` equals the `Affiliate` id the target resource belongs to — the affiliate itself, or the `affiliate_id` on the agreement, the inspection or the allocation being touched — otherwise `ERR_AFFILIATE_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (403). This scope check guards four operations: drafting an agreement under an affiliate, filing an inspection under an affiliate, disposing of an agreement, and disposing of a facility inspection.
5. On every disposition endpoint, the requested `action` is one the acting role may request, otherwise `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (403).
6. On a transaction-report `FILE` action, `X-User-ID` differs from the report's `initiated_by_user_id`, otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (403).
7. Every id embedded in the request path resolves to a stored resource, validated strictly left to right, otherwise the not-found error for the leftmost unresolved segment (404) — one of `ERR_AFFILIATE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FACILITY_REVIEW_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STAFF_MEMBER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STAFF_ALLOCATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PLAYER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DEVELOPMENT_PLAN_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MEDICAL_CLEARANCE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SIGNING_POOL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SIGNING_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_TRANSACTION_REPORT_NOT_FOUND`.
8. The request body is well-shaped, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400). A body is well-shaped when every required field is present, every enum field carries one of its declared values, every numeric field lies inside its declared range, every date field parses as `YYYY-MM-DD` and every datetime field parses as ISO 8601, `term_end_date` is strictly after `term_start_date`, `effective_end` is strictly after `effective_start`, `end_date` is the same day as or later than `effective_date`, and `curriculum_modules` is a non-empty array free of duplicates.
9. Every foreign key supplied in the request body resolves to a stored resource (404). The body foreign keys are `player_id`, `affiliate_id`, `staff_member_id`, `pool_id`, `academy_affiliate_id`, `superseded_assignment_id` and `assignment_id`, and this check guards assignment creation, staff-allocation creation and international-signing submission.
10. The supplied `expected_<parent>_version` equals the parent's current `version`, otherwise the matching stale-version error (412) as described in Section 2.7.
11. The target resource is in a state from which the requested action is a declared transition, otherwise the state error for that resource type (409). This check guards every disposition endpoint plus the two child-creation endpoints that attach a development plan or a workload outing to an assignment.
12. A `SETTLE` action requires the agreement's `settlement_status` to be `NOT_SETTLED`, otherwise `ERR_ALREADY_SETTLED` (409).
13. Uniqueness holds (409): at most one agreement per affiliate in status `SUBMITTED` or `ACTIVE` (`ERR_DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_AGREEMENT`), `season_year` unique across signing pools (`ERR_DUPLICATE_POOL_SEASON`), and at most one `ACTIVE` development plan per assignment (`ERR_DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_PLAN`).
14. The affiliate receiving the operation has an agreement in status `ACTIVE`, otherwise `ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_ACTIVE` (409).
15. That agreement's `posture` is not `FROZEN`, otherwise `ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_FROZEN` (409).
16. That agreement's `posture` is not `RESTRICTED` for the two operations `RESTRICTED` gates, otherwise `ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_RESTRICTED` (409).
17. Issuing a `REHAB_COMPLETE` clearance requires an `ACTIVE` rehab assignment, otherwise `ERR_REHAB_ASSIGNMENT_REQUIRED` (409).
18. A matching `ISSUED` medical clearance exists, otherwise `ERR_MEDICAL_CLEARANCE_REQUIRED` (409).
19. That matching clearance has not lapsed, otherwise `ERR_MEDICAL_CLEARANCE_EXPIRED` (409).
20. A workload outing does not drive the actual rolling workload index above the effective ceiling, otherwise `ERR_WORKLOAD_CEILING_EXCEEDED` (422).
21. The affiliate's `facility_status` is not `DEFICIENT`, otherwise `ERR_FACILITY_DEFICIENCY_BLOCKING` (409).
22. Curriculum versions agree, otherwise `ERR_CURRICULUM_VERSION_MISMATCH` (422).
23. A staff member's certification has not lapsed before the allocation starts, otherwise `ERR_CERTIFICATION_EXPIRED` (422).
24. A new level or complex assignment neither overlaps nor crowds a prior non-terminal assignment of the same player, otherwise `ERR_ASSIGNMENT_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (409).
25. A facility remediation is submitted inside its window, otherwise `ERR_REMEDIATION_WINDOW_CLOSED` (409).
26. A promotion satisfies all three promotion criteria, otherwise `ERR_PROMOTION_CRITERIA_NOT_MET` (422).
27. Roster capacity is available for a level or complex assignment, otherwise `ERR_ROSTER_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (409).
28. A rehab slot is available, otherwise `ERR_REHAB_SLOT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (409).
29. A domestic reserve list slot is available, otherwise `ERR_DOMESTIC_RESERVE_LIST_FULL` (409).
30. An option-year-charging activation has an option year left, otherwise `ERR_OPTION_YEARS_EXHAUSTED` (422).
31. The signing pool is `OPEN`, otherwise `ERR_POOL_HARD_CAPPED` (409).
32. The academy has a housing bed free, otherwise `ERR_ACADEMY_HOUSING_FULL` (409).
33. The staff member's aggregate committed time stays within its cap, otherwise `ERR_STAFF_FTE_OVERCOMMITTED` (422).
34. A cycle cap has not been reached, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_LIMIT_REACHED` (409).
35. A waiver is permitted for the inspection's severity, otherwise `ERR_WAIVER_NOT_PERMITTED` (422).
36. An international signing satisfies its eligibility pair, otherwise `ERR_SIGNING_ELIGIBILITY_VIOLATION` (422).
37. The declared meal stipend meets the league floor, otherwise `ERR_MEAL_STIPEND_BELOW_MINIMUM` (422).

Three consequences of this ordering deserve emphasis. Because agreement existence and posture (checks 14 through 16) precede every medical, capacity and fiscal check, an activation attempted at an affiliate whose licence has been suspended reports the posture problem and never reveals whether the roster was also full. Because the medical-clearance checks (18 and 19) precede the capacity and option-year checks (27 through 30), an activation with no clearance at all reports the missing clearance even when the roster is simultaneously full. And because promotion criteria (26) precede roster capacity (27), a promotion that is both unready and unhoused reports the readiness failure.

### 2.7. Optimistic Concurrency on Shared Parents

Three creation operations write against a parent record that is itself a shared, contended resource, and each of them requires the client to declare which revision of that parent it was looking at. The declared value is a mandatory field on the request body — not an optional hint — and if it does not equal the parent's current `version` at the moment the server processes the request, the create is refused and nothing is written.

- A `POST /assignments` request must carry `expected_player_version` holding the client's last observed `version` of the target `Player`. If the player's `version` has advanced since the client read it, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PLAYER_VERSION` (HTTP 412). This matters because a new level assignment may legitimately supersede the player's prior assignment; a writer holding a stale view of the player would otherwise displace a movement it never observed, and the stale-version check turns that into a deterministic refusal.
- A `POST /international-signings` request must carry `expected_pool_version` holding the client's last observed `version` of the target `SigningPool`. A mismatch with the pool's current `version` is rejected with `ERR_STALE_POOL_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Pool space is a shared quota, so a submission built on a stale view of `remaining_cents` would commit bonus money the writer never saw claimed.
- A `POST /staff-allocations` request must carry `expected_affiliate_version` holding the client's last observed `version` of the target `Affiliate`. A mismatch with the affiliate's current `version` is rejected with `ERR_STALE_AFFILIATE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Curriculum coverage at an affiliate is a single shared plan, so an allocation proposed from a stale view of the affiliate's staffing would silently duplicate or contradict a coverage change the writer never observed.

No other endpoint takes a version field. Disposition endpoints, child-collection creations such as agreements, facility inspections, medical clearance requests, development plans and workload outings, and the creation of affiliates, staff members, players and signing pools all proceed without one. Note also that each of the three guarded creates itself bumps the parent's `version` by one on success — creating an assignment advances the player's `version`, submitting a signing advances the pool's `version`, and proposing an allocation advances the affiliate's `version` — so a client that issues two creates in a row against the same parent must re-read the parent between them.

### 2.8. Money, Counts and Rounding Discipline

All monetary amounts in this system are integers denominated in cents. Field names ending in `_cents` are whole-cent integers and are returned as JSON numbers, never as strings, and never with a fractional part. Where a monetary rule multiplies an amount by a share or a tax rate, the product is rounded to the nearest whole cent using the round-half-up convention, meaning a value exactly halfway between two cents rounds upward in magnitude.

The point at which rounding is applied is part of the specification, not an implementation detail, and two rules govern it. First, where a calculation rounds per line item, the rounding happens on each line before any summing — the total is the sum of the rounded lines, never the rounding of the sum of exact products. Second, where a calculation rounds once at the end, every intermediate term is carried as an exact decimal and rounded only in the final step.

Non-monetary derived numbers each have a fixed scale. An affiliate's `facility_score` is a number with 2 decimal places, round-half-up. A player's `workload_index` and `workload_index_projected` are numbers with 2 decimal places, round-half-up. A player's `promotion_readiness` is a number with 4 decimal places, round-half-up. A development plan's `completion_pct` is a number with 2 decimal places, round-half-up. Counters such as `active_roster_count`, `rehab_assignment_count`, `reserve_list_count`, `open_major_deficiency_count`, `reopen_count`, `review_cycle_count`, `refile_count`, `option_years_used`, `option_years_remaining`, `total_committed_fte_pct`, `optional_days` and `projected_optional_days` are integers and are never rounded. Interval arithmetic is performed in whole days, comparing endpoints as calendar dates in UTC.

### 2.9. Facility Standing and the Affiliate Posture Gate

Two derived signals govern whether work may proceed at an affiliate, and they are computed together whenever anything relevant changes. The first is the affiliate's facility standing, held in `facility_status`. The second is the operational gate on the affiliate's agreement, held in `posture`.

Facility standing rolls up the affiliate's league inspections. Each inspection carries a `deficiency_level` derived from its `score` with inclusive boundaries: a score of 85 or above is `NONE`, a score from 70 through 84 inclusive is `MINOR`, and a score of 69 or below is `MAJOR`. For the score roll-up, the server takes, for each inspected category, the single most recent inspection for that affiliate and category whose status is anything other than `WAIVED`, choosing the one with the greatest `review_date` and breaking a tie on `review_date` in favour of the later `created_at`. The affiliate's `facility_score` is the arithmetic mean of those per-category latest scores, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places; when the affiliate has no non-waived inspection at all, `facility_score` reads `100.00`. Separately, `open_major_deficiency_count` is the number of the affiliate's inspections whose `deficiency_level` is `MAJOR` and whose status is `OPEN`, `REMEDIATION_SUBMITTED` or `REOPENED`, and the parallel minor tally counts inspections whose `deficiency_level` is `MINOR` in those same three statuses. From these, `facility_status` is `DEFICIENT` when the open-major tally is at least 1; `PROVISIONAL` when the open-major tally is exactly 0 and the open-minor tally is at least 1; and `COMPLIANT` otherwise.

The agreement's `posture` is then a single derived gate with three values. It is `FROZEN` when the agreement's own status is anything other than `ACTIVE`, or when the affiliate's `facility_status` is `DEFICIENT`. Failing that, it is `RESTRICTED` when the affiliate's `active_roster_count` is below the agreement's `minimum_active_players`, or when any role listed in the agreement's `required_staff_roles` has no allocation at this affiliate that is both in status `ACTIVE` and filling that `staff_role`. Otherwise it is `OPEN`.

What the gate blocks is precise. `FROZEN` blocks every child write at the affiliate: creating an assignment, activating or resuming an assignment, proposing a staff allocation, and approving an international signing at that academy — each rejected with `ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_FROZEN`. Facility-inspection dispositions are deliberately exempt from the posture gate, because a major deficiency is itself what freezes the posture, and gating remediation submission on posture would leave the deficiency permanently unrecoverable. `RESTRICTED` blocks exactly two operations — creating an assignment whose `movement_reason` is `PROMOTION`, and approving an international signing at the academy — each rejected with `ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`; every other write proceeds normally while `RESTRICTED`. `OPEN` blocks nothing.

Because posture is derived rather than stored by hand, it is recomputed on inspection creation and on every inspection disposition, on agreement activation, suspension, reinstatement and termination, on staff-allocation activation, ending and recall, on every player-assignment status change, and whenever the logical clock is set. The inputs to each recomputation are the affiliate's non-waived inspections (latest per category), its `ACTIVE` staff allocations, and its single agreement in status `SUBMITTED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`.

### 2.10. Cascade and Reconciliation Discipline

The system is immediately consistent: when one write implies changes elsewhere, those changes land inside the same request, not on a later sweep. Three families of consequence recur across the domain and are worth stating once here.

Parent-to-child cascades are conditional, never blanket. When an agreement is suspended, only the affiliate's assignments currently in status `ACTIVE` move to `SUSPENDED`; assignments sitting in `PENDING_CLEARANCE`, `COMPLETED` or `REVOKED` are left exactly as they are. When an agreement is terminated, assignments in `PENDING_CLEARANCE`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` all move to `REVOKED`, and staff allocations in `PROPOSED` or `ACTIVE` all move to `RECALLED`, while rows already in a terminal state are untouched. When a signing pool hard-caps, only its sibling signings still in status `SUBMITTED` are auto-rejected; `APPROVED` and already-`REJECTED` siblings are untouched.

Compensation reverses the ledger entries a transition created, and only those. Revoking an assignment rescinds the option year that assignment charged, returning the option year to the player; voiding an approved international signing removes that signing's pool charge, its accrued overage tax, and — only if that signing was the one that breached the third tax tier — the forfeit that breach imposed. Compensation is not a general undo: suspending an assignment does not return an option year, and returning a hard-capped pool to `OPEN` does not revive the signings that the cap auto-rejected.

Derived-field reconciliation propagates upward. An affiliate's roster counters, a staff member's committed time, a player's option-year and workload figures, a signing pool's ledger and an agreement's posture are all functions of their children, and every write that changes a child recomputes each affected parent in the same request. Each such recomputation is itself a mutation of the parent as described in Section 2.4, refreshing `updated_at` and incrementing `version`.

### 2.11. Curriculum Consistency

The organization teaches one player-development curriculum at a time, and the current organization-wide curriculum version is `2026.1`. Any `curriculum_version` supplied on a staff allocation or a development plan must equal that value; for a staff allocation it must additionally equal the staff member's own `curriculum_version_certified`, because a coach may not teach a version they are not certified on. A mismatch on either comparison is rejected with `ERR_CURRICULUM_VERSION_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422). This constraint guards three operations: proposing a staff allocation, attaching a development plan to an assignment, and activating a development plan.

### 2.12. Segregation of Duties in League Filings

The organization separates the person who moves a player from the person who certifies that movement to the league. The `X-User-ID` of the `FarmDirector` that created an assignment is stored on the assignment as `initiated_by_user_id` and copied verbatim onto every transaction report that assignment produces. When a `ComplianceOfficer` files such a report, the server compares the acting `X-User-ID` against the report's `initiated_by_user_id`, and an exact match is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). The comparison is on the report's copy, so it survives even if the assignment is later touched by someone else. The same separation principle informs the international-signing flow, where the `InternationalScout` role may submit a signing but only a `ComplianceOfficer` may approve one.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up an Affiliate Club and Its League Standards

Registration of a club is the entry point to everything else, and either a `FarmDirector` or a `ComplianceOfficer` may perform it. The registrant supplies the club's `name` — between 1 and 120 characters, and not blank — the competition `level` at which it operates, and its home `market_city`. Six competition levels exist: `TRIPLE_A`, `DOUBLE_A`, `HIGH_A`, `SINGLE_A`, `COMPLEX` and `DOMINICAN_ACADEMY`. The registrant must also declare the daily meal stipend paid to assigned players, `meal_stipend_cents_per_day`, and the league sets a floor here: a stipend below twenty-five dollars a day, that is below 2500 cents, is refused with `ERR_MEAL_STIPEND_BELOW_MINIMUM`.

Three capacity numbers and one service commitment may be supplied and otherwise default. `roster_limit` bounds how many level or complex assignments the club may carry concurrently; it accepts integers from 1 through 40, and when it is omitted the server applies the league default for the declared level — 28 for both `TRIPLE_A` and `DOUBLE_A`, 30 for both `HIGH_A` and `SINGLE_A`, 35 for `COMPLEX`, and 40 for `DOMINICAN_ACADEMY`. `reserve_slots` records how many domestic reserve list places the organization has allotted to this club; it accepts integers from 0 through 40 and defaults to 35. `housing_beds` records the league-certified player housing beds available at the club or academy complex; it accepts integers from 0 through 200 and defaults to 40. And `travel_standard_tier` records the travel standard the club commits to, one of `CHARTER`, `BUS_SLEEPER` or `BUS_STANDARD`, defaulting to `BUS_STANDARD`.

A freshly registered club has no history, so its derived fields start clean: `active_roster_count`, `rehab_assignment_count` and `reserve_list_count` all begin at 0, `facility_score` begins at `100.00`, `open_major_deficiency_count` begins at 0 and `facility_status` begins at `COMPLIANT`. Clubs may be listed with filters on `level` and on the derived `facility_status`, which makes it straightforward to pull every affiliate currently sitting in a deficient posture.

League inspections arrive as a stream of per-category results, and only a `ComplianceOfficer` may file one. Each inspection names the `category` inspected — one of `CLUBHOUSE`, `WEIGHT_ROOM`, `FIELD_SURFACE`, `LIGHTING`, `MEDICAL_ROOM`, `HOUSING`, `MEALS` or `TRAVEL` — the `review_date` on which the inspection was performed, and the `score` awarded, an integer from 0 through 100. The server bands the score into `deficiency_level` on the spot, and the banding is the one given in Section 2.9. A clean inspection never waits for anyone: when `deficiency_level` comes out `NONE`, the row is persisted directly in status `CLOSED` with `remediation_due_date` left null. A deficient inspection is persisted in status `OPEN` with a remediation clock attached: a `MAJOR` finding gives the club thirty days from the `review_date` to remediate, and a `MINOR` finding gives it sixty days, and that deadline is written into `remediation_due_date`. Either way, filing an inspection recomputes the affiliate's `facility_score`, `open_major_deficiency_count` and `facility_status`, and then re-evaluates the posture of the affiliate's non-terminal agreement — which is how a single 62-point clubhouse score can drive an otherwise healthy club straight to `FROZEN`.

Remediation is the club's own business, and the club's `AffiliateOperator` drives it. Submitting remediation moves the inspection from `OPEN` to `REMEDIATION_SUBMITTED`, or from `REOPENED` back to `REMEDIATION_SUBMITTED`, and requires two things: the acting `X-User-ID` must be the affiliate the inspection belongs to, and the logical clock date must not be later than `remediation_due_date` — a submission on the due date itself is in time, a submission the day after is refused with `ERR_REMEDIATION_WINDOW_CLOSED`. The submission stores the operator's narrative in `remediation_note` and stamps `remediation_submitted_at` with the logical clock instant; a submission made from `REOPENED` overwrites both of those fields rather than accumulating a second copy. Separately, an inspection that has run past its deadline advertises the fact: `is_overdue` reads true whenever the inspection's status is `OPEN` or `REOPENED` and the logical clock date is strictly after `remediation_due_date`.

Closing the loop belongs to compliance. A `ComplianceOfficer` closes a `REMEDIATION_SUBMITTED` inspection, moving it to `CLOSED`, which recomputes the affiliate's `open_major_deficiency_count` and `facility_status` and re-evaluates the posture of the affiliate's `ACTIVE` agreement — this is the transition that can lift a club out of `FROZEN`. The officer may also reopen a `CLOSED` inspection, moving it to `REOPENED`, which increments `reopen_count`, resets `remediation_due_date` to fifteen days after the current logical clock date, and again recomputes the affiliate's deficiency tally and standing. Reopening is bounded: an inspection may be reopened at most twice, and a third attempt — one made when `reopen_count` has already reached 2 — is refused with `ERR_CYCLE_LIMIT_REACHED`. Taken together these transitions form the cycle `CLOSED` → `REOPENED` → `REMEDIATION_SUBMITTED` → `CLOSED`.

Finally, a `ComplianceOfficer` may waive an inspection outright, from either `OPEN` or `REOPENED`, sending it to the terminal state `WAIVED`. Waiver is available only for a `MINOR` finding; attempting it on a `MAJOR` finding, or on a clean `NONE` finding, is refused with `ERR_WAIVER_NOT_PERMITTED`. A waiver from `OPEN` records `waiver_granted_by_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`, drops the inspection out of both the `facility_score` mean and the deficiency tallies, and recomputes `facility_status`. A waiver from `REOPENED` does exactly the same three things: it records `waiver_granted_by_user_id`, excludes the inspection from the score mean and from both deficiency counts, and recomputes `facility_status`. Any inspection action that is not a declared transition out of the row's current status — waiving an already-closed inspection, closing one that has not yet submitted remediation, submitting remediation on a `WAIVED` row — is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REVIEW_STATE`, and each successful disposition increments the inspection's `version` by one. Inspections may be listed per affiliate with filters on `category` and `status`.

### 3.2. The Player Development License: Drafting, Certification and Termination

An affiliation agreement — the Player Development License — is drafted under a specific club, and either that club's `AffiliateOperator` or a `ComplianceOfficer` may draft it. The draft declares the licence term through `term_start_date` and `term_end_date`, where the end date must be strictly after the start date; the league facility tier the club is licensed at, `facility_standard_tier`, one of `TIER_1`, `TIER_2` or `TIER_3`; and the ceiling on the major league club's annual share of reimbursable expense, `club_reimbursement_cap_cents`, which must be zero or positive. Two operational commitments may be declared and otherwise default: `minimum_active_players`, the contractual floor on the affiliate's `active_roster_count`, an integer from 0 through 40 defaulting to 22; and `required_staff_roles`, the list of staff roles that must each be covered by at least one active allocation at the club, defaulting to an empty list and drawn without duplicates from `HITTING_COACH`, `PITCHING_COACH`, `ATHLETIC_TRAINER`, `STRENGTH_COACH` and `MANAGER`. The four reimbursable expense categories are declared as gross annual figures in cents — `housing_expense_cents`, `meal_expense_cents`, `travel_expense_cents` and `upkeep_expense_cents` — each defaulting to 0 when omitted.

A newly drafted licence sits in status `DRAFT` with `posture` `FROZEN`, `provisional_club_obligation_cents` and `affiliate_overflow_cents` at 0, `final_club_obligation_cents` null, `settlement_status` `NOT_SETTLED`, and `submitted_by_user_id` null. The `affiliate_id` of the club under which the draft was created is recorded on the agreement, and every agreement drafted for a club can be listed back from that club with a filter on `status`.

Submission is the club's act. When the club's `AffiliateOperator` submits, the licence moves `DRAFT` → `SUBMITTED`, and three conditions must hold: the acting `X-User-ID` must equal the agreement's `affiliate_id`, `term_end_date` must be strictly after `term_start_date`, and no other agreement for the same club may currently sit in status `SUBMITTED` or `ACTIVE` — a second concurrent licence is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_AGREEMENT`. Submission records `submitted_by_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`, runs the provisional expense allocation described in Section 3.3 — writing `provisional_club_obligation_cents` and `affiliate_overflow_cents` — and recomputes `posture`, which stays `FROZEN` because the licence is not yet `ACTIVE`.

Certification is compliance's act. A `ComplianceOfficer` activates a `SUBMITTED` licence, moving it `SUBMITTED` → `ACTIVE`, which is permitted only when the affiliate's `facility_status` is not `DEFICIENT`; an activation attempted over an open major deficiency is refused with `ERR_FACILITY_DEFICIENCY_BLOCKING`. Activation then runs the posture derivation, writing `posture` to either `OPEN` or `RESTRICTED` according to the rules in Section 2.9 — a club that satisfies its staffing floor and role coverage comes out `OPEN`, a club that does not comes out `RESTRICTED` and is immediately barred from promotions. Alternatively the officer may reject a `SUBMITTED` licence, moving it `SUBMITTED` → `REJECTED`, a terminal state, and setting `posture` to `FROZEN`.

Two suspension paths exist and both belong to compliance. Suspending an `ACTIVE` licence moves it `ACTIVE` → `SUSPENDED`, sets `posture` to `FROZEN`, and cascades to the club's players: every assignment at that affiliate currently in status `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED`, while assignments in `PENDING_CLEARANCE`, `COMPLETED` or `REVOKED` are left untouched. Reinstating a `SUSPENDED` licence moves it `SUSPENDED` → `ACTIVE` and, like the original activation, requires the affiliate's `facility_status` not to be `DEFICIENT`, failing which the reinstatement is refused with `ERR_FACILITY_DEFICIENCY_BLOCKING`; reinstatement re-runs the posture derivation and writes `posture` to `OPEN` or `RESTRICTED`. Note that reinstatement does not itself resume the assignments that suspension cascaded — those must be resumed individually, as described in Section 3.6.

Termination is final and destructive, and it may be reached from either `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`. From `ACTIVE`, terminating moves the licence to the terminal state `TERMINATED`, sets `posture` to `FROZEN`, drives every assignment at that affiliate in status `PENDING_CLEARANCE`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` to `REVOKED` — each one cascading exactly as an individually revoked assignment does, with the option-year, transaction-report and development-plan consequences spelled out in Section 3.6 — and drives every staff allocation at that affiliate in status `PROPOSED` or `ACTIVE` to `RECALLED`. From `SUSPENDED`, terminating produces the identical outcome: the licence reaches `TERMINATED`, `posture` becomes `FROZEN`, the affiliate's `PENDING_CLEARANCE`, `ACTIVE` and `SUSPENDED` assignments all reach `REVOKED` with the same cascade, and its `PROPOSED` and `ACTIVE` staff allocations all reach `RECALLED`. Once terminated or rejected, a licence accepts no further disposition; requesting one yields `ERR_INVALID_AGREEMENT_STATE`, as does any action that is not a declared transition out of the licence's current status. Each disposition also increments the agreement's `version` by one, and an optional free-text `note` may accompany any disposition request.

### 3.3. Expense Allocation and Settlement of the Licence

Reimbursable affiliate expense is split between the major league club and the affiliate across four categories, and the club's share differs by category: the club funds three quarters of housing, sixty per cent of meals, all of team travel, and one quarter of facility upkeep. The per-category club line is therefore the gross figure for that category multiplied by that category's club share, rounded half-up to a whole number of cents. This per-line rounding happens before any addition: the obligation is the sum of the four rounded lines, and never the rounding of a sum of exact products.

Which gross figures feed the computation depends on when it runs. The provisional computation, which runs on submission of the licence, reads the four declared figures `housing_expense_cents`, `meal_expense_cents`, `travel_expense_cents` and `upkeep_expense_cents`. The final computation, which runs on settlement, reads the audited figures `audited_housing_expense_cents`, `audited_meal_expense_cents`, `audited_travel_expense_cents` and `audited_upkeep_expense_cents` wherever they are non-null, and falls back to the corresponding declared figure for any audited figure that was not supplied. All four audited fields are null until settlement.

The two computations then diverge in how they apply the cap, and this divergence is the point of the design.

The provisional variant treats `club_reimbursement_cap_cents` as one aggregate bucket and drains it in a fixed category order — housing first, then meals, then travel, then facility upkeep, never in identifier order. Starting with the whole cap available and nothing allocated, the server walks the four categories in that order and for each one takes the smaller of that category's club line and the cap remaining at that moment; the amount taken is added to the running allocation and subtracted from the cap remaining. When all four categories have been walked, `provisional_club_obligation_cents` is the running allocation, and `affiliate_overflow_cents` is the sum of the four club lines minus that allocation. Because the bucket is shared, an under-share category can consume headroom left over by an over-share category, and overflow appears only once the entire cap has been exhausted.

The final variant splits the cap first and refuses to let categories borrow from one another. For each category the server computes a pro-rata sub-cap: `club_reimbursement_cap_cents` multiplied by that category's club share, divided by 2.60 — the sum of the four club shares — and rounded half-up to a whole number of cents. Each category's final line is then the smaller of its club line and its own sub-cap. `final_club_obligation_cents` is the sum of the four final lines, and `affiliate_overflow_cents` is overwritten with the sum of the four club lines minus the sum of the four final lines. Because no category may draw on another's sub-cap, overflow here can be non-zero even when the aggregate cap was never exhausted, and for identical inputs the provisional obligation is strictly larger than the final obligation whenever any single category line exceeds its own pro-rata sub-cap.

Settlement is a self-transition available to a `ComplianceOfficer` on a licence that is `ACTIVE`: the licence remains `ACTIVE` and only its settlement ledger changes. The request may carry any subset of the four audited figures, which are stored as supplied; the final computation then runs, writing `final_club_obligation_cents` and overwriting `affiliate_overflow_cents`; and `settlement_status` moves from `NOT_SETTLED` to `SETTLED`. A licence may be settled exactly once — a second settlement attempt is refused with `ERR_ALREADY_SETTLED`. Only the single licence being submitted or settled participates in either computation; no other club's figures enter it, and licences that have reached `TERMINATED` or `REJECTED` are never recomputed.

### 3.4. Allocating Coaching and Support Staff Across Affiliates

Coaches and support staff are organization assets shared across the affiliate chain, and only a `FarmDirector` registers them. Registration records the member's `full_name`, their primary `discipline` — one of `HITTING`, `PITCHING`, `ATHLETIC_TRAINING`, `STRENGTH` or `MANAGEMENT` — the curriculum version they are certified to teach in `curriculum_version_certified`, and the date that certification lapses in `certification_expires_on`. A newly registered member has `total_committed_fte_pct` initialised to 0 and a `status` of `ACTIVE`, which is the only value that field ever takes: no transition in this system moves a staff member out of `ACTIVE`. Staff may be listed with filters on `discipline` and on `curriculum_version_certified`.

An allocation binds one member to one club for one date interval at a declared intensity. Proposing an allocation names the member through `staff_member_id`, the club through `affiliate_id`, the `staff_role` the member will fill at that club — one of `HITTING_COACH`, `PITCHING_COACH`, `ATHLETIC_TRAINER`, `STRENGTH_COACH` or `MANAGER` — and the share of the member's time committed in `fte_pct`, an integer from 1 through 100. The interval runs from `effective_start` up to but not including `effective_end`, which must be strictly after the start; because the interval is half-open, one allocation ending on the very day another begins does not overlap it, and two such adjacent allocations may each commit the member fully. The proposal also declares the `curriculum_version` it will teach, which must satisfy both comparisons in Section 2.11, and it must carry `expected_affiliate_version` as described in Section 2.7. A proposed allocation is persisted in status `PROPOSED`, and proposing one is a child write at the affiliate, so it is refused with `ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_FROZEN` when the club's licence posture is `FROZEN`.

Overcommitment is guarded at both proposal and activation. The server gathers the member's allocations that are in status `ACTIVE` and whose half-open intervals genuinely overlap the interval under consideration — that is, whose own start falls before the candidate's end and whose own end falls after the candidate's start — sums their `fte_pct`, adds the candidate's `fte_pct`, and refuses with `ERR_STAFF_FTE_OVERCOMMITTED` if that total exceeds 100. Intervals that merely touch at a shared boundary date are not overlapping and contribute nothing to the sum.

Activation is the `FarmDirector`'s act and moves the allocation `PROPOSED` → `ACTIVE`. It requires the club to hold a licence in status `ACTIVE`, failing which it is refused with `ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_ACTIVE`. It further requires that the member's `certification_expires_on` is not earlier than the allocation's `effective_start` — a certification lapsing before the assignment even begins is refused with `ERR_CERTIFICATION_EXPIRED` — and it re-runs the overcommitment check just described, refusing with `ERR_STAFF_FTE_OVERCOMMITTED` when the member's overlapping active commitments plus this allocation exceed 100. On success, the member's `total_committed_fte_pct` is recomputed as the sum of `fte_pct` across their `ACTIVE` allocations whose half-open interval contains the current logical clock date, and the posture of the club's `ACTIVE` licence is re-derived — which is how filling the last uncovered entry in `required_staff_roles` lifts a club from `RESTRICTED` to `OPEN`.

Two ways out of `ACTIVE` exist and they behave identically. Ending an allocation moves it `ACTIVE` → `ENDED`; recalling an active allocation moves it `ACTIVE` → `RECALLED`. Both recompute the member's `total_committed_fte_pct` and both re-derive the club's licence posture, which is how a required role losing its last `ACTIVE` allocation drives a club from `OPEN` down to `RESTRICTED`. Recall is additionally available straight out of `PROPOSED`, moving the allocation `PROPOSED` → `RECALLED`; that path performs neither recomputation, because an allocation that was never activated never contributed to the member's committed time nor to the club's role coverage. Both `ENDED` and `RECALLED` are terminal. Any action that is not a declared transition out of the allocation's current status is refused with `ERR_INVALID_ALLOCATION_STATE`, each successful disposition increments the allocation's `version` by one, and a free-text `note` may accompany the request. Allocations can be listed from the member's side with a `status` filter, and from the club's side with filters on `staff_role` and `status`.

### 3.5. Players, Their Two Independent Standings, and Medical Clearance

Registering a player is deliberately cheap: either a `FarmDirector` or an `InternationalScout` records the player's `full_name`, the acquisition channel in `origin` — one of `DOMESTIC_DRAFT`, `INTERNATIONAL_FA` or `FOREIGN_PRO` — the `signed_date`, and optionally `service_days_accrued`, the professional service days credited to the player before this system's records begin, an integer from 0 through 2000 that defaults to 0. Everything else about a player is derived or driven by later events. On registration, `roster_status` is `RESERVE_LIST` and `medical_status` is `PENDING_CLEARANCE`; `option_years_used` is 0 and `option_years_remaining` is 3; `workload_index` and `workload_index_projected` are both `0.00`; `promotion_readiness` is `0.0000`; and `current_affiliate_id` is null. Players may be listed with filters on `origin` and, independently, on each of the two standings.

A player carries two standings on the same row, and they evolve independently. **Roster standing**, held in `roster_status`, takes the values `RESERVE_LIST`, `ACTIVE` and `RESTRICTED`, and it moves on roster and assignment events. **Medical standing**, held in `medical_status`, takes the values `PENDING_CLEARANCE`, `CLEARED` and `RESTRICTED`, and it is a pure projection of the player's clearance records. Because neither dimension constrains the other, all nine combinations of the two are reachable, and a reader must not assume that a medically restricted player has been pulled off a roster or that an active player is medically cleared. Medical standing never moves for a purely roster reason. Roster standing never moves for a purely medical reason either, with one deliberate exception: revoking the clearance that underpins a player's active assignment is simultaneously a medical event and a roster event, and it moves both dimensions.

Roster standing advances as follows, always as a server-driven consequence rather than a direct client write. A player moves `RESERVE_LIST` → `ACTIVE` when one of their level or complex assignments moves into `ACTIVE`, and that transition sets `current_affiliate_id` to that assignment's affiliate. A player moves `ACTIVE` → `RESERVE_LIST` when their last level or complex assignment in status `ACTIVE` leaves `ACTIVE` — whether by completion, revocation or suspension — and that transition clears `current_affiliate_id` to null. A player moves `ACTIVE` → `RESTRICTED` when an issued clearance covering their active assignment is revoked, which likewise clears `current_affiliate_id` to null. A player moves `RESTRICTED` → `ACTIVE` when a suspended assignment is resumed on the strength of a freshly issued level-assignment clearance, which restores `current_affiliate_id` from the resumed assignment. A player moves `RESERVE_LIST` → `RESTRICTED` when any clearance for them is denied, with no further side effect. And a player moves `RESTRICTED` → `RESERVE_LIST` when a clearance for them is issued while they hold no active assignment, again with no further side effect. Neither `roster_status` value is terminal; a player may cycle through all three across a season.

Medical standing advances on clearance outcomes alone. A player moves `PENDING_CLEARANCE` → `CLEARED` when a clearance for them is issued, and `PENDING_CLEARANCE` → `RESTRICTED` when a clearance for them is denied. A `CLEARED` player moves to `RESTRICTED` in two distinct situations, and both must be honoured: when their most recent issued clearance is revoked and no other non-expired issued clearance remains, and — independently — when any clearance for them is denied. The second of those is worth dwelling on, because a denial restricts the medical dimension without touching `roster_status` and without disturbing any active assignment; a player may therefore be simultaneously `ACTIVE` on the roster dimension and `RESTRICTED` on the medical dimension. A `RESTRICTED` player returns to `CLEARED` when a new clearance for them is issued.

Whether a player is carried on the organization's domestic reserve list is derived, never supplied. `is_on_domestic_reserve_list` reads true when the player's `roster_status` is any of `RESERVE_LIST`, `ACTIVE` or `RESTRICTED` and their `origin` is either `DOMESTIC_DRAFT` or `FOREIGN_PRO`. Players whose `origin` is `INTERNATIONAL_FA` are never carried on the domestic reserve list, whatever their roster standing. Likewise `current_affiliate_id` is a denormalized mirror of the affiliate on the player's active level or complex assignment, null when no such assignment exists, and never accepted from a client.

Clearance requests open the medical loop and either a `FarmDirector` or a `MedicalDirector` may open one, supplying only `clearance_type`. Three types exist and each gates a different moment: `LEVEL_ASSIGNMENT` gates activation of a level or complex assignment, `REHAB_START` gates activation of a rehab assignment, and `REHAB_COMPLETE` gates completion of a rehab assignment. A new request is persisted in status `REQUESTED` with `requested_by_user_id` taken from the acting `X-User-ID`, and `issued_by_user_id`, `cleared_workload_ceiling_index`, `expires_on` and `restriction_note` all null. A player's clearances may be listed with filters on `clearance_type` and `status`.

Disposition of a clearance belongs exclusively to a `MedicalDirector`. Issuing moves the record `REQUESTED` → `ISSUED` and requires that the supplied `expires_on` be strictly after the current logical clock date — a clearance expiring today or in the past is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. For a `REHAB_COMPLETE` clearance there is an additional precondition: the player must already hold an assignment of type `REHAB` in status `ACTIVE`, failing which the issue is refused with `ERR_REHAB_ASSIGNMENT_REQUIRED`. Issuing records `issued_by_user_id`, `cleared_workload_ceiling_index` — the workload-index ceiling the medical staff is willing to clear the player for — and `expires_on`; it advances the player's `medical_status` to `CLEARED`; and it returns a player sitting in `RESTRICTED` roster standing to `RESERVE_LIST` when that player holds no active assignment. A clearance advertises its own lapse through `is_expired`, which reads true when its status is `ISSUED` and the logical clock date is strictly after `expires_on`.

Denying moves the record `REQUESTED` → `DENIED`, a terminal state; it stores the reason in `restriction_note` and advances the player's `medical_status` to `RESTRICTED`. Revoking moves an issued record `ISSUED` → `REVOKED`, also terminal, and it is the single most consequential medical act in the system. Revocation stores its reason in `restriction_note`. It advances the player's `medical_status` to `RESTRICTED`, but only when no other non-expired issued clearance remains for that player. It moves every assignment for that player in status `ACTIVE` whose required clearance type matches the revoked clearance's `clearance_type` to `SUSPENDED`. It moves the player's `roster_status` to `RESTRICTED` and clears `current_affiliate_id`. Any development plan attached to a newly suspended assignment stays `ACTIVE` rather than lapsing, but the player's `promotion_readiness` is recomputed. And the affected affiliate's roster counters and its licence posture are both recomputed, which may drive that licence from `OPEN` down to `RESTRICTED` if the suspension pushes the club below its `minimum_active_players` floor. Any clearance action that is not a declared transition out of the record's current status is refused with `ERR_INVALID_CLEARANCE_STATE`, and each successful disposition increments the clearance's `version` by one.

### 3.6. Moving Players Through the Pipeline: Assignment, Option Years and Roster Capacity

Every movement of a player inside the organization is one assignment row, and only a `FarmDirector` creates them. Creation names the player through `player_id` and the receiving club through `affiliate_id`, declares the `assignment_type` — `LEVEL` for a full-season club, `COMPLEX` for complex-level work, `REHAB` for a rehabilitation assignment that overlays an existing level assignment — and declares the `movement_reason` recorded for league reporting, one of `INITIAL`, `PROMOTION`, `DEMOTION`, `REHAB_START` or `LATERAL`. The assignment covers a closed date interval from `effective_date` through `end_date` inclusive, and the end date may be the same day as the start but never earlier. Two optional inputs shape the row further. `workload_ceiling_index` sets the upper bound on the player's rolling workload index while this assignment is active; it must be greater than `0.00`, may not exceed `25.00`, and defaults to `12.00`. `superseded_assignment_id` names the prior level or complex assignment this movement displaces on a promotion, demotion or lateral move; it is null on a player's first assignment. The request must also carry `expected_player_version` as described in Section 2.7.

Creation is a child write at the receiving club, so it is subject to the licence gates: the club must hold a licence in status `ACTIVE`, otherwise `ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_ACTIVE`; that licence's posture must not be `FROZEN`, otherwise `ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_FROZEN`; and when the `movement_reason` is `PROMOTION`, the posture must not be `RESTRICTED` either, otherwise `ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`. A `PROMOTION` creation is additionally held to the full promotion criteria described in Section 3.7, and a movement that fails any of the three is refused with `ERR_PROMOTION_CRITERIA_NOT_MET`.

Assignment windows must not collide, and the rule is stricter than plain non-overlap. For the new row the server examines every other assignment of the same player that is in status `PENDING_CLEARANCE`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` and whose type is `LEVEL` or `COMPLEX`. Two exemptions apply before any comparison: a new assignment whose own type is `REHAB` is exempt from this rule entirely, because a rehabilitation assignment legitimately overlays a level assignment; and the one prior row named in `superseded_assignment_id` is exempt, because naming it is an explicit statement that the new movement replaces it. For every prior row that survives both exemptions, two tests run. First, if the new closed interval and the prior closed interval intersect at all, the creation is refused with `ERR_ASSIGNMENT_WINDOW_CONFLICT`. Second, the server measures the gap in whole days from the prior row's `end_date` to the new row's `effective_date`; if that gap is positive but no greater than two days, the creation is again refused with `ERR_ASSIGNMENT_WINDOW_CONFLICT`. In other words, a player leaving a club needs a genuine transfer buffer: the gap between the old end date and the new start date must be strictly greater than two days, so at least three.

On success the row is persisted in status `PENDING_CLEARANCE` and three derived values are fixed. `initiated_by_user_id` records the acting `X-User-ID`, and it is this value that later blocks the same operator from certifying the league filing. `level_at_assignment` snapshots the receiving club's `level` at the moment of creation, and it is this pinned value — never the club's live `level` — that the workload multiplier and the promotion level-rank comparison both read; a club that is later re-levelled does not retroactively change the arithmetic of an existing assignment. And `projected_optional_days` is computed as the number of days from `effective_date` to `end_date` plus one, so a single-day assignment projects one optional day and a thirty-day window projects thirty. The row also starts with `option_year_charged` false. Assignments can be listed with filters on `player_id`, on `affiliate_id`, on `superseded_assignment_id` — which is how the chain of a player's movements is walked — and on `status` and `assignment_type`.

Activation is where the organization's capacity, medical and quota rules all land at once, and a `FarmDirector` drives it, moving the row `PENDING_CLEARANCE` → `ACTIVE`. Nine conditions must hold, and they are checked in the order given here. The club must hold an `ACTIVE` licence (`ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_ACTIVE`) whose posture is not `FROZEN` (`ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_FROZEN`). The player must hold the matching clearance: for a `LEVEL` or `COMPLEX` assignment, a clearance of type `LEVEL_ASSIGNMENT` in status `ISSUED` whose `is_expired` is false; for a `REHAB` assignment, the same but of type `REHAB_START`. When no clearance of the required type is issued at all the refusal is `ERR_MEDICAL_CLEARANCE_REQUIRED`; when the only issued match has lapsed the refusal is `ERR_MEDICAL_CLEARANCE_EXPIRED`. The club's `facility_status` must not be `DEFICIENT` (`ERR_FACILITY_DEFICIENCY_BLOCKING`). A `PROMOTION` movement must satisfy the promotion criteria of Section 3.7 (`ERR_PROMOTION_CRITERIA_NOT_MET`). For a `LEVEL` or `COMPLEX` assignment the club's `active_roster_count` must be strictly below its `roster_limit` (`ERR_ROSTER_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`); for a `REHAB` assignment the club's `rehab_assignment_count` must be strictly below the organization's rehab slot allowance of three concurrent rehab assignments per club (`ERR_REHAB_SLOT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`). Adding a domestic-reserve-list player to a club requires the club's `reserve_list_count` to be strictly below its `reserve_slots` (`ERR_DOMESTIC_RESERVE_LIST_FULL`). And for a `LEVEL` or `COMPLEX` assignment whose `projected_optional_days` is 20 or more, the player must have at least one option year left (`ERR_OPTION_YEARS_EXHAUSTED`). Every capacity comparison is made against the counters as they stand *before* this activation increments them.

The counters themselves are pure aggregations over the club's assignment rows. `active_roster_count` is the number of assignments at the club with status `ACTIVE` and type `LEVEL` or `COMPLEX`. `rehab_assignment_count` is the number at the club with status `ACTIVE` and type `REHAB`. `reserve_list_count` is the number of players whose derived `current_affiliate_id` is this club and whose `is_on_domestic_reserve_list` is true. Only `ACTIVE` assignments occupy a slot: rows in `PENDING_CLEARANCE`, `SUSPENDED`, `COMPLETED` or `REVOKED` release their slot immediately.

Activation carries seven consequences. When the assignment is `LEVEL` or `COMPLEX` and its `projected_optional_days` is 20 or more, the server charges an option year: it creates an option-year record in status `CHARGED` whose `triggering_assignment_id` is this assignment, whose `season_year` is the calendar year of the assignment's `effective_date`, whose `optional_days` snapshots the assignment's `projected_optional_days` at charge time, and whose `charged_at` is the current logical clock instant; the assignment's `option_year_charged` flips to true. A player has three option years in total, so `option_years_used` is the number of `CHARGED` option-year records belonging to that player's assignments, and `option_years_remaining` is three minus that count, floored at zero — the two always sum to three, because rescinded records are excluded from the used count and the remainder is derived rather than independently decremented. For a `LEVEL` or `COMPLEX` assignment, the player's `roster_status` advances to `ACTIVE` and `current_affiliate_id` is set to the receiving club. If `superseded_assignment_id` was supplied and that prior assignment is currently `ACTIVE`, it is moved to `COMPLETED`. The club's roster counters are recomputed, along with its `reserve_list_count`. A transaction report is created in status `PENDING`, with its `transaction_type` derived from the movement: `INITIAL`, `LATERAL` and `DEMOTION` all produce `OPTIONAL_ASSIGNMENT`, `PROMOTION` produces `PROMOTION`, and `REHAB_START` produces `REHAB_ASSIGNMENT`; its `filing_deadline` is set to two days after the assignment's `effective_date`. Finally the club's licence posture is re-derived, which is how activating enough players to reach `minimum_active_players` lifts a licence from `RESTRICTED` to `OPEN`.

Completion ends an assignment cleanly, moving it `ACTIVE` → `COMPLETED`, and it is a `FarmDirector` act. For a `REHAB` assignment there is one precondition: the player must hold a clearance of type `REHAB_COMPLETE` in status `ISSUED`, failing which the completion is refused with `ERR_MEDICAL_CLEARANCE_REQUIRED`. Completion recomputes the club's roster counters; returns the player's `roster_status` to `RESERVE_LIST` and clears `current_affiliate_id` when this was the player's last `ACTIVE` level or complex assignment; creates, for a `REHAB` assignment only, a further transaction report in status `PENDING` with `transaction_type` `REHAB_COMPLETE`; lapses any development plan on the assignment that is still in `DRAFT`, `ACTIVE` or `REVISION_REQUESTED`; and re-derives the club's licence posture, which may drop it to `RESTRICTED` if the club has now fallen below its staffing floor. Note that completion does not rescind the option year the assignment charged — a completed optional assignment has been used.

Suspension takes an assignment `ACTIVE` → `SUSPENDED` and reaches the system three ways: directly, by a `FarmDirector`; as the cascade from suspending the club's licence; and as the cascade from revoking the clearance that underpinned the assignment. In every case the club's roster counters are recomputed, because a suspended assignment no longer occupies a roster slot, and the player's `current_affiliate_id` is cleared. The player's `roster_status` depends on what caused the suspension: it moves to `RESTRICTED` when the cause was a clearance revocation, and to `RESERVE_LIST` otherwise. Critically, suspension performs no compensation: option-year records stay `CHARGED`, and the player does not get an option year back for having been suspended.

Resumption reverses suspension, moving the row `SUSPENDED` → `ACTIVE`, and it is again a `FarmDirector` act with three preconditions. The club's licence must be `ACTIVE` (`ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_ACTIVE`) with a posture other than `FROZEN` (`ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_FROZEN`). The player must hold a non-expired `ISSUED` clearance of the type the assignment's `assignment_type` requires, otherwise `ERR_MEDICAL_CLEARANCE_REQUIRED`. And the club's `active_roster_count` must be strictly below its `roster_limit`, otherwise `ERR_ROSTER_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` — a player suspended into a club that has since filled every slot cannot simply walk back in. Resumption recomputes the club's roster counters, restores the player's `roster_status` to `ACTIVE` along with `current_affiliate_id`, and creates a transaction report in status `PENDING` with `transaction_type` `RECALL`.

Revocation is the compensating exit and it is reachable from three states, with two different consequence sets. From `PENDING_CLEARANCE`, revoking moves the row to the terminal state `REVOKED` and does exactly one thing: any development plan on that assignment in status `DRAFT`, `ACTIVE` or `REVISION_REQUESTED` moves to `LAPSED`. Nothing else needs undoing, because a never-activated assignment never charged an option year, never occupied a roster slot and never produced a transaction report. From `ACTIVE`, revoking moves the row to `REVOKED` and unwinds everything the activation did: every option-year record whose `triggering_assignment_id` is this assignment and whose status is `CHARGED` moves to `RESCINDED`, which restores the player's `option_years_used` and `option_years_remaining`; the assignment's `option_year_charged` flips back to false; every transaction report for this assignment in status `PENDING`, `FILED` or `REJECTED_BY_LEAGUE` moves to `VOIDED`, and exactly one new report is created in status `PENDING` with `transaction_type` `REVOCATION`; any development plan on the assignment in `DRAFT`, `ACTIVE` or `REVISION_REQUESTED` moves to `LAPSED`; the club's roster counters are recomputed and the player's `roster_status` returns to `RESERVE_LIST` with `current_affiliate_id` cleared; and the club's licence posture is re-derived. From `SUSPENDED`, revoking moves the row to `REVOKED` and performs that same compensation cascade in full: charged option-year records are rescinded and `option_year_charged` returns to false, non-terminal transaction reports are voided and a `REVOCATION` report is created in status `PENDING`, non-terminal development plans lapse, the club's roster counters are recomputed with the player returned to `RESERVE_LIST` and `current_affiliate_id` cleared, and the licence posture is re-derived.

`COMPLETED` and `REVOKED` are terminal for an assignment; any action that is not a declared transition out of the row's current status is refused with `ERR_INVALID_ASSIGNMENT_STATE`, as is any attempt to attach a development plan or a workload outing to an assignment sitting in the wrong status. Each successful disposition increments the assignment's `version` by one and may carry a free-text `note`. The option-year records an assignment produced are readable only from that assignment, filterable by `status`, and this is the only way to discover records the server created as a cascade.

### 3.7. Development Plans, Workload Governance and Promotion Readiness

A development plan attaches a curriculum to one assignment, and a `FarmDirector` creates it. The plan declares the `target_level` it is preparing the player for — drawn from the same six competition levels — the `curriculum_version` it follows, which must equal the organization-wide version per Section 2.11, and `curriculum_modules`, an ordered list of module codes the player must complete, holding between 1 and 12 members with no duplicates and never empty. It may also declare `workload_limit_weekly_index`, the weekly workload-index allowance the plan budgets, which must be greater than `0.00`, may not exceed `10.00`, and defaults to `1.50`. That single number does double duty: it is the forward-looking allowance added to the player's projected workload, and it is the denominator of the promotion pressure term. A new plan is persisted in status `DRAFT` with `completed_module_count` 0, `completion_pct` `0.00`, `review_cycle_count` 0 and `revision_note` null; plans on an assignment may be listed with a `status` filter.

Activating a plan moves it `DRAFT` → `ACTIVE` and is a `FarmDirector` act with three preconditions: the owning assignment must be in status `ACTIVE`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_ASSIGNMENT_STATE`; the plan's `curriculum_version` must equal the organization-wide version, otherwise `ERR_CURRICULUM_VERSION_MISMATCH`; and no other plan on the same assignment may already be `ACTIVE`, otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_PLAN`. Activation recomputes the player's `workload_index_projected` and `promotion_readiness`, since the plan's `workload_limit_weekly_index` now enters both.

Progress accumulates through a self-transition: recording progress leaves the plan `ACTIVE` and is driven by a `FarmDirector` supplying `completed_module_count`. The supplied count must be strictly greater than the value currently stored and no greater than the number of members in `curriculum_modules`; a repeat of the same figure, a decrease, or a figure beyond the module list is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. Recording progress updates `completed_module_count`, recomputes `completion_pct` as the completed count divided by the number of modules, multiplied by one hundred and rounded half-up to two decimal places, and recomputes the player's `promotion_readiness`.

Curriculum consistency is policed by compliance. A `ComplianceOfficer` may send an `ACTIVE` plan back for rework, moving it `ACTIVE` → `REVISION_REQUESTED`; the request carries `revision_note`, which is stored, and `review_cycle_count` is incremented. A plan may enter revision at most three times: a fourth request, made when `review_cycle_count` has already reached 3, is refused with `ERR_CYCLE_LIMIT_REACHED`. Because the player now has no `ACTIVE` plan, requesting a revision sets that player's `promotion_readiness` to `0.0000`. A `FarmDirector` resubmits, moving the plan `REVISION_REQUESTED` → `ACTIVE`, which requires the owning assignment to still be in status `ACTIVE` (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_ASSIGNMENT_STATE`) and recomputes the player's `workload_index_projected` and `promotion_readiness`. Together these close the bounded cycle `ACTIVE` → `REVISION_REQUESTED` → `ACTIVE`.

A plan reaches its happy terminal state when a `FarmDirector` marks it achieved, moving it `ACTIVE` → `ACHIEVED`. This requires `completion_pct` to be exactly `100.00`; an attempt made while any module remains outstanding is refused with `ERR_INVALID_PLAN_STATE`, which is also the error for any plan action that is not a declared transition out of the plan's current status. Marking achieved recomputes the player's `promotion_readiness`. The unhappy terminal state is `LAPSED`, reached only as a server-driven cascade when the owning assignment leaves the live part of its lifecycle: a plan in `DRAFT` lapses when the owning assignment goes to a terminal state out of either `ACTIVE` or `PENDING_CLEARANCE`, with no further side effect; a plan in `ACTIVE` lapses when the owning assignment moves to `COMPLETED` or `REVOKED`, and that lapse sets the player's `promotion_readiness` to `0.0000`; and a plan in `REVISION_REQUESTED` lapses when the owning assignment moves to `COMPLETED` or `REVOKED`, with no further side effect. Each successful plan disposition increments the plan's `version` by one.

Workload is recorded outing by outing. Either a `FarmDirector` or a `MedicalDirector` appends an outing to an assignment, supplying the `log_date` on which it was worked and any of three effort measures: `pitches_thrown`, an integer from 0 through 250 defaulting to 0; `innings_pitched`, a number with one decimal place from `0.0` through `15.0` defaulting to `0.0`; and `high_intensity_minutes`, the conditioning minutes charged to the outing, an integer from 0 through 300 defaulting to 0. The outing also carries `is_rehab_outing`, defaulting to false, which marks the outing as worked under a rehabilitation protocol; this flag is independent of the owning assignment's `assignment_type`, so a rehab-protocol outing may perfectly well be logged against a level assignment. Outings are immutable once written and may be listed per assignment with a filter on `is_rehab_outing`.

Each outing's raw contribution is a weighted blend of its three measures: fifty-five hundredths of the pitch count divided by one hundred, plus thirty hundredths of the innings figure divided by ten, plus fifteen hundredths of the conditioning minutes divided by sixty. That raw contribution is then multiplied by a level multiplier read from the owning assignment's pinned `level_at_assignment`: `SINGLE_A` multiplies by `1.00`, `HIGH_A` by `1.05`, `DOUBLE_A` by `1.12`, `TRIPLE_A` by `1.18`, `COMPLEX` by `1.25` and `DOMINICAN_ACADEMY` by `1.30`. No rounding is applied to any of this arithmetic; every intermediate term is carried as an exact decimal.

Which outings count is governed by two filters. An outing qualifies only if the assignment it was logged against belongs to the player in question and that assignment's status is `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` or `COMPLETED` — outings on `REVOKED` and `PENDING_CLEARANCE` assignments are excluded from both workload figures. And the outing's `log_date` must fall inside a rolling fourteen-day window that is inclusive at both ends: the window runs from thirteen days before the current logical clock date through the clock date itself. Because the window is anchored on the clock, setting the clock forward silently drops outings out of the window and the figures must be recomputed.

Two workload figures are then derived from the same qualifying set, and they differ in exactly two respects. The player's `workload_index` sums the contributions of only those qualifying outings whose `is_rehab_outing` is false, adds no plan allowance at all, rounds the sum half-up to two decimal places, and finally takes the smaller of that rounded figure and the system ceiling of `25.00`. The player's `workload_index_projected` sums the contributions of every qualifying outing, taking rehab outings at half weight — each rehab outing's contribution is multiplied by `0.50` while each non-rehab outing's contribution is taken in full — then adds a forward-looking plan allowance equal to the `workload_limit_weekly_index` of the player's `ACTIVE` development plan, or zero when the player has no `ACTIVE` plan, then rounds half-up to two decimal places and takes the smaller of that figure and `25.00`. For an identical set of outings the projected figure therefore exceeds the actual figure, which is the intent: the projected figure asks what the player's load would look like if the plan's weekly budget were spent.

Recording an outing is refused when it would drive the *actual* rolling figure above the effective ceiling for that assignment. The effective ceiling is the smaller of the assignment's own `workload_ceiling_index` and the `cleared_workload_ceiling_index` on the governing medical clearance when that value is non-null; when the clearance carries no cleared ceiling, the assignment's own figure stands alone. An outing that would breach the effective ceiling is refused with `ERR_WORKLOAD_CEILING_EXCEEDED` and nothing is written. Both workload figures and the player's `promotion_readiness` are recomputed on every accepted outing, on any assignment status change, on plan activation, resubmission, revision request and lapse, and whenever the logical clock is set.

Promotion readiness condenses a player's development state into one number between zero and one. It reads the player's single `ACTIVE` development plan; plans in `DRAFT`, `REVISION_REQUESTED`, `ACHIEVED` or `LAPSED` contribute nothing, and a player with no `ACTIVE` plan scores `0.0000` regardless of how much workload history they have. Given an active plan, three ratios are formed. The module ratio is `completed_module_count` divided by the number of members in `curriculum_modules`. The workload pressure is the player's `workload_index_projected` divided by the plan's `workload_limit_weekly_index` multiplied by a two-week pressure horizon, capped at one; the workload headroom is one minus that pressure, floored at zero. The service ratio is the player's `service_days_accrued` divided by 120, capped at one. The score is then forty hundredths of the module ratio, plus twenty-five hundredths of the workload headroom, plus thirty-five hundredths of the service ratio — three weights that sum to exactly one — rounded half-up to four decimal places in a single final step, with every intermediate term carried as an exact decimal.

A promotion is admitted only when three conditions hold together. The player's `promotion_readiness` must be at least `0.7500`, the threshold being inclusive so a score of exactly `0.7500` passes. The player's `service_days_accrued` must be at least 120, again inclusive. And the receiving club's `level` must rank strictly above the level pinned on the player's current assignment, where the ranking runs `DOMINICAN_ACADEMY` lowest, then `COMPLEX`, then `SINGLE_A`, then `HIGH_A`, then `DOUBLE_A`, then `TRIPLE_A` highest — a move that stays at the same rank or drops is not a promotion and must be recorded under a different `movement_reason`. Any of the three failing produces `ERR_PROMOTION_CRITERIA_NOT_MET`, and both assignment creation and assignment activation enforce the check.

### 3.8. Reporting Player Movement to the League Office

Every material movement produces a league transaction report, and the organization never creates one by hand — reports appear only as the consequence of an assignment transition. The report records the movement it describes through `assignment_id` and a `transaction_type` derived from the producing transition rather than supplied: activation yields `OPTIONAL_ASSIGNMENT` for an `INITIAL`, `LATERAL` or `DEMOTION` movement, `PROMOTION` for a promotion and `REHAB_ASSIGNMENT` for a rehab start; completing a rehab assignment yields `REHAB_COMPLETE`; resuming a suspended assignment yields `RECALL`; and revoking an assignment yields `REVOCATION`. Every new report begins in status `PENDING` with `certified_by_user_id`, `filed_at` and `league_note` all null, `refile_count` 0 and `late_fee_cents` 0. It also carries `initiated_by_user_id`, copied verbatim from the producing assignment. Because cascade-created reports are never returned by the operation that caused them, they are discoverable only by listing the reports an assignment produced, filterable on `status` and `transaction_type`.

The league gives two days. A report's `filing_deadline` is two days after the producing assignment's `effective_date`. Lateness is measured against that deadline using a reference date that depends on the report's state: once the report has been filed the reference date is the calendar date of `filed_at`; while it is still `PENDING` the reference date is the current logical clock date, which means a pending report grows later on its own as the clock advances. The number of days late is the reference date minus the deadline in whole days, floored at zero, and `is_late` reads true whenever that count is positive.

The late fee is charged in three tiers of increasing severity, applied to the days late as marginal segments. The first two late days cost fifty dollars each, that is 5000 cents per day. The next five late days — days three through seven — cost one hundred and fifty dollars each, 15000 cents per day. Every late day beyond the seventh costs three hundred dollars, 30000 cents per day. The fee written to `late_fee_cents` is the sum of the three segment amounts: 5000 multiplied by the smaller of the days late and two, plus 15000 multiplied by the days late minus two clamped to lie between zero and five, plus 30000 multiplied by the days late minus seven floored at zero. All terms are whole cents so no rounding is required, and a report that is not late carries a fee of 0. Only the single report being priced participates, together with its producing assignment's `effective_date`; reports in `VOIDED` or `ACCEPTED` are never re-priced.

Filing is a `ComplianceOfficer` act that moves the report `PENDING` → `FILED`. It has two preconditions: the acting `X-User-ID` must differ from the report's `initiated_by_user_id`, per Section 2.12, otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`; and the producing assignment must not be in status `REVOKED`, otherwise `ERR_INVALID_ASSIGNMENT_STATE`. Filing records `certified_by_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID` and stamps `filed_at` with the logical clock instant, then recomputes `is_late` and writes `late_fee_cents`.

The league then responds. Acceptance moves the report `FILED` → `ACCEPTED`, a terminal state, with no further side effect. Rejection moves it `FILED` → `REJECTED_BY_LEAGUE` and stores the league's feedback in `league_note`. A rejected report may be refiled, moving it `REJECTED_BY_LEAGUE` → `PENDING`, which increments `refile_count` and clears both `certified_by_user_id` and `filed_at` so that the next filing is certified afresh; a report may be refiled at most twice, and a third attempt made when `refile_count` has already reached 2 is refused with `ERR_CYCLE_LIMIT_REACHED`. These transitions close the cycle `PENDING` → `FILED` → `REJECTED_BY_LEAGUE` → `PENDING`. All four of these actions belong to a `ComplianceOfficer`, and each successful disposition increments the report's `version` by one. Any action that is not a declared transition out of the report's current status is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REPORT_STATE`.

Finally, revoking the producing assignment voids the report wherever it sits in the live part of its lifecycle: a report in `PENDING` moves to `VOIDED`, a report in `FILED` moves to `VOIDED`, and a report in `REJECTED_BY_LEAGUE` moves to `VOIDED`. `VOIDED` is terminal, as is `ACCEPTED`.

### 3.9. International Signing Pool and Academy Operations

The organization's international spending is governed by one bonus pool per signing period, and only a `ComplianceOfficer` opens one. Opening a pool declares the `season_year` — an integer from 2020 through 2040 that must be unique across pools, a duplicate being refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_POOL_SEASON` — the `base_pool_cents` allotment, which must be strictly positive, and optionally `trade_adjustment_cents`, the net pool space acquired as a positive figure or traded away as a negative figure, defaulting to 0. A new pool is persisted in status `OPEN` with `forfeited_cents`, `allocated_cents` and `overage_tax_accrued_cents` all at 0, and with its derived ledger computed: `effective_pool_cents` is the base allotment plus the trade adjustment minus the forfeited amount, and `remaining_cents` is the effective pool minus the allocated amount. Once signings begin, `allocated_cents` is the sum of `pool_charge_cents` over the pool's `APPROVED` signings and `overage_tax_accrued_cents` is the sum of their `overage_tax_cents`; signings in `SUBMITTED`, `REJECTED` or `VOIDED` contribute nothing to either. `remaining_cents` may go negative once the pool is over-allocated.

Submitting a signing is the `InternationalScout`'s exclusive act. The submission names the pool through `pool_id`, the amateur through `player_id`, the academy complex that will house them through `academy_affiliate_id`, and the `bonus_cents` to be paid, which must be zero or positive; it must also carry `expected_pool_version` as described in Section 2.7. An eligibility pair is enforced at submission: the named player's `origin` must be `INTERNATIONAL_FA` and the named academy's `level` must be `DOMINICAN_ACADEMY`, and a violation of either half is refused with `ERR_SIGNING_ELIGIBILITY_VIOLATION`. The submission is persisted in status `SUBMITTED` with `pool_charge_cents` 0, `overage_tax_cents` 0, `tier3_breach` false, and `rejection_reason` and `approved_by_user_id` both null. Signings may be listed with filters on `pool_id`, `player_id`, `academy_affiliate_id` and `status`.

Approval belongs to a `ComplianceOfficer` and moves the signing `SUBMITTED` → `APPROVED`. Three preconditions apply. The pool must be in status `OPEN`, otherwise `ERR_POOL_HARD_CAPPED`. The academy must hold a licence in status `ACTIVE` (otherwise `ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_ACTIVE`) whose posture is neither `FROZEN` nor `RESTRICTED` — a frozen academy yields `ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_FROZEN` and a restricted one yields `ERR_AGREEMENT_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`, making signing approval one of only two operations that `RESTRICTED` gates. And the academy must have a bed free: its count of housed players — its `reserve_list_count` plus the signings already `APPROVED` at that academy — must be strictly below its `housing_beds`, otherwise `ERR_ACADEMY_HOUSING_FULL`.

The charge arithmetic on approval runs in four ordered steps, and the order is load-bearing. First the server snapshots the pool as it stands *before* any forfeit this approval might impose: the effective pool at that moment is the base allotment plus the trade adjustment minus the forfeited amount already recorded; the prior overage is the pool's current `allocated_cents` minus that effective figure, floored at zero; the new allocated figure is the current `allocated_cents` plus this signing's `bonus_cents`; and the total overage is the new allocated figure minus that same effective figure, again floored at zero. Second, the marginal tax is computed. For any overage amount, the tax on that amount is seventy-five hundredths of the first five thousand dollars of overage — the portion up to 500000 cents — plus the full amount of the next five thousand dollars of overage, that is the portion between 500000 and 1000000 cents, plus the full amount of any overage above 1000000 cents. The signing's `overage_tax_cents` is the tax on the total overage minus the tax on the prior overage, rounded half-up to whole cents once, at that subtraction; its `pool_charge_cents` is simply its `bonus_cents`.

Third, and only after the tax has been computed, the third-tier forfeit is applied. `tier3_breach` is set true when the total overage is strictly above 1000000 cents *and* the prior overage was at most 1000000 cents — that is, when this particular approval is the one that crossed the second ceiling — and on such a breach 250000 cents is added to the pool's `forfeited_cents`. Computing the overage against the post-forfeit effective pool instead would inflate the taxable overage by that same 250000 cents and yield a materially different tax, so the two steps must not be reordered. Fourth, the pool's ledger is recomputed with the post-forfeit figure: `effective_pool_cents` becomes the base allotment plus the trade adjustment minus the now-updated `forfeited_cents`; `allocated_cents` becomes the new allocated figure; `remaining_cents` becomes the effective pool minus the allocated amount, which may be negative; and the pool's status becomes `HARD_CAPPED` when the allocated amount is strictly greater than the effective pool, or stays `OPEN` otherwise. Approval also records `approved_by_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID` and refreshes `overage_tax_accrued_cents`.

Hard-capping the pool cascades. When an approval drives the pool `OPEN` → `HARD_CAPPED`, every sibling signing against that pool still in status `SUBMITTED` moves to `REJECTED` with `rejection_reason` set to `POOL_HARD_CAPPED`; siblings already `APPROVED` or already `REJECTED` are untouched. An explicit rejection by a `ComplianceOfficer` also moves a signing `SUBMITTED` → `REJECTED`, but sets `rejection_reason` to `COMPLIANCE_DENIED` instead. `REJECTED` is terminal.

Voiding reverses an approval. A `ComplianceOfficer` moves an `APPROVED` signing to the terminal state `VOIDED`, which removes that signing's `pool_charge_cents` from the pool's `allocated_cents` and its `overage_tax_cents` from the pool's `overage_tax_accrued_cents`; subtracts 250000 cents from the pool's `forfeited_cents` if and only if that signing's `tier3_breach` was true; recomputes `effective_pool_cents` and `remaining_cents`; and returns the pool `HARD_CAPPED` → `OPEN` when the allocated amount is once again at most the effective pool. The reversal is deliberately partial in one respect: siblings that the hard cap auto-rejected are not revived, and their rejection stands even after the pool reopens. A pool may therefore be capped, reopened and capped again over a signing period. Any signing action that is not a declared transition out of the signing's current status is refused with `ERR_INVALID_SIGNING_STATE`, each successful disposition increments the signing's `version` by one, and a free-text `note` may accompany the request.

## 4. Endpoint Definitions
