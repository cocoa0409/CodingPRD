# Driver Recruiting DQ File API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A motor carrier's recruiting office lives or dies by paperwork. Between the moment a driver walks in and the moment that driver is legally allowed to turn a wheel, the office must reconstruct three years of employment history, order and adjudicate a motor vehicle record, a Pre-Employment Screening Program report and a criminal background check, obtain the driver's electronic consent to query the federal Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse, collect and verify a pre-employment specimen, run a road test, seat the driver in an orientation class, and assemble a Driver Qualification file that a DOT auditor can open at random for the next three years without finding a hole in it. Today that work is tracked in spreadsheets, shared inboxes and the recruiter's memory. The consequence is predictable: files go stale, medical certificates expire unnoticed until a CDL is downgraded at the state level, and a single missing previous-employer response silently blocks a hire that everyone believed was closed.

This document specifies the backend service that replaces those spreadsheets. The service is a compliance-audit system first and a workflow orchestrator second: every qualification artifact carries a weight, every file carries a completeness percentage, every derived posture is recomputed from its inputs rather than being set by hand, and no operator may both build a file and certify it. Two independent pipelines advance across the same application record — a background-screening pipeline and a qualification pipeline — and they join only when both have cleared. The service also owns the money: partner CDL-school tuition is reimbursed monthly against a service commitment, and a driver who leaves early triggers a clawback that propagates all the way back into their re-hire eligibility.

Because much of the domain is date-driven — certificate expiry, annual review windows, consent validity, cooldown periods — the service does not read the host machine's wall clock. It maintains a **logical clock** that tests and operators advance explicitly, so that a scenario spanning fourteen months can be exercised deterministically in a single session.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to the service must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role that principal is acting in. If either header is absent or empty, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). The same rejection, with the same error identifier and the same status, applies when `X-User-Role` carries a value that is not one of the six roles the service recognises. Those six roles are, spelled exactly: `Recruiter`, `DER`, `SafetyDirector`, `ComplianceAuditor`, `SchoolCoordinator`, and `Applicant`.

The roles partition the work along segregation-of-duties lines. A `Recruiter` staffs the sourcing desk: they create applicant records and applications, submit them, chase previous employers, order screening reports and record the raw results, schedule and reschedule road tests, run orientation logistics, and file qualification documents by hand. A `Recruiter` never adjudicates a report, never certifies a file, and never hires. A `DER` — the carrier's Designated Employer Representative — is the sole owner of the Clearinghouse queries and of the entire drug and alcohol testing chain, from specimen collection through laboratory result to Medical Review Officer verification. A `SafetyDirector` registers and reconfigures the carrier, adjudicates screening reports, conducts road tests, requests further information on an application or disqualifies it outright, and makes the hire, terminate and rescind decisions. A `ComplianceAuditor` does exactly two things and nothing else: certify an annual review of a qualification file, and revoke such a certification. A `SchoolCoordinator` owns the training-school partnership desk — tuition agreements, monthly accruals, and clawback reversals. Finally, an `Applicant` is the driver themselves; they may grant or deny Clearinghouse consent on their own queries and dispute a pre-adverse screening finding.

Where a role is not listed as authorised for the endpoint it calls, the request fails with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE` (HTTP 403). Read access is deliberately broad: every one of the six roles may call every read endpoint and may advance the logical clock.

Identity semantics differ between the operator roles and the driver role, and the distinction matters for ownership checks. When `X-User-Role` is `Recruiter`, `DER`, `SafetyDirector`, `ComplianceAuditor`, or `SchoolCoordinator`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier that is not tied to any persisted entity. The server may record it — for example as `recruiter_user_id` on an application when a `Recruiter` submits it, as `certified_by_user_id` on a qualification file when a `ComplianceAuditor` certifies it, or compare it against the `examiner_user_id` recorded on a road test — but it never resolves it to a row's `id`. When `X-User-Role` is `Applicant`, by contrast, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting driver applicant record; ownership checks on any resource that references a driver applicant compare the resource's applicant reference to `X-User-ID`, following the chain from the targeted row through its `application_id` to that application's `applicant_id` where the link is indirect. An `Applicant` who acts on a Clearinghouse consent decision or a screening dispute belonging to somebody else is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403).

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.


### 2.3. Pagination

Every list endpoint accepts two optional query parameters, `limit` and `offset`. The `limit` parameter defaults to 10 when omitted, may not be smaller than 1, and may not be larger than 100. The `offset` parameter defaults to 0 and may not be negative. A `limit` or `offset` that is not an integer, or that falls outside those bounds, is rejected with `ERR_VALIDATION` (HTTP 422).

List results are ordered by `created_at` ascending. Every list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside the `meta` object, where `total_count` is the number of rows matching the filters before the window is applied and `has_more` indicates whether any row remains beyond the returned window.

Beyond pagination, most list endpoints accept filter parameters that narrow the collection by a foreign key or by a state value. Those filters are named after the field they filter on and are described alongside the workflows that produce the data.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and the Version Counter

Every persisted resource carries a server-minted `id`, which is a UUID with no type prefix of any kind. Clients never supply an `id`; a client-supplied identifier in a create payload is ignored. Each resource also carries `created_at` and `updated_at`, both ISO 8601 UTC timestamps rendered with exactly three fractional-second digits, and an integer `version` that starts at 1 when the row is minted.

The `version` counter is the backbone of concurrency control described in §2.7. It increments by exactly one on every mutation of the row it belongs to, and `updated_at` is refreshed to the logical-clock instant at which the mutation was applied. A cascade that changes a row as a side effect of an operation targeted at a different row still counts as a mutation of that row: when hiring a driver advances the carrier's derived posture, the carrier's `version` increments and its `updated_at` moves, exactly as if the carrier had been edited directly. Every entity in this system carries the full set — `id`, `created_at`, `updated_at` and `version`; there is no entity that omits `updated_at` or `version`.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock

The service keeps a single logical clock that stands in for wall-clock time everywhere a date or an instant is evaluated: certificate expiry, annual-review windows, consent validity, the no-response waiting period, the pre-adverse dispute window, and re-hire cooldown. On startup the clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and until it is explicitly moved the service must report that instant and must never fall back to the host machine's real time.

Any of the six roles may advance the clock by supplying a `to_iso8601` instant; the clock endpoint answers with the resulting `current_utc`. Moving the clock is not a passive operation — it triggers a full reconciliation sweep, described in detail in §3.7 and §3.8: every non-archived qualification file has its next review date and review posture recomputed, every file has its completeness re-evaluated so that newly expired documents flip it out of a complete posture, every applicant's CDL downgrade risk band and governing medical certificate expiry are recomputed, every applicant's re-hire eligibility is recomputed, and every carrier's filled seat count, deficiency ratio and posture are recomputed. A separate read of the clock returns the same `current_utc` field without changing anything.

### 2.6. Left-to-Right Path Resolution and Referential Integrity

Nested and parameterised paths are resolved strictly left to right. Each identifier in the path is looked up in turn, and the first one that fails to resolve determines the error: a missing carrier yields `ERR_CARRIER_NOT_FOUND`, a missing applicant `ERR_APPLICANT_NOT_FOUND`, a missing application `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND`, a missing employment verification `ERR_VERIFICATION_NOT_FOUND`, a missing screening check `ERR_SCREENING_CHECK_NOT_FOUND`, a missing Clearinghouse query `ERR_CLEARINGHOUSE_QUERY_NOT_FOUND`, a missing drug test order `ERR_DRUG_TEST_NOT_FOUND`, a missing road test `ERR_ROAD_TEST_NOT_FOUND`, a missing orientation session `ERR_SESSION_NOT_FOUND`, a missing enrollment `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_FOUND`, a missing qualification file `ERR_DQ_FILE_NOT_FOUND`, a missing qualification document `ERR_DQ_DOCUMENT_NOT_FOUND`, a missing tuition agreement `ERR_TUITION_AGREEMENT_NOT_FOUND`, and a missing hire record `ERR_HIRE_RECORD_NOT_FOUND`. All of these carry HTTP 404. The path variables themselves are named `carrier_id`, `applicant_id`, `application_id`, `verification_id`, `screening_check_id`, `clearinghouse_query_id`, `drug_test_order_id`, `road_test_id`, `session_id`, `enrollment_id`, `dq_file_id`, `document_id`, `tuition_agreement_id` and `hire_record_id`.

Foreign keys arriving in a request body are resolved with the same catalogue of not-found codes and the same 404 status, and this check runs immediately after path resolution. It covers `applicant_id`, `carrier_id`, `application_id`, `session_id`, `prior_application_id`, and every member of an `attended_enrollment_ids` list.

### 2.7. Optimistic Concurrency on Parent Records

Five write operations mutate a parent record's derived state as part of their work, and each of those five requires the client to declare which version of that parent it last observed. The declared value is a required, non-optional field in the request body; if it does not equal the parent's current `version` at the moment the write is evaluated, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` (HTTP 412) and nothing is written. No other endpoint accepts a version field.

Creating a hire record consumes one of the carrier's funded requisition seats and advances the carrier's `version`, so it must carry `expected_carrier_version` matching the target carrier's current `version`. If another writer has advanced the carrier since the client last read it, the client's view of how many seats remain is stale and its seat arithmetic cannot be trusted, so the create is refused with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` at HTTP 412. Reconfiguring a carrier carries the same `expected_carrier_version` field against the same carrier and is refused the same way. Creating an orientation enrollment consumes one of the session's capacity seats and advances the session's `version`, so it must carry `expected_session_version`; a value older than the session currently holds means the client's reading of the enrolled count predates a seat that has already been taken or released, and the create is refused with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` at HTTP 412. Writing a qualification document recomputes the parent file's completeness figures and status and advances that file's `version`, so it must carry `expected_dq_file_version`; a stale value means the completeness picture the edit was based on has already been superseded by a cascade from screening, drug testing or the road test, and the write is refused with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` at HTTP 412. Re-applying mints a superseding application against a predecessor and advances that predecessor's `version`, so it must carry `expected_application_version`; a stale value means a superseding application has already been minted against that same predecessor, and the request is refused with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` at HTTP 412.

### 2.8. Numeric Precision and the Cents Discipline

Monetary amounts in this system are integer cents throughout; there are no fractional cents and no floating-point currency. Where a division produces a remainder, the specification names the direction explicitly at the point of use — the monthly tuition base is floored, while a percentage-derived bonus is rounded half up to a whole cent.

Percentages and scores round half up. A qualification file's completeness percentage is carried to exactly 2 decimal places. A screening check's adjudication score is carried to exactly 2 decimal places. A road test's weighted section values are each carried to 2 decimal places and the composite they produce to exactly 1 decimal place. A carrier's deficiency ratio is carried to exactly 4 decimal places. Day counts — employment coverage, unexplained gaps, tenure, cooldown windows, certificate countdowns — are whole days computed from calendar dates with no time-of-day component.

### 2.9. Validation Order and the First-Failure Rule

Requests are validated against an ordered ladder, and the first check that fails determines both the error identifier and the HTTP status; no later check is consulted. The ladder is fixed across the whole API and reads as follows.

First, authentication and authorisation: the two headers must be present and non-empty, then `X-User-Role` must name one of the six declared roles (both failures reporting `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` at HTTP 401), then the acting role must appear in the endpoint's authorised list (`ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE`, 403), then — on the Clearinghouse consent decision and on the screening dispute only — an acting `Applicant` must own the targeted resource (`ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`, 403).

Second, existence: every identifier in the path is resolved left to right, then every foreign key in the body, each producing its own 404 code from the catalogue in §2.6.

Third, shape: every required body field must be present, every enum value must be a declared member of its enumeration, every integer must fall inside its declared range, and every date must parse as a `YYYY-MM-DD` calendar date or an ISO 8601 instant as appropriate — all failures reporting `ERR_VALIDATION` at HTTP 422. Pagination bounds are checked at the next rung, also as `ERR_VALIDATION`. Then uniqueness: a carrier's `dot_number` must be unique across all carriers and an applicant's `email` must be unique within its carrier, both violations reporting `ERR_DUPLICATE_DOT_NUMBER` at HTTP 409.

Fourth, optimistic concurrency: the supplied parent version must match, as described in §2.7, reporting `ERR_STALE_VERSION` at HTTP 412.

The remaining rungs are business rules, and three of them are deliberately hoisted above the generic state checks so that the specific cause is reported rather than a generic one. Recording a Clearinghouse result against a query whose own status is not consent-granted reports `ERR_CONSENT_NOT_GRANTED`, outranking the generic state check. Accruing a month against an agreement whose served months already equal its commitment reports `ERR_COMMITMENT_ALREADY_FULFILLED`, outranking the generic terminal check. Reversing a clawback on an agreement that carries no clawback balance reports `ERR_NO_CLAWBACK_TO_REVERSE`, also outranking the generic state check. Immediately below those sits the duplicate-active-application rule reporting `ERR_DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_APPLICATION`.

Then the generic state ladder: the target row must be in a declared origin state for the requested transition (`ERR_INVALID_STATE`, 409), and must not already sit in one of its terminal states (`ERR_ALREADY_TERMINAL`, 409). Then the application-posture rungs reporting `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_QUALIFIED`; then the two segregation-of-duties rungs reporting `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`; then the Clearinghouse block (`ERR_CLEARINGHOUSE_PROHIBITED`), the drug-test block (`ERR_DRUG_TEST_NOT_NEGATIVE`), the MVR points block (`ERR_MVR_POINTS_EXCEEDED`) and the medical-certificate block (`ERR_MEDICAL_CERT_INVALID`). Below those sit the referential-temporal rungs: consent expiry (`ERR_CONSENT_EXPIRED`), MVR currency for an annual review (`ERR_MVR_EXPIRED`), and review-window eligibility (`ERR_REVIEW_NOT_DUE`). Then the interval-ordering rung reporting `ERR_VALIDATION` at 422, followed by orientation overlap (`ERR_ORIENTATION_OVERLAP`), examiner slot conflict (`ERR_EXAMINER_SLOT_CONFLICT`), session capacity (`ERR_SESSION_AT_CAPACITY`) and road-test attempt exhaustion (`ERR_ROAD_TEST_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`). Finally the hire-gate rungs, in this order: file completeness (`ERR_DQ_FILE_INCOMPLETE`), employment-history coverage (`ERR_EMPLOYMENT_HISTORY_INCOMPLETE`), re-hire eligibility (`ERR_REHIRE_INELIGIBLE`), carrier posture (`ERR_CARRIER_POSTURE_BLOCKED`) and, last of all, seat availability (`ERR_NO_OPEN_REQUISITION`). Every rung below the concurrency check carries HTTP 409 unless stated otherwise above.

One nuance of the generic state rung deserves calling out. On the annual-review certification and on its revocation, that rung governs only the file's completeness-posture axis — any posture other than archived passes it. The review-clock axis is owned by its own rung further down and always surfaces as `ERR_REVIEW_NOT_DUE`, never as `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.

### 2.10. Segregation of Duties

Two duties in this system may never be discharged by the principal who created the work. A `ComplianceAuditor` may not certify, nor revoke the certification of, a qualification file whose application was submitted by that same `X-User-ID`; the file records the submitter as `recruiter_user_id`, and equality between that value and the acting `X-User-ID` is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 409). A road-test result may not be recorded when the test's `examiner_user_id` equals the submitting recruiter's `recruiter_user_id` on the parent application, and it is refused with the same code. Separately and immediately after, recording a road-test result requires that the acting `X-User-ID` equal the test's `examiner_user_id` — an operator may only report on a test they were assigned — and a mismatch reports `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` as well.

### 2.11. Derived Fields, Cascades and Reconciliation

No derived value in this system is ever set by a client. Completeness percentages, deficiency counts, adjudication scores, composite road-test scores, coverage days, unexplained gap days, tuition ledger balances, carrier postures, deficiency ratios, filled seat counts, downgrade risk bands and re-hire eligibility are all recomputed by the service from their inputs whenever any of those inputs change. When a single operation changes several rows, all of them are written, all of their `version` counters increment, and all of their `updated_at` values move — the cascade is atomic, not eventual. Where a cascade has several hops, each hop is enumerated in the workflow narrative in §3 so that an implementer can trace the whole chain from origin to final effect.

Three operations in this system are exact compensating inverses of earlier ones: revoking an annual-review certification restores every field and document that the certification wrote; reversing a tuition clawback restores the principal balance the termination moved; and rescinding a hire restores the seat and the application posture that the hire consumed. After each compensating pair, the corresponding invariant must read exactly as it did before the original operation — the total qualification weight of a file, the cents identity of a tuition agreement, and the carrier's filled seat count respectively.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up a Recruiting Office and Reading Its Posture

Everything begins with a carrier. A `SafetyDirector` registers the recruiting office by supplying its `legal_name`, its `dot_number` and the number of funded driver seats it is allowed to fill as `open_requisitions`, which may be zero but never negative. The `dot_number` is the USDOT number as printed, between 1 and 12 characters long, and it must be unique across every carrier the service holds; a second registration reusing an existing number is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_DOT_NUMBER`. The registration payload also carries the office's policy knobs, all optional and all defaulted: `safety_rating`, which is one of `SATISFACTORY`, `CONDITIONAL` or `UNSATISFACTORY` and defaults to `SATISFACTORY`; `mvr_validity_days`, the number of days a motor vehicle record stays current after the date it was issued, defaulting to 365 and never less than 1; `annual_review_interval_days`, the spacing between qualification-file annual reviews, defaulting to 365 and never less than 1; `annual_review_grace_days`, the number of days of slack allowed after a review comes due before the file is treated as overdue, defaulting to 30 and never negative; `mvr_point_threshold`, the adjudication score at or above which a motor vehicle record is disqualifying, defaulting to 7 and never less than 1; `rehire_cooldown_days`, the number of days a voluntarily separated driver must wait before being considered again, defaulting to 180 and never negative; and `medical_cert_grace_days`, the number of days a driver may run past a medical certificate's expiry before the CDL is treated as downgraded, defaulting to 15 and never negative.

Three of a carrier's fields are never written by a client. The `filled_requisitions` count is the number of hire records at that carrier whose status is `ACTIVE`; terminated and rescinded hires do not count. The `deficiency_ratio` is the number of qualification files in `DEFICIENT` status that belong to an application reachable from one of those active hires, divided by `filled_requisitions`, rounded half up to four decimal places; when `filled_requisitions` is zero the ratio is 0.0 rather than undefined. From those two numbers the office's `posture` follows, and the branches are evaluated in a fixed order with the first match winning: the posture is `FROZEN` when the carrier's `safety_rating` is `UNSATISFACTORY`; otherwise it is `RESTRICTED` when the deficiency ratio is greater than or equal to 0.2500 — a ratio of exactly one quarter already restricts the office; otherwise it is `OPEN`. Seat exhaustion is deliberately absent from this computation: running out of funded seats is enforced as its own hiring gate reporting `ERR_NO_OPEN_REQUISITION`, so that the two conditions remain independently observable. At registration time, with no hires in existence, the ratio is 0.0 and the posture therefore resolves to `OPEN` unless the office was registered with an `UNSATISFACTORY` rating.

The triple is recomputed whenever a hire record is created, terminated or rescinded, whenever a `SafetyDirector` reconfigures the carrier, whenever any qualification file changes its completeness posture, and on every advance of the logical clock. Reconfiguration adjusts `open_requisitions` and `safety_rating` under the concurrency rule of §2.7, taking `expected_carrier_version` alongside whichever of the two knobs the caller wishes to change; both are optional, the version is not. Carriers may be listed and narrowed with a `posture` filter, and a single carrier may be read back to see its live posture.

### 3.2. Applicant Intake and the Two-Axis Application Record

A `Recruiter` opens a driver applicant record under a carrier, supplying `carrier_id`, the driver's `full_name`, their `date_of_birth` as a calendar date, a contact `email`, the `cdl_number` as printed, the two-uppercase-letter issuing jurisdiction `cdl_state`, the licence `cdl_class` — one of `CLASS_A`, `CLASS_B` or `CLASS_C` — and `experience_months`, the count of verified Class-A or Class-B driving months, which may be zero but never negative. The `email` must be unique within its carrier; a collision reports `ERR_DUPLICATE_DOT_NUMBER`, the same code the carrier-level uniqueness rule uses. A new applicant opens with `cdl_downgrade_risk` at `NO_CERTIFICATE`, `governing_medical_cert_expires_on` at null, `rehire_eligibility` at `ELIGIBLE` and `outstanding_clawback_cents` at 0; all four are derived and are described in §3.7 and §3.8. Reading a single applicant is not a passive operation: the downgrade risk band, the governing certificate expiry, the re-hire eligibility and the outstanding clawback total are all recomputed before the record is returned. Applicants may be listed and narrowed by `carrier_id`, by `rehire_eligibility`, or by `cdl_downgrade_risk`.

Against an applicant, a `Recruiter` opens an application by supplying `applicant_id`, a `position_type` of `OTR`, `REGIONAL` or `LOCAL`, and optionally a `prior_application_id` naming the superseded application this one re-applies against — null for a first application. The service copies `carrier_id` from the applicant onto the application; a client never supplies it. An applicant may not hold two applications at the same carrier whose `qualification_status` is non-terminal, meaning anything other than `HIRED` or `DISQUALIFIED`; a second such application is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_APPLICATION`. Opening an application also requires that the applicant's `rehire_eligibility` read `ELIGIBLE`; a driver in `COOLDOWN` or `PERMANENTLY_INELIGIBLE` is turned away with `ERR_REHIRE_INELIGIBLE`.

The application carries **two independent state axes**. The `screening_status` axis tracks the background and consent pipeline through `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `SCREENING`, `INFO_REQUESTED`, `SCREEN_CLEARED` and the terminal `SCREEN_FAILED`. The `qualification_status` axis tracks the road-test, orientation and file pipeline through `NOT_STARTED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `ROAD_TEST_PASSED`, `ORIENTATION_COMPLETE`, `QUALIFIED`, and the two terminal values `HIRED` and `DISQUALIFIED`. A fresh application opens at `DRAFT` on the first axis and `NOT_STARTED` on the second. The axes advance independently and join in exactly one place, at `ORIENTATION_COMPLETE → QUALIFIED`. Applications may be listed and narrowed by `applicant_id`, by `carrier_id`, by `prior_application_id`, by `screening_status` or by `qualification_status`, and a single application read exposes both axes together with its strict verified coverage figures.

Submission is the pivot of the whole system, and it is a `Recruiter`'s privilege. It is accepted only while `screening_status` is `DRAFT`, only while the owning carrier's `posture` is `OPEN` or `RESTRICTED` — a `FROZEN` office is refused with `ERR_CARRIER_POSTURE_BLOCKED` — and only while the applicant's `rehire_eligibility` reads `ELIGIBLE`, otherwise `ERR_REHIRE_INELIGIBLE`. On success the axis moves `DRAFT → SUBMITTED`, `submitted_at` is stamped with the current logical-clock instant, `recruiter_user_id` is set to the calling `X-User-ID` — the anchor for every segregation-of-duties check that follows — and the second axis moves `NOT_STARTED → IN_PROGRESS`. Submission is also the sole producer of the qualification file and its documents: it creates the one-to-one file in `OPEN` status with `review_state` at `NOT_DUE`, `opened_on` set to the current logical-clock calendar date and `total_weight` at 33, then seeds twelve document rows, one per artifact type, all in `MISSING` status. It immediately sets the `APPLICATION_FORM` document to `PRESENT` with `effective_on` at the current logical-clock date, then recomputes the file's `completeness_pct` and `deficiency_count`, recomputes `next_review_due_on` and `review_state`, and recomputes the application's `verified_coverage_days` and `gap_days_unexplained`. There is no way to create a qualification file or a qualification document by any other route.

Between `SCREENING` and `INFO_REQUESTED` sits a bounded revision loop. A `SafetyDirector` may push a screening application back for more information by supplying a `reason`; the axis moves `SCREENING → INFO_REQUESTED` and nothing else on the record changes — `disqualifying_reason` stays null. The `Recruiter` closes the round by supplying a `response_note`, moving the axis `INFO_REQUESTED → SCREENING` and incrementing `revision_round` by one. That return trip is accepted only while `revision_round` is strictly less than 3, so the third resubmission is the last one the service accepts; once `revision_round` reaches 3 no further round may be opened or closed and the attempt reports `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.

Re-application supersedes a closed attempt. A `Recruiter` names the predecessor in the path, supplies `expected_application_version` matching that predecessor's current `version` and a fresh `position_type`; the service increments the predecessor's `version` by one and mints a brand-new application for the same applicant at `DRAFT` and `NOT_STARTED`, with `prior_application_id` pointing back at the predecessor. Because the new record is itself an application, the duplicate-active-application rule applies to it exactly as it does to a first-time application.

Disqualification is a `SafetyDirector`'s decision and it terminates both axes at once. Supplying a `reason`, the director drives `screening_status` to `SCREEN_FAILED` from `SUBMITTED`, `SCREENING` or `INFO_REQUESTED`, and drives `qualification_status` to `DISQUALIFIED` from `IN_PROGRESS`, `ROAD_TEST_PASSED`, `ORIENTATION_COMPLETE` or `QUALIFIED`, recording the supplied token in `disqualifying_reason`. The consequences fan out: every orientation enrollment of that application still in `RESERVED` becomes `CANCELLED`, and each such cancellation decrements the owning session's `enrolled_count` by one and increments that session's `version` by one; the one-to-one qualification file moves to `ARCHIVED` and its `version` increments by one; and the carrier's `filled_requisitions`, `deficiency_ratio` and `posture` are recomputed. The same fan-out fires whenever a disqualification arrives as a cascade rather than as a direct decision.

The `screening_status` axis reaches `SCREEN_CLEARED` on its own, without anybody calling for it. After every terminal transition of a screening check, a Clearinghouse query or a drug test order, the service re-evaluates the application: the axis moves `SCREENING → SCREEN_CLEARED` when exactly one screening check row exists for each of the three check types `MVR`, `PSP` and `CRIMINAL` and all three sit in `ADJUDICATED_PASS`, and at least one Clearinghouse query of the application sits in `RESULT_CLEAR`, and at least one drug test order of the application whose `test_type` is `PRE_EMPLOYMENT` sits in `MRO_VERIFIED_NEGATIVE`. Reaching `SCREEN_CLEARED` immediately re-evaluates the qualification join described in §3.6. Conversely, the axis is driven to `SCREEN_FAILED` as a system cascade whenever a screening check reaches `ADVERSE_FINAL`, a Clearinghouse query reaches `CONSENT_DENIED` or `RESULT_PROHIBITED`, or a drug test order reaches `MRO_VERIFIED_POSITIVE`; each of those cascades also sets `qualification_status` to `DISQUALIFIED` with the corresponding reason token, which in turn archives the file and cancels reserved enrollments.

### 3.3. Reconstructing Three Years of Employment History

Federal practice requires the recruiting office to account for the driver's previous three years of employment, and the service models that as a set of employment segments hung off the application. A `Recruiter` records one segment per previous employer by supplying `application_id`, the `employer_name`, a boolean `is_dot_regulated` marking whether that employer was itself a DOT-regulated motor carrier — which is what triggers the safety-performance history duty — and the segment's boundaries as `employed_from`, an inclusive calendar date, and `employed_to`, an exclusive calendar date that must fall strictly after `employed_from`; an inverted or degenerate interval is refused with `ERR_VALIDATION` at HTTP 422. A segment may not be recorded against an application whose `qualification_status` is `DISQUALIFIED` or whose `screening_status` is `SCREEN_FAILED`, which reports `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_QUALIFIED`. Segments start in `PENDING` and may be listed and narrowed by `application_id` or by `status`.

Chasing an employer is a loop. Issuing the request moves the segment to `REQUESTED` from either `PENDING` or `NO_RESPONSE`, increments `request_round` by one and stamps `last_requested_at` with the current logical-clock instant; a segment whose parent application has already reached `SCREEN_FAILED` may not be chased further, and a re-request out of `NO_RESPONSE` is accepted only while `request_round` is strictly less than 3. Marking a segment as unanswered is accepted only from `REQUESTED` and only once the logical clock has reached or passed two weeks — 14 days — after `last_requested_at`. That single trigger branches on the round counter, and the two branches are exact mirrors of one another. While `request_round` is strictly less than 3, the segment falls back to `NO_RESPONSE`, changing nothing else, and the loop `REQUESTED → NO_RESPONSE → REQUESTED` may run again. Once `request_round` has reached 3, the same trigger instead lands the segment in the terminal `GOOD_FAITH_DOCUMENTED` state, and that transition recomputes the parent application's `verified_coverage_days` and `gap_days_unexplained` — which, under the strict accounting those two fields use, does *not* credit the good-faith segment.

Recording a response is accepted only from `REQUESTED` and carries three fields: an `outcome` of either `VERIFIED` or `DISPUTED`, plus the booleans `safety_performance_history_received` and `drug_alcohol_violation_reported`, both of which are stored on the segment whichever branch is taken. A `VERIFIED` outcome moves the segment to `RESPONDED_VERIFIED` and recomputes the application's coverage figures; if the recomputed `gap_days_unexplained` is at most 30 days, the recomputation also sets the `EMPLOYMENT_VERIFICATION` document of the application's qualification file to `PRESENT` with `effective_on` at the current logical-clock date, recomputes that file's `completeness_pct`, `deficiency_count` and status, and increments the file's `version` by one. A `DISPUTED` outcome moves the segment to `RESPONDED_DISPUTED`; it does not credit any coverage, and when `drug_alcohol_violation_reported` came back true it cascades the parent application's `screening_status` to `SCREEN_FAILED` with the reason token `PRIOR_DA_VIOLATION`, which in turn disqualifies the application, archives its file and cancels its reserved enrollments.

Coverage itself is an interval-merge computation over a three-year lookback window of exactly 1,095 days. The window opens 1,095 days before the current logical-clock calendar date and closes at that date, and it is half-open: the opening day is included and the closing day is not. Each segment whose status belongs to the variant's accepted set is clipped to the window — its lower bound becomes the later of `employed_from` and the window opening, its upper bound the earlier of `employed_to` and the window close — and is discarded when the clipped upper bound is not strictly greater than the clipped lower bound. The surviving clipped segments are sorted by lower bound ascending and, where lower bounds tie, by upper bound ascending; no identifier is ever consulted in the ordering. They are then merged left to right: the next segment joins the run in progress when its lower bound is less than or equal to the run's current upper bound, so that two segments which merely touch at a shared date *do* merge, while a one-day hole between them does *not*. The `verified_coverage_days` figure is the total number of days spanned by the merged runs, and `gap_days_unexplained` is 1,095 minus that figure. Segments in `PENDING`, `REQUESTED`, `NO_RESPONSE` or `RESPONDED_DISPUTED` are excluded by both accountings.

Two accountings exist over the same rows. The strict accounting, named `strict_verified`, admits only segments in `RESPONDED_VERIFIED`; it is the default, it is the one persisted into the application's `verified_coverage_days` and `gap_days_unexplained`, and it is the one the hiring gate enforces. The projected accounting, named `good_faith_projected`, admits segments in `RESPONDED_VERIFIED` and `GOOD_FAITH_DOCUMENTED` alike, so an employer that never answered three documented requests still counts as covered; identical rows therefore yield a larger coverage and a smaller gap under the projected accounting than under the strict one. The projected figure exists so a recruiter can demonstrate diligence to an auditor, and it is obtained by asking the coverage read for a `variant` of `good_faith_projected`; omitting the parameter yields `strict_verified`. That read answers with the `variant` it applied, the resulting `verified_coverage_days` and `gap_days_unexplained`, and a `merged_segment_count` giving the number of runs left after merging.

### 3.4. Third-Party Reports, Adjudication and the Pre-Adverse Window

A `Recruiter` orders a third-party report by naming the `application_id` and a `check_type` of `MVR`, `PSP` or `CRIMINAL`. At most one non-terminal row may exist for a given application and check type at a time, and a report may not be ordered against an application already at `DISQUALIFIED` or `SCREEN_FAILED`, which reports `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_QUALIFIED`. Orders open in `ORDERED`, and the very first order placed against a `SUBMITTED` application advances that application's `screening_status` from `SUBMITTED` to `SCREENING` as a system side effect. Checks may be listed and narrowed by `application_id`, by `check_type` or by `status`.

When the report comes back, the `Recruiter` records it. That call carries the `issued_on` calendar date the source stamped on the report, plus the three raw counts the report contains: `violation_points`, the raw moving-violation points; `serious_violation_count`, the number of FMCSA serious violations; and `crash_preventable_count`, the number of preventable crashes. It is accepted from `ORDERED` and, identically, from `DISPUTED` — a corrected report simply overwrites the earlier figures — and in both cases the row lands in `RECEIVED`. For a motor vehicle record the service also computes `expires_on` as `issued_on` advanced by the carrier's `mvr_validity_days`; for a Pre-Employment Screening Program report and for a criminal check `expires_on` stays null.

The `adjudication_score` is computed on that same call, and it takes one of two shapes. The raw score is always the sum of three weighted terms: each moving-violation point contributes 1.0, each serious violation contributes 4.0, and each preventable crash contributes 6.0. For a `PSP` or `CRIMINAL` report the adjudication score is that raw sum rounded half up to two decimal places and nothing more — no experience credit, no multiplier — and it is informational only, never blocking a pass. For an `MVR` the raw sum is first reduced by an experience credit, then scaled. The credit is 1.5 for each full year of the applicant's `experience_months`, taking the whole number of years by discarding any remaining months, and it is capped at 6.0 however long the driver has been on the road; the credit is not rounded before it is subtracted. The net is the raw sum minus that credit, floored at zero so a very experienced clean driver never scores below nothing. The net is then multiplied by the carrier's safety-rating multiplier — 1.00 when the carrier's `safety_rating` is `SATISFACTORY`, 1.25 when it is `CONDITIONAL`, and 1.50 when it is `UNSATISFACTORY` — and only that product is rounded half up to two decimal places to produce `adjudication_score`.

Adjudication belongs to the `SafetyDirector` and takes a single `decision` field valued `PASS`, `PRE_ADVERSE` or `ADVERSE_FINAL`. A `PASS` decision is accepted only from `RECEIVED`, and for a motor vehicle record only when the `adjudication_score` is strictly below the carrier's `mvr_point_threshold` — a score exactly equal to the threshold is disqualifying and reports `ERR_MVR_POINTS_EXCEEDED`. Non-MVR checks are not gated by the threshold at all. On success the row moves `RECEIVED → ADJUDICATED_PASS` and the corresponding artifact of the application's qualification file is filed automatically: an `MVR` check satisfies the `MVR_INITIAL` document, a `PSP` check satisfies the `PSP_REPORT` document, and a `CRIMINAL` check satisfies the `CRIMINAL_CHECK` document. That document is set to `PRESENT` with `effective_on` equal to the check's `issued_on` and `expires_on` equal to the check's own `expires_on`; the file's `completeness_pct`, `deficiency_count` and status are recomputed, the file's `version` increments by one, and the application is re-evaluated for the `SCREENING → SCREEN_CLEARED` move.

A `PRE_ADVERSE` decision is also accepted only from `RECEIVED`, and it forks the story completely: no document is filed, no completeness changes, and instead `pre_adverse_at` is stamped with the current logical-clock instant, opening a five-day window in which the driver may contest the report. Inside that window — while the logical clock is strictly earlier than `pre_adverse_at` advanced by 5 days — the `Applicant` themselves, and only the applicant behind that check, may dispute it by supplying a `dispute_note`; the row moves `PRE_ADVERSE → DISPUTED` and `pre_adverse_at` is cleared back to null, reopening the cycle `RECEIVED → PRE_ADVERSE → DISPUTED → RECEIVED` for a corrected report. Once the window has elapsed — the logical clock having reached or passed `pre_adverse_at` advanced by 5 days — the `SafetyDirector` may finalise with an `ADVERSE_FINAL` decision, moving the row `PRE_ADVERSE → ADVERSE_FINAL` and cascading the parent application's `screening_status` to `SCREEN_FAILED` with the reason token `ADVERSE_SCREENING`, which sets `qualification_status` to `DISQUALIFIED`, which archives the qualification file and cancels every reserved orientation enrollment of that application.

### 3.5. Clearinghouse Consent and the Drug & Alcohol Chain

The `DER` opens a Clearinghouse query by naming an `application_id` and a `query_type` of either `LIMITED` or `FULL`. A limited query needs only the driver's general consent; a full query needs electronic consent recorded in the Clearinghouse itself. Queries open in `CONSENT_PENDING` with `result` at `PENDING`, `consent_granted_at` and `consent_expires_on` both null, and `follow_up_full_query_id` null. Opening a query against an application already at `DISQUALIFIED` or `SCREEN_FAILED` reports `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_QUALIFIED`. When a `FULL` query is opened while a limited query of the same application sits in `RESULT_INFO_EXISTS`, the service writes the new query's identifier into that earlier row's `follow_up_full_query_id`, tying the follow-up back to the hit that demanded it. Queries may be listed and narrowed by `application_id`, by `query_type`, by `status` or by `follow_up_full_query_id`.

Consent is the driver's own decision and nobody else's. Acting as `Applicant`, and only on a query whose application belongs to them, the driver supplies a `decision` of `GRANT` or `DENY` while the query is in `CONSENT_PENDING`. A grant moves the row to `CONSENT_GRANTED`, stamps `consent_granted_at` with the current logical-clock instant, and sets `consent_expires_on` to the calendar date of that instant advanced by one year — 365 days. A denial moves the row to the terminal `CONSENT_DENIED` and cascades the parent application's `screening_status` to `SCREEN_FAILED` with the reason token `CONSENT_REFUSED`, which sets `qualification_status` to `DISQUALIFIED`, which archives the qualification file and cancels the application's reserved enrollments.

Recording a result is the `DER`'s job and takes a single `result` field valued `NO_INFO`, `INFORMATION_EXISTS` or `PROHIBITED`. Two gates precede the branch. First, the consent must exist on that very row: a query whose own status is not `CONSENT_GRANTED` is refused with `ERR_CONSENT_NOT_GRANTED`, and a sibling limited query's consent does not stand in for it — this rule is deliberately checked ahead of the generic origin-state rule so that a missing consent is reported specifically rather than as `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. Second, the consent must still be live: the current logical-clock calendar date must be on or before `consent_expires_on`, and a later date is refused with `ERR_CONSENT_EXPIRED`. A `NO_INFO` result moves the row to `RESULT_CLEAR`, sets the `CLEARINGHOUSE_QUERY` document of the application's qualification file to `PRESENT` with `effective_on` at the current logical-clock date, recomputes that file's `completeness_pct`, `deficiency_count` and status, increments the file's `version` by one, and re-evaluates the `SCREENING → SCREEN_CLEARED` move. An `INFORMATION_EXISTS` result is accepted only on a query whose `query_type` is `LIMITED`; the row moves to `RESULT_INFO_EXISTS` and no document changes at all — the file remains short of its Clearinghouse artifact until a full query resolves the hit. A `PROHIBITED` result is accepted only on a query whose `query_type` is `FULL`, and it is the origin of a four-hop cascade: the parent application's `screening_status` becomes `SCREEN_FAILED` with the reason token `CLEARINGHOUSE_PROHIBITED`; that in turn drives the same application's `qualification_status` to `DISQUALIFIED`; that in turn moves the one-to-one qualification file to `ARCHIVED`, increments its `version` by one, and cancels every `RESERVED` orientation enrollment of that application; and that in turn recomputes the carrier's `filled_requisitions`, `deficiency_ratio` and `posture` and increments the carrier's `version` by one.

An unresolved Clearinghouse picture blocks downstream work even when nothing has formally failed. Three operations refuse to proceed with `ERR_CLEARINGHOUSE_PROHIBITED` when any query of the application sits in `RESULT_PROHIBITED`, or any query sits in `CONSENT_DENIED`, or a limited query is still sitting in `RESULT_INFO_EXISTS` with `follow_up_full_query_id` null: creating a hire record, reserving an orientation seat, and recording a road-test result. On the road-test result the rule is evaluated only when the outcome being recorded is a pass, so a failure or a no-show may be filed against an application whose Clearinghouse picture is still unresolved.

Specimen collection runs on its own chain, entirely owned by the `DER`. An order is opened by naming an `application_id` and a `test_type` of `PRE_EMPLOYMENT` or `RETURN_TO_DUTY`; it opens in `ORDERED` with `collected_at` null and `dilute_recollect_count` at zero, and it may not be opened against an application at `DISQUALIFIED` or `SCREEN_FAILED`, which reports `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_QUALIFIED`. Orders may be listed and narrowed by `application_id` or by `status`. Recording a collection supplies the `collected_at` instant and moves the order to `COLLECTED` from either `ORDERED` or `LAB_DILUTE`; the second of those is a recollection and is accepted only while `dilute_recollect_count` is strictly below the single permitted recollection, meaning a count of zero, and it increments that counter by one. A second dilute recollection is therefore refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, closing the `COLLECTED → LAB_DILUTE → COLLECTED` loop after exactly one lap.

The laboratory then reports. From `COLLECTED`, a `lab_result` of `NEGATIVE`, `POSITIVE` or `DILUTE` moves the order to `LAB_NEGATIVE`, `LAB_POSITIVE` or `LAB_DILUTE` respectively; none of the three changes anything beyond the status, and in particular a positive laboratory result on its own triggers no cascade whatsoever — a Medical Review Officer must verify it first. Verification supplies an `mro_decision` of `VERIFIED_NEGATIVE` or `VERIFIED_POSITIVE`. A `VERIFIED_NEGATIVE` decision is accepted from `LAB_NEGATIVE` and, identically, from `LAB_POSITIVE` — the latter being the case where the officer accepts a legitimate medical explanation — and both branches carry exactly the same consequences: the order lands in `MRO_VERIFIED_NEGATIVE`, the `DRUG_TEST_RESULT` document of the application's qualification file becomes `PRESENT` with `effective_on` at the current logical-clock date, that file's `completeness_pct`, `deficiency_count` and status are recomputed, its `version` increments by one, and the application is re-evaluated for the `SCREENING → SCREEN_CLEARED` move. A `VERIFIED_POSITIVE` decision is accepted only from `LAB_POSITIVE`; the order lands in `MRO_VERIFIED_POSITIVE` and cascades the parent application's `screening_status` to `SCREEN_FAILED` with the reason token `DRUG_TEST_POSITIVE`, which sets `qualification_status` to `DISQUALIFIED`, which archives the file and cancels the reserved enrollments.

Downstream, a verified-negative pre-employment specimen is a hard prerequisite in two places. Creating a hire record and recording a passing road-test result both require that some drug test order of the application with `test_type` `PRE_EMPLOYMENT` sits in `MRO_VERIFIED_NEGATIVE`; the absence of one reports `ERR_DRUG_TEST_NOT_NEGATIVE`. As with the Clearinghouse block, the road-test rung is evaluated only when the outcome is a pass, so a failed or no-show road test may be recorded before any specimen has even been collected.

### 3.6. The Road Test, the Orientation Class, and the Qualification Join

A `Recruiter` books a road test by naming the `application_id`, the `examiner_user_id` of the `SafetyDirector` who will conduct it, the `scheduled_at` start instant of the examiner's slot, and the `vehicle_class` the test will be conducted in, which is one of `CLASS_A`, `CLASS_B` or `CLASS_C`. The test opens in `SCHEDULED` with `attempt_number` at 1 and all four score fields null. Booking against an application at `DISQUALIFIED` or `SCREEN_FAILED` reports `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_QUALIFIED`. Each booking consumes three hours of examiner time: the slot runs from `scheduled_at` to `scheduled_at` advanced by 180 minutes, and that interval is **closed at both ends**. A new or rescheduled test is refused with `ERR_EXAMINER_SLOT_CONFLICT` when its closed slot intersects the identically-constructed closed slot of any other road test that is still `SCHEDULED` and carries the same `examiner_user_id` — because the interval is closed, two slots that merely touch endpoint-to-endpoint already collide. Road tests may be listed and narrowed by `application_id`, by `status`, or by `examiner_user_id`, the last of which is how an examiner's slot book is read.

Only the assigned examiner may report on the test, and they report a single `outcome` valued `PASSED`, `FAILED` or `NO_SHOW`, accepted only while the test is `SCHEDULED`. The three sub-scores `score_pretrip`, `score_skills` and `score_road` accompany a pass or a fail and are omitted for a no-show; each is an integer between 0 and 100 inclusive. Two identity rules apply here, in this order: first, the test's `examiner_user_id` may not equal the `recruiter_user_id` recorded on the parent application when the submission happened, and second, the acting `X-User-ID` must equal that same `examiner_user_id`; both violations report `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`.

The composite score is computed on a pass or a fail, and its rounding order is pinned. Each section is weighted and rounded on its own first: the pre-trip inspection section is `score_pretrip` multiplied by 0.25 and rounded half up to two decimal places, the basic control skills section is `score_skills` multiplied by 0.35 and rounded half up to two decimal places, and the on-road section is `score_road` multiplied by 0.40 and rounded half up to two decimal places. Those three already-rounded section values are then added together and the sum is rounded half up to one decimal place, and that one-decimal figure is `composite_score`. Summing the unrounded products and rounding only once at the end is a different computation and is not what this specification asks for. A pass requires two thresholds to hold together: the `composite_score` must be at least 80.0, and the raw `score_road` must be at least 70 on its own, so a candidate cannot compensate for weak on-road driving with a flawless pre-trip. An outcome of `PASSED` whose figures fail either threshold is not a declared transition and is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.

A pass also requires the safety prerequisites of §3.5 — an unblocked Clearinghouse picture and a verified-negative pre-employment specimen — and both are evaluated only on the pass branch. On success the test moves `SCHEDULED → PASSED`, which is its terminal state; the application's `qualification_status` advances `IN_PROGRESS → ROAD_TEST_PASSED`; the `ROAD_TEST_CERT` document of the application's qualification file becomes `PRESENT` with `effective_on` at the current logical-clock date; and the file's `completeness_pct`, `deficiency_count` and status are recomputed with its `version` incremented by one. A `FAILED` outcome moves the test `SCHEDULED → FAILED` and computes `composite_score` from the supplied sub-scores, but changes nothing on the application or the file. A `NO_SHOW` outcome moves the test `SCHEDULED → NO_SHOW` and leaves `composite_score` null.

Retesting is a `Recruiter`'s call and reopens the cycle from either `FAILED` or `NO_SHOW` back to `SCHEDULED`. The reschedule supplies a fresh `scheduled_at` and a fresh `examiner_user_id`, which replace the previous values; `attempt_number` increments by one, and `score_pretrip`, `score_skills`, `score_road` and `composite_score` are all cleared back to null. A reschedule is accepted only while `attempt_number` is strictly below three attempts; a test already sitting at its third attempt reports `ERR_ROAD_TEST_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`. The rescheduled slot is checked against the examiner slot rule exactly as a first booking is.

Orientation runs as classes with fixed seats. A `Recruiter` creates a class by supplying `carrier_id`, the `starts_at` inclusive start and the `ends_at` exclusive end of its interval — `ends_at` must fall strictly after `starts_at`, and an inverted interval reports `ERR_VALIDATION` at HTTP 422 — a `capacity` of at least one seat, and a `location_code` naming the terminal or classroom. Classes open in `PLANNED` with `enrolled_count` at zero, and may be listed and narrowed by `carrier_id` or by `status`. Opening the class for enrollment moves it `PLANNED → OPEN` and is accepted only while the logical clock is strictly earlier than `starts_at`; the move increments the session's `version` by one.

Seats are reserved one at a time. A `Recruiter` reserves one by supplying `session_id`, `application_id` and `expected_session_version` matching the session's current `version`, and the reservation is only accepted while the session is `OPEN`. The application must have reached `ROAD_TEST_PASSED` on its qualification axis — anything earlier or later, and anything disqualified, reports `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_QUALIFIED`. The applicant's medical picture must not be at `DOWNGRADED`, which reports `ERR_MEDICAL_CERT_INVALID`; note that an applicant with no certificate on file at all may still be seated in a class, even though they may not be hired. The Clearinghouse picture must be unblocked as described in §3.5. Beyond those, two interval rules apply. The **buffered overlap** rule protects the driver: the candidate session's interval is widened by twelve hours on each side, forming the half-open span from `starts_at` less twelve hours up to but not including `ends_at` plus twelve hours, and if that widened span intersects the plain half-open interval of any other session the same application already occupies through a `RESERVED` or `ATTENDED` enrollment, the reservation is refused with `ERR_ORIENTATION_OVERLAP`. The **capacity** rule protects the classroom: the session's `enrolled_count` must be strictly below its `capacity`, and a full class reports `ERR_SESSION_AT_CAPACITY`. On success the enrollment is minted in `RESERVED`, the session's `enrolled_count` increments by one and the session's `version` increments by one. The `enrolled_count` figure counts every enrollment of the session in `RESERVED`, `ATTENDED` or `ABSENT`, so an absent driver still occupies the seat they consumed; only a cancellation gives a seat back. Enrollments may be listed and narrowed by `session_id`, by `application_id` or by `status`.

Cancelling a reservation is a `Recruiter` action carrying a `reason`; it moves the enrollment `RESERVED → CANCELLED`, decrements the session's `enrolled_count` by one and increments the session's `version` by one. The identical effect fires when a cancellation arrives as a cascade from a disqualified application.

Completing the class is the last `Recruiter` step. It is accepted only while the session is `OPEN` and only once the logical clock has reached or passed `ends_at`, and it carries `attended_enrollment_ids`, a list in which every identifier must be an enrollment of *this* session currently in `RESERVED`. The session moves `OPEN → COMPLETED`; every listed enrollment moves `RESERVED → ATTENDED` and every other still-reserved enrollment of the session moves `RESERVED → ABSENT`; the session's `version` increments by one. An absence changes nothing on the application and does not release the seat. Each attendance, by contrast, advances the linked application's `qualification_status` from `ROAD_TEST_PASSED` to `ORIENTATION_COMPLETE`.

That advance sets up the only join between the two axes. An application moves `ORIENTATION_COMPLETE → QUALIFIED` when three conditions hold at once: its `qualification_status` is `ORIENTATION_COMPLETE`, its `screening_status` is `SCREEN_CLEARED`, and its one-to-one qualification file is in `COMPLETE` status. Because the three conditions are satisfied by three different pipelines, the service re-evaluates the join after orientation completion, after the screening axis reaches `SCREEN_CLEARED`, and after any change to the file's completeness posture — whichever condition is satisfied last performs the transition.

### 3.7. The Qualification File: Weights, Completeness, Expiry and the Annual Review

The qualification file is the artifact a DOT auditor asks for, and it is the only entity in this system nobody can create or delete — it is minted one-to-one with the application at submission and it lives as long as that application does. It carries `application_id` back to its owner, an `opened_on` calendar date recording when submission created it, and a fixed `total_weight` of 33 which is the sum of the weights of all twelve documents it holds, in scope or not. Files may be read one at a time — a read recomputes `review_state` without incrementing `version` — and may be listed and narrowed by `application_id`, by `status` or by `review_state`.

Each of the twelve rows the file holds carries `dq_file_id` back to its file, a `doc_type` naming the regulatory artifact it tracks, and a `weight` fixed per artifact type and never supplied by a client: `APPLICATION_FORM` weighs 3, `CDL_COPY` weighs 2, `MVR_INITIAL` weighs 3, `PSP_REPORT` weighs 2, `CRIMINAL_CHECK` weighs 1, `EMPLOYMENT_VERIFICATION` weighs 4, `CLEARINGHOUSE_QUERY` weighs 3, `DRUG_TEST_RESULT` weighs 3, `ROAD_TEST_CERT` weighs 3, `MEDICAL_CERT` weighs 4, `MVR_ANNUAL` weighs 3 and `ANNUAL_REVIEW_CERT` weighs 2 — thirty-three in total, and that sum must read identically before and after every operation, compensating operations included. A document's presence is tracked by its `status`, one of `MISSING`, `PRESENT`, `EXPIRED` or `WAIVED`, together with the calendar dates `effective_on` — the date the artifact took effect, null while missing — and `expires_on` — the date it lapses, null for an artifact that never expires — and a free-text `source_ref` pointing at whatever originated it, such as a screening check identifier or a medical examiner registry number. Documents may be listed and narrowed by `dq_file_id`, by `doc_type` or by `status`.

Most documents are filed automatically by the workflows of §3.3 through §3.6. The rest a `Recruiter` files by hand, supplying the new `status`, the mandatory `expected_dq_file_version` matching the parent file's current `version`, and optionally `effective_on`, `expires_on` and `source_ref`. Every such write recomputes the parent file's `completeness_pct`, `deficiency_count` and `status`, increments the file's `version` by one, re-evaluates the application's qualification join and the carrier's posture, and — when the row being written is the `MEDICAL_CERT` row — recomputes the applicant's downgrade risk.

Completeness is a weight ratio, and its rounding order is pinned. The service first selects the in-scope rows for the accounting being applied and adds up their integer weights to form the denominator. It then selects the satisfied rows: a row is satisfied when its `status` belongs to the accounting's satisfying set *and* it is not lapsed, where lapsed means the row carries a non-null `expires_on` strictly earlier than the current logical-clock calendar date. It adds up the integer weights of the satisfied rows, divides that total by the denominator, multiplies by 100, and rounds half up to two decimal places **once**. Computing each document's own percentage contribution, rounding each of those, and adding them up is a different computation and produces a different number. The `deficiency_count` is the plain count of in-scope rows minus the count of satisfied rows — a count of documents, not of weight.

Two accountings exist over the same twelve rows. The provisional accounting, named `pre_hire_provisional`, is the default and the only one persisted: it is what `completeness_pct` and `deficiency_count` hold on the file and what drives the file's status between `OPEN`, `COMPLETE` and `DEFICIENT`. It excludes `MVR_ANNUAL` and `ANNUAL_REVIEW_CERT` from scope, leaving a denominator of 28, and it treats both `PRESENT` and `WAIVED` as satisfying, so a waived artifact counts toward the score. The final accounting, named `annual_review_final`, is the figure a DOT auditor certifies against and is available only on demand: it excludes `PSP_REPORT` and `CRIMINAL_CHECK`, which are pre-employment-only artifacts, and brings `MVR_ANNUAL` and `ANNUAL_REVIEW_CERT` into scope, leaving a denominator of 30, and it treats only `PRESENT` as satisfying, so a `WAIVED` artifact does not count. The same twelve rows therefore yield two different percentages. The on-demand completeness read takes an optional `variant` of `pre_hire_provisional` or `annual_review_final`, defaulting to the former, and answers with the `variant` it applied, the resulting `completeness_pct` and `deficiency_count`, the `satisfied_weight` and `denominator_weight` that produced them, and `missing_doc_types`, the list of in-scope artifact types that were not satisfied.

The file's completeness posture follows from that number and is re-evaluated after every document mutation and after every cascade that touches a document. A file in `OPEN` or `DEFICIENT` moves to `COMPLETE` the moment its provisional `completeness_pct` reads exactly 100.00; that move re-evaluates the application's `ORIENTATION_COMPLETE → QUALIFIED` join and recomputes the carrier's `deficiency_ratio` and `posture`. A file in `COMPLETE` falls back to `DEFICIENT` the moment its provisional percentage drops below 100.00 — most often because a document lapsed when the logical clock moved past its `expires_on` — and that move likewise recomputes the carrier's ratio and posture; the `COMPLETE → DEFICIENT → COMPLETE` cycle is how the system tracks expiry and remediation over a driver's tenure. From `OPEN`, `COMPLETE` or `DEFICIENT`, a file moves to the terminal `ARCHIVED` when its application is disqualified, and that move too recomputes the carrier's ratio and posture.

The review clock is the file's second axis and it turns on its own. The review baseline is the calendar date of `last_annual_review_at` when a review has ever happened, and `opened_on` otherwise. From that baseline, `next_review_due_on` is the baseline advanced by the carrier's `annual_review_interval_days`, and the end of grace is `next_review_due_on` advanced by the carrier's `annual_review_grace_days`. The `review_state` reads `NOT_DUE` while the current logical-clock date is strictly earlier than `next_review_due_on`; it reads `DUE` from `next_review_due_on` through the end of grace inclusive — a file whose grace end equals the clock date is still `DUE` — and it reads `OVERDUE` from the day after that. The schedule is recomputed at submission, at certification, at revocation, and on every advance of the logical clock.

Certifying the annual review belongs exclusively to a `ComplianceAuditor` and carries a `certification_note`. Four conditions guard it, and their relative order matters. The file must not be `ARCHIVED`, which is enforced on the completeness axis and reports `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. The auditor's `X-User-ID` must differ from the `recruiter_user_id` on the linked application, reporting `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. The application must have a motor vehicle record check in `ADJUDICATED_PASS` whose `expires_on` is on or after the current logical-clock date, reporting `ERR_MVR_EXPIRED`. And the file's `review_state` must be `DUE` or `OVERDUE` — an early review is refused with `ERR_REVIEW_NOT_DUE`, never with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, because the review-clock axis is owned by its own rung of the ladder in §2.9 and sits below the three checks just named. On success the review clock returns to `NOT_DUE`, `last_annual_review_at` is stamped with the current logical-clock instant and `certified_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`; the `MVR_ANNUAL` and `ANNUAL_REVIEW_CERT` documents both become `PRESENT` with `effective_on` at the current logical-clock date; `next_review_due_on` is recomputed as the calendar date of `last_annual_review_at` advanced by the carrier's `annual_review_interval_days`; `completeness_pct` and `deficiency_count` are recomputed; and the file's `version` increments by one.

Revocation is the exact compensating inverse and belongs to the same role, carrying a `reason`. It requires that `last_annual_review_at` be non-null and that the file not be `ARCHIVED`, both reported as `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, and it is bound by the same segregation-of-duties rule as the certification it undoes. It clears `last_annual_review_at` and `certified_by_user_id` back to null, returns the `MVR_ANNUAL` and `ANNUAL_REVIEW_CERT` documents to `MISSING` with `effective_on` null, recomputes `next_review_due_on` from `opened_on` again — which typically pushes the review clock back to `DUE` — recomputes `completeness_pct` and `deficiency_count`, and increments the file's `version` by one. Every field the certification wrote is restored, and the file's total weight of 33 reads identically before the certification, after it, and after the revocation.

Medical certificates deserve their own treatment because they are the one artifact whose lapse costs the driver their licence class rather than merely their paperwork. The applicant's `cdl_downgrade_risk` and `governing_medical_cert_expires_on` are derived across every qualification file that applicant owns — every `MEDICAL_CERT` document reachable from the applicant through its file and that file's application, restricted to files whose status is not `ARCHIVED`, whose `status` is `PRESENT` or `EXPIRED`, and whose `expires_on` is not null. When no such document exists at all, the risk band reads `NO_CERTIFICATE` and `governing_medical_cert_expires_on` is null. Otherwise the governing certificate is the candidate with the greatest `expires_on`, ties broken by the greatest `created_at` and never by identifier; its `expires_on` is copied into `governing_medical_cert_expires_on`. Let the countdown be the number of whole days from the current logical-clock calendar date to that expiry. The band reads `NONE` when the countdown is greater than 60 days; `WATCH` when it is greater than 15 days and at most 60 days; `IMMINENT` when it is between 0 and 15 days inclusive, meaning the expiry has not yet passed; `EXPIRED_GRACE` when the expiry has passed but the number of days since it passed is at most the carrier's `medical_cert_grace_days`; and `DOWNGRADED` in every remaining case. The band is recomputed whenever a document is written by hand, whenever the logical clock advances, and on every read of the applicant.

### 3.8. Hiring, Turnover and the Re-Hire Verdict

Hiring is a `SafetyDirector`'s decision and the most heavily guarded operation in the system. It names the `application_id` being hired, the `hired_on` calendar date on which employment begins, and `expected_carrier_version` matching the carrier's current `version`. Beyond the concurrency check, eight business gates stand between the request and a new hire record, and they are evaluated in the ladder order of §2.9 so that the first failure is always the one reported. The application's `qualification_status` must read `QUALIFIED`, else `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_QUALIFIED`. The Clearinghouse picture must be unblocked, else `ERR_CLEARINGHOUSE_PROHIBITED`. A pre-employment specimen must sit in `MRO_VERIFIED_NEGATIVE`, else `ERR_DRUG_TEST_NOT_NEGATIVE`. The applicant's `cdl_downgrade_risk` must be neither `DOWNGRADED` nor `NO_CERTIFICATE` — a hire, unlike an orientation seat, demands that a certificate actually exist — else `ERR_MEDICAL_CERT_INVALID`. The application's qualification file must be in `COMPLETE` status, else `ERR_DQ_FILE_INCOMPLETE`. The application's `gap_days_unexplained` under the strict accounting must be at most 30 days, else `ERR_EMPLOYMENT_HISTORY_INCOMPLETE`. The applicant's `rehire_eligibility` must read `ELIGIBLE`, else `ERR_REHIRE_INELIGIBLE`. The carrier's `posture` must not be `FROZEN`, else `ERR_CARRIER_POSTURE_BLOCKED` — note that a `RESTRICTED` office may still hire. And finally the carrier's `filled_requisitions` must be strictly below its `open_requisitions` at the moment the seat is consumed, else `ERR_NO_OPEN_REQUISITION`.

On success the service mints the hire record in `ACTIVE`, copying `applicant_id` and `carrier_id` from the application and leaving `terminated_on` null, `termination_type` at `NONE`, `rehire_flag` at `ELIGIBLE` and `tenure_days` at zero. It also resolves `prior_hire_id`, which points at the most recent `TERMINATED` hire record of the same applicant at the same carrier, chosen by ordering those records by `terminated_on` descending and, where those tie, by `created_at` descending; when the applicant has never been terminated there, the field stays null. The application's `qualification_status` advances `QUALIFIED → HIRED`, and the carrier's `filled_requisitions`, `deficiency_ratio` and `posture` are recomputed with the carrier's `version` incremented by one. Hire records may be listed and narrowed by `application_id`, by `applicant_id`, by `carrier_id`, by `prior_hire_id` or by `status`.

Termination is where the turnover cascade starts, and it runs four hops deep. The `SafetyDirector` supplies `terminated_on`, which must fall on or after `hired_on` — an earlier date reports `ERR_VALIDATION` at HTTP 422 — plus a `termination_type` of `VOLUNTARY`, `INVOLUNTARY_PERFORMANCE`, `INVOLUNTARY_SAFETY`, `ABANDONMENT` or `NONE`, and a recruiter-visible `rehire_flag` of `ELIGIBLE`, `NOT_ELIGIBLE` or `CONDITIONAL`. The first hop moves the record `ACTIVE → TERMINATED` and writes `terminated_on`, `termination_type`, `rehire_flag` and `tenure_days`, the last being the whole number of days from `hired_on` to `terminated_on`. The second hop, caused by the first, moves every tuition agreement of that applicant still in `ACTIVE` into `TERMINATED_CLAWBACK`, shifting each agreement's whole `outstanding_cents` balance into its `clawback_cents`. The third hop, caused by the second, recomputes the applicant's `outstanding_clawback_cents` and `rehire_eligibility`, and any positive clawback forces that eligibility to `PERMANENTLY_INELIGIBLE`. The fourth hop, caused by the third, fires only when the eligibility has become `PERMANENTLY_INELIGIBLE`: every application of that applicant whose `qualification_status` is not already terminal is driven to `DISQUALIFIED` with the reason token `REHIRE_INELIGIBLE`, which archives each of those applications' files and cancels their reserved enrollments. Alongside the four hops, the carrier's `filled_requisitions`, `deficiency_ratio` and `posture` are recomputed and the carrier's `version` increments by one.

Rescission is the exact compensating inverse of the hire and exists for offers withdrawn before they took effect. The `SafetyDirector` supplies a `reason`; the record moves `ACTIVE → RESCINDED`, the application's `qualification_status` returns `HIRED → QUALIFIED`, and the carrier's `filled_requisitions`, `deficiency_ratio` and `posture` are recomputed with the carrier's `version` incremented by one. Because rescission must leave no financial trace behind it, it is refused while any tuition agreement of that applicant sits in `TERMINATED_CLAWBACK`. After a hire and its rescission, the carrier's seat count must read exactly what it read before the hire.

The re-hire verdict itself is derived and never set by hand. The applicant's `outstanding_clawback_cents` is the sum of `clawback_cents` across that applicant's tuition agreements in `TERMINATED_CLAWBACK`. The applicant's terminated hire records are ordered by `terminated_on` descending and, where those tie, by `created_at` descending — never by identifier — and the first of them is the latest separation. Active and rescinded hires are excluded from that list entirely, which is why a rescinded hire leaves eligibility untouched. The verdict is then read off an ordered list of branches, the first match winning: `ELIGIBLE` when there are no terminated hires at all and the outstanding clawback total is zero; `PERMANENTLY_INELIGIBLE` when the outstanding clawback total is greater than zero; `PERMANENTLY_INELIGIBLE` when the latest separation's `termination_type` is `INVOLUNTARY_SAFETY` or `ABANDONMENT`; `PERMANENTLY_INELIGIBLE` when the latest separation's `rehire_flag` is `NOT_ELIGIBLE`; `COOLDOWN` when the current logical-clock date is strictly earlier than the latest `terminated_on` advanced by the carrier's `rehire_cooldown_days`, so the cooldown expires on the very day that sum is reached; and `ELIGIBLE` otherwise. The verdict is recomputed on termination, on rescission, on the clawback cascade, on a clawback reversal, on every advance of the logical clock, and on every read of the applicant.

### 3.9. Training-School Partnerships and the Tuition Ledger

A `SchoolCoordinator` registers a tuition reimbursement agreement by naming the `applicant_id` whose CDL school tuition is being reimbursed, the partner `school_name`, the principal `tuition_total_cents`, which must be at least one cent, and the `commitment_months` of service required to fully retire that principal, which must be at least one month. The agreement opens in `PENDING` with every ledger figure at zero. Agreements may be listed and narrowed by `applicant_id` or by `status`.

Activation is accepted only from `PENDING` and only once the applicant holds at least one hire record in `ACTIVE` status — the carrier does not fund a school bill for somebody it has not employed. Activation runs the schedule in a pinned four-step order. Step one divides `tuition_total_cents` by `commitment_months` and takes the floor, giving `base_monthly_cents`, the principal retired per served month. Step two keys a retention tier off that base figure and off nothing else: a base of at least 20,000 cents — two hundred dollars a month — earns a bonus rate of zero percent, a base of at least 10,000 cents but below that earns five percent, and anything below 10,000 cents earns ten percent. The tier is deliberately keyed on `base_monthly_cents` and is never re-keyed on the larger figure step three produces, even though that larger figure can fall in a different tier; re-keying would be circular and yields a wrong answer. Step three sets `monthly_reimbursement_cents` to `base_monthly_cents` plus the bonus, where the bonus is `base_monthly_cents` multiplied by the tier rate and rounded half up to a whole cent. Step four sets `forgiven_cents` to `tuition_total_cents` minus the product of `base_monthly_cents` and `commitment_months` — the floor remainder the carrier writes off at activation — and opens `outstanding_cents` at `tuition_total_cents` minus `forgiven_cents`. The agreement moves `PENDING → ACTIVE`.

Each month of service is credited one at a time; a single accrual call credits exactly one month and carries the `service_month` it applies to. The call is refused when `months_served` already equals `commitment_months`, reporting `ERR_COMMITMENT_ALREADY_FULFILLED` — and that rule deliberately outranks the generic terminal check, so accruing against an already-fulfilled agreement reports the specific code rather than `ERR_ALREADY_TERMINAL`. On acceptance `months_served` increments by one; `reimbursed_cents` becomes `months_served` multiplied by `base_monthly_cents`, the principal retired so far; `bonus_paid_cents` becomes `months_served` multiplied by the difference between `monthly_reimbursement_cents` and `base_monthly_cents`, the retention incentive paid on top; and `outstanding_cents` becomes `tuition_total_cents` less `forgiven_cents`, less `reimbursed_cents`, less `clawback_cents`. When `months_served` reaches `commitment_months` the agreement moves `ACTIVE → FULFILLED` with `outstanding_cents` left at zero. All of these figures are integer cents and no rounding occurs at this stage, because `base_monthly_cents` is already whole.

Leaving early costs money. As the second hop of the termination cascade of §3.8, an `ACTIVE` agreement moves to `TERMINATED_CLAWBACK`: its entire `outstanding_cents` balance is moved into `clawback_cents` and `outstanding_cents` is set to zero. The third hop then recomputes the applicant's `outstanding_clawback_cents` and `rehire_eligibility`, and because a positive clawback total forces `PERMANENTLY_INELIGIBLE`, a driver who walks away from an unretired school bill is barred from re-hire until that bill is settled.

Reversing a clawback is the compensating inverse and belongs to the `SchoolCoordinator`, carrying a `reason`. It is accepted only on an agreement in `TERMINATED_CLAWBACK` whose `clawback_cents` is greater than zero; an agreement with nothing to reverse reports `ERR_NO_CLAWBACK_TO_REVERSE`, and that rule deliberately outranks the generic origin-state check so a reversal attempted against an `ACTIVE` agreement with no clawback reports the specific code rather than `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. The reversal moves the whole `clawback_cents` balance back into `outstanding_cents`, sets `clawback_cents` to zero, returns the agreement `TERMINATED_CLAWBACK → ACTIVE`, and recomputes the applicant's `outstanding_clawback_cents` and `rehire_eligibility` — which is how a driver's re-hire eligibility is restored once the school bill is resolved.

One identity holds after every one of those three operations without exception: `tuition_total_cents` always equals `reimbursed_cents` plus `clawback_cents` plus `outstanding_cents` plus `forgiven_cents`. The figure `bonus_paid_cents` sits deliberately outside that identity — it is carrier retention money paid on top of the principal, not part of the tuition being retired — and it must never be included when the identity is checked.
