# Dairy Digester RNG API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A dairy anaerobic digestion project sits at the intersection of four businesses that rarely share a system of record. Upstream, a handful of dairies contract to truck manure to a central digester on commercial terms that are negotiated, countered and revised before anyone signs. Inside the plant, an operations team must hold a living biological reactor inside a narrow temperature and loading envelope, then scrub hydrogen sulfide, water and carbon dioxide out of the raw biogas until it is clean enough for a utility to accept. Downstream, the same molecules become two different products at once: pipeline-quality gas sold as a commodity, and environmental credits generated under low carbon fuel and renewable fuel programs whose value depends entirely on a certified carbon intensity score that an accredited third party re-measures every year. What is left over — separated cake and liquid digestate — goes back onto dairy cropland under an agronomic nitrogen budget that regulators police field by field.

This document specifies the API that runs that project as one coherent record. The system's central design commitment is that nothing is recorded twice and nothing is allowed to drift. A truckload of manure is weighed once and that single measurement propagates into the day's organic loading rate, into the biological stability score that decides whether the digester keeps accepting feedstock, into the volatile-solids tonnage that determines avoided methane in the carbon intensity calculation, and into the mass balance that must close to zero after every separation and every compensating reversal. A credit batch that is issued and later reversed must leave the pathway's credit ledger exactly where it started. A settlement statement that pays dairy partners must allocate every last cent, with none created and none lost.

The second commitment is that authority is divided. Seven distinct roles operate the platform, and the boundaries between them are enforced, not advisory: the accredited verifier who checks a credit batch may never be the administrator who issues it, and the finance controller who approves a settlement statement may never be the one who releases its payment. Layered on top of that is a pair of independent risk postures on every facility — one biological, one financial — that can gate operations and distributions separately, and that only a platform administrator may deliberately soften.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

This section defines the contract-level rules that apply across every endpoint. All resource paths are rooted directly at the service origin; there is no shared URL prefix.

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to the API must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, declaring the capacity in which that principal is acting. The platform recognises exactly seven roles: `PlatformAdmin`, `ProjectOperator`, `DairyPartner`, `PipelineScheduler`, `CreditVerifier`, `ProgramAdministrator` and `FinanceController`. If either header is absent, or if `X-User-ID` is present but blank or whitespace-only, or if the value of `X-User-Role` is not one of those seven exact strings, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). If both headers are well-formed but the declared role is not among the roles authorised for the requested operation, the request is rejected with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE` (HTTP 403). The role check is evaluated before any resource is looked up, so an unauthorised role acting on a non-existent resource receives the permission error rather than a not-found error.

The roles carry different relationships between `X-User-ID` and the persisted data. When `X-User-Role` is `DairyPartner`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Dairy`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Dairy` compare that resource's dairy foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, a `DairyPartner` countering or signing a feedstock agreement must present an `X-User-ID` equal to that agreement's `dairy_id`; a mismatch is rejected with `ERR_NOT_OWNING_DAIRY` (HTTP 403). For each of the other six roles — `PlatformAdmin`, `ProjectOperator`, `PipelineScheduler`, `CreditVerifier`, `ProgramAdministrator` and `FinanceController` — `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server may record it (for example, as `approved_by_user_id` and `paid_by_user_id` on a `RevenueStatement`, which are written from the acting `FinanceController`'s `X-User-ID`, and as the retained identity of the `CreditVerifier` who verified a credit batch) but it never compares such an identifier to any entity's `id`.

Role authority is deliberately partitioned. A `PlatformAdmin` registers master data — dairies and facilities — moves the logical clock and grants posture overrides. A `ProjectOperator` runs the plant: it drafts, offers, revises and status-changes feedstock agreements, books and rejects manure loads, opens, closes, reopens, conditions and separates operations days, plans, applies and voids nutrient applications, and opens remediation rounds in response to audit findings. A `DairyPartner` may only counter and sign agreements naming its own dairy, plus read its own records. A `PipelineScheduler` owns interconnection agreements and the whole nomination lifecycle. A `CreditVerifier` opens and runs annual verification audits and verifies credit batches, but may never issue them. A `ProgramAdministrator` owns credit pathways and the batch registry — creating, claiming, issuing and reversing batches, and making certification decisions — but may never verify a batch. A `FinanceController` funds the debt-service reserve, produces settlement statements, approves them, pays them and claws them back. Read access is broad but not universal: the facility financial summary is readable only by `PlatformAdmin`, `ProjectOperator` and `FinanceController`, while the pathway credit ledger is readable only by `ProgramAdministrator`, `CreditVerifier` and `FinanceController`. The precise authorisation set for every operation is tabulated in §4.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_` followed by exactly twenty characters drawn from the uppercase letters `A`–`Z` and the digits `0`–`9`, giving a twenty-four-character string in total. It is freshly generated per response and is never reused or echoed back from the request.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.
- Failures that never reach an operation at all use the same envelope: a path that matches no route in §4 returns `ERR_NOT_FOUND` with HTTP 404, a path that exists under a different verb returns `ERR_METHOD_NOT_ALLOWED` with HTTP 405, and any unanticipated server-side failure returns `ERR_INTERNAL` with HTTP 500. These three are the only error identifiers outside the §5 catalogue, and because they are decided by routing they are not subject to the §2.9 ladder — they precede even the authentication check, so an unrouted path is a 404 regardless of the headers presented. A trailing slash is not significant: `/dairies` and `/dairies/` route identically.

### 2.3. Pagination and List Filters

List endpoints are paginated through two query parameters. The `limit` parameter caps the number of records returned; when omitted it defaults to 10, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. The `offset` parameter is the number of records to skip and defaults to 0. Every list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside the `meta` object of the envelope; `has_more` is true exactly when `offset` plus the number of records actually returned in this page is less than `total_count`. A `limit` outside the 1-to-100 band, a negative `offset`, or either parameter supplied as something that is not an integer, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400); an empty-string value for either parameter falls back to its default. Unless a specific endpoint says otherwise, list results are sorted by `created_at` in ascending order. Because `created_at` is published only to millisecond precision, two rows written inside the same millisecond can share the identical `created_at` string; wherever this specification orders anything by `created_at` — list responses, cascade sweeps, the dairy share allocation's tie-break, the driving-statement selection of §3.9 — such ties are resolved by insertion order, the row created first sorting first. Ordering never consults `id`.

Filters are additive and optional; supplying none returns the whole collection under the pagination window. Each filter is an exact string comparison against the field as it appears in the serialised resource — so derived fields such as a dairy's `status` filter exactly as they read — and a filter supplied as an empty string is ignored rather than treated as a value to match. A record whose filtered field is null matches no filter value at all, so filtering `superseded_by_batch_id` or `superseded_by_statement_id` returns only records that actually carry a superseding identifier. Filtering is applied before the pagination window is cut, so `total_count` counts the filtered collection. Dairies may be filtered by `status` and `county`. Facilities may be filtered by `ops_posture` and by `distribution_posture` independently. Feedstock agreements accept `dairy_id`, `facility_id` and `status`. Deliveries accept `agreement_id`, `ops_day_id` and `status`. Operations days accept `facility_id`, `status`, `gas_state` and `ops_date`. Interconnection agreements accept `facility_id` and `status`. Nominations accept `interconnect_id`, `ops_day_id` and `status`. Credit pathways accept `facility_id`, `program` and `status`. Credit batches accept `pathway_id`, `facility_id`, `status` and `superseded_by_batch_id`. Verification audits accept `pathway_id` and `status`. Nutrient applications accept `dairy_id`, `facility_id`, `ops_day_id`, `status` and `land_unit_code`. Revenue statements accept `facility_id`, `status` and `superseded_by_statement_id`.

### 2.4. Common Resource Properties and Version Semantics

Every resource in this system — `Dairy`, `DigesterFacility`, `FeedstockAgreement`, `FeedstockDelivery`, `OperationsDay`, `InterconnectAgreement`, `InjectionNomination`, `CreditPathway`, `CreditBatch`, `VerificationAudit`, `NutrientApplication` and `RevenueStatement` — carries the same four system-managed properties:

- **`id`** (string): a server-minted UUID. There is no type prefix and no domain-flavoured encoding; clients must treat it as opaque.
- **`created_at`** (string): an ISO 8601 timestamp in UTC with three fractional digits, e.g. `2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z`, recorded when the row was first written.
- **`updated_at`** (string): an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, refreshed on every mutation of the row.
- **`version`** (integer): a monotonically increasing counter that increments by one on every mutation of the row.

No entity in this system is exempt: all twelve resource types carry all four properties, so `updated_at` and `version` are always present.

The version counter is not decorative. Derived fields count as mutations: when a delivery rejection recomputes an operations day's `organic_loading_rate`, that day's `version` increments and its `updated_at` refreshes even though no client touched the day directly. When one operation cascades into several rows, each affected row's `version` increments independently. The subsections below and the workflow narratives in §3 name, for each transition, exactly which rows are bumped.

Free-text notes and reasons are, by contrast, write-only. Every `reason`, `memo`, `justification`, `cover_note`, `comment`, `signatory_name`, `claim_note`, `verifier_note`, `approval_note`, `closing_note`, `narrative`, `remediation_plan`, `void_reason`, `claw_back_reason`, `applicator_name`, `issuance_reference` and `payment_reference` named in §3 is validated exactly as described there — mandatory ones rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` when missing or not a string — and then discarded: it is written to no field of any resource and appears in no response body. The sole exception is a credit batch's `reversal_reason`, which is persisted on the batch and returned in its representation.

### 2.5. Numeric Precision, Rounding and Units

Unless a rule states otherwise, every rounding operation in this specification uses the round-half-up convention. Each quantity has a fixed scale that is part of the contract:

- Monetary amounts are integers denominated in cents. There is no sub-cent representation anywhere in the system; every step of the revenue waterfall rounds to a whole cent before the next step consumes it, and no intermediate value is carried at sub-cent precision into a later step. The single exception to half-up rounding on money is the per-line dairy share, which floors (see §3.9).
- Tonnages — `gross_tonnes`, `tare_tonnes`, `net_tonnes`, `vs_tonnes`, `committed_tonnes_per_day`, `hydraulic_capacity_tonnes_per_day`, the digestate production, applied and on-hand tonnages, `digestate_solids_tonnes`, `digestate_liquid_tonnes`, `bedding_recovered_tonnes`, `solids_tonnes_applied`, `liquid_tonnes_applied` and the audited volatile-solids actuals — carry 3 decimal places.
- `haul_tonne_km` and `actual_haul_tonne_km` carry 2 decimal places; `haul_distance_km` carries 1 decimal place.
- Percentage fields — `total_solids_pct`, `volatile_solids_pct_of_ts`, `minimum_total_solids_pct`, `raw_ch4_pct`, `raw_co2_pct`, `treated_ch4_pct`, `treated_co2_pct`, `max_co2_pct`, `solids_capture_pct`, `bedding_recovery_pct` — carry 1 decimal place, as do the concentration readings `raw_h2s_ppm`, `treated_h2s_ppm`, `max_h2s_ppm`, `raw_moisture_lb_per_mmscf`, `treated_moisture_lb_per_mmscf`, `max_moisture_lb_per_mmscf`, `vfa_mg_per_l`, `alkalinity_mg_per_l` and `lab_total_nitrogen_mg_per_l`.
- Temperatures `target_temperature_c` and `avg_digester_temp_c` carry 1 decimal place; `temp_excursion_minutes` and `window_minutes` are whole minutes; `ramp_buffer_minutes` and `nomination_lead_hours` are whole integers.
- Heating values `raw_hhv_btu_per_scf`, `pipeline_hhv_btu_per_scf` and `min_hhv_btu_per_scf` carry 2 decimal places. Gas volumes `raw_biogas_scf` and `pipeline_gas_scf` and energies `raw_gas_mmbtu`, `pipeline_gas_mmbtu`, `nominated_mmbtu`, `confirmed_mmbtu`, `delivered_mmbtu`, `hourly_rate_mmbtu`, `max_hourly_injection_mmbtu` and `actual_pipeline_mmbtu` carry 3 decimal places.
- Carbon intensity values — `certified_ci_score`, `verified_ci_score`, `ci_adjustment`, `ci_score_used`, `reported_ci_score`, `ci_delta` and `actual_process_g_per_mj` — carry 2 decimal places, expressed in grams of carbon dioxide equivalent per megajoule.
- `design_max_olr`, `organic_loading_rate` and `vfa_alkalinity_ratio` carry 3 decimal places; the loading rate is expressed in kilograms of volatile solids per cubic metre of working digester volume per day. `dscr_minimum`, `dscr_current` and `dscr_at_close` carry 3 decimal places.
- `revenue_share_pct` and `management_fee_rate` are decimal fractions with 4 decimal places, not percentages: a 12.5 percent share is stored as 0.1250. `credit_quantity_provisional` and `credit_quantity_final` and the pathway credit totals carry 3 decimal places. `annual_nitrogen_budget_kg`, `pan_applied_ytd_kg`, `liquid_total_nitrogen_kg`, `solids_total_nitrogen_kg` and `plant_available_nitrogen_kg` carry 3 decimal places.
- `herd_size`, `active_agreement_count`, `contracted_agreement_count`, `negotiation_round`, `remediation_round`, `conditioning_attempts`, `reopen_count`, `curtailment_count`, `stress_points` and `audit_year` are integers.

Requests that supply more decimal places than a field's declared scale, or that violate a field's numeric constraint, are rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400).

A declared scale governs the precision to which a value is computed, rounded and stored; it does not govern how that value is rendered. Every non-monetary quantity is emitted as an ordinary JSON number rather than a string, so trailing zeros are not preserved on the wire: a `design_max_olr` of 3.500 appears as `3.5`, a residual of 0.000 appears as `0.0`, and a `dscr_at_close` of 1.250 appears as `1.25`. Monetary fields, being integer cents, are emitted as JSON integers. A field that is null is emitted as JSON `null`.

### 2.6. The Logical Clock

The server does not read wall-clock time for business decisions. It maintains a logical clock which, before any client repositions it, reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and which never falls back to real time. A `PlatformAdmin` repositions it by supplying a `to_iso8601` instant to an administrative test-driving capability. Three behaviours read that clock: the injection nomination lead-time check, the `signed_at` stamp written when a dairy signs its agreement, and the calendar-year window used to aggregate a dairy's year-to-date plant-available nitrogen. The calendar year in question is always the year of the logical clock, not the year of any request.

### 2.7. Stale-Version Guards on Parent Resources

Two creation operations depend on a parent row whose state may have moved under the client's feet, and both are guarded by optimistic concurrency control on the parent's `version`.

Creating a `CreditBatch` requires `expected_pathway_version` as a mandatory, non-optional field in the request body, carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `CreditPathway`. If that value does not equal the pathway's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). The guard matters because a new batch legitimately drives any overlapping `DRAFT` batch on the same pathway to `SUPERSEDED`; without the guard, a writer holding an outdated view of the pathway would silently retire a batch it never saw.

Creating a `RevenueStatement` requires `expected_facility_version` as a mandatory, non-optional field in the request body, carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `DigesterFacility`. A mismatch with the facility's current `version` is rejected with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Here the guard protects two things at once: a new statement legitimately supersedes any overlapping `DRAFT` statement on the same facility, and the whole distribution waterfall is computed against the facility's `reserve_balance_cents`, `reserve_target_cents` and `distribution_posture` as the client read them.

No other endpoint accepts a version field. Operations not named above are processed without any concurrency check; supplying an unexpected version field to them is a schema violation.

### 2.8. Two Conserved Quantities

The system enforces two arithmetic invariants that must hold after every mutation, including after every compensating reversal.

The **mass-control invariant** lives on `DigesterFacility`. `total_manure_received_tonnes` is the sum of `net_tonnes` over every `ACCEPTED` delivery on every operations day of the facility. `unseparated_manure_tonnes` is the same sum restricted to days whose `separation_recorded` is still false. `digestate_solids_produced_tonnes` and `digestate_liquid_produced_tonnes` are the sums of `digestate_solids_tonnes` and `digestate_liquid_tonnes` over days whose `separation_recorded` is true. The conserved quantity `mass_control_residual_tonnes` is the total received less the unseparated portion less the solids produced less the liquid produced, rounded to 3 decimal places, and it must always equal exactly 0.000. Booking a load, rejecting a load, cancelling a load by cascade, recording a separation and reopening a day all recompute these five figures, and each must leave the residual at zero.

The **credit-control invariant** lives on `CreditPathway`. `credits_issued_total` sums `credit_quantity_final` over every batch of the pathway that has ever reached `ISSUED`. `credits_reversed_total` sums the same field over batches currently `REVERSED`, and `credits_outstanding` sums it over batches currently `ISSUED`. Batches in `DRAFT`, `CLAIMED`, `VERIFIED` or `SUPERSEDED` contribute nothing to any of the three. The conserved quantity `credit_control_residual` is the issued total less the reversed total less the outstanding total, rounded to 3 decimal places, and it must always equal exactly 0.000 — after an issuance, after its compensating reversal, after a pathway revocation, and after an audit finding rewrites `credit_quantity_final` on batches that are already issued.

Two computed read operations exist specifically to expose these invariants: a facility financial summary that recomputes the mass-control figures before responding and also surfaces `gross_revenue_to_date_cents`, `dairy_share_paid_cents` and `retained_to_date_cents`, and a pathway credit ledger that recomputes the credit-control figures before responding. Each returns the complete resource representation — the whole `DigesterFacility` object and the whole `CreditPathway` object respectively, identical in shape to the corresponding single-resource read — rather than a reduced projection of just the invariant fields.

### 2.9. Request Validation Order

When more than one rule could reject a request, the outcome is determined by a single global priority ladder. Lower-numbered checks are evaluated first and their error is what the client sees; a later check never masks an earlier one.

1. **Authentication** — headers present and role recognised: `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (401).
2. **Authorisation** — the acting role appears in the operation's role list: `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE` (403).
3. **Path resolution** — every identifier embedded in the URL is resolved strictly left to right, and the first one that does not exist produces its own not-found error: `ERR_DAIRY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FACILITY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DELIVERY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_OPS_DAY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INTERCONNECT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_NOMINATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PATHWAY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_BATCH_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AUDIT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_STATEMENT_NOT_FOUND`, all HTTP 404.
4. **Body reference resolution** — on creation operations, every foreign key supplied in the request body *as a non-empty string* is resolved in the exact order the fields are listed for that operation, yielding `ERR_DAIRY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FACILITY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_OPS_DAY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INTERCONNECT_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_PATHWAY_NOT_FOUND` (404). A foreign-key field that is absent, null, blank or not a string is not resolved at this rank at all; it falls through to the schema check at rank 6. Consequently, when one foreign key is missing and a later one names a row that does not exist, the client sees the later field's not-found error rather than `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.
5. **Dairy ownership** — a `DairyPartner` countering or signing an agreement must own it: `ERR_NOT_OWNING_DAIRY` (403).
6. **Schema conformance** — required fields present, types correct, numeric ranges honoured, enum members valid, decimal scale not exceeded: `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400). An absent or whitespace-only request body is read as an empty JSON object, so an operation with mandatory fields reports them missing rather than reporting an unparseable body; a body that is present but is not valid JSON is itself `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. Numeric literals are decoded as exact decimals, so the number of decimal places a client writes is exactly the number the scale check sees. Properties the operation does not name are ignored rather than rejected, the two version fields of §2.7 being the sole exception. Typing is strict and an explicit JSON `null` counts as absence, so a mandatory field supplied as `null` is reported missing while an optional one supplied as `null` takes its stated default: string fields must be strings and must not be blank or whitespace-only, boolean fields must be JSON booleans rather than strings or numbers, integer fields must be JSON integers rather than decimals or numeric strings, enum fields must be one of the listed members exactly, date fields must be exactly ten characters in `YYYY-MM-DD` form naming a real calendar date, and numeric fields must be finite JSON numbers. A body that is a JSON array or scalar rather than an object is itself `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.
7. **Range coherence** — every supplied date range satisfies start on or before end, and every injection window satisfies `window_start_utc` strictly before `window_end_utc`: `ERR_INVALID_DATE_RANGE` (422). This applies to agreement drafting, nomination creation, credit batch creation, nutrient application planning, revenue statement creation and pathway certification decisions.
8. **Stale parent version** — `expected_pathway_version` on credit batch creation and `expected_facility_version` on revenue statement creation: `ERR_STALE_VERSION` (412).
9. **Generic state check** — the target resource is in a from-state that declares the requested transition: `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (409). This is the fallback for every action operation that has no more specific state error.
10. **Specific state checks**, all evaluated at the same rank: `ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_ACTIVE`, `ERR_PATHWAY_NOT_CERTIFIED`, `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_INTERCONNECT`, `ERR_GAS_NOT_PIPELINE_SPEC`, `ERR_GAS_NOT_CONDITIONABLE`, `ERR_SEPARATION_ALREADY_RECORDED` and `ERR_SEPARATION_NOT_RECORDED` (all 409). Every one of these specific errors concerns something other than the transition target's own `status` — a related resource's state, or a non-`status` flag on the target — and each operation tests its own target's from-state first. So when the target itself is in a from-state that does not declare the requested transition, `ERR_INVALID_STATE` is what the client sees even if one of these specific errors would also have fired.
11. **Distribution lock** — `ERR_DISTRIBUTION_LOCKED` (409) on statement approval and statement payment.
12. **Segregation of duties** — `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (403) on statement payment and batch issuance. Note that this identity check deliberately sits *below* the state and lock checks: a statement that is not `APPROVED`, or whose facility is `LOCKED`, fails on those grounds first even when the payer is also the approver.
13. **Round and attempt ceilings** — `ERR_NEGOTIATION_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`, `ERR_REMEDIATION_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`, `ERR_CONDITIONING_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED` and `ERR_REOPEN_LIMIT_REACHED` (all 422).
14. **Operations day uniqueness** — `ERR_OPS_DAY_EXISTS` (409).
15. **Biological upset safety gate** — `ERR_FACILITY_UPSET` (422).
16. **Agreement term window** — `ERR_OUTSIDE_AGREEMENT_TERM` (422).
17. **Feedstock quality floor** — `ERR_SOLIDS_BELOW_MINIMUM` (422).
18. **Nomination lead time** — `ERR_NOMINATION_LEAD_TIME` (422).
19. **Interval conflicts** — `ERR_INJECTION_WINDOW_OVERLAP` and `ERR_LAND_UNIT_SETBACK_CONFLICT` (both 409), at the same rank.
20. **Referential and period-overlap rules** — `ERR_FACILITY_PATHWAY_MISMATCH` (422), `ERR_CREDIT_PERIOD_OVERLAP` (409) and `ERR_STATEMENT_PERIOD_OVERLAP` (409), at the same rank.
21. **Nomination volume ceiling** — `ERR_NOMINATION_EXCEEDS_PRODUCTION` (422).
22. **Hourly injection capacity** — `ERR_HOURLY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (422).
23. **Capacity and sufficiency rules**, at the same rank: `ERR_HYDRAULIC_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`, `ERR_NO_DELIVERED_VOLUME` and `ERR_DIGESTATE_INVENTORY_INSUFFICIENT` (all 422).
24. **Budget ceilings**, at the same rank: `ERR_NITROGEN_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` and `ERR_SHARE_EXCEEDS_DISTRIBUTABLE` (both 422).
25. **Restricted distribution cap** — `ERR_DISTRIBUTION_RESTRICTED` (422).
26. **Delivered volume ceiling** — `ERR_DELIVERY_EXCEEDS_CONFIRMED` (422).
27. **Reserve funding ceiling** — `ERR_RESERVE_TARGET_EXCEEDED` (422).

### 2.10. The Two Facility Postures

Every `DigesterFacility` carries two posture dimensions that evolve entirely independently of one another on the same row. A facility can be in biological upset while its distributions remain wide open, and it can be financially locked while the digester runs perfectly.

The **operational posture** `ops_posture` takes the values `STABLE`, `WATCH`, `RESTRICTED` and `UPSET`, starts at `STABLE`, and is driven only by the outcome of closing an operations day — never by financing state. The **distribution posture** `distribution_posture` takes the values `OPEN`, `RESTRICTED` and `LOCKED`, starts at `OPEN`, and is driven only by recomputation of the debt-service coverage ratio — never by biological state.

A single boolean, `posture_override_active`, mediates both. It starts false. A `PlatformAdmin` sets it true by exercising an override, supplying a `dimension` of either `OPS` or `DISTRIBUTION` together with a mandatory `justification`. An `OPS` override moves `ops_posture` from `UPSET` to `RESTRICTED`; a `DISTRIBUTION` override moves `distribution_posture` from `LOCKED` to `RESTRICTED`. An override whose named dimension is not sitting in that exact from-state — an `OPS` override on a facility whose `ops_posture` is anything but `UPSET`, or a `DISTRIBUTION` override on a facility whose `distribution_posture` is anything but `LOCKED` — is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (HTTP 409), the dimension enum and the `justification` having been schema-checked first. Once the flag is true, a later coverage-ratio recomputation that would otherwise produce `LOCKED` produces `RESTRICTED` instead. The flag is cleared back to false in two ways: when an operations day closes `STABLE` and lifts `ops_posture` from `RESTRICTED` back to `STABLE`, and when a coverage-ratio recomputation actually moves `distribution_posture` to `OPEN` from some other value — a recomputation that leaves an already-`OPEN` posture where it was clears nothing. Either override increments the facility's `version`.

### 2.11. Segregation of Duties

Two decisions in this system may never be made twice by the same person. Releasing payment on a settlement statement requires that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from the `approved_by_user_id` already recorded on that statement. Issuing a credit batch requires that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from the `X-User-ID` that verified that same batch. Either violation is rejected with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). The role partition reinforces this at a second level: verification is exclusive to `CreditVerifier` while issuance is exclusive to `ProgramAdministrator`, so the two acts cannot even be attempted under one role.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Master Data: Dairies and Digester Facilities

A `PlatformAdmin` registers each manure supplier as a `Dairy`, supplying its legal `name`, the `county` it operates in, its milking `herd_size` (which must be greater than zero), the `manure_management_baseline` describing how manure was handled before the project existed, the one-way `haul_distance_km` from the dairy to the digester (which may be zero for an on-site dairy but never negative), and the `annual_nitrogen_budget_kg` that caps plant-available nitrogen across all of that dairy's land units in a calendar year (which must be greater than zero). The baseline is one of `LAGOON`, `SOLID_STORAGE` or `DAILY_SPREAD`, and it is far more than descriptive: it selects the methane conversion factor that governs how much avoided methane the project may claim for that dairy's manure, so a lagoon-baseline dairy is worth substantially more to the carbon intensity calculation than a daily-spread one.

A newly registered dairy begins as `PROSPECT` with `active_agreement_count` at 0, `contracted_agreement_count` at 0 and `pan_applied_ytd_kg` at 0.000. Its `status` is never set directly by a client; it is re-derived from those two counters after every agreement mutation. `active_agreement_count` is the number of feedstock agreements naming this dairy whose status is `ACTIVE`; `contracted_agreement_count` is the number whose status is either `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`. Whenever the active count rises to one or more, a `PROSPECT` dairy becomes `CONTRACTED` and a `SUSPENDED` dairy returns to `CONTRACTED`. When the active count falls to zero while the contracted count is still one or more, a `CONTRACTED` dairy becomes `SUSPENDED`. When the contracted count falls to zero, the dairy returns to `PROSPECT` from either `CONTRACTED` or `SUSPENDED`. Each of these five re-derivations increments the dairy's `version` by one.

Facility registration is also a `PlatformAdmin` act. A `DigesterFacility` is created with a `name`, the working `digester_volume_m3` used as the denominator of the loading-rate calculation (greater than zero), the `hydraulic_capacity_tonnes_per_day` ceiling on wet feedstock accepted per operations day (greater than zero), the `thermal_regime`, the set-point `target_temperature_c`, and three financing inputs: the `reserve_target_cents` funding target for the debt-service reserve account (zero or more), the `annual_debt_service_cents` scheduled principal plus interest that forms the coverage-ratio denominator (greater than zero), and the covenant floor `dscr_minimum` (greater than zero). The regime is either `MESOPHILIC` or `THERMOPHILIC`, and it alone fixes the derived design ceiling `design_max_olr`: a mesophilic digester is assigned a design ceiling of 3.500 kilograms of volatile solids per cubic metre per day, a thermophilic digester 4.500, in both cases stored to 3 decimal places. Creation also sets `ops_posture` to `STABLE`, `distribution_posture` to `OPEN`, `posture_override_active` to false, `reserve_balance_cents` to 0, `dscr_current` to 0.000, and all of the mass-control and revenue-ledger accumulators — `total_manure_received_tonnes`, `unseparated_manure_tonnes`, `digestate_solids_produced_tonnes`, `digestate_liquid_produced_tonnes`, `digestate_solids_applied_tonnes`, `digestate_liquid_applied_tonnes`, `digestate_solids_on_hand_tonnes`, `digestate_liquid_on_hand_tonnes`, `mass_control_residual_tonnes`, `gross_revenue_to_date_cents`, `dairy_share_paid_cents` and `retained_to_date_cents` — to zero. The creation body is schema-validated in the order the fields were just named, and two of those fields are looser than the rest: `digester_volume_m3` is the one quantity in the system with no fixed decimal scale, so any strictly positive decimal is accepted however many places it carries, and `target_temperature_c` carries 1 decimal place with no range constraint at all, so a sub-zero set point is accepted.

Equity may be injected into the reserve account at any time by a `FinanceController`, who supplies a positive `amount_cents` and an optional `memo`. The funding is accepted only if `reserve_balance_cents` plus `amount_cents` would not exceed `reserve_target_cents`; a request that would overfund the account, or one whose `amount_cents` is not strictly positive, is rejected with `ERR_RESERVE_TARGET_EXCEEDED`. A successful funding raises `reserve_balance_cents` by `amount_cents` and increments the facility's `version` by one.

### 3.2. Contracting Manure Supply

A `ProjectOperator` drafts a `FeedstockAgreement` by naming the supplying dairy through `dairy_id` and the receiving digester through `facility_id` — resolved in that order — and stating the commercial terms: `committed_tonnes_per_day` of wet manure (greater than zero), the `minimum_total_solids_pct` acceptable for delivered loads (greater than zero and less than 100), the inclusive `term_start_date` and `term_end_date` bounding the dates on which loads may be received, the `revenue_share_pct` payable to the dairy as a decimal fraction (greater than zero and at most 0.95), the `revenue_share_basis` fixing whether that fraction applies to the statement's net distributable amount (`NET`) or its gross revenue (`GROSS`), and the boolean `curtailable` that decides whether this agreement is swept into suspension when the digester enters biological upset. A drafted agreement begins in `DRAFT` with `negotiation_round` at 0, `countered_tonnes_per_day` and `countered_revenue_share_pct` null, `suspension_reason` null and `signed_at` null.

Negotiation proceeds as a bounded cycle. Offering the agreement moves it `DRAFT → PENDING_DAIRY_SIGNATURE`; the acting `ProjectOperator` may attach an optional `cover_note`, and the transition is permitted only when the agreement is in `DRAFT` and the named dairy's `status` is either `PROSPECT` or `CONTRACTED` — a `SUSPENDED` dairy cannot be offered new business; a violation of either condition is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. The transition increments the agreement's `version` by one and touches nothing else. From `PENDING_DAIRY_SIGNATURE` the dairy chooses. Countering moves the agreement `PENDING_DAIRY_SIGNATURE → COUNTERED`: the `DairyPartner` submits `countered_tonnes_per_day` (greater than zero, 3 decimal places) and `countered_revenue_share_pct` (greater than zero, 4 decimal places, and deliberately *not* capped at 0.95 the way the operator's own `revenue_share_pct` is — a dairy may counter with any positive fraction), optionally with a `comment`, and those two values are stored on the agreement while `negotiation_round` increments by one and the agreement's `version` increments by one. A counter is permitted only while `negotiation_round` is still below three; a fourth counter attempt on an agreement whose `negotiation_round` has already reached three is rejected with `ERR_NEGOTIATION_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`. Revising closes the cycle, moving the agreement `COUNTERED → DRAFT`: the `ProjectOperator` supplies fresh values for `committed_tonnes_per_day`, `minimum_total_solids_pct` and `revenue_share_pct` — all three mandatory and each bounded exactly as it was at drafting, `revenue_share_pct` included, so a revision may not exceed 0.95 even to meet a dairy's counter — which overwrite the previous terms, while `countered_tonnes_per_day` and `countered_revenue_share_pct` are cleared back to null and the agreement's `version` increments by one. From `DRAFT` the agreement can be offered again, and the loop may run at most three times.

Signing is the other exit from `PENDING_DAIRY_SIGNATURE`. A `DairyPartner` acting for the named dairy supplies a `signatory_name`, and the agreement moves `PENDING_DAIRY_SIGNATURE → ACTIVE`. Signing stamps `signed_at` from the logical clock, increments both `active_agreement_count` and `contracted_agreement_count` on the dairy by one, and — because the active count has risen to at least one — re-derives the dairy's `status` from `PROSPECT` to `CONTRACTED`. Both the agreement's `version` and the dairy's `version` increment by one.

Once active, an agreement's service state is managed through a single status-change capability available to a `ProjectOperator`, which takes an `action` of `SUSPEND`, `REINSTATE` or `TERMINATE` and an optional `reason`. Each has a distinct footprint.

An operator-initiated `SUSPEND` moves the agreement `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` and sets `suspension_reason` to `COMPLIANCE`. It decrements the dairy's `active_agreement_count` by one and, when that count has thereby reached zero, moves the dairy `CONTRACTED → SUSPENDED`. It then sweeps the agreement's open bookings: every `FeedstockDelivery` booked against this agreement that is still `ACCEPTED` and whose operations day is still `OPEN` moves `ACCEPTED → CANCELLED` with `rejection_reason` set to `AGREEMENT_SUSPENDED`. Because those loads have left the accepted set, each affected operations day's `organic_loading_rate` is recomputed, and the facility's mass-control figures are recomputed and must remain at a residual of 0.000. The agreement's `version`, the dairy's `version` and each affected operations day's `version` all increment by one. Deliveries sitting on an already-`CLOSED` day are deliberately left alone, because that day's loading rate is already baked into the stability assessment that closed it.

`REINSTATE` moves the agreement `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`, and is permitted only while the facility's `ops_posture` is not `UPSET` — an operator cannot quietly restore supply into a digester that is still in upset; a reinstatement attempted into an upset facility is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, not with `ERR_FACILITY_UPSET`, because the reinstatement gate is a plain state check on related rows rather than the intake safety gate of §2.9 rank 15. Reinstatement clears `suspension_reason` back to null, increments the dairy's `active_agreement_count` by one and, because that count has risen to at least one, moves the dairy `SUSPENDED → CONTRACTED`. The agreement's `version` and the dairy's `version` each increment by one.

`TERMINATE` is accepted from either `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` and moves the agreement to the terminal `TERMINATED` state. When the prior status was `ACTIVE`, termination decrements the dairy's `active_agreement_count` by one; from either prior status it decrements `contracted_agreement_count` by one, and the dairy's `status` is then re-derived from the resulting pair of counters by exactly the five rules of §3.1 — so it reaches `PROSPECT` only once the contracted count is genuinely zero, and a dairy still holding other agreements simply stays where those counters put it. Both the agreement's `version` and the dairy's `version` increment by one; no delivery is swept and no operations day is touched by a termination.

### 3.3. Daily Intake: Booking Loads and the Organic Loading Rate

Operations are organised around the `OperationsDay`. A `ProjectOperator` opens one by naming the `facility_id` and the calendar `ops_date`; the pair is unique, and a second attempt at the same facility and date is rejected with `ERR_OPS_DAY_EXISTS`. A fresh day begins with `status` `OPEN`, `gas_state` `NOT_PRODUCED`, `organic_loading_rate` 0.000, `separation_recorded` false, `conditioning_attempts` 0, `reopen_count` 0, `raw_biogas_scf` 0.000, `raw_gas_mmbtu` 0.000, `pipeline_gas_scf` 0.000, `pipeline_gas_mmbtu` 0.000, all three digestate tonnages at 0.000, and every monitoring and laboratory reading — `avg_digester_temp_c`, `temp_excursion_minutes`, `vfa_mg_per_l`, `alkalinity_mg_per_l`, `vfa_alkalinity_ratio`, `thermal_status`, `stability_status`, `raw_ch4_pct`, `raw_co2_pct`, `raw_h2s_ppm`, `raw_moisture_lb_per_mmscf`, `raw_hhv_btu_per_scf`, the four `treated_` readings, `pipeline_hhv_btu_per_scf`, `offspec_reason`, `solids_capture_pct`, `bedding_recovery_pct` and `lab_total_nitrogen_mg_per_l` — still null. The bookkeeping dimension `status` and the gas dimension `gas_state` then evolve independently of one another on the same row.

Booking a manure load creates a `FeedstockDelivery` in `ACCEPTED` status. The `ProjectOperator` supplies `agreement_id` and `ops_day_id`, resolved in that order, the `gross_tonnes` loaded weight (greater than zero), an optional `tare_tonnes` deduction that defaults to 0.000 when omitted and must be zero or more and strictly less than `gross_tonnes`, the measured `total_solids_pct` of the load (greater than zero and less than 100), and `volatile_solids_pct_of_ts`, the volatile fraction of those solids (greater than zero and at most 100). Booking is gated by five business rules in the order established in §2.9: the supplying agreement must be `ACTIVE`, or the request fails with `ERR_AGREEMENT_NOT_ACTIVE`; the receiving facility's `ops_posture` must not be `UPSET`, or the request fails with `ERR_FACILITY_UPSET`; the day's `ops_date` must fall inside the inclusive term bounded by the agreement's `term_start_date` and `term_end_date`, or the request fails with `ERR_OUTSIDE_AGREEMENT_TERM`; the measured `total_solids_pct` must be greater than or equal to the agreement's `minimum_total_solids_pct`, with exact equality accepted, or the request fails with `ERR_SOLIDS_BELOW_MINIMUM`; and finally the sum of `net_tonnes` across the day's already-`ACCEPTED` deliveries plus the new load's `net_tonnes` must be less than or equal to the facility's `hydraulic_capacity_tonnes_per_day`, with equality accepted, or the request fails with `ERR_HYDRAULIC_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. Two things booking deliberately does *not* consult: the receiving day's own `status`, so a load may legitimately be booked onto a day that is already `CLOSED` (only rejection of a load requires an `OPEN` day), and the agreement's own `facility_id`, which is never compared with the day's — the hydraulic ceiling, the `digester_volume_m3` denominator and the mass-control totals are all taken from the facility that owns the operations day, whatever facility the agreement names.

Three derived quantities are computed on every accepted load. `net_tonnes` is `gross_tonnes` less `tare_tonnes`, rounded to 3 decimal places. `vs_tonnes`, the volatile-solids mass, is `net_tonnes` multiplied by `total_solids_pct` divided by 100 and then multiplied by `volatile_solids_pct_of_ts` divided by 100, rounded to 3 decimal places. `haul_tonne_km` is `net_tonnes` multiplied by the supplying dairy's `haul_distance_km`, rounded to 2 decimal places; it is the sole activity measure for transport emissions later on.

The day's `organic_loading_rate` is then rebuilt from scratch. Only deliveries on that day whose `status` is `ACCEPTED` participate; `REJECTED` and `CANCELLED` loads are excluded entirely. The already-rounded per-delivery `vs_tonnes` values are summed — the rounding happens per delivery first and the sum is taken over those rounded values, never the other way round — the tonne total is converted to kilograms by multiplying by 1000, and that single kilogram figure is divided once by the facility's `digester_volume_m3`. The quotient, rounded to 3 decimal places, is `organic_loading_rate`. Booking a load also recomputes the facility's mass-control figures, and increments both the operations day's `version` and the facility's `version` by one.

A load may be repudiated after re-sampling. The `ProjectOperator` supplies a `rejection_reason` of `OFF_SPEC_SOLIDS`, `CONTAMINATION` or `SCALE_ERROR`, and the delivery moves `ACCEPTED → REJECTED`. Rejection is permitted only while the delivery is `ACCEPTED` and its operations day is still `OPEN`; both conditions are generic state checks reported as `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, the delivery's own status being tested first and the day's second. It removes that load's `net_tonnes` and `vs_tonnes` from the accepted set, so the day's `organic_loading_rate` is recomputed by exactly the rule above, and the facility's `total_manure_received_tonnes`, `unseparated_manure_tonnes` and `mass_control_residual_tonnes` are recomputed with the residual required to stay at 0.000. The delivery's `version`, the operations day's `version` and the facility's `version` each increment by one. The only other way a load leaves the accepted set is the cascade described in §3.2, which sets `rejection_reason` to `AGREEMENT_SUSPENDED` and moves the delivery `ACCEPTED → CANCELLED` with the same recomputation footprint. Both `REJECTED` and `CANCELLED` are terminal.

### 3.4. Closing the Day: Temperature, Biological Stability and Upset

Closing an operations day is the plant's daily reckoning. A `ProjectOperator` submits the day's process monitoring and raw gas analysis together: `avg_digester_temp_c`, the whole-minute count `temp_excursion_minutes` spent outside the target temperature band, the volatile fatty acid concentration `vfa_mg_per_l`, the total alkalinity `alkalinity_mg_per_l` as calcium carbonate, and five raw gas readings — `raw_biogas_scf`, `raw_ch4_pct`, `raw_co2_pct`, `raw_h2s_ppm` and `raw_moisture_lb_per_mmscf`. Closing is permitted only when the day's `status` is `OPEN`, at least one `ACCEPTED` delivery exists on the day, and the facility's `ops_posture` is not `UPSET` (otherwise `ERR_FACILITY_UPSET`); the first two are both generic state checks yielding `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, and they are evaluated in that order ahead of the upset gate, which sits lower on the ladder. The submitted readings carry their §2.5 scales plus these bounds: `avg_digester_temp_c` is unconstrained in range, `temp_excursion_minutes` is a whole number of zero or more, `alkalinity_mg_per_l` must be strictly positive because it is the divisor of the stress ratio, `vfa_mg_per_l`, `raw_biogas_scf`, `raw_h2s_ppm` and `raw_moisture_lb_per_mmscf` are zero or more, and `raw_ch4_pct` and `raw_co2_pct` lie between 0 and 100 inclusive. The day moves `OPEN → CLOSED` on the bookkeeping dimension and simultaneously `NOT_PRODUCED → RAW` on the gas dimension, the latter conditional on `raw_biogas_scf` being greater than zero. Entering `RAW` derives `raw_hhv_btu_per_scf` as `raw_ch4_pct` divided by 100 and multiplied by the higher heating value of pure methane, 1010.0 British thermal units per standard cubic foot, rounded to 2 decimal places, and `raw_gas_mmbtu` as `raw_biogas_scf` multiplied by `raw_hhv_btu_per_scf` and divided by one million, rounded to 3 decimal places. Closing runs the loading-rate calculation first and the stability assessment second, then increments the day's `version` and the facility's `version` by one.

The stability assessment has three steps and the first is entirely independent of loading. The temperature deviation is the absolute difference between the day's `avg_digester_temp_c` and the facility's `target_temperature_c`. `thermal_status` is `CRITICAL` when that deviation is greater than 3.0 degrees Celsius **or** `temp_excursion_minutes` is greater than 120 — that is, more than two hours out of band. Otherwise it is `EXCURSION` when the deviation is greater than 1.0 degree Celsius **or** `temp_excursion_minutes` is greater than 30, that is more than half an hour out of band. Otherwise it is `IN_BAND`.

Step two accumulates a stress score from three independent contributions, each worth zero, one or two points. The ratio `vfa_alkalinity_ratio` is `vfa_mg_per_l` divided by `alkalinity_mg_per_l`, rounded to 3 decimal places; it contributes two points when it is greater than or equal to 0.400, one point when it is greater than or equal to 0.300 but below 0.400, and zero points otherwise. Loading contributes two points when the day's `organic_loading_rate` is strictly greater than the facility's `design_max_olr` multiplied by 1.10 — a ten percent overload — one point when it is strictly greater than `design_max_olr` but not beyond that ten percent margin, and zero points when it is at or below the design ceiling. Temperature contributes two points for a `CRITICAL` `thermal_status`, one point for `EXCURSION` and zero for `IN_BAND`. The three contributions are added into `stress_points`, an integer that therefore ranges from 0 to 6.

Step three classifies. `stability_status` is `INHIBITED` when `stress_points` is greater than or equal to 4, `STRESSED` when it is greater than or equal to 2 but below 4, and `STABLE` when it is 0 or 1. Only the day being closed contributes; no other day's readings enter the assessment.

The classification then re-derives the facility's `ops_posture`. A close producing `STRESSED` moves the posture `STABLE → WATCH`. A close producing `STABLE` moves it `WATCH → STABLE`, and also `RESTRICTED → STABLE`, the latter additionally clearing `posture_override_active` back to false. A close producing `INHIBITED` moves the posture to `UPSET` from either `STABLE` or `WATCH`. Each of these re-derivations increments the facility's `version` by one.

Entering `UPSET` starts the intake-curtailment cascade. Every `FeedstockAgreement` on that facility that is `ACTIVE` and whose `curtailable` flag is true moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `OPS_UPSET`. Agreements whose `curtailable` flag is false are skipped and remain `ACTIVE` — that is the whole point of the flag. Each suspension then carries the same consequences described in §3.2: the dairy's `active_agreement_count` decrements by one, a dairy whose active count reaches zero moves `CONTRACTED → SUSPENDED`, and every `ACCEPTED` delivery booked against the suspended agreement whose operations day is still `OPEN` moves `ACCEPTED → CANCELLED` with `rejection_reason` `AGREEMENT_SUSPENDED`, forcing recomputation of each affected day's `organic_loading_rate` and of the facility's mass-control totals. While the facility sits in `UPSET`, no further feedstock may be booked into it and no day may be closed on it, both rejected with `ERR_FACILITY_UPSET`. The only way out is a `PlatformAdmin` override with `dimension` `OPS`, which moves `ops_posture` `UPSET → RESTRICTED`, sets `posture_override_active` true and increments the facility's `version`. Intake resumes at that moment, but the suspended agreements stay `SUSPENDED` until an operator reinstates each of them explicitly.

A closed day can be reopened once, and reopening is the compensating action for separation under the mass-control invariant. The `ProjectOperator` supplies a mandatory `reason`. Reopening is permitted only when the day is `CLOSED`, no nomination on that day is `CONFIRMED` or `DELIVERED`, no credit batch of that facility in `CLAIMED`, `VERIFIED` or `ISSUED` status covers the day's `ops_date`, no nutrient application drawing on that day has reached `APPLIED`, and `reopen_count` is still below one — a second reopen attempt is rejected with `ERR_REOPEN_LIMIT_REACHED`. Each of the first four conditions is a generic state check yielding `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, and they are evaluated in exactly the order just listed, all of them ahead of the reopen ceiling. The day moves `CLOSED → OPEN` and the gas dimension rewinds all the way to `NOT_PRODUCED`: `treated_ch4_pct`, `treated_co2_pct`, `treated_h2s_ppm`, `treated_moisture_lb_per_mmscf`, `pipeline_hhv_btu_per_scf` and `offspec_reason` are cleared to null, `pipeline_gas_scf` and `pipeline_gas_mmbtu` return to 0.000, and `conditioning_attempts` resets to 0. What the rewind does *not* touch is the upstream record: the raw-gas readings `raw_biogas_scf`, `raw_ch4_pct`, `raw_co2_pct`, `raw_h2s_ppm`, `raw_moisture_lb_per_mmscf`, `raw_hhv_btu_per_scf` and `raw_gas_mmbtu`, and the entire stability assessment `avg_digester_temp_c`, `temp_excursion_minutes`, `vfa_mg_per_l`, `alkalinity_mg_per_l`, `vfa_alkalinity_ratio`, `thermal_status`, `stress_points` and `stability_status`, all survive the reopen exactly as the close wrote them, and the facility's `ops_posture` is not re-derived either. The digestate dimension rewinds too, but only partly: `separation_recorded` returns to false and `digestate_solids_tonnes`, `digestate_liquid_tonnes` and `bedding_recovered_tonnes` are zeroed, while the three separation *inputs* — `solids_capture_pct`, `bedding_recovery_pct` and `lab_total_nitrogen_mg_per_l` — are left exactly as the earlier separation wrote them, so a reopened day still reports the laboratory nitrogen that a nutrient application planned against it will read. The facility's `unseparated_manure_tonnes`, `digestate_solids_produced_tonnes`, `digestate_liquid_produced_tonnes` and `mass_control_residual_tonnes` are recomputed, and the residual must return to exactly 0.000. `reopen_count`, the day's `version` and the facility's `version` each increment by one.

### 3.5. Conditioning Biogas to Pipeline Quality

Raw digester gas is predominantly methane and carbon dioxide, saturated with water and laced with hydrogen sulfide. Getting it into a utility pipeline means removing all three contaminants, and the system records each attempt. A conditioning pass is submitted by a `ProjectOperator` with a `disposition` of either `RECONDITION` or `FLARE`. A `RECONDITION` pass carries the post-treatment analysis: `treated_ch4_pct`, `treated_co2_pct`, `treated_h2s_ppm` and `treated_moisture_lb_per_mmscf`, all four mandatory at 1 decimal place, with `treated_ch4_pct` strictly greater than zero and at most 100 because it is the divisor of the volume step, `treated_co2_pct` between 0 and 100 inclusive, and the two concentration readings zero or more. A `FLARE` pass carries none of the four and requires nothing beyond the `disposition` itself. Conditioning requires that the day's `gas_state` be `RAW`, `PIPELINE_SPEC` or `OFFSPEC` for a `RECONDITION` disposition, or exactly `OFFSPEC` for a `FLARE` disposition; anything else is rejected with `ERR_GAS_NOT_CONDITIONABLE`. A `RECONDITION` pass additionally requires that an `InterconnectAgreement` for the day's facility exists in `ACTIVE` status, or the request is rejected with `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_INTERCONNECT`, and that `conditioning_attempts` is still below two — a third reconditioning pass on the same day is rejected with `ERR_CONDITIONING_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`, the interconnect requirement being checked first and the ceiling second. A `FLARE` pass is exempt from both of those requirements: it needs no interconnection agreement, ignores the attempt ceiling, and does not increment `conditioning_attempts` at all — it increments only the day's `version`. When more than one `ACTIVE` interconnection agreement exists for the facility, the one with the earliest `created_at` supplies the specification.

Every `RECONDITION` pass runs the conditioning yield calculation in three steps. First, volume: methane is conserved through the scrubbing train except for a parasitic slip of two percent, so `pipeline_gas_scf` is `raw_biogas_scf` multiplied by the ratio of `raw_ch4_pct` to `treated_ch4_pct`, then multiplied by the quantity one minus 0.020, rounded to 3 decimal places. In the same step `pipeline_hhv_btu_per_scf` is `treated_ch4_pct` divided by 100 and multiplied by 1010.0, rounded to 2 decimal places.

Second, the specification screen, evaluated against the selected interconnection agreement in a pinned order where the **first** failure wins and no later test is consulted. If `treated_h2s_ppm` is strictly greater than the agreement's `max_h2s_ppm`, `offspec_reason` is `H2S`. Otherwise, if `treated_moisture_lb_per_mmscf` is strictly greater than `max_moisture_lb_per_mmscf`, `offspec_reason` is `MOISTURE`. Otherwise, if `treated_co2_pct` is strictly greater than `max_co2_pct`, `offspec_reason` is `CO2`. Otherwise, if the freshly computed `pipeline_hhv_btu_per_scf` is strictly less than `min_hhv_btu_per_scf`, `offspec_reason` is `HHV`. If none of the four fires, `offspec_reason` is null. Each threshold accepts exact equality as passing.

Third, energy: when `offspec_reason` is non-null, `pipeline_gas_mmbtu` is 0.000. When it is null, `pipeline_gas_mmbtu` is `pipeline_gas_scf` multiplied by `pipeline_hhv_btu_per_scf` and divided by one million, rounded to 3 decimal places.

The gas dimension then branches, and the two branches have deliberately different footprints. A passing pass moves `gas_state` `RAW → PIPELINE_SPEC` (or `OFFSPEC → PIPELINE_SPEC` on a later retry), sets `offspec_reason` to null, writes the three yield fields and increments `conditioning_attempts` and the day's `version` by one. A failing pass moves `gas_state` `RAW → OFFSPEC`, sets `offspec_reason` to the first failing specification, forces `pipeline_gas_scf` and `pipeline_gas_mmbtu` to 0.000 and likewise increments `conditioning_attempts` and the day's `version` by one.

The dangerous case is a laboratory re-test on gas that had already passed. A `RECONDITION` pass on a day whose `gas_state` is `PIPELINE_SPEC` that now fails specification moves the day `PIPELINE_SPEC → OFFSPEC`, sets `offspec_reason` to the first failing specification, zeroes `pipeline_gas_scf` and `pipeline_gas_mmbtu`, and starts the curtailment cascade. Because the day has gone off specification, every `InjectionNomination` drawing on that day whose status is `NOMINATED` or `CONFIRMED` moves to the terminal `REJECTED_OFFSPEC` state. Because those nominations were rejected, the interconnection agreement's `curtailment_count` increases by the number rejected — counted separately per interconnection agreement, so each agreement is charged only for the rejected nominations that actually named it, and each such agreement's `version` increments by one whether or not its status changes. And because `curtailment_count` has reached or passed three, an agreement still `ACTIVE` moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED`; while it is `SUSPENDED` no new nomination may be created on it.

Gas that cannot be salvaged is flared. A `FLARE` disposition is accepted only from `OFFSPEC` and moves the day `OFFSPEC → FLARED`, a terminal gas state, forcing `pipeline_gas_scf` and `pipeline_gas_mmbtu` to 0.000 while leaving the four `treated_` readings, `pipeline_hhv_btu_per_scf` and `offspec_reason` exactly as the failed reconditioning pass wrote them, so the record of why the gas was unsalvageable survives the flare. A flared day can never contribute delivered energy to a credit batch. The other terminal gas state, `INJECTED`, is reached only from `PIPELINE_SPEC` when a nomination is actually delivered.

### 3.6. Interconnection, Nomination Windows and Injection

A `PipelineScheduler` registers each `InterconnectAgreement` against a `facility_id`, naming the receiving `pipeline_operator_name` and pinning the gas quality specification: `max_h2s_ppm`, `max_moisture_lb_per_mmscf` and `max_co2_pct` as upper bounds, `min_hhv_btu_per_scf` as a lower bound, all four strictly positive. Two scheduling parameters come with it: `max_hourly_injection_mmbtu`, the strictly positive injection rate ceiling, and `ramp_buffer_minutes`, a whole-minute buffer of zero or more applied symmetrically to both ends of every nomination window when checking for collisions. A third, `nomination_lead_hours`, is the minimum number of whole hours of notice the pipeline requires, also zero or more. A new agreement starts `ACTIVE` with `curtailment_count` at 0.

Nominating gas creates an `InjectionNomination` in `NOMINATED` status. The scheduler names `interconnect_id` and `ops_day_id`, resolved in that order, and supplies the half-open window bounds `window_start_utc` (inclusive) and `window_end_utc` (exclusive) together with the energy `nominated_mmbtu`, which must be greater than zero. Both bounds are required strings that must parse as ISO 8601 instants — a trailing `Z`, an explicit numeric offset and a bare instant with no zone at all are all accepted, the last being read as UTC — and every comparison the server makes is performed in UTC, but the strings are stored and echoed back exactly as the client wrote them rather than normalised to any canonical form. `window_start_utc` must be strictly earlier than `window_end_utc` or the request fails with `ERR_INVALID_DATE_RANGE`. The source day's `gas_state` must be `PIPELINE_SPEC` or the request fails with `ERR_GAS_NOT_PIPELINE_SPEC`, and both the named `interconnect_id` must itself be in `ACTIVE` status and an `ACTIVE` interconnection agreement must exist for the day's facility, or the request fails with `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_INTERCONNECT`.

Four further gates run in the order fixed by §2.9. First, notice: the logical clock must be at least `nomination_lead_hours` whole hours earlier than `window_start_utc`, or the request is rejected with `ERR_NOMINATION_LEAD_TIME`; exact equality passes, so a window starting precisely `nomination_lead_hours` after the clock is accepted, and a `nomination_lead_hours` of 0 lets a window start at the clock instant itself. Second, collision: the requested window is expanded by `ramp_buffer_minutes` at each end — the buffered lower bound is `window_start_utc` moved earlier by that many minutes, the buffered upper bound is `window_end_utc` moved later by the same amount — and compared against every existing nomination on the same interconnection agreement whose status is `NOMINATED`, `CONFIRMED`, `CURTAILED` or `DELIVERED`, each of which is expanded the same way. Two buffered windows conflict when the new buffered lower bound is strictly earlier than the existing buffered upper bound **and** the existing buffered lower bound is strictly earlier than the new buffered upper bound; because both comparisons are strict, windows that merely touch at a buffered endpoint do not conflict. A conflict is rejected with `ERR_INJECTION_WINDOW_OVERLAP`. Nominations already in `REJECTED_OFFSPEC` release their window and are excluded from the comparison entirely. Third, production: `nominated_mmbtu` must be less than or equal to the source day's `pipeline_gas_mmbtu`, or the request is rejected with `ERR_NOMINATION_EXCEEDS_PRODUCTION`. Fourth, rate: `window_minutes` is the count of whole minutes from `window_start_utc` to `window_end_utc`, truncated downwards, so a window shorter than one whole minute — legal under the strict start-before-end test yet leaving no minute to inject in — yields zero minutes and is rejected with `ERR_HOURLY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` before any division is attempted. Otherwise `hourly_rate_mmbtu` is `nominated_mmbtu` divided by the window length expressed in hours, where that length in hours is `window_minutes` divided by 60, with the quotient rounded to 3 decimal places. That rate must be less than or equal to `max_hourly_injection_mmbtu`, with exact equality accepted and anything strictly greater rejected with `ERR_HOURLY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. A created nomination has `confirmed_mmbtu` and `delivered_mmbtu` null.

The scheduler then makes a scheduling decision, supplying a `decision` of `CONFIRM` or `CURTAIL` with an optional `reason`. `CONFIRM` moves the nomination `NOMINATED → CONFIRMED` and sets `confirmed_mmbtu` equal to `nominated_mmbtu`; it requires that the nomination is `NOMINATED`, that the interconnection agreement is `ACTIVE`, and that the source day's `gas_state` is still `PIPELINE_SPEC`. A non-`ACTIVE` interconnection agreement is reported here as `ERR_INVALID_STATE` rather than `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_INTERCONNECT`, while a day that has fallen off specification is reported as `ERR_GAS_NOT_PIPELINE_SPEC`. The nomination's `version` increments by one. `CURTAIL` moves the nomination `NOMINATED → CURTAILED` and requires a `curtailed_mmbtu` value that is strictly greater than zero and strictly less than `nominated_mmbtu`, a value outside that band being a schema violation rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`; `confirmed_mmbtu` is set to that curtailed volume. `CURTAIL` checks nothing beyond the nomination being `NOMINATED` — neither the interconnection agreement's status nor the day's `gas_state` — so gas may always be curtailed even where it could no longer be confirmed. Curtailment also increments the interconnection agreement's `curtailment_count` by one, and when that counter reaches or passes three an agreement still `ACTIVE` moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED`; both the nomination's `version` and the interconnection agreement's `version` increment by one.

Recording actual injection is the last step. The scheduler supplies `delivered_mmbtu`, which must be zero or more at 3 decimal places — a wholly failed injection is recorded as 0.000 — and less than or equal to `confirmed_mmbtu`, or the request is rejected with `ERR_DELIVERY_EXCEEDS_CONFIRMED`. Delivery is accepted from either `CONFIRMED` or `CURTAILED` and moves the nomination to the terminal `DELIVERED` state, writing `delivered_mmbtu` from the request. It requires the source day's `gas_state` to be `PIPELINE_SPEC` — though not that the interconnection agreement still be `ACTIVE` — and it moves that day `PIPELINE_SPEC → INJECTED`, incrementing both the nomination's `version` and the day's `version` by one. The three checks run in this order: the nomination's own from-state (`ERR_INVALID_STATE`), then the day's `gas_state` (`ERR_GAS_NOT_PIPELINE_SPEC`), then the confirmed-energy ceiling (`ERR_DELIVERY_EXCEEDS_CONFIRMED`). `DELIVERED` energy is the only energy that ever reaches a credit batch.

A suspended interconnection agreement is recovered, or an agreement is retired, through a status-change capability taking an `action` of `REINSTATE` or `TERMINATE` with an optional `reason`. `REINSTATE` moves it `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE` and resets `curtailment_count` to 0, incrementing its `version` by one. `TERMINATE` is accepted from `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` and moves it to the terminal `TERMINATED` state, incrementing its `version` by one; from `ACTIVE` it additionally requires that no nomination on the agreement is still `NOMINATED` or `CONFIRMED`, so live schedule commitments cannot be orphaned, while from `SUSPENDED` that live-commitment check is skipped entirely and the agreement retires regardless. Every one of these preconditions — reinstating something that is not `SUSPENDED`, terminating something that is neither `ACTIVE` nor `SUSPENDED`, and terminating an `ACTIVE` agreement that still carries live nominations — is reported as `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.

### 3.7. Digestate Separation, Bedding and Nutrient Management

What comes out of the digester is as commercially important as what goes in. A `ProjectOperator` records the screw-press run on a day by supplying `solids_capture_pct`, the total-solids capture efficiency of the press; `bedding_recovery_pct`, the percentage of the separated cake that is recovered as animal bedding; and `lab_total_nitrogen_mg_per_l`, the laboratory total nitrogen of the liquid fraction. All three are required: the two percentages are between 0 and 100 inclusive at 1 decimal place, and the nitrogen figure is zero or more at 1 decimal place with no upper bound. Separation may be recorded only once per day: if `separation_recorded` is already true, the request is rejected with `ERR_SEPARATION_ALREADY_RECORDED`, and only a reopen can clear the flag. That flag is the *only* thing about the day this operation inspects — its `status` may be `OPEN` or `CLOSED`, its `gas_state` may be anything, and separation is accepted regardless.

The separation calculation draws on the day's `ACCEPTED` deliveries only. The feed tonnage is the sum of their `net_tonnes`. The total solids tonnage is the sum, over those deliveries, of each load's `net_tonnes` multiplied by its own `total_solids_pct` divided by 100, with that sum rounded to 2 decimal places. `digestate_solids_tonnes` is then the total solids tonnage multiplied by `solids_capture_pct` divided by 100 and divided by the cake dry-matter fraction of 0.30 — the press leaves the cake at thirty percent dry matter, so the captured dry solids account for less than a third of the cake's wet mass — rounded to 3 decimal places. `digestate_liquid_tonnes` is the feed tonnage less `digestate_solids_tonnes`, rounded to 3 decimal places, which closes the mass balance by construction: the cake plus the liquid equals the feed. `bedding_recovered_tonnes` is `digestate_solids_tonnes` multiplied by `bedding_recovery_pct` divided by 100, rounded to 3 decimal places; bedding is recovered *from* the cake and does not leave the solids inventory. Recording separation sets `separation_recorded` true, moves the day's tonnage out of the facility's `unseparated_manure_tonnes` and into `digestate_solids_produced_tonnes` and `digestate_liquid_produced_tonnes`, recomputes `mass_control_residual_tonnes` — which must stay at exactly 0.000 — and increments the day's `version` and the facility's `version` by one.

Land application is planned before it is committed. A `ProjectOperator` creates a `NutrientApplication` naming `dairy_id`, `facility_id` and `ops_day_id`, resolved in that order, and supplies the `land_unit_code` identifying the field within the dairy, the inclusive `application_start_date` and `application_end_date` bounding the application window, the `solids_tonnes_applied` and `liquid_tonnes_applied` (each zero or more), and the `pan_basis`, which selects how plant-available nitrogen is computed. The plan is created in `PLANNED` status.

The land unit is the contended resource, and it is protected by an asymmetric setback rule. Every existing application on the same combination of `dairy_id` and `land_unit_code` whose status is `PLANNED` or `APPLIED` is examined; `VOIDED` applications release their window and are excluded. Two conditions each constitute a conflict. The first is a closed-interval overlap: the new window's `application_start_date` falls on or before the existing window's `application_end_date` **and** the existing window's `application_start_date` falls on or before the new window's `application_end_date`; both ends are inclusive. The second is the re-application setback: the existing window's `application_end_date` falls strictly before the new window's `application_start_date` **and** the new window's `application_start_date` falls on or before the existing `application_end_date` advanced by twenty-one calendar days of mandatory soil rest. Either condition rejects the plan with `ERR_LAND_UNIT_SETBACK_CONFLICT`. Note that the setback has no mirror image: an application placed entirely *before* an existing one is never blocked by the setback, only by the closed-interval overlap test.

Plant-available nitrogen is computed at planning time and recomputed when the application is committed. The nitrogen in the liquid fraction, `liquid_total_nitrogen_kg`, is `liquid_tonnes_applied` multiplied by the source day's `lab_total_nitrogen_mg_per_l` and divided by 1000, rounded to 3 decimal places; a source day on which separation has not yet been recorded still carries a null nitrogen figure, and at planning time that null is read as zero rather than rejected, so a plan against such a day is created with its liquid nitrogen at 0.000 and is corrected by the recomputation at commit time. The nitrogen in the cake fraction, `solids_total_nitrogen_kg`, is `solids_tonnes_applied` multiplied by the standard cake nitrogen content of 5.20 kilograms per tonne, rounded to 3 decimal places. The liquid availability factor depends on `pan_basis`. Under the `LAB` basis it is the flat certified laboratory coefficient of 0.5500. Under the `AGRONOMIC` basis it is derived from the extension service's two-pool model: the ammonium fraction of 0.62 multiplied by the volatilisation retention of 0.75, plus the remaining organic fraction — one minus 0.62, that is 0.38 — multiplied by the organic mineralisation rate of 0.35, with the result rounded to 4 decimal places, giving 0.5980. The agronomic basis therefore charges more nitrogen against the dairy's budget than the laboratory basis does for identical digestate. Finally, `plant_available_nitrogen_kg` is `liquid_total_nitrogen_kg` multiplied by that liquid availability factor, plus `solids_total_nitrogen_kg` multiplied by the organic mineralisation rate of 0.35, rounded to 3 decimal places.

Committing the application moves it `PLANNED → APPLIED`, and the `ProjectOperator` may record an optional `applicator_name`. Four preconditions guard the commit, and they are evaluated in the order given here. The application must itself still be `PLANNED`, or the request is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. The source day's `separation_recorded` must be true, or the request is rejected with `ERR_SEPARATION_NOT_RECORDED`. `solids_tonnes_applied` must be less than or equal to the facility's `digestate_solids_on_hand_tonnes` and `liquid_tonnes_applied` less than or equal to `digestate_liquid_on_hand_tonnes`, or the request is rejected with `ERR_DIGESTATE_INVENTORY_INSUFFICIENT`. And the dairy's `pan_applied_ytd_kg` plus this application's `plant_available_nitrogen_kg` must be less than or equal to the dairy's `annual_nitrogen_budget_kg`, with exact equality accepted; exceeding it is rejected with `ERR_NITROGEN_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`. The year-to-date figure is itself the sum of `plant_available_nitrogen_kg` over that dairy's `APPLIED` applications whose `application_start_date` falls in the same calendar year as the logical clock.

A successful commit increases the facility's `digestate_solids_applied_tonnes` and `digestate_liquid_applied_tonnes` by the applied amounts, decreases `digestate_solids_on_hand_tonnes` and `digestate_liquid_on_hand_tonnes` by the same amounts, and increases the dairy's `pan_applied_ytd_kg` by `plant_available_nitrogen_kg`. The on-hand figures are always the produced totals less the applied totals, to 3 decimal places. The application's `version`, the facility's `version` and the dairy's `version` each increment by one.

Voiding is the compensating action, and it takes a mandatory `void_reason`. From `APPLIED` it moves the application to the terminal `VOIDED` state, decreases the facility's `digestate_solids_applied_tonnes` and `digestate_liquid_applied_tonnes` by the amounts that were applied, restores `digestate_solids_on_hand_tonnes` and `digestate_liquid_on_hand_tonnes` to their pre-application values, decreases the dairy's `pan_applied_ytd_kg` by `plant_available_nitrogen_kg`, and increments the application's `version`, the facility's `version` and the dairy's `version` by one — the digestate inventory must show no drift once the round trip completes. Voiding is also accepted directly from `PLANNED`, in which case no inventory or nitrogen movement occurs at all, because a merely planned application never charged anything, and only the application's own `version` increments — neither the facility nor the dairy is touched. Voiding an application already `VOIDED`, or one in any other state, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.

### 3.8. Environmental Credits: Pathways, Batches and Annual Verification

A `ProgramAdministrator` registers a `CreditPathway` against a `facility_id`, declaring the `program` — either `LCFS` for the low carbon fuel standard or `RFS_D3` for cellulosic renewable fuel — and the regulator-assigned `pathway_code`. The pathway starts `PROVISIONAL` with `reference_start_date`, `reference_end_date`, `certified_ci_score`, `verified_ci_score` and `ci_adjustment` all null, and `credits_issued_total`, `credits_reversed_total`, `credits_outstanding` and `credit_control_residual` all 0.000.

Certification decisions carry a `decision` of `CERTIFY`, `REINSTATE` or `REVOKE`, an optional `reason`, and — for `CERTIFY` — the `reference_start_date` and `reference_end_date` bounding the inclusive reference window. `CERTIFY` moves the pathway `PROVISIONAL → CERTIFIED` and requires that at least one operations day of the facility whose `gas_state` is `PIPELINE_SPEC` or `INJECTED` lies inside that window. Both of those conditions are generic state checks reported as `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, evaluated in that order — the pathway's `PROVISIONAL` status first, the qualifying-day search second — after the two reference dates have been parsed and range-checked. It stores the two dates and runs the `CERTIFIED` variant of the carbon intensity calculation, writing the result to `certified_ci_score`, then increments the pathway's `version` by one.

The carbon intensity calculation produces a figure in grams of carbon dioxide equivalent per megajoule and runs in four steps. The denominator first: the pipeline energy for the window, expressed in MMBtu, is multiplied by 1055.06 megajoules per MMBtu and rounded to 3 decimal places. Next, avoided methane from displacing the dairies' pre-project manure storage. For each of the three baselines, the volatile-solids tonnage sourced from dairies carrying that baseline is multiplied by 1000 to convert tonnes to kilograms, then by the biochemical methane potential of 0.21 cubic metres of methane per kilogram of volatile solids, then by that baseline's methane conversion factor — 0.75 for `LAGOON`, 0.30 for `SOLID_STORAGE` and 0.10 for `DAILY_SPREAD` — and then by the methane density of 0.6785 kilograms per cubic metre. The three products are summed to give avoided methane in kilograms. That figure is multiplied by the global warming potential of methane, 25, and then by 1000 to convert kilograms to grams, rounded to 3 decimal places; dividing that by the energy denominator and rounding to 2 decimal places gives the raw avoidance intensity. A zero energy denominator is guarded rather than divided: when the window's pipeline energy works out to exactly 0.000 megajoules, both the raw avoidance intensity and the transport intensity of step three are taken as 0.00, leaving the direct intensity equal to the process intensity alone.

Step three handles the project's own emissions. The transport intensity is the hauling activity in tonne-kilometres multiplied by 95.0 grams of carbon dioxide equivalent per tonne-kilometre, divided by the energy denominator, rounded to 2 decimal places. The direct intensity is the process intensity plus the transport intensity, rounded to 2 decimal places.

Step four applies a cap and then a floor, in that nesting. The avoidance credit actually granted is the lesser of the raw avoidance intensity and the direct intensity multiplied by 20.0 and rounded to 2 decimal places — the regulator will not let a project claim avoidance credit beyond twenty times its own direct emissions. The carbon intensity is then the direct intensity less that granted avoidance credit, rounded to 2 decimal places, and finally floored at negative 400.00: if the subtraction yields anything below negative 400.00, the score is negative 400.00 exactly.

The two variants differ only in where the inputs come from, and that difference is the whole point of annual verification. In the `CERTIFIED` variant, the pipeline energy is the sum of `pipeline_gas_mmbtu` over operations days of the pathway's facility whose `gas_state` is `PIPELINE_SPEC` or `INJECTED` and whose `ops_date` lies inside the inclusive reference window; the per-baseline volatile-solids tonnages are the sums of `vs_tonnes` over `ACCEPTED` deliveries on those same days, grouped by the supplying dairy's `manure_management_baseline`; the hauling activity is the sum of `haul_tonne_km` over that same delivery set; and the process intensity is the design constant 15.40 grams of carbon dioxide equivalent per megajoule. In the `VERIFIED` variant, every one of those inputs is replaced by the auditor's measurement — `actual_pipeline_mmbtu`, `actual_vs_tonnes_lagoon`, `actual_vs_tonnes_solid_storage`, `actual_vs_tonnes_daily_spread`, `actual_haul_tonne_km` and a measured `actual_process_g_per_mj` that supplants the design constant — so identical gas can yield a different carbon intensity depending on which variant ran.

Credit batches convert delivered energy into tradable quantity. A `ProgramAdministrator` opens a `CreditBatch` by naming `pathway_id` and `facility_id`, in that order, and the inclusive `period_start_date` and `period_end_date` of the settlement period, together with the mandatory `expected_pathway_version` described in §2.7. The denormalised `facility_id` must equal the pathway's own facility, or the request is rejected with `ERR_FACILITY_PATHWAY_MISMATCH`. No batch on the same pathway whose status is `CLAIMED`, `VERIFIED` or `ISSUED` may have a period overlapping the requested period, or the request is rejected with `ERR_CREDIT_PERIOD_OVERLAP`. Those two checks run in exactly that order and both run after the stale-version guard, so a request that is simultaneously stale, mismatched and overlapping reports `ERR_STALE_VERSION`, and one that is mismatched and overlapping reports `ERR_FACILITY_PATHWAY_MISMATCH`. A new batch does, however, legitimately retire stale drafts: any `DRAFT` batch on the same pathway whose period overlaps the new one — that is, whose `period_start_date` falls on or before the new batch's `period_end_date` and whose `period_end_date` falls on or after the new batch's `period_start_date` — moves `DRAFT → SUPERSEDED` with `superseded_by_batch_id` set to the new batch's `id` and its own `version` incremented by one. The create also increments the pathway's `version` by one. A new batch starts `DRAFT` with `delivered_mmbtu` 0.000, `superseded_by_batch_id`, `ci_score_used`, `credit_quantity_provisional`, `credit_quantity_final`, `credit_value_cents` and `reversal_reason` all null or zero as appropriate.

Claiming moves the batch `DRAFT → CLAIMED`, optionally recording a `claim_note`. It requires the pathway to be `CERTIFIED`, or the request fails with `ERR_PATHWAY_NOT_CERTIFIED`. Claiming first recomputes `delivered_mmbtu` as the sum of `delivered_mmbtu` over nominations whose status is `DELIVERED`, whose source operations day belongs to the batch's facility, and whose `ops_date` lies inside the inclusive period bounded by `period_start_date` and `period_end_date`. Nominations in `NOMINATED`, `CONFIRMED`, `CURTAILED` or `REJECTED_OFFSPEC` are excluded. If the resulting set is empty, `delivered_mmbtu` stays at 0.000 and the claim is rejected with `ERR_NO_DELIVERED_VOLUME`. Emptiness of the set, not the size of the sum, is what triggers that rejection: a period that does contain qualifying `DELIVERED` nominations whose `delivered_mmbtu` all happen to be 0.000 is not empty, and the claim succeeds with `delivered_mmbtu` 0.000 and a credit quantity computed from it. Otherwise `ci_score_used` is set to the pathway's `certified_ci_score`, the `PROVISIONAL` variant of the credit quantity calculation writes `credit_quantity_provisional`, and the batch's `version` increments by one. The `PROVISIONAL` variant deliberately does **not** write `credit_value_cents`: a claimed batch carries no monetary value yet and contributes nothing to any settlement statement.

The credit quantity calculation branches on the pathway's `program`. Under `LCFS`, the delivered energy is converted to megajoules by multiplying `delivered_mmbtu` by 1055.06; the credit quantity is then the benchmark carbon intensity of 79.50 less `ci_score_used`, multiplied by the energy economy ratio of 0.90, multiplied by that megajoule figure, divided by one million, rounded to 3 decimal places; and `credit_value_cents` is that quantity multiplied by the credit price of 7450 cents, rounded half up to a whole cent. Under `RFS_D3`, renewable identification numbers are whole instruments and are truncated rather than rounded up: the credit quantity is `delivered_mmbtu` multiplied by 11.727 RINs per MMBtu and then rounded **down** to the nearest whole number, and `credit_value_cents` is that whole count multiplied by the RIN price of 285 cents, rounded half up to a whole cent.

Verification is exclusive to a `CreditVerifier`, who may attach a `verifier_note`. It moves the batch `CLAIMED → VERIFIED` and again requires the pathway to be `CERTIFIED`. Verification sets `ci_score_used` to the pathway's `verified_ci_score` when that field is not null and to `certified_ci_score` otherwise, then runs the `FINAL` variant of the credit quantity calculation, which writes both `credit_quantity_final` and `credit_value_cents`. It is `credit_quantity_final` and `credit_value_cents` that the revenue waterfall consumes. The batch's `version` increments by one.

Issuance returns to the `ProgramAdministrator`, who may record an optional `issuance_reference`. It moves the batch `VERIFIED → ISSUED`, requires the pathway to be `CERTIFIED`, and requires that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from the `X-User-ID` that verified this batch — otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. Issuance increases the pathway's `credits_issued_total` and `credits_outstanding` by `credit_quantity_final`, recomputes `credit_control_residual` which must equal 0.000, and increments both the batch's `version` and the pathway's `version` by one.

Reversal is the compensating action for issuance and takes a mandatory `reversal_reason` of `MATERIAL_FINDING`, `REGULATOR_ORDER` or `OPERATOR_WITHDRAWAL`. It moves the batch `ISSUED → REVERSED`, a terminal state, increases the pathway's `credits_reversed_total` by `credit_quantity_final` and decreases `credits_outstanding` by the same amount, and recomputes `credit_control_residual`, which must return to exactly 0.000. Because that batch's `credit_value_cents` has left the issued set, every `DRAFT` revenue statement of the facility whose period contains the batch's period — statement start on or before the batch start and statement end on or after the batch end — recomputes `credit_revenue_cents` and the entire waterfall behind it, in `created_at` order, each such statement's own `version` incrementing by one. That tail then re-derives the facility's `dscr_current` and `distribution_posture` from whichever statement now drives them, and increments the facility's `version` only if `distribution_posture` actually moved — a re-derivation that rewrites `dscr_current` while leaving the posture where it was is not counted as a mutation of the facility row. If the facility has no driving statement at all, nothing is re-derived. The batch's `version` and the pathway's `version` each increment by one.

Annual verification is a separate lifecycle with its own remediation loop. A `CreditVerifier` opens a `VerificationAudit` naming `pathway_id`, the `audit_year` under verification — a required integer strictly greater than zero, with no upper bound and no relation to the logical clock's year — and the accredited `verifier_body_name`. The audit starts in `FIELDWORK` with `remediation_round` at 0 and every audited actual, `reported_ci_score`, `ci_delta` and `finding_severity` null. Recording fieldwork supplies the six audited actuals — `actual_pipeline_mmbtu`, `actual_vs_tonnes_lagoon`, `actual_vs_tonnes_solid_storage`, `actual_vs_tonnes_daily_spread`, `actual_haul_tonne_km` and `actual_process_g_per_mj`, all six mandatory and each zero or more at its §2.5 scale — and requires the pathway to be `CERTIFIED` with a non-null `certified_ci_score`, otherwise `ERR_PATHWAY_NOT_CERTIFIED`. Fieldwork runs the `VERIFIED` variant of the carbon intensity calculation, writing `reported_ci_score`, and sets `ci_delta` to `reported_ci_score` less the pathway's `certified_ci_score`, rounded to 2 decimal places. Fieldwork is a self-transition on `FIELDWORK`: it may be re-recorded any number of times while the audit remains in that state, each time incrementing the audit's `version` by one. It is also the way back from `REMEDIATION`, moving the audit `REMEDIATION → FIELDWORK` with the corrected actuals, recomputing `reported_ci_score` and `ci_delta`, and clearing `finding_severity` back to null.

Issuing findings moves the audit `FIELDWORK → FINDINGS_ISSUED`, optionally with a `narrative`, and requires `reported_ci_score` to be non-null — an audit sitting in `FIELDWORK` on which no fieldwork has yet been recorded is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, the same code the wrong-from-state check uses, and the from-state is tested first. The severity classification works on the absolute value of `ci_delta`, compared at 2 decimal places on the already-rounded delta: `finding_severity` is `NONE` when that absolute value is exactly 0.00, `MINOR` when it is greater than 0.00 and less than or equal to the material tolerance of 2.00 — the minor band is closed at the top, so a delta of exactly 2.00 is still `MINOR` — and `MATERIAL` when it is strictly greater than 2.00. The audit's `version` increments by one.

The three severities have deliberately different consequences, and the difference is where the revaluation cascade begins. When `finding_severity` is `NONE`, the pathway's `verified_ci_score` is set equal to its `certified_ci_score` and `ci_adjustment` to 0.00; the pathway's status is unchanged and no batch is touched. When `finding_severity` is `MINOR`, `verified_ci_score` is set from the audit's `reported_ci_score` and `ci_adjustment` to `verified_ci_score` less `certified_ci_score`; the pathway's status is still unchanged and, importantly, no batch is recomputed. When `finding_severity` is `MATERIAL`, the pathway moves `CERTIFIED → SUSPENDED`, `verified_ci_score` is set from `reported_ci_score` and `ci_adjustment` to `verified_ci_score` less `certified_ci_score`, and the cascade runs: because `verified_ci_score` changed, every batch of that pathway whose status is `VERIFIED` or `ISSUED` recomputes `ci_score_used`, `credit_quantity_final` and `credit_value_cents` from the `FINAL` variant of the credit quantity calculation; `credits_issued_total`, `credits_reversed_total`, `credits_outstanding` and `credit_control_residual` are then recomputed, with the residual required to remain 0.000; and because the issued batches' `credit_value_cents` have changed, every `DRAFT` revenue statement whose period contains an affected batch's period recomputes its `credit_revenue_cents` and its whole waterfall, which in turn re-derives the facility's `dscr_current` and `distribution_posture`. The move to `SUSPENDED` is conditional on the pathway being `CERTIFIED` at that instant, so a second material finding against an already-suspended pathway revalues its batches and statements exactly as described but leaves its status where it is. The pathway's `version` increments by one on all three severities and not only on `MATERIAL` — even a `NONE` finding, which merely copies `certified_ci_score` into `verified_ci_score` and zeroes `ci_adjustment`, is a mutation of the pathway row.

Remediation is the operator's answer to findings. A `ProjectOperator` submits a mandatory `remediation_plan`, moving the audit `FINDINGS_ISSUED → REMEDIATION`. It is permitted only when `finding_severity` is `MINOR` or `MATERIAL` — a clean audit has nothing to remediate, and one whose severity is `NONE` or still null is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_STATE` — and only while `remediation_round` is below two; a third remediation round on the same audit is rejected with `ERR_REMEDIATION_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`. The three checks fire in that order: from-state, then severity, then the round ceiling. `remediation_round` and the audit's `version` each increment by one. Closing the audit, optionally with a `closing_note`, moves it to the terminal `CLOSED` state from either `REMEDIATION` or `FINDINGS_ISSUED`; from `FINDINGS_ISSUED` it is permitted only when `finding_severity` is `NONE`, so a findings-bearing audit must pass through remediation before it can close; both the wrong-from-state rejection and the findings-bearing rejection are reported as `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, in that order. Closing increments the audit's `version` by one.

A suspended pathway has two exits. `REINSTATE` moves it `SUSPENDED → CERTIFIED` and requires that every verification audit on that pathway is `CLOSED`, a violation of either the `SUSPENDED` precondition or the all-audits-closed precondition being reported as `ERR_INVALID_STATE`; it increments the pathway's `version` by one. `REVOKE` moves it `SUSPENDED → REVOKED`, a terminal state — attempting it from any other status is likewise `ERR_INVALID_STATE` — and takes every batch of the pathway that is still `ISSUED`, in `created_at` order, from `ISSUED → REVERSED` with `reversal_reason` set to `REGULATOR_ORDER`, then recomputes `credits_reversed_total`, `credits_outstanding` and `credit_control_residual`, which must remain exactly 0.000. Each batch so reversed has its own `version` incremented by one and the pathway's `version` increments by one, but this path deliberately recomputes no revenue statement: unlike a single batch reversal, a wholesale revocation leaves every `DRAFT` statement's `credit_revenue_cents` untouched.

### 3.9. Revenue Settlement, Dairy Shares and the Financing Covenant

Settlement is the point where gas sales, credit sales, management fees, debt-service reserves, dairy revenue sharing and lender covenants all collide, and the order in which they are evaluated is pinned. A `FinanceController` opens a `RevenueStatement` naming the `facility_id`, the inclusive `period_start_date` and `period_end_date` of the settlement period, the `gas_revenue_cents` commodity sales for the period (zero or more, entered by the controller), and the mandatory `expected_facility_version` described in §2.7. No statement on the same facility whose status is `APPROVED` or `PAID` may have a period overlapping the requested period, or the create is rejected with `ERR_STATEMENT_PERIOD_OVERLAP`; that overlap test runs after the stale-version guard, so a request that is both stale and overlapping reports `ERR_STALE_VERSION`. Any `DRAFT` statement on the same facility whose period overlaps the new one — whose `period_start_date` falls on or before the new statement's `period_end_date` and whose `period_end_date` falls on or after the new statement's `period_start_date` — moves `DRAFT → SUPERSEDED` with `superseded_by_statement_id` set to the new statement's `id` and its own `version` incremented by one. A created statement is `DRAFT` with `approved_by_user_id` and `paid_by_user_id` null.

Creation runs three calculations in a fixed sequence: the distributable waterfall first, the dairy share allocation second, and the covenant test third.

The **waterfall** has seven pinned steps, and each consumes only values produced above it. The fee tier is keyed on gross revenue while the reserve sweep is keyed on the post-fee figure, which makes them look mutually dependent; the pinned order is what makes them not. Step one computes `credit_revenue_cents` as the sum of `credit_value_cents` over credit batches of this facility whose status is `ISSUED` and whose period lies entirely inside the statement period — that is, whose `period_start_date` is on or after the statement's `period_start_date` and whose `period_end_date` is on or before the statement's `period_end_date`. Batches in `DRAFT`, `CLAIMED`, `VERIFIED`, `REVERSED` or `SUPERSEDED` contribute nothing. Step two sets `gross_revenue_cents` to `gas_revenue_cents` plus `credit_revenue_cents`. Step three selects `management_fee_rate` from `gross_revenue_cents` and never from any post-fee figure: 0.0800 when `gross_revenue_cents` is at most 1,000,000 cents (ten thousand dollars), 0.0650 when it is above that but at most 2,500,000 cents (twenty-five thousand dollars), and 0.0500 above that. Step four sets `management_fee_cents` to `gross_revenue_cents` multiplied by `management_fee_rate`, rounded half up to a whole cent. Step five sets `after_fee_cents` to `gross_revenue_cents` less `management_fee_cents`. Step six sets `reserve_sweep_cents` to the lesser of two figures: `after_fee_cents` multiplied by the sweep rate of 0.2500 and rounded half up to a whole cent, and the unfunded portion of the reserve, which is the facility's `reserve_target_cents` less its `reserve_balance_cents` or zero if that difference is negative. Step seven sets `net_distributable_cents` to `after_fee_cents` less `reserve_sweep_cents`.

The **dairy share allocation** then distributes the pool with no cent created and none lost. The participating agreements are those on the statement's facility whose status is `ACTIVE` at the moment the statement is computed and whose term overlaps the statement period — that is, whose `term_start_date` is on or before the statement's `period_end_date` and whose `term_end_date` is on or after the statement's `period_start_date`. Agreements in `DRAFT`, `PENDING_DAIRY_SIGNATURE`, `COUNTERED`, `SUSPENDED` or `TERMINATED` are excluded. Those agreements are ordered by `committed_tonnes_per_day` descending, and ties are broken by `created_at` ascending; the resource `id` is never consulted for ordering.

For each participating agreement, the base amount is `net_distributable_cents` when its `revenue_share_basis` is `NET` and `gross_revenue_cents` when it is `GROSS`. The exact line amount is that base multiplied by the agreement's `revenue_share_pct`, kept as an exact decimal and not rounded. `floor_cents` for the line is that exact amount rounded **down** to a whole cent. The residual pool is then the floor of the sum of all lines' exact amounts, less the sum of all lines' `floor_cents`; should that difference come out negative — which a `GROSS`-basis line against a negative distributable pool can produce — it is clamped to zero, and no line ever receives more than a single residual cent, so a residual pool larger than the number of lines simply leaves the surplus undistributed. One cent is handed to each of the first lines in the residual pool, walking the same ordering — `committed_tonnes_per_day` descending, then `created_at` ascending — so `residual_cents` is 1 for the first as many lines as the residual pool holds and 0 for every line thereafter. Each line's `share_cents` is its `floor_cents` plus its `residual_cents`. Rounding each line half up, or rounding the total and pro-rating it, would produce a different cent split; the floor-then-residual order is the contract. The resulting `share_lines` array is stored in that same order, each element carrying `agreement_id`, `dairy_id`, `revenue_share_basis`, `revenue_share_pct`, `committed_tonnes_per_day`, `floor_cents`, `residual_cents` and `share_cents`. `total_dairy_share_cents` is the sum of every line's `share_cents`, and `retained_cents` is `net_distributable_cents` less `total_dairy_share_cents`.

The **covenant test** closes creation. The annualised net operating income is `after_fee_cents` multiplied by 12 months. `dscr_at_close` is that annualised figure divided by the facility's `annual_debt_service_cents`, rounded to 3 decimal places, and it is copied onto the facility as `dscr_current`. The facility's `distribution_posture` is then `OPEN` when `dscr_at_close` is greater than or equal to `dscr_minimum` — the boundary is inclusive, so meeting the covenant exactly keeps distributions open. Otherwise it is `RESTRICTED` when `dscr_at_close` is greater than or equal to `dscr_minimum` less the restricted band of 0.150. Otherwise, below even that band, it is `RESTRICTED` when `posture_override_active` is true and `LOCKED` when it is false. Only one statement drives this at any time: the statement with the latest `created_at` among those on the facility whose status is `DRAFT`, `APPROVED` or `PAID`. A `SUPERSEDED` or `CLAWED_BACK` statement never sets `dscr_current`. Statement creation increments the facility's `version` by one for the creation itself, and by one more — two in total — when the covenant test also moved `distribution_posture`; a covenant test that rewrites `dscr_current` while leaving the posture unchanged adds no second increment. `distribution_posture` moves `OPEN → RESTRICTED`, `OPEN → LOCKED`, `RESTRICTED → LOCKED`, `RESTRICTED → OPEN` and `LOCKED → OPEN` purely as a consequence of these recomputations, and the last two of those also clear `posture_override_active` back to false. A recomputation may only reach `LOCKED` while `posture_override_active` is false; the sole path from `LOCKED` back to `RESTRICTED` is the `PlatformAdmin` override with `dimension` `DISTRIBUTION` described in §2.10. Every one of these posture moves increments the facility's `version` by one.

Approval moves the statement `DRAFT → APPROVED`, optionally with an `approval_note`. It requires that the facility's `distribution_posture` is not `LOCKED`, otherwise `ERR_DISTRIBUTION_LOCKED`, and that `total_dairy_share_cents` is less than or equal to `net_distributable_cents`, otherwise `ERR_SHARE_EXCEEDS_DISTRIBUTABLE`. Approval writes the acting `X-User-ID` into `approved_by_user_id` and increments the statement's `version` by one.

Payment moves the statement `APPROVED → PAID` and takes a mandatory `payment_reference`, which the server validates as a required string but does not retain: it is written to no field of the statement and appears nowhere in the response body. Three conditions apply. The facility's `distribution_posture` must not be `LOCKED`, otherwise `ERR_DISTRIBUTION_LOCKED`. The acting `X-User-ID` must differ from `approved_by_user_id`, otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. And the posture must be either `OPEN`, or `RESTRICTED` with `net_distributable_cents` no greater than the restricted payment cap of 1,000,000 cents (ten thousand dollars); a restricted facility attempting to pay out more than that cap is rejected with `ERR_DISTRIBUTION_RESTRICTED`. Payment writes the acting `X-User-ID` into `paid_by_user_id`, increases the facility's `dairy_share_paid_cents` by `total_dairy_share_cents`, its `retained_to_date_cents` by `retained_cents` and its `reserve_balance_cents` by `reserve_sweep_cents`, and increments both the statement's `version` and the facility's `version` by one. Payment neither recomputes the waterfall nor re-derives `dscr_current` and `distribution_posture`: the statement's own arrival at `PAID` leaves it the same driving statement it already was, so the covenant figures stand as creation left them.

Claw-back is the compensating action for payment and takes a mandatory `claw_back_reason`. From `PAID` it moves the statement to the terminal `CLAWED_BACK` state, decreases the facility's `dairy_share_paid_cents` by `total_dairy_share_cents`, its `retained_to_date_cents` by `retained_cents` and its `reserve_balance_cents` by `reserve_sweep_cents`, and then recomputes `dscr_current` and re-derives `distribution_posture` from whichever statement is now the latest `DRAFT`, `APPROVED` or `PAID` statement on the facility — leaving both figures exactly where they were if no such statement remains. The statement's `version` increments by one and the facility's `version` increments by one for the ledger movement, plus one more if that re-derivation moved `distribution_posture`. The facility's cumulative `gross_revenue_to_date_cents` counts `gross_revenue_cents` across statements in `DRAFT`, `APPROVED` or `PAID` status, while `dairy_share_paid_cents` and `retained_to_date_cents` count only statements that are `PAID`.
