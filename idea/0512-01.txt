## 53. Laboratory Equipment Sharing Platform

**Extended System Functional Description:**

Laboratory equipment sharing platform: equipment registration and qualification with equipment profile (model/specification/owner-lab), equipment-status classification (available/maintenance/retired), open-sharing scope (within-school/inter-school/external), qualification certificate (calibration/safety inspection), and equipment-photo and operation-manual upload; reservation scheduling with available time-slot configuration, slot granularity (hour/half-day/day), reservation lead time, single-user duration limit, peak-period priority rules, and cancellation rules; usage authorization approval with user qualification check (training certificate/sample-type matching), equipment-owner approval, multi-level approval for high-value equipment, urgent-use expedited approval, and authorization expiration; billing (by time / by sample) with hybrid billing modes, internal and external differentiated pricing, sample-quantity-based pricing, consumables-cost separation, and billing-rule version control; maintenance window with periodic maintenance plan, ad-hoc maintenance requirement, maintenance-period reservation blocking, maintenance record archiving, and post-maintenance acceptance; usage logs with check-in and check-out records, actual usage duration, sample information, abnormal-event records, and equipment-status handover; cost allocation with reservation-based fee accrual, owner-lab revenue share, platform service fee, refund handling, periodic settlement to owner-lab, and inter-department cost transfer; event-driven synchronization with payment, IM, and identity-verification systems; audit logging for reservation changes and authorization records.

## 54. Commercial Real Estate Leasing Management System

**Extended System Functional Description:**

Commercial real estate leasing management system: building/floor/unit hierarchy with property hierarchy modeling, unit attributes (area/orientation/usage/rent reference), unit status (vacant/leased/reserved/under renovation), and unit merge-and-split handling; tenant move-in with tenant profile, business license and qualification verification, intent letter and deposit handling, move-in checklist, and unit handover record; lease contract management with contract template, escalating rent (annual/biennial/stepped), rent-free period configuration, rent payment schedule, security deposit and guarantee, contract amendment, and renewal workflow; property fee and energy fee allocation with property-fee billing rules (by area/fixed-rate), energy-fee metering (sub-meter/allocated), public-area shared cost allocation, multi-tenant shared-meter allocation algorithm, fee bill generation, and dispute handling; move-out and handover with move-out application lead time, deposit deduction items (overdue rent/damage/cleaning), unit inspection record, restoration work confirmation, deposit settlement, and final account closing; deposit management with deposit collection and freeze, deposit adjustment on lease change, deduction approval workflow, refund execution, and deposit-account reconciliation; event-driven synchronization with payment, finance, energy-metering, and access-control systems; audit logging for contract changes and fee adjustments.
