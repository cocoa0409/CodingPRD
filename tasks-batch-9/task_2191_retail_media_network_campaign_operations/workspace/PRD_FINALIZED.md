# Retail Media Network Campaign Ops API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A retail media network sells its own shopper attention. The retailer owns the search results page, the category browse rail, the product detail module and the homepage banner; it also syndicates inventory to social and display partners off its own property. Suppliers — the brands whose products sit on the shelves — buy that attention, and they pay for it out of two very different pockets: hard cash and the co-op trade funds they have already accrued with the retailer. The platform described here is the operational backbone of that business. It onboards advertisers and sets their credit, holds their fiscal-period budgets, runs the sponsored placement auction that decides which brand appears in which slot for which shopper query, closes the loop from a click to a till receipt, measures whether the advertising was actually incremental, and finally bills the whole thing against a mixture of cash and trade fund deductions.

Three properties make this domain unusually demanding to implement. First, almost every quantity of interest is a **derived aggregate** rather than a stored balance: a budget plan's available headroom, a fund pool's drawable amount, an advertiser's credit exposure and a slot's occupancy are all recomputed from their defining populations after every mutation, and two conservation identities must hold after each one. Second, the system is **cascading**: a single stock adjustment on one SKU can pause product assignments, then line items, then whole campaigns, three levels deep, and an upheld measurement dispute can unwind an invoice, thaw a frozen trade fund pool and reactivate an exhausted budget plan in the same request. Third, delivery and billing advance on **independent status dimensions** — a campaign can still be delivering while its previous month is already invoiced and disputed — so no operation may assume the two are in step.

Everything the network exposes is mounted under `/rmn`, with two operational capabilities (a settable logical clock and a deterministic daily sweep) mounted outside that prefix so that test harnesses and schedulers can drive time explicitly. There is no wall-clock dependency anywhere in the specification: every window, flight, pacing ratio, service-level clock and payment term is evaluated against the logical clock described in §2.5.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request — without exception, including collection reads and the two operational capabilities — must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting operator, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role it acts under. If either header is absent or blank, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). If `X-User-Role` carries any string other than the eight role names enumerated below, the rejection is `ERR_UNKNOWN_ROLE`, also HTTP 401. Once the role is recognised, it is matched against the authorised-role list published for the target endpoint in §4; a role absent from that list is refused with `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403).

The eight roles are spelled exactly as follows.

- **`NetworkAdmin`** — the retail media network superuser. It owns inventory definition, the logical clock, the daily checkpoint sweep, advertiser termination and invoice write-off, and it appears on the authorised-role list of every endpoint in the system. Being a superuser does not exempt it from the segregation-of-duties rule of §2.11: a `NetworkAdmin` that submits a step is still barred from deciding that same step. `X-User-ID` for a `NetworkAdmin` is an opaque operator identifier that is not tied to any persisted entity; the server records it on fields such as `onboarding_submitted_by_user_id`, `kyc_decided_by_user_id`, `approved_by_user_id` or `issued_by_user_id` where a transition writes it, but never compares it to any row's `id`.
- **`AccountManager`** — the managed-service seller. It builds and runs campaigns on behalf of managed advertisers, submits budget plans and campaigns for review, tops plans up, manages line items and product assignments, and files or withdraws disputes for its book of business. Its identifier is the value an advertiser's `account_manager_user_id` records. `X-User-ID` for an `AccountManager` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server may record it on operator fields but does not compare it to any entity's `id`.
- **`AdvertiserUser`** — the self-serve advertiser seat. This is the only entity-backed identity in the system: when `X-User-Role` is `AdvertiserUser`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting advertiser, and ownership checks on any resource that references an advertiser compare that resource's advertiser reference to `X-User-ID`. The resolution path is explicit: a campaign resolves through its own advertiser reference, a line item resolves through its campaign's advertiser reference, an invoice resolves through its own advertiser reference, and a budget plan resolves through its own advertiser reference. Any mismatch is refused with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403). An `AdvertiserUser` can therefore never read or mutate another advertiser's plans, campaigns, invoices or disputes.
- **`CreditAnalyst`** — the credit and risk decision-maker. It renders the KYC fork, approves or returns budget plans, and suspends or reinstates advertisers. It cannot create campaigns or line items, so it structurally can never approve work it authored. Its `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity, recorded on `kyc_decided_by_user_id` and `approved_by_user_id` where the relevant transitions write it.
- **`MeasurementAnalyst`** — owner of closed-loop attribution and incrementality. It posts attributed sales, designs, starts, measures, publishes and invalidates holdout studies, and adjudicates measurement disputes. Its `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server records it on `published_by_user_id`, `assigned_analyst_user_id` and `resolved_by_user_id` where those transitions write it.
- **`BillingOps`** — the money side. It opens trade fund accounts, books accruals, posts manual deductions, freezes and unfreezes pools, opens, issues, adjusts and settles invoices, closes budget plans, completes campaigns, settles auctions and loads off-site reconciliation figures. It cannot decide disputes, so credit awards remain with measurement. Its `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity, recorded on `issued_by_user_id` where invoice issuance writes it.
- **`CatalogOps`** — the retail merchandising side. It registers SKUs, posts the stock movements that drive the out-of-stock pausing cascade, restricts, reinstates and delists products, renders the line-item eligibility fork, manages product assignments and may resume a line item whose catalog blocker has cleared. Its `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity, recorded on `eligibility_decided_by_user_id` where the eligibility fork writes it.
- **`AuctionService`** — the machine identity of the ad server. It opens, ranks, awards and settles sponsored placement auctions and records impressions, clicks and off-site views. It holds no financial or measurement authority. Its `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity and is never compared to any row's `id`.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every collection read accepts two pagination parameters. `limit` defaults to 10 when omitted, with a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 100. `offset` defaults to 0 when omitted, with a minimum of 0 and no upper bound. A `limit` that is not an integer in the inclusive range 1 through 100, or an `offset` that is not an integer of at least 0, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION` (HTTP 400). Collections are always returned sorted by `created_at` ascending; no endpoint offers an alternative sort. The `meta` block of a collection response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` (the number of rows matching the filters before the window is applied) and `has_more` (true when `offset` plus the number of returned rows is strictly less than `total_count`).

Filters are all optional, are combined with logical conjunction, and never change the sort order. The advertiser collection filters on `parent_advertiser_id`, `onboarding_status`, `service_model` and `brand_code`. Budget plans filter on `advertiser_id`, `fiscal_period_code` and `plan_status`. Trade fund accounts filter on `advertiser_id`, `fiscal_period_code` and `account_status`. Campaigns filter on `advertiser_id`, `budget_plan_id`, `delivery_status`, `billing_status` and `channel_mix`. Placement slots filter on `channel`, `page_type`, `slot_status` and `slot_code`. Line items filter on `campaign_id`, `placement_slot_id`, `line_item_status`, `priority_tier` and `category_code`. Catalog products filter on `category_code`, `eligibility_status` and `sku`. Product assignments filter on `line_item_id`, `catalog_product_id`, `assignment_status` and `is_hero`. Auction events filter on `placement_slot_id`, `winning_line_item_id`, `status` and `shopper_segment`. The bid ledger filters on `auction_event_id`, `line_item_id`, `outcome` and `filter_reason`. Engagement events filter on `line_item_id`, `auction_event_id`, `catalog_product_id`, `event_type` and `channel`. Attributed sales filter on `engagement_event_id`, `line_item_id`, `catalog_product_id`, `restated` and `within_window`. Incrementality studies filter on `campaign_id`, `supersedes_study_id`, `design_method` and `study_status`. Invoices filter on `advertiser_id`, `budget_plan_id`, `trade_fund_account_id`, `billing_period_code` and `invoice_status`. Measurement disputes filter on `invoice_id`, `line_item_id`, `dispute_category`, `dispute_status` and `sla_breached`.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and Version Counters

Every persisted row carries an `id` that is a server-minted UUID with no type prefix and no embedded meaning, and a `created_at` timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC notation with exactly three fractional-second digits. Most resources additionally carry `updated_at`, in the same format, refreshed on every mutation that touches the row, and `version`, an integer that starts at 1 when the row is created and increases by exactly 1 on every mutation of that row — including mutations the row suffers as a bystander in somebody else's cascade. The full set of resources carrying `updated_at` and `version` is: advertisers, budget plans, trade fund accounts, campaigns, placement slots, line items, catalog products, product assignments, auction events, attributed sales, incrementality studies, invoices and measurement disputes. Every other timestamp-valued field of a resource is rendered in exactly that same notation — ISO 8601 UTC with exactly three fractional-second digits — whether the client supplied it or a transition stamped it from the logical clock, and it is rendered as `null` when the row holds no instant for it. This applies without exception to the window, flight, occurrence, stamping and deadline fields alike, so a client that supplied `2026-08-05T10:00:00Z` reads it back as `2026-08-05T10:00:00.000Z`; a supplied instant carrying finer precision than a millisecond is truncated to whole milliseconds before it is stored, compared or rendered.

Two resources are deliberately append-only and therefore carry **only** `id` and `created_at` — they have no `updated_at` field and no `version` field, and no endpoint accepts a version expectation against them: the auction bid ledger rows and the engagement event rows. A bid row is written once at ranking time and its `outcome` is fixed at award time as part of the auction's own mutation; an engagement event is written once at ingestion and its `billable_cents` is filled in by the auction's settlement, again as part of the auction's mutation rather than the event's.

Every computed field named anywhere in this document is part of the resource representation and is returned in `data` alongside the input fields — a client reading a budget plan sees `cash_share_cents`, `reserved_cents`, `consumed_cents`, `available_cents` and `top_up_total_cents`; a client reading a campaign sees `delivered_spend_cents`, `offsite_variance_pct`, `attributed_revenue_cents`, `incremental_revenue_cents`, `roas`, `iroas`, `pacing_index` and `oos_paused_line_item_count`; a client reading an invoice sees the full waterfall decomposition. Computed fields are never accepted as inputs, and a request that supplies one is treated as carrying an unrecognised field rather than as an instruction.

Nested paths are validated left to right. A request whose leading path segment names a row that does not exist is refused on that row before any deeper segment or any body field is examined, and the existence codes of §2.12 fire in the entity order given there.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock and the Daily Checkpoint

Nothing in this system reads real wall-clock time. A single logical clock, referred to throughout as the current logical time, governs every flight window, plan window, measurement window, pacing ratio, service-level deadline and payment term. Before the clock has ever been set it reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`; this startup default is mandatory and the system never falls back to real time. A `NetworkAdmin` moves the clock by supplying a `current_utc` instant, which replaces the current logical time outright — forwards or backwards. Setting the clock has deliberately narrow effects: it does **not** re-evaluate any derived field, does not fire any elapsed-window transition, and does not refresh any breach flag.

Re-evaluation is the job of a separate `NetworkAdmin`-only sweep, the daily checkpoint, which accepts an optional `as_of_date`; when `as_of_date` is omitted the sweep evaluates at the current logical time, and when it is supplied the sweep evaluates as of that date. The sweep is deterministic and idempotent for a given clock position, and performs exactly four families of work: it recomputes every campaign's `pacing_index` and pacing allowance; it refreshes every dispute's `sla_breached` flag where the clock has passed the dispute's `sla_due_at` with no recorded first response, escalating the dispute where the state machine says so; it closes campaign flights whose end instant the clock has reached, promoting approved plans whose windows have opened, activating scheduled campaigns whose flights have started and completing delivering or paused campaigns whose flights have ended; and it exhausts budget plans whose recomputed headroom has drained. It is also the mechanism by which a trade fund pool reaches reconciliation at fiscal-period end and closure one day later.

### 2.6. Numeric Precision and Rounding

All money in this system is expressed in integer cents. No monetary field is ever fractional, and every monetary rounding uses round-half-up: a value whose fractional part is exactly one half rounds away from zero. Where a formula in §3 specifies a rounding point, that point is normative — rounding earlier or later than specified produces a different, wrong figure, and the differences are frequently one cent.

Fractional quantities are held to a fixed number of decimal places, again with round-half-up. Relevance scores, quality-adjusted ranking scores, attribution weights, lift ratios, significance scores and pacing indices are held to four decimal places. Off-site variance percentages are held to two decimal places. Return-on-ad-spend ratios are held to four decimal places. Rate-style inputs — a managed-service fee rate, a deduction cap fraction, a holdout fraction, a relevance exponent, a gross-margin fraction — are stored as supplied and are not rounded.

Two ratio fields divide by a quantity that can legitimately be zero. A campaign's `roas` is its `attributed_revenue_cents` divided by the sum of `delivered_spend_cents` and `offsite_reported_spend_cents`; its `iroas` is its `incremental_revenue_cents` divided by that same sum. When that sum is zero, both ratios are `0.0` rather than undefined. Both are rounded half-up to four decimal places.

### 2.7. Stale-Parent Rejection on Child Creation

Six creation endpoints hang a new child off an existing parent whose state the client must have observed before it chose the child's parameters. Each of those endpoints takes a **non-optional** version expectation naming its parent, and a mismatch against the parent's current `version` is a hard rejection with HTTP 412. No other endpoint in the system takes a version expectation of any kind, and supplying one elsewhere has no effect.

- Opening a budget plan requires `expected_advertiser_version` to equal the target advertiser's current `version`. A freshly created draft plan for a fiscal period legitimately supersedes any earlier draft plan for that same advertiser and period, so a writer holding a stale view of the advertiser would silently discard budget setup performed after its read. A mismatch is refused with `ERR_STALE_ADVERTISER_VERSION` (HTTP 412).
- Creating a line item requires `expected_campaign_version` to equal the parent campaign's current `version`. Line-item creation consumes placement-slot capacity and rewrites the campaign's channel coverage, so a writer whose view of the campaign predates another line-item creation would base its slot and flight-window choices on superseded campaign state. A mismatch is refused with `ERR_STALE_CAMPAIGN_VERSION` (HTTP 412).
- Creating a product assignment requires `expected_line_item_version` to equal the parent line item's current `version`. A new assignment marked as the hero product supersedes the previous hero on the same line item, so a stale writer would demote a hero product chosen after its read. A mismatch is refused with `ERR_STALE_LINE_ITEM_VERSION` (HTTP 412).
- Posting a manual trade fund deduction requires `expected_trade_fund_account_version` to equal the target pool's current `version`. Manual deductions are legitimate whenever the pool's drawable amount covers the request, so this check exists purely to stop a writer from drawing against a balance it read before an earlier deduction, accrual or invoice-driven recomputation changed it. A mismatch is refused with `ERR_STALE_TRADE_FUND_VERSION` (HTTP 412).
- Filing a measurement dispute requires `expected_invoice_version` to equal the target invoice's current `version`. Filing is legitimate against any issued or already-disputed invoice inside the grace window, so the check exists to stop a writer from claiming an amount computed against a net-cash figure that an earlier dispute, adjustment or credit already changed. A mismatch is refused with `ERR_STALE_INVOICE_VERSION` (HTTP 412).
- Designing an incrementality study requires `expected_campaign_version` to equal the parent campaign's current `version`. A restatement study that names a study it supersedes replaces the campaign's currently published measurement, so a stale writer would restate a figure that a newer study already replaced. A mismatch is refused with `ERR_STALE_CAMPAIGN_VERSION` (HTTP 412) — the same code the line-item guard uses, because both guard the same parent entity.

### 2.8. Interval Geometry: Flights, Windows and Buffers

Every time range in this specification is **half-open**: the start instant is inclusive and the end instant is exclusive. Two ranges overlap when, and only when, the first range's start is strictly before the second range's end and the second range's start is strictly before the first range's end. Adjacency is consequently never a collision: a range that ends at exactly the instant another begins does not overlap it. Timestamp comparison is performed at millisecond resolution.

Containment follows the same convention. A line item's flight lies inside its campaign's flight when the line item starts at or after the campaign's start and ends at or before the campaign's end, and a campaign's flight lies inside its funding plan's window on the same terms. In both cases the range's own start must be strictly before its own end; a zero-length or inverted range is invalid.

Two rules deliberately break plain overlap. On an off-site placement slot that declares a non-zero adjacency buffer, the candidate line item's flight is inflated by that many whole minutes on **both** sides before the overlap test is applied, so touching and near-touching flights do collide even though they pass the plain-overlap capacity test. And measurement study windows on the same campaign are compared by **clear gap** rather than by overlap: two windows collide when the gap between them is strictly less than the washout period, so even fully disjoint windows separated by three days collide.

### 2.9. The Two Conservation Ledgers

The platform maintains two independent cent-conserving identities, and both must hold after **every** mutation, not merely at rest.

The **budget identity** governs a budget plan: its `committed_total_cents` plus its `top_up_total_cents` always equals its `available_cents` plus its `reserved_cents` plus its `consumed_cents`. Reservation and release are therefore strictly compensating. When a campaign is approved for delivery its allocation becomes reserved; when that campaign is archived straight out of the scheduled state, the allocation is released and the plan's headroom returns to exactly the value it held before the reservation. `reserved_cents` is not a stored balance: it is at all times the sum of `allocated_budget_cents` over the plan's campaigns whose delivery status is scheduled, delivering or paused. Draft, pending-review, completed, rejected and archived campaigns reserve nothing.

The **trade fund identity** governs a fund pool: its `accrued_cents` always equals its `available_cents` plus its `reserved_cents` plus its `deducted_cents`. Accruals raise the accrued total and the drawable amount together. A plan submission moves its trade-fund share from drawable to reserved; a revision return or a plan closure moves it back. An invoice issuance moves the computed deduction from drawable to deducted; the write-off that compensates that issuance returns exactly the same amount, leaving `accrued_cents` untouched throughout. `available_cents` on a pool is never allowed below zero by a manual deduction, and invoice issuance may drive it to exactly zero — never below — because the deduction is capped at the drawable amount when it would otherwise overdraw.

### 2.10. Parallel Status Dimensions and Cascade Depth

A campaign has **two** independent status dimensions. `delivery_status` tracks how the campaign is running and takes the values `DRAFT`, `PENDING_REVIEW`, `SCHEDULED`, `DELIVERING`, `PAUSED`, `COMPLETED`, `REJECTED` and `ARCHIVED`, of which `REJECTED` and `ARCHIVED` are terminal. `billing_status` tracks how the campaign is being paid for and takes the values `UNBILLED`, `ACCRUING`, `PENDING_INVOICE`, `INVOICED`, `DISPUTED` and `RECONCILED`, of which `RECONCILED` is terminal. The two advance on separate event streams — a campaign may be `DELIVERING` while already `INVOICED` for a prior period, or `COMPLETED` while still `ACCRUING`. No delivery transition is permitted to write the billing field directly; where the narrative in §3 says a delivery event moves the billing dimension, what happens is that the delivery event fires the corresponding *billing* transition, which then applies its own preconditions and bumps the campaign's `version` in the same request.

Cascades run to depth three and are described level by level in §3. The invariant that makes them tractable is that all of the following are derived counts or sums, recomputed from their defining populations rather than incremented ad hoc: a slot's `occupancy_count` (the number of line items on the slot whose status is `ELIGIBLE`, `SERVING`, `OOS_PAUSED` or `MANUAL_PAUSED`), a line item's `eligible_product_count` (the number of its active assignments whose product is eligible), a campaign's `oos_paused_line_item_count` (the number of its line items in `OOS_PAUSED`), a plan's `reserved_cents` and `consumed_cents`, a pool's `reserved_cents` and `deducted_cents`, and an advertiser's `credit_exposure_cents` and `dispute_credit_balance_cents`. A direct consequence, and one implementers routinely get wrong: **pausing a line item does not change its slot's `occupancy_count`, and does not change its plan's `reserved_cents`**, because a paused line item still occupies its slot and its campaign is still in a reserving delivery status. Occupancy is surrendered only when a line item reaches `ENDED`, and a plan's reservation is released only when the owning campaign leaves the scheduled/delivering/paused set.

Every row a cascade mutates has its `version` incremented by one and its `updated_at` refreshed, including rows several levels down that the caller never named.

### 2.11. Segregation of Duties Across Approval Chains

Five approval chains in this system have two sides, and one operator may not stand on both. The acting `X-User-ID` must differ from the operator recorded for the preceding step: the advertiser's `onboarding_submitted_by_user_id` when a KYC decision is rendered; the plan's `submitted_by_user_id` when a budget plan is approved; the campaign's `submitted_for_review_by_user_id` when a campaign review decision is rendered; the invoice's `issued_by_user_id` when an invoice is written off; and the `published_by_user_id` of the campaign's most recent published incrementality study when a dispute whose category is `INCREMENTALITY_RESTATEMENT` is resolved. Any of these five collisions is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). The rule binds every role including `NetworkAdmin`.

### 2.12. Rejection Precedence

When a single request violates several rules, the response is determined by exactly one of them: the checks below are evaluated **strictly in the order listed**, and the first one that fails supplies both the `error_id` and the HTTP status. A later check never masks an earlier one, and an earlier failure means later checks are not evaluated at all. The list is the complete rejection contract of the platform.

1. Both `X-User-Role` and `X-User-ID` are present and non-blank on every request — otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`, 401.
2. `X-User-Role` is one of `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager`, `AdvertiserUser`, `CreditAnalyst`, `MeasurementAnalyst`, `BillingOps`, `CatalogOps`, `AuctionService`, on every request — otherwise `ERR_UNKNOWN_ROLE`, 401.
3. The acting role appears in the target endpoint's authorised-role list, on every request — otherwise `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED`, 403.
4. On every endpoint whose authorised roles include `AdvertiserUser`: when the acting role is `AdvertiserUser`, `X-User-ID` equals the advertiser that owns the targeted resource, resolved through a campaign reference to that campaign's advertiser, through a line-item reference to its campaign's advertiser, through an invoice reference to that invoice's advertiser, and through a budget-plan reference to that plan's advertiser — otherwise `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`, 403.
5. On every creating, replacing and patching request: the body carries every required input field with its declared type, every enum-typed field carries a declared member of that enum, and every integer cent field meets its stated minimum — otherwise `ERR_VALIDATION`, 422.
6. On every collection read: `limit` is an integer between 1 and 100 inclusive and `offset` is an integer of at least 0 — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION`, 400.
7. On every endpoint referencing an advertiser: the advertiser named in the path, in `advertiser_id`, or in `parent_advertiser_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_ADVERTISER_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
8. On every endpoint referencing a budget plan: the plan named in the path or in `budget_plan_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_BUDGET_PLAN_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
9. On every endpoint referencing a trade fund account: the account named in the path or in `trade_fund_account_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_TRADE_FUND_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
10. On every endpoint referencing a campaign: the campaign named in the path or in `campaign_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_CAMPAIGN_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
11. On every endpoint referencing a placement slot: the slot named in the path or in `placement_slot_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_PLACEMENT_SLOT_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
12. On every endpoint referencing a line item: the line item named in the path or in `line_item_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_LINE_ITEM_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
13. On every endpoint referencing a catalog product: the product named in the path or in `catalog_product_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_CATALOG_PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
14. On the single-assignment read and the assignment removal: the product assignment named in the path exists — otherwise `ERR_PRODUCT_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
15. On every endpoint referencing an auction event: the auction named in the path or in `auction_event_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_AUCTION_EVENT_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
16. On the single engagement-event read and on attributed-sale ingestion: the engagement named in the path or in `engagement_event_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_ENGAGEMENT_EVENT_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
17. On every endpoint referencing a study: the study named in the path or in `supersedes_study_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_STUDY_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
18. On every endpoint referencing an invoice: the invoice named in the path or in `invoice_id` exists — otherwise `ERR_INVOICE_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
19. On every dispute action endpoint: the dispute named in the path exists — otherwise `ERR_DISPUTE_NOT_FOUND`, 404.
20. On budget plan creation: `expected_advertiser_version` equals the advertiser's current `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_ADVERTISER_VERSION`, 412.
21. On line item creation and on study creation: `expected_campaign_version` equals the campaign's current `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_CAMPAIGN_VERSION`, 412.
22. On product assignment creation: `expected_line_item_version` equals the line item's current `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_LINE_ITEM_VERSION`, 412.
23. On manual trade fund deduction: `expected_trade_fund_account_version` equals the pool's current `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_TRADE_FUND_VERSION`, 412.
24. On dispute filing: `expected_invoice_version` equals the invoice's current `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_INVOICE_VERSION`, 412.
25. On every mutating endpoint that resolves to an advertiser: that advertiser's `onboarding_status` is neither `REJECTED` nor `TERMINATED` — otherwise `ERR_ADVERTISER_TERMINATED`, 409.
26. On onboarding submission, KYC decision, suspension, reinstatement and termination: the requested onboarding transition is one declared in §3.1 for the advertiser's current `onboarding_status` — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_ONBOARDING_TRANSITION`, 409.
27. On budget plan approval, campaign review decision, campaign resume, line item activation and study start: the advertiser's `onboarding_status` is `ACTIVE` — otherwise `ERR_ADVERTISER_NOT_ACTIVE`, 409. This check governs those five capabilities only; other transitions tolerate a non-active advertiser as long as check 25 passes.
28. On plan submission, approval, return-for-revision, top-up and closure: the requested plan transition is one declared for the current `plan_status` — otherwise `ERR_PLAN_INVALID_STATE`, 409.
29. On campaign review submission the funding plan's `plan_status` is `APPROVED` or `ACTIVE`, and on campaign resume and line item activation it is `ACTIVE` — otherwise `ERR_PLAN_NOT_FUNDABLE`, 409.
30. On campaign review submission, review decision, pause, resume, complete and archive: the requested delivery transition is one declared for the current `delivery_status` — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_CAMPAIGN_TRANSITION`, 409.
31. On line item activation and line item resume: the owning campaign's `delivery_status` is `DELIVERING` — otherwise `ERR_CAMPAIGN_NOT_DELIVERING`, 409.
32. On line item eligibility submission, eligibility decision, activation, pause, resume and end: the requested line item transition is one declared for the current `line_item_status` — otherwise `ERR_LINE_ITEM_INVALID_STATE`, 409.
33. On the line item eligibility decision, on opening an auction, and on a slot amendment: the slot's `slot_status` is `OPEN` where occupancy or an auction is being requested, and a `RETIRED` slot can never be reopened — otherwise `ERR_SLOT_NOT_OPEN`, 409.
34. On product assignment creation, stock adjustment, restriction, reinstatement and delisting: the catalog product's `eligibility_status` is not `DELISTED` — otherwise `ERR_PRODUCT_DELISTED`, 409.
35. On auction ranking, award and settlement: the requested auction transition is declared for the current auction `status` — ranking requires `OPEN`, awarding requires `RANKED`, settling requires either `AWARDED` or `NO_FILL` — otherwise `ERR_AUCTION_INVALID_STATE`, 409.
36. On study start, measure, publish and invalidate: the requested transition is one declared for the current `study_status` — otherwise `ERR_STUDY_INVALID_STATE`, 409.
37. On invoice issuance, adjustment, settlement and write-off: the requested transition is one declared for the current `invoice_status` — otherwise `ERR_INVOICE_INVALID_STATE`, 409.
38. On dispute start-review, request-info, provide-info, resolve, withdraw and escalate: the requested transition is one declared for the current `dispute_status` — otherwise `ERR_DISPUTE_INVALID_STATE`, 409.
39. On fund accruals, manual deductions and invoice issuance drawing on a pool: the pool's `account_status` is `OPEN`; `FROZEN`, `RECONCILING` and `CLOSED` all reject — otherwise `ERR_TRADE_FUND_FROZEN`, 409.
40. On KYC decision, plan approval, campaign review decision, invoice write-off and dispute resolution: the segregation-of-duties rule of §2.11 holds — otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, 403.
41. On product assignment creation and on the line item eligibility decision: every referenced catalog product has `eligibility_status` equal to `ELIGIBLE`; a product that is `RESTRICTED` or `OUT_OF_STOCK` is refused regardless of any other defect in the request — otherwise `ERR_PRODUCT_RESTRICTED`, 422.
42. On onboarding submission: `kyc_document_count` is at least 2 on a first submission and at least 3 on a re-submission out of `CREDIT_HOLD` — otherwise `ERR_KYC_DOCUMENTS_INSUFFICIENT`, 422.
43. On dispute resolution: a dispute whose `sla_breached` is true and whose `escalation_level` is 1 is not resolved with a rejection decision — otherwise `ERR_SLA_BREACH_REJECTION_FORBIDDEN`, 409.
44. On study publication: the study's `significance_flag` is true — otherwise `ERR_STUDY_NOT_SIGNIFICANT`, 422.
45. On advertiser creation and advertiser amendment: a `SELF_SERVE` advertiser carries a `managed_service_fee_pct` of exactly 0.0 and a null `account_manager_user_id`, and a `MANAGED` advertiser carries a non-null `account_manager_user_id` — otherwise `ERR_SERVICE_MODEL_CONFLICT`, 422.
46. On line item creation: the line item's flight lies entirely inside the owning campaign's flight under half-open semantics, with its own start strictly before its own end — otherwise `ERR_FLIGHT_WINDOW_OUTSIDE_CAMPAIGN`, 422.
47. On campaign creation: the campaign's flight lies entirely inside the funding plan's window — otherwise `ERR_FLIGHT_WINDOW_OUTSIDE_PLAN`, 422.
48. On plan approval: no other plan of the same advertiser whose `plan_status` is `APPROVED` or `ACTIVE` has a window overlapping this plan's window under half-open semantics — otherwise `ERR_PLAN_WINDOW_OVERLAP`, 409.
49. On engagement ingestion: the engagement's `occurred_at` lies inside the winning line item's flight and is at or after the auction's `requested_at` — otherwise `ERR_ENGAGEMENT_OUTSIDE_FLIGHT`, 422.
50. On study creation and study start: the washout gap rule holds against every study on the same campaign whose `study_status` is `RUNNING`, `MEASURING` or `PUBLISHED`, unless the candidate supersedes that exact study — otherwise `ERR_STUDY_WASHOUT_VIOLATION`, 409.
51. On dispute filing the current logical time is strictly before the invoice's `due_at` plus the dispute grace period, and on invoice write-off the current logical time is at or after the invoice's `due_at` plus the write-off period — otherwise `ERR_DISPUTE_WINDOW_CLOSED`, 409.
52. On study start the current logical time is at or after the study's `window_start_at`, and on study measurement it is at or after the study's `window_end_at` — otherwise `ERR_STUDY_WINDOW_NOT_ELAPSED`, 409.
53. On the line item eligibility decision: the aggregate-capacity rule holds — the number of overlapping occupying line items on the slot is strictly less than the slot's `max_concurrent_line_items` — otherwise `ERR_SLOT_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`, 409.
54. On the line item eligibility decision: the category-exclusivity rule holds — no overlapping occupying line item on an exclusivity slot shares the candidate's `category_code` — otherwise `ERR_SLOT_CATEGORY_EXCLUSIVE`, 409.
55. On the line item eligibility decision: the adjacency-buffer rule holds — on an off-site slot with a non-zero `buffer_minutes`, no occupying line item overlaps the candidate flight inflated by that buffer on both sides — otherwise `ERR_SLOT_BUFFER_CONFLICT`, 409.
56. On advertiser creation: `brand_code` is unique across all advertisers — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_BRAND_CODE`, 409.
57. On catalog product creation and placement slot creation: `sku` is unique across all catalog products and `slot_code` is unique across all placement slots — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_RESOURCE_CODE`, 409.
58. On trade fund account creation: the pairing of `advertiser_id` with `fiscal_period_code` is unique across fund accounts — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_TRADE_FUND_PERIOD`, 409.
59. On attributed sale ingestion: the triple of `retail_order_ref`, `catalog_product_id` and `line_item_id` is unique across attributed sales — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_ATTRIBUTED_SALE`, 409.
60. On invoice creation: the pairing of `advertiser_id` with `billing_period_code` is unique across invoices — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_INVOICE_PERIOD`, 409.
61. On line item eligibility submission the line item has at least one assignment whose `assignment_status` is `ACTIVE` and whose `is_hero` is true, and on assignment removal the last such hero assignment of a line item that is not in `DRAFT` may not be removed — otherwise `ERR_HERO_ASSIGNMENT_REQUIRED`, 422.
62. On line item eligibility submission and line item activation: `eligible_product_count` is at least 1 — otherwise `ERR_NO_ELIGIBLE_PRODUCTS`, 422.
63. On advertiser creation and advertiser amendment: `parent_advertiser_id` differs from the advertiser's own `id`, the referenced parent itself has a null `parent_advertiser_id`, and that parent's `onboarding_status` is `ACTIVE` — otherwise `ERR_PARENT_ADVERTISER_INVALID`, 422.
64. On line item creation and campaign review submission: a campaign whose `channel_mix` is `ONSITE_ONLY` has no line item on an off-site slot, a campaign whose `channel_mix` is `OFFSITE_ONLY` has no line item on an on-site slot, and an `OMNI` campaign has at least one line item on each channel before review submission — otherwise `ERR_OFFSITE_CHANNEL_MISMATCH`, 422.
65. On a dispute information request: `info_request_count` is strictly less than the maximum of 2 — otherwise `ERR_MAX_INFO_REQUESTS_EXCEEDED`, 409.
66. On invoice adjustment `adjustment_round` is strictly less than the maximum of 2, and on a plan return-for-revision `revision_round` does not exceed the maximum of 3 — otherwise `ERR_MAX_ROUNDS_EXCEEDED`, 409.
67. On campaign creation, line item creation, product assignment creation, attributed sale ingestion and invoice creation: all cross-referenced rows resolve to the same advertiser — a campaign's funding plan belongs to the campaign's advertiser, a line item's campaign belongs to the acting advertiser, an attributed sale's engagement belongs to the same line item, and an invoice's fund account belongs to the invoice's advertiser — otherwise `ERR_CROSS_ADVERTISER_REFERENCE`, 422.
68. On invoice creation: the referenced fund account's `fiscal_period_code` equals the `fiscal_period_code` of the invoice's funding plan — otherwise `ERR_FISCAL_PERIOD_MISMATCH`, 422.
69. On engagement ingestion: the engagement's `line_item_id` equals the referenced auction's `winning_line_item_id`, and its `catalog_product_id` is assigned to that line item with `assignment_status` equal to `ACTIVE` — otherwise `ERR_ENGAGEMENT_AUCTION_MISMATCH`, 422.
70. On study creation: `supersedes_study_id` references a study on the same campaign whose `study_status` is `PUBLISHED` — otherwise `ERR_SUPERSEDED_STUDY_INVALID`, 422.
71. On budget plan creation and campaign creation: `trade_fund_share_cents` does not exceed `committed_total_cents`, `daily_cap_cents` does not exceed `allocated_budget_cents`, and for a `SELF_SERVE` advertiser `daily_cap_cents` does not exceed `self_serve_daily_spend_cap_cents` — otherwise `ERR_CAP_EXCEEDS_PARENT_LIMIT`, 422.
72. On campaign review submission the campaign's `allocated_budget_cents` does not exceed the plan's `available_cents`, and on line item activation, line item resume and campaign resume the line item's `daily_budget_cents` does not exceed the plan's `available_cents` — otherwise `ERR_PLAN_BUDGET_INSUFFICIENT`, 402.
73. On plan approval: the advertiser's `credit_exposure_cents` plus the plan's `cash_share_cents` does not exceed the effective credit limit, which is the parent advertiser's `credit_limit_cents` when `parent_advertiser_id` is non-null and the advertiser's own `credit_limit_cents` otherwise — otherwise `ERR_CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, 402.
74. On manual deduction the pool's `available_cents` is at least the requested amount, and on plan submission it is at least the plan's `trade_fund_share_cents` — otherwise `ERR_TRADE_FUND_INSUFFICIENT`, 402.
75. On plan top-up `top_up_amount_cents` is at least 50,000 cents, and on a fund accrual `accrual_amount_cents` is at least 1,000 cents — otherwise `ERR_AMOUNT_BELOW_MINIMUM`, 422.
76. On dispute filing: `claimed_amount_cents` does not exceed the invoice's `net_cash_due_cents` plus its `trade_fund_deduction_cents` — otherwise `ERR_CLAIM_EXCEEDS_INVOICE`, 422.
77. On dispute resolution: `approved_credit_amount_cents` does not exceed the dispute's `claimed_amount_cents` — otherwise `ERR_CREDIT_EXCEEDS_CLAIM`, 422.
78. On invoice settlement: `paid_amount_cents` equals the invoice's `net_cash_due_cents` exactly — otherwise `ERR_SETTLEMENT_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`, 422.
79. On invoice issuance: the computed `gross_spend_cents` is at least 1; an invoice with nothing to bill cannot be issued — otherwise `ERR_NOTHING_TO_INVOICE`, 422.

Two consequences of this ordering deserve emphasis because they are easy to get backwards. A stale parent version is detected *after* existence but *before* any state or business check, so a client that both holds a stale version and requests an undeclared transition sees the stale-version code, not the transition code. And the restricted-product refusal at position 41 sits *above* every window, capacity and fiscal check, so a line item carrying a restricted product fails with `ERR_PRODUCT_RESTRICTED` even when it also breaches slot capacity or the campaign's channel mix.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Advertiser Onboarding, Credit Exposure and the Agency Umbrella

An advertiser is registered by a `NetworkAdmin` or an `AccountManager`. Registration requires `legal_name`, `brand_code`, `service_model` and `credit_limit_cents`, and optionally accepts `parent_advertiser_id`, `account_manager_user_id`, `managed_service_fee_pct` and `self_serve_daily_spend_cap_cents`. `brand_code` is an uppercase alphanumeric token of at least three and at most twelve characters drawn from the letters A through Z and the digits 0 through 9, and it must be unique across every advertiser row regardless of that row's onboarding state — a rejected or terminated advertiser still holds its code. `service_model` is either `SELF_SERVE` or `MANAGED` and defaults to `SELF_SERVE` when omitted. `credit_limit_cents` is at least 0 and defaults to 0. `managed_service_fee_pct` is a rate between 0.0 and 0.20 inclusive, defaulting to 0.0. `self_serve_daily_spend_cap_cents` defaults to 500,000 cents. The service-model consistency rule binds both at registration and at amendment: a `SELF_SERVE` advertiser must carry a `managed_service_fee_pct` of exactly 0.0 and a null `account_manager_user_id`, while a `MANAGED` advertiser must carry a non-null `account_manager_user_id`. The advertiser is born with `onboarding_status` `DRAFT`, `suspension_reason` `NONE`, `kyc_document_count` 0, and `credit_exposure_cents` and `dispute_credit_balance_cents` both 0.

`parent_advertiser_id` models the agency umbrella and is exactly one level deep. It may not equal the advertiser's own `id`, the referenced parent must itself carry a null `parent_advertiser_id`, and the parent must be `ACTIVE`. A child does not roll up into the parent's exposure, but a child evaluates its own exposure against the **parent's** `credit_limit_cents` rather than its own. Amendment accepts `legal_name`, `service_model`, `credit_limit_cents`, `parent_advertiser_id`, `account_manager_user_id`, `managed_service_fee_pct` and `self_serve_daily_spend_cap_cents`, all optional; omitted fields keep their stored values, and the service-model and umbrella rules are re-applied to the post-amendment combination.

Onboarding runs as a linear submission followed by a three-way credit fork. Submission is available to an `AdvertiserUser`, an `AccountManager` or a `NetworkAdmin`, requires `kyc_document_count`, and is accepted from `DRAFT` and from `CREDIT_HOLD`. A first submission out of `DRAFT` requires at least 2 documents; a remediation submission out of `CREDIT_HOLD` requires at least 3, because remediation costs one extra document. In both cases the parent advertiser, when one is named, must be `ACTIVE`, and a `MANAGED` advertiser must have a non-null `account_manager_user_id`. Submission stamps `onboarding_submitted_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID` and leaves the advertiser in `KYC_REVIEW`: the record passes transiently through `SUBMITTED` and the observable `onboarding_status` in the response is `KYC_REVIEW`. Should an advertiser ever be observed in `SUBMITTED`, a `CreditAnalyst` may advance it to `KYC_REVIEW` by rendering a `decision` of `START_REVIEW`, which likewise stamps `kyc_decided_by_user_id`.

The credit fork is rendered by a `CreditAnalyst` or a `NetworkAdmin` from `KYC_REVIEW` with a `decision` of `APPROVE`, `HOLD` or `REJECT`, an optional `decision_note`, and an optional `adjusted_credit_limit_cents` which, when supplied, replaces `credit_limit_cents` **before** the approval test is applied. All three branches stamp `kyc_decided_by_user_id` and increment the advertiser's `version` by 1, and all three are blocked when the acting `X-User-ID` equals `onboarding_submitted_by_user_id`. Beyond that they diverge sharply. `APPROVE` requires that `credit_limit_cents` be at least 100,000 cents **or** that `parent_advertiser_id` be non-null (an umbrella child borrows the parent's limit and so needs no floor of its own); it lands the advertiser in `ACTIVE`, sets `suspension_reason` to `NONE`, and recomputes `credit_exposure_cents`. `HOLD` lands the advertiser in `CREDIT_HOLD`; unlike approval it does **not** clear `suspension_reason` and does not unlock budget-plan approval, and the advertiser stays there until a remediation submission carrying at least three documents returns it to `KYC_REVIEW` — a cycle that may repeat without bound. `REJECT` lands the advertiser in `REJECTED`, which is terminal: no further onboarding transition is accepted and every mutating request that resolves to that advertiser is refused.

Suspension and reinstatement form the second cycle. A `CreditAnalyst` or `NetworkAdmin` suspends an `ACTIVE` advertiser, supplying `suspension_reason` as one of `CREDIT_BREACH`, `COMPLIANCE` or `MANUAL`; the system itself suspends with `CREDIT_BREACH` when invoice issuance drives exposure past the limit. Suspension runs a three-level cascade. At level one, every campaign of the advertiser whose `delivery_status` is `SCHEDULED` or `DELIVERING` moves to `PAUSED`. At level two, each campaign the first level paused moves every one of its own line items whose `line_item_status` is `ELIGIBLE` or `SERVING` to `MANUAL_PAUSED`, stamping `pause_reason` as `MANUAL` and `last_pause_at` with the current logical time. At level three, each line item that just entered `MANUAL_PAUSED` triggers a recomputation of `occupancy_count` on its placement slot; the recomputed figure is **unchanged**, because `MANUAL_PAUSED` remains an occupying status. Every campaign and line item touched by levels one and two has its `version` incremented by 1, as does the advertiser. Plan-level `reserved_cents` is likewise unchanged, since `PAUSED` is still a reserving delivery status. Reinstatement, available to the same two roles, requires that `credit_exposure_cents` be no greater than `credit_limit_cents` and that no invoice of the advertiser be sitting in `ISSUED` with a `due_at` already in the past; it accepts an optional `reinstatement_note`, returns the advertiser to `ACTIVE` and sets `suspension_reason` to `NONE`. Reinstatement deliberately does **not** auto-resume anything: every paused campaign must be resumed explicitly. The suspend-reinstate cycle may repeat any number of times.

Termination is exclusive to `NetworkAdmin`, requires a `termination_reason`, and is accepted from `ACTIVE` and from `SUSPENDED`. It requires that every invoice of the advertiser already be `SETTLED` or `WRITTEN_OFF` and that no budget plan of the advertiser be in `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` or `ACTIVE`. It then closes every open financial container: each campaign not already `ARCHIVED` moves to `ARCHIVED` and releases whatever it had reserved on its plan; each budget plan not already `CLOSED` or `REJECTED` moves to `CLOSED`; and each trade fund pool whose `account_status` is `OPEN` or `FROZEN` moves to `CLOSED` with `frozen_reason` set to `NONE`. `TERMINATED` is terminal.

Credit exposure is a pure aggregate, recomputed on every plan approval, closure and top-up, on every campaign review approval and archive, and on every invoice issuance, adjustment, settlement and write-off. It is the sum of `committed_total_cents` over the advertiser's own budget plans whose `plan_status` is `APPROVED`, `ACTIVE` or `EXHAUSTED`, plus the sum of `net_cash_due_cents` over the advertiser's own invoices whose `invoice_status` is `ISSUED`, `DISPUTED` or `ADJUSTED`. The effective limit against which that figure is judged is the parent advertiser's `credit_limit_cents` when `parent_advertiser_id` is non-null and the advertiser's own `credit_limit_cents` otherwise. `dispute_credit_balance_cents` is a parallel aggregate: the sum of `approved_credit_cents` over every upheld measurement dispute whose invoice is not yet `SETTLED`. A dedicated computed read returns these figures together with the remaining headroom and, for an umbrella child, the parent limit that backstops it; that read is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `CreditAnalyst`, `BillingOps` and the owning `AdvertiserUser`.

### 3.2. Fiscal-Period Funding: Budget Plans and the Trade Fund Pool

A budget plan is one advertiser's money for one retailer fiscal quarter. Creation is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser`, and requires `advertiser_id`, `fiscal_period_code`, `committed_total_cents`, `trade_fund_share_cents`, `window_start_at`, `window_end_at` and the mandatory `expected_advertiser_version`. `fiscal_period_code` is a four-digit year, a hyphen, the letter Q and a single digit from 1 to 4 — nothing else is accepted. `committed_total_cents` is at least 100,000 cents. `trade_fund_share_cents` is at least 0, at most `committed_total_cents`, and defaults to 0. `window_start_at` is the inclusive start of the spend window and `window_end_at` its exclusive end. The plan is born `DRAFT` with `revision_round` 0, a null `exhausted_at`, and a null `submitted_by_user_id` and `approved_by_user_id`.

Five figures on the plan are derived and never supplied. `cash_share_cents` is `committed_total_cents` less `trade_fund_share_cents`. `top_up_total_cents` is the cumulative sum of accepted top-up amounts. `reserved_cents` is the sum of `allocated_budget_cents` over the plan's campaigns whose `delivery_status` is `SCHEDULED`, `DELIVERING` or `PAUSED`; campaigns in `DRAFT`, `PENDING_REVIEW`, `COMPLETED`, `REJECTED` or `ARCHIVED` reserve nothing. `consumed_cents` is the sum of `settled_spend_cents` over settled auctions whose winning line item belongs to a campaign on this plan, plus the sum of `offsite_reported_spend_cents` over the plan's campaigns whose `delivery_status` is not `REJECTED`. `available_cents` is `committed_total_cents` plus `top_up_total_cents`, less `reserved_cents`, less `consumed_cents`; it may go negative only through over-delivery. No rounding occurs anywhere in this arithmetic.

Submission, available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser` with an optional `submission_note`, moves the plan from `DRAFT` to `PENDING_APPROVAL`. It requires `committed_total_cents` of at least 100,000 cents, and — when `trade_fund_share_cents` is greater than 0 — an `OPEN` trade fund pool existing for the same advertiser and the same `fiscal_period_code` with `available_cents` of at least `trade_fund_share_cents`. Submission stamps `submitted_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID` and recomputes `cash_share_cents`.

From `PENDING_APPROVAL` a `CreditAnalyst` or `NetworkAdmin` can take one of two directions. A return-for-revision, which requires a `revision_reason`, sends the plan back to `DRAFT`, increments `revision_round` by 1 and clears `submitted_by_user_id`, and is permitted while `revision_round` is strictly below 3. When the plan arrives at `PENDING_APPROVAL` with `revision_round` already equal to 3, the same request instead terminates the plan in `REJECTED`, releasing the trade fund reservation: the pool's `reserved_cents` is recomputed downward and its `available_cents` upward. Approval, which accepts an optional `approval_note`, requires that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from `submitted_by_user_id`, that the advertiser be `ACTIVE`, that `credit_exposure_cents` plus `cash_share_cents` not exceed the effective credit limit, and that no other plan of the same advertiser in `APPROVED` or `ACTIVE` have a window overlapping this plan's window. Approval stamps `approved_by_user_id`, recomputes the matching pool's `reserved_cents` and `available_cents`, and recomputes the advertiser's `credit_exposure_cents`. The approved plan lands in `APPROVED`; when the current logical time is already at or after `window_start_at` and the advertiser is `ACTIVE`, it is promoted to `ACTIVE` in the same request. Otherwise it waits, and reaches `ACTIVE` either when the daily checkpoint sweep finds the clock at or after `window_start_at`, or when the first campaign on the plan begins delivering.

An `ACTIVE` plan drains and can be refilled. When a recomputation following an auction settlement or an off-site spend posting yields `available_cents` of 0 or less, the plan moves to `EXHAUSTED` and `exhausted_at` is stamped with the current logical time. That transition cascades two levels: every campaign on the plan whose `delivery_status` is `DELIVERING` moves to `PAUSED`, and each of those campaigns moves every one of its own `SERVING` line items to `MANUAL_PAUSED` with `pause_reason` set to `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`. A top-up, available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and `CreditAnalyst`, returns an `EXHAUSTED` plan to `ACTIVE`. It requires `top_up_amount_cents` of at least 50,000 cents, accepts an optional `top_up_reason`, and requires that the advertiser be `ACTIVE` and that the current logical time be strictly before `window_end_at`. It raises `top_up_total_cents` by the supplied amount, recomputes `available_cents`, clears `exhausted_at` back to null, and recomputes the advertiser's exposure. Campaigns and line items that the exhaustion cascade paused are **not** auto-resumed by a top-up; they must be resumed explicitly. The active-exhausted-active cycle may repeat.

Closure is exclusive to `BillingOps` and `NetworkAdmin`, accepts an optional `closure_note`, and is accepted from both `ACTIVE` and `EXHAUSTED`. It requires the current logical time to be at or after `window_end_at`, no campaign on the plan to be in `SCHEDULED`, `DELIVERING` or `PAUSED`, and every invoice referencing the plan to be `SETTLED` or `WRITTEN_OFF`. Closure releases the residual trade fund reservation — the pool's `reserved_cents` recomputed downward and its `available_cents` upward — and recomputes the advertiser's exposure. `CLOSED` and `REJECTED` are the plan's terminal states.

The trade fund pool is the co-op money. A pool is created by `BillingOps` or `NetworkAdmin` from `advertiser_id`, `fiscal_period_code`, `accrual_basis` and an optional `deduction_cap_pct`. `fiscal_period_code` uses the same quarter pattern, and the pairing of advertiser and period is unique across pools. `accrual_basis` is `PURCHASE_VOLUME` or `FIXED` and defaults to `PURCHASE_VOLUME`. `deduction_cap_pct` is a fraction between 0.0 and 1.0 inclusive and defaults to 0.6. The pool is born `OPEN` with `frozen_reason` `NONE` and a null `last_reconciled_at`. Its four money figures are derived: `accrued_cents` is the cumulative sum of every accrual posted to it; `reserved_cents` is the sum of `trade_fund_share_cents` over budget plans of the same advertiser and period whose `plan_status` is `APPROVED` or `ACTIVE`; `deducted_cents` is the sum of `trade_fund_deduction_cents` over invoices referencing this pool whose `invoice_status` is `ISSUED`, `DISPUTED`, `ADJUSTED` or `SETTLED`, plus the sum of every manual deduction posted to it; and `available_cents` is `accrued_cents` less `reserved_cents` less `deducted_cents`. Draft and written-off invoices contribute nothing to `deducted_cents`.

An accrual, posted by `BillingOps` or `NetworkAdmin` with `accrual_amount_cents` of at least 1,000 cents and a mandatory `accrual_reference`, raises `accrued_cents` and `available_cents` together and requires the pool to be `OPEN`. A manual deduction, posted with `deduction_amount_cents`, a mandatory `deduction_reference` and the mandatory `expected_trade_fund_account_version`, moves the amount from `available_cents` to `deducted_cents` and requires `available_cents` to be at least the requested amount; the pool identity is preserved throughout, and a manual deduction can never drive `available_cents` below zero.

Freezing is available to `BillingOps` and `NetworkAdmin` from `OPEN` only, requires `frozen_reason` as `AUDIT`, `OVERDRAWN` or `MANUAL`, and the system itself freezes with `OVERDRAWN` when a deduction posting would drive the drawable amount below zero. Unfreezing is available to the same roles from `FROZEN`, requires `available_cents` to be at least 0, accepts an optional `run_reconciliation` flag which defaults to false, sets `frozen_reason` back to `NONE`, and lands the pool in `OPEN`. When `run_reconciliation` is true the pool passes through `RECONCILING` on the way and `last_reconciled_at` is stamped with the current logical time; when it is false or omitted the pool goes directly to `OPEN` and `last_reconciled_at` is left as it was. Unfreezing also cascades: any budget plan of the same advertiser and period that is `EXHAUSTED` with a positive `available_cents` is returned to `ACTIVE`. The system unfreezes automatically when a dispute credit restores an `OVERDRAWN` pool's drawable amount to zero or above. The open-frozen-open cycle may repeat. A pool also reaches `RECONCILING` through the daily checkpoint sweep once the clock passes the end of its fiscal period, provided every invoice referencing it is `SETTLED`, `WRITTEN_OFF` or `ADJUSTED`; that sweep stamps `last_reconciled_at` and recomputes `available_cents`. A `RECONCILING` pool cannot be frozen. It reaches `CLOSED` through the sweep once `reserved_cents` is 0 and the clock is at or beyond `last_reconciled_at` plus twenty-four hours, and it also reaches `CLOSED` directly from `OPEN` or `FROZEN` as part of advertiser termination. `CLOSED` is terminal, and accruals, manual deductions and invoice-driven draws are all refused against a pool that is `FROZEN`, `RECONCILING` or `CLOSED`.

### 3.3. Campaign Construction, Review and the Delivery Dimension

A campaign is created by `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` or the owning `AdvertiserUser` from `advertiser_id`, `budget_plan_id`, `name`, `channel_mix`, `flight_start_at`, `flight_end_at`, `allocated_budget_cents`, `pacing_mode`, `daily_cap_cents`, `attribution_model` and `attribution_window_days`. `channel_mix` is `ONSITE_ONLY`, `OFFSITE_ONLY` or `OMNI`. `pacing_mode` is `EVEN` or `ACCELERATED` and defaults to `EVEN`. `daily_cap_cents` is at least 1,000 cents; `allocated_budget_cents` is at least 100,000 cents. `attribution_model` is `LAST_TOUCH` or `FRACTIONAL` and defaults to `LAST_TOUCH`. `attribution_window_days` is an integer from 1 to 30 inclusive and defaults to 14. `offsite_reported_spend_cents` is not accepted at creation and starts at 0. The campaign's flight must lie entirely inside the funding plan's window, `daily_cap_cents` may not exceed `allocated_budget_cents`, and for a `SELF_SERVE` advertiser `daily_cap_cents` may additionally not exceed the advertiser's `self_serve_daily_spend_cap_cents`. The funding plan must belong to the same advertiser named on the campaign. The campaign is born with `delivery_status` `DRAFT` and `billing_status` `UNBILLED`, and every computed figure at zero or null. Amendment, available to the same three roles, optionally accepts `name`, `flight_start_at`, `flight_end_at`, `allocated_budget_cents`, `pacing_mode`, `daily_cap_cents`, `attribution_model`, `attribution_window_days` and `channel_mix`; omitted fields are left untouched and the containment, cap and channel rules are re-applied to the resulting combination.

Review submission, available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser` with an optional `submission_note`, moves the campaign from `DRAFT` to `PENDING_REVIEW`. It requires the funding plan to be `APPROVED` or `ACTIVE`, at least one own line item in `PENDING_ELIGIBILITY` or `ELIGIBLE`, `allocated_budget_cents` no greater than the plan's `available_cents`, and channel coverage consistent with `channel_mix`: a campaign that is not `ONSITE_ONLY` must have at least one own line item on an off-site slot, and an `OMNI` campaign must have at least one line item on each channel. Submission stamps `submitted_for_review_by_user_id`.

The review fork is rendered by `NetworkAdmin` or `AccountManager` with a `decision` of `APPROVE` or `REJECT` and an optional `decision_note`. Both branches stamp `review_decided_by_user_id` and both refuse a caller whose `X-User-ID` equals `submitted_for_review_by_user_id`. `APPROVE` additionally requires the advertiser to be `ACTIVE` and the current logical time to be strictly before `flight_end_at`; it lands the campaign in `SCHEDULED`, at which point `allocated_budget_cents` becomes reserved, so the plan's `reserved_cents` and `available_cents` are recomputed and the advertiser's exposure is recomputed with it. `REJECT` lands the campaign in `REJECTED`, terminates every own line item whose `line_item_status` is `DRAFT`, `PENDING_ELIGIBILITY` or `ELIGIBLE` by moving it to `REJECTED`, and reserves nothing — the plan's `reserved_cents` is explicitly unchanged, because nothing was reserved while the campaign sat in `PENDING_REVIEW`.

Activation moves a `SCHEDULED` campaign to `DELIVERING` and is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser`; the daily checkpoint sweep performs the same transition once the clock reaches `flight_start_at`. It requires the current logical time to be at or after `flight_start_at` and strictly before `flight_end_at`, the advertiser to be `ACTIVE`, and the plan to be `APPROVED` or `ACTIVE`. A plan still in `APPROVED` is promoted to `ACTIVE` by this event. `pacing_index` is recomputed, and the billing dimension fires its own `UNBILLED` to `ACCRUING` transition in the same request.

Pausing a `DELIVERING` campaign is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser` and carries `pause_reason`, the value stamped on every own line item the pause moves to `MANUAL_PAUSED`; an operator-initiated campaign pause supplies `MANUAL`. Only line items in `SERVING` are moved, and only when the actor is an operator: the system-driven pauses described elsewhere handle their own line-item treatment. The plan's `reserved_cents` is unchanged, since `PAUSED` remains a reserving status. Resuming a `PAUSED` campaign is available to the same three roles and requires the advertiser to be `ACTIVE`, the plan to be `ACTIVE` with a strictly positive `available_cents`, the current logical time to be strictly before `flight_end_at`, and at least one own line item in `ELIGIBLE` or `MANUAL_PAUSED`. Resume returns every own line item in `MANUAL_PAUSED` whose `pause_reason` is `MANUAL` or `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` to `SERVING` with `pause_reason` reset to `NONE`, recomputes `pacing_index`, and deliberately leaves line items in `OOS_PAUSED` alone — a catalog blocker is not cleared by a delivery resume. The delivering-paused-delivering cycle may repeat.

Completion is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and `BillingOps` from `DELIVERING`, and the daily checkpoint sweep completes both `DELIVERING` and `PAUSED` campaigns once the clock reaches `flight_end_at`; an operator may complete early, but the sweep only acts at or after `flight_end_at`. Completion moves every own line item in `ELIGIBLE`, `SERVING`, `OOS_PAUSED` or `MANUAL_PAUSED` to `ENDED`, and each line item entering `ENDED` surrenders its slot occupancy, so `occupancy_count` falls by one on each affected slot. `COMPLETED` is not a reserving status, so the plan's `reserved_cents` is recomputed downward and its `available_cents` upward. The billing dimension fires its own `ACCRUING` to `PENDING_INVOICE` transition in the same request.

Archiving is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `AccountManager`, accepts an optional `archive_reason`, and is accepted from exactly two states. From `COMPLETED` it requires `billing_status` to be `RECONCILED` or `UNBILLED` and no measurement dispute referencing a line item of this campaign to be in `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `INFO_REQUESTED` or `ESCALATED`; it recomputes the plan's `reserved_cents` and `available_cents`. From `SCHEDULED` it requires `delivered_spend_cents` to be exactly 0; it moves every own line item in `ELIGIBLE`, `PENDING_ELIGIBILITY` or `DRAFT` to `ENDED`, each surrendering its slot occupancy, and releases the reservation outright — the plan's `reserved_cents` falls by exactly `allocated_budget_cents` and its `available_cents` rises by exactly the same amount, restoring the plan's headroom to the value it held before the campaign was scheduled. The advertiser's exposure is recomputed, and the campaign's, the plan's and the advertiser's `version` are each incremented by 1. Archiving a `SCHEDULED` campaign leaves the billing dimension at `UNBILLED`, which is where it already was. `REJECTED` and `ARCHIVED` are the delivery dimension's terminal states.

Pacing is recomputed by the daily checkpoint sweep, by every auction settlement, and by both transitions into `DELIVERING`. Let the total flight length be the number of whole twenty-four-hour periods between `flight_start_at` inclusive and `flight_end_at` exclusive, and let the elapsed length be the number of whole twenty-four-hour periods from `flight_start_at` up to the current logical time, floored at 0 and clipped above at the total. The ideal spend is 0 when the total is 0, and otherwise `allocated_budget_cents` multiplied by the elapsed count divided by the total count, rounded half-up to whole cents. `pacing_index` is 0.0000 when the ideal spend is 0, and otherwise `delivered_spend_cents` divided by the ideal spend, rounded half-up to four decimal places. Note that only settled auction spend feeds pacing: `offsite_reported_spend_cents` does not. The daily allowance is built next. Remaining days is the total less the elapsed count, floored at 1. Remaining budget is `allocated_budget_cents` less `delivered_spend_cents`, floored at 0. Under `EVEN` pacing the base allowance is the remaining budget divided by the remaining days, truncated toward zero to whole cents; under `ACCELERATED` pacing the base allowance is the entire remaining budget. Catch-up applies only under `EVEN` pacing and only when `pacing_index` is **strictly** below 0.8000 — a `pacing_index` of exactly 0.8000 does not trigger it. When catch-up applies, the base allowance is multiplied by 1.25 and truncated toward zero to whole cents. The result, catch-up applied or not, is then clamped down to `daily_cap_cents`. The truncation happens before the cap clamp, never after.

### 3.4. Inventory, Line Items and Slot Occupancy Arbitration

A placement slot is inventory, and only `NetworkAdmin` may create or amend one. Creation requires `slot_code`, `channel`, `page_type`, `position_index`, `max_concurrent_line_items`, `floor_cpc_cents` and `relevance_weight`, and optionally accepts `exclusivity_category_code` and `buffer_minutes`. `slot_code` is globally unique. `channel` is `ONSITE` or `OFFSITE`. `page_type` is one of `SEARCH_RESULTS`, `CATEGORY_BROWSE`, `PRODUCT_DETAIL`, `HOMEPAGE`, `OFFSITE_SOCIAL` or `OFFSITE_DISPLAY`. `position_index` is at least 1, where 1 is topmost. `max_concurrent_line_items` is at least 1. `floor_cpc_cents` is at least 1 and acts as the reserve price. `relevance_weight` is the exponent applied to relevance when the ranking score is formed; it lies between 0.5 and 2.0 inclusive and defaults to 1.0. `exclusivity_category_code` is nullable; when non-null, at most one overlapping line item per category may occupy the slot. `buffer_minutes` is at least 0, defaults to 0, and has effect only on an off-site slot. The slot is born `OPEN`. Amendment optionally accepts `slot_status`, `max_concurrent_line_items`, `floor_cpc_cents`, `relevance_weight`, `exclusivity_category_code` and `buffer_minutes`. Moving `slot_status` from `OPEN` to `MAINTENANCE` moves every line item on the slot that is `SERVING` to `MANUAL_PAUSED` with `pause_reason` `MANUAL`, and blocks any further auction from opening on the slot. Moving from `MAINTENANCE` back to `OPEN` is permitted and the cycle may repeat. Moving from `MAINTENANCE` to `RETIRED` requires `occupancy_count` to be exactly 0. `RETIRED` is terminal and a retired slot can never be reopened. `occupancy_count` itself is derived: the number of line items on the slot whose `line_item_status` is `ELIGIBLE`, `SERVING`, `OOS_PAUSED` or `MANUAL_PAUSED`. Line items in `DRAFT`, `PENDING_ELIGIBILITY`, `REJECTED` or `ENDED` are excluded.

A line item binds one campaign to one slot. Creation is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser`, and requires `campaign_id`, `placement_slot_id`, `bid_cents`, `bid_type`, `flight_start_at`, `flight_end_at`, `daily_budget_cents`, `category_code`, `keyword_match_terms` and the mandatory `expected_campaign_version`. `bid_cents` is at least 1. `bid_type` is `CPC` or `CPM` and defaults to `CPC`. `daily_budget_cents` is at least 100 cents. `keyword_match_terms` is an array of lowercased match terms. `category_code` is the merchandising category the line item competes in and is what the slot's exclusivity rule is matched against. The line item's flight must lie entirely inside its campaign's flight with its own start strictly before its own end, and the slot's channel must be consistent with the campaign's `channel_mix` — an `ONSITE_ONLY` campaign may hold no line item on an off-site slot and an `OFFSITE_ONLY` campaign none on an on-site slot. The line item is born `DRAFT` with `reserved_cents` 0, `spend_cents` 0, `eligible_product_count` 0, `relevance_score` 0.0, `pause_reason` `NONE` and a null `last_pause_at`; the parent campaign's `version` is incremented by 1 by the creation.

Eligibility submission, available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser`, moves the line item from `DRAFT` to `PENDING_ELIGIBILITY`. It requires `eligible_product_count` of at least 1, at least one product assignment on the line item that is `ACTIVE` with `is_hero` true, and the owning campaign to be in `DRAFT`, `PENDING_REVIEW`, `SCHEDULED` or `DELIVERING`.

The eligibility fork is rendered by `CatalogOps` or `NetworkAdmin` with a `decision` of `APPROVE` or `REJECT` and an optional `decision_note`. Both branches stamp `eligibility_decided_by_user_id`, and the two branches diverge on what they do with capacity. `APPROVE` requires that every `ACTIVE` product assignment on the line item reference a product whose `eligibility_status` is `ELIGIBLE`, that the slot be `OPEN`, and that all three occupancy rules hold. It lands the line item in `ELIGIBLE`, raises the slot's `occupancy_count` by 1, and computes `reserved_cents` as `daily_budget_cents` multiplied by the number of whole twenty-four-hour periods in the line item's flight, with that day count floored at 1 — a flight shorter than a day reserves exactly one day of budget. `REJECT` lands the line item in `REJECTED`, does **not** raise `occupancy_count`, and instead moves every product assignment on the line item to `REMOVED`.

The three occupancy rules operate over the same population: the line items on the same slot whose `line_item_status` is `ELIGIBLE`, `SERVING`, `OOS_PAUSED` or `MANUAL_PAUSED`, excluding the candidate itself. Rule A is aggregate capacity and applies to every slot regardless of channel: the number of those rows whose flight overlaps the candidate's flight must be strictly less than `max_concurrent_line_items`. Rule B is category exclusivity and applies only when the slot names an `exclusivity_category_code` that equals the candidate's `category_code`: in that case no overlapping occupying row may share that `category_code`. When the slot names no category, or names one that differs from the candidate's, Rule B passes trivially. Rule C is the adjacency buffer and applies only when the slot's `channel` is `OFFSITE` and `buffer_minutes` is greater than 0: the candidate's flight is widened by that many whole minutes at the start **and** at the end, and no occupying row may overlap the widened interval. Because of the widening, flights that merely touch — one ending exactly where another begins — do collide under Rule C even though they pass Rule A. Overlap itself is always the half-open test of §2.8, compared at millisecond resolution.

Activation moves an `ELIGIBLE` line item to `SERVING` and is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser`; the system performs the same transition for every eligible line item when the owning campaign reaches `DELIVERING`. It requires the campaign to be `DELIVERING`, the advertiser to be `ACTIVE`, the plan to be `ACTIVE` with `available_cents` at least `daily_budget_cents`, the current logical time to be at or after `flight_start_at` and strictly before `flight_end_at`, and `eligible_product_count` of at least 1. Activation sets `pause_reason` to `NONE` and recomputes `priority_tier`.

`priority_tier` is derived, not supplied. Let the denominator be the funding plan's `committed_total_cents` plus its `top_up_total_cents`, and let the remaining ratio be 0.0 when that denominator is 0 and otherwise the plan's `available_cents` divided by that denominator, compared without rounding. When the advertiser's `service_model` is `MANAGED` **and** the remaining ratio is at least 0.50, the tier is `TIER_1`. Otherwise, when the advertiser is `MANAGED` **or** the remaining ratio is at least 0.20, the tier is `TIER_2`. Otherwise the tier is `TIER_3`. A ratio of exactly 0.50 therefore yields `TIER_1` for a managed advertiser, and a ratio of exactly 0.20 yields `TIER_2` for a self-serve one. The tier is recomputed on activation, on every transition into `SERVING`, and again at ranking time.

Pausing a `SERVING` line item is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser` and carries `pause_reason`; it lands the line item in `MANUAL_PAUSED`, stamps `last_pause_at` with the current logical time, and stamps the supplied reason — `MANUAL` for an operator pause and `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` when the trigger is plan exhaustion. When the pause leaves the campaign with no line item still `SERVING`, the campaign rolls up from `DELIVERING` to `PAUSED`. Pausing changes neither the slot's `occupancy_count` nor the plan's `reserved_cents`. Resuming is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager`, `CatalogOps` and the owning `AdvertiserUser`, and requires the campaign to be `DELIVERING`, `eligible_product_count` of at least 1, the plan's `available_cents` to be at least `daily_budget_cents`, and the current logical time to be strictly before `flight_end_at`; it returns the line item to `SERVING` with `pause_reason` reset to `NONE`. The serving-paused-serving cycle may repeat.

Ending is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser` with an optional `end_reason`, and is accepted from `ELIGIBLE`, `SERVING`, `OOS_PAUSED` and `MANUAL_PAUSED`. From `SERVING`, `OOS_PAUSED` or `MANUAL_PAUSED` it requires that no auction won by this line item still be in `OPEN`, `RANKED` or `AWARDED`; from `ELIGIBLE` it requires `spend_cents` to be exactly 0. In every case ending lowers the slot's `occupancy_count` by 1 and recomputes the plan's `reserved_cents` and `available_cents`, which is the compensating release of the occupancy granted at eligibility approval. `REJECTED` and `ENDED` are the line item's terminal states.

### 3.5. Catalog Eligibility, Product Assignments and the Out-of-Stock Chain

A catalog product is registered by `CatalogOps` or `NetworkAdmin` from `sku`, `category_code`, `title`, `price_cents`, `in_stock_units`, `margin_pct` and an optional `min_stock_units_for_serving`. `sku` is globally unique. `price_cents` is at least 1. `in_stock_units` is at least 0 and is thereafter mutated only by stock adjustments. `min_stock_units_for_serving` is at least 0 and defaults to 3. `margin_pct` is a gross-margin fraction between 0.0 and 0.9 inclusive and defaults to 0.2. The product's `eligibility_status` at creation is derived rather than supplied: `ELIGIBLE` when `in_stock_units` is at least `min_stock_units_for_serving`, and `OUT_OF_STOCK` otherwise. `restricted_reason` starts at `NONE` and `last_stock_change_at` starts null.

A product assignment attaches a product to a line item and is created by `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager`, `CatalogOps` or the owning `AdvertiserUser` from `line_item_id`, `catalog_product_id`, `is_hero` and the mandatory `expected_line_item_version`. `is_hero` defaults to false. The referenced product must be `ELIGIBLE` — a product that is `RESTRICTED` or `OUT_OF_STOCK` is refused, and a `DELISTED` product is refused earlier still on state grounds. The assignment is born `ACTIVE` with a null `removed_at`, and its creation recomputes the line item's `eligible_product_count`, `relevance_score` and `quality_adjusted_score` and increments the line item's `version` by 1. Removal is available to the same four roles; it is accepted while the assignment is `ACTIVE`, `PAUSED_OOS` or `PAUSED_RESTRICTED`, requires the owning line item not to be `ENDED`, and — when the actor is an operator rather than the system — requires the line item to retain at least one other assignment that is `ACTIVE` with `is_hero` true, unless the line item is still in `DRAFT`. Removal lands the assignment in `REMOVED`, stamps `removed_at` with the current logical time, and recomputes the line item's `eligible_product_count` and `relevance_score`. Removal returns no body. `REMOVED` is terminal.

`eligible_product_count` on a line item is the number of its assignments that are `ACTIVE` **and** whose product is `ELIGIBLE`. Assignments in `PAUSED_OOS`, `PAUSED_RESTRICTED` or `REMOVED` count zero, as do `ACTIVE` assignments whose product has slipped out of eligibility.

Stock adjustments are posted by `CatalogOps` or `NetworkAdmin` with a signed `delta_units` and an optional `adjustment_reference`. The adjustment updates `in_stock_units` and stamps `last_stock_change_at` with the current logical time, and it is refused outright against a `DELISTED` product. When the resulting `in_stock_units` falls strictly below `min_stock_units_for_serving` and the product was `ELIGIBLE`, the product moves to `OUT_OF_STOCK` and the depth-three pausing chain fires. Level one: every assignment referencing this product that is `ACTIVE` moves to `PAUSED_OOS`, with its `relevance_contribution` zeroed. Level two: each of those assignments triggers a recomputation of `eligible_product_count` on its line item, and any line item whose recomputed count reaches 0 while it is `SERVING` moves to `OOS_PAUSED` with `pause_reason` `OUT_OF_STOCK` and `last_pause_at` stamped. Level three: each line item entering `OOS_PAUSED` raises `oos_paused_line_item_count` on its campaign by 1, and a campaign whose every non-ended line item is now in `OOS_PAUSED` or `MANUAL_PAUSED` and whose `delivery_status` is `DELIVERING` moves to `PAUSED`. The slot's `occupancy_count` is unchanged throughout, because `OOS_PAUSED` remains an occupying status. Every assignment, line item and campaign the chain touches has its `version` incremented by 1, as does the product.

The reverse adjustment is the compensating action. When the resulting `in_stock_units` returns to `min_stock_units_for_serving` or above, the product moves back to `ELIGIBLE`, every assignment in `PAUSED_OOS` returns to `ACTIVE` with `relevance_contribution` recomputed, and each affected line item recomputes `eligible_product_count`. A line item in `OOS_PAUSED` whose recomputed count reaches 1 or more returns to `SERVING` with `pause_reason` `NONE` when its campaign is `DELIVERING`, and otherwise — when the campaign is `SCHEDULED` or `PAUSED` — returns to `ELIGIBLE` instead, again with `pause_reason` cleared. Either way `oos_paused_line_item_count` falls by 1 on the campaign. Campaigns paused by the level-three roll-up are not themselves auto-resumed. The eligible-out-of-stock-eligible cycle may repeat with every stock swing and conserves the slot occupancy identity.

Restriction is posted by `CatalogOps` or `NetworkAdmin` with a mandatory `restricted_reason` of `AGE_RESTRICTED`, `REGULATORY` or `BRAND_SAFETY` — `NONE` is not an acceptable restriction reason — and is accepted from `ELIGIBLE` and from `OUT_OF_STOCK`. It moves the product to `RESTRICTED`, moves every assignment referencing it that is `ACTIVE` or `PAUSED_OOS` to `PAUSED_RESTRICTED`, zeroing `relevance_contribution` on those coming from `ACTIVE`, and recomputes `eligible_product_count` on each affected line item; a line item in `SERVING` or `ELIGIBLE` whose recomputed count reaches 0 moves to `OOS_PAUSED` with `pause_reason` `CATALOG_RESTRICTED`.

Reinstatement is posted by `CatalogOps` or `NetworkAdmin` with an optional `reinstatement_note` and is accepted only from `RESTRICTED`. It always clears `restricted_reason` to `NONE`, and it branches on stock. When `in_stock_units` is at least `min_stock_units_for_serving`, the product returns to `ELIGIBLE`, every assignment in `PAUSED_RESTRICTED` returns to `ACTIVE` with `relevance_contribution` recomputed, and each line item in `OOS_PAUSED` whose `pause_reason` is `CATALOG_RESTRICTED` returns to `ELIGIBLE`. When `in_stock_units` is strictly below the threshold, the product instead lands in `OUT_OF_STOCK` and each assignment in `PAUSED_RESTRICTED` lands in `PAUSED_OOS` rather than `ACTIVE`, leaving the affected line items paused. The low-stock branch is not a rejection: the reinstatement succeeds either way. The eligible-restricted-eligible cycle may repeat.

Delisting is posted by `CatalogOps` or `NetworkAdmin` with a mandatory `delist_reason` and is accepted from `RESTRICTED` and from `OUT_OF_STOCK`; from `OUT_OF_STOCK` it additionally requires `in_stock_units` to be exactly 0. It moves every assignment referencing the product — in any non-terminal status — to `REMOVED` with `removed_at` stamped, and recomputes `eligible_product_count` on each affected line item; a line item whose recomputed count reaches 0 while it is `SERVING`, `ELIGIBLE` or `OOS_PAUSED` moves to `ENDED`, surrendering its slot occupancy. `DELISTED` is terminal: no assignment, stock adjustment, restriction, reinstatement or further delisting is accepted against a delisted product.

### 3.6. The Sponsored Placement Auction

An auction event is one shopper request against one slot, and only `NetworkAdmin` and `AuctionService` may open, rank, award or settle one. Opening requires `placement_slot_id`, `request_context_query`, `shopper_segment` and `requested_at`. `request_context_query` is the shopper's lowercased query text or browse context. `shopper_segment` is `NEW`, `RETURNING` or `LOYALTY`. `requested_at` is the moment the auction is deemed to have occurred and is the timestamp every flight-window test in the filter chain is compared against, not the current logical time. The auction is born `OPEN` with `candidate_count` 0, a null `winning_line_item_id`, a null `winner_score`, `second_best_score` and `clearing_price_cents`, `settled_spend_cents` 0 and `no_fill_reason` `NONE`.

Ranking moves the auction from `OPEN` to `RANKED` and requires the slot's `slot_status` to be `OPEN`. It materialises exactly one auction bid row per candidate line item, where the candidate population is every line item whose `placement_slot_id` is the auctioned slot and nothing else — line items on other slots are not candidates. Each bid row carries `auction_event_id`, `line_item_id`, `raw_bid_cents` copied from the line item's `bid_cents`, the `relevance_score` and `quality_adjusted_score` computed for this auction, a `rank`, an `outcome` and a `filter_reason`. Bid rows carry only `id` and `created_at` from the common fields; they have no `updated_at` and no `version` and are never mutated after the award assigns their outcome. Ranking also sets `candidate_count` to the number of bid rows created, sets `winner_score` and `second_best_score` from the surviving pool, persists the computed `relevance_score` and `quality_adjusted_score` back onto each ranked line item, and leaves `winning_line_item_id` null until the award.

Relevance is computed first, per candidate. The overlap ratio is the count of the line item's `keyword_match_terms` that appear in the auction's `request_context_query`, divided by the total count of `keyword_match_terms`; when the line item carries no match terms at all the overlap ratio is 0.0 rather than undefined. The margin average is the arithmetic mean of `margin_pct` over the products of the line item's assignments that are `ACTIVE` and whose product is `ELIGIBLE`, and it is 0.0 when that set is empty. The hero boost is 0.10 when at least one of those `ACTIVE` assignments has `is_hero` true, and 0.0 otherwise. The segment bonus is 0.00 for `NEW`, 0.03 for `RETURNING` and 0.06 for `LOYALTY`. `relevance_score` is the sum of 0.60 times the overlap ratio, 0.25 times the margin average, the hero boost and the segment bonus, capped above at 1.0000, and rounded half-up to four decimal places. The per-assignment `relevance_contribution` is, for each row in that same active-and-eligible set, 0.25 multiplied by the product's `margin_pct` divided by the count of rows in the set with that count floored at 1, rounded half-up to four decimal places; every assignment outside the set carries 0.0000.

The filter chain then runs in a strictly ordered five-clause sequence, and each candidate is stamped with the reason of the **first** clause it fails; later clauses are not evaluated once one has matched. Clause one is `NOT_SERVING`: the candidate fails when its `line_item_status` is anything other than `SERVING`, or its campaign's `delivery_status` is anything other than `DELIVERING`, or its advertiser's `onboarding_status` is anything other than `ACTIVE`, or its plan's `plan_status` is anything other than `ACTIVE`, or `requested_at` is strictly before the line item's `flight_start_at`, or `requested_at` is at or after the line item's `flight_end_at`. Clause two is `NO_ELIGIBLE_PRODUCTS`: the candidate fails when its `eligible_product_count` is 0. Clause three is `ZERO_TERM_OVERLAP`: the candidate fails when its computed `relevance_score` is exactly 0.0000. Clause four is `DAILY_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`: the candidate fails when its daily headroom is 0 or less. Clause five is `BELOW_FLOOR`: the candidate fails when its `quality_adjusted_score` is strictly less than the slot's `floor_cpc_cents`. A candidate failing any of clauses one through four is stamped with `outcome` `FILTERED_INELIGIBLE`; a candidate failing clause five alone is stamped `FILTERED_BELOW_FLOOR`. Filtered rows carry a null `rank`. A candidate that survives all five clauses carries `filter_reason` `NONE`.

Daily headroom is measured over the day window that begins at the current logical time truncated down to the start of its UTC day and ends twenty-four hours later, exclusive of the upper bound. Three caps apply. The line-item cap is `daily_budget_cents` less the sum of `billable_cents` over the line item's engagement events whose `occurred_at` falls in the day window. The campaign cap is the campaign's daily pacing allowance from §3.3 less the sum of `settled_spend_cents` over settled auctions won by any line item of that campaign inside the day window. The advertiser cap is `self_serve_daily_spend_cap_cents` less the same day's spend across all of the advertiser's campaigns, and it applies **only** when the advertiser's `service_model` is `SELF_SERVE`; a `MANAGED` advertiser contributes no third cap. The headroom is the smallest of the applicable caps. The effective bid is the smaller of the line item's `bid_cents` and that headroom, so a constrained line item bids down rather than being excluded outright.

`quality_adjusted_score` is the effective bid multiplied by `relevance_score` raised to the power of the slot's `relevance_weight`, multiplied by the tier multiplier, rounded half-up to four decimal places. The tier multiplier is 1.10 for `TIER_1`, 1.00 for `TIER_2` and 0.90 for `TIER_3`, using the `priority_tier` recomputed at ranking time. The surviving pool is then ordered by `quality_adjusted_score` descending, then by effective bid descending, then by `priority_tier` ascending so that `TIER_1` precedes `TIER_2` precedes `TIER_3`, and finally by the line item's `created_at` ascending. Row identifiers are never used as a tiebreaker at any level. `rank` is assigned 1, 2, 3 and so on down that ordering. `winner_score` is the rank-one row's `quality_adjusted_score` and `second_best_score` is the rank-two row's, or null when no rank-two row exists.

The award fork runs from `RANKED` and splits on whether the surviving pool is empty. When at least one bid row has an `outcome` other than `FILTERED_INELIGIBLE` and `FILTERED_BELOW_FLOOR`, the auction moves to `AWARDED`: `winning_line_item_id` is set to the rank-one row's `line_item_id`, that row's `outcome` becomes `WON`, every other surviving row's `outcome` becomes `LOST`, `no_fill_reason` is set to `NONE`, and `clearing_price_cents` is computed. When every bid row is filtered, the auction moves to `NO_FILL` instead: `winning_line_item_id` and `clearing_price_cents` are both left null, `settled_spend_cents` is set to 0, and `no_fill_reason` is set to `NO_ELIGIBLE_CANDIDATES` when `candidate_count` is 0 or every row was `FILTERED_INELIGIBLE`, and to `ALL_BELOW_FLOOR` otherwise — that is, whenever at least one row reached clause five. `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` is a declared value of `no_fill_reason` that the award rule never produces; a budget-starved candidate is filtered at clause four and therefore contributes to `NO_ELIGIBLE_CANDIDATES`. Both branches terminate the auction through `SETTLED`.

The clearing price is second-price. Let the winner's multiplier be the winner's `relevance_score` raised to the power of the slot's `relevance_weight`, multiplied by the winner's tier multiplier. When no runner-up survived, the raw clearing figure is the slot's `floor_cpc_cents` exactly. When a runner-up exists, the raw clearing figure is `second_best_score` divided by the winner's multiplier, plus one — the one-cent increment that puts the winner just above the runner-up's willingness. That raw figure is rounded **up** to the next whole cent, and only then clamped: the result is the larger of `floor_cpc_cents` and the smaller of the winner's effective bid and the rounded-up figure. The rounding-up happens exactly once, before the clamp, never after it, so a raw figure below the floor still yields exactly the floor and a raw figure above the winner's own effective bid still yields exactly that effective bid.

Engagement events record what the shopper did. Recording is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `AuctionService` and requires `line_item_id`, `auction_event_id`, `catalog_product_id`, `event_type`, `occurred_at`, `shopper_hash` and `channel`. `event_type` is `IMPRESSION`, `CLICK` or `OFFSITE_VIEW`. `shopper_hash` is the pseudonymous shopper identifier used later for attribution matching. `channel` must be supplied, but the persisted value is always derived from the slot the winning line item occupies rather than taken from the request, so an inconsistent supplied value is silently corrected to the slot's `channel`. An `IMPRESSION` or `CLICK` may only be recorded on an on-site slot's auction and an `OFFSITE_VIEW` only on an off-site slot's auction. The referenced auction must be `AWARDED` or `SETTLED`, the referenced line item must be its `winning_line_item_id`, and the referenced `catalog_product_id` must be assigned to that line item by an assignment whose `assignment_status` is `ACTIVE`. The supplied `occurred_at` must lie inside the winning line item's flight under the half-open rule — at or after its `flight_start_at` and strictly before its `flight_end_at` — and must be at or after the auction's `requested_at`, since a shopper cannot engage with a placement before the request that produced it. Engagement events carry only `id` and `created_at` from the common fields — no `updated_at`, no `version` — and are never amended or deleted. `billable_cents` is 0 at the moment the event is recorded and is populated when the auction settles; recording an engagement therefore moves neither `spend_cents` nor `delivered_spend_cents` on its own.

Settlement moves an `AWARDED` or `NO_FILL` auction to `SETTLED`. From `AWARDED` it requires at least one engagement event on the auction with `event_type` `IMPRESSION` and requires the winning line item's plan to be `ACTIVE` or `EXHAUSTED`. From `NO_FILL` it requires nothing beyond the state itself and simply sets `settled_spend_cents` to 0 with no ledger recomputation whatsoever. The `AWARDED` settlement amount depends on `bid_type`. Under `CPC`, only `CLICK` events are billable: `settled_spend_cents` is `clearing_price_cents` multiplied by the count of `CLICK` events on the auction, each `CLICK` event's `billable_cents` is set to the full `clearing_price_cents`, and every `IMPRESSION` and `OFFSITE_VIEW` event's `billable_cents` is set to 0. Under `CPM`, the billable set is every `IMPRESSION` and `OFFSITE_VIEW` event on the auction: `settled_spend_cents` is `clearing_price_cents` multiplied by the count of billable events divided by 1000, rounded half-up to whole cents. That total is then distributed across the billable events so the parts sum exactly to the whole: each billable event receives a base of `clearing_price_cents` divided by 1000 truncated toward zero, and the remainder — the settled total less the base multiplied by the billable count — is distributed one additional cent at a time to the billable events in ascending order of `occurred_at`, with ties broken by ascending `created_at` and never by identifier. Non-billable events under `CPM` receive `billable_cents` 0.

Settlement then runs the depth-three exhaustion cascade. Level one adds `settled_spend_cents` to the winning line item's `spend_cents` and to its campaign's `delivered_spend_cents`. Level two recomputes the plan's `consumed_cents` and `available_cents`, moving the amount out of the reservation and into consumption; a plan whose recomputed `available_cents` is 0 or less while `plan_status` is `ACTIVE` moves to `EXHAUSTED` with `exhausted_at` stamped. Level three is that exhaustion's own two-step consequence: every `DELIVERING` campaign on the plan moves to `PAUSED`, and every `SERVING` line item of those campaigns moves to `MANUAL_PAUSED` with `pause_reason` `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`. The settling campaign's `pacing_index` is recomputed, and the auction's `version` is incremented by 1 along with every mutated row's. `SETTLED` is the auction's terminal state.

### 3.7. Closed-Loop Attribution and Incrementality Measurement

An attributed sale closes the loop between an engagement and a retail order line. Creation is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `MeasurementAnalyst` from `engagement_event_id`, `catalog_product_id`, `retail_order_ref`, `sale_cents`, `units`, `occurred_at` and `match_type`. `retail_order_ref` is the retailer's order-line reference and the triple of `retail_order_ref`, `catalog_product_id` and `line_item_id` is unique, so the same order line may not be credited twice to the same line item and product. `sale_cents` is at least 1 and `units` is at least 1. `line_item_id` is not supplied; it is copied from the referenced engagement event. `match_type` must be supplied but the persisted value is always derived: it is `CLICK_THROUGH` when the credited engagement's `event_type` is `CLICK` and `VIEW_THROUGH` for an `IMPRESSION` or `OFFSITE_VIEW`. `restated` starts false. `attribution_model_applied` records which of the campaign's two models was in force when the credit was computed, and it is never taken from the request.

The attribution window is `attribution_window_days` multiplied by twenty-four hours, read from the campaign that owns the engagement's line item. `within_window` is true when the sale's `occurred_at` is at or after the engagement's `occurred_at` **and** the elapsed interval between them is strictly less than the window length; a sale landing exactly at the window boundary is therefore outside the window, while a sale landing at the same instant as the engagement is inside it. A row whose `within_window` is false receives `attribution_weight` 0.0 and `attributed_credit_cents` 0 and contributes nothing to any campaign total, but it is still persisted and is still readable.

The candidate engagement set for a sale is every engagement event sharing the sale's `shopper_hash` and `catalog_product_id` whose `occurred_at` falls in the closed interval running from the window length before the sale back-stop up to the sale's `occurred_at` inclusive, and whose line item's campaign is not `REJECTED`. Under `LAST_TOUCH` the credit goes entirely to one engagement: the candidate with the greatest `occurred_at`, with ties broken by `event_type` in the fixed precedence `CLICK` first, then `OFFSITE_VIEW`, then `IMPRESSION`, and any remaining tie broken by ascending `created_at`. The credited engagement's row receives `attribution_weight` 1.0 and every other candidate's row receives 0.0. Under `FRACTIONAL` the credit is split by match weight: `CLICK` carries a weight of 1.0, `OFFSITE_VIEW` 0.4 and `IMPRESSION` 0.2, and each candidate's `attribution_weight` is its own match weight divided by the sum of match weights across all in-window candidates, rounded half-up to four decimal places. In both variants `attributed_credit_cents` is `sale_cents` multiplied by the row's `attribution_weight`, rounded half-up to whole cents.

A campaign's `attributed_revenue_cents` is the sum of the already-rounded `attributed_credit_cents` across its attributed sales whose `restated` is false. The per-row rounding happens first and the summation second, so the campaign total is the sum of rounded parts and not the rounding of a summed product; rows with `restated` true are excluded from the total but retain their own stored credit. `roas` is `attributed_revenue_cents` divided by the sum of `delivered_spend_cents` and `offsite_reported_spend_cents`, rounded half-up to four decimal places, and is 0.0 when that denominator is 0. `iroas` is `incremental_revenue_cents` over the same denominator under the same rounding and the same zero-denominator rule.

An incrementality study measures what the campaign actually caused. Design is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `MeasurementAnalyst` from `campaign_id`, `design_method`, `holdout_pct`, `window_start_at`, `window_end_at`, the mandatory `expected_campaign_version` and an optional `supersedes_study_id`. `design_method` is `GEO_HOLDOUT` or `USER_HOLDOUT`. `holdout_pct` is the control-group share and lies between 0.05 and 0.50 inclusive. The study window must sit inside the campaign's flight. `supersedes_study_id`, when supplied, must reference a study on the **same** campaign whose `study_status` is `PUBLISHED`; a reference to a study on another campaign, or to one that is not published, is refused. The study is born `DESIGNED` with `exposed_group_size`, `control_group_size`, `exposed_conversions`, `control_conversions`, `exposed_sales_cents` and `control_sales_cents` all 0, every result figure null, `invalidated_reason` `NONE` and a null `published_by_user_id`; design increments the campaign's `version` by 1.

The washout rule bounds how closely two studies on the same campaign may sit. It is measured as a gap, not an overlap: the comparison set is every other study on the same campaign whose `study_status` is `RUNNING`, `MEASURING` or `PUBLISHED`, and for each such study the gap is the number of whole hours of clear air between the two windows — the later window's start less the earlier window's end. Two windows that genuinely overlap are treated as a gap of minus one hour. A collision occurs when the gap is strictly less than one hundred and sixty-eight hours, which is the seven-day washout expressed in hours; a gap of exactly one hundred and sixty-eight hours passes. A candidate study whose `supersedes_study_id` names a particular study is exempt from the washout test **against that one study only** and is still tested normally against every other study in the comparison set. The washout rule is enforced both at design time and again when the study starts.

Starting a study moves it from `DESIGNED` to `RUNNING`, is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `MeasurementAnalyst`, and requires `exposed_group_size` and `control_group_size`, each at least 1. It requires the campaign's `delivery_status` to be `SCHEDULED` or `DELIVERING`, the current logical time to be at or after `window_start_at`, and the washout rule to still hold. Measuring moves a `RUNNING` study to `MEASURING`, requires `exposed_conversions`, `control_conversions`, `exposed_sales_cents` and `control_sales_cents`, and requires the current logical time to be at or after `window_end_at` with both group sizes at least 1.

Lift is computed in two variants. The exposed rate is `exposed_conversions` divided by `exposed_group_size` and the control rate is `control_conversions` divided by `control_group_size`. Under `GEO_HOLDOUT`, `lift_pct` is 1.0000 when the control rate is 0, and otherwise the exposed rate divided by the control rate less 1.0, rounded half-up to four decimal places; `incremental_sales_cents` is `exposed_sales_cents` multiplied by `lift_pct` divided by the quantity one plus `lift_pct`, rounded half-up to whole cents, when `lift_pct` is greater than 0, and exactly 0 otherwise. Under `USER_HOLDOUT` the control side is first rescaled to the exposed group's size: the scaled control sales are `control_sales_cents` multiplied by `exposed_group_size` divided by `control_group_size`; `incremental_sales_cents` is `exposed_sales_cents` less the scaled control sales, floored at 0, rounded half-up to whole cents; and `lift_pct` is 1.0000 when the scaled control sales are 0, and otherwise `exposed_sales_cents` less the scaled control sales, divided by the scaled control sales, rounded half-up to four decimal places. The two variants can disagree sharply on the same inputs, which is why `design_method` is fixed at design time and never amended. `significance_score` is the absolute difference between the exposed rate and the control rate, multiplied by the square root of the smaller of the two group sizes, rounded half-up to four decimal places. `significance_flag` is true when `significance_score` is at or above 1.96 and false below it, so a score of exactly 1.9600 is significant.

Publication moves a `MEASURING` study to `PUBLISHED`, accepts an optional `publication_note`, and requires `significance_flag` to be true and no other study on the same campaign to be `PUBLISHED` with an overlapping window. It stamps `published_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`, writes the study's `incremental_sales_cents` onto the campaign's `incremental_revenue_cents` and recomputes `iroas`. When `supersedes_study_id` is non-null, the named study is moved to `INVALIDATED` with `invalidated_reason` `SUPERSEDED` by the same request. A campaign's `incremental_revenue_cents` is always the `incremental_sales_cents` of its `PUBLISHED` study with the greatest `window_end_at`, ties broken by ascending `created_at`, and 0 when no published study remains.

Invalidation requires `invalidated_reason` as `DATA_DEFECT`, `HOLDOUT_CONTAMINATION` or `SUPERSEDED`, is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `MeasurementAnalyst`, and is accepted from `MEASURING` and from `PUBLISHED`. Both branches land the study in `INVALIDATED` and stamp the supplied reason, but they differ entirely in consequence. From `MEASURING`, no campaign-level recomputation occurs at all, because the study never wrote anything onto the campaign. From `PUBLISHED`, a depth-three restatement cascade runs. Level one recomputes the campaign's `incremental_revenue_cents` from the remaining published study with the greatest `window_end_at`, falling back to 0 when none remains, and recomputes `iroas`. Level two flags every attributed sale on the campaign's line items whose `occurred_at` falls inside the study window — inclusive of `window_start_at`, exclusive of `window_end_at` — with `restated` true. Level three recomputes the campaign's `attributed_revenue_cents` and `roas` from the surviving non-restated rows. The study's, the campaign's and every restated sale's `version` is incremented by 1. Invalidation itself files no dispute and moves no invoice; a restatement that warrants a credit is pursued through the dispute channel of §3.9 in a separate request. Independently of the branch taken, a study invalidated with `invalidated_reason` `DATA_DEFECT` remains re-measurable: it may be returned to `MEASURING` with a fresh set of outcome figures, which clears `invalidated_reason` back to `NONE` and recomputes all four result figures, provided the current logical time is strictly before `window_end_at` plus thirty days. An invalidation carrying `HOLDOUT_CONTAMINATION` or `SUPERSEDED` is final: `INVALIDATED` is the study's terminal state for every reason other than a data defect.

### 3.8. Invoicing, Trade Fund Deduction and the Billing Dimension

An invoice is opened by `NetworkAdmin` or `BillingOps` from `advertiser_id`, `budget_plan_id`, `trade_fund_account_id` and `billing_period_code`. `billing_period_code` is a four-digit year, a hyphen and a two-digit month from 01 to 12, and the pairing of advertiser and billing period is unique across invoices. `trade_fund_account_id` is nullable — an advertiser with no co-op pool is invoiced entirely in cash — and when it is non-null the pool must belong to the same advertiser and its `fiscal_period_code` must equal the funding plan's `fiscal_period_code`, which is in turn the quarter containing `billing_period_code`. The funding plan must belong to the same advertiser. The invoice is born `DRAFT` with `adjustment_round` 0, every money figure at 0, and `issued_at`, `due_at`, `settled_at` and `issued_by_user_id` all null.

Issuance moves the invoice from `DRAFT` to `ISSUED`, is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `BillingOps`, and accepts an optional `issue_note`. It requires the advertiser's `onboarding_status` to be `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` — a suspended advertiser can still be billed for what it already consumed — every campaign on the funding plan to have `billing_status` in `PENDING_INVOICE`, `INVOICED` or `RECONCILED`, the trade fund pool, when named, to be `OPEN`, and the computed `gross_spend_cents` to be at least 1. It stamps `issued_at` with the current logical time, `due_at` thirty days later, and `issued_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`, and it moves every campaign on the plan whose `billing_status` is `PENDING_INVOICE` to `INVOICED`.

The waterfall runs in ten pinned steps and the order is not negotiable, because each step consumes the previous step's output. Step one computes `onsite_spend_cents` as the sum of `settled_spend_cents` over settled auctions on on-site slots won by the plan's campaigns whose `requested_at` falls in the billing month. Step two computes `offsite_spend_cents` from the off-site side using whichever basis the reconciliation of the previous paragraph selected — the logged figure or the partner-reported figure. Step three computes `gross_spend_cents` as `onsite_spend_cents` plus `offsite_spend_cents`. Step four computes `managed_service_fee_cents` as `gross_spend_cents` multiplied by the advertiser's `managed_service_fee_pct`, rounded half-up to whole cents; for a self-serve advertiser this is necessarily 0. Step five computes `dispute_credit_cents` as the sum of `approved_credit_cents` over the upheld measurement disputes on this invoice. Step six computes the billable-before-funds figure as `gross_spend_cents` plus `managed_service_fee_cents` less `dispute_credit_cents`, floored at 0. Step seven selects the trade-fund tier rate from that billable-before-funds figure: 0.20 when it is strictly below 1,000,000 cents, 0.35 when it is at least 1,000,000 and strictly below 5,000,000, and 0.50 when it is at or above 5,000,000. Note that the tier is keyed on the post-credit billable figure, not on `gross_spend_cents`, so a large dispute credit can drop the invoice into a lower tier. Step eight computes the deduction ceiling as the smallest of four quantities: `gross_spend_cents` multiplied by the pool's `deduction_cap_pct` rounded half-up to whole cents; the billable-before-funds figure multiplied by the tier rate rounded half-up to whole cents; the pool's `available_cents`; and the plan's `trade_fund_share_cents` less the sum of `trade_fund_deduction_cents` already taken by the plan's other invoices whose `invoice_status` is `ISSUED`, `DISPUTED`, `ADJUSTED` or `SETTLED`. Each of the two rounded quantities is rounded before it enters the comparison, never after. Step nine sets `trade_fund_deduction_cents` to that ceiling floored at 0, and to exactly 0 when the invoice names no pool. Step ten sets `net_cash_due_cents` to the billable-before-funds figure less `trade_fund_deduction_cents`, floored at 0.

Off-site reconciliation is what makes step two determinate, and it is run by `NetworkAdmin`, `BillingOps` or `MeasurementAnalyst` against a campaign with `offsite_reported_spend_cents` and a `reporting_partner_code`. The logged off-site figure is the sum of `settled_spend_cents` over settled auctions on off-site slots won by that campaign's line items. `offsite_variance_pct` is 0.00 when both the logged and the reported figures are 0, exactly 100.00 when the logged figure is 0 but the reported figure is positive, and otherwise the reported figure less the logged figure, divided by the logged figure, multiplied by 100 and rounded half-up to two decimal places; the sign is preserved, so a partner under-reporting yields a negative variance. The basis is `LOGGED_BASIS` when the absolute value of the variance is at or above 2.00 and `REPORTED_BASIS` when it is strictly below — a variance of exactly 2.00 selects `LOGGED_BASIS`, meaning the network trusts its own logs once the discrepancy reaches two percent in either direction. The invoice's `variance_pct` is the greatest absolute `offsite_variance_pct` across the plan's campaigns, with ties broken by ascending campaign `created_at`, and 0.00 when the plan has no campaigns with off-site delivery.

Issuance has three further consequences beyond the arithmetic. `trade_fund_deduction_cents` moves out of the pool's `available_cents` and into its `deducted_cents`. A pool whose recomputed `available_cents` would fall below 0 has the deduction capped at whatever `available_cents` it actually held and moves from `OPEN` to `FROZEN` with `frozen_reason` `OVERDRAWN`; unlike a manual deduction, invoice issuance may drive the pool to exactly 0 and freeze it rather than being refused. The advertiser's `credit_exposure_cents` is recomputed, and an advertiser whose recomputed exposure exceeds its effective credit limit moves from `ACTIVE` to `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` `CREDIT_BREACH`, dragging the full three-level suspension cascade of §3.1 behind it. The advertiser's `dispute_credit_balance_cents` is also consumed up to the `dispute_credit_cents` the invoice applied.

Filing a measurement dispute moves the invoice to `DISPUTED`, from `ISSUED` or from `ADJUSTED`, and moves every covered campaign's `billing_status` from `INVOICED` to `DISPUTED`. The reverse transition back to `ISSUED` is automatic: as soon as no dispute on the invoice remains in `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `INFO_REQUESTED`, `ESCALATED` or `UPHELD`, the invoice returns to `ISSUED` and every covered campaign's `billing_status` returns from `DISPUTED` to `INVOICED`. The issued-disputed-issued cycle may repeat for as long as the invoice remains unsettled.

Adjustment moves a `DISPUTED` invoice to `ADJUSTED`, is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `BillingOps`, and requires `adjustment_amount_cents` and an `adjustment_reason`. It requires at least one dispute on the invoice to be `UPHELD` and `adjustment_round` to be strictly below 2. It raises `adjustment_round` by 1, sets `dispute_credit_cents` to the sum of upheld `approved_credit_cents` plus the supplied `adjustment_amount_cents`, and re-runs the waterfall **from step six onward**: `onsite_spend_cents`, `offsite_spend_cents` and `gross_spend_cents` are left exactly as issued, while the tier, the deduction ceiling, `trade_fund_deduction_cents` and `net_cash_due_cents` are all recomputed. The excess trade fund deduction is returned — the pool's `deducted_cents` falls and its `available_cents` rises by the delta — and the advertiser's `credit_exposure_cents` and `dispute_credit_balance_cents` are recomputed.

Settlement is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `BillingOps` from both `ISSUED` and `ADJUSTED` and requires `paid_amount_cents` and a `payment_reference`. It requires that no dispute on the invoice be in `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `INFO_REQUESTED` or `ESCALATED`, and that `paid_amount_cents` equal `net_cash_due_cents` exactly — partial payment and overpayment are both refused. It stamps `settled_at`, moves every campaign on the plan whose `billing_status` is `INVOICED` or `DISPUTED` to `RECONCILED` with `roas` and `iroas` recomputed, and recomputes the advertiser's `credit_exposure_cents` and `dispute_credit_balance_cents`.

Write-off is exclusive to `NetworkAdmin`, requires a `write_off_reason`, and is accepted from `ISSUED` and from `ADJUSTED`. It requires the current logical time to be at or after `due_at` plus sixty days and requires the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from `issued_by_user_id`. It is the compensating transaction for issuance and conserves the pool identity: the full `trade_fund_deduction_cents` — the post-adjustment figure when the invoice passed through `ADJUSTED` — moves back from the pool's `deducted_cents` to its `available_cents`, restoring the invariant that `accrued_cents` equals `available_cents` plus `reserved_cents` plus `deducted_cents`. A pool whose `frozen_reason` is `OVERDRAWN` and whose `available_cents` thereby returns to 0 or above moves from `FROZEN` back to `OPEN` with `frozen_reason` cleared to `NONE`. Every campaign on the plan whose `billing_status` is `INVOICED` or `DISPUTED` moves to `RECONCILED`, the advertiser's `credit_exposure_cents` is recomputed, and an advertiser whose `onboarding_status` is `ACTIVE` is suspended with `suspension_reason` `CREDIT_BREACH`, because a written-off invoice is by definition a credit failure. `SETTLED` and `WRITTEN_OFF` are the invoice's terminal states, and `RECONCILED` is the billing dimension's.

### 3.9. Measurement Disputes and the Reporting Service Level

A measurement dispute is filed by `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` or the owning `AdvertiserUser` from `invoice_id`, `dispute_category`, `claimed_amount_cents`, the mandatory `expected_invoice_version`, and an optional `line_item_id` and `evidence_note`. `dispute_category` is `IMPRESSION_DISCREPANCY`, `ATTRIBUTION_WINDOW`, `OFFSITE_SPEND_VARIANCE` or `INCREMENTALITY_RESTATEMENT`. `claimed_amount_cents` is at least 1 and may not exceed the invoice's `net_cash_due_cents` plus its `trade_fund_deduction_cents`, which together are the whole of what the invoice can give back. `line_item_id` is nullable — a dispute may be invoice-wide — and when non-null the named line item must belong to a campaign on the invoice's funding plan. The invoice must be `ISSUED` or `ADJUSTED` and the current logical time must be strictly before `due_at` plus fifteen days, which is the dispute grace window. The dispute is born `SUBMITTED` with `filed_at` stamped at the current logical time, `info_request_count` 0, `review_rounds` 0, `escalation_level` 0, `sla_breached` false, `approved_credit_cents` 0, and `first_response_at`, `assigned_analyst_user_id` and `resolved_by_user_id` all null. Filing increments the invoice's `version` by 1.

The reporting service level is computed at filing and recomputed on every event that touches it. The base hours come from `dispute_category`: 72 for `IMPRESSION_DISCREPANCY`, 120 for `ATTRIBUTION_WINDOW`, 96 for `OFFSITE_SPEND_VARIANCE` and 240 for `INCREMENTALITY_RESTATEMENT`. A managed advertiser earns a reduction of 24 hours; a self-serve advertiser earns none. `sla_response_hours` is the base hours less the managed reduction plus 48 hours for each information request already made, with the whole result floored at 24 hours — so no dispute ever carries a service level tighter than a single day. `sla_due_at` is `filed_at` plus `sla_response_hours`, and it therefore moves outward as the information-request count grows. The breach test is deliberately asymmetric at the boundary. When a first response exists, `sla_breached` is true only when `first_response_at` is **strictly after** `sla_due_at`, so a response landing exactly at the deadline is on time. When no first response exists, `sla_breached` is true when the current logical time is **at or after** `sla_due_at`, so an absent response at exactly the deadline is already a breach.

Review assignment moves a `SUBMITTED` dispute to `UNDER_REVIEW`, is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `MeasurementAnalyst`, and requires `assigned_analyst_user_id`. It requires the invoice to be `DISPUTED` and, when `dispute_category` is `INCREMENTALITY_RESTATEMENT`, requires the referenced campaign to hold at least one study whose `study_status` is `PUBLISHED` or `INVALIDATED` — a restatement claim cannot precede a measurement. It stamps `first_response_at` with the current logical time when that field is still null, sets `assigned_analyst_user_id` to the acting `X-User-ID`, raises `review_rounds` by 1, and recomputes `sla_breached` under the strict-after rule.

An information request moves an `UNDER_REVIEW` dispute to `INFO_REQUESTED`, is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `MeasurementAnalyst`, and requires an `information_request_note`. It requires `info_request_count` to be strictly below 2, raises that count by 1, and extends `sla_due_at` by 48 hours with `sla_response_hours` recomputed accordingly. Providing the requested evidence moves the dispute back to `UNDER_REVIEW`, is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser`, requires an `evidence_note`, requires the current logical time to be strictly before `sla_due_at`, raises `review_rounds` by 1 and recomputes `sla_breached`. The review-request-review cycle is bounded at two information requests: a third request against a dispute whose `info_request_count` has already reached 2 is refused as a conflict, and the review must be concluded or escalated instead.

Escalation lands the dispute in `ESCALATED` and carries two distinct meanings distinguished by `escalation_level`. The first meaning is a service-level failure. When the daily checkpoint sweep finds the current logical time at or after `sla_due_at` with no information provided, an `INFO_REQUESTED` dispute escalates with `sla_breached` set true and `escalation_level` set to 1; when the sweep finds the same condition against an `UNDER_REVIEW` dispute, it escalates the same way. The second meaning is round exhaustion, which carries `escalation_level` 2 and leaves `sla_breached` as it stood, because no deadline was missed. An operator escalates explicitly: `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser` may escalate an `UNDER_REVIEW` or `INFO_REQUESTED` dispute with an `escalation_reason`. An explicit escalation of a dispute whose `info_request_count` has already reached 2 is a round-exhaustion escalation and sets `escalation_level` to 2 outright; any other explicit escalation raises `escalation_level` by 1 from whatever it held, so a dispute escalated once for a breach and escalated again reaches level 2. Every escalation, whichever route it took, re-runs the service-level clock at the escalated base hours and clears `first_response_at` back to null, so the escalated review must earn its own first response. The distinction between level 1 and level 2 is load-bearing at resolution.

The resolution fork is rendered from `UNDER_REVIEW` and from `ESCALATED` with a `decision` of `UPHOLD` or `REJECT`, a mandatory `approved_credit_amount_cents` and an optional `resolution_note`. From `UNDER_REVIEW` the fork is available to `NetworkAdmin` and `MeasurementAnalyst`; from `ESCALATED` it is reserved to `NetworkAdmin`. Both branches stamp `resolved_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID` and both require `approved_credit_amount_cents` to be no greater than `claimed_amount_cents`. From `UNDER_REVIEW` an upholding resolution additionally requires the invoice to be `DISPUTED`, and — when `dispute_category` is `INCREMENTALITY_RESTATEMENT` — requires the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from the `published_by_user_id` of the campaign's latest published study, so the analyst who published a measurement may not adjudicate a claim against it. From `UNDER_REVIEW` a rejecting resolution requires `sla_breached` to be false: a dispute the network answered late cannot simply be dismissed. From `ESCALATED` an upholding resolution requires `escalation_level` to be at least 1, while a rejecting resolution requires `escalation_level` to be exactly 2 — a dispute escalated for a service-level breach can no longer be rejected at all and must be upheld, whereas one escalated for round exhaustion may be rejected.

The two branches then diverge completely. `UPHOLD` lands the dispute in `UPHELD`, computes `approved_credit_cents`, and runs a depth-three unwind. Level one recomputes the invoice's `dispute_credit_cents` and moves the invoice from `DISPUTED` to `ADJUSTED` with `trade_fund_deduction_cents` and `net_cash_due_cents` recomputed through the waterfall. Level two returns the freed deduction to the pool, lowering `deducted_cents` and raising `available_cents`; a pool whose `frozen_reason` is `OVERDRAWN` and whose `available_cents` reaches 0 or above moves from `FROZEN` to `OPEN`. Level three is that unfreezing's own consequence: any budget plan of the same advertiser and fiscal period that is `EXHAUSTED` with a positive `available_cents` returns to `ACTIVE`. The advertiser's `dispute_credit_balance_cents` and `credit_exposure_cents` are recomputed, and every mutated row's `version` is incremented by 1. `REJECT` lands the dispute in `REJECTED` with `approved_credit_cents` set to exactly 0 and moves no money at all; when no other non-terminal dispute remains on the invoice, the invoice returns from `DISPUTED` to `ISSUED` — never to `ADJUSTED` — and each affected campaign's `billing_status` returns from `DISPUTED` to `INVOICED`.

The awarded credit is bounded by an evidence cap that depends on the category. For `IMPRESSION_DISCREPANCY` the cap is the invoice's `onsite_spend_cents` multiplied by 0.25, rounded half-up to whole cents. For `ATTRIBUTION_WINDOW` the cap is the sum of `attributed_credit_cents` over the disputed campaign's attributed sales whose `within_window` is false — the credit the advertiser argues should never have been excluded. For `OFFSITE_SPEND_VARIANCE` the cap is the campaign's `offsite_reported_spend_cents` less its logged off-site figure, floored at 0. For `INCREMENTALITY_RESTATEMENT` the cap is the invoice's `gross_spend_cents` multiplied by the quantity one less the smaller of 1.0 and the campaign's `iroas` divided by 3.0, rounded half-up to whole cents — a campaign whose incremental return already reaches three times its spend earns a cap of 0. An uplift of 1.10 is applied to the cap when, and only when, `sla_breached` is true **and** `escalation_level` is exactly 1; a level-2 escalation earns no uplift even if the service level was also breached, and a breach without escalation earns none either. `approved_credit_cents` is then the smallest of four quantities: the supplied `approved_credit_amount_cents`; `claimed_amount_cents`; the evidence cap multiplied by the uplift and rounded half-up to whole cents; and the invoice's `net_cash_due_cents` plus its `trade_fund_deduction_cents`, which is the total the invoice can give back. The uplifted cap is rounded before it enters the comparison, never after the comparison has chosen it.

Withdrawal is available to `NetworkAdmin`, `AccountManager` and the owning `AdvertiserUser`, requires a `withdrawal_reason`, and is accepted from `SUBMITTED` and from `INFO_REQUESTED`. It lands the dispute in `WITHDRAWN` with `approved_credit_cents` set to 0, and — when no other non-terminal dispute remains on the invoice — returns the invoice from `DISPUTED` to `ISSUED` and every covered campaign's `billing_status` from `DISPUTED` to `INVOICED`. `UPHELD`, `REJECTED` and `WITHDRAWN` are the dispute's terminal states.
