# Official Grain Inspection API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Official grain inspection is a public trust exercise carried out by two very different kinds of office: state agencies operating under a delegation of federal authority, and designated agencies operating under direct federal supervision. Both draw samples at the point of loading or unloading, determine grade from test weight, moisture, damage and foreign material against published factor tables, run protein and specialty analyses on instruments enrolled in a comparison programme, weigh lots on certified scales under countersignature, and issue the official certificates that foreign and domestic buyers pay against. The work is unforgiving in two directions at once: a determination that is wrong costs the applicant money and gives them a statutory right to reinspection, appeal and board review; a determination that is right but recorded sloppily — an uncalibrated instrument, an unsupervised weighing, a sample whose seal cannot be accounted for — destroys the certificate's evidentiary value entirely.

This API is the system of record for that work. It tracks the request from the moment an elevator or exporter orders service through sampling, grading, analysis, weighing, vessel loading supervision, certification, fee assessment and settlement, and — where the applicant exercises their rights — through reinspection, appeal and board appeal. Because grading and weighing proceed on independent timetables and are certified independently, every inspection request carries two status dimensions that advance separately. Because equipment is discovered to be out of tolerance *after* it has been used, the system must be able to walk backwards: void the weighings and analyses that depended on the faulty device, void the certificates that carry their numbers, and reverse the fees that were charged, all without ever losing the two-sided arithmetic of the applicant's account.

A second concern runs through the whole design: the people. Inspectors are licensed, trained, monitored and — when appeals are reversed against them, when their proficiency check samples drift, or when they certify late — suspended automatically. Segregation of duties is not advisory: a weigher may not countersign their own weighing, an inspector may not regrade or vote on an appeal against a determination they themselves produced, and a supervisor may not file a proficiency check against themselves. The requirements below describe the whole of that behaviour. All resources except the logical-clock control are mounted under `/grain-inspection`.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Authority, Users and Role-Based Access Control

Every request must carry a single identity header, `X-User-Id`, naming the acting user. That one value both authenticates the caller and determines the role they act in. A request with a missing or unknown `X-User-Id` is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). A request whose resolved role is not among the roles permitted for the target operation is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403). Authentication is always evaluated before authorization.

Five roles exist, spelled exactly as follows: `ServiceAdmin`, `SupervisingInspector`, `LicensedInspector`, `ApplicantUser` and `Auditor`.

- A `ServiceAdmin` is the field-office administrator of the delegated or designated agency. They configure service areas and fee schedules, register applicant accounts, licence and discipline inspectors, register and certify equipment, and may dismiss appeals.
- A `SupervisingInspector` is a licensed inspector holding the supervision endorsement. They countersign weighings, break seals, run vessel watches, sit on appeal panels, file proficiency comparison checks, and may perform every line-inspection task as well.
- A `LicensedInspector` is a line inspector: they sample, grade, analyse, weigh and issue certificates, but never countersign a weighing, never break a seal, never run a vessel watch and never vote on an appeal.
- An `ApplicantUser` is the elevator or exporter representative. They order service, pay fees, and exercise appeal and reinspection rights — on their own account only.
- An `Auditor` has unrestricted read access across the whole system and no write authority whatsoever, save for the logical-clock control which every role may drive.

For all five roles, `X-User-Id` is an **opaque operator identifier** and is not the primary key of any persisted entity; the canonical values are `admin_user` for `ServiceAdmin`, `supervisor_user` for `SupervisingInspector`, `inspector_user` for `LicensedInspector`, `applicant_user` for `ApplicantUser` and `auditor_user` for `Auditor`. The server does not compare the header to an `Inspector` row's `id`: the inspector actually performing a task is always named explicitly in the request body (`inspector_id`, `weigher_inspector_id`, `supervisor_inspector_id`, `issuing_inspector_id`), never inferred from the header. The one place ownership matters is applicant scope: when the resolved role is `ApplicantUser`, the acting account is the applicant named or reachable from the request (through `applicant_id`, or through the request, certificate or appeal being addressed), and if the header value is itself the `id` of an existing `Applicant` row then that row is the acting account. An `ApplicantUser` who reads or acts on an inspection request, certificate, appeal, sample, grading result, analysis, weighing, watch or payment belonging to a different applicant account is refused with `ERR_APPLICANT_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (HTTP 403).

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination and List Ordering

Collection endpoints accept two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of rows returned; it defaults to 10, may not be smaller than 1 and may not be larger than 100. `offset` skips rows from the head of the result and defaults to 0, its smallest permitted value. Both are echoed back in `meta` alongside `total_count` (the number of rows matching the filters before the window is applied) and `has_more` (true when rows remain beyond the returned window).

Every list endpoint returns rows sorted by `created_at` in ascending order unless a specific requirement says otherwise. Collection endpoints also accept the filter parameters named in their §4 descriptions and in the workflow narratives — filters narrow the result set but never change the ordering or the pagination arithmetic.

### 2.4. Identity, Versioning and Common Record Properties

Every persisted resource carries a server-minted `id`: a UUID string with no type prefix and no embedded meaning. Clients never supply an `id`. Every resource also carries `created_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits recording when the row was first written.

All resources except appeal votes additionally carry `updated_at` — the same format, refreshed on every mutation of the row — and `version`, an integer that begins at 1 and increments by exactly one on every mutation. A recomputation of a derived field counts as a mutation: when a certificate fee assessment pushes an applicant's `charges_total_cents` and `open_balance_cents`, the applicant's `updated_at` and `version` move; when a comparison check updates an instrument's `rolling_bias_pct`, the equipment row's `updated_at` and `version` move; when a vessel watch is created or aborted and the area's `open_watch_count` changes, the service area's `updated_at` and `version` move.

An `AppealVote` is the exception. A cast vote is an immutable act of a panel member, so an appeal vote row carries only `id` and `created_at` — it has **no** `updated_at` and **no** `version` field at all.

Path parameters are resolved strictly left to right. Where a path names a parent collection member before a child — the certificates belonging to one inspection request, or the appeals filed against one certificate — the parent is resolved first, and a missing parent produces the parent's own not-found error (`ERR_INSPECTION_REQUEST_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CERTIFICATE_NOT_FOUND`) before the child collection is ever consulted. The same precedence governs foreign keys carried in a request body: each named row must exist, and the failure is reported with that entity's not-found code — `ERR_SERVICE_AREA_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_APPLICANT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INSPECTOR_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EQUIPMENT_NOT_FOUND` (for `sampling_device_id`, `scale_id` and `instrument_id` alike), `ERR_SAMPLE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_GRADING_RESULT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PROTEIN_ANALYSIS_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_WEIGHING_RECORD_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_APPEAL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_APPEAL_VOTE_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_VESSEL_WATCH_NOT_FOUND`. All of these carry HTTP 404.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock and the Expiry Sweep

Nothing in this system reads the wall clock. A logical clock supplies "now" to every deadline, window and expiry comparison, and it starts at `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`. Any role may advance it by supplying a `current_utc` instant. The clock may move forward or be set to its present value; a strictly earlier instant is refused with `ERR_CLOCK_NOT_MONOTONIC`.

Advancing the clock runs a deterministic expiry sweep, processing candidate rows in ascending order of `created_at` and, where two rows share a `created_at`, in ascending order of `sequence_no`. Three sweeps run: equipment whose `certified_until` is now in the past moves from `APPROVED` or `DRIFT_WATCH` to `EXPIRED` and can no longer be used for new samples, weighings or analyses; samples whose `file_retention_until` has passed move from `SEALED` or `CONSUMED` to `EXPIRED` and can no longer be analysed or assigned to an appeal; inspectors whose `license_expires_at` has passed move from `ACTIVE` or `PROBATIONARY` to `EXPIRED`, and each such inspector's `SCHEDULED` vessel watches are aborted with the owning area's `open_watch_count` decremented once per aborted watch. The clock response reports the accepted `current_utc` together with `expired_equipment_count`, `expired_sample_count` and `expired_inspector_count`.

### 2.6. Rounding and Precision Discipline

Official numbers are rounded once, at defined points, and compared only in their rounded form.

- The five grading factors are each rounded to **1 decimal place, round half up**, before any comparison against a factor table; the rounded set is retained on the determination and is what every downstream consumer reads.
- Dockage deductions are rounded to **whole pounds, round half up**. Net weights and all draft weights are whole pounds.
- Bushel quantities and every percentage the system derives — reported protein, basis adjustment, comparison deviation, rolling bias — are rounded to **2 decimal places, round half up**.
- Money is held exclusively in integer cents. Within fee assessment, the volume discount is truncated downward to whole cents (floor), while the overtime surcharge and the authority adjustment are rounded half up to whole cents. Deposit halving on a dismissed appeal truncates downward.
- The inspector performance score is an integer clamped into the range 0 through 100, and its training credit truncates downward.
- Supervision duration is a whole number of minutes obtained by truncating the elapsed seconds downward.

### 2.7. Version Echo on Appeal Filings and Panel Votes

Two operations mutate a shared aggregate that other actors are concurrently advancing, and both therefore demand that the caller echo the version they last observed. This is optimistic concurrency control: the check is about *staleness of the caller's read*, not about timing.

Filing an appeal requires `expected_certificate_version` in the body — a non-optional integer carrying the `version` of the challenged certificate as the client last read it. If it does not equal the certificate's current `version`, the filing is refused with `ERR_CERTIFICATE_VERSION_CONFLICT` (HTTP 412), the certificate is not moved to `UNDER_APPEAL`, and no deposit is charged. A client that read the certificate at `version` 1 and then filed after an equipment cascade had voided it (advancing the certificate to `version` 2) is exactly the case this rule refuses.

Casting a panel vote requires `expected_appeal_version` in the body — again non-optional — carrying the `version` of the appeal as that panel member last read it. A mismatch with the appeal's current `version` is refused with `ERR_APPEAL_VERSION_CONFLICT` (HTTP 412) and no vote is recorded; the member must re-read the tally and resubmit. Since every accepted vote increments the appeal's `version`, a member whose read predates another member's vote is refused.

No other endpoint takes a version echo field. Where these rules do not apply, no `expected_..._version` field is accepted at all.

### 2.8. Cascade Integrity and the Conserved Applicant Ledger

An applicant's account is a strict two-sided ledger and never a mutable running figure. `charges_total_cents` accumulates every charge event — each certificate fee assessed and each appeal deposit charged. `credits_total_cents` accumulates every credit event — each payment received, the full `fee_total_cents` of each certificate whose settlement is reversed, and each appeal refund. `open_balance_cents` is always `charges_total_cents` minus `credits_total_cents`. No event ever edits an earlier event, which means the balance may legitimately go negative when a certificate that was already paid is later reversed.

Reversal is the mechanism by which the system unwinds work founded on equipment that turned out to be untrustworthy, or on a determination an appeal panel has overturned. Whenever a certificate leaves the trustworthy world — voided by an equipment cascade, voided by an appeal decided in the applicant's favour, or superseded by a re-issued certificate after a grade revision — its `fee_settlement_status` becomes `REVERSED` and its full `fee_total_cents` is credited to the applicant, regardless of how much of it had actually been paid. Cascades reach through to their dependents in one atomic step: the record that carried the bad number is invalidated, the certificates carrying that number as a snapshot are voided with their fees reversed, the parent inspection request's status dimension is walked back, and every touched row's `updated_at` and `version` advance.

### 2.9. Segregation of Duties and Licence Gating

Three separations are absolute and are enforced on the operation that would breach them. A weighing must be countersigned by an inspector other than the one who performed it (`ERR_SELF_SUPERVISION_FORBIDDEN`). The inspector who produced the challenged operative grading determination may neither perform the appeal regrade nor cast a vote on that appeal (`ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`). A supervising inspector may not file a proficiency comparison check naming themselves as the checked inspector (`ERR_SELF_CHECK_FORBIDDEN`).

Licence state gates participation. An inspector whose `license_status` is `PROBATIONARY` or `ACTIVE` may sample, grade, analyse and weigh. Only an inspector whose `license_status` is `ACTIVE` may countersign a weighing, hold a vessel watch, perform an appeal regrade or cast a panel vote. `SUSPENDED`, `REVOKED` and `EXPIRED` licences may do none of these. A violation is reported as `ERR_INSPECTOR_LICENSE_INVALID` (HTTP 409). Independently, the named inspector must hold the endorsement the task requires, or the operation fails with `ERR_ENDORSEMENT_MISSING`: `SAMPLING` to draw a sample, `GRADING` to record a grading determination, `PROTEIN` to run an instrument analysis, `WEIGHING` to record a weighing, and — for the inspector issuing a certificate — every endorsement the certificate's content requires (`GRADING` for grade content, `WEIGHING` for weight content, and additionally `SUPERVISION` for an export cargo certificate).

### 2.10. Delegated Versus Designated Authority

A service area declares its `authority_type` as either `DELEGATED` (a state agency acting under delegation) or `DESIGNATED` (a designated agency under federal supervision), and the distinction is load-bearing rather than descriptive. Export cargo certification for foreign buyers may only be performed in a `DESIGNATED` area; attempting it elsewhere fails with `ERR_EXPORT_REQUIRES_DESIGNATED_AREA`. Each area also carries its own cost posture in `fee_multiplier_bp` and its own supervision policy in `supervision_required`, so the same lot inspected under two authorities can produce different fees and different countersignature obligations.

Service is territorial. The inspector and the equipment named in a service-producing operation must belong to the same area as the inspection request being serviced — their `service_area_id` must equal the request's `service_area_id` — otherwise the operation is refused with `ERR_SERVICE_AREA_MISMATCH`. The operations that carry this check are exactly the four enumerated at rung 7 of §2.11 and no others; weighing countersignature in particular does not test the supervisor's area. An area whose `status` is `SUSPENDED` accepts no new work: creating an inspection request, sample, weighing record, protein analysis, certificate or vessel watch in a suspended area fails with `ERR_SERVICE_AREA_SUSPENDED` (HTTP 409), while rows already in flight are left untouched.

### 2.11. The Validation Ladder

When more than one rule could reject a request, the system evaluates them in a fixed order and reports only the **first** violation it finds. The ladder has thirteen rungs, in this sequence:

1. **Authentication** — `X-User-Id` must be present and known, else `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (401). Applies to every endpoint.
2. **Authorization** — the declared role must be permitted for the endpoint, else `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (403). Applies to every endpoint.
3. **Path existence** — every `{id}` in the path must resolve, else that entity's not-found code (404). Applies to every endpoint with a path parameter.
4. **Applicant scope** — an `ApplicantUser` acting outside their own account is refused with `ERR_APPLICANT_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (403). Applies to every endpoint an `ApplicantUser` may reach.
5. **Schema** — required body fields present, correctly typed, inside their declared ranges and enum domains, with unknown fields rejected, else `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (422). Applies to every write endpoint. A body field that carries an instant is correctly typed only when it is a string bearing both the `T` date-time designator and an explicit zone designator — `Z`, or a numeric UTC offset which is then normalised to UTC. A looser value that omits either, such as `2026-08-03 09:00` or `2026-08-03T09:00`, is rejected here as `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` and is never silently read as UTC and carried on to a later rung.
6. **Body reference existence** — every foreign key named in the body must resolve, else that entity's not-found code (404). Applies to every write endpoint carrying a foreign key.
7. **Business referential integrity** — the family of rules that check that the rows named in a body belong together. `ERR_SAMPLE_REQUEST_MISMATCH` guards grading-result creation, protein-analysis creation and appeal sample assignment. `ERR_SERVICE_AREA_MISMATCH` guards sample creation, weighing-record creation, protein-analysis creation and certificate issuance. `ERR_EQUIPMENT_KIND_MISMATCH` guards sample creation, weighing-record creation, protein-analysis creation and comparison-check filing. `ERR_EQUIPMENT_FIELD_APPLICABILITY` guards equipment registration and equipment certification. `ERR_APPEAL_REGRADE_BINDING_INVALID` guards grading-result creation. `ERR_SERVICE_NOT_REQUESTED` guards inspection-request creation, weighing-record creation, protein-analysis creation, vessel-watch creation and — for the `EXPORT_CARGO` type alone — certificate issuance. All are 422.
8. **Concurrency** — the version echoes of §2.7: `ERR_CERTIFICATE_VERSION_CONFLICT` on appeal filing and `ERR_APPEAL_VERSION_CONFLICT` on vote casting, both 412.
9. **State** — the entity must be in a state the requested action is declared from. This rung carries `ERR_SERVICE_AREA_SUSPENDED` (all service-producing writes), `ERR_EQUIPMENT_NOT_APPROVED` (sample, weighing-record and protein-analysis creation), `ERR_EQUIPMENT_WITHDRAWN` (equipment certification and comparison-check filing), `ERR_INSPECTOR_LICENSE_INVALID` (every write naming an inspector), `ERR_SAMPLE_INTEGRITY_BROKEN` (grading-result creation, protein-analysis creation, appeal sample assignment), `ERR_APPEAL_STATE_INVALID` (appeal decision, dismissal and sample assignment), `ERR_CERTIFICATE_UNDER_APPEAL` (appeal filing and certificate issuance), `ERR_WEIGHING_SUPERVISION_REQUIRED`, `ERR_VESSEL_WATCH_NOT_CLOSED` and `ERR_GRADING_RESULT_MISSING` (all three on certificate issuance), `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_NOT_DRAFT` (fee-schedule activation), and the general `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` for any action endpoint invoked from a state the state machine does not declare it from. All are 409.
10. **Segregation of duties** — `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (grading-result creation and vote casting), `ERR_SELF_SUPERVISION_FORBIDDEN` (weighing countersignature), `ERR_SELF_CHECK_FORBIDDEN` (comparison-check filing), `ERR_DUPLICATE_PANEL_VOTE` (vote casting), `ERR_ENDORSEMENT_MISSING` (sample, grading-result, protein-analysis, weighing-record creation and certificate issuance), `ERR_EXPORT_REQUIRES_DESIGNATED_AREA` (certificate issuance). All are 422.
11. **Business constraints** — `ERR_SAMPLE_PORTIONS_EXHAUSTED` (grading-result creation, protein-analysis creation, appeal sample assignment), `ERR_DUPLICATE_OPERATIVE_RECORD` (grading-result creation, weighing-record creation, appeal filing), `ERR_DUPLICATE_IDENTIFIER` (sample, applicant, inspector and equipment creation), `ERR_SCALE_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (weighing-record creation), `ERR_FACTOR_SET_INCONSISTENT` (grading-result creation), `ERR_INSPECTOR_NOT_QUALIFIED` (licence activation and reinstatement), `ERR_APPEAL_QUORUM_NOT_MET` (appeal decision), `ERR_BOARD_PANEL_COMPOSITION_INVALID` (vote casting), `ERR_HOLDS_NOT_FULLY_SUPERVISED` (watch closure), `ERR_ANALYSIS_RECOMPUTE_NOT_APPLICABLE` (analysis restatement). All are 422.
12. **Temporal constraints** — `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_EXPIRED` and `ERR_APPEAL_ESCALATION_INVALID` (appeal filing), `ERR_WATCH_WINDOW_INVALID` (watch creation and watch start), `ERR_INSPECTOR_WATCH_OVERLAP` and `ERR_AREA_WATCH_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (watch creation), `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (fee-schedule drafting and activation), `ERR_EXPIRY_IN_PAST` (licence activation and equipment certification), `ERR_CLOCK_NOT_MONOTONIC` (clock advance). All are 422.
13. **Fiscal constraints** — `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_FEE_SCHEDULE` (certificate issuance and appeal filing), `ERR_CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (certificate issuance), `ERR_PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_ALLOCATABLE` (payment recording). All are 422.

The consequence of the ordering is worth stating plainly: a suspended area surfaces as `ERR_SERVICE_AREA_SUSPENDED` rather than being masked by a later portion or fee failure; a stale version echo surfaces as a 412 before any state or business rule is consulted; and a fee that would breach the credit limit is the very last thing checked, because it can only be evaluated after the fee has been computed.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up a Field Office: Areas, Fee Schedules and Applicant Accounts

A `ServiceAdmin` opens a field office by registering a **service area** with a `name` of 1 to 120 characters, a `state_code` of exactly two uppercase letters, and an `authority_type` of `DELEGATED` or `DESIGNATED`. Four further settings may be supplied and otherwise default: `status` starts `ACTIVE` (the alternative is `SUSPENDED`); `fee_multiplier_bp` starts at 10000, meaning no adjustment, and may be set anywhere from 10000 up to 15000; `supervision_required` starts true; and `concurrent_watch_capacity` starts at 2 and must be at least 1. The area also exposes `open_watch_count`, a derived count — always beginning at 0 — of the area's vessel watches whose `status` is `SCHEDULED` or `IN_PROGRESS`. The same administrator may later retune `status`, `fee_multiplier_bp`, `supervision_required` and `concurrent_watch_capacity` on an existing area; suspending an area stops new intake without disturbing work already recorded. Areas may be listed with `state_code`, `authority_type` and `status` filters.

Pricing lives in **fee schedules**, each drafted against one area through its `service_area_id` and one `service_class` drawn from `GRADING`, `WEIGHING`, `PROTEIN`, `EXPORT_SUPERVISION` and `APPEAL`. A schedule states a `base_fee_cents` of zero or more, a `per_thousand_bushel_rate_cents` that defaults to 0, an `overtime_multiplier_bp` between 0 and 10000 that defaults to 2500, a `minimum_fee_cents` floor that defaults to 0, and an applicability window: `effective_from` is the inclusive start and `effective_to` the exclusive end, with a null `effective_to` meaning open-ended. `effective_to`, when given, must be strictly after `effective_from`, or the draft is refused with `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_WINDOW_CONFLICT`. A new schedule is born `DRAFT`.

Activation is the moment a schedule starts pricing work. Only a `DRAFT` schedule may be activated — a schedule already `ACTIVE` or already `SUPERSEDED` is refused with `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_NOT_DRAFT` — and activation is refused with `ERR_FEE_SCHEDULE_WINDOW_CONFLICT` when the schedule's window overlaps that of another `ACTIVE` schedule for the same area and the same `service_class`. On success the schedule becomes `ACTIVE`, and any previously `ACTIVE` schedule for that same area-and-class pair whose window has already ended is marked `SUPERSEDED`. Schedules may be listed by `service_area_id`, `service_class` and `status`.

Applicants are the billed parties: an elevator, an exporter or a producer. A `ServiceAdmin` registers one with a legal `name`, an `account_code` that must be globally unique and consist of 3 to 20 characters drawn from uppercase letters, digits and hyphens, a `contact_email` to which certificates and appeal decisions are notified, and an optional `credit_limit_cents` that defaults to 500000 and may not be negative. A duplicate `account_code` is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_IDENTIFIER`. Three derived figures complete the account and all start at 0: `charges_total_cents`, `credits_total_cents` and `open_balance_cents`, governed by the conservation rule of §2.8. The applicant list may be filtered by `account_code`, and reading one applicant returns the ledger totals.

### 3.2. Inspector Licensing, Training and Performance Monitoring

Licensing an **inspector** attaches a person to one area through `service_area_id` and records a `license_number` — unique within that area, so a collision yields `ERR_DUPLICATE_IDENTIFIER` — a `full_name` that is printed on certificates, a non-empty set of `endorsements` drawn from `SAMPLING`, `GRADING`, `PROTEIN`, `WEIGHING` and `SUPERVISION`, and a `license_expires_at` instant. Every new licence begins `PROBATIONARY` with `performance_score` 100, `training_hours_completed` 0, `appeal_reversal_count` 0, `check_sample_deviation_count` 0, `late_certificate_count` 0 and a null `suspension_reason`. Inspectors may be listed by `service_area_id` and `license_status`, and reading one returns the performance counters.

Training is filed against an inspector as a `course_code` and a number of `hours`, which is added to `training_hours_completed` and immediately triggers a recomputation of `performance_score`. The score is a weighted deduction from a perfect 100: subtract 18 for each appeal reversed against the inspector, subtract 10 for each proficiency deviation counted against them, subtract 3 for each late certificate, then add a training credit equal to the whole number of complete ten-hour blocks of `training_hours_completed`, capped at 12. The result is clamped so that it never falls below 0 and never rises above 100. A late certificate, for this purpose, is one the inspector issued more than 48 hours — two full days — after the inspection request was created. A proficiency deviation counts against the inspector when the absolute value of a comparison check's deviation exceeds 0.25 percentage points, a deliberately tighter bar than the one that troubles the instrument itself. The score is recomputed after every appeal decision, every comparison check, every certificate issuance and every training filing.

Activation moves the licence `PROBATIONARY → ACTIVE`, and it is the gate through which supervision authority is granted. It requires at least 24 completed training hours and a `performance_score` of at least 80; falling short of either yields `ERR_INSPECTOR_NOT_QUALIFIED`. It also requires that `license_expires_at` still lie strictly in the future relative to the logical clock, otherwise `ERR_EXPIRY_IN_PAST`. Only after activation may the inspector countersign weighings, hold vessel watches, regrade on appeal or sit on an appeal panel.

Suspension arrives in two ways, and both record why. A `ServiceAdmin` may suspend a `PROBATIONARY` or `ACTIVE` licence at will, supplying a `suspension_reason` that is stored verbatim; the inspector's `SCHEDULED` and `IN_PROGRESS` vessel watches are aborted and the owning area's `open_watch_count` is decremented once for each. Automatic suspension is the threshold trigger: whenever a recomputation leaves an `ACTIVE` inspector with a `performance_score` strictly below 70, the licence moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` on the spot with `suspension_reason` recorded as `AUTO_PERFORMANCE`, that inspector's `SCHEDULED` watches are aborted with the area's `open_watch_count` decremented accordingly, and any non-terminal appeal on which they are the sole panel member stays `UNDER_REVIEW` while their further votes are refused. A score of exactly 70 does not trigger it. Because the recomputation runs on appeal decisions, comparison checks and certificate issuances, an inspector can be suspended as a side effect of someone else's operation.

Recovery and finality close the licence lifecycle. Reinstatement moves `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`, requires a `performance_score` of at least 80 (`ERR_INSPECTOR_NOT_QUALIFIED` otherwise) and a `license_expires_at` still in the future, and clears `suspension_reason`. Revocation moves `SUSPENDED → REVOKED` with a `suspension_reason`; `REVOKED` is terminal and every later operation naming that inspector is refused. Expiry, driven only by the clock, moves `ACTIVE → EXPIRED` or `PROBATIONARY → EXPIRED`; when an `ACTIVE` licence expires the inspector's `SCHEDULED` watches are aborted, and when a `PROBATIONARY` one expires there are no watches to abort because a probationary inspector may never hold one.

### 3.3. Approved Equipment: Certification, Calibration and the Comparison Programme

Equipment is registered against an area with `service_area_id`, an `equipment_kind` of `SAMPLING_DEVICE`, `SCALE` or `PROTEIN_INSTRUMENT`, an `asset_code` of 1 to 40 characters that must be unique within the area (`ERR_DUPLICATE_IDENTIFIER` otherwise) and a manufacturer `model`. Two fields are scale-only: `capacity_lbs`, the rated capacity in pounds, and `tolerance_lbs`, the maintenance tolerance. Both are required when `equipment_kind` is `SCALE` and both must be omitted for the other two kinds; either mistake is `ERR_EQUIPMENT_FIELD_APPLICABILITY`. Registration leaves the row in `PENDING_APPROVAL` with a null `certified_until`, a null `last_certification_error_lbs`, an empty `recent_deviations_pct`, a null `rolling_bias_pct`, a null `last_comparison_deviation_pct`, a `comparison_check_count` of 0 and a `consecutive_out_of_tolerance_checks` of 0. Equipment may be listed by `service_area_id`, `equipment_kind` and `approval_status`.

Certification is how a device becomes usable and how it recovers. The administrator supplies the new `certified_until`, which must be strictly in the future relative to the logical clock or the attempt fails with `ERR_EXPIRY_IN_PAST`. For a `SCALE`, `test_error_lbs` — the error observed on the test — is required, and must be omitted for a sampling device or protein instrument (`ERR_EQUIPMENT_FIELD_APPLICABILITY`). A scale passes when the absolute value of `test_error_lbs` is at most its `tolerance_lbs`. On a pass, the row moves `PENDING_APPROVAL → APPROVED` (or recovers `EXPIRED → APPROVED`, or `OUT_OF_TOLERANCE → APPROVED` provided `consecutive_out_of_tolerance_checks` is still below 3), `certified_until` is stored, `consecutive_out_of_tolerance_checks` is reset to 0, and for a scale `last_certification_error_lbs` records the observed error. Recalibrating a protein instrument out of `OUT_OF_TOLERANCE` additionally empties `recent_deviations_pct` and returns `rolling_bias_pct` to null, so the comparison window starts fresh. A `WITHDRAWN` row accepts no certification at all and is refused with `ERR_EQUIPMENT_WITHDRAWN`.

When a scale's certification test fails — the absolute `test_error_lbs` exceeds `tolerance_lbs` — the system does not merely mark the scale; it repudiates the weighings that scale produced. Any weighing record on that scale whose `status` is `WEIGHED` or `SUPERVISED` becomes `VOIDED`; every certificate carrying that record's `net_lbs_snapshot` becomes `VOIDED` with `fee_settlement_status` `REVERSED` and its whole `fee_total_cents` credited to the applicant; and the affected inspection request walks its weighing dimension back from `WEIGHT_CERTIFIED` to `PENDING`, with `certified_quantity_bushels` reverting to the applicant's own `declared_quantity_bushels`.

Protein instruments live inside a comparison programme. A `ServiceAdmin` or `SupervisingInspector` files a check naming the `instrument_id`, the `inspector_id` whose proficiency is under examination, the official `reference_value_pct` and the `instrument_reading_pct` obtained. The instrument must be a `PROTEIN_INSTRUMENT` (`ERR_EQUIPMENT_KIND_MISMATCH`); a `WITHDRAWN`, `PENDING_APPROVAL` or `EXPIRED` instrument accepts no check and is refused with `ERR_EQUIPMENT_WITHDRAWN`; and a supervisor may not name themselves as the checked inspector (`ERR_SELF_CHECK_FORBIDDEN`). The check's deviation is the instrument reading minus the reference value, rounded half up to 2 decimals, and it is stored as `last_comparison_deviation_pct` while `comparison_check_count` increases by one. The instrument's `recent_deviations_pct` keeps at most the three most recent deviations including this one, oldest first in ascending creation order, and `rolling_bias_pct` is the arithmetic mean of those retained deviations, rounded half up to 2 decimals. A comparison-check filing is the recording of a new check, so it answers `201 Created` rather than `200 OK`, and its `data` carries the updated instrument row.

The resolution ladder is evaluated in exactly this order. The check resolves to `OUT_OF_TOLERANCE` when the absolute deviation of this single check exceeds 0.50 percentage points **or** the absolute `rolling_bias_pct` exceeds 0.30. Otherwise, when the absolute `rolling_bias_pct` exceeds 0.15, it resolves to `DRIFT_WATCH`. Otherwise it resolves to `APPROVED`. Boundaries are exclusive on the failing side: a rolling bias of exactly 0.30 is drift, not out of tolerance, and a rolling bias of exactly 0.15 is a clean `APPROVED`. Note also that a deviation of 0.30 counts against the inspector's `check_sample_deviation_count`, because that counter's bar is 0.25, while leaving the instrument merely on drift watch.

Each resolution has its own consequences, and they are symmetric in weight. An `APPROVED` resolution keeps an `APPROVED` instrument approved and recovers a `DRIFT_WATCH` instrument to `APPROVED` provided its `certified_until` is still in the future, resetting `consecutive_out_of_tolerance_checks` to 0. A `DRIFT_WATCH` resolution moves `APPROVED → DRIFT_WATCH`, resets `consecutive_out_of_tolerance_checks` to 0, and leaves the instrument fully usable for new analyses. An `OUT_OF_TOLERANCE` resolution moves `APPROVED → OUT_OF_TOLERANCE` or `DRIFT_WATCH → OUT_OF_TOLERANCE` — the two paths run the identical cascade — and increments `consecutive_out_of_tolerance_checks` by one. When that counter reaches 3, the instrument moves `OUT_OF_TOLERANCE → WITHDRAWN` and is permanently unusable: no further certification and no further comparison check will be accepted on it.

The out-of-tolerance cascade is the largest compensation in the system. Every analysis on that instrument whose `status` is `ACCEPTED` or `RECOMPUTED` and which was created at or after the instrument's previous comparison check is set to `REJECTED_CALIBRATION`, processed in ascending order of `created_at` with `sequence_no` breaking ties. Each rejected analysis releases the sample portion it had consumed: the sample's `portions_consumed` decreases by one so that `portions_available` rises again, and a sample sitting in `CONSUMED` returns `CONSUMED → SEALED`. Every certificate carrying a rejected analysis's `protein_reported_pct_snapshot` becomes `VOIDED` with its fee `REVERSED` and credited. Finally the checked inspector's counters and `performance_score` are recomputed, which may auto-suspend them.

### 3.4. Ordering Service and Drawing the Official Sample

An **inspection request** is the order for service, raised by an `ApplicantUser` for their own account or by a `ServiceAdmin` on their behalf. It names the `applicant_id` to be billed and the `service_area_id` that will perform the work, states the `commodity` as `CORN` or `SOYBEANS`, records whether sampling happens at `LOADING` or at `UNLOADING` in `point_of_service`, carries the applicant's own `lot_identifier`, declares a `declared_quantity_bushels` strictly greater than zero, and lists the `requested_services` ordered from `GRADING`, `WEIGHING`, `PROTEIN` and `EXPORT_SUPERVISION`. That list may not be empty and **must** include `GRADING`; a request without it is refused with `ERR_SERVICE_NOT_REQUESTED`. The same error guards every later operation whose service was never ordered: `WEIGHING` for a weighing record, `PROTEIN` for an analysis, and `EXPORT_SUPERVISION` for a vessel watch or an export cargo certificate.

Each request carries **two independent status dimensions** that advance on disjoint triggers. `grading_status` starts at `OPEN` and runs `OPEN → SAMPLED → GRADED → GRADE_CERTIFIED`, with `GRADE_CERTIFIED → UNDER_APPEAL` and back again when rights are exercised. `weighing_status` starts at `PENDING` when `requested_services` includes `WEIGHING` and at `NOT_REQUIRED` when it does not, and runs through `WEIGHED`, `SUPERVISED` and `WEIGHT_CERTIFIED`. Neither dimension constrains the other. Two derived figures sit alongside them: `grade_revision_count` starts at 0 and counts the appeals on this request decided in the applicant's favour, and `certified_quantity_bushels` is the quantity fees are assessed on — it is initialised from `declared_quantity_bushels` and becomes the `net_bushels` of the operative weighing record once one exists in `WEIGHED` or `SUPERVISED` status. Requests may be listed by `applicant_id`, `service_area_id`, `commodity`, `grading_status` and `weighing_status`, and the certificates issued on a request may be listed from the request itself with `certificate_type` and `status` filters.

Sampling is where physical custody begins. A `LicensedInspector` or `SupervisingInspector` draws a sample against the `inspection_request_id`, using the approved `sampling_device_id` and acting as the `inspector_id`. The `sample_type` defaults to `ORIGINAL` and may instead be `FILE` for a retained duplicate or `APPEAL` for a sample drawn for a challenge. A tamper-evident `seal_number` is recorded and must be unique within that inspection request (`ERR_DUPLICATE_IDENTIFIER` otherwise), and `portions_count` states into how many official portions the sample was divided — at least 1 and at most 10. The device must be of kind `SAMPLING_DEVICE` (`ERR_EQUIPMENT_KIND_MISMATCH`), must be `APPROVED` with a `certified_until` still in the future (`ERR_EQUIPMENT_NOT_APPROVED`), and must belong to the request's area; the inspector must hold the `SAMPLING` endorsement, must hold a `PROBATIONARY` or `ACTIVE` licence, and must belong to the request's area. On success the sample is `SEALED`, `collected_at` is stamped from the logical clock, `file_retention_until` is set to exactly 90 days after `collected_at`, `portions_consumed` and `portions_retained` are 0 so that `portions_available` equals `portions_count`, and — for an `ORIGINAL` sample — the request advances `OPEN → SAMPLED`.

Portion accounting is the sample's scarce resource. `portions_available` is always `portions_count` less `portions_consumed` less `portions_retained`. A grading determination or a protein analysis consumes one portion; an appeal sample assignment retains one. Any of those three operations attempted when `portions_available` is 0 is refused with `ERR_SAMPLE_PORTIONS_EXHAUSTED`. When a consumption drives `portions_available` to 0 the sample moves `SEALED → CONSUMED`; the compensating release described in §3.3 moves it back `CONSUMED → SEALED`.

Integrity is the sample's other scarce resource, and its loss is final. A `SupervisingInspector` may record a broken seal with an optional `reason`, moving the sample `SEALED → BROKEN` or `CONSUMED → BROKEN`; from `SEALED` this is only permitted when no non-terminal appeal currently holds the sample as its assigned regrade sample, and from `CONSUMED` there is no further precondition. A broken sample is treated as having no available portions and can never be analysed again. Expiry, driven by the clock passing `file_retention_until`, likewise moves the sample `SEALED → EXPIRED` or `CONSUMED → EXPIRED`. Both `BROKEN` and `EXPIRED` are terminal, and a sample in either state that is offered for a grading determination, an analysis or an appeal assignment is refused with `ERR_SAMPLE_INTEGRITY_BROKEN`. Samples may be listed by `inspection_request_id`, `sampling_device_id`, `inspector_id`, `sample_type` and `integrity_status`.

### 3.5. Determinations on the Sample: Grading and Specialty Analysis

A **grading determination** is filed against the `inspection_request_id`, on a specific `sample_id`, by a specific `inspector_id`, and reports five observed factors: `test_weight_lbs_per_bushel` between 20.0 and 70.0 pounds per bushel, `moisture_pct` between 0.0 and 40.0, `damaged_total_pct` between 0.0 and 100.0, `heat_damaged_pct` between 0.0 and 100.0, and `foreign_material_pct` between 0.0 and 100.0. Since heat damage is a subset of total damage, `heat_damaged_pct` may not exceed `damaged_total_pct`, and a set that breaks this is refused with `ERR_FACTOR_SET_INCONSISTENT`. An optional `appeal_id` marks the determination as an appeal or reinspection regrade rather than the original work; when it is absent the determination is the original one, requires the request to be in `SAMPLED`, and is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_OPERATIVE_RECORD` if a `FINAL` determination with no appeal linkage already exists for the request. The sample must belong to the same request (`ERR_SAMPLE_REQUEST_MISMATCH`), must be neither `BROKEN` nor `EXPIRED`, and must have at least one portion available; the inspector must hold the `GRADING` endorsement and a `PROBATIONARY` or `ACTIVE` licence. On success one portion is consumed and the original determination advances the request `SAMPLED → GRADED`.

Grade assignment reads only rounded numbers. Each of the five observed factors is first rounded to one decimal place, half up, and the rounded set is stored on the determination as `rounded_factors` under the keys `test_weight_lbs_per_bushel`, `moisture_pct`, `damaged_total_pct`, `heat_damaged_pct` and `foreign_material_pct`. The official table for the request's `commodity` is then walked from the best grade downwards, and `assigned_grade` becomes the first grade all of whose limits the rounded set satisfies: the rounded test weight must be at least the grade's minimum test weight, and the rounded moisture, total damage, heat damage and foreign material must each be at most the grade's respective maximum. When no grade in the table is satisfied, `assigned_grade` is `SAMPLE_GRADE`.

| Commodity  | Grade     | Min test weight (lb/bu) | Max moisture (%) | Max damaged total (%) | Max heat damaged (%) | Max foreign material (%) |
| ---------- | --------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ |
| `CORN`     | `US_NO_1` | 56.0                    | 14.0             | 3.0                   | 0.1                  | 2.0                      |
| `CORN`     | `US_NO_2` | 54.0                    | 15.0             | 5.0                   | 0.2                  | 3.0                      |
| `CORN`     | `US_NO_3` | 52.0                    | 17.5             | 7.0                   | 0.5                  | 4.0                      |
| `CORN`     | `US_NO_4` | 49.0                    | 20.0             | 10.0                  | 1.0                  | 5.0                      |
| `CORN`     | `US_NO_5` | 46.0                    | 23.0             | 15.0                  | 3.0                  | 7.0                      |
| `SOYBEANS` | `US_NO_1` | 56.0                    | 13.0             | 2.0                   | 0.2                  | 1.0                      |
| `SOYBEANS` | `US_NO_2` | 54.0                    | 14.0             | 3.0                   | 0.5                  | 2.0                      |
| `SOYBEANS` | `US_NO_3` | 52.0                    | 16.0             | 5.0                   | 1.0                  | 3.0                      |
| `SOYBEANS` | `US_NO_4` | 49.0                    | 18.0             | 8.0                   | 3.0                  | 5.0                      |

Soybeans have no fifth numerical grade: a soybean lot that fails `US_NO_4` is `SAMPLE_GRADE`. The `limiting_factor` explains what stood in the way. When the assigned grade is the best grade of the commodity's table, `limiting_factor` is `NONE`. Otherwise the system looks at the grade immediately better than the assigned one — and for `SAMPLE_GRADE`, at the worst numerical grade of that commodity's table — and reports the first factor, in the fixed order `TEST_WEIGHT`, `MOISTURE`, `DAMAGED_TOTAL`, `HEAT_DAMAGED`, `FOREIGN_MATERIAL`, whose rounded value violates that better grade's limit. Because rounding happens first, a corn lot reading 14.04 moisture is compared at 14.0 and can still make `US_NO_1`, while one reading 14.05 is compared at 14.1 and lands at `US_NO_2` with `MOISTURE` as the limiting factor.

Each determination carries a `status` of `FINAL` — the operative determination for the request — or `SUPERSEDED` once displaced, and `supersedes_grading_result_id` naming the `FINAL` determination it displaced, which is null for the first determination on a request. Determinations may be listed by `inspection_request_id`, `sample_id`, `inspector_id`, `appeal_id`, `supersedes_grading_result_id` and `status`.

**Protein and specialty analysis** is ordered separately and consumes its own portion. The analysis names the `inspection_request_id`, the `sample_id`, the `instrument_id` and the `inspector_id`, and reports `protein_pct_as_is` between 0.0 and 60.0 on the instrument's as-is moisture basis together with the `instrument_moisture_pct` between 0.0 and 40.0 that the instrument read at the same time. The instrument must be of kind `PROTEIN_INSTRUMENT`, must belong to the request's area, and — uniquely among equipment uses — may be either `APPROVED` or `DRIFT_WATCH` provided its `certified_until` is still in the future; anything else is `ERR_EQUIPMENT_NOT_APPROVED`. The inspector needs the `PROTEIN` endorsement and a `PROBATIONARY` or `ACTIVE` licence, the sample must belong to the request, must not be `BROKEN` or `EXPIRED`, and must have a portion available. Every accepted analysis receives a `sequence_no`, a counter that is monotonically increasing per instrument and starts at 1, used to break ties when two analyses share a `created_at` during an ordered cascade.

Reported protein is always restated onto the official 13.0 percent moisture basis. Which moisture the conversion uses depends on what the office already knows: when no `FINAL` grading determination exists for the request at the moment the analysis is created, `basis_source` is `INSTRUMENT_MOISTURE` and `moisture_source_pct` is the `instrument_moisture_pct`; when a `FINAL` determination does exist, `basis_source` is `OFFICIAL_MOISTURE` and `moisture_source_pct` is that determination's rounded `moisture_pct` from `rounded_factors`. In both cases `protein_reported_pct` is the as-is protein multiplied by the difference between 100 and 13.0, divided by the difference between 100 and `moisture_source_pct`, rounded half up to 2 decimals, and `basis_adjustment_pct` is `protein_reported_pct` less `protein_pct_as_is`, likewise rounded half up to 2 decimals. A sample whose governing moisture is exactly 13.0 therefore reports its as-is protein unchanged with an adjustment of 0.00.

An analysis created before grading finished can be restated afterwards. Restatement is permitted only when a `FINAL` grading determination now exists on the request, the analysis's `status` is still `ACCEPTED`, and its `basis_source` is still `INSTRUMENT_MOISTURE`; any other combination is refused with `ERR_ANALYSIS_RECOMPUTE_NOT_APPLICABLE`. Restatement sets `basis_source` to `OFFICIAL_MOISTURE`, adopts the official rounded moisture as `moisture_source_pct`, recomputes `protein_reported_pct` and `basis_adjustment_pct` by the same arithmetic, and sets `status` to `RECOMPUTED`. The third `status` value, `REJECTED_CALIBRATION`, is never requested by a client: it is written only by the out-of-tolerance cascade of §3.3. Analyses may be listed by `inspection_request_id`, `sample_id`, `instrument_id`, `inspector_id` and `status`.

### 3.6. Official Weighing and Countersignature

Official weighing is recorded draft by draft. The record names the `inspection_request_id`, the certified `scale_id` and the `weigher_inspector_id`, and carries `drafts` — an ordered list of between 1 and 20 objects, each with an integer `gross_lbs` and an integer `tare_lbs` where `gross_lbs` is strictly greater than `tare_lbs` and `tare_lbs` is at least 0. An optional `dockage_pct` between 0.0 and 15.0 defaults to 0. The scale must be of kind `SCALE`, `APPROVED`, with `certified_until` in the future, and belong to the request's area; the weigher must hold the `WEIGHING` endorsement and a `PROBATIONARY` or `ACTIVE` licence. The sum of `gross_lbs` across all drafts may not exceed the scale's `capacity_lbs`, and a draft whose gross does not strictly exceed its tare is equally invalid — both fail with `ERR_SCALE_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. A request that already has a weighing record in `WEIGHED` or `SUPERVISED` status cannot have another (`ERR_DUPLICATE_OPERATIVE_RECORD`), and the request's weighing dimension must be `PENDING`, which in turn means `WEIGHING` must have been among the `requested_services`.

Two net weights are computed and **both** are stored, because the office advises the applicant on one basis and certifies on another. The official variant works draft by draft: for each draft, subtract its `tare_lbs` from its `gross_lbs` to get that draft's gross-net, multiply that gross-net by `dockage_pct` and divide by 100, and round the product half up to a whole pound to obtain the draft's dockage deduction; the draft's net is its gross-net less its deduction. `dockage_lbs` is the sum of the per-draft deductions and `net_lbs` is the sum of the per-draft nets — these are the numbers printed on the certificate. The provisional variant works on the aggregate: sum every draft's gross-net, multiply that single total by `dockage_pct`, divide by 100, round half up to a whole pound, and subtract to get `provisional_net_lbs`, which is what the applicant's advance advice quotes. `rounding_variance_lbs` is `provisional_net_lbs` less `net_lbs` and exposes the divergence between the two roundings. Finally, `net_bushels` is `net_lbs` divided by the commodity's pounds per bushel — 56 for `CORN` and 60 for `SOYBEANS` — rounded half up to 2 decimals. Six corn drafts with gross-nets of 33700, 33900, 34100, 34300, 34500 and 34700 pounds at a `dockage_pct` of 1.5 give per-draft deductions of 506, 509, 512, 515, 518 and 521 for a `dockage_lbs` of 3081 and a `net_lbs` of 202119, while the aggregate route deducts 3078 from 205200 for a `provisional_net_lbs` of 202122; `rounding_variance_lbs` is 3 and `net_bushels` is 3609.27. Creating the record advances the request `PENDING → WEIGHED` and sets `certified_quantity_bushels` to `net_bushels`.

Countersignature is a `SupervisingInspector`'s act and is where segregation of duties bites. The record must be in `WEIGHED`, the supplied `supervisor_inspector_id` must differ from the record's `weigher_inspector_id` (`ERR_SELF_SUPERVISION_FORBIDDEN`), and the supervisor must hold the `SUPERVISION` endorsement with an `ACTIVE` licence. On success the record moves `WEIGHED → SUPERVISED`, `supervisor_inspector_id` is recorded where it had been null, and the request's weighing dimension advances `WEIGHED → SUPERVISED`. The record's third `status` value, `VOIDED`, is written only by the failed-scale cascade of §3.3. Weighing records may be listed by `inspection_request_id`, `scale_id`, `weigher_inspector_id`, `supervisor_inspector_id` and `status`.

### 3.7. Export Cargo: Vessel Loading Supervision

Export cargo is supervised through a **vessel watch**, scheduled by a `ServiceAdmin` or `SupervisingInspector` against an `inspection_request_id` whose `requested_services` includes `EXPORT_SUPERVISION`. The watch names the `supervisor_inspector_id` who will stand it and the `vessel_name` of the ship or barge, brackets the supervision period with `window_start_at` and `window_end_at`, and declares in `holds_declared` how many vessel holds — at least one — are to be supervised. The area is not supplied by the client: `service_area_id` is copied from the inspection request at creation and is the resource against which area capacity is measured. A new watch is `SCHEDULED` with `stowage_certified` false, a null `started_at`, a null `closed_at` and a null `supervision_minutes`, and the owning area's `open_watch_count` increases by one.

Two scheduling constraints are checked at creation. First, `window_end_at` must be strictly after `window_start_at`, or the watch is refused with `ERR_WATCH_WINDOW_INVALID`. Second, a supervising inspector cannot be in two places at once, and needs travel time between them: each of the inspector's non-terminal watch windows is notionally extended by a three-quarter-hour travel buffer at both ends, and the new window conflicts with an existing one when the new window's start falls before the existing window's end plus 45 minutes **and** the existing window's start falls before the new window's end plus 45 minutes. A conflict is refused with `ERR_INSPECTOR_WATCH_OVERLAP`. Third, the area itself has a ceiling: count the area's non-terminal watches whose windows intersect the new window, treating windows that merely touch at an endpoint as intersecting, and that count must be strictly below the area's `concurrent_watch_capacity`, otherwise `ERR_AREA_WATCH_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`.

Standing the watch moves it `SCHEDULED → IN_PROGRESS`, stamping `started_at` from the logical clock. It may only be done inside the window — the clock must be at or after `window_start_at` and strictly before `window_end_at`, otherwise `ERR_WATCH_WINDOW_INVALID` — and only by a supervising inspector whose licence is `ACTIVE` and who holds the `SUPERVISION` endorsement. The watch's own state is the first of these gates: a watch that is not `SCHEDULED` — one already `IN_PROGRESS`, or terminal in `CLOSED` or `ABORTED` — is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` before the holder's licence, their endorsement or the window is consulted.

Closing the watch moves it `IN_PROGRESS → CLOSED` and is the act that certifies stowage. The closer reports `holds_supervised`, which must equal `holds_declared` exactly, or the closure is refused with `ERR_HOLDS_NOT_FULLY_SUPERVISED`, and reports `stowage_certified`, whose value is stored on the watch and which must be true before an export cargo certificate can be issued. On closure `closed_at` is stamped, `supervision_minutes` is computed as the whole minutes elapsed from `started_at` to `closed_at` with any remaining seconds discarded, and the area's `open_watch_count` decreases by one.

Abortion is the escape hatch and releases capacity. A watch may be aborted with an optional `reason` from either `SCHEDULED` or `IN_PROGRESS`; in both cases the area's `open_watch_count` decreases by one and the window is released for capacity purposes, so a later watch may occupy it. A `SCHEDULED` watch is also aborted without anyone asking when its supervising inspector is suspended, expires or is revoked, as described in §3.2. `CLOSED` and `ABORTED` are both terminal. Watches may be listed by `inspection_request_id`, `service_area_id`, `supervisor_inspector_id` and `status`.

### 3.8. Certificate Issuance, Fee Assessment and Settlement

Issuing an official **certificate** is the moment the office puts its name to a set of numbers and the moment the applicant is billed. The issuer supplies only three things: the `inspection_request_id`, their own `issuing_inspector_id`, and the `certificate_type` — `GRADE`, `WEIGHT`, `COMBINED`, `EXPORT_CARGO` or `APPEAL_GRADE`. Everything else is derived.

The gates depend on what the certificate asserts, and they are all evaluated before any money is computed. A `GRADE`, `COMBINED`, `EXPORT_CARGO` or `APPEAL_GRADE` certificate requires a `FINAL` grading determination on the request, else `ERR_GRADING_RESULT_MISSING`. A `WEIGHT`, `COMBINED` or `EXPORT_CARGO` certificate requires an operative weighing record, and how operative is defined depends on the area: where `supervision_required` is true the record must be `SUPERVISED`, and where it is false a record in either `WEIGHED` or `SUPERVISED` will do; falling short — including on a request that never ordered `WEIGHING` at all and so has no record that could be operative — is `ERR_WEIGHING_SUPERVISION_REQUIRED`. An `EXPORT_CARGO` certificate additionally requires `EXPORT_SUPERVISION` among the `requested_services`, a `DESIGNATED` area, and a `CLOSED` vessel watch on the request whose `stowage_certified` is true, else `ERR_VESSEL_WATCH_NOT_CLOSED`. The issuing inspector must belong to the request's area and must hold every endorsement the content requires. A certificate whose predecessor of the same type is currently `UNDER_APPEAL` cannot be issued (`ERR_CERTIFICATE_UNDER_APPEAL`).

Issuance stamps `issued_at` from the logical clock, assigns a `sequence_no` that is monotonically increasing per applicant starting at 1, and freezes the state of the request into snapshots: `grade_snapshot` and `limiting_factor_snapshot` copy the `assigned_grade` and `limiting_factor` of the request's `FINAL` determination and are null on a `WEIGHT` certificate; `net_lbs_snapshot` and `net_bushels_snapshot` copy the operative weighing record's `net_lbs` and `net_bushels` and are null when no weighing is certified; `protein_reported_pct_snapshot` copies the operative analysis's `protein_reported_pct` and is null when protein was not ordered; and `certified_quantity_bushels` copies the request's own `certified_quantity_bushels`, which is what the fee is assessed on. Two rights deadlines are computed and stored: `reinspection_deadline_at` is exactly 5 days after `issued_at` and `appeal_deadline_at` exactly 10 days after `issued_at`. Where the request has been revised on appeal — its `grade_revision_count` is greater than zero — issuing a newer certificate of the same `certificate_type` moves the prior `ISSUED` one `ISSUED → SUPERSEDED` with its `fee_settlement_status` set to `REVERSED` and its fee credited back, and the new row records `superseded_certificate_id` pointing at it. Certificates may be listed by `inspection_request_id`, `issuing_inspector_id`, `superseded_certificate_id`, `appeal_id`, `certificate_type`, `status` and `fee_settlement_status`.

Issuance is also what advances the request's two status dimensions, and it advances only the dimensions the certificate actually asserts. A `GRADE`, `COMBINED` or `EXPORT_CARGO` certificate moves the grading dimension `GRADED → GRADE_CERTIFIED`. A `WEIGHT`, `COMBINED` or `EXPORT_CARGO` certificate moves the weighing dimension `SUPERVISED → WEIGHT_CERTIFIED`; where the area's `supervision_required` is false, a `WEIGHT` or `COMBINED` certificate may instead move it `WEIGHED → WEIGHT_CERTIFIED` directly, and an `EXPORT_CARGO` certificate always requires the countersigned path. An `APPEAL_GRADE` certificate moves neither dimension, since the grading dimension is returned to `GRADE_CERTIFIED` by the appeal decision itself.

Fee assessment proceeds in a fixed sequence. First the applicable classes are determined from the certificate type: a `GRADE` certificate prices `GRADING`; a `WEIGHT` certificate prices `WEIGHING`; a `COMBINED` certificate prices `GRADING` and `WEIGHING`; an `EXPORT_CARGO` certificate prices `GRADING`, `WEIGHING` and `EXPORT_SUPERVISION`; and an `APPEAL_GRADE` certificate prices nothing whatsoever — its `fee_total_cents` is 0, no schedule is consulted, and none of the arithmetic below runs. To that set, `PROTEIN` is added whenever `PROTEIN` appears in the request's `requested_services` and the certificate type is anything other than `WEIGHT`. For each applicable class the system takes the `ACTIVE` fee schedule of the request's area whose window covers the logical clock — `effective_from` at or before now and either a null `effective_to` or an `effective_to` strictly after now — ignoring `DRAFT` and `SUPERSEDED` schedules and any schedule outside its window. If a class has no such schedule, issuance fails with `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_FEE_SCHEDULE`.

The arithmetic then runs as follows. Divide `certified_quantity_bushels` by 1000 and round the result **up** to the next whole number to obtain the billed thousands. The service charge is the sum of `base_fee_cents` over the applicable schedules plus the billed thousands multiplied by the sum of `per_thousand_bushel_rate_cents` over the same schedules. `overtime_applied` is true when `issued_at` falls outside standard hours, which run from 07:00:00Z inclusive to 17:00:00Z exclusive; when it is true, the overtime surcharge is the service charge multiplied by the **largest** `overtime_multiplier_bp` among the applicable schedules and divided by 10000, rounded half up to whole cents, and when it is false the surcharge is 0. The pre-discount subtotal is the service charge plus the surcharge. A volume discount tier is chosen from that subtotal: below 50000 cents the discount rate is 0 basis points; from 50000 cents up to but not including 150000 cents it is 500 basis points; at 150000 cents and above it is 1000 basis points. The discount is the subtotal multiplied by the tier rate divided by 10000, truncated downward to whole cents, and is subtracted from the subtotal. The area's authority adjustment is then the discounted amount multiplied by the area's `fee_multiplier_bp` less 10000, divided by 10000, rounded half up to whole cents, and added. Finally `fee_total_cents` is the greater of that adjusted amount and the **largest** `minimum_fee_cents` among the applicable schedules. Because the tier is chosen from the subtotal rather than applied progressively, a larger lot can attract a smaller total than a slightly smaller one that just misses a tier boundary.

The assessed fee is charged before the certificate is returned: it is added to the applicant's `charges_total_cents`, `open_balance_cents` is recomputed, and the certificate begins with `fee_paid_cents` 0 and `fee_settlement_status` `UNPAID`. The credit check is the last gate of all, because it needs the finished number: the applicant's `open_balance_cents` plus the newly computed `fee_total_cents` may not exceed their `credit_limit_cents`, otherwise the whole issuance is refused with `ERR_CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` and nothing is written. Issuance also updates the issuing inspector's `late_certificate_count` where the certificate was issued more than 48 hours after the request was created, and recomputes their `performance_score`, which may auto-suspend them.

Settlement runs as a waterfall. A payment of `amount_cents` — which must be positive — is allocated across the applicant's certificates whose `fee_settlement_status` is `UNPAID` and whose `status` is `ISSUED` or `UNDER_APPEAL`; `REVERSED` settlements and `VOIDED` or `SUPERSEDED` certificates are excluded from the pool entirely. The allocatable total is the sum, over that pool, of `fee_total_cents` less `fee_paid_cents`. A payment larger than the allocatable total is refused outright with `ERR_PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_ALLOCATABLE` and nothing is mutated — a payment is never partially applied. Otherwise the pool is walked oldest first, ordered by `issued_at` ascending with `sequence_no` ascending breaking ties at the same instant, and each certificate is filled to its full fee before the next is touched: the amount taken is the smaller of what remains of the payment and what remains outstanding on that certificate, it is added to that certificate's `fee_paid_cents`, and the certificate's `fee_settlement_status` becomes `PAID` the moment `fee_paid_cents` equals `fee_total_cents`. The walk stops when the payment is exhausted, and the whole `amount_cents` is added to the applicant's `credits_total_cents`.

### 3.9. Appeal, Reinspection and Board Review Rights

An applicant who disputes a certificate has three escalating remedies, each with its own window, panel size and round. A `REINSPECTION` is round 1, handled in the same office by a single reviewer, and must be filed no later than the certificate's `reinspection_deadline_at`, five days after issuance. An `APPEAL` is round 2, decided by a three-member panel, and must be filed no later than `appeal_deadline_at`, ten days after issuance. A `BOARD_APPEAL` is round 3, decided by a five-member panel drawn from other service areas, and may only be filed when a prior `APPEAL` on the same certificate was decided `DECIDED_UPHELD` — otherwise `ERR_APPEAL_ESCALATION_INVALID` — and no later than 20 days after that appeal's `decided_at`. Filing outside the applicable window is `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_EXPIRED`. The `round_number` and `required_panel_size` are derived from `appeal_type` and are never supplied by the client: 1 and 1 for a `REINSPECTION`, 2 and 3 for an `APPEAL`, 3 and 5 for a `BOARD_APPEAL`.

Filing names the `certificate_id` under challenge, the `applicant_id` filing it, the `appeal_type`, and the `expected_certificate_version` echo of §2.7. The certificate must be `ISSUED` and its `certificate_type` must not be `APPEAL_GRADE`; a certificate already `UNDER_APPEAL` is refused with `ERR_CERTIFICATE_UNDER_APPEAL`, and a certificate that already carries a non-terminal appeal is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_OPERATIVE_RECORD`. The deposit charged is the `base_fee_cents` of the area's `ACTIVE` `APPEAL` fee schedule covering the logical clock; where no such schedule exists the filing fails with `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_FEE_SCHEDULE`. On success the appeal is created `FILED` with `deposit_cents` set, `refund_cents` 0, all four vote counters (`votes_cast_count`, `revise_votes_count`, `uphold_votes_count`, `abstain_votes_count`) and `effective_votes_count` at 0, a null `assigned_sample_id`, a null `outcome_grade` and a null `decided_at`; the deposit is added to the applicant's `charges_total_cents` with `open_balance_cents` recomputed; the certificate moves `ISSUED → UNDER_APPEAL`; and the inspection request's grading dimension moves `GRADE_CERTIFIED → UNDER_APPEAL`. Appeals may be listed globally or from the challenged certificate, filtered by `certificate_id`, `applicant_id`, `assigned_sample_id`, `appeal_type` and `status`.

The regrade needs grain to work on. Assigning a file sample moves the appeal `FILED → SAMPLE_ASSIGNED`: the offered `sample_id` must belong to the challenged certificate's inspection request (`ERR_SAMPLE_REQUEST_MISMATCH`), must be neither `BROKEN` nor `EXPIRED`, and must have at least one portion available. The assignment retains a portion — `portions_retained` increases by one — and records `assigned_sample_id`. Attempting assignment from any state other than `FILED` is `ERR_APPEAL_STATE_INVALID`.

The regrade itself is a grading determination carrying the `appeal_id`, and it moves the appeal `SAMPLE_ASSIGNED → UNDER_REVIEW`. The named appeal must be in `SAMPLE_ASSIGNED`, its certificate must belong to the same inspection request as the determination, and the determination's `sample_id` must equal the appeal's `assigned_sample_id`; any of these broken yields `ERR_APPEAL_REGRADE_BINDING_INVALID`. The regrading inspector must hold the `GRADING` endorsement with an `ACTIVE` licence and must not be the inspector who produced the challenged `FINAL` determination (`ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`). A regrade does not displace anything while review is pending: it is held aside and becomes the operative `FINAL` determination only if and when the appeal is decided in the applicant's favour.

Panel members vote one at a time. A vote names the `appeal_id`, the voting `inspector_id`, a `vote` of `UPHOLD`, `REVISE` or `ABSTAIN`, an optional written `rationale`, and the `expected_appeal_version` echo. A member may cast at most one vote per appeal (`ERR_DUPLICATE_PANEL_VOTE`); the inspector who produced the challenged determination may not vote at all (`ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`); the voter must hold an `ACTIVE` licence; and on a `BOARD_APPEAL` every panel member must belong to a service area other than the challenged certificate's area (`ERR_BOARD_PANEL_COMPOSITION_INVALID`). Each accepted vote increments `votes_cast_count` and the counter for its own value, recomputes `effective_votes_count` as `revise_votes_count` plus `uphold_votes_count` — abstentions count toward neither side and are excluded from the denominator — and advances the appeal's `version`. Votes are immutable once cast and may be listed by `appeal_id` and `inspector_id`.

Deciding tallies the panel. The appeal must be `UNDER_REVIEW`, otherwise `ERR_APPEAL_STATE_INVALID`, and `effective_votes_count` must be at least `required_panel_size`, otherwise `ERR_APPEAL_QUORUM_NOT_MET` — which is why a panel of three that has produced two revise votes and one abstention cannot yet decide. The outcome is `DECIDED_REVISED` when twice the `revise_votes_count` strictly exceeds `effective_votes_count` **and** the regrade's `assigned_grade` differs from the challenged certificate's `grade_snapshot`; in every other case the outcome is `DECIDED_UPHELD`. Either way `decided_at` is stamped, `outcome_grade` records the regrade's `assigned_grade`, and the inspection request's grading dimension returns `UNDER_APPEAL → GRADE_CERTIFIED`.

The two outcomes differ sharply in their consequences, and both are spelled out here in full. On `DECIDED_REVISED`: `refund_cents` is set to the full `deposit_cents` and credited to the applicant's `credits_total_cents`; the regrade becomes the `FINAL` determination while the previous `FINAL` becomes `SUPERSEDED` and is named by the new determination's `supersedes_grading_result_id`; the challenged certificate moves `UNDER_APPEAL → VOIDED` with `fee_settlement_status` `REVERSED` and its whole `fee_total_cents` credited; a fee-free `APPEAL_GRADE` certificate is issued in its place, carrying `superseded_certificate_id` pointing at the voided row, `appeal_id` naming the appeal, and a `fee_total_cents` of 0; the request's `grade_revision_count` increases by one; the inspector who produced the overturned determination has their `appeal_reversal_count` increased by one and their `performance_score` recomputed, which may auto-suspend them; and the retained sample portion is released as `portions_retained` decreases by one. On `DECIDED_UPHELD`: `refund_cents` stays 0, the challenged certificate returns `UNDER_APPEAL → ISSUED`, and the retained sample portion is likewise released as `portions_retained` decreases by one. Returning to `ISSUED` does not extend anything: the original `reinspection_deadline_at` and `appeal_deadline_at` stand exactly as they were.

Dismissal is the administrator's early exit and is available from either `FILED` or `SAMPLE_ASSIGNED`, with an optional `reason`. In both cases `refund_cents` is half of `deposit_cents` truncated downward, credited to the applicant, and the challenged certificate returns to `ISSUED`; where the appeal had already reached `SAMPLE_ASSIGNED`, the retained portion is additionally released with `portions_retained` decreasing by one. `DECIDED_UPHELD`, `DECIDED_REVISED` and `DISMISSED` are all terminal. Because a full-deposit refund on revision, a half-deposit refund on dismissal and a fee reversal on voiding are all credit events and never edits, the conservation rule of §2.8 holds throughout: an applicant charged 44850 for a combined certificate and 25000 for a deposit, whose appeal is then decided `DECIDED_REVISED`, ends with 69850 charged, 69850 credited and an `open_balance_cents` of 0, the free `APPEAL_GRADE` certificate adding nothing to either side.
