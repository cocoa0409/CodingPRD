# Money Transmitter Licensing API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A money services business that moves customer funds across state lines does not hold one licence; it holds dozens, each granted by a separate state regulator with its own net-worth floor, its own surety bond formula, its own reporting calendar and its own view of who is allowed to control the company. Today the licensing team tracks that estate in spreadsheets, and the failure modes are predictable and expensive: a bond that quietly falls below the statutory requirement after an agent is onboarded, a call report filed a day late that silently disqualifies the licence from renewal, a coordinated multistate examination whose information requests go unanswered until the lead state escalates, or a change of control that is approved in four states and forgotten in the fifth.

This document specifies the Money Transmitter Licensing API — the system of record for that estate. It governs the full arc of a licence: the application filed with a single state, the deficiency correspondence that follows, the bond that activates the licence, the authorized delegates that transmit under it, the permissible investments that must at all times cover outstanding transmission obligations, the periodic tests that prove that coverage, the coordinated examinations that verify it, and finally the enforcement, remediation and surrender paths that wind a licence down. Every deadline in the system is measured against a server-held logical clock rather than wall-clock time, so a compliance calendar can be exercised deterministically.

Two design commitments shape everything that follows. First, **segregation of duties is structural, not advisory**: the officer who prepares a coverage test may never be the officer who certifies it, and a state regulator may only act on the matters of its own jurisdiction. Second, **coverage posture is the master gate**: when permissible investments stop covering outstanding obligations, the consequences propagate outward automatically — growth is frozen at every licence, pending delegate authorizations are parked, and the affected agents are suspended — without any operator needing to remember to do it.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Identity, Roles and Regulatory Scope

Every request must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, declaring the capacity in which it acts. A request missing either header, or presenting a role string that is not one of the six declared roles, is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). A well-identified caller invoking an operation that its role is not authorized for is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403). The six roles are `PlatformAdmin`, `LicensingManager`, `ComplianceOfficer`, `ChiefComplianceOfficer`, `StateRegulator` and `DelegateOperator`.

`PlatformAdmin` is the administrative fixture actor: it configures jurisdiction reference data and drives the logical clock, and nothing else. `LicensingManager` is the licensee-side owner of the estate — it registers the business, files applications, posts bonds, adjusts obligations, files call reports and renewals, appoints delegates and answers enforcement actions. `ComplianceOfficer` records investment holdings, opens and prepares permissible-investment tests, and conducts delegate oversight reviews. `ChiefComplianceOfficer` holds the certification right and shares the holdings and test-creation rights with the `ComplianceOfficer`. `StateRegulator` is the adjudicating side: it reviews applications, adjudicates control-person background checks and change-of-control filings, decides surrenders, schedules and drives coordinated examinations, issues information requests and dispositions, and issues and resolves enforcement actions. `DelegateOperator` is an agent's own operator and may self-report oversight metrics for its own delegate row only.

Four of the six roles carry an opaque identity. When `X-User-Role` is `PlatformAdmin`, `LicensingManager`, `ComplianceOfficer` or `ChiefComplianceOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server may record it — for example as `prepared_by_user_id` when a `ComplianceOfficer` prepares a coverage test, or as `certified_by_user_id` when a `ChiefComplianceOfficer` certifies one — but it never compares it to any entity's `id`.

Two roles are entity-backed. When `X-User-Role` is `StateRegulator`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Jurisdiction`; ownership checks compare the resource's jurisdiction-of-record field to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, the acting jurisdiction must equal the application's `jurisdiction_id`, the licence's `jurisdiction_id`, the exam's `lead_jurisdiction_id`, the information request's `jurisdiction_id`, or the enforcement action's `jurisdiction_id`, depending on the target. For control-person adjudications and change-of-control decisions the test is broader: `X-User-ID` must be a jurisdiction in which the licensee either holds a `StateLicense` whose `license_status` is `ACTIVE` or has a `LicenseApplication` in a non-terminal status. When `X-User-Role` is `DelegateOperator`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Delegate`; an oversight review may only be submitted for the delegate whose `id` equals `X-User-ID`, and the delegate's own read access is likewise limited to its own rows. Any scope violation on a `StateRegulator` or `DelegateOperator` write is rejected with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_SCOPE` (HTTP 403), and so is a `DelegateOperator` single-resource read of another delegate's row or of an authorization naming another delegate; the two collection reads a `DelegateOperator` may call behave differently, being silently narrowed to the rows whose `delegate_id` equals `X-User-ID` rather than refused. Reads by the four opaque roles and by a `StateRegulator` carry no scope check at all: a regulator may read any licensee, licence, application, exam, request, action or test regardless of jurisdiction, and only writes are confined to its own matters.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`, followed by twenty characters drawn from the uppercase letters and the digits.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits. It is the one timestamp in the system that reads real wall-clock time rather than the logical clock of §2.4, so it does not move when the clock is set and does not match the `*_at` stamps on the resources in the same response.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination and Collection Filters

List endpoints are paginated with two query parameters. `limit` is the maximum number of rows returned; it defaults to 10, has a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 100. `offset` is the number of rows to skip; it defaults to 0 and its minimum is 0. A `limit` outside the range of 1 through 100, or a negative `offset`, is a malformed request and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400). Unless a specific endpoint says otherwise, every list is sorted by `created_at` ascending, with ties broken by the order in which the server created the rows — a common case rather than an edge case, since the logical clock stands still unless an administrator moves it, so a whole fixture of rows usually shares one `created_at`. List responses carry `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` in the `meta` object of the envelope, where `total_count` is the size of the filtered set before the window is applied and `has_more` is true exactly when `offset` plus the number of rows actually returned is still less than `total_count`.

Collections also accept domain filters, all of them optional and all of them combinable. Every filter over an enumerated field is validated against that field's declared value set and a value outside it is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`; every filter over an id field is matched literally and is never existence-checked, so an unknown or malformed id is not an error and simply yields an empty page. The control-person roster filters on `licensee_id`, `parent_control_person_id`, `status` and `control_determination`. The application register filters on `licensee_id`, `jurisdiction_id` and `status`. The licence register filters on `licensee_id`, `jurisdiction_id`, `application_id`, `license_status` and `reporting_status` — and the `application_id` filter is the only way to discover the licence that an approval minted, since approval returns the application rather than the new licence. The delegate roster filters on `licensee_id` and `status`; the authorization register filters on `delegate_id`, `state_license_id` and `status`. A licensee's holdings listing filters on `status` and `asset_class`. The coverage-test register filters on `licensee_id`, `status`, `method` and `result`. The examination register filters on `licensee_id`, `lead_jurisdiction_id` and `status`; the information-request register filters on `exam_id`, `jurisdiction_id` and `status`; the enforcement register filters on `state_license_id`, `jurisdiction_id`, `originating_exam_id` and `status`. A licence's own call-report listing filters on `status`.

### 2.4. Record Identity, Audit Stamps and the Logical Clock

Every resource carries an `id`, which is a server-minted UUID with no type prefix, and a `created_at`, which is an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits. Almost every resource additionally carries an `updated_at` — refreshed on every mutation, in the same format — and a `version`, an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increments by one on every mutation of that row, but at most once per request: an operation whose cascade reaches the same row along several paths still advances that row's `version` by exactly one and stamps one `updated_at`. `Licensee`, `ControlPerson`, `LicenseApplication`, `StateLicense`, `Delegate`, `DelegateAuthorization`, `InvestmentHolding`, `PermissibleInvestmentTest`, `MultistateExam`, `InformationRequest`, `CallReport` and `EnforcementAction` all carry the full set. `Jurisdiction` is the single exception: a jurisdiction configuration row is immutable once posted, so it carries only `id` and `created_at` — it has no `updated_at`, no `version`, and no update endpoint. Every downstream calculation reads the jurisdiction configuration exactly as it was posted.

Time in this system is not wall-clock time. The server holds a **logical clock** that starts at `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z` when the process starts and never advances on its own. An administrative test-driving capability moves it: the caller supplies a `to_iso8601` instant and the server responds with `current_utc`, the new clock reading, as the only field of the response `data`. The clock is set, not advanced, so it may be moved backwards as freely as forwards and the supplied instant is accepted without comparison to the reading it replaces. Every deadline, window and `*_at` stamp in the specification reads that clock — response windows, certification windows, deficiency deadlines, bond-cancellation notice periods, call-report lateness and enforcement response windows are all evaluated against it. Moving the clock is not itself an event: pushing the clock past an information request's `due_at` does not flip that request to `OVERDUE`; the flip happens on the next disposition call. Day arithmetic on the clock adds exact multiples of twenty-four hours, and all instants are millisecond-exact ISO 8601 UTC. That normalisation covers the stamps the server computes; a date or datetime supplied by the client — `net_worth_statement_date`, a bond or authorization `effective_date`, an `expiry_date`, a `bond_cancellation_effective_date`, a `remediation_deadline_at`, the coverage-period bounds and the fieldwork bounds — is echoed back exactly as it arrived, while being parsed to UTC internally for every comparison. A well-formed value may be a bare calendar date, which reads as midnight UTC, and may carry an explicit offset, which is converted to UTC.

### 2.5. Money, Basis Points and Rounding Discipline

All monetary amounts are integers denominated in cents; all rates, ratios and ownership stakes are integers denominated in basis points, where 10,000 basis points is one hundred percent. No value in this system is ever stored or returned as a decimal fraction, and there is therefore no notion of "two decimal places" — instead, the rounding behaviour of each division is pinned individually and is part of the contract.

Three rounding behaviours appear. **Floor** (round down toward zero) governs every division inside the permissible-investment coverage computation, every hop of the ownership attribution chain, the class-multiplier step of the surety bond computation, and the weighted delegate oversight score. **Round half up** governs the assessment step of the renewal fee, the per-report call-report assessment fee, and the per-line whole-dollar rounding of the annual assessment invoice. **Exact integer arithmetic with no rounding at all** governs the obligation aggregate, which is a plain sum of integer cents. Where a formula prescribes a specific evaluation order, that order is normative: applying the same operations in a different order generally produces a different integer, and the different integer is wrong.

### 2.6. Version Guards on Licensee and Licence Writes

Two write operations are guarded by optimistic concurrency control, and only those two.

Creating a permissible-investment test requires the request body to carry `expected_licensee_version`, a non-optional field holding the client's last observed `version` of the parent `Licensee`. If that value does not equal the licensee's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_LICENSEE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). The guard exists because a new test row supersedes any `DRAFT` or `PREPARED` test already open for that licensee: a writer holding a stale licensee view would silently discard work it never observed. A successful create bumps `Licensee.version`, so replaying the same `expected_licensee_version` a second time is rejected. Many other operations bump `Licensee.version` too — a financials update, a holding creation or disposal, an obligations adjustment, a test certification — and each of them therefore invalidates a client's cached version.

Filing a surrender request requires the request body to carry `expected_state_license_version`, a non-optional field holding the client's last observed `version` of the parent `StateLicense`. If it does not equal the licence's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_LICENSE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Obligation adjustments, bond events, delegate activations, renewals and surrender decisions all bump the licence version, and every one of them changes whether surrender is admissible at all, so a client working from a stale view must not be allowed to move a licence into `SURRENDER_REQUESTED`. A successful surrender request bumps `StateLicense.version`, so a replayed request with the same expected version is rejected.

No other endpoint accepts a version field, and no other endpoint performs a version check.

### 2.7. Order of Evaluation

When several rules could reject the same request, the system evaluates them in a fixed order and returns the error of the first rule that fails. Text order in this document is not the order; the order is the following ladder.

1. **Identity** — headers present and the role declared: `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. **Role authorization** — the acting role appears in the operation's authorized list: `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`.
3. **Request shape** — required fields present, enum values inside their declared sets, money and count fields integers no less than zero, ISO 8601 datetimes well formed, `limit` within 1 through 100 and `offset` no less than zero: `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. A required field supplied as an explicit null counts as absent; a required string that is empty or only whitespace is refused; a boolean is not an acceptable integer; and a field the operation does not name is ignored rather than refused. This band also carries every cross-field shape rule and every regex-shaped format rule — `bond_cap_cents` against `base_bond_cents`, `coverage_period_end` against `coverage_period_start`, `fieldwork_end` against `fieldwork_start`, `expiry_date` against `effective_date`, the `state_code`, `ein` and `period_label` patterns — so those precede every lookup.
4. **Reference resolution**, left to right along the path and then through the body, in this exact sequence: licensee references (`ERR_LICENSEE_NOT_FOUND`), then jurisdiction references (`ERR_JURISDICTION_NOT_FOUND`), then control-person references including `parent_control_person_id` (`ERR_CONTROL_PERSON_NOT_FOUND`), then application references (`ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND`), then state-licence references whether in the path or in a body field (`ERR_LICENSE_NOT_FOUND`), then delegate references (`ERR_DELEGATE_NOT_FOUND`), then delegate-authorization references (`ERR_AUTHORIZATION_NOT_FOUND`), then investment-holding references (`ERR_HOLDING_NOT_FOUND`), then coverage-test references (`ERR_TEST_NOT_FOUND`), then examination references including `exam_id` and `originating_exam_id` (`ERR_EXAM_NOT_FOUND`), then information-request references (`ERR_INFO_REQUEST_NOT_FOUND`), then enforcement-action references (`ERR_ENFORCEMENT_NOT_FOUND`). A request naming a non-existent licensee and a non-existent jurisdiction reports the licensee first.
5. **Regulatory scope** — the entity-backed checks of §2.1: `ERR_FORBIDDEN_SCOPE`.
6. **Version guards** — `expected_licensee_version` (`ERR_STALE_LICENSEE_VERSION`), then `expected_state_license_version` (`ERR_STALE_LICENSE_VERSION`). Both sit above every state and business check, so a stale writer learns that it is stale rather than learning about a business condition it may no longer be seeing correctly.
7. **Entity state admissibility**, in this order: application status (`ERR_APPLICATION_INVALID_STATE`), licence authorisation status (`ERR_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`), licence terminality (`ERR_LICENSE_TERMINAL`), coverage-test status (`ERR_TEST_INVALID_STATE`), authorization status (`ERR_AUTHORIZATION_INVALID_STATE`), examination status (`ERR_EXAM_INVALID_STATE`), information-request status (`ERR_INFO_REQUEST_INVALID_STATE`), enforcement status (`ERR_ENFORCEMENT_INVALID_STATE`), control-person status (`ERR_CONTROL_PERSON_INVALID_STATE`).
8. **Business safety**, in this order: segregation of duties (`ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`), background-check completeness (`ERR_BACKGROUND_CHECK_INCOMPLETE`), control-chain block (`ERR_CONTROL_CHAIN_BLOCKED`), frozen coverage posture (`ERR_POSTURE_FROZEN`), expansion gate (`ERR_EXPANSION_GATE_BLOCKED`), oversight score (`ERR_OVERSIGHT_SCORE_INSUFFICIENT`), remediation plan presence (`ERR_REMEDIATION_PLAN_REQUIRED`).
9. **Referential integrity**, in this order: cross-licensee references (`ERR_CROSS_LICENSEE_REFERENCE`), enforcement jurisdiction match (`ERR_JURISDICTION_MISMATCH`), requesting jurisdiction participation (`ERR_JURISDICTION_NOT_PARTICIPATING`), multistate eligibility of participants (`ERR_JURISDICTION_NOT_MULTISTATE`), duplicate change-of-control decision (`ERR_DUPLICATE_APPROVAL`).
10. **Temporal windows**, in this order: examination window conflict (`ERR_EXAM_WINDOW_CONFLICT`), delegate period overlap (`ERR_DELEGATE_PERIOD_OVERLAP`), certification window (`ERR_CERTIFICATION_WINDOW_CLOSED`), information-request response window (`ERR_RESPONSE_WINDOW_CLOSED`), deficiency deadline (`ERR_DEFICIENCY_DEADLINE_PASSED`), enforcement response window (`ERR_ENFORCEMENT_RESPONSE_WINDOW_CLOSED`), remediation deadline (`ERR_REMEDIATION_DEADLINE_PASSED`), bond cancellation notice period (`ERR_BOND_NOTICE_TOO_SHORT`).
11. **Uniqueness and structural constraints**, in this order: duplicate live application (`ERR_DUPLICATE_LICENSE_APPLICATION`), duplicate `ein` (`ERR_DUPLICATE_EIN`), duplicate `state_code` (`ERR_DUPLICATE_STATE_CODE`), a `period_label` that repeats one already filed for the licence or that contradicts the jurisdiction's cadence (`ERR_DUPLICATE_CALL_REPORT`) — a label that does not even match the `<YYYY>Q<1-4>` or `<YYYY>FY` shape is a request-shape failure and is caught far earlier, at band 3, with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` — incomplete information-request response (`ERR_RESPONSE_INCOMPLETE`), active delegates remaining at surrender (`ERR_ACTIVE_DELEGATES_REMAIN`), control-chain cycle or excessive depth (`ERR_CONTROL_CHAIN_CYCLE`), reporting status not current at renewal (`ERR_REPORTING_NOT_CURRENT`).
12. **Fiscal sufficiency**, last, in this order: net worth (`ERR_NET_WORTH_INSUFFICIENT`), bond amount (`ERR_BOND_INSUFFICIENT`), outstanding obligations at surrender (`ERR_OUTSTANDING_OBLIGATIONS_REMAIN`), renewal fee match (`ERR_RENEWAL_FEE_MISMATCH`), non-negative obligations (`ERR_NEGATIVE_OBLIGATIONS`).

Two consequences of this ladder are worth stating outright. Because reference resolution precedes state admissibility, an operation on a non-existent licence reports the missing licence rather than an invalid state. And because state admissibility precedes fiscal sufficiency, an approval on an application that is not `UNDER_REVIEW` reports the invalid state rather than a net-worth shortfall, even when both conditions hold.

### 2.8. The Two Independent Dimensions of a State Licence

A `StateLicense` carries two status fields that move independently and must never be conflated. `license_status` is the authorisation dimension and takes the values `PENDING_BOND`, `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, `SURRENDER_REQUESTED`, `SURRENDERED` and `REVOKED`; it starts at `PENDING_BOND`, and `SURRENDERED` and `REVOKED` are terminal. `reporting_status` is the reporting-calendar dimension and takes the values `CURRENT`, `DELINQUENT` and `DEFICIENT_FILING`; it starts at `CURRENT` and has no terminal value. A suspended licence still files call reports and its `reporting_status` still moves; a licence whose `reporting_status` is `DELINQUENT` is still authorised to transmit. Only `license_status` reaching `SURRENDERED` or `REVOKED` stops filings altogether. Which error a refused licence-level operation carries then follows the ladder of §2.7 rather than a single rule. The operations whose only licence-status precondition is non-terminality — filing a call report against the licence, adjusting its outstanding obligations, and issuing a new enforcement action against it — are rejected with `ERR_LICENSE_TERMINAL`. An operation that is instead admissible only from an enumerated authorisation status fails that enumeration first, and because the authorisation-status band sits above the terminality band it is rejected with `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`: posting a bond is admissible only from `PENDING_BOND`, a bond-cancellation notice and a renewal only from `ACTIVE`, a surrender request only from `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`, a surrender decision only from `SURRENDER_REQUESTED`, and creating or activating a delegate authorization only against an `ACTIVE` licence — a `SURRENDERED` or `REVOKED` licence satisfies none of them.

A third field, `expansion_gate`, is neither dimension but a derived gate with the values `OPEN` and `BLOCKED`, defaulting to `BLOCKED`. It is `OPEN` exactly when all five of the following hold: `license_status` is `ACTIVE`, `bond_status` is `POSTED`, `reporting_status` is `CURRENT`, `exam_findings_open` is false, and the owning licensee's `coverage_posture` is either `COMPLIANT` or `WATCH`. If any one of the five fails, the gate is `BLOCKED`. The gate is what stops growth: it is re-derived on every event that touches one of its five inputs.

### 2.9. Cascade Discipline

Several operations move more than one row. When they do, the full chain is part of the specification, not an implementation detail, and each affected row's `updated_at` is refreshed and its `version` incremented — once, per §2.4, however many times the chain reaches that row. Every counter a cascade decrements is floored at zero and can never be driven negative, so a compensating decrement applied to a counter already reading zero leaves it reading zero rather than going below. A cascade level that would rewrite a field to the value it already holds writes nothing and bumps no `version`: re-deriving an `expansion_gate` that is already `BLOCKED`, or re-blocking a `control_chain_status` that is already `BLOCKED`, is not a mutation.

The **posture cascade** begins whenever a licensee's `coverage_posture` becomes `DEFICIENT` or `FROZEN`. Every `StateLicense` of that licensee whose `license_status` is `ACTIVE` has `expansion_gate` set to `BLOCKED`. Every `DelegateAuthorization` of those licences whose `status` is `PENDING_OVERSIGHT` moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_POSTURE`. Every `Delegate` that is left with no `ACTIVE` authorization has its `status` set to `SUSPENDED` and its `oversight_hold` set to true — including a delegate still in `ONBOARDING`, and including one already `SUSPENDED` whose `oversight_hold` was false. The cascade runs in that order and is four levels deep. The word *becomes* is load-bearing on the third and fourth levels: they fire only when the posture crosses into `DEFICIENT` or `FROZEN` from a posture that was neither, so a second obligations adjustment or holding movement that leaves an already-`DEFICIENT` licensee still `DEFICIENT` re-derives every gate but parks no further `PENDING_OVERSIGHT` authorization. The one exception is a test certification (§3.7), which parks unconditionally whenever the resulting posture is `DEFICIENT` or `FROZEN`, whatever the posture had been before. The first two levels — the gate re-derivation — are not conditioned on a transition and run on every recomputation.

The **licence-suspension cascade** begins whenever a licence moves from `ACTIVE` to `SUSPENDED`. The licence's `expansion_gate` becomes `BLOCKED` and the licensee's `active_license_count` is reduced by one. Every `DelegateAuthorization` of that licence whose `status` is `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED`, the licence's `active_delegate_count` is set to zero, and `bond_requirement_cents` is recomputed with a delegate count of zero. For each suspended authorization, the owning `Delegate` has `authorized_state_count` reduced by one, and a delegate whose count reaches zero moves from `ACTIVE` to `SUSPENDED` with `oversight_hold` set to true. This cascade is three levels deep.

The **revocation cascade** begins whenever a licence moves to `REVOKED`. First the licence itself: `license_status` becomes `REVOKED`, `expansion_gate` becomes `BLOCKED`, `bond_status` becomes `CANCELLED`, and the licensee's `active_license_count` is reduced by one if and only if the licence had been `ACTIVE` immediately before. Second, every `DelegateAuthorization` of that licence whose `status` is any of `PENDING_OVERSIGHT`, `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED_BY_POSTURE` or `SUSPENDED` moves to `TERMINATED`, and the licence's `active_delegate_count` is set to zero. Third, for each authorization that had been `ACTIVE`, the owning `Delegate` has `authorized_state_count` reduced by one; and then, separately and over every authorization the licence held rather than only the formerly-`ACTIVE` ones, a delegate left with no non-`TERMINATED` authorization at all has `status` set to `TERMINATED` — so a delegate whose sole authorization under this licence had merely been `PENDING_OVERSIGHT` or `SUSPENDED_BY_POSTURE` is terminated too, from whatever status it held, without any counter having moved on its behalf. Fourth, because the licence has left the obligation aggregate set, the licensee's `aggregate_outstanding_obligations_cents`, `coverage_ratio_bps` and `coverage_posture` are all recomputed. This cascade is four levels deep.

Compensating actions restore counters to the exact values that held before the action they compensate. Terminating an authorization that was `ACTIVE` reduces both `active_delegate_count` and `authorized_state_count` by one and recomputes `bond_requirement_cents`, returning all three to their pre-activation values. Terminating an authorization that was only `PENDING_OVERSIGHT` moves no counter at all, because the row never counted. Resolving a consent order returns a `SUSPENDED` licence to `ACTIVE` and clears `exam_findings_open`, but it deliberately does **not** reactivate the authorizations that the suspension suspended — those must be terminated and re-appointed, or re-activated by an explicit operator action where the state machine allows it.

### 2.10. Segregation of Duties on Coverage Certification

The preparation and the certification of a permissible-investment test are separate acts by separate people. `prepared_by_user_id` records the `X-User-ID` of whoever ran the prepare step; `certified_by_user_id` records the `X-User-ID` of whoever certified. The certifying identity must differ from the preparing identity; a certification attempt whose `X-User-ID` equals the recorded `prepared_by_user_id` is rejected with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 409). Because only a `ChiefComplianceOfficer` may certify, and both a `ComplianceOfficer` and a `ChiefComplianceOfficer` may prepare, the practical effect is that a chief compliance officer who prepared a test must hand certification to a colleague.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Reference Data and the Licensee of Record

Before anything can be licensed, a `PlatformAdmin` configures the states. Each `Jurisdiction` row is posted with a two-letter `state_code` in uppercase — exactly two characters, and unique across all jurisdictions, with a second row for the same code rejected as `ERR_DUPLICATE_STATE_CODE` — together with the regulator's display `name` and the statutory parameters that drive every downstream computation for that state: `min_net_worth_cents`, the minimum tangible net worth a licensee must report to hold a licence there; `base_bond_cents`, the statutory floor of the surety bond; `per_delegate_bond_cents`, the incremental bond amount charged for each active authorized delegate; `bond_cap_cents`, the statutory ceiling, which may never be below `base_bond_cents` — that cross-field condition, like the two-uppercase-letter shape of `state_code` itself, is part of the request shape and a violation is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, not with a fiscal or uniqueness error; `response_window_days`, at least one, the number of days a licensee gets to answer an information request from that state; `renewal_fee_cents`, the base annual renewal fee for one licence; and `assessment_rate_bps`, the annual assessment rate in basis points applied to outstanding obligations. All of the money and day figures must be zero or greater. Two further fields shape process rather than arithmetic: `participates_in_multistate`, a boolean defaulting to true that says whether the state may join a coordinated examination, and `call_report_frequency`, which is either `QUARTERLY` or `ANNUAL` and fixes the shape of the reporting calendar for every licence issued there.

A `LicensingManager` then registers the money services business itself. A `Licensee` is created with its `legal_name` and its nine-digit `ein`, which must be unique across licensees — a second registration under an existing employer identification number is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_EIN`. The financial position may be supplied at registration or later: `reported_tangible_net_worth_cents`, which defaults to zero and, unlike every other money field in the system, is explicitly permitted to be negative, and `net_worth_statement_date`, the as-of date of that figure, which is likewise optional at registration and defaults to null. The nine-digit shape of `ein` is a request-shape rule, so a figure of the wrong length or carrying non-digits is `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` and only a well-formed duplicate reaches `ERR_DUPLICATE_EIN`. A freshly created licensee starts with `version` at 1, `coverage_posture` at `COMPLIANT`, `coverage_ratio_bps` at 10000 — vacuously perfect, since there are no obligations yet — `control_chain_status` at `VERIFIED`, and every counter at zero: `aggregate_outstanding_obligations_cents`, `eligible_investment_value_cents`, `book_investment_value_cents`, `consecutive_failed_test_count` and `active_license_count`.

Refreshing the financial statement is its own operation: a `LicensingManager` supplies a new `reported_tangible_net_worth_cents` and `net_worth_statement_date` — both mandatory here, unlike at registration, the net worth again permitted to be negative — and the licensee's `version` is bumped. Nothing else moves: net worth is not an input to coverage, so no investment value, ratio or posture is re-derived and no cascade runs. That version bump matters far beyond the financial statement itself, because it invalidates any `expected_licensee_version` a client was holding for a coverage test it had not yet created.

### 3.2. The Control Chain: Ownership, Attribution and Change of Control

Who controls a money transmitter is a question with two different correct answers, and the system computes both. A `LicensingManager` records a holder as a `ControlPerson` under a `licensee_id`, giving the holder's `full_name`, a `role_title` drawn from `CEO`, `CFO`, `COO`, `CCO`, `DIRECTOR`, `ENTITY_HOLDER` or `PASSIVE_INVESTOR`, and `direct_ownership_bps`, the basis points held directly in the immediate parent link, constrained to the range 0 through 10000. Where the holder owns the licensee through an intermediate holding company rather than directly, `parent_control_person_id` names that intermediate `ControlPerson`; it is null — its default — for a direct holder. The chain must be sane: a parent pointer that names the row itself, that reaches back into the row's own descendants, or that would make the chain more than five links deep is rejected with `ERR_CONTROL_CHAIN_CYCLE` (HTTP 422). Because a parent pointer is fixed at creation and there is no operation that repoints one afterwards, the self-reference and descendant-reference forms are unreachable in practice and the depth rule is the whole of the check: the chain is counted as rows from the root down to and including the row being created, five such rows are admissible, and only the sixth link is refused. A parent holder belonging to a different licensee than the row being created is rejected with `ERR_CROSS_LICENSEE_REFERENCE` (HTTP 422).

On creation the server walks the parent chain from the new row up to the root and computes two ownership figures. The **economic** figure, `effective_ownership_bps`, multiplies the chain: for a direct holder it is simply `direct_ownership_bps`; otherwise it is the row's `direct_ownership_bps` multiplied by the parent's `effective_ownership_bps`, divided by 10000 with the result rounded down at that hop. The **attributed** figure, `attributed_ownership_bps`, applies the regulatory attribution convention instead: for a direct holder it is again `direct_ownership_bps`; if the parent's own `direct_ownership_bps` is at least 2500 basis points, the parent link is treated as controlling and the row's stake is attributed at full value, so `attributed_ownership_bps` equals the row's `direct_ownership_bps` unchanged; only when the parent's direct stake is below 2500 basis points does the row's stake get diluted, becoming the row's `direct_ownership_bps` multiplied by the parent's `attributed_ownership_bps` divided by 10000 and rounded down. The divergence is deliberate and observable: a holder owning forty percent of a holding company that itself owns sixty percent of the licensee reads 2400 basis points economically — below the control threshold — but 4000 basis points on attribution, and is therefore a control person. A `REMOVED` ancestor contributes zero to both figures, and both are recomputed for every descendant whenever an ancestor is removed.

The control test uses the attributed figure, never the economic one. `control_determination` is `CONTROL` when `attributed_ownership_bps` is at least 2500 basis points **or** the holder's `role_title` is one of the four officer titles `CEO`, `CFO`, `COO` or `CCO`; otherwise it is `NON_CONTROL`. Exactly 2500 basis points is `CONTROL`; 2499 is `NON_CONTROL`. A `DIRECTOR`, an `ENTITY_HOLDER` and a `PASSIVE_INVESTOR` are officers for no purpose here — they reach `CONTROL` only through the attributed stake. From `control_determination` two obligations follow immediately. `background_check_status` is set to `PENDING` for a control person and to `NOT_REQUIRED` otherwise; its full value set is `NOT_REQUIRED`, `PENDING`, `CLEARED` and `ADVERSE`, and it defaults to `NOT_REQUIRED`. `change_of_control_status` is set to `PENDING_APPROVAL` when the holder is a control person **and** the licensee's `active_license_count` is greater than zero, and to `NOT_REQUIRED` in every other case; its value set is `NOT_REQUIRED`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` and `DENIED`. At the same moment the server pins `required_approval_count` to the licensee's `active_license_count` as it stands at creation time — the quorum is frozen then and does not move as licences come and go. `approving_jurisdiction_ids` starts as an empty array, `disqualifying_finding` starts false, and the row's own `status` starts at `PROPOSED`, the other values being `SEATED` and `REMOVED`. A `REMOVED` row is excluded from every control-chain aggregate.

A `StateRegulator` adjudicates the background check, supplying an `outcome` and, where relevant, a `disqualifying_finding` flag that defaults to false when the field is absent. An `outcome` of `CLEARED` sets `background_check_status` to `CLEARED`. An `outcome` of `ADVERSE` accompanied by `disqualifying_finding` true sets `background_check_status` to `ADVERSE`, sets the row's `disqualifying_finding` to true, and drives the holder from `PROPOSED` straight to the terminal `REMOVED`; the licensee's `control_chain_status` is then forced to `BLOCKED`, and every descendant of the removed row — its children, their children and so on down the whole subtree, not merely the immediate children — has both `effective_ownership_bps` and `attributed_ownership_bps` recomputed to zero, has `control_determination` re-derived from that zeroed attributed stake and its own `role_title`, and is itself set to `REMOVED`. An `outcome` of `ADVERSE` *without* the flag is the milder half of the branch: `background_check_status` becomes `ADVERSE` and nothing else moves at all — the holder stays `PROPOSED`, `disqualifying_finding` stays false, no descendant is touched, and `control_chain_status` is recomputed by the ordinary precedence below rather than forced, which lands it on `BLOCKED` anyway whenever the adverse holder is a non-`REMOVED` control person. Adjudication requires the row's `background_check_status` to be `PENDING`; an adjudication aimed at a `SEATED` or `REMOVED` holder is rejected with `ERR_CONTROL_PERSON_INVALID_STATE`.

Change of control is a quorum, not a single decision. A `StateRegulator` records a `decision` of `APPROVED` or `DENIED`, optionally with a `rationale`. An `APPROVED` decision from a jurisdiction that is not yet present in `approving_jurisdiction_ids` appends that jurisdiction's `id` to the array and leaves the holder in `PROPOSED` — a self-loop that repeats once per approving state. Only when the length of `approving_jurisdiction_ids` reaches `required_approval_count` does `change_of_control_status` flip to `APPROVED`. A second decision from a jurisdiction that has already decided on this holder is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_APPROVAL` (HTTP 409). A `decision` of `DENIED` short-circuits everything: `change_of_control_status` becomes `DENIED` and the holder moves to the terminal `REMOVED` immediately, no matter how many approvals had already been collected, and every descendant of the denied holder has both ownership figures recomputed to zero and `control_determination` re-derived — but, unlike the disqualifying-finding path, the descendants are *not* themselves removed and keep whatever `status` they held. Either decision requires `change_of_control_status` to be `PENDING_APPROVAL` and the row itself to be `PROPOSED`, and the duplicate-decision test is a membership test on `approving_jurisdiction_ids`, so it is the approving states that are remembered; a denial needs no such bookkeeping because it ends the filing outright.

A holder becomes `SEATED` — the system's way of saying the appointment is effective — when its `background_check_status` is either `NOT_REQUIRED` or `CLEARED` **and** its `change_of_control_status` is either `NOT_REQUIRED` or `APPROVED`, while the row itself is still `PROPOSED`. The transition is automatic; no operator asks for it. The test is applied to the row being acted on at creation, once the two dimensions have just been derived; after a background-check adjudication whose `outcome` is `CLEARED`; and after a change-of-control `decision` of `APPROVED` — nothing else re-evaluates it, and in particular a recomputation triggered on a descendant by an ancestor's removal never seats anything. Because a holder that needs neither a background check nor a change-of-control approval already reads `NOT_REQUIRED` on both dimensions the instant it is created, such a holder is seated inside the creation request itself and the creation response already carries `status` `SEATED`; a row is only ever observed as `PROPOSED` while at least one of its two dimensions has not yet reached a seating value.

Above the individual holders sits the licensee's `control_chain_status`, an aggregate over the non-`REMOVED` holders whose `control_determination` is `CONTROL`, with values `VERIFIED`, `PENDING_CHECKS` and `BLOCKED` and a default of `VERIFIED`. It is recomputed on every control-person creation, every background-check adjudication and every change-of-control decision, by this precedence: `BLOCKED` if any such holder has `background_check_status` of `ADVERSE` or `change_of_control_status` of `DENIED`; otherwise `PENDING_CHECKS` if any such holder has `background_check_status` of `PENDING` or `change_of_control_status` of `PENDING_APPROVAL`; otherwise `VERIFIED`. A `BLOCKED` chain stops three things dead — application submission, application approval and licence renewal — each with `ERR_CONTROL_CHAIN_BLOCKED` (HTTP 409).

### 3.3. Filing an Application, the Deficiency Loop, and Issuance

A `LicensingManager` opens a `LicenseApplication` against one `licensee_id` and one `jurisdiction_id`, declaring the `license_class` applied for — `MONEY_TRANSMISSION`, `PAYMENT_INSTRUMENTS` or `VIRTUAL_CURRENCY` — a `projected_annual_volume_cents` of zero or more, and a `new_product_narrative` carrying the expansion and new-product analysis the state expects. The row starts in `DRAFT`; the remaining statuses are `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `DEFICIENT`, `APPROVED`, `DENIED` and `WITHDRAWN`, of which the last three are terminal. It also starts with `required_bond_cents` and `required_net_worth_cents` both zero — they are placeholders until submission pins them — `deficiency_round_count` zero, and `deficiency_deadline_at`, `submitted_at`, `decision_at` and `denial_reason` all null. Nothing is checked for duplication at this point — a licensee may keep several drafts for the same state.

Submission is the moment the arithmetic gets pinned. The application must be in `DRAFT`; a submit from any other status is rejected with `ERR_APPLICATION_INVALID_STATE`. The licensee's `coverage_posture` must not be `FROZEN`, or the submission is rejected with `ERR_POSTURE_FROZEN`, and its `control_chain_status` must not be `BLOCKED`. There must be no other application for the same licensee and the same jurisdiction whose `status` is `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `DEFICIENT` or `APPROVED`; a second live filing is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_LICENSE_APPLICATION`. On success the application moves `DRAFT → SUBMITTED`, `submitted_at` is stamped from the logical clock, `required_net_worth_cents` is pinned as a copy of the jurisdiction's `min_net_worth_cents`, and `required_bond_cents` is pinned to the surety bond figure computed as described in §3.4, using the jurisdiction configuration, the submitted `projected_annual_volume_cents`, the declared `license_class`, and a delegate count of zero — an applicant has no active delegates yet by definition.

Review is the regulator's. A `StateRegulator` whose `X-User-ID` equals the application's `jurisdiction_id` posts a review `action` with an optional `note`. The action `START_REVIEW` moves `SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW` and refreshes `updated_at`. The action `ISSUE_DEFICIENCY`, taken while the application is `UNDER_REVIEW`, increments `deficiency_round_count` and has two outcomes that depend entirely on how many rounds have already been spent. The increment is attached to the action rather than to either branch, so it fires on both edges; the branch is then chosen from the count as it stood *before* this action. While that pre-action count is below 3, the application moves `UNDER_REVIEW → DEFICIENT` and `deficiency_deadline_at` is set to the current logical clock plus thirty days. But when the pre-action count already stands at 3, the same action is a denial rather than a fourth round: the application moves `UNDER_REVIEW → DENIED`, `denial_reason` is recorded as `EXCESSIVE_DEFICIENCY_ROUNDS`, `decision_at` is stamped from the clock, `deficiency_deadline_at` is cleared to null, and the counter — incremented by this action like any other — is left reading 4. The two edges are siblings out of the same state and the round counter alone distinguishes them.

Correspondence closes the loop from the other side. A `LicensingManager` resubmits a `DEFICIENT` application with a fresh `projected_annual_volume_cents` and an optional `remediation_note`. The resubmission must arrive on or before `deficiency_deadline_at` — the boundary is inclusive, and a resubmission after the deadline is rejected with `ERR_DEFICIENCY_DEADLINE_PASSED`. On success the application moves `DEFICIENT → UNDER_REVIEW`, `deficiency_deadline_at` is cleared to null, and `required_bond_cents` is recomputed from the newly supplied projected volume. `deficiency_round_count` is deliberately **not** reset: it is the loop counter that eventually forces the automatic denial, and an application may therefore bounce between `UNDER_REVIEW` and `DEFICIENT` at most three times. A `DEFICIENT` application may instead be closed out: the regulator records the applicant's withdrawal with the action `WITHDRAW`, moving `DEFICIENT → WITHDRAWN`, clearing `deficiency_deadline_at` and stamping `decision_at`, and no licence is minted.

Approval and outright denial both come out of `UNDER_REVIEW`. The action `DENY` moves `UNDER_REVIEW → DENIED` with `denial_reason` recorded as `REGULATOR_DENIAL` and `decision_at` stamped. The action `APPROVE` carries three substantive preconditions on top of the state and scope checks: the licensee's `reported_tangible_net_worth_cents` must be at least the application's pinned `required_net_worth_cents`, or the approval is rejected with `ERR_NET_WORTH_INSUFFICIENT`; every non-`REMOVED` control person of the licensee whose `control_determination` is `CONTROL` must have `background_check_status` of `CLEARED`, or the approval is rejected with `ERR_BACKGROUND_CHECK_INCOMPLETE`, which is the per-holder statement of the licensee-level condition that `control_chain_status` be `VERIFIED`; and the licensee's `coverage_posture` must not be `FROZEN`. The licensee-level `control_chain_status` block and the frozen-posture block report `ERR_CONTROL_CHAIN_BLOCKED` and `ERR_POSTURE_FROZEN` respectively.

Approval stamps `decision_at` and mints a licence. The new `StateLicense` records its `licensee_id`, its `jurisdiction_id` and the `application_id` of the approved application; it copies `license_class` from the application; it receives a server-minted `license_number` of the form `MT-` followed by the jurisdiction's `state_code`, a second hyphen, and a six-digit sequence number that starts at `000001` and advances independently within each jurisdiction. It starts with `license_status` `PENDING_BOND`, `reporting_status` `CURRENT`, `expansion_gate` `BLOCKED`, `bond_status` `NONE`, `outstanding_obligations_cents` zero, and `bond_requirement_cents` copied from the application's `required_bond_cents`. The rest of the row starts empty in the obvious way: `annual_volume_cents`, `active_delegate_count`, `bond_posted_cents` and `renewal_total_fee_cents` are zero, `renewal_status` is `NOT_RENEWED`, `exam_findings_open` is false, and `bond_issuer`, `bond_cancellation_effective_date`, `renewal_year` and `authorized_at` are null. Minting a licence moves no counter on the licensee — `active_license_count` does not rise until the bond activates the licence. The approval response returns the application, not the licence, so the minted licence is discovered by filtering the licence register on its `application_id`.

### 3.4. Bonds, Obligations and the Surety Requirement

A licence in `PENDING_BOND` does nothing until the bond is on file. A `LicensingManager` posts one with an `amount_cents`, the `bond_issuer` — the surety company that wrote it — and an `effective_date`. The posted amount must be at least the licence's current `bond_requirement_cents`; a smaller bond is rejected with `ERR_BOND_INSUFFICIENT` (HTTP 422). Posting is admissible only from `PENDING_BOND`; from any other authorisation status the request is rejected with `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`. The `effective_date` is required and must be a well-formed date, but it is a filing formality only: it is not persisted on the licence and no later rule reads it. On success the licence moves `PENDING_BOND → ACTIVE`: `bond_posted_cents` records the face amount, `bond_status` becomes `POSTED`, the `bond_issuer` is stored, `authorized_at` is stamped from the logical clock, the licensee's `active_license_count` is increased by one, and the licensee's `aggregate_outstanding_obligations_cents`, both investment values, `coverage_ratio_bps` and `coverage_posture` are recomputed — a recomputation that in practice changes nothing, since a `PENDING_BOND` licence was already inside the obligation aggregate set of the paragraph below and activation does not alter its membership. `expansion_gate` is re-derived at the same moment and becomes `OPEN` when the licensee's `coverage_posture` is `COMPLIANT` or `WATCH`, and stays `BLOCKED` when it is `DEFICIENT` or `FROZEN`. The licence's `version` is bumped. `bond_status` has four values in all — `NONE`, `POSTED`, `CANCELLATION_NOTICED` and `CANCELLED` — with `NONE` as the default.

**How the requirement is computed.** The surety bond requirement is a four-step calculation whose order is pinned. Step one selects a tier bond from the relevant volume figure: the application's `projected_annual_volume_cents` in an application context, or the licence's `annual_volume_cents` in a licence context. A volume of at most one billion cents — that is, ten million dollars — is tier one at a tier bond of two and a half million cents; a volume above one billion cents but at most ten billion cents is tier two at ten million cents; a volume above ten billion cents is tier three at twenty-five million cents. Step two takes the greater of the jurisdiction's `base_bond_cents` and the sum of the tier bond plus the delegate count multiplied by the jurisdiction's `per_delegate_bond_cents`; the delegate count is zero in an application context and the licence's `active_delegate_count` otherwise. Step three multiplies that figure by the class multiplier for the `license_class` and divides by 10000, rounding down: `MONEY_TRANSMISSION` carries a multiplier of 10000 basis points and therefore leaves the figure unchanged, `PAYMENT_INSTRUMENTS` carries 12000 basis points, and `VIRTUAL_CURRENCY` carries 15000 basis points. Step four takes the lesser of that result and the jurisdiction's `bond_cap_cents`. Applying the statutory floor after the multiplier, or the cap before it, produces a different figure and is wrong. The requirement is recomputed on application submission, on application resubmission, on every delegate authorization activation, termination or suspension, and on every call report that changes the licence's annual volume.

**Obligations.** A `LicensingManager` adjusts what a licence owes to its customers in that state by supplying a `delta_cents`, which may be negative, together with a `memo` explaining the movement. The delta is added to the licence's `outstanding_obligations_cents`, and the resulting balance may never go below zero — an adjustment that would drive it negative is rejected with `ERR_NEGATIVE_OBLIGATIONS` (HTTP 422). The adjustment then re-derives, in order: the licensee's `aggregate_outstanding_obligations_cents`, both permissible-investment values, `coverage_ratio_bps` and `coverage_posture`. If the posture drops to `DEFICIENT` or `FROZEN`, the posture cascade of §2.9 runs. If the posture recovers to `COMPLIANT` or `WATCH`, `expansion_gate` is re-derived on every `ACTIVE` licence of the licensee against the five conditions of §2.8 — and that recovery is precisely what makes a `SUSPENDED_BY_POSTURE` authorization activatable again. Both the licence's `version` and the licensee's `version` are bumped.

The aggregate itself is the exact sum, in integer cents with no rounding, of `outstanding_obligations_cents` over the licensee's licences whose `license_status` is `PENDING_BOND`, `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` or `SURRENDER_REQUESTED`. There is no time window and the sum is order-insensitive. A licence that reaches `SURRENDERED` or `REVOKED` leaves the set and its obligations stop being counted as needing coverage, which is why both of those transitions force a recomputation of the aggregate, the ratio and the posture.

**Cancelling a bond.** A surety may withdraw. The licensee files the notice with a `bond_cancellation_effective_date`, which must be at least thirty days after the current logical clock; a shorter notice is rejected with `ERR_BOND_NOTICE_TOO_SHORT` (HTTP 422). The notice is admissible only while the licence is `ACTIVE` with `bond_status` `POSTED`. On acceptance, `bond_status` becomes `CANCELLATION_NOTICED`, the effective date is stored, and the licence moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED`, triggering the licence-suspension cascade of §2.9 in full. The licence's `version` is bumped.

**Surrendering a licence.** A licensee may hand a licence back. The request carries an `effective_date` — required and well-formed, but, exactly like the bond's, not persisted and never read again — a `wind_down_narrative` and the `expected_state_license_version` guard of §2.6, and is admissible from either `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`. Two substantive conditions apply: the licence's `outstanding_obligations_cents` must be exactly zero, or the request is rejected with `ERR_OUTSTANDING_OBLIGATIONS_REMAIN`; and no `DelegateAuthorization` of the licence may still be `ACTIVE`, or the request is rejected with `ERR_ACTIVE_DELEGATES_REMAIN`. On success the licence moves to `SURRENDER_REQUESTED`, `expansion_gate` is set to `BLOCKED`, `version` is bumped, and `active_license_count` is reduced by one — but only when the prior status was `ACTIVE`; surrendering an already-suspended licence moves no counter, because the suspension already decremented it.

The regulator of record then decides. A `decision` of `APPROVED`, with an optional `rationale`, moves `SURRENDER_REQUESTED → SURRENDERED`, which is terminal, sets `bond_status` to `CANCELLED`, sets `expansion_gate` to `BLOCKED`, drives every non-`TERMINATED` `DelegateAuthorization` of the licence to `TERMINATED` and `active_delegate_count` to zero, and — because the licence has left the aggregate set — recomputes the licensee's `aggregate_outstanding_obligations_cents`, both investment values, `coverage_ratio_bps` and `coverage_posture`. It does **not** touch `active_license_count`: the surrender *request* already decremented it on the way into `SURRENDER_REQUESTED`, and decrementing again here would double-count, so the decision leaves the counter exactly where the request left it. A `decision` of `DENIED` puts the licence back to work: it moves `SURRENDER_REQUESTED → ACTIVE`, increases `active_license_count` by one, and re-derives `expansion_gate`, which becomes `OPEN` when the licensee's `coverage_posture` is `COMPLIANT` or `WATCH` and the other gate conditions hold. `bond_status` is not a precondition on this path and no error is raised over it; a licence whose bond had lapsed simply returns to `ACTIVE` with its gate re-derived to `BLOCKED`, because `bond_status` `POSTED` is one of the five gate conditions. Either decision bumps the licence's `version`.

### 3.5. The Reporting Calendar: Call Reports and Renewals

Every licence files on the cadence its state prescribes. A `LicensingManager` files a `CallReport` against a `state_license_id` with a `period_label`, the `transmission_volume_cents` moved during the period and the `transaction_count` of transmissions, both of which must be zero or greater. The label is a reporting-period key: for a jurisdiction whose `call_report_frequency` is `QUARTERLY` it is a four-digit year followed by the letter Q and a digit from 1 to 4; for an `ANNUAL` jurisdiction it is a four-digit year followed by the letters FY. The label must be unique for the licence, and its form must match the jurisdiction's cadence — a repeat label, or a quarterly label filed against an annual state, is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CALL_REPORT` (HTTP 409); the cadence test is applied before the uniqueness test, so a label that is both off-cadence and already filed reports the cadence failure. A label that does not match either shape at all — a wrong-length year, a `Q5`, a lowercase suffix, anything else — never reaches that rule: it fails the request-shape band with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, ahead of even the licence lookup. Filing is impossible against a `SURRENDERED` or `REVOKED` licence, which reports `ERR_LICENSE_TERMINAL`.

From the label the server derives `period_end`, at midnight UTC on the last day of the labelled period in the labelled year: the 31st of March for Q1, the 30th of June for Q2, the 30th of September for Q3, and the 31st of December for both Q4 and a full fiscal year. `due_at` is then `period_end` plus forty-five days, again at midnight UTC. `filed_at` is stamped from the logical clock. The report's `status` follows a strictly ordered two-test rule: the report is `LATE` if the logical clock is strictly past `due_at`; otherwise it is `FILED_DEFICIENT` if `transaction_count` is exactly zero while `transmission_volume_cents` is greater than zero; otherwise it is `FILED`. Lateness is tested first, so a filing that is both late and internally inconsistent is `LATE`, not `FILED_DEFICIENT`. The report also carries its own `assessment_fee_cents`, computed as `transmission_volume_cents` multiplied by the jurisdiction's `assessment_rate_bps`, divided by 10000, with the result rounded half up to whole cents.

The filing then drives the reporting dimension of the licence, entirely independently of its authorisation status — a `SUSPENDED` licence still files and still flips this dimension. A `LATE` report moves `reporting_status` from `CURRENT` to `DELINQUENT`. A `FILED_DEFICIENT` report moves it from `CURRENT` to `DEFICIENT_FILING`; the zero-count, non-zero-volume filing is accepted and flagged rather than refused. Recovery from `DEFICIENT_FILING` back to `CURRENT` happens when a later report is filed with a `transaction_count` greater than zero on or before the `due_at` of its own period; that report's `status` is `FILED`. Recovery from `DELINQUENT` back to `CURRENT` is stricter: the new report must itself come out `FILED` — on or before the `due_at` of its own period — and no other call report of the licence may still be `LATE` for a period label greater than the one being filed; when both hold, `reporting_status` returns to `CURRENT`. Period labels are ordered by year and then by period within the year, with `FY` ranking alongside `Q4`. It is that second clause, not the label of the new report, that carries the sense of *later*: an on-time filing for an earlier period recovers the licence just as well, provided no `LATE` report survives above it. The dimension only ever leaves `CURRENT`, never moves sideways: a `LATE` filing against a licence already `DEFICIENT_FILING`, and a `FILED_DEFICIENT` filing against one already `DELINQUENT`, both leave `reporting_status` exactly where it was. Regardless of status, every filing adds its `transmission_volume_cents` to the licence's `annual_volume_cents` — a running total that no operation resets, not even a year boundary — and recomputes `bond_requirement_cents`, since the volume tier may have moved. Any `reporting_status` other than `CURRENT` forces `expansion_gate` to `BLOCKED`; the gate is re-derived on every filing, so a recovery to `CURRENT` reopens it whenever the other four conditions of §2.8 hold. The licence's `version` is bumped.

Renewal is annual and paid. A `LicensingManager` supplies the `renewal_year` and a `payment_amount_cents`. Three gates guard it: the licensee's `control_chain_status` must not be `BLOCKED` (`ERR_CONTROL_CHAIN_BLOCKED`); the licence's `reporting_status` must be exactly `CURRENT`, so a `DELINQUENT` or `DEFICIENT_FILING` licence cannot renew (`ERR_REPORTING_NOT_CURRENT`); and the licensee's `reported_tangible_net_worth_cents` must be at least the jurisdiction's `min_net_worth_cents` (`ERR_NET_WORTH_INSUFFICIENT`). The fee itself is computed in a pinned order that only looks circular. First the subtotal: the jurisdiction's `renewal_fee_cents` plus the licence's current `active_delegate_count` multiplied by twenty-five thousand cents — two hundred and fifty dollars per active delegate. Second, a tier rate is selected **from that pre-assessment subtotal and never from the final total**: 500 basis points when the subtotal is at most one hundred thousand cents, 350 basis points when the subtotal is above one hundred thousand cents but at most two hundred and fifty thousand cents, and 200 basis points when the subtotal exceeds two hundred and fifty thousand cents. Third, the assessment is the subtotal multiplied by the tier rate divided by 10000, rounded half up to whole cents. Fourth, the total is the subtotal plus that assessment. The supplied `payment_amount_cents` must equal that total exactly; any other figure is rejected with `ERR_RENEWAL_FEE_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422). On a match, `renewal_status` moves from `NOT_RENEWED` to `RENEWED`, `renewal_year` records the year renewed — stored as supplied, with no calendar validation beyond being an integer of zero or more, and never compared to the clock — `renewal_total_fee_cents` records the computed total, and the licence's `version` is bumped. `renewal_status` is not itself a precondition: a licence already reading `RENEWED` may be renewed again for a later year against the same three gates, and the second filing simply overwrites `renewal_year` and `renewal_total_fee_cents`.

Separately, a licensee-level read assembles the annual assessment invoice across the estate. Only licences whose `license_status` is `ACTIVE` produce a line; `PENDING_BOND`, `SUSPENDED`, `SURRENDER_REQUESTED`, `SURRENDERED` and `REVOKED` licences contribute nothing at all. Lines are taken in `created_at` ascending order, which affects no value but is pinned for reproducibility, and `line_item_count` reports how many there were. Each line's raw amount is that licence's `outstanding_obligations_cents` multiplied by its jurisdiction's `assessment_rate_bps` divided by 10000, kept exact in cents and never rounded at this step; `unrounded_sum_cents` reports the sum of those exact raw amounts, rendered as a JSON integer when that exact sum happens to be a whole number of cents and as a fractional number otherwise — it is the one figure in the system allowed to be non-integral, precisely because it is the unrounded witness. Each line is then rounded to a whole dollar — divide the raw cents by one hundred, round half up, and multiply by one hundred again — and `annual_assessment_total_cents` is the sum of the rounded lines. The rounding order is pinned: round every line first, then add. Adding first and rounding once produces a different number and is wrong.

### 3.6. Agents and Authorized Delegates

A money transmitter rarely serves customers directly in every store. A `LicensingManager` appoints an agent by creating a `Delegate` under a `licensee_id` with the agent's `legal_name` and a `delegate_type` of either `AGENT` or `AUTHORIZED_DELEGATE`. The row starts in `ONBOARDING`, the other statuses being `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` and the terminal `TERMINATED`. Crucially, `oversight_score` starts null and `last_oversight_review_at` starts null, so a freshly appointed delegate cannot be put to work until someone has actually reviewed it. `authorized_state_count` starts at zero and `oversight_hold`, the flag that records that every one of the delegate's authorizations has been suspended by a posture or licence cascade, starts false.

Oversight is scored. Either a `ComplianceOfficer` — for any delegate — or a `DelegateOperator` acting for its own delegate row submits three component scores, each on a nought-to-one-hundred scale: `training_score`, `aml_test_score` and `recordkeeping_score`. The resulting `oversight_score` is the training score weighted at 4000 basis points, plus the anti-money-laundering test score weighted at 3500 basis points, plus the recordkeeping score weighted at 2500 basis points, that sum divided by 10000 and rounded down to a whole number. Only the most recent review counts: the new score **replaces** the old one outright and earlier reviews are not averaged in. `last_oversight_review_at` is stamped from the logical clock. A `DelegateOperator` submitting for a delegate other than the one its `X-User-ID` names is rejected with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_SCOPE`. The delegate's own `status` is not a precondition and the review changes nothing but the score, its stamp and the row's `version`: a `SUSPENDED` or even `TERMINATED` delegate may be rescored, and rescoring alone never revives one, never clears `oversight_hold` and never moves an authorization.

The appointment is made concrete per state by a `DelegateAuthorization`, which pairs a `delegate_id` with a `state_license_id` and defines a period and a limit: `effective_date` is the first day of the authorization period, `expiry_date` is the last day and may not precede the effective date, and `monthly_volume_cap_cents` is the contractual monthly transmission cap, which must be strictly greater than zero. The interval is closed at both ends. Three checks guard creation. The delegate and the licence must belong to the same licensee — a delegate authorized under another licensee's licence is rejected with `ERR_CROSS_LICENSEE_REFERENCE`. The licensee's `coverage_posture` must not be `FROZEN`, or creation is rejected with `ERR_POSTURE_FROZEN`. And within a single jurisdiction, one delegate may not hold two overlapping authorizations: a new authorization is rejected with `ERR_DELEGATE_PERIOD_OVERLAP` (HTTP 409) whenever some other non-`TERMINATED` authorization of the same delegate under a licence of the **same** jurisdiction has an interval that overlaps or merely touches the new one — formally, whenever the new `effective_date` is on or before the existing `expiry_date` and the existing `effective_date` is on or before the new `expiry_date`. Touching endpoints collide; at least a one-day gap is required. The authorization must also target a licence in an admissible authorisation status, or it is rejected with `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`. A new authorization starts in `PENDING_OVERSIGHT` and moves no counter — only activation makes a delegate count against the bond. The remaining statuses are `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED_BY_POSTURE`, `SUSPENDED` and the terminal `TERMINATED`; `activated_at` and `oversight_score_at_activation` both start null.

Activation is the gated step. It is admissible from `PENDING_OVERSIGHT` or from `SUSPENDED_BY_POSTURE`, and from nothing else — an attempt from `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` or `TERMINATED` is rejected with `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_INVALID_STATE`. The underlying licence's `license_status` must be `ACTIVE` (`ERR_LICENSE_NOT_ACTIVE`), its `expansion_gate` must be `OPEN` (`ERR_EXPANSION_GATE_BLOCKED`), the delegate's `oversight_score` must be non-null and at least 70 (`ERR_OVERSIGHT_SCORE_INSUFFICIENT`) — a score of exactly 70 satisfies the gate, 69 does not — and the delegate and licence must share a licensee. On success, and identically for both admissible source statuses, the authorization moves to `ACTIVE`, `activated_at` is stamped from the logical clock, and `oversight_score_at_activation` pins the delegate's `oversight_score` as it stood at that instant. The licence's `active_delegate_count` increases by one and its `bond_requirement_cents` is recomputed with the new delegate count — which is how onboarding an agent can raise a bond requirement above the posted bond. The delegate's `authorized_state_count` increases by one. The first successful activation moves the delegate `ONBOARDING → ACTIVE`; an activation out of `SUSPENDED_BY_POSTURE` for a delegate that had been parked moves it `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE` and clears `oversight_hold` to false. Because the gate must be `OPEN`, that second path only becomes reachable once the posture recovers.

Termination is the compensating action and is admissible from every non-`TERMINATED` status, and from any licence status whatever — the underlying licence may be `PENDING_BOND`, `SUSPENDED` or even terminal, because winding an appointment down must never be blocked by the state of the thing it hangs off. A second attempt on an already-`TERMINATED` row is rejected with `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_INVALID_STATE`. The authorization moves to the terminal `TERMINATED` with an optional `reason`, which is validated but not persisted. If it had been `ACTIVE`, the licence's `active_delegate_count` and the delegate's `authorized_state_count` each drop by one and `bond_requirement_cents` is recomputed with the reduced count, restoring exactly the values that held before activation; and a delegate left with no non-`TERMINATED` authorization at all moves to the terminal `TERMINATED`. If it had only been `PENDING_OVERSIGHT`, no counter moves, because the row was never counted.

Two suspension paths reach an authorization without any operator asking. When a licence's `expansion_gate` flips to `BLOCKED` — by a posture drop or by an issued examination report with findings — every `PENDING_OVERSIGHT` authorization of that licence moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_POSTURE`, and any delegate thereby left with no `ACTIVE` authorization moves to `SUSPENDED` with `oversight_hold` true; a delegate still in `ONBOARDING` whose only authorizations were parked this way moves `ONBOARDING → SUSPENDED` on the same terms. When a licence is suspended by a bond cancellation notice or a consent order, every `ACTIVE` authorization of that licence moves to `SUSPENDED`, decrementing both counters and recomputing the bond requirement, and a delegate whose `authorized_state_count` reaches zero moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` with `oversight_hold` true. When a licence is revoked, every non-`TERMINATED` authorization moves to `TERMINATED` and a delegate left with none moves `ACTIVE → TERMINATED`.

### 3.7. Permissible Investments, Coverage Testing and Posture

A money transmitter must hold permissible investments that cover what it owes to customers, and it must prove that periodically. A `ComplianceOfficer` or `ChiefComplianceOfficer` records each asset as an `InvestmentHolding` under a `licensee_id`, with an `asset_class` drawn from `CASH`, `US_TREASURY`, `AGENCY_BOND`, `MONEY_MARKET_FUND`, `RECEIVABLE_FROM_DELEGATE` and `CORPORATE_DEBT`; a `counterparty_name` naming the obligor or custodian, which is the grouping key for the concentration cap; a `book_value_cents` and a `market_value_cents`, both zero or greater; and, for a receivable, an `age_days` in days, defaulting to zero and ignored entirely for every non-receivable class. The row starts with `status` `HELD`; disposing of it moves it to `DISPOSED`, and only `HELD` rows enter any coverage computation. Disposal carries no status precondition and is idempotent: disposing of a row already `DISPOSED` succeeds, leaves the status where it is, and still runs the recomputation below. Both recording a holding and disposing of one recompute the licensee's coverage figures and posture and bump `Licensee.version`, and both run the posture cascade of §2.9 on the transition terms set out there.

**The two coverage variants.** Over the same set of holdings, the system computes two deliberately different investment values, and the divergence between them is the point. The candidate set for both is the licensee's holdings whose `status` is `HELD` and whose `asset_class` is not among the non-permissible classes — and `CORPORATE_DEBT` is the sole non-permissible class, so corporate debt is excluded from both variants outright, as are all disposed rows.

The `BOOK_VALUE` variant is the generous one: `book_investment_value_cents` is simply the sum of `book_value_cents` over the candidate set. No haircut is applied, no receivable is aged out, and no counterparty concentration cap is imposed. For the same holdings it is therefore always at least as large as the other variant.

The `ELIGIBLE_MARKET` variant is the regulatory one and its evaluation order is pinned. First, the concentration cap is fixed for this licensee as its `aggregate_outstanding_obligations_cents` multiplied by 1000 basis points — ten percent — divided by 10000 and rounded down; that figure is exposed as `concentration_cap_cents`. Then each candidate holding is processed one at a time, in an order that does not affect the result. A `RECEIVABLE_FROM_DELEGATE` holding whose `age_days` exceeds five is skipped entirely: a receivable older than five days is not a permissible investment at all. Every other holding contributes an adjusted amount equal to its `market_value_cents` multiplied by 10000 minus its class haircut, divided by 10000 and rounded down at that step. The haircuts, in basis points, are: `CASH` none, `US_TREASURY` 200, `MONEY_MARKET_FUND` 300, `AGENCY_BOND` 500 and `RECEIVABLE_FROM_DELEGATE` 1000. Each adjusted amount then goes into one of two buckets. Holdings in the concentration-capped classes — `AGENCY_BOND`, `MONEY_MARKET_FUND` and `RECEIVABLE_FROM_DELEGATE` — accumulate into a running total per `counterparty_name`. Holdings in the uncapped classes — `CASH` and `US_TREASURY` — accumulate into a single uncapped total. Finally, `eligible_investment_value_cents` is the uncapped total plus, for each counterparty group, the lesser of that group's running total and the concentration cap. The order is normative: haircut each holding first, then group by counterparty, then cap each group, then take one final sum. Capping before the haircut, or summing the groups before capping them, yields a different number and is wrong.

**The ratio and the posture.** A coverage ratio in basis points is derived from an investment value the same way for both variants: it is exactly 10000 when the licensee's `aggregate_outstanding_obligations_cents` is zero, and otherwise the investment value multiplied by 10000, divided by the obligations, rounded down. The licensee's `coverage_ratio_bps` is always the `ELIGIBLE_MARKET` ratio and never the book-value one; its default is 10000. From it, and from `consecutive_failed_test_count`, the licensee's `coverage_posture` is determined by a strictly ordered test: the posture is `FROZEN` if `consecutive_failed_test_count` is at least 2, regardless of the ratio; otherwise `COMPLIANT` if `coverage_ratio_bps` is at least 10000; otherwise `WATCH` if `coverage_ratio_bps` is at least 9500; otherwise `DEFICIENT`. The boundaries are exact: 10000 is `COMPLIANT`, 9999 is `WATCH`, 9500 is `WATCH`, 9499 is `DEFICIENT`. The default posture on a new licensee is `COMPLIANT`. A licensee-level coverage read exposes both variants side by side over identical holdings — `obligations_cents`, `book_investment_value_cents`, `book_coverage_ratio_bps`, `eligible_investment_value_cents`, `coverage_ratio_bps`, `concentration_cap_cents`, `coverage_posture` and `consecutive_failed_test_count` — so that the divergence is observable in a single response. The first six are computed afresh at read time from the licensee's current licences and holdings; the last two are read from the stored licensee row, `coverage_posture` in particular being the posture that the last mutation left behind rather than one re-derived from the ratio just computed. The read mutates nothing and bumps no `version`.

**The periodic test.** A `ComplianceOfficer` or `ChiefComplianceOfficer` opens a `PermissibleInvestmentTest` for a `licensee_id` over a closed coverage period defined by `coverage_period_start` and a `coverage_period_end` that must be strictly later, declaring which variant this filing uses via `method`, either `BOOK_VALUE` or `ELIGIBLE_MARKET`, and carrying the `expected_licensee_version` guard of §2.6. The row starts in `DRAFT` with `prepared_by_user_id`, `certified_by_user_id`, `result` and `certified_at` all null and with `obligations_cents`, `investment_value_cents`, `coverage_ratio_bps` and `shortfall_cents` all zero — placeholders that mean nothing until preparation fills them, and in particular a `DRAFT` row reads `coverage_ratio_bps` 0 rather than the vacuous 10000. The other statuses are `PREPARED`, and the terminal `CERTIFIED` and `SUPERSEDED`. Creating a new test supersedes the old work: any existing test of the same licensee whose status is `DRAFT` or `PREPARED` moves to the terminal `SUPERSEDED`, and the create bumps `Licensee.version`. The test names no holdings and takes no holding references, so the restriction that only holdings of the same licensee may inform it is structural rather than validated — the computation simply reads that licensee's own holdings, and `ERR_CROSS_LICENSEE_REFERENCE` is never raised anywhere on this entity.

Preparation, admissible only from `DRAFT`, is where the numbers are frozen. `obligations_cents` snapshots the licensee's `aggregate_outstanding_obligations_cents`; `investment_value_cents` snapshots the permissible investment value computed under this row's own `method`; `coverage_ratio_bps` is that value multiplied by 10000 divided by `obligations_cents` and rounded down, or exactly 10000 when `obligations_cents` is zero; `shortfall_cents` is `obligations_cents` minus `investment_value_cents`, or zero when that difference would be negative — never a negative number; and `result` becomes `PASS` when `coverage_ratio_bps` is at least 10000 and `SHORTFALL` otherwise, having been null until preparation. `prepared_by_user_id` records the acting `X-User-ID`. An operation on a test in the wrong status — preparing anything that is not `DRAFT`, certifying anything that is not `PREPARED`, or touching a `SUPERSEDED` or `CERTIFIED` row — is rejected with `ERR_TEST_INVALID_STATE`.

Certification is the `ChiefComplianceOfficer`'s alone and carries an optional `attestation_note`. It is admissible only from `PREPARED`; the certifying `X-User-ID` must differ from `prepared_by_user_id` per §2.10; and it must arrive on or before forty-five days after `coverage_period_end` — the boundary is inclusive, and a late certification is rejected with `ERR_CERTIFICATION_WINDOW_CLOSED`. The consequences run four levels deep. First, the test moves `PREPARED → CERTIFIED`, `certified_at` is stamped from the logical clock, `certified_by_user_id` records the acting identity, and the licensee's `consecutive_failed_test_count` either increases by one — when the test's `result` is `SHORTFALL` — or resets to zero when it is `PASS`; `coverage_posture` is then recomputed, and because two consecutive certified shortfalls set the count to 2, the second one alone forces the posture to `FROZEN` no matter how good the ratio looks. Second, if the posture is now `DEFICIENT` or `FROZEN`, every `StateLicense` of the licensee whose `license_status` is `ACTIVE` has `expansion_gate` set to `BLOCKED`. Third, every `DelegateAuthorization` of those licences whose `status` is `PENDING_OVERSIGHT` moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_POSTURE`. Fourth, every `Delegate` thereby left with no `ACTIVE` authorization has `status` set to `SUSPENDED` and `oversight_hold` set to true. Certification bumps `Licensee.version`. A `FROZEN` posture then blocks application submission, application approval and new delegate authorizations with `ERR_POSTURE_FROZEN`.

### 3.8. Coordinated Multistate Examinations

States examine a multistate transmitter together rather than one at a time. A `StateRegulator` schedules a `MultistateExam` for a `licensee_id`, naming itself as `lead_jurisdiction_id` and listing the `participating_jurisdiction_ids` that are joining, together with a field window: `fieldwork_start` is inclusive and `fieldwork_end`, which must be strictly later, is exclusive. Every listed participant must be eligible — its `participates_in_multistate` must be true and the examined licensee must hold a `StateLicense` there whose `license_status` is `ACTIVE`; a participant failing either test is rejected with `ERR_JURISDICTION_NOT_MULTISTATE` (HTTP 422), the `participates_in_multistate` test being applied first, and participants being tested in the order they were listed. The eligibility test bites only on the listed participants: the lead names itself in `lead_jurisdiction_id`, not in the array, and is therefore never tested — a lead may schedule an examination of a licensee it does not itself licence, and may sit out of `participates_in_multistate` — unless it also appears among the participants, where it is tested like any other. The array may be empty, which schedules a one-state examination, and it is stored verbatim, duplicates and all. Examinations of one licensee are also kept apart by a thirty-day cooling-off buffer: a new window is rejected with `ERR_EXAM_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (HTTP 409) whenever some other examination of the same licensee has a window such that the new `fieldwork_start` falls before that examination's `fieldwork_end` extended by thirty days **and** that examination's `fieldwork_start` falls before the new `fieldwork_end` extended by thirty days. The conflict test looks at every other examination of the licensee whatever its status, so even a `CLOSED` examination keeps its thirty-day shadow. On success the exam starts in `SCHEDULED` and `participating_state_count` is set to the distinct count of participating jurisdictions including the lead — so a repeated id is counted once, and an empty array yields 1. The remaining statuses are `FIELDWORK`, `REPORT_DRAFT`, `REPORT_ISSUED` and the terminal `CLOSED`; `open_request_count` and `findings_count` both start at zero.

Only the lead drives the examination: every status change requires the acting `X-User-ID` to equal `lead_jurisdiction_id`, and any other regulator is rejected with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_SCOPE`. The lead supplies a `to_status`, and a transition that is not a declared edge is rejected with `ERR_EXAM_INVALID_STATE`. The declared edges are exactly the four forward steps below and nothing else: each status has at most one successor, so a backward move, a skipped step and a restatement of the current status are all equally refused, and `CLOSED` has no successor at all. `SCHEDULED → FIELDWORK` needs nothing beyond the status and the lead check and simply refreshes `updated_at`. `FIELDWORK → REPORT_DRAFT` is gated on the record requests being finished: `open_request_count` must be exactly zero, meaning no `InformationRequest` of the exam remains in `OPEN`, `OVERDUE` or `RESPONDED`. `REPORT_DRAFT → REPORT_ISSUED` carries a `findings_count`, which is stored — it is optional, and an omitted or null count is stored as zero, which is the no-findings branch; on every other edge the field is ignored entirely and the stored count is left alone — and here the examination reaches out into the estate: when `findings_count` is 3 or more, every `StateLicense` of the examined licensee whose `jurisdiction_id` appears in `participating_jurisdiction_ids` has `exam_findings_open` set to true and `expansion_gate` set to `BLOCKED`, and every `DelegateAuthorization` of those licences whose `status` is `PENDING_OVERSIGHT` moves to `SUSPENDED_BY_POSTURE`; when `findings_count` is 2 or fewer, no licence is touched at all. It is the array that is consulted, not the lead, so the lead state's own licence escapes the flag unless the lead listed itself among the participants; and `license_status` is not consulted either, so a `PENDING_BOND`, `SUSPENDED` or even terminal licence in a participating state is flagged along with the `ACTIVE` ones. `REPORT_ISSUED → CLOSED` is compound: beyond the status and lead checks, no `EnforcementAction` whose `originating_exam_id` names this exam may still be in `ISSUED`, `CONTESTED` or `CONSENT_ORDER`. An examination cannot be closed over an unresolved enforcement matter it produced, and the attempt is rejected with `ERR_EXAM_INVALID_STATE`.

Record demands are `InformationRequest` rows. A `StateRegulator` opens one against an `exam_id` with its own `jurisdiction_id`, a `subject` describing the records wanted, and a `requested_item_count` of at least one. The requesting state must be the exam's lead or appear in `participating_jurisdiction_ids`; an outsider's request is rejected with `ERR_JURISDICTION_NOT_PARTICIPATING` (HTTP 422). The exam must be in `FIELDWORK`, or the request is rejected with `ERR_EXAM_INVALID_STATE`. The row starts in `OPEN` — the other statuses being `RESPONDED`, `OVERDUE` and the terminal `ACCEPTED` — with `reopen_count` and `submitted_item_count` at zero and `responded_at` null. Its `due_at` is the current logical clock plus the requesting jurisdiction's `response_window_days`, expressed as that many exact twenty-four-hour periods. Because `open_request_count` is by definition the number of the exam's requests sitting in `OPEN`, `OVERDUE` or `RESPONDED`, opening a request raises it by one.

The licensee side answers. A `LicensingManager` or `ComplianceOfficer` responds with a `submitted_item_count` and a `narrative`. The exam must be in `FIELDWORK`, or the response is rejected with `ERR_EXAM_INVALID_STATE`; the request must be in `OPEN` or `OVERDUE`, or the response is rejected with `ERR_INFO_REQUEST_INVALID_STATE`; the logical clock must be on or before `due_at`, the boundary being inclusive, or the response is rejected with `ERR_RESPONSE_WINDOW_CLOSED`; and `submitted_item_count` must be at least `requested_item_count`, or the response is rejected with `ERR_RESPONSE_INCOMPLETE` (HTTP 422). A partial delivery is not a response. On success the request moves to `RESPONDED`, `submitted_item_count` is stored and `responded_at` is stamped. `open_request_count` does not move: a `RESPONDED` request is still an open matter as far as the exam is concerned, which is exactly why responding to every request is not enough to let the lead draft its report — each one must also be accepted.

The requesting state dispositions the answer with a `decision` and an optional `rationale`, and only the state that issued the request may do so; the examination's own status is not a precondition here, so a request may be dispositioned after the examination has moved on. Only an `OPEN` or a `RESPONDED` request may be dispositioned at all: an `ACCEPTED` request, already terminal, and an `OVERDUE` one, which has nothing pending to judge, are both rejected with `ERR_INFO_REQUEST_INVALID_STATE`. A `decision` of `ACCEPTED`, admissible from `RESPONDED`, moves the request to the terminal `ACCEPTED` and takes it out of the open set, lowering `open_request_count` by one; aimed at an `OPEN` request it is rejected with `ERR_INFO_REQUEST_INVALID_STATE`. A `decision` of `REJECTED` from `RESPONDED` sends the request back to `OPEN`: `reopen_count` increases by one, `submitted_item_count` is reset to zero, and `due_at` is re-armed to the current logical clock plus five days — a deliberately short second clock, and notably not the jurisdiction's own `response_window_days`. The request never leaves the open set on this path, so `open_request_count` is unchanged. A `decision` of `REJECTED` taken while the request is still `OPEN` and the logical clock has already passed `due_at` moves it `OPEN → OVERDUE`; this is the only way a request becomes overdue, since moving the clock alone never flips a status. The same rejection taken while the request is `OPEN` and the clock is still on or before `due_at` is admissible and does nothing whatever — it is neither an error nor a mutation, the request stays `OPEN` on its original `due_at`, `reopen_count` and `submitted_item_count` are untouched, and no `version` is bumped, so the call returns the row exactly as it stood. An `OVERDUE` request remains answerable on the same response rules, including the inclusive `due_at` boundary, moving it `OVERDUE → RESPONDED`; since no operation extends a request's window and its `due_at` does not move on the overdue flip, in practice that means winding the logical clock back to on or before the unchanged `due_at`.

### 3.9. Enforcement, Remediation and Revocation

When an examination finding or an operational failure warrants it, a `StateRegulator` issues an `EnforcementAction` against a `state_license_id`. The action names its own `jurisdiction_id`, which must equal the target licence's `jurisdiction_id` — a state may not police another state's licence, and the attempt is rejected with `ERR_JURISDICTION_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422). It may name an `originating_exam_id` where the finding came from a coordinated examination, or leave it null for a stand-alone matter. It declares its `basis`, one of `PERMISSIBLE_INVESTMENT_SHORTFALL`, `BOND_LAPSE`, `DELINQUENT_REPORTING`, `UNAUTHORIZED_DELEGATE` or `EXAM_FINDING`; a `penalty_cents` civil money penalty of zero or more; and a `remediation_deadline_at` by which the licensee must have remediated under a consent order. The action starts in `ISSUED` and `issued_at` is stamped from the logical clock, which starts a twenty-day response window; `response_at`, `remediation_plan_narrative` and `resolved_at` start null and `remediation_accepted` starts false rather than null. A licence that is already `SURRENDERED` or `REVOKED` accepts no new action, reporting `ERR_LICENSE_TERMINAL`. The remaining statuses are `CONSENT_ORDER`, `CONTESTED`, and the terminal `RESOLVED` and `REVOCATION`.

The licensee's `LicensingManager` responds, and the branch it takes has sharply different consequences. Either branch requires the action to be in `ISSUED` — a response to anything else is rejected with `ERR_ENFORCEMENT_INVALID_STATE` — and requires the logical clock to be on or before `issued_at` plus twenty days, a late response being rejected with `ERR_ENFORCEMENT_RESPONSE_WINDOW_CLOSED`.

A `response` of `CONSENT_ORDER` settles: the action moves `ISSUED → CONSENT_ORDER`, `response_at` is stamped, and the supplied `remediation_plan_narrative` is stored. That narrative is mandatory on this branch — a consent order response with the field absent or empty is rejected with `ERR_REMEDIATION_PLAN_REQUIRED` (HTTP 422). Settling also costs the licence its authorisation, but only where there is an authorisation to cost: the licence transition and its cascade run if and only if the licence is `ACTIVE` at that moment, and against a licence in any other status — `PENDING_BOND`, already `SUSPENDED`, `SURRENDER_REQUESTED` — the action still settles into `CONSENT_ORDER` with its narrative stored and its window checked, while the licence, the counters, the authorizations and the gate are all left untouched and no error is raised. Where the licence is `ACTIVE`, the target `StateLicense` moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED`, `expansion_gate` becomes `BLOCKED`, the licensee's `active_license_count` drops by one, every `ACTIVE` `DelegateAuthorization` of the licence moves to `SUSPENDED` with `active_delegate_count` reset to zero and `bond_requirement_cents` recomputed with no delegates, and each affected `Delegate` has `authorized_state_count` reduced by one and moves to `SUSPENDED`, with `oversight_hold` set to true, when that count reaches zero. `StateLicense.version` is bumped on this branch.

A `response` of `CONTEST` litigates instead: the action moves `ISSUED → CONTESTED` and `response_at` is stamped, and that is the entire effect. The licence is **not** suspended, no delegate authorization is touched, `expansion_gate` is unchanged, `active_license_count` is unchanged, and the licence's `version` is not bumped. The business keeps operating while the matter is argued. This asymmetry between the two branches is intentional and is the sharpest divergence in the model.

Resolution belongs to the issuing regulator, which supplies an `outcome` and, on the settlement path, a `remediation_accepted` flag. An `outcome` of `RESOLVED` is admissible from either `CONSENT_ORDER` or `CONTESTED`; out of `CONSENT_ORDER` it additionally requires the logical clock to be on or before `remediation_deadline_at`, and a late resolution is rejected with `ERR_REMEDIATION_DEADLINE_PASSED`. Out of `CONSENT_ORDER` with `remediation_accepted` true, the action moves to the terminal `RESOLVED`, `resolved_at` is stamped, `remediation_accepted` is recorded as true, and the compensating licence transition runs: the `SUSPENDED` licence returns to `ACTIVE` — which requires its `bond_status` still to be `POSTED` — the licensee's `active_license_count` rises by one, `exam_findings_open` is cleared to false, and `expansion_gate` is re-derived, becoming `OPEN` when the licensee's `coverage_posture` is `COMPLIANT` or `WATCH` and `exam_findings_open` is false. Those two licence conditions are a filter on the compensation, not a validation of the request: an action whose licence is not `SUSPENDED`, or whose `bond_status` is no longer `POSTED`, still resolves cleanly with `remediation_accepted` true, but no licence transition, counter movement, findings clearance or gate re-derivation happens and no error is raised. The authorizations the suspension suspended are deliberately not reactivated. With `remediation_accepted` false or absent — false being the default — the action still resolves but the licence stays `SUSPENDED`. Out of `CONTESTED`, an `outcome` of `RESOLVED` moves the action to `RESOLVED` and stamps `resolved_at` with no licence transition whatsoever — there is nothing to compensate, because this branch never suspended anything — and `remediation_accepted` is forced to false on the row whatever the request supplied, since there was no remediation plan to accept.

An `outcome` of `REVOCATION` is admissible from either `CONSENT_ORDER` or `CONTESTED` and is the end of the licence; it is not subject to the remediation deadline, which guards the `RESOLVED` outcome alone, so a state may revoke long after the deadline it set has lapsed. The action moves to the terminal `REVOCATION`, `resolved_at` is stamped from the clock on this outcome too — the field records when the matter left the open set, not that it ended well — and the licence runs the full four-level revocation cascade of §2.9: `license_status` becomes the terminal `REVOKED`, `expansion_gate` becomes `BLOCKED`, `bond_status` becomes `CANCELLED`, and `active_license_count` drops by one when — and only when — the licence had been `ACTIVE` immediately before, so revoking an already-suspended licence moves no counter; every non-`TERMINATED` `DelegateAuthorization` of the licence moves to `TERMINATED` with `active_delegate_count` reset to zero; each `Delegate` that had an `ACTIVE` authorization has `authorized_state_count` reduced by one and moves `ACTIVE → TERMINATED` when it has no non-`TERMINATED` authorization left anywhere; and, because the licence has left the obligation aggregate set, the licensee's `aggregate_outstanding_obligations_cents`, `coverage_ratio_bps` and `coverage_posture` are recomputed. `StateLicense.version` is bumped. Until every action originating from an examination has reached `RESOLVED` or `REVOCATION`, that examination cannot be closed.

## 4. Endpoint Definitions

Operations that create a new resource return `201 Created`. Every other operation — state transitions, adjudications, filings against an existing resource, reads, and the administrative clock capability — returns `200 OK`. All request and response bodies follow the envelope of §2.2; error statuses are those catalogued in §5. The free-text annotations that accompany many transitions are accepted, type-checked and then discarded: a review `note`, a resubmission's `remediation_note`, an obligations `memo`, a `wind_down_narrative`, a surrender, change-of-control or disposition `rationale`, a certification's `attestation_note`, a termination `reason` and an information-request response `narrative` are none of them persisted and none of them appear on the returned resource. The narratives that *are* persisted are exactly the three the domain reads back: an application's `new_product_narrative`, an information request's `subject`, and a consent order's `remediation_plan_narrative`.
