# Foreign Supplier Verification Program API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

An FSVP importer is legally answerable for food it did not make, in facilities it does not own, in countries whose regulators it does not control. The compliance office that carries that responsibility works from a single unit of accountability: one imported food paired with one foreign supplier establishment. For every such combination it must identify the hazards requiring a control, decide who applies that control, evaluate the establishment's performance history and regulatory posture, select verification activities of a defensible type and frequency, keep the resulting evidence available for inspection, and — when verification fails — decide between remediating the supplier and suspending imports altogether. This API is the system of record for that programme.

The document below specifies a compliance back office rather than a document repository. Every score in the system is derived, never asserted: hazard risk falls out of severity, probability, consumer exposure and who owns the control; supplier performance falls out of attested verification evidence weighted by activity type and recency; the adequacy of a verification plan falls out of the plan's declared coverage measured against the hazard load it is supposed to cover. Because those derivations gate real regulatory outcomes — whether an entry line may be filed at all — they are specified here to the decimal place and to the boundary. A threshold written as "at least" is inclusive; a threshold written as "strictly below" is not.

Two structural properties shape the whole design. First, a supplier-food combination carries three *independent* status dimensions on one row — its verification posture, its entry eligibility, and its exemption posture — and the interesting failures of a naive implementation are the places where one dimension is wrongly assumed to follow another. Second, most writes in this system are not local: attesting one verification record can suspend a supplier, lapse its pairs, open corrective actions, hold entry lines already accepted at the border, and flip the importer's programme status, all inside one request. The cascade rules and their compensating reversals are therefore treated as first-class specification, not as incidental side effects.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

This section defines the contracts that hold across every endpoint: authentication, envelope shape, pagination, the logical clock, record identity, cascade discipline, arithmetic precision, version pinning, and the fixed order in which the system refuses a request.

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to the API must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, declaring the capacity in which that principal acts. A request missing either header, carrying an empty value for either, or naming a role outside the five recognised names is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). A request whose role is recognised but is not authorised for the target operation is rejected with `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403).

The system recognises exactly five roles: `ComplianceOfficer`, `QualifiedIndividual`, `EntryFiler`, `FdaInspector`, and `PlatformAdmin`.

- `ComplianceOfficer` is the importer's compliance office of record. It registers importers, suppliers and foods, creates supplier-food combinations, designates qualified individuals, records regulatory compliance status learned from FDA sources, and decides and closes corrective actions including import suspension. It never performs the technical FSVP judgements. When `X-User-Role` is `ComplianceOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server does not compare it to any entity's `id`.
- `QualifiedIndividual` is the designated qualified individual who performs the technical work: supplier evaluation, hazard analysis authorship and approval, verification-activity selection and review, record filing and attestation, and the pair's verification approval. When `X-User-Role` is `QualifiedIndividual`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `QualifiedIndividual` row; ownership and separation checks on any resource that references a `QualifiedIndividual` compare the resource's foreign-key field — `designated_qi_id`, `authored_by_qi_id`, `proposed_by_qi_id`, `assigned_qi_id`, `reviewed_by_qi_id`, `submitted_by_qi_id`, `attested_by_qi_id` — to `X-User-ID` directly.
- `EntryFiler` is the customs broker or filer that transmits importer identification at entry. It may create entry lines and read entry lines, and nothing else. When `X-User-Role` is `EntryFiler`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it as `filed_by_user_id` on the `EntrySubmission` it creates but never compares it to any entity's `id`.
- `FdaInspector` is the regulator performing an FSVP inspection. It is strictly read-only across the importer's programme, including the records-availability package. When `X-User-Role` is `FdaInspector`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity and is not compared to any entity's `id`.
- `PlatformAdmin` is the operator of the test harness. Its only capability is advancing the logical clock; it owns no business operation. When `X-User-Role` is `PlatformAdmin`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity and is not compared to any entity's `id`.

Read access is broad but not uniform. `ComplianceOfficer`, `QualifiedIndividual` and `FdaInspector` may read the importer roll-up, qualified individuals, suppliers, foods, supplier-food combinations, hazard analyses, hazards, verification activities, verification records and corrective actions. Entry-line reads are available to `ComplianceOfficer`, `EntryFiler` and `FdaInspector` — a `QualifiedIndividual` has no read access to entry lines. The records-availability package is available to `FdaInspector` and `ComplianceOfficer` only.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, read from the host's real time rather than from the logical clock of §2.4.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

List endpoints are paginated through two query parameters. `limit` is the maximum number of rows returned; it defaults to 10, has a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 100. `offset` is the number of rows skipped before the page begins; it defaults to 0, its minimum is 0 and it has no upper bound. A `limit` outside the range 1 through 100, a negative `offset`, or either parameter supplied as something other than a base-10 integer, is a request-shape violation and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400); a parameter that is omitted or supplied as an empty string falls back to its default instead.

Unless a specific endpoint states otherwise, every list endpoint sorts its rows by `created_at` in ascending order, with rows sharing an identical `created_at` ordered by the sequence in which the server wrote them.

List responses carry `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside the envelope's `meta` object, where `total_count` is the number of rows matching the filter before pagination is applied and `has_more` is true exactly when `offset` plus the number of rows on this page is strictly less than `total_count`. Every filter named in this document is optional, and an omitted or empty-string filter is simply not applied: an enum-valued filter whose value is outside its enumeration is an `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, an identifier-valued filter is compared as an opaque string and matches nothing when it names a row that does not exist, and query parameters the endpoint does not recognise are ignored.

### 2.4. The Logical Compliance Clock

Retention, recency, reassessment cycles, training currency and execution windows are all evaluated against a server-side logical UTC clock, never against wall-clock time. On startup the clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and it must not fall back to the host's real time under any circumstance. A `PlatformAdmin` advances or rewinds it by supplying `to_iso8601` — any valid ISO 8601 instant is accepted, forwards or backwards — and the response's `data` carries the resulting instant as `current_utc` and nothing else. Setting the clock writes no entity rows — it only changes the instant against which every derived read is evaluated, so record availability, performance recency factors, reassessment-overdue determinations and qualified-individual training currency all re-evaluate against the new instant on the next read.

Two derived date fields are computed with calendar-month arithmetic rather than fixed day counts: a qualified individual's `training_valid_until` is the individual's `training_completed_at` advanced by 36 calendar months, and a supplier-food combination's `reassessment_due_at` is its `last_reassessed_at` advanced by 36 calendar months. Where advancing a calendar month would overflow the target month's length, the result clamps to the last day of that month.

### 2.5. Record Identity, Mutation Stamps and Immutable Rows

Every persisted resource carries an `id`, which is a server-minted UUID with no type prefix (a lowercase hyphenated version-4 UUID), and a `created_at`, which is an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits recording when the row was first written. Both `created_at` and `updated_at` are read from the host's real time, not from the logical clock of §2.4; the logical clock stamps only the fields this document explicitly says it stamps — `last_evaluated_at`, `approved_at`, `last_reassessed_at`, `decided_at`, `closed_at` and the records package's `generated_at` — and drives every derived evaluation.

Most resources additionally carry `updated_at` — an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp refreshed on every mutation — and `version`, a monotonically increasing integer that starts at 1 and increments by exactly one on every mutation of that row, including mutations the row's owner never requested (a recomputed roll-up counts as a mutation). Importers, qualified individuals, foreign suppliers, supplier-food combinations, hazard analyses, verification activities, verification records, corrective actions and entry submissions all carry both fields.

Two resource types are immutable after creation and therefore carry only `id` and `created_at`: a `Food` row and a `Hazard` row. For those two, both `updated_at` and `version` are absent from the response entirely — there is no update endpoint for either, and neither participates in version pinning.

### 2.6. The Three Status Dimensions of a Supplier-Food Combination

A `SupplierFoodPair` — the food × foreign-supplier combination that FSVP treats as the unit of compliance — carries three independent status dimensions on the same row, and none of them is derivable from the others.

`verification_status` tracks the technical compliance work and takes one of `DRAFT`, `HAZARD_ANALYZED`, `VERIFICATION_APPROVED`, `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`, `VERIFICATION_LAPSED`, or `RETIRED`. A new combination starts at `DRAFT`; `RETIRED` is terminal.

`entry_eligibility_status` tracks whether entry lines may be filed against the combination and takes one of `PENDING`, `ELIGIBLE`, or `BLOCKED`. A new combination starts at `PENDING`, and `ELIGIBLE` and `BLOCKED` are mutually reachable from each other — a blocked combination can be restored, and an eligible one can be blocked again.

`exemption_status` tracks the exemption or modified-requirements claim and takes one of `NONE`, `CLAIMED`, `VERIFIED`, `REJECTED`, or `REVOKED`, starting at `NONE`.

The independence is deliberate and load-bearing in three specific places. A verified exemption makes a combination entry-eligible while leaving `verification_status` at `DRAFT`, because an exempt combination needs no hazard analysis and no verification plan at all. A voluntary reassessment moves `verification_status` from `VERIFICATION_APPROVED` to `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED` and deliberately leaves `entry_eligibility_status` untouched. And a fresh verification approval that lifts `verification_status` back to `VERIFICATION_APPROVED` does *not* clear a `BLOCKED` eligibility — only the closure of the corrective action that caused the block restores eligibility.

### 2.7. Cascade Discipline: How One Write Propagates

Consistency in this system is immediate: when a write changes a value that another row's specification depends on, the dependent row is updated inside the same request, and its own `updated_at` and `version` move accordingly. The trigger is an actual change of value, not the act of recomputing: a dependent row whose recomputed derived fields all come out identical to what they already were is not a mutation and its `version` and `updated_at` stay exactly where they are. Cascades are described in this document as numbered hops, and every hop that the specification names must actually fire — an implementation that performs hop 1 and hop 2 but stops before hop 5 leaves entry lines accepted at the border for a combination that is no longer eligible.

Three roll-up counters on the `Importer` are maintained by cascade rather than by direct write. `blocked_pair_count` is the number of that importer's combinations whose `entry_eligibility_status` is `BLOCKED`. `open_corrective_action_count` is the number of corrective actions on that importer's suppliers whose `status` is `OPEN`, `SUPPLIER_REMEDIATION` or `IMPORT_SUSPENSION`. `qualified_individual_designated` is true exactly when the importer has at least one qualified individual whose `status` is `ACTIVE` and whose `training_valid_until` is at or after the logical clock. A fourth field, `program_status`, is recomputed from those three plus combination state on every write that changes a designation, a combination status dimension, or a corrective-action status. A `ForeignSupplier` carries its own `open_corrective_action_count` over the same three non-closed statuses, scoped to that supplier.

`program_status` is evaluated top-down and short-circuits on the first clause that holds. The importer is `NON_COMPLIANT` when `blocked_pair_count` is greater than zero, or when `qualified_individual_designated` is false, or when at least one of its combinations has `verification_status` equal to `VERIFICATION_LAPSED`. Otherwise the importer is `AT_RISK` when `open_corrective_action_count` is greater than zero, or when at least one of its combinations has `verification_status` equal to `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`, or when at least one of its combinations has a non-null `reassessment_due_at` that is strictly earlier than the logical clock. Otherwise the importer is `COMPLIANT`. Because the `NON_COMPLIANT` clause is evaluated first, it wins whenever both bands would hold. An importer with no combinations at all but one `ACTIVE` qualified individual is `COMPLIANT`; an importer with no `ACTIVE` qualified individual is `NON_COMPLIANT` no matter how healthy its combinations look, which is why lapsing a single designation is enough to flip the programme status.

Compensating reversals are exact, not approximate. When a corrective action is closed as resolved, `Importer.blocked_pair_count` is restored to the value snapshotted in that action's `pre_action_blocked_pair_count` — the count as it stood immediately *before* the cascade that synthesised the action. When an attested verification record is rescinded, the supplier's `performance_sample_weight_total` and `performance_score_final` return to exactly the values they held before that record was attested.

### 2.8. Scoring Precision and the Round-Then-Sum Rule

Every monetary-style and score-style number in this system is carried to exactly two decimal places and rounded half up. Rounding half up means a digit sequence exactly at the midpoint rounds away from zero: 12.345 becomes 12.35. Integer-valued fields — counts, frequencies, capacities, retention months, line numbers — are plain integers and are not rounded. Two-decimal values are serialised as JSON numbers rather than strings, so trailing zeros are not preserved on the wire: a score of 100.00 appears as `100.0` and a weight of 22.50 as `22.5`.

The order of rounding is pinned and is not an implementation detail. Wherever this document describes a total as the sum of per-item contributions, each per-item contribution is rounded to two decimal places *first* and the already-rounded contributions are then summed; the sum is rounded again to two decimal places. Summing unrounded intermediate products and rounding once at the end produces a different number and is wrong. This applies specifically to the sum of hazard risk scores inside a hazard analysis, to the sum of per-activity coverage weights inside an adequacy score, and to the sums of per-record weights and weighted points inside a supplier performance score.

Clamping and rounding also have a fixed order: where a value is both clamped to a range and rounded, the clamp is applied to the unrounded product and the clamped result is then rounded.

### 2.9. Version Pinning on Program-Critical Writes

Two writes in this system change a parent record's meaning so thoroughly that they may only be performed by a client that has actually observed the parent's current state. Both use optimistic concurrency control against the parent's `version`.

Creating a hazard analysis — `POST /fsvp/hazard-analyses` — requires a non-optional `expected_pair_version` field carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `SupplierFoodPair`; it must be a non-negative integer, and a negative or non-integer value is an `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` at rung 3 rather than a version failure. If that value does not equal the combination's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PAIR_VERSION` (HTTP 412). This is the only guard on a second analysis: opening a new analysis legitimately supersedes the combination's prior work, so no state precondition stands in the way, and a stale `expected_pair_version` is the sole signal that the author was reasoning about a hazard picture the combination no longer has. The accepted value is retained on the analysis as `pair_version_at_create`.

Recording a corrective-action decision — `POST /fsvp/corrective-actions/{id}/decision` — requires a non-optional `expected_supplier_version` field carrying the officer's last observed `version` of the action's `ForeignSupplier`, again a non-negative integer whose shape is checked at rung 3. If that value does not equal the supplier's current `version`, the decision is rejected with `ERR_STALE_SUPPLIER_VERSION` (HTTP 412). One suspension cascade opens one corrective action per lapsed combination, so deciding a second open action on the same supplier satisfies every state precondition; only the stale `expected_supplier_version` reveals that the officer's picture of the supplier's status, performance score and block count predates the earlier decision. The accepted value is retained on the action as `supplier_version_at_decision`.

No other endpoint takes a version field. Every other write proceeds without any concurrency check.

### 2.10. Importer Scope, Identifier Uniqueness and Path Resolution

The importer is the tenancy boundary. Every entity referenced within a single request must belong to the same importer: on combination create, the supplier's `importer_id`, the food's `importer_id` and the body's `importer_id` must all agree, and the named `designated_qi_id` must belong to that same importer; on activity create or revision, the `assigned_qi_id` must belong to the combination's importer, and the combination's importer must match the acting qualified individual's own importer; on hazard-analysis create, the same importer agreement is required. A crossing of that boundary is rejected with `ERR_IMPORTER_SCOPE_MISMATCH` (HTTP 409).

Six uniqueness scopes are enforced, all reported as `ERR_DUPLICATE_IDENTIFIER` (HTTP 409). An importer's `dnb_number` is unique across all importers. An importer's `unique_facility_identifier` is unique across all importers. A qualified individual's `email` is unique within its `importer_id`. A supplier's `facility_registration_number` is unique within its `importer_id`. A food's `fda_product_code` is unique within its `importer_id`. And the combination of an entry line's `entry_number` and `entry_line_number` is unique globally.

When a path carries more than one resource identifier, identifiers are resolved strictly left to right and the request fails on the *first* segment that does not resolve, with that segment's own 404 code. Body-borne foreign keys are resolved in the same fixed order as the refusal ladder in §2.11: importer first, then qualified individual, then supplier, then food, then combination, then hazard analysis, then verification activity, then verification record, then corrective action, then entry submission. A request naming both a missing importer and a missing food therefore reports `ERR_IMPORTER_NOT_FOUND`, never `ERR_FOOD_NOT_FOUND`.

### 2.11. The Order of Refusal

Validation is not a set of independent checks; it is a strictly ordered ladder. Rules are evaluated in ascending priority order, the first violated rule determines the response, and no later rule is evaluated. The ordering below is normative — a request that violates several rules always reports the earliest one.

1. Header presence and role recognition — `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (401), on every endpoint.
2. Role authorisation for the target endpoint — `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (403), on every endpoint.
3. Request shape: required fields present, types correct, enum values in range, integers inside their declared bounds, datetimes valid ISO 8601 UTC, string lengths inside their declared constraints, `limit` between 1 and 100, `offset` at least 0 — `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400), on every endpoint. Body fields and query parameters the endpoint does not recognise are ignored rather than refused. The same `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` code also answers a path that matches no route (HTTP 404) and a method the matched path does not support (HTTP 405).
4. The `importer_id` in path or body resolves to an `Importer` — `ERR_IMPORTER_NOT_FOUND` (404), on the importer read, the records package, the importer's qualified-individual / supplier / food lists, and on qualified-individual, supplier, food and combination creates.
5. The `{id}` path segment, or `designated_qi_id`, or `assigned_qi_id`, resolves to a `QualifiedIndividual` — `ERR_QI_NOT_FOUND` (404), on designation, combination create, activity create and activity revision.
6. The `{id}` path segment or `supplier_id` resolves to a `ForeignSupplier` — `ERR_SUPPLIER_NOT_FOUND` (404), on the supplier read, the compliance-status update, supplier evaluation, and combination create.
7. `food_id` resolves to a `Food` — `ERR_FOOD_NOT_FOUND` (404), on combination create.
8. The `{id}` path segment or `pair_id` resolves to a `SupplierFoodPair` — `ERR_PAIR_NOT_FOUND` (404), on the combination read, verification approval, reassessment, exemption, hazard-analysis create, activity create and entry-line create.
9. The `{id}` path segment resolves to a `HazardAnalysis` — `ERR_HAZARD_ANALYSIS_NOT_FOUND` (404), on the analysis read, analysis approval, hazard create and hazard list.
10. The `{id}` path segment or `activity_id` resolves to a `VerificationActivity` — `ERR_ACTIVITY_NOT_FOUND` (404), on the activity read, activity revision, activity review and record create.
11. The `{id}` path segment resolves to a `VerificationRecord` — `ERR_RECORD_NOT_FOUND` (404), on the record read, attestation and rescission.
12. The `{id}` path segment resolves to a `CorrectiveAction` — `ERR_CORRECTIVE_ACTION_NOT_FOUND` (404), on the action read, decision and closure.
13. The `{id}` path segment resolves to an `EntrySubmission` — `ERR_ENTRY_SUBMISSION_NOT_FOUND` (404), on the entry-line read.
14. Single-importer scope — `ERR_IMPORTER_SCOPE_MISMATCH` (409), on combination create, activity create, activity revision and hazard-analysis create.
15. Identifier uniqueness — `ERR_DUPLICATE_IDENTIFIER` (409), on importer create, qualified-individual create, supplier create, food create and entry-line create.
16. No existing combination with the same `supplier_id` and `food_id` whose `verification_status` is anything other than `RETIRED` — `ERR_DUPLICATE_SUPPLIER_FOOD_PAIR` (409), on combination create.
17. `expected_pair_version` matches the combination's current `version` — `ERR_STALE_PAIR_VERSION` (412), on hazard-analysis create.
18. `expected_supplier_version` matches the supplier's current `version` — `ERR_STALE_SUPPLIER_VERSION` (412), on corrective-action decision.
19. The requested from-state and action form a transition that exists in the target entity's transition list, including the case of any write against a terminal state — `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` (409), on designation, supplier evaluation, verification approval, reassessment, exemption, corrective-action decision, corrective-action closure and activity review.
20. The combination's `entry_eligibility_status` is `ELIGIBLE` — `ERR_PAIR_ENTRY_BLOCKED` (409), on entry-line create.
21. The combination has either `verification_status` equal to `VERIFICATION_APPROVED` or `exemption_status` equal to `VERIFIED` — `ERR_PAIR_VERIFICATION_INCOMPLETE` (409), on entry-line create.
22. The target hazard analysis has `status` equal to `DRAFT` — `ERR_ANALYSIS_NOT_DRAFT` (409), on hazard create and analysis approval.
23. The target activity's `status` is `PROPOSED` or `CHANGES_REQUESTED` — `ERR_ACTIVITY_NOT_REVISABLE` (409), on activity revision.
24. The target record's `record_status` is `RECEIVED` for attestation, and is `RECEIVED` or `ATTESTED` for rescission — `ERR_RECORD_NOT_ATTESTABLE` (409), on attestation and rescission.
25. The activity named by `activity_id` has `status` equal to `APPROVED` — `ERR_ACTIVITY_NOT_APPROVED` (409), on record create.
26. The acting qualified individual has `status` equal to `ACTIVE` and `training_valid_until` at or after the logical clock; on a training renewal the submitted `training_completed_at` must itself still be within 36 calendar months of the clock — `ERR_QI_DESIGNATION_INVALID` (422), on designation, supplier evaluation, hazard-analysis create, analysis approval, hazard create, activity create, activity revision, activity review, record create, attestation and verification approval. On activity review the rung is reached only when the `decision` is `APPROVE`; a `REQUEST_CHANGES` or `WITHDRAW` review is not gated on training currency at all. Because the check is a lookup of `X-User-ID` in the `QualifiedIndividual` collection, an `X-User-ID` that resolves to no such row fails this rung with `ERR_QI_DESIGNATION_INVALID` rather than any 404, and the importer-scope comparisons of rung 14 are simply skipped for such a principal.
27. The supplier's `status` is `APPROVED` or `CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED` — `ERR_SUPPLIER_NOT_APPROVED` (422), on verification approval always, and on entry-line create only when the combination's eligibility rests on full verification or on a modified-requirements exemption; a combination whose `exemption_status` is `VERIFIED` with `modified_requirements_only` false skips this check entirely at entry.
28. No corrective action on the combination's supplier has `status` equal to `IMPORT_SUSPENSION` — `ERR_IMPORT_SUSPENSION_ACTIVE` (422), on entry-line create and verification approval.
29. The supplier's `regulatory_compliance_status` permits the requested evaluation decision — `ERR_COMPLIANCE_STATUS_BLOCKS_APPROVAL` (422), on supplier evaluation.
30. The target analysis has `hazard_count` of at least 1 — `ERR_HAZARD_ANALYSIS_EMPTY` (422), on analysis approval.
31. The combination's `active_hazard_analysis_id` is not null — `ERR_HAZARD_ANALYSIS_REQUIRED` (422), on verification approval and activity create.
32. At least one activity on the combination has `status` equal to `APPROVED` — `ERR_NO_VERIFICATION_ACTIVITY` (422), on verification approval.
33. Where a severity-5 hazard requiring control is owned by the foreign supplier, an approved onsite audit exists — `ERR_ONSITE_AUDIT_REQUIRED` (422), on verification approval.
34. The summed annual frequency of approved activities reaches `required_annual_frequency` — `ERR_FREQUENCY_BELOW_REQUIRED` (422), on verification approval.
35. `adequacy_score_planned` reaches the applicable floor — `ERR_ADEQUACY_BELOW_FLOOR` (422), on verification approval.
36. `window_start` is strictly earlier than `window_end` on an activity, and `performed_start` is at or before `performed_end` on a record — `ERR_INVALID_WINDOW` (422), on activity create, activity revision and record create.
37. The record's performed interval lies inside its activity's window — `ERR_RECORD_OUTSIDE_ACTIVITY_WINDOW` (422), on record create.
38. `performed_end` is at or before the logical clock — `ERR_RECORD_IN_FUTURE` (422), on record create.
39. Onsite-audit separation with its cool-off — `ERR_AUDIT_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (409), on record create.
40. The assigned qualified individual's concurrent window capacity is not exceeded — `ERR_QI_WINDOW_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (409), on activity create and activity revision.
41. The combination's `reassessment_due_at` is null or at or after the logical clock — `ERR_REASSESSMENT_OVERDUE` (422), on verification approval.
42. `justification_text` is at least 20 characters on activity create and revision, and `methodology_note` is at least 20 characters on hazard-analysis create — `ERR_JUSTIFICATION_INSUFFICIENT` (422), on activity create, activity revision and hazard-analysis create.
43. Segregation of duties — `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (409), on activity review, activity revision and attestation.
44. The claimed `exemption_type` is applicable to the combination's food — `ERR_EXEMPTION_NOT_APPLICABLE` (422), on exemption.
45. A small-importer modified claim requires the importer's `small_importer_flag` to be true — `ERR_MODIFIED_REQUIREMENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE` (422), on exemption.
46. The identifiers transmitted at entry match the importer's registered identifiers — `ERR_IDENTIFIER_MISMATCH` (422), on entry-line create.
47. A resolved closure requires post-decision passing re-verification — `ERR_REVERIFICATION_MISSING` (422), on corrective-action closure.
48. The acting `X-User-ID` equals the combination's `designated_qi_id` — `ERR_QI_NOT_DESIGNATED_FOR_PAIR` (403), on verification approval.

Two consequences of this ladder deserve emphasis because they are easy to get backwards. On a verification approval, the training-currency check at rung 26 is evaluated *before* the designation-match check at rung 48: a lapsed qualified individual who is also not the designated one reports `ERR_QI_DESIGNATION_INVALID`, not `ERR_QI_NOT_DESIGNATED_FOR_PAIR`. And on the same operation the eight substantive gates run in the fixed order supplier approval, import suspension, hazard analysis present, activity present, onsite audit, frequency, adequacy, reassessment currency — so a combination that fails several of them reports the earliest.

### 2.12. Segregation of Duties inside the Qualified-Individual Role

FSVP requires that technical judgements be reviewed by someone other than their author, and this system enforces that separation *inside* a single role rather than between roles. All separations compare `X-User-ID` against a stored foreign key, and every violation reports `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 409).

The individual who proposed a verification activity may not review it: on review, `X-User-ID` must differ from the activity's `proposed_by_qi_id`. Symmetrically, only the proposer may revise it: on revision, `X-User-ID` must equal `proposed_by_qi_id`. The individual who filed a verification record may not attest it: on attestation, `X-User-ID` must differ from the record's `submitted_by_qi_id`. And a combination's verification approval may be signed only by the individual named in its `designated_qi_id` — that particular check is reported separately as `ERR_QI_NOT_DESIGNATED_FOR_PAIR` (HTTP 403) because it is an authorisation failure rather than a duty collision.

Regulatory judgements are separated between roles instead. Recording a supplier's `regulatory_compliance_status` and deciding or closing a corrective action, including import suspension, are reserved to `ComplianceOfficer` and are never available to the qualified individual who authored the underlying technical judgement.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up the Compliance Office and its Qualified-Individual Roster

A `ComplianceOfficer` opens the programme by registering the importer of record, supplying its `legal_name`, the `dnb_number` that will be transmitted as the importer identifier at entry, and the `unique_facility_identifier` (the UFI) that accompanies it. The `dnb_number` is a Dun & Bradstreet DUNS number and must be exactly nine ASCII digits; the `unique_facility_identifier` is between 1 and 32 characters long. Two optional fields shape the importer's regime: `small_importer_flag`, which defaults to false and marks an importer that qualifies as a very small importer eligible for modified requirements, and `record_retention_months`, which defaults to 24 and must be between 24 and 120 inclusive — it is the retention period applied to every verification record the importer ever files.

A freshly registered importer is not compliant. Its `blocked_pair_count` and `open_corrective_action_count` are both 0, its `qualified_individual_designated` is false, and its `program_status` is therefore `NON_COMPLIANT` — no qualified individual exists yet, and under the programme-status rule of §2.7 the absence of an active designation is by itself disqualifying. Reading the importer back returns those four roll-up fields live.

Naming a qualified individual is the officer's next act. The proposal carries the `importer_id` the individual serves, their `full_name`, a contact `email` unique within that importer — any non-empty string, since no address format is enforced — a `qualification_summary` documenting training and experience, and `training_completed_at`, the instant the individual completed FSVP or PCQI training. On create that instant is accepted as any valid ISO 8601 datetime, including one in the future; only the designation and renewal paths below compare it to the clock. The `qualification_summary` must be at least 20 characters — a shorter one is a request-shape violation reported as `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. An optional `concurrent_activity_capacity`, defaulting to 2 and constrained to between 1 and 6 inclusive, caps how many already-assigned execution windows may overlap a new assignment for this individual. The row is created in `status` `PROPOSED`, and `training_valid_until` is derived immediately as `training_completed_at` advanced by 36 calendar months. A computed `active_assignment_count` — the number of verification activities assigned to this individual whose `status` is `PROPOSED`, `CHANGES_REQUESTED` or `APPROVED` — starts at 0.

The designation lifecycle is driven entirely by a `ComplianceOfficer` supplying an `action` of `DESIGNATE`, `RENEW_TRAINING`, `LAPSE` or `REVOKE`, optionally with a free-text `note`. Designating a `PROPOSED` individual moves them `PROPOSED → ACTIVE`, but only if their `training_valid_until` is at or after the logical clock; stale training is refused with `ERR_QI_DESIGNATION_INVALID`. That transition recomputes the importer's `qualified_individual_designated` and then its `program_status`. Lapsing an active individual moves them `ACTIVE → LAPSED` with no precondition, recomputing the same two importer fields; critically, a lapse does *not* touch the verification activities already assigned to that individual — those rows keep whatever `status` they hold, including `APPROVED`, and only new operations are gated on currency. Renewing training on a lapsed individual moves them `LAPSED → ACTIVE` and requires a `training_completed_at` in the body: that instant must be at or before the logical clock (otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`) and, advanced by 36 calendar months, must land at or after the logical clock (otherwise `ERR_QI_DESIGNATION_INVALID`); the stored `training_completed_at` is overwritten, `training_valid_until` is recomputed, and both importer fields are recomputed. Renewing training on an already-active individual is a permitted self-loop `ACTIVE → ACTIVE` that only requires the submitted `training_completed_at` to be at or before the clock; it overwrites `training_completed_at` and recomputes `training_valid_until`, moving the expiry forward without changing status. Revocation is available from `PROPOSED`, `ACTIVE` or `LAPSED` and always lands in `REVOKED`, which is terminal; revoking from `ACTIVE` recomputes `qualified_individual_designated` as well as `program_status`, while revoking from `PROPOSED` or `LAPSED` recomputes `program_status` alone because a non-active individual never contributed to the designation flag. Any other combination of current status and requested `action` — including any write against `REVOKED` — is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.

The importer's qualified individuals can be listed with an optional `status` filter alongside `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.2. Identifying Foreign Suppliers, Foods and the Unit of Compliance

Supplier identification is an officer task. A supplier is created against an `importer_id` with the establishment's `name`, a `country_code` that is exactly two uppercase letters (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2), and a `facility_registration_number` unique within the importer. An optional `regulatory_compliance_status` records the establishment's FDA posture and defaults to `NO_ADVERSE_FINDINGS`; the permitted values are `NO_ADVERSE_FINDINGS`, `WARNING_LETTER`, `IMPORT_ALERT` and `REGISTRATION_SUSPENDED`. A new supplier starts in `status` `UNEVALUATED`, with `performance_score_provisional` and `performance_score_final` both at 100.00, `performance_sample_weight_total` at 0.00, and `open_corrective_action_count` at 0. Its `compliance_status_source_reference` and `last_evaluated_at` are both null until the first compliance-status update and the first evaluation respectively. Suppliers can be listed per importer with optional `status` and `regulatory_compliance_status` filters plus `limit` and `offset`, and a single supplier read returns both performance variants alongside its `version`.

Food identification is likewise an officer task, and a food row is immutable once written. It carries the `importer_id`, a `food_name` as it appears on the entry line, an `fda_product_code` between 3 and 16 characters long and unique within the importer, a boolean `is_raw_agricultural_commodity` marking a raw agricultural commodity, and an `intended_consumer_group` drawn from `GENERAL`, `ELDERLY`, `IMMUNOCOMPROMISED` and `INFANT`. Two optional fields describe the regulatory and culinary context: `process_control_category`, defaulting to `NONE` and drawn from `NONE`, `JUICE_HACCP`, `SEAFOOD_HACCP`, `LOW_ACID_CANNED`, `DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT` and `ALCOHOL_BEVERAGE`, which records the process-control regime already covering the food; and `ready_to_eat`, defaulting to false and true when the food is consumed without a kill step. Foods are listed per importer with an optional `process_control_category` filter plus `limit` and `offset`.

The unit of compliance is created when a `ComplianceOfficer` pairs one supplier with one food. The payload names the `importer_id`, the `supplier_id`, the `food_id` and the `designated_qi_id` — the single qualified individual who will be permitted to sign this combination's verification approval. The supplier, the food, the named individual and the body's importer must all belong to the same importer, or the create is refused with `ERR_IMPORTER_SCOPE_MISMATCH`. A combination whose `supplier_id` and `food_id` duplicate an existing combination is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_SUPPLIER_FOOD_PAIR` unless every such existing combination has `verification_status` equal to `RETIRED` — retirement frees the pairing for a fresh start.

Creation initialises all three dimensions — `verification_status` `DRAFT`, `entry_eligibility_status` `PENDING`, `exemption_status` `NONE` — and leaves `exemption_type`, `active_hazard_analysis_id`, `last_reassessed_at` and `reassessment_due_at` null, `modified_requirements_only` false, and `hazard_analysis_count`, `uncontrolled_significant_hazard_count`, `adequacy_score_planned` and `adequacy_score_realized` at their zero values. The risk fields, however, are computed immediately from an empty hazard set plus the supplier's penalty, which means a combination created against a supplier under an import alert already carries a non-zero `composite_hazard_risk`, a `risk_tier`, a `required_annual_frequency` and a `required_coverage` before any hazard has been written. The importer's `program_status` is recomputed as part of the create. Combinations can be listed with optional filters on `importer_id`, `supplier_id`, `food_id`, `designated_qi_id`, `active_hazard_analysis_id`, `verification_status`, `entry_eligibility_status`, `exemption_status` and `risk_tier`, plus `limit` and `offset`; a single read returns all three dimensions, both adequacy variants, the risk fields, the reassessment dates and the row's `version`.

Before any combination can be verified, its supplier must be evaluated. A `QualifiedIndividual` performs that evaluation, supplying a `decision` of `APPROVE` or `APPROVE_CONDITIONALLY` together with a `performance_basis_note` recording the reasoning about the establishment's performance history, compliance status and hazard controls. The acting individual must be `ACTIVE` with current training, or the evaluation is refused with `ERR_QI_DESIGNATION_INVALID`. An `UNEVALUATED` supplier moves `UNEVALUATED → APPROVED` on `APPROVE`, which additionally requires `regulatory_compliance_status` to be exactly `NO_ADVERSE_FINDINGS`; it moves `UNEVALUATED → CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED` on `APPROVE_CONDITIONALLY`, which requires `regulatory_compliance_status` to be either `NO_ADVERSE_FINDINGS` or `WARNING_LETTER`. A conditionally approved supplier can be promoted `CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED → APPROVED` by a later `APPROVE`, again only when its `regulatory_compliance_status` is exactly `NO_ADVERSE_FINDINGS`. A posture too adverse for the requested decision is refused with `ERR_COMPLIANCE_STATUS_BLOCKS_APPROVAL` — so an establishment on `IMPORT_ALERT` or `REGISTRATION_SUSPENDED` cannot be approved at all through this route. Every accepted evaluation sets `last_evaluated_at` to the logical clock and increments the supplier's `version` by one; an evaluation that lands on `APPROVED` from `UNEVALUATED` also recomputes both performance variants, which are 100.00 while no records exist. Any other combination of supplier status and decision is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.

A `ComplianceOfficer` records the establishment's evolving regulatory posture separately, supplying a new `regulatory_compliance_status` together with a `source_reference` citing where the finding came from; the citation is retained as `compliance_status_source_reference`. This write increments the supplier's `version` by one and then fires a three-hop cascade. Hop 1 recomputes, for every combination of that supplier, `composite_hazard_risk`, `risk_tier`, `required_annual_frequency`, `required_coverage`, `adequacy_score_planned` and `adequacy_score_realized` — a worsened posture raises the supplier penalty and can therefore raise the tier and the required frequency. Hop 2 examines each combination that was `VERIFICATION_APPROVED`: if, after the hop-1 recompute, either the summed `frequency_per_year` of its `APPROVED` activities is now less than its `required_annual_frequency`, or its `adequacy_score_planned` is now below its applicable adequacy floor, that combination moves `VERIFICATION_APPROVED → REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`. Hop 3 follows from hop 2: every activity on a combination that just entered `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED` and whose `status` was `APPROVED` moves `APPROVED → CHANGES_REQUESTED`, because the plan must be re-justified against the new frequency; with the approved set now empty, that combination's `adequacy_score_planned` recomputes to 0.00. The importer's `program_status` is recomputed at the end of the cascade and lands on `AT_RISK` by virtue of the combination sitting in `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`.

### 3.3. Hazard Analysis and Hazard Scoring

Hazard analysis is authored by a `QualifiedIndividual`, one analysis per attempt at a combination's hazard picture. Opening one requires the `pair_id`, the `expected_pair_version` described in §2.9, and a `methodology_note` documenting the hazard-identification methodology. The note must be at least 20 characters; unlike the qualification summary, that minimum is a semantic check reported as `ERR_JUSTIFICATION_INSUFFICIENT`, not a request-shape failure. The acting individual must be `ACTIVE` with current training. The new row starts in `status` `DRAFT` with `risk_total` 0.00, `hazard_count` 0 and `significant_hazard_count` 0; its `authored_by_qi_id` is copied from `X-User-ID`, and the accepted `expected_pair_version` is retained as `pair_version_at_create`. Opening a new analysis is a create-with-supersede: any analysis on the same combination that was still `DRAFT` moves `DRAFT → WITHDRAWN`, and no further hazards may be added to the withdrawn row. The combination's `hazard_analysis_count` is incremented — it counts every analysis ever opened on the combination, withdrawn ones included — and precisely because that counter moves, the create increments the combination's `version` by one, which is what makes a second create with the same `expected_pair_version` stale.

Individual hazards are then written into the draft, each one carrying the `hazard_analysis_id` of the analysis it belongs to. Each hazard names a `hazard_type` — `BIOLOGICAL`, `CHEMICAL_INCL_RADIOLOGICAL` or `PHYSICAL` — a `hazard_name` such as "Salmonella spp.", a `severity` and a `probability` each between 1 and 5 inclusive, and a `control_owner` drawn from `FOREIGN_SUPPLIER`, `SUPPLIER_OF_SUPPLIER`, `IMPORTER` and `CUSTOMER_DOWNSTREAM`. Hazards may only be added while the analysis is `DRAFT`; an attempt against an `APPROVED`, `SUPERSEDED` or `WITHDRAWN` analysis is refused with `ERR_ANALYSIS_NOT_DRAFT`. A hazard row is immutable once written.

Each hazard's `risk_score` is computed once, at create time, from the hazard's own attributes together with the food attributes of the parent combination. Start from the product of `severity` and `probability`. Multiply that by the exposure multiplier selected by the food's `intended_consumer_group`: 1.0 for `GENERAL`, 1.1 for `ELDERLY`, 1.25 for `IMMUNOCOMPROMISED`, and 1.4 for `INFANT`. Multiply by a raw-agricultural-commodity relief of 0.85 when the food's `is_raw_agricultural_commodity` is true *and* the `hazard_type` is not `BIOLOGICAL`, and by 1.00 otherwise. Multiply by a ready-to-eat penalty of 1.15 when the food's `ready_to_eat` is true *and* the `hazard_type` is `BIOLOGICAL`, and by 1.00 otherwise. Finally multiply by the control factor selected by `control_owner`: 1.00 for `FOREIGN_SUPPLIER`, 1.10 for `SUPPLIER_OF_SUPPLIER`, 0.70 for `IMPORTER`, and 0.55 for `CUSTOMER_DOWNSTREAM`. The resulting product is clamped so that anything below 1.00 becomes 1.00 and anything above 25.00 becomes 25.00, and the clamped value is then rounded half up to two decimal places to give `risk_score`. Note that the two food adjustments can never both apply to the same hazard: the relief is excluded for biological hazards and the penalty applies only to biological hazards, so a ready-to-eat raw agricultural commodity carrying a biological hazard receives a relief of 1.00 and a penalty of 1.15.

A hazard's `requires_control` flag is true exactly when its `risk_score` is at or above 12.00 — the boundary is inclusive, so a hazard scoring exactly 12.00 *is* a significant hazard requiring a control. A `severity` of 5 marks a SAHCODHA hazard (one reasonably likely to cause serious adverse health consequences or death), and that marking matters later at approval time.

Writing a hazard recomputes its analysis's `hazard_count` as the number of hazard rows, its `significant_hazard_count` as the number of those rows whose `requires_control` is true, and its `risk_total`. The `risk_total` is the sum of the `risk_score` values of every hazard in the analysis, whether or not they require control, rounded half up to two decimal places. Because each `risk_score` was already rounded to two decimal places when its hazard was written, this is a round-then-sum: the already-rounded per-hazard values are added, and the sum is rounded. Summing the unrounded products and rounding once at the end produces a different total and is not the specified behaviour. The hazards of one analysis can be listed with optional `hazard_type` and `requires_control` filters plus `limit` and `offset`, and the analyses themselves can be listed with optional `pair_id`, `authored_by_qi_id` and `status` filters plus `limit` and `offset`.

Approval by a `QualifiedIndividual` promotes the draft into the combination's active picture. The analysis must still be `DRAFT` (`ERR_ANALYSIS_NOT_DRAFT`), the acting individual must be `ACTIVE` with current training (`ERR_QI_DESIGNATION_INVALID`), and the analysis must contain at least one hazard row — an empty analysis documents nothing and is refused with `ERR_HAZARD_ANALYSIS_EMPTY`. An `approval_note` accompanies the request. On success the analysis moves `DRAFT → APPROVED`, its `approved_at` is set to the logical clock and its `risk_total` is recomputed. The combination's `active_hazard_analysis_id` is pointed at this analysis, and whatever analysis previously held that position moves `APPROVED → SUPERSEDED`; a superseded row accepts no further hazards but remains readable and available for inspection. The combination's `verification_status` advances `DRAFT → HAZARD_ANALYZED`, or self-loops `HAZARD_ANALYZED → HAZARD_ANALYZED` when a newer analysis replaces an earlier approved one; from any other verification state — `VERIFICATION_APPROVED`, `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`, `VERIFICATION_LAPSED` or even `RETIRED` — the approval still succeeds and still repoints `active_hazard_analysis_id`, and `verification_status` is left exactly as it was rather than being pulled back to `HAZARD_ANALYZED`. Approval also recomputes the combination's `uncontrolled_significant_hazard_count` — the number of hazards in the newly active analysis whose `requires_control` is true and whose `control_owner` is `FOREIGN_SUPPLIER` or `SUPPLIER_OF_SUPPLIER`, that is, the significant hazards the importer does not itself control — along with `composite_hazard_risk`, `risk_tier`, `required_annual_frequency`, `required_coverage` and both adequacy variants, and increments the combination's `version` by one.

The combination's composite risk is the bridge from hazards to plan requirements, and it is computed in a fixed five-step order. **Step one** derives the hazard load and the gross risk: the hazard load is the sum of the `risk_score` values of only those hazards in the active analysis whose `requires_control` is true, rounded half up to two decimal places, and it is 0.00 when `active_hazard_analysis_id` is null; the gross risk is that hazard load plus the supplier penalty selected by the supplier's `regulatory_compliance_status` — 0.00 for `NO_ADVERSE_FINDINGS`, 6.00 for `WARNING_LETTER`, 15.00 for `IMPORT_ALERT`, and 25.00 for `REGISTRATION_SUSPENDED` — again rounded half up to two decimal places. Because only controlled-significant hazards enter the hazard load, a hazard scoring below 12.00 contributes to the analysis's `risk_total` but not to the combination's composite risk. **Step two** fixes `risk_tier` from the gross risk and from nothing else: `HIGH` when gross risk is at or above 40.00, otherwise `MODERATE` when it is at or above 18.00, otherwise `LOW`. Both boundaries are inclusive. **Step three** computes a performance relief. The relief base is 0.00 for a `LOW` tier, 4.00 for `MODERATE`, and 9.00 for `HIGH`. The relief factor is 1.0 when the supplier's `performance_score_final` is at or above 85.00, 0.5 when it is at or above 70.00, and 0.0 otherwise. The relief is the relief base multiplied by the relief factor, rounded half up to two decimal places. **Step four** produces `composite_hazard_risk` as the gross risk minus the relief, floored at 0.00 so a negative result becomes 0.00, rounded half up to two decimal places. **Step five** reads the plan requirements off the tier fixed in step two and never re-derives that tier from the composite: `required_annual_frequency` is 1 for a `LOW` tier, 2 for `MODERATE`, and 4 for `HIGH`; and `required_coverage` is `composite_hazard_risk` multiplied by 1.5, rounded half up to two decimal places. The step ordering is the discriminator here — relief can push the composite back under the boundary that produced the tier, and the required frequency must still reflect the pre-relief tier.

### 3.4. Selecting Verification Activities and Justifying their Frequency

With an active hazard analysis in place, a `QualifiedIndividual` selects the verification activities that will confirm the supplier is controlling the hazards requiring control. A proposal names the `pair_id`, an `activity_type` drawn from `ONSITE_AUDIT`, `SAMPLING_AND_TESTING`, `RECORDS_REVIEW` and `OTHER_SUPPLIER_EVALUATION`, a `frequency_per_year` between 0 and 12 inclusive, a written `justification_text` for that frequency, an inclusive `window_start` and an exclusive `window_end` bounding the planned execution window, and the `assigned_qi_id` of the individual who will actually execute it. The combination must already have a non-null `active_hazard_analysis_id`, or the proposal is refused with `ERR_HAZARD_ANALYSIS_REQUIRED` — activities cannot be selected before the hazards they address are known. Beyond that pointer the proposal is not gated on the combination's `verification_status` at all: even a `RETIRED` combination that still carries an active analysis accepts a new proposal, and it is the reviewer's `APPROVE` decision, not the proposal, that a retired combination refuses. The new row starts in `status` `PROPOSED` with `revision_count` 0, `reviewed_by_qi_id` null, and `proposed_by_qi_id` copied from `X-User-ID`; its `coverage_weight` is computed immediately, and the assigned individual's `active_assignment_count` is recomputed.

Windows are checked in two ways. First, `window_start` must be strictly earlier than `window_end`; an inverted or empty window is refused with `ERR_INVALID_WINDOW`. Second, the assigned individual's concurrency is respected. Count the existing activities that share the same `assigned_qi_id`, whose `status` is `PROPOSED`, `CHANGES_REQUESTED` or `APPROVED`, and whose half-open window from `window_start` up to but excluding `window_end` overlaps the requested half-open window; the count spans every combination and every importer that individual serves, and on a revision the activity being revised is excluded from its own count. That count must be strictly less than the assigned individual's `concurrent_activity_capacity`, otherwise the request is refused with `ERR_QI_WINDOW_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. Because the windows are half-open, adjacency does not overlap: an existing window whose `window_end` equals the new `window_start` never counts, and is always allowed. The rule is an aggregate count measured against capacity, not a pairwise veto — an individual with a capacity of 2 may hold two overlapping windows and is refused only on the third.

The written justification carries real weight. A `justification_text` shorter than 20 characters is refused outright with `ERR_JUSTIFICATION_INSUFFICIENT`. A justification that clears 20 characters but is shorter than 40 characters is accepted and halves the activity's contribution to coverage. Concretely, the activity's `coverage_weight` is the type weight for its `activity_type` — 10.0 for `ONSITE_AUDIT`, 6.0 for `SAMPLING_AND_TESTING`, 3.0 for `RECORDS_REVIEW`, and 2.0 for `OTHER_SUPPLIER_EVALUATION` — multiplied by the smaller of its `frequency_per_year` and 4, multiplied by a justification factor of 1.0 when `justification_text` is at least 40 characters long and 0.5 otherwise, with the product rounded half up to two decimal places. The frequency cap of four visits a year means that raising a `frequency_per_year` from 4 to 6 changes the required-frequency arithmetic at approval time without changing the coverage weight at all.

A combination's adequacy score expresses how much of its `required_coverage` the plan actually covers, and it exists in two variants that share one formula and differ only in which activities feed it. Sum the `coverage_weight` of every activity in the variant's input set; because each `coverage_weight` is already rounded to two decimal places, this is again a round-then-sum. Divide 100.00 times that sum by the larger of the combination's `required_coverage` and 1.00 — the denominator floor of 1.00 keeps a zero-coverage combination from dividing by zero. Cap the quotient at 100.00 and round it half up to two decimal places. The **PLANNED** variant, surfaced as `adequacy_score_planned`, takes as its input set every activity on the combination whose `status` is `APPROVED`; it is recomputed whenever an activity's status or fields change, when a hazard analysis is approved, and when the supplier's compliance status or final performance score moves, and it is the variant that gates verification approval. The **REALIZED** variant, surfaced as `adequacy_score_realized`, takes as its input set the `APPROVED` activities that have at least one verification record with `record_status` `ATTESTED`; it is recomputed on record attestation and rescission and is reporting-only — it never blocks a write. Because the realized input set is a subset of the planned one, the planned score is always at least the realized score.

Review is where segregation of duties bites. A second `QualifiedIndividual` reviews a proposal with a `decision` of `APPROVE`, `REQUEST_CHANGES` or `WITHDRAW` and a `reviewer_note`; in every case the reviewer's `X-User-ID` must differ from the activity's `proposed_by_qi_id`, or the review is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. Approving moves the activity `PROPOSED → APPROVED`; it additionally requires the reviewer to be `ACTIVE` with current training and requires the parent combination not to be `RETIRED`, a stale combination being refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`. Approval records `reviewed_by_qi_id` as the reviewer's `X-User-ID`, recomputes the activity's `coverage_weight`, and recomputes both of the combination's adequacy variants. Requesting changes moves the activity `PROPOSED → CHANGES_REQUESTED` and records `reviewed_by_qi_id`, with no further precondition beyond the duty separation. Withdrawing moves the activity `PROPOSED → WITHDRAWN` or `CHANGES_REQUESTED → WITHDRAWN`, again requiring a reviewer other than the proposer, records `reviewed_by_qi_id` just as the other two decisions do, and recomputes the assigned individual's `active_assignment_count`. Every accepted review increments the activity's `version` by one. Any other pairing of current status and decision — a review of an already `APPROVED`, `WITHDRAWN` or `RETIRED` activity, for instance — is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.

Revision closes the review loop, and only the proposer may perform it: the revising individual's `X-User-ID` must equal `proposed_by_qi_id`, otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. Only a `PROPOSED` or `CHANGES_REQUESTED` activity is revisable; an `APPROVED`, `WITHDRAWN` or `RETIRED` row is frozen and reports `ERR_ACTIVITY_NOT_REVISABLE`. A revision may change any of `frequency_per_year`, `justification_text`, `window_start`, `window_end` and `assigned_qi_id`, and each supplied value is re-validated exactly as on create — window ordering, the 20-character justification minimum, and, whenever the assignee or either window bound changes, the concurrency count against the assigned individual's `concurrent_activity_capacity`. Revising a `CHANGES_REQUESTED` activity moves it `CHANGES_REQUESTED → PROPOSED` and increments `revision_count` by one, since a review loop was closed. Revising an already `PROPOSED` activity is a self-loop `PROPOSED → PROPOSED` that overwrites the fields but leaves `revision_count` untouched, because no review loop was closed. Either way the activity's `coverage_weight` is recomputed, the combination's `adequacy_score_planned` is recomputed, and the activity's `version` is incremented by one. Activities can be read individually — the response exposes `coverage_weight` and `revision_count` — or listed with optional filters on `pair_id`, `proposed_by_qi_id`, `assigned_qi_id`, `reviewed_by_qi_id`, `status` and `activity_type`, plus `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.5. Signing the Pair's Verification Approval

Verification approval is the single most heavily guarded operation in the system: it is the qualified individual's signed statement that the selected activities and their frequency justification are adequate for the hazards requiring control. Only the individual named in the combination's `designated_qi_id` may sign it, and the request carries an `approval_note`. It is available from `verification_status` `HAZARD_ANALYZED` and from `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`, both landing in `VERIFICATION_APPROVED`, with an identical precondition list in either case; requesting it from any other verification state is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.

Eight substantive gates follow, in this order. The acting individual must be `ACTIVE` with `training_valid_until` at or after the logical clock, or `ERR_QI_DESIGNATION_INVALID`. The combination's supplier must have `status` `APPROVED` or `CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED`, or `ERR_SUPPLIER_NOT_APPROVED`. No corrective action on that supplier may sit in `status` `IMPORT_SUSPENSION`, or `ERR_IMPORT_SUSPENSION_ACTIVE`. The combination's `active_hazard_analysis_id` must be non-null, or `ERR_HAZARD_ANALYSIS_REQUIRED`. At least one activity on the combination must have `status` `APPROVED`, or `ERR_NO_VERIFICATION_ACTIVITY`. If the active analysis contains any hazard whose `severity` is 5, whose `requires_control` is true, and whose `control_owner` is `FOREIGN_SUPPLIER` — a SAHCODHA hazard the supplier itself controls — then the combination must also carry an `APPROVED` activity whose `activity_type` is `ONSITE_AUDIT` and whose `frequency_per_year` is at least 1; absent that, `ERR_ONSITE_AUDIT_REQUIRED`. The sum of `frequency_per_year` across the combination's `APPROVED` activities must be at least its `required_annual_frequency`, or `ERR_FREQUENCY_BELOW_REQUIRED`. And `adequacy_score_planned` must be at least the applicable adequacy floor — 30.00 when the combination's `modified_requirements_only` is true, and 60.00 otherwise — or `ERR_ADEQUACY_BELOW_FLOOR`. Finally, the combination's `reassessment_due_at` must be either null or at or after the logical clock; a combination past its 36-month reassessment date must be reassessed before it can be approved again, and otherwise reports `ERR_REASSESSMENT_OVERDUE`. Only after all of those does the designation-match check compare `X-User-ID` with `designated_qi_id` and, on a mismatch, report `ERR_QI_NOT_DESIGNATED_FOR_PAIR`.

A successful approval sets `verification_status` to `VERIFICATION_APPROVED`, sets `last_reassessed_at` to the logical clock, recomputes `reassessment_due_at` as that instant advanced by 36 calendar months, increments the combination's `version` by one, and recomputes the importer's `program_status`. It also drives the second dimension: `entry_eligibility_status` moves `PENDING → ELIGIBLE` inside the same request. It does *not* clear a `BLOCKED` eligibility — a combination whose eligibility was blocked by a corrective action stays blocked through re-approval, and only that action's resolved closure restores it.

Reassessment is the deliberate opposite. Either a `ComplianceOfficer` or a `QualifiedIndividual` may trigger it, supplying a `trigger_reason` — required free text of at least one character, drawn from no enumeration — and an optional `note`, on a combination whose `verification_status` is `VERIFICATION_APPROVED`; the combination moves `VERIFICATION_APPROVED → REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`, `last_reassessed_at` is set to the logical clock, `reassessment_due_at` is recomputed from it, the combination's `version` is incremented by one, and the importer's `program_status` is recomputed — landing on `AT_RISK` while any combination sits in `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`. Its `entry_eligibility_status` is deliberately left exactly as it was: a voluntary reassessment of new hazard information does not, by itself, stop shipments at the border. Requesting a reassessment from any other verification state is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.

### 3.6. Verification Records, Attestation and Supplier Performance History

Evidence is filed against an approved activity. A `QualifiedIndividual` submits the documentary result of performed work — an audit report, a sampling result, a records-review memo — naming the `activity_id`, an `outcome` of `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_FINDINGS` or `FAIL`, the inclusive `performed_start` and inclusive `performed_end` of the window in which the work actually happened, an optional `findings_count` defaulting to 0 and constrained to between 0 and 50 inclusive, and a `document_reference` of at least four characters locating the retained document that satisfies the records-availability requirement — that minimum is a request-shape constraint, so a shorter reference is an `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` rather than a semantic refusal. The named activity's `status` must be `APPROVED`, or the filing is refused with `ERR_ACTIVITY_NOT_APPROVED` — evidence may not be filed against a plan step that was never approved, nor against one that has been retired.

Four temporal rules govern the filing, in ladder order. `performed_start` must be at or before `performed_end`, or `ERR_INVALID_WINDOW`. The record's closed performed interval must lie inside the activity's half-open planned window: `performed_start` at or after the activity's `window_start`, and `performed_end` strictly before the activity's `window_end`; a record that spills past the plan is refused with `ERR_RECORD_OUTSIDE_ACTIVITY_WINDOW`. `performed_end` must be at or before the logical clock, since evidence cannot be filed for work that has not happened yet, or `ERR_RECORD_IN_FUTURE`. And onsite audits on one supplier must be genuinely separated — a rule engaged only when the new record's own parent activity has `activity_type` `ONSITE_AUDIT`, so a sampling or records-review filing is never subject to it: take the new record's closed interval from `performed_start` through `performed_end`, widen it by 30 days on each side, and compare it against the closed interval of every existing non-rescinded onsite-audit record on the same supplier. If the widened interval touches or overlaps any of them the filing is refused with `ERR_AUDIT_WINDOW_CONFLICT`. Because the intervals are closed, endpoints that merely touch are a conflict, and the comparison is made pairwise against each existing record rather than against any merged union of them.

A new record starts in `record_status` `RECEIVED` with `submitted_by_qi_id` copied from `X-User-ID` and `attested_by_qi_id` null. Three derived fields are computed at once. Its `record_points` is the outcome points for its `outcome` — 100 for `PASS`, 75 for `PASS_WITH_FINDINGS`, 20 for `FAIL` — reduced by a findings penalty of 5 points per documented finding capped at 20 points in total, floored at 0 so the result never goes negative, and rounded half up to two decimal places. Its `weight` is the record type weight for the parent activity's `activity_type` — 3.0 for `ONSITE_AUDIT`, 2.0 for `SAMPLING_AND_TESTING`, 1.0 for `RECORDS_REVIEW`, and 0.5 for `OTHER_SUPPLIER_EVALUATION` — multiplied by a recency factor and rounded half up to two decimal places, where the recency factor is 1.0 when `performed_end` is within 365 days of the logical clock, 0.5 when it is within 730 days, and 0.0 beyond that. Its `retained_until` is `performed_end` advanced by the importer's `record_retention_months` in calendar months. Filing recomputes the supplier's `performance_score_provisional` and leaves `performance_score_final` untouched — a merely received record moves the advisory number and has no state effect at all.

Both performance variants share one formula over their own input set. For each record in the set, take its `record_points` and its `weight` as defined above. The weight total is the sum of the per-record weights, rounded half up to two decimal places; the weighted sum is the sum of each record's `record_points` multiplied by its `weight`, rounded half up to two decimal places. Both are round-then-sum over values that are themselves already rounded to two decimal places. If the weight total is 0.00 the score is exactly 100.00 — no evidence is not bad evidence — and otherwise the score is the weighted sum divided by the weight total, rounded half up to two decimal places. The **PROVISIONAL** variant, surfaced as `performance_score_provisional`, takes every record on the supplier's combinations whose `record_status` is `RECEIVED` or `ATTESTED`; it is recomputed on filing, attestation and rescission and never gates a state transition. The **FINAL** variant, surfaced as `performance_score_final`, takes only records whose `record_status` is `ATTESTED`; its weight total is published as the supplier's `performance_sample_weight_total`, and it is the only variant that can suspend a supplier or scale the performance relief inside the composite-risk computation. A record whose `performed_end` is more than 730 days before the clock has a `weight` of 0.00 and therefore drops out of both variants entirely, which is exactly how a weight total can return to 0.00 and a score back to 100.00.

Attestation is the moment evidence becomes consequential, and it is a six-hop cascade. A second `QualifiedIndividual` attests the record with an `attestation_note`; the record must be `RECEIVED` (otherwise `ERR_RECORD_NOT_ATTESTABLE`), the attester must be `ACTIVE` with current training (otherwise `ERR_QI_DESIGNATION_INVALID`), and the attester's `X-User-ID` must differ from the record's `submitted_by_qi_id` (otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`). **Hop 1**: the record moves `RECEIVED → ATTESTED`, `attested_by_qi_id` is set to the attester's `X-User-ID`, and the supplier's `performance_score_final` and `performance_sample_weight_total` are recomputed over attested records only; that recompute is itself a mutation of the supplier row, so every accepted attestation increments the supplier's `version` by exactly one whether or not the later hops fire. **Hop 2**: because the final score changed, every combination of that supplier recomputes `composite_hazard_risk`, `required_coverage`, `adequacy_score_planned` and `adequacy_score_realized`; and if `performance_score_final` is now strictly below 60.00, the supplier moves `APPROVED → SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW` or `CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED → SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW`, with identical consequences from either origin. The threshold is strict: a final score of exactly 60.00 leaves the supplier approved. **Hop 3**: because the supplier entered `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW`, each of its combinations whose `verification_status` was `VERIFICATION_APPROVED` moves `VERIFICATION_APPROVED → VERIFICATION_LAPSED`, and one corrective action is synthesised for each lapsed combination with `status` `OPEN`, `trigger_reason` `PERFORMANCE_SCORE_BREACH`, its `pair_id` set to that combination, and its `pre_action_blocked_pair_count` set to the importer's `blocked_pair_count` as read *before* this cascade began; the supplier's `open_corrective_action_count` is recomputed. **Hop 4**: because each of those combinations became `VERIFICATION_LAPSED`, its `entry_eligibility_status` moves `ELIGIBLE → BLOCKED`. **Hop 5**: because those combinations became `BLOCKED`, every entry line on them whose `status` is `SUBMITTED` or `ACCEPTED` moves to `HELD` with `held_reason` `PAIR_BLOCKED`. **Hop 6**: the importer's `blocked_pair_count`, `open_corrective_action_count` and `program_status` are recomputed — the programme lands on `NON_COMPLIANT` — and the supplier's `version` is incremented by one.

Rescission withdraws a filed record — wrong document, wrong combination, superseded lab result — and is a compensating action. A `QualifiedIndividual` supplies a `reason`; the record's `record_status` must be `RECEIVED` or `ATTESTED`, and a record already `RESCINDED` is refused with `ERR_RECORD_NOT_ATTESTABLE`. That status check is the only precondition: rescission is gated on neither training currency nor duty separation, so any `QualifiedIndividual` may rescind any record, including one they themselves filed or attested. Rescinding a `RECEIVED` record moves it `RECEIVED → RESCINDED` and recomputes only `performance_score_provisional`, since the record was never in the final input set; `performance_score_final` is unchanged. Rescinding an `ATTESTED` record moves it `ATTESTED → RESCINDED` and recomputes the supplier's `performance_sample_weight_total` and `performance_score_final` with that record removed, restoring both to exactly the values they held before the attestation, and recomputes `adequacy_score_realized` for the record's combination. Either way the supplier's `version` is incremented by one, and the rescinded record leaves the availability bucket. Rescission never reinstates a suspended supplier: once a suspension cascade has fired, reinstatement flows only through corrective-action closure. Records can be read individually — with an optional `as_of_iso8601` query parameter that re-evaluates the availability determination, and only that determination, at a chosen instant, leaving `weight`, `record_points` and `retained_until` evaluated against the logical clock as always — or listed with optional filters on `activity_id`, `submitted_by_qi_id`, `attested_by_qi_id`, `record_status` and `outcome`, plus `limit` and `offset`.

### 3.7. Failed Verification: Corrective Action, Import Suspension and the Compensating Close

Corrective actions are never created directly. Each one is synthesised by a suspension cascade, one per lapsed combination, and the filtered corrective-action list is therefore the only channel through which a compliance office discovers them — that list accepts optional `supplier_id`, `pair_id`, `status` and `trigger_reason` filters plus `limit` and `offset`. A synthesised action records its `supplier_id`, the `pair_id` whose verification lapsed, a `trigger_reason` drawn from `PERFORMANCE_SCORE_BREACH`, `REGULATORY_IMPORT_ALERT` and `ADEQUACY_SHORTFALL`, and the `pre_action_blocked_pair_count` snapshot. Of those three reasons only `PERFORMANCE_SCORE_BREACH` is ever actually written, because the attestation suspension of §3.6 is the one cascade that synthesises actions — the replanning cascade of §3.2 opens none; the other two values remain accepted as list-filter arguments but never appear on a row. Its `status` starts at `OPEN`, and its `decision`, `rationale`, `decided_at`, `closed_at` and `supplier_version_at_decision` are all null until the officer acts. Reading one back exposes the snapshot alongside those fields.

The decision is a fork with two branches, reserved to a `ComplianceOfficer`, who supplies a `decision` of `SUPPLIER_REMEDIATION` or `IMPORT_SUSPENSION`, a `rationale`, and the `expected_supplier_version` required by §2.9. A mismatched version is refused with `ERR_STALE_SUPPLIER_VERSION` before either branch runs. Both branches record the chosen `decision`, set `decided_at` to the logical clock, store the `rationale`, retain the accepted version as `supplier_version_at_decision`, and increment the supplier's `version` by one. Both are only available from `status` `OPEN`; a decision on an already-decided or closed action is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.

**Branch A — remediation.** The action moves `OPEN → SUPPLIER_REMEDIATION`. The supplier moves `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW → CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED`, because the officer has judged the establishment salvageable. Every combination of that supplier whose `verification_status` is `VERIFICATION_LAPSED` moves `VERIFICATION_LAPSED → REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`, and the importer's `program_status` is recomputed. Entry eligibility is deliberately untouched by this branch: no combination is blocked and no combination is unblocked, so the importer's `blocked_pair_count` does not move at all.

**Branch B — suspension.** The action moves `OPEN → IMPORT_SUSPENSION`. The supplier stays in `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW`. Every combination of that supplier whose `entry_eligibility_status` is `ELIGIBLE` moves `ELIGIBLE → BLOCKED` — every one, not only the combination named in the action's `pair_id`. Each newly blocked combination's entry lines whose `status` is `SUBMITTED` or `ACCEPTED` move to `HELD` with `held_reason` `PAIR_BLOCKED`, and the importer's `blocked_pair_count` and `program_status` are recomputed. While any corrective action on a supplier sits in `IMPORT_SUSPENSION`, that supplier's combinations can neither be verification-approved nor accept new entry lines, both refused with `ERR_IMPORT_SUSPENSION_ACTIVE`.

Closure is the join point where both branches converge, again reserved to a `ComplianceOfficer` supplying an `action` of `CLOSE_RESOLVED` or `CLOSE_DISQUALIFY` together with a `closure_note`. Closure is available from `SUPPLIER_REMEDIATION` and from `IMPORT_SUSPENSION` and is refused from any other status with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.

A resolved closure is the compensating path and it demands evidence. There must exist a verification record on an activity of the action's `pair_id` whose `record_status` is `ATTESTED`, whose `outcome` is `PASS`, and whose `performed_end` is strictly after the action's `decided_at` — re-verification performed *after* the decision, not evidence recycled from before it. Absent such a record the closure is refused with `ERR_REVERIFICATION_MISSING`. On success the action moves `SUPPLIER_REMEDIATION → CLOSED_RESOLVED` or `IMPORT_SUSPENSION → CLOSED_RESOLVED`, and `closed_at` is set to the logical clock. The compensating chain then runs identically from either branch. The action's own combination moves `VERIFICATION_LAPSED → VERIFICATION_APPROVED` or `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED → VERIFICATION_APPROVED`, with `last_reassessed_at` set to the logical clock. Its `entry_eligibility_status` moves `BLOCKED → ELIGIBLE`, and every entry line on it whose `status` is `HELD` moves back to `ACCEPTED` with `held_reason` cleared to null. The importer's `blocked_pair_count` is restored to exactly the action's `pre_action_blocked_pair_count`, but never by assignment — the counter is always recomputed as the number of the importer's combinations still sitting in `BLOCKED`, and the restoration is achieved by moving back to `ELIGIBLE` the action's own combination together with every combination that this same action's branch-B decision had blocked; when the closure followed a suspension decision, that is what returns the count exactly to the snapshot and not merely close to it. The supplier moves `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW → APPROVED`, or `CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED → APPROVED` where remediation had already restored it, but only when the action being closed was the last non-closed action on that supplier; while other actions remain open the supplier keeps its current posture. Finally the supplier's `version` is incremented by one and the importer's `open_corrective_action_count` and `program_status` are recomputed.

A disqualifying closure is the terminal path and needs no evidence at all. The action moves `IMPORT_SUSPENSION → CLOSED_SUPPLIER_DISQUALIFIED` or `SUPPLIER_REMEDIATION → CLOSED_SUPPLIER_DISQUALIFIED`, `closed_at` is set to the logical clock, and the supplier moves to `DISQUALIFIED`, which is terminal, from whatever status it currently holds — `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW` when the closure follows a suspension decision, `CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED` when it follows a remediation decision that had already restored the establishment. Its full cascade then runs: every combination of that supplier moves its `verification_status` to `RETIRED` and its `entry_eligibility_status` to `BLOCKED` — a retired combination is permanently ineligible, whether it arrived at retirement from `VERIFICATION_LAPSED` or from `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`; every activity on those combinations whose `status` was `APPROVED` moves `APPROVED → RETIRED`, the assigned individuals' `active_assignment_count` values are recomputed, and no further records may be submitted against them; and every entry line on those combinations whose `status` is `SUBMITTED`, `ACCEPTED` or `HELD` moves to `REJECTED` with `held_reason` `SUPPLIER_DISQUALIFIED`. The supplier's `version` is incremented by one, and the importer's `blocked_pair_count`, `open_corrective_action_count` and `program_status` are recomputed.

### 3.8. Exemptions and Modified Requirements

Not every imported food needs a full verification programme. Some are already covered by a regulated process control, some arrive in research quantities, some are destined for a US customer who has given written assurance, and a very small importer may operate under modified requirements. The exemption dimension captures that analysis, and either a `ComplianceOfficer` or a `QualifiedIndividual` drives it by supplying an `action` of `CLAIM`, `VERIFY`, `REJECT` or `REVOKE`, with `exemption_type` accompanying a claim; the narrative `basis_note` on a claim and `determination_note` on a determination are optional strings, so a claim or determination submitted without its note is accepted rather than refused. Every accepted action increments the combination's `version` by one, and any action that does not correspond to a permitted transition out of the current `exemption_status` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.

Claiming is the qualified individual's act and is available from `exemption_status` `NONE`, `REJECTED` and `REVOKED`, in each case landing in `CLAIMED` and recording or overwriting the combination's `exemption_type`. The permitted values are `JUICE_HACCP_CONTROL`, `SEAFOOD_HACCP_CONTROL`, `LOW_ACID_CANNED_MICROBIO`, `DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT_CGMP`, `ALCOHOL_BEVERAGE`, `RESEARCH_QUANTITY`, `US_CUSTOMER_WRITTEN_ASSURANCE` and `SMALL_IMPORTER_MODIFIED`, and each is applicable only against a matching food. `JUICE_HACCP_CONTROL` requires the food's `process_control_category` to be `JUICE_HACCP`; `SEAFOOD_HACCP_CONTROL` requires `SEAFOOD_HACCP`; `LOW_ACID_CANNED_MICROBIO` requires `LOW_ACID_CANNED`; `DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT_CGMP` requires `DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT`; `ALCOHOL_BEVERAGE` requires `ALCOHOL_BEVERAGE`; and `RESEARCH_QUANTITY` requires `process_control_category` to be exactly `NONE`, since a research or evaluation quantity carries no regulated process control of its own. `US_CUSTOMER_WRITTEN_ASSURANCE` and `SMALL_IMPORTER_MODIFIED` accept any `process_control_category`. A mismatch is refused with `ERR_EXEMPTION_NOT_APPLICABLE`. One further gate applies only to `SMALL_IMPORTER_MODIFIED`: the importer's `small_importer_flag` must be true, or the claim is refused with `ERR_MODIFIED_REQUIREMENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE`. Those two checks run in that order, so an inapplicable small-importer claim by a non-small importer reports the applicability failure first.

Verification of a claim is the officer's determination and moves `CLAIMED → VERIFIED` with no further precondition. It recomputes `modified_requirements_only`, which is true exactly when `exemption_status` is `VERIFIED` and `exemption_type` is either `US_CUSTOMER_WRITTEN_ASSURANCE` or `SMALL_IMPORTER_MODIFIED`, and false for every other verified exemption type. That flag is what lowers the combination's adequacy floor from 60.00 to 30.00 while keeping supplier-approval gating in force. Verification also moves `entry_eligibility_status` `PENDING → ELIGIBLE` in the same request while deliberately leaving `verification_status` at `DRAFT`: a verified exemption makes a combination entry-eligible with no hazard analysis and no verification plan whatsoever. The importer's `program_status` is recomputed. Rejecting a claim moves `CLAIMED → REJECTED` and leaves `entry_eligibility_status` exactly as it was — a rejected combination must reach eligibility the long way, through full verification.

Revoking a verified exemption is the officer's act and moves `VERIFIED → REVOKED`, setting `modified_requirements_only` back to false. Whether that revocation stops shipments depends entirely on whether the combination has an independent basis for eligibility. If its `verification_status` is `VERIFICATION_APPROVED`, the combination stands on its own verification and its eligibility is untouched. If it is anything else, the exemption was the only thing supporting eligibility, and a second hop follows: `entry_eligibility_status` moves `ELIGIBLE → BLOCKED`. A third hop follows from that block: every entry line on the combination whose `status` is `SUBMITTED` or `ACCEPTED` moves to `HELD` with `held_reason` `PAIR_BLOCKED`, and the importer's `blocked_pair_count` and `program_status` are recomputed.

### 3.9. Importer Identification at Entry and the Inspection Records Package

At the border, an `EntryFiler` transmits importer identification on the entry line. The submission names the `pair_id` of the supplier-food combination being imported, the CBP `entry_number` of at least six characters, the `entry_line_number` within that entry between 1 and 999 inclusive, the `dnb_number_submitted` and `ufi_submitted` actually transmitted — each merely a non-empty string here, since the nine-digit DUNS shape and the 32-character UFI bound are not re-imposed at entry and only equality against the importer's registered values matters — and the declared `arrival_date` of the shipment. The pairing of `entry_number` and `entry_line_number` is unique globally, a collision being refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_IDENTIFIER`. The filer's opaque `X-User-ID` is recorded on the row as `filed_by_user_id`.

Four compliance gates stand between a filing and an accepted line, and they run in a fixed order. First the combination's `entry_eligibility_status` must be `ELIGIBLE`; a `PENDING` or `BLOCKED` combination is refused with `ERR_PAIR_ENTRY_BLOCKED`. Second the combination must have an established compliance basis: either its `verification_status` is `VERIFICATION_APPROVED` or its `exemption_status` is `VERIFIED`; a combination with neither is refused with `ERR_PAIR_VERIFICATION_INCOMPLETE`. Third, supplier approval is required conditionally rather than universally — the supplier's `status` must be `APPROVED` or `CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED` when the combination's eligibility rests on full verification or on a modified-requirements exemption, that is when `modified_requirements_only` is true, and the check is skipped entirely for a combination whose `exemption_status` is `VERIFIED` with `modified_requirements_only` false. The skip is decided by the exemption dimension alone: a combination holding a verified non-modified exemption skips the supplier-posture check even when its `verification_status` also happens to be `VERIFICATION_APPROVED`. A failure reports `ERR_SUPPLIER_NOT_APPROVED`. Fourth, no corrective action on the combination's supplier may sit in `status` `IMPORT_SUSPENSION`, or the filing is refused with `ERR_IMPORT_SUSPENSION_ACTIVE`. Last of all, the identifiers must match: `dnb_number_submitted` must equal the importer's registered `dnb_number` and `ufi_submitted` must equal the importer's registered `unique_facility_identifier`. The comparison result is persisted as the boolean `identifier_match`, and a mismatch on either identifier is refused with `ERR_IDENTIFIER_MISMATCH`.

An entry line is created in `status` `SUBMITTED` and, once every validation has passed and `identifier_match` is true against an `ELIGIBLE` combination, it auto-advances `SUBMITTED → ACCEPTED` inside the same request — the create response already carries `ACCEPTED`, with `held_reason` null. Because every gate above must pass before the row is written, and `identifier_match` is therefore always true on a persisted row, no entry line ever comes to rest in `SUBMITTED`: the state remains part of the machine and a valid `status` filter value, but only `ACCEPTED`, `HELD` and `REJECTED` are ever observed. Thereafter the line follows its combination. When the combination's `entry_eligibility_status` becomes `BLOCKED`, whether by the attestation cascade, by a suspension decision, or by an exemption revocation, an `ACCEPTED` line moves `ACCEPTED → HELD` and a line still in `SUBMITTED` moves `SUBMITTED → HELD`, in both cases with `held_reason` set to `PAIR_BLOCKED`. When the combination's eligibility returns to `ELIGIBLE` through a resolved corrective-action closure, each `HELD` line moves `HELD → ACCEPTED` and `held_reason` is cleared to null. When the supplier is disqualified through a disqualifying closure, a line in `HELD` moves `HELD → REJECTED` and a line in `ACCEPTED` moves `ACCEPTED → REJECTED`, in both cases with `held_reason` set to `SUPPLIER_DISQUALIFIED`; `REJECTED` is terminal. Entry lines can be read individually — the response exposes `identifier_match` and `held_reason` — or listed with optional filters on `pair_id`, `status`, `entry_number` and `held_reason`, plus `limit` and `offset`.

Records retention closes the loop back to the regulator. Every verification record's `retained_until` is its `performed_end` advanced by the importer's `record_retention_months` in calendar months, and its `available_for_inspection` flag is true exactly when the record's `record_status` is not `RESCINDED` — that is, when it is `RECEIVED` or `ATTESTED` — and the evaluation instant is at or before `retained_until`. The retention boundary is inclusive: an evaluation instant exactly equal to `retained_until` still counts as available. The evaluation instant is the supplied `as_of_iso8601` when one is given and the logical clock otherwise.

An `FdaInspector` or a `ComplianceOfficer` can pull the whole picture as a records-availability package over every verification record reachable from one importer, optionally narrowed by a `record_type` filter naming the `activity_type` of the parent activity, and optionally evaluated at a supplied `as_of_iso8601` rather than at the logical clock. The package reports the `importer_id`, the importer's live `program_status` and `qualified_individual_designated`, a `generated_at` instant recording when the package was produced — read from the logical clock, not from the host's real time, and unaffected by any supplied `as_of_iso8601` — and four counts: `retained_record_count`, the number of records regardless of status; `available_for_inspection_count`, the number of non-rescinded records whose `retained_until` is at or after the evaluation instant; `expired_record_count`, the number of non-rescinded records whose `retained_until` is strictly before the evaluation instant; and `rescinded_record_count`, the number of records whose `record_status` is `RESCINDED`. A rescinded record is counted in `retained_record_count` but is neither available nor expired, so the latter three buckets are mutually disjoint and only the first is a superset of them. The package performs no writes.

## 4. Endpoint Definitions

Operations that bring a new resource into existence — registering an importer, proposing a qualified individual, identifying a supplier or a food, creating a supplier-food combination, opening a hazard analysis, adding a hazard, proposing a verification activity, filing a verification record, or transmitting an entry line — return `201 Created`. Every operation that modifies an existing resource, drives a state machine, or reads data returns `200 OK`. Corrective actions have no create endpoint at all: they are only ever synthesised by cascade.
