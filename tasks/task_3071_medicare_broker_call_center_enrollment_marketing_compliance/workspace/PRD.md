# Medicare Broker Compliance API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A licensed Medicare brokerage that runs its business through a telephone call center lives or dies on documentation. Every plan discussion must sit on top of a recorded, current Scope of Appointment; every recording must survive for the retention period CMS expects; every agent who talks about a plan must hold the licenses, carrier certifications and carrier appointments that authorize that conversation in that state. This document specifies the API that a brokerage's compliance platform must expose so that the staffing pipeline, the call floor, the enrollment desk, the commission ledger and the complaint function all read from, and write to, one consistent record.

The system is built around an **Agency** — a single call-center site with its own Annual Enrollment Period dates, its own retention windows and its own derived compliance posture. Agencies own **Agents** and **Members**; agents accumulate state licenses, carrier certifications and carrier appointments before they may work a call; members grant scopes of appointment that authorize which plan types may be discussed. Calls, enrollments, commission entries, complaint findings and coaching assignments hang off those roots. The platform's central design commitment is that a compliance failure anywhere in that chain — a missing disclaimer, a voided scope, a rapid disenrollment, a substantiated complaint, an overdue coaching assignment — must propagate upward into a posture that the whole agency feels, rather than staying local to the record that caused it.

Two audiences consume the system. The brokerage's operational roles (licensing coordinators, agents, carrier integration, the sales director, the compliance officer, coaches) drive day-to-day work through it. Compliance reviewers and carrier auditors read it to reconstruct, after the fact, exactly what an agent said, under what authority, and whether the money that followed was earned. Because of that second audience, the API is held to unusually strict standards of precision: money is tracked in cents, timestamps carry millisecond precision, every derived quantity is recomputed from standing state rather than accumulated drift, and every rejected operation reports a stable machine-readable identifier so that a downstream audit can be automated.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

The rules in this section apply to every endpoint in the API. Section 2.2 is a fixed envelope contract; the remaining subsections describe cross-cutting principles that the business workflows of Section 3 rely on.

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request must carry two headers. `X-User-ID` identifies the acting user and `X-User-Role` names their capacity. If either header is absent or malformed, or if the role they name is not one of the declared roles below, the request is refused with `ERR_MISSING_OR_INVALID_USER_CONTEXT_HEADER`. The recognized roles are spelled exactly as follows: `PlatformAdmin`, `DirectorOfSales`, `ComplianceOfficer`, `LicensingCoordinator`, `Coach`, `CarrierIntegration`, `LicensedAgent`, and `Any`.

Authorization beyond authentication is a strict allow-list: each endpoint names the roles permitted to call it, and a role that is not named is denied outright rather than silently downgraded to a read-only view. A denied call returns `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_PERMISSIONS`. The `Any` role is a read-only aggregation — it marks the GET list and GET single-resource endpoints that every authenticated role may call — and it never appears on a mutating endpoint.

Seven of the eight roles are opaque operator identities. When `X-User-Role` is `PlatformAdmin`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the same is true for `DirectorOfSales`, `ComplianceOfficer`, `LicensingCoordinator`, `Coach`, `CarrierIntegration`, and `Any`. The server may record such an identifier (for instance as the submitting agent on a record it acts upon) but does not compare it to any entity's `id`.

The eighth role is different. When `X-User-Role` is `LicensedAgent`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Agent`; ownership checks on any resource that references an `Agent` — a call session's `agent_id`, a scope of appointment's `agent_id`, an enrollment's `agent_id` — compare that resource's foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. A `LicensedAgent` therefore acts only in their own name. `LicensedAgent` is the sole role that may capture a scope of appointment, open a call, generate a plan comparison, or submit an enrollment. `PlatformAdmin` configures agencies, carriers, plans, formulary rows, pharmacy network entries and provider directory entries, sets the logical clock, and never performs an agent's call or enrollment work. `DirectorOfSales` owns the agent lifecycle on the staffing side, plus every ledger finalization, reversal, correction and carrier reconciliation; it may not substantiate complaints and may not capture a scope of appointment. `ComplianceOfficer` governs posture, adjudicates complaints, oversees coaching, reinstates agents, amends calls and voids scopes, and may not perform any money-desk operation. `LicensingCoordinator` records state licenses, carrier certifications and carrier appointments, and touches neither enrollments nor money. `Coach` works only the coaching assignments, and may not open or close a complaint. `CarrierIntegration` files appeals and records acknowledgments, effectuations, rejections and disenrollments, and may not touch ledger entries or complaint adjudication.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

List endpoints accept `limit` and `offset`. `limit` caps the number of items returned, defaults to 10, and must fall between a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 100. `offset` skips that many items and defaults to 0. Unless a specific rule says otherwise, list endpoints order their results by creation timestamp ascending. The `meta` object of a list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, and `has_more`; single-resource responses omit those four fields.

### 2.4. Staffing Authority: Licenses, Certifications and Appointments

An agent moves through a four-stage staffing pipeline before being allowed to work the phone. The pipeline states are exhaustive and case-sensitive: `PROVISIONING`, `LICENSED`, `CERTIFIED`, `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, and `TERMINATED`. Every new agent is created in `PROVISIONING` with `chargeback_tier` at 0, `compliance_risk_score` at 0.00, `substantiated_complaint_count` at 0, `coaching_flag` false, and `licensed_state_count`, `certified_carrier_count`, `appointed_carrier_count`, `live_call_count`, `active_enrollment_count` and `open_coaching_count` all at 0.

A state license is recorded against the agent with a `state_code`, `license_number`, `issued_on` and `expires_on`, and takes the agent from `PROVISIONING` to `LICENSED`. Two guards apply: the license interval must be coherent — `issued_on` must not be later than `expires_on`, else `ERR_INVALID_LICENSE_DATES` — and the agent must not already hold another license for the same `state_code` that is still unexpired at the logical clock, else `ERR_DUPLICATE_STATE_LICENSE`. A license's `license_status` is not stored but derived on every read from the logical clock against `expires_on`, and reads either `VALID` or `LAPSED`.

A certification is recorded with a `carrier_id`, `product_type` and `expires_on`, and carries the agent from `LICENSED` to `CERTIFIED`. The `product_type` must be one of `MA`, `MAPD`, `PDP` or `SNP`. A certification may only be recorded against a carrier whose `contract_type` is `CONTRACTED`; a carrier flagged `MARKETING_ONLY` cannot be appointed or enrolled through, and yields `ERR_CARRIER_NOT_CONTRACTED`. A duplicate certification — same agent, same carrier, same product type, still in status `CERTIFIED` — is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CERTIFICATION`. Certificates move through `PENDING`, `CERTIFIED`, `EXPIRED` and `REVOKED`.

A carrier appointment is requested with a `carrier_id`, `state_code` and `requested_on`, and carries the agent from `CERTIFIED` to `ACTIVE`. Recording an appointment requires three things simultaneously: a certification in status `CERTIFIED` for the requested carrier and product type, else `ERR_CERTIFICATION_NOT_FOUND`; a state license whose `license_status` is `VALID` for the requested `state_code` at the logical clock, else `ERR_INVALID_STATE_LICENSE`; and a non-`FROZEN` agency posture. Appointments themselves run through `REQUESTED`, `APPOINTED`, `REJECTED` and `TERMINATED`.

Terminating an agent unwinds their authority comprehensively: every appointment in status `APPOINTED` becomes `TERMINATED`, every certification in status `CERTIFIED` becomes `EXPIRED`, and every call in `RINGING` or `IN_PROGRESS` is dropped. A terminated agent is not permanently barred — a rehire returns them to `PROVISIONING`, resets `chargeback_tier` to 0, and clears `coaching_flag` and `open_coaching_count`. Rehire is guarded: the incoming `hire_date` must be strictly after the instant of the most recent termination, else `ERR_REHIRE_BEFORE_TERMINATION`, and every certification the agent holds must already have expired before the rehire instant, else `ERR_RECERTIFICATION_REQUIRED`.

### 2.5. Scope of Appointment: The Precondition for Every Plan Conversation

No plan type may be discussed with a beneficiary until that beneficiary has granted a Scope of Appointment to the specific agent involved. A scope is captured with a `member_id`, the capturing `agent_id`, a `capture_channel`, the `plan_types_authorized` array and the `captured_on` instant. `capture_channel` is one of `TELEPHONIC_RECORDED`, `WRITTEN` or `ELECTRONIC`. `plan_types_authorized` is drawn from `MA`, `MAPD`, `PDP` and `SNP`. The scope begins in status `CAPTURED`.

Two derived dates are stamped on the scope at capture. Its `expires_on` is the `captured_on` instant plus the owning agency's `soa_max_age_days`, which defaults to 30. Its `retained_until` is `captured_on` plus the agency's `soa_retention_days`, which defaults to 3650 — the ten-year record the compliance function is obliged to keep. A scope is usable only while all three of the following hold simultaneously: it is still in status `CAPTURED`, the logical clock is at or after `captured_on`, and the logical clock is at or before `expires_on`. The expiry boundary is inclusive — a clock reading exactly at `expires_on` is still usable. A scope that has been consumed is no longer usable: consuming a scope writes the consuming enrollment's id into `consumed_by_enrollment_id` and moves the scope to `CONSUMED`. A scope can also be voided by a compliance officer into `VOIDED`. `CAPTURED`, `CONSUMED` and `VOIDED` are the only three scope statuses, and a scope that is `CONSUMED` or `VOIDED` is permanently unusable.

Three separate rules protect the scope's integrity across the plan-comparison, call-connect and enrollment paths. First, a usable `CAPTURED` scope must exist for the member, must have been captured at or before the logical clock, and must authorize at least one plan type — an empty authorization list is treated as no scope at all; the failure identifier is `ERR_SCOPE_OF_APPOINTMENT_MISSING`. Second, the scope's `agent_id` must equal the id of the agent doing the discussing, because a scope one agent captured can never be spent by another; the failure identifier is `ERR_SCOPE_AGENT_MISMATCH`. Third, the plan type actually under discussion must appear in `plan_types_authorized`, else `ERR_PLAN_TYPE_NOT_AUTHORIZED`. A scope that is already `CONSUMED` or `VOIDED` fails with `ERR_SCOPE_OF_APPOINTMENT_CONSUMED`. These scope rules apply to plan-comparison generation, to connecting a call, and to submitting an enrollment — all three.

### 2.6. Recording, Disclaimer and Marketing-Rule Obligations

A call session records the live interval during which plan discussion occurs as the half-open range from `started_at` up to but excluding `scheduled_end_at`. Each call also carries a `recording_id`, a `disclaimer_script_code` naming the CMS disclaimer that must be played, a `disclaimer_delivered` boolean, and a `marketing_rule_set_version` identifying the marketing rule set in force for that conversation. A call's recording retention boundary is derived, not stored by hand: `recording_retained_until` is the call's `started_at` plus the owning agency's `recording_retention_days`, which defaults to 2555.

The `recording_status` of a call is derived fresh on every read against the logical clock and takes exactly one of three values. It reads `MISSING` when `recording_id` is null. It reads `EXPIRED` when the logical clock is strictly past `recording_retained_until`. Otherwise it reads `RECORDED` — a clock reading exactly at `recording_retained_until` is still `RECORDED`.

Before any plan type is discussed, two obligations must be satisfied together: a recording must exist for the call, and the CMS disclaimer identified by `disclaimer_script_code` must have been delivered. The single rejection for both failures is `ERR_DISCLAIMER_NOT_DELIVERED`. This pair of obligations is waived only when the attached scope's `capture_channel` is `WRITTEN`. The mirror-image rule is that a call must carry a `recording_id` unless its scope's `capture_channel` is `WRITTEN`, and a missing recording in any other case is `ERR_RECORDING_MISSING`. If a recording's retention window has already elapsed while the call is still the subject of an open compliance review, the reference fails with `ERR_RECORDING_EXPIRED`.

Call sessions move through `RINGING`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `DROPPED` and `AMENDED`. A completed call may be reopened for amendment without disturbing the enrollment already submitted from it, which is precisely the compliance gap the amendment exists to record. The marketing rule set version in force at the time of the call is retained on the call record so that the marketing conduct of that conversation can be judged against the rules that applied then, not against the rules in force at review time.

### 2.7. The Agency Compliance Posture

Every agency carries a derived `compliance_posture` that is recomputed from standing state rather than adjusted incrementally. The stored value is one of `OPEN`, `RESTRICTED` or `FROZEN`, and the instant it was last set is recorded in `posture_since`. The recomputation reads only the standing complaint, call, ledger and coaching state and never reads the stored posture, so a single recompute is permitted to jump an agency from `OPEN` directly to `FROZEN` when two different thresholds are crossed at once.

The recomputation evaluates its branches in order and the first match wins. It computes a strike count — the number of complaints against the agency's agents that are in status `SUBSTANTIATED` or `APPEALED` and were substantiated on or after a point 365 days before the logical clock. If that strike count reaches 5, the posture is `FROZEN`. Otherwise, if the agency's loss ratio reaches 0.20, the posture is `FROZEN`. Otherwise, if the agency's `substantiated_complaint_rate` reaches 0.05, the posture is `RESTRICTED`. Otherwise, if any coaching assignment belonging to any of the agency's agents has an overdue day count of at least 1, the posture is `RESTRICTED`. Otherwise the posture is `OPEN`.

The `substantiated_complaint_rate` is the count of complaints against the agency's agents in status `SUBSTANTIATED` or `APPEALED`, divided by the count of the agency's agents' call sessions in status `COMPLETED` or `AMENDED`, where the denominator is never allowed to fall below 1. It is stored to 6 decimal places, rounded half up. The agency's `loss_ratio` is defined separately in Section 3. The agency also maintains `open_complaint_count` — the number of complaints in status `OPEN` or `UNDER_REVIEW` — and `commission_balance_cents`, the conserved money quantity defined as the sum of `amount_cents` over every commission ledger entry whose status is `PROVISIONAL` or `FINALIZED`.

A `FROZEN` posture is the strongest stop the system has: every mutating child operation on the agency is refused with `ERR_AGENCY_FROZEN`. The refusal is checked before any other business rule, so a caller who attempts a blocked mutation while frozen learns that the agency is frozen rather than the more specific reason their request was also malformed. An `RESTRICTED` posture permits the ordinary mutating operations but is the state from which a frozen agency may be partially recovered.

### 2.8. The Commission Ledger Principle

Commission is tracked as a signed ledger keyed to cents, never as a mutable balance. Each entry is classified by `entry_type`, one of `INITIAL`, `RENEWAL`, `CHARGEBACK`, `ADJUSTMENT` or `REVERSAL`, and carries an `amount_cents` whose sign follows the classification — chargebacks and reversals are negative. Every entry carries a `period_month` in `YYYY-MM` form, a `posted_on` instant, a `status` of `PROVISIONAL`, `FINALIZED` or `REVERSED`, a `clawback_pct_applied` recording the percentage actually applied on a chargeback, an optional free-text `memo`, the `enrollment_id` it accrues against, and the `carrier_reconciliation_id` of the reconciliation period it is presented in.

The ledger's accessor is `commission_balance_cents`, and it is conserved: reversals never break the ledger's arithmetic. A reversing entry is posted against every non-reversed entry of the same enrollment rather than against a single figure, and it carries the negated sum of those entries. A subsequent reinstatement returns the entry to the live ledger — either as `PROVISIONAL` or directly as `FINALIZED` — and the compensating pair must restore the pre-reversal balance exactly; if a reverse-then-reinstate pair would leave the agency's balance different from where it started, the operation is refused with `ERR_COMMISSION_LEDGER_INVARIANT_VIOLATION`.

Carrier reconciliation is the process that closes a month. A reconciliation period is opened against a carrier with a `period_month`, a `carrier_reported_total_cents` figure and an `internal_total_variant` selector, and begins in status `OPEN`. Its `internal_total_cents` is the sum of the ledger amounts presented in that period, its `variance_cents` is the carrier-reported total minus the internal total, and `variance_variant` records which variant produced the internal total. The selector has exactly two values, `PROVISIONAL` and `FINAL`. Under `PROVISIONAL`, both `PROVISIONAL` and `FINALIZED` entries are summed; under `FINAL`, only `FINALIZED` entries are summed. `REVERSED` entries are excluded under both variants. Closing the month as `RECONCILED` requires a variance of exactly zero and is refused with `ERR_RECONCILIATION_VARIANCE_NONZERO` otherwise; disputing the month requires a non-zero variance and is refused with `ERR_RECONCILIATION_VARIANCE_ZERO` when the variance is exactly zero. A disputed period may be reopened for another round, and a period that carries no carrier-reported total may instead be closed by level recognition, which adopts the internal final-variant total as the reported total. Because level recognition fabricates a reported total, it can never be used to dismiss a dispute — it is refused outright when a carrier-reported total is already present, with `ERR_CARRIER_REPORTED_TOTAL_PRESENT`.

Corrections and adjustments are operator-initiated entries that are only admissible inside an open reconciliation period. A correction naming a carrier and `period_month` with no `OPEN` reconciliation is refused with `ERR_NO_OPEN_RECONCILIATION`.

### 2.9. Nested Resource Retrieval

Many paths in this API carry more than one identifier. Where they do, the identifiers are validated left to right in the order they appear in the path, and the request is rejected at the first segment that does not resolve, producing that segment's entity-specific not-found error. A request naming a plan under a carrier that does not exist fails on the carrier, not the plan. Identifiers supplied in a request body are validated separately, in the order the endpoint's input list declares them, and the first unresolvable one produces its entity-specific not-found error. The catalogue of not-found identifiers includes `ERR_NOT_FOUND` for a generic miss, along with `ERR_AGENCY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CARRIER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AGENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PLAN_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SCOPE_OF_APPOINTMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CALL_SESSION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_COACHING_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LEDGER_ENTRY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_RECONCILIATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_APPOINTMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CERTIFICATION_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_STATE_LICENSE_NOT_FOUND`. All of these carry HTTP 404 except `ERR_CERTIFICATION_NOT_FOUND`, which is a semantic miss rather than a path miss but is reported the same way. Note that `ERR_CARRIER_NOT_FOUND` additionally covers a carrier that resolves but is not in status `ACTIVE`.

### 2.10. Common Resource Properties

Every primary resource in the system — `Agency`, `Carrier`, `Agent`, `AgentStateLicense`, `CarrierAppointment`, `CarrierCertification`, `Member`, `ScopeOfAppointment`, `CallSession`, `Plan`, `FormularyDrug`, `ProviderDirectoryEntry`, `Enrollment`, `CommissionLedgerEntry`, `CarrierReconciliation`, `Complaint` and `CoachingAssignment` — shares a common set of system-managed fields.

- `id` is a server-minted universally unique identifier. It carries no type prefix and no embedded meaning; two resources of different types may never be distinguished by inspecting their `id` strings.
- `created_at` is an ISO 8601 timestamp in UTC with three fractional digits, recording when the resource was created.
- `updated_at` is an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, refreshed on every mutation of the resource.
- `version` is an integer that increments by one on every mutation of the resource and is used for optimistic concurrency control.

`PharmacyNetworkEntry` is the one exception: it is declared without the common field set, so it carries only `id` and `created_at`. It has no `updated_at` and no `version`, and no request may supply a version for it.

The distinction matters operationally. Any mutation that changes a derived or stored field on a versioned resource — a posture change on an agency, a status advance on an agent, a recomputed count — refreshes `updated_at` and increments `version`. Derived values are recomputed on every mutation and every read rather than being stored and patched, which is what keeps `licensed_state_count`, `certified_carrier_count`, `appointed_carrier_count`, `live_call_count`, `active_enrollment_count`, `open_coaching_count`, `coaching_flag`, `enrolled_member_count`, `enrollment_count`, `open_complaint_count`, `substantiated_complaint_count`, `substantiated_complaint_rate`, `loss_ratio`, `commission_balance_cents`, `compliance_risk_score`, `overdue_days` and `chargeback_tier` consistent with the rows beneath them.

### 2.11. Precision and Rounding

All monetary quantities in this system — `monthly_premium_cents`, `annual_drug_deductible_cents`, `monthly_copay_cents`, `amount_cents`, `commission_balance_cents`, `carrier_reported_total_cents`, `internal_total_cents`, `variance_cents`, `provisional_commission_cents`, `final_commission_cents` — are integer counts of cents, and every intermediate computation over them is carried out in exact decimal arithmetic, never in floating point.

Every derived monetary value is rounded to 2 decimal places using round-half-up, and the point at which that rounding is applied is part of the specification rather than an implementation detail: rounding happens at the outer multiplication of each term, before any summation. The two derived rates that are stored on an agency — `substantiated_complaint_rate` and `loss_ratio` — are rounded to 6 decimal places using round-half-up.

One non-monetary derived score shares the 2-decimal rule: `compliance_risk_score` on an agent. Its per-session component is rounded before the five components are summed, and the resulting sum is rounded once more. Rate configuration inputs such as `initial_commission_rate_pct`, `renewal_commission_rate_pct`, `chargeback_clawback_pct` and `cost_share_multiplier_pct` are decimal fractions and preserve the precision with which they were supplied.

### 2.12. Optimistic Concurrency Control

Two write paths in this API are protected by optimistic concurrency control, and only the protected paths accept a version field. Any endpoint not covered by the two rules below does not take an `expected_*_version` input at all.

Submitting an enrollment is protected against a stale view of the call session it is submitted from. The request must carry `expected_call_session_version`, and that value must equal the current `version` of the target call session. If the call session's version has advanced since the client last read it, the submission is refused with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` at HTTP 412. This is deliberately not a business-state rule: a single call may legitimately yield more than one plan submission, and a strictly sequential second submission is a perfectly valid piece of business — the request is refused only because the caller's view of the call session is stale.

Capturing a scope of appointment is protected the same way against a stale view of the member. The request must carry `expected_member_version`, and that value must equal the current `version` of the target member. A mismatch is refused with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` at HTTP 412. As with the enrollment path, a beneficiary granting a fresh scope after a prior one was voided is ordinary business — the second capture is not a state error, it is refused only because the caller's view of the member is stale.

Both rules are stated purely in terms of a client's observed version versus the entity's current version. Neither depends on requests overlapping in time.

### 2.13. Validation Precedence

When several rules could reject the same request, the order in which they are evaluated is fixed, and the caller is told about the earliest failure rather than an arbitrary one. The full precedence, from first-checked to last, is as follows.

Authentication precedes everything. Both `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` must be present and well formed, and the role named must be one of the declared roles; a failure is `ERR_MISSING_OR_INVALID_USER_CONTEXT_HEADER`. Immediately after authentication comes authorization: the acting role must appear in the endpoint's allow-list, and a role absent from that list is denied with `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_PERMISSIONS` rather than downgraded.

Next come existence checks. Path resource identifiers are resolved left to right in path order and the first unresolvable segment produces its entity-specific not-found error at HTTP 404. The same treatment is then given to identifiers supplied in the request body, resolved in the order the endpoint's input list declares them; the first unresolvable id produces its own entity-specific not-found error.

Business-safety checks follow existence. The agency posture gate is evaluated first among them: the agency must be `OPEN` or `RESTRICTED`, and a `FROZEN` agency produces `ERR_AGENCY_FROZEN` for every mutating child operation. Then the scope-of-appointment gate: a usable, unconsumed `CAPTURED` scope must exist with a non-empty authorization list, else `ERR_SCOPE_OF_APPOINTMENT_MISSING`. Then scope ownership, where the scope's capturing agent must be the agent acting, else `ERR_SCOPE_AGENT_MISMATCH`. Then plan-type authorization, where the plan type under discussion must appear in the scope's authorized list, else `ERR_PLAN_TYPE_NOT_AUTHORIZED`.

Temporal compliance follows safety. The disclaimer-and-recording obligation is checked next and produces `ERR_DISCLAIMER_NOT_DELIVERED`. The live-call overlap rule is checked after it: an agent's live-call interval must not overlap another of that agent's live-call intervals, and an overlap produces `ERR_AGENT_HAS_ACTIVE_CALL`. Intervals that merely touch at an endpoint — one ending exactly where the next begins — do not count as overlapping.

Then the staffing-authority check: the agent must hold an appointment in status `APPOINTED`, a license whose `license_status` is `VALID`, and a certification in status `CERTIFIED` for the carrier, product type and state of the plan being submitted; otherwise `ERR_AGENT_NOT_APPOINTED`. Then the agency-coherence check that the plan, the member and the agent all belong to the same agency, else `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_IN_AGENCY`. Then service-area compatibility, where the member's `state_code` and `county_code` must match the plan's and the plan must be in status `ACTIVE`, else `ERR_PLAN_NOT_IN_SERVICE_AREA`. Then the annual-enrollment-window rule: when the enrollment's `effective_date` falls inside the agency's Annual Enrollment Period window, the plan type must be `MA`, `MAPD` or `SNP`, and a `PDP` election inside the window is refused with `ERR_AEP_WINDOW_VIOLATION`. Then the daily capacity rule: the count of non-`REJECTED`, non-`WITHDRAWN` enrollments whose `effective_date` falls on the same UTC calendar day must be strictly below the agency's `aep_daily_submission_capacity`, and a count that has reached capacity produces `ERR_AEP_DAILY_SUBMISSION_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`.

State-machine legality is evaluated after all of the above, and reports the target entity's own `ERR_*_NOT_*` identifier at HTTP 409 — for example `ERR_CALL_NOT_IN_PROGRESS`, `ERR_CALL_NOT_COMPLETED`, `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_SUBMITTED`, `ERR_LEDGER_ENTRY_NOT_PROVISIONAL` or `ERR_AGENT_NOT_CERTIFIED`. The disenrollment preconditions follow, requiring the enrollment to be `EFFECTUATED` on the submission dimension and `ENROLLED` on the membership dimension, else `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_ENROLLED`. Then the carrier-side window rules, where an acknowledgment must land within the carrier's expected acknowledgment window and an effectuation within its expected effectuation window, else `ERR_ACK_WINDOW_EXPIRED` or `ERR_EFFECTUATION_WINDOW_EXPIRED`.

The remaining fiscal checks close out the sequence. A rapid-disenrollment classification may only be applied when the enrollment genuinely qualifies as rapid, else `ERR_NOT_RAPID_DISENROLLMENT`; and a standard classification may not be applied when the day number falls inside the rapid window, else `ERR_RAPID_DISENROLLMENT_REQUIRES_CHARGEBACK`. A correction or adjustment must name a carrier and `period_month` with an `OPEN` reconciliation, else `ERR_NO_OPEN_RECONCILIATION`. Finally, the ledger conservation invariant: a reverse-then-reinstate pair must restore the agency's `commission_balance_cents` to its exact pre-reversal value, else `ERR_COMMISSION_LEDGER_INVARIANT_VIOLATION`.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1 Standing Up the Call Center

Everything in this system hangs off an **Agency**. A `PlatformAdmin` creates one by naming the brokerage with `name`, giving the physical site its `service_center_code`, and declaring the Annual Enrollment Period with `aep_start_date` and `aep_end_date`. The `aep_end_date` is treated as the closing boundary of the window and the window is closed at that instant inclusively. The same creation call declares the site's operating limits: `aep_daily_submission_capacity` caps how many enrollment submissions may carry an `effective_date` on any single day of the enrollment period; `recording_retention_days` sets the call-recording retention obligation and defaults to 2555 days, which is seven years; `soa_retention_days` sets the scope-of-appointment retention obligation and defaults to 3650 days, which is ten years; `soa_max_age_days` defaults to 30 and sets how quickly a captured scope goes stale; `rapid_disenrollment_day_limit` defaults to 90 and sets the boundary between a rapid and a standard disenrollment; `max_live_calls_per_agent` defaults to 1 and caps how many live call intervals one agent may have open; and `max_open_coaching_per_agent` defaults to 2 and caps how many coaching assignments may be open against one agent at a time.

A newly created agency starts its derived life in a known configuration: `compliance_posture` begins at `OPEN`, `commission_balance_cents` begins at 0, `open_complaint_count` begins at 0, and `substantiated_complaint_rate` begins at 0. All of these are derived from standing state thereafter.

Configuration is not frozen at creation. An administrator may later revise the agency through an update that accepts any of `name`, `service_center_code`, `aep_start_date`, `aep_end_date`, `aep_daily_submission_capacity`, `recording_retention_days`, `soa_retention_days`, `soa_max_age_days`, `rapid_disenrollment_day_limit`, `max_live_calls_per_agent` and `max_open_coaching_per_agent`. Because the retention and staleness windows are read through the agency rather than copied onto children, changing `recording_retention_days`, `soa_retention_days` or `soa_max_age_days` re-derives every child's `recording_retained_until`, `expires_on` and `retained_until` on that child's next read. An update to an agency must carry `expected_agency_version`; the agency's `version` advances by one with every such update.

Carriers come next, and they configure the money. A carrier is created with its `legal_name`, its `naic_number`, its `contract_type`, and three rate inputs: `initial_commission_rate_pct` for the first year, `renewal_commission_rate_pct` for renewal years, and `chargeback_clawback_pct` for the fraction clawed back on a rapid disenrollment. The `contract_type` enum has exactly two values, `CONTRACTED` and `MARKETING_ONLY`, and the distinction is load-bearing — the agency may only appoint and enroll through `CONTRACTED` carriers. Two timing expectations are configured here as well: `ack_expected_within_days` defaults to 5 and gives the carrier that many days from the submission instant to acknowledge, while `effectuation_expected_within_days` defaults to 30 and gives it that many days from the acknowledgment instant to effectuate. A new carrier begins with `status` `ACTIVE`; the status enum is `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`. Carrier rates and contractual status may be revised later through an update accepting `legal_name`, `initial_commission_rate_pct`, `renewal_commission_rate_pct`, `chargeback_clawback_pct`, `ack_expected_within_days`, `effectuation_expected_within_days` and `status`, and that update must carry `expected_carrier_version`, advancing the carrier's `version` by one.

Plans are the third layer of master data. A plan is created under a carrier with its `plan_id_code` — the CMS-style identifier such as a contract-and-plan-and-segment string — its `plan_type` from the closed set `MA`, `MAPD`, `PDP` or `SNP`, its service area given as `state_code` and `county_code`, its `monthly_premium_cents` for Part C and D, its `annual_drug_deductible_cents`, and its `star_rating`, a single-decimal figure between 1.0 and 5.0. Three version identifiers pin the reference data the plan was built from: `formulary_id`, `pharmacy_network_id` and `provider_directory_id`. A new plan starts in status `ACTIVE` with `enrolled_member_count` at 0; the plan status enum is `ACTIVE` or `TERMINATED`, and only `ACTIVE` plans may be recommended or enrolled into.

Reference data is attached to a plan after the fact. Formulary rows are added one at a time with a `drug_name`, an `rxcui`, a `tier` between 1 and 5, a `monthly_copay_cents` for a standard fill, a `prior_authorization_required` flag, a `covered` flag defaulting to true, and a `quantity_limit_days` defaulting to 30 that states how many days of supply each copay covers. Pharmacy network rows are added with a `pharmacy_npi`, a `pharmacy_name`, a `network_tier` from `PREFERRED`, `STANDARD` or `OUT_OF_NETWORK`, and a `cost_share_multiplier_pct` applied to the tier copay, which is 1.0 for a preferred pharmacy. Provider directory rows are added with a `provider_npi`, a `provider_name`, a `specialty`, a `network_status` from `IN_NETWORK` or `OUT_OF_NETWORK`, an `accepting_new_patients` flag, and a `last_attested_on` instant recording when the directory row was most recently confirmed.

The logical clock governs every date comparison in the system. Before an operator sets it, the clock reads exactly the first instant of 2026-09-01 in UTC and never falls back to real wall-clock time. An administrative capability allows the clock to be set to a supplied `current_utc` instant, after which every clock-derived computed field is re-derived on its next read. The clock is the reference point for license validity, scope expiry, recording retention, acknowledgment and effectuation windows, appeal windows, coaching due dates and the posture strike window — so moving it is how an operator advances the whole compliance narrative at once.

### 3.2 Building the Licensed Agent Bench

The staffing pipeline is a chain of four gates, and an agent who has not cleared all four cannot legally work a call. The roster itself is inspectable from several angles: it can be narrowed to one agency, to a particular `status`, to agents carrying an open coaching flag, to those at or above a given `min_compliance_risk_score`, or to the agents contracted with a particular carrier, which is how a carrier's appointed book is discovered. A `PlatformAdmin` or a sales director creates the agent record under an agency with the agent's `full_name`, `national_producer_number` — unique within the agency — and `hire_date`. The agent begins in `PROVISIONING`. `licensed_state_count`, `certified_carrier_count`, `appointed_carrier_count`, `live_call_count`, `active_enrollment_count` and `open_coaching_count` all start at 0, `chargeback_tier` starts at 0, `substantiated_complaint_count` starts at 0, `coaching_flag` starts false and `compliance_risk_score` starts at 0.00.

The first gate is licensure, and it is the work of a `LicensingCoordinator`. Recording a state license supplies the `state_code`, the `license_number`, the `issued_on` and the `expires_on` instants. Two things can go wrong. If `issued_on` falls later than `expires_on`, the interval is incoherent and the request fails with `ERR_INVALID_LICENSE_DATES`. If the agent already holds another license for the same `state_code` whose `expires_on` is strictly after the current logical clock, the new one is a duplicate and fails with `ERR_DUPLICATE_STATE_LICENSE`. On success the agent advances from `PROVISIONING` to `LICENSED` and `licensed_state_count` is recomputed as the number of the agent's licenses whose `license_status` reads `VALID`. That status is never stored — it is derived on every read by comparing `expires_on` against the logical clock — so a license silently lapses the moment the clock passes its expiry.

The second gate is carrier certification, and it is where the pipeline branch becomes interesting. The coordinator supplies a `carrier_id`, a `product_type` from `MA`, `MAPD`, `PDP` or `SNP`, and an `expires_on` instant for the annual re-certification. Before the certification is granted the system confirms that the agent currently stands at `LICENSED` — an agent at any other status is refused with `ERR_AGENT_NOT_LICENSED` — that the agency is not `FROZEN`, that the carrier resolves and is `ACTIVE` (else `ERR_CARRIER_NOT_FOUND`), that the carrier's `contract_type` is `CONTRACTED` (else `ERR_CARRIER_NOT_CONTRACTED`), and that no certification in status `CERTIFIED` already exists for the same agent, carrier and product type (else `ERR_DUPLICATE_CERTIFICATION`). On success the certification is stamped `CERTIFIED` with `certified_on` set to the logical clock and the agent advances to `CERTIFIED`.

This transition carries a deliberate cascade hop. When one agent becomes certified for a carrier, every other agent of the same agency who shares a carrier appointment with this agent on that same carrier and is currently `CERTIFIED` is advanced to `ACTIVE`, and their certification for that carrier is set to `CERTIFIED`. The recomputation of `licensed_state_count`, `certified_carrier_count` and `appointed_carrier_count` propagates to every affected agent, and this propagation is a hop triggered by the certification transition rather than by the original request that named one agent. The hop is conservative: if an affected agent's recomputed carrier count is zero, that agent stays at `CERTIFIED` and no appointment is fabricated for them. Only agents whose appointment count would otherwise be positive are advanced.

The third gate is the carrier appointment, which is what actually authorizes work. The coordinator supplies the `carrier_id`, the `state_code` the appointment authorizes, and the `requested_on` instant. Three preconditions must hold together: the agent must be at `CERTIFIED`, else `ERR_AGENT_NOT_CERTIFIED`; a certification in status `CERTIFIED` must exist for the requested carrier and product type, else `ERR_CERTIFICATION_NOT_FOUND`; and a state license whose `license_status` reads `VALID` for the requested `state_code` must exist as of the logical clock, else `ERR_INVALID_STATE_LICENSE`. The agency must also not be `FROZEN`. On success the appointment is stamped `APPOINTED` with `appointed_on` set to the logical clock, the agent advances to `ACTIVE`, `appointed_carrier_count` is recomputed, and — mirroring the certification hop — the three carrier counts are recomputed for every other agent of the same agency whose certification now spans that `state_code`. Appointments themselves move through `REQUESTED`, `APPOINTED`, `REJECTED` and `TERMINATED`, and both `appointed_on` and `terminated_on` are system-stamped.

An `ACTIVE` agent is not permanently `ACTIVE`. A compliance officer may suspend the agent to `SUSPENDED`, which drops every call the agent has in `RINGING` or `IN_PROGRESS` and recomputes `live_call_count`. Recovery from `SUSPENDED` back to `ACTIVE` is a reinstatement by the compliance officer and is guarded by no fewer than six conditions evaluated together: the agent must currently be `SUSPENDED`, else `ERR_AGENT_NOT_SUSPENDED`; the agency posture must be exactly `OPEN`, else `ERR_AGENCY_NOT_OPEN`; no coaching assignment against this agent may be in status `ASSIGNED`, `IN_PROGRESS` or `OVERDUE`, else `ERR_COACHING_OPEN`; the agency must not be `FROZEN`, else `ERR_AGENCY_FROZEN`; an appointment in status `APPOINTED` must still exist for the agent, else `ERR_APPOINTMENT_MISSING`; a certification in status `CERTIFIED` must still exist, else `ERR_CERTIFICATION_NOT_FOUND`; and a license whose `license_status` reads `VALID` must still exist as of the logical clock, else `ERR_INVALID_STATE_LICENSE`. Only when all of them hold does the agent return to `ACTIVE`.

The sales director can also terminate an agent, and termination is a comprehensive unwind rather than a status flip. Terminating an agent who is already `TERMINATED` is refused with `ERR_AGENT_ALREADY_TERMINATED`, but any of `PROVISIONING`, `LICENSED`, `CERTIFIED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` is a legitimate starting point, and the `effective_on` instant is recorded. The termination sets the agent to `TERMINATED`, moves every `APPOINTED` appointment to `TERMINATED` with `terminated_on` stamped, moves every `CERTIFIED` certification to `EXPIRED`, drops every call in `RINGING` or `IN_PROGRESS`, and recomputes `live_call_count`, `certified_carrier_count` and `appointed_carrier_count`.

`TERMINATED` is listed among the terminal states, but this system makes it re-entrant through exactly one guarded door. The sales director may rehire the agent, supplying a fresh `hire_date`, and the agent re-enters the pipeline at `PROVISIONING` with `chargeback_tier` reset to 0 and `coaching_flag` and `open_coaching_count` cleared, and with the three carrier counts recomputed. The door is guarded twice: the agent must actually be `TERMINATED`, else `ERR_AGENT_NOT_TERMINATED`; the `hire_date` must fall strictly after the instant recorded by the most recent termination, else `ERR_REHIRE_BEFORE_TERMINATION`; the agency must not be `FROZEN`, else `ERR_AGENCY_FROZEN`; and every certification the agent holds must already have expired before the rehire instant, else `ERR_RECERTIFICATION_REQUIRED`. That last guard is the system's insistence that a returned agent is recertified from scratch rather than resuming a stale annual certification. Because the agent lands at `PROVISIONING`, the entire licensure-then-certification-then-appointment ladder must be climbed again.

### 3.3 Authorizing and Running the Conversation

A **Member** is the beneficiary on the other end of the phone. An agent or administrator creates the record with the member's `full_name`, their `medicare_beneficiary_id`, their `date_of_birth`, and their residence as `state_code` and `county_code`. Two lists are carried on the member and drive plan comparison later: `drug_list`, an array of entries each holding a `drug_name`, an `rxcui` and a `monthly_fills` count, and `provider_npis`, an array of the provider identifiers the beneficiary wants kept in network. Both lists may be edited after creation through an update that also requires `expected_member_version`; `enrollment_count` starts at 0 and is recomputed as enrollments accumulate.

The conversation itself cannot begin without a **Scope of Appointment**, and capturing one is exclusively an agent's act. The `LicensedAgent` supplies the `member_id`, their own `agent_id`, the `capture_channel` — `TELEPHONIC_RECORDED`, `WRITTEN` or `ELECTRONIC` — the `plan_types_authorized` array drawn from `MA`, `MAPD`, `PDP` and `SNP`, and the `captured_on` instant. The capture also requires `expected_member_version`, and a mismatch against the member's current `version` is refused with `ERR_STALE_VERSION`. The new scope starts `CAPTURED`, and two derived dates are stamped immediately: `expires_on` as `captured_on` plus the agency's `soa_max_age_days`, and `retained_until` as `captured_on` plus the agency's `soa_retention_days`. `consumed_by_enrollment_id` stays null until an enrollment consumes the scope.

Once captured, a scope is only usable while it is still `CAPTURED`, the logical clock is at or after `captured_on`, and the logical clock is at or before `expires_on`. A compliance officer may void a captured scope at any time, supplying a `reason`, which moves it to `VOIDED`. Anything that is not `CAPTURED` is unusable, and a caller who presents a `CONSUMED` or `VOIDED` scope is told `ERR_SCOPE_OF_APPOINTMENT_CONSUMED`. A scope whose authorization list is empty is treated as no scope at all and fails with `ERR_SCOPE_OF_APPOINTMENT_MISSING`, as does a scope that has passed its expiry.

With a scope in hand the agent opens a **CallSession**, attaching the scope through `scope_of_appointment_id` and naming the `agent_id` and `member_id`. The live interval is declared up front as `started_at` and `scheduled_end_at`, and the interval is half-open — it runs from `started_at` up to but not including `scheduled_end_at`. Optional fields captured at open time include the `recording_id`, the `disclaimer_script_code` naming the CMS disclaimer that must be played, the `disclaimer_delivered` flag, and the `marketing_rule_set_version` identifying the marketing rule set in force for this conversation. The call begins in `RINGING`, `recording_retained_until` is derived as `started_at` plus the agency's `recording_retention_days`, and `live_call_count` is recomputed for the agent.

Two rules can stop a call before it goes live. An agent may not run two live calls at once: if another call of the same agent is in `IN_PROGRESS` and its half-open interval contains the current logical clock, the new call is refused with `ERR_AGENT_HAS_ACTIVE_CALL`. The boundary is deliberately exclusive at the touching point — an interval that ends exactly where the next begins is not an overlap. Separately, the agency's `max_live_calls_per_agent` caps the number of live intervals an agent may have open at once, and exceeding that cap produces `ERR_LIVE_CALL_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`.

Connecting the call is the moment the conversation goes live, and it is the moment the pre-discussion obligations are enforced. The agent supplies `disclaimer_delivered`, and may supply a `plan_type_discussed`, a `recording_id`, a `disclaimer_script_code` and a `marketing_rule_set_version`. For the call to move from `RINGING` to `IN_PROGRESS` the agent must be `ACTIVE`, else `ERR_AGENT_NOT_ACTIVE`; the agency must not be `FROZEN`; the attached scope must exist and have been captured at or before the logical clock, must carry a non-empty authorization list, must belong to this same agent, and must still be `CAPTURED`; and the plan type under discussion must appear in that authorization list, else `ERR_PLAN_TYPE_NOT_AUTHORIZED`. The disclaimer-and-recording obligation is enforced here too: `disclaimer_delivered` must be true whenever a recording exists, and a recording must exist, and either failure reports `ERR_DISCLAIMER_NOT_DELIVERED` — waived, in both halves, only when the scope's `capture_channel` is `WRITTEN`. On success the call advances to `IN_PROGRESS` and `live_call_count` is recomputed.

Completing a call is what turns it into evidence. The agent may supply a final `recording_id`, a `disclaimer_delivered` value and free-text `notes`. The call must be `IN_PROGRESS`, else `ERR_CALL_NOT_IN_PROGRESS`. The same disclaimer-and-recording obligations must hold, now at completion: `disclaimer_delivered` must be true whenever `recording_id` is non-null, else `ERR_DISCLAIMER_NOT_DELIVERED`; `recording_id` must be non-null unless the scope's `capture_channel` is `WRITTEN`, else `ERR_RECORDING_MISSING`; and the member must belong to the same agency as the agent, else `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_IN_AGENCY`. On success the call becomes `COMPLETED`, `actual_end_at` takes the logical clock, `live_call_count` is recomputed, the agency's `substantiated_complaint_rate` is recomputed, the agency's posture is recomputed, and the agent's `compliance_risk_score` is recomputed.

Dropping a call ends it without completing it. A call in either `RINGING` or `IN_PROGRESS` may be dropped, with an optional `reason`, and anything else fails with `ERR_CALL_NOT_DROPPABLE`. Both an agent and a platform administrator may drop. The call becomes `DROPPED` with `actual_end_at` stamped and `live_call_count` recomputed; a drop from `IN_PROGRESS` also recomputes the agency's `substantiated_complaint_rate` and posture, while a drop from `RINGING` does not.

A completed call can be reopened for review. A compliance officer amends a `COMPLETED` call by supplying `amendment_reason`, which must be a non-empty string or the request is malformed with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`; the call must be `COMPLETED` or it fails with `ERR_CALL_NOT_COMPLETED`; and the agency must not be `FROZEN`, else `ERR_AGENCY_FROZEN`. The amendment moves the call to `AMENDED` and mutates nothing else. This is deliberate rather than an oversight: an enrollment already submitted from that call keeps the superseded `recording_id` and disclaimer flags it was submitted with, and that retained inconsistency is exactly the compliance gap the amendment records. Closing the loop is the agent's job — finalizing the amendment requires the call to be `AMENDED`, else `ERR_CALL_NOT_AMENDED`, and requires both `disclaimer_delivered` to be true and `recording_id` to be non-null. The call returns to `COMPLETED` with `actual_end_at` refreshed and the agent's `compliance_risk_score` recomputed. Separately, the recording identifier on a call may be attached or corrected after the fact with a supplied `recording_id`, by the agent or a platform administrator.

### 3.4 Comparing Plans for the Beneficiary

Once a scope authorizes the discussion, the agent may generate a ranked plan comparison. The request names the `member_id`, the `agent_id`, the `scope_of_appointment_id` and an optional `model_variant`. The candidate set is every plan whose status is `ACTIVE` and whose `state_code` and `county_code` both equal the member's; plans in status `TERMINATED` never enter the comparison. The scope, agency-coherence, service-area and staffing-authority rules all apply to this operation exactly as they do to an enrollment: a missing or unusable scope gives `ERR_SCOPE_OF_APPOINTMENT_MISSING`, a scope captured by another agent gives `ERR_SCOPE_AGENT_MISMATCH`, a plan whose type is not in the member's authorized list gives `ERR_PLAN_TYPE_NOT_AUTHORIZED`, a plan outside the member's service area or not `ACTIVE` gives `ERR_PLAN_NOT_IN_SERVICE_AREA`, an agency mismatch gives `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_IN_AGENCY`, and an agent who lacks the appointment, the valid license or the certification for the plan gives `ERR_AGENT_NOT_APPOINTED`.

Each candidate plan is costed from the member's own drug list. For every drug the member takes, the plan's formulary rows are searched for a row matching either the drug's `rxcui` or its `drug_name`. If no row matches, or if every matching row carries `covered` false, the drug is reported against that plan with a status of `NOT_COVERED` and contributes nothing to the cost. Otherwise the single covered row with the lowest `tier` is selected, with ties broken first by ascending `monthly_copay_cents` and then by ascending creation order. The number of fills the member needs per year is the member's `monthly_fills` multiplied by 30 and divided by the row's `quantity_limit_days`, with the result rounded up to the next whole fill. That fill count is multiplied by the row's `monthly_copay_cents`, and the product is multiplied by 1.05 — a days-supply ratio multiplier that reflects the difference between the plan's assumed 30-day fill and real-world dispensing. The resulting drug cost is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at that point, before any summation. Whether a drug requires prior authorization does not change its cost.

The plan's annual out-of-pocket figure is then assembled from three parts: twelve times the plan's `monthly_premium_cents`, plus its `annual_drug_deductible_cents`, plus the sum of the per-drug costs already rounded. The first two parts are exact integer cent amounts and are added unchanged.

Scoring combines five weighted components. The first is plan-type affinity: worth 1 when the plan's `plan_type` appears among the plan types authorized by the member's most recent scope that is still `CAPTURED`, and 0 otherwise. The second is a pharmacy component: worth 1 when the plan has at least one pharmacy network entry whose `network_tier` is `PREFERRED` or `STANDARD`, and 0 otherwise. The third is a provider ratio, computed as the count of the plan's provider directory rows that are `IN_NETWORK`, are accepting new patients, and whose `provider_npi` appears on the member's `provider_npis` list, divided by the count of the member's `provider_npis` where that count is never allowed to fall below 1. The fourth is a cost score, computed as 1000 minus the plan's annual out-of-pocket figure divided by 100 cents, floored at a minimum of 0 so that a very expensive plan cannot score below zero. The fifth component is the plan's own `star_rating`.

Each component is weighted before it is summed: plan-type affinity by 10, `star_rating` by 12, the pharmacy component by 8, the provider ratio by 15, and the cost score by 1.0. Each of the five weighted terms is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places, and then the five rounded terms are summed and rounded once more to 2 decimal places — that final figure is the plan's composite score. Plans are ranked by descending composite score, with ties broken first by ascending `monthly_premium_cents` and then by ascending creation order, and the top-ranked plan becomes the recommendation. The response reports the `member_id`, the `scope_of_appointment_id`, a `candidate_plan_count`, the recommended plan's `recommended_plan_id` and `recommended_plan_id_code`, its `recommended_composite_score`, and the full `ranked_plans` array.

### 3.5 Forecasting and Earning Commission

Two very different commission projections coexist in this system, and conflating them is the most expensive mistake an implementer can make here. The first is forward-looking; the second is chronological.

The forward-looking projection answers a sales question: what is the whole book worth? It is computed at enrollment creation and is also the figure behind the agency-wide book-of-business capability, which accepts an optional `window_days` and returns the `agency_id`, the `window_start`, the `window_end`, the `provisional_book_commission_cents` and a `qualifying_enrollment_count`. The projection window starts at the logical clock and runs forward 365 days. An enrollment qualifies when its agency matches, its `submission_status` is `SUBMITTED`, `ACKNOWLEDGED` or `EFFECTUATED`, and its `effective_date` falls at or after the window start and strictly before the window end. Enrollments that have been `REJECTED` or `WITHDRAWN` are excluded because their scope has been voided.

Each qualifying enrollment contributes two terms. The first is the plan's `monthly_premium_cents` multiplied by 12 and by the carrier's `initial_commission_rate_pct`. The second estimates the renewal tail within the first calendar year: the plan's `monthly_premium_cents` multiplied by a fill-month count and by the carrier's `renewal_commission_rate_pct`, where the fill-month count is 12 minus the whole calendar months from the enrollment's `effective_date` to 31 December of that same year, counting the effective month itself. Each term is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places before summation, and the two subtotals are then added. The agency-wide book total is the sum of every qualifying enrollment's two rounded terms; a single enrollment's own share of that projection is reported as its `provisional_commission_cents`, computed from the same two rounded terms without the agency-wide grouping. A method-fee multiplier of 1.06 is carried in the configuration register for this projection.

The chronological projection answers an entirely different question: what has actually been earned so far? This is the figure reconciled against carrier statements, and it is computed on effectuation, on disenrollment, and on any ledger finalization. The agency's enrollments are ordered ascending by `effective_date`, with ties broken by ascending creation order, and the pass walks them in that order maintaining a running effective date that starts empty and an accumulated figure that starts at zero. Enrollments whose `submission_status` is not `EFFECTUATED` are skipped entirely. For each enrollment that is processed, the applicable rate is the carrier's `initial_commission_rate_pct` when the running effective date is still empty or when this enrollment's `effective_date` is more than the lapse-gap threshold ahead of it — and that threshold is 0 days, so any later effective date than the previous one restarts the initial rate. Otherwise the carrier's `renewal_commission_rate_pct` applies. The running effective date is then set to this enrollment's `effective_date`.

Each processed enrollment is billed a whole number of months. The cutoff instant is the earlier of the logical clock and the enrollment's `disenrollment_date` when a disenrollment date exists, and simply the logical clock when it does not. An enrollment whose `disenrollment_status` is not `ENROLLED` is billed exactly 1 month, and an enrollment still `ENROLLED` is billed the greater of 1 and the whole number of months from its `effective_date` to that cutoff. The contributing amount is the plan's `monthly_premium_cents` multiplied by the billed month count and by the applicable rate, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places, added to the running total. The agency-level `earned_commission_cents` is that running total; the single enrollment's own contribution to the pass is reported as its `final_commission_cents`, and it is 0 for any enrollment that is not `EFFECTUATED`. An enrollment that ended as a `RAPID_DISENROLLED` always contributes exactly 1 billed month, because the plan never ran a full month.

### 3.6 Submitting an Enrollment and Tracking Two Independent Dimensions

Submitting an enrollment is the agent's act, and it is the point at which every compliance precondition in the system converges on one request. The agent supplies `call_session_id`, `member_id`, `plan_id`, their own `agent_id`, the authorizing `scope_of_appointment_id`, the requested `effective_date` and the `plan_type_discussed`, plus an optional `model_variant`, and must also supply `expected_call_session_version`. The enrollment's own `carrier_id` is not supplied — it is taken from the elected plan's carrier.

The whole precondition chain described in Section 2.13 runs against this submission, and the ordering matters as much as the rules: authentication and authorization first, then the path and body identifiers in declaration order, then the agency posture gate, then the scope gate, then scope ownership, then plan-type authorization, then the disclaimer obligation, then the live-call overlap rule, then staffing authority, then agency coherence, then service-area compatibility, then the annual-enrollment-window rule, then the daily capacity rule, then state legality, and finally the fiscal checks.

Three of those deserve spelling out in place. The daily capacity rule compares the agency's `aep_daily_submission_capacity` against the count of that agency's enrollments whose `effective_date` falls on the same UTC calendar day and whose `submission_status` is neither `REJECTED` nor `WITHDRAWN`; the comparison is strict, so a count that has merely reached capacity blocks the next submission. The annual-enrollment-window rule only bites when the requested `effective_date` lands inside the agency's enrollment window: inside that window the elected plan's type must be `MA`, `MAPD` or `SNP`, and a `PDP` election is refused outright. The staffing-authority rule requires the agent to hold, for the plan being elected, an `APPOINTED` appointment with its carrier, a license whose `license_status` reads `VALID` for the plan's `state_code`, and a `CERTIFIED` certification spanning the plan's product type.

A successful submission initializes a great deal at once. The `submission_status` begins at `SUBMITTED` and the `disenrollment_status` begins at `NOT_APPLICABLE`; `submitted_on` takes the logical clock. `disenrollment_date` stays null, `disenrolled_day_number` and `is_rapid_disenrollment` stay unset, `acknowledged_on` and `effectuated_on` stay null. `provisional_commission_cents` is computed by the forward-looking projection for this single enrollment, and `final_commission_cents` is the enrollment's contribution to the chronological pass. The member's `enrollment_count` is recomputed, an `INITIAL` ledger entry is posted in status `PROVISIONAL` for the current `period_month`, the agency's `commission_balance_cents` is recomputed, and the call session's `version` advances by one — which is precisely why the submission had to carry `expected_call_session_version`, since a client holding a stale view of the call session is refused with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` even though a call may legitimately yield a second submission.

With the submission placed, the enrollment lives on two dimensions that evolve independently and must never be collapsed into one another. The submission dimension tracks what the carrier has done with the paperwork, and its values are exactly `SUBMITTED`, `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `EFFECTUATED`, `REJECTED` and `WITHDRAWN`. The membership dimension tracks whether the beneficiary is actually on the plan, and its values are exactly `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `ENROLLED`, `RAPID_DISENROLLED` and `STANDARD_DISENROLLED`. A submission can be acknowledged while the membership dimension remains `NOT_APPLICABLE`; membership only becomes real at effectuation. Every rule in this section is written against one dimension or the other, and a request that passes one dimension's gate may still fail the other's.

The carrier-side integration identity records the carrier's response. An acknowledgment requires the enrollment to be `SUBMITTED`, else `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_SUBMITTED`, and requires the `carrier_id` supplied on the request to equal the carrier recorded on the enrollment, else `ERR_CARRIER_MISMATCH`; a carrier that resolves but is not `ACTIVE` surfaces as `ERR_CARRIER_NOT_FOUND`. The acknowledgment must also land inside the carrier's expected window — the logical clock must be at or before `submitted_on` plus the carrier's `ack_expected_within_days`, else `ERR_ACK_WINDOW_EXPIRED`. On success the submission advances to `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `acknowledged_on` takes the logical clock, a `RENEWAL` ledger entry is posted in status `PROVISIONAL`, and the agency's `commission_balance_cents` is recomputed.

Effectuation is what starts the membership. It is reachable from either `SUBMITTED` or `ACKNOWLEDGED`, and anything else fails with `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_ACKNOWLEDGED`. From `ACKNOWLEDGED`, a second window applies: the logical clock must be at or before `acknowledged_on` plus the carrier's `effectuation_expected_within_days`, else `ERR_EFFECTUATION_WINDOW_EXPIRED`. From `SUBMITTED` no acknowledgment window is evaluated, because none has been recorded. Both paths require the attached scope to still be in status `CAPTURED`, else `ERR_SCOPE_OF_APPOINTMENT_CONSUMED`. On success the submission advances to `EFFECTUATED`, `effectuated_on` takes the logical clock, the membership dimension advances to `ENROLLED`, the scope is consumed — its status moves to `CONSUMED` and the enrollment's id is written into its `consumed_by_enrollment_id` — the agent's `active_enrollment_count` and the plan's `enrolled_member_count` are recomputed, and the open reconciliation period for that carrier has its `internal_total_cents` and `variance_cents` recomputed. No new ledger entry is posted at effectuation: the `INITIAL` entry already carries its amount, and the `RENEWAL` entry posted at acknowledgment is what the carrier reconciliation will finalize. The agency's `commission_balance_cents` is recomputed.

Rejection is recorded by the same carrier-side identity and is reachable from both `SUBMITTED` and `ACKNOWLEDGED`. The reason is optional and carried as `reason`. On rejection the submission advances to `REJECTED`, the agent's `active_enrollment_count` is recomputed, and the attached scope is voided only when it had been consumed — a scope still in `CAPTURED` remains `CAPTURED` and stays available.

Withdrawal is the beneficiary's exit, performed by the agent or a compliance officer with an optional `reason`. It is reachable from `SUBMITTED`, from `ACKNOWLEDGED`, and from `REJECTED`; an enrollment already `EFFECTUATED` or already `WITHDRAWN` cannot be withdrawn, and the refusal is `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_WITHDRAWABLE`. All three paths move the submission to `WITHDRAWN`, void the scope when it had been consumed while leaving a still-`CAPTURED` scope alone, and increment the agent's `chargeback_tier` by one. The paths diverge after that, and the divergence is intentional. Withdrawal from `SUBMITTED` and withdrawal from `ACKNOWLEDGED` each additionally post a `REVERSAL` ledger entry against every non-reversed entry of that enrollment, recompute the agent's `compliance_risk_score`, and recompute the agency's `commission_balance_cents`. Withdrawal from `REJECTED` does none of those three things — it voids the scope if consumed and increments the chargeback tier, and stops there.

An enrollment may also carry operator-posted money against it. A sales director may post an operator-initiated `ADJUSTMENT` against an enrollment by supplying a `period_month`, a `memo` and a `carrier_reconciliation_id`; the entry is posted in status `PROVISIONAL`, and both the named reconciliation period's `internal_total_cents` and the agency's `commission_balance_cents` are recomputed. The same director may instead post a standalone ledger entry naming an `enrollment_id`, a `carrier_reconciliation_id`, an `entry_type`, a `period_month` and an optional `memo`, which starts `PROVISIONAL` with `posted_on` at the logical clock. A correction is a third variant of the same idea and carries an explicit `amount_cents` alongside the `enrollment_id`, `carrier_reconciliation_id`, `period_month` and `memo`.

### 3.7 Disenrollment and the Chargeback Schedule

A disenrollment is recorded by the carrier-side integration identity and is supplied with a `disenrollment_date` and a `disenrollment_reason`. It is only admissible when the enrollment is `EFFECTUATED` on the submission dimension and `ENROLLED` on the membership dimension; any other combination is refused with `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_ENROLLED`. The supplied `disenrollment_date` must fall on or after the enrollment's `effective_date`, and a date before it is a malformed request reported as `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

The system derives two figures before choosing a branch. The first is `disenrolled_day_number`, the whole number of days from `effective_date` to `disenrollment_date`, measured half-open — a disenrollment recorded on the same day as the effective date has a day number of 0. The second is `is_rapid_disenrollment`, which is true when that day number is less than or equal to the agency's `rapid_disenrollment_day_limit`, a limit that defaults to 90 days. A day number of exactly 90 with a default-configured agency is therefore still rapid, and a day number of 91 is not.

The branch is selected by which classification the caller is claiming, and each branch forbids the other's claim. A rapid disenrollment may only be recorded when the enrollment genuinely qualifies as rapid; requesting the rapid path for an enrollment whose day number exceeds the agency's limit is refused with `ERR_NOT_RAPID_DISENROLLMENT`. Conversely, a standard disenrollment may only be recorded when the day number falls outside the rapid window; requesting the standard path for an enrollment that qualifies as rapid is refused with `ERR_RAPID_DISENROLLMENT_REQUIRES_CHARGEBACK`, because the chargeback is mandatory in that case and cannot be waived by relabelling the exit.

The rapid path is the expensive one, and it touches a wide swath of the system at once. It moves `disenrollment_status` to `RAPID_DISENROLLED`, records `disenrollment_date` and `disenrollment_reason`, and increments the agent's `chargeback_tier` by one. It posts a `CHARGEBACK` ledger entry in status `FINALIZED` whose `amount_cents` is strictly negative. It recomputes the agency's `loss_ratio`. It moves every open reconciliation period of that enrollment's carrier to `DISPUTED`, because an unexpected clawback is precisely the kind of event that throws a settlement out of balance. It recomputes the plan's `enrolled_member_count` and the agent's `active_enrollment_count`, recomputes the agent's `compliance_risk_score`, and recomputes the agency's `commission_balance_cents`.

The standard path is deliberately cheaper, and the asymmetry is normative rather than an oversight. It moves `disenrollment_status` to `STANDARD_DISENROLLED` and records `disenrollment_date` and `disenrollment_reason`. It posts a `CHARGEBACK` entry in status `FINALIZED` whose `amount_cents` is exactly 0 — the entry exists so that the period's arithmetic balances, but no money moves. It recomputes the plan's `enrolled_member_count` and the agent's `active_enrollment_count` and recomputes the agent's `compliance_risk_score`. It does not increment the agent's `chargeback_tier`, does not recompute the agency's `loss_ratio`, does not dispute any reconciliation period, and does not recompute the agency's `commission_balance_cents`.

The size of a rapid-disenrollment clawback is governed by a day band schedule whose evaluation order is pinned and must not be re-associated. The first step is the band lookup, and there are exactly three bands. The first spans day 0 through day 30 inclusive and carries a base percentage of 1.00. The second spans day 31 through day 60 inclusive and carries 0.75. The third spans day 61 through day 90 inclusive and carries 0.50. Exactly one band matches any given day number, and if no band matches — that is, when the day number exceeds 90 — the enrollment is not rapid at all and no chargeback is posted.

With the band selected, the base percentage is taken from it. The second step is a hard escalation: if the agent's `chargeback_tier` has reached 7 or more, the base percentage becomes 1.00 regardless of which band was selected. The third step is a softer escalation, evaluated only when the hard escalation did not apply: if the agent's `chargeback_tier` has reached 4 or more, the base percentage becomes the base percentage of the band one step higher in the list. The 0-to-30 band has no higher band, so it stays at 1.00.

The fourth step is a complaint-driven escalation, and it is bounded by the rapid window. If at least two complaints against this agent were substantiated within the 30 days preceding the `disenrollment_date` — that is, in the window running from `disenrollment_date` minus 30 days up to and including `disenrollment_date` — and the day number is 90 or less, the base percentage becomes 1.00. The day-number bound is what keeps this escalation scoped to the rapid path.

The remaining steps compute the money. The absolute initial commission is the plan's `monthly_premium_cents` multiplied by 12 and by the carrier's `initial_commission_rate_pct`, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. The clawback amount is that absolute initial figure multiplied by the final base percentage, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places, and then negated. The percentage actually applied is recorded on the ledger entry as `clawback_pct_applied`. The agent's `chargeback_tier` is then incremented by one, and the agent's `compliance_risk_score` is recomputed.

Two variants of this calculation exist and they differ only in how many escalation steps they run. The provisional variant is the contingent reserve posted when the enrollment is acknowledged, before any disenrollment exists — it surfaces as `provisional_commission_cents` — and it uses a base percentage of 1.00 unconditionally, skipping the band lookup and all three escalation steps entirely. Consequently a plan whose absolute initial commission is 3600 carries a provisional chargeback of -3600 no matter what the eventual day band or the agent's tier turns out to be. The final variant is the one posted as the `FINALIZED` chargeback when the disenrollment is actually recorded, and it surfaces as `final_commission_cents` and as `clawback_pct_applied` on the ledger entry; it runs the band lookup and all escalations. The same absolute initial figure of 3600 therefore yields -3600 for a day-30 disenrollment by an agent at `chargeback_tier` 0, because the 0-to-30 band applies at 1.00; -2700 for a day-31 disenrollment by that same tier-0 agent, because the 31-to-60 band applies at 0.75; and -3600 again for a day-45 disenrollment by an agent whose `chargeback_tier` has reached 4, because the 31-to-60 band is escalated one step up to the 1.00 band.

The agency's `loss_ratio` is the second of the two agency-level rates, and it is computed separately from the chargeback itself. Its numerator is the count of commission ledger entries belonging to the agency that are of type `CHARGEBACK`, are in status `FINALIZED`, and carry an `amount_cents` strictly below zero — counted once per entry, never summed by magnitude. Its denominator is the count of the agency's enrollments whose `disenrollment_status` is `ENROLLED` — the live book — and that denominator is never permitted to fall below 1. The ratio is rounded half-up to 6 decimal places. Chargeback entries are attributed to the agency through their enrollment, which is what makes the ratio agency-wide.

### 3.8 The Ledger Entry Amounts and Their Posting

Every amount the ledger carries is derived from the enrollment's plan and carrier by a single rule set, and the point at which rounding is applied inside that rule set is pinned. Two annual figures are computed first. The annual initial commission is the plan's `monthly_premium_cents` multiplied by 12 and by the carrier's `initial_commission_rate_pct`, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. The annual renewal commission is the plan's `monthly_premium_cents` multiplied by 12 and by the carrier's `renewal_commission_rate_pct`, also rounded half-up to 2 decimal places.

From those two figures the five entry types are derived. An `INITIAL` entry takes the annual initial commission as its `amount_cents`, unmodified. A `RENEWAL` entry takes the annual renewal commission as its `amount_cents`. A `CHARGEBACK` entry takes the annual initial commission multiplied by the selected chargeback percentage, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places, and negated. A `REVERSAL` entry takes the negated sum of `amount_cents` over every entry of that same enrollment whose status is not `REVERSED`. An `ADJUSTMENT` entry takes the operator-supplied `amount_cents` when the request carries one — as corrections and enrollment adjustments do — and otherwise takes the plan's `monthly_premium_cents` multiplied by 1 renewal-adjustment month and by the carrier's `renewal_commission_rate_pct`, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places.

The ordering rule that governs all of this is that rounding to the cent happens at the outer multiplication, and only after that rounding is the result negated or multiplied by the chargeback percentage. The order is pinned and must not be re-associated — computing the negation first and rounding afterwards would drift the ledger by fractions of a cent against carrier statements. Reversed entries are excluded from every base, including the base of the reversal calculation itself.

An entry posted by an operator starts in status `PROVISIONAL` with `posted_on` set to the logical clock, and its posting immediately recomputes both the agency's `commission_balance_cents` and the named reconciliation period's `internal_total_cents`.

Finalizing an entry is the sales director's act and moves it from `PROVISIONAL` to `FINALIZED`, requiring the entry to currently be `PROVISIONAL` or the request fails with `ERR_LEDGER_ENTRY_NOT_PROVISIONAL`. For entries of type `INITIAL` or `RENEWAL`, finalization additionally requires the enrollment's `disenrollment_status` to be `ENROLLED`, else `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_ENROLLED` — there is no reason to recognize a commission on an enrollment whose membership has already ended. At finalization the entry's `amount_cents` is recomputed from current inputs under the final variant of the amount rule, so a rate change or a plan change that occurred since posting is picked up rather than carried forward. If the entry's recomputed amount does not match the final-variant amount the rule produces for its type, the finalization is refused with `ERR_AMOUNT_VARIANT_MISMATCH`. Finalization also recomputes the reconciliation period's `internal_total_cents` and `variance_cents` for that entry's carrier and `period_month`, recomputes the agency's `commission_balance_cents`, and recomputes the agency's `loss_ratio`.

Reversing an entry is reachable from either `PROVISIONAL` or `FINALIZED` and takes an optional `reason`; an entry that is already `REVERSED` cannot be reversed again, and the refusal is `ERR_LEDGER_ENTRY_NOT_REVERSIBLE`. Reversal moves the entry to `REVERSED` and recomputes the agency's `commission_balance_cents`, the reconciliation period's `internal_total_cents` and `variance_cents`, and the agency's `loss_ratio`. When the reversal is applied to an entry whose enrollment is simultaneously `EFFECTUATED` on the submission dimension and `ENROLLED` on the membership dimension, an `ADJUSTMENT` entry is additionally posted in status `PROVISIONAL`. Reversal also recomputes the agency's posture, and if that recomputation lands the agency at `FROZEN`, the agency-wide freeze cascade described in Section 3.9 runs against the enrollment rows still sitting in `SUBMITTED` or `ACKNOWLEDGED`.

A reversed entry re-enters the live ledger through one of two doors, both the sales director's. The first returns it to `PROVISIONAL`, and both doors require the entry to currently be `REVERSED`, else `ERR_LEDGER_ENTRY_NOT_REVERSED`; both also require the agency not to be `FROZEN`, else `ERR_AGENCY_FROZEN`. The door back to `PROVISIONAL` recomputes the agency's `commission_balance_cents` and the reconciliation period's `internal_total_cents` and `variance_cents`. The door straight back to `FINALIZED` carries an additional precondition: the enrollment's `disenrollment_status` must be `ENROLLED`, else `ERR_ENROLLMENT_NOT_ENROLLED`, since recognizing a finalized commission on a membership that has ended is not a legitimate end state. It performs the same three recomputations. Whichever door is used, the compensating pair of a reversal followed by a reinstatement must leave the agency's `commission_balance_cents` at exactly the value it held before the reversal; a pair that would leave it elsewhere is refused with `ERR_COMMISSION_LEDGER_INVARIANT_VIOLATION`.

Reconciliation periods close and reopen around these entries. A period in status `OPEN` may be closed as `RECONCILED`, which requires the computed variance to be exactly zero. It may instead be moved to `DISPUTED` by supplying a `dispute_note`, which requires the variance to be non-zero. A `DISPUTED` period may be reopened back to `OPEN`, which requires the freshly recomputed variance to be non-zero. Level recognition is the alternative closing path for a period that carries no carrier-reported total: it adopts the internal final-variant total as the carrier-reported total, moves the period to `RECONCILED`, and recomputes the agency's `loss_ratio`. Every one of these closing paths — closing, disputing, and level recognition — moves every `PROVISIONAL` entry presented in that period to `FINALIZED`, and closing and disputing each recompute the agency's `commission_balance_cents` as well. Reopening instead recomputes the period's `internal_total_cents` and `variance_cents`.

### 3.9 Complaints, Secret Shoppers and Coaching

A complaint against an agent is filed by a compliance officer or by the carrier-side integration identity. It carries an `agent_id`, a `complaint_type`, a `source`, a `severity` and a `filed_on` instant. The `complaint_type` names the alleged failure and is drawn from a closed set of seven values: `MISREPRESENTATION`, `SOA_MISSING`, `DISCLAIMER_MISSING`, `RECORDING_MISSING`, `UNAPPROVED_MARKETING`, `PLAN_MISREPRESENTATION` and `DISENROLLMENT_DELAY`. The `source` records who raised it and is one of `BENEFICIARY`, `CMS`, `CARRIER` or `SECRET_SHOPPER`. The `severity` is `LOW`, `MEDIUM` or `HIGH`. A new complaint begins `OPEN`, and the agency's `open_complaint_count` is recomputed.

Secret-shopper complaints are a distinct species because they carry the shopper's own observations. Four additional fields are used when the source is a secret shopper. The `shopper_score` is an integer between 0 and 100 and is required for that source; a value outside that range is a malformed request reported as `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. The three observation flags are `soa_requested_correctly`, recording whether the agent requested the scope of appointment; `disclaimer_played`, recording whether the disclaimer was delivered; and `approved_marketing_used`, recording whether only approved marketing material was used. These observations are what a substantiation later converts into a finding.

Complaints move through a life of their own. A compliance officer begins review, moving an `OPEN` complaint to `UNDER_REVIEW`; a complaint that is not `OPEN` cannot be reviewed and fails with `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_OPEN`. From `UNDER_REVIEW` a complaint may be substantiated or dismissed, and any other starting status fails with `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_UNDER_REVIEW`.

Substantiation is the consequential act. It requires the complaint to be `UNDER_REVIEW` and requires `filed_on` to be at or before the logical clock — a complaint filed in the future cannot be substantiated, and that failure is `ERR_COMPLAINT_FILED_IN_FUTURE`. On substantiation the complaint moves to `SUBSTANTIATED`, `substantiated_on` takes the logical clock, and a `finding_code` is stamped on it by the matching procedure described below. The agent's `substantiated_complaint_count` increases by one, the agency's `substantiated_complaint_rate` is recomputed, the agency's `open_complaint_count` is recomputed, and the agent's `compliance_risk_score` is recomputed. A coaching assignment is then created automatically with status `ASSIGNED` and `triggered_by` set to `COMPLAINT`; the agent's `open_coaching_count` increases by one and `coaching_flag` becomes true. Finally the agency's posture is recomputed, and if that recomputation crosses a threshold, the restricting or freezing cascade described later in this subsection runs.

The `finding_code` is produced by walking a fixed table from the top down and taking the first row whose condition holds. The rows, in order, are as follows. A secret-shopper complaint whose `soa_requested_correctly` is false yields `SHOPPER_SOA_FAILURE`. Failing that, a secret-shopper complaint whose `disclaimer_played` is false yields `SHOPPER_DISCLAIMER_FAILURE`. Failing that, a secret-shopper complaint whose `approved_marketing_used` is false yields `SHOPPER_MARKETING_FAILURE`. Failing that, a secret-shopper complaint whose `shopper_score` is below 70 yields `SHOPPER_LOW_SCORE`. Failing all four shopper rows, a complaint whose `complaint_type` is `SOA_MISSING` or `DISENROLLMENT_DELAY` yields `SOA_VIOLATION`. Failing that, a complaint whose type is `DISCLAIMER_MISSING` yields `DISCLAIMER_VIOLATION`. Failing that, a complaint whose type is `UNAPPROVED_MARKETING`, `MISREPRESENTATION` or `PLAN_MISREPRESENTATION` yields `MARKETING_VIOLATION`. Failing that, a complaint whose type is `RECORDING_MISSING` yields `RECORDING_VIOLATION`. Anything left over yields `GENERAL_FINDING`. The table's row order is the priority order, so a secret-shopper complaint with a low score and a failed disclaimer is a `SHOPPER_DISCLAIMER_FAILURE`, not a `SHOPPER_LOW_SCORE`.

A substantiated complaint does not stay final forever. The carrier-side integration identity may appeal it, supplying an `appeal_note`, which requires the complaint to be `SUBSTANTIATED` — the refusal for any other status is `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_SUBSTANTIATED` — and requires the appeal to land within 30 days of the substantiating instant, else `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_EXPIRED`. The same appeal is also available against a dismissal, and there it is bounded by 30 days from the dismissing instant, with the same `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_EXPIRED` failure. Either route moves the complaint to `APPEALED` and recomputes the agent's `compliance_risk_score`.

From `APPEALED` the complaint can go three ways. A compliance officer may remand it back to `UNDER_REVIEW`, which recomputes the agency's `open_complaint_count`; a complaint that is not `APPEALED` fails with `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_APPEALED`. A compliance officer may dismiss an appealed complaint, which is the reversal path — it requires `APPEALED`, and it requires the agent's `substantiated_complaint_count` to be at least 1, else `ERR_NO_SUBSTANTIATION_TO_REVERSE`, because there is nothing left to reverse. On that path the complaint moves to `DISMISSED`, the agent's `substantiated_complaint_count` decreases by one, and the agency's `substantiated_complaint_rate`, the agent's `compliance_risk_score` and the agency's posture are all recomputed. Or the complaint may be closed.

Closing is available from `APPEALED`, from `DISMISSED` and from `SUBSTANTIATED`, and from nowhere else; any other status fails with `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_CLOSABLE`. Closing moves the complaint to `CLOSED`, recomputes the agency's `open_complaint_count`, and — this is the coaching coupling — moves every coaching assignment tied to that complaint that is in status `ASSIGNED`, `IN_PROGRESS` or `OVERDUE` to `COMPLETED` with `completed_on` set to the logical clock. The agent's `open_coaching_count` is recomputed, and `coaching_flag` returns to false when that count reaches zero. The agent's `compliance_risk_score` and the agency's posture are then recomputed.

Dismissal from `UNDER_REVIEW` follows the same shape as the other dismissals but is available directly, moving the complaint to `DISMISSED` and recomputing the agency's `open_complaint_count` and the agency's posture.

Coaching assignments are the field intervention that complaint findings produce, and they can also be raised by hand. A coach or a compliance officer creates one with an `agent_id`, an optional `complaint_id` and a `coaching_type` from the closed set `SOA_RETRAINING`, `DISCLAIMER_RETRAINING`, `MARKETING_RULES`, `PLAN_DETAIL_ACCURACY` and `RECORDING_RETENTION`. The `triggered_by` classification is derived rather than supplied: it reads `COMPLAINT` when a `complaint_id` is present, and `MANUAL` when it is absent. A new assignment starts `ASSIGNED`, takes `assigned_on` from the logical clock, has its `due_on` derived as `assigned_on` plus 14 days, and leaves `completed_on` null. Creating one recomputes the agent's `open_coaching_count`, sets `coaching_flag` true, and recomputes `compliance_risk_score`. The enum also admits `SECRET_SHOPPER` as a triggering classification, alongside `COMPLAINT` and `MANUAL`.

An assignment then walks a short pipeline. A coach starts it, moving `ASSIGNED` to `IN_PROGRESS`; an assignment that is not `ASSIGNED` cannot be started, and the refusal is `ERR_COACHING_NOT_ASSIGNED`. Completion is reachable from `IN_PROGRESS` and also from `OVERDUE`, and any other status fails with `ERR_COACHING_NOT_IN_PROGRESS`. Completion moves the assignment to `COMPLETED`, stamps `completed_on` from the logical clock, and accepts an optional `notes` field for the coach's record. It recomputes the agent's `open_coaching_count` and clears `coaching_flag` once that count reaches zero, recomputes the agent's `compliance_risk_score`, and recomputes the agency's posture. The status enum is `ASSIGNED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED` and `OVERDUE`.

Overdue is the system's own doing, not an operator's. An assignment's `overdue_days` is derived on every read and on every mutation of its agent: the `due_on` date is `assigned_on` plus 14 days, and if the logical clock is at or before `due_on` the figure is 0, while a clock strictly past `due_on` yields the whole number of days from `due_on` to the clock. The boundary is inclusive on the `due_on` side — a clock reading exactly `due_on` is not yet overdue. When that derived figure recomputes to 1 or more on an assignment still in status `ASSIGNED` or `IN_PROGRESS`, the system automatically moves it to `OVERDUE` and recomputes both the agent's `compliance_risk_score` and the agency's posture. The agency's `max_open_coaching_per_agent` setting caps how many assignments may be open against one agent at once, defaulting to 2.

### 3.10 The Compliance Risk Score

`compliance_risk_score` is the system's single-number read on how much trouble an agent is in, and it is assembled from exactly five components, all scoped to that one agent's own rows. The per-session component walks every one of the agent's call sessions whose status is `COMPLETED` or `DROPPED` and, for each, sums three conditional penalties: 3.0 points when the call has no `recording_id` or its derived `recording_status` reads `MISSING`; 7.0 points when `disclaimer_delivered` is false; and 2.0 points when the call has no `scope_of_appointment_id` or the scope it attaches to has an empty `plan_types_authorized` list. That per-session subtotal is rounded to 2 decimal places. Calls still in `RINGING` or `IN_PROGRESS` contribute nothing, since the conduct is not yet finished.

The remaining four components are simpler. The enrollment component adds 10.0 for each of the agent's enrollments whose `disenrollment_status` is `RAPID_DISENROLLED`. The complaint component adds, for each of the agent's complaints that is in status `SUBSTANTIATED` or `APPEALED` and was substantiated within the trailing 365 days, a severity-derived weight: 8.0 points for `LOW`, 16.0 for `MEDIUM` and 24.0 for `HIGH`. The coaching component adds 5.0 for each of the agent's coaching assignments in status `ASSIGNED`, `IN_PROGRESS` or `OVERDUE`. The overdue component adds 3.0 multiplied by the sum of the positive `overdue_days` figures across the agent's assignments in status `OVERDUE`.

The five component subtotals are summed in the order listed above and the result is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places, giving the agent's `compliance_risk_score`. The score is recomputed on call completion and call drop, on enrollment disenrollment, on complaint substantiation and on appeal reversal, on coaching assignment creation and completion, and on every overdue recomputation.

The agency's derived posture is the aggregate read, and it is recomputed on complaint substantiation, on appeal reversal, on coaching creation and closure, on call completion and drop, on enrollment withdrawal, and on ledger reversal. Its strike count, its rate threshold, its loss-ratio threshold and its overdue-coaching trigger, along with the branch order in which they are evaluated, are specified in Section 2.7.

### 3.11 What a Posture Change Sets Off

A posture recomputation that actually changes the stored posture does not merely relabel the agency; it runs a cascade, and the cascades are level-specific.

An agency that moves from `OPEN` to `RESTRICTED` records the new `compliance_posture`, stamps `posture_since` from the logical clock, and then acts on its people and its live calls. Every agent of the agency in status `ACTIVE` is moved to `SUSPENDED`. Every call session belonging to any of the agency's agents that is in status `RINGING` or `IN_PROGRESS` is moved to `DROPPED` with `actual_end_at` set to the logical clock, and `live_call_count` is recomputed for every agent whose call was dropped. The agency's `substantiated_complaint_rate` is recomputed. Finally, if that recomputed rate has reached 0.05, the agency's `loss_ratio` is recomputed as well.

A move from `OPEN` straight to `FROZEN` is the harshest, and it is permitted in a single recompute precisely because the recomputation reads only standing state and never the stored posture. It records the new posture and `posture_since`, suspends every `ACTIVE` agent of the agency, and drops every `RINGING` or `IN_PROGRESS` call of every one of the agency's agents with `actual_end_at` stamped at the logical clock. Beyond the restricting cascade it also withdraws every enrollment of the agency whose `submission_status` is `SUBMITTED` or `ACKNOWLEDGED`. For each withdrawn enrollment it posts a `REVERSAL` ledger entry against every non-reversed entry of that enrollment, and then recomputes the agency's `commission_balance_cents`. It recomputes the agency's `substantiated_complaint_rate` and its `loss_ratio` — and the freezing loss-ratio threshold is 0.20 rather than the restricting rate threshold. Finally it recomputes `compliance_risk_score` for every agent of the agency.

A move from `RESTRICTED` to `FROZEN` — which is what happens when an agency reaches 5 or more substantiated complaints within the trailing 365 days while already restricted — records the new posture and `posture_since`, withdraws every enrollment of the agency in `SUBMITTED` or `ACKNOWLEDGED`, and posts a `REVERSAL` against each withdrawn enrollment's non-reversed entries with an `amount_cents` equal to the negated sum of those entries. It moves every reconciliation period of the agency's carriers that is in status `DISPUTED` back to `OPEN` and recomputes their `internal_total_cents`. It recomputes the agency's `loss_ratio` against the freezing threshold of 0.20, and recomputes `compliance_risk_score` for every agent of the agency. Unlike the `OPEN`-to-`FROZEN` jump, it does not suspend agents or drop calls, because the restricting cascade already suspended them when the agency first reached `RESTRICTED`.

Recovery is the compliance officer's act and steps the posture back exactly one level at a time. The request names the level being stepped toward as `target_posture`, and only the immediate next level is accepted. A reset toward `RESTRICTED` requires the agency to actually be `FROZEN`, else `ERR_AGENCY_NOT_FROZEN`; a reset toward `OPEN` requires the agency to actually be `RESTRICTED`, else `ERR_AGENCY_NOT_RESTRICTED`. The two directions are guarded by different conditions, and neither shares the other's. A reset toward `RESTRICTED` requires the agency's `open_complaint_count` to be 0, else `ERR_SUBSTANTIATED_COMPLAINT_OPEN`, and requires that no coaching assignment belonging to any of the agency's agents be in status `ASSIGNED`, `IN_PROGRESS` or `OVERDUE`, else `ERR_COACHING_OVERDUE_BLOCKS_RESET`. It lands the agency at `RESTRICTED` and does not reinstate anyone — the agents the freeze suspended remain `SUSPENDED`, because landing at `RESTRICTED` is not the same as being clean. A reset toward `OPEN` requires instead that the agency's `substantiated_complaint_rate` be strictly below 0.05, else `ERR_SUBSTANTIATED_COMPLAINT_OPEN`, and requires the same absence of open coaching, else `ERR_COACHING_OVERDUE_BLOCKS_RESET`. Only on that leg does the agency return to `OPEN`, and only then are its suspended agents reinstated: every agent of the agency in status `SUSPENDED` who has no open coaching assignment is moved back to `ACTIVE`. Both legs stamp `posture_since` from the logical clock. An operator may also force a posture recomputation for an agency directly, which is the capability used after a bulk data load; it recomputes the posture and runs whichever cascade the recomputation makes due.
