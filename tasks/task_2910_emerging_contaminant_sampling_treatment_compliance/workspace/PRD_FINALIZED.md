# Emerging Contaminant Compliance API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Drinking water utilities entering the era of regulated emerging contaminants face a problem that no single existing system solves: the same handful of nanogram-per-litre numbers must simultaneously satisfy an analytical chain of custody, a regulatory running-average calculation, a public-notification clock, an engineering technology evaluation, and a capital finance ledger. A laboratory result that arrives one hour past its holding time is worthless to all five. A sample invalidated three weeks after the fact must silently unwind a running average, a compliance posture, a monitoring-frequency privilege, and possibly a treatment mandate. Utilities today reconcile these by hand, in spreadsheets, and they get it wrong in the direction that costs ratepayers money and delays public warnings.

This document specifies the Emerging Contaminant Compliance API, the system of record for that entire chain at a single utility. It spans initial monitoring at every entry point to the distribution system, the contamination-prevention protocol that governs field collection, laboratory selection against certified analytical methods and detection-limit floors, the locational running annual average computed against maximum contaminant levels, violation determination and the public notification that follows it, treatment technology evaluation across granular activated carbon, ion exchange and reverse osmosis with pilot testing, residuals handling and waste classification for spent media, capital planning with funding applications and rate impact analysis, source water investigation with cost recovery from responsible parties, and — at the far end of a clean record — the reduction of ongoing monitoring frequency.

Two design commitments run through everything that follows. First, **every number is derived, never asserted**: compliance postures, running averages, hazard indices, technology scores, debt service, surcharge tiers and bill impacts are recomputed by the server from primary evidence on every write that could change them, and a client that submits a derived value is submitting nothing. Second, **every reversal is exact**: invalidating a sample, rescinding a determination, voiding a residuals manifest or withdrawing a funding award must return the affected ledgers to precisely the numbers they held before, because regulators audit the arithmetic and not the intention.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User Identity and Role-Based Access Control

Every request carries two mandatory headers: `X-User-ID`, which identifies the acting operator, and `X-User-Role`, which declares the capacity in which that operator acts. The only exception is the logical-clock endpoint used by test harnesses, which is reachable without either header; every other endpoint in the system rejects a request whose `X-User-ID` or `X-User-Role` is absent, empty, or consists only of whitespace with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). The same 401 and the same error identifier are returned when `X-User-Role` carries a value outside the seven roles the system recognises — there is no separate "unknown role" code.

The seven roles are `UtilityAdmin`, `SamplingTechnician`, `LaboratoryManager`, `ComplianceOfficer`, `TreatmentEngineer`, `FinanceOfficer` and `StateRegulator`. Each endpoint declares the roles permitted to invoke it; a recognised role calling an endpoint outside its permission set is refused with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE` (HTTP 403). Read access is deliberately universal — all seven roles may retrieve any resource and run any list query — while every write is restricted to the role that owns the act. `UtilityAdmin` keeps the registry (contaminants, laboratories, entry points) and holds the emergency monitoring switch. `SamplingTechnician` collects and ships samples. `LaboratoryManager` receives shipments and reports analytical results. `ComplianceOfficer` reviews results, freezes and issues determinations, drafts and certifies public notifications, requests monitoring reductions, invalidates and reinstates samples, and opens cost-recovery investigations. `TreatmentEngineer` runs technology evaluations, pilot skids and the residuals chain through shipment. `FinanceOfficer` adjusts project costs, advances construction, files funding applications, withdraws them and resolves recovery claims. `StateRegulator` is the primacy agency: it decides monitoring-reduction requests and funding applications, reviews public notifications, classifies waste, and rescinds determinations.

Identity semantics differ by role, and this distinction is load-bearing. When `X-User-Role` is `LaboratoryManager`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Laboratory`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Laboratory` compare the resource's laboratory reference to `X-User-ID`. Consequently a `LaboratoryManager` whose `X-User-ID` does not resolve to an existing laboratory row is refused with `ERR_LABORATORY_NOT_OWNED` (HTTP 403) before any other laboratory-scoped rule runs, and a laboratory that presents a valid identity but acts on a sample consigned to a different laboratory is refused with `ERR_LABORATORY_ASSIGNMENT_MISMATCH` (HTTP 403). These two 403s are distinct on purpose: the first says "you are not a laboratory", the second says "you are the wrong laboratory".

When `X-User-Role` is `UtilityAdmin`, `SamplingTechnician`, `ComplianceOfficer`, `TreatmentEngineer`, `FinanceOfficer` or `StateRegulator`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it where a workflow demands provenance — as `collector_user_id` on a sample collected by a `SamplingTechnician`, as `determined_by_user_id` on a determination and `drafted_by_user_id` and `certified_by_user_id` on a public notification for a `ComplianceOfficer`, as `classified_by_user_id` on a residuals manifest and `decided_by_user_id` on a funding application for a `StateRegulator` — but it never resolves any of these values against an entity table.

One duty is segregated by identity rather than by role. Both drafting and certifying a public notification belong to `ComplianceOfficer`, so the guard is a comparison of operator identifiers: the officer whose `X-User-ID` was captured in `drafted_by_user_id` at submit time may not certify that notification's distribution, and an attempt to do so is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). Two distinct officer identifiers are therefore required to take a notification from draft to distributed.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every list endpoint accepts two pagination query parameters. `limit` controls page size: its default is 10, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` controls the starting position: its default is 0 and its minimum accepted value is 0, with no upper bound. A `limit` below 1, a `limit` above 100, or a negative `offset` is refused with `ERR_PAGINATION_OUT_OF_RANGE` (HTTP 422); the request is not silently clamped.

List results are ordered by `created_at` ascending unless a specific workflow section states otherwise, so pagination is stable across pages. The `meta` object of every list response carries `limit` and `offset` as they were applied, `total_count` as the number of rows matching the filters before pagination, and `has_more` as a boolean indicating whether rows remain beyond the current page.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and the Logical Clock

Every resource in the system carries four common properties. `id` is a server-minted UUID with no type prefix, and clients never supply it. `created_at` is the ISO 8601 UTC instant of creation with exactly three fractional digits. `updated_at` is the ISO 8601 UTC instant of the most recent mutation, in the same format, and is set equal to `created_at` at creation. `version` is an integer that begins at 1 when the row is created and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation of that row, including mutations reached indirectly as a cascade from a write on another resource. All thirteen resource kinds — contaminants, laboratories, entry points, samples, lab results, compliance determinations, public notifications, treatment evaluations, pilot tests, residuals manifests, capital projects, funding applications and cost recovery claims — carry all four of these properties.

Time in this system is a **logical clock**, not the wall clock. Its default value before any client sets it is `2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z`. An administrative test-driving capability accepts a `current_utc` instant and sets the clock that every subsequent request reads, echoing the value it stored; a malformed instant is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. Wherever a workflow below says "the logical clock", it means this value: shipment instants, receipt instants, issuance instants, distribution instants, filing and settlement instants and every "not in the future" comparison read from it. The clock may be moved backwards as well as forwards, which is why some temporal checks — shipment before collection, for example — are expressed against the clock rather than against a monotonic assumption.

Every datetime the API accepts or emits is an ISO 8601 UTC instant with exactly three fractional digits. Every monitoring-period label the API accepts is a four-digit calendar year followed by the letter `Q` and a quarter digit from 1 to 4; a label in any other shape is a request-shape failure.

### 2.5. Numeric Precision, Money and Rounding Discipline

All monetary amounts in this system are integer counts of cents, and every field name that ends in `_cents` is an integer. There is no floating-point money and no fractional cent: wherever a monetary quantity is the result of a division or a percentage, the result is rounded to a whole number of cents using round-half-up, meaning a value exactly halfway between two integers rounds away from zero to the larger magnitude. Rounding is never truncation and never bankers' rounding.

Non-monetary decimals carry a declared precision, and the declared precision is enforced both on input and on output. Concentrations in nanograms per litre, masses in kilograms, criterion scores, percentages, hazard indices and empty-bed contact times are all carried to two decimal places with round-half-up. The single exception is the compliance ratio held in `max_compliance_ratio`, which is carried to four decimal places with round-half-up, because a ratio against a maximum contaminant level is compared to boundaries that two decimals would blur. Where a request body supplies a value declared as a two-decimal quantity and the client sends more than two decimal places, the request is refused as a shape failure rather than quietly rounded.

Two accumulation modes exist in this system and they are deliberately different from one another. The mixture hazard index **accumulates unrounded and rounds exactly once at the end**: each contributing ratio is computed in exact decimal arithmetic, added to the running total at full precision, and only the final sum is rounded to two decimal places. The pilot technology score does the opposite — it **rounds each weighted term to two decimal places first and then sums the already-rounded terms**, with no further rounding of the total. These two modes yield different answers on the same inputs, and each calculation's own section states which one governs.

### 2.6. Optimistic Concurrency on Entry Points and Capital Projects

Two writes in this system are guarded by optimistic concurrency control, because each of them freezes numbers copied from a parent row whose derived fields move constantly.

`POST /compliance-determinations` requires a non-optional `expected_entry_point_version` field in its request body, carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target entry point. If that value differs from the entry point's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_ENTRY_POINT_VERSION` (HTTP 412) and the client must re-read the entry point before trying again. The reason is that a determination freezes a running annual average, a hazard index and a violation classification at creation time; every accepted or superseded result advances the entry point's `version`, so a compliance officer who computed a determination against an older read is refused rather than allowed to issue a determination against numbers that no longer exist. This check runs after the path resource has been resolved and after body foreign keys have resolved, and before every state and business check.

`POST /capital-projects/{id}/funding-applications` requires a non-optional `expected_capital_project_version` field in its request body, carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target capital project. A mismatch with the project's current `version` is rejected with `ERR_STALE_CAPITAL_PROJECT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). An application's requested amount is sized against the project's remaining local share, and awards, award withdrawals, settled cost recoveries and cost adjustments all advance the project's `version`; an application built on a stale project read is refused instead of over-subscribing the project. This check runs after the path project has been resolved and before the fiscal constraint checks that compare award totals against project cost.

No other endpoint in this system accepts a version field. `expected_entry_point_version` and `expected_capital_project_version` appear on exactly the two writes named above and nowhere else, and supplying either of them to any other endpoint is a request-shape failure.

### 2.7. The Validation Ladder: One Error per Request

Every write in this system is evaluated against a single total order of checks, and **exactly one error is ever reported**. The first condition that holds, reading the ladder from the top, determines the response; no request reports two failures, and no lower check masks a higher one. The bands, in order, are: authentication and authorisation, then ownership, then existence, then request shape and pagination, then optimistic concurrency, then state, then business safety, then business referential integrity, then business temporal rules, then business constraints, then fiscal rules.

Spelled out in full, the order is:

1. **Authentication** — missing, blank or whitespace-only `X-User-ID` or `X-User-Role`, on every endpoint except the logical-clock capability: `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. **Role recognition** — an `X-User-Role` outside the seven declared roles: `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
3. **Role permission** — a recognised role not listed for the endpoint: `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE`.
4. **Laboratory identity** — an `X-User-Role` of `LaboratoryManager` whose `X-User-ID` is not a laboratory `id`: `ERR_LABORATORY_NOT_OWNED`. This applies to exactly four endpoints: sample receipt, lab-result creation, the lab-result list, and the sample list.
5. **Path existence** — any `{id}` path segment that does not resolve to a live row: the not-found identifier of the addressed collection (`ERR_ENTRY_POINT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SAMPLE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LAB_RESULT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DETERMINATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_NOTIFICATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EVALUATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PILOT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MANIFEST_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PROJECT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CLAIM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CONTAMINANT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LABORATORY_NOT_FOUND`), always HTTP 404.
6. **Request shape** — a body field the endpoint does not declare, an unknown enum value, a wrong JSON type, a missing required body field, a negative number where non-negative is required, more than two decimal places on a two-decimal field, a malformed ISO 8601 instant, a malformed period label, or a value outside a declared range: `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 422), on every write endpoint. Each write endpoint reads its body against a closed set of declared field names — exactly the fields this document introduces for that endpoint, and no others — so any further field name in the body is a shape failure, whether it is a derived value, a concurrency token that belongs to a different endpoint, or a name this document never gives that endpoint at all. This closed-set check is part of the shape band, so it pre-empts every business check below it.
7. **Pagination range** — on every list endpoint: `ERR_PAGINATION_OUT_OF_RANGE`.
8. **Body and filter foreign keys** — a foreign key supplied in a request body or as a list filter that does not resolve returns the not-found identifier of the *referenced* entity, HTTP 404. This applies to sample creation, sample shipment, lab-result creation, determination creation, treatment-evaluation creation, pilot-test creation, residuals-manifest creation, cost-recovery-claim creation, and every filtered list.
9. **Sample custody ownership** — an `X-User-ID` that differs from the sample's assigned laboratory, on sample receipt and lab-result creation: `ERR_LABORATORY_ASSIGNMENT_MISMATCH`.
10. **Entry-point concurrency** — on determination creation: `ERR_STALE_ENTRY_POINT_VERSION`.
11. **Capital-project concurrency** — on funding-application creation: `ERR_STALE_CAPITAL_PROJECT_VERSION`.
12. **State checks**, in this internal order: monitoring suspension blocking new collection (`ERR_MONITORING_SUSPENDED`, priority 20); sample state (`ERR_SAMPLE_STATE_INVALID`, 21); invalidation reversibility (`ERR_INVALIDATION_NOT_REVERSIBLE`, 22); result state (`ERR_RESULT_STATE_INVALID`, 23); determination state (`ERR_DETERMINATION_STATE_INVALID`, 24); notification state (`ERR_NOTIFICATION_STATE_INVALID`, 25); evaluation state (`ERR_EVALUATION_STATE_INVALID`, 26); pilot state (`ERR_PILOT_STATE_INVALID`, 27); manifest state (`ERR_MANIFEST_STATE_INVALID`, 28); application state (`ERR_APPLICATION_STATE_INVALID`, 29); claim state (`ERR_CLAIM_STATE_INVALID`, 30); monitoring state (`ERR_MONITORING_STATE_INVALID`, 31); project state (`ERR_PROJECT_STATE_INVALID`, 32); project costs frozen in service (`ERR_PROJECT_IN_SERVICE`, 33); and the cross-entity requirement that a result's parent sample has been received (`ERR_SAMPLE_NOT_RECEIVED`, 34).
13. **Business safety**, in this internal order: prohibited sampling material (`ERR_PROHIBITED_SAMPLING_MATERIAL`, 60); missing field blank (`ERR_FIELD_BLANK_REQUIRED`, 61); prohibited disposal route (`ERR_DISPOSAL_METHOD_PROHIBITED`, 62); missing concentrate permit (`ERR_CONCENTRATE_PERMIT_REQUIRED`, 63); segregation of duties (`ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, 64).
14. **Business referential integrity**, in this internal order: `ERR_CONTAMINANT_NOT_IN_PANEL` (70), `ERR_METHOD_NOT_CERTIFIED` (71), `ERR_DETECTION_LIMIT_TOO_HIGH` (72), `ERR_DETECTION_LIMIT_BELOW_CAPABILITY` (73), `ERR_DUPLICATE_PERIOD_RESULT` (74), `ERR_RESAMPLE_TARGET_NOT_INVALIDATED` (75), `ERR_PILOT_NOT_COMPLETED` (76), `ERR_MEDIA_TYPE_MISMATCH` (77), `ERR_FUNDING_NOT_SECURED` (78), `ERR_ENTRY_POINT_MISMATCH` (79).
15. **Business temporal rules**, in this internal order: `ERR_HOLDING_TIME_EXCEEDED` (80), `ERR_LABORATORY_CERTIFICATION_EXPIRED` (81), `ERR_ANALYZED_BEFORE_COLLECTION` (82), `ERR_COLLECTED_IN_FUTURE` (83), `ERR_MONITORING_PERIOD_MISMATCH` (84), `ERR_SHIP_BEFORE_COLLECTION` (85), `ERR_PILOT_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (86), `ERR_PILOT_WINDOW_NOT_STARTED` (87).
16. **Business constraints**, in this internal order: `ERR_VIOLATION_PENDING_BLOCKS_REDUCTION` (90), `ERR_OPEN_VIOLATION_BLOCKS_REDUCTION` (91), `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_MONITORING_HISTORY` (92), `ERR_TRIENNIAL_HISTORY_INSUFFICIENT` (93), `ERR_LAB_DAILY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (94), `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_PILOT_EVIDENCE` (95), `ERR_NO_QUALIFYING_TECHNOLOGY` (96), `ERR_NO_QUALIFYING_RESULTS` (97), `ERR_NOTIFICATION_REVISION_LIMIT` (98), `ERR_NOTIFICATION_CONTENT_INCOMPLETE` (99), `ERR_DELIVERY_METHOD_INSUFFICIENT` (100), `ERR_MASS_EXCEEDS_SPENT_MEDIA` (101), `ERR_EFFLUENT_EXCEEDS_INFLUENT` (102), `ERR_DUPLICATE_ENTRY_POINT_LABEL` (103), `ERR_DUPLICATE_CONTAMINANT_CODE` (104), `ERR_DUPLICATE_CERTIFICATION_ID` (105).
17. **Fiscal rules**, in this internal order: `ERR_RESIDUALS_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` (110), `ERR_AWARD_EXCEEDS_REQUEST` (111), `ERR_AWARD_EXCEEDS_PROJECT_COST` (112), `ERR_RECOVERY_EXCEEDS_CLAIM` (113).

Three orderings inside this ladder deserve emphasis because the naive reading gets them backwards. A collection attempted at an entry point whose monitoring is suspended reports the suspension and not the kit defect, even when the kit is also non-compliant: the suspension check sits above the sampling-material check, so a suspended entry point refuses the collection before anyone inspects the kit. A residuals shipment that is both unsafely routed and unaffordable reports the prohibited disposal route and not the budget overrun: landfilling hazardous spent media is refused on safety grounds even when it is the only affordable route. And a lab result submitted both past its holding time and into a full laboratory day reports the holding-time failure: an expired sample is refused on its own merits before the laboratory's workload is considered.

### 2.8. Flat Collections and Reference Discovery

Every collection in this system is mounted at the root of the URL space; no resource owns a sub-tree of another. There are no nested read paths — a sample is not read beneath its entry point, and a lab result is not read beneath its sample. Relationships are instead discovered through filtered list queries on the child collection, and every list endpoint accepts the foreign-key filters relevant to its own rows in addition to `limit` and `offset`.

The entry-point list filters on `source_type`, `monitoring_status`, `compliance_posture` and `treatment_status`. The sample list filters on `entry_point_id`, `assigned_laboratory_id`, `resample_of_sample_id`, `panel_contaminant_ids`, `collector_user_id`, `monitoring_period` and `status`. The lab-result list filters on `sample_id`, `laboratory_id`, `contaminant_id` and `status`. The determination list filters on `entry_point_id`, `contaminant_id`, `status` and `determination`. The public-notification list filters on `determination_id`, `status` and `tier`. The treatment-evaluation list filters on `entry_point_id`, `status` and `mandated`. The pilot-test list filters on `treatment_evaluation_id`, `technology`, `status` and `selected`. The residuals-manifest list filters on `pilot_test_id`, `status` and `waste_classification`. The capital-project list filters on `entry_point_id`, `treatment_evaluation_id`, `funding_status` and `construction_status`. The funding-application list filters on `capital_project_id`, `program` and `status`. The cost-recovery-claim list filters on `entry_point_id`, `capital_project_id` and `status`.

Where an endpoint's URL carries an `{id}` path parameter, that segment is resolved before any body content is examined, and an unresolvable segment yields the 404 of the addressed collection. Where a request supplies an identifier in its body or as a list filter, resolution failure yields the 404 of the referenced collection instead — so a sample creation naming a non-existent entry point reports `ERR_ENTRY_POINT_NOT_FOUND`, one naming a non-existent contaminant in its panel reports `ERR_CONTAMINANT_NOT_FOUND`, and one whose resample reference does not resolve reports `ERR_SAMPLE_NOT_FOUND`. When several identifiers in the same body are unresolvable, path segments are resolved before body references, and the check order within the body follows the order in which this document introduces the fields for that workflow.

### 2.9. Derived-Field Reconciliation and Cascade Discipline

A large fraction of the fields in this system are derived, and derived fields are **recomputed from primary evidence on every write that could change them** rather than incrementally patched. When a single request touches more than one resource, every touched resource has its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` incremented, including resources reached at the second, third or fourth level of a cascade. A client that observes only the resource named in the response body will therefore find sibling rows advanced as well, which is precisely why the two optimistic-concurrency guards of §2.6 exist.

Cascades in this system are exact in both directions. The chain that begins with accepting a lab result reaches four levels deep: the result's own state, the entry point's counters and running averages, the parent sample's completion state, and the treatment mandate and monitoring-reduction denial that a violation posture forces. The chain that begins with invalidating a sample runs the same distance in reverse, and reinstating that sample must restore every affected number to the value it held before the invalidation — not an approximation of it, and not a recomputation from a different input set. Where a reversal is not permitted to be exact, this document says so explicitly: de-escalating a compliance posture, for instance, never retracts a treatment evaluation that an earlier violation mandated.

Finally, derived fields are never writable. A request body that attempts to set a derived value is a shape failure, and the fields this document introduces as computed — compliance postures, running averages, hazard indices, counters, scores, ledger totals, rate impacts and bill increases — are read-only projections of the evidence beneath them.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Establishing the Regulated Universe

Before any monitoring can happen, a `UtilityAdmin` populates three registries, and the values recorded there govern every calculation downstream.

A **contaminant** is a regulated emerging compound. Registering one requires a `code` — one to sixteen characters drawn from uppercase letters, digits and the underscore, such as PFOA or PFHXS — and that code must be unique across all contaminants; a repeat is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CONTAMINANT_CODE`. Three concentrations in nanograms per litre accompany it, each supplied with exactly two decimal places: `mcl_ng_per_l` is the maximum contaminant level and must be greater than zero; `trigger_level_ng_per_l` is the early-warning threshold and must be greater than zero and strictly less than the maximum contaminant level; and `required_mrl_ng_per_l` is the coarsest reporting level any laboratory may report for this compound, which must be greater than zero and no greater than the maximum contaminant level. A contaminant may also participate in the mixture hazard index: `hazard_index_member` defaults to false, and `hbwc_ng_per_l` — the health-based water concentration that serves as the hazard-index denominator — is required and must be greater than zero when membership is true, and must be null when membership is false. Finally, `notification_tier` records the baseline public-notification tier the utility owes on a maximum-contaminant-level violation of this compound; it defaults to `TIER_2` and must be one of `TIER_1`, `TIER_2` or `TIER_3`.

A **laboratory** is a contract analytical facility. Registration captures a `name` of one to one hundred and twenty characters, a state `certification_id` of one to forty characters that must be unique across all laboratories — a repeat is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CERTIFICATION_ID` — and `certified_methods`, a non-empty array of distinct analytical methods drawn from `METHOD_533`, `METHOD_537_1` and `METHOD_1633`. Two capability limits follow: `lowest_achievable_dl_ng_per_l`, greater than zero and carried to two decimals, is the finest detection limit the facility can physically reach, and `certification_expires_on` is the instant at which its state certification lapses. Throughput is bounded by `daily_analysis_capacity`, an integer of at least 1 that defaults to 3 and caps how many live analyses the laboratory may hold inside a single UTC calendar day. The `id` minted for a new laboratory doubles as the `X-User-ID` its manager presents, which is what makes `LaboratoryManager` the one identity-backed role in the system.

An **entry point** is a point of entry to the distribution system, and registering one requires only three inputs: a `label` of one to forty characters that must be unique across all entry points — a repeat is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_ENTRY_POINT_LABEL` — a `source_type` of `GROUNDWATER`, `SURFACE_WATER` or `PURCHASED`, and a `population_served` of at least 1. Everything else on a new entry point is set by the server. Its `monitoring_status` begins at `INITIAL` and may later occupy `QUARTERLY`, `REDUCTION_PENDING`, `ANNUAL`, `TRIENNIAL` or `SUSPENDED`; its `requested_monitoring_status` begins null and only ever holds `ANNUAL` or `TRIENNIAL`; its `treatment_status` begins at `NONE` and later becomes `PILOT` and then `INSTALLED`; its `compliance_posture` begins at `COMPLIANT`. The derived monitoring counters all begin at zero: `max_compliance_ratio` at 0.0, `hazard_index` at 0.0, `accepted_result_count` at 0, `consecutive_qualifying_quarters` at 0, `consecutive_invalidated_samples` at 0 and `open_violation_count` at 0.

Registration also freezes the rate base, and it is frozen permanently — later changes to `population_served` are not contemplated. The utility bills one account for every two and a half residents, so `served_account_count` is the population served divided by two and a half and then rounded up to the next whole account. Each account is assumed to buy sixty thousand gallons of water a year, so `annual_billed_kgal` is the account count multiplied by 60 thousand-gallon units. Water sells at nine dollars and fifty cents per thousand gallons, so `baseline_annual_revenue_cents` is the annual billed volume multiplied by 950 cents. These three integers are copied onto any capital project the entry point later spawns, and they are the denominators of every rate-impact percentage the utility will ever quote for it.

### 3.2. Field Collection, Contamination Prevention and Custody Transfer

Collection is the act of a `SamplingTechnician`, and a new sample arrives in `COLLECTED` status with `collector_user_id` set from the acting `X-User-ID`. The collection payload names the `entry_point_id` being sampled and a `panel_contaminant_ids` array — a non-empty list of distinct contaminant identifiers the sample must be analysed for. It records `monitoring_period` as a four-digit year, the letter Q and a quarter digit; `collected_at` as the instant of collection; and `protocol` as either `LOW_FLOW_GRAB` or `COMPOSITE`. It carries the chain-of-custody paperwork detail `preservative_lot`, a lot code of one to forty characters. When the collection replaces an earlier failed one, the optional `resample_of_sample_id` points at the sample being replaced.

Two booleans on that same payload exist purely to prevent the sample contaminating itself, and they are the reason emerging-contaminant sampling differs from ordinary compliance sampling. `ptfe_free_kit` asserts that no wetted part of the collection kit contained PTFE or any other fluoropolymer; a false value is a contamination-protocol breach and the collection is refused outright with `ERR_PROHIBITED_SAMPLING_MATERIAL`. `field_blank_included` asserts that a paired field blank accompanied the sample into the field; a false value is refused with `ERR_FIELD_BLANK_REQUIRED`, and this applies equally to `LOW_FLOW_GRAB` and `COMPOSITE` collections — there is no protocol that excuses the blank. Both of these checks sit below the suspension gate, so a collection attempted at an entry point whose monitoring is `SUSPENDED` reports `ERR_MONITORING_SUSPENDED` rather than either protocol failure.

Three further gates guard the collection. A resample reference must point at a sample whose status is `INVALIDATED`; pointing it at a live sample is refused with `ERR_RESAMPLE_TARGET_NOT_INVALIDATED`, since a resample exists only to replace a collection that failed. The collection instant must be at or before the logical clock; a future instant is refused with `ERR_COLLECTED_IN_FUTURE`. And the declared monitoring period must be exactly the calendar quarter containing the collection instant — the first quarter covering January through March, the second April through June, the third July through September and the fourth October through December — with any disagreement refused as `ERR_MONITORING_PERIOD_MISMATCH`.

Shipping a sample is also the technician's act. The shipment payload names the receiving `laboratory_id`, and on success the sample advances `COLLECTED → IN_TRANSIT` with `assigned_laboratory_id` set to the named laboratory and `shipped_at` set to the logical clock. A sample in any status other than `COLLECTED` is refused with `ERR_SAMPLE_STATE_INVALID`; a laboratory reference that does not resolve is refused with `ERR_LABORATORY_NOT_FOUND`; and because the logical clock can be moved backwards, a shipment attempted while the clock reads earlier than the sample's collection instant is refused with `ERR_SHIP_BEFORE_COLLECTION`.

Receipt is the branching moment of the sample lifecycle, and only the `LaboratoryManager` whose `X-User-ID` equals the sample's `assigned_laboratory_id` may perform it — anyone else, including a validly registered but unassigned laboratory, is refused with `ERR_LABORATORY_ASSIGNMENT_MISMATCH`. The receipt payload records two pieces of evidence: `chain_of_custody_intact`, the custody-seal verdict, and `field_blank_result_ng_per_l`, the measured concentration in the paired field blank. Both are stored on the sample. When the custody seal was intact **and** the field blank read strictly below 2.00 ng/L, the sample advances `IN_TRANSIT → RECEIVED`, `received_at` is set to the logical clock, and the entry point's `consecutive_invalidated_samples` streak is reset to 0. When the custody seal was broken, or when the field blank read at or above 2.00 ng/L, the same request instead drives the sample `IN_TRANSIT → INVALIDATED`: `invalidation_reason` becomes `CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY_BROKEN` when custody failed and `FIELD_BLANK_CONTAMINATED` otherwise, with custody winning when both conditions hold at once; `status_before_invalidation` stays null because the sample never reached `RECEIVED`; and the entry point's `consecutive_invalidated_samples` increases by one.

That streak is a safety interlock. When `consecutive_invalidated_samples` reaches 3 at an entry point, the entry point's `monitoring_status` becomes `SUSPENDED` as a cascade of the third invalidation, its `requested_monitoring_status` is cleared, and no further collection is accepted there until an administrator resumes monitoring. A `SUSPENDED` entry point is a statement about the sampling programme, not about water quality: three consecutive failures means the field procedure itself is untrustworthy.

### 3.3. Analytical Reporting: Methods, Detection Limits and Holding Times

A `LaboratoryManager` reports one analysis at a time. The submission names the `sample_id` analysed and the `contaminant_id` measured, the `method` used — one of `METHOD_533`, `METHOD_537_1` or `METHOD_1633` — the `reported_ng_per_l` concentration (at least zero, exactly two decimal places), the `detection_limit_ng_per_l` actually achieved for that analysis (greater than zero, exactly two decimal places), and `analyzed_at`, the instant of analysis. The `laboratory_id` recorded on the new result is taken from the submitting manager's `X-User-ID` and never from the request body; a result therefore cannot be attributed to a laboratory other than the one submitting it. The new result arrives in `SUBMITTED` status with `review_note` and `reject_reason_code` both null.

Three computed values are derived at submission and stored on the result. `censored` is true when the reported concentration is strictly below the achieved detection limit — that is, when the analysis is a non-detect. `compliance_value_ng_per_l` is the value the regulatory average will use: exactly 0.00 when the result is censored, and the reported concentration otherwise. `planning_value_ng_per_l` is the value the capital-planning average will use: half the achieved detection limit, rounded half-up to two decimal places, when the result is censored, and the reported concentration otherwise. A single non-detect therefore contributes nothing to the regulatory picture and something to the engineering picture, and that asymmetry is intentional.

The submission passes through a battery of admissibility rules before the row is created. The parent sample must currently be in `RECEIVED` status — a result offered against a `COLLECTED`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `ANALYZED` or `INVALIDATED` sample is refused with `ERR_SAMPLE_NOT_RECEIVED`, and note that this is a state read on the sample rather than on the result being created. The contaminant must be a member of the sample's `panel_contaminant_ids`; a result for a compound the sample was never collected for is refused with `ERR_CONTAMINANT_NOT_IN_PANEL`. The method must appear in the laboratory's `certified_methods`, else `ERR_METHOD_NOT_CERTIFIED`. The achieved detection limit is squeezed between two bounds: it may not exceed the contaminant's `required_mrl_ng_per_l`, or the analysis is too coarse to demonstrate compliance and is refused with `ERR_DETECTION_LIMIT_TOO_HIGH`; and it may not fall below the laboratory's own `lowest_achievable_dl_ng_per_l`, because a limit finer than the facility can physically reach is not credible, and that is refused with `ERR_DETECTION_LIMIT_BELOW_CAPABILITY`.

Three temporal rules follow, and their order matters. **Holding time** is measured as the exact number of hours between the sample's `collected_at` and the submitted `analyzed_at`, with no truncation to whole hours before the comparison. `METHOD_533` and `METHOD_537_1` each tolerate 336 hours — fourteen days — while `METHOD_1633` tolerates 720 hours, or thirty days. An elapsed time exactly equal to the method's limit passes; anything greater is refused with `ERR_HOLDING_TIME_EXCEEDED`. The same collection instant and the same analysis instant can therefore fail under one method and pass under another. Next, the laboratory's certification must still have been valid at the moment of analysis: an `analyzed_at` at or after the laboratory's `certification_expires_on` is refused with `ERR_LABORATORY_CERTIFICATION_EXPIRED`. And an analysis instant that precedes the collection instant is physically impossible and refused with `ERR_ANALYZED_BEFORE_COLLECTION`.

Finally, the laboratory's own throughput is checked, and it is checked last of these so that an expired sample is refused on its own merits before the facility's workload is considered. The system counts the laboratory's existing results whose status is `SUBMITTED` or `ACCEPTED` and whose `analyzed_at` falls on the same UTC calendar day as the submitted analysis instant; when that count already equals or exceeds the laboratory's `daily_analysis_capacity`, the submission is refused with `ERR_LAB_DAILY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. Results that were rejected or superseded do not occupy capacity, so a rejected analysis frees the slot it held.

### 3.4. Compliance Review, Running Annual Averages and Posture

A submitted result is worth nothing until a `ComplianceOfficer` reviews it. The review payload carries an `action` of `ACCEPT` or `REJECT`, and optionally a `review_note`; a rejection additionally requires a `reject_reason_code` of `QC_FAILURE`, `TRANSCRIPTION_ERROR` or `METHOD_DEVIATION`, and a rejection without one is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. Only a result in `SUBMITTED` status may be reviewed; a second review of the same result is refused with `ERR_RESULT_STATE_INVALID`. Rejection drives the result `SUBMITTED → REJECTED`, stores the reason code and the note, and changes nothing at all on the entry point — `REJECTED` is terminal.

Acceptance is where the arithmetic lives. Before the transition `SUBMITTED → ACCEPTED` is allowed, two further conditions must hold: the parent sample must still be in `RECEIVED` status, else `ERR_SAMPLE_NOT_RECEIVED`; and no other result may already hold `ACCEPTED` status for the same combination of entry point, contaminant and monitoring period, else `ERR_DUPLICATE_PERIOD_RESULT`. That uniqueness rule is what guarantees at most one accepted value per contaminant per quarter per entry point, and it is why the running average needs no tie-break inside a period.

On acceptance, the entry point's `accepted_result_count` rises by one, and if the entry point was still at `INITIAL`, its `monitoring_status` advances `INITIAL → QUARTERLY` — initial monitoring is considered under way once the first result is accepted, and no other field changes on that transition. The parent sample advances `RECEIVED → ANALYZED` when, and only when, every contaminant identifier in its `panel_contaminant_ids` now has exactly one accepted result; a partially analysed sample stays in `RECEIVED`. The entry point's running averages, `max_compliance_ratio`, `hazard_index`, `consecutive_qualifying_quarters` and `compliance_posture` are then all re-derived, and if that re-derivation lands the posture on `VIOLATION_PENDING` two further consequences fire: a mandated treatment evaluation is opened when none is already open for the entry point, and any monitoring reduction awaiting a regulator's decision is auto-denied.

The **locational running annual average** is computed per pair of entry point and contaminant. Its input set is every result in `ACCEPTED` status whose sample belongs to that entry point, whose `contaminant_id` is that contaminant, and whose sample has reached `ANALYZED` status. Results attached to a sample that has not yet completed its whole panel are deliberately excluded until the sample reaches `ANALYZED`, so a half-reported quarter contributes nothing. The input set is grouped by the sample's `monitoring_period`, the distinct periods are sorted in descending order by the year-and-quarter pair decoded from the period label, and the four most recent periods are kept. `quarters_used` is the number of periods kept, from zero to four. The average is 0.00 when no period is kept, and otherwise the sum of the values across the kept periods divided by the number of periods kept, rounded half-up to two decimal places. **The divisor is the number of periods actually kept, not four** — three quarters of data average over three.

The same input set yields two different averages, and the difference between them is exactly the treatment of non-detects. The **compliance average** uses each result's `compliance_value_ng_per_l`, so every non-detect contributes exactly 0.00 and censored quarters drag the average down; this is the only average ever compared against a maximum contaminant level or a trigger level, and it feeds `max_compliance_ratio`, `hazard_index`, the frozen `lraa_ng_per_l` on a determination, and monitoring-reduction qualification. The **planning average** uses each result's `planning_value_ng_per_l`, so every non-detect contributes half its detection limit and the same data yields a higher number; it is recorded as `planning_lraa_ng_per_l` on a determination and is used for sizing treatment. It is never used to decide a violation.

`max_compliance_ratio` is derived from the compliance averages. For every contaminant with at least one accepted result in the window, the system divides that contaminant's compliance average by the contaminant's `mcl_ng_per_l` and rounds the quotient half-up to four decimal places. `max_compliance_ratio` is 0.0 when no contaminant qualifies, and otherwise the largest of those per-contaminant ratios.

The mixture `hazard_index` is computed over contaminants whose `hazard_index_member` is true and which have at least one accepted result at the entry point; a member contaminant with no accepted result is absent from the sum entirely rather than contributing a zero term. Each member's term is its compliance average divided by its `hbwc_ng_per_l`, capped at 4.00 — the lesser of the quotient and 4.00. **These terms accumulate unrounded**: each is computed in exact decimal arithmetic and added to the running total at full precision, and the single rounding to two decimal places is applied once to the final sum. Rounding each term before adding would produce a different and wrong answer, and this is deliberately the opposite of how a pilot technology score accumulates. Because the terms add, a mixture can violate its index while every individual contaminant sits far below its own maximum contaminant level.

`compliance_posture` is then derived from those two numbers and is never set by a request body. The posture is `VIOLATION_PENDING` when `max_compliance_ratio` is strictly greater than 1.0 or when `hazard_index` is strictly greater than 1.00. Failing that, it is `TRIGGERED` when `max_compliance_ratio` is at least 0.5 and at most 1.0. Otherwise it is `COMPLIANT`. A ratio of exactly 1.0 is `TRIGGERED` and not a violation: the maximum-contaminant-level comparison is strictly greater than, so the boundary is inclusive of compliance and exclusive of violation. Escalation out of `COMPLIANT` — whether `COMPLIANT → TRIGGERED` or `COMPLIANT → VIOLATION_PENDING` — requires at least one accepted result at the entry point.

Each posture transition carries its own consequences. On `COMPLIANT → TRIGGERED`, a reduced monitoring frequency is revoked: an entry point sitting at `ANNUAL` or `TRIENNIAL` returns to `QUARTERLY`, and its `consecutive_qualifying_quarters` is left untouched, because history is not erased when only the frequency is restored. On `COMPLIANT → VIOLATION_PENDING`, the same frequency revocation applies, a mandated treatment evaluation is auto-created when none is open for the entry point, and a pending monitoring reduction is auto-denied. On `TRIGGERED → VIOLATION_PENDING`, a mandated treatment evaluation is likewise auto-created when none is open. De-escalation is asymmetric and intentionally so: on `VIOLATION_PENDING → TRIGGERED`, reached when a compensating write leaves `max_compliance_ratio` at least 0.5 and at most 1.0, existing treatment evaluations are left untouched — de-escalation never retracts a mandated evaluation. On `TRIGGERED → COMPLIANT`, reached when a compensating write leaves `max_compliance_ratio` strictly below 0.5, nothing else changes. And on `VIOLATION_PENDING → COMPLIANT`, reached when every accepted result at the entry point has been superseded or rejected so that `accepted_result_count` falls to 0, both `max_compliance_ratio` and `hazard_index` are reset to 0.0.

Sometimes evidence must be withdrawn. A `ComplianceOfficer` invalidating a sample supplies an `invalidation_reason` of `CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY_BROKEN`, `FIELD_BLANK_CONTAMINATED`, `HOLDING_TIME_EXCEEDED`, `QC_FAILURE` or `OPERATOR_ERROR`. Invalidation is permitted only from `RECEIVED` or `ANALYZED`, and anything else is refused with `ERR_SAMPLE_STATE_INVALID`. The sample moves `RECEIVED → INVALIDATED` or `ANALYZED → INVALIDATED`, and `status_before_invalidation` captures exactly which of the two it left — `RECEIVED` or `ANALYZED` — so that reinstatement can restore it precisely. Every result of that sample still in `SUBMITTED` status is driven `SUBMITTED → REJECTED` with `reject_reason_code` set to `QC_FAILURE`. Every result of that sample in `ACCEPTED` status is driven `ACCEPTED → SUPERSEDED`, which removes it from every running-average input set; the entry point's `accepted_result_count` falls by the number of results superseded, and its running averages, `max_compliance_ratio`, `hazard_index`, `consecutive_qualifying_quarters` and `compliance_posture` are all re-derived. The entry point's `consecutive_invalidated_samples` also rises by one on every invalidation.

Reinstatement is the exact inverse, and it is deliberately narrow. Only a sample in `INVALIDATED` status may be reinstated, else `ERR_SAMPLE_STATE_INVALID`. Beyond that, the invalidation must have been recorded as `OPERATOR_ERROR` and `status_before_invalidation` must not be null; a sample invalidated for any substantive reason — broken custody, a contaminated field blank, an exceeded holding time or a quality-control failure — can never be reinstated, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_INVALIDATION_NOT_REVERSIBLE`. On success the sample returns to whichever status `status_before_invalidation` recorded, `INVALIDATED → RECEIVED` or `INVALIDATED → ANALYZED`; both `invalidation_reason` and `status_before_invalidation` are cleared to null; every result of that sample in `SUPERSEDED` status returns `SUPERSEDED → ACCEPTED` and re-enters the running-average input sets; the entry point's `accepted_result_count`, running averages, `max_compliance_ratio`, `hazard_index`, `consecutive_qualifying_quarters` and `compliance_posture` are re-derived; and `consecutive_invalidated_samples` is reduced by one, with a floor of zero.

### 3.5. Determinations, Violation Classification and Public Notification

A compliance determination is the formal, frozen record of an entry point's standing for one contaminant at the close of a monitoring period. A `ComplianceOfficer` drafts it by naming the `entry_point_id`, the `contaminant_id`, the `period_end` instant marking the close of the period being determined, and the `expected_entry_point_version` for concurrency control. Drafting takes a snapshot: the entry point's current compliance average for that contaminant is frozen onto the determination as `lraa_ng_per_l`, its planning average as `planning_lraa_ng_per_l`, the number of periods those averages drew on as `quarters_used`, and the entry point's current mixture `hazard_index` as the determination's own `hazard_index`. Those four numbers never change afterwards, even when later results move the entry point; a determination is a historical statement, not a live view. The new determination is created in `DRAFT` status with `determined_by_user_id` set from the drafting officer, and `issued_at`, `rescind_reason` and `open_violation_count`-affecting consequences all deferred until issuance.

The `determination` verdict is decided at drafting time by walking four branches from the top and stopping at the first that matches. First, a **monitoring violation**: when `quarters_used` is fewer than four and the entry point's `monitoring_status` is `QUARTERLY` and neither an individual nor a mixture exceedance is present, the utility has simply failed to monitor, and `determination` is `MONITORING_VIOLATION`. Incomplete data at an entry point that is legitimately on `ANNUAL` or `TRIENNIAL` frequency is not a monitoring violation, because fewer than four quarters is exactly what reduced monitoring produces. Second, an **exceedance**: when the frozen compliance average is strictly greater than the contaminant's `mcl_ng_per_l`, or when the contaminant is a `hazard_index_member` and the frozen `hazard_index` is strictly greater than 1.00, `determination` is `MCL_VIOLATION`. Third, a **trigger**: when the frozen compliance average is greater than or equal to the contaminant's `trigger_level_ng_per_l`, `determination` is `TRIGGER_EXCEEDED`. Otherwise `determination` is `NO_VIOLATION`. Note the ordering consequence: a data gap is only a monitoring violation when the available data is otherwise clean — a substantive exceedance always outranks the paperwork failure, even when both are present.

Severity is then classified. An exceedance is **acute** when it is both large and widely felt: the frozen compliance average must be at least four times the contaminant's `mcl_ng_per_l`, or the frozen `hazard_index` must be at least 4.00, and simultaneously the entry point's `population_served` must be at least 10,000. Either magnitude condition alone suffices on the concentration side, but the population condition is mandatory — a fourfold exceedance at a system serving 9,000 people is not acute. From that, `violation_type` is `ACUTE` for an acute `MCL_VIOLATION`, `NON_ACUTE` for any other `MCL_VIOLATION`, `MONITORING_AND_REPORTING` for a `MONITORING_VIOLATION`, and `NONE` for `TRIGGER_EXCEEDED` or `NO_VIOLATION`.

Issuing the determination is what makes it operative. Issuance requires `DRAFT` status, else `ERR_DETERMINATION_STATE_INVALID`, and requires `quarters_used` to be at least one — a determination frozen over an empty window has nothing to determine and is refused with `ERR_NO_QUALIFYING_RESULTS`. On success the determination moves `DRAFT → ISSUED` and `issued_at` is set to the current logical clock. When `determination` is `MCL_VIOLATION`, the entry point's `open_violation_count` rises by one, which is the counter that later blocks any monitoring reduction. A public notification is auto-created in `DRAFT` status, but only for the two verdicts that oblige the utility to tell its customers: `MCL_VIOLATION` and `MONITORING_VIOLATION`. A `TRIGGER_EXCEEDED` or `NO_VIOLATION` determination issues without producing any notification at all. The auto-created notification carries the `determination_id`, `revision_count` of zero, empty `content_elements` and `delivery_methods`, and a `tier` chosen as follows: `TIER_1` when `violation_type` is `ACUTE`; `TIER_3` when `determination` is `MONITORING_VIOLATION`; and otherwise the contaminant's own `notification_tier`. The notification's `required_by_at` is then the determination's `issued_at` advanced by the tier's deadline — 24 hours for `TIER_1`, 720 hours for `TIER_2` and 8,760 hours for `TIER_3`.

A `StateRegulator` may rescind an issued determination, supplying a mandatory `rescind_reason`. Rescission requires `ISSUED` status, else `ERR_DETERMINATION_STATE_INVALID`, and moves the determination `ISSUED → RESCINDED`. When the rescinded determination was an `MCL_VIOLATION`, the entry point's `open_violation_count` falls by one with a floor of zero. Any linked public notification still in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `APPROVED` status is driven to `VOIDED`, because a notice that has not yet reached the public need not go out. A notification already in `DISTRIBUTED` or `LATE_DISTRIBUTED` status is left untouched: the public cannot be un-notified, and the delivery record stands as history.

Preparing the notice itself is a drafting exercise with three content gates. A `ComplianceOfficer` submits the notification with its `content_elements` — the required disclosure sections — and its `delivery_methods` — the channels through which it will reach customers. Submission requires `DRAFT` status, else `ERR_NOTIFICATION_STATE_INVALID`. The `revision_count` must be no greater than three; a notification sent back for revision a fourth time can no longer be submitted, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_NOTIFICATION_REVISION_LIMIT`. The `content_elements` array must contain all six mandatory disclosures — `VIOLATION_DESCRIPTION`, `HEALTH_EFFECTS_LANGUAGE`, `POPULATION_AT_RISK`, `CORRECTIVE_ACTION`, `UTILITY_CONTACT` and `ALTERNATE_WATER_GUIDANCE` — in any order and with any additional elements permitted; a missing element is refused with `ERR_NOTIFICATION_CONTENT_INCOMPLETE`. The `delivery_methods` array must contain every channel the tier demands: `TIER_1` requires both `BROADCAST_MEDIA` and `POSTING`, `TIER_2` requires both `MAIL` and `POSTING`, and `TIER_3` requires `ANNUAL_REPORT`; additional channels beyond the required set are always allowed, but an incomplete set is refused with `ERR_DELIVERY_METHOD_INSUFFICIENT`. On success the notification moves `DRAFT → SUBMITTED` and `drafted_by_user_id` records the submitting officer.

A `StateRegulator` reviews the submitted notice, supplying `approve` and an optional `review_note`. Review requires `SUBMITTED` status, else `ERR_NOTIFICATION_STATE_INVALID`. Approval moves the notification `SUBMITTED → APPROVED` and stores the note. Rejection moves it `SUBMITTED → DRAFT`, raises `revision_count` by one, clears both `content_elements` and `delivery_methods` back to empty arrays so the officer must re-assemble the notice from scratch, and requires a `review_note` explaining the deficiency, else `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

Certifying distribution closes the loop, and it is the one place where separation of duties is enforced by identity. A `ComplianceOfficer` certifies with a mandatory `delivery_evidence_ref`, and the certifying officer's `X-User-ID` must differ from the notification's `drafted_by_user_id`; the person who wrote the notice may not attest that it was delivered, and a self-certification is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. Certification requires `APPROVED` status, else `ERR_NOTIFICATION_STATE_INVALID`. The outcome then depends on timeliness measured against the logical clock. When the current clock instant is at or before `required_by_at`, the notification moves `APPROVED → DISTRIBUTED` and `late_by_hours` is 0 — meeting the deadline exactly is on time. When the clock instant is strictly after `required_by_at`, the notification moves `APPROVED → LATE_DISTRIBUTED` and `late_by_hours` is the overrun in hours rounded up to the next whole hour, with a minimum of 1, so that even a one-minute overrun records a full hour late. Either way, `distributed_at` is set to the clock instant, `certified_by_user_id` records the certifying officer, and `delivery_evidence_ref` is stored.

### 3.6. Monitoring Frequency Reduction and Suspension

Sustained clean results earn relief from quarterly sampling, and the qualifying history is computed rather than asserted. The system takes every monitoring period at the entry point that has at least one accepted result, sorts those periods in descending order by year and quarter, and then walks the list from the most recent period backwards. A period qualifies when every contaminant that has an accepted result in that period shows a compliance value strictly below that contaminant's `trigger_level_ng_per_l`. `consecutive_qualifying_quarters` is the length of the qualifying run at the front of that list: the walk stops at the first period that does not qualify, and older clean periods behind it do not count. Two clean quarters, one dirty quarter, then four more clean quarters yields two, not six — the streak is a prefix, not a total.

A `ComplianceOfficer` requests a reduction by naming a `target_status` of `ANNUAL` or `TRIENNIAL` and optionally a `justification`. Five conditions are checked in a fixed order. The entry point must currently be at `QUARTERLY`, else `ERR_MONITORING_STATE_INVALID` — an entry point already reduced, awaiting a decision, or suspended cannot request again. Its `compliance_posture` must not be `VIOLATION_PENDING`, else `ERR_VIOLATION_PENDING_BLOCKS_REDUCTION`. Its `open_violation_count` must be zero, else `ERR_OPEN_VIOLATION_BLOCKS_REDUCTION`; an unrescinded exceedance blocks relief even after the posture itself has recovered, which is why the two checks are separate. `consecutive_qualifying_quarters` must be at least four, else `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_MONITORING_HISTORY`. And a `TRIENNIAL` request demands a longer record still: at least eight consecutive qualifying quarters, else `ERR_TRIENNIAL_HISTORY_INSUFFICIENT`. On success the entry point moves `QUARTERLY → REDUCTION_PENDING` and `requested_monitoring_status` records the requested target.

A `StateRegulator` decides the request with `approve` and an optional `decision_note`. The entry point must be at `REDUCTION_PENDING`, else `ERR_MONITORING_STATE_INVALID`, and the decision is also refused with `ERR_VIOLATION_PENDING_BLOCKS_REDUCTION` when the posture has deteriorated to `VIOLATION_PENDING` while the request was outstanding. Approval moves the entry point to whichever status `requested_monitoring_status` holds — `REDUCTION_PENDING → ANNUAL` or `REDUCTION_PENDING → TRIENNIAL`. Denial moves it `REDUCTION_PENDING → QUARTERLY`. Either way `requested_monitoring_status` is cleared to null.

A `UtilityAdmin` may also suspend and resume monitoring outright, supplying an `action` of `SUSPEND` or `RESUME` and an optional `reason`. Suspension is permitted from any status other than `SUSPENDED` and drives the entry point to `SUSPENDED`, clearing `requested_monitoring_status` so an in-flight reduction request evaporates. Resumption is permitted only from `SUSPENDED` and returns the entry point to `QUARTERLY` — never to whatever reduced frequency it held before — while resetting `consecutive_invalidated_samples` to zero, which clears the invalidation streak that may have prompted the suspension in the first place. Any other combination is refused with `ERR_MONITORING_STATE_INVALID`.

### 3.7. Treatment Evaluation, Pilot Skids and Technology Selection

When an entry point needs treatment, a `TreatmentEngineer` opens a treatment evaluation against it. The evaluation names its `entry_point_id` and may set a `residuals_budget_cents` of at least zero, which defaults to 250000 — two thousand five hundred dollars — when omitted. It is created in `SCREENING` status with `mandated` false, `residuals_spent_cents` zero and `selected_technology` null. The `mandated` flag can never be set by a request: it is true only on an evaluation that the violation cascade created automatically when an entry point's posture reached `VIOLATION_PENDING`, which is how a regulator can distinguish a voluntary study from a compelled one.

Each candidate technology is proven on a pilot skid. A pilot names its `treatment_evaluation_id`, its `technology` — one of `GAC` for granular activated carbon, `IX` for ion exchange, or `RO` for reverse osmosis — and its operating window as `window_start_at` and a `window_end_at` strictly after it, else `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. It also carries the design and cost assumptions the eventual comparison will use: `ebct_min`, the empty-bed contact time in minutes, greater than zero with exactly two decimal places; `lifecycle_cost_cents`, `capital_cost_estimate_cents` and `annual_om_estimate_cents`, each at least zero; and three qualitative burdens scored from 0.00 to 100.00 with exactly two decimal places — `residuals_burden_score`, `footprint_score` and `om_complexity_score`. Note that a *higher* score on any of those three means a *worse* burden is being weighed favourably, because the weighted comparison adds them as-is; the scores are supplied by the engineer as merit scores on a hundred-point scale. The pilot is created in `PLANNED` status with `unmanifested_media_kg` of 0.00 and `selected` false. The first pilot registered against an evaluation moves it `SCREENING → PILOTING` and sets the parent entry point's `treatment_status` to `PILOT`; subsequent pilots leave both alone.

Pilot skids at one entry point cannot share a window, and the exclusion zone is wider than the window itself. When a new pilot is registered, the system examines every other pilot in `PLANNED` or `RUNNING` status belonging to any evaluation at the same entry point. Each such existing window is first widened by 48 hours on both sides — a decontamination buffer, so that a skid finishing on Monday cannot be followed by another starting Tuesday. The requested window is then tested for overlap against each widened window, treating both as half-open intervals that include their start instant and exclude their end instant. Any overlap is refused with `ERR_PILOT_WINDOW_CONFLICT`. Pilots in `COMPLETED` status impose no exclusion, and pilots at other entry points are irrelevant.

A pilot moves `PLANNED → RUNNING` when the engineer starts it, which requires `PLANNED` status — else `ERR_PILOT_STATE_INVALID` — and requires the logical clock to have reached the pilot's `window_start_at`; starting exactly at the window start instant is permitted, and starting before it is refused with `ERR_PILOT_WINDOW_NOT_STARTED`.

Completing a running pilot records its performance: `influent_ng_per_l` and `effluent_ng_per_l`, the concentrations entering and leaving the skid, `bed_volumes_treated`, and `spent_media_kg`, the mass of exhausted media the run produced, greater than zero with exactly two decimal places. The effluent concentration may not exceed the influent concentration — a treatment train cannot add contaminant — and a violation is refused with `ERR_EFFLUENT_EXCEEDS_INFLUENT`; equality is permitted and simply means zero removal. Completion requires `RUNNING` status, else `ERR_PILOT_STATE_INVALID`. On success the pilot moves `RUNNING → COMPLETED` and its `unmanifested_media_kg` is set to the full `spent_media_kg`, opening the residuals ledger described in the next subsection.

Three derived figures are then computed. `removal_pct` is the influent concentration minus the effluent concentration, divided by the influent concentration, multiplied by 100, rounded half-up to two decimal places. `lifecycle_cost_score` converts money into a merit score: 100 minus the pilot's `lifecycle_cost_cents` divided by 100,000,000, with the result floored at zero so that an extremely expensive technology scores 0.00 rather than a negative number, and then rounded half-up to two decimal places. `technology_score` is the weighted composite, and its accumulation rule is exact and deliberate: the five terms are `removal_pct` multiplied by 0.35, `lifecycle_cost_score` multiplied by 0.25, `residuals_burden_score` multiplied by 0.20, `footprint_score` multiplied by 0.10 and `om_complexity_score` multiplied by 0.10; **each of the five products is rounded half-up to two decimal places individually, and the five rounded products are then added with no further rounding of the sum**. This is the exact opposite of the mixture hazard index, which accumulates unrounded terms and rounds once at the end; the two accumulation orders can differ in the final hundredth, and each is required where it is specified.

The evaluation is closed by a decision carrying an `action` of `SELECT` or `ABANDON` and an optional `decision_note`. The evaluation must be in `PILOTING` status, else `ERR_EVALUATION_STATE_INVALID`. At least two of its pilots must have reached `COMPLETED` status, else `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_PILOT_EVIDENCE` — one data point is never enough to choose a technology, and this floor applies to both actions, so an evaluation cannot even be abandoned until two pilots have run. A `SELECT` additionally requires at least one completed pilot whose `removal_pct` is at least 90.00, else `ERR_NO_QUALIFYING_TECHNOLOGY`. An `ABANDON` requires a `decision_note`, else `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

Selection ranks the field, and the removal floor is applied as a filter before ranking rather than as a tie-break within it. First, only completed pilots with `removal_pct` of at least 90.00 remain candidates. Those candidates are then ordered by `technology_score` descending, then by `lifecycle_cost_cents` ascending, then by creation instant ascending, and the first is the winner. A pilot with the highest composite score in the whole evaluation therefore loses outright if its removal falls a hundredth below the floor. The winner's `selected` becomes true, the evaluation's `selected_technology` becomes the winner's `technology`, and the evaluation moves `PILOTING → SELECTED`.

Selection also brings a capital project into being — projects are never created directly, and only this cascade can produce one. The new project is seeded from the winner and from the entry point: the winner's `capital_cost_estimate_cents` becomes the project's `capital_cost_cents` and its `annual_om_estimate_cents` becomes the project's `annual_om_cost_cents`, while the entry point's `served_account_count`, `annual_billed_kgal` and `baseline_annual_revenue_cents` become the project's `served_account_count`, `annual_billed_kgal` and `current_annual_revenue_cents`. The project records the `entry_point_id` and the `treatment_evaluation_id`, and starts with `construction_status` `PLANNED`, `funding_status` `NOT_APPLIED` and `rate_design` `UNIFORM`. The selected technology then dictates two residuals-handling attributes: `GAC` sets `required_disposal_method` to `REACTIVATION` and `concentrate_permit_required` false; `IX` sets `required_disposal_method` to `INCINERATION_1100C` and `concentrate_permit_required` false; and `RO` sets `required_disposal_method` to `DEEP_WELL` and `concentrate_permit_required` true, because a membrane process produces a liquid concentrate stream that needs its own discharge permit. Finally the project's full financial ladder is computed from those seeded figures, so a freshly created project already carries a complete, unfunded rate impact.

An `ABANDON` decision moves the evaluation `PILOTING → REJECTED_ALL`, creates no capital project, and returns the entry point's `treatment_status` to `NONE`.

### 3.8. Residuals Manifests, Waste Classification and Disposal

Exhausted media is hazardous inventory and is tracked mass-for-mass. A `TreatmentEngineer` raises a manifest against a pilot by naming the `pilot_test_id`, the `media_type` being shipped — `GAC_CARBON`, `IX_RESIN`, `RO_MEMBRANE` or `RO_CONCENTRATE` — and the `mass_kg`, greater than zero with exactly two decimal places. The pilot must be in `COMPLETED` status, else `ERR_PILOT_NOT_COMPLETED`, since only a completed run has spent media to ship. The media type must be consistent with the pilot's technology: a `GAC` pilot may only manifest `GAC_CARBON`, an `IX` pilot only `IX_RESIN`, and an `RO` pilot either `RO_MEMBRANE` or `RO_CONCENTRATE`; a mismatch is refused with `ERR_MEDIA_TYPE_MISMATCH`. The mass may not exceed the pilot's remaining `unmanifested_media_kg`, else `ERR_MASS_EXCEEDS_SPENT_MEDIA`; a mass exactly equal to the remainder is permitted and empties the ledger. On success the manifest is created in `DRAFT` status with `waste_classification`, `disposal_method`, `disposal_cost_cents` and `leachate_pfas_ng_per_l` all null, and the pilot's `unmanifested_media_kg` is reduced by the manifested mass. Across the life of a pilot, the sum of the masses on its live manifests plus its remaining `unmanifested_media_kg` always equals the `spent_media_kg` recorded at completion.

Classification is a regulator's act, not a utility's. A `StateRegulator` classifies a draft manifest by supplying the `leachate_pfas_ng_per_l` reading from the leaching test, at least zero. The manifest moves `DRAFT → CLASSIFIED`, else `ERR_MANIFEST_STATE_INVALID`, and `classified_by_user_id` records the regulator. The `waste_classification` follows two inclusive thresholds read from the top: `HAZARDOUS` when the reading is at or above 500.0 nanograms per litre, `SPECIAL_WASTE` when it is at or above 50.0, and `NON_HAZARDOUS_SOLID` otherwise. A reading of exactly 500.0 is hazardous and a reading of exactly 50.0 is special waste; both boundaries belong to the more severe class.

Shipping commits the manifest to a disposal route. A `TreatmentEngineer` ships a classified manifest with a `disposal_method` of `LANDFILL`, `REACTIVATION`, `INCINERATION_1100C` or `DEEP_WELL` and a `carrier_ref`. The manifest must be in `CLASSIFIED` status, else `ERR_MANIFEST_STATE_INVALID`. The route must be permitted for the assigned classification: `NON_HAZARDOUS_SOLID` waste may take any of the four routes; `SPECIAL_WASTE` may take `REACTIVATION`, `INCINERATION_1100C` or `DEEP_WELL` but never `LANDFILL`; and `HAZARDOUS` waste may only be incinerated at 1,100 degrees Celsius or injected into a deep well. A prohibited route is refused with `ERR_DISPOSAL_METHOD_PROHIBITED`. The `disposal_cost_cents` is then the manifest's `mass_kg` multiplied by the route's per-kilogram rate — 12 cents for `LANDFILL`, 95 cents for `REACTIVATION`, 340 cents for `INCINERATION_1100C` and 210 cents for `DEEP_WELL` — rounded half-up to whole cents. That cost must fit the evaluation's residuals budget: the parent evaluation's `residuals_spent_cents` plus the new cost may not exceed its `residuals_budget_cents`, and an overrun is refused with `ERR_RESIDUALS_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`, spending exactly to the budget being permitted. Note the ordering: a hazardous manifest routed to landfill is refused as a prohibited route even when the landfill price would have fit a budget that the lawful incineration price would blow, so the cheap illegal option is never reported as a budget problem. On success the manifest moves `CLASSIFIED → SHIPPED`, the route, `carrier_ref` and cost are stored, and the evaluation's `residuals_spent_cents` rises by the cost.

A manifest raised in error can be voided, but only before it ships. A `TreatmentEngineer` voids it with a `void_reason_code` of `DUPLICATE_ENTRY`, `MASS_CORRECTION` or `CLASSIFICATION_ERROR`. Voiding is permitted from `DRAFT` or `CLASSIFIED` status only — a shipped manifest is a completed physical movement and cannot be recalled, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_MANIFEST_STATE_INVALID`. The manifest moves `DRAFT → VOIDED` or `CLASSIFIED → VOIDED`, and the pilot's `unmanifested_media_kg` rises by exactly the voided mass, restoring the mass invariant to the penny of a kilogram. Voiding from `CLASSIFIED` retains the recorded `waste_classification` and `leachate_pfas_ng_per_l` on the voided row rather than clearing them, because the leaching evidence remains valid even though the shipment was abandoned. Because no cost was ever charged on an unshipped manifest, voiding never touches `residuals_spent_cents`.

### 3.9. Capital Funding, Rate Impact and Cost Recovery

A capital project is the financial shadow of a selected technology, and it comes into existence only through the selection cascade described in §3.7 — there is no endpoint that creates one. A project carries two independent lifecycles at once. `construction_status` tracks the physical works through `PLANNED`, `BIDDING`, `CONSTRUCTION` and `IN_SERVICE`. `funding_status` is not a status a request may set at all: it is derived from the project's funding applications, taking the value `AWARDED` when any application of the project is in `AWARDED` status; otherwise `APPLIED` when any is in `SUBMITTED` status; otherwise `DENIED` when at least one application exists and every one of them is in `DENIED` or `WITHDRAWN` status; and otherwise `NOT_APPLIED`. The two dimensions advance independently, so a project can be in construction with funding denied, or awarded funding while still merely planned.

A `FinanceOfficer` may adjust the project's cost assumptions, supplying any of `capital_cost_cents`, `annual_om_cost_cents` and `rate_design`, with omitted fields left unchanged; those three are the only fields this write accepts, and it carries no concurrency token at all — consistent with §2.6, supplying `expected_capital_project_version` here is a request-shape failure rather than a concurrency check. Once `construction_status` reaches `IN_SERVICE` the cost basis is frozen and any adjustment is refused with `ERR_PROJECT_IN_SERVICE`. Every accepted adjustment recomputes the entire financial ladder and both bill-impact variants from scratch.

**The financial ladder.** Seven steps run in exactly this order, and the order is load-bearing because step 5 chooses the surcharge tier from the *provisional* figure computed in step 4 while step 7 reports the *final* figure that already contains that surcharge. Re-reading the step 7 answer back into step 5 — treating the ladder as a loop that settles — would select a different tier and produce a different answer, so the sequence runs exactly once, top to bottom.

1. `funding_awarded_cents` is the sum of `awarded_cents` across the project's applications in `AWARDED` status; `grant_equivalent_cents` is the sum of their `forgiven_cents`; `loan_principal_cents` is the sum of `awarded_cents` minus `forgiven_cents` across the same applications, that is, the awarded money that must still be repaid; and `local_share_cents` is `capital_cost_cents` minus `funding_awarded_cents` minus `recovered_cents`.
2. `borrowed_cents` is `local_share_cents` plus `loan_principal_cents`. `annual_debt_service_cents` is `borrowed_cents` multiplied by the twenty-year annuity factor 0.0611567181, rounded half-up to whole cents. Because the local share and the loan principal are simply added here, a loan with zero forgiveness moves money from one term to the other and leaves the borrowing total — and therefore the whole rest of the ladder — exactly where it was.
3. `base_annual_requirement_cents` is `annual_debt_service_cents` plus `annual_om_cost_cents`.
4. `provisional_rate_impact_pct` is `base_annual_requirement_cents` multiplied by 100, divided by `current_annual_revenue_cents`, rounded half-up to two decimal places.
5. `surcharge_bp` is selected from the provisional figure alone: 0 basis points when the provisional impact is strictly below 5.00 percent, 150 basis points when it is at least 5.00 and strictly below 15.00 percent, and 400 basis points when it is at least 15.00 percent.
6. `final_annual_requirement_cents` is `base_annual_requirement_cents` plus the surcharge, where the surcharge is `base_annual_requirement_cents` multiplied by `surcharge_bp`, divided by 10,000, rounded half-up to whole cents.
7. `rate_impact_pct` is `final_annual_requirement_cents` multiplied by 100, divided by `current_annual_revenue_cents`, rounded half-up to two decimal places. A project whose provisional impact sits just under 15.00 percent will therefore report a final `rate_impact_pct` above 15.00 percent while still carrying only 150 basis points of surcharge, and that is the correct result.

**Bill impact.** Both variants are always computed and stored, and they routinely disagree. `uniform_monthly_increase_cents` is `final_annual_requirement_cents` divided by the product of `served_account_count` and twelve months, rounded half-up to whole cents. The volumetric variant is computed in two stages with a rounding between them: `volumetric_rate_increase_cents_per_kgal` is `final_annual_requirement_cents` divided by `annual_billed_kgal`, rounded half-up to a whole cent per thousand gallons; and `volumetric_monthly_increase_cents` is that already-rounded whole-cent rate multiplied by the average annual consumption per account — `annual_billed_kgal` divided by `served_account_count` — divided by twelve months, and rounded half-up to whole cents. The intermediate rounding of the per-thousand-gallon rate is exactly why the two variants can differ by a cent or more on identical inputs, and it must not be skipped. `monthly_bill_increase_cents` then reports whichever variant `rate_design` selects: the uniform figure under `UNIFORM` and the volumetric figure under `VOLUMETRIC`.

**Construction progression.** A `FinanceOfficer` advances construction by naming a `target_status` and optionally a `concentrate_permit_ref`. Only the immediate successor is reachable — `PLANNED → BIDDING`, `BIDDING → CONSTRUCTION`, `CONSTRUCTION → IN_SERVICE` — and any skip, repeat or reversal is refused with `ERR_PROJECT_STATE_INVALID`. The move into `BIDDING` carries two extra preconditions, checked in this order: when the project's `concentrate_permit_required` is true, a `concentrate_permit_ref` must be supplied, else `ERR_CONCENTRATE_PERMIT_REQUIRED`; and the project's `funding_status` must be `AWARDED`, else `ERR_FUNDING_NOT_SECURED`. A reverse-osmosis project with secured funding but no concentrate permit is therefore refused on the permit, not on the funding. The supplied `concentrate_permit_ref` is read only to satisfy that precondition: unlike the manifest shipment of §3.8, which stores its route and `carrier_ref`, the project carries no field for the permit reference and neither stores nor echoes it. Reaching `IN_SERVICE` sets the parent entry point's `treatment_status` to `INSTALLED` and freezes the cost basis against further adjustment.

**Funding applications.** A `FinanceOfficer` applies against a project by naming the `program` — `SRF_LOAN`, `EC_GRANT` or `BIL_SUPPLEMENTAL` — the `amount_requested_cents`, greater than zero, and optionally `disadvantaged_community`, which defaults to false; `expected_capital_project_version` is required. There is no draft stage: the application is created directly in `SUBMITTED` status with its `capital_project_id` recorded and its `forgiveness_pct` resolved immediately from the program and the community designation. An `EC_GRANT` forgives 100.00 percent. A `BIL_SUPPLEMENTAL` forgives 100.00 percent for a disadvantaged community and 25.00 percent otherwise. An `SRF_LOAN` forgives 49.00 percent for a disadvantaged community and 0.00 percent otherwise. Creating the application changes the project's derived `funding_status` to `APPLIED`, but nothing about the ladder, because a request for money is not money.

A `StateRegulator` decides an application with an `action` of `AWARD` or `DENY`, an optional `awarded_cents` and an optional `decision_note`. The application must be in `SUBMITTED` status, else `ERR_APPLICATION_STATE_INVALID`. An award requires `awarded_cents`, which may not exceed the application's own `amount_requested_cents`, else `ERR_AWARD_EXCEEDS_REQUEST`; and the project's existing `funding_awarded_cents` plus the new award may not exceed the project's `capital_cost_cents`, else `ERR_AWARD_EXCEEDS_PROJECT_COST` — the project cannot be funded beyond its own cost even across several programs. An award moves the application `SUBMITTED → AWARDED`, stores `awarded_cents`, computes `forgiven_cents` as `awarded_cents` multiplied by `forgiveness_pct` divided by 100 and rounded half-up to whole cents, records `decided_by_user_id` and `decision_note`, re-derives the project's `funding_status`, and recomputes the whole ladder. A denial requires a `decision_note`, else `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, moves the application `SUBMITTED → DENIED`, leaves `awarded_cents` and `forgiven_cents` null, and re-derives `funding_status` without disturbing the ladder.

An application may be withdrawn by a `FinanceOfficer` with a mandatory `withdraw_reason`, from either `SUBMITTED` or `AWARDED` status — else `ERR_APPLICATION_STATE_INVALID` — but only while the project's `construction_status` is still `PLANNED`; once bidding has begun the funding cannot be pulled out from under it, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_PROJECT_STATE_INVALID`. The application moves to `WITHDRAWN`. Withdrawing an awarded application clears both `awarded_cents` and `forgiven_cents` back to null, and the project's ledger and full ladder are recomputed, returning every figure to exactly what it was before the award — the reversal is complete, not approximate.

**Cost recovery.** A `ComplianceOfficer` opens a claim against a responsible party by naming the `entry_point_id`, the `capital_project_id`, the `responsible_party_name` between 1 and 120 characters, the `investigation_basis` — `UPSTREAM_DISCHARGER`, `AFFF_TRAINING_SITE`, `LANDFILL_LEACHATE` or `MANUFACTURING_SITE` — the `allocation_pct` from 1.00 to 100.00 with exactly two decimal places, and the `investigation_cost_cents`, at least zero. The named entry point must be the same entry point the named project serves, else `ERR_ENTRY_POINT_MISMATCH`. The claim is created in `INVESTIGATING` status, and `claim_basis_cents` is computed once as the project's `capital_cost_cents` plus the claim's `investigation_cost_cents`, multiplied by `allocation_pct`, divided by 100 and rounded half-up to whole cents. That figure is informational: it never constrains what may be claimed or recovered.

Filing the claim requires `INVESTIGATING` status, else `ERR_CLAIM_STATE_INVALID`, and a `claimed_cents` greater than zero. The claim moves `INVESTIGATING → CLAIM_FILED` and `filed_at` records the logical clock.

A `FinanceOfficer` resolves a filed claim with an `action` of `SETTLE` or `DISMISS`, an optional `recovered_cents` and an optional `resolution_note`. A settlement is permitted only from `CLAIM_FILED` status, else `ERR_CLAIM_STATE_INVALID`, requires `recovered_cents`, and that recovery may not exceed the `claimed_cents` on the claim, else `ERR_RECOVERY_EXCEEDS_CLAIM`. The claim moves `CLAIM_FILED → SETTLED` and `settled_at` records the clock. The recovery is then divided among four buckets, filled strictly in order, each taking the lesser of what remains and its own ceiling: `alloc_investigation_cents` takes the lesser of the remainder and the claim's `investigation_cost_cents`; `alloc_capital_cents` takes the lesser of the remainder and the project's `local_share_cents` as it stands immediately before this settlement is applied; `alloc_om_reserve_cents` takes the lesser of the remainder and 25.00 percent of the project's `annual_om_cost_cents`, that ceiling being rounded half-up to whole cents; and `alloc_rate_stabilization_cents` absorbs whatever remains, including zero. The four buckets always sum to exactly `recovered_cents`, whether the money runs out in the first bucket or overflows every ceiling. Only the capital bucket touches the project: `recovered_cents` on the project rises by `alloc_capital_cents`, which reduces `local_share_cents`, and the full ladder and both bill variants are recomputed. Money landing in the investigation, O&M reserve or rate stabilization buckets changes nothing about the rate impact.

A dismissal is permitted from either `INVESTIGATING` or `CLAIM_FILED` status, else `ERR_CLAIM_STATE_INVALID`, and requires a `resolution_note`, else `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. The claim moves to `DISMISSED` with `recovered_cents` of zero and all four allocation buckets zero, and the project is left entirely untouched.
