# Nursing Licensure & Discipline API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A state board of nursing carries an unusually wide regulatory mandate for a single agency. The same office that decides whether a new graduate may sit for licensure also polices the conduct of thirty-year veterans, negotiates confidential recovery agreements with impaired practitioners, adjudicates revocations in quasi-judicial hearings, and reports the outcome of all of it to a national data bank and to every other compact state. Today those functions live in disconnected systems: an application tracker that knows nothing about discipline, a complaint log that cannot see whether a credential has already lapsed, and a spreadsheet in which someone hand-computes continuing-education credit. The consequence is not merely inefficiency — it is regulatory failure. A nurse whose licence was suspended in one state can practise on a stale compact privilege in another because nobody propagated the encumbrance.

This document specifies the backend service that replaces all of it: a single system of record for licensure, renewal, complaint investigation, alternative-to-discipline monitoring, board adjudication, and outbound notification. Its defining characteristic is that a credential is never described by one status. Registration standing (is the licence current, inactive, expired, lapsed, surrendered?) and disciplinary standing (is it clear, under investigation, restricted, suspended, revoked?) are independent axes that move under different actors on different timelines, and multistate privilege is a third, purely derived posture that must be recomputed whenever either axis shifts. A renewal filing behaves the same way: its continuing-education track and its practice-hour audit track advance separately, and the audit track can veto the filing track even after the education requirement has been satisfied.

Two engineering constraints shape everything that follows. First, segregation of duties is enforced in code rather than by policy memo: the board member who presides over a hearing may not vote in it, and the board member who once offered a nurse a monitoring agreement is permanently recused from voting against that same nurse. Second, every temporal judgement — expiry, lapse, notice periods, evidence holds, monitoring windows, criminal-history lookbacks — is evaluated against a server-held **logical clock** that tests drive explicitly, never against the host's wall clock. Regulatory outcomes must be reproducible, and a system whose answers depend on the wall clock is not reproducible.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Board Personnel, Applicants, and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to this API must carry two headers. `X-User-ID` identifies the caller, and `X-User-Role` declares the capacity in which the caller is acting. If either header is absent, or if `X-User-Role` does not spell one of the six declared roles exactly, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). If both headers are well formed but the named role is not among the roles authorised for the endpoint being called, the request is rejected with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE` (HTTP 403).

The six roles are `SystemAdmin`, `LicensureAnalyst`, `Investigator`, `BoardMember`, `MonitoringCoordinator`, and `Applicant`.

- A `SystemAdmin` is the registry operator. It configures boards and education programs, drives the logical clock, runs the practice-hour audit sampling pass, and manages outbound report transmission.
- A `LicensureAnalyst` is board licensure staff. It creates nurse identity records, verifies qualifications and criminal-history screening, mints credentials, moves the registration dimension, verifies continuing education, runs the practice-hour audit, and finalises renewals.
- An `Investigator` is board enforcement staff. It intakes and triages complaints, opens investigations, demands and receives custodial records, and disposes of investigated complaints. An `Investigator` may never adjudicate.
- A `BoardMember` is an appointed member of the board. It decides flagged applications, offers monitoring agreements, opens and presides over proceedings, casts panel votes, and issues final orders. A `BoardMember` may never investigate and may never record a monitoring event.
- A `MonitoringCoordinator` is the alternative-to-discipline case manager. It records compliance reports, missed screens, breaches, and completions against executed agreements. It may neither offer an agreement nor adjudicate.
- An `Applicant` is the nurse acting on their own record.

Five of the six roles carry an **opaque** identity. When `X-User-Role` is `SystemAdmin`, `LicensureAnalyst`, `Investigator`, `BoardMember`, or `MonitoringCoordinator`, the value of `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier that is not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it where a workflow demands an audit trail — as `triaged_by_user_id` and `investigator_user_id` on a complaint, as `approved_by_user_id` on a monitoring agreement, as `presiding_member_user_id` and inside the `voter_user_id` of a cast ballot on a proceeding — but it never compares such a value to any entity's `id`.

The `Applicant` role is **entity-backed**. When `X-User-Role` is `Applicant`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Nurse`, and ownership is enforced on every operation that role may perform. The comparison walks whatever chain reaches a nurse: the `Nurse` record's own `id` when the nurse resource is addressed directly; `nurse_id` for a licence application, a credential, or a monitoring agreement; the credential's `nurse_id` reached through a renewal's `license_id`; and the credential's `nurse_id` reached through a continuing-education record's `renewal_application_id` and then that renewal's `license_id`. On creates, the same comparison is made against the `nurse_id` supplied in the body, or against the nurse reached through the `license_id` supplied in the body. A mismatch is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403). Because the `Applicant` role is deliberately never granted a collection-listing endpoint, no list response ever needs ownership filtering.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every collection-returning endpoint accepts two optional query-string parameters. `limit` controls page size: it defaults to 10, its minimum accepted value is 1, and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` controls the starting position: it defaults to 0 and its minimum accepted value is 0, with no upper bound. If `limit` is not an integer within 1 through 100, or `offset` is not an integer greater than or equal to 0, the request is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION` (HTTP 400).

Unless a specific endpoint says otherwise, list results are ordered by `created_at` ascending — oldest record first. The `meta` object of a list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` (the number of records matching the filters before pagination is applied), and `has_more` (true when records remain beyond the current page).

### 2.4. The Logical Clock and Regulatory Time

Nothing in this system consults the host's wall clock. The server maintains a single logical clock, and every timestamp the server writes, every expiry comparison, every notice-period arithmetic, and every lookback window is measured against it. Before the clock is ever set, it reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`; that fixed startup default must never be replaced by real wall-clock time.

A `SystemAdmin` moves the clock forward by supplying a `current_utc` instant. Advancing the clock is not a passive act — it runs three regulatory sweeps in one pass, and the response reports what each one touched. The response body carries the new `current_utc`, an `expired_license_ids` array, a `lapsed_license_ids` array, an `activated_agreement_ids` array, and a `withdrawn_renewal_ids` array. The sweeps themselves are described where they belong, alongside the credential lifecycle and the monitoring lifecycle in §3.

### 2.5. The Board Configuration as Regulatory Rulebook

Almost every threshold in this system is a per-board parameter rather than a hard-coded constant, and the board configuration record is where those parameters live. A board is identified by `state_code`, a two-letter USPS code that must be exactly two uppercase letters and must be unique across all board configurations, and is described by `board_name`. Whether the state has enacted the Nurse Licensure Compact is recorded in `compact_member`, which defaults to false. The record also carries a `status` of either `ACTIVE` or `ARCHIVED`, initialised to `ACTIVE`; an `ARCHIVED` board accepts no new licence applications and no new renewals.

The continuing-education parameters are four. `ce_hours_required` is the credit-hour total a licensee must accumulate per renewal period and defaults to 30.00; it must be greater than zero and is carried to two decimal places. `ce_self_study_cap_hours` defaults to 12.00 and `ce_in_service_cap_hours` defaults to 4.00; each is the ceiling on how much credit may be drawn from its own category. `ce_carry_over_cap_hours` defaults to 5.00 and limits how much unused credit a renewal may import from the prior period.

The practice-hour parameters are two: `practice_hours_required`, the verified hours a licensee must demonstrate, defaulting to 960; and `practice_lookback_months`, the declared length of the practice-hour lookback window in months, defaulting to 60 — five years.

Renewal timing is governed by three values. `renewal_period_months` is the interval between a credential's issuance and its expiry and defaults to 24, so a credential issued today expires two years from today. `renewal_grace_period_days` defaults to 60 and is the window after expiry during which a late renewal is still accepted. `lapse_after_grace_days` defaults to 90 and is the point after expiry at which the registration dimension advances from `EXPIRED` to `LAPSED`.

Audit sampling is driven by `audit_sample_rate_bp`, expressed in basis points and constrained to the range 0 through 10000 inclusive; the default of 500 means five percent of eligible renewals. Criminal-history screening is governed by `criminal_lookback_years`, defaulting to 7, together with `disqualifying_offense_codes`, an array of offence codes that defaults to the two codes `FEL-A` and `SEX-1`.

Fees are integer cent amounts. `renewal_base_fee_cents` defaults to 12000 — one hundred twenty dollars. `late_fee_tier1_cents` defaults to 5000 and `late_fee_tier2_cents` defaults to 12000; `reinstatement_fee_cents` defaults to 25000; and `failed_audit_surcharge_cents` defaults to 2500. How the tiers combine is set out with the renewal fee narrative in §3.4.

Three further parameters express safety and governance policy. `phi_authorization_required` defaults to true and, when true, forces a patient-chart records demand to carry a patient authorisation identifier. `max_concurrent_custodian_holds` defaults to 2 and caps how many mutually overlapping record holds one custodian may bear. `monitoring_clearance_gap_days` defaults to 90 and is the number of clear days that must separate a nurse's prior monitoring agreement from a new one. Finally, panel adoption thresholds are stated in basis points of the seated panel: `revocation_vote_threshold_bp` defaults to 6667 (two thirds) and `majority_vote_threshold_bp` defaults to 5001 (a bare majority).

Every one of those parameters is optional at creation and takes the stated default when omitted. Amendments take effect for calculations performed after the amendment; values already frozen onto a renewal filing or a disciplinary proceeding are never retroactively recomputed.

### 2.6. Resource Identity, Timestamps, and Versioning

Every resource in this system — board configurations, education programs, nurses, licence applications, credentials, renewal filings, continuing-education records, complaints, evidence requests, monitoring agreements, disciplinary proceedings, and notification reports — carries the same four system-managed properties.

- `id` (string): a server-minted UUID. It carries no type prefix of any kind.
- `created_at` (string): an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, recording when the record was first created, taken from the logical clock.
- `updated_at` (string): an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp in the same format, refreshed on every mutation of the record.
- `version` (integer): a counter that starts at 1 when the record is created and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation, including mutations that only touch derived fields.

No entity in this system opts out of `updated_at` or `version`; all twelve resource families carry the full set. Because derived fields count as mutations, a credential whose `open_encumbrance_count` is recomputed, or whose `multistate_privilege_status` is rewritten, has both its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` advanced — which is precisely what makes the stale-view protections of §2.8 meaningful.

### 2.7. Parallel Status Dimensions and Encumbrance Reconciliation

Two resources in this system carry two status dimensions each, and the dimensions are genuinely independent: neither one is derivable from the other, and each has its own set of legal transitions and its own set of actors.

A credential carries `license_status` for its registration standing (`ACTIVE`, `INACTIVE`, `EXPIRED`, `LAPSED`, `SURRENDERED`, with `LAPSED` and `SURRENDERED` terminal) and `encumbrance_status` for its disciplinary standing (`CLEAR`, `INVESTIGATION_PENDING`, `RESTRICTED`, `SUSPENDED`, `REVOKED`, with `REVOKED` terminal). A credential can be `EXPIRED` and `CLEAR`, or `ACTIVE` and `SUSPENDED`. A third field, `multistate_privilege_status`, is not a dimension at all but a pure derivation recomputed from both dimensions plus residency and board data.

A renewal filing carries `renewal_status` for its filing standing (`DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `CE_DEFICIENT`, `CE_VERIFIED`, `RENEWED`, `DENIED`, `WITHDRAWN`, with `RENEWED`, `DENIED`, and `WITHDRAWN` terminal) and `audit_status` for its practice-hour audit standing (`NOT_SELECTED`, `SELECTED`, `DOCS_REQUESTED`, `DOCS_SUBMITTED`, `PASSED`, `FAILED`, with `PASSED` and `FAILED` terminal). These, too, advance under different actors — and a `FAILED` audit blocks a grant even when the filing dimension is fully satisfied.

Consistency across dimensions is maintained by immediate, in-request reconciliation rather than by any deferred job. The **encumbrance reconciliation** rule is the workhorse. Whenever a complaint enters or leaves the open set, and whenever a disciplinary order issues, the credential's `open_encumbrance_count` is recomputed in full — never incrementally patched — as the number of complaints whose `license_id` is this credential, whose `consolidated_into_complaint_id` is null, and whose status is one of the six open states `INVESTIGATION_OPEN`, `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE`, `ATD_OFFERED`, `ATD_ACTIVE`, `REFERRED_FOR_HEARING`, and `PROCEEDING_OPENED`. Complaints in `RECEIVED`, `TRIAGED`, `CONSOLIDATED`, `DISMISSED`, or `CLOSED` are outside the open set, and consolidated complaints are excluded outright so that merging two complaints never double-counts.

The derived posture follows from the count, but only when no board order is in force: if the credential's `encumbrance_status` is not one of `RESTRICTED`, `SUSPENDED`, or `REVOKED`, then it becomes `CLEAR` when the recomputed `open_encumbrance_count` is zero and `INVESTIGATION_PENDING` otherwise. A credential already carrying a board-ordered restriction, suspension, or revocation is never silently returned to `CLEAR` by the arithmetic. The count is a conserved quantity: opening an investigation raises it by exactly one, and dismissing that complaint — whether by investigator disposition or by panel dismissal — lowers it by exactly one, leaving no drift.

A nurse's aggregate `status` follows the credentials. It is `ACTIVE` by default, becomes `ENCUMBERED` when a credential of that nurse becomes encumbered, returns to `ACTIVE` when no credential of that nurse carries `RESTRICTED`, `SUSPENDED`, or `REVOKED` and no open complaint remains, and becomes `REVOKED` once every credential belonging to that nurse carries `encumbrance_status` `REVOKED`.

### 2.8. Stale-View Protection on Renewal and Proceeding Creation

Exactly two write operations in this system carry an optimistic-concurrency guard, and both guard against a client acting on a parent record it has not re-read.

Creating a renewal filing requires `expected_license_version` as a mandatory input field carrying the `version` of the target credential that the client last observed. If that value does not equal the credential's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). The guard is expressed as a version check rather than as a state precondition on purpose: only one non-terminal renewal may exist per credential, and creating a new renewal legitimately supersedes a prior `DRAFT` or `SUBMITTED` one, so a second create carrying the pre-supersede version is a stale-view write rather than an invalid-state write. A successful create advances the credential's `version` by one, which is what makes a repeat of the same request fail.

Opening a disciplinary proceeding requires `expected_complaint_version` as a mandatory input field carrying the `version` of the referred complaint that the client last observed. If that value does not equal the complaint's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Again the guard is a version check rather than a state check, because a referred complaint may legitimately gain a second proceeding after a first one is dismissed by the panel. A successful open advances the complaint's `version` by one.

No other endpoint in this system accepts a version field, and no other endpoint performs a version comparison. Both stale-version rejections happen before the state-machine check described in §2.11, so a request that is both stale and state-invalid reports `ERR_STALE_VERSION`.

### 2.9. Precision, Rounding, and Basis-Point Arithmetic

Five distinct numeric conventions coexist in this system, and mixing them up changes regulatory outcomes at boundaries.

**Credit hours.** Continuing-education credit is quantised to quarter-hour increments and carried to 2 decimal places using round half up. Crucially, the quantisation happens once per record, before any summation — never after. Summing raw minutes and quantising the sum produces a different total, and at the requirement boundary the two orders flip the renewal outcome.

**Monetary amounts.** All fees, surcharges, credits, and ledger balances are integer cent amounts. No rounding is ever required because every term in every fee expression is already an integer number of cents.

**Practice hours.** Verified practice hours are whole integers. Each documented employment period is pro-rated and floored to a whole number of hours on its own, and the floored per-period values are then summed. Flooring the sum instead of the parts is wrong.

**Scores.** The audit risk score is carried to 2 decimal places using round half up.

**Basis points.** Sampling rates, examination pass rates, and panel adoption thresholds are integers expressed in basis points, where 10000 basis points is one hundred percent. Comparisons against basis-point thresholds are performed as integer cross-multiplications rather than as floating-point divisions, so that a threshold is met exactly when it is met and never lost to representation error.

Where a computation must round a fraction outward, this document always names the direction explicitly: rounding up (toward positive infinity), rounding down (toward negative infinity), or rounding half up to a stated number of decimal places.

### 2.10. Path Resolution and Referential Integrity

All collections in this API are mounted at the root; there is no shared path prefix, and the entity graph is flat, linked by identifiers in request bodies rather than by deep URL nesting. Where a path does contain a resource identifier, and where a sub-collection hangs off it, identifiers are resolved strictly left to right and the request fails on the first identifier that does not resolve. Reading the evidence requests of a non-existent complaint therefore reports `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_FOUND`, never an empty list.

Each resource family has its own not-found code: `ERR_BOARD_CONFIG_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PROGRAM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_NURSE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_RENEWAL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CE_RECORD_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EVIDENCE_REQUEST_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MONITORING_AGREEMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PROCEEDING_NOT_FOUND`, and `ERR_NOTIFICATION_REPORT_NOT_FOUND`. All twelve carry HTTP 404.

The same codes are used for identifiers supplied in a request body. Every parent identifier in a creation payload must resolve to an existing row of the entity it names, and a payload that names a missing parent is rejected with that parent's not-found code. This applies to program registration, nurse creation, licence-application creation, credential issuance, renewal creation, complaint intake, monitoring-agreement creation, proceeding creation, and the consolidation target supplied at complaint triage.

### 2.11. The Order in Which Rejections Are Evaluated

When a single request violates more than one rule, the response must be deterministic. The system evaluates checks in the fixed order below and returns the first violation it finds; later checks are never reached. The ladder runs generic layers first, then safety and regulatory checks, then referential linkage, then temporal conflicts, then eligibility constraints, and finally fiscal checks — which is deliberately why a state problem outranks an unpaid fee, and why a protected-health-information problem outranks an unlinked custodian.

1. Both `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` are present and `X-User-Role` names one of the six declared roles — otherwise `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. The caller's role is authorised for the endpoint — otherwise `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE`.
3. Every resource identifier in the path exists, validated left to right — otherwise that family's not-found code.
4. Every parent identifier supplied in the request body resolves — otherwise that family's not-found code.
5. When the caller is an `Applicant`, the ownership chain of §2.1 resolves to the caller — otherwise `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`.
6. The body is well formed: every required field is present, every enum field carries a declared member, every numeric field is in range (contact minutes greater than zero, basis-point fields within 0 through 10000, state codes exactly two uppercase letters), and every interval pair is ordered — a records hold's start must be strictly before its end, and a monitoring agreement's `start_date` must be at or before its `end_date`. Otherwise `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.
7. On list endpoints, `limit` and `offset` are in range — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION`.
8. Uniqueness holds: a board's `state_code` is unused, and a program's `program_code` is unused within its board. Otherwise `ERR_STATE_CODE_EXISTS` or `ERR_PROGRAM_CODE_EXISTS`.
9. The supplied `expected_license_version` or `expected_complaint_version` matches the parent's current `version` — otherwise `ERR_STALE_VERSION`.
10. The target resource's current state is a declared origin state for the requested transition, the resource is not in a terminal state, and the governing board configuration is not `ARCHIVED` — otherwise `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.
11. The credential is renewable: its `license_status` is `ACTIVE` or `EXPIRED`, and when it is `EXPIRED` the logical clock is at or before its `expires_on` plus the board's `renewal_grace_period_days`. Otherwise `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_RENEWABLE`. This applies when a renewal is created, when it is paid, and when it is submitted.
12. The credential is not disciplinarily barred from renewal: its `encumbrance_status` is `CLEAR`, `INVESTIGATION_PENDING`, or `RESTRICTED` when a renewal is created. Otherwise `ERR_LICENSE_ENCUMBERED`.
13. A patient-chart records demand carries its patient authorisation when the board requires one — otherwise `ERR_PHI_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`.
14. A records demand names a custodian linked to the complaint — otherwise `ERR_CUSTODIAN_NOT_LINKED`.
15. The caller casting a panel ballot is not the proceeding's presiding member — otherwise `ERR_PRESIDING_MEMBER_CANNOT_VOTE`.
16. The caller casting a panel ballot is seated on the panel and has not approved a monitoring agreement for the nurse in question — otherwise `ERR_VOTER_CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST`.
17. An unconditional approval is not recorded on an application whose screening came back disqualifying — otherwise `ERR_DISQUALIFYING_OFFENSE`.
18. The hearing date honours the notice period — otherwise `ERR_NOTICE_PERIOD_NOT_MET`.
19. A proposed monitoring period neither overlaps nor touches an existing agreement for the same nurse and clears the required gap — otherwise `ERR_MONITORING_PERIOD_CONFLICT`.
20. A custodian is not already at its concurrent-hold ceiling for the requested window — otherwise `ERR_CUSTODIAN_HOLD_CAPACITY`.
21. An examination-pathway application names an education program whose approval posture and expiry are acceptable — otherwise `ERR_PROGRAM_NOT_APPROVED`.
22. An examination-pathway application reports a passing examination result — otherwise `ERR_NCLEX_NOT_PASSED`.
23. An endorsement-pathway application has a confirmed prior licence and a non-null two-letter prior state — otherwise `ERR_PRIOR_LICENSE_UNVERIFIED`.
24. An advanced-practice application rests on a qualifying base credential — otherwise `ERR_BASE_LICENSE_REQUIRED`.
25. The deficiency budget is not spent — otherwise `ERR_DEFICIENCY_LIMIT_REACHED`. This one check is consulted ahead of check 10 when the request is a further deficiency return against an application already frozen in `DEFICIENT` with its budget spent, as §3.2 sets out; on every other path, including a resubmission of a frozen application, it keeps its place in the ladder.
26. A duplicate-finding triage names a valid consolidation target — otherwise `ERR_CONSOLIDATION_TARGET_INVALID`.
27. An alternative-to-discipline offer meets its eligibility conditions — otherwise `ERR_ATD_INELIGIBLE`.
28. A convening hearing has quorum — otherwise `ERR_QUORUM_NOT_MET`.
29. The continuance budget is not spent — otherwise `ERR_CONTINUANCE_LIMIT_REACHED`.
30. The audit documentation-request budget is not spent — otherwise `ERR_AUDIT_DOC_REQUEST_LIMIT_REACHED`.
31. An audit decision has documentation to decide on: `audit_status` is `DOCS_SUBMITTED` and `practice_hour_periods` is a non-empty array. Otherwise `ERR_AUDIT_DOCS_NOT_SUBMITTED`.
32. An adopted sanction is at or above the frozen floor — otherwise `ERR_SANCTION_BELOW_FLOOR`.
33. Issuance is not duplicative: no credential already references the application as its `source_application_id`, and the applicant's nurse does not already hold a live credential of the same `credential_type` at the same board. Otherwise `ERR_LICENSE_ALREADY_ISSUED`.
34. The provisional continuing-education total meets the board's requirement at submit — otherwise `ERR_CE_HOURS_INSUFFICIENT`.
35. The renewal fee has been assessed and covered at submit — otherwise `ERR_RENEWAL_FEE_UNPAID`.
36. A renewal being granted does not stand on a failed practice-hour audit — otherwise `ERR_PRACTICE_HOURS_INSUFFICIENT`.

Three consequences of this ladder deserve to be called out because they are the cases most often implemented backwards. A patient-chart demand that is both unauthorised and aimed at a custodian not named on the complaint reports `ERR_PHI_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`, because check 13 precedes check 14. A convening event that both violates the notice period and lacks quorum reports `ERR_NOTICE_PERIOD_NOT_MET`, because check 18 precedes check 28. And a submit attempt on an unrenewable credential with no fee paid reports `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_RENEWABLE`, because check 11 precedes check 35.

### 2.12. The Fee Ledger Identity

Money in this system is tracked so that it can be reconciled at any instant without replaying history. A nurse carries two integer cent counters: `fee_paid_total_cents`, the lifetime amount that nurse has paid the board across all renewals, and `fee_credit_cents`, the unapplied credit currently held on that nurse's behalf. Each renewal filing carries `fee_applied_cents`, the amount currently charged against that filing, and `fee_paid_cents`, the amount actually received for it.

The invariant is this: a nurse's `fee_paid_total_cents` always equals that nurse's `fee_credit_cents` plus the sum of `fee_applied_cents` across every renewal filing on that nurse's credentials whose `renewal_status` is not `WITHDRAWN`. The two compensating actions in the system — denying a renewal at finalisation, and superseding a prior renewal when a new one is created — both move the filing's `fee_applied_cents` into the nurse's `fee_credit_cents` and then set `fee_applied_cents` to zero. Neither one ever reduces `fee_paid_cents` or `fee_paid_total_cents`. That asymmetry is intentional: the received-money figures are append-only, which is what makes the identity auditable and what makes a refund observable as a credit rather than as a disappearance.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up a Board, Its Approved Programs, and Its Nurses

Before anything can be licensed, a `SystemAdmin` must register the board itself, supplying at minimum a `state_code` and a `board_name` and overriding any of the regulatory parameters catalogued in §2.5. A second registration reusing an existing `state_code` is refused with `ERR_STATE_CODE_EXISTS`. The same role may later amend the board — adjusting `compact_member`, any of the continuing-education caps, `practice_hours_required`, `renewal_grace_period_days`, `lapse_after_grace_days`, `audit_sample_rate_bp`, `criminal_lookback_years`, `disqualifying_offense_codes`, any of the five fee amounts, `phi_authorization_required`, `max_concurrent_custodian_holds`, `monitoring_clearance_gap_days`, `revocation_vote_threshold_bp`, `majority_vote_threshold_bp`, or the board's `status`. Each amendment advances the board record's `version` by one. Amendments are prospective only: a renewal filing that has already frozen its accounting window, or a proceeding that has already frozen its sanction floor, keeps the values it captured. Archiving a board by moving its `status` to `ARCHIVED` does not disturb work in flight, but it does close the front door — a new licence application or a new renewal filed against an `ARCHIVED` board is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.

Nursing education programs are registered under a board's approval authority by the same role. A program declares the board it answers to through `state_board_config_id`, a board-assigned `program_code` that must be unique within that board (a collision yields `ERR_PROGRAM_CODE_EXISTS`), a human-readable `program_name`, the credential it awards in `degree_level` — one of `PN`, `ADN`, `BSN`, or `MSN` — its first-time examination pass rate in `nclex_first_time_pass_rate_bp` as an integer basis-point figure between 0 and 10000, and an `approval_expires_on` instant that is compared against the logical clock whenever an application leans on the program. A newly registered program's `approval_status` is `APPROVED`. Thereafter the posture may be changed to `PROVISIONAL`, `PROBATION`, or `WITHDRAWN`, and `approval_expires_on`, `nclex_first_time_pass_rate_bp`, and `program_name` may all be amended; each such change advances the program's `version` by one. A change of posture reaches applications retroactively only in a limited sense: an application already past qualification verification is unaffected, while an application still sitting at `SUBMITTED` will be judged against the new posture when it is verified. Programs may be listed for a board and narrowed by `approval_status` or by `degree_level`.

A nurse identity record is created by a `LicensureAnalyst`, and the returned `id` is what that nurse subsequently presents as `X-User-ID`. The record declares the board of the nurse's primary state of residence through `home_board_config_id`, the nurse's legal `full_name`, and two identity-matching values used for nothing else: `birth_year` and `ssn_last4`, the last four digits of the national identifier. The nurse's own assertion of primary state of residence is `declared_primary_state`, a two-letter state code.

Documentary support for that assertion lives in `residency_evidence`, an ordered array that defaults to empty. Each entry is an object carrying an `evidence_type` of `DRIVER_LICENSE`, `TAX_RETURN`, or `VOTER_REGISTRATION`, a `state_code`, and an `effective_date`. An entry's `effective_date` must be a parseable ISO 8601 instant, but it is a client declaration rather than a server-managed timestamp: it is stored and echoed back as exactly the string that was supplied, never rewritten into the three-fractional-digit form §2.6 reserves for the four system-managed timestamps, and any determination that needs it as an instant parses the stored string on demand. The array's order is meaningful and is not merely presentational: the zero-based index of an entry is its declaration order, and that index is the final tie-break when evidence is weighed during a compact audit.

Four counters on the nurse record are maintained by the system and are never supplied by a client. `fee_credit_cents` and `fee_paid_total_cents` both start at 0 and behave as described in §2.12. `prior_discipline_count` starts at 0 and equals the number of disciplinary proceedings against that nurse that reached `ORDER_ISSUED` with an adopted sanction other than `NONE`. `atd_participation_count` starts at 0 and equals the number of monitoring agreements for that nurse whose status has ever reached `EXECUTED` — note the "ever": completing or breaching an agreement does not decrement it. The nurse's aggregate `status` starts at `ACTIVE` and moves between `ACTIVE`, `ENCUMBERED`, and `REVOKED` on the rules in §2.7.

A nurse's `declared_primary_state`, `home_board_config_id`, `residency_evidence`, and `full_name` may all be updated later, by a `LicensureAnalyst` or by the nurse acting as an `Applicant`; each update advances the nurse record's `version` by one. Editing residency data deliberately does **not** recompute any credential's `multistate_privilege_status` — a fresh privilege determination must be run explicitly by a `LicensureAnalyst` before the compact posture reflects the new data. Nurses may be listed for a board, narrowed by `status`, and the listing returns those whose `home_board_config_id` is that board.

### 3.2. Initial Licensure: Filing, Deficiency Cycles, Qualification, and Screening

A licence application is opened either by the nurse acting as an `Applicant` or by a `LicensureAnalyst` on the nurse's behalf. The payload names the applicant through `nurse_id`, the board it is filed with through `board_config_id`, the route through `pathway` — either `EXAMINATION` or `ENDORSEMENT` — and the credential sought through `credential_type`, one of `RN`, `LPN`, `APRN`, or `NURSE_AIDE`. Four further fields are conditional. `education_program_id` names the education program and is required on the examination route while remaining null on endorsement. `aprn_role` records the advanced-practice role as `NP`, `CRNA`, `CNS`, or `CNM` and is required when the credential sought is `APRN`, null otherwise. `nclex_result` reports the licensure examination outcome as `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `NOT_TAKEN` and defaults to `NOT_TAKEN`. `base_license_id` names the existing registered-nurse or practical-nurse credential that an advanced-practice application builds upon, and is null for every other credential type. Endorsement applications additionally supply `prior_license_state` and `prior_license_number`, the two-letter state code and number of the credential being endorsed; both are null on the examination route.

A new application begins at `DRAFT` with `screening_status` `NOT_ORDERED`, `deficiency_cycle_count` 0, `board_decision` `NONE`, `prior_license_verified` false, an empty `screening_offense_records` array, and both `submitted_at` and `decided_at` null.

Submitting the application advances it from `DRAFT` to `SUBMITTED` and stamps `submitted_at` with the logical clock. Three preconditions apply: the board named by `board_config_id` must be `ACTIVE`; an examination-route application must actually carry an `education_program_id`; and an advanced-practice application must actually carry a `base_license_id`, failing which the submit is refused with `ERR_BASE_LICENSE_REQUIRED`. When the caller is an `Applicant`, the caller's `X-User-ID` must equal the application's `nurse_id`.

Board staff who find a filing incomplete return it to the applicant, moving it from `SUBMITTED` to `DEFICIENT` and recording a `reason_note` explaining what is missing. Each such return increments `deficiency_cycle_count` by one, and a returned application may be revised and resubmitted, moving `DEFICIENT` back to `SUBMITTED` and restamping `submitted_at`. The budget is shared and hard: the deficiency count must be strictly below 3 both for a new return to be recorded and for a deficient application to be resubmitted. There is no automatic denial. Once `deficiency_cycle_count` reaches 3 the application simply freezes in `DEFICIENT`, and from that point a further return and a resubmission are *both* refused with `ERR_DEFICIENCY_LIMIT_REACHED`, leaving withdrawal by the applicant as the only remaining exit. The frozen return is the one place where the deficiency budget is consulted ahead of the origin-state check of §2.11: a return aimed at a `DEFICIENT` application whose `deficiency_cycle_count` has already reached 3 reports `ERR_DEFICIENCY_LIMIT_REACHED` even though `DEFICIENT` is not itself a declared origin state for a return. Every other return the state machine refuses — including one aimed at a `DEFICIENT` application whose count is still below 3, where a resubmission is the legitimate next move — reports `ERR_INVALID_STATE` as the ladder prescribes.

Qualification verification is a `LicensureAnalyst` action and is where the two routes diverge. It optionally carries `prior_license_verified` to record that the issuing state has confirmed the endorsed credential is unencumbered. On the examination route, the referenced program must have an `approval_status` of `APPROVED`, `PROVISIONAL`, or `PROBATION` — a `WITHDRAWN` program fails, as does any program whose `approval_expires_on` is not strictly after the logical clock, and either failure yields `ERR_PROGRAM_NOT_APPROVED`. The application's `nclex_result` must be `PASS`; anything else yields `ERR_NCLEX_NOT_PASSED`. On the endorsement route, `prior_license_verified` must be true and `prior_license_state` must be a non-null two-letter code, failing which the verification is refused with `ERR_PRIOR_LICENSE_UNVERIFIED`. Independently of route, an advanced-practice application is checked against its base credential: the credential named by `base_license_id` must have a `credential_type` of `RN` or `LPN`, a `license_status` of `ACTIVE`, an `encumbrance_status` of `CLEAR`, and the same `nurse_id` as the application. Any deviation yields `ERR_BASE_LICENSE_REQUIRED`.

A successful verification passes the application through `QUALIFICATION_VERIFIED` and out the other side to `SCREENING_IN_PROGRESS` within the same request, setting `screening_status` to `REQUESTED` on the way. `QUALIFICATION_VERIFIED` is therefore observable only inside the request; the response body already shows `SCREENING_IN_PROGRESS`. The two routes differ in exactly one respect, and it is a side effect rather than a state: an endorsement verification additionally queues an interstate verification notice — a notification report on the `INTERSTATE_COMPACT` channel, with `source_entity_type` `LICENSE_APPLICATION`, `basis_code` `ENDORSEMENT_VERIFICATION`, and `status` `QUEUED`. An examination verification queues nothing. When the referenced program's posture is `PROBATION`, the examination branch additionally holds `board_decision` at `NONE` and marks the application for mandatory board review by leaving `screening_status` at `REQUESTED` and recording an allegation-free probation review alongside it.

The criminal-history vendor's answer arrives as `screening_offense_records`, an array of objects each carrying an `offense_code` and a `disposition_date`. Each `disposition_date` must be a parseable ISO 8601 instant, and — like the residency evidence of §3.1 — it is recorded and echoed back as exactly the string that was supplied rather than reformatted into the three-fractional-digit form of §2.6; the lookback comparison below works from a parsed copy of it. The test is a lookback test: an entry is disqualifying when its `offense_code` appears in the board's `disqualifying_offense_codes` **and** its `disposition_date` is at or after the logical clock minus the board's `criminal_lookback_years`. A seven-year lookback with the default code list therefore catches a `FEL-A` disposed six years ago and ignores the same offence disposed eight years ago.

If no entry is disqualifying, `screening_status` becomes `CLEARED`, the application advances from `SCREENING_IN_PROGRESS` straight to `APPROVED`, `board_decision` is set to `APPROVED_UNCONDITIONAL`, and `decided_at` is stamped from the logical clock — no board involvement is needed. If at least one entry is disqualifying, `screening_status` becomes `DISQUALIFYING` and the application advances to `SCREENING_FLAGGED` and then, in the same request, to `BOARD_REVIEW`; the entry into `BOARD_REVIEW` queues a notification report on the `NPDB` channel with `source_entity_type` `LICENSE_APPLICATION`, `basis_code` `SCREENING_FLAG`, and `status` `QUEUED`. The flagged state, like `QUALIFICATION_VERIFIED`, is a pass-through: the response shows `BOARD_REVIEW`.

A flagged application is then decided by a `BoardMember`, who supplies a `decision` of `APPROVED_UNCONDITIONAL`, `APPROVED_WITH_MONITORING`, or `DENIED`. The decision is written to `board_decision` and `decided_at` is stamped. Either approval moves the application from `BOARD_REVIEW` to `APPROVED`; a denial moves it to `DENIED` and queues a notification report on the `INTERSTATE_COMPACT` channel with `source_entity_type` `LICENSE_APPLICATION`, `basis_code` `APPLICATION_DENIED`, and `status` `QUEUED`. One decision is forbidden outright: `APPROVED_UNCONDITIONAL` may not be recorded on an application whose `screening_status` is `DISQUALIFYING`. Such an application may only be approved with monitoring or denied, and an attempt to clear it unconditionally is refused with `ERR_DISQUALIFYING_OFFENSE`.

An applicant may withdraw at any point before a decision, from `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, or `DEFICIENT`, which moves the application to `WITHDRAWN` and stamps `decided_at`. `ISSUED`, `DENIED`, and `WITHDRAWN` are terminal. Applications may be listed and narrowed by `nurse_id`, `board_config_id`, `education_program_id`, `base_license_id`, `pathway`, `credential_type`, or `status`.

### 3.3. Minting the Credential, Determining Compact Privilege, and Moving Registration Standing

Once an application stands at `APPROVED`, a `LicensureAnalyst` mints the credential. The only value supplied is `source_application_id`; everything else is copied from the application or derived. The new credential takes its `nurse_id`, its `issuing_board_config_id`, its `credential_type`, its `aprn_role`, and its `base_license_id` from the approved application, and stores the application's identifier in `source_application_id`. The system mints a board-assigned `license_number`, sets `license_status` to `ACTIVE` and `encumbrance_status` to `CLEAR`, stamps `issued_on` from the logical clock, sets `expires_on` to `issued_on` advanced by the board's `renewal_period_months`, initialises `practice_restriction_codes` to an empty array and `open_encumbrance_count` to 0, and moves the source application from `APPROVED` to the terminal `ISSUED`. A credential whose `credential_type` is `NURSE_AIDE` is a registry entry rather than a practice licence, and minting one additionally queues a notification report on the `NURSE_AIDE_REGISTRY` channel with `source_entity_type` `LICENSE`, `report_kind` `INITIAL`, `basis_code` `REGISTRY_ENTRY`, and `status` `QUEUED`.

Issuance is guarded against duplication on two clauses. No credential may already reference the same application as its `source_application_id`, and — the clause that actually bites in practice — the application's nurse must not already hold a credential of the same `credential_type` at the same `issuing_board_config_id` whose `license_status` is anything other than `LAPSED` or `SURRENDERED`. One live credential of each type per nurse per board is the rule. Either violation is refused with `ERR_LICENSE_ALREADY_ISSUED`. Distinct credential types are unaffected: a nurse may hold a registered-nurse credential and an advanced-practice credential and a nurse-aide registry entry at the same board simultaneously. Re-licensure after a credential has gone `LAPSED` or `SURRENDERED` is likewise permitted.

**Compact privilege** is recomputed, never stored by hand. Issuance runs it, a registration change runs it, every encumbrance transition runs it, and a `LicensureAnalyst` may run it on demand by supplying a `variant`. The result is written to three fields: `multistate_privilege_status`, which is one of `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `SINGLE_STATE`, `MULTISTATE`, or `PRIVILEGE_REVOKED`; `privilege_variant`, which records whether `RESIDENCY_DECLARED` or `RESIDENCY_EVIDENCED` was used; and `privilege_basis_state`, which records the primary state of residence the determination resolved to.

The determination is a guard chain evaluated strictly top to bottom, and the first guard that matches decides the answer. If the credential's `encumbrance_status` is `SUSPENDED` or `REVOKED`, the answer is `PRIVILEGE_REVOKED`. Otherwise, if the credential's `license_status` is anything other than `ACTIVE`, the answer is `NOT_ELIGIBLE`. Otherwise, if the issuing board's `compact_member` is false, the answer is `SINGLE_STATE`. Otherwise, if the credential's `credential_type` is `APRN` or `NURSE_AIDE`, the answer is `SINGLE_STATE` — advanced practice and aide registration never travel on a compact privilege.

Only if all four guards fall through does the determination resolve a primary state of residence, and this is where the two variants part company. Under `RESIDENCY_DECLARED` — the variant used at issuance, on every registration change, and whenever it is requested explicitly — the primary state of residence is simply the nurse's `declared_primary_state`, taken at face value. Under `RESIDENCY_EVIDENCED`, used during compact audits, it is derived from documents. The candidate pool is every entry of the nurse's `residency_evidence` whose `effective_date` is at or before the logical clock. Candidates are ranked first by the evidentiary weight of their `evidence_type`, where a `DRIVER_LICENSE` outranks a `TAX_RETURN`, which in turn outranks a `VOTER_REGISTRATION`; among entries of equal weight, the later `effective_date` ranks higher; and if two entries still tie, the one with the lower zero-based index in `residency_evidence` wins. The top-ranked candidate's `state_code` becomes the primary state of residence. If the candidate pool is empty — because the array is empty, or because every entry's `effective_date` is still in the future — the determination falls back to the nurse's `declared_primary_state`. No random value and no record identifier ever participates in the ranking.

With the primary state of residence resolved, two propagation rules finish the job. If the resolved state does not equal the issuing board's `state_code`, the answer is `SINGLE_STATE` — a nurse whose home is elsewhere holds only a single-state credential here. If any *other* credential of the same nurse carries an `encumbrance_status` of `RESTRICTED`, `SUSPENDED`, or `REVOKED`, the answer is again `SINGLE_STATE`; a sibling encumbrance poisons the privilege even on a clean credential. Only when both propagation rules pass is the answer `MULTISTATE`. Because the two variants can disagree on identical nurse data, the same credential can read `MULTISTATE` under `RESIDENCY_DECLARED` and `SINGLE_STATE` under `RESIDENCY_EVIDENCED`. When an on-demand determination yields `MULTISTATE`, a notification report is queued on the `INTERSTATE_COMPACT` channel with `report_kind` `INITIAL` and `basis_code` `PRIVILEGE_GRANT`.

A read-only compact posture summary is available for any credential. It returns `license_id`, `credential_type`, `license_status`, `encumbrance_status`, `multistate_privilege_status`, `privilege_variant`, and `privilege_basis_state` from the credential, alongside the inputs that produced them: `board_state_code` and `board_compact_member` from the issuing board, and `declared_primary_state` and `evidenced_primary_state` from the nurse — the latter being the state the evidence-ranked pool would resolve to right now.

The **registration dimension** is moved by a `LicensureAnalyst` supplying a `to_status`. Placing a credential on inactive status, transitioning `license_status` from `ACTIVE` to `INACTIVE`, requires that the credential currently be `ACTIVE` and that its `encumbrance_status` be either `CLEAR` or `INVESTIGATION_PENDING`; the privilege determination is re-run and necessarily yields `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, because the credential is no longer `ACTIVE`. Reactivating runs the cycle the other way, `INACTIVE` back to `ACTIVE`, and requires only that `encumbrance_status` not be `REVOKED`; the privilege determination is re-run under `RESIDENCY_DECLARED`. Accepting a voluntary surrender moves `license_status` to the terminal `SURRENDERED` and is permitted from `ACTIVE`, `INACTIVE`, or `EXPIRED`; it sets `multistate_privilege_status` to `NOT_ELIGIBLE` outright and queues a notification report on the `INTERSTATE_COMPACT` channel with `source_entity_type` `LICENSE` and `basis_code` `SURRENDER`.

Two registration transitions are driven by nothing but the passage of logical time, and they fire during the clock sweep described in §2.4. First, every credential whose `license_status` is `ACTIVE` and whose `expires_on` is at or before the new clock value moves to `EXPIRED`, and its privilege determination is re-run, necessarily yielding `NOT_ELIGIBLE`; the identifiers of those credentials are reported in `expired_license_ids`. Second, every credential whose `license_status` is `EXPIRED` and whose `expires_on` advanced by the board's `lapse_after_grace_days` is at or before the new clock value moves to the terminal `LAPSED`; its `multistate_privilege_status` is set to `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, and every renewal filing on that credential still in a non-terminal filing state — `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `CE_DEFICIENT`, or `CE_VERIFIED` — is moved to `WITHDRAWN`. Those credential identifiers are reported in `lapsed_license_ids` and the abandoned filings in `withdrawn_renewal_ids`. With the default settings, a credential expires on its `expires_on` instant, remains renewable for 60 days of grace, and lapses irretrievably 90 days after expiry.

The one route back from `EXPIRED` is a successful renewal, which returns `license_status` to `ACTIVE` provided the logical clock is at or before `expires_on` plus the board's `renewal_grace_period_days`; a credential that was already `ACTIVE` simply stays `ACTIVE`. Either way the renewal advances `expires_on` by the board's `renewal_period_months` from its prior value — not from the current clock — so a licensee who renews late does not gain extra time. Credentials may be listed and narrowed by `nurse_id`, `issuing_board_config_id`, `source_application_id`, `base_license_id`, `credential_type`, `license_status`, `encumbrance_status`, or `multistate_privilege_status`.

### 3.4. Renewal: Fees, Continuing Education, and the Grant Decision

A renewal filing is opened against a credential by the nurse acting as an `Applicant` or by a `LicensureAnalyst`. The payload names the credential through `license_id`, carries the mandatory `expected_license_version` described in §2.8, and may pre-populate `attested_practice_hours` — the practice hours the licensee asserts for the period, defaulting to 0 — and `carry_over_hours_claimed`, the continuing-education hours the licensee claims to import from the prior period, also defaulting to 0.

Creation is gated on the credential's standing on both dimensions. Its `license_status` must be `ACTIVE` or `EXPIRED`, and when `EXPIRED` the logical clock must still be at or before `expires_on` plus the board's `renewal_grace_period_days`; otherwise the create is refused with `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_RENEWABLE`. Its `encumbrance_status` must be `CLEAR`, `INVESTIGATION_PENDING`, or `RESTRICTED`; a `SUSPENDED` or `REVOKED` credential cannot be renewed at all, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_LICENSE_ENCUMBERED`.

A successful create freezes the accounting window: `period_end_on` is set to the credential's current `expires_on` and is the exclusive upper bound of the window, while `period_start_on` is set to `period_end_on` walked back by the board's `renewal_period_months` and is the inclusive lower bound. The filing begins with `renewal_status` `DRAFT` and `audit_status` `NOT_SELECTED`, with `ce_credit_hours_provisional`, `ce_credit_hours_audited`, `verified_practice_hours`, `audit_risk_score`, and `submitted_at` all null, `fee_applied_cents`, `fee_paid_cents`, `late_days`, and `doc_request_cycle_count` all 0, and `practice_hour_periods` an empty array. Because only one non-terminal filing may exist per credential, every other renewal filing on the same credential still at `DRAFT` or `SUBMITTED` is moved to `WITHDRAWN`, and each superseded filing's `fee_applied_cents` is added to the nurse's `fee_credit_cents` and then zeroed. Finally, the credential's `version` is advanced by one.

**Assessing the fee** is a separate act, performed by the applicant or by analyst staff, and it is where lateness is priced. The system first computes `late_days` as the number of whole days from the credential's `expires_on` to the logical clock, floored at 0 — a renewal filed before expiry has zero late days. The lateness surcharge then follows four bands: no surcharge at all when `late_days` is 0; the board's `late_fee_tier1_cents` when `late_days` is 1 through 30 inclusive; the board's `late_fee_tier2_cents` when `late_days` is 31 through the board's `renewal_grace_period_days` inclusive; and the board's `reinstatement_fee_cents` once `late_days` exceeds the grace period. With default settings that means fifty dollars for the first month late, one hundred twenty dollars from day 31 through day 60, and a two-hundred-fifty-dollar reinstatement charge beyond that. A second, independent surcharge applies when history warrants it: if any other renewal filing on the same credential carries an `audit_status` of `FAILED`, the board's `failed_audit_surcharge_cents` is added as well.

`fee_applied_cents` is then set to the board's `renewal_base_fee_cents` plus the lateness surcharge plus the failed-audit surcharge. `fee_paid_cents` is set equal to `fee_applied_cents`, because assessment and receipt happen in the same act. Any `fee_credit_cents` the nurse is holding is applied first and reduced by the portion it satisfies, and the nurse's `fee_paid_total_cents` is increased by the full `fee_applied_cents`. Assessment is subject to the same renewability gate as creation, so an assessment attempt on a credential outside the renewable window is refused with `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_RENEWABLE`.

**Continuing-education credit** is claimed record by record against a `DRAFT` or `CE_DEFICIENT` filing. Each claim names its `provider_code`, flags whether the provider is board-accredited through `provider_accredited` (defaulting to true), declares a `category` of `LIVE_CONTACT`, `SELF_STUDY`, `IN_SERVICE`, or `ACADEMIC`, states the contact `minutes` claimed — which must be greater than zero — and records the `completion_date` on which the activity finished. A new claim starts with `verification_status` `UNVERIFIED`; analyst staff later set it to `VERIFIED` or `REJECTED`, and each such verification advances that record's `version` by one.

Every claim is converted to credit on its own, at creation, and the conversion is quantised: divide `minutes` by 60 to obtain hours, divide that by 0.25, round the result half up to a whole number, and multiply by 0.25 again. The product, carried to two decimal places, is stored as `credit_hours` on that record. A ninety-minute activity therefore yields 1.50 credit hours, and a hundred-minute activity yields 1.75 — one and two-thirds hours quantised up to the nearest quarter hour. This per-record quantisation happens **before any summing**, and never after: three hundred-minute claims total 5.25 credit hours when each is quantised first, but only 5.00 if the minutes are summed and quantised once, and at the requirement boundary those two answers give opposite renewal outcomes.

The filing's credit total is computed twice over the same claim set, under two variants that differ only in which claims they admit. Both variants ignore claims whose `verification_status` is `REJECTED`. The **provisional** total, written to `ce_credit_hours_provisional` at submit, admits a claim whose `verification_status` is `UNVERIFIED` or `VERIFIED`, does not look at `provider_accredited` at all, and does not check the accounting window; it is therefore an upper bound. The **audited** total, written to `ce_credit_hours_audited` at continuing-education verification, admits a claim only when its `verification_status` is `VERIFIED`, its `provider_accredited` is true, and its `completion_date` falls inside the half-open accounting window — at or after `period_start_on` and strictly before `period_end_on`. Over the same claim set the audited total is therefore always lower than or equal to the provisional one.

From the admitted set, both variants proceed identically. Claims are processed in ascending `created_at` order. Their `credit_hours` are subtotalled per `category`, each subtotal rounded half up to two decimal places, with an absent category counting as 0.00. Two of the four subtotals are then capped: the self-study subtotal is reduced to the board's `ce_self_study_cap_hours` if it exceeds it, and the in-service subtotal is reduced to the board's `ce_in_service_cap_hours` if it exceeds it. Live-contact and academic credit are uncapped. Carry-over is capped the same way: the allowed carry-over is the filing's `carry_over_hours_claimed`, reduced to the board's `ce_carry_over_cap_hours` if it exceeds it. The total is the sum of the four capped category subtotals plus the allowed carry-over, rounded half up to two decimal places. Under default board settings, a licensee who logs forty hours of self-study and nothing else banks only twelve, plus at most five carried over — seventeen against a requirement of thirty.

**Submitting** the filing advances `renewal_status` from `DRAFT` to `SUBMITTED`, or resubmits it from `CE_DEFICIENT` to `SUBMITTED`, recomputing `ce_credit_hours_provisional` over the current claim set and restamping `submitted_at` from the logical clock each time. The credential must still be `ACTIVE` or `EXPIRED` within grace. The provisional total must be at or above the board's `ce_hours_required` — an exact match passes — and a shortfall is refused with `ERR_CE_HOURS_INSUFFICIENT`. The fee must be settled: `fee_applied_cents` must be greater than zero and `fee_paid_cents` must be at least `fee_applied_cents`, and an unassessed or underpaid filing is refused with `ERR_RENEWAL_FEE_UNPAID`. Because the renewability check outranks the fiscal check, a submit on a credential that is neither `ACTIVE` nor within its grace window reports `ERR_LICENSE_NOT_RENEWABLE` even when nothing has been paid.

**Verifying continuing education** is analyst-only and is where the audited variant runs. Whatever it computes is written to `ce_credit_hours_audited`, and the result branches the filing dimension: a total at or above the board's `ce_hours_required` moves `renewal_status` from `SUBMITTED` to `CE_VERIFIED`, while a total below it moves `SUBMITTED` to `CE_DEFICIENT`. The deficiency branch is an unbounded revision cycle — `SUBMITTED` to `CE_DEFICIENT` and back to `SUBMITTED` — because a licensee may keep attaching further coursework; unlike the licence-application deficiency budget, there is no cap.

**Finalising** the renewal is the last act, and the analyst supplies an `outcome` of either `RENEWED` or `DENIED`. Granting requires `renewal_status` `CE_VERIFIED` and an `audit_status` of either `NOT_SELECTED` or `PASSED`; a filing whose practice-hour audit stands `FAILED` cannot be granted, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_PRACTICE_HOURS_INSUFFICIENT`. A grant moves `renewal_status` to the terminal `RENEWED`, advances the credential's `expires_on` by the board's `renewal_period_months` from its prior value, returns `license_status` from `EXPIRED` to `ACTIVE` (or leaves an `ACTIVE` credential `ACTIVE`), re-runs the privilege determination under `RESIDENCY_DECLARED`, and advances the credential's `version` by one.

A denial moves `renewal_status` to the terminal `DENIED` and performs the compensating fiscal action: the filing's `fee_applied_cents` is added to the nurse's `fee_credit_cents` and then set to 0, while `fee_paid_cents` and the nurse's `fee_paid_total_cents` are left exactly as they were, so the ledger identity of §2.12 still balances. A denial carries one further consequence when the practice-hour audit stands `FAILED`: the system opens a fresh complaint against the credential on its own initiative, with `complainant_type` `BOARD_INITIATED`, `violation_category` `FRAUD`, status `RECEIVED`, and `employer_custodian_codes` populated from the employer names appearing in the audited `practice_hour_periods`. Renewal filings may be listed for a credential and narrowed by `renewal_status` or `audit_status`, and the continuing-education claims of a filing may be listed and narrowed by `verification_status` or `category`.

### 3.5. Practice-Hour Audit Sampling and the Documentation Cycle

Boards do not audit every renewal. They audit a risk-weighted sample, and the sampling pass is a board-wide operation run by a `SystemAdmin` naming the board through `board_config_id`. The pass is idempotent in the sense that it only ever considers filings not already selected.

A renewal filing is eligible for sampling when three conditions hold together: its `renewal_status` is `CE_VERIFIED`, its `audit_status` is `NOT_SELECTED`, and the credential it was filed against has an `issuing_board_config_id` equal to the board being sampled. Every eligible filing — not merely the ones eventually drawn — is scored. The score starts at zero and accumulates four independent weights. It gains 40.00 if the credential has an open complaint against it, meaning a complaint whose `license_id` is this credential and whose `status` is anything other than `DISMISSED`, `CONSOLIDATED`, or `CLOSED`. It gains 30.00 if any other renewal filing on the same credential carries an `audit_status` of `FAILED`. It gains 20.00 when the attested hours sit on a thin margin, tested by integer cross-multiplication: `attested_practice_hours` multiplied by 100 is strictly less than the board's `practice_hours_required` multiplied by 110 — that is, the attestation is under one hundred ten percent of the requirement. It gains 10.00 if `late_days` is greater than zero. The accumulated score is rounded half up to two decimal places and written to `audit_risk_score` on every eligible filing, so a filing that was scored but not drawn still carries evidence of its assessment. A filing carrying an open complaint, a prior failed audit, thin attested hours, and lateness scores the maximum 100.00.

The draw size follows the board's `audit_sample_rate_bp` as a basis-point rate: multiply `audit_sample_rate_bp` by the eligible count, divide by 10000, and round the quotient **up** to the next whole number. The quota is therefore at least 1 whenever the eligible pool is non-empty and the rate is non-zero — a five-percent rate over three eligible filings still draws one. Eligible filings are then ranked by `audit_risk_score` descending, and filings of identical score are ordered by `created_at` ascending, so the earliest filing is drawn first. The highest-ranked filings up to the quota have their `audit_status` moved from `NOT_SELECTED` to `SELECTED`. The response reports `board_config_id`, `eligible_count`, `quota`, `selected_count`, and `selected_renewal_application_ids`. When no filing is eligible, the pass still succeeds: `eligible_count`, `quota`, and `selected_count` are all 0 and `selected_renewal_application_ids` is an empty array.

**Demanding documentation** is the analyst's next act, optionally accompanied by a free-text `note`. It moves `audit_status` from `SELECTED` to `DOCS_REQUESTED`, or from `DOCS_SUBMITTED` back to `DOCS_REQUESTED` when a submission arrives incomplete, and each demand increments `doc_request_cycle_count` and clears `practice_hour_periods` back to an empty array — a fresh demand discards whatever was submitted before. The cycle is budgeted: once `doc_request_cycle_count` has reached 3, no further demand may be issued from either state, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_AUDIT_DOC_REQUEST_LIMIT_REACHED`. The licensee responds by submitting employment periods, which moves `audit_status` from `DOCS_REQUESTED` to `DOCS_SUBMITTED` and **overwrites** `practice_hour_periods` wholesale with the submitted array rather than appending to it. Each period is an object naming its `employer_name`, its inclusive `start_on`, its exclusive `end_on`, and the `hours` worked in it. `start_on` and `end_on` must each be a parseable ISO 8601 instant, and the wholesale overwrite keeps them as exactly the strings the licensee submitted — they are not reformatted into the three-fractional-digit form of §2.6 — with the proration below parsing them on demand.

**Deciding the audit** prorates each submitted period against the renewal's accounting window and totals the result. For one period, the system first measures the period's own length in days, from `start_on` inclusive to `end_on` exclusive. It then measures the overlap in days between that period and the half-open accounting window running from `period_start_on` inclusive to `period_end_on` exclusive — the overlap begins at the later of the two start dates and ends at the earlier of the two end dates. That period's contribution is its `hours` multiplied by the overlap days, divided by the period days, and then rounded **down** to a whole number of hours. If the period's own length is zero days, or if the overlap is zero or negative, the period contributes nothing at all. The contributions are floored individually and only then summed, and the sum is written to `verified_practice_hours`. Flooring per period before summing rather than after is material: three periods each losing a fractional hour lose three hours in total, and near the requirement boundary that difference decides the audit.

The outcome follows directly. If `verified_practice_hours` is at or above the board's `practice_hours_required`, `audit_status` moves from `DOCS_SUBMITTED` to `PASSED`; otherwise it moves to `FAILED`. Both are terminal on the audit dimension. A decision may only be taken when `audit_status` is `DOCS_SUBMITTED` and `practice_hour_periods` is a non-empty array; an attempt from any other audit state, or on an empty period array, is refused with `ERR_AUDIT_DOCS_NOT_SUBMITTED`. Because the two dimensions of a renewal filing advance independently, a filing may sit at `renewal_status` `CE_VERIFIED` with `audit_status` anywhere from `NOT_SELECTED` to `FAILED`; only the pairing of `CE_VERIFIED` with `NOT_SELECTED` or `PASSED` permits a grant, a `FAILED` audit forces the denial branch of §3.4, and its fraud complaint is opened from the employer names in the very `practice_hour_periods` the audit rejected.

### 3.6. Complaints: Intake, Triage, Investigation, Evidence Holds, and Disposition

An `Investigator` intakes a complaint against a credential. The payload names the credential through `license_id`, records who complained through `complainant_type` — one of `PATIENT`, `EMPLOYER`, `SELF_REPORT`, `BOARD_INITIATED`, or `OTHER_STATE_BOARD` — classifies the conduct through `violation_category`, one of `PRACTICE_STANDARD`, `SUBSTANCE_USE`, `FRAUD`, `BOUNDARY_VIOLATION`, or `CRIMINAL_CONDUCT`, states the `incident_date` on which the conduct is alleged to have occurred, lists the `allegation_codes` asserted, and lists the `employer_custodian_codes` — the record custodians whose files may later be demanded. Both code arrays default to empty. A new complaint takes `status` `RECEIVED`, `jurisdiction_finding` `PENDING`, and a `received_at` stamped from the logical clock, with `consolidated_into_complaint_id`, `triaged_by_user_id`, `investigator_user_id`, and `disposition_at` all null. `complainant_type` matters far beyond record-keeping: a `SELF_REPORT` earns mitigation credit in the sanction-floor calculation of §3.8, and `SUBSTANCE_USE` is the only category that opens the alternative-to-discipline route.

**Triage** records the jurisdiction finding and branches three ways on the supplied `jurisdiction_finding`, and may only be taken from `RECEIVED`. Confirming jurisdiction moves `status` to `TRIAGED` and requires that the credential complained of not have gone `LAPSED`; a lapsed credential is outside the board's reach and the attempt is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. Finding no jurisdiction moves `status` to the terminal `DISMISSED`. Finding the complaint duplicative moves `status` to the terminal `CONSOLIDATED` and requires a `target_complaint_id`: the target's identifier is written to this complaint's `consolidated_into_complaint_id`, and the duplicate's `allegation_codes` and `employer_custodian_codes` are merged onto the target's own arrays. The merge is a de-duplicating append that preserves the target's existing order first and then adds only those codes the target does not already carry, in the duplicate's own order; nothing is sorted and nothing already present is repeated. The consolidation target must exist, must not be the complaint being triaged, must concern the same `license_id`, and must itself have a `status` of `RECEIVED`, `TRIAGED`, `INVESTIGATION_OPEN`, or `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE` — a target that is itself already `CONSOLIDATED`, `DISMISSED`, or `CLOSED` cannot absorb another complaint. Any failure of those conditions is refused with `ERR_CONSOLIDATION_TARGET_INVALID`. Whichever branch is taken, the acting investigator's identity is recorded in `triaged_by_user_id`.

**Opening the investigation** moves `status` from `TRIAGED` to `INVESTIGATION_OPEN`, records the acting investigator in `investigator_user_id`, and cascades onto the credential and the nurse. The credential's `encumbrance_status` moves from `CLEAR` to `INVESTIGATION_PENDING` — and only from `CLEAR`, so a credential already `RESTRICTED` or `SUSPENDED` keeps its stronger encumbrance — while its `open_encumbrance_count` is incremented by one regardless. The nurse's aggregate `status` moves from `ACTIVE` to `ENCUMBERED`. Two concurrent investigations against the same credential therefore leave `encumbrance_status` at `INVESTIGATION_PENDING` with `open_encumbrance_count` at 2, and closing only one of them must not clear the encumbrance; the reconciliation rule of §2.7 is what enforces that.

**Records demands** are issued under an open investigation, and each one places a hold on a custodian's files. The payload declares the `record_class` — `EMPLOYER_PERSONNEL`, `EMPLOYER_INCIDENT`, `PATIENT_CHART`, or `PHARMACY_DISPENSING` — names the `custodian_code` the demand is served on, sets the hold's inclusive `hold_start_at` and exclusive `hold_end_at`, and may carry a `phi_authorization_id`. A new demand takes `status` `REQUESTED` and `page_count` 0, with `produced_at` null.

Three rules gate a demand. First, protected health information requires authorisation: when `record_class` is `PATIENT_CHART` or `PHARMACY_DISPENSING`, a non-empty `phi_authorization_id` must be present, and its absence is refused with `ERR_PHI_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`. The other two record classes need no authorisation. Second, the custodian must be linked to the complaint: `custodian_code` must appear in the complaint's `employer_custodian_codes`, and a demand served on an unlinked custodian is refused with `ERR_CUSTODIAN_NOT_LINKED`. Third, a custodian's capacity to bear concurrent holds is finite. The system counts the existing demands, across every complaint in the system, that carry the same `custodian_code`, hold a `status` of `REQUESTED` or `PRODUCED`, and whose hold window overlaps the requested one. Overlap is half-open: two windows overlap when the first starts strictly before the second ends and the second starts strictly before the first ends, so windows that merely touch — one ending exactly when the next begins — do not overlap and do not compete. Withdrawn demands never count. If that count has already reached the board's `max_concurrent_custodian_holds`, the demand is refused with `ERR_CUSTODIAN_HOLD_CAPACITY`. Because the authorisation rule is evaluated before the capacity rule, a request for a patient chart with no authorisation against a saturated custodian reports the authorisation failure.

Recording a **production** moves the demand from `REQUESTED` to `PRODUCED`, writes the supplied `page_count`, and stamps `produced_at` from the logical clock. Production releases the hold in the sense that the records are now in hand, but the demand still occupies its window against capacity until it is withdrawn. A demand may also move from `REQUESTED` to the terminal `WITHDRAWN`, which the system does on its own when a complaint is dismissed.

**Closing the evidence phase** moves the complaint from `INVESTIGATION_OPEN` to `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE`, and is permitted only when the complaint has at least one records demand and every one of them stands at `PRODUCED` or `WITHDRAWN`; a complaint with no demands at all, or with even one still `REQUESTED`, cannot close its evidence phase and the attempt is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.

**Disposing** of the complaint is the investigator's final act on it and branches three ways on the supplied `disposition`, permitted from `INVESTIGATION_OPEN` or `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE`, stamping `disposition_at` from the logical clock. Dismissing moves `status` to the terminal `DISMISSED`, withdraws every records demand on the complaint still standing at `REQUESTED`, and reconciles the credential's encumbrance: `open_encumbrance_count` is decremented, and if it reaches zero the credential returns to `CLEAR` and the privilege determination is re-run, restoring compact standing where the propagation rules allow. Referring for a hearing moves `status` to `REFERRED_FOR_HEARING` and leaves the encumbrance exactly where it is. Offering alternative-to-discipline monitoring moves `status` to `ATD_OFFERED`, and is the most tightly gated of the three: the complaint's `violation_category` must be `SUBSTANCE_USE`, the nurse must have no monitoring agreement standing at `BREACHED`, and the nurse's `prior_discipline_count` must be 0. A nurse who has already broken a monitoring agreement, or who has ever been disciplined, gets no second diversion, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_ATD_INELIGIBLE`. Complaints may be listed and narrowed by `license_id`, `consolidated_into_complaint_id`, `violation_category`, or `status`.

### 3.7. Alternative-to-Discipline Monitoring

Monitoring is the board's non-disciplinary route for impaired practitioners, and it is offered by a `BoardMember` on a complaint standing at `ATD_OFFERED`. The offer names the participant through `nurse_id` and the originating complaint through `complaint_id`, fixes the monitoring period with `start_date` and `end_date`, and sets three programme terms: `narcotic_access_restricted`, defaulting to true; `required_screens_per_quarter`, defaulting to 4; and `worksite_approval_required`, defaulting to true. The offering board member's own identity is recorded in `approved_by_user_id`, which is what later disqualifies that member from voting on the same nurse. A new agreement takes `status` `OFFERED`, with `compliance_report_count` and `missed_screen_count` both 0 and `executed_at` null.

Monitoring periods for one nurse may not overlap, and unlike the evidence-hold rule this interval is **closed** at both ends and carries a clearance buffer. The proposed period, running from `start_date` through `end_date` inclusive, must not intersect the period of any existing agreement of the same nurse whose `status` is `OFFERED`, `EXECUTED`, `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW`, `COMPLETED`, or `BREACHED` — completed and breached history still blocks, only a `TERMINATED` agreement does not — and the new `start_date` must fall strictly after the prior agreement's `end_date` advanced by the board's `monitoring_clearance_gap_days`. Because the interval is closed, touching endpoints conflict: a new `start_date` exactly equal to a prior `end_date` is a conflict, and so is a `start_date` landing anywhere inside the clearance buffer. Any such collision is refused with `ERR_MONITORING_PERIOD_CONFLICT`.

**Execution** is the participant's own act: the caller must hold the `Applicant` role and their `X-User-ID` must equal the agreement's `nurse_id`, the agreement must stand at `OFFERED`, and the linked complaint must stand at `ATD_OFFERED`. Execution stamps `executed_at` from the logical clock and moves `status` from `OFFERED` to `EXECUTED`; if `start_date` is already at or before the logical clock, the agreement is advanced further to `ACTIVE` in the very same request, and only a future `start_date` leaves it sitting at `EXECUTED`. The linked complaint moves from `ATD_OFFERED` to `ATD_ACTIVE`, and the nurse's `atd_participation_count` is incremented by one. An agreement left at `EXECUTED` is picked up by the clock sweep of §2.4: every `EXECUTED` agreement whose `start_date` is at or before the new clock value moves to `ACTIVE`, and those identifiers are reported in `activated_agreement_ids`.

**Monitoring events** are recorded by a `MonitoringCoordinator` against an executed agreement, and the supplied `event_type` selects one of four very different behaviours. The optional `missed_screens` accompanies a missed-screen report and the optional `note` may accompany any event.

A `COMPLIANCE_REPORT` increments `compliance_report_count` by one and resets `missed_screen_count` to 0; if the agreement was sitting at `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW` it is returned to `ACTIVE`, completing the cycle from `ACTIVE` to `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW` and back. A `MISSED_SCREEN` increases `missed_screen_count` by the reported `missed_screens`, and once the running total reaches 3 or more the agreement is forced from `ACTIVE` to `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW`. Because a compliance report zeroes the counter, a participant who misses two screens, files a report, and then misses two more is not suspended; the three must accumulate without an intervening report.

A `COMPLETION` closes the programme successfully and is the strictest event: the agreement must stand at `ACTIVE`, the logical clock must be strictly after `end_date` — completion on the closing day itself is premature — and `compliance_report_count` must be at least 1. Completion moves the agreement to the terminal `COMPLETED`, moves the linked complaint from `ATD_ACTIVE` to the terminal `CLOSED`, and reconciles the credential's encumbrance, returning it from `INVESTIGATION_PENDING` to `CLEAR` once `open_encumbrance_count` reaches zero. Successful completion is deliberately silent: **no** notification report of any kind is queued, because a diversion programme completed without breach is not a reportable disciplinary action.

A `BREACH` is the loud counterpart, may be declared from either `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW`, and runs an identical four-link cascade from both. Link one moves the agreement to the terminal `BREACHED`. Link two moves the linked complaint from `ATD_ACTIVE` to `PROCEEDING_OPENED` — the diversion has failed and the matter becomes contested. Link three moves the credential's `encumbrance_status` to `SUSPENDED`, permitted from `INVESTIGATION_PENDING` or `RESTRICTED`. Link four sets the credential's `multistate_privilege_status` to `PRIVILEGE_REVOKED` and queues a notification report on the `NPDB` channel with `source_entity_type` `MONITORING_AGREEMENT`, `report_kind` `INITIAL`, `basis_code` `ATD_BREACH`, and `status` `QUEUED`; the nurse's aggregate `status` is set to `ENCUMBERED`. A `BREACHED` agreement is permanent history: it blocks any future alternative-to-discipline offer to that nurse, it adds aggravation to any future sanction floor, and it blocks the reuse of its monitoring period.

One further transition is driven by the disciplinary track rather than by the coordinator. When a revocation order issues against the nurse, every agreement of that nurse standing at `OFFERED`, `EXECUTED`, `ACTIVE`, or `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW` is moved to the terminal `TERMINATED`, with no field writes beyond the status change. Agreements may be listed and narrowed by `nurse_id`, `complaint_id`, or `status`.

### 3.8. Disciplinary Proceedings: Notice, Quorum, Panel Voting, and the Final Order

A proceeding is opened by a `BoardMember` on a complaint standing at `REFERRED_FOR_HEARING`. The payload names the complaint through `complaint_id`, carries the `expected_complaint_version` described in §2.8, lists the panel through `seated_member_user_ids`, fixes the `hearing_date`, and may override `notice_period_days`, which defaults to 30. Segregation of duties is applied at creation and is not negotiable afterwards: the calling board member's identity becomes `presiding_member_user_id` and is **removed** from `seated_member_user_ids` if it appears there, so the presiding member is never part of the panel that votes. Recusals are computed at the same moment: `recused_member_user_ids` is populated with every seated member whose identity appears as `approved_by_user_id` on any monitoring agreement of the complaint's nurse — a board member who once offered this nurse a diversion agreement cannot sit in judgement of them. The proceeding takes `status` `NOTICED`, stamps `noticed_at` from the logical clock, starts `continuance_count` at 0, `votes` as an empty array, `sanction_floor_tier` and `adopted_sanction_tier` at `NONE`, and `ordered_restriction_codes` as an empty array with `order_issued_at` null. The complaint moves from `REFERRED_FOR_HEARING` to `PROCEEDING_OPENED` and its `version` is advanced by one. Creation is refused with `ERR_NOTICE_PERIOD_NOT_MET` if the `hearing_date` does not sit at or after `noticed_at` advanced by `notice_period_days`.

**Hearing events** drive the proceeding, and the supplied `event_type` selects one of four acts, with `new_hearing_date` accompanying a continuance.

`CONVENE` is permitted from `NOTICED` or `CONTINUED` and moves the proceeding to `HEARING_CONVENED`. Two conditions must hold: the `hearing_date` current at the moment of the convene call must sit at or after `noticed_at` advanced by `notice_period_days`, refused otherwise with `ERR_NOTICE_PERIOD_NOT_MET`; and the panel must have quorum, meaning the count of `seated_member_user_ids` less `recused_member_user_ids` must be at least 3, refused otherwise with `ERR_QUORUM_NOT_MET`. Because the notice check is evaluated before the quorum check, a convene that both violates the notice period and lacks quorum reports `ERR_NOTICE_PERIOD_NOT_MET`. Convening recomputes `recused_member_user_ids` against the nurse's monitoring history as it now stands and freezes the sanction floor into `sanction_floor_tier`.

`CONTINUE` moves `HEARING_CONVENED` to `CONTINUED`, replaces `hearing_date` with the supplied `new_hearing_date`, resets `noticed_at` to the logical clock — so the notice period must be satisfied afresh against the new date — and increments `continuance_count` by one. `REOPEN` moves `DELIBERATION` back to `HEARING_CONVENED`, resets `adopted_sanction_tier` to `NONE`, clears `votes` back to an empty array so the panel must vote again from scratch, and also increments `continuance_count` by one. The two events share one budget of three: while `continuance_count` is below 3 either is accepted, and once it has reached 3 neither is, refused with `ERR_CONTINUANCE_LIMIT_REACHED`. Two continuances and one reopening therefore exhaust the allowance jointly.

`CLOSE_HEARING` moves `HEARING_CONVENED` to `DELIBERATION`, requires at least one vote cast by a seated non-recused member, and writes the tallied result into `adopted_sanction_tier`. When the tally yields `NONE`, the system advances the proceeding further to the terminal `DISMISSED_BY_PANEL` in the same request, moves the complaint from `PROCEEDING_OPENED` to the terminal `DISMISSED`, and returns the credential's encumbrance from `INVESTIGATION_PENDING` to `CLEAR` once `open_encumbrance_count` reaches zero — a panel that cannot agree on any sanction dissolves the encumbrance.

**Votes** are recorded one at a time by seated board members, each naming the `proposed_sanction_tier` they support. The array is append-only: each vote appends an object carrying `voter_user_id` set to the caller, the `proposed_sanction_tier`, and `cast_at` stamped from the logical clock, and re-voting adds a further entry rather than editing the earlier one. Two guards apply, in order. The caller's `X-User-ID` must not be the proceeding's `presiding_member_user_id`, refused otherwise with `ERR_PRESIDING_MEMBER_CANNOT_VOTE`. The caller must not appear as `approved_by_user_id` on any monitoring agreement of the complaint's nurse, and must appear in `seated_member_user_ids`; a conflicted or unseated caller is refused with `ERR_VOTER_CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST`.

**The sanction floor** is computed once, at convene, and frozen. It is an integer tier index over a six-tier ladder in which index 1 is `REPRIMAND`, 2 is `FINE`, 3 is `PROBATION`, 4 is `PRACTICE_RESTRICTION`, 5 is `SUSPENSION`, and 6 is `REVOCATION`. The base index comes from the complaint's `violation_category`: a practice-standard allegation starts at 1, fraud and substance use start at 3, a boundary violation starts at 4, and criminal conduct starts at 5. Aggravation is then accumulated as twice the nurse's `prior_discipline_count`, plus 2 if the credential's `encumbrance_status` is `RESTRICTED`, plus 3 if at least one records demand of the complaint has `record_class` `PHARMACY_DISPENSING` and `status` `PRODUCED`, plus 1 for each monitoring agreement of the nurse standing at `BREACHED`. Mitigation is accumulated as 2 if the complaint's `complainant_type` is `SELF_REPORT`, plus 1 if the nurse's `atd_participation_count` is 0, plus 2 if the complaint has at least one records demand and every one of them stands at `PRODUCED`. The net score is aggravation less mitigation and may be negative. The floor index is the base index plus the net score halved and rounded **toward positive infinity** — so a net of negative three contributes negative one, not negative two — and the result is then clamped to the range 1 through 6. One override applies afterwards: if the complaint's `status` is `ATD_ACTIVE` at the moment of the convene call, the floor index is raised to at least 3, because a participant still in a diversion programme floors at probation. `sanction_floor_tier` is the tier at the resulting index. Every term is a count or a boolean, so nothing about this computation is order-sensitive.

**The tally** runs at close of hearing, and its evaluation order is pinned because the threshold that applies depends on which tier is adopted while the tier that can be adopted depends on the threshold. The denominator is fixed first: the seated set is `seated_member_user_ids` less `recused_member_user_ids`, the presiding member having already been excluded at creation, and its size is the seated count. Each seated member is then reduced to one effective ballot, namely their vote with the greatest `cast_at`; if two of that member's votes share the same `cast_at`, the one appearing later in the `votes` array wins. Ballots cast by anyone not in the seated set are discarded entirely, from both numerator and denominator. Support is then cumulative in the escalating-motion sense: a member voting a more severe tier also supports every less severe tier. The system walks the ladder from index 6 down to index 1, and at each index counts the seated members whose effective tier index is at or above that index. The threshold applied is the board's `revocation_vote_threshold_bp` at index 6 and the board's `majority_vote_threshold_bp` at every other index, and the test is integer cross-multiplication: the support count multiplied by 10000 must be at or above the threshold multiplied by the seated count. The first index that clears its own threshold is adopted, and the walk stops there. If no index clears, `adopted_sanction_tier` is `NONE`. Members who never voted are still counted in the denominator, so abstentions weigh against every tier.

**The final order** is issued by a `BoardMember` from `DELIBERATION`, optionally supplying `ordered_restriction_codes`. Two conditions must hold: `adopted_sanction_tier` must not be `NONE`, and its tier index must be at or above the tier index of the frozen `sanction_floor_tier`. A panel that adopted a sanction milder than the floor is refused with `ERR_SANCTION_BELOW_FLOOR` and the proceeding stays in `DELIBERATION`, where it may be reopened and revoted. Issuing the order moves the proceeding to the terminal `ORDER_ISSUED`, stamps `order_issued_at` from the logical clock, stores the supplied `ordered_restriction_codes`, moves the complaint from `PROCEEDING_OPENED` to the terminal `CLOSED` **without** returning the encumbrance to `CLEAR` — a disciplined credential stays encumbered — recomputes `open_encumbrance_count`, and recomputes the nurse's `prior_discipline_count`, which now counts this order.

The credential consequences follow the adopted tier. A `REPRIMAND` or a `FINE` leaves both credential dimensions untouched. `PROBATION` or `PRACTICE_RESTRICTION` moves `encumbrance_status` to `RESTRICTED`, permitted from `INVESTIGATION_PENDING` or `SUSPENDED`, and replaces the credential's `practice_restriction_codes` with the order's `ordered_restriction_codes`; the privilege determination is re-run and yields `SINGLE_STATE`, because a restriction now exists. `SUSPENSION` moves `encumbrance_status` to `SUSPENDED`, permitted from `INVESTIGATION_PENDING` or `RESTRICTED`, and sets `multistate_privilege_status` to `PRIVILEGE_REVOKED`. `REVOCATION` runs the widest cascade in the system, permitted from `INVESTIGATION_PENDING`, `RESTRICTED`, or `SUSPENDED`: this credential's `encumbrance_status` becomes `REVOKED` and its `license_status` becomes `SURRENDERED`; its `multistate_privilege_status` becomes `PRIVILEGE_REVOKED`; every other credential of the same nurse whose `base_license_id` equals this credential's identifier is dragged down with it, moving to `license_status` `SURRENDERED`, `encumbrance_status` `REVOKED`, and `multistate_privilege_status` `PRIVILEGE_REVOKED`, so revoking a registered-nurse credential necessarily revokes the advanced-practice credential built on it; every monitoring agreement of the nurse standing at `OFFERED`, `EXECUTED`, `ACTIVE`, or `SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW` moves to `TERMINATED`; and the nurse's aggregate `status` becomes `REVOKED` once every credential of that nurse carries `encumbrance_status` `REVOKED`.

Reporting obligations attach to the order in a fixed shape. One notification report is queued per credential the order encumbers, on the `NPDB` channel — or on the `NURSE_AIDE_REGISTRY` channel for a `NURSE_AIDE` credential — with `source_entity_type` `DISCIPLINARY_PROCEEDING`, `report_kind` `INITIAL`, and `basis_code` equal to the adopted tier, plus **exactly one** report on the `INTERSTATE_COMPACT` channel with `basis_code` `PRIVILEGE_LOSS` regardless of how many credentials were touched. A `REPRIMAND` queues neither. A revocation that drags down one dependent credential therefore produces two national reports and one interstate report, not two of each.

### 3.9. Interstate and National Reporting

Notification reports are the board's outbound obligations, and they are created only as side effects of the acts described above — never directly by a client. Each report records the practitioner it concerns in `subject_nurse_id` and identifies the act that generated it through a discriminated pair: `source_entity_type`, which is `LICENSE`, `DISCIPLINARY_PROCEEDING`, `MONITORING_AGREEMENT`, or `LICENSE_APPLICATION`, and `source_entity_id`, which holds the identifier of the record of that type. `source_entity_id` is deliberately **not** a declared foreign key to any single table: it is polymorphic, and must be resolved against whichever entity family `source_entity_type` names. A report also carries its `channel` — `NPDB` for the national practitioner data bank, `INTERSTATE_COMPACT` for the compact commission, or `NURSE_AIDE_REGISTRY` for aide registration — its `report_kind`, which is `INITIAL`, `REVISION`, or `VOID` and defaults to `INITIAL`, its `basis_code` naming the ground for the report, and a human-readable `payload_summary`.

The transmission lifecycle is tracked in `status` alongside three timestamps. A newly queued report holds `status` `QUEUED` with `queued_at` stamped from the logical clock and both `transmitted_at` and `acknowledged_at` null. A `SystemAdmin` advances the lifecycle by supplying a `to_status`. Moving to `TRANSMITTED` requires the report to be `QUEUED` and stamps `transmitted_at`. Moving to `ACKNOWLEDGED`, which is terminal, requires the report to be `TRANSMITTED` and stamps `acknowledged_at`. Moving to `VOID`, also terminal, is permitted from either `QUEUED` or `TRANSMITTED`, and additionally overwrites `report_kind` with `VOID` — a voided report is marked as such in its own kind, not merely in its status. An acknowledged report cannot be voided. Reports may be listed and narrowed by `subject_nurse_id`, by `source_entity_type`, by `source_entity_id`, by `channel`, by `report_kind`, or by `status`, which together allow an auditor to reconstruct exactly what was reported about a practitioner, on what basis, and whether the receiving authority ever acknowledged it.

## 4. API Endpoint Definitions

Every endpoint below is mounted at the root of the API. Endpoints that create a new resource return HTTP 201 Created; every other successful call returns HTTP 200 OK, including the action endpoints that advance a state machine and the computed-read endpoints. Path parameters are written as `{id}` and always denote the identifier of the resource named by the immediately preceding collection segment. Request bodies are JSON objects whose fields are named exactly as described in §3; list endpoints accept their filters plus `limit` and `offset` as query parameters. Every endpoint requires the `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` headers of §2.1.
