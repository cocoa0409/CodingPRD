# Wrestling Weight Certification API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

State high school wrestling is one of the few interscholastic sports where an athlete's competitive category is defined by body weight, and where the temptation to shed weight quickly has produced a long history of documented harm. The regulatory answer adopted across state athletic associations is a certification regime: before a wrestler may step onto a mat, a credentialed assessor measures body composition, verifies hydration, and derives a *minimum certified weight* below which that wrestler may never compete. From that single measurement flows a chain of downstream artefacts — a lowest permissible weight class, a certified week-by-week descent schedule that caps how fast the athlete may lose weight, published growth-allowance dates that relax the class limits as the season progresses, and per-event weigh-in allowances that account for multi-day tournaments.

This document specifies the API that operates that regime end to end for a single state program. The service is the authoritative certification database: it holds the season's regulatory constants, the assessor roster and its credentialing state, every assessment and its appeal history, every descent plan and its milestone schedule, every event with its brackets and weigh-ins, and the health-and-safety flag ledger that can escalate into disqualification for unhealthy weight management. It is also the system of record consulted immediately before each competition entry — the pre-competition eligibility check that decides whether a given wrestler may be entered in a given class on a given date is a first-class, logged operation rather than an incidental side calculation.

Two design pressures shape almost every rule below. The first is **auditability**: because a certification result can retroactively invalidate a season's worth of competition, the service records who measured what, when, and under which credential, and it exposes an audit facility that can retroactively void a suspect assessor's work and cascade the consequences through wrestlers, plans and brackets. The second is **segregation of duties**: the assessor who produced a result may neither review the appeal against it nor perform the appeal-ordered retest, a physician may escalate a health concern but may not disqualify anyone, and only state association staff may disqualify. All resources are mounted at the root of the host; there is no additional URL prefix.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to every endpoint must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role that principal is acting in. If either header is absent, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the five declared role names, the request is refused with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). When both headers are well formed but the named role is not among the roles authorized for the endpoint being called, the refusal is `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403). Authentication is always evaluated before authorization, and both are evaluated before any resource in the URL is looked up.

The five roles are `StateOffice`, `Assessor`, `TeamCoach`, `WeighInOfficial` and `MedicalReviewer`.

`StateOffice` is state athletic association staff. It owns the season and its regulatory constants, growth-allowance publication, the logical clock, assessor registration, training records and administrative suspension, team registration, reviewer assignment on appeals, descent-plan certification, the event lifecycle, seeding and bracket overrides, program audits, the season compliance run, and wrestler disqualification. When `X-User-Role` is `StateOffice`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server never compares it to any entity's `id`.

`Assessor` is a credentialed body-composition assessor. When `X-User-Role` is `Assessor`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Assessor` row; ownership checks on any resource that references an `Assessor` compare the resource's assessor reference to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, an assessment may only be opened with an `assessor_id` equal to the caller's `X-User-ID`, only that same assessor may finalize it, and only the assessor recorded as an appeal's `reviewing_assessor_id` may order retests or decide that appeal.

`TeamCoach` is a school coach. When `X-User-Role` is `TeamCoach`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Team`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Team` compare the resource's team reference to `X-User-ID`. A coach may therefore only manage wrestlers whose `team_id` equals `X-User-ID`, only file appeals carrying their own `team_id`, only open or revise descent plans for their own wrestlers, and only enter their own wrestlers in competitions. A violation is `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403).

`WeighInOfficial` is the event-day official who operates the scale. When `X-User-Role` is `WeighInOfficial`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it as `official_user_id` on each `WeighIn` the official creates, but never compares it to any entity's `id`. This role holds no authority over certification, plans or brackets.

`MedicalReviewer` is the program physician who triages health flags. When `X-User-Role` is `MedicalReviewer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it as `reviewer_user_id` on the flag being reviewed, but never compares it to any entity's `id`. Escalation of a health flag belongs to this role alone; disqualification does not.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every list endpoint accepts two optional query parameters. `limit` defaults to 10, has a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 100. `offset` defaults to 0 and has a minimum of 0. A `limit` below 1 or above 100, or a negative `offset`, is a malformed request and is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400).

Unless a specific endpoint states a different order, list results are sorted by `created_at` ascending. Two endpoints override that default because their natural reading order is domain-defined: the season's growth-allowance list is returned in `effective_date` ascending order, and a plan's milestone list is returned in `week_index` ascending order.

List responses carry `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside `meta`. `total_count` is the number of rows matching the filter before the window is applied, and `has_more` is true when rows remain beyond the current window. Single-resource responses omit all four.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and the Logical Clock

Every stored resource carries a server-minted `id` that is a UUID string with no type prefix, and a `created_at` timestamp in ISO 8601 UTC format with three fractional digits. Most resources additionally carry `updated_at`, refreshed on every mutation and formatted identically, and an integer `version` that starts at 1 and increments by one on every mutation of that row.

Three resource types are deliberately append-only and therefore carry only `id` and `created_at` — for `WeighIn`, `EligibilityCheck` and `GrowthAllowance`, both `updated_at` and `version` are absent from the stored row and from every response. A weigh-in reading, a logged certification-database check and a published growth-allowance date are historical facts; the service never edits them in place.

All time-sensitive evaluation reads a **logical clock** rather than wall-clock time. The clock's startup default is exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`. Until the clock is explicitly set, every `filed_at`, `raised_at`, `recorded_at`, `checked_at` and `disqualified_at` stamp, and every window, expiry and grace-period comparison, reads that fixed default and never falls back to real time. A `StateOffice` operator may move the clock to any UTC instant by supplying a `to_iso8601` value; the appeal filing window, the milestone grace window, assessor credential expiry and the compliance run all consult the clock afterwards.

### 2.5. Season Regulatory Constants and the Weight-Class Ladder

A `Season` is the container for every regulatory constant the rest of the system reads, and each constant has a default that applies when the season is created without it. The `weight_classes` array — a non-empty list of distinct positive integers in strictly ascending order — is the ladder every eligibility determination walks; its default runs 106, 113, 120, 126, 132, 138, 144, 150, 157, 165, 175, 190, 215 and 285 pounds. The body-fat floors are `min_body_fat_pct_male`, defaulting to 7.0 percent and required to be greater than 0 and less than 30, and `min_body_fat_pct_female`, defaulting to 12.0 percent and required to be greater than 0 and less than 40. Hydration is governed by `hydration_max_specific_gravity`, defaulting to 1.025 and required to be greater than 1.000 and less than 1.060. Descent speed is capped by `max_weekly_descent_pct`, defaulting to 1.5 percent and required to be greater than 0 and no more than 3.0.

The remaining constants govern the program's administrative machinery. `assessor_min_training_hours` (default 8, at least 1) is the cumulative training total at which a provisional assessor becomes certified. `audit_fail_threshold_pct` (default 2.0, greater than 0) and `audit_min_sample_count` (default 3, at least 1) drive audit scoring. `max_consecutive_day_allowance_lbs` (default 2.0, at least 0) caps the multi-day weigh-in allowance. `appeal_filing_window_days` (default 5, at least 1) bounds how long a coach has to contest a result. `max_appeal_retest_rounds` (default 2, at least 1) bounds the retest cycle. `assessor_daily_assessment_capacity` (default 3, at least 1) bounds how many assessments one assessor may stamp with the same UTC calendar day. `plan_breach_limit` (default 2, at least 1) is the number of missed milestones at which a certified descent plan is declared breached. A season also carries a human-readable `name` of at most 120 characters, a `start_date` given as an ISO 8601 calendar date in `YYYY-MM-DD` form, a `status` that begins at `SETUP`, and two derived counters: `team_count`, the number of teams registered into it, and `certified_wrestler_count`, the number of wrestlers on its teams whose certification dimension currently reads `CERTIFIED`.

Any supplied `weight_class_lbs`, `target_class_lbs` or `requested_class_lbs` must be a member of the owning season's `weight_classes` array. A value that is numerically well formed but absent from the ladder is refused with `ERR_INVALID_WEIGHT_CLASS` (HTTP 422), which is distinct from the HTTP 400 `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` used for a value that is not a number at all.

### 2.6. Measurement Precision and Rounding Discipline

Weights, allowances and body-fat percentages are carried to **one decimal place, rounded half up**. Where a formula rounds more than once, the rounding order is pinned and load-bearing: an intermediate value that the specification says is rounded must be rounded *before* it is used as an input to the next step, and computing a closed-form equivalent from unrounded intermediates is incorrect. Audit discrepancy figures are the sole exception to the one-decimal rule: they are carried to **two decimal places, rounded half up**, applied both to each per-sample deviation and again to any mean taken over those deviations. Weight comparisons — a recorded weight against an allowable weight, a class limit against a floor — are performed on the one-decimal values. Seed numbers, weight classes, week indices, severity figures and counters are integers.

Every threshold in this specification states its own boundary inclusivity explicitly. As a general rule, the regime is written so that landing exactly on a limit is permitted: a minimum certified weight that equals a class limit certifies into that class rather than the next one up, a recorded weight exactly equal to an effective limit passes, an appeal filed at the exact boundary instant is inside the window, and a descent rate exactly equal to the permitted maximum is accepted.

### 2.7. Version Pinning on Wrestler and Entry Writes

Two write operations mutate rows that other concurrent workflows also rewrite, so both require the client to prove it is working from a current view.

Opening a descent plan for a wrestler requires a non-optional `expected_wrestler_version` field in the request body, carrying the client's last observed `version` of that `Wrestler`. Plan creation legitimately supersedes the wrestler's prior non-terminal plan and rewrites the wrestler's certification dimension, so a writer holding a stale view would silently discard a plan, or an assessment repoint, that it never saw. If `expected_wrestler_version` does not equal the wrestler's current stored `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_WRESTLER_VERSION` (HTTP 412).

Changing an entry's weight class requires a non-optional `expected_entry_version` field carrying the client's last observed `version` of that `TournamentEntry`. A class change looks idempotent but is not: it reseeds two bracket groups, so a writer whose view predates an earlier change — or predates a seeding run that assigned a `seed_number` — would move the entry out of a bracket state it never observed. If `expected_entry_version` does not equal the entry's current stored `version`, the change is rejected with `ERR_STALE_ENTRY_VERSION` (HTTP 412).

Both checks are pure stale-version comparisons against the parent row's stored `version`. No other endpoint in this specification accepts a version field, and none may be made to require one.

### 2.8. Path Resolution and Validation Precedence

Path identifiers are resolved strictly left to right, and the first unresolvable segment determines the error. The not-found codes are `ERR_SEASON_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TEAM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_WRESTLER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ASSESSOR_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ASSESSMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_APPEAL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PLAN_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EVENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ENTRY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_WEIGH_IN_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ELIGIBILITY_CHECK_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_HEALTH_FLAG_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_AUDIT_NOT_FOUND`, all HTTP 404. Foreign keys supplied inside a request body are resolved after every path identifier, in the order the fields are listed for that operation; the same not-found codes apply. Body-level foreign-key resolution is what guards plan creation, roster-scoped assessment creation, reviewer assignment, competition entry, weigh-in recording and audit opening.

Because many operations stack a dozen checks, the order in which they are evaluated is itself part of the contract. Across the whole API the precedence is, from first to last: header authentication (`ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`); endpoint role authorization (`ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`); path-identifier existence; body foreign-key existence; resource ownership (`ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`); request-body shape, enum membership, numeric range, pagination bounds and weight-class membership (`ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` or `ERR_INVALID_WEIGHT_CLASS`); version pinning (`ERR_STALE_WRESTLER_VERSION` or `ERR_STALE_ENTRY_VERSION`); the disqualified-wrestler freeze (`ERR_WRESTLER_DISQUALIFIED`); the season-open requirement (`ERR_SEASON_NOT_OPEN`); the from-state check for the requested transition; and only then the domain-specific safety, referential, temporal, capacity and uniqueness rules described in section 3. The disqualified-wrestler freeze sitting *ahead of* the season-open check is deliberate: a disqualified wrestler is frozen out of every downstream write regardless of whether the season would otherwise have accepted the operation.

Field ranges enforced by the shape check are: `measured_weight_lbs` and `recorded_weight_lbs` between 60.0 and 400.0 inclusive; `body_fat_pct` between 1.0 and 50.0 inclusive; `urine_specific_gravity` between 1.000 and 1.060 inclusive; `allowance_lbs` greater than 0 and no more than 4.0; `requested_weeks` at least 1; and `grade` between 9 and 12 inclusive.

### 2.9. Conserved Quantities: the Severity Ledger and the Seed Sum

Two aggregate figures in this system are **conserved**: they are recomputed from their constituent rows on every relevant mutation rather than incremented blindly, and a compensating action must restore them to exactly their prior value.

`Team.open_flag_severity_total` is the severity ledger. It is the sum of `severity` over every `HealthFlag` whose wrestler belongs to the team and whose `status` is `OPEN` or `ESCALATED`; flags in `REVIEWED` or `DISMISSED` are excluded, which is precisely what makes medical review a true compensating action on the ledger. Wrestlers of every certification state contribute to it, including those already `DISQUALIFIED`. Severity is pinned per flag source and is never client-supplied: a hydration failure contributes 1, a rapid-loss flag contributes 2, a plan breach contributes 3, and a weigh-in deficit contributes 4. From that single integer the team's derived `compliance_status` follows: `CLEAR` when the total is exactly 0, `WATCH` when the total is at least 1 and no more than 4, and `PROBATION` when the total is 5 or more. Both bounds are inclusive lower bounds on integer arithmetic. The posture is recomputed on every flag raise and every flag review.

`Event.seed_integrity_sum` is the seeding invariant. It is the sum of `seed_number` over every entry of the event whose `seed_number` is not null, and after any seeding, reseeding or class-change operation it must equal the sum, taken over each occupied weight class, of the count of seeded entries in that class multiplied by one more than that count, divided by two. That is the arithmetic consequence of every class group holding a contiguous run of seed numbers beginning at 1. A class change followed by the reverse class change therefore restores `seed_integrity_sum` to exactly its pre-change value.

### 2.10. Cascade Discipline and Hierarchical Consistency

Several operations in this domain are the head of a multi-hop cascade that rewrites rows the caller never named. The rule is uniform: every hop of a cascade is applied within the same request, immediately and completely, before the response is emitted. There is no deferred or eventual reconciliation, and a partially applied cascade is never a valid outcome. Each row a cascade touches has its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` incremented, exactly as a directly addressed mutation would. Derived counters on parent rows — `team_count`, `certified_wrestler_count`, `roster_count`, `open_flag_severity_total`, `compliance_status`, `entry_count`, `weighed_in_count`, `seeded_entry_count`, `seed_integrity_sum`, `validated_assessment_count`, `voided_assessment_count`, `open_health_flag_count`, `milestone_count`, `missed_milestone_count`, `breach_count`, `revision_count`, `weight_class_change_count`, `sample_count` and `voided_assessment_count` on an audit — are all brought current in the same request.

Cascade-created rows are frequently invisible to the caller that triggered them, so each has a dedicated discovery channel: milestones generated by plan certification are readable only through the plan's milestone listing, and flags raised by hydration failures, weigh-in deficits and plan breaches are readable only through the filtered health-flag listing. Neither is ever created by direct client input; `DescentMilestone.plan_id` in particular is set only by the plan-certification cascade.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up a Season: Constants, Growth-Allowance Dates and Rosters

A season begins life in `SETUP`. In that state the state office may still adjust nothing but may register the surrounding scaffolding; the operations that actually produce regulatory facts — opening an assessment, certifying a plan, scheduling an event, entering a competitor, recording a weigh-in, publishing a growth allowance, registering a team, adding a wrestler, running the compliance sweep and opening an audit — all require the owning season to be `OPEN`, and are otherwise refused with `ERR_SEASON_NOT_OPEN`. Advancing `SETUP` to `OPEN` requires the season to actually be in `SETUP` and its `weight_classes` array to be non-empty, and increments the season's `version` by one.

Closing a season is the mirror image and is guarded. `OPEN` to `CLOSED` requires the season to be in `OPEN` and requires that no event of that season is currently in `WEIGH_IN_OPEN` or `SEEDED`; a season asked to close while an event is mid-weigh-in or sitting on an unfinished bracket is refused with `ERR_RESEED_REQUIRED`. When the close succeeds, every event of the season whose `status` is `SCHEDULED` or `WEIGH_IN_CLOSED` is swept forward to `COMPLETED`, and the season's `version` increments. `CLOSED` is terminal. Requesting a season status change that is not declared out of the current status — reopening a closed season, for instance — is `ERR_SEASON_INVALID_STATE`.

Growth-allowance dates are the mechanism by which the state relaxes every class limit as adolescent athletes grow through the season. Each published allowance carries an `effective_date` as an ISO 8601 calendar date, an `allowance_lbs` figure greater than 0 and no more than 4.0, and an optional administrative `note` which defaults to null. Publication is exclusive to the state office and requires an open season. At most one allowance may exist per season and effective date; a second publication on the same date is `ERR_GROWTH_ALLOWANCE_DATE_CONFLICT`. Allowances are cumulative and inclusive of their own effective date: wherever this specification refers to "the growth allowance total as of a date", it means the sum of `allowance_lbs` across every allowance of that season whose `effective_date` is on or before that date. Allowances are readable per season through a listing ordered by `effective_date` ascending, and each row carries the `season_id` it belongs to.

Team registration is likewise a state-office act on an open season and takes the `season_id` and a `school_name` of at most 160 characters. School names must be unique within the owning season; a collision is `ERR_DUPLICATE_SCHOOL_NAME`. A new team starts with `compliance_status` at `CLEAR`, `open_flag_severity_total` at 0 and `roster_count` at 0, and registering it increments the season's `team_count`. Teams of a season are listable and may be narrowed by `compliance_status`.

Adding a wrestler to a roster may be done by that team's own `TeamCoach` or by `StateOffice`, again only while the season is open. The payload carries the wrestler's `full_name` of at most 120 characters, a `sex` of either `M` or `F`, and a `grade` between 9 and 12. The new wrestler's `team_id` is taken from the roster being written to, and a coach whose `X-User-ID` is not that team is refused with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. Every new wrestler starts with `certification_status` at `UNCERTIFIED`, `eligibility_status` at `INELIGIBLE`, `open_health_flag_count` at 0, and `source_assessment_id`, `alpha_weight_lbs`, `body_fat_pct`, `minimum_certified_weight_lbs`, `min_weight_class_lbs`, `lowest_eligible_class_lbs`, `disqualified_at` and `certification_hold_reason` all null. Adding a wrestler increments the team's `roster_count`, which counts roster members excluding those whose certification dimension has reached `DISQUALIFIED`. Wrestlers are listable across the program with optional narrowing by `team_id`, `source_assessment_id`, `certification_status` and `eligibility_status`.

### 3.2. Assessor Credentialing, Training and Loss of Credential

An assessor is registered by the state office with a `full_name` of at most 120 characters, a `credential_number` of at most 40 characters that must be globally unique across every assessor — a duplicate is `ERR_DUPLICATE_CREDENTIAL` — a non-empty `certified_methods` array whose members are drawn from `SKINFOLD`, `BIA`, `HYDROSTATIC` and `ADP`, and a `certification_expires_on` date in `YYYY-MM-DD` form. A freshly registered assessor sits in `PROVISIONAL` with `training_hours_completed` at 0, `validated_assessment_count` and `voided_assessment_count` at 0, and `last_audit_outcome` and `suspension_reason` null.

Training is logged one record at a time, each carrying a `credential_code` identifying the course and an `hours` figure. The hours are always added to the assessor's cumulative `training_hours_completed`. When the assessor is in `PROVISIONAL` and the new cumulative total reaches or exceeds the `assessor_min_training_hours` of the most recently created season, the assessor auto-transitions `PROVISIONAL → CERTIFIED` and `version` increments. When the threshold is not reached, the hours are still added and the status stays `PROVISIONAL`. With the shipped default of eight hours, an assessor who logs three hours and then four remains provisional; a further one-hour record takes the cumulative total to exactly eight and certifies them, because the threshold comparison is inclusive.

Two paths remove a credential, and both terminate the assessor's lifecycle. Administratively, the state office may suspend a `CERTIFIED` assessor by supplying a `reason`; the assessor moves `CERTIFIED → SUSPENDED`, `suspension_reason` records the supplied text, every `DRAFT` assessment belonging to that assessor is voided — its `status` becomes `VOIDED` and its `void_reason` becomes `ASSESSOR_SUSPENDED` — and the assessor's `version` increments. Attempting to suspend an assessor who is `PROVISIONAL`, already `SUSPENDED`, or `EXPIRED` is `ERR_ASSESSOR_INVALID_STATE`. Separately, credential lapse is discovered lazily at finalize time: when a `CERTIFIED` assessor attempts to finalize an assessment and the current logical clock date is on or after their `certification_expires_on`, the assessor is moved `CERTIFIED → EXPIRED` and that status change is persisted, `version` increments, and the finalize request that discovered the lapse is then rejected with `ERR_ASSESSOR_NOT_CERTIFIED`. The same error guards every other operation that requires a credential in force: opening an assessment, being assigned as an appeal reviewer, and being named as the subject of a sampled program audit all require the referenced assessor's `status` to be exactly `CERTIFIED`, never `PROVISIONAL`, `SUSPENDED` or `EXPIRED`.

An assessor's record doubles as their quality dossier. `validated_assessment_count` counts their assessments whose `status` is `VALIDATED` or `SUPERSEDED` — a superseded assessment was validly produced and still counts. `voided_assessment_count` counts those whose `status` is `VOIDED`. `last_audit_outcome` reports the verdict of the most recently created finalized audit naming that assessor, taken in `created_at` descending order, and reads null when no such audit exists.

### 3.3. Body-Composition Assessment and the Hydration Fork

Opening an assessment is exclusive to the `Assessor` role and requires the wrestler's season to be open. The payload names the acting `assessor_id`, which must equal the caller's `X-User-ID` — a mismatch is `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` — the `method` used, an `assessed_at` instant in ISO 8601 UTC, the `measured_weight_lbs` read off the scale, the measured `body_fat_pct`, and the `urine_specific_gravity` from the hydration sample. Two optional fields, `appeal_id` and `retest_of_assessment_id`, are supplied only when the assessment is an appeal-ordered retest and are otherwise null; `retest_of_assessment_id` is the self-reference back to the earlier assessment being redone. The new row lands in `DRAFT` with `hydration_passed`, `lean_body_weight_lbs`, `minimum_certified_weight_lbs`, `min_weight_class_lbs`, `alpha_weight_lbs` and `void_reason` all null — no certified figure whatsoever is computed until finalize.

Three guards sit on creation beyond the ordinary shape and ownership checks. The chosen `method` must be a member of the acting assessor's `certified_methods`; a credentialed skinfold assessor attempting a hydrostatic reading is refused with `ERR_METHOD_NOT_CERTIFIED`. The assessor must have fewer than `assessor_daily_assessment_capacity` non-voided assessments already recorded whose `assessed_at` falls on the same UTC calendar day as the new one, counted across every wrestler and every team the assessor works with; exceeding it is `ERR_ASSESSOR_DAY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. And when the assessment is an appeal-ordered retest, segregation of duties forbids the assessor who produced the contested result from performing the retest — that collision is `ERR_SAME_ASSESSOR_CONFLICT`. A referenced `retest_of_assessment_id` that belongs to a different wrestler than the one being assessed is `ERR_ASSESSMENT_WRESTLER_MISMATCH`. A wrestler frozen at `DISQUALIFIED` cannot be assessed at all, which surfaces as `ERR_WRESTLER_DISQUALIFIED`.

Finalizing is where hydration verification runs and where the assessment forks into exactly one of two terminal-or-certified outcomes. The from-state must be `DRAFT`; a second finalize of the same assessment is `ERR_ASSESSMENT_INVALID_STATE`. The acting assessor must be the assessment's own `assessor_id`, their `status` must be `CERTIFIED` after the credential-expiry evaluation described above, the assessment's `method` must still be within their `certified_methods`, and the season must be `OPEN`. The fork itself is decided by a single comparison: `hydration_passed` is set true when the assessment's `urine_specific_gravity` is less than or equal to the season's `hydration_max_specific_gravity`, and false when it is strictly greater.

On the `DRAFT → VALIDATED` arm the certified figures are produced. The body-fat floor is chosen by the wrestler's `sex`: the season's `min_body_fat_pct_male` when the wrestler is `M`, and `min_body_fat_pct_female` when the wrestler is `F`. `lean_body_weight_lbs` is the assessment's `measured_weight_lbs` multiplied by the quantity one minus the assessment's `body_fat_pct` divided by one hundred, and that product is rounded half up to one decimal place. `minimum_certified_weight_lbs` is then the **already-rounded** `lean_body_weight_lbs` divided by the quantity one minus the chosen floor percentage divided by one hundred, with the quotient rounded half up to one decimal place. The rounding order is pinned: the unrounded lean weight must never be carried into the division. `alpha_weight_lbs` is set to the assessment's `measured_weight_lbs` unchanged, and becomes the descent baseline for every later projection. Finally `min_weight_class_lbs` is the smallest member of the season's `weight_classes` that is greater than or equal to `minimum_certified_weight_lbs`; the comparison is inclusive, so a minimum landing exactly on a class limit certifies into that class rather than the next one up. If no class in the season is greater than or equal to the computed minimum, the finalize is refused with `ERR_NO_ELIGIBLE_WEIGHT_CLASS` (HTTP 422).

The validated arm also rewrites the wrestler and its neighbours. Any *other* assessment of the same wrestler currently in `VALIDATED` is moved `VALIDATED → SUPERSEDED` and its `version` increments — with one deliberate exception: when the assessment being finalized is an appeal-ordered retest, the specific assessment contested by that appeal stays `VALIDATED`, so that the appeal-upheld void cascade still has a `VALIDATED` from-state to work with. The wrestler's `source_assessment_id` is repointed at the newly validated assessment and the wrestler's own `alpha_weight_lbs`, `body_fat_pct`, `minimum_certified_weight_lbs`, `min_weight_class_lbs` and `lowest_eligible_class_lbs` are recomputed from it. The wrestler's certification dimension moves `UNCERTIFIED → PROVISIONAL`, or `SUSPENDED → PROVISIONAL` when the prior source assessment had been voided out from under them — in that restoration case `certification_hold_reason` is cleared and every certified figure is repopulated, and the restoration requires the finalizing assessor's `status` to be `CERTIFIED`. The wrestler's `eligibility_status` is deliberately left exactly as it was: the certification dimension moves alone. The assessor's `validated_assessment_count` increments by one, and both the assessment's and the wrestler's `version` increment.

On the `DRAFT → HYDRATION_FAILED` arm the divergence is total and deliberate. `hydration_passed` is set false, and `lean_body_weight_lbs`, `minimum_certified_weight_lbs`, `min_weight_class_lbs` and `alpha_weight_lbs` are all left null — no certified figure is produced. A `HealthFlag` is raised on the wrestler with `source` `HYDRATION_FAIL`, `severity` 1 and `status` `OPEN`, and the owning team's `open_flag_severity_total` and `compliance_status` are recomputed immediately. The wrestler's `certification_status` is left unchanged and `source_assessment_id` is *not* repointed. The assessment's `version` increments. `HYDRATION_FAILED` is terminal — a failed hydration reading is redone as a new assessment, never rehabilitated in place. Both arms share one piece of appeal bookkeeping: when the finalized assessment carries a non-null `appeal_id`, that appeal's `retest_assessment_id` is set to this assessment's id and the appeal moves `RETEST_ORDERED → RETEST_COMPLETED`, regardless of which arm the fork took.

Assessments are readable individually and as a filtered list narrowed by `wrestler_id`, `assessor_id`, `retest_of_assessment_id`, `appeal_id` and `status`. Beyond `VALIDATED`, `SUPERSEDED` and `HYDRATION_FAILED`, the fifth state an assessment can hold is `VOIDED`, reached only by cascade — either from an upheld appeal, from an audit failure, or from the suspension of its own assessor — and always accompanied by a `void_reason` recording which of the three did it.

### 3.4. Contesting a Result: Appeals, Retest Rounds and Segregation of Duties

A coach who believes a certified minimum is wrong files an appeal against the offending assessment. Filing is exclusive to `TeamCoach` and carries the filing `team_id` and a `reason` of at most 500 characters that must be non-empty. The supplied `team_id` must equal the caller's `X-User-ID` and must also be the team that owns the contested assessment's wrestler; either mismatch is `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. Timeliness is measured against the logical clock: the appeal must be filed within `appeal_filing_window_days` of the contested assessment's `assessed_at`, and the boundary instant — `assessed_at` advanced by exactly that many days — is still inside the window. Past it, filing is `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_EXPIRED`. One further gate protects against endless litigation of the same measurement: if the assessment under appeal already has as many retest rounds recorded across any appeal of that assessment as `max_appeal_retest_rounds` allows, a fresh filing is refused with `ERR_APPEAL_ROUND_LIMIT`. A newly filed appeal sits in `SUBMITTED` with `filed_at` stamped from the logical clock, `retest_round_count` at 0, and `reviewing_assessor_id`, `retest_assessment_id` and `decision_note` null. Appeals are readable individually and as a list narrowed by `assessment_id`, `team_id`, `reviewing_assessor_id`, `retest_assessment_id` and `status`.

Reviewer assignment is a state-office act and drives `SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW`. It supplies a `reviewing_assessor_id`, which must not be the assessor who produced the contested assessment — this is the structural heart of the segregation-of-duties model, and a violation is `ERR_SAME_ASSESSOR_CONFLICT` — and whose `status` must be `CERTIFIED`, otherwise `ERR_ASSESSOR_NOT_CERTIFIED`. The appeal's `version` increments. Assigning a reviewer to an appeal that has already left `SUBMITTED` is `ERR_APPEAL_INVALID_STATE`.

From there the reviewer, and only the assessor whose `X-User-ID` matches `reviewing_assessor_id`, drives the retest cycle. Ordering a retest is permitted out of `UNDER_REVIEW` and out of `RETEST_COMPLETED`, and in both cases requires `retest_round_count` to be strictly less than the season's `max_appeal_retest_rounds`. Ordering increments `retest_round_count` by one, increments `version`, and lands the appeal in `RETEST_ORDERED`. When the ordered retest is subsequently finalized — the retest assessment being the one carrying this appeal's id in its `appeal_id` — the appeal advances `RETEST_ORDERED → RETEST_COMPLETED` and its `retest_assessment_id` is set to that assessment. A reviewer unconvinced by the retest may order another, closing the cycle `RETEST_ORDERED → RETEST_COMPLETED → RETEST_ORDERED`; the cycle is bounded by `max_appeal_retest_rounds`, which with the shipped default of two permits at most two retests before a decision becomes mandatory. Attempting a third order is refused by the round-count precondition as `ERR_APPEAL_INVALID_STATE`.

Deciding the appeal supplies an `outcome` and a `decision_note`. An outcome of `DENIED` is accepted out of `UNDER_REVIEW` and out of `RETEST_COMPLETED`; it records the `decision_note`, leaves the contested assessment in `VALIDATED` with the wrestler's certified figures completely untouched, and increments the appeal's `version`. An outcome of `UPHELD` is accepted only out of `RETEST_COMPLETED`, and only when `retest_assessment_id` is non-null and that retest assessment's own `status` is `VALIDATED` — a retest that failed hydration cannot ground an upheld appeal. Deciding out of `SUBMITTED` is `ERR_APPEAL_INVALID_STATE`.

An upheld appeal launches a four-hop cascade, applied in full within the deciding request. Hop one moves the contested assessment `VALIDATED → VOIDED` and stamps its `void_reason` with `APPEAL_UPHELD:` followed by the appeal's id; the producing assessor's `voided_assessment_count` increments by one. Hop two, *because* that assessment was voided, repoints the wrestler's `source_assessment_id` at the appeal's `retest_assessment_id` and recomputes every certified figure from the retest — `alpha_weight_lbs`, `body_fat_pct`, `minimum_certified_weight_lbs`, `min_weight_class_lbs` and `lowest_eligible_class_lbs`. Hop three, *because* the wrestler's `min_weight_class_lbs` may have moved upward, cancels any descent plan of that wrestler whose `status` is `CERTIFIED` or `BREACHED` and whose `target_class_lbs` is now strictly below the new `min_weight_class_lbs`, stamping `cancel_reason` as `MINIMUM_RAISED`. Hop four, *because* that plan was cancelled, scratches every tournament entry of that wrestler whose `status` is `ENTERED` or `WEIGHED_IN`, moving each to `SCRATCHED` with `scratch_reason` set to `PLAN_CANCELLED`. The appeal's `decision_note` is recorded and its `version` increments. `UPHELD` and `DENIED` are both terminal.

There is a third terminal outcome no human ever requests. `WITHDRAWN` is reached only by cascade, when a program audit failure voids the contested assessment out from under a live appeal: an appeal in any of `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `RETEST_ORDERED` or `RETEST_COMPLETED` whose contested assessment has just moved `VALIDATED → VOIDED` with a `void_reason` beginning `AUDIT_FAIL` is moved to `WITHDRAWN` with `decision_note` set to the literal `WITHDRAWN_ASSESSMENT_VOIDED`. There is nothing left to adjudicate, so the appeal is closed rather than decided.

### 3.5. Descent Plans and the Certified Progression Schedule

A descent plan is the certified contract governing how fast a wrestler may come down to a chosen class. Opening one is exclusive to `TeamCoach` and requires an open season. The payload names the `source_assessment_id` the plan is built on, the `plan_variant` selecting the projection algorithm, the `target_class_lbs` the wrestler intends to certify into, the `plan_start_date` that becomes week zero of the schedule, the `expected_wrestler_version` described in section 2.7, and optionally a `requested_weeks` figure of at least 1 expressing how quickly the coach hopes to arrive.

Creation is heavily guarded. A wrestler at `DISQUALIFIED` is frozen out with `ERR_WRESTLER_DISQUALIFIED`. A wrestler with no validated source assessment on file — that is, whose `certification_status` is `UNCERTIFIED` or `SUSPENDED` — is refused with `ERR_WRESTLER_NOT_CERTIFIED`. A wrestler whose team is currently on `PROBATION` cannot have a new plan opened at all; that refusal is `ERR_TEAM_ON_PROBATION`. The referenced `source_assessment_id` must be an assessment in `status` `VALIDATED` and must belong to the same wrestler the plan is being opened for; either failure is `ERR_ASSESSMENT_WRESTLER_MISMATCH`. The `target_class_lbs` must be a member of the season's `weight_classes` and must be greater than or equal to the wrestler's `min_weight_class_lbs`; a target below that hard floor is `ERR_TARGET_BELOW_MINIMUM` (HTTP 422).

Creating a plan supersedes whatever the wrestler had before. Any prior descent plan of the same wrestler whose `status` is `DRAFT`, `CERTIFIED` or `BREACHED` is moved to `CANCELLED` with `cancel_reason` set to `SUPERSEDED`, and the new plan's `supersedes_plan_id` self-reference is set to that cancelled plan's id; when the wrestler had no prior non-terminal plan, `supersedes_plan_id` stays null. When the superseded plan had been `CERTIFIED`, the consequences reach further: the wrestler's certification dimension steps back `CERTIFIED → PROVISIONAL`, the wrestler's `eligibility_status` drops to `INELIGIBLE`, and the season's `certified_wrestler_count` is decremented by one. When the superseded plan was merely `DRAFT`, neither dimension of the wrestler moves and the counter is untouched. In every case the wrestler's `version` increments by one, which is exactly why the operation is version-pinned. The new plan itself starts in `DRAFT`, with `weekly_rate_pct` copied from the season's `max_weekly_descent_pct` at creation time and **pinned for the life of the plan** even if the season constant is later different, `weekly_delta_lbs` null, `total_weeks`, `projected_certification_date` and `cancel_reason` null, and `milestone_count`, `missed_milestone_count`, `breach_count` and `revision_count` all at 0.

Certification of the plan belongs to the state office and drives `DRAFT → CERTIFIED`. It requires the plan to actually be in `DRAFT` — otherwise `ERR_PLAN_INVALID_STATE` — the season to be `OPEN`, the plan's source assessment to *still* be in `status` `VALIDATED` (a source assessment that has since been superseded or voided yields `ERR_ASSESSMENT_INVALID_STATE`), the plan's `target_class_lbs` to still be greater than or equal to the wrestler's current `min_weight_class_lbs`, and the wrestler's `certification_status` to be `PROVISIONAL` or `CERTIFIED`.

The certifying act generates the whole weekly progression schedule in one shot. Let the descent baseline be the wrestler's `alpha_weight_lbs`, the pinned rate be the plan's `weekly_rate_pct`, and the destination be the plan's `target_class_lbs`. Under the `WEEKLY_STEP` variant the weekly allowable weight compounds: week zero's allowable weight is the baseline itself, and each later week's allowable weight is the *previous week's already-rounded* allowable weight multiplied by the quantity one minus the rate divided by one hundred, rounded half up to one decimal place. The per-step rounding is load-bearing — each week's value is rounded to one decimal before it becomes the base of the next week, and computing the baseline multiplied by the rate factor raised to the week index in a single shot is incorrect and diverges from the fifth week onward. Because the base shrinks every week, the pound reduction shrinks with it. Under the `LINEAR_RATE` variant a single fixed pound delta is computed once as the baseline multiplied by the rate divided by one hundred, rounded half up to one decimal place, and stored as `weekly_delta_lbs`; week k's allowable weight is then the untouched baseline minus k times that delta, rounded half up to one decimal place. Because the delta never shrinks, a linear plan reaches the target sooner than a compounding plan built from identical inputs. For a baseline of 159.6 pounds, a rate of 1.5 percent and a target of 150 pounds, `WEEKLY_STEP` produces weekly allowable weights of 159.6, 157.2, 154.8, 152.5, 150.2 and 147.9, arriving in five weeks, while `LINEAR_RATE` fixes `weekly_delta_lbs` at 2.4 pounds and produces 159.6, 157.2, 154.8, 152.4 and 150.0, arriving in four. `weekly_delta_lbs` remains null for a `WEEKLY_STEP` plan.

`total_weeks` is the smallest week index, counting from zero, at which the variant's allowable weight is less than or equal to the `target_class_lbs`. One `DescentMilestone` row is generated for every week index from zero through `total_weeks` inclusive, each starting in `status` `SCHEDULED` with `evaluated_recorded_weight_lbs` null. Each milestone records its `plan_id`, its zero-based `week_index`, an `effective_date` equal to the plan's `plan_start_date` advanced by seven days times the week index, the `allowable_weight_lbs` the variant produced for that week, and a `min_eligible_class_lbs` equal to the smallest member of the season's `weight_classes` that is greater than or equal to the larger of that week's allowable weight and the wrestler's `minimum_certified_weight_lbs`, ignoring growth allowances entirely. The plan's `milestone_count` is set to `total_weeks` plus one, and `projected_certification_date` is the `plan_start_date` advanced by seven days times `total_weeks`. Milestones are the only discovery channel for the generated schedule and are listable per plan in `week_index` ascending order, optionally narrowed by `status`.

Certification also moves the wrestler. The certification dimension steps `PROVISIONAL → CERTIFIED`, and — independently — the eligibility dimension leaves `INELIGIBLE`: it becomes `ELIGIBLE` when the owning team's `compliance_status` is `CLEAR` or `WATCH`, and `RESTRICTED` when the team's `compliance_status` is `PROBATION`. The season's `certified_wrestler_count` increments by one, and the plan's and wrestler's `version` both increment.

One admissibility gate is evaluated **before any milestone is generated**, and only when the client supplied `requested_weeks`. The weekly delta the request implies is the baseline minus the target, divided by `requested_weeks`, rounded half up to one decimal place. The maximum permitted weekly delta is the baseline multiplied by the plan's `weekly_rate_pct` divided by one hundred, rounded half up to one decimal place. If the implied delta is strictly greater than the permitted maximum, the request is refused with `ERR_DESCENT_RATE_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 422); equality is accepted. The same gate applies both when a plan is opened and when a breached plan is revised. Plans are readable individually and as a filtered list narrowed by `wrestler_id`, `source_assessment_id`, `supersedes_plan_id` and `status`; `COMPLETED` and `CANCELLED` are the plan's terminal states.

### 3.6. The Season Compliance Run: Judging Milestones, Breach and Revision

Compliance is not evaluated continuously; it is swept. A state-office operator triggers the season compliance run, which requires the season to be `OPEN` and returns the season with refreshed counters. The sweep walks every descent plan of the season whose `status` is `CERTIFIED`, in `created_at` ascending order — never in id order, because a plan's breach can raise a flag that changes a team's posture and therefore the eligibility of wrestlers on other plans, making the order observable. Within each plan, milestones are processed in `week_index` ascending order, and only those whose `status` is `SCHEDULED` and whose `effective_date` is on or before the current logical clock date are considered. A milestone whose `effective_date` is still in the future is skipped and stays `SCHEDULED`.

Judging one milestone means finding the weigh-in that speaks for it. The evaluation window is half-open and three days long: it opens at midnight UTC on the milestone's `effective_date` and closes at midnight UTC three days later, so a weigh-in recorded at the instant the window opens counts and one recorded at the instant it closes does not. Candidates are every `WeighIn` whose wrestler is the plan's wrestler and whose `recorded_at` falls inside that window, drawn from any event. The qualifying weigh-in is the candidate with the greatest `recorded_at`, with ties broken by `created_at` ascending. If no candidate exists at all, the milestone becomes `MISSED` and its `evaluated_recorded_weight_lbs` stays null. If the qualifying weigh-in's `recorded_weight_lbs` is less than or equal to the milestone's `allowable_weight_lbs`, the milestone becomes `MET` and `evaluated_recorded_weight_lbs` records that weight. If the qualifying weigh-in's `recorded_weight_lbs` is strictly greater, the milestone becomes `MISSED` and `evaluated_recorded_weight_lbs` still records that weight — a missed milestone with a recorded number is diagnostically different from a missed milestone with none. `MET`, `MISSED` and `WAIVED` are all terminal for a milestone.

Once a plan's due milestones have been judged, the plan itself is reassessed. `missed_milestone_count` is recomputed as the number of milestones on the plan whose `status` is `MISSED`; waived milestones are excluded from that count by construction. When `missed_milestone_count` reaches or exceeds the season's `plan_breach_limit` — two, by default — the plan moves `CERTIFIED → BREACHED`, its `breach_count` increments by one, and, *because* the plan became breached, a `HealthFlag` with `source` `PLAN_BREACH`, `severity` 3 and `status` `OPEN` is raised on the wrestler, after which the team's `open_flag_severity_total` and `compliance_status` are recomputed and any `ELIGIBLE` wrestler of a team that has just landed on `PROBATION` is demoted to `RESTRICTED`. The plan's `version` increments. Otherwise, when every milestone of the plan has a `status` of `MET` or `WAIVED` and the plan's `milestone_count` equals `total_weeks` plus one, the plan moves `CERTIFIED → COMPLETED` and its `version` increments. A plan that is neither breached nor complete simply stays `CERTIFIED` and waits for the next sweep.

Revision is the coach's route back from breach, and it closes the `CERTIFIED → BREACHED → CERTIFIED` cycle. Revision requires the plan to be in `BREACHED` — a revision request against any other status is `ERR_PLAN_INVALID_STATE` — an open season, a wrestler whose `certification_status` is anything other than `DISQUALIFIED`, and a revised `target_class_lbs` still greater than or equal to the wrestler's `min_weight_class_lbs`, failing which the refusal is `ERR_TARGET_BELOW_MINIMUM`. The request must carry a new `plan_start_date`; it may optionally override `target_class_lbs`, `plan_variant` and `requested_weeks`, and when `plan_variant` is not overridden the plan keeps the variant it already had. The `requested_weeks` admissibility gate described in section 3.5 applies here identically.

What happens to the existing schedule is the delicate part. Every milestone still in `SCHEDULED` whose `effective_date` is strictly before the new `plan_start_date` is retained and becomes `WAIVED`; it stays on the plan as a historical row and is excluded from `missed_milestone_count`. Every other `SCHEDULED` milestone is deleted outright. Milestones already in `MET`, `MISSED` or `WAIVED` are left exactly where they are, and `missed_milestone_count` is emphatically **not** reset — a breach that has happened stays on the record. The schedule is then regenerated from the new `plan_start_date` using the plan's variant, recomputing the weekly allowable weights from the wrestler's `alpha_weight_lbs` exactly as at first certification. To keep week indices unique on the plan, regenerated rows continue numbering from one greater than the highest `week_index` still present on the plan after the deletions; the k-th regenerated row, counting from zero, therefore carries an `effective_date` of the new `plan_start_date` advanced by seven days times k and an `allowable_weight_lbs` equal to the variant's week-k value, even though its stored `week_index` is offset by the continuation. Once regeneration is done, `milestone_count` becomes the total number of milestone rows on the plan, `revision_count` increments by one, `version` increments, and the plan returns to `CERTIFIED`.

### 3.7. Scheduling Competitions and Entering Wrestlers

Events are scheduled by the state office into an open season and carry a `name` of at most 160 characters, an `event_type` of `DUAL`, `TOURNAMENT` or `STATE_CHAMPIONSHIP`, an `event_date` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form, and three optional shape parameters. `competition_days` defaults to 1 and must be between 1 and 4; it defines the half-open competition window that opens at midnight UTC on `event_date` and closes at midnight UTC that many days later. `weigh_in_days` defaults to 1 and must also be between 1 and 4; it declares how many weigh-in day indices the event permits, so valid indices run from zero through `weigh_in_days` minus one. `consecutive_day_allowance_lbs` defaults to 1.0 and must be between 0 and 2.0 inclusive; it is the pounds of allowance granted per additional consecutive weigh-in day at that event, before the season ceiling is applied. Each event records the `season_id` it belongs to and starts in `SCHEDULED` with `bracket_locked` false, `reseed_required` false, and `entry_count`, `weighed_in_count`, `seeded_entry_count` and `seed_integrity_sum` all 0. Events are readable individually and as a list narrowed by `season_id` and `status`.

Entering a wrestler is exclusive to `TeamCoach` and carries the `wrestler_id`, the `weight_class_lbs` being entered, and an optional `season_win_count` that defaults to 0, must be at least 0, and later becomes the primary seeding sort key. The coach must own the wrestler's team. Beyond that, entry is the single most heavily guarded operation in the system, because it is the pre-competition certification-database check. A `DISQUALIFIED` wrestler is frozen out with `ERR_WRESTLER_DISQUALIFIED`. The season must be `OPEN`. A wrestler with no validated source assessment on file — `certification_status` of `UNCERTIFIED` or `SUSPENDED` — is refused with `ERR_WRESTLER_NOT_CERTIFIED`. A wrestler carrying any health flag in `status` `ESCALATED` is refused with `ERR_UNHEALTHY_WEIGHT_MANAGEMENT`, and stays refused until a physician clears the escalation. The requested `weight_class_lbs` must be a member of the season's `weight_classes` and must be greater than or equal to the wrestler's lowest eligible class computed as of the event's `event_date`; a class beneath that is `ERR_CLASS_BELOW_CERTIFIED_MINIMUM` (HTTP 422), and the degenerate case in which no class in the season satisfies the wrestler's floor at all is `ERR_NO_ELIGIBLE_WEIGHT_CLASS` (HTTP 422). A wrestler whose team is on `PROBATION` is refused with `ERR_TEAM_ON_PROBATION`. Once the event's `bracket_locked` flag has been set true by a first successful seeding run, no new entry may be added to that event at all — that refusal is `ERR_BRACKET_LOCKED`, and state-office weight-class changes and reseeds are the only sanctioned way to alter a locked bracket, so they are exempt from it. Two further conflicts are checked: the wrestler must not already hold a non-`SCRATCHED` entry on another event whose half-open competition window overlaps this event's window, refused as `ERR_ENTRY_WINDOW_CONFLICT` — adjacency with a gap of exactly zero days is permitted, so an event ending at the instant another begins is fine — and at most one non-`SCRATCHED` entry may exist per event and wrestler pair, a second being `ERR_DUPLICATE_ENTRY`. A successful entry lands in `ENTERED` with `seed_number`, `latest_effective_weight_lbs` and `scratch_reason` null and `weight_class_change_count` at 0, and increments the event's `entry_count`. Entries are readable individually and as a list narrowed by `event_id`, `wrestler_id`, `status` and `weight_class_lbs`.

The class-floor arithmetic behind that check is also exposed as a standalone, purely informational operation available to `StateOffice`, `TeamCoach` and `WeighInOfficial`. It names an `event_id` and a `requested_class_lbs` and always succeeds with HTTP 201, logging a check row rather than rejecting on any business rule. Each logged check records the `wrestler_id` it examined, a `checked_at` stamp taken from the logical clock, and an `as_of_date` copied from the referenced event's `event_date` — the single date at which every allowance and schedule lookup is evaluated.

The floor is derived in four steps whose order is pinned and which never iterate, even though the dependency looks circular. **Step one** totals the growth allowance: `growth_allowance_lbs` is the sum of `allowance_lbs` over every growth allowance of the season whose `effective_date` is on or before `as_of_date`, the lower bound being inclusive. **Step two** finds the governing schedule value. The governing plan is the wrestler's descent plan whose `status` is `CERTIFIED` or `BREACHED`; at most one such plan exists, and in the event two ever coexist the one with the greatest `created_at` governs. If there is no such plan, `schedule_allowable_weight_lbs` is null. Otherwise, among that plan's milestones whose `effective_date` is on or before `as_of_date`, the one with the greatest `effective_date` supplies `schedule_allowable_weight_lbs`; if no milestone has yet come due, the milestone at `week_index` zero supplies it; and an `as_of_date` beyond the last milestone simply keeps the last milestone's value. **Step three** sets the eligibility floor: it is the wrestler's `minimum_certified_weight_lbs` when `schedule_allowable_weight_lbs` is null, and otherwise the larger of the wrestler's `minimum_certified_weight_lbs` and `schedule_allowable_weight_lbs`. **Step four** selects the class and then clamps it. The candidate classes are those members of the season's `weight_classes` whose limit plus `growth_allowance_lbs` is greater than or equal to the floor; the candidate class is the smallest of them. The hard-floor class is the wrestler's `min_weight_class_lbs`, which was computed at assessment-validation time from the *unadjusted* minimum — growth allowance must never be folded back into it. The reported `lowest_eligible_class_lbs` is the larger of the candidate class and the hard-floor class. When the candidate set is empty, `lowest_eligible_class_lbs` is null and the check's `outcome` is `BLOCKED_CLASS_BELOW_MINIMUM`. Running the check also refreshes the wrestler's own `lowest_eligible_class_lbs` field.

The check's `outcome` reports what a real entry attempt would do, and `blocking_error_id` names the error that attempt would return, reading null when the outcome is `CLEARED`. The outcomes are evaluated in the same precedence the entry operation itself uses: `BLOCKED_DISQUALIFIED` when the wrestler's `certification_status` is `DISQUALIFIED`, carrying `ERR_WRESTLER_DISQUALIFIED`; then `BLOCKED_NOT_CERTIFIED` when `certification_status` is `UNCERTIFIED` or `SUSPENDED`, carrying `ERR_WRESTLER_NOT_CERTIFIED`; then `BLOCKED_ESCALATED_FLAG` when the wrestler holds an `ESCALATED` flag, carrying `ERR_UNHEALTHY_WEIGHT_MANAGEMENT`; then, when the `requested_class_lbs` is below the computed `lowest_eligible_class_lbs` or no candidate class exists, `BLOCKED_PLAN_BREACHED` if the wrestler's governing plan is in `BREACHED` and otherwise `BLOCKED_CLASS_BELOW_MINIMUM`, both carrying `ERR_CLASS_BELOW_CERTIFIED_MINIMUM`; then `BLOCKED_TEAM_PROBATION` when the wrestler's team is on `PROBATION`, carrying `ERR_TEAM_ON_PROBATION`; and `CLEARED` when none of the above applies. Logged checks are readable individually and as a list narrowed by `wrestler_id`, `event_id` and `outcome`.

### 3.8. Weigh-In Day, Allowances, Bracket Seeding and Class Changes

The event lifecycle is driven entirely by the state office. Moving `SCHEDULED → WEIGH_IN_OPEN` requires the event to be in `SCHEDULED` and its season to be `OPEN`, and increments the event's `version`. Moving `WEIGH_IN_OPEN → WEIGH_IN_CLOSED` requires the event to be in `WEIGH_IN_OPEN` and performs the day's reckoning: every entry of the event still in `ENTERED` is moved to `SCRATCHED` with `scratch_reason` set to `NO_WEIGH_IN`, every entry whose most recent weigh-in has a `result` of `FAIL` is moved to `SCRATCHED` with `scratch_reason` set to `WEIGH_IN_FAILED`, and the event's `version` increments. An event may be cancelled out of `SCHEDULED` or out of `WEIGH_IN_OPEN`, which scratches every entry in `ENTERED` or `WEIGHED_IN` with `scratch_reason` set to `EVENT_CANCELLED` and increments `version`. Completion is accepted out of `WEIGH_IN_CLOSED` and out of `SEEDED`, but only while `reseed_required` is false; an event asked to complete with an invalidated bracket is refused with `ERR_RESEED_REQUIRED`. `COMPLETED` and `CANCELLED` are terminal. Any transition not declared out of the event's current status — including recording a weigh-in while the event is not `WEIGH_IN_OPEN`, and seeding an event that is neither `WEIGH_IN_CLOSED` nor awaiting a reseed — is `ERR_EVENT_INVALID_STATE`.

Recording a weigh-in may be done by the `WeighInOfficial` who operates the scale or by `StateOffice`. The payload names the `entry_id` being weighed, the zero-based `day_index`, and the `recorded_weight_lbs` read off the scale, which must be between 60.0 and 400.0 inclusive. The server fills in the rest: `wrestler_id` is copied from the referenced entry so that weigh-ins remain queryable per wrestler across events, `recorded_at` is stamped from the logical clock, and `official_user_id` records the acting operator's opaque `X-User-ID`. The season must be `OPEN`, the wrestler must not be `DISQUALIFIED`, the wrestler must have a validated source assessment on file or the attempt is `ERR_WRESTLER_NOT_CERTIFIED`, the event must be in `WEIGH_IN_OPEN`, and an entry already in `SCRATCHED` or `DISQUALIFIED` cannot be weighed at all — that refusal is `ERR_ENTRY_INVALID_STATE`. The `day_index` must lie between zero and the event's `weigh_in_days` minus one inclusive, and `recorded_at` must fall on the event's `event_date` advanced by `day_index` days; either violation is `ERR_WEIGH_IN_DAY_OUT_OF_RANGE` (HTTP 422). At most one weigh-in may exist per entry and day index, a second being `ERR_DUPLICATE_WEIGH_IN`.

The allowance arithmetic runs at recording time. The growth-allowance component is the sum of `allowance_lbs` over every growth allowance of the season whose `effective_date` is on or before the event's `event_date`. The consecutive-day component rewards athletes who must hold weight across a multi-day tournament: it is the `day_index` multiplied by the event's `consecutive_day_allowance_lbs`, rounded half up to one decimal place, and then capped at the season's `max_consecutive_day_allowance_lbs` — with the shipped defaults of one pound per additional day and a two-pound ceiling, day zero earns nothing, day one earns one pound, day two earns two pounds, and day three still earns only two. The stored `allowance_lbs` is the sum of the growth-allowance component and the capped consecutive-day component, rounded half up to one decimal place, and `effective_limit_lbs` is the entry's `weight_class_lbs` plus that `allowance_lbs`, rounded half up to one decimal place.

The `result` then follows from three ordered comparisons. It is `PASS` when `recorded_weight_lbs` is less than or equal to the entry's `weight_class_lbs` outright. It is `PASS_WITH_ALLOWANCE` when `recorded_weight_lbs` is strictly greater than the entry's `weight_class_lbs` but less than or equal to `effective_limit_lbs` — the allowance was needed and was sufficient. It is `FAIL` in every other case, meaning `recorded_weight_lbs` strictly exceeds `effective_limit_lbs`. Separately, `below_minimum` is true when `recorded_weight_lbs` is strictly below the wrestler's `minimum_certified_weight_lbs`, which is the sport's central safety signal: the athlete has descended past the floor the certification regime set for them.

A weigh-in whose `result` is `PASS` or `PASS_WITH_ALLOWANCE` advances the entry `ENTERED → WEIGHED_IN` and sets the entry's `latest_effective_weight_lbs`, which is the `recorded_weight_lbs` of that entry's passing weigh-in with the greatest `day_index`, ties broken by `created_at` ascending; the entry's `version` increments and the event's `weighed_in_count`, which counts entries in `WEIGHED_IN` or `SEEDED`, is refreshed. A further passing weigh-in on a strictly later `day_index` than every weigh-in already recorded for that entry keeps the entry in `WEIGHED_IN` and simply updates `latest_effective_weight_lbs`. A weigh-in whose `result` is `FAIL` never overwrites `latest_effective_weight_lbs` and leaves the prior value intact, and it does not advance the entry — the entry stays where it was and is scratched later when weigh-ins close. When a weigh-in has `below_minimum` true, a `HealthFlag` is raised on the wrestler with `source` `WEIGH_IN_DEFICIT`, `severity` 4 and `status` `OPEN`, carrying the `weigh_in_id` of the reading that raised it; the team's `open_flag_severity_total` and `compliance_status` are recomputed immediately, and any `ELIGIBLE` wrestler of a team that has just landed on `PROBATION` is demoted to `RESTRICTED`. Weigh-ins are readable individually and as a list narrowed by `event_id`, `wrestler_id`, `entry_id` and `result`.

Seeding is a state-office act that turns the weighed-in field into a bracket. On the initial run, out of `WEIGH_IN_CLOSED`, the admitted pool is exactly the entries of that event whose `status` is `WEIGHED_IN`; on a reseed, out of `SEEDED`, the admitted pool is those whose `status` is `WEIGHED_IN` or `SEEDED`, so a reseed re-ranks the already-seeded field together with any competitor who weighed in after the first run. Entries in `ENTERED`, `SCRATCHED` or `DISQUALIFIED` are excluded and receive no `seed_number`. If the admitted pool is globally empty the run is refused with `ERR_NO_SEEDABLE_ENTRIES`; a weight class that happens to contain no admitted entry is simply skipped in silence. Working through the classes present in the pool in ascending order of class limit, the entries of each class are ranked by `season_win_count` descending, then by `latest_effective_weight_lbs` ascending with nulls sorting last, then by `created_at` ascending as the final tie-break — the row `id` is never consulted. Each entry in a class receives a `seed_number` counting from 1 within that class and moves to `SEEDED`. The initial run additionally requires at least one entry in `WEIGHED_IN`, sets `bracket_locked` to true and `reseed_required` to false, and refreshes `seeded_entry_count` and `seed_integrity_sum`; a reseed requires `reseed_required` to be true, resets it to false, and likewise refreshes both counters. The event's `version` increments on either branch.

Once a bracket is locked, the only sanctioned way to move a competitor between classes is a state-office weight-class change, and it is version-pinned as described in section 2.7. The request carries the new `weight_class_lbs` and the `expected_entry_version`; the entry must be in `SEEDED` and the event's `bracket_locked` must be true. The new class must be a member of the season's `weight_classes` and must be greater than or equal to the wrestler's lowest eligible class as of the event date, else `ERR_CLASS_BELOW_CERTIFIED_MINIMUM`; a wrestler carrying an `ESCALATED` flag cannot be moved at all, which surfaces as `ERR_UNHEALTHY_WEIGHT_MANAGEMENT`; and an entry already `SCRATCHED` or `DISQUALIFIED` is `ERR_ENTRY_INVALID_STATE`. Ownership is evaluated on this operation as it is on roster additions, appeal filings, plan openings, competition entries and assessment finalization, so any actor whose identity does not own the resource is refused with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. A successful change sets the entry's `weight_class_lbs`, increments `weight_class_change_count` by one, and reseeds *both* the vacated and the destination class groups so that every group again holds a contiguous run of seed numbers beginning at 1. The event's `seed_integrity_sum` is recomputed from the resulting groups, which is why a class change followed by the reverse class change restores it to exactly its pre-change value. Both the entry's and the event's `version` increment. The entry remains in `SEEDED` throughout.

### 3.9. Health and Safety: Flags, Team Posture, Disqualification and Program Audit

Health flags are never created by a client. Each one is raised by a cascade and records the `wrestler_id` it concerns, a `raised_at` stamp from the logical clock, a `source` naming what raised it, and a pinned `severity`. The four sources are `HYDRATION_FAIL` at severity 1, raised when an assessment finalizes on the hydration-failure arm; `RAPID_LOSS` at severity 2; `PLAN_BREACH` at severity 3, raised when a compliance run declares a plan breached; and `WEIGH_IN_DEFICIT` at severity 4, raised when a recorded weigh-in comes in below the wrestler's certified minimum. Only the weigh-in-sourced flag carries a `weigh_in_id`; plan- and hydration-sourced flags leave it null. Every new flag starts in `OPEN` with `review_note` and `reviewer_user_id` null, and each wrestler's `open_health_flag_count` reports how many of their flags currently sit in `OPEN` or `ESCALATED`. Flags are readable individually and as a filtered list narrowed by `wrestler_id`, `weigh_in_id`, `source` and `status`; that listing is the only discovery channel for cascade-raised flags.

Medical review belongs exclusively to `MedicalReviewer` and supplies an `outcome` and a `review_note`. Only flags in `OPEN` or `ESCALATED` are reviewable; reviewing one already in `REVIEWED` or `DISMISSED` is `ERR_HEALTH_FLAG_INVALID_STATE`. Out of `OPEN` there are three landing states, and each records `review_note` and `reviewer_user_id` and increments the flag's `version`. `OPEN → REVIEWED` closes the concern: the flag's `severity` is removed from the team's `open_flag_severity_total` and `compliance_status` is recomputed. `OPEN → DISMISSED` is the compensating action: it likewise removes the flag's `severity`, restoring the ledger to exactly its pre-raise value, recomputes `compliance_status`, and — when the team thereby leaves `PROBATION` — returns every wrestler of that team whose `eligibility_status` is `RESTRICTED` and whose `certification_status` is `CERTIFIED` to `ELIGIBLE`. That same restoration applies when a `REVIEWED` outcome drops the team out of `PROBATION`. `OPEN → ESCALATED` does the opposite: the `severity` deliberately **stays** inside `open_flag_severity_total`, and the wrestler becomes disqualification-eligible, meaning the state office may now cite this flag when disqualifying them.

Escalation has exactly one exit. `ESCALATED → DISMISSED`, driven by the same physician role, overwrites `review_note` and `reviewer_user_id`, removes the `severity` from the ledger restoring its exact pre-raise value, recomputes `compliance_status`, returns any `RESTRICTED` and `CERTIFIED` wrestler of the team to `ELIGIBLE` when the team leaves `PROBATION`, and — critically — makes the wrestler no longer disqualification-eligible, so that `ERR_UNHEALTHY_WEIGHT_MANAGEMENT` stops blocking their competition entries and class changes. This is what makes the competition block clearable rather than permanent. `REVIEWED` and `DISMISSED` are terminal for a flag.

A team's compliance posture is a compound state derived entirely from that ledger, and it moves on every raise and every review. From `CLEAR` the team moves to `WATCH` as soon as `open_flag_severity_total` sits between 1 and 4 inclusive; `WATCH` is purely advisory and changes no wrestler's eligibility. From `WATCH` the team moves to `PROBATION` when a raise pushes the total to 5 or more, at which point every wrestler of that team whose `eligibility_status` is `ELIGIBLE` is demoted to `RESTRICTED` and new competition entries for that team's wrestlers are refused with `ERR_TEAM_ON_PROBATION`. A team can also jump straight from `CLEAR` to `PROBATION` in a single step, which is exactly what a `WEIGH_IN_DEFICIT` raise worth 4 does when combined with one already-open flag worth at least 1. Reviews walk the posture back down: a `REVIEWED` or `DISMISSED` outcome that drops the total into the 1-to-4 band moves `PROBATION → WATCH`, and one that drops it to exactly 0 moves `WATCH → CLEAR`; both restorations return every `RESTRICTED`, `CERTIFIED` wrestler of the team to `ELIGIBLE`.

It is worth restating that a wrestler's two state dimensions move independently. The certification dimension — `UNCERTIFIED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `CERTIFIED`, `SUSPENDED`, `DISQUALIFIED`, with `DISQUALIFIED` terminal — is driven by assessments, plans and audits. The eligibility dimension — `INELIGIBLE`, `ELIGIBLE`, `RESTRICTED`, with no terminal state — is driven by team posture and health flags. Plan certification is the only event that moves both at once, taking the eligibility dimension out of `INELIGIBLE` to `ELIGIBLE` or `RESTRICTED` according to team posture. A probation demotion `ELIGIBLE → RESTRICTED` leaves `certification_status` completely untouched; the return `RESTRICTED → ELIGIBLE` requires only that the team has come back to `CLEAR` or `WATCH` and that the wrestler's `certification_status` is still `CERTIFIED`. Conversely, any cascade that removes the wrestler's certified plan or source assessment — anything that pushes `certification_status` to `PROVISIONAL`, `SUSPENDED` or `DISQUALIFIED` — drops the eligibility dimension to `INELIGIBLE`, whether it was `ELIGIBLE` or `RESTRICTED` beforehand.

Disqualification for unhealthy weight management is reserved to `StateOffice`, and this is the segregation of duties at the heart of the safety workflow: the physician who escalates cannot disqualify, and the state office cannot disqualify without a physician's escalation. The request cites a `health_flag_id` and a `reason`. The cited flag must be in `status` `ESCALATED` and must belong to the wrestler named in the request; a flag that is `OPEN`, `REVIEWED` or `DISMISSED`, or one belonging to somebody else, is refused with `ERR_ESCALATED_FLAG_REQUIRED`. The wrestler's `certification_status` must be `PROVISIONAL` or `CERTIFIED`. On success, `certification_status` becomes `DISQUALIFIED`, `disqualified_at` is stamped from the logical clock, `certification_hold_reason` records the supplied `reason`, and `eligibility_status` drops to `INELIGIBLE`. Every descent plan of that wrestler in `DRAFT`, `CERTIFIED` or `BREACHED` is moved to `CANCELLED` with `cancel_reason` set to `DISQUALIFIED`. Every tournament entry of that wrestler in `ENTERED`, `WEIGHED_IN` or `SEEDED` is moved to `DISQUALIFIED` with `scratch_reason` set to `UNHEALTHY_WEIGHT_MANAGEMENT`; for any such entry that held a `seed_number`, the seed is cleared, the owning event's `seeded_entry_count` and `seed_integrity_sum` are reduced accordingly, and that event's `reseed_required` is set true so the bracket must be rebuilt before it can complete. The team's `roster_count` is decremented by one. When the disqualified wrestler had been `CERTIFIED` rather than `PROVISIONAL`, the cascade additionally decrements the season's `certified_wrestler_count`; from `PROVISIONAL` that counter is untouched, because a provisional wrestler was never inside it.

Program audits close the loop on assessor quality. An audit is opened by the state office against an open season and carries an `audit_type` of either `ASSESSOR_SAMPLE` or `TEAM_COMPLIANCE`, a `scoring_variant` of either `WORST_CASE` or `SAMPLE_MEAN`, a `sample_window_start` date in `YYYY-MM-DD` form, an optional `assessor_id` that is required for an `ASSESSOR_SAMPLE` audit and null for a `TEAM_COMPLIANCE` one, and an optional `samples` array that is empty or omitted for a `TEAM_COMPLIANCE` audit. Each element of `samples` is an object pairing an `assessment_id` with the auditor's `remeasured_body_fat_pct`. Every sampled assessment must have been produced by the audit's own assessor; a sample pointing at somebody else's work is `ERR_AUDIT_SAMPLE_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422). The referenced assessor's `status` must be `CERTIFIED`, else `ERR_ASSESSOR_NOT_CERTIFIED`. A new audit sits in `OPEN` with `sample_count` set to the length of `samples`, and `discrepancy_score`, `outcome` and `voided_assessment_count` all at their unset values — the first two null, the last 0. Audits are readable individually and as a list narrowed by `season_id`, `assessor_id`, `audit_type` and `outcome`, and both `StateOffice` and `Assessor` may read them.

Finalizing an audit scores it. It is accepted only out of `OPEN`; finalizing an already-`FINALIZED` audit is `ERR_AUDIT_INVALID_STATE`. Each sample's deviation is the absolute difference between the auditor's `remeasured_body_fat_pct` and the `body_fat_pct` recorded on the referenced assessment, rounded half up to two decimal places; assessments already in `VOIDED` are still scored, and only the failure cascade skips them. If `sample_count` is strictly less than the season's `audit_min_sample_count`, the audit is unscoreable: `outcome` becomes `INCONCLUSIVE` and `discrepancy_score` stays null. Otherwise the two scoring variants diverge sharply on identical inputs. `WORST_CASE` takes the single largest per-sample deviation as the `discrepancy_score`. `SAMPLE_MEAN` takes the sum of the per-sample deviations divided by `sample_count`, rounded half up to two decimal places. The `outcome` is then `FAIL` when `discrepancy_score` is greater than or equal to the season's `audit_fail_threshold_pct` and `PASS` otherwise. With the shipped threshold of 2.0 percent, deviations of 1.20, 2.40 and 0.60 score 2.40 under `WORST_CASE` and therefore fail, but score 1.40 under `SAMPLE_MEAN` and therefore pass — identical audit inputs, opposite regulatory consequence. In every case the audit lands in `FINALIZED` and the subject assessor's `last_audit_outcome` reflects the verdict.

A `FAIL` outcome on an `ASSESSOR_SAMPLE` audit launches the longest cascade in the system, five hops deep and applied in full inside the finalizing request. Hop one moves the assessor `CERTIFIED → SUSPENDED`, sets `suspension_reason` to `AUDIT_FAIL:` followed by the audit's id, sets `last_audit_outcome` to `FAIL`, and increments the assessor's `version`. Hop two, *because* the assessor is now suspended, moves every assessment of that assessor whose `status` is `VALIDATED` and whose `assessed_at` date is on or after the audit's `sample_window_start` to `VOIDED`, stamping each `void_reason` as `AUDIT_FAIL:` followed by the audit's id; the audit's `voided_assessment_count` and the assessor's `voided_assessment_count` both increment per voided row, and any live appeal against a voided assessment is closed to `WITHDRAWN` as described in section 3.4. Hop three, *because* those assessments were voided, touches every wrestler whose `source_assessment_id` was one of them: the wrestler's certification dimension moves to `SUSPENDED` from either `CERTIFIED` or `PROVISIONAL`, `source_assessment_id` is cleared, all five certified figures — `alpha_weight_lbs`, `body_fat_pct`, `minimum_certified_weight_lbs`, `min_weight_class_lbs` and `lowest_eligible_class_lbs` — are set to null, `certification_hold_reason` is set to `SOURCE_ASSESSMENT_VOIDED`, and `eligibility_status` drops to `INELIGIBLE`. The width of hop three depends on which certification state each affected wrestler was in, and the asymmetry is deliberate: a wrestler coming from `CERTIFIED` also causes the season's `certified_wrestler_count` to be decremented by one and carries the cascade into hop four, whereas a wrestler coming from `PROVISIONAL` leaves `certified_wrestler_count` untouched — they were never counted in it — and instead has every descent plan of theirs in `DRAFT` moved to `CANCELLED` with `cancel_reason` set to `WRESTLER_SUSPENDED`. Hop four, *because* a formerly certified wrestler is now suspended, moves that wrestler's `CERTIFIED` descent plan to `CANCELLED` with `cancel_reason` set to `WRESTLER_SUSPENDED`. Hop five, *because* the plan was cancelled, moves every tournament entry of that wrestler whose `status` is `ENTERED`, `WEIGHED_IN` or `SEEDED`, sitting on an event whose `status` is `SCHEDULED`, `WEIGH_IN_OPEN`, `WEIGH_IN_CLOSED` or `SEEDED`, to `SCRATCHED`. A `PASS` or `INCONCLUSIVE` outcome triggers no cascade whatsoever: the assessor keeps their credential, every assessment stands, and no wrestler, plan or entry is touched.
