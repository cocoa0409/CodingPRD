# Chemical Exposure Warning Compliance API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Consumer-product manufacturers that sell into California carry a standing obligation to warn about listed chemicals, and the operational burden of that obligation is almost entirely evidentiary. A single finished good may draw on dozens of raw materials, packaging items and manufacturing aids, each purchased from a supplier who may or may not disclose what is actually in the shipment; each of those materials may itself be a compound built from other materials; and the question that eventually matters — "how many micrograms per day of this listed chemical does a consumer take up from this product?" — is answered by rolling concentration data up a bill-of-materials tree that the regulatory affairs team does not own. When a private-party notice of violation arrives, the company has sixty days to decide whether to cure, negotiate or litigate, and the quality of that decision depends on records that were either assembled years earlier or not at all.

This API is the system of record for that programme. It maintains the listed-chemical registry with its safe harbor levels, the supplier and material inventory with its data-collection state, the laboratory testing pipeline that upgrades supplier assertions into measured values, the exposure assessments that turn concentrations into daily intake figures under two competing methodologies, the warning determinations that select label content and placement channels, the notice-of-violation intake with its cure-window arithmetic and settlement money waterfall, and the reformulation projects that drive concentrations back below threshold. Every one of these objects is versioned, every derived quantity is recomputed deterministically from its inputs, and every state change that can affect a product's exposure picture propagates through the graph in the same request that caused it.

Two design commitments shape the whole surface. First, **segregation of duties is enforced by the server, not by convention**: the analyst who submits an exposure assessment cannot peer-review it, the specialist who prepares a warning determination cannot approve it, and the counsel who proposes a settlement cannot execute it. Second, **time is explicit**: nothing in this system's stored data or business arithmetic reads wall-clock time — the sole exception is the transport-level `api_request_timestamp` of the response envelope, which describes the call rather than the business record. Deadlines, coverage windows and audit stamps all read a logical clock that test harnesses and operators set directly, so that a sixty-day cure window can be walked to its exact final microsecond and back.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Actors and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to every endpoint — including the logical-clock endpoint — must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role that principal is acting in. If `X-User-Role` is absent, or carries a string that is not one of the six declared role names, the request is refused with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403). The same error and status are returned when the header names a real role that is simply not authorised for the target endpoint. Authorisation is checked before anything else about the request is examined: header validity first, then endpoint permission, and only then request shape.

The six roles are `RegistryAdmin`, `ComplianceAnalyst`, `ToxicologyReviewer`, `LabelingSpecialist`, `RegulatoryCounsel` and `SupplierPortalUser`.

A `RegistryAdmin` maintains the listed-chemical registry, the product catalogue and the logical clock. It owns chemical registration and the whole registry lifecycle, product creation, product configuration updates and the commercial lifecycle actions, and it is the only role that may move the clock. It may not touch exposure science, labelling or enforcement.

A `ComplianceAnalyst` builds and maintains the inventory: suppliers, materials, the bill of materials, supplier data collection, laboratory test requests and reformulation projects. It prepares exposure assessments and may submit and reopen them, but it can never peer-review, reject or finalize one.

A `ToxicologyReviewer` is the independent scientific reviewer. It peer-reviews, rejects and finalizes exposure assessments, and it adjudicates disputed laboratory results by verifying or disputing a reported figure. It cannot create the assessments it reviews.

A `LabelingSpecialist` prepares warning determinations, submits them for approval, creates the resulting placements across the label, catalogue, internet point-of-sale and posted-sign channels, and implements and remediates those placements. It cannot approve its own determination and cannot verify its own placement.

A `RegulatoryCounsel` owns approval, enforcement and money. It approves, returns for revision and revokes determinations; verifies placements and flags them non-compliant; executes and revokes supplier warranties; intakes notices of violation and runs the cure evaluation; and proposes, counters, revises, executes and voids settlements. It never touches the inventory it is defending.

A `SupplierPortalUser` is a supplier acting on its own behalf through the data-collection portal. It may only file disclosure responses and declare chemical content for materials it ships, and it may read only supplier-facing resources — it can see no product, assessment, determination, notice or settlement at all.

Identity semantics differ across the roles and must be implemented exactly. When `X-User-Role` is `RegistryAdmin`, `ComplianceAnalyst`, `ToxicologyReviewer`, `LabelingSpecialist` or `RegulatoryCounsel`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier that is not tied to any persisted entity: the server records it where a transition stamps an actor — as `submitted_by_user_id` and `reviewed_by_user_id` on an exposure assessment, as `prepared_by_user_id` and `approved_by_user_id` on a warning determination, as `implemented_by_user_id` on a placement, and as `proposed_by_user_id` and `executed_by_user_id` on a settlement — but it never compares that value against any entity's `id`. When `X-User-Role` is `SupplierPortalUser`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Supplier` row; ownership checks on any resource that references a supplier compare the resource's supplier foreign key to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, when a `SupplierPortalUser` files a disclosure response on a material or declares a chemical present in a material, the material's `supplier_id` must equal the caller's `X-User-ID`, and a mismatch is refused with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`. The same comparison guards the single-row reads a supplier may perform: its own supplier row, a material, and a material/chemical link — whose ownership is tested through the material the link names. It is evaluated as soon as the path identifier has resolved, ahead of body foreign-key resolution, state legality and writability, so an ownership mismatch outranks those failures. The collection reads are deliberately **not** narrowed by ownership: `GET /materials` and `GET /material-chemicals` return every row matching the supplied filters whatever supplier ships it.

Only `X-User-Role` is validated as an authorisation input. A missing `X-User-ID` is not itself an authorisation failure: it is recorded as null wherever a transition stamps an actor, and it participates as null in the ownership and segregation-of-duties comparisons described here.

Segregation-of-duties comparisons are the one place where an opaque `X-User-ID` is compared against stored data. The value is compared against the recorded actor of an earlier, incompatible step on the same row, never against an entity identifier. Those comparisons are described where the workflows that enforce them are described, and all of them report `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. A comparison whose acting `X-User-ID` is absent never holds: an action taken with no `X-User-ID` is never refused as a segregation-of-duties violation, not even against a row whose recorded actor is itself null.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_` followed by exactly twenty characters drawn from the upper-case ASCII letters and the digits.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits. It is the one timestamp in the system taken from the host wall clock rather than from the logical clock of Section 2.4, because it stamps the transport rather than a business record; it is generated freshly for every response, including error responses.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every collection read accepts two optional query parameters. `limit` defaults to 10 when omitted, has a minimum accepted value of 1 and a maximum accepted value of 200. `offset` defaults to 0 when omitted and has a minimum accepted value of 0 with no upper bound. A `limit` below 1, a `limit` above 200, or a negative `offset` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION` (HTTP 400), and so is either parameter carrying a value that is not a whole decimal integer at all. A parameter supplied as an empty string is treated exactly as if it had been omitted and takes its default.

Unless a specific endpoint states otherwise, list results are ordered by `created_at` ascending, with insertion order as the tie-break: because the logical clock only moves when an operator moves it, many rows share a `created_at` to the millisecond, and among those the row created earlier in the run sorts earlier. Every documented list filter is an exact match against the stored field rendered as a string; multiple filters combine conjunctively; a filter supplied as an empty string is ignored; and a row whose filtered field is null never matches a filter that was supplied. The `meta` object of a list response carries `limit` (the effective page size), `offset` (the effective starting index), `total_count` (the number of rows matching the filters before the page window is applied) and `has_more` (true when rows remain beyond the current window).

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps, Versioning and the Logical Clock

Every stored resource in this system carries a server-minted `id` in UUID form. Identifiers carry no type prefix — a chemical identifier and a settlement identifier are indistinguishable by shape, and clients must not attempt to infer entity type from the string. Clients never supply an `id`; a request body that tries to is treated as supplying a non-writable field.

All twelve persisted entity types — listed chemicals, suppliers, materials, material/chemical links, products, product components, exposure assessments, warning determinations, warning placements, notices of violation, settlements and reformulation projects — carry the full set of common properties: `id`, `created_at`, `updated_at` and `version`. `created_at` and `updated_at` are ISO 8601 UTC timestamps rendered with exactly three fractional-second digits, and every other timestamp this API renders is rendered the same way. Every timestamp this API *accepts* in a request body — `current_utc`, `listing_effective_date`, `warranty_effective_from`, `warranty_effective_to`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, `earliest_violation_date`, `served_at`, `planned_start_date` and `planned_end_date` — must be a string carrying a calendar date and a time to whole seconds; the fractional-second part and the zone designator are both optional, a value with no zone designator is read as UTC, a value carrying `Z` or a numeric offset is converted to UTC, and anything else, including a date with no time part, is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. `version` is an integer that starts at 1 when the row is created and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation of that row, whether the mutation was requested directly by a client or applied as a cascade from a change elsewhere. `updated_at` is refreshed on the same occasions. No entity in this system omits `updated_at` or `version`. Each entity type serialises to one fixed field set — its own inputs, its derived fields and the four common properties — and every write and every read of that entity returns all of them: a field that has not been set yet is present and null rather than omitted, and no internal bookkeeping beyond this set is ever exposed.

Time is a first-class, controllable input. Every deadline, coverage window and audit stamp in this API reads the server's **logical clock** and never the host's wall clock. A `RegistryAdmin` may set that clock to any instant by supplying `current_utc`; the response reports the new `current_utc` alongside the `previous_utc` value it replaced, and no stored entity is modified by the call. Before the clock has ever been set it reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, which sits at or before every default fixture date, so an unset clock is a fixed, well-defined earliest instant rather than a drifting one. Every subsequent cure-window, warranty-window, placement-window and reformulation-window evaluation reads the new instant.

### 2.5. Numeric Precision and Rounding Discipline

Rounding in this system is always **round half up** — a value exactly at the midpoint rounds away from zero — and the number of decimals is fixed per quantity rather than global. Concentrations rolled up through the material tree are carried at 4 decimal places. Daily exposure figures and product-level chemical mass loads are carried at 3 decimal places. Margin ratios against a safe harbor level are carried at 4 decimal places. Mitigation factors on settlements are carried at 4 decimal places. Monetary amounts are integers denominated in cents; where a monetary step is specified to truncate rather than round, the specification says "floor" explicitly and the implementation must floor toward zero rather than round. A rounded quantity is rendered in JSON at its own value and not padded to its nominal decimal width, so a three-decimal figure that lands on a whole number is emitted as a JSON integer — `250`, never `250.000` — and a `margin_ratio` of two is emitted as `2`.

Two rounding disciplines in this system are **order-sensitive and the order is pinned**. Where a total is described as "round each term, then sum the rounded terms, then round the sum", that is exactly the order to implement; summing raw terms and rounding once at the end produces different values and is explicitly not what this system does. Sections 3.5 and 3.3 state the pinned orders for daily exposure and for the product chemical load respectively, and Section 3.8 states the pinned order for the settlement money waterfall.

### 2.6. Optimistic Concurrency on Parent Rows

Two creates in this system are guarded by optimistic concurrency control against a parent row's `version`, because each of them legitimately supersedes or voids a sibling and would therefore silently discard work the client had not observed.

Creating a warning determination requires a non-optional `expected_product_version` field in the request body, carrying the `version` of the target `Product` as the client last read it. If that value does not equal the product's current `version` — because any other writer has advanced it in the meantime — the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PRODUCT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). A determination create legitimately supersedes the product's prior in-flight determination and, once approved, its prior approved determination, so a second create observing the same product state would itself succeed under the product's own semantics; the version check is what makes the outcome deterministic.

Creating a settlement under a notice of violation requires a non-optional `expected_notice_version` field in the request body, carrying the `version` of the target `NoticeOfViolation` as the client last read it. If that value does not equal the notice's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_NOTICE_VERSION` (HTTP 412). A settlement create legitimately voids the notice's prior proposed or countered settlement, so a second create against the same observed notice state would itself succeed; the version check reduces that to a deterministic stale-version rejection.

Both fields are accepted as a JSON integer, as a JSON number with no fractional part, or as a string of decimal digits with an optional leading minus sign; a fractional number, a boolean or a non-numeric string is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, and an absent or null value is refused the same way because neither field is optional.

No other endpoint in this API accepts a version field. Requests to any other create, update or transition endpoint must not carry `expected_product_version`, `expected_notice_version`, or any other expected-version input; supplying either name on an endpoint that does not accept it is treated as supplying a non-writable field and is refused with `ERR_FIELD_NOT_WRITABLE`.

### 2.7. Path Resolution and Referential Integrity

URL path parameters are resolved left to right, and each segment must resolve to a stored row of the entity that segment names before the next segment is considered. Any path identifier that fails to resolve produces `ERR_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404). Foreign keys supplied in a request body are resolved with the same rule and the same error: `supplier_id`, `parent_material_id`, `material_id`, `chemical_id`, `assessment_id`, `material_chemical_id`, `product_id` and the `as_of_notice_id` query parameter all produce `ERR_NOT_FOUND` when they name nothing. A body foreign key whose JSON type is not a string never reaches resolution: it fails the shape layer first and reports `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. The compliance-summary read applies an additional ownership test to `as_of_notice_id`: a notice that exists but belongs to a different product is treated as not found.

Uniqueness constraints span five shapes and all report `ERR_DUPLICATE_RESOURCE` (HTTP 409): `chemical_name` is unique across all listed chemicals by case-sensitive exact match, `sku` is unique across all products, `legal_name` is unique across all suppliers, the pair of `material_id` and `chemical_id` is unique across all material/chemical links, and the pair of `product_id` and `material_id` is unique across product components whose status is `ACTIVE` — a component that has left `ACTIVE` no longer occupies the pair.

### 2.8. The Validation Ladder

Validation in this API is a single strictly ordered ladder, not a set of independent checks. Rules are evaluated in ascending priority order; the first rule whose condition holds short-circuits the request and returns its error, and no later rule is evaluated. Priorities are globally unique, so the outcome of any request against any state is fully determined. The ladder has nine layers, and the ordering within each layer is as listed below.

**Layer 1 — envelope, authorisation and shape.** In order: (1) the `X-User-Role` header is absent or is not one of the six declared roles → `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`; (2) the caller's role is not among the roles the target endpoint authorises → `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`; (3) on every create and every update, a required input field is absent, explicitly null, of the wrong JSON type, or carries an enum member that is not declared → `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`; (4) on every list read, `limit` is below 1 or above 200, or `offset` is negative → `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION`; (5) a numeric input is negative, or zero where the domain requires strictly positive → `ERR_VALUE_OUT_OF_RANGE`; (6) `contact_factor` falls outside the range from 0.0 through 1.0 inclusive, or `parent_mass_fraction` falls outside the range greater than 0.0 through 1.0 inclusive → `ERR_VALUE_OUT_OF_RANGE`; (7) `cas_number` does not match the CAS registry shape of two to seven digits, a hyphen, two digits, a hyphen and one final digit → `ERR_INVALID_CAS_NUMBER`; (8) the safe-harbor basis and level disagree → `ERR_SAFE_HARBOR_BASIS_MISMATCH`; (9) the safe-harbor basis contradicts the toxicological endpoint → `ERR_SAFE_HARBOR_BASIS_MISMATCH`; (10) an interval's end is not strictly after its start → `ERR_INVALID_INTERVAL`; (11) the `action` supplied to a transition surface or to the disclosure surface is not a declared trigger of that entity's state machine at all → `ERR_UNKNOWN_ACTION`; (12) `distribution_channels` is not an array at all, is an empty array, contains a value outside the declared set, or repeats a value → `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, those four conditions being tested in that order.

The numeric-domain rule at position 5 covers, specifically: a `safe_harbor_level_ug_per_day` that is zero or negative while the basis is not `NONE`; a negative `declared_concentration_ppm`; a negative `lab_result_ppm`; a `lab_loq_ppm` that is zero or negative; a `quantity_grams_per_unit` that is zero or negative; a `uses_per_day` that is zero or negative; a `label_area_sq_in` that is zero or negative; a negative `annual_units`; a negative `alleged_units`; a negative `requested_attorney_fees_cents`; a negative `indemnity_cap_cents`; and a negative `target_concentration_ppm`. It guards chemical registration, chemical declaration on a material, the laboratory transition surface on a material/chemical link where the two laboratory figures are supplied, product creation, component creation, exposure-assessment creation, notice intake, settlement proposal, the settlement transition surface on which those money inputs may be restated, supplier creation, reformulation-project creation, component update and product update. The fraction rule at position 6 guards component creation, component update, material creation and material update. The CAS rule at position 7 guards chemical registration only. The basis/level coherence rule at position 8 guards both chemical registration and the registry transition surface; the basis/endpoint alignment rule at position 9 guards chemical registration only. The interval-ordering rule at position 10 guards placement creation, supplier creation and reformulation-project creation. The channel-array rule at position 12 guards product creation and product update.

Three further details of layer 1 are pinned. A request body that is absent, empty or not parseable as JSON is treated as the empty object, so the request fails on whichever required field it is missing; a body that parses to a JSON value that is not an object is itself refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. An `action` that is absent, null or not a string is a shape failure under position 3 and reports `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` — position 11 fires only for a string that is genuinely not a declared trigger. And where a shared transition surface reserves individual actions to a subset of the roles the endpoint itself authorises — the disclosure surface, the laboratory surface and the assessment, determination and placement transition surfaces — that per-action role check is evaluated immediately after position 11, so an undeclared `action` reports `ERR_UNKNOWN_ACTION` even when the caller's role could not have performed any action on that surface, while a declared action attempted by the wrong role reports `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` even when the action's own required fields are missing and even when the path identifier does not resolve.

**Layer 2 — referential existence.** In order: path identifiers that do not resolve → `ERR_NOT_FOUND`; body foreign keys that do not resolve → `ERR_NOT_FOUND`; then the five uniqueness constraints → `ERR_DUPLICATE_RESOURCE`. Section 2.7 states the scopes.

**Layer 3 — optimistic concurrency.** First `expected_product_version` against the product's current `version` on determination create → `ERR_STALE_PRODUCT_VERSION`; then `expected_notice_version` against the notice's current `version` on settlement create → `ERR_STALE_NOTICE_VERSION`. Both sit above every state and business rule, so a stale version is reported even when the request would also have failed a later check.

**Layer 4 — state legality.** In order: (1) the requested action is not a declared transition out of the entity's current state — including every action attempted from a terminal state — → `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`; (2) a child write is attempted under a parent in a blocking state → `ERR_PARENT_STATE_FORBIDS_WRITE`; (3) an update targets a row that has become immutable → `ERR_ENTITY_IMMUTABLE`; (4) a request body supplies a field whose value the server computes or stamps → `ERR_FIELD_NOT_WRITABLE`; (5) a bill-of-materials write is attempted while the product is under enforcement hold → `ERR_ENFORCEMENT_HOLD_FREEZE`.

Rule 2 of this layer covers exactly five cases: creating a product component on a product whose `lifecycle_state` is `DISCONTINUED`, removing a product component from a product whose `lifecycle_state` is `DISCONTINUED`, creating an exposure assessment on a product whose `lifecycle_state` is `DISCONTINUED`, creating a placement whose determination's product has a `lifecycle_state` of `DISCONTINUED`, and creating a material/chemical link whose chemical's `status` is `WITHDRAWN`. A determination's **own** status is deliberately not policed here — it is policed much later in the ladder, so that the placement overlap rule can win against it. Placement creation is also the single endpoint on which rule 4 is evaluated ahead of rule 2 rather than behind it: a placement request that both supplies a non-writable name and names a discontinued product reports `ERR_FIELD_NOT_WRITABLE`, whereas on each of the other four cases the parent-state refusal comes first. Rule 3 covers an update against a product whose `lifecycle_state` is `DISCONTINUED`, an update against a component whose status is `REMOVED` or `SUPERSEDED`, and an update against a material reachable only through superseded components — that last case meaning precisely that at least one product component names this material directly and every component that names it has status `SUPERSEDED`; a single `ACTIVE` or `REMOVED` component naming it, or no component naming it at all, leaves the material mutable. Rule 4 applies to every create, every update and every transition surface, and the set of non-writable names is **global rather than per-entity**: any name that the server computes or stamps on any entity in this system, plus `expected_product_version` and `expected_notice_version` outside the two endpoints of Section 2.6, is refused on every endpoint that does not itself accept that name as an input. A name that is neither an input of the endpoint nor a server-computed name anywhere in the system is simply ignored rather than refused — though on the endpoints listed above as guarded by positions 5 and 6, those two rules are keyed on field names rather than on endpoints, so a stray name that happens to appear in their lists is still range-checked long before rule 4 would have ignored it.

**Layer 5 — business safety.** In order: (1) submission of an exposure assessment whose product has zero active components → `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_COMPONENTS`; (2) submission of a laboratory-method assessment while some contributing link is not on a laboratory basis or lacks its laboratory figures → `ERR_LAB_RESULT_MISSING`; (3) submission of a laboratory-method assessment while a contributing material's disclosure was refused and no naturally-occurring claim is on file → `ERR_SUPPLIER_DATA_INCOMPLETE`; (4) a determination created with a no-warning decision while a qualifying exposure exists → `ERR_EXPOSURE_ABOVE_SAFE_HARBOR`; (5) a determination created with a warning-required decision while no qualifying exposure exists → `ERR_NO_QUALIFYING_EXPOSURE`; (6) finalization of an assessment that has no recorded peer reviewer → `ERR_PEER_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; (7) a segregation-of-duties violation → `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`; (8) a placement whose channel is not required by its determination → `ERR_CHANNEL_NOT_REQUIRED`; (9) a reformulation target or verified exposure that is not strictly below its threshold → `ERR_TARGET_NOT_BELOW_THRESHOLD`.

Note the interaction between layer 4's state-legality rule and layer 5's peer-review rule. Finalization requested on an assessment that is currently `SUBMITTED` is a legal transition, so the state-legality rule passes and the peer-review rule decides the request. Finalization requested on an assessment that is currently `DRAFT` is not a legal transition at all, so the state-legality rule fires first and the response is `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` — never `ERR_PEER_REVIEW_REQUIRED` — regardless of whether a reviewer was ever recorded.

The segregation-of-duties rule covers exactly three comparisons: the `X-User-ID` performing a peer review equals the assessment's `submitted_by_user_id`; the `X-User-ID` performing an approval on a warning determination equals its `prepared_by_user_id`; the `X-User-ID` performing an execution on a settlement equals its `proposed_by_user_id`. It therefore guards the assessment transition surface, the determination transition surface and the settlement transition surface. The reformulation threshold rule guards both reformulation-project creation and the reformulation transition surface.

**Layer 6 — temporal business rules.** In order: (1) a placement whose closed effective window intersects an existing window → `ERR_PLACEMENT_OVERLAP`; (2) a reformulation project whose half-open planned window intersects an existing one → `ERR_REFORMULATION_OVERLAP`; (3) cure completion requested after the cure deadline has passed → `ERR_CURE_WINDOW_CLOSED`; (4) a notice whose `earliest_violation_date` is later than its `served_at`, or whose `served_at` is later than the current logical clock → `ERR_INVALID_INTERVAL`; (5) warranty revocation while the warranty window still covers a live notice → `ERR_WARRANTY_IN_USE`.

**Layer 7 — referential business rules.** In order: (1) an exposure assessment opened for a chemical that is not currently listed → `ERR_CHEMICAL_NOT_LISTED`; (2) a placement created against a determination that is not approved → `ERR_DETERMINATION_NOT_APPROVED`; (3) a settlement created against a notice that is not in settlement negotiation → `ERR_NOTICE_NOT_NEGOTIABLE`; (4) a material parent that would create a cycle or exceed the depth ceiling → `ERR_MATERIAL_CYCLE`, guarding both material creation and material update; (5) a reformulation project whose named link is inconsistent → `ERR_REFORMULATION_LINK_MISMATCH`; (6) a determination whose basis assessment is not final or belongs to another product → `ERR_BASIS_ASSESSMENT_NOT_FINAL`.

The second rule of this layer is the lower half of a deliberate conflict pair: because the placement overlap rule sits in layer 6, a placement request that both overlaps an existing window **and** targets a determination that is not approved reports `ERR_PLACEMENT_OVERLAP`, not `ERR_DETERMINATION_NOT_APPROVED`.

**Layer 8 — business constraints.** In order: (1) a determination created for a product that already has one in flight → `ERR_OPEN_DETERMINATION_EXISTS`; (2) a settlement created for a notice that already has a live one → `ERR_OPEN_SETTLEMENT_EXISTS`; (3) a counter or revision on a settlement already at the round ceiling → `ERR_SETTLEMENT_ROUND_LIMIT`; (4) a rejection on an assessment already at the rejection ceiling → `ERR_REJECTION_LIMIT_REACHED`; (5) marketing requested on a product whose compliance posture forbids it → `ERR_PRODUCT_ON_ENFORCEMENT_HOLD`.

**Layer 9 — fiscal rules.** In order: (1) `requested_attorney_fees_cents` above the absolute request ceiling → `ERR_FEE_REQUEST_EXCEEDS_CEILING`; (2) execution of a warranty-mitigated settlement whose adjusted penalty exceeds available indemnity → `ERR_INDEMNITY_CAP_EXCEEDED`; (3) a settlement create whose computed violation window is not positive → `ERR_NO_VIOLATION_WINDOW`; (4) a void requested on a settlement whose reformulation consideration has already been delivered → `ERR_SETTLEMENT_NOT_VOIDABLE`.

Layer 9's fee-ceiling rule is the lower half of a second deliberate conflict pair: a settlement create that both names a notice which already has a live settlement **and** requests fees above the ceiling reports `ERR_OPEN_SETTLEMENT_EXISTS`, because the open-settlement rule sits in layer 8. By the same ordering, a determination create that would trip both the in-flight-determination rule and a layer-9 rule reports `ERR_OPEN_DETERMINATION_EXISTS`.

### 2.9. Interval Conventions

This system uses three different interval conventions deliberately, and confusing them is the single most common source of wrong answers.

**Warning placement windows are CLOSED at both ends.** A placement is described by `effective_from` and an optional `effective_to`; a null `effective_to` means open-ended and is treated as positive infinity. Two windows for the same determination and the same channel collide when the new window's start is at or before the existing window's end **and** the existing window's start is at or before the new window's end. Because both ends are inclusive, two windows that merely touch — where the new start equals the existing end — **do** collide. Separately, when `effective_to` is supplied it must be strictly later than `effective_from`.

**Reformulation project windows are HALF-OPEN.** A project is described by `planned_start_date` (inclusive) and `planned_end_date` (exclusive). Two projects for the same product and chemical collide when the new project's start is strictly before the existing project's end **and** the existing project's start is strictly before the new project's end. Because the end is exclusive, two projects that merely touch — where the new start equals the existing end — do **not** collide. `planned_end_date` must be strictly later than `planned_start_date`.

**Supplier warranty coverage is HALF-OPEN.** A warranty covers an instant when `warranty_effective_from` is at or before that instant and the instant is strictly before `warranty_effective_to`; a null `warranty_effective_to` means open-ended and is treated as positive infinity. When `warranty_effective_to` is supplied it must be strictly later than `warranty_effective_from`; because both ends are optional at onboarding, a `warranty_effective_to` supplied while `warranty_effective_from` is absent has nothing to be compared against and the ordering check is simply not applied.

**The cure window is CLOSED at its end.** A cure completed at the exact instant of the deadline is inside the window; one microsecond later it is outside.

### 2.10. Posture Recomputation and Cascade Depth

A product carries two independent state dimensions on the same row. `lifecycle_state` is the commercial dimension and is driven only by explicit operator action. `compliance_posture` is a derived dimension that the server recomputes from the product's children — its components, their materials, the material/chemical links, the exposure assessments, the warning determination and its placements, and the notices of violation — on every mutation that can affect any of them. The two never drive each other: marketing a product does not change its posture, and a discontinued product keeps recomputing its posture, so it can still enter and leave an enforcement hold.

`compliance_posture` is evaluated as a **top-down ladder in which the first matching clause wins**, and the value is one of `EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`, `CLEAR`, `WARNING_REQUIRED`, `WARNING_ACTIVE` or `ENFORCEMENT_HOLD`. The clauses, in order:

1. **`ENFORCEMENT_HOLD`** — `open_notice_count` is at least 1, meaning at least one notice of violation on this product is in one of the statuses `RECEIVED`, `UNDER_EVALUATION`, `CURE_ELECTED`, `CURE_FAILED`, `SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION` or `LITIGATION`.
2. **`EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`** — clause 1 does not apply, and either the product has zero components in status `ACTIVE`, or some active component's material (or any material anywhere in that material's subtree) has a `disclosure_status` of `NOT_REQUESTED`, `REQUESTED`, `INCOMPLETE` or `REFUSED`, or some listed chemical reachable from the active components has no exposure assessment for this product in status `FINAL`.
3. **`WARNING_ACTIVE`** — clauses 1 and 2 do not apply, and there exists a warning determination for this product whose status is `APPROVED`, whose `decision` is `WARNING_REQUIRED`, and whose `covered_channel_count` equals the number of entries in its `required_channels`.
4. **`WARNING_REQUIRED`** — clauses 1 through 3 do not apply, and some exposure assessment of this product in status `FINAL` has a `result` of `AT_OR_ABOVE_SAFE_HARBOR` or `NO_SAFE_HARBOR`.
5. **`CLEAR`** — no earlier clause applies. This is the fallback.

Every posture recomputation refreshes the product's `updated_at`, and re-derives `open_notice_count` and `accrued_penalty_cents` from the product's notices and their settlements in the same pass, but it never by itself increments the product's `version`; a recomputation of `total_listed_chemical_load_ug` likewise rewrites that field alone, touching neither `updated_at` nor `version`. A product's `version` therefore moves only where the workflow subsection that caused the change says it moves. The initial posture of a newly created product is `EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`.

While a product's posture is `ENFORCEMENT_HOLD`, its bill of materials is **frozen**: adding a component, updating a component and removing a component are all refused with `ERR_ENFORCEMENT_HOLD_FREEZE`. The reformulation route stays open during a hold, because reformulation verification rewrites a concentration rather than the bill of materials.

Cascades in this system are explicit and can run four levels deep. Where a workflow subsection below describes a cascade, every named level fires within the same request, each affected row's `version` increments by 1 and its `updated_at` refreshes, and posture recomputation is the last step. Conversely, an assessment already in status `FINAL` is deliberately insulated from ordinary data churn: routine recomputation triggered by component, disclosure or laboratory activity touches only assessments in status `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `PEER_REVIEWED`. Among those open rows it rewrites — and so increments the `version` and refreshes the `updated_at` of — only the ones whose four derived numbers `daily_exposure_ug`, `margin_ratio`, `result` and `contribution_count` actually come out different from the values already stored; an open assessment that recomputes to exactly the numbers it already held is left completely untouched. A `FINAL` row's numbers change only through the four explicit supersede cascades — a safe-harbor revision, a delisting, a reformulation verification touching a contributing material, and a newer finalization on the same product and chemical.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. The Listed-Chemical Registry

Everything downstream begins with the registry. A `RegistryAdmin` registers a chemical by supplying its `chemical_name` — one to one hundred and twenty characters, used verbatim in warning label text and unique across the registry by exact case-sensitive match — its `cas_number` of one to twenty characters, its `endpoint_type`, its `safe_harbor_basis`, and, when a safe harbor exists, its `safe_harbor_level_ug_per_day`. The `endpoint_type` names the toxicological endpoint the listing rests on and is one of `CANCER`, `REPRODUCTIVE_MALE`, `REPRODUCTIVE_FEMALE` or `DEVELOPMENTAL`. From it the server derives `harm_class`, which is `CANCER` when `endpoint_type` is `CANCER` and `REPRODUCTIVE` for all three of the other endpoints; `harm_class` is therefore always one of `CANCER` or `REPRODUCTIVE`, and it is what later drives named-chemical grouping and harm-clause wording on labels. A newly registered chemical is created with `status` set to `PROPOSED` and `revision_count` set to 0, with `listing_effective_date` and `warning_required_from` both null.

The safe harbor level is expressed in micrograms per day and is the quantity at or above which a warning becomes necessary. `safe_harbor_basis` is one of `NSRL`, `MADL` or `NONE`, where `NONE` declares that no safe harbor exists for this chemical at all. Two coherence rules bind basis and level together and both report `ERR_SAFE_HARBOR_BASIS_MISMATCH`. The first is arithmetic: when the basis is `NSRL` or `MADL` the level must be present and strictly greater than zero, and when the basis is `NONE` the level must be null. The second is toxicological: an `endpoint_type` of `CANCER` may not be paired with a basis of `MADL`, and an `endpoint_type` of `REPRODUCTIVE_FEMALE`, `REPRODUCTIVE_MALE` or `DEVELOPMENTAL` may not be paired with a basis of `NSRL`. Only the arithmetic rule is re-checked when a safe harbor is later revised; the toxicological alignment rule is applied at registration only.

The registry lifecycle is a cycle rather than a line, and `status` moves among `PROPOSED`, `LISTED`, `DELISTED` and `WITHDRAWN` under five actions, all reserved to a `RegistryAdmin`. Listing a chemical takes it `PROPOSED → LISTED` and requires `listing_effective_date` in the payload; the server stores that date and computes `warning_required_from` as the effective date plus a one-year grace period of exactly three hundred and sixty-five days. Withdrawing a proposal takes it `PROPOSED → WITHDRAWN`, which is terminal — a withdrawn proposal can never be listed, and every subsequent action against it fails as an illegal transition. Delisting takes a chemical `LISTED → DELISTED`, and relisting takes it `DELISTED → LISTED`, again requiring `listing_effective_date` in the payload, overwriting the stored date and recomputing `warning_required_from` from it. `warning_required_from` stays null for as long as `listing_effective_date` is null. The transition surface accepts `listing_effective_date`, `safe_harbor_basis` and `safe_harbor_level_ug_per_day` on any of the five actions, but a field the requested action does not read is ignored rather than refused and is not even validated: a `WITHDRAW` carrying a malformed `listing_effective_date` or an undeclared `safe_harbor_basis` still succeeds and stores neither.

Revising a safe harbor is a self-loop on `LISTED`: the state does not change, but it is the deepest cascade in the system. The action requires `safe_harbor_basis` in the payload and, unless the new basis is `NONE`, `safe_harbor_level_ug_per_day`; the pair must satisfy the arithmetic coherence rule. On success the server overwrites both fields and increments `revision_count` by 1, then fires four cascade levels in order. At level one, every exposure assessment naming this chemical whose status is `FINAL` keeps the `daily_exposure_ug` it was finalized with but has its `margin_ratio` and `result` recomputed against the new level, and then transitions `FINAL → SUPERSEDED`. At level two, because those assessments have left `FINAL`, every warning determination that cites one of them and whose status is `APPROVED` transitions `APPROVED → REVOKED`. At level three, because those determinations have left `APPROVED`, every placement of each revoked determination whose status is `IMPLEMENTED` or `VERIFIED` transitions to `NON_COMPLIANT`. At level four, `covered_channel_count` on each revoked determination is recomputed and each affected product's `compliance_posture` is recomputed through the posture ladder. The chemical's own `version` increments by 1.

Delisting has a shallower but related cascade: every exposure assessment for the chemical whose status is `FINAL` transitions `FINAL → SUPERSEDED`, and `compliance_posture` is recomputed for every product that held such an assessment. A delisted chemical also stops contributing to product chemical-load totals, so those totals are recomputed for every product carrying it. Relisting recomputes `compliance_posture` for every product holding a component material linked to this chemical. Each of these five actions increments the chemical's `version` by 1.

Registry reads expose the derived fields directly. The single-chemical read returns `harm_class`, `warning_required_from` and `revision_count` alongside the input fields; the registry list accepts optional `status` and `harm_class` filters.

### 3.2. Suppliers, Warranties and the Indemnity Backstop

A `ComplianceAnalyst` onboards a supplier with a `legal_name` of one to one hundred and sixty characters, unique across all suppliers, and a `contact_email` that must contain an at-sign. Warranty terms may be supplied at onboarding but stay dormant: `warranty_scope`, `pass_through_warning_obligation`, `indemnity_cap_cents`, `warranty_effective_from` and `warranty_effective_to` are all optional at create time, and `warranty_status` is always set to `NONE` regardless of what was supplied. `pass_through_warning_obligation` defaults to false, `indemnity_cap_cents` defaults to 0, must be a whole number of cents — a fractional value is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` — and must never be negative, and `warranty_scope`, `warranty_effective_from` and `warranty_effective_to` all default to null.

`warranty_status` runs through three values — `NONE`, `ACTIVE` and `REVOKED` — and only a `RegulatoryCounsel` may move it. Executing a warranty takes it `NONE → ACTIVE` and requires that `warranty_scope` and `warranty_effective_from` are already stored and non-null; if either is null the request is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. That precondition reads the stored row rather than the payload, so it is evaluated only after the state-legality check has confirmed that `warranty_status` is `NONE`: an `EXECUTE_WARRANTY` against a supplier that is already `ACTIVE` reports `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` whatever the stored warranty terms look like. `warranty_scope` names the material classes the executed warranty covers and is one of `ALL_MATERIALS`, `RAW_MATERIAL_ONLY` or `PACKAGING_ONLY`; it defaults to null but keeps whatever was stored at onboarding while `warranty_status` is still `NONE` — that dormant value is exactly what execution requires to be present — and no action on this surface ever rewrites it. `ALL_MATERIALS` covers every material class, `RAW_MATERIAL_ONLY` covers only materials whose class is `RAW_MATERIAL`, and `PACKAGING_ONLY` covers only materials whose class is `PACKAGING` — note that neither of the narrow scopes covers a manufacturing aid. Execution also causes the defence-strength score of every product touched by this supplier's materials to be recomputed the next time that score is read.

Revoking a warranty takes it `ACTIVE → REVOKED`, which is terminal on this dimension. Revocation is blocked while the warranty is doing live defensive work: if the supplier's warranty window currently covers the `served_at` instant of at least one notice of violation whose status is `UNDER_EVALUATION` or `SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION`, the request is refused with `ERR_WARRANTY_IN_USE`. When revocation succeeds and `warranty_effective_to` was null, the server stamps it with the current logical clock, closing the open-ended window; from that instant the warranty mitigation factor no longer applies to settlements created afterwards. The supplier's `version` increments by 1 on each of the two warranty actions.

`pass_through_warning_obligation` records whether the warranty obliges the supplier to pass warning obligations up to its own upstream suppliers, and it is the discriminator between a full-credit and a half-credit warranty defence in the defence-strength score described in Section 3.9. `indemnity_cap_cents` is the maximum indemnity the supplier owes, in cents, and it becomes decisive at settlement execution: when a settlement's mitigation includes the warranty factor, executing it is refused with `ERR_INDEMNITY_CAP_EXCEEDED` if the settlement's `adjusted_penalty_cents` exceeds the summed `indemnity_cap_cents` of every supplier with an active warranty standing behind the product's active components.

A supplier read exposes `warranty_status`, `warranty_scope`, `indemnity_cap_cents` and both ends of the warranty window. A `SupplierPortalUser` may read its own supplier row.

### 3.3. Materials, the Bill of Materials and the Product Catalogue

Materials are the inventory layer, and they form a tree rather than a flat list. A `ComplianceAnalyst` creates a material with a `material_code` of one to forty characters that is unique across all materials, a `material_class` of `RAW_MATERIAL`, `PACKAGING` or `MANUFACTURING_AID`, and the `supplier_id` of the supplier that ships it. Supplying `parent_material_id` declares this material to be a constituent of a compound material and then `parent_mass_fraction` becomes mandatory; conversely, `parent_mass_fraction` may be supplied only when `parent_material_id` is also supplied. The fraction must be strictly greater than 0 and at most 1, and it is the weight with which this material's chemical content rolls up into its parent's. A top-level material leaves both fields null. `naturally_occurring_claim` is an optional boolean defaulting to false which records that the supplier asserts the listed chemical content of this material to be naturally occurring; it can also be set later through the disclosure surface.

`bom_depth` is derived, never supplied: it is 0 for a material whose `parent_material_id` is null, and otherwise one more than its parent's `bom_depth`. The largest permitted value is 4, so the tree can be at most five levels tall counting the root. Re-parenting an existing material is the only other way to disturb the tree; that update accepts `parent_material_id`, `parent_mass_fraction` and `naturally_occurring_claim`, recomputes `bom_depth` for the material and every one of its descendants, and rejects the whole request when any descendant would end up deeper than 4. The update is additive only: it can move a material to a different parent but never detach it, because an omitted or explicitly null `parent_material_id` leaves the stored parent exactly as it was. On a re-parent, `parent_mass_fraction` may be omitted only when the material already carries one from an earlier write, and is otherwise refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`; conversely `parent_mass_fraction` supplied on a material that neither has nor is being given a parent is refused the same way. An update whose body changes nothing — an empty body, or one that merely restates the stored values — is still a mutation: the material's `version` increments by 1, its `updated_at` refreshes, and the recomputation cascade below runs as usual. The same error, `ERR_MATERIAL_CYCLE`, also covers the cycle case: naming the material itself as its own parent, or naming any material that is already one of its own descendants. Both material creation and material re-parenting recompute the chemical mass load of every product whose active components reach the affected material through the parent chain, and re-parenting increments the material's `version` by 1. Because a re-parent changes *which* products reach the material, the set to recompute is the **union** of the products that reached it through its old parent chain, evaluated before the move, and the products that reach it through its new one, evaluated after the move: a product that reached the material only through the old chain has just lost the whole moved subtree from its roll-up, so its total must be refreshed in the same request alongside the totals of the products that have just gained it. Each product in that union also has its exposure assessments in status `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `PEER_REVIEWED` recomputed and its `compliance_posture` refreshed along with its load. A newly created material starts with `disclosure_status` set to `NOT_REQUESTED`, `data_request_sent_at` null and `data_received_at` null.

Products are created by a `RegistryAdmin` from a `sku` of one to forty characters unique across all products, a `product_name` of one to one hundred and sixty characters, a `product_category`, a non-empty `distribution_channels` array with no duplicates, a strictly positive `label_area_sq_in` measured in square inches, and a non-negative `annual_units` count of units sold into California per year, which must be a whole number — a fractional `annual_units` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, while `label_area_sq_in` is free to carry a fraction. `product_category` is one of `CONSUMER_GOOD`, `FOOD`, `DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT`, `FURNITURE` or `VEHICLE_PART`, and it drives symbol and website-reference selection. `distribution_channels` is a subset of `RETAIL`, `CATALOG` and `INTERNET`. `occupancy_type` is optional and defaults to `NONE`; it is one of `NONE`, `ENCLOSED_PARKING_FACILITY`, `AMUSEMENT_PARK` or `PETROLEUM_REFINERY`, and anything other than `NONE` forces the occupancy warning format and adds a posted-sign placement obligation. A newly created product has `lifecycle_state` set to `DEVELOPMENT`, `compliance_posture` set to `EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`, and every derived counter — `open_notice_count`, `accrued_penalty_cents` and `total_listed_chemical_load_ug` — set to 0.

The commercial dimension is a short line. Marketing a product takes `lifecycle_state` from `DEVELOPMENT` to `MARKETED`; discontinuing it takes `lifecycle_state` from either `DEVELOPMENT` or `MARKETED` to `DISCONTINUED`, which is terminal. Both actions are reserved to a `RegistryAdmin`, both increment the product's `version` by 1, and neither touches `compliance_posture` — the two dimensions are genuinely independent. Marketing is refused with `ERR_PRODUCT_ON_ENFORCEMENT_HOLD` while `compliance_posture` is `ENFORCEMENT_HOLD` or `EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`; a product may only go to market once its exposure picture is settled and no enforcement is live. Once a product is `DISCONTINUED`, creating a component on it, creating an exposure assessment for it and creating a placement under one of its determinations are all refused with `ERR_PARENT_STATE_FORBIDS_WRITE`, while posture recomputation continues normally, so a discontinued product can still enter and later leave an enforcement hold.

Product configuration can be updated by a `RegistryAdmin` through `product_name`, `product_category`, `occupancy_type`, `distribution_channels`, `label_area_sq_in` and `annual_units`. Neither state dimension is writable: supplying `lifecycle_state` or `compliance_posture` is rejected with `ERR_FIELD_NOT_WRITABLE`, and a `DISCONTINUED` product rejects the whole update with `ERR_ENTITY_IMMUTABLE`. Changing `distribution_channels` or `occupancy_type` changes the channel obligations that the **next** determination will compute; an already-approved determination keeps the channels it was computed with, while a determination still sitting in `DRAFT` or `PENDING_APPROVAL` recomputes them from the product's configuration as it stands at the moment of approval. The update increments the product's `version` by 1.

The bill of materials is the set of components a `ComplianceAnalyst` attaches to a product. Each component names a `material_id`, a strictly positive `quantity_grams_per_unit` giving the grams of that material present in one finished unit, and a `contact_factor` between 0.0 and 1.0 inclusive giving the fraction of the component mass that is available for exposure through the assessed route. A component's own `status` is `ACTIVE`, `REMOVED` or `SUPERSEDED`, and it starts `ACTIVE`; only `ACTIVE` components participate in any exposure or load calculation, and both `REMOVED` and `SUPERSEDED` are terminal. Because the active pair of `product_id` and `material_id` is unique, replacing a component that uses the same material requires the optional `supersedes_component_id` field: supplying it moves the named component `ACTIVE → SUPERSEDED` **before** the uniqueness check runs, so the check only ever sees the replacement, and the replacement is created `ACTIVE` in the same request. The superseded row's `version` increments by 1, the product's chemical load is recomputed from the replacement's quantity, and `compliance_posture` is recomputed. The named component must currently be `ACTIVE` and must belong to the same product, and the two failures land in different layers: a `supersedes_component_id` that resolves to no component at all, or to a component of another product, is reported as `ERR_NOT_FOUND` in layer 2 and therefore outranks the uniqueness check, while one that resolves to a component of this product whose status is already `REMOVED` or `SUPERSEDED` is reported as `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` under layer 4 and therefore loses to it.

Adjusting a component in place accepts `quantity_grams_per_unit` and `contact_factor`. Removing a component takes it `ACTIVE → REMOVED` and excludes it from every exposure and load calculation from that instant on; a product whose last `ACTIVE` component is removed falls back to `EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN` through clause 2 of the posture ladder. Removal is also blocked while the parent product is `DISCONTINUED`, with `ERR_PARENT_STATE_FORBIDS_WRITE`. Component creation, component adjustment and component removal each recompute the product's `total_listed_chemical_load_ug`, recompute `daily_exposure_ug` for every exposure assessment of that product whose status is `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `PEER_REVIEWED`, recompute `compliance_posture`, and increment both the product's `version` and the component's `version` by 1. All three of these bill-of-materials writes are frozen with `ERR_ENFORCEMENT_HOLD_FREEZE` while the product's posture is `ENFORCEMENT_HOLD`.

`total_listed_chemical_load_ug` is a conserved bookkeeping quantity that answers "how many micrograms of listed chemical does one finished unit of this product contain, ignoring route and use frequency?". It is computed by iterating over every chemical whose registry `status` is `LISTED`, taken in ascending order of `chemical_name`, and within each chemical over every component of the product whose status is `ACTIVE`, taken in ascending order of `created_at`. For each such chemical-and-component pair the server evaluates the modelled effective concentration of that chemical in that component's material — the roll-up described in Section 3.5, always under the modelled variant regardless of any assessment — multiplies it by that component's `quantity_grams_per_unit`, and rounds the product half up to three decimal places. Those rounded per-pair terms are then summed, and the sum is rounded half up to three decimal places. Chemicals whose registry status is `PROPOSED`, `DELISTED` or `WITHDRAWN` contribute nothing, and components that are `REMOVED` or `SUPERSEDED` contribute nothing. Because the rounding order is pinned in exactly this way, the quantity is conserved: setting a component's `quantity_grams_per_unit` to some value and then back to its original restores the identical three-decimal total with no accumulated drift.

The load is recomputed on every mutation that can change an active component's material, quantity or concentration: component creation, component adjustment, component removal or supersession, declaring a chemical present in a material, any laboratory-testing action on a material/chemical link, re-parenting a material, any supplier-disclosure action (because a disclosure gap raises the affected material to the modelled default concentration), reformulation verification, and the listing, delisting and relisting registry actions.

### 3.4. Supplier Data Collection

Knowing what a supplier will admit to is a state machine of its own, and `disclosure_status` tracks it through six values: `NOT_REQUESTED`, `REQUESTED`, `RECEIVED`, `INCOMPLETE`, `REFUSED` and `VERIFIED`. All of the moves are driven through a single disclosure surface that takes an `action` and, depending on the action, an optional `response_completeness` and an optional `naturally_occurring_claim`. There is no terminal state here; disclosure is an ongoing obligation.

A `ComplianceAnalyst` opens the process by requesting data, taking the material `NOT_REQUESTED → REQUESTED` and stamping `data_request_sent_at` with the current logical clock. The same request action also serves as the re-request after a bad outcome: it takes a material `REFUSED → REQUESTED` and `INCOMPLETE → REQUESTED`, re-stamping `data_request_sent_at` each time. Recording a response requires `response_completeness`, and its value forks the outcome: `COMPLETE` takes the material `REQUESTED → RECEIVED`, while `INCOMPLETE` takes it `REQUESTED → INCOMPLETE`. Both stamp `data_received_at` with the current logical clock, and both may be filed either by the supplier itself acting as a `SupplierPortalUser` — in which case the caller's `X-User-ID` must equal the material's `supplier_id` — or transcribed by a `ComplianceAnalyst`. An incomplete response has a specific modelling consequence: from that point on, every modelled exposure recomputation for products holding this material substitutes the modelled default concentration for this material's own contribution. Marking a response refused takes the material `REQUESTED → REFUSED` and is reserved to a `ComplianceAnalyst`, as is verifying a received response, which takes the material `RECEIVED → VERIFIED`; neither of those two actions touches `data_request_sent_at` or `data_received_at`, so requesting and recording are the only actions that stamp a timestamp here. The optional `naturally_occurring_claim` may be restated on any of the four actions, and when it is omitted the stored value is left exactly as it stands.

Every one of these actions increments the material's `version` by 1 and recomputes `compliance_posture` for every product that holds this material, whether directly as a component or indirectly through the material tree. Beyond that, any change to `disclosure_status` recomputes every modelled exposure assessment in status `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `PEER_REVIEWED` belonging to every product that reaches this material, and recomputes those products' `total_listed_chemical_load_ug` and `compliance_posture`. Assessments already in `FINAL` keep the numbers they were finalized with — only the four explicit supersede cascades may touch a `FINAL` row. Note that a `RECEIVED` or `VERIFIED` disclosure carries no worst-case substitution, while `NOT_REQUESTED`, `REQUESTED`, `INCOMPLETE` and `REFUSED` all do; this is why simply requesting data does not improve a product's exposure picture, and why clause 2 of the posture ladder holds a product in `EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN` until every material in every active component's subtree has reached `RECEIVED` or `VERIFIED`.

A supplier acting on its own behalf may also declare that a listed chemical is present in one of its materials, supplying the `chemical_id` and a non-negative `declared_concentration_ppm` in parts per million — equivalently, micrograms of chemical per gram of material. A `ComplianceAnalyst` may do the same. The pair of `material_id` and `chemical_id` is unique, so a chemical can be declared at most once per material; a second declaration is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_RESOURCE`. A link may not be created against a chemical whose registry `status` is `WITHDRAWN`, which is refused with `ERR_PARENT_STATE_FORBIDS_WRITE`. A newly declared link starts with `measurement_basis` set to `SUPPLIER_DECLARED` and `status` set to `DECLARED`, with `lab_name`, `lab_result_ppm`, `lab_loq_ppm`, `lab_reported_at` and `dispute_note` all null. Creating the link recomputes `total_listed_chemical_load_ug` and `compliance_posture` for every product that reaches this material.

### 3.5. Laboratory Testing and the Concentration Roll-Up

A supplier's declared figure is an assertion; a laboratory result is evidence. The `status` of a material/chemical link tracks that upgrade through five values — `DECLARED`, `TEST_REQUESTED`, `TEST_REPORTED`, `DISPUTED` and `VERIFIED` — and the path through them is a cycle, because a disputed result must be able to go back for re-testing.

A `ComplianceAnalyst` orders a test, taking the link `DECLARED → TEST_REQUESTED`. When the laboratory answers, the same analyst reports the result, taking the link `TEST_REQUESTED → TEST_REPORTED`; that action requires all three of `lab_name`, `lab_result_ppm` and `lab_loq_ppm` in the payload, where `lab_name` identifies the analytical laboratory, `lab_result_ppm` is the reported concentration in parts per million and must not be negative, and `lab_loq_ppm` is the laboratory's limit of quantitation in parts per million and must be strictly positive. Reporting stores all three, stamps `lab_reported_at` with the current logical clock, and flips `measurement_basis` from `SUPPLIER_DECLARED` to `LAB_TESTED`, which is what makes this link admissible under the laboratory exposure method.

A `ToxicologyReviewer` then adjudicates. Verifying takes the link `TEST_REPORTED → VERIFIED`, but only when the underlying material's `disclosure_status` is `RECEIVED` or `VERIFIED` — a measured value on a material whose supplier never answered cannot be signed off, and an attempt against any other `disclosure_status` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`, the same identifier the link's own state machine uses, evaluated immediately after the link's own state check. Disputing takes the link `TEST_REPORTED → DISPUTED` and requires `dispute_note` in the payload; disputing stores the note, resets `measurement_basis` to `SUPPLIER_DECLARED`, and clears `lab_name`, `lab_result_ppm`, `lab_loq_ppm` and `lab_reported_at` all back to null, so the disputed evidence stops influencing any calculation. From `DISPUTED`, the analyst orders another test, taking the link `DISPUTED → TEST_REQUESTED` and closing the cycle. Reporting a result, verifying and disputing each recompute `daily_exposure_ug` for every exposure assessment in status `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `PEER_REVIEWED` of every product that reaches this material, each under that assessment's own method variant, and each recomputes those products' `total_listed_chemical_load_ug` and `compliance_posture`; assessments already `FINAL` are left alone. Every action on this surface increments the link's `version` by 1.

There is one further way a link changes state, and it is driven by the system rather than by an operator: when a reformulation project that names this link submits its verification, the link's `declared_concentration_ppm` is overwritten with the project's `target_concentration_ppm` and its `status` resets to `DECLARED`. That reset is reached from `VERIFIED → DECLARED`, from `TEST_REPORTED → DECLARED`, and as a self-loop `DECLARED → DECLARED`; in the first two cases `measurement_basis` resets to `SUPPLIER_DECLARED` and all four laboratory fields are cleared to null, and in all three cases the link's `version` increments by 1.

**Effective concentration** is the transitive roll-up that every exposure number in this system is built on, and it is never exposed as a resource of its own. It is evaluated for a triple of one material, one chemical and one variant, where the variant is either modelled or laboratory-based and is inherited from the calling assessment's `method`. The evaluation is a depth-first post-order walk down `parent_material_id` links, bounded by the depth ceiling of 4 that material writes already enforce, so the recursion always terminates.

For any given material the walk first establishes that material's **own** contribution, using the at-most-one link whose `material_id` is this material and whose `chemical_id` is the chemical in question. Under the **modelled** variant the own contribution is decided by the first of these cases that applies: if the material's `disclosure_status` is `NOT_REQUESTED`, `REQUESTED`, `INCOMPLETE` or `REFUSED`, the own contribution is the modelled default of 250.0 parts per million, applied as a worst-case substitution regardless of any declared or measured value on the link; otherwise, if no link exists for this material and chemical, the own contribution is 0.0; otherwise, if `measurement_basis` is `LAB_TESTED` and `lab_result_ppm` is strictly below `lab_loq_ppm`, the own contribution is half of `lab_loq_ppm`, the standard half-limit substitution for a non-detect; otherwise, if `measurement_basis` is `LAB_TESTED`, the own contribution is `lab_result_ppm` as reported; otherwise the own contribution is `declared_concentration_ppm`.

Under the **laboratory** variant the own contribution is decided differently, and `disclosure_status` is ignored entirely on the principle that measured data supersedes supplier gaps. If no link exists for this material and chemical, the own contribution is 0.0. If a link exists but its `measurement_basis` is not `LAB_TESTED`, or either laboratory figure is null, the link carries no admissible measured figure. That condition is turned into `ERR_LAB_RESULT_MISSING` at exactly one point in the system — the submission gate of Section 3.6 — and the roll-up itself never raises: everywhere else the laboratory variant is evaluated, which is at assessment create time, at finalization and in every routine recomputation cascade, an inadmissible link simply contributes 0.0, so a `LAB_TESTED` assessment whose contributing links are all still supplier-declared sits in `DRAFT` reporting a `daily_exposure_ug` of 0 and a `contribution_count` of 0 rather than reporting an error. If the link is admissible and `lab_result_ppm` is strictly below `lab_loq_ppm`, the own contribution is 0.0 — under this variant a non-detect is treated as a true zero rather than as half the limit. Otherwise the own contribution is `lab_result_ppm`.

Having established the own contribution, the walk adds the children. The children of a material are the materials whose `parent_material_id` is this material, taken in ascending order of `created_at`. For each child, the child's own effective concentration is computed by the same rules under the same variant and multiplied by that child's `parent_mass_fraction`, and the products are added to the running total. The total for the level is then rounded half up to four decimal places, and that rounded value is what the level returns to its parent. Rounding is therefore applied once per tree level, in post-order — not once at the end of the whole walk.

The two variants are the system's first deliberate dual-variant calculation: for identical stored rows, the laboratory variant yields a strictly smaller or equal concentration than the modelled variant whenever a contributing link is a non-detect (0.0 rather than half the limit of quantitation) or a contributing material has an incomplete or refused disclosure (its measured value rather than the modelled default of 250.0).

### 3.6. Exposure Assessment and Independent Peer Review

An exposure assessment answers the regulatory question for exactly one pair of a product and a chemical. A `ComplianceAnalyst` opens one against a product by supplying the `chemical_id`, a `method` of either `LAB_TESTED` or `MODELED`, a `route` of `ORAL`, `DERMAL` or `INHALATION`, and a strictly positive `uses_per_day` giving the reasonably anticipated number of uses of the product per day, which need not be a whole number. The chemical must be in registry `status` `LISTED` at create time, otherwise the request is refused with `ERR_CHEMICAL_NOT_LISTED`, and the product must not be `DISCONTINUED`. A newly created assessment has `status` `DRAFT`, `submitted_by_user_id`, `reviewed_by_user_id` and `finalized_at` all null, `rejection_count` 0 and `review_note` null; its numbers are computed immediately from the product's active components. The create leaves the product row itself entirely alone: a `DRAFT` assessment cannot move any clause of the posture ladder, so no counter, no `compliance_posture`, no `updated_at` and no `version` on the product is disturbed by it.

**Daily exposure.** `daily_exposure_ug` is the micrograms per day of the chemical a consumer takes up, and it is assembled component by component. The server walks the product's components whose status is `ACTIVE` in ascending order of `created_at`. For each one it evaluates the effective concentration of the assessment's chemical in that component's material, under the variant named by the assessment's own `method`. A component whose effective concentration comes out as exactly 0.0 is skipped entirely and does not count toward `contribution_count`. Otherwise the server multiplies together five factors — the effective concentration, the component's `quantity_grams_per_unit`, the component's `contact_factor`, the absorption factor for the assessment's `route`, and the assessment's `uses_per_day` — and rounds that product half up to three decimal places, giving one per-component contribution. The absorption factors are 1.00 for the `ORAL` route, 0.10 for the `DERMAL` route and 0.25 for the `INHALATION` route. Because one part per million is one microgram of chemical per gram of material, parts per million multiplied by grams and by dimensionless factors yields micrograms directly, with no unit conversion.

`contribution_count` is the number of per-component contributions that survived the zero-skip, and `daily_exposure_ug` is the sum of those already-rounded contributions, itself rounded half up to three decimal places. **The order of these two roundings is pinned and is not interchangeable**: each contribution is rounded to three decimals first, the rounded contributions are then summed, and the sum is rounded to three decimals last. Summing the raw products and rounding once at the end is explicitly not what this system does and produces a different figure whenever two or more components each carry a non-zero fourth decimal — two contributions that each come to 1.2345 before rounding give 2.470 under the pinned order and 2.469 under the forbidden one.

**The safe-harbor verdict.** Immediately after every daily-exposure evaluation the server compares the figure against the chemical's current safe harbor level and writes `margin_ratio` and `result`. When the chemical's `safe_harbor_basis` is `NONE` there is no level to compare against: `margin_ratio` is set to null and `result` is set to `NO_SAFE_HARBOR`, which is the most adverse of the three verdicts because it means no exposure is presumptively safe. Otherwise `margin_ratio` is `daily_exposure_ug` divided by `safe_harbor_level_ug_per_day`, rounded half up to four decimal places, and `result` is `AT_OR_ABOVE_SAFE_HARBOR` when `daily_exposure_ug` is greater than or equal to the level and `BELOW_SAFE_HARBOR` when it is strictly less. The comparison is **inclusive**: an exposure exactly equal to the safe harbor level is `AT_OR_ABOVE_SAFE_HARBOR`, and it is the three-decimal `daily_exposure_ug` value that is compared, not any intermediate. The same verdict recomputation runs for every `FINAL` assessment of a chemical whose safe harbor is revised.

**The review cycle.** `status` moves through `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `PEER_REVIEWED`, `REJECTED`, `FINAL` and `SUPERSEDED`, with `SUPERSEDED` terminal, and the shape is a cycle so that a rejected assessment can be reworked. A `ComplianceAnalyst` submits, taking the assessment `DRAFT → SUBMITTED`; submission recomputes `daily_exposure_ug`, `margin_ratio`, `result` and `contribution_count` from current data and stamps `submitted_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`. Submission is where the three data-quality gates fire, in this order. First, if the product has zero components in status `ACTIVE` the submission is refused with `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_COMPONENTS`, because the exposure sum would be over the empty set. Second, if the assessment's `method` is `LAB_TESTED` and some contributing link has a `measurement_basis` other than `LAB_TESTED`, or has a null `lab_result_ppm`, or has a null `lab_loq_ppm`, the submission is refused with `ERR_LAB_RESULT_MISSING`. A **contributing link**, for this gate and the next alike, is any material/chemical link naming the assessment's own chemical and carried by any material in the subtree of a material named by a component of this product in status `ACTIVE` — the component's own material or anything below it in the tree. Whether that link's figure would actually survive into a non-zero contribution is irrelevant: a link deep under a small `parent_mass_fraction` chain is still a contributing link. Third, if the assessment's `method` is `LAB_TESTED` and some contributing material — meaning the material that carries such a link, so a material holding no link for this chemical is never examined by this gate at all — has `disclosure_status` `REFUSED` while its `naturally_occurring_claim` is false, the submission is refused with `ERR_SUPPLIER_DATA_INCOMPLETE`; a naturally-occurring claim on file excuses the refusal.

A `ToxicologyReviewer` takes it from there. Peer review takes the assessment `SUBMITTED → PEER_REVIEWED` and stamps `reviewed_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`; it is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` when the acting `X-User-ID` equals the assessment's `submitted_by_user_id`. Rejection is available from both `SUBMITTED → REJECTED` and `PEER_REVIEWED → REJECTED`, requires `review_note` in the payload, stores that note and increments `rejection_count` by 1; from `SUBMITTED` it additionally carries the same submitter-cannot-review separation. Rejection is capped: an assessment whose `rejection_count` already equals 2 refuses a further rejection with `ERR_REJECTION_LIMIT_REACHED`. From `REJECTED`, a `ComplianceAnalyst` reopens the assessment, taking it `REJECTED → DRAFT` and closing the `SUBMITTED → REJECTED → DRAFT → SUBMITTED` revision cycle. Reopening clears `submitted_by_user_id` back to null but **deliberately preserves** `reviewed_by_user_id`, which is what allows an assessment that was peer-reviewed on an earlier turn of the cycle to be finalized directly out of `SUBMITTED` on a later turn. Reopening also leaves `rejection_count` exactly as it stands, so the ceiling of 2 is a lifetime budget for the row rather than a per-turn allowance. `review_note` is an accepted optional input on all five actions of this surface, but the actions treat it differently: `REJECT` requires it and always overwrites the stored note; `PEER_REVIEW`, `REOPEN` and `FINALIZE` overwrite the stored note when it is supplied and leave it exactly as it stands when it is omitted; and `SUBMIT` accepts it and ignores it, never writing it at all.

Finalization is performed by a `ToxicologyReviewer` and is reachable from two states with the same effect. From `PEER_REVIEWED → FINAL` it needs only that the assessment's chemical is still in registry `status` `LISTED`. From `SUBMITTED → FINAL` it additionally needs `reviewed_by_user_id` to be non-null — that is, this assessment must have been peer-reviewed on an earlier turn of the cycle — and when it is null the request is refused with `ERR_PEER_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Both arms carry exactly the same side-effect set: `daily_exposure_ug`, `margin_ratio`, `result` and `contribution_count` are recomputed one final time, `finalized_at` is stamped with the current logical clock, any other assessment of the same product and chemical whose status is `FINAL` transitions `FINAL → SUPERSEDED`, and `compliance_posture` is recomputed. The assessment's `version` increments by 1 on every action on this surface, and any status change that adds or removes a `FINAL` assessment also increments the product's `version` by 1 — so a finalization that additionally supersedes an earlier `FINAL` assessment of the same product and chemical increments the product's `version` by 2, once for the arrival and once for the departure. The four actions that neither add nor remove a `FINAL` row — submission, peer review, rejection and reopening — leave the product's `version` alone and only refresh its `updated_at` through the posture recomputation they trigger.

Leaving `FINAL` is always system-driven, never requested directly. An assessment goes `FINAL → SUPERSEDED` when a newer finalization lands on the same product and chemical, when its chemical's safe harbor is revised, when its chemical is delisted, or when a reformulation verification touches one of its contributing materials. Whichever the cause, `margin_ratio` and `result` are recomputed against the current safe harbor level **before** the status flips, then every warning determination whose `assessment_id` is this assessment and whose status is `APPROVED` transitions `APPROVED → REVOKED`, then `compliance_posture` is recomputed, and the assessment's `version` increments by 1.

Assessment reads expose `daily_exposure_ug`, `margin_ratio`, `result`, `contribution_count` and the peer-review audit fields; the assessment list accepts optional `product_id`, `chemical_id`, `status`, `method` and `result` filters.

### 3.7. Warning Determination, Label Content and Channel Placement

A **warning determination** is the company's formal position on whether a product needs a warning and, if so, what that warning says and where it appears. A `LabelingSpecialist` creates one by naming the `product_id`, the `assessment_id` of the primary basis assessment being cited, a `decision` of either `WARNING_REQUIRED` or `NO_WARNING_REQUIRED`, and the `expected_product_version` described in Section 2.6. The cited assessment must belong to the named product and must be in status `FINAL` at create time; if it belongs to a different product or is in any other status, the create is refused with `ERR_BASIS_ASSESSMENT_NOT_FINAL`. A product may hold only one in-flight determination: if one already exists in status `DRAFT` or `PENDING_APPROVAL`, the create is refused with `ERR_OPEN_DETERMINATION_EXISTS`. An already-`APPROVED` determination does **not** block a successor — the successor is created in `DRAFT` alongside it and supersedes it only when the successor is itself approved. The create stamps `prepared_by_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`, sets `status` to `DRAFT`, and increments the product's `version` by 1.

The decision must be consistent with the exposure evidence, and the two directions have separate errors. A determination created with `decision` `NO_WARNING_REQUIRED` while the product has at least one `FINAL` assessment whose `result` is `AT_OR_ABOVE_SAFE_HARBOR` or `NO_SAFE_HARBOR` is refused with `ERR_EXPOSURE_ABOVE_SAFE_HARBOR`. A determination created with `decision` `WARNING_REQUIRED` while the product has **no** `FINAL` assessment whose `result` is `AT_OR_ABOVE_SAFE_HARBOR` or `NO_SAFE_HARBOR` is refused with `ERR_NO_QUALIFYING_EXPOSURE`, because the named-chemical selector would have nothing to name.

**Content selection.** Everything else on the determination is computed, never supplied: `warning_type`, `symbol_required`, `named_chemicals`, `website_url`, `warning_text` and `required_channels`. The computation runs at create time and again on approval, against the data current at that moment, and it proceeds in seven steps. Because the qualifying set is re-derived at approval while approval itself only requires the cited basis assessment to still be `FINAL`, a `WARNING_REQUIRED` determination can reach approval after every qualifying assessment of its product has left `FINAL`. Approval is not refused for that; the seven steps simply run against an empty qualifying set, so `named_chemicals` comes out empty, the chemical list renders as the empty string, the verb is `are`, and no harm clause and no harm label are selected at all, leaving those two slots of the chosen template filled with the literal text `None`.

The first step establishes the **qualifying set**: every exposure assessment of this determination's product whose `status` is `FINAL` and whose `result` is `AT_OR_ABOVE_SAFE_HARBOR` or `NO_SAFE_HARBOR`. Assessments in any other status, and `FINAL` assessments whose `result` is `BELOW_SAFE_HARBOR`, are excluded. When the `decision` is `NO_WARNING_REQUIRED` the computation stops here and writes the empty position: `warning_type` is `NONE`, `symbol_required` is false, `named_chemicals` is the empty array, `website_url` is null, `warning_text` is null, and `required_channels` is the empty array.

The second step selects `warning_type`, which is one of `NONE`, `PRODUCT_SPECIFIC_LONG`, `PRODUCT_SPECIFIC_SHORT` or `OCCUPANCY`, from a three-way branch taken in this order: if the product's `occupancy_type` is anything other than `NONE`, the type is `OCCUPANCY`; otherwise, if the product's `label_area_sq_in` is strictly less than five square inches, the type is `PRODUCT_SPECIFIC_SHORT`, the abbreviated format available to small labels; otherwise the type is `PRODUCT_SPECIFIC_LONG`. A label of exactly five square inches therefore takes the long form.

The third step fills `named_chemicals`, which holds at most one chemical name per harm class. The two harm classes are considered in the pinned order `CANCER` first and then `REPRODUCTIVE`. For each class in turn the server takes the subset of the qualifying set whose chemical's `harm_class` equals that class; an empty subset is simply skipped, which is not an error. Within a non-empty subset the assessments are ranked and the top one's chemical name is appended. The ranking is: assessments whose `result` is `NO_SAFE_HARBOR` outrank every assessment with a finite `margin_ratio`; among the remainder, a larger `margin_ratio` outranks a smaller one; the first tie-break is the chemical's `safe_harbor_level_ug_per_day` ascending, with a null level sorting ahead of every numeric level; the second tie-break is the chemical's `chemical_name` ascending. The result is therefore an ordered array with the cancer name first when a cancer-class chemical qualifies, followed by the reproductive name when a reproductive-class chemical qualifies.

The fourth step sets `symbol_required`, which governs whether the black-triangle-in-yellow symbol must appear alongside the warning. It is false in exactly one situation — the product's `product_category` is `FOOD` **and** the `warning_type` is not `OCCUPANCY` — and true in every other situation. The default for the field is true.

The fifth step selects `website_url`, and occupancy precedence beats the food carve-out. If `warning_type` is `OCCUPANCY` the reference is `https://www.P65Warnings.ca.gov/places`; otherwise, if `product_category` is `FOOD` the reference is `https://www.P65Warnings.ca.gov/food`; otherwise the reference is the base address `https://www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`.

The sixth step assembles `warning_text`, and the assembly is byte-exact — including the trailing period on the long and occupancy formats, which the short format does not carry. Two intermediate values are derived from the set of harm classes present across the **whole qualifying set** (not merely across the named chemicals). When that set contains only `CANCER`, the harm clause is `cancer` and the harm label is `Cancer`. When it contains only `REPRODUCTIVE`, the harm clause is `birth defects or other reproductive harm` and the harm label is `Reproductive Harm`. When it contains both, the harm clause is `cancer and birth defects or other reproductive harm` and the harm label is `Cancer and Reproductive Harm`. A verb form is chosen from the number of entries in `named_chemicals`: `is` when there is exactly one entry, and `are` for any other count. The chemical list is the entries of `named_chemicals` joined with a comma followed by a single space, in the order that array holds them. The three templates are then:

```
PRODUCT_SPECIFIC_LONG:
WARNING: This product can expose you to chemicals including {chem_list}, which {verb} known to the State of California to cause {harm_clause}. For more information go to {website_url}.

PRODUCT_SPECIFIC_SHORT:
WARNING: {harm_label} risk from exposure to {first_named_chemical} - {website_url}

OCCUPANCY:
WARNING: Entering this area can expose you to chemicals including {chem_list}, which {verb} known to the State of California to cause {harm_clause}. For more information go to {website_url}.
```

The short format names only the first entry of `named_chemicals` and ends with the bare website reference — no trailing period, and the separator before it is a space, a single ASCII hyphen and a space.

The seventh step fills `required_channels`, the placement channels this determination must cover, in the pinned order `LABEL`, `CATALOG`, `INTERNET_POS`, `POSTED_SIGN`. `LABEL` is included when the product's `distribution_channels` contains `RETAIL`; `CATALOG` when it contains `CATALOG`; `INTERNET_POS` when it contains `INTERNET`; and `POSTED_SIGN` is appended when the product's `occupancy_type` is anything other than `NONE`.

**The approval cycle.** A determination's `status` moves through `DRAFT`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED`, `REVOKED` and `SUPERSEDED`, with the last two terminal. A `LabelingSpecialist` submits for approval, taking the determination `DRAFT → PENDING_APPROVAL`. A `RegulatoryCounsel` may return it for revision, taking it `PENDING_APPROVAL → DRAFT` and closing that cycle; that action requires `review_note` in the payload, and `review_note` is ignored on every other action on this surface. Approval, also by a `RegulatoryCounsel`, takes it `PENDING_APPROVAL → APPROVED` and requires two things beyond the state: the acting `X-User-ID` must differ from `prepared_by_user_id`, otherwise the request is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, and the assessment named by `assessment_id` must still be in status `FINAL`. Approval re-runs the whole content selection against current data, stamps `approved_by_user_id`, moves any other determination of the same product whose status is `APPROVED` to `SUPERSEDED`, recomputes `covered_channel_count`, and recomputes `compliance_posture` — which will read `WARNING_REQUIRED` or `WARNING_ACTIVE` depending on channel coverage.

Revocation takes a determination `APPROVED → REVOKED`. It may be requested by a `RegulatoryCounsel`, and it is also applied by the system whenever the basis assessment leaves `FINAL`. Either way, every placement of this determination whose status is `IMPLEMENTED` or `VERIFIED` transitions to `NON_COMPLIANT`, `covered_channel_count` falls to 0, and `compliance_posture` is recomputed. Supersession is only ever system-driven and only ever happens because a newer determination for the same product was approved; it takes the older determination `APPROVED → SUPERSEDED`, stamps `effective_to` with the current logical clock on every placement of that determination whose `effective_to` was null while leaving those placements' statuses **unchanged**, recomputes `covered_channel_count` and recomputes `compliance_posture`. Because a placement window is closed at its end, an `effective_to` stamped at the current instant still covers that instant, so the recomputation reproduces the count the superseded determination already held rather than dropping it to 0; the drop to 0 belongs to revocation alone. The distinction matters: a reformulation verification never routes through supersession — it supersedes the basis assessment, and a determination whose basis assessment leaves `FINAL` goes to `REVOKED`. Every action on this surface increments the determination's `version` by 1 and the product's `version` by 1.

**Placements.** A `LabelingSpecialist` places the approved warning on one channel at a time. A placement names a `channel` of `LABEL`, `CATALOG`, `INTERNET_POS` or `POSTED_SIGN`, an `effective_from` instant, an optional `effective_to` instant, and a `placement_detail` of one to two hundred characters describing the physical or digital placement. `catalog_page_ref` is required when the `channel` is `CATALOG` and gives the catalogue page or item reference; `internet_display_position` is required when the `channel` is `INTERNET_POS` and is one of `BEFORE_PURCHASE_LINK`, `ON_PRODUCT_PAGE` or `HYPERLINK_WARNING`, describing where the warning sits in the internet purchase flow. Both default to null, and either may also be supplied on a channel that does not require it: the value is then checked against its own shape and simply stored, not refused. The channel must be a member of the determination's computed `required_channels`, otherwise the create is refused with `ERR_CHANNEL_NOT_REQUIRED`. The determination must be in status `APPROVED`, otherwise the create is refused with `ERR_DETERMINATION_NOT_APPROVED` — but recall from Section 2.8 that the closed-window overlap check is evaluated first, so a request that both overlaps and targets a non-approved determination reports `ERR_PLACEMENT_OVERLAP`. Overlap is judged against existing placements of the same determination on the same channel whose status is not `NON_COMPLIANT`; a flagged placement no longer reserves its window. A newly created placement has `status` `PLANNED`, `implemented_by_user_id` null and `remediation_count` 0, and its creation recomputes the determination's `covered_channel_count` and the product's `compliance_posture`. Neither recomputation can actually move anything: a `PLANNED` placement is neither `IMPLEMENTED` nor `VERIFIED`, so the count comes out unchanged and the determination's `version` does not increment, and the posture recomputation refreshes the product's `updated_at` without incrementing the product's `version`. Placement creation is therefore the one write on this surface that leaves both the determination's and the product's `version` exactly where they were.

`covered_channel_count` is the number of distinct entries of `required_channels` for which at least one placement of this determination has a `status` of `IMPLEMENTED` or `VERIFIED` and an effective window that covers the current logical clock. A product moves from `WARNING_REQUIRED` to `WARNING_ACTIVE` exactly when that count reaches the number of entries in `required_channels`.

A placement's own `status` runs `PLANNED`, `IMPLEMENTED`, `VERIFIED` and `NON_COMPLIANT`, and the last three form a cycle. A `LabelingSpecialist` implements a `PLANNED` placement, taking it `PLANNED → IMPLEMENTED` and stamping `implemented_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`; implementation requires the parent determination to be in status `APPROVED`. A `RegulatoryCounsel` verifies it, taking it `IMPLEMENTED → VERIFIED`. A `RegulatoryCounsel` may flag a placement non-compliant from either `VERIFIED → NON_COMPLIANT` or `IMPLEMENTED → NON_COMPLIANT`, and the system applies the same flag as part of a determination revocation. A `LabelingSpecialist` then remediates, taking it `NON_COMPLIANT → IMPLEMENTED`, incrementing `remediation_count` by 1 and again requiring the parent determination to be `APPROVED`; this closes the `IMPLEMENTED → VERIFIED → NON_COMPLIANT → IMPLEMENTED` cycle, and the compensating invariant is that `covered_channel_count` returns to its pre-flag value. Every one of these five placement actions recomputes the determination's `covered_channel_count` and the product's `compliance_posture`, and increments the placement's `version` by 1 and the product's `version` by 1. The determination's own `version`, by contrast, increments only when that recomputation actually changes `covered_channel_count`: verifying a placement that was already counted as `IMPLEMENTED`, or acting on a placement whose effective window does not cover the current logical clock, changes nothing and leaves the determination's `version` and `updated_at` where they were.

Determination reads expose `warning_type`, `symbol_required`, `named_chemicals`, `website_url`, `warning_text`, `required_channels` and `covered_channel_count`, and the determination list accepts optional `product_id`, `assessment_id`, `status` and `warning_type` filters. Placement reads expose the `channel`, both ends of the closed window and `remediation_count`, and the placement list accepts optional `determination_id`, `channel` and `status` filters.

### 3.8. Enforcement: Notice Intake, the Sixty-Day Cure Window and Settlement Money

Enforcement begins when a notice of violation is served. A `RegulatoryCounsel` intakes it by supplying the `product_id` and `chemical_id` it targets, a `noticing_party_name` of one to one hundred and sixty characters, a `noticing_party_type` of `PRIVATE_PARTY`, `ATTORNEY_GENERAL` or `DISTRICT_ATTORNEY`, an `alleged_violation_type` of `FAILURE_TO_WARN`, `INADEQUATE_WARNING_CONTENT` or `EXPOSURE_LEVEL_DISPUTE`, a non-negative whole-number `alleged_units` count of units the noticing party says were sold without a compliant warning, an `earliest_violation_date` marking the start of the alleged violation period, and the `served_at` instant. Two temporal sanity checks apply and both report `ERR_INVALID_INTERVAL`: `earliest_violation_date` may not be later than `served_at`, and `served_at` may not be later than the current logical clock — a notice cannot have been served in the future.

Intake computes `cure_deadline_at` as `served_at` plus a cure window of exactly sixty days, that is sixty times eighty-six thousand four hundred seconds, and the window is **inclusive** of that instant. `cure_eligible` stays null until the evaluation action runs, `cure_attempted` starts false, `cure_completed_at` starts null, `evaluation_note` starts null and `status` starts `RECEIVED`. Intake also increments the product's `open_notice_count` by 1 and, through clause 1 of the posture ladder, drives the product's `compliance_posture` to `ENFORCEMENT_HOLD` — which outranks every other clause and freezes the bill of materials — and increments the product's `version` by 1.

**Cure eligibility.** The evaluation action takes the notice `RECEIVED → UNDER_EVALUATION` and computes `cure_eligible` from four conditions that must **all** hold. First, the `alleged_violation_type` must be `FAILURE_TO_WARN` — a content-adequacy or exposure-level theory can never be cured. Second, the `noticing_party_type` must be `PRIVATE_PARTY`; notices from the Attorney General or a District Attorney are never curable. Third, the product's `annual_units` must be at most one hundred thousand units, and this bound is inclusive, so a product selling exactly one hundred thousand units into California per year is still eligible. Fourth, there must be **no** prior settlement in status `EXECUTED` attached to any notice — including this one — that targets the same product and chemical pair and whose `executed_at` falls inside the quiet period, defined as the closed interval running from one thousand and ninety-five days (three years) before this notice's `served_at` up to and including `served_at` itself. If all four hold, `cure_eligible` is true; otherwise it is false.

**The enforcement fork.** `status` moves through `RECEIVED`, `UNDER_EVALUATION`, `CURE_ELECTED`, `CURED_WITHIN_WINDOW`, `CURE_FAILED`, `SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION`, `SETTLED`, `LITIGATION` and `DISMISSED`, with `CURED_WITHIN_WINDOW`, `SETTLED` and `DISMISSED` terminal. Every action on this surface is reserved to a `RegulatoryCounsel` and increments the notice's `version` by 1 and the product's `version` by 1. From `UNDER_EVALUATION` the workflow forks three ways.

The first branch elects to cure, taking the notice `UNDER_EVALUATION → CURE_ELECTED`. Election requires that the current logical clock is at or before `cure_deadline_at`, that `cure_eligible` is true, and that no settlement in status `EXECUTED` on any notice of the same product and chemical pair — this notice included, exactly as in the eligibility test — has an `executed_at` inside the same three-year quiet period preceding `served_at`. All three refusals report the same identifier, `ERR_CURE_NOT_AVAILABLE`: when `cure_eligible` is false or has not been computed yet, when the cure window has already closed, and when the quiet period is occupied. Election attempted after the deadline is therefore `ERR_CURE_NOT_AVAILABLE` and never `ERR_CURE_WINDOW_CLOSED`, which is reserved for cure *completion*. Election sets `cure_attempted` to true, and **that flag is never cleared** — it is the durable marker of the cure fork, and the settlement calculation reads it rather than the notice's current status. No penalty accrues on this branch unless the cure fails. From `CURE_ELECTED`, completing the cure takes the notice to `CURED_WITHIN_WINDOW`, which requires that the current logical clock is at or before `cure_deadline_at` (the instant exactly equal to the deadline is still inside the window; one microsecond later the request is refused with `ERR_CURE_WINDOW_CLOSED`) and that the product now holds a determination in status `APPROVED` every entry of whose `required_channels` is covered — the warning must actually be up on every required channel. That coverage test is recomputed live against the current logical clock from the determination's own placements in status `IMPLEMENTED` or `VERIFIED` whose window contains that instant, rather than read off the stored `covered_channel_count` field, and an `APPROVED` determination whose `required_channels` list is empty satisfies it vacuously. A completion whose warning is not up on every required channel is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`, and that refusal is evaluated **after** the cure-deadline check, so a completion attempted both past the deadline and without full coverage reports `ERR_CURE_WINDOW_CLOSED`. Completion stamps `cure_completed_at` with the current logical clock, then cascades: the product's `open_notice_count` decrements by 1, and because that count changed, `compliance_posture` is recomputed and leaves `ENFORCEMENT_HOLD` once the count reaches 0. Alternatively, abandoning the cure takes the notice `CURE_ELECTED → CURE_FAILED`.

The second branch escalates straight to negotiation, taking the notice `UNDER_EVALUATION → SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION` and opening the settlement surface for it. The same escalation action is what rejoins the two branches: from `CURE_FAILED → SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION`, so a failed cure converges on exactly the same negotiation state.

The third branch dismisses the notice, taking it `UNDER_EVALUATION → DISMISSED`; that action requires `evaluation_note` in the payload, decrements the product's `open_notice_count` by 1 and recomputes `compliance_posture`, which leaves `ENFORCEMENT_HOLD` when the count reaches 0. `evaluation_note` is an optional field accepted on **every** action of this surface, not only on evaluation and escalation: wherever it is supplied it overwrites the stored note, and omitting it leaves whatever note was already stored exactly as it was rather than clearing it back to null.

From `SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION` two further moves exist. Litigation, taking the notice `SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION → LITIGATION`, is available only when some settlement of this notice has a `round_count` equal to the ceiling of 3 — negotiation must be genuinely exhausted first, and a litigation request made before any settlement has reached the ceiling is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` rather than with `ERR_SETTLEMENT_ROUND_LIMIT`, which belongs to the counter and revision actions alone — and it voids every settlement of the notice whose status is `PROPOSED` or `COUNTERED`. Settlement, taking the notice `SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION → SETTLED`, is system-driven and happens exactly when a settlement of this notice reaches `EXECUTED`; it decrements the product's `open_notice_count` by 1 and recomputes `compliance_posture`. The reverse edge `SETTLED → SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION` is also system-driven and fires when the executed settlement is voided; it increments `open_notice_count` by 1 and recomputes `compliance_posture`, which re-enters `ENFORCEMENT_HOLD`.

**Proposing a settlement.** A `RegulatoryCounsel` proposes a settlement under a notice by supplying a non-negative whole-number `requested_attorney_fees_cents`, an optional `reformulation_commitment` boolean defaulting to false that records whether the settlement includes a binding reformulation commitment, and the `expected_notice_version` described in Section 2.6. The notice must be in status `SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION`, otherwise the create is refused with `ERR_NOTICE_NOT_NEGOTIABLE`, and the notice must not already hold a settlement in status `PROPOSED`, `COUNTERED` or `EXECUTED`, otherwise it is refused with `ERR_OPEN_SETTLEMENT_EXISTS`. `requested_attorney_fees_cents` may not exceed the absolute request ceiling of five million cents (fifty thousand dollars), which is refused with `ERR_FEE_REQUEST_EXCEEDS_CEILING`. The create stamps `proposed_by_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`, sets `status` to `PROPOSED` with `round_count` 0, and increments the notice's `version` by 1; it also carries the rule that voids any earlier `PROPOSED` or `COUNTERED` settlement of the same notice, though the `ERR_OPEN_SETTLEMENT_EXISTS` guard above fires first on exactly that state, so no sibling ever actually survives to be voided on this path. The create touches the new settlement row and the notice only: the product's `version` does not move and its `compliance_posture` is not recomputed.

**The money waterfall.** The settlement money fields are computed in a **pinned order — penalty, then cap, then fees** — and that order is what resolves an apparent circularity: the fee cap is a tier lookup on the penalty, while a capped fee award is exactly what a higher penalty tier would enlarge. The allowed fee figure never re-enters the penalty steps.

Step one freezes the exposure window. `violation_days` is the number of whole days from the notice's `earliest_violation_date` to the logical clock at the moment the settlement row is created: the elapsed seconds divided by eighty-six thousand four hundred, taken as a floor to a whole number. This value is frozen on the row at create time and is **never** recomputed by a later counter or revision. If the computed `violation_days` is zero or negative at create time there is no penalty window to price, and the create is refused with `ERR_NO_VIOLATION_WINDOW`.

Step two computes `base_penalty_cents` as the **lesser** of two statutory measures: a day-based measure of two hundred and fifty thousand cents (2,500 dollars) per day of violation multiplied by `violation_days`, and a unit-based measure of five hundred cents (5 dollars) per unit multiplied by the notice's `alleged_units`.

Step three computes `mitigation_factor` by multiplying together the applicable mitigation factors, starting from 1 and then applying the floor. The warranty factor of 0.60 applies when the warranty defence holds. The cure factor of 0.50 applies when the cure branch was taken. The reformulation factor of 0.75 applies when this settlement's `reformulation_commitment` is true. The product of whichever factors apply is then floored at 0.25 — that is, the larger of the product and 0.25 is taken — and the result is rounded half up to four decimal places. That rounding happens **before** step four uses the value.

The warranty defence holds only when **every** component of the product in status `ACTIVE` has a material whose supplier satisfies all four of the following: `warranty_status` is `ACTIVE`; `pass_through_warning_obligation` is true; `warranty_scope` covers that material's `material_class`, with `ALL_MATERIALS` covering every class, `RAW_MATERIAL_ONLY` covering only `RAW_MATERIAL` and `PACKAGING_ONLY` covering only `PACKAGING`; and the supplier's half-open warranty window covers the notice's `served_at`, meaning `warranty_effective_from` is at or before `served_at` and `served_at` is strictly before `warranty_effective_to`, with a null `warranty_effective_to` treated as positive infinity. A product with zero `ACTIVE` components does **not** get the warranty defence — the empty product is explicitly excluded rather than vacuously qualifying. The cure branch counts as taken when the notice's `cure_attempted` flag is true or its `cure_completed_at` is non-null; it is deliberately not read off the notice's current status, because a settlement can only be created from `SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION`, which the cure branch reaches only by way of `CURE_FAILED`, so a status-based test would make the cure factor unreachable.

Step four computes `adjusted_penalty_cents` as `base_penalty_cents` multiplied by the already-rounded `mitigation_factor`, rounded half up to whole cents.

Step five computes `fee_cap_cents`, a tiered statutory cap keyed on the **adjusted** penalty and never on the base penalty. When `adjusted_penalty_cents` is strictly below one hundred thousand cents (1,000 dollars), the cap is the adjusted penalty multiplied by 1.50, floored to whole cents. When it is at least one hundred thousand cents but strictly below one million cents (10,000 dollars), the cap is the adjusted penalty multiplied by 1.00, floored. At one million cents or above, the cap is the adjusted penalty multiplied by 0.60, floored.

Step six computes `allowed_fees_cents` as the lesser of `requested_attorney_fees_cents` and `fee_cap_cents`.

Step seven splits the penalty. `state_share_cents` is the adjusted penalty multiplied by 0.75, floored to whole cents — the statutory state share. `noticing_party_share_cents` is `adjusted_penalty_cents` minus `state_share_cents`, so it absorbs the flooring remainder and the two shares sum to exactly `adjusted_penalty_cents`.

Step eight computes `total_settlement_cents` as `adjusted_penalty_cents` plus `allowed_fees_cents`.

**The negotiation cycle.** A settlement's `status` runs `PROPOSED`, `COUNTERED`, `EXECUTED` and `VOID`, with `VOID` terminal, and the first two form a cycle. A `RegulatoryCounsel` counters, taking the settlement `PROPOSED → COUNTERED`, and revises, taking it `COUNTERED → PROPOSED`. Both consume a round: each increments `round_count` by 1, both require `round_count` to be strictly below the ceiling of 3 beforehand, and a counter or revision requested on a settlement whose `round_count` already equals 3 is refused with `ERR_SETTLEMENT_ROUND_LIMIT`. Both actions may restate `requested_attorney_fees_cents` and `reformulation_commitment`; when either is supplied it overwrites the stored value. Every counter and every revision then recomputes the money fields from step three onward in the pinned order — `mitigation_factor`, `adjusted_penalty_cents`, `fee_cap_cents`, `allowed_fees_cents`, `state_share_cents`, `noticing_party_share_cents` and `total_settlement_cents` — **whether or not either input was restated**, so a bare counter that carries nothing but its `action` still re-prices the settlement against the current state of the warranty defence and of the notice's cure flag and can move `mitigation_factor` on its own; the frozen `violation_days` and the `base_penalty_cents` derived from it are kept as they were. A counter or revision touches the settlement row alone: it moves neither the notice's nor the product's `version` and does not recompute the product's `compliance_posture`.

Execution takes the settlement `PROPOSED → EXECUTED` or `COUNTERED → EXECUTED`, with an identical three-level cascade from either state. It requires that the acting `X-User-ID` differs from `proposed_by_user_id`, otherwise it is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`; a request that carries no `X-User-ID` header at all is not caught by this comparison, even against a settlement whose `proposed_by_user_id` is itself null. It is also refused with `ERR_INDEMNITY_CAP_EXCEEDED` when this settlement's mitigation included the warranty factor of 0.60 — the warranty defence having held at the most recent recomputation of `mitigation_factor`, which is recorded even where the 0.25 floor absorbed that factor's arithmetic effect — and its `adjusted_penalty_cents` exceeds the summed `indemnity_cap_cents` of every supplier with an `ACTIVE` warranty standing behind the product's `ACTIVE` components, where each such supplier contributes its cap once however many components lead to it, and where the sum is taken from the `warranty_status` alone with no further test of that supplier's `warranty_scope`, warranty window or pass-through obligation. An `adjusted_penalty_cents` exactly equal to that sum is allowed. On success, `executed_by_user_id` and `executed_at` are stamped, and the cascade runs: at level one the product's `accrued_penalty_cents` increases by this settlement's `adjusted_penalty_cents`; at level two, because this settlement reached `EXECUTED`, its notice transitions `SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION → SETTLED` and the product's `open_notice_count` decrements by 1; at level three, because `open_notice_count` changed, `compliance_posture` is recomputed and leaves `ENFORCEMENT_HOLD` when the count reaches 0.

Voiding is the compensating action and takes the settlement `EXECUTED → VOID`. It reverses every effect execution applied: the product's `accrued_penalty_cents` decreases by the same `adjusted_penalty_cents`, restoring the pre-execution value exactly with no drift; the notice transitions `SETTLED → SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION` and `open_notice_count` increments by 1; and `compliance_posture` is recomputed. Voiding is blocked when the promised consideration has already been delivered: if the settlement's `reformulation_commitment` is true and the product already has a reformulation project in status `COMPLETED` naming the notice's `chemical_id`, the request is refused with `ERR_SETTLEMENT_NOT_VOIDABLE`. Separately, a settlement in status `PROPOSED` or `COUNTERED` is moved to `VOID` by the system when a newer settlement is created for the same notice — a branch the `ERR_OPEN_SETTLEMENT_EXISTS` guard of the create keeps unreachable in practice, as noted above — or when the notice enters `LITIGATION`, which is the branch that actually fires. Every action on this surface increments the settlement's `version` by 1, and the actions that touch the notice and product increment their `version` values by 1 as well.

`accrued_penalty_cents` on a product is, by construction, the sum of `adjusted_penalty_cents` over every settlement in status `EXECUTED` whose `notice_id` points at a notice of that product, and `open_notice_count` is the number of notices of that product whose status is one of `RECEIVED`, `UNDER_EVALUATION`, `CURE_ELECTED`, `CURE_FAILED`, `SETTLEMENT_NEGOTIATION` or `LITIGATION`. Notice reads expose `cure_deadline_at`, `cure_eligible` and `cure_completed_at`, and the notice list accepts optional `product_id`, `chemical_id`, `status` and `noticing_party_type` filters. Settlement reads expose the whole waterfall from `violation_days` through `total_settlement_cents` alongside `round_count`, and the settlement list accepts optional `notice_id` and `status` filters.

### 3.9. Reformulation Projects and the Defence File

Reformulation is the structural remedy: instead of warning about an exposure, the manufacturer engineers it down below the safe harbour level so no warning is owed. A `ComplianceAnalyst` opens a project by naming the `product_id`, the `chemical_id` whose exposure must fall, the `material_chemical_id` identifying the exact concentration datum the project will rewrite, a non-negative `target_concentration_ppm` the reformulated material must reach, and a `planned_start_date` and `planned_end_date` with the end strictly after the start.

The link must be coherent, and an incoherent one is refused with `ERR_REFORMULATION_LINK_MISMATCH`: the referenced material-chemical link's own `chemical_id` must equal the project's `chemical_id`, and the material carrying that link must be the material of some component of the project's product in status `ACTIVE`. A project may not be opened whose window collides with an existing non-terminal project on the same product and chemical pair; collision is refused with `ERR_REFORMULATION_OVERLAP`. Project windows are **half-open**, so two windows collide only when the new start is strictly before the existing end and the existing start is strictly before the new end — a project starting at the exact instant another ends does **not** collide. This is the deliberate counterpart to the closed windows used for warning placements: two intervals with touching endpoints collide on a label placement and do not collide on a reformulation project.

At create time the project also captures its baseline. `baseline_daily_exposure_ug` is the `daily_exposure_ug` of the newest assessment in status `FINAL` for the same product and chemical pair, where "newest" means ranked by `finalized_at` descending, then by `created_at` descending as the tie-break, and then — because the logical clock lets two assessments share both stamps to the millisecond — by insertion order, the row created later in the run winning; when the product has no `FINAL` assessment for that chemical the baseline is 0. `verified_daily_exposure_ug` and `reduction_pct` both start null and `status` starts `PLANNED`. Create-time validation also requires the target to be a genuine improvement: `target_concentration_ppm` must be strictly less than the named link's **own** current concentration figure, and a target equal to or above that figure is refused with `ERR_TARGET_NOT_BELOW_THRESHOLD`. That figure is the link's own datum and deliberately **not** the transitive effective concentration of Section 3.5: it is half of `lab_loq_ppm` when the link's `measurement_basis` is `LAB_TESTED`, both laboratory figures are present and `lab_result_ppm` is strictly below `lab_loq_ppm`; `lab_result_ppm` itself when the basis is `LAB_TESTED`, both figures are present and the result is at or above the limit; and `declared_concentration_ppm` in every other case, a null reading as 0. No child material's contribution enters it and the modelled 250.0 disclosure-gap substitution is never applied to it, so a link whose own declared figure is 0 can never host a project at all.

**The verification cycle.** `status` runs `PLANNED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `VERIFICATION`, `COMPLETED` and `ABANDONED`, with the last two terminal, and every action belongs to a `ComplianceAnalyst` and increments the project's `version` by 1; the verification action additionally increments the affected material-chemical link's `version` by 1 and the `version` of each product its cascade reaches by 1 — the project's own product always among them, and any other product the affected material also feeds along with it. Starting takes the project `PLANNED → IN_PROGRESS`. Submitting for verification takes it `IN_PROGRESS → VERIFICATION` and is the one action on this surface with teeth, because it actually rewrites the formulation and then tears down everything that depended on the old numbers, in three levels. At level one the linked material-chemical's `declared_concentration_ppm` is overwritten with the project's `target_concentration_ppm`, its `status` resets to `DECLARED`, its `measurement_basis` resets to `SUPPLIER_DECLARED`, and `lab_name`, `lab_result_ppm`, `lab_loq_ppm` and `lab_reported_at` are all cleared — the old laboratory evidence describes a formulation that no longer exists. At level two, because that concentration changed, every assessment in status `FINAL` belonging to a product that holds the affected material — the project's own product always counting as one such product — has its `daily_exposure_ug`, `margin_ratio`, `result` and `contribution_count` recomputed and then transitions `FINAL → SUPERSEDED`, and each affected product's `total_listed_chemical_load_ug` is recomputed. The sweep is not narrowed to the project's `chemical_id`: **every** `FINAL` assessment of an affected product is recomputed and superseded, whichever chemical it assesses. This is one of the four explicit supersede cascades permitted to touch a `FINAL` row. At level three, because those assessments left `FINAL`, every determination in status `APPROVED` citing one of them transitions `APPROVED → REVOKED` — the basis-assessment-left-`FINAL` edge, distinct from the `SUPERSEDED` edge reserved for a newer approved determination on the same product — so that determination's `IMPLEMENTED` and `VERIFIED` placements go to `NON_COMPLIANT`, its `covered_channel_count` falls to 0, and the product's `compliance_posture` is recomputed. Submitting for verification also stamps `verified_daily_exposure_ug` with the recomputed exposure for the project's product and chemical pair. That recomputation always runs under the **modelled** variant of Section 3.5, whatever `method` the assessments of that pair were opened with, over the product's `ACTIVE` components and against the just-rewritten link; it takes its `route` and `uses_per_day` from the newest assessment of that pair in status `FINAL`, ranked exactly as the baseline above is ranked, falling back to the most recently created assessment of that pair in any status when the pair has no `FINAL` assessment at that moment — which is what a second submission after a rework sees, the first submission having superseded the `FINAL` row — and falling back again, when the pair has no assessment at all, to the `ORAL` route at one use per day. It then computes `reduction_pct` as one hundred times the difference between `baseline_daily_exposure_ug` and `verified_daily_exposure_ug` divided by `baseline_daily_exposure_ug`, rounded half up to two decimal places; when `baseline_daily_exposure_ug` is 0 the percentage is undefined and `reduction_pct` is null. Submission is refused with `ERR_TARGET_NOT_BELOW_THRESHOLD` when the resulting `verified_daily_exposure_ug` is not strictly less than the chemical's `safe_harbor_level_ug_per_day`. That gate is part of the submission itself and is evaluated before anything is kept: the level-one rewrite is applied only tentatively, the modelled exposure resulting from it is priced against the safe harbour, and only when it clears the gate does the submission stand. On refusal the seven rewritten link fields — `declared_concentration_ppm`, `status`, `measurement_basis`, `lab_name`, `lab_result_ppm`, `lab_loq_ppm` and `lab_reported_at` — are restored to the values they held before the attempt, levels two and three never fire, and `verified_daily_exposure_ug` and `reduction_pct` are not stamped, so the link, the `FINAL` assessment it fed, that assessment's determination and the affected products' `total_listed_chemical_load_ug` are all left exactly as they were, the project stays `IN_PROGRESS`, and no `version` is incremented on the project, the link or the product. A chemical that has no safe harbour at all — a `safe_harbor_basis` of `NONE`, so `safe_harbor_level_ug_per_day` is null — can never satisfy this strictly-below comparison, so both this submission gate and the completion gate below refuse with `ERR_TARGET_NOT_BELOW_THRESHOLD` for such a chemical.

Reworking closes the cycle, taking the project `VERIFICATION → IN_PROGRESS` and clearing both `verified_daily_exposure_ug` and `reduction_pct` back to null, so a project can go around the verify-and-rework loop any number of times. Completing takes the project `VERIFICATION → COMPLETED` and requires `verified_daily_exposure_ug` to be **strictly** below the chemical's `safe_harbor_level_ug_per_day`; exposure exactly equal to the safe harbour level fails with `ERR_TARGET_NOT_BELOW_THRESHOLD`, which is the mirror image of the assessment verdict rule, where exposure exactly at the level is treated as `BELOW_SAFE_HARBOR`. Completion recomputes the product's `compliance_posture` and leaves the rewritten concentration in place permanently. Abandoning takes the project to `ABANDONED` from any of `PLANNED`, `IN_PROGRESS` or `VERIFICATION` with no cascade beyond the version bump. The reformulation route is the one write path that stays open while a product is on enforcement hold, because submitting for verification rewrites a concentration rather than the bill of materials. Project reads expose `baseline_daily_exposure_ug`, `verified_daily_exposure_ug` and `reduction_pct`, and the project list accepts optional `product_id`, `chemical_id`, `material_chemical_id` and `status` filters.

**The defence file.** The compliance summary is a computed read that scores how well a product's records would hold up as a defence. It reports a `product_id`, the `variant` it evaluated, the `as_of` instant it evaluated against, an integer `defense_strength_score` from 0 to 100, the four evidence components `warning_evidence`, `assessment_evidence`, `warranty_evidence` and `recordkeeping_evidence`, and the six counts they were derived from: `required_channel_count`, `covered_channel_count`, `verified_channel_count`, `final_assessment_count`, `verified_material_count` and `active_component_count`. Only live rows enter the score: components in `REMOVED` or `SUPERSEDED`, determinations that are not `APPROVED`, and `REVOKED` or `SUPERSEDED` determinations are excluded from both variants.

The score has two variants that read the **same stored rows** and differ only in the instant they are evaluated against. The `AS_OF_TODAY` variant applies when the summary is requested without `as_of_notice_id`, and `as_of` is the current logical clock; every `FINAL` assessment counts regardless of its `finalized_at`, and recordkeeping evidence reads the current `disclosure_status` with no `data_received_at` cut-off. The `AS_OF_SERVICE_DATE` variant applies when a non-empty `as_of_notice_id` is supplied — an `as_of_notice_id` present but empty is treated exactly as if it had been omitted, and takes the `AS_OF_TODAY` variant rather than failing to resolve — and `as_of` becomes that notice's `served_at`; the notice must belong to this product, otherwise the read fails with `ERR_NOT_FOUND`. In that variant, placements must have been in force at `served_at`, `FINAL` assessments count only when their `finalized_at` is at or before `served_at`, warranty coverage is tested against `served_at`, and disclosure responses count only when `data_received_at` is at or before `served_at`. Those cut-offs move the instant the evidence is tested against rather than merely subtracting from it, so the two scores are **not** ordered in general. Evidence that was in force at `served_at` and has since lapsed still counts under `AS_OF_SERVICE_DATE` while no longer counting under `AS_OF_TODAY` — a placement whose closed window has since closed, or a supplier whose warranty is still `ACTIVE` but whose `warranty_effective_to` now lies in the past — so the service-date score can come out strictly higher than today's for the same product. Only the assessment and recordkeeping components are individually monotone, each of them being a one-sided cut-off — `finalized_at` at or before `as_of`, `data_received_at` at or before `as_of` — laid over status values that are always read as they stand today; the warning and warranty components each read a window that `served_at` can sit inside and the current clock can sit outside.

Each of the four components is exactly 0.0, 0.5 or 1.0. Warning evidence looks at the product's single determination in status `APPROVED`, of which at most one can exist; when there is none, or when its `required_channels` list is empty, warning evidence is 0.0. Otherwise the covered set is those entries of `required_channels` having a placement of that determination whose `status` is `IMPLEMENTED` or `VERIFIED` and whose window contains `as_of`, meaning `effective_from` is at or before `as_of` and either `effective_to` is null or `as_of` is at or before `effective_to`; the verified set is the same restricted to `status` `VERIFIED`. Warning evidence is 1.0 when the verified set covers every required channel, 0.5 when the covered set covers every required channel, and 0.0 otherwise. Assessment evidence is 1.0 when the product has at least one qualifying `FINAL` assessment, 0.5 when it has none but has at least one assessment in status `PEER_REVIEWED`, and 0.0 otherwise; that `PEER_REVIEWED` fallback carries no `as_of` restriction under either variant, a peer review having no timestamp of its own to compare, and it counts assessments of any chemical. Warranty evidence looks at the materials of the product's `ACTIVE` components: with zero such components it is 0.0; it is 1.0 when every one of those materials has a supplier whose `warranty_status` is `ACTIVE`, whose half-open warranty window covers `as_of`, whose `warranty_scope` covers the material's `material_class`, and whose `pass_through_warning_obligation` is true; it is 0.5 when all of those hold except the pass-through obligation; and 0.0 otherwise. Recordkeeping evidence is 0.0 when there are no `ACTIVE` components; under `AS_OF_TODAY` it is 1.0 when every one of those materials has `disclosure_status` `VERIFIED`, 0.5 when every one is `RECEIVED` or `VERIFIED`, and 0.0 otherwise; under `AS_OF_SERVICE_DATE` the same two tiers apply but each material must additionally have a non-null `data_received_at` at or before `as_of`, and 0.0 otherwise. The material counts are taken once per `ACTIVE` component rather than over a deduplicated set, so a single material carried by two active components is counted twice in both `verified_material_count` and `active_component_count`; `verified_material_count` counts exactly the component materials that clear the `VERIFIED` test under the variant in force, and it is 0 whenever the product has no `ACTIVE` components.

The four components are then weighted and summed: warning evidence carries a weight of 40, assessment evidence 25, warranty evidence 20 and recordkeeping evidence 15, for a maximum of 100. The weighted sum is rounded half up to zero decimal places, so `defense_strength_score` is always an integer between 0 and 100. Note that revoking a supplier's warranty does not eagerly rewrite anything: it changes the inputs, and every product touched by that supplier's materials simply scores differently the next time its compliance summary is read.

## 4. API Endpoint Definitions

All endpoints are served directly under the API root, so a resource collection such as the product catalogue is addressed at `/products`. Successful resource creations return HTTP 201 and every other successful call returns HTTP 200; both wrap their payload in the standard success envelope of Section 2.2. Every write endpoint that changes stored state returns the created or updated resource in `data`, except for the clock endpoint, which returns the two-field object described below. Every list endpoint accepts `limit` and `offset` and reports pagination in `meta`. Authorization for each endpoint is restricted to the roles named in the final column; a request whose `X-User-Role` is absent, unrecognised or not listed is refused with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`. A trailing slash is accepted on every path as equivalent to the path without one. Only the paths and methods in this table are served: a request to a path no endpoint serves, and a request whose method is not served on an otherwise recognised path, are both refused with `ERR_NOT_FOUND` and HTTP 404 in the standard error envelope — there is no method-specific status and no collection read for suppliers.
