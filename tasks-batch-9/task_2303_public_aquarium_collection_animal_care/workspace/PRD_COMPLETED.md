# Public Aquarium Collection & Animal Care API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A public aquarium is two institutions wearing one uniform. To the visitor it is a gallery; to the regulator, the accrediting body and the veterinary profession it is a permanently inspected animal-holding facility whose every acquisition, water sample, treatment and transfer must be defensible on paper years after the fact. Today those two identities are served by unconnected records: the collection plan lives in a curator's spreadsheet, water chemistry in the plant room logbook, permits in a filing cabinet, husbandry notes on clipboards beside the holding tanks. When an accreditation inspector arrives, the readiness of the institution has to be reconstructed by hand; and when a life support plant alarms at three in the morning nobody can say with authority which exhibits must close and which guest programmes must be pulled from the morning schedule.

This document specifies the Public Aquarium Collection & Animal Care API, the single system of record that closes that gap. It governs the whole chain of custody of a living animal: the exhibit-by-exhibit collection plan and the sourcing standard each plan line imposes; the acquisition request, its wild-take quota accounting and the permit dossier that authorises import, transport or possession of a protected species; the quarantine protocol the arrival must complete; the feeding, behavioural and clinical record accumulated while the animal is in the collection; the negotiation with a receiving institution when the animal moves on; and the readiness score an accreditation inspection derives from all of it. Running underneath is the life support estate — its water-quality parameters, its alarm thresholds and its redundancy testing regime — whose posture gates what may be placed on an exhibit and whether guests may get into the water at all.

Two design commitments shape everything that follows. First, **derived state is never client-supplied**: an exhibit's operating posture, a specimen's welfare index, an audit's readiness score and a plant's alarm band are all computed by the server from primary records, so an operator can never assert a compliance position the underlying data does not support. Second, **safety and compliance gates are ordered, not parallel**: when several rules would reject the same request, the specification fixes exactly which rejection the caller sees, so that a mid-flight plant alarm always surfaces as a plant alarm and is never masked by a downstream capacity complaint. The remainder of this document defines the global contract (§2), the domain workflows (§3), the endpoint surface (§4) and the error catalogue (§5).

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

Everything in this section applies to every endpoint unless a narrower scope is stated. The whole domain surface is mounted under the `/aquarium` prefix, with the single exception of the logical-clock control endpoint described in §2.5.

### 2.1. User Identity and Role-Based Access Control

Every request — reads included — must carry two headers. `X-User-ID` identifies the acting user and `X-User-Role` declares the capacity in which they are acting. If `X-User-ID` is absent or empty — and a value consisting only of whitespace counts as empty — the request is rejected with `ERR_MISSING_USER_CONTEXT` (401). If `X-User-Role` is absent, empty, or is not one of the six recognised role names, the request is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_ROLE` (401); role names are matched exactly, so casing and surrounding whitespace are not forgiven — this check runs before any authorization decision, so an unknown role never produces a permission error. When both headers are well-formed but the role is not permitted to invoke the endpoint, the answer is `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (403).

The six roles are:

- **`CuratorOfCollections`** — owns the collection plan and the acquisition pipeline. It alone registers exhibits and species, writes collection plan entries, opens, submits and cancels acquisition requests, and accessions arriving specimens.
- **`ComplianceDirector`** — the approving authority. It alone registers life support plants and partner institutions, edits institutions, re-capacitates exhibits, drives plan-line status actions, clears acquisition requests, decides permits, and schedules and progresses accreditation audits. It shares the transfer-decision endpoint (the `APPROVE` decision only) and the session-transition endpoint (the `SUSPEND` and `REINSTATE` actions only). A single director user may never stand on both sides of a segregated pair (§2.11).
- **`VeterinaryOfficer`** — owns clinical care. It alone records quarantine reviews, opens veterinary cases, closes them, and declares mortalities. It shares husbandry-log creation with the aquarist.
- **`AquaristTechnician`** — owns daily floor operations. It alone records water-quality readings and redundancy tests, places specimens onto exhibits and removes them, and books interaction sessions. It shares husbandry-log creation, and it drives the `ACTIVATE`, `COMPLETE` and `CANCEL` session actions.
- **`PermitOfficer`** — prepares and files permit dossiers and drives outbound transfers, but decides neither: it alone drafts and submits permits, and drafts, submits and completes transfer requests.
- **`PartnerInstitutionLiaison`** — speaks for one receiving institution. It answers the transfer requests addressed to that institution with the `COUNTER` and `DECLINE` decisions, and it may read transfer requests (scoped to its own institution) and the institution directory, both the list and the single-institution read. It may read nothing else.

Role authorization has an **action-level** dimension as well as an endpoint-level one, and both are enforced by the same `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (403) rule. On the interaction-session transition endpoint, `SUSPEND` and `REINSTATE` are reserved to `ComplianceDirector` while `ACTIVATE`, `COMPLETE` and `CANCEL` are reserved to `AquaristTechnician`. On the transfer-request decision endpoint, `COUNTER` and `DECLINE` are reserved to `PartnerInstitutionLiaison` while `APPROVE` is reserved to `ComplianceDirector`. A director attempting to counter a transfer, or a liaison attempting to approve one, is refused even though both roles are listed on the endpoint. The action-level test can only be applied once the supplied value is known to be a recognised member of the endpoint's action or decision vocabulary, so on those two endpoints the `action` or `decision` field is parsed first: an unrecognised value is a rank 7 `ERR_VALIDATION` (422) even from a caller whose role would have been refused for every legal value, while the endpoint-level role test still runs before that parse. The clock control endpoint is open to all six roles.

Identity semantics differ by role, and the difference matters for ownership checks. When `X-User-Role` is `CuratorOfCollections`, `ComplianceDirector`, `VeterinaryOfficer`, `AquaristTechnician` or `PermitOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier that is not tied to any persisted entity; the server records it where a workflow demands an audit anchor — as `approved_by_user_id` on a plan line, `cleared_by_user_id` on an acquisition or a quarantine case, `opened_by_user_id` and `closed_by_user_id` on a veterinary case or an audit, `recorded_by_user_id` on a husbandry log, `decided_by_user_id` on a transfer — but it never compares it to any entity's `id`. When `X-User-Role` is `PartnerInstitutionLiaison`, `X-User-ID` **is** the `id` of the acting `Institution`; ownership checks compare a transfer request's `receiving_institution_id` to `X-User-ID`. A liaison that decides on, or reads, a transfer request addressed to a different institution is rejected with `ERR_INSTITUTION_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (403), and a liaison listing transfer requests sees only the rows whose `receiving_institution_id` equals its own `X-User-ID`. On the list endpoint the scoping is enforced twice over: a liaison that supplies a `receiving_institution_id` filter naming any institution other than itself is refused outright with `ERR_INSTITUTION_SCOPE_VIOLATION` rather than served an empty page, while a filter naming its own institution, or no filter at all, is served the implicitly scoped rows. That refusal is decided after the query parameters have been parsed and range-checked, so a malformed filter or an out-of-range `limit` on the same request answers first.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`, the remainder being a freshly generated 32-character lowercase hexadecimal UUID.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, read from the logical aquarium clock of §2.5 rather than from the host wall clock, so every response in a batch issued at one clock setting carries the identical instant.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination, Sorting and List Filters

Every list endpoint is paginated by two query parameters. `limit` is the maximum number of rows to return; it defaults to 10, may not be smaller than 1 and may not be larger than 100. `offset` is the number of rows to skip; it defaults to 0 and may not be negative. A `limit` outside the inclusive range 1 to 100, or a negative `offset`, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION` (400); a `limit` or `offset` that is present but not parseable as an integer at all is a plain shape complaint and is rejected one rank earlier with `ERR_VALIDATION` (422), while an empty-string value for either parameter is treated as absent and falls back to the default. Every list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` in the `meta` object of the envelope, where `has_more` is true exactly when `offset` plus the number of rows actually returned is still less than `total_count`.

Unless a list is explicitly documented otherwise, rows are sorted by their creation timestamp in ascending order. Because that timestamp is the logical clock (§2.5) and many rows are therefore written at the identical instant, ties are broken by the order in which the rows were inserted, so a list is always totally and stably ordered. Two lists are documented otherwise and return their rows newest first: a plant's water-quality readings and a specimen's husbandry records. Those two reverse the whole ordering, insertion tiebreak included, so among rows created at one instant the most recently written appears first.

Lists also accept optional filters, which narrow the result set without changing the pagination contract. Every filter is optional in the same way: an absent parameter, or one supplied as an empty string, imposes no narrowing at all, while a value supplied for an enumerated filter that is not a member of that enumeration is rejected with `ERR_VALIDATION` (422) — filter parsing therefore happens at rank 7, before the rank 8 pagination range check. Identifier-valued filters are compared as exact strings and are not required to name an existing row: a filter naming an unknown identifier yields an empty page rather than a 404. Plants may be filtered by `alarm_state` and `design_habitat`; a plant's readings by `severity`; the exhibits a plant serves by `posture`. Exhibits may be filtered by `life_support_system_id` and `posture`, and the sessions booked on one exhibit by `status` and `program_type`. Species may be filtered by `taxon_group`, `protection_status` and `sustainability_rating`. Institutions may be filtered by `accreditation_status` and `is_receiving_eligible`. Collection plan entries accept `exhibit_id`, `species_id`, `superseded_by_entry_id` and `status`. Acquisition requests accept `collection_plan_entry_id`, `source_institution_id`, `status` and `source_type`, and the permits filed beneath one request accept `status` and `permit_type`. Specimens accept `species_id`, `acquisition_request_id`, `exhibit_id`, `sire_specimen_id`, `dam_specimen_id`, `disposition_status` and `health_status`; a specimen's husbandry records accept `log_type`, its quarantine cases accept `status`, and its veterinary cases accept `status` and `case_type`. Transfer requests accept `specimen_id`, `receiving_institution_id`, `transport_permit_id` and `status`. Accreditation audits accept `status` and `scope`.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and Record Versioning

Every persisted resource carries a server-minted `id`: a UUID string with no type prefix, never supplied or influenced by the client. Every resource also carries `created_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits recording when the row was written — read, like every other server-stamped instant, from the logical clock of §2.5 and not from the host wall clock, so rows written under one clock setting all share one `created_at`. That rendering is uniform across the whole surface: every instant-valued field of every representation is emitted in UTC with exactly three fractional digits and a trailing `Z` — `created_at` and `updated_at`, a server-stamped domain instant such as `approved_at`, `issued_at` or `projected_release_at`, and equally a client-supplied instant such as `arrival_at`, `taken_at` or `proposed_transfer_at` echoed back, whatever fractional precision or UTC offset the client's own literal used (digits beyond the third are truncated, not rounded). On input an instant must be a string carrying at minimum a full date and a whole second; a fractional part of one to nine digits and a trailing `Z` or a `±HH:MM` offset are each optional, an omitted zone is read as UTC, an offset is normalised to UTC before any comparison, and a calendar-impossible literal such as a thirty-first of February is a rank 7 `ERR_VALIDATION`. Every temporal comparison in this document — future-instant guards, interval bounds, deadline and release tests — is made on the normalised UTC value.

Most resources additionally carry `updated_at` — an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp refreshed on every mutation — and `version`, an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increments by exactly one on every mutation of that resource. This includes mutations the client did not directly request: when a cascade recomputes an exhibit's ledger, downgrades a plant's alarm band, releases a species' quota or restores a specimen's disposition, the affected resource's `updated_at` is refreshed and its `version` is incremented just as if the change had been made by a direct call. A cascade counts as a mutation only when it actually moves a stored value, however. An exhibit posture recomputation that re-derives the identical `occupied_bioload_units`, `bioload_utilization_pct`, `posture`, `last_posture_reason`, `suspended_session_count` and `interaction_program_enabled` is a no-op: nothing is rewritten, so `updated_at` and `version` both stand still, and a clock advance or a reading append that changes nothing about an exhibit leaves that exhibit's concurrency token usable. The two exceptions are the recomputations the mortality chain and the clinical-escalation coupling perform on the exhibit the animal has just vacated (§3.7), which always register as a mutation of that exhibit because the vacating itself is the change. A single request may therefore increment the `version` of many resources at once, and the response already reflects every such change — the system is immediately consistent, and no cascade is deferred to a background pass.

Two record types are append-only observations rather than living resources, and they are deliberately narrower: a **water-quality reading** and a **husbandry log** carry only `id` and `created_at`. Neither has an `updated_at` and neither has a `version`; both fields are absent from their representations entirely, because these rows are never modified after they are written.

### 2.5. The Logical Aquarium Clock

The system does not read the host machine's wall clock. It maintains a **logical UTC clock** which every temporal rule in this document consults, and which an operator of any of the six roles may set by supplying a `current_utc` instant to the clock control endpoint. That instant is the endpoint's only input. Before any call sets it, the current instant is exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`. A request that would move the clock backwards — a `current_utc` earlier than the current value — is rejected with `ERR_VALIDATION` (422); a `current_utc` exactly equal to the current value is accepted, and re-runs the cascades below against an unchanged instant. The endpoint answers 200 with an `object` payload that is not a persisted resource and carries neither `id`, `created_at` nor `version`: it holds `current_utc`, the instant now in force, and three arrays of identifier strings reporting what the advance did — `expired_permit_ids`, the permits this call moved to `EXPIRED` in creation order; `rejected_acquisition_request_ids`, the acquisition requests this call moved to `REJECTED`, permit-expiry casualties first and permit-deadline lapses second; and `recomputed_exhibit_ids`, the exhibits whose derived ledger, posture, posture reason, suspended-session counter or interaction flag actually changed value during the final posture pass. Each array is empty when the corresponding pass changed nothing, and an exhibit that the pass re-derived to the identical figures is deliberately absent from `recomputed_exhibit_ids`.

Advancing the clock is not a passive assignment; it drives the time-triggered cascades, in exactly this order. **First, permit expiry:** every permit in `ISSUED` status whose `valid_until` is at or before the new instant moves to `EXPIRED`, which rejects the acquisition request it was authorising, releases that request's wild-take quota reservation, and declines any transfer request riding on the permit (§3.5). **Second, permit-deadline lapse:** every acquisition request still in `PENDING_PERMIT` whose `permit_deadline_at` is at or before the new instant and which holds no permit in `ISSUED` status is likewise rejected with `closure_reason` `PERMIT_EXPIRED` and its quota reservation released; a request that did obtain an issued permit is left in `PENDING_PERMIT` for the director to clear, however far past its deadline the clock now stands. **Third**, `redundancy_overdue` is recomputed on every life support plant against the new instant; because that flag is derived live from the clock and the plant's own interval and is never stored, this pass leaves no trace on the plant record — it refreshes neither `updated_at` nor `version`, and a plant whose interval lapses during an advance is reported as overdue by the very next read without ever having been rewritten. **Fourth**, the operating posture is recomputed on every exhibit, so that a redundancy interval which lapsed during the advance is reflected in the gallery's posture before the response is returned (§3.1, §3.2). All four passes complete synchronously within the call, each iterating its collection in ascending creation order.

### 2.6. Optimistic Concurrency on Shared Parents

Several writes are legitimate on their own terms but dangerous when made against a parent record the client has not re-read. Those writes are guarded by optimistic concurrency control on the parent's `version`, and the guard is not optional: the named field must be present in the request body and its value must equal the parent's currently stored `version`. If the value is present but does not match, the write is rejected outright — nothing is persisted — with a 412 status. Presence and shape are a separate, earlier question: the field is a required non-negative integer, so omitting it, sending `null`, sending a non-integer or sending a negative number is a rank 7 `ERR_VALIDATION` (422) rather than a 412, and the 412 is reserved for a well-formed guess at a version the parent does not currently hold.

- **Creating a collection plan entry** requires `expected_exhibit_version`, matching the current `version` of the `Exhibit` named in the body. The create is a legitimate supersede operation, retiring the exhibit's previous approved line for the same species, so a client working from a stale read would be superseding a plan it has not observed and sizing its line against bioload figures that have already moved. A mismatch yields `ERR_STALE_EXHIBIT_VERSION` (412).
- **Re-capacitating an exhibit** — the same call that renames it, reassigns its plant or re-enables its interaction programme — requires `expected_exhibit_version`. A stale value means the director's view of `occupied_bioload_units` and `posture` predates a placement or a posture cascade. A mismatch yields `ERR_STALE_EXHIBIT_VERSION` (412).
- **Placing a specimen onto an exhibit** and **removing a specimen from its exhibit** both require `expected_exhibit_version`, because both rewrite the exhibit's bioload ledger. A mismatch yields `ERR_STALE_EXHIBIT_VERSION` (412).
- **Clearing an acquisition request** requires `expected_species_version`, matching the current `version` of the `Species` whose wild-take quota pool the clearance draws on. If another writer has advanced that species' `version` since the director last read it, the pool the decision was sized against has already moved, and the clearance is rejected with `ERR_STALE_SPECIES_VERSION` (412).

No other endpoint accepts a version field, and no other endpoint performs a version check. Where the guard does apply it is evaluated early — after the request has been shown to be well-formed and to reference existing rows, but before any state machine or business rule is consulted — so a stale version always wins over a state complaint, a posture complaint or a capacity complaint (§2.8).

### 2.7. Scores, Indices and Rounding Discipline

The domain has no money in it, but it is full of scores, indices and percentages, and they are held to monetary discipline. Every stored score — a reading's `water_quality_index_snapshot` and the plant-level `water_quality_index` derived from it, an exhibit's `occupied_bioload_units` and `bioload_utilization_pct`, an acquisition's `sustainability_score`, a specimen's `welfare_score`, an audit's `readiness_score` and each of the five entries of its `domain_subscores` — is a decimal number rounded to exactly **2 decimal places** using **round half up**.

Rounding is applied only at the step the calculation names, and intermediate values are never pre-rounded. Deviation ratios, arithmetic means, division results and running sums are carried at full precision until the named rounding step. Where a calculation rounds more than once — the readiness score rounds each domain subscore, then rounds the weighted sum of those already-rounded subscores, then rounds again after the blocking-finding deduction — each rounding step is a genuine step, and summing the unrounded inputs instead is a different, wrong answer. Integer-valued quantities (quarantine day counts, extension days, submission and negotiation round counters, reading counters, guest counts, blocking-finding counts) are exact integers and are never rounded to decimals; where a calculation rounds a day count up to a whole number it says so explicitly.

Clamping is applied only where the owning calculation names it. The water-quality index, the sustainability score and the welfare score are each clamped to the inclusive range 0.00 to 100.00; each readiness domain subscore is clamped to the same range, and the final readiness score is floored at 0. `bioload_utilization_pct` is deliberately **not** clamped: an exhibit whose occupied bioload exceeds its capacity reports a percentage above 100.00, and that is the honest figure the posture rule reads. For the sustainability score, the additive part is capped at 100 **before** the protection penalty and the quota pressure are subtracted, so the cap and the deductions do not commute.

### 2.8. The Order of Rejection

When more than one rule would refuse the same request, the outcome is not a matter of implementation convenience. Rules are evaluated in the fixed ascending order below and the **first** failing rule short-circuits the request; no later rule may mask an earlier one, and no earlier rule may be skipped in order to report a later one. The business meaning of each rule is developed in §3; the list here fixes only the order, the code and the scope.

1. `X-User-ID` present and non-empty — `ERR_MISSING_USER_CONTEXT` (401). Every endpoint, including all reads and the clock control endpoint.
2. `X-User-Role` present and equal to one of the six role names — `ERR_INVALID_ROLE` (401). Every endpoint.
3. The caller's role is listed for the endpoint and, on the two action-driven endpoints that carry per-action restrictions, for the specific value supplied — `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (403). Every endpoint.
4. A `PartnerInstitutionLiaison` acts only on a transfer request whose `receiving_institution_id` equals its `X-User-ID` — `ERR_INSTITUTION_SCOPE_VIOLATION` (403). Applies to the transfer-request decision endpoint and to both transfer-request read endpoints. This rank has one unavoidable and two deliberate departures from its position in the ladder, because the check needs material the earlier ranks produce. On the single-read and decision endpoints the transfer must first be fetched, so a liaison naming a transfer that does not exist receives `ERR_TRANSFER_REQUEST_NOT_FOUND` (404, rank 5) rather than a scope refusal. On the decision endpoint the `decision` value is parsed and its action-level authorisation settled before the scope test, so a liaison sending an unrecognised decision against a foreign transfer receives `ERR_VALIDATION` and a director sending `COUNTER` receives `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`. And on the list endpoint the filters and the pagination range are settled first, so a bad filter value or an out-of-range `limit` answers ahead of the scope refusal.
5. Every identifier taken from the path resolves to an existing row of the expected entity, resolved left to right — the entity-specific not-found code, at 404. Every endpoint that takes a path identifier.
6. Every foreign key supplied in the request body resolves to an existing row, checked in the order the fields are declared for that endpoint — the entity-specific not-found code, at 404. Applies to the creation of exhibits, collection plan entries, acquisition requests, permits, specimens, veterinary cases, transfer requests and interaction sessions, and to exhibit placements. Only a value that is actually present and actually a string is resolved here: a foreign key that is absent, `null` or of the wrong JSON type is not a 404 but is carried forward to rank 7, where its required-ness and its shape are judged.
7. Required fields present; types, enum members and numeric ranges as specified — `ERR_VALIDATION` (422). Every endpoint. Throughout, an explicit `null` is indistinguishable from an omitted field: a required field sent as `null` fails as missing, and an optional field sent as `null` takes its documented default. A request body that is absent, empty or unparseable as JSON is read as the empty object, so its endpoint's required fields fail here; a body that parses to a JSON value other than an object — an array, a string, a number — fails here as well. Types are strict: a boolean is not an integer and not a number, a numeric string is not a number, and an integer-valued field rejects a fractional number. Every string-typed field must be at least one character long unless a longer minimum is named for it, and that applies to optional prose fields as much as to required ones, so an empty string is a rank-7 failure and never a synonym for absence. Code-list fields must be arrays of recognised vocabulary members, free of duplicates, and non-empty unless the owning endpoint expressly permits the empty list. This rule also rejects any attempt to set a server-computed or server-assigned field from the request body: the mere presence of such a key is the failure, whatever value it carries — even `null` — so these fields are never silently ignored. It is also the code returned when the logical clock is asked to move backwards.
8. `limit` within the inclusive range 1 to 100 and `offset` at least 0 — `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION` (400). Every list endpoint.
9. The supplied `expected_exhibit_version` or `expected_species_version` equals the stored parent `version` — `ERR_STALE_EXHIBIT_VERSION` or `ERR_STALE_SPECIES_VERSION` (412). Applies to collection-plan-entry creation, the exhibit edit, exhibit placement, exhibit removal and acquisition clearance.
10. The requested transition is declared from the target entity's current state — `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (409). The terminal test of rank 11 is settled first in practice, because "already finished" is never reported as "wrong non-terminal state"; read ranks 10 to 12 as a single block whose internal order is terminal, then state, then action. Rank 10 is the answer for: plan-entry status actions (every action the current non-terminal state does not admit); acquisition submission, where the request must be `DRAFT` and its parent plan line `APPROVED`; acquisition clearance, where the request must be `PENDING_PERMIT` or `READY_FOR_CLEARANCE`; acquisition cancellation, where the request must be cancellable and its `fulfilled_count` still 0; permit submission, both for the permit's own status and for the acquisition-status gate; the `ISSUE` permit decision's acquisition-status gate, and nothing else on that endpoint; exhibit placement, where the specimen must be `CLEARED`; exhibit removal, both when the specimen holds no exhibit at all and when its disposition is not `ON_EXHIBIT`; veterinary case creation, where a `TREATMENT` demands a `health_status` of `HEALTHY`, `UNDER_TREATMENT` or `CRITICAL_CARE`; transfer submission, for the transfer's own status and the specimen's disposition; the transfer decision endpoint for a `DRAFT` transfer only; and transfer completion, for the transfer's status and the specimen's disposition. It is deliberately **not** the answer anywhere on mortality declaration, quarantine reviews, veterinary case closure, session transitions or audit progressions: those five endpoints answer only with rank 11 or rank 12, and never return `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.
11. The target entity is not already in one of its terminal states — `ERR_ENTITY_TERMINAL` (409). Applies to plan-entry status actions (`RETIRED`), acquisition cancellation (`FULFILLED`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`), permit decisions (`DENIED`, `REVOKED`, `EXPIRED`), mortality declaration (`DECEASED` or `TRANSFERRED` disposition), quarantine reviews (`CLEARED`, `FAILED`), veterinary case closure (`CLOSED`), transfer decisions (`DECLINED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `COMPLETED`), session transitions (`COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`) and audit progressions (`PASSED`, `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`). This code distinguishes "terminal, nothing further is possible" from "wrong non-terminal state": a second mortality declaration on a `DECEASED` specimen is `ERR_ENTITY_TERMINAL`, not `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.
12. The supplied `action`, `outcome` or `decision` value is legal from the entity's current state — `ERR_INVALID_ACTION` (422). Applies to permit decisions, quarantine reviews, veterinary case closure, transfer decisions, session transitions and audit progressions, and on all six it is the sole answer for an illegal value-and-state pairing. For plan-entry status actions, by contrast, rank 10 answers for every such value: an `action` that the entry's current non-terminal state does not admit — a `SUSPEND` on a `DRAFT` line, an `APPROVE` on an already-`APPROVED` one — is reported as `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, and that endpoint never returns `ERR_INVALID_ACTION`. On the transfer decision endpoint the two codes split by state: a `DRAFT` transfer answers `ERR_INVALID_STATE` at rank 10, while a `COUNTERED` or `APPROVED` transfer that is sent `APPROVE` or `COUNTER` answers `ERR_INVALID_ACTION` here. Examples of the code proper: a `CLEAR` outcome on an `EXTENDED` quarantine case is an invalid action, because the case must be resumed first; a `NECROPSY_FINALIZED` outcome on a `TREATMENT` case is an invalid action; a `COMPLETE` on a `SCHEDULED` session and a `START` on an `IN_PROGRESS` audit are likewise invalid actions rather than invalid states.
13. Segregation of duties: the acting `X-User-ID` differs from the user who performed the paired earlier step — `ERR_SOD_VIOLATION` (409). Applies to acquisition clearance, audit progressions and veterinary case closure.
14. The specimen's `health_status` is `HEALTHY` — `ERR_SPECIMEN_NOT_HEALTHY` (409). Applies to exhibit placement, transfer submission, transfer completion and quarantine reviews. This is a cross-dimension guard: the disposition dimension may be perfectly correct while the clinical dimension blocks the move.
15. The target exhibit's `posture` is not `CLOSED` — `ERR_EXHIBIT_CLOSED` (409). Applies to exhibit placement, session creation and, among session transitions, to `ACTIVATE` and `REINSTATE` only; `COMPLETE`, `CANCEL` and `SUSPEND` are always available on a closed exhibit, since all three only ever wind a session down. An exhibit that has vanished from beneath a session is treated as closed for both gated actions.
16. When the target exhibit's `posture` is `RESTRICTED`, `restricted_posture_override` is `true` — `ERR_RESTRICTED_POSTURE_OVERRIDE_REQUIRED` (409). Applies to exhibit placement only, and is only evaluated when the posture actually is `RESTRICTED`; an `OPEN` exhibit ignores the flag entirely.
17. The exhibit's serving plant has `alarm_state` equal to `NORMAL` and `redundancy_overdue` false — `ERR_LIFE_SUPPORT_ALARM` (409). Applies to the `ACTIVATE` transition only. This is stricter than rule 15, because a plant in `WARNING` leaves the posture merely `RESTRICTED`. An exhibit that has no serving plant at all fails here too, since there is then no plant that can be shown to be quiet.
18. The exhibit's `interaction_program_enabled` is `true` — `ERR_INTERACTION_PROGRAM_DISABLED` (409). Applies to session creation and, among session transitions, to `REINSTATE` alone: reinstatement returns a session to the booking pool and so must re-clear the booking gate, whereas `ACTIVATE` acts on a booking already granted and does not re-check the flag.
19. `safety_briefing_confirmed` is `true` — `ERR_SAFETY_BRIEFING_REQUIRED` (422). Applies to the `ACTIVATE` transition only; a session may be **created** with the flag false, and only activation demands it. The flag is read from the stored session, never from the transition body.
20. For a `DIVE_ENCOUNTER` session, no specimen assigned to the exhibit is in `CRITICAL_CARE` or `DECEASED_PENDING_NECROPSY` — `ERR_DIVE_ENCOUNTER_BLOCKED` (409). Applies to the `ACTIVATE` transition only. Every specimen whose `exhibit_id` is the session's exhibit counts, whatever its `disposition_status`.
21. The logical clock has reached the quarantine case's `projected_release_at` — `ERR_QUARANTINE_NOT_COMPLETE` (409). Applies to quarantine reviews, and only to the `CLEAR` outcome; `EXTEND`, `RESUME` and `FAIL` are legal at any time. The boundary is inclusive.
22. The quarantine case's `clearance_exam_count` is at least 2 — `ERR_CLEARANCE_EXAMS_INSUFFICIENT` (409). Applies to quarantine reviews. Only closed preventive exams with no findings, recorded while the case was `ACTIVE` or `EXTENDED`, count towards it.
23. The candidate treatment withdrawal window does not overlap an open treatment's window — `ERR_TREATMENT_WITHDRAWAL_ACTIVE` (409). Applies to veterinary case creation. The interval is half-open, so a new window starting exactly at an existing window's end is accepted.
24. An `APPROVED` collection plan entry exists for the target exhibit and the specimen's species — `ERR_NO_APPROVED_PLAN_ENTRY` (409). Applies to exhibit placement.
25. The exhibit's `habitat_type` equals the serving plant's `design_habitat` — `ERR_HABITAT_MISMATCH` (422). Applies to exhibit creation and to plan-entry status actions — to all four of those actions, not merely approval — because the exhibit's plant assignment can be edited in between. The exhibit edit itself does not check it.
26. The permit the context requires exists in `ISSUED` status — `ERR_PERMIT_NOT_ISSUED` (409). Applies to acquisition clearance and, among transfer decisions, to `APPROVE` alone, and there only when the specimen's species has `permit_required` true.
27. The permit's `permit_type` matches the context — `ERR_PERMIT_TYPE_MISMATCH` (422). Applies to acquisition clearance and transfer-request creation. A `WILD_COLLECTION` acquisition requires an `IMPORT` permit and any other source requires a `POSSESSION` permit; a `transport_permit_id` must reference a `TRANSPORT` permit belonging to the same specimen's acquisition request.
28. The receiving institution's `is_receiving_eligible` is `true` — `ERR_RECEIVER_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (409). Applies to transfer submission and, among transfer decisions, to `APPROVE` alone; on approval an institution that can no longer be found is treated as ineligible rather than as a 404.
29. The permit's required-document set is fully satisfied, leaving `missing_document_codes` empty — `ERR_MISSING_DOCUMENTS` (422). Applies to permit submission.
30. The transfer's required condition set is fully satisfied by `welfare_conditions` — `ERR_TRANSFER_CONDITIONS_INCOMPLETE` (422). Applies to transfer submission; on a resubmission after a counter, every code in `counter_conditions` is additionally required.
31. Client-supplied event instants are not later than the logical clock — `ERR_TIMESTAMP_IN_FUTURE` (422). Applies to water-quality reading creation, redundancy test recording, specimen accession, husbandry log creation, mortality declaration and veterinary case creation. The boundary is inclusive: an instant exactly equal to the current clock is accepted.
32. At session creation, `starts_at` is strictly later than the logical clock — `ERR_SESSION_IN_PAST` (422). Applies to session creation. Strict: a start instant exactly equal to the clock is rejected.
33. An interval's upper bound is strictly greater than its lower bound — `ERR_INTERVAL_INVALID` (422). Applies to session creation (`ends_at` after `starts_at`) and to permit decisions (`valid_until` after `valid_from` on an `ISSUE` decision).
34. The 30-minute turnover buffer against other live sessions on the same exhibit is respected — `ERR_SESSION_BUFFER_CONFLICT` (409). Applies to session creation and, among session transitions, to `REINSTATE` alone, which re-tests the session's own stored window against every other live session while excluding itself from the comparison. `ACTIVATE` does not re-test the buffer.
35. The logical clock has reached the audit's `scheduled_for` — `ERR_AUDIT_NOT_DUE` (409). Applies to audit progressions, and only to the `START` action; `REOPEN` ignores `scheduled_for`.
36. The logical clock has reached the transfer's `proposed_transfer_at` — `ERR_TRANSFER_DATE_NOT_REACHED` (409). Applies to transfer completion. The boundary is inclusive.
37. An issued permit's validity window spans no more than 730 days — `ERR_PERMIT_VALIDITY_TOO_LONG` (422). Applies to permit decisions, and only to the `ISSUE` decision; exactly 730 days is accepted.
38. `guest_count` is no greater than `max_guests` — `ERR_GUEST_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (422). Applies to session creation and, among session transitions, to `ACTIVATE`, which re-tests the stored pair.
39. A round counter would not exceed its ceiling — `ERR_ROUND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to permit submission (at most 3 submission rounds), transfer decisions (at most 2 counters) and quarantine reviews (at most 2 extensions). Reaching the ceiling is allowed; the attempt that would pass it fails.
40. No other collection plan entry for the same exhibit and species is in `DRAFT` or `APPROVED` status — `ERR_DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_PLAN_ENTRY` (409). Applies to plan-entry status actions, where it guards reinstatement. Creation is deliberately exempt, because creation supersedes the previous approved entry by design.
41. The entity's unique code is not already taken — `ERR_DUPLICATE_CODE` (409). Applies to the creation of life support plants (`system_code`), exhibits (`exhibit_code`), species (`scientific_name`), institutions (`institution_code`), specimens (`accession_code`) and accreditation audits (`audit_code`).
42. `justification` is present when the parent plan line's `sustainability_requirement` is `ANY_WITH_JUSTIFICATION` — `ERR_JUSTIFICATION_REQUIRED` (422). Applies to acquisition submission. The rule is driven by the parent plan entry's configuration, not by the request's own fields.
43. The species' `sustainability_rating` satisfies the plan line's `sustainability_requirement` — `ERR_SUSTAINABILITY_REQUIREMENT_VIOLATION` (409). Applies to acquisition-request creation and acquisition submission.
44. The number of specimens of this species already `ON_EXHIBIT` or `TRANSFER_PENDING` in the exhibit is below the plan line's `planned_count` — `ERR_PLANNED_COUNT_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to exhibit placement. This is an aggregate rule: each placement is individually legal, and it is the collection that exceeds the plan.
45. The exhibit's `occupied_bioload_units` plus the candidate specimen's `bioload_units` does not exceed `bioload_capacity_units` — `ERR_BIOLOAD_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to exhibit placement. Landing exactly on capacity is allowed, and may itself flip the posture to `RESTRICTED`.
46. The species' `wild_take_quota_reserved` plus the request's `requested_count` does not exceed `wild_take_quota_annual` — `ERR_QUOTA_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to acquisition clearance, and only for a `WILD_COLLECTION` source. Because the reservation is released again by cancellation, plan suspension and permit expiry, the pool must reconcile exactly.
47. The `FINAL` sustainability score is at least 40.00 — `ERR_CLEARANCE_SCORE_TOO_LOW` (409). Applies to acquisition clearance. This is a cascading validation: the `FINAL` variant must be computed before the rule can be evaluated, and the score is **not** persisted when the rule fails.
48. The acquisition request's `fulfilled_count` is below its `requested_count` — `ERR_REQUESTED_COUNT_EXCEEDED` (409). Applies to specimen accession; a `FULFILLED` request accepts no further specimens.

Three consequences of this ladder are worth stating explicitly, because they are the cases where an intuitive reading of "which complaint matters more" disagrees with the specification. A placement of a specimen whose `disposition_status` is not `CLEARED` returns `ERR_INVALID_STATE` (rank 10) even when the exhibit is simultaneously over its bioload capacity (rank 45). A placement into a `CLOSED` exhibit returns `ERR_EXHIBIT_CLOSED` (rank 15) even when there is also no approved plan entry for that exhibit-and-species pair (rank 24). And a session created with a start instant already in the past returns `ERR_SESSION_IN_PAST` (rank 32) even when its `guest_count` also exceeds `max_guests` (rank 38).

### 2.9. Cascade Discipline and Derived-Flag Reconciliation

A great deal of this system's behaviour is second-order: one write moves one record, and the consequences propagate. Five propagation chains recur often enough to be named here, and each is developed in full where its trigger lives.

The **plant-alarm chain** runs when a water-quality reading or a failed redundancy test drives a plant into `CRITICAL` or `OFFLINE`: every exhibit served by that plant re-derives its posture and lands on `CLOSED`; every session on each such exhibit whose status is `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `EXHIBIT_CLOSED`, processed in ascending creation order; each such suspension increments that exhibit's `suspended_session_count`; and once that counter reaches 2 or more the exhibit's `interaction_program_enabled` is set to false (§3.1, §3.2, §3.9). The session link fires only on the transition into `CLOSED`, so an exhibit that was already shut is left alone.

The **mortality chain** runs when a specimen's death is declared: the clinical dimension moves to `DECEASED_PENDING_NECROPSY`, an open quarantine case fails with `failure_reason` set to `MORTALITY`, live transfer negotiations for that animal are withdrawn — moved to `WITHDRAWN` and carrying no `decline_reason` — and only after those two unwinds does the exhibit it vacated recompute its ledger and posture, and then, when that exhibit retains no specimen of that species in `ON_EXHIBIT`, `TRANSFER_PENDING` or `CLEARED` standing, the approved plan line for that exhibit-and-species pair is suspended with `suspension_reason` set to `LAST_SPECIMEN_LOST`, which itself cancels the live acquisition requests beneath it with `closure_reason` set to `PLAN_SUSPENDED`, releases their quota, and revokes their non-terminal permits with `decision_reason` set to `ACQUISITION_CANCELLED` (§3.7).

The **clinical-escalation coupling** runs when a veterinary case closes with the most severe findings: the specimen moves to `CRITICAL_CARE`, is pulled off its exhibit if it was on one, the vacated exhibit recomputes its ledger and posture, and — only if that recomputation lands the exhibit on `CLOSED` — the exhibit's live dive-encounter sessions are suspended with `suspension_reason` set to `EXHIBIT_CLOSED` (§3.7).

The **receiver-ineligibility chain** runs when an institution's accreditation standing or welfare audit result is edited such that it ceases to be an eligible receiver: its inbound transfer requests in `SUBMITTED`, `COUNTERED` or `APPROVED` status are declined with `decline_reason` set to `RECEIVER_INELIGIBLE`, and each affected specimen's disposition is restored from `pre_transfer_disposition` (§3.8).

The **permit-expiry chain** runs when the logical clock passes an issued permit's `valid_until`: the permit expires, the acquisition it authorised is rejected with `closure_reason` set to `PERMIT_EXPIRED`, that request's quota reservation is released, and any transfer riding on the permit as its `transport_permit_id` is declined with `decline_reason` set to `TRANSPORT_PERMIT_EXPIRED` and the specimen's disposition restored (§3.5).

Two further reason codes complete the vocabulary: a curator's own cancellation of an acquisition records `closure_reason` `CURATOR_CANCELLED`, a director's discretionary suspension of a plan line or a session records `DIRECTOR_ACTION`, and a liaison's refusal of a transfer records `decline_reason` `LIAISON_DECLINED`.

Alongside the chains sit the small **derived flags** that gate half of the rule ladder. An institution is an eligible receiver when its `accreditation_status` is `ACCREDITED` or `PROVISIONAL` **and** its `welfare_audit_score` is at least 70.0 — the boundary is inclusive, so exactly 70.0 qualifies and 69.99 does not, and an institution whose status is `NON_ACCREDITED` or `SUSPENDED` is never eligible regardless of score. A species requires a permit when its `protection_status` is anything other than `UNLISTED`. An acquisition request requires a permit when its species requires one **or** its `source_type` is `WILD_COLLECTION`. A specimen's `bioload_units` is snapshotted from its species at accession and never re-read afterwards, so editing a species does not retroactively change specimens already placed. A specimen is captive-bred when both `sire_specimen_id` and `dam_specimen_id` are recorded. Each of these is recomputed on write of the owning record and consulted fresh by every dependent rule.

### 2.10. Nested Path Resolution

Many endpoints address a child through its parent — a plant's readings, an exhibit's sessions, a specimen's quarantine cases. For any such route the system resolves identifiers strictly left to right and rejects on the **first** one that does not exist, with the not-found code of that entity. A request for the husbandry records of a specimen that does not exist returns `ERR_SPECIMEN_NOT_FOUND` (404); the system does not attempt to look for the records first. The same ordering governs body-supplied references: they are resolved after every path segment has resolved, in the order the fields are declared for that endpoint, and the first unresolvable one determines the error.

### 2.11. Segregation of Duties

Three decision pairs may never be performed by the same user, and each pair pins the first actor's `X-User-ID` on the record precisely so the second step can refuse them. The director who approved a collection plan line may not clear an acquisition request that serves that line. The director who started (or reopened) an accreditation audit may not close it. The veterinary officer who opened a necropsy case may not be the one who finalises it. All three refusals carry `ERR_SOD_VIOLATION` (409), and all three are evaluated at rank 13 of the ladder — after the state machine has agreed the transition is legal, but before any domain-specific safety, document, quota or score rule is consulted.

## 3. Domain Requirements

### 3.1. The Life Support Estate and Water Chemistry

A **life support system** — a plant — is the engineering unit that conditions water for one or more exhibits. The compliance director registers one with an operator-facing `system_code` of 1 to 32 characters, unique across all plants; a `system_type` of `RECIRCULATING_MARINE`, `FLOW_THROUGH` or `CLOSED_FRESHWATER`; a `design_habitat` of `COLD_MARINE`, `TROPICAL_MARINE`, `FRESHWATER` or `TOUCH_POOL`, which selects the water-chemistry target band table used throughout this section; a `redundancy_class` of `N`, `N_PLUS_1` or `TWO_N`, describing the spare pump and chiller capacity designed into the plant; and a `redundancy_test_interval_days`, an integer of at least 7 and no more than 365, being the longest gap the estate tolerates between passing redundancy tests. A duplicate `system_code` is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CODE`.

Everything else about a plant is derived. `alarm_state` is its alarm band and begins at `NORMAL`. `consecutive_normal_readings` and `consecutive_breach_readings` are the two counters that drive escalation and recovery. `last_redundancy_test_at` holds the `performed_at` instant of the most recent redundancy test that passed, and is null until one has; `last_redundancy_test_result` holds `PASS`, `FAIL` or null and, unlike the timestamp, reflects the most recent test whatever its outcome. `redundancy_overdue` is true when the logical clock has reached or passed the reference instant — `last_redundancy_test_at` if a test has ever passed, otherwise the plant's own `created_at` — advanced by `redundancy_test_interval_days` days. `water_quality_index` is the index of the plant's most recent reading, and is 100.00 while the plant has no readings at all.

**Readings.** An aquarist appends an immutable water-quality reading to a plant by supplying `taken_at`, `temperature_c`, `salinity_ppt`, `ph`, `dissolved_oxygen_mg_l`, `ammonia_mg_l` and `nitrite_mg_l`. The plant reference, `life_support_system_id`, is taken from the route and never from the body. `salinity_ppt`, `dissolved_oxygen_mg_l`, `ammonia_mg_l` and `nitrite_mg_l` must each be at least 0; `ph` must lie between 0 and 14; `temperature_c` is unconstrained. `taken_at` must not be later than the logical clock, on pain of `ERR_TIMESTAMP_IN_FUTURE`, and an instant exactly equal to the clock is accepted. A reading is an observation, not a resource: it carries `id` and `created_at` and nothing else of the common set, and it is never edited or deleted.

**Scoring a reading.** Every reading is scored the instant it lands, producing `water_quality_index_snapshot`, `severity` and `breach_parameters`. The plant's `design_habitat` selects a row from the habitat target table:

| `design_habitat` | temp target / band | salinity target / band | pH target / band | oxygen warn / crit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `COLD_MARINE` | 12.0 / 1.5 | 33.0 / 1.0 | 8.1 / 0.2 | 7.0 / 5.5 |
| `TROPICAL_MARINE` | 25.0 / 1.5 | 35.0 / 1.0 | 8.2 / 0.2 | 6.0 / 4.5 |
| `FRESHWATER` | 22.0 / 2.0 | 0.5 / 0.5 | 7.2 / 0.3 | 6.5 / 5.0 |
| `TOUCH_POOL` | 14.0 / 1.0 | 33.0 / 1.0 | 8.1 / 0.2 | 7.5 / 6.0 |

Six deviation ratios are then computed, one per measured parameter, each a dimensionless multiple of tolerance. For the three band parameters the ratio is the absolute difference between the measured value and the target, divided by the band: the ratio for `temperature_c` is the absolute value of `temperature_c` minus the temperature target, divided by the temperature band, and the ratios for `salinity_ppt` and `ph` are formed the same way against their own targets and bands. For the three one-sided parameters the ratio is a scaled shortfall or excess, doubled so that reaching the critical limit yields exactly 2.0: the ratio for `dissolved_oxygen_mg_l` is the greater of 0 and (the oxygen warn threshold minus `dissolved_oxygen_mg_l`), divided by (the oxygen warn threshold minus the oxygen critical threshold), multiplied by 2; the ratio for `ammonia_mg_l` is the greater of 0 and (`ammonia_mg_l` minus 0.02), divided by (0.05 minus 0.02), multiplied by 2; and the ratio for `nitrite_mg_l` is the greater of 0 and (`nitrite_mg_l` minus 0.1), divided by (0.25 minus 0.1), multiplied by 2.

The raw index starts at 100 and subtracts two penalties: 6.0 multiplied by the sum of all six ratios, and 15.0 multiplied by the single largest of them. The largest ratio is therefore charged twice — once inside the sum and once again through the maximum term — which is what makes one badly out-of-range parameter cost far more than six mildly imperfect ones. A `TOUCH_POOL` plant, where guests put their hands in the water, carries one further deduction: 10.0 multiplied by the greater of 0 and (the ammonia ratio minus 0.5). That surcharge is keyed on the ammonia ratio alone, applies only to `TOUCH_POOL`, and can bite at an ammonia ratio well below the breach threshold. `water_quality_index_snapshot` is then the raw index clamped into the inclusive range 0 to 100 and rounded half up to two decimals.

`severity` is read off the largest ratio alone: `CRITICAL` when it is at least 2.0, otherwise `WARNING` when it is at least 1.0, otherwise `NORMAL`. `breach_parameters` lists the field names of every parameter whose own ratio is at least 1.0, always emitted in the fixed canonical order `temperature_c`, `salinity_ppt`, `ph`, `dissolved_oxygen_mg_l`, `ammonia_mg_l`, `nitrite_mg_l` regardless of which breached first or worst, and is an empty array whenever `severity` is `NORMAL`. Two boundaries follow from the scaling and are deliberate: a parameter sitting exactly on its warn limit has a ratio of 0.0 and is **not** a breach, while a parameter sitting exactly on its critical limit has a ratio of 2.0 and **is** a breach.

**Which reading speaks for the plant.** The plant-level `water_quality_index` is the `water_quality_index_snapshot` of the plant's latest reading, where "latest" means the greatest `taken_at`; ties on `taken_at` are broken by the greatest `created_at`, and any remaining tie by insertion order. A reading's `id` is never a tiebreaker. The consequence matters for back-dated sampling: if an aquarist appends a reading whose `taken_at` is older than one already recorded, the plant's `water_quality_index` continues to report the newer sample's snapshot, because the selector wins — yet the appended reading's own `severity` still drives the alarm transition and still moves the counters, because the alarm ladder consumes readings in the order they are recorded.

**The alarm ladder.** `alarm_state` has no terminal state; a plant can always come back. Every reading and every redundancy test is an event on this machine.

From `NORMAL`, a `NORMAL` reading holds the band and increments `consecutive_normal_readings` while zeroing `consecutive_breach_readings`. A `WARNING` reading moves the plant to `WARNING`, increments `consecutive_breach_readings`, zeroes `consecutive_normal_readings`, updates `water_quality_index`, and drives every exhibit the plant serves to `RESTRICTED` with `last_posture_reason` set to `LSS_WARNING`. A `CRITICAL` reading moves the plant straight to `CRITICAL` and runs the full plant-alarm cascade.

From `WARNING`, a further `WARNING` reading holds `WARNING` and increments the breach counter. A `CRITICAL` reading escalates to `CRITICAL` with the same cascade. A `NORMAL` reading is where the hysteresis lives: the plant returns to `NORMAL` only when this reading brings `consecutive_normal_readings` to 3 or more; while the incremented count is still below 3 the plant holds `WARNING` and merely records the progress. On the genuine return to `NORMAL`, every served exhibit recomputes its posture and lands on `OPEN` with `last_posture_reason` null unless some other driver still holds it back — but sessions that were suspended while the plant was in trouble are **not** automatically reinstated; a director must reinstate them explicitly.

From `CRITICAL`, a further `CRITICAL` reading holds `CRITICAL` and a further `WARNING` reading also holds `CRITICAL` — the band never steps down on the strength of a breach, however mild — both incrementing `consecutive_breach_readings` and zeroing `consecutive_normal_readings`. A `NORMAL` reading de-escalates only as far as `WARNING`, and only once the incremented `consecutive_normal_readings` has reached 2; below that the plant holds `CRITICAL`. The de-escalation does **not** reset `consecutive_normal_readings` — it keeps counting, so the same run of clean readings that lifted the plant out of `CRITICAL` can go on to satisfy the stricter threshold of 3 and clear it to `NORMAL` — while `consecutive_breach_readings` is zeroed. Exhibits held at `CLOSED` by the critical alarm move up to `RESTRICTED` with `last_posture_reason` set to `LSS_WARNING`.

Every one of those reading-driven escalations and de-escalations carries the same precondition: the plant's `alarm_state` must not be `OFFLINE`. A plant that is offline is not listening to water chemistry; readings still land, are still scored and still update `water_quality_index`, but they do not move the band and they do not move the two counters either. Every appended reading none the less counts as a mutation of the plant, so the plant's `updated_at` and `version` advance on each one whatever it does to the band, and each one then re-derives the posture of every exhibit the plant serves.

**Redundancy tests.** An aquarist records a redundancy test on a plant by supplying `performed_at`, a `result` of `PASS` or `FAIL`, and an optional `note`. `performed_at` must not be later than the logical clock. A test is not a stored resource and there is no way to read one back: nothing is persisted for the test itself, the `note` is discarded, and the endpoint answers 201 with the **plant's** own representation, showing the fields the test has just moved. A `FAIL` sets `last_redundancy_test_result` to `FAIL`, leaves `last_redundancy_test_at` **unchanged** — a failed test does not restart the redundancy interval — and takes the plant to `OFFLINE` from `NORMAL`, `WARNING`, `CRITICAL` or `OFFLINE` alike; the plant's `redundancy_class` is irrelevant to this, so even a `TWO_N` plant goes offline on a single failed test. Every served exhibit then moves to `CLOSED` with `last_posture_reason` set to `LSS_OFFLINE`, and the session-suspension links of the plant-alarm cascade run.

A `PASS` sets `last_redundancy_test_at` to `performed_at`, sets `last_redundancy_test_result` to `PASS` and recomputes `redundancy_overdue`, which becomes false because the interval has just restarted. From `OFFLINE`, a `PASS` does not simply return the plant to `NORMAL`: it returns it to the band its water chemistry actually justifies. If the plant's latest reading has `severity` `NORMAL`, or the plant has no readings at all, it returns to `NORMAL`; if the latest reading is a `WARNING`, it returns to `WARNING`; and if the latest reading is a `CRITICAL`, it returns to `CRITICAL`. Recovery from an engineering fault never launders a chemistry problem. A `PASS` recorded on a plant that is **not** offline leaves `alarm_state` exactly where it was. Either result counts as a mutation of the plant, so a recorded test always advances the plant's `updated_at` and `version` and always re-derives the posture of every exhibit it serves.

**The plant-alarm cascade.** When a reading drives a plant into `CRITICAL`, five things happen in order: the reading is scored and stored; the plant's band, counters and `water_quality_index` are updated; every exhibit the plant serves recomputes its posture and lands on `CLOSED` with `last_posture_reason` set to `LSS_CRITICAL`; every interaction session on each of those exhibits whose `status` is `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `EXHIBIT_CLOSED`, processed in ascending `created_at` order, each incrementing the exhibit's `suspended_session_count`; and any exhibit whose `suspended_session_count` has thereby reached 2 or more has `interaction_program_enabled` set to false. The session-suspension link fires only on the **transition** into `CLOSED`: an exhibit that was already `CLOSED` before the recomputation and is still `CLOSED` after it leaves its live sessions alone, so a second critical reading on an already-shut exhibit does not suspend a second time. The offline path triggered by a failed redundancy test runs the last three of those links, having already set the plant's fields itself.

Reads over the estate are open to all roles except the partner liaison: the plant list (filterable by `alarm_state` and `design_habitat`), a single plant, a plant's readings newest first (filterable by `severity`), and the exhibits a plant serves (filterable by `posture`).

### 3.2. Exhibits, Operating Posture and the Bioload Ledger

An **exhibit** is a display habitat served by exactly one plant. The curator creates one with a unique `exhibit_code`, a public `name`, a `habitat_type` drawn from the same four habitats as a plant's `design_habitat`, the `life_support_system_id` of the serving plant, a `display_volume_liters` greater than 0, a `bioload_capacity_units` greater than 0 and an optional `interaction_program_enabled` flag that defaults to false when omitted. At creation the exhibit's `habitat_type` must equal the serving plant's `design_habitat`, or the request is refused with `ERR_HABITAT_MISMATCH`; a duplicate `exhibit_code` is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CODE`. The exhibit's `posture` is derived immediately from the plant it was attached to, so an exhibit created on an already-critical plant is born `CLOSED`.

**The ledger.** `occupied_bioload_units` is the sum of `bioload_units` over every specimen whose `exhibit_id` is this exhibit and whose `disposition_status` is `ON_EXHIBIT` or `TRANSFER_PENDING`, rounded half up to two decimals. Specimens in `QUARANTINE`, `CLEARED`, `TRANSFERRED` or `DECEASED` contribute nothing — a `TRANSFER_PENDING` animal is still in the tank and still consumes capacity, whereas a `CLEARED` animal has been moved to holding and does not. `bioload_utilization_pct` is `occupied_bioload_units` divided by `bioload_capacity_units`, multiplied by 100 and rounded half up to two decimals; when the capacity is 0 the utilisation is 0.00 by definition rather than an arithmetic error. The utilisation is not clamped, so an exhibit carrying more bioload than its capacity honestly reports a figure above 100.00. Placement and removal are exact inverses: removing the specimen that was just placed restores both `occupied_bioload_units` and `bioload_utilization_pct` to precisely their pre-placement values.

**Posture.** `posture` is `OPEN`, `RESTRICTED` or `CLOSED` and is never settable by a client. It is derived by walking five conditions in order and stopping at the **first** that matches, which is what makes `last_posture_reason` a statement about the dominant driver rather than a list of grievances:

1. the serving plant's `alarm_state` is `CRITICAL` — posture `CLOSED`, `last_posture_reason` `LSS_CRITICAL`;
2. the serving plant's `alarm_state` is `OFFLINE` — posture `CLOSED`, `last_posture_reason` `LSS_OFFLINE`;
3. the serving plant's `alarm_state` is `WARNING` — posture `RESTRICTED`, `last_posture_reason` `LSS_WARNING`;
4. the serving plant's `redundancy_overdue` is true — posture `RESTRICTED`, `last_posture_reason` `REDUNDANCY_OVERDUE`;
5. `bioload_utilization_pct` is strictly greater than 95.00 — posture `RESTRICTED`, `last_posture_reason` `BIOLOAD_PRESSURE`;

and otherwise posture `OPEN` with `last_posture_reason` null. Because the plant conditions are tested first, an overloaded exhibit on a critical plant reports `LSS_CRITICAL`, not `BIOLOAD_PRESSURE`. The bioload comparison is strict: an exhibit sitting at exactly 95.00 per cent utilisation stays `OPEN`, and only 95.01 and above tips it to `RESTRICTED`.

Posture is recomputed at five distinct moments: whenever the serving plant's `alarm_state` changes; whenever a redundancy test is recorded on the serving plant; whenever a clock advance flips the serving plant's `redundancy_overdue`; whenever `occupied_bioload_units` changes, whether by placement, removal, mortality, transfer completion or the clinical-escalation coupling; and whenever the exhibit's `bioload_capacity_units` is edited. Two recoveries deliberately leave sessions alone: an exhibit moving from `CLOSED` to `RESTRICTED`, and an exhibit moving from `CLOSED` to `OPEN`, both leave already-suspended sessions in `SUSPENDED`.

**Editing an exhibit.** The compliance director may re-capacitate an exhibit, rename it, reassign its plant or toggle its interaction programme in a single guarded call, supplying `expected_exhibit_version` together with any of `bioload_capacity_units`, `interaction_program_enabled`, `name` and `life_support_system_id`. Those four are the only mutable fields: `exhibit_code` and `habitat_type` are neither writable nor rejected, they are simply **ignored**, and an explicit `null` for any of the four is likewise a no-op rather than an error, so there is no way to clear a field through this endpoint. The edit recomputes the ledger and the posture, and increments the exhibit's `version` unconditionally — even a call that carries nothing but the guard, and even one whose values are identical to the stored ones, advances `version` and `updated_at`, which is what makes the guard usable as a lock. It has one further and easily missed effect: when `interaction_program_enabled` flips from false to true, `suspended_session_count` is **reset to 0**, which gives the exhibit a fresh allowance of two suspensions before the programme auto-disables again. Note also that the habitat consistency rule is enforced at exhibit creation and again at plan-line status actions, but not by this edit — so reassigning an exhibit to a plant of a different `design_habitat` succeeds here and surfaces later, when a plan line for that exhibit is next approved, suspended, reinstated or retired.

Reads: the exhibit list (filterable by `life_support_system_id` and `posture`), a single exhibit including its ledger and posture, and the sessions booked on it (filterable by `status` and `program_type`).

### 3.3. Species Parameters and the Partner Institution Registry

A **species** record carries the husbandry and regulatory parameters every downstream calculation consults. The curator registers one with a `scientific_name` unique across all species, a `common_name`, a `taxon_group` of `ELASMOBRANCH`, `TELEOST`, `INVERTEBRATE`, `MARINE_MAMMAL`, `REPTILE` or `BIRD`, a `protection_status` of `UNLISTED`, `CITES_APPENDIX_II`, `CITES_APPENDIX_I` or `ESA_LISTED`, a `sustainability_rating` of `GREEN`, `YELLOW` or `RED`, a `bioload_units_per_specimen` greater than 0, a `base_quarantine_days` of at least 1 and no more than 180, and an optional `wild_take_quota_annual` of at least 0 that defaults to 0 when omitted. A duplicate `scientific_name` is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CODE`. A species is never edited or deleted once registered; only the derived `wild_take_quota_reserved` moves afterwards.

Two species fields are derived. `permit_required` is true exactly when `protection_status` is anything other than `UNLISTED`. `wild_take_quota_reserved` is the running total of annual wild-collection allowance currently held by cleared and fulfilled wild-collection acquisitions; it begins at 0, rises when a wild-collection request is cleared and falls when one is cancelled, suspended out of the plan or rejected because its permit expired. A `wild_take_quota_annual` of 0 means wild collection is simply not permitted for the species, and no wild-collection request against it can ever clear.

An **institution** is a peer aquarium, rescue centre or supplier that may act as a source, a receiver, or both. The compliance director registers one with a `name`, an `institution_code` unique across all institutions, an `accreditation_status` of `ACCREDITED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `NON_ACCREDITED` or `SUSPENDED`, a `welfare_audit_score` between 0.00 and 100.00, and an optional `last_audit_at`. The derived `is_receiving_eligible` is true when `accreditation_status` is `ACCREDITED` or `PROVISIONAL` **and** `welfare_audit_score` is at least 70.0, both conditions required and the score boundary inclusive.

The director may later update an institution's `accreditation_status`, `welfare_audit_score` or `last_audit_at`. Those three are the only mutable fields — `name` and `institution_code` are silently ignored rather than refused, and an explicit `null` for any of the three leaves the stored value in place — and the edit carries no concurrency guard of its own. The update recomputes `is_receiving_eligible` and increments the institution's `version` unconditionally, even when nothing changed; when eligibility flips from true to false it also runs the receiver-ineligibility cascade of §3.8, and only that direction of flip triggers it. Species may be listed with the `taxon_group`, `protection_status` and `sustainability_rating` filters and read singly, showing the live quota reservation; institutions may be listed with the `accreditation_status` and `is_receiving_eligible` filters and read singly, and these two institution reads are the only ones a partner liaison may perform outside its own transfer requests.

### 3.4. The Collection Plan

A **collection plan entry** is the authorisation to hold a given species on a given exhibit in a given number. The curator creates one against an `exhibit_id` and a `species_id`, with a `planned_count` of at least 1, a `sustainability_requirement` of `GREEN_ONLY`, `GREEN_OR_YELLOW` or `ANY_WITH_JUSTIFICATION`, and the concurrency guard `expected_exhibit_version`. Creation is also a supersede operation: if the exhibit already has an `APPROVED` entry for the same species, that predecessor is retired, its `superseded_by_entry_id` is set to the new entry's `id`, and the exhibit's `version` is incremented. Acquisition requests already filed beneath the retired predecessor are deliberately left untouched, so a supersede does not silently dismantle work in flight. Creation is exempt from the duplicate-active-entry rule precisely because superseding is its job.

The entry's `status` begins at `DRAFT` and ends, if it ends, at `RETIRED`. The compliance director moves it with an `action` and an optional `reason`. The habitat consistency check is not attached to any one of those actions: whichever is requested, once it has been found legal for the current status the exhibit's `habitat_type` must equal the serving plant's `design_habitat`, catching an exhibit that was reassigned to an incompatible plant after it was created. A mismatch therefore blocks retirement and suspension just as firmly as approval, and the check is skipped only when the exhibit or its plant can no longer be found at all.

- **Approve** takes a `DRAFT` entry to `APPROVED`. Approval stamps `approved_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID` and `approved_at` with the logical clock; that user identifier is the anchor the segregation-of-duties check at acquisition clearance will refuse to match.
- **Suspend** takes an `APPROVED` entry to `SUSPENDED` and sets `suspension_reason` to `DIRECTOR_ACTION`. It then dismantles the pipeline beneath the line: every acquisition request whose `status` is `DRAFT`, `PENDING_PERMIT`, `READY_FOR_CLEARANCE` or `CLEARED` moves to `CANCELLED` with `closure_reason` set to `PLAN_SUSPENDED`, processed in ascending `created_at` order; each such cancellation releases that request's `quota_units_reserved` back into the species' `wild_take_quota_reserved` and then sets `quota_units_reserved` to 0 — a release only touches the species, and only bumps its `version`, when the reservation was actually non-zero, and the pool is floored at 0 so it can never be driven negative; and every permit of those requests whose `status` is `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `INFO_REQUESTED` or `ISSUED` moves to `REVOKED` with `decision_reason` set to `ACQUISITION_CANCELLED`.
- **Reinstate** takes a `SUSPENDED` entry back to `APPROVED`, but only when no other entry for the same exhibit-and-species pair is currently in `DRAFT` or `APPROVED` — otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_PLAN_ENTRY`. It clears `suspension_reason` to null and restamps `approved_at` and `approved_by_user_id` with the reinstating user and the current clock. The acquisition requests cancelled by the suspension are **not** restored; the pipeline must be rebuilt by hand.
- **Retire** takes an entry to the terminal `RETIRED` from `DRAFT`, `APPROVED` or `SUSPENDED` alike.

The other value `suspension_reason` can take is `LAST_SPECIMEN_LOST`, written not by this endpoint but by the mortality cascade of §3.7 when the death of an animal empties an exhibit of its species. Plan entries may be listed with the `exhibit_id`, `species_id`, `superseded_by_entry_id` and `status` filters, and read singly.

### 3.5. Acquisition Requests, Wild-Take Quota and Permits

An **acquisition request** is the instrument by which the aquarium proposes to obtain animals for an approved plan line. The curator opens one with a `collection_plan_entry_id`, a `source_type` of `CAPTIVE_BRED`, `RESCUE_STRANDING`, `INSTITUTIONAL_TRANSFER` or `WILD_COLLECTION`, a `requested_count` of at least 1, an optional `source_institution_id` and an optional `justification`. The `source_institution_id` is required when `source_type` is `INSTITUTIONAL_TRANSFER` and optional for every other channel. Creation checks the species' `sustainability_rating` against the plan line's `sustainability_requirement` — `GREEN_ONLY` admits only `GREEN`, `GREEN_OR_YELLOW` admits `GREEN` and `YELLOW`, and `ANY_WITH_JUSTIFICATION` admits any rating but demands written reasoning at submission — refusing a mismatch with `ERR_SUSTAINABILITY_REQUIREMENT_VIOLATION`. Creation also stamps the request's `permit_required`, which is true when the species' own `permit_required` is true **or** `source_type` is `WILD_COLLECTION`, and computes the `PROVISIONAL` variant of the sourcing score.

**The sourcing score.** `sustainability_score` exists in two variants and `sustainability_score_variant` records which one produced the stored value. Both share a base: a rating component of 40.0 for `GREEN`, 24.0 for `YELLOW` or 8.0 for `RED`, plus a source component of 35.0 for `CAPTIVE_BRED`, 30.0 for `RESCUE_STRANDING`, 22.0 for `INSTITUTIONAL_TRANSFER` or 10.0 for `WILD_COLLECTION`. Both add an institution component: a flat 20.0 when `source_institution_id` is null, and otherwise 0.25 multiplied by the source institution's `welfare_audit_score`. Both then cap the additive total at 100 and subtract a protection penalty of 0.0 for `UNLISTED`, 6.0 for `CITES_APPENDIX_II`, 14.0 for `CITES_APPENDIX_I` or 18.0 for `ESA_LISTED`, before clamping into 0 to 100 and rounding half up to two decimals.

The variants differ in two terms. The `PROVISIONAL` variant, computed at creation, awards no permit bonus and applies no quota pressure. The `FINAL` variant, computed at clearance, adds a permit bonus of 5.0 when any permit of the request has `status` `ISSUED`, and — only when `source_type` is `WILD_COLLECTION` **and** the species' `wild_take_quota_annual` is greater than 0 — subtracts a quota pressure term of 20.0 multiplied by ((`wild_take_quota_reserved` plus `requested_count`) divided by `wild_take_quota_annual`). The `FINAL` variant also re-reads the source institution's current `welfare_audit_score`, so an institution whose welfare audit deteriorated between drafting and clearance drags the score down. Because the additive cap is applied before the deductions, a request whose base and institution component already exceed 100 loses the excess before the penalty and quota pressure are taken off.

**Submission.** The curator submits the request, optionally supplying or replacing `justification`. The parent plan line must be `APPROVED`, the sustainability requirement is re-checked, and when that requirement is `ANY_WITH_JUSTIFICATION` a non-null `justification` is mandatory, on pain of `ERR_JUSTIFICATION_REQUIRED`. Submission then forks on `permit_required`. When it is true the request moves from `DRAFT` to `PENDING_PERMIT` and `permit_deadline_at` is set to the logical clock advanced by 30 days. When it is false the request moves from `DRAFT` to `READY_FOR_CLEARANCE` and `permit_deadline_at` stays null — that null is the branch discriminator, and it is how a reader can tell which of the two roads a request took.

**Clearance.** The compliance director clears the request, supplying `expected_species_version` and an optional `note`. Clearance is legal from `PENDING_PERMIT` and from `READY_FOR_CLEARANCE`. Because the guard is a guard on the **species**, the species must first be reachable through the request's plan line; when the plan line or the species behind it can no longer be resolved the call answers `ERR_SPECIES_NOT_FOUND` (404), ahead of the version comparison. It requires that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from the plan line's `approved_by_user_id`, a test that is simply skipped when that anchor is still null; that the recomputed `FINAL` score be at least 40.00, else `ERR_CLEARANCE_SCORE_TOO_LOW`; and, for a `WILD_COLLECTION` source, that `wild_take_quota_reserved` plus `requested_count` not exceed `wild_take_quota_annual`, else `ERR_QUOTA_EXCEEDED`. On the `PENDING_PERMIT` road it additionally requires at least one permit of the request in `ISSUED` status whose `permit_type` is `IMPORT` when `source_type` is `WILD_COLLECTION` and `POSSESSION` for every other source — the wrong type is `ERR_PERMIT_TYPE_MISMATCH`, no issued permit at all is `ERR_PERMIT_NOT_ISSUED`. The `READY_FOR_CLEARANCE` road consults no permit whatsoever and, having none, earns no permit bonus.

A successful clearance stores the `FINAL` score with `sustainability_score_variant` set to `FINAL`, stamps `cleared_by_user_id` and `cleared_at`, and — for a `WILD_COLLECTION` source only — sets `quota_units_reserved` to `requested_count`, adds `requested_count` to the species' `wild_take_quota_reserved` and increments the species' `version` by 1. When the score rule fails, the recomputed score is not persisted at all.

**Fulfilment and cancellation.** Each specimen accessioned against a `CLEARED` request increments its `fulfilled_count`; when that count reaches `requested_count` the request moves to the terminal `FULFILLED`, and below that it simply stays `CLEARED`. A fulfilled request retains its `quota_units_reserved` — the animals are in the building, and the annual allowance has genuinely been consumed. The curator may cancel a request from `DRAFT`, `PENDING_PERMIT`, `READY_FOR_CLEARANCE` or `CLEARED`, supplying an optional `reason` that is read but never stored, provided `fulfilled_count` is 0 — a partially fulfilled request in one of those four states is `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, indistinguishable from a request in the wrong state altogether; the cancellation sets `closure_reason` to `CURATOR_CANCELLED`, releases `quota_units_reserved` back into the species' pool and sets it to 0, and revokes every non-terminal permit of the request with `decision_reason` set to `ACQUISITION_CANCELLED`. A request may also be rejected by the system rather than a person, which is the subject of the expiry cascade below.

**Permits.** A **permit** is a dossier filed with an authority against one acquisition request. The permit officer drafts one with an `acquisition_request_id`, a `permit_type` of `IMPORT`, `TRANSPORT` or `POSSESSION`, an `authority_reference` and a non-empty `document_codes` array with no duplicates, drawn from the vocabulary `HEALTH_CERTIFICATE`, `ORIGIN_DECLARATION`, `IMPORT_LICENSE`, `TRANSPORT_PLAN`, `POSSESSION_AFFIDAVIT`, `CITES_PERMIT`, `ESA_AUTHORIZATION`, `ACQUISITION_PROVENANCE` and `CAPTIVE_BRED_CERT`. Drafting computes `missing_document_codes` without blocking, so an officer can see the gap before filing; nor does drafting care what state the acquisition request is in, and a request whose plan line or species can no longer be resolved is treated as `UNLISTED` for the purpose of that first computation.

The mandatory subset depends on the instrument and on the species. The base set is `HEALTH_CERTIFICATE`, `ORIGIN_DECLARATION` and `IMPORT_LICENSE` for an `IMPORT` permit; `HEALTH_CERTIFICATE` and `TRANSPORT_PLAN` for a `TRANSPORT` permit; `HEALTH_CERTIFICATE` and `POSSESSION_AFFIDAVIT` for a `POSSESSION` permit. `CITES_PERMIT` is added when the species' `protection_status` is `CITES_APPENDIX_II` or `CITES_APPENDIX_I`; `ESA_AUTHORIZATION` is added when it is `CITES_APPENDIX_I` or `ESA_LISTED`. A `CITES_APPENDIX_I` species therefore requires **both** additions. `missing_document_codes` is the required codes absent from `document_codes`, emitted in the order the requirement itself enumerates them.

The permit's `status` begins at `DRAFT`. The permit officer submits or resubmits it — from `DRAFT` or from `INFO_REQUESTED` — optionally supplying a `document_codes` array which replaces the stored one entirely when present. Submission gates first on the acquisition's own status: an `IMPORT` or `POSSESSION` permit may only be submitted while the acquisition is `PENDING_PERMIT`, whereas a `TRANSPORT` permit may be submitted while the acquisition is `PENDING_PERMIT`, `CLEARED` or `FULFILLED`, because transport paperwork is needed long after the animal has been acquired; a permit whose acquisition request has vanished altogether fails this gate too, and either way the answer is `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. It then requires that the required document set be complete, else `ERR_MISSING_DOCUMENTS`, and only after that that `submission_round` plus 1 be no greater than 3, else `ERR_ROUND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` — so an officer who is both out of rounds and short of paperwork is told about the paperwork. The incomplete-dossier refusal is the one refusal in the system that still writes: the attempted `document_codes` array is stored and `missing_document_codes` is left showing the recomputed gap, while `status`, `submission_round`, `updated_at` and `version` all stay exactly where they were, because the transition did not happen. A successful submission moves the permit to `SUBMITTED`, increments `submission_round` by 1, empties `missing_document_codes` and clears `decision_reason` to null so that a revision round does not carry the previous round's complaint.

The compliance director then records a `decision` with an optional `reason`, `valid_from` and `valid_until`:

- **`REQUEST_INFO`** moves a `SUBMITTED` permit to `INFO_REQUESTED` and stores the reason in `decision_reason`. The officer may then revise and resubmit, subject to the round ceiling.
- **`ISSUE`** moves a `SUBMITTED` permit to `ISSUED`, subject to the same acquisition-status gates as submission, and those gates are tested **before** the two validity instants are demanded — so an `ISSUE` against a permit whose acquisition has moved on answers `ERR_INVALID_STATE` even when `valid_from` and `valid_until` were both omitted. Both `valid_from` and `valid_until` must be present, `valid_until` must be strictly later than `valid_from` (`ERR_INTERVAL_INVALID`), and the span between them must be no more than 730 days (`ERR_PERMIT_VALIDITY_TOO_LONG`); exactly 730 days is accepted. `issued_at` is stamped with the logical clock and `decision_reason` is cleared to null, so an issue erases the complaint that preceded it. `ISSUED` is **not** terminal — an issued permit can still be revoked or expire.
- **`DENY`** moves a `SUBMITTED` or `INFO_REQUESTED` permit to the terminal `DENIED` and stores the reason. Crucially the acquisition request does **not** move: it stays `PENDING_PERMIT`, and a fresh permit may be drafted and submitted for it right up until `permit_deadline_at`.
- **`REVOKE`** moves a permit in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `INFO_REQUESTED` or `ISSUED` to the terminal `REVOKED`, storing the director's own `reason` in `decision_reason` — or null when none was given, which is how a hand-driven revocation reads differently from a cascade-driven one. Revocation also declines every transfer request that named this permit as its `transport_permit_id` and whose `status` is `SUBMITTED`, `COUNTERED` or `APPROVED`, with `decline_reason` set to `TRANSPORT_PERMIT_EXPIRED`. The same `REVOKED` outcome and `decision_reason` of `ACQUISITION_CANCELLED` is written by the acquisition-cancellation and plan-suspension cascades.

**The expiry cascade.** When the logical clock advances to or past an `ISSUED` permit's `valid_until`, four links run for that permit. The permit moves to the terminal `EXPIRED`. Its acquisition request, if `PENDING_PERMIT` or `CLEARED`, moves to the terminal `REJECTED` with `closure_reason` set to `PERMIT_EXPIRED` — the same rejection that occurs when `permit_deadline_at` passes with no issued permit ever obtained. That request's `quota_units_reserved` is released back into the species' `wild_take_quota_reserved` and set to 0. And every transfer request riding on the expired permit as its `transport_permit_id`, in `SUBMITTED`, `COUNTERED` or `APPROVED`, moves to `DECLINED` with `decline_reason` `TRANSPORT_PERMIT_EXPIRED`, each affected specimen's `disposition_status` restored from its `pre_transfer_disposition`, which is then set to null.

Acquisition requests may be listed with the `collection_plan_entry_id`, `source_institution_id`, `status` and `source_type` filters and read singly, showing the score and its variant; the permits of one request may be listed with the `status` and `permit_type` filters, and a permit read singly.

### 3.6. Accession, Quarantine and Placement

A **specimen** is an individual animal in the collection. The curator accessions one with an `accession_code` unique across all specimens, a `species_id`, an `acquisition_request_id`, an `arrival_at` no later than the logical clock, and optionally a `holding_area`, a `sire_specimen_id` and a `dam_specimen_id`. The named acquisition request must be `CLEARED` or already `FULFILLED` — anything else is `ERR_INVALID_STATE` — and its `fulfilled_count` must be below its `requested_count` on pain of `ERR_REQUESTED_COUNT_EXCEEDED`, which is what closes the door a `FULFILLED` request appeared to leave ajar. The `acquisition_request_id` is optional, and is null only for in-house births; when it is absent the state, count, quarantine-surcharge and fulfilment steps are all simply skipped. Accession snapshots `bioload_units` from the species' current `bioload_units_per_specimen` and never re-reads it afterwards, derives `captive_bred` as true exactly when both `sire_specimen_id` and `dam_specimen_id` are present, computes `quarantine_required_days`, opens the quarantine case, and increments the acquisition request's `fulfilled_count`.

**How long quarantine lasts.** `quarantine_required_days` is computed in six ordered steps, and the order is load-bearing. Step one takes the species' `base_quarantine_days` as the base. Step two multiplies the base by the taxon multiplier — 1.5 for `ELASMOBRANCH`, 1.0 for `TELEOST`, 0.5 for `INVERTEBRATE`, 2.0 for `MARINE_MAMMAL`, 1.25 for `REPTILE`, 1.25 for `BIRD` — and rounds the product **up** to a whole number of days; this is the only rounding in the calculation. Step three computes a surcharge as the sum of two independent additions: 14 days when the acquisition's `source_type` is `WILD_COLLECTION`, and a source-institution term of 10 days when the source institution's `accreditation_status` is `NON_ACCREDITED`, or 5 days when it is `PROVISIONAL`, or 0 days otherwise — including 0 when there is no source institution at all. Step four adds the taxon days and the surcharge into a subtotal. Step five classifies the risk tier as high when the **taxon days from step two**, not the subtotal, are at least 45. Step six adds 7 further days when the tier is high, and the result is `quarantine_required_days`.

Keying the tier on step two rather than the subtotal is what makes the calculation counter-intuitive, and worked cases show why. A species with a `base_quarantine_days` of 24 and `taxon_group` `ELASMOBRANCH`, acquired by `WILD_COLLECTION` from a `NON_ACCREDITED` institution, yields taxon days of 36, a surcharge of 24 and a subtotal of 60 — but the tier is standard, because 36 is below 45, so the answer is 60 and not 67. A species with a `base_quarantine_days` of 30, also `ELASMOBRANCH`, acquired by `WILD_COLLECTION` from an `ACCREDITED` institution, yields taxon days of 45, a surcharge of 14 and a subtotal of 59; here the tier is high, because 45 meets the threshold exactly, so the answer is 66. And a `TELEOST` with a `base_quarantine_days` of 30 acquired by `WILD_COLLECTION` from a `PROVISIONAL` institution yields taxon days of 30 and a surcharge of 19, for 49 days.

**The quarantine case.** Accession creates a **quarantine case** automatically; there is no endpoint that creates one, and its `specimen_id` is written by the system. Its `protocol_days` is a snapshot of the specimen's `quarantine_required_days`; its `started_at` equals the specimen's `arrival_at`; its `projected_release_at` is `started_at` advanced by (`protocol_days` plus `total_extension_days`) days and is recomputed on every extension; its `status` begins at `ACTIVE`; and `extension_count`, `total_extension_days` and `clearance_exam_count` begin at 0. `clearance_exam_count` is the number of veterinary cases for the specimen whose `case_type` is `PREVENTIVE_EXAM`, whose `status` is `CLOSED`, whose `findings_severity` is `NONE`, and whose `opened_at` falls at or after `started_at` and strictly before the logical clock.

The veterinary officer records a review with an `outcome`, an optional `extension_days` and an optional `reason`:

- **`EXTEND`** moves an `ACTIVE` case to `EXTENDED`. `extension_days` is mandatory for this outcome and must be between 1 and 30, and its absence is a rank-7 `ERR_VALIDATION` decided before the outcome is even matched against the case's status. `extension_count` plus 1 must then be no greater than 2, else `ERR_ROUND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`. It increments `extension_count`, adds `extension_days` into `total_extension_days` and recomputes `projected_release_at` from `started_at` plus (`protocol_days` plus `total_extension_days`) days.
- **`RESUME`** moves an `EXTENDED` case back to `ACTIVE` with no further change.
- **`CLEAR`** moves an `ACTIVE` case to the terminal `CLEARED`. Its three gates are tested in the order the ladder gives them: the specimen's `health_status` must be `HEALTHY`, else `ERR_SPECIMEN_NOT_HEALTHY` — a test skipped outright if the specimen can no longer be found; then the logical clock must have reached `projected_release_at`, inclusive, else `ERR_QUARANTINE_NOT_COMPLETE`; then `clearance_exam_count`, recounted live at this moment rather than read from the stored figure, must be at least 2, else `ERR_CLEARANCE_EXAMS_INSUFFICIENT`. It stamps `cleared_by_user_id` and `cleared_at`, and moves the specimen's `disposition_status` from `QUARANTINE` to `CLEARED`. Clearing is legal **only** from `ACTIVE`: an `EXTENDED` case must be resumed first, and a `CLEAR` outcome against it is `ERR_INVALID_ACTION`.
- **`FAIL`** moves an `ACTIVE` or `EXTENDED` case to the terminal `FAILED` and copies the review's own `reason` into `failure_reason` verbatim, leaving it null when none was supplied. The specimen's `disposition_status` stays `QUARANTINE`; only the mortality cascade moves it out to `DECEASED`, and when the cascade writes the failure it records a `failure_reason` of `MORTALITY`.

**The disposition dimension.** A specimen's `disposition_status` tracks where in the collection it is, independently of its clinical state, and begins at `QUARANTINE`. Quarantine clearance takes it to `CLEARED`. Placement takes it from `CLEARED` to `ON_EXHIBIT`. Removal, or the clinical-escalation coupling, takes it from `ON_EXHIBIT` back to `CLEARED`. Transfer submission takes it from `CLEARED` or `ON_EXHIBIT` to `TRANSFER_PENDING`; a declined or unwound transfer brings it back; a completed one takes it to the terminal `TRANSFERRED`. A mortality takes it to the terminal `DECEASED` from `QUARANTINE`, `CLEARED`, `ON_EXHIBIT` or `TRANSFER_PENDING` alike.

**Placement.** The aquarist places a cleared specimen by supplying the target `exhibit_id`, the guard `expected_exhibit_version` and an optional `restricted_posture_override`. Placement demands a great deal at once: the specimen must be `CLEARED` in the disposition dimension and `HEALTHY` in the clinical one; the exhibit must not be `CLOSED`; if the exhibit is `RESTRICTED`, `restricted_posture_override` must be explicitly true — an omitted flag defaults to false — and if the exhibit is `OPEN` the flag is ignored; an `APPROVED` collection plan entry must exist for that exhibit and the specimen's species, else `ERR_NO_APPROVED_PLAN_ENTRY`; the count of specimens of that species already `ON_EXHIBIT` or `TRANSFER_PENDING` on the exhibit must be below the plan line's `planned_count`, else `ERR_PLANNED_COUNT_EXCEEDED`; and the exhibit's `occupied_bioload_units` plus the specimen's `bioload_units` must not exceed `bioload_capacity_units`, else `ERR_BIOLOAD_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. Landing exactly on capacity is permitted, and since the utilisation then reads 100.00 per cent, the very placement that just squeezed in may flip the exhibit to `RESTRICTED` with `last_posture_reason` `BIOLOAD_PRESSURE`. Placement sets the specimen's `exhibit_id`, clears `holding_area` to null, recomputes the ledger and the posture, and increments both the specimen's and the exhibit's `version` by 1.

**Removal.** The aquarist removes a specimen from its exhibit by supplying `expected_exhibit_version` and an optional `holding_area`. Because the guard names the exhibit and the exhibit is discovered through the specimen, a specimen that is on no exhibit at all cannot be version-checked, and the endpoint answers `ERR_INVALID_STATE` **before** it compares versions — the one place where a state complaint outranks a stale-version complaint. Once the exhibit is in hand the version is compared, and only then is the specimen required to be `ON_EXHIBIT`. The specimen's `disposition_status` returns to `CLEARED`, its `exhibit_id` becomes null, its `holding_area` takes the supplied value or becomes null when none was supplied, and the exhibit's `occupied_bioload_units` and `bioload_utilization_pct` are restored exactly before the posture is recomputed; the exhibit's `version` advances whether or not the ledger figures actually moved. When it is the clinical-escalation coupling rather than an aquarist that performs the removal, the `holding_area` written is the literal label `LAST_KNOWN_HOLDING`.

Specimens may be listed across both dimensions with the `species_id`, `acquisition_request_id`, `exhibit_id`, `sire_specimen_id`, `dam_specimen_id`, `disposition_status` and `health_status` filters, and read singly with `welfare_score` and its variant; a specimen's quarantine cases may be listed with the `status` filter, and a quarantine case read singly.

### 3.7. Husbandry Records, Clinical Care, Welfare and Mortality

**Husbandry records.** An aquarist or a veterinary officer appends an immutable husbandry record to a specimen with a `log_type` of `FEEDING`, `BEHAVIOR`, `TREATMENT` or `WEIGHT`, a `logged_at` no later than the logical clock, and the fields the type calls for: an `appetite_score` between 0 and 5, required when `log_type` is `FEEDING` and forbidden otherwise; a `behavior_code` of `NORMAL`, `LETHARGIC`, `AGGRESSIVE` or `ABNORMAL_SWIM`, required when `log_type` is `BEHAVIOR` and forbidden otherwise; an optional `feed_grams` of at least 0; a `weight_grams` greater than 0, required when `log_type` is `WEIGHT`; and an optional `note`. Only the two typed fields are mutually exclusive with their types: `feed_grams`, `weight_grams` and `note` are accepted on any `log_type` whatsoever, so a `BEHAVIOR` record may carry a weight and a `TREATMENT` record — which demands nothing of its own beyond the common fields — may carry both. The `specimen_id` comes from the route and `recorded_by_user_id` from the acting `X-User-ID`; neither may be supplied. Like a water-quality reading, a husbandry record carries only `id` and `created_at`. Every append recomputes the specimen's `welfare_score` under the `ROLLING` variant and sets `welfare_score_variant` to `ROLLING`, but the specimen's own `updated_at` and `version` advance only when that recomputation actually moves the stored score or the stored variant; an append that leaves both exactly as they were leaves the specimen record untouched.

**The welfare index.** `welfare_score` is null until the first qualifying record exists, and `welfare_score_variant` records which variant produced the stored value. Both variants share a behaviour component: the arithmetic mean of the behaviour points over the `BEHAVIOR` records in the window, where `NORMAL` scores 10.0, `LETHARGIC` 4.0, `AGGRESSIVE` 2.0 and `ABNORMAL_SWIM` 0.0, and which is 0 when the window holds no behaviour records. Both start from a base of 40.0 and both finish by clamping into 0 to 100 and rounding half up to two decimals. Everything else about them differs.

The `ROLLING` variant, recomputed on every husbandry append, on the opening of a `TREATMENT` case, and on the closure of a case as `RESOLVED` or `CHRONIC_MANAGED` — but not on the opening of a `PREVENTIVE_EXAM` or a `NECROPSY`, which touch the specimen not at all, and not on a `MORTALITY_REFERRED` or `NECROPSY_FINALIZED` closure, which have larger business with the clinical dimension — takes as its window the 5 most recent records whose `log_type` is `FEEDING` or `BEHAVIOR`, ordered by `logged_at` descending and then by `created_at` descending. An empty window yields no value at all rather than a zero, and a recomputation that yields no value is abandoned: whatever `welfare_score` and `welfare_score_variant` already held survives untouched, which is why a specimen with no qualifying records still reads null for both. Its feeding component is the mean `appetite_score` over the `FEEDING` records in the window multiplied by 8.0, and 0 when the window holds no feeding records. Its raw score is 40.0 plus the feeding component plus the behaviour component, minus 6.0 for each veterinary case of the specimen whose `status` is `OPEN` and whose `case_type` is `TREATMENT` — so a specimen carrying two concurrent open treatments is charged that penalty twice.

The `AUDIT_SNAPSHOT` variant, computed only by an accreditation audit, takes as its window every `FEEDING` or `BEHAVIOR` record whose `logged_at` falls within the 14 days ending at the logical clock, both bounds inclusive. Its feeding component is not a mean over records but a mean over days: the sum of `appetite_score` across the `FEEDING` records in the window, divided by 14, multiplied by 8.0. It then counts the distinct UTC calendar dates on which a `FEEDING` record was made and subtracts 3.0 for each of the 14 days on which none was, so its raw score is 40.0 plus the feeding component plus the behaviour component minus that missing-day penalty. The asymmetry between the two variants is deliberate: the `ROLLING` variant charges the open-treatment penalty and knows nothing of missing days, while the `AUDIT_SNAPSHOT` variant charges the missing-day penalty and ignores open treatments entirely.

**Veterinary cases.** The veterinary officer opens a case with a `specimen_id`, a `case_type` of `PREVENTIVE_EXAM`, `TREATMENT` or `NECROPSY`, an `opened_at` no later than the logical clock, an optional `diagnosis_code`, and — required when `case_type` is `TREATMENT` and forbidden otherwise — a `treatment_start_at`, together with a `withdrawal_days` of at least 0 and no more than 90 that is required for a treatment. `status` begins at `OPEN`, `opened_by_user_id` is taken from the acting `X-User-ID`, and `outcome` and `findings_severity` are null until closure.

A treatment's withdrawal window is the half-open interval beginning at `treatment_start_at` and ending at `treatment_start_at` advanced by `withdrawal_days` days, the end instant excluded. A new treatment is refused with `ERR_TREATMENT_WITHDRAWAL_ACTIVE` when its candidate window overlaps the window of any of the same specimen's `OPEN` `TREATMENT` cases — overlap meaning the new window starts strictly before the existing one ends and the existing one starts strictly before the new one ends. Because the interval is half-open, a new window starting exactly where an existing one ends does not conflict, and a `withdrawal_days` of 0 produces an empty window that can never conflict with anything. `treatment_withdrawal_until` on the specimen is the latest window end across its `OPEN` `TREATMENT` cases, and null when it has none.

Opening a `TREATMENT` case moves the clinical dimension to `UNDER_TREATMENT` — from `HEALTHY`, from `CRITICAL_CARE`, or from `UNDER_TREATMENT` itself for a second concurrent treatment, and from nowhere else, so a treatment opened on a `DECEASED_PENDING_NECROPSY` or `NECROPSY_COMPLETE` animal is `ERR_INVALID_STATE` — recomputes `treatment_withdrawal_until`, and recomputes the `ROLLING` welfare score, which drops because a new open-treatment penalty now applies; the specimen's `version` advances on any treatment opening whether or not those two derived values moved. That entry-state gate is one of the few places where the ladder bends: a future `opened_at` is caught first, so an ineligible animal and an impossible instant together answer `ERR_TIMESTAMP_IN_FUTURE`. The withdrawal-overlap test then runs after the entry-state gate has passed.

**Closure.** The officer closes a case with an `outcome`, a mandatory `findings_severity` and an optional `note` that is read but never stored. Four outcomes branch the specimen's clinical dimension in four different ways, and each carries its own preconditions. Those preconditions are stated entirely in terms of the case's `case_type` and the supplied `findings_severity`; none of them consults the specimen's `health_status`, and a pairing that fails any of them is always `ERR_INVALID_ACTION`. Whatever the branch, closure recomputes `treatment_withdrawal_until` from the specimen's remaining open treatments, and the specimen record is written only when the branch actually moved something on it.

`RESOLVED` requires a `case_type` of `PREVENTIVE_EXAM` or `TREATMENT` and a `findings_severity` of `NONE` or `MINOR`. It moves `health_status` from `UNDER_TREATMENT` to `HEALTHY`, but only when the case being closed is the specimen's only `OPEN` case whose `case_type` is `TREATMENT` or `PREVENTIVE_EXAM`; the `ROLLING` welfare score is recomputed. When the case type is `PREVENTIVE_EXAM` and the severity is `NONE`, the specimen's `ACTIVE` or `EXTENDED` quarantine case is itself touched, advancing its `updated_at` and `version`. That figure is never stored: `clearance_exam_count` is recounted from the veterinary record every time the case is read or tested, and the touch merely records that the count has moved.

`CHRONIC_MANAGED` requires a `case_type` of `TREATMENT` and a `findings_severity` of `MINOR` or `MAJOR`. `health_status` stays `UNDER_TREATMENT` — the animal is managed, not cured — but the `ROLLING` welfare score is still recomputed, because closing the case drops one open-treatment penalty.

`MORTALITY_REFERRED` requires a `findings_severity` of `CRITICAL` and nothing else — any `case_type` may be closed this way — and drives the **clinical-escalation coupling** in four links. First, `health_status` becomes `CRITICAL_CARE`, from whatever it was: there is no entry-state gate, so even a `DECEASED_PENDING_NECROPSY` animal is walked back to `CRITICAL_CARE` by this outcome. Second, if the specimen's `disposition_status` is `ON_EXHIBIT` it is pulled off: the disposition returns to `CLEARED`, `exhibit_id` becomes null and `holding_area` becomes `LAST_KNOWN_HOLDING`. Third, the vacated exhibit recomputes its ledger and its posture. Fourth — and **only** when that recomputation lands the exhibit on `CLOSED` — every interaction session on that exhibit whose `program_type` is `DIVE_ENCOUNTER` and whose `status` is `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `EXHIBIT_CLOSED`. If the recomputed posture is `OPEN` or `RESTRICTED`, the sessions are left entirely alone; unlike the plant-alarm cascade, this link does not care whether the exhibit was already `CLOSED` beforehand, so a dive programme on an exhibit that was shut all along is still suspended. The vacated exhibit's `version` advances in any case, and the `ROLLING` welfare score is deliberately not recomputed here. No necropsy case is created by this outcome; a critical finding is an escalation of care, not a declaration of death.

`NECROPSY_FINALIZED` requires a `case_type` of `NECROPSY`, a `status` of `OPEN`, a non-null `findings_severity` — which, since the field is mandatory on every closure, is satisfied automatically, so `case_type` is the whole of the test — and an acting `X-User-ID` different from the case's `opened_by_user_id`, else `ERR_SOD_VIOLATION`. Because a cascade-created necropsy records the declaring officer as its `opened_by_user_id`, the officer who declared the death can never be the one to finalise it. It writes `health_status` as the terminal `NECROPSY_COMPLETE` unconditionally, with no entry-state gate of its own — so finalising a necropsy that a veterinary officer opened by hand on a living animal drives that animal straight into the terminal clinical state without any death having been declared. Every closure sets `status` to the terminal `CLOSED` and stamps `closed_by_user_id` and `closed_at`.

The one remaining clinical recovery runs from `CRITICAL_CARE` back to `HEALTHY`: closing a `PREVENTIVE_EXAM` with a `findings_severity` of `NONE` when the specimen has no other `OPEN` case restores it.

**Mortality.** The veterinary officer declares a death with an `occurred_at` no later than the logical clock and an optional `cause_note` that is read but never stored. The declaration writes `health_status` as `DECEASED_PENDING_NECROPSY` and `disposition_status` as the terminal `DECEASED` whatever the specimen's clinical state was, and it also clears `exhibit_id` to null, which is how the vacated exhibit's ledger comes to exclude the animal. Every `OPEN` `TREATMENT` case of the specimen is closed with `outcome` set to `MORTALITY_REFERRED`, `findings_severity` set to `CRITICAL`, and `closed_by_user_id` and `closed_at` stamped with the declaring officer and the logical clock — this happens on the strength of the open cases alone, not on the strength of the clinical state that preceded the declaration. A necropsy investigation is opened. The only bar on the endpoint is the disposition dimension: a specimen already `DECEASED` **or** already `TRANSFERRED` is `ERR_ENTITY_TERMINAL`, not `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, and there is no clinical state that refuses a declaration.

The mortality cascade then runs five links. The necropsy case is created, with its `opened_at` set to the declaration's own `occurred_at` rather than to the clock, its `opened_by_user_id` set to the declaring officer, its `status` `OPEN` and its `case_type`, `treatment_start_at`, `withdrawal_days`, `diagnosis_code`, `outcome` and `findings_severity` set to `NECROPSY` and nulls respectively. The exhibit the animal occupied recomputes its ledger and its posture, its `version` advancing in any case. If that exhibit now holds no specimen of the dead animal's species — the test being for any other specimen whose `exhibit_id` is still that exhibit, whose `species_id` matches, and whose `disposition_status` is `ON_EXHIBIT`, `TRANSFER_PENDING` or `CLEARED` — the `APPROVED` collection plan entry for that exhibit-and-species pair moves to `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `LAST_SPECIMEN_LOST`. Every acquisition request beneath that plan line whose `status` is `DRAFT`, `PENDING_PERMIT`, `READY_FOR_CLEARANCE` or `CLEARED` moves to `CANCELLED` with `closure_reason` set to `PLAN_SUSPENDED`, releasing its `quota_units_reserved` into the species' `wild_take_quota_reserved`. And every permit of those requests in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `INFO_REQUESTED` or `ISSUED` moves to `REVOKED` with `decision_reason` set to `ACQUISITION_CANCELLED`. Two further unwinds run **before** the exhibit link rather than after it: the specimen's `ACTIVE` or `EXTENDED` quarantine case moves to `FAILED` with `failure_reason` set to `MORTALITY`, and every transfer request for the specimen in `SUBMITTED`, `COUNTERED` or `APPROVED` moves to the terminal `WITHDRAWN` — no `decline_reason` is written, since a withdrawal is not a decline, and `pre_transfer_disposition` is deliberately **not** restored and not even cleared, because the specimen's disposition is now terminal and the field is left standing as a record of where the animal had been.

A specimen's veterinary cases may be listed with the `status` and `case_type` filters, and a case read singly.

### 3.8. Outbound Transfers

A **transfer request** is a negotiated proposal to move an animal to a receiving institution. The permit officer drafts one with a `specimen_id`, a `receiving_institution_id`, a `proposed_transfer_at`, a non-empty `welfare_conditions` array with no duplicates, and an optional `transport_permit_id`. The condition vocabulary is `WATER_CHEMISTRY_MATCH`, `TRANSPORT_OXYGEN_PLAN`, `QUARANTINE_ON_ARRIVAL`, `CITES_TRANSPORT_CLEARANCE`, `DIET_TRANSITION_PLAN`, `NECROPSY_POLICY`, `NO_COMMERCIAL_RESALE` and `BREEDING_LOAN_TERMS`. When a `transport_permit_id` is supplied at drafting it must reference a permit whose `permit_type` is `TRANSPORT` and which belongs to the same specimen's acquisition request, else `ERR_PERMIT_TYPE_MISMATCH` — and since an in-house birth has no acquisition request, no transport permit can ever be attached to one. `status` begins at `DRAFT`, `counter_conditions` is an empty array and `negotiation_round` is 0.

**Which conditions are mandatory.** The required set always contains `WATER_CHEMISTRY_MATCH` and `TRANSPORT_OXYGEN_PLAN`. `QUARANTINE_ON_ARRIVAL` is added when the receiving institution's `accreditation_status` is `PROVISIONAL`. `CITES_TRANSPORT_CLEARANCE` is added when the specimen's species has a `protection_status` other than `UNLISTED`. `DIET_TRANSITION_PLAN` is added when the specimen has at least one veterinary case whose `case_type` is `TREATMENT`, of any `status` whatsoever — a long-closed treatment still obliges the sender to hand over a diet plan. Finally every code in `counter_conditions` that is not already present is appended, which is how a receiving institution's demands become binding on the next submission.

**Submission.** The permit officer submits from `DRAFT`, and resubmits from `COUNTERED`, optionally supplying a `welfare_conditions` array. Submission requires the specimen's `disposition_status` to be `CLEARED` or `ON_EXHIBIT`; the specimen's `health_status` to be `HEALTHY`, else `ERR_SPECIMEN_NOT_HEALTHY`; the receiving institution's `is_receiving_eligible` to be true, else `ERR_RECEIVER_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; and `welfare_conditions` to cover every required code, else `ERR_TRANSFER_CONDITIONS_INCOMPLETE`. The specimen and the institution are both resolved along the way, and a request whose specimen or institution has since vanished answers `ERR_SPECIMEN_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_INSTITUTION_NOT_FOUND` (404) at the point the resolution is attempted, ahead of the gate that needed it. On a resubmission after a counter the rules tighten: every code in `counter_conditions` is additionally required, the supplied `welfare_conditions` array replaces the stored one, and the specimen must already be `TRANSFER_PENDING` rather than being moved there again.

The first submission moves the specimen to `TRANSFER_PENDING` and records its previous `disposition_status` in `pre_transfer_disposition`, which may be `CLEARED` or `ON_EXHIBIT`. When the animal was `ON_EXHIBIT` its `exhibit_id` is retained and the exhibit's ledger is unchanged, because a `TRANSFER_PENDING` animal still occupies the tank and still consumes bioload.

**Decisions.** A `decision` is recorded with an optional `counter_conditions` array and an optional `reason`, and the endpoint is shared by two roles with disjoint vocabularies.

`COUNTER` belongs to the partner liaison. It moves a `SUBMITTED` request to `COUNTERED`, requires the acting `X-User-ID` to equal the request's `receiving_institution_id`, requires a non-empty `counter_conditions` array — a rank-7 `ERR_VALIDATION` when it is missing or empty, decided before the request's own state is even examined — and requires `negotiation_round` plus 1 to be no greater than 2 (else `ERR_ROUND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`), stores those conditions, increments `negotiation_round` and stamps `decided_by_user_id` and `decided_at`.

`APPROVE` belongs to the compliance director. It moves a `SUBMITTED` request to `APPROVED`. When the specimen's species has `permit_required` true, `transport_permit_id` must be non-null and must reference a permit whose `permit_type` is `TRANSPORT` and whose `status` is `ISSUED`, else `ERR_PERMIT_NOT_ISSUED`; a species that can no longer be resolved waives the permit gate entirely. The receiving institution must still be eligible at the moment of approval, not merely at submission, and an institution that has vanished counts as ineligible rather than as a 404. Neither gate applies to `COUNTER` or `DECLINE`, which are available on a request whose receiver has since become ineligible.

`DECLINE` belongs to the partner liaison and moves a `SUBMITTED`, `COUNTERED` or `APPROVED` request to the terminal `DECLINED` with `decline_reason` set to `LIAISON_DECLINED`. Whatever the reason, a decline restores the specimen's `disposition_status` from `pre_transfer_disposition` and then sets `pre_transfer_disposition` to null; if the animal was `ON_EXHIBIT` before the transfer it returns to `ON_EXHIBIT`, and if it was `CLEARED` it returns to `CLEARED`. The restoration is conditional on the specimen still being `TRANSFER_PENDING` with a non-null `pre_transfer_disposition`, and it is silently skipped otherwise — so a second decline arriving through a cascade cannot undo a disposition twice. Restoring an animal to `ON_EXHIBIT` does **not** re-derive the former exhibit's ledger, because a `TRANSFER_PENDING` animal was consuming its bioload throughout. The same terminal `DECLINED`, with the same restoration, is written by the receiver-ineligibility cascade with `decline_reason` `RECEIVER_INELIGIBLE` and by the permit-expiry cascade with `decline_reason` `TRANSPORT_PERMIT_EXPIRED`.

**Completion.** The permit officer completes an `APPROVED` transfer with no request body at all. The logical clock must have reached `proposed_transfer_at`, inclusive, else `ERR_TRANSFER_DATE_NOT_REACHED`; the specimen must be `TRANSFER_PENDING` and `HEALTHY`. Completion stamps `completed_at`, moves the request to the terminal `COMPLETED`, moves the specimen's `disposition_status` to the terminal `TRANSFERRED` with `exhibit_id` and `pre_transfer_disposition` both set to null, and recomputes the former exhibit's ledger and posture, advancing that exhibit's `version` whether or not the figures moved — the animal has left the building, and the bioload it was holding is released at last.

**The receiver-ineligibility cascade.** When an institution edit flips `is_receiving_eligible` from true to false, three links run: every transfer request whose `receiving_institution_id` is that institution and whose `status` is `SUBMITTED`, `COUNTERED` or `APPROVED` moves to `DECLINED`, processed in ascending `created_at` order; each carries `decline_reason` `RECEIVER_INELIGIBLE`; and each affected specimen's `disposition_status` is restored from `pre_transfer_disposition`, which is then nulled. The cascade leaves `decided_by_user_id` and `decided_at` untouched, which is what distinguishes a system decline from a liaison's.

Transfer requests may be listed with the `specimen_id`, `receiving_institution_id`, `transport_permit_id` and `status` filters and read singly, in both cases with the liaison scoping of §2.1 applied.

### 3.9. Guest Interaction Programmes and Accreditation Inspections

**Interaction sessions.** An aquarist books a guest programme on an exhibit with an `exhibit_id`, a `program_type` of `TOUCH_POOL_CONTACT`, `FEEDING_ENCOUNTER` or `DIVE_ENCOUNTER`, a `starts_at`, an `ends_at`, a `max_guests` of at least 1, an optional `guest_count` of at least 0 that defaults to 0, and an optional `safety_briefing_confirmed` that defaults to false. Both interval bounds are inclusive, and `ends_at` must be strictly later than `starts_at`, else `ERR_INTERVAL_INVALID`. `starts_at` must be strictly later than the logical clock, else `ERR_SESSION_IN_PAST` — a session starting exactly now is rejected. Creation also requires that the exhibit's `posture` not be `CLOSED`, that its `interaction_program_enabled` be true, that `guest_count` not exceed `max_guests`, and that the turnover buffer be respected. A session may be created with `safety_briefing_confirmed` false; only activation demands it.

**The turnover buffer.** Two sessions on the same exhibit conflict when the candidate starts strictly before the existing session's `ends_at` advanced by 30 minutes **and** the existing session starts strictly before the candidate's `ends_at` advanced by 30 minutes. The consequences are worth spelling out: any genuine overlap conflicts; two adjacent sessions with a gap of zero conflict, because a keeper needs half an hour to turn a programme around; a gap of exactly 30 minutes does **not** conflict; and a gap of 29 minutes does. Only sessions on the same exhibit whose `status` is `SCHEDULED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` are considered — `COMPLETED` and `CANCELLED` sessions are ignored entirely, so a cancelled booking never blocks a rebooking of the same slot.

**Session transitions.** `status` begins at `SCHEDULED`. An `action` is supplied with an optional `reason`, and the endpoint is shared between two roles with disjoint vocabularies.

`ACTIVATE` belongs to the aquarist and moves a `SCHEDULED` session to `ACTIVE`. It is the strictest gate in the system, and its five checks fall in the ladder's order: the exhibit's `posture` must not be `CLOSED`, an exhibit that has vanished counting as closed; the serving plant's `alarm_state` must be exactly `NORMAL` and its `redundancy_overdue` must be false, else `ERR_LIFE_SUPPORT_ALARM` — which is stricter than the posture rule, since a plant in `WARNING` leaves the posture merely `RESTRICTED`, and an exhibit with no serving plant fails here as well; the stored `safety_briefing_confirmed` must be true, else `ERR_SAFETY_BRIEFING_REQUIRED`; for a `DIVE_ENCOUNTER` no specimen assigned to the exhibit may be in `CRITICAL_CARE` or `DECEASED_PENDING_NECROPSY`, else `ERR_DIVE_ENCOUNTER_BLOCKED`, because divers do not share water with an animal in crisis; and `guest_count` must not exceed `max_guests`. Notably absent from that list are the two gates creation applies which activation does not: `interaction_program_enabled` is not re-read, and the turnover buffer is not re-tested, so a booking already granted survives a later disabling of the programme.

`COMPLETE` belongs to the aquarist and moves an `ACTIVE` session to the terminal `COMPLETED`. `CANCEL` belongs to the aquarist and moves a `SCHEDULED` or `SUSPENDED` session to the terminal `CANCELLED`.

`SUSPEND` belongs to the compliance director and moves a `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE` session to `SUSPENDED` with `suspension_reason` set to `DIRECTOR_ACTION`; the plant-alarm cascade and the clinical-escalation coupling reach the same state with `suspension_reason` set to `EXHIBIT_CLOSED`. Every suspension, whoever caused it, increments the exhibit's `suspended_session_count`, and when that lifetime counter reaches 2 or more the exhibit's `interaction_program_enabled` is set to false, closing the exhibit to new bookings until a director re-enables it. A director's own `SUSPEND` is no exception, and it also advances the exhibit's `version` alongside the session's.

`REINSTATE` belongs to the compliance director and moves a `SUSPENDED` session back to `SCHEDULED`. It re-checks everything that could have changed while the session was out: the exhibit's `posture` must not be `CLOSED`, its `interaction_program_enabled` must be true, and the session must not conflict with the current live bookings under the turnover buffer. It clears `suspension_reason` to null, and it does **not** decrement `suspended_session_count` — the suspension is a matter of record, and only an interaction-programme re-enable resets that counter.

**Accreditation inspections.** An **accreditation audit** is a point-in-time assessment of the whole institution. The compliance director schedules one with an `audit_code` unique across all audits, a `scope` of `FULL`, `ANIMAL_CARE_ONLY` or `LIFE_SUPPORT_ONLY`, and a `scheduled_for` instant. It is born `SCHEDULED` with `readiness_score` and `domain_subscores` both null, `blocking_finding_count` 0, and `opened_by_user_id`, `closed_by_user_id`, `started_at`, `closed_at` and `determination_note` all null. The `scope` is recorded and is available as a list filter, but it does not narrow anything: every progression scores the whole estate across all five domains regardless of it. The director progresses it with an `action` and an optional `determination_note`.

`START` moves a `SCHEDULED` audit to `IN_PROGRESS` and requires the logical clock to have reached `scheduled_for`, else `ERR_AUDIT_NOT_DUE`. It runs two strictly ordered steps. **Step one** re-scores every specimen whose `disposition_status` is `CLEARED` or `ON_EXHIBIT`, in ascending creation order, using the `AUDIT_SNAPSHOT` welfare variant, writing the new `welfare_score` and setting `welfare_score_variant` to `AUDIT_SNAPSHOT`. A specimen whose 14-day window holds no qualifying record yields no value and is passed over entirely: its existing `welfare_score` and `welfare_score_variant` survive the audit unchanged, so a long-neglected animal can still be carrying a stale `ROLLING` figure into step two. Only specimens whose stored score or variant actually moved have their `version` advanced. **Step two**, which runs strictly after step one and reads the values step one has just written, computes `domain_subscores`, `blocking_finding_count` and `readiness_score`. It stamps `opened_by_user_id` and `started_at`.

The five domain subscores are each clamped into 0 to 100 and rounded half up to two decimals. `LIFE_SUPPORT` is the mean over every plant of that plant's `water_quality_index` reduced by 10.0 when its `redundancy_overdue` is true and by a further 25.0 when its `alarm_state` is `OFFLINE`, each plant's figure clamped into 0 to 100 before averaging; with no plants at all it is 100.00. `ANIMAL_HEALTH` is the mean `welfare_score` over specimens whose `disposition_status` is `CLEARED` or `ON_EXHIBIT` and whose `welfare_score` is non-null; with no such specimens it is 100.00. `RECORDS` is 100 reduced by 5.0 for each specimen whose `disposition_status` is `QUARANTINE`, `CLEARED` or `ON_EXHIBIT` and which has no husbandry records at all. `PERMITS` is 100 reduced by 20.0 for each acquisition request whose `status` is `CLEARED` and whose `permit_required` is true but which has no `ISSUED` permit. `PUBLIC_SAFETY` is 100 reduced by 15.0 for each interaction session whose `status` is `SUSPENDED` and by a further 25.0 for each exhibit whose `interaction_program_enabled` is false **and** which has at least one session — an exhibit with the programme disabled but no sessions ever booked costs nothing.

`readiness_score` is then built in two further steps. The already-rounded subscores are weighted 0.30 for `LIFE_SUPPORT`, 0.25 for `ANIMAL_HEALTH`, 0.20 for `RECORDS`, 0.15 for `PERMITS` and 0.10 for `PUBLIC_SAFETY`, and the weighted sum is rounded half up to two decimals. `blocking_finding_count` is the number of plants whose `alarm_state` is `CRITICAL` or `OFFLINE`, plus the number of exhibits whose `posture` is `CLOSED`, plus the number of specimens whose `health_status` is `DECEASED_PENDING_NECROPSY`. `readiness_score` is the greater of 0 and the rounded weighted sum reduced by 4.0 for each blocking finding, rounded half up to two decimals again. Rounding the subscores, then the weighted sum, then the final figure is three distinct roundings, and collapsing them gives a different answer.

`CLOSE` moves an `IN_PROGRESS` audit to one of three determinations and requires the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from `opened_by_user_id`, else `ERR_SOD_VIOLATION`. The determination is not a choice: the audit becomes the terminal `PASSED` when `readiness_score` is at least 85.00 **and** `blocking_finding_count` is 0; it becomes `CONDITIONAL` when `readiness_score` is at least 70.00 but the pass test failed, whether because the score is below 85.00 or because a blocking finding exists; and it becomes the terminal `FAILED` when `readiness_score` is below 70.00. `closed_by_user_id`, `closed_at` and `determination_note` are stamped.

`REOPEN` is the one route back, and it exists only from `CONDITIONAL`: it returns the audit to `IN_PROGRESS`, clears `closed_by_user_id` and `closed_at` but deliberately leaves the previous round's `determination_note` standing, sets `opened_by_user_id` to the reopening director — which re-anchors the segregation-of-duties test for the next `CLOSE` — and `started_at` to the current clock, and re-runs both scoring steps against the estate as it now stands — deliberately ignoring `scheduled_for`, since the inspection window has already opened. `PASSED` and `FAILED` admit no reopening. `CANCEL` moves a `SCHEDULED` audit to the terminal `CANCELLED`.

Audits may be listed with the `status` and `scope` filters and read singly, showing `domain_subscores` and `readiness_score`.
