# Cooperative Purchasing Contract Administration API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Public agencies rarely have the staff, the calendar, or the legal appetite to run their own competitive solicitation for every commodity they buy. A cooperative purchasing organization solves that by having one **lead agency** conduct a single competitive solicitation whose documentation is strong enough to satisfy *other* members' procurement statutes, awarding a nationwide master contract to the winning supplier, and then letting hundreds of member agencies "piggyback" on that award. The commercial engine of the arrangement is an administrative fee: the awarded supplier remits a small percentage of everything members buy back to the cooperative, and the cooperative in exchange polices pricing, publishes the price sheet, and keeps the audit trail.

This API is the system of record for that arrangement. It has to hold four constituencies to four different standards at once. The lead agency's procurement officer needs a defensible competitive record — posted notice, responsive/non-responsive determinations, weighted scoring, a duty-segregated award, and an assembled documentation package a member's external auditor can reconstruct years later. The cooperative's own administrators need a membership desk that verifies eligibility across government and nonprofit categories, a piggyback authority opinion that is scored against the *member's own* state law rather than the lead agency's, and a fee ledger that never silently drifts. Awarded suppliers need to report usage by period, remit the fee, propose renewals and price adjustments inside contractual caps, and appeal complaints that would otherwise cost them their qualification. Member agencies need accurate net pricing, a spend ceiling they cannot blow through, and a way to complain.

The hardest requirements in this document are the ones where those four views disagree. A usage report's administrative fee is computed twice — once in bulk by the supplier at submission, once per order by the cooperative's audit desk at reconciliation — and the signed difference between the two is a real number that must be applied to two ledgers. A member's discount tier is keyed on cumulative spend, and cumulative spend is the running sum of the very orders the tier prices, so the evaluation order has to be pinned or the arithmetic is undefined. Two states looking at the identical lead-agency solicitation can reach different piggyback determinations because their statutes score the same facts differently. Every one of those behaviours is specified below; none of them is left to the implementer's judgement.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

This section defines the system-wide contracts: authentication, response shape, pagination, resource identity, precision, concurrency, validation precedence, and the interval arithmetic that the rest of the document relies on.

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to the API carries two mandatory headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, declaring the capacity in which that principal is acting. Five roles exist and their spelling is exact: `CoopAdministrator`, `LeadAgencyOfficer`, `SupplierRepresentative`, `MemberRepresentative`, and `MemberAuditor`. If either header is absent or empty, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of those five strings, the request is rejected with `ERR_MISSING_AUTH_HEADERS` (HTTP 401). If both headers are well formed but the role is not among the roles the target endpoint admits, the rejection is `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403).

`CoopAdministrator` is the cooperative purchasing organization's own staff: they run the membership desk, register state law configuration and suppliers, determine and void piggyback authority, move contract execution status, run the delinquency sweep, review and reconcile usage reports, decide amendments, adjudicate complaints, and drive the logical clock. `LeadAgencyOfficer` is the procurement officer of the lead agency conducting the solicitation on the cooperative's behalf: they create and drive solicitations, evaluate responses, award and publish the master contract price sheet, and assemble and certify the documentation package. `SupplierRepresentative` acts for a single bidding or awarded supplier. `MemberRepresentative` acts for a single participating agency. `MemberAuditor` is strictly read-only: it holds no write operation anywhere in the API, and every `GET` endpoint in the system admits it — including the responses list of a solicitation, the usage-report list of a contract, and the price-quote computation.

Identity binding differs by role, and it determines how ownership is checked:

- When `X-User-Role` is `SupplierRepresentative`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Supplier`. Ownership checks compare `X-User-ID` against the `supplier_id` reachable from the target resource — the `supplier_id` on a `SolicitationResponse`, the `supplier_id` of the `MasterContract` behind a `UsageReport`, `ContractAmendment` or appealed `MemberComplaint`, or the `supplier_id` supplied in the request body.
- When `X-User-Role` is `MemberRepresentative`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `MemberAgency`. Ownership checks compare `X-User-ID` against the `member_agency_id` reachable from the target resource — the `member_agency_id` on a `ContractUsage` or `MemberComplaint`, or the `member_agency_id` supplied in the request body.
- When `X-User-Role` is `CoopAdministrator`, `LeadAgencyOfficer` or `MemberAuditor`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it where a transition says so — as `recommended_by_user_id`, `awarded_by_user_id` and `certified_by_user_id` on a `Solicitation`, as `verified_by_user_id` on a `MemberAgency`, as `decided_by_user_id` on a `ContractAmendment`, and as `investigator_user_id` and `adjudicator_user_id` on a `MemberComplaint` — and it compares two such recorded values against each other for duty segregation, but it never compares an opaque `X-User-ID` to any entity's `id`.

A scope violation by an entity-backed role — a supplier representative writing against another supplier's resource, or a member representative writing against another member's — is rejected with `ERR_ACTOR_SCOPE_MISMATCH` (HTTP 403). Read endpoints are not scope-checked: any role listed on a `GET` endpoint may read any row it returns.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

List endpoints paginate through two query parameters. `limit` is the maximum number of rows returned; it defaults to 10, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` is the number of rows skipped; it defaults to 0 and must not be negative. A `limit` outside 1 through 100, or a negative `offset`, is a request-shape failure and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

Unless a specific endpoint says otherwise, every list endpoint returns its rows sorted by `created_at` ascending. The `meta` object of a list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more`.

Most list endpoints also accept optional equality filters drawn from the fields of the entity they return, and the filters compose. The available filter names are `state_code`, `piggyback_doctrine`, `lead_agency_state_code`, `status`, `audit_package_status`, `home_state_code`, `qualification_status`, `performance_tier`, `solicitation_id`, `supplier_id`, `contract_id`, `compliance_posture`, `origin`, `fee_status`, `member_category`, `registration_status`, `determination`, `line_item_id`, `member_agency_id`, `piggyback_analysis_id`, `usage_report_id`, `target_line_item_id`, `amendment_type`, `severity` and `related_usage_id`; §4 records which of them each endpoint honours.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and the Logical Clock

Every resource the API mints carries an `id`: a server-generated UUID with no type prefix and no client control. Every resource also carries `created_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits recording when the row was created.

Mutable resources — `Solicitation`, `Supplier`, `SolicitationResponse`, `MasterContract`, `ContractLineItem`, `MemberAgency`, `PiggybackAnalysis`, `ContractUsage`, `UsageReport`, `ContractAmendment` and `MemberComplaint` — additionally carry `updated_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp refreshed on every change to the row, and `version`, an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation. A change to a *derived* field counts as a mutation: when a member's `committed_spend_cents` moves, when a contract's `fee_outstanding_cents` is recomputed, or when a supplier's `performance_tier` is rewritten by a cascade, that row's `updated_at` is refreshed and its `version` increments.

`StateLawRule` is the one exception. A state law configuration row is immutable after creation: it carries only `id` and `created_at`. It has no `updated_at` field and no `version` field, there is no update endpoint for it, and it therefore never participates in optimistic concurrency.

All deadline, expiry and window arithmetic in this system is evaluated against a **logical clock**, never against real wall-clock time. The clock's startup value is exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and until an administrator moves it, every comparison — `response_due_at`, `posted_at`, `ordered_at`, `determined_at`, `expires_on`, `fee_due_at`, `appeal_deadline_at`, the price-stability window, the reporting-period elapse test — resolves against that fixed instant. An administrator-only test-driving capability sets the clock to a supplied `to_iso8601` instant and echoes back the new `current_utc`; every subsequent logical-clock read uses it. The server must not fall back to system time under any circumstance.

### 2.5. Segregation of Duties Inside Operator Roles

Two of the cooperative's control objectives are enforced *within* a role rather than between roles, by comparing `X-User-ID` values recorded on the resource:

- The officer who recommends an award may not sign it. The `RECOMMEND_AWARD` action stamps `recommended_by_user_id`; the later `AWARD` action requires that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from that stored value.
- The administrator who opens a complaint investigation may not adjudicate it, and the administrator who adjudicates it may not decide its appeal. `OPEN_INVESTIGATION` stamps `investigator_user_id`; `SUBSTANTIATE` and `DISMISS` require that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from `investigator_user_id` and stamp `adjudicator_user_id`; `OVERTURN` and `UPHOLD` require that the acting `X-User-ID` differ from `adjudicator_user_id`.

Any of those collisions is rejected with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403).

### 2.6. Optimistic Concurrency on Analysis and Amendment Creation

Exactly two write endpoints require the client to pin the version of a parent row it read before writing. Both freeze figures copied off that parent, so writing against a parent the client has not observed would silently produce an opinion or a ceiling computed from facts that no longer hold.

- `POST /coop/piggyback-analyses` requires a non-optional `expected_member_agency_version` field carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `MemberAgency`. If it does not equal the member's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_MEMBER_AGENCY_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Opening a fresh analysis for a `(member_agency_id, contract_id)` pair is itself a legitimate operation that supersedes the prior opinion, so the check exists purely to guarantee that the eligibility facts the analysis will freeze — `registration_status`, `member_category`, `state_code` — are the ones the client actually read.
- `POST /coop/contract-amendments` requires a non-optional `expected_contract_version` field carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `MasterContract`. If it does not equal the contract's current `version`, the proposal is rejected with `ERR_STALE_CONTRACT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Proposing an amendment is legal at any point while the contract is `ACTIVE`, and it supersedes the supplier's own prior proposal of the same `amendment_type`; the check exists because the ceiling arithmetic reads `price_escalation_cap_pct`, `current_term_end` and `renewals_used` straight off the contract row the client believed it was amending.

No other endpoint in the API accepts a version field, and no other endpoint performs a version check. Version pinning is not optional on those two endpoints: omitting the field is a request-shape failure (`ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`).

### 2.7. Money, Basis Points, Percentages and Rounding

Every monetary amount in this system is an integer number of **cents**. No money field is ever a decimal. Field names ending in `_cents` are integers, and the API returns them as integers.

The administrative fee rate is expressed in **basis points** (hundredths of one percent) in `admin_fee_rate_bps` and `effective_fee_rate_bps`; a rate of 250 basis points is two and a half percent, and converting a basis-point rate to a fee means multiplying by the base and dividing by 10,000. Discount ladders, escalation caps, add-on rates and scope-overlap shares are expressed as **fractions** with four decimal places — `discount_pct`, `rate_pct`, `price_escalation_cap_pct`, `index_change_pct` and `expansion_scope_overlap_pct` all live on a 0.0000-to-1.0000 scale, so 0.0300 is three percent. Scores and evaluation weights are expressed as **percentages** with two decimal places on a 0.00-to-100.00 scale: `technical_weight_pct`, `price_weight_pct`, `technical_score`, `price_score`, `composite_score`, `authority_score`, `performance_score`, `audit_completeness_pct` and `variance_pct`.

One rounding method is used everywhere: **round half up**. Cent amounts round half up to zero decimal places. Scores, percentages and variance figures round half up to exactly two decimal places. Rates in basis points round half up to whole basis points.

Where a calculation rounds more than once, the order of rounding is pinned by the calculation that owns it and is never at the implementer's discretion. Three orderings matter enough to call out here, and each is spelled out again where it is used: an add-on charge is rounded to whole cents *before* its per-code cap is applied and before the per-code charges are summed; a weighted score component is rounded to two decimals *before* the two components are added; and a unit price is rounded to whole cents *before* it is compared against the contractual price floor.

### 2.8. Path Resolution, Natural Keys and Referential Order

When a request path carries an identifier, that identifier is resolved before anything in the body is examined. Where a path carries more than one identifier, they are resolved strictly left to right, and the first one that does not resolve produces its own 404 — a request against a line item of a non-existent contract reports `ERR_CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND`, never `ERR_LINE_ITEM_NOT_FOUND`. Each entity has its own not-found code: `ERR_SOLICITATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SUPPLIER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_RESPONSE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LINE_ITEM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ANALYSIS_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_USAGE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REPORT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AMENDMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_STATE_LAW_RULE_NOT_FOUND`, all HTTP 404.

Identifiers supplied in a request body are resolved next, in the order the fields are declared on the receiving entity, and they are resolved *before* any cross-reference consistency rule runs. A usage naming a line item that does not exist is therefore a 404, not a 422 mismatch. `ERR_LINE_ITEM_NOT_FOUND` covers both `line_item_id` and `target_line_item_id`; `ERR_USAGE_NOT_FOUND` covers both a path usage id and `related_usage_id`.

Some references in this system are **natural keys**, not UUIDs. A state law configuration row is addressed by its two-letter uppercase `state_code`, and three other fields resolve to it by exact string match rather than by foreign key: a solicitation's `lead_agency_state_code`, a supplier's `home_state_code` and a member agency's `state_code`. When an operation must resolve one of those natural keys and no matching configuration row exists, the rejection is `ERR_STATE_LAW_RULE_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404). The four operations that must resolve one are solicitation posting (on `lead_agency_state_code`), supplier qualification (on `home_state_code`), member verification (on `state_code`) and piggyback determination (on the member's `state_code`).

### 2.9. Validation Precedence

Many endpoints carry several independent checks, and which error a caller sees when more than one would fail is part of the contract, not an implementation detail. All checks in the system are evaluated in the following fixed order; the first failing check produces the response and no later check runs.

1. Auth headers present and `X-User-Role` recognised — `ERR_MISSING_AUTH_HEADERS`, 401, on every endpoint.
2. The endpoint's role list contains `X-User-Role` — `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`, 403, on every endpoint.
3. Request body and query string conform to the endpoint's input schema: no unknown fields, no missing required fields, correct scalar types, enum values (including the endpoint's `action` set) inside their declared sets, `limit` within 1 to 100, `offset` at least 0, `quantity` at least 1, `amount_cents` at least 0, `technical_score` within 0.00 to 100.00, `technical_weight_pct` and `price_weight_pct` summing to exactly 100, `base_term_start` strictly before `base_term_end`, `period_start` at or before `period_end`, and `discount_tiers` and `permitted_add_ons` well formed per their own constraints — `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, 400, on every endpoint.
4. Every identifier in the path exists, validated left to right — the matching not-found code, 404, on every endpoint with a path parameter.
5. Every foreign key in the request body resolves, validated in entity field order — the matching not-found code, 404, on `POST /coop/solicitation-responses`, `POST /coop/master-contracts`, `POST /coop/master-contracts/{id}/line-items`, `POST /coop/piggyback-analyses`, `POST /coop/contract-usages`, `POST /coop/usage-reports`, `POST /coop/contract-amendments`, `POST /coop/complaints` and `GET /coop/master-contracts/{id}/price-quote`.
6. The required state law configuration row exists — `ERR_STATE_LAW_RULE_NOT_FOUND`, 404, on `POST /coop/solicitations/{id}/lifecycle`, `POST /coop/suppliers/{id}/qualification`, `POST /coop/member-agencies/{id}/registration` and `POST /coop/piggyback-analyses/{id}/determination`.
7. Entity-backed actor scope — `ERR_ACTOR_SCOPE_MISMATCH`, 403, on every write endpoint whose role list contains `SupplierRepresentative` or `MemberRepresentative`: `POST /coop/solicitation-responses`, `POST /coop/solicitation-responses/{id}/submission`, `POST /coop/usage-reports`, `POST /coop/usage-reports/{id}/submission`, `POST /coop/usage-reports/{id}/fee-remittance`, `POST /coop/contract-amendments`, `POST /coop/contract-amendments/{id}/withdrawal`, `POST /coop/complaints/{id}/appeal`, `POST /coop/piggyback-analyses`, `POST /coop/contract-usages`, `POST /coop/contract-usages/{id}/disposition` and `POST /coop/complaints`. Read endpoints are never scope-checked.
8. Segregation of duties — `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, 403, on `POST /coop/solicitations/{id}/lifecycle`, `POST /coop/complaints/{id}/investigation`, `POST /coop/complaints/{id}/decision` and `POST /coop/complaints/{id}/appeal-decision`.
9. Optimistic concurrency — `ERR_STALE_MEMBER_AGENCY_VERSION` or `ERR_STALE_CONTRACT_VERSION`, 412, on `POST /coop/piggyback-analyses` and `POST /coop/contract-amendments` respectively.
10. The `MasterContract` the operation depends on is `ACTIVE` — `ERR_CONTRACT_NOT_ACTIVE`, 409, on `POST /coop/contract-usages`, `POST /coop/piggyback-analyses/{id}/determination`, `POST /coop/contract-amendments` and `POST /coop/contract-amendments/{id}/decision`. No other operation is gated on contract status: opening a usage report and filing a complaint are deliberately permitted against a non-`ACTIVE` contract.
11. The `ContractAmendment` is `PROPOSED` — `ERR_AMENDMENT_NOT_PROPOSED`, 409, on `POST /coop/contract-amendments/{id}/decision` and `POST /coop/contract-amendments/{id}/withdrawal`.
12. The `MemberComplaint` is in a from-state permitting the requested action — `ERR_COMPLAINT_INVALID_STATE`, 409, on `POST /coop/complaints/{id}/investigation`, `POST /coop/complaints/{id}/decision`, `POST /coop/complaints/{id}/appeal` and `POST /coop/complaints/{id}/appeal-decision`.
13. The `Solicitation` is in a from-state permitting the requested lifecycle or audit-package action, and responses may only be created while its `status` is `POSTED` — `ERR_SOLICITATION_INVALID_STATE`, `ERR_SOLICITATION_CLOSED_FOR_RESPONSES` or `ERR_AUDIT_PACKAGE_NOT_ASSEMBLED`, 409, on `POST /coop/solicitations/{id}/lifecycle`, `POST /coop/solicitations/{id}/audit-package` and `POST /coop/solicitation-responses`.

16. The `ContractUsage` `status` permits the requested disposition — `ERR_USAGE_INVALID_STATE`, 409, on `POST /coop/contract-usages/{id}/disposition`.

18. The `MemberAgency` is `ACTIVE` where the operation demands it, or is in a from-state permitting the requested registration action — `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_ACTIVE` or `ERR_MEMBER_INVALID_STATE`, 409, on `POST /coop/contract-usages`, `POST /coop/piggyback-analyses/{id}/determination` and `POST /coop/member-agencies/{id}/registration`. 47. A usage's `ordered_at` falls inside the contract's term — `ERR_OUTSIDE_CONTRACT_TERM`, 409, on `POST /coop/contract-usages`.
48. An appeal is filed inside the appeal window — `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`, 409, on `POST /coop/complaints/{id}/appeal`.
49. A substantiated complaint is closed only after the appeal window elapses — `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_OPEN`, 409, on `POST /coop/complaints/{id}/decision`.
50. Award recommendation requires enough responsive responses — `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_RESPONSIVE_RESPONSES`, 422, on `POST /coop/solicitations/{id}/lifecycle`.
51. Technical scores must be present — `ERR_TECHNICAL_SCORE_MISSING`, 422, on `POST /coop/solicitations/{id}/lifecycle` and `POST /coop/solicitation-responses/{id}/review`.
52. Information-request rounds are bounded — `ERR_INFO_REQUEST_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`, 409, on `POST /coop/solicitation-responses/{id}/review`.
53. Document-request rounds are bounded — `ERR_DOCS_REQUEST_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`, 409, on `POST /coop/member-agencies/{id}/registration`.
54. Renewals are bounded by the contract's allowance — `ERR_RENEWAL_LIMIT_REACHED`, 409, on `POST /coop/contract-amendments/{id}/decision`.
55. A product line expansion stays inside the solicited scope — `ERR_EXPANSION_OUT_OF_SCOPE`, 422, on `POST /coop/contract-amendments` and `POST /coop/contract-amendments/{id}/decision`.
56. Catalogue numbers are unique within a contract — `ERR_DUPLICATE_SKU`, 409, on `POST /coop/master-contracts/{id}/line-items` and `POST /coop/contract-amendments/{id}/decision`.
57. Global natural-key uniqueness — `ERR_DUPLICATE_STATE_LAW_RULE`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_SOLICITATION_NUMBER`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_CONTRACT_NUMBER`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_SUPPLIER_NAME` or `ERR_DUPLICATE_MEMBER_NAME`, 409, on `POST /coop/state-law-rules`, `POST /coop/solicitations`, `POST /coop/master-contracts`, `POST /coop/suppliers` and `POST /coop/member-agencies` respectively.
58. A price adjustment stays within its ceiling and above its floor — `ERR_PRICE_ESCALATION_EXCEEDS_CAP` or `ERR_PRICE_BELOW_FLOOR`, 422, on `POST /coop/contract-amendments` and `POST /coop/contract-amendments/{id}/decision`.
59. Execution requires at least one active price-sheet line — `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_LINE_ITEMS`, 409, on `POST /coop/master-contracts/{id}/award-status`.
60. A remittance settles the report exactly — `ERR_REMITTANCE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`, 422, on `POST /coop/usage-reports/{id}/fee-remittance`.
61. A member's spend ceiling is respected — `ERR_MEMBER_CEILING_EXCEEDED`, 422, on `POST /coop/contract-usages`.
62. The fee ledger stays internally consistent — `ERR_FEE_LEDGER_INCONSISTENT`, 409, on `POST /coop/usage-reports/{id}/fee-remittance` and `POST /coop/usage-reports/{id}/review`.

The practical consequence worth internalising: a supplier's own scope error is reported before any state gate, a state gate is reported before any business-safety gate, and a fiscal ceiling is reported last of all. A usage request that is simultaneously outside its member's spend ceiling *and* against a delinquent contract reports the posture failure, not the ceiling failure.

### 2.10. Parallel State Dimensions and Cascade Propagation

Three entities carry two state dimensions that advance independently and must never be conflated. A `Solicitation` has a competitive-process `status` and a documentation `audit_package_status`; a solicitation may sit in `EVALUATION` with an `ASSEMBLED` package. A `MasterContract` has an execution `status` and a remittance-health `compliance_posture`; a contract can be `ACTIVE` with a `DELINQUENT` posture. A `UsageReport` has an acceptance `status` and a remittance `fee_status`; a report can be `ACCEPTED` with `fee_status` `DELINQUENT`. Each dimension has its own transition table and its own gate error, and an action illegal on one dimension is never silently absorbed by the other.

Cascades in this system are **multi-hop and immediate**. When one operation moves a state that another entity's state depends on, the dependent moves within the same request, and the dependent's own side effects fire in turn. Suspending a supplier suspends its active contracts, and each of those suspensions voids that contract's determined authority opinions — two hops from one action. Substantiating a complaint charges performance points, which rewrites the supplier's score and tier, which can suspend the supplier, which suspends its active contracts, which voids their opinions — five hops. Every row touched anywhere in a cascade has its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` incremented, exactly as if it had been written directly.

Cascades are deliberately **not** symmetric. Reinstating a supplier does not reinstate its contracts; each contract must be reinstated explicitly. Reinstating a member or a contract does not revive a voided `PiggybackAnalysis`; the member must request a fresh analysis. Overturning a complaint on appeal restores the supplier's points and score exactly but does not restore its `qualification_status`. Retracting a usage releases the member's committed spend but does not release anything on a member who has since been suspended. Where a reversal is exact, this document says so; where it is not, the asymmetry is the requirement.

### 2.11. Interval Geometry: Closed Periods, Half-Open Terms

Two families of intervals coexist in this system and their endpoint semantics are deliberately opposite. Getting them backwards is the single most common way to produce plausible-looking but wrong behaviour.

**Contract terms are half-open.** A term runs from `base_term_start` inclusive to `current_term_end` exclusive. An order timestamped exactly at `base_term_start` is inside the term; an order timestamped exactly at `current_term_end` is outside it. A renewal occupies the interval from the old `current_term_end` to the new `requested_term_end`, so a renewal that begins exactly where the previous term ended is *adjacent*, not overlapping, and is allowed.

**Reporting periods are closed.** A `UsageReport` period runs from `period_start` inclusive to `period_end` inclusive. An order timestamped exactly at either endpoint is inside the period. Two periods that merely touch at a single instant therefore *do* overlap: a candidate period overlaps an existing one whenever the candidate's start is at or before the existing period's end and the existing period's start is at or before the candidate's end.

Elsewhere, boundary inclusivity is stated at each rule. As a set: the piggyback determination bands, the performance tier bands, the compliance-posture count and amount tests, the member spend ceiling, the price-adjustment ceiling, the price-stability window and the appeal filing deadline are all inclusive at their boundary; a piggyback opinion's expiry, a solicitation's response deadline at posting, and a reporting period's elapse test are all strict.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. State Procurement Law Configuration and the Supplier Roster

Nothing in this system works until the cooperative has told it what the states require. A `CoopAdministrator` registers one procurement-law configuration row per state, keyed by `state_code` — exactly two uppercase letters, unique across the whole configuration table, and a second row for the same code is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_STATE_LAW_RULE`. Each row records `piggyback_doctrine`, which is either `STATUTORY_STRICT` or `RECIPROCITY_LIBERAL` and selects the whole scoring variant used later for that state's members; `min_notice_days`, the minimum number of public-notice days the state demands between posting and the response deadline, at least 0 and at most 90; `requires_lead_agency_in_state`, true when the state only recognises solicitations whose lead agency sits inside the member's own state; `allows_nonprofit_participation`, true when the state lets a member whose category is `NONPROFIT_501C3` piggyback at all; `max_contract_term_months`, the longest total term the state recognises in whole months, at least 12 and at most 120; and `requires_local_solicitation_waiver`, true when a member of that state must have recorded a local governing-body waiver reference before it may piggyback. A `RECIPROCITY_LIBERAL` state additionally lists `reciprocity_state_codes`, an array of two-letter uppercase codes whose lead-agency solicitations it accepts without penalty; the array defaults to empty, must not contain the row's own `state_code`, and is read only when the doctrine is `RECIPROCITY_LIBERAL`. Configuration rows are immutable and unversioned once written.

Suppliers enter the roster next. An administrator registers one with a `legal_name` — 1 to 200 characters, globally unique, with a collision rejected as `ERR_DUPLICATE_SUPPLIER_NAME` — and a `home_state_code` naming the state of its principal place of business. A newly registered supplier starts `UNQUALIFIED` with a pristine record: `performance_score` 100.00, `deduction_points_total` 0.00, `performance_tier` `PREFERRED`, `substantiated_complaint_count` 0, `delinquent_report_count` 0, and `fee_assessed_cents_total` and `fee_remitted_cents_total` both 0.

Cooperative standing then moves through four values — `UNQUALIFIED`, `QUALIFIED`, `SUSPENDED` and `DEBARRED`, with `DEBARRED` terminal — driven by an administrator's `QUALIFY`, `SUSPEND`, `REINSTATE` and `DEBAR` actions on the supplier's qualification dimension. `QUALIFY` moves `UNQUALIFIED → QUALIFIED` and requires that a configuration row exist for the supplier's `home_state_code`; from that point the supplier may be named on responses. `SUSPEND` moves `QUALIFIED → SUSPENDED` and cascades two hops: every `MasterContract` of this supplier whose `status` is `ACTIVE` becomes `SUSPENDED`, and each of those contract suspensions in turn sets every `PiggybackAnalysis` on that contract whose `status` is `DETERMINED` to `VOIDED` with `voided_reason` `CONTRACT_SUSPENDED`. `REINSTATE` moves `SUSPENDED → QUALIFIED` and is refused while `performance_tier` is `PROBATION`; it deliberately leaves the contracts `SUSPENDED` — each must be reinstated explicitly on its own execution dimension — and it does not revive the voided opinions. `DEBAR` is reachable from either `QUALIFIED` or `SUSPENDED` and cascades harder: every `MasterContract` of the supplier whose `status` is `PENDING_EXECUTION`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` becomes `TERMINATED`, and each of those terminations in turn withdraws that contract's line items and voids that contract's `DETERMINED` opinions with `voided_reason` `CONTRACT_TERMINATED`. An action that is not a declared transition out of the supplier's current standing is `ERR_SUPPLIER_INVALID_STATE`, and a `REINSTATE` blocked by a `PROBATION` tier carries the same code.

The system also suspends a supplier by itself: whenever a performance cascade rewrites `performance_tier` to `PROBATION`, the same `QUALIFIED → SUSPENDED` transition and both of its hops fire without any administrator request.

### 3.2. The Lead-Agency Solicitation: Posting, Evaluation, Award, and the Audit File

A `LeadAgencyOfficer` opens a solicitation with a `title` of 1 to 200 characters, a `solicitation_number` of 1 to 40 characters that must be unique across all solicitations (a collision is `ERR_DUPLICATE_SOLICITATION_NUMBER`), a `lead_agency_name`, a `lead_agency_state_code`, a `response_due_at` deadline, and the two evaluation weights `technical_weight_pct` and `price_weight_pct` — each a percentage with two decimals between 0 and 100, and together summing to exactly 100. The officer may file the archived `specification_document_ref` and `method_justification_ref` at creation or leave them null. A new solicitation lands in `status` `DRAFT` and `audit_package_status` `NOT_ASSEMBLED`, with `addenda_count` and `responsive_response_count` both 0 and `posted_at`, `notice_days` and `audit_completeness_pct` all null.

The competitive dimension runs `DRAFT`, `POSTED`, `EVALUATION`, `AWARD_PENDING` and then one of the three terminal values `AWARDED`, `FAILED` or `CANCELLED`, driven by an officer's `POST_NOTICE`, `OPEN_EVALUATION`, `RECOMMEND_AWARD`, `AWARD`, `FAIL` and `CANCEL` actions, each of which may carry an optional `note`.

`POST_NOTICE` moves `DRAFT → POSTED`. It requires a configuration row for `lead_agency_state_code`, a `response_due_at` strictly after the logical clock (otherwise `ERR_RESPONSE_DUE_IN_PAST`), and weights that still sum to exactly 100. On success it sets `posted_at` to the current logical instant, freezes `notice_days` as the whole number of 86,400-second days between `posted_at` and `response_due_at` — that is, the elapsed seconds divided by 86,400 and rounded *down* to a whole number — and sets `addenda_count` to 0. Note carefully that a notice period shorter than the state's `min_notice_days` does **not** block posting: the shortfall is priced later as a deduction inside a member's authority analysis. `notice_days` never changes again once frozen.

`OPEN_EVALUATION` moves `POSTED → EVALUATION` and requires the logical clock to have reached or passed `response_due_at`; attempting it earlier is `ERR_RESPONSE_WINDOW_OPEN`. From that moment no further response may be filed on this solicitation, which surfaces as `ERR_SOLICITATION_CLOSED_FOR_RESPONSES`.

While a solicitation is `POSTED`, a `SupplierRepresentative` files a response for its own supplier, supplying `solicitation_id`, `supplier_id`, a `proposed_annual_value_cents` of at least 1 representing its nationwide annual contract value, and optionally a `responsiveness_note`. The acting supplier must be `QUALIFIED` (`ERR_SUPPLIER_NOT_QUALIFIED`), and the same supplier may not hold two live responses on one solicitation: a prior response in any status other than `WITHDRAWN` or `NON_RESPONSIVE` blocks the new one with `ERR_DUPLICATE_RESPONSE`. Because responses may only be created while the solicitation is `POSTED` and non-responsiveness is only ever recorded during `EVALUATION`, in practice only a withdrawal frees the pair. A new response starts in `SUBMITTED` with `info_request_rounds` 0 and every score null.

The responsiveness dimension of a response cycles. The officer's `REQUEST_INFO` moves `SUBMITTED → INFO_REQUESTED`, requires the parent solicitation to be in `EVALUATION` and `info_request_rounds` to be strictly below two, and stores the `responsiveness_note`; exhausting the allowance is `ERR_INFO_REQUEST_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`. The supplier's own `RESUBMIT` closes the cycle `INFO_REQUESTED → SUBMITTED`, again requires the solicitation to be in `EVALUATION`, requires the acting `X-User-ID` to be the response's own `supplier_id`, and increments `info_request_rounds` by 1 — so the `SUBMITTED → INFO_REQUESTED → SUBMITTED` loop can run at most twice. `MARK_RESPONSIVE` moves `SUBMITTED → RESPONSIVE`, requires the solicitation to be in `EVALUATION` and the supplier to be `QUALIFIED`, and requires a `technical_score` in the request body between 0.00 and 100.00; a missing or out-of-range score is `ERR_TECHNICAL_SCORE_MISSING`. It stores `technical_score` and increments the solicitation's `responsive_response_count` by 1. `MARK_NON_RESPONSIVE` is reachable from `SUBMITTED`, from `INFO_REQUESTED` and from `RESPONSIVE`, always requires the solicitation to be in `EVALUATION`, and stores the `responsiveness_note`; when it is applied to a response that was already `RESPONSIVE` it additionally decrements `responsive_response_count` by 1 and clears `technical_score`, `price_score`, `composite_score` and `award_rank` to null. The supplier's `WITHDRAW` is reachable from `SUBMITTED` and from `INFO_REQUESTED`, requires the acting `X-User-ID` to be the response's `supplier_id`, and requires the solicitation to be either `POSTED` or `EVALUATION`; no score is ever written on a `WITHDRAWN` response. `NON_RESPONSIVE` and `WITHDRAWN` are terminal. Any action that is not a declared transition out of the response's current status is `ERR_RESPONSE_INVALID_STATE`.

The `EVALUATION` state forks. `RECOMMEND_AWARD` moves `EVALUATION → AWARD_PENDING` and requires at least two responses in `RESPONSIVE` status — fewer is `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_RESPONSIVE_RESPONSES` — and requires every `RESPONSIVE` response to carry a non-null `technical_score`, failing which it is `ERR_TECHNICAL_SCORE_MISSING`. It then scores the whole responsive field and stamps `recommended_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`. `FAIL` moves `EVALUATION → FAILED` and is available only when the responsive count is strictly below two; it sets every response whose status is `SUBMITTED` or `INFO_REQUESTED` to `NON_RESPONSIVE`, and — this is the difference from the sibling branch — writes no `price_score`, no `composite_score` and no `award_rank` on any response at all. Both branches rejoin on the documentation dimension: a successful action on either moves `audit_package_status` from `ASSEMBLED` or `CERTIFIED` to `STALE`.

**Scoring the responsive field.** Take every response on this solicitation whose status is `RESPONSIVE`; responses in `SUBMITTED`, `INFO_REQUESTED`, `NON_RESPONSIVE` or `WITHDRAWN` are excluded entirely and keep their `price_score`, `composite_score` and `award_rank` null. Find the smallest `proposed_annual_value_cents` in that responsive set. Then, walking the set in ascending `created_at` order, give each response a `price_score` of one hundred multiplied by that smallest proposed value and divided by this response's own `proposed_annual_value_cents`, rounded half up to two decimals — so the cheapest bid scores 100.00 and a bid twice as expensive scores 50.00. Compute a weighted technical component as `technical_score` multiplied by `technical_weight_pct` and divided by 100, rounded half up to two decimals; compute a weighted price component as `price_score` multiplied by `price_weight_pct` and divided by 100, rounded half up to two decimals. `composite_score` is the sum of those two *already-rounded* components, rounded half up to two decimals. Rounding each component first and adding afterwards is the rule; adding the two unrounded products and rounding once at the end is explicitly not the rule and differs by one hundredth whenever both products carry a third decimal digit that rounds up together. Finally rank the same responsive set by `composite_score` descending, breaking ties by `created_at` ascending and never by `id`, and write `award_rank` as 1 through N.

`AWARD` moves `AWARD_PENDING → AWARDED`. It requires the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from the stored `recommended_by_user_id` and it requires the supplier owning the rank-1 responsive response to still be `QUALIFIED`. On success it stamps `awarded_by_user_id` and unlocks master-contract creation for that rank-1 supplier's `supplier_id`.

`CANCEL` is reachable from `DRAFT`, from `POSTED` and from `EVALUATION`, and in all three cases requires that no master contract already reference this solicitation. From `DRAFT` there are no responses to cascade to. From `POSTED` and from `EVALUATION` every response whose status is `SUBMITTED` or `INFO_REQUESTED` becomes `WITHDRAWN`; cancelling from `EVALUATION` additionally leaves responses that already reached `RESPONSIVE` untouched, keeping their status and their scores for the audit file. Any lifecycle action that is not a declared transition out of the current `status` is `ERR_SOLICITATION_INVALID_STATE`.

**The documentation package.** Members cite this solicitation in their own audit files, so the officer maintains a second, independent dimension whose values are `NOT_ASSEMBLED`, `ASSEMBLED`, `CERTIFIED` and `STALE`, driven by `ASSEMBLE` and `CERTIFY` actions that may carry an `amendment_log_document_ref`. `ASSEMBLE` is reachable from `NOT_ASSEMBLED`, from `ASSEMBLED` (re-assembly is legal and idempotent in shape though not in value — the recomputed percentage may differ) and from `STALE`; in all three cases it requires `posted_at` to be non-null. When an `amendment_log_document_ref` is supplied it is stored *first*, before the completeness recompute reads it.

Assembly recomputes three fields. The required list is a fixed eight-code base, in exactly this order: `NOTICE_OF_SOLICITATION`, `SPECIFICATION_PACKAGE`, `PROCUREMENT_METHOD_JUSTIFICATION`, `ADDENDA_LOG`, `RESPONSE_TABULATION`, `EVALUATION_SCORESHEET`, `AWARD_RECOMMENDATION`, `CONTRACT_EXECUTION`. If any amendment with status `APPROVED` exists on a master contract of this solicitation, `AMENDMENT_LOG` is appended, taking the denominator to nine. `audit_required_doc_codes` holds that list. `audit_present_doc_codes` holds the subset whose evidence exists, tested code by code in the same order: `NOTICE_OF_SOLICITATION` is present when `posted_at` is non-null; `SPECIFICATION_PACKAGE` when `specification_document_ref` is non-null; `PROCUREMENT_METHOD_JUSTIFICATION` when `method_justification_ref` is non-null; `ADDENDA_LOG` whenever `posted_at` is non-null, because a solicitation that issued no addenda still has an addenda log and an `addenda_count` of 0 is a legitimate value that does not make the code absent; `RESPONSE_TABULATION` when `responsive_response_count` is at least 1; `EVALUATION_SCORESHEET` when `responsive_response_count` is at least 1 *and* every `RESPONSIVE` response carries a non-null `technical_score`; `AWARD_RECOMMENDATION` when `status` is `AWARD_PENDING` or `AWARDED`; `CONTRACT_EXECUTION` when a master contract on this solicitation exists whose status is `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, `EXPIRED` or `TERMINATED`; and `AMENDMENT_LOG` when `amendment_log_document_ref` is non-null. `audit_completeness_pct` is one hundred multiplied by the number of present codes and divided by the number of required codes, rounded half up to two decimals. A solicitation with no responsive responses at all therefore loses exactly the four response-dependent codes against an eight-code denominator and lands on 50.00.

`CERTIFY` moves `ASSEMBLED → CERTIFIED`. It requires `audit_completeness_pct` to be exactly 100.00 — anything less is `ERR_AUDIT_PACKAGE_INCOMPLETE` — and it requires the competitive dimension to have reached `AWARDED` or `FAILED`. On success it stamps `certified_at` with the current logical instant and `certified_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`. Requesting certification while the package is `NOT_ASSEMBLED` or `STALE` is `ERR_AUDIT_PACKAGE_NOT_ASSEMBLED`.

Staleness is imposed by the system, not requested. Whenever an amendment on this solicitation's master contract is approved, or whenever the competitive `status` changes, an `ASSEMBLED` package becomes `STALE` and `audit_completeness_pct` is cleared to null so the stale figure cannot be cited; a `CERTIFIED` package likewise becomes `STALE` with `audit_completeness_pct` cleared *and* `certified_at` and `certified_by_user_id` cleared, so certification must be performed again from scratch. Nothing recovers a stale package except a fresh assembly.

### 3.3. Awarding, Executing and Retiring the Master Contract Price Sheet

Once a solicitation is `AWARDED`, the `LeadAgencyOfficer` writes the master contract. The payload names `solicitation_id` and `supplier_id`, a `contract_number` of 1 to 40 characters unique across all contracts (a collision is `ERR_DUPLICATE_CONTRACT_NUMBER`), an `admin_fee_rate_bps` between 1 and 1000 basis points that the supplier will remit to the cooperative on the chargeable base, a `base_term_start` and a `base_term_end` bounding the half-open base term, a `max_renewals` allowance between 0 and 5, a `price_escalation_cap_pct` between 0.0000 and 0.2500 with four decimals capping any single price adjustment as a fraction of the affected line's base price, and the two pricing structures below. `nationwide_pricing_flag` is optional and defaults to true, asserting that the awarded price sheet applies in every member state without regional uplift. The named solicitation must be `AWARDED` (`ERR_SOLICITATION_NOT_AWARDED`), and the named supplier must be the one holding `award_rank` 1 among that solicitation's `RESPONSIVE` responses — any other supplier, even on a properly awarded solicitation, is `ERR_AWARD_NOT_HIGHEST_RANKED`. A new contract starts in `status` `PENDING_EXECUTION` with `compliance_posture` `COMPLIANT`, `current_term_end` initialised to `base_term_end`, `renewals_used` 0, and every ledger total — `usage_chargeable_cents_total`, `fee_assessed_cents_total`, `fee_remitted_cents_total`, `fee_outstanding_cents` and `delinquent_report_count` — at 0.

`discount_tiers` is the volume-discount ladder: 1 to 5 entries, each an object of `tier_index`, `min_annual_spend_cents` and `discount_pct`. `tier_index` ascends from 0, `min_annual_spend_cents` ascends strictly, the entry at `tier_index` 0 must carry a `min_annual_spend_cents` of 0, and each `discount_pct` is a four-decimal fraction between 0.0000 and 0.2000. `permitted_add_ons` is the add-on catalogue: 0 to 4 entries, each an object of `code`, `rate_pct` and `cap_cents`. Each `code` is drawn from `FREIGHT`, `INSIDE_DELIVERY`, `TRAINING` and `INSTALLATION` and appears at most once in the array; each `rate_pct` is a four-decimal fraction between 0.0000 and 0.2000; each `cap_cents` is a non-negative integer absolute ceiling on that add-on's charge. A malformed ladder or catalogue — non-ascending thresholds, a duplicated code, an out-of-range fraction — is `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`.

The officer then publishes price-sheet lines against the contract, the contract being taken from the path rather than the body. Each line carries a `sku` of 1 to 40 characters, an `item_description` printed on the price sheet, a `unit_of_measure` that is one of `EACH`, `CASE`, `PALLET` or `HOUR`, a `base_unit_price_cents` of at least 1 giving the awarded nationwide unit price before any member discount, and a `price_floor_cents` of at least 0 that must not exceed `base_unit_price_cents`; a floor above the base price is `ERR_PRICE_BELOW_FLOOR`. A published line starts with `origin` `AWARD`, `status` `ACTIVE`, `price_change_count` 0, `last_price_change_at` null, and `price_effective_from` initialised to the contract's `base_term_start`. The `sku` must be unique within the contract, counting both `AWARD` and `EXPANSION` lines whose `status` is not `WITHDRAWN`; a collision is `ERR_DUPLICATE_SKU`. A line's `status` is either `ACTIVE` or `WITHDRAWN`, and only an `ACTIVE` line may be purchased or repriced (`ERR_LINE_ITEM_NOT_ACTIVE`).

The execution dimension runs `PENDING_EXECUTION`, `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, and the terminal `EXPIRED` and `TERMINATED`, moved by a `CoopAdministrator`'s `EXECUTE`, `SUSPEND`, `REINSTATE`, `EXPIRE` and `TERMINATE` actions, each optionally carrying a `note`. `EXECUTE` moves `PENDING_EXECUTION → ACTIVE` and requires the supplier to be `QUALIFIED`, the solicitation to be `AWARDED`, at least one `ACTIVE` line on the price sheet (otherwise `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_LINE_ITEMS`), and the logical clock to be strictly before `base_term_end`; it sets `price_effective_from` to `base_term_start` on every `ACTIVE` line and sets `current_term_end` to `base_term_end` if that field is still null. `SUSPEND` moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED`, voids every `DETERMINED` opinion on this contract with `voided_reason` `CONTRACT_SUSPENDED`, and blocks new usage. `REINSTATE` moves `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE` and requires `compliance_posture` other than `DELINQUENT`, a `QUALIFIED` supplier, and a logical clock strictly before `current_term_end`; it does not revive voided opinions. `EXPIRE` is reachable from `ACTIVE` and from `SUSPENDED` and requires the logical clock to have reached or passed `current_term_end`; from `ACTIVE` it voids the contract's `DETERMINED` opinions with `voided_reason` `CONTRACT_TERMINATED`, and from `SUSPENDED` it simply closes the contract so that no further amendment may be proposed. `TERMINATE` is reachable from `PENDING_EXECUTION`, `ACTIVE` and `SUSPENDED`; in every case it sets every line item of the contract to `WITHDRAWN`, and from `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` it additionally voids the contract's `DETERMINED` opinions with `voided_reason` `CONTRACT_TERMINATED`. An award-status action that is not a declared transition — including an `EXECUTE` past `base_term_end` or an `EXPIRE` before `current_term_end` — is `ERR_CONTRACT_INVALID_STATE`. The system itself drives `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` from a supplier suspension or from a count-triggered delinquency, and drives `TERMINATE` from a supplier debarment.

### 3.4. Member Agency Registration and Eligibility Verification

The cooperative's membership desk is staffed by `CoopAdministrator`s. Registering a member records a `legal_name` of 1 to 200 characters that must be unique across all member agencies (`ERR_DUPLICATE_MEMBER_NAME`), a `state_code` naming the state whose procurement law governs it, a `member_category`, and an `annual_spend_ceiling_cents` of at least 1 giving the board-approved ceiling on cumulative cooperative spend. `member_category` is one of `STATE_AGENCY`, `CITY`, `COUNTY`, `K12_DISTRICT`, `HIGHER_ED`, `SPECIAL_DISTRICT` or `NONPROFIT_501C3`. Evidence numbers may be supplied at registration or later: `governing_body_resolution_number` is the resolution by which the member's governing body joined the cooperative, and `irs_determination_letter_number` is the IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter number. Both default to null. A new member starts in `registration_status` `PENDING_VERIFICATION` with `committed_spend_cents` 0, `docs_request_count` 0, and `verified_at` and `verified_by_user_id` null.

The registration dimension takes the values `PENDING_VERIFICATION`, `DOCS_REQUESTED`, `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` and the terminal `TERMINATED`, and it cycles twice over: `PENDING_VERIFICATION → DOCS_REQUESTED → PENDING_VERIFICATION`, and `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`. The administrator's actions are `REQUEST_DOCS`, `RESUBMIT_DOCS`, `VERIFY`, `SUSPEND`, `REINSTATE` and `TERMINATE`; the payload may carry a `governing_body_resolution_number`, an `irs_determination_letter_number` and a `note`.

`REQUEST_DOCS` moves `PENDING_VERIFICATION → DOCS_REQUESTED` and requires `docs_request_count` to be strictly below three; a fourth request is `ERR_DOCS_REQUEST_ROUNDS_EXHAUSTED`. A member sitting in `DOCS_REQUESTED` may not record usage. `RESUBMIT_DOCS` closes the cycle `DOCS_REQUESTED → PENDING_VERIFICATION`, increments `docs_request_count` by 1, and stores whichever of the two evidence numbers the request supplied — so the document-chase loop can run at most three times.

`VERIFY` moves `PENDING_VERIFICATION → ACTIVE` and is where eligibility is actually adjudicated. It requires a configuration row for the member's `state_code`. It then requires evidence matching the asserted category: a member whose `member_category` is `NONPROFIT_501C3` must have a non-null `irs_determination_letter_number`, failing which the rejection is `ERR_NONPROFIT_EVIDENCE_MISSING`; a member in any other category must have a non-null `governing_body_resolution_number`, failing which the rejection is `ERR_GOVERNING_RESOLUTION_MISSING`. Beyond evidence, a `NONPROFIT_501C3` member may only be verified when its state's `allows_nonprofit_participation` is true; a nonprofit in a state that forbids nonprofit participation is refused with `ERR_NONPROFIT_NOT_PERMITTED`. On success `verified_at` is stamped with the current logical instant and `verified_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`.

`SUSPEND` moves `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` and voids every `PiggybackAnalysis` of this member whose `status` is `DETERMINED`, setting `voided_reason` to `MEMBER_SUSPENDED`. Suspension does **not** release `committed_spend_cents`: orders already recorded stand and continue to count against the ceiling. `REINSTATE` moves `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE` and does not revive the voided opinions — a fresh analysis is required before the member may buy again. `TERMINATE` is reachable from `PENDING_VERIFICATION`, `DOCS_REQUESTED`, `ACTIVE` and `SUSPENDED`, and voids every analysis of this member whose `status` is `PENDING` *or* `DETERMINED` with `voided_reason` `MEMBER_TERMINATED` — a wider net than suspension, which touches only determined opinions. A registration action that is not a declared transition is `ERR_MEMBER_INVALID_STATE`; an operation that requires a verified member and finds one that is not `ACTIVE` is `ERR_MEMBER_NOT_ACTIVE`.

### 3.5. Piggyback Authority Analysis by State Law

Before a member may buy anything it needs a written opinion that its own state's law lets it ride the cooperative's award. Either the member itself or a `CoopAdministrator` opens the analysis, naming `member_agency_id` and `contract_id`, pinning `expected_member_agency_version`, and optionally supplying the `waiver_reference` up front. Opening one is a supersession event: any prior analysis for the same member-and-contract pair whose `status` is `PENDING` or `DETERMINED` becomes `VOIDED` with `voided_reason` `SUPERSEDED_BY_NEWER_ANALYSIS`, and the member's own `version` increments. The new row starts in `status` `PENDING` with `determination`, `doctrine_applied`, `authority_score`, `determined_at` and `expires_on` all null, `deduction_detail` empty and `local_waiver_required` false.

The opinion's dimension has three values — `PENDING`, `DETERMINED` and the terminal `VOIDED` — moved by a `CoopAdministrator`'s `DETERMINE` or `VOID` action, the latter optionally carrying a `void_reason`. `DETERMINE` moves `PENDING → DETERMINED` and requires the member to be `ACTIVE`, the contract to be `ACTIVE`, and — when the member's state sets `requires_local_solicitation_waiver` to true — a non-null `waiver_reference`, which the request may supply if it was not supplied at creation; a missing waiver is `ERR_LOCAL_WAIVER_REQUIRED`. `VOID` is reachable from `PENDING` and from `DETERMINED` and sets `voided_reason` to `ADMIN_VOID` when an administrator requests it explicitly. The full set of reasons an opinion can carry is `MEMBER_SUSPENDED`, `MEMBER_TERMINATED`, `CONTRACT_SUSPENDED`, `CONTRACT_TERMINATED`, `SUPERSEDED_BY_NEWER_ANALYSIS` and `ADMIN_VOID`. Once an opinion is `VOIDED` no new order may cite it (`ERR_PIGGYBACK_NOT_AUTHORIZED`), but orders already recorded under it are untouched. An action that is not a declared transition is `ERR_ANALYSIS_INVALID_STATE`.

**Scoring the authority.** Determination reads exactly one configuration row — the one whose `state_code` equals the member's `state_code` — plus the member, the contract, and the contract's solicitation. Nothing is aggregated across other analyses. `doctrine_applied` is frozen to that state's `piggyback_doctrine` at this moment. The contract's total term in whole months is computed by calendar arithmetic between `base_term_start` and `current_term_end`: the difference in years multiplied by twelve, plus the difference in months, minus one further month when the end's day-of-month is earlier than the start's day-of-month.

The score starts at 100.00 and deductions are appended to `deduction_detail` — each an object of `reason_code` and `points` — in a fixed order that depends on which doctrine applies. Under `STATUTORY_STRICT`, the four tests fire in this sequence: if the state sets `requires_lead_agency_in_state` to true and the solicitation's `lead_agency_state_code` differs from the member's `state_code`, append `OUT_OF_STATE_LEAD` worth 30.00; if the solicitation's frozen `notice_days` is strictly below the state's `min_notice_days`, append `SHORT_NOTICE` worth the smaller of 25.00 and five points for each day of shortfall (the shortfall being `min_notice_days` minus `notice_days`); if the contract's total term in months exceeds the state's `max_contract_term_months`, append `TERM_EXCEEDS_STATE_MAX` worth 20.00; and if the member's `member_category` is `NONPROFIT_501C3` while the state's `allows_nonprofit_participation` is false, append `NONPROFIT_NOT_PERMITTED` worth 40.00. Reciprocity lists are never consulted under this doctrine.

Under `RECIPROCITY_LIBERAL` the same four tests fire in the same sequence but score differently, and the first one changes shape: if the solicitation's `lead_agency_state_code` differs from the member's `state_code` *and* is not listed in the state's `reciprocity_state_codes`, append `OUT_OF_STATE_LEAD` worth 15.00 — a lead state that appears in the reciprocity list costs nothing at all, and `requires_lead_agency_in_state` is not consulted. A notice shortfall appends `SHORT_NOTICE` worth the smaller of 10.00 and two points for each day of shortfall. An over-long term appends `TERM_EXCEEDS_STATE_MAX` worth 5.00. A nonprofit in a state that forbids nonprofit participation appends `NONPROFIT_NOT_PERMITTED` worth 40.00, exactly as under the strict doctrine. Identical member and contract facts therefore land in different bands under the two doctrines, which is the point.

`authority_score` is 100.00 less the sum of the appended points, floored at 0.00 and carried to two decimals with half-up rounding. `local_waiver_required` is frozen to the state's `requires_local_solicitation_waiver`. `determination` bands the score with two inclusive thresholds: a score of 85.00 or more is `AUTHORIZED`, a score of 60.00 or more but below 85.00 is `CONDITIONAL`, and anything below 60.00 is `DENIED` — so exactly 85.00 is `AUTHORIZED` and exactly 60.00 is `CONDITIONAL`. `determined_at` is stamped with the current logical instant, and `expires_on` is frozen as the *earlier* of the contract's `current_term_end` and 365 days after `determined_at`. A renewal approved later does not extend an already-frozen `expires_on`; the member must obtain a fresh opinion.

### 3.6. Recording, Pricing and Disposing of Member Usage

A `MemberRepresentative` records a purchase against a contract line by naming `contract_id`, `member_agency_id`, `line_item_id` and the `piggyback_analysis_id` of the opinion it relies on, a `quantity` of at least 1, and optionally the `add_on_codes` it elects. Everything else on the resulting `ContractUsage` is computed by the server and frozen at creation.

Recording is gated hard. The member must be `ACTIVE`. The contract must be `ACTIVE`, and its `compliance_posture` must be `COMPLIANT` or `WATCH` — a `DELINQUENT` posture blocks the order with `ERR_CONTRACT_POSTURE_DELINQUENT` even while the contract's own status is still `ACTIVE`. The line must be `ACTIVE` and must belong to the named contract, a mismatch being `ERR_LINE_ITEM_CONTRACT_MISMATCH`. The cited opinion must be `DETERMINED` with a `determination` of `AUTHORIZED` or `CONDITIONAL` — a `DENIED`, `PENDING` or `VOIDED` opinion is `ERR_PIGGYBACK_NOT_AUTHORIZED` — its frozen `expires_on` must be strictly after the logical clock, so an instant exactly equal to `expires_on` is already expired (`ERR_PIGGYBACK_EXPIRED`), and its own `member_agency_id` and `contract_id` must match the order's, a mismatch being `ERR_ANALYSIS_PAIR_MISMATCH`. Every elected code must appear in the contract's `permitted_add_ons`, an unknown or repeated code being `ERR_ADD_ON_NOT_PERMITTED`. The order's `ordered_at`, taken from the logical clock at creation, must fall inside the contract's half-open term: an instant equal to `base_term_start` is inside, an instant equal to `current_term_end` is outside, and either violation is `ERR_OUTSIDE_CONTRACT_TERM`. Finally the member's `committed_spend_cents` plus this order's own `total_cents` must not exceed its `annual_spend_ceiling_cents`; landing exactly on the ceiling is permitted, and exceeding it is `ERR_MEMBER_CEILING_EXCEEDED`.

**Pricing, in pinned order.** The arithmetic looks circular — the member's discount tier is keyed on cumulative spend, and cumulative spend is the running sum of order totals — so the evaluation order is fixed and an order never influences its own tier.

*Step one, the spend basis.* Read the member's `committed_spend_cents` as it stands **before** this order is added; that snapshot is the `spend_basis_cents`. (`committed_spend_cents` is itself defined as the sum of `total_cents` over that member's orders whose `status` is `RECORDED` or `CONFIRMED`.)

*Step two, the tier and the net unit price.* Select the entry of the contract's `discount_tiers` with the largest `min_annual_spend_cents` that is less than or equal to the spend basis; since the ladder always contains a `tier_index` 0 entry whose threshold is 0 the candidate set is never empty, and a spend basis exactly equal to a tier's threshold selects that tier. Record that entry's `tier_index` as the order's `discount_tier_index` and take its `discount_pct` as the base discount. A member whose `member_category` is `NONPROFIT_501C3` earns a bonus of three percentage points on top — the fraction 0.0300 — and every other category earns nothing extra. Add base and bonus, then cap the sum at a total discount of twenty percent (the fraction 0.2000); the result is `total_discount_pct`. Multiply the line's `base_unit_price_cents` by one minus `total_discount_pct` and round half up to a whole cent. `net_unit_price_cents` is the larger of that rounded figure and the line's `price_floor_cents` — the rounding happens *before* the floor comparison, so the stored net price is always a whole cent and never below the floor. The two bounds are independent: the twenty-percent ceiling caps the discount, the floor caps the resulting price, and either, both or neither may bind.

*Step three, the subtotal.* `subtotal_cents` is `net_unit_price_cents` multiplied by `quantity`.

*Step four, the add-ons.* Walk the contract's `permitted_add_ons` array in **array order**, not in the order the member listed its codes, so that `add_on_detail` and every remainder behaviour are deterministic. For each catalogue entry whose `code` the member elected, compute a `raw_cents` charge as `subtotal_cents` multiplied by that entry's `rate_pct`, rounded half up to a whole cent; compute `capped_cents` as the smaller of `raw_cents` and that entry's `cap_cents`; and append an object of `code`, `raw_cents` and `capped_cents` to `add_on_detail`. Sum the `capped_cents` values across the detail. Separately compute an aggregate ceiling as `subtotal_cents` multiplied by fifteen percent (the fraction 0.1500), rounded half up to a whole cent. `add_on_charges_cents` is the smaller of the per-code sum and that aggregate ceiling. The rounding order is pinned: round each per-code charge to whole cents, *then* apply its per-code cap, *then* sum, *then* apply the aggregate cap. Summing unrounded charges and rounding once at the end is explicitly not the rule.

*Step five, the chargeable base.* `chargeable_base_cents` is `subtotal_cents` plus `add_on_charges_cents`. This is the base on which the administrative fee is assessed.

*Step six, the tier-rebated fee rate.* The cooperative concedes part of its fee to high-volume members. The rebate is keyed on the `discount_tier_index` chosen in step two: tier 0 earns no rebate, tier 1 earns a ten percent rebate (the fraction 0.1000), tier 2 earns a twenty-five percent rebate (the fraction 0.2500), and any tier index not in that map earns no rebate at all. `effective_fee_rate_bps` is the contract's `admin_fee_rate_bps` multiplied by one minus the applicable rebate fraction, rounded half up to whole basis points.

*Step seven, the fee.* `admin_fee_cents` is `chargeable_base_cents` multiplied by `effective_fee_rate_bps` and divided by 10,000, rounded half up to a whole cent.

*Step eight, the total.* `total_cents` is `chargeable_base_cents` plus `admin_fee_cents`.

*Step nine, the debits.* Only now is `total_cents` added to the member's `committed_spend_cents`, and `chargeable_base_cents` added to the contract's `usage_chargeable_cents_total`. Both parent rows have their `version` incremented. The consequence is worth stating plainly: two identical orders placed back to back can carry different `net_unit_price_cents`, because the first order's step-nine debit moves the second order's step-one snapshot across a tier threshold. Neither order's own total ever feeds its own tier.

A recorded order starts with `status` `RECORDED`, `usage_report_id` null and `retracted_at` null. The member's own disposition then moves it: `CONFIRM` requires the acting `X-User-ID` to be the order's `member_agency_id` and moves `RECORDED → CONFIRMED`, which is what makes the order eligible for a supplier's period sweep. `RETRACT` is the compensating action, reachable from `RECORDED` and from `CONFIRMED`, again requiring the acting member to own the order, and permitted only while `usage_report_id` is null or the report it names is in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `REJECTED`. Once the sweeping report has reached `ACCEPTED` or `RECONCILED` the fee ledger can no longer be unwound and retraction is refused with `ERR_USAGE_ALREADY_SETTLED`. A disposition that is not a declared transition out of the order's current status is `ERR_USAGE_INVALID_STATE`. `RETRACTED` is terminal.

Retraction cascades three hops. First it subtracts `total_cents` from the member's `committed_spend_cents` and `chargeable_base_cents` from the contract's `usage_chargeable_cents_total`, and stamps `retracted_at`. Second, if the order had been swept, that debit reversal re-runs the sweep of the report named by `usage_report_id`, recomputing `swept_usage_count`, `reported_chargeable_cents`, `reported_usage_cents` and `reported_fee_cents`, and clears the order's own `usage_report_id`. Third, the recomputed report figures re-run the contract's compliance posture. Afterwards the committed-spend arithmetic must balance exactly: the member's `committed_spend_cents` equals the sum of `total_cents` over its `RECORDED` and `CONFIRMED` orders and nothing else.

Members and administrators can also price a hypothetical order without recording it. That read-only computation takes a `line_item_id`, a `member_agency_id`, a `quantity` and optional `add_on_codes`, runs exactly the same steps one through eight against the member's *current* `committed_spend_cents`, creates no row and moves no counter, and returns `line_item_id`, `member_agency_id`, `spend_basis_cents`, `discount_tier_index`, `total_discount_pct`, `net_unit_price_cents`, `subtotal_cents`, `add_on_charges_cents`, `chargeable_base_cents`, `effective_fee_rate_bps`, `admin_fee_cents` and `total_cents`, plus two diagnostic booleans: `floor_binding`, true when the price floor rather than the discounted price determined `net_unit_price_cents`, and `aggregate_add_on_cap_binding`, true when the fifteen-percent aggregate ceiling rather than the per-code sum determined `add_on_charges_cents`. The quote is subject to the same line-item liveness, line-contract matching and add-on catalogue checks as a real order.

### 3.7. Period Usage Reporting, Administrative Fee Remittance and Delinquency

The supplier reports its cooperative sales by period and remits the administrative fee on them. A `SupplierRepresentative` opens a report on its own contract with a `contract_id`, a `period_start` and a `period_end` bounding a **closed** period. The period must not overlap any other report on the same contract whose `status` is `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `ACCEPTED` or `RECONCILED`: overlap means the candidate's `period_start` is at or before the other's `period_end` and the other's `period_start` is at or before the candidate's `period_end`, so two periods that merely touch at a single instant *do* overlap and the later one is refused with `ERR_REPORT_PERIOD_OVERLAP`. A new report starts in `status` `DRAFT` with `fee_status` `NOT_DUE`, every figure at 0, and `audited_fee_cents`, `fee_true_up_cents`, `variance_pct`, `accepted_at` and `fee_due_at` null.

The acceptance dimension runs `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `ACCEPTED` and the terminal `RECONCILED`, with `REJECTED` folding back to `SUBMITTED` so a rejected report can be fixed and resubmitted. `SUBMIT` is reachable from `DRAFT` and from `REJECTED`. In both cases it requires the acting `X-User-ID` to be the contract's `supplier_id`, requires the logical clock to be strictly past `period_end` so the period has fully elapsed (otherwise `ERR_REPORT_PERIOD_NOT_ELAPSED`), and re-runs the overlap test against every *other* non-rejected report on the contract, excluding this report itself. Submission from `DRAFT` additionally requires the contract's `status` to be `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED` or `EXPIRED` — a supplier can and must still report on a suspended or expired contract. Resubmission from `REJECTED` clears `rejection_reason`.

**The sweep.** Submission gathers the orders the report covers: every order on the report's own `contract_id` whose `status` is `CONFIRMED`, whose `ordered_at` lies inside the closed period with both endpoints inclusive, and whose `usage_report_id` is either null or already this report's own id. Orders still in `RECORDED` are never swept, because the member has not confirmed receipt, and `RETRACTED` orders are removed from a sweep by the retraction cascade. The swept set is ordered by `ordered_at` ascending and then by `created_at` ascending, never by `id`. `swept_usage_count` is the count of the set, `reported_chargeable_cents` is the exact unrounded sum of the set's `chargeable_base_cents` values, and `reported_usage_cents` is the exact unrounded sum of the set's `total_cents` values — the figure printed on the member usage statement. Every swept order has its `usage_report_id` stamped with this report's id. All rounding already happened per order, so these sums are exact.

**The fee, computed twice.** Submission also computes the *reported* variant of the administrative fee: `reported_fee_cents` is `reported_chargeable_cents` multiplied by the contract's `admin_fee_rate_bps` and divided by 10,000, rounded half up to a whole cent. This is one bulk application of the contract's base rate to the period aggregate, with a single rounding at the end; it ignores every order's tier rebate entirely, and it is the amount printed on the supplier's remittance advice and the amount actually invoiced and remitted. The *audited* variant is computed later, at reconciliation: `audited_fee_cents` is the plain sum of the swept orders' own `admin_fee_cents`, each of which was frozen at that order's tier-rebated `effective_fee_rate_bps` and rounded individually. The two figures diverge for two independent reasons — tier rebates present on some orders, and per-order rounding — so the same swept set genuinely yields two different totals.

Cooperative review is a `CoopAdministrator`'s job, through `ACCEPT`, `REJECT` and `RECONCILE` actions, the second optionally carrying a `rejection_reason`. `ACCEPT` moves `SUBMITTED → ACCEPTED`, stamps `accepted_at` with the current logical instant, sets `fee_due_at` to thirty days after `accepted_at`, moves the parallel `fee_status` from `NOT_DUE` to `DUE`, and adds `reported_fee_cents` to both the contract's and the supplier's `fee_assessed_cents_total` before recomputing the contract's `fee_outstanding_cents`. `REJECT` moves `SUBMITTED → REJECTED`, clears `usage_report_id` on every order this report had swept, zeroes `swept_usage_count`, `reported_chargeable_cents`, `reported_usage_cents` and `reported_fee_cents`, and stores the `rejection_reason`. A review or submission action that is not a declared transition on the acceptance dimension is `ERR_REPORT_INVALID_STATE`.

The remittance dimension advances `NOT_DUE`, `DUE`, `PAID`, with `DUE → DELINQUENT` when the sweep finds an overdue report and `DELINQUENT → PAID` when it is finally settled; `PAID` is terminal. `NOT_DUE → DUE` is driven by the system the moment the acceptance dimension reaches `ACCEPTED`, and from then on `fee_due_at` is the delinquency deadline. `REMIT`, performed by the supplier on its own report, is reachable from `DUE` and from `DELINQUENT`, requires the acting `X-User-ID` to be the contract's `supplier_id`, and requires the supplied `amount_cents` to equal `reported_fee_cents` minus `remitted_cents` exactly — partial payments and over-payments are both refused with `ERR_REMITTANCE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`. A successful remittance adds `amount_cents` to the report's `remitted_cents`, to the contract's `fee_remitted_cents_total` and to the supplier's `fee_remitted_cents_total`, then recomputes the contract's `fee_outstanding_cents`. A remittance against a `NOT_DUE` or `PAID` report, or an acceptance of a report whose `fee_status` is not `NOT_DUE`, is `ERR_FEE_STATUS_INVALID`.

The two remittance branches are not equivalent. A remittance from `DUE` does exactly the ledger work just described and nothing more. A remittance from `DELINQUENT` is the compensating action of the delinquency sweep and must undo it exactly: on top of the same ledger work it decrements both the contract's and the supplier's `delinquent_report_count` by 1, re-runs the contract's compliance posture with the reduced count, and subtracts the three delinquency points from the supplier's `deduction_points_total`, which re-runs `performance_score` and `performance_tier`. After a delinquent report is settled the supplier's point total must be exactly what it was before the sweep charged it. What the compensation does *not* do is reinstate a contract that the count trigger suspended, or revive an opinion that suspension voided.

The fee ledger has an invariant the system must never violate: a contract's `fee_outstanding_cents` always equals its `fee_assessed_cents_total` minus its `fee_remitted_cents_total` after every fee mutation, and no remittance may drive `fee_outstanding_cents` below zero. A mutation that would break either half is refused with `ERR_FEE_LEDGER_INCONSISTENT`. Both `fee_assessed_cents_total` figures — the contract's and the supplier's — are defined as the sum of `reported_fee_cents` over reports whose `status` is `ACCEPTED` or `RECONCILED`, plus every applied `fee_true_up_cents`; both `fee_remitted_cents_total` figures are the sum of `remitted_cents` over the same population of reports.

**The delinquency sweep.** An administrator runs a sweep over one contract, optionally with a `note`. Every report on that contract whose `fee_status` is `DUE` and whose `fee_due_at` is strictly before the logical clock moves `DUE → DELINQUENT`, and the sweep then cascades four hops. First, the contract's `delinquent_report_count` and the supplier's `delinquent_report_count` each increment by 1 per newly delinquent report. Second, that counter change re-runs the contract's compliance posture. Third, a posture that reaches `DELINQUENT` **on the count trigger** drives the contract's execution dimension `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED`. Fourth, that suspension voids every `DETERMINED` opinion on the contract with `voided_reason` `CONTRACT_SUSPENDED`. Independently, three performance points are charged per newly delinquent report against the supplier's `deduction_points_total`, which re-runs its `performance_score` and `performance_tier`. The sweep reports back `contract_id`, `swept_report_count`, `newly_delinquent_report_ids`, `delinquent_report_count`, `compliance_posture`, `contract_status`, `supplier_performance_score` and `supplier_performance_tier`, and increments the contract's `version` when anything changed.

**Compliance posture.** The contract's second dimension takes the values `COMPLIANT`, `WATCH` and `DELINQUENT`, and the system recomputes it after every change to any of the contract's reports' `fee_status` — on acceptance, on remittance, on the delinquency sweep, and after a retraction re-sweeps a report. The computation looks only at reports on this contract whose `fee_status` is `DELINQUENT`; `NOT_DUE`, `DUE` and `PAID` reports are excluded, as are `REJECTED` reports, which can never have left `NOT_DUE`. `delinquent_report_count` is the count of that set, and the outstanding delinquent amount is the sum, over that set, of `reported_fee_cents` minus `remitted_cents`. If the count reaches two or more, **or** the outstanding delinquent amount reaches 5,000.00 dollars (500,000 cents) or more, the posture is `DELINQUENT`. Otherwise, if the count is exactly one, the posture is `WATCH`. Otherwise it is `COMPLIANT`. Both tests are inclusive: exactly two delinquent reports is `DELINQUENT`, and exactly 500,000 cents outstanding on a single report is `DELINQUENT`.

Which trigger fired matters, because only the count trigger suspends the contract. A move to `DELINQUENT` reached because the count hit two or more drives the execution dimension `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED`, whose own side effects void the contract's `DETERMINED` opinions. A move to `DELINQUENT` reached on the amount test alone with a single delinquent report leaves the contract `ACTIVE` and voids nothing: new orders are blocked by the posture gate `ERR_CONTRACT_POSTURE_DELINQUENT` rather than by the status gate `ERR_CONTRACT_NOT_ACTIVE`. A single large delinquency therefore skips `WATCH` entirely, moving `COMPLIANT → DELINQUENT` in one step. `COMPLIANT → WATCH` happens when exactly one report is delinquent and its outstanding amount is strictly below 500,000 cents; `WATCH → DELINQUENT` happens when either threshold is subsequently crossed. Recovery runs the other way as remittances land: `DELINQUENT → WATCH` when the count falls back to exactly one with an outstanding amount strictly below the threshold, `DELINQUENT → COMPLIANT` and `WATCH → COMPLIANT` when the count reaches zero. Recovery is deliberately asymmetric — a contract suspended by a count-triggered delinquency stays `SUSPENDED` until an administrator reinstates it explicitly, and a contract that only ever reached `WATCH` was never suspended and needs no reinstatement.

**Reconciliation.** `RECONCILE` moves `ACCEPTED → RECONCILED` and requires `fee_status` to be `PAID` or `DELINQUENT`. It computes the audited variant and its consequences: `audited_fee_cents` as described above; `fee_true_up_cents` as `audited_fee_cents` minus `reported_fee_cents`, a signed figure; and `fee_rebate_cents` as `reported_fee_cents` minus `audited_fee_cents`, which is the cooperative's tier concession expressed positively and equals the negation of `fee_true_up_cents` wherever no rounding divergence exists. `variance_pct` is one hundred multiplied by the absolute value of `fee_true_up_cents` and divided by `audited_fee_cents`, rounded half up to two decimals; when `audited_fee_cents` is zero, `variance_pct` is 0.00 rather than undefined. `fee_true_up_cents` is then applied to both the contract's and the supplier's `fee_assessed_cents_total`, and the contract's `fee_outstanding_cents` is recomputed.

Reconciliation can also cost the supplier. When `variance_pct` is strictly greater than fifteen percent, `variance_flagged` is set to true and five performance points are added to the supplier's `deduction_points_total`. That deduction in turn re-runs the supplier's `performance_score` and `performance_tier`, and a resulting `performance_tier` of `PROBATION` in turn drives the supplier's standing `QUALIFIED → SUSPENDED`, whose own side effects suspend the supplier's `ACTIVE` contracts and void their `DETERMINED` opinions. `RECONCILED` is terminal on the acceptance dimension.

### 3.8. Contract Amendments: Renewals, Price Adjustments and Product Line Expansions

Amendments are proposed by the awarded supplier and decided by the cooperative. A proposal names its `contract_id`, its `amendment_type` — one of `RENEWAL`, `PRICE_ADJUSTMENT` or `PRODUCT_LINE_EXPANSION` — and its `expected_contract_version`, and carries exactly the optional payload block its type requires. The contract must be `ACTIVE` and its supplier `QUALIFIED`. Proposing supersedes: any prior amendment on the same contract with the same `amendment_type` whose `status` is `PROPOSED` becomes `WITHDRAWN`. A new amendment starts in `status` `PROPOSED` with `proposed_by_user_id` set to the acting `X-User-ID`, and `effective_from` and `decided_by_user_id` null.

The payload block must match the type exactly, and a mismatch in either direction — a missing required field or a field belonging to a different type being present — is `ERR_AMENDMENT_PAYLOAD_MISMATCH`. A `PRICE_ADJUSTMENT` requires `target_line_item_id`, `new_unit_price_cents`, `price_index` (either `CPI_U` or `PPI_COMMODITY`) and `index_change_pct` (a four-decimal fraction between 0.0000 and 1.0000), and forbids `requested_term_end`, `new_line_items` and `expansion_scope_overlap_pct`. A `RENEWAL` requires `requested_term_end` and nothing else. A `PRODUCT_LINE_EXPANSION` requires a non-empty `new_line_items` array — each entry an object of `sku`, `item_description`, `unit_of_measure`, `base_unit_price_cents` and `price_floor_cents` — together with `expansion_scope_overlap_pct`, a four-decimal fraction between 0.0000 and 1.0000 declaring what share of the requested lines falls inside the original solicitation's specification scope, and nothing else.

**The price-adjustment ceiling.** For a `PRICE_ADJUSTMENT` the server computes and stores `price_ceiling_cents` at proposal time and recomputes it at approval. The cited index carries its own statutory escalation cap: `CPI_U` caps escalation at four percent (the fraction 0.0400) and `PPI_COMMODITY` at six percent (the fraction 0.0600). The effective cap is the smallest of three four-decimal fractions — the contract's `price_escalation_cap_pct`, the index's own cap, and the `index_change_pct` the supplier actually cited — and that three-way minimum is taken on the fractions *before* any multiplication. `price_ceiling_cents` is then the target line's current `base_unit_price_cents` multiplied by one plus the effective cap, rounded half up to a whole cent. The boundary is inclusive: a `new_unit_price_cents` exactly equal to `price_ceiling_cents` is allowed, and one cent above it is `ERR_PRICE_ESCALATION_EXCEEDS_CAP`. A `new_unit_price_cents` below the target line's `price_floor_cents` is `ERR_PRICE_BELOW_FLOOR`. `price_ceiling_cents` stays null on amendments of the other two types.

**Renewal interval arithmetic.** For a `RENEWAL`, the requested end must genuinely extend the term: because a term is half-open, a `requested_term_end` equal to or earlier than the contract's `current_term_end` extends nothing and is `ERR_RENEWAL_TERM_NOT_EXTENDING`. The extension in whole months is computed by calendar arithmetic between `current_term_end` and `requested_term_end` — the year difference multiplied by twelve, plus the month difference, minus one further month when the requested end's day-of-month is earlier than the current end's day-of-month — and it must not exceed twelve months, a longer extension being `ERR_RENEWAL_TERM_TOO_LONG`. The merged total term, computed by the same calendar arithmetic between `base_term_start` and `requested_term_end`, must not exceed sixty months, a longer total being `ERR_TOTAL_TERM_EXCEEDS_MAX`. The day-of-month correction is what makes a term running from the first of September 2026 to the first of September 2027 exactly twelve months. All three tests run at proposal time and again at approval. Approval additionally requires `renewals_used` to be strictly below `max_renewals`, otherwise `ERR_RENEWAL_LIMIT_REACHED`. Note that a renewal beginning exactly at the previous term's exclusive end is *adjacent*, not overlapping, and is allowed — the opposite of the closed-interval geometry that governs reporting periods.

**Scope for expansions.** A `PRODUCT_LINE_EXPANSION` requires `expansion_scope_overlap_pct` to be at least 0.8000 — that is, at least eighty percent of the requested lines must fall inside the competitively solicited scope. A lower share is `ERR_EXPANSION_OUT_OF_SCOPE`, checked both at proposal and at approval.

A `CoopAdministrator` then decides, with `APPROVE` or `REJECT` and an optional `decision_note`. Both require the amendment to be `PROPOSED`, otherwise `ERR_AMENDMENT_NOT_PROPOSED`. `APPROVE` additionally requires the contract to still be `ACTIVE` and the supplier still `QUALIFIED`, and re-runs every type-specific check above. A `PRICE_ADJUSTMENT` approval carries one further temporal gate: the target line's price may only move again once the price-stability window has elapsed, meaning `last_price_change_at` is null or the logical clock has reached or passed ninety days after `last_price_change_at`. Exactly ninety days later is permitted; eighty-nine days is `ERR_PRICE_WINDOW_TOO_SOON`.

Approval stamps `effective_from` with the current logical instant and `decided_by_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`, then mutates whichever target its type selects. The `RENEWAL` branch sets the contract's `current_term_end` to `requested_term_end` and increments `renewals_used` by 1; already-determined authority opinions keep their frozen `expires_on` and are not extended. The `PRICE_ADJUSTMENT` branch sets the target line's `base_unit_price_cents` to `new_unit_price_cents`, sets both its `price_effective_from` and its `last_price_change_at` to `effective_from`, and increments its `price_change_count` by 1. The `PRODUCT_LINE_EXPANSION` branch creates one new price-sheet line per `new_line_items` entry, each with `origin` `EXPANSION` and `status` `ACTIVE`, and each subject to the same within-contract `sku` uniqueness rule as an awarded line (`ERR_DUPLICATE_SKU`). In every branch, approval moves the solicitation's `audit_package_status` from `ASSEMBLED` or `CERTIFIED` to `STALE`, and `AMENDMENT_LOG` joins that solicitation's `audit_required_doc_codes` at the next assembly.

`REJECT` moves `PROPOSED → REJECTED`, stamps `decided_by_user_id`, stores the `decision_note`, and changes no field on the contract or on any line item. The supplier's own `WITHDRAW` moves `PROPOSED → WITHDRAWN` with the same guarantee of no collateral change, and requires the acting `X-User-ID` to be the contract's `supplier_id`. `APPROVED`, `REJECTED` and `WITHDRAWN` are all terminal.

### 3.9. Supplier Performance Monitoring and Member Complaint Handling

A `MemberRepresentative` files a complaint about a cooperative contract, naming `contract_id`, its own `member_agency_id`, a `severity` of `MINOR`, `MAJOR` or `CRITICAL`, a `category` of `PRICING`, `DELIVERY`, `PRODUCT_QUALITY`, `BILLING` or `SERVICE`, and a `narrative` of 1 to 2000 characters. It may optionally cite one of its own orders through `related_usage_id`, which when present must belong to the same `contract_id` *and* the same `member_agency_id` as the complaint, a mismatch being `ERR_USAGE_PAIR_MISMATCH`. Filing is deliberately not gated on the contract's status or the member's registration status — a member must be able to complain about a contract that has already been suspended. A new complaint starts in `status` `INTAKE` with `points_reversed` false and `deduction_points`, `investigator_user_id`, `adjudicator_user_id`, `substantiated_at`, `appeal_filed_at` and `appeal_deadline_at` all null. The supplier who absorbs any resulting deduction is the supplier of the named contract.

The complaint lifecycle forks at investigation and then loops through appeal. Values are `INTAKE`, `UNDER_INVESTIGATION`, `SUBSTANTIATED`, `APPEALED`, and the terminal `DISMISSED`, `OVERTURNED` and `CLOSED`. Any action that is not a declared transition out of the current status is `ERR_COMPLAINT_INVALID_STATE`.

`OPEN_INVESTIGATION` moves `INTAKE → UNDER_INVESTIGATION` and stamps `investigator_user_id` with the acting `X-User-ID`; the payload may carry a `note`. From `UNDER_INVESTIGATION` the case forks into two sibling branches, both requiring the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from `investigator_user_id` and both stamping `adjudicator_user_id`. `DISMISS` moves `UNDER_INVESTIGATION → DISMISSED` and charges the supplier no performance points at all. `SUBSTANTIATE` moves `UNDER_INVESTIGATION → SUBSTANTIATED` and additionally requires the contract's supplier not to be `DEBARRED` (`ERR_SUPPLIER_DEBARRED`); it stamps `substantiated_at` with the current logical instant, sets `appeal_deadline_at` to fourteen days after `substantiated_at`, and writes `deduction_points` from the asserted `severity` — 2.50 points for `MINOR`, 7.50 for `MAJOR`, and 20.00 for `CRITICAL`.

Substantiation then cascades five hops. First, `deduction_points` is added to the supplier's `deduction_points_total` and its `substantiated_complaint_count` increments. Second, that total re-runs the supplier's `performance_score` and `performance_tier`. Third, a `performance_tier` of `PROBATION` drives the supplier's standing `QUALIFIED → SUSPENDED`. Fourth, that suspension sets every `ACTIVE` master contract of the supplier to `SUSPENDED`. Fifth, each of those contract suspensions voids that contract's `DETERMINED` authority opinions with `voided_reason` `CONTRACT_SUSPENDED`.

**The performance score.** A supplier's `deduction_points_total` is the sum of three independent charges. First, the severity points of every complaint on any of this supplier's contracts whose `status` is `SUBSTANTIATED`, `APPEALED` or `CLOSED` and whose `points_reversed` is false — complaints in `INTAKE` or `UNDER_INVESTIGATION`, complaints that were `DISMISSED`, and complaints that were `OVERTURNED` all contribute nothing. Second, five points for each report on this supplier's contracts whose `variance_flagged` is true. Third, three points for each report on this supplier's contracts whose `fee_status` is `DELINQUENT`. `performance_score` is 100.00 minus that total, clamped to the closed range 0.00 through 100.00, carried to two decimals with half-up rounding. `performance_tier` bands the score with two inclusive thresholds: 85.00 or more is `PREFERRED`, 70.00 or more but below 85.00 is `STANDARD`, and below 70.00 is `PROBATION` — so exactly 85.00 is `PREFERRED` and exactly 70.00 is `STANDARD`. The whole score is recomputed, never incrementally patched, whenever a complaint is substantiated or overturned, whenever a reconciliation flips `variance_flagged` to true, and whenever a report's `fee_status` moves into or out of `DELINQUENT`. Every point charged anywhere in the system is a conserved quantity: `deduction_points_total` must always equal the sum of the three charges above, and no compensating action may leave it drifted.

`FILE_APPEAL`, performed by the supplier itself, moves `SUBSTANTIATED → APPEALED`. It requires the acting `X-User-ID` to be the contract's `supplier_id` and requires the logical clock to be at or before `appeal_deadline_at` — the instant exactly equal to the deadline is still inside the window, and anything later is `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`. It stamps `appeal_filed_at`, and the deduction points stay charged for the duration of the appeal.

The appeal fork is decided by a `CoopAdministrator` whose `X-User-ID` differs from `adjudicator_user_id`. `OVERTURN` moves `APPEALED → OVERTURNED` and is the exact compensating action of substantiation: it subtracts `deduction_points` from the supplier's `deduction_points_total`, sets `points_reversed` to true, decrements the supplier's `substantiated_complaint_count`, and re-runs the score so that `performance_score` and `performance_tier` are restored exactly. The complaint's own `deduction_points` value is retained for the audit record even though it no longer counts toward the supplier's total. What `OVERTURN` deliberately does *not* do is reinstate `qualification_status`: a supplier suspended by the substantiation stays `SUSPENDED` until an administrator reinstates it explicitly. `UPHOLD` moves `APPEALED → CLOSED`, leaves the points charged, and changes `performance_score` not at all.

Finally, `CLOSE` moves `SUBSTANTIATED → CLOSED` when no appeal was ever filed. It requires the logical clock to be strictly past `appeal_deadline_at`; closing while the window is still open is `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_OPEN`. The deduction points then stay charged permanently.
