# Animal ID Traceability API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

When a foreign animal disease is suspected in a commercial herd, the epidemiological value of every hour is enormous. The national programme this API supports exists to answer one question quickly and defensibly: given an infected animal, which other animals shared space with it, at which premises, and where are they now? Answering that question today means telephoning sale barns, faxing paper certificates of veterinary inspection, and reconciling hand-written tag books — work that routinely takes weeks. The programme's stated operational goal is to complete a traceback within **48 hours** of opening it, with a documented completeness standard, and this API is the system of record that makes such a goal measurable rather than aspirational.

The service covers the full chain of custody for official animal identification devices. Approved device manufacturers are vetted and scored on the field performance of their tags; serial ranges are issued from those manufacturers to registered premises; individual devices are applied to individual animals; every departure, arrival, dispute and harvest is captured as an immutable observation; and each device is eventually retired at a harvest facility and reconciled against the physical inventory the producer counted. Layered over that chain are the regulatory instruments a state animal health official actually uses — quarantine hold orders, movement disputes, and inventory adjudication — plus a federal traceback engine that replays the observation log as a contact graph.

Participation in this programme is voluntary for producers, and the incentive scheme is therefore a first-class part of the specification rather than an afterthought. Producers who enrol, keep their premises registrations in good standing, and report movements promptly earn a per-head payment whose size scales with herd size and reporting discipline; producers whose inventory reconciliation reveals material discrepancies lose their registration, their enrolment, and their accrual eligibility until the discrepancy is cleared. Because producers are also commercially sensitive about their herd data, the API additionally enforces a privacy posture that narrows what regulators and analysts may read about an animal until that animal is actually implicated in an investigation.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

This section defines the system-wide contracts, enforcement ordering, and cross-cutting principles that govern every endpoint in the API. All resource collections are mounted at the API root; there is no versioned or namespaced prefix.

### 2.1. Participants and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to the API must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting user, and `X-User-Role`, declaring the capacity in which that user is acting. If either header is absent, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the six recognised role names, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`. The six recognised roles are `Producer`, `StateAnimalHealthOfficial`, `FederalTraceAnalyst`, `DeviceSupplierRep`, `HarvestFacilityOperator`, and `ProgramAdmin`. Each endpoint declares the roles permitted to invoke it (see the table in Section 4); a syntactically valid request from a role that is not on that endpoint's list is rejected with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE`.

Three of the six roles are **entity-backed**, meaning `X-User-ID` is the identifier of a persisted record rather than an opaque operator handle:

- When `X-User-Role` is `Producer`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Producer` record; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Producer` compare the resource's producer foreign key to `X-User-ID`. For premises the comparison is direct against `producer_id`; for animals, tag lots, reconciliation runs and hold orders the comparison walks through the owning premises to its `producer_id`.
- When `X-User-Role` is `DeviceSupplierRep`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `DeviceSupplier` record; ownership checks on any resource that references a `DeviceSupplier` compare the resource's `supplier_id` to `X-User-ID`.
- When `X-User-Role` is `HarvestFacilityOperator`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting harvest `Premises` record — the facility is its own principal; ownership checks compare the request's `harvest_premises_id` to `X-User-ID`.

The remaining three roles are opaque. When `X-User-Role` is `StateAnimalHealthOfficial`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server records it (for example as `issued_by_user_id` on a hold order, `approved_by_user_id` on a reconciliation run, or `submitted_by_user_id` where a transition writes it) but never compares it to any entity's `id`. When `X-User-Role` is `FederalTraceAnalyst`, `X-User-ID` is likewise an opaque operator identifier; the server records it as `opened_by_user_id` and `closed_by_user_id` on trace requests but does not resolve it to an entity. When `X-User-Role` is `ProgramAdmin`, `X-User-ID` is again opaque; it is recorded as `registered_by_user_id` and `approved_by_user_id` on device suppliers, but it is never matched against any entity identifier.

Ownership enforcement covers exactly twelve write operations: `POST /premises`, `POST /producers/{id}/enroll`, `POST /tag-lots`, `POST /tag-lots/{id}/apply-tag`, `POST /tags/{id}/replace`, `POST /tags/{id}/retire`, `POST /movement-events`, `POST /movement-events/{id}/receive`, `POST /movement-events/{id}/correct`, `POST /hold-orders/{id}/appeal`, `POST /reconciliation-runs`, and `POST /reconciliation-runs/{id}/submit`. An entity-backed role acting on a resource that belongs to a different entity instance receives `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. Enrolment is self-service only: a `Producer` may enrol only the producer record whose `id` equals its own `X-User-ID`.

Two decisions in the programme are subject to **segregation of duties**, and both are enforced by comparing the acting `X-User-ID` against a previously stored user identifier. The `ProgramAdmin` who approves a device supplier must not be the same administrator recorded in `registered_by_user_id`, and the `FederalTraceAnalyst` who closes a trace request must not be the same analyst recorded in `opened_by_user_id`. Either violation returns `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_` followed by exactly twenty characters drawn from the uppercase letters A–Z and the digits 0–9.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits; it is the one instant in the API taken from the host's real clock rather than from the programme's logical clock, so setting the logical clock never moves it.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

List endpoints accept two query parameters. `limit` caps the number of rows returned; it defaults to 10 when omitted, may not be smaller than 1, and may not exceed 100. `offset` skips a number of leading rows and defaults to 0; it may not be negative. A `limit` outside its permitted range or a negative `offset` is a request-shape failure, not a silent clamp; a value that is not a base-ten integer fails the same way, while an empty-string value for either parameter is read as though the parameter had been omitted. The documented filter parameters of each list endpoint are matched by exact string equality against the stored value and are not themselves validated, so a filter naming a value no row carries — including a value outside the relevant enum — simply matches nothing rather than failing the request, and an empty-string filter value is likewise read as absent; the boolean filters `include_reversed`, `on_time` and `trace_goal_met` count `true`, `1` and `yes` in any letter case as true and treat every other value as false, and a trace request whose `trace_goal_met` is still null matches neither `true` nor `false`.

Unless a specific endpoint states otherwise, list results are ordered by `created_at` ascending. The one documented exception is the sighting history, which is ordered by `sequence_no` ascending so that an animal's event log always reads in append order. Because the logical clock leaves many rows sharing a single `created_at`, ties in either ordering are settled by server insertion order — the sequence in which the rows were created — and the sighting history, which is ordered globally rather than per animal, falls back to `created_at` and then to insertion order once `sequence_no` ties.

Every list response reports `limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, and `has_more` inside the `meta` object of the envelope. `total_count` reflects the number of rows the caller is entitled to see after filtering, and `has_more` is true when rows remain beyond the current window.

### 2.4. Common Resource Properties and the Programme Clock

Every persisted record carries a server-minted `id` formatted as a UUID with no type prefix, and a `created_at` timestamp rendered as an ISO 8601 UTC string with three fractional digits (for example `2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z`). Most records additionally carry `updated_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp refreshed on every mutation, and `version`, an integer that starts at 1 and increments by one on every mutation of that record — including mutations that only touch derived counters such as an animal count or a conformance score.

`Sighting` rows are the single exception. A sighting is an immutable observation, so a sighting carries only `id` and `created_at`; it has no `updated_at` field and no `version` field. Marking a sighting as retracted is modelled by its own `reversed` flag rather than by a mutation stamp.

Time in this system is a **logical clock**, never the host's wall clock. Before any client sets it, the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and it never falls back to real time. A `ProgramAdmin` may advance or rewind it through an administrative test-driving capability that accepts a single `to_iso8601` field and answers with HTTP 200 and a `data` object whose only field is `current_utc`, carrying the resulting instant. Every system-stamped instant in the programme — `reported_at`, `observed_at`, `opened_at`, `closed_at`, `released_at`, `applied_at`, `retired_at`, `enrolled_at`, `last_hold_released_at`, and `last_reconciliation_run_at` — is read from this logical clock at the moment the operation succeeds.

### 2.5. The Order in Which a Request Is Vetted

Validation is not a bag of independent checks. Rules are evaluated **strictly in ascending priority order**, the first failing rule alone decides the response, and no side effect of any kind is applied once a rule has failed. The ordering matters because several rules are capable of masking one another: a caller lacking permission must never learn from the error code whether a record exists, and a state conflict must never be reported as a capacity failure. The sixteen priority bands are:

1. **Authentication.** Both `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` are present and `X-User-Role` names one of the six declared roles, else `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. **Role authorisation.** The acting role appears in the endpoint's authorised-role list, else `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE`.
3. **Per-field request shape.** Every required body field is present; every scalar has the right type; every enum value names a recognised member; `pin` is exactly seven characters drawn from the digits 0–9 and the uppercase letters A–Z; `supplier_code` is exactly four characters drawn from the uppercase letters A–Z and the digits 0–9; `ain` is exactly fifteen digits; `capacity` is at least 1; `defect_count` is at least 1; `forgiven_defects` is at least 0; `physical_unapplied_count` is at least 0; `lookback_days` is between 1 and 365 inclusive; `max_hops` is between 1 and 5 inclusive; `serial_start` and `serial_end` each lie between 100000000000 and 999999999999 inclusive; `limit` lies between 1 and 100 inclusive; and `offset` is at least 0. Any failure here is `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. Comparisons **between** two fields are deliberately not performed in this band, with one exception: a lot issuance whose `serial_end` is lower than its `serial_start` is rejected here as `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, ahead of every existence check.
4. **Path existence.** Every resource named by a path segment resolves, validated strictly left to right — for a trace-hit response path, the trace request is resolved before the hit animal. The first unresolved segment decides the error, drawn from `ERR_PRODUCER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PREMISES_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SUPPLIER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TAG_LOT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TAG_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SIGHTING_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_MOVEMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TRACE_REQUEST_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_HOLD_ORDER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_RECONCILIATION_RUN_NOT_FOUND`, or `ERR_AWARD_NOT_FOUND`.
5. **Body reference existence.** Every foreign key supplied in the request body resolves, yielding `ERR_PRODUCER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PREMISES_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SUPPLIER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TAG_LOT_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_TRACE_REQUEST_NOT_FOUND` as appropriate. Additionally, when a state official records a response against one hit animal of a trace request, that animal must already appear in that request's frozen hit set, else `ERR_TRACE_HIT_NOT_FOUND`.
6. **Ownership.** The entity-backed ownership rule described in Section 2.1 is applied over its twelve write operations, yielding `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`.
7. **Producer privacy gate.** Applied to reading one animal (`GET /animals/{id}`) and reading one sighting (`GET /sightings/{id}`), yielding `ERR_DATA_ACCESS_DENIED`. The gate is specified in Section 2.7.
8. **Segregation of duties.** Applied to `POST /device-suppliers/{id}/approve` and `POST /trace-requests/{id}/close`, yielding `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`.
9. **Version guard.** The optimistic-concurrency check described in Section 2.6, applied to `POST /tag-lots`, `POST /reconciliation-runs` and `POST /hold-orders`, yielding `ERR_STALE_VERSION`.
10. **Own-state gate.** The target record is currently in a state the requested transition declares as a valid origin state. A tag lot whose status was forced by an external event — `RECALLED` or `VOIDED` — cannot supply an application and yields `ERR_LOT_NOT_ISSUABLE`; a `DEPLETED` lot is instead treated as a capacity failure and is handled in band 16; every other invalid transition yields `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.
11. **Cross-entity state gates.** A lot's device supplier must be `APPROVED` to issue a lot or to apply or replace a tag, else `ERR_SUPPLIER_NOT_APPROVED`. A premises involved in applying a tag, reporting a movement, receiving a movement, retiring a tag or being issued a lot must have `registration_status` equal to `ACTIVE`, else `ERR_PREMISES_NOT_ACTIVE`. A premises involved in applying a tag, departing, receiving, correcting or retiring must not have `health_status` equal to `QUARANTINED`, else `ERR_PREMISES_UNDER_HOLD`. A hold cannot be released, and a trace cannot be cancelled, while the linked trace request is `RUNNING`, else `ERR_TRACE_STILL_OPEN`. A trace cannot be closed while a hold order linked to it sits in `PROPOSED` or `UNDER_APPEAL`, else `ERR_HOLD_PENDING`. An incentive award cannot be accrued or paid unless the owning producer's `enrollment_status` is `ENROLLED`, else `ERR_PRODUCER_NOT_ENROLLED`. Enrolment itself requires at least one premises in `registration_status` `ACTIVE`, else `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_PREMISES`. This band guards `POST /tag-lots`, `POST /tag-lots/{id}/apply-tag`, `POST /tags/{id}/replace`, `POST /tags/{id}/retire`, `POST /movement-events`, `POST /movement-events/{id}/receive`, `POST /movement-events/{id}/correct`, `POST /hold-orders/{id}/release`, `POST /trace-requests/{id}/close`, `POST /trace-requests/{id}/cancel`, `POST /incentive-awards`, `POST /incentive-awards/{id}/pay` and `POST /producers/{id}/enroll`.
12. **Regulatory interval safety.** Guards `POST /movement-events`, `POST /movement-events/{id}/correct` and `POST /hold-orders`, yielding `ERR_ICVI_REQUIRED`, `ERR_HOLD_COOLDOWN_ACTIVE` or `ERR_HOLD_WINDOW_OVERLAP` as detailed in Sections 3.5 and 3.6.
13. **Cross-field temporal checks.** Guards `POST /movement-events`, `POST /movement-events/{id}/receive`, `POST /movement-events/{id}/correct`, `POST /tags/{id}/retire`, `POST /hold-orders`, `POST /reconciliation-runs` and `POST /incentive-awards`, yielding `ERR_INVALID_WINDOW`, `ERR_FUTURE_TIMESTAMP` or `ERR_BACKDATED_EVENT`. Specifically: `effective_until` must be strictly later than `effective_from`, and `period_end` must be the same date as or later than `period_start`, and `arrived_at` must be the same instant as or later than `departed_at` — any of these failing gives `ERR_INVALID_WINDOW`. None of `departed_at`, `arrived_at` or `harvested_at` may be later than the logical clock's current instant, else `ERR_FUTURE_TIMESTAMP`. Both `departed_at` and `harvested_at` must be the same instant as or later than the `observed_at` of the animal's non-reversed sighting bearing the highest `sequence_no`, else `ERR_BACKDATED_EVENT`.
14. **Referential coherence.** Guards `POST /tag-lots/{id}/apply-tag`, `POST /tags/{id}/replace`, `POST /tags/{id}/retire` and `POST /movement-events`, yielding `ERR_AIN_OUT_OF_LOT_RANGE`, `ERR_LOT_PREMISES_MISMATCH`, `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_AT_PREMISES`, `ERR_NOT_HARVEST_FACILITY` or `ERR_SAME_PREMISES_MOVE` as detailed in Sections 3.4, 3.5 and 3.8.
15. **Uniqueness and bounded repetition.** Guards `POST /premises`, `POST /device-suppliers`, `POST /tag-lots`, `POST /tag-lots/{id}/apply-tag`, `POST /tags/{id}/replace`, `POST /movement-events`, `POST /movement-events/{id}/correct`, `POST /hold-orders/{id}/appeal` and `POST /incentive-awards`, yielding `ERR_DUPLICATE_PIN`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_SUPPLIER_CODE`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_AIN`, `ERR_SERIAL_RANGE_OVERLAP`, `ERR_CORRECTION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `ERR_APPEAL_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `ERR_RESIDENCY_OVERLAP` or `ERR_AWARD_PERIOD_DUPLICATE`.
16. **Capacity and quota, evaluated last and against pre-request state.** Guards `POST /tag-lots`, `POST /tag-lots/{id}/apply-tag`, `POST /tags/{id}/replace` and `POST /movement-events/{id}/receive`, yielding `ERR_ISSUANCE_CAP_EXCEEDED`, `ERR_LOT_EXHAUSTED` or `ERR_PREMISES_AT_CAPACITY`.

Because band 16 reads the state as it stood before the request, a lot can never widen the very issuance cap that admits it, and an animal can never be counted into the destination premises it is trying to enter.

### 2.6. Version Guards on Parent Records

Three create operations in this programme make a decision whose correctness depends on a parent record the client read earlier. Each therefore requires an optimistic-concurrency field in its request body; the field is mandatory, not optional, and it carries the `version` the client last observed on the parent.

A `POST /tag-lots` request must carry `expected_supplier_version` matching the current `version` of the `DeviceSupplier` named by `supplier_id`. If the supplier's version has advanced since the client read it, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` and HTTP 412. Every issued lot consumes part of the supplier's serial space and re-derives `issued_tag_count`, `conformance_score` and `issuance_cap`, so a writer holding a stale supplier version would be sizing its lot against an obsolete cap.

A `POST /reconciliation-runs` request must carry `expected_premises_version` matching the current `version` of the `Premises` named by `premises_id`. A mismatch is rejected with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` and HTTP 412. Because a newer run legitimately supersedes any `DRAFT` run covering an overlapping period, a writer holding a stale premises version would silently discard a count it never saw.

A `POST /hold-orders` request must likewise carry `expected_premises_version` matching the target premises' current `version`, and a mismatch is rejected with `ERR_STALE_VERSION` and HTTP 412. Both the cooldown anchor `last_hold_released_at` and the `pending_hold_count` that the overlap check reads may have moved since the issuing official last observed the premises.

No other endpoint accepts a version field. Endpoints that are not one of these three do not take `expected_supplier_version` or `expected_premises_version` at all and perform no version comparison.

### 2.7. Producer Privacy and Read Scope

Producers regard herd composition and movement history as commercially sensitive, so every `Producer` record carries a `privacy_level` of either `STANDARD` or `RESTRICTED`, defaulting to `STANDARD` when the field is omitted at registration. The privacy posture narrows who may read an individual animal or an individual sighting, and it interacts with the animal's epidemiological standing:

- A `Producer` may read only animals whose birth premises or current premises belongs to that producer.
- A `HarvestFacilityOperator` may read only animals whose `current_premises_id` equals its own `X-User-ID`.
- A `StateAnimalHealthOfficial` may read any animal owned by a producer whose `privacy_level` is `STANDARD`; an animal owned by a `RESTRICTED` producer becomes readable only once its `trace_status` is `TRACE_HIT` or `CLEARED`. The producer an animal counts as owned by, for this bullet, is the operator of its `current_premises_id`, falling back to the operator of its `birth_premises_id` whenever the animal is in transit and so has no current premises.
- A `FederalTraceAnalyst` may read only animals that are the index animal of some trace request or that appear in some trace request's hit set, and for those animals the producer's `privacy_level` is irrelevant.
- A `ProgramAdmin` reads without restriction.

Reading a single sighting inherits the gate applied to that sighting's animal. A denied single-resource read returns `ERR_DATA_ACCESS_DENIED`. List endpoints behave differently and deliberately: rather than erroring, they **silently omit** rows the caller may not read, so that a caller cannot probe the existence of a restricted herd through pagination totals. Only the animal listing and the sighting history filter this way, the gate being defined over animals alone; every other collection endpoint returns every row matching its filters to any role authorised to call it.

### 2.8. Derived Counters, Cascades and Hierarchical Consistency

A large share of this system's data is derived rather than supplied. Producer-level figures (`enrolled_head_count`, `incentive_tier`, `on_time_movement_pct`, `incentive_balance_cents`, `lifetime_clawback_cents`), premises-level figures (`animal_count`, `pending_hold_count`), supplier-level figures (`issued_tag_count`, `retired_tag_count`, `retired_returned_count`, `conformance_score`, `probation`, `issuance_cap`), lot-level figures (`quantity`, `applied_count`, `retired_count`, `replaced_count`, `voided_count`, `unapplied_count`) and animal-level figures (`ain`, `current_premises_id`, `lifecycle_status`, `sighting_count`, `last_sequence_no`) are all recomputed by the server the moment their inputs change. No client ever writes them.

Recomputation propagates upward through the ownership chain, and the programme names four recurring cascades so that they can be reasoned about consistently. **Cascade A** runs when a movement is received: the movement's own status advances, an arrival observation is appended, the animal is re-projected to the destination, the destination premises' `animal_count` rises, and the destination producer's `enrolled_head_count` and `incentive_tier` are re-derived. **Cascade B** runs when a device is retired at harvest: the device's status advances, the lot's counters shift, the supplier's retirement statistics and conformance figures are re-derived, and a sufficiently poor score suspends the supplier outright. **Cascade C** runs when a quarantine hold is activated: the premises is quarantined, reported outbound movements are disputed, the animals on those movements are flagged as trace hits, and the destination premises of each disputed movement is moved under investigation. **Cascade D** runs when a reconciliation run is flagged for variance: the run is flagged, the premises registration is suspended, and the owning producer's head count is re-derived, lapsing the producer's enrolment when no active premises remain.

Every record a cascade actually changes is a record that was mutated: its `updated_at` is refreshed and its `version` is incremented, even when the only change was a derived counter — but a recomputation that leaves every derived value exactly as it found it is no mutation at all and re-stamps nothing. A producer sits at the top of every cascade and is recomputed once at the end of the request that marked it, so a request whose hops reach the same producer several times still leaves it with one `updated_at` refresh and one `version` increment. Eight operations stamp unconditionally against that rule: supplier approval, a defect report, reinstatement, lot issuance and lot voiding each increment the supplier's `version`, and proposing a hold, releasing a hold and opening a reconciliation run each increment the premises' `version`, whether or not any derived figure moved. Two invariants hold across all of this. First, a lot's `quantity` always equals the sum of its `unapplied_count`, `applied_count`, `retired_count`, `replaced_count` and `voided_count`. Second, compensating actions restore the totals they unwind: voiding a movement returns the animal to its origin so that the sum of origin and destination `animal_count` returns to its pre-departure value, and clawing back an award returns a producer's `incentive_balance_cents` to its pre-payment value.

### 2.9. Numeric Precision and Money

All monetary amounts in this programme are integers denominated in cents; there are no fractional-cent fields and no currency strings. Rates, percentages, scores and elapsed durations are decimal numbers.

Wherever rounding is required, the method is **round half up**. Scores, percentages and hour measurements (`conformance_score`, `on_time_movement_pct`, `variance_pct`, `reporting_lag_hours`, `elapsed_hours`, `completeness_score`) are rounded to two decimal places. Every such rounded decimal travels in the JSON payload as a number rather than a string, so a two-decimal value of exactly one hundred renders as `100.0`. The single exception is the supplier retention rate, an internal ratio computed to four decimal places before it enters the conformance formula. Monetary results (`fee_cents`, `surcharge_cents`, `awarded_cents`, `clawback_cents`, `incentive_balance_cents`, `lifetime_clawback_cents`, and each `line_cents` inside an award's `line_items`) are whole cents.

Where a monetary total is assembled from several lines, the rounding point is pinned: each line is rounded to whole cents first, and the rounded lines are then summed. Summing unrounded products and rounding once at the end is not equivalent and is not what this programme specifies.

### 2.10. Nested Path Resolution

Two endpoint shapes in this API carry more than one identifier in the path. Identifiers are always resolved left to right, and the first identifier that fails to resolve determines the 404 returned. For `POST /trace-requests/{id}/hits/{animalId}/respond`, the `{id}` segment is resolved to a trace request first — an unknown trace request yields `ERR_TRACE_REQUEST_NOT_FOUND` before the server ever looks at `animalId`. Only once the trace request exists is `animalId` checked, and because the response is recorded against a frozen hit row rather than against the animal table, an animal that exists but is not in that request's hit set yields `ERR_TRACE_HIT_NOT_FOUND`. The same left-to-right discipline applies to the reverse-lookup list paths such as `GET /premises/{id}/reconciliation-runs` and `GET /producers/{id}/incentive-awards`, where the parent must resolve before any listing occurs.

### 2.11. The Sighting Log as the System of Record

Animal location in this programme is never stored as a directly-writable attribute. It is **projected** from an append-only log of observations. Each observation is a `Sighting` row carrying the `animal_id` observed, the `premises_id` where the observation occurred, the `source_movement_id` of the movement that produced it when one applies, a `sighting_type` of `TAG_APPLIED`, `DEPARTURE`, `ARRIVAL` or `HARVEST`, the business instant `observed_at` at which the observation happened, a per-animal `sequence_no`, and a `reversed` flag.

The `sequence_no` starts at 1 for an animal's first observation and increases by exactly one on every subsequent append, including appends that are later reversed. Sequence numbers are never reused and never renumbered. Compensating actions never delete a sighting; they set `reversed` to true, and the retracted row remains in storage and remains retrievable, which is what the `include_reversed` flag on the sighting history exists to expose. Reversed rows are nevertheless excluded from the location projection, from `sighting_count`, from every inventory book balance, and from every traceback computation.

The projection itself replays an animal's non-reversed sightings in ascending `sequence_no` order — that is, in **append order rather than observation order**, so a back-dated `observed_at` never reorders the replay. Walking the rows in that order: a `TAG_APPLIED` row sets the animal's current premises to that row's premises and its `lifecycle_status` to `ACTIVE`; a `DEPARTURE` row clears the current premises to null and sets `lifecycle_status` to `IN_TRANSIT`; an `ARRIVAL` row sets the current premises to that row's premises and `lifecycle_status` back to `ACTIVE`; and a `HARVEST` row sets the current premises to that row's premises and `lifecycle_status` to `HARVESTED`. If no non-reversed rows remain at all, the animal's `lifecycle_status` becomes `VOIDED` and its current premises is null. After each replay, `sighting_count` is set to the number of non-reversed rows and `last_sequence_no` is set to the highest `sequence_no` across all of that animal's rows, reversed rows included.

A premises' `animal_count` is the corresponding roll-up: the number of animals whose `current_premises_id` is that premises **and** whose `lifecycle_status` is `ACTIVE`. It is recomputed for every premises whose membership changed by a replay, which is why an in-transit, harvested or voided animal contributes to no premises' head count.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Bringing Producers and Premises into the Programme

A `ProgramAdmin` opens the file on a new operator by registering a producer with its `legal_name`, a `contact_email` that must contain an "@" character, and a `state_code` giving the two-letter uppercase USPS abbreviation of its home state. The optional `privacy_level` may be supplied as `RESTRICTED`; when it is omitted the producer is created as `STANDARD`. Newly registered producers begin with `enrollment_status` `NOT_ENROLLED`, a null `enrolled_at`, an `enrolled_head_count` of 0, an `incentive_tier` of `TIER_0`, an `on_time_movement_pct` of 100.00, an `incentive_balance_cents` of 0, and a `lifetime_clawback_cents` of 0.

Physical locations are registered by the producer that operates them. A registration supplies `producer_id`, a `pin` — the Premises Identification Number, exactly seven characters over the digits 0–9 and uppercase A–Z, and globally unique across the programme, with a collision rejected as `ERR_DUPLICATE_PIN` — an `operation_type` of `COW_CALF`, `FEEDLOT`, `MARKET` or `HARVEST_FACILITY`, the two-letter uppercase `state_code` of the location itself, and a `capacity` of at least one animal. The location's state code is what later determines whether a movement crosses a state line, so it is recorded separately from the producer's own home state. A freshly registered premises begins with `registration_status` `PENDING_REVIEW`, `health_status` `CLEAR`, `animal_count` 0, `pending_hold_count` 0, and a null `last_hold_released_at` and `last_reconciliation_run_at`.

Registration review belongs to the state. Approval by a `StateAnimalHealthOfficial` moves a premises from `PENDING_REVIEW` to `ACTIVE`, and is permitted only while the premises is in `PENDING_REVIEW`; attempting it from any other registration state is an invalid transition. Because an active premises now contributes its head to the programme, approval re-derives the owning producer's `enrolled_head_count` and `incentive_tier`. That head count is the sum of `animal_count` across every premises the producer owns whose `registration_status` is `ACTIVE`; premises still in `PENDING_REVIEW`, and premises that have been suspended, contribute nothing.

Enrolment is the producer's own act and is self-service: the acting `X-User-ID` must be the producer being enrolled. A producer may enrol from `NOT_ENROLLED` or re-enrol from `LAPSED`, and either way at least one of its premises must currently be in `registration_status` `ACTIVE`, else the attempt is refused with `ERR_NO_ACTIVE_PREMISES`. A successful enrolment moves `enrollment_status` to `ENROLLED`, stamps `enrolled_at` from the logical clock — re-enrolment overwrites the earlier instant rather than preserving it — and recomputes `enrolled_head_count` and `incentive_tier`. Enrolment can also be lost without any producer action: when a variance-flagged reconciliation suspends the producer's last remaining `ACTIVE` premises, the producer's `enrollment_status` moves from `ENROLLED` to `LAPSED`, its `enrolled_head_count` is recomputed to 0 and its `incentive_tier` falls to `TIER_0`, and further incentive accrual is blocked with `ERR_PRODUCER_NOT_ENROLLED` until the producer re-enrols.

Premises can be listed and narrowed by `producer_id`, `state_code`, `operation_type`, `registration_status` and `health_status`; each producer's accrued awards can be listed through its own reverse-lookup listing, optionally narrowed by award `status`; and each premises exposes its own reconciliation history, likewise narrowable by run `status`.

### 3.2. Approving Device Manufacturers and Policing Their Conformance

Official identification devices may only originate from a vetted manufacturer. A `ProgramAdmin` registers a supplier with its `company_name`, a globally unique four-character `supplier_code` over uppercase A–Z and digits — a collision is `ERR_DUPLICATE_SUPPLIER_CODE` — and an optional `aid_prefix`, the exactly-three-digit country prefix that will lead every AIN the supplier stamps. When `aid_prefix` is omitted it defaults to `840`, the United States prefix. The new record begins in `PENDING_REVIEW` with `registered_by_user_id` set to the acting administrator's `X-User-ID`, a null `approved_by_user_id`, a `defect_report_count` of 0, a `conformance_score` of 100.00, `probation` false and an `issuance_cap` of 10000.

Approval moves the supplier from `PENDING_REVIEW` to `APPROVED` and is available only from `PENDING_REVIEW`. It is the segregation-of-duties decision described in Section 2.1: a second `ProgramAdmin`, whose `X-User-ID` differs from the stored `registered_by_user_id`, must perform it, and the approving administrator's identifier is written to `approved_by_user_id`. Approval also re-derives `conformance_score`, `probation` and `issuance_cap`.

Field failures are recorded against the supplier as defect reports. A `StateAnimalHealthOfficial` or a `ProgramAdmin` files one by supplying a `defect_count` of at least 1 and an optional free-text `note`; `defect_report_count` increases by that amount, the conformance figures are re-derived immediately, and the updated supplier is returned; the note is validated as a string but neither stored nor turned into a defect-report record of its own, and a report may be filed against a supplier in any status, `PENDING_REVIEW` and `REVOKED` included. A supplier that is `APPROVED` and whose recomputed score falls below 70.00 transitions to `SUSPENDED`; a supplier that is already `SUSPENDED` and whose recomputed score falls below 40.00 transitions to `REVOKED`, which is terminal — no further transition out of `REVOKED` is permitted. Both of those transitions carry the same collateral consequence: every tag lot belonging to that supplier whose status is `ISSUED` or `ACTIVE` transitions to `RECALLED`. Devices already applied keep their `APPLIED` device status; only the unapplied serials of a recalled lot become unusable, and lot issuance and tag application from that supplier are refused with `ERR_SUPPLIER_NOT_APPROVED`.

Recovery is possible but not automatic. Reinstatement by a `ProgramAdmin` is available only from `SUSPENDED` and requires a `forgiven_defects` count of at least 0 representing the corrective action credited to the manufacturer. The supplier's `defect_report_count` is reduced by that amount, floored at zero, and the conformance figures are re-derived; the supplier returns to `APPROVED` only if the recomputed `conformance_score` is at least 70.00. Lots already recalled are **not** restored — they remain `RECALLED` permanently. Because the approval dimension is cyclic, a manufacturer may be suspended and reinstated repeatedly.

The **conformance score** itself is a weighted deduction from a perfect 100.00. Two ingredients feed it. The first is defect density: the supplier's `defect_report_count` divided by the greater of 1.0 and its `issued_tag_count` expressed in thousands, giving defects per thousand devices issued. The `issued_tag_count` sums the `quantity` of every lot belonging to the supplier whose status is `ISSUED`, `ACTIVE`, `DEPLETED` or `RECALLED`; voided lots are excluded entirely. The second is device retention: when the supplier's `retired_tag_count` is zero the retention rate is taken as 1.0000, giving a brand-new supplier full credit rather than a zero-over-zero penalty; otherwise it is `retired_returned_count` divided by `retired_tag_count`, rounded half up to four decimal places. Both those counts are taken over tags with `device_status` `RETIRED` across the same set of lots. The raw score is 100.00 less 12.0 multiplied by defects per thousand, less 20.0 multiplied by the shortfall of the retention rate below 1.0000; the result is clamped to lie no lower than 0.00 and no higher than 100.00, then rounded half up to two decimal places.

Two derived postures follow from the score and are recomputed alongside it. `probation` is true whenever `conformance_score` is strictly below 85.00, and probation is what attaches the issuance surcharge described in Section 3.3. `issuance_cap` — the largest `quantity` permitted on a single new lot — is 10000 when the score is at least 95.00, 5000 when the score is at least 85.00 but below 95.00, 2000 when the score is at least 70.00 but below 85.00, and 0 when the score is below 70.00. The score, and with it these two postures, is re-derived at supplier approval, on every defect report, on reinstatement, whenever a lot is issued or voided, and as the third hop of the harvest-retirement cascade; the automatic `SUSPENDED` and `REVOKED` transitions, by contrast, fire only on a defect report and at the fourth hop of that cascade, so a re-derivation prompted by approval, reinstatement, issuance or voiding can leave an `APPROVED` supplier sitting below 70.00 without suspending it.

### 3.3. Issuing Serial Ranges to Premises

Only a `DeviceSupplierRep` may issue a lot, and only against its own supplier record. An issuance names the `supplier_id`, the `premises_id` receiving the devices — the two references are resolved in that order, so an unknown supplier masks an unknown premises — the inclusive `serial_start` and `serial_end` bounding the 12-digit AIN serial range, and the `expected_supplier_version` version guard from Section 2.6. Both serial bounds must fall between 100000000000 and 999999999999 inclusive, and `serial_end` must not be lower than `serial_start` — a violation of the latter being an `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` raised in the per-field shape band, before the supplier or the premises is resolved at all. The lot's `quantity` is derived as `serial_end` minus `serial_start` plus one. A new lot's closed serial interval may not intersect the interval of any existing non-voided lot belonging to the same supplier; abutting ranges — where the previous lot's `serial_end` plus one equals the new `serial_start` — are explicitly permitted, while any true intersection is refused with `ERR_SERIAL_RANGE_OVERLAP`. Issuance additionally requires the supplier to be `APPROVED`.

The **issuance fee** is computed once at creation and frozen on the lot's `fee_cents`. Its evaluation order is pinned, because the volume discount and the probation surcharge would otherwise define each other circularly. Step one derives the `quantity` as described above. Step two forms a pre-discount subtotal of a flat base charge of 25,000 cents plus 42 cents for every device in the lot. Step three selects the volume discount percentage **from that pre-surcharge subtotal, never from the post-surcharge total**: 0 percent when the subtotal is below 50,000 cents; 5 percent when the subtotal is at least 50,000 cents but below 150,000 cents; 12 percent when the subtotal is at least 150,000 cents but below 400,000 cents; and 18 percent when the subtotal is 400,000 cents or more. Step four multiplies the subtotal by that percentage and rounds half up to whole cents to give the discount. Step five subtracts the discount from the subtotal to give the discounted total. Step six computes the surcharge: if — and only if — the supplier's `probation` flag is true, the surcharge is 4 percent of the **discounted** total, rounded half up to whole cents; otherwise the surcharge is 0. Step seven sets `fee_cents` to the discounted total plus the surcharge, records the surcharge in `surcharge_cents`, and records the selected percentage in `discount_pct_applied` as a two-decimal fraction of one rather than as a whole-number percentage, so the 12 percent tier is recorded as `0.12` and an undiscounted lot as `0.0`.

Both the surcharge decision and the cap check read the supplier exactly as it stood **before** this lot was counted into `issued_tag_count`. A lot whose `quantity` exceeds the supplier's current `issuance_cap` is refused with `ERR_ISSUANCE_CAP_EXCEEDED`, which is why a supplier sitting at a zero cap cannot issue anything at all until its conformance recovers.

A successful issuance creates the lot in status `ISSUED`, increments the supplier's `version` by one, and re-derives the supplier's `issued_tag_count`, `conformance_score`, `probation` and `issuance_cap`. From `ISSUED` the lot moves to `ACTIVE` on the first successful application against it — that is, when its `applied_count` goes from 0 to 1 as part of that request — and from `ACTIVE` to `DEPLETED` whenever any application, retirement, replacement or void drives its `unapplied_count` to 0. A `DEPLETED` lot has no serial left to give and refuses further applications with `ERR_LOT_EXHAUSTED`. The lot's `unapplied_count` is always its `quantity` less its `applied_count`, `retired_count`, `replaced_count` and `voided_count` — the never-materialised remainder of the range.

An untouched lot can be withdrawn. Voiding by a `ProgramAdmin` moves a lot from `ISSUED` to `VOIDED` and is permitted only while the lot is still `ISSUED`, meaning no tag has ever been applied from it. Voiding removes the lot's `quantity` from the supplier's `issued_tag_count`, re-derives `conformance_score`, `probation` and `issuance_cap`, increments the supplier's `version` by one, and removes the lot from every book balance at its premises under both reconciliation bases. `VOIDED` and `RECALLED` are both terminal lot states, and a lot in either of them that is addressed for an application is refused with `ERR_LOT_NOT_ISSUABLE` — a distinct outcome from the exhaustion of a depleted lot.

Lots may be listed and filtered by `supplier_id`, `premises_id` and `status`.

### 3.4. Identifying an Animal: Application, Replacement and Correction

Applying a device is the moment an animal enters the traceability system, and it materialises three records in a single request: the device, the animal, and the animal's first observation. A `Producer` performs it against one of its own lots — ownership is judged against the operator of the lot's own `premises_id`, so addressing another operator's lot is refused with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` before any coherence rule runs — supplying the `ain` being stamped, the animal's `species` (`BOVINE`, `OVINE`, `CAPRINE` or `CERVID`), its `birth_date` as a `YYYY-MM-DD` string, and the `birth_premises_id` at which the device is being applied. The request answers with the newly created animal.

An `ain` is exactly fifteen digits: the supplier's three-digit `aid_prefix` followed by a twelve-digit serial. Referential coherence is checked on both halves — the leading three digits must equal the `aid_prefix` of the supplier owning the addressed lot, and the trailing twelve digits must fall inside that lot's inclusive `serial_start` to `serial_end` range, with either failure returning `ERR_AIN_OUT_OF_LOT_RANGE`. The lot's own premises and the premises the device is being applied at must belong to the same producer, else `ERR_LOT_PREMISES_MISMATCH`. No two devices in the programme may carry the same `ain`, a collision being `ERR_DUPLICATE_AIN`. Beyond the referential rules, the supplier must be `APPROVED`, the birth premises must be `ACTIVE` in registration and must not be `QUARANTINED` in health, the lot must be neither `RECALLED` nor `VOIDED` nor `DEPLETED`, and admitting one more active animal must not push the birth premises past its `capacity`, else `ERR_PREMISES_AT_CAPACITY`.

On success the device is materialised directly in `device_status` `APPLIED` — a device record exists only from the moment of application, so `APPLIED` is its initial state — with `tag_lot_id` pointing at the supplying lot, `animal_id` pointing at the animal created in the same request, `applied_at` stamped from the logical clock, a null `replaces_tag_id`, a null `retired_at`, a null `carcass_ref` and a null `tag_returned`. A device's `animal_id` is written once at application and is retained thereafter, including after retirement. The animal is created with `lifecycle_status` `ACTIVE`, `trace_status` `NORMAL`, `current_premises_id` equal to the birth premises, and its `ain` denormalised from the device. A `TAG_APPLIED` sighting is appended with `sequence_no` 1 and `observed_at` taken from the logical clock. The lot's `applied_count` rises by one and its `unapplied_count` falls by one, moving the lot from `ISSUED` to `ACTIVE` on the first such application and from `ACTIVE` to `DEPLETED` if `unapplied_count` reaches 0. The birth premises' `animal_count` rises by one, and the owning producer's `enrolled_head_count` and `incentive_tier` are re-derived. Device, animal and sighting are all minted at the logical clock's current instant, and because the animal is created with its projection already applied it is returned at `version` 1.

Devices are lost and become unreadable, so an animal's identity may be carried forward onto a fresh device. Replacement is a producer action available only while the old device is `APPLIED` and the tagged animal is `ACTIVE`, and it supplies a `replacement_ain` and the `tag_lot_id` of the lot the replacement serial comes from. The old device moves `APPLIED → REPLACED`. A new device is materialised in `APPLIED` with `animal_id` unchanged and `replaces_tag_id` pointing back at the device it supersedes, which is what makes replacement chains traversable. The old lot's `applied_count` falls by one while its `replaced_count` rises by one; the new lot's `applied_count` rises by one and its `unapplied_count` falls by one, moving it from `ISSUED` to `ACTIVE` or from `ACTIVE` to `DEPLETED` as applicable. The animal's `ain` is re-derived from the replacement device. Notably, **no sighting is appended** — a replacement is a device event, not an observation, so the animal's location and `sighting_count` are untouched. Replacement is subject to the same supplier-approval, AIN-range, lot-issuability, exhaustion and uniqueness rules as an original application, but to none of its premises rules: no capacity check, no registration or health check and no lot-premises coherence check runs, because the animal is already resident and no observation is appended. Ownership is judged against the animal rather than against the lot — the acting producer must operate the animal's current or birth premises, while the replacement serial may come from a lot issued to any premises. The request answers with the newly created device under HTTP 201.

Erroneous applications are unwound rather than deleted. Voiding an application is a `ProgramAdmin` compensating action, available only while the device is `APPLIED` and the tagged animal is `ACTIVE` — an animal that has been harvested or is in transit cannot have its application voided, and the attempt returns `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. The device moves `APPLIED → VOIDED`. On the lot, `applied_count` falls by one and `voided_count` rises by one, preserving the invariant that `quantity` equals the sum of `unapplied_count`, `applied_count`, `retired_count`, `replaced_count` and `voided_count`. Every one of the animal's sightings is marked `reversed`, so its `sighting_count` becomes 0 and the projection leaves it in `lifecycle_status` `VOIDED`. The `animal_count` at the animal's current premises falls by one and the owning producer's `enrolled_head_count` and `incentive_tier` are re-derived.

Devices may be listed and filtered by `tag_lot_id`, `animal_id`, `replaces_tag_id`, `device_status` and `ain`. Animals may be listed and filtered by `birth_premises_id`, `current_premises_id`, `species`, `lifecycle_status`, `trace_status` and `ain`, with rows the caller may not read silently omitted. The sighting history may be filtered by `animal_id`, `premises_id`, `source_movement_id` and `sighting_type`, and reversed rows are excluded from it unless `include_reversed` is supplied as true.

### 3.5. Reporting Movements Between Premises

A movement is opened by the producer at the departure end. The report supplies the `animal_id` being moved, the `origin_premises_id` it is leaving, the `destination_premises_id` it is bound for, the business instant `departed_at`, and — where required — an `icvi_number`; those three references are resolved in exactly that order, so the first one that fails to resolve decides the 404. The two premises must differ, else `ERR_SAME_PREMISES_MOVE`, and the origin must be exactly where the animal currently stands: the animal's `current_premises_id` must equal `origin_premises_id`, else `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_AT_PREMISES`. The animal must itself be in `lifecycle_status` `ACTIVE` — reporting a departure for an animal already `IN_TRANSIT`, `HARVESTED` or `VOIDED` is an invalid transition answered with `ERR_INVALID_STATE` — and the origin premises must be `ACTIVE` in registration and must not be `QUARANTINED`, while the destination premises' own registration and health are not examined until arrival is confirmed.

Whether a movement crosses a state line is derived, not declared: `interstate` is true exactly when the origin premises' `state_code` differs from the destination premises' `state_code`. An interstate movement requires a non-null `icvi_number` — the Interstate Certificate of Veterinary Inspection — and omitting it is refused with `ERR_ICVI_REQUIRED`. Intrastate movements may leave it null.

Reporting the departure advances the animal from `ACTIVE` to `IN_TRANSIT`. The movement is created with `status` `REPORTED` and a `reported_at` stamped from the logical clock; that stamp is **frozen at creation and is never re-stamped**, not even by a later correction. A `DEPARTURE` sighting is appended with `observed_at` equal to `departed_at`, which re-projects `current_premises_id` to null; the origin premises' `animal_count` falls by one; and the origin producer's `on_time_movement_pct`, `enrolled_head_count` and `incentive_tier` are all re-derived.

**Reporting timeliness** is the programme's discipline metric. A movement's `reporting_lag_hours` is the interval from `departed_at` to the frozen `reported_at`, taken in seconds, divided by 3600, and rounded half up to two decimal places. The movement's `on_time` flag is true when that lag is at most 72.00 hours — three days, with a lag of exactly 72.00 counting as on time. Rolled up to the producer, `on_time_movement_pct` counts every movement whose origin premises belongs to that producer and whose `status` is `REPORTED`, `RECEIVED` or `DISPUTED` as the denominator, and those of them whose `on_time` is true as the numerator; the percentage is 100.0 multiplied by numerator over denominator, rounded half up to two decimal places. A producer with no qualifying movements at all scores 100.00 rather than a zero-over-zero result. Voided movements leave both the numerator and the denominator entirely.

Arrival is confirmed by the receiving producer, who supplies the business instant `arrived_at`. Receiving is available only from `REPORTED`; `arrived_at` may not precede `departed_at`, else `ERR_INVALID_WINDOW`; the destination premises must be `ACTIVE` in registration and must not be `QUARANTINED`; and admitting one more animal must keep the destination's `animal_count` within its `capacity`, else `ERR_PREMISES_AT_CAPACITY`. Success runs Cascade A end to end: the movement advances `REPORTED → RECEIVED`; an `ARRIVAL` sighting is appended with `observed_at` equal to `arrived_at`; the new sighting re-projects the animal from `IN_TRANSIT` back to `ACTIVE` with `current_premises_id` set to the destination; the destination premises' `animal_count` rises by one; and, because that head count changed, the destination producer's `enrolled_head_count` is recomputed and its `incentive_tier` re-derived.

A state official who doubts a movement disputes it, optionally recording a `reason` — validated as a string when supplied but not persisted, the movement carrying no `reason` field of its own. Disputing is available from either `REPORTED` or `RECEIVED`, and the consequences differ sharply between the two. Disputing a movement that was merely `REPORTED` reverses no sighting and leaves the animal's `lifecycle_status` untouched — it stays `IN_TRANSIT`. Disputing a movement that had already been `RECEIVED` triggers a conditional cascade: the `ARRIVAL` sighting is marked `reversed`, the animal re-projects from `ACTIVE` back to `IN_TRANSIT`, and the destination premises' `animal_count` falls by one.

Disputes are answered by correction, and the correction cycle between `REPORTED` and `DISPUTED` is deliberately bounded. The producer that operates the origin premises corrects a disputed movement by supplying a revised `departed_at` and, where the movement is interstate, a revised `icvi_number`. Correction is available only from `DISPUTED`; it requires the movement's `correction_count` to be strictly below 3, with a fourth attempt refused as `ERR_CORRECTION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`; and it requires the origin premises not to be `QUARANTINED`, else `ERR_PREMISES_UNDER_HOLD`. A corrected `departed_at` may not be later than an `arrived_at` already recorded on that movement, because the animal would then be resident at the origin after it had already arrived at the destination; that contradiction is refused with `ERR_RESIDENCY_OVERLAP`. Correction moves the movement `DISPUTED → REPORTED`, increments `correction_count` by one, overwrites `departed_at` and `icvi_number` with the corrected values, and rewrites the `DEPARTURE` sighting's `observed_at` to match. Because `reported_at` stays frozen while `departed_at` moves, `reporting_lag_hours` and `on_time` are recomputed from the frozen report instant against the corrected departure instant — which means moving `departed_at` forward can legitimately flip a late movement to on time — and the origin producer's `on_time_movement_pct` is re-derived.

The last resort is voiding, a `ProgramAdmin` compensating action available only from `DISPUTED`. The movement moves `DISPUTED → VOIDED`; every sighting whose `source_movement_id` is that movement is marked `reversed`; the animal is re-projected from its surviving sightings, returning it to the origin premises with `lifecycle_status` `ACTIVE`; the origin premises' `animal_count` rises by one, restoring the sum of the origin and destination head counts to its pre-departure value; and the movement leaves the origin producer's `on_time_movement_pct` denominator entirely. Note the asymmetry with a receive-then-void sequence: voiding is reachable only through a dispute, so a received movement must first be disputed before it can be voided.

Movements may be listed and filtered by `animal_id`, `origin_premises_id`, `destination_premises_id`, `status` and `on_time`.

### 3.6. Quarantine Holds, Appeals and the Investigation Cascade

A hold order is the state's instrument for freezing activity at a location. A `StateAnimalHealthOfficial` proposes one against a `premises_id`, bounding it with an inclusive `effective_from` and an exclusive `effective_until`, carrying the `expected_premises_version` guard, and optionally naming the `trace_request_id` of the investigation it is issued under. The window must be non-degenerate: `effective_until` must be strictly later than `effective_from`, else `ERR_INVALID_WINDOW`.

Two regulatory interval rules protect producers from being held continuously. First, a **72-hour cooldown**: a premises that has had a hold released records the release instant in `last_hold_released_at`, and a new hold whose `effective_from` falls earlier than three days after that instant is refused with `ERR_HOLD_COOLDOWN_ACTIVE`. Second, an **overlap rule**: the new half-open window from `effective_from` up to but not including `effective_until` may not intersect the window of any existing hold on the same premises whose `status` is `PROPOSED`, `ACTIVE` or `UNDER_APPEAL`, and an intersection is refused with `ERR_HOLD_WINDOW_OVERLAP`. Windows that merely touch — where the previous hold's `effective_until` equals the new hold's `effective_from` — do not intersect and are permitted.

A proposed hold is created in `PROPOSED` with `issued_by_user_id` set to the acting official's `X-User-ID`, a null `released_at`, and an `appeal_count` of 0. Creating it raises the target premises' `pending_hold_count` by one — that counter being the number of hold orders on the premises whose `status` is `PROPOSED`, `ACTIVE` or `UNDER_APPEAL` — and increments the premises' `version` by one, which is precisely why the next writer against that premises needs a fresh version number.

Activation is where the epidemiological cascade fires. Activating is available only from `PROPOSED`, and the target premises must not still be in `registration_status` `PENDING_REVIEW`, else `ERR_PREMISES_NOT_ACTIVE`. The hold moves `PROPOSED → ACTIVE` and Cascade C unfolds in three linked hops. Hop one quarantines the premises: its `health_status` becomes `QUARANTINED`, whether it was previously `CLEAR` or `UNDER_INVESTIGATION`. Hop two disputes the outbound traffic: every movement event whose `origin_premises_id` is that premises and whose `status` is `REPORTED` transitions `REPORTED → DISPUTED`. Hop two-b flags the animals: each animal that was the subject of one of those disputed movements transitions `trace_status` from `NORMAL` to `TRACE_HIT`, and it does so independently of its `lifecycle_status`, which remains `IN_TRANSIT`. Hop three widens the investigation: the `destination_premises_id` of each movement disputed by hop two transitions its own `health_status` from `CLEAR` to `UNDER_INVESTIGATION`. A premises under investigation remains fully operable; the significance of the state is that a subsequent hold activation there moves it straight to `QUARANTINED`.

Producers may contest an active hold, and the appeal cycle between `ACTIVE` and `UNDER_APPEAL` is bounded at two appeals. Appealing is available only from `ACTIVE`, may be performed only by the producer that owns the held premises — a different producer receives `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` — and requires `appeal_count` to be strictly below 2, with a third attempt refused as `ERR_APPEAL_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`. The appellant may attach free-text `grounds`, validated as a string when supplied but not persisted on the hold order. A successful appeal moves the hold `ACTIVE → UNDER_APPEAL` and increments `appeal_count` by one; the premises stays `QUARANTINED` for the duration of the appeal. Denying the appeal, available only from `UNDER_APPEAL`, returns the hold to `ACTIVE` and changes no counter.

Release ends the hold, moving it to the terminal `RELEASED` state from either `ACTIVE` or `UNDER_APPEAL`, with an appeal in flight simply abandoned; the side effects are identical from both origin states. When the hold names a trace request, that trace must not still be `RUNNING`, else the release is refused with `ERR_TRACE_STILL_OPEN`. Release stamps `released_at` from the logical clock, lowers the premises' `pending_hold_count` by one, moves the premises' `health_status` from `QUARANTINED` back to `CLEAR` — but only when no other hold on that premises remains in `ACTIVE` or `UNDER_APPEAL` — and stamps `last_hold_released_at`, arming the 72-hour cooldown for the next proposal. Every animal currently resident at the released premises whose `trace_status` is `TRACE_HIT` transitions to `CLEARED`. Two consequences deserve emphasis: movement events disputed by the quarantine cascade are **not** automatically restored by release and must be corrected explicitly by their producers; and the read exposure that `TRACE_HIT` granted is retained through `CLEARED`, so a cleared animal belonging to a `RESTRICTED` producer stays readable by a `StateAnimalHealthOfficial`.

Hold orders may be listed and filtered by `premises_id`, `trace_request_id` and `status`.

### 3.7. Disease Traceback: Scope, Response and Scoring

A traceback is opened by a `FederalTraceAnalyst` naming the `index_animal_id` the investigation starts from, a `pathogen_class` of `DIRECT_CONTACT` or `ENVIRONMENTAL`, a `direction` of `BACKWARD`, `FORWARD` or `BOTH`, a `lookback_days` window between 1 and 365 inclusive, and a `max_hops` depth between 1 and 5 inclusive. The request is created in `status` `SUBMITTED` with `opened_at` stamped from the logical clock — this is the instant the 48-hour programme goal is measured from — and `opened_by_user_id` set to the acting analyst. Its `hits` array starts empty, its `hit_count`, `premises_touched_count`, `hops_total` and `hops_unreachable` start at 0, and its `variant_applied`, `closed_at`, `closed_by_user_id`, `elapsed_hours`, `completeness_score` and `trace_goal_met` all start null.

Running the traceback moves the request `SUBMITTED → RUNNING` and is available only from `SUBMITTED`; the index animal must not be in `lifecycle_status` `VOIDED`, else `ERR_INVALID_STATE`. The run freezes a hit set by replaying the sighting graph.

**Residency intervals** are the unit of contact. For a given animal, its non-reversed sightings are ordered by `observed_at` ascending with `sequence_no` ascending as the tie-break — deliberately a different ordering from the location projection of Section 2.11, which replays by append order. Every row that sets a location, meaning every `TAG_APPLIED` and every `ARRIVAL`, opens a residency at that row's premises beginning at its `observed_at`; the residency ends at the `observed_at` of the next `DEPARTURE` or `HARVEST` row for that animal, or runs open-ended if no such row exists. Residency intervals are half-open: they include their start instant and exclude their end instant.

**Contact** between the index animal's residency and a candidate animal's residency is judged by the variant the pathogen class selects, and the selected variant is recorded on `variant_applied`. A `DIRECT_CONTACT` pathogen selects `STRICT_INTERVAL`: the two residencies must overlap for a strictly positive duration, so animals that stood at the same premises days apart are not contacts. An `ENVIRONMENTAL` pathogen selects `PREMISES_WINDOW`: the index residency is first widened by 14 days before its start and 14 days after its end, and the widened interval must then overlap the candidate residency for a strictly positive duration, so animals that occupied the premises up to a fortnight before the index animal arrived or up to a fortnight after it left do count as contacts.

The traversal is a breadth-first walk. A cutoff instant is fixed at the run instant less `lookback_days` days, and only residencies ending after that cutoff are explored. The frontier begins as the index animal alone at depth 0; while the depth is below `max_hops` and the frontier is non-empty, the depth increases by one and every residency of every frontier animal is examined. The `direction` filter narrows which residencies are explored relative to the start of the index animal's first residency: `BACKWARD` keeps residencies starting at or before it, `FORWARD` keeps residencies starting at or after it, and `BOTH` keeps all of them. For each explored residency, candidate animals are any animals other than the index animal holding a residency at the same premises that satisfies the contact test, considered in order of candidate residency start ascending and then by the candidate animal's `created_at` ascending. A candidate already recorded as a hit is skipped and is never re-added at a deeper level. Each new candidate is appended to `hits` as an object carrying its `animal_id`, the `premises_id` of the residency it was found at, the `hop_depth` at which it was found, a `first_contact_at` equal to the later of the two residency start instants, and `responded` and `resolved` flags both starting false; the candidate then joins the next frontier. The frozen `hits` array is ordered by `hop_depth` ascending, then `first_contact_at` ascending, then the hit animal's `created_at` ascending, with server insertion order settling any tie that survives all three keys — the same fallback that settles ties in the candidate scan preceding the sort.

Three counters are frozen alongside the hit set. `hit_count` is the number of hits, never including the index animal itself. `premises_touched_count` is the number of distinct premises across the hits taken together with the premises the index animal itself occupied. `hops_total` is the number of distinct movement events that produced any traversed arrival or departure sighting. Every animal that appears in the frozen hit set transitions `trace_status` from `NORMAL` to `TRACE_HIT`, which is what grants the widened read access described in Section 2.7 to federal analysts and to state officials even where the owning producer is `RESTRICTED`.

Each hit is worked by the states. A `StateAnimalHealthOfficial` records the outcome for one hit animal by supplying a boolean `resolved`, and the request's own status is not gated — a response may be recorded while the trace is `RUNNING` and equally after it has been `COMPLETED` or `CANCELLED`, since the hit rows are frozen; the corresponding hit row's `responded` becomes true and its `resolved` takes the supplied value, and the response carries the whole trace request back. An animal not present in that request's hit set yields `ERR_TRACE_HIT_NOT_FOUND`.

Closing is available only from `RUNNING` and is the second segregation-of-duties decision: the closing analyst's `X-User-ID` must differ from the stored `opened_by_user_id`, else `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. A trace may not be closed while any hold order naming it is still `PROPOSED` or `UNDER_APPEAL`, else `ERR_HOLD_PENDING`. Closing moves the request `RUNNING → COMPLETED`, stamps `closed_at` and `closed_by_user_id`, and recomputes `hops_unreachable` as the number of traversed movement events whose `status` at close is either `DISPUTED` or `VOIDED`.

The **completeness score** is then computed. An empty hit set short-circuits the whole formula: when `hit_count` is 0 the `completeness_score` is exactly 100.00, on the principle that a traceback with nothing to trace is fully traced. Otherwise three ratios are formed over the frozen hits as they stand at close: the responded ratio is the number of hits whose `responded` is true divided by `hit_count`; the resolved ratio is the number of hits whose `resolved` is true divided by `hit_count`; and the reach ratio is 1.0 less the smaller of 1.0 and `hops_unreachable` divided by the greater of 1 and `hops_total`. The raw score weights them at 40.0 for the responded ratio, 35.0 for the resolved ratio and 25.0 for the reach ratio, each of the first two ratios first capped at 1.0. Two penalties are then deducted. A flat 10.0 is deducted if any premises appearing in the hits has a `registration_status` other than `ACTIVE`. A further 5.0 is deducted for each traversed movement event whose `status` at close is `DISPUTED`, with that dispute penalty capped at 15.0 in total — note that a voided hop lowers the reach ratio without contributing to this dispute penalty, because `hops_unreachable` counts both disputed and voided hops while the penalty counts only disputed ones. The raw score less the penalties is clamped to lie no lower than 0.00 and no higher than 100.00, then rounded half up to two decimal places.

Finally the programme goal is evaluated. The request's `elapsed_hours` is the interval from `opened_at` to `closed_at` in seconds, divided by 3600, rounded half up to two decimal places. `trace_goal_met` is true only when `elapsed_hours` is at most 48.00 **and** `completeness_score` is at least 80.00; both boundaries are inclusive, so exactly 48.00 hours and exactly 80.00 points still meet the goal.

Abandoning a traceback is also available. Cancellation moves the request to the terminal `CANCELLED` state and is permitted from `SUBMITTED` or from `RUNNING`, but a trace cancelled out of `RUNNING` may not be cancelled while a hold order naming it is `ACTIVE` or `UNDER_APPEAL`, else `ERR_TRACE_STILL_OPEN`; cancelling out of `SUBMITTED` carries no such hold precondition. A `CANCELLED` request computes no hits, and its `completeness_score` and `trace_goal_met` remain null; animals already flagged keep `trace_status` `TRACE_HIT`, because cancellation does not clear them. Trace requests may be listed and filtered by `index_animal_id`, `status` and `trace_goal_met`.

### 3.8. Closing Out at Harvest and Reconciling the Tag Inventory

Retirement is the end of an animal's traceable life and the point at which the device leaves circulation. Only a `HarvestFacilityOperator` may retire a device, and only at its own facility: the supplied `harvest_premises_id` must equal the operator's `X-User-ID`. The request additionally supplies the business instant `harvested_at`, the establishment's `carcass_ref`, and a boolean `tag_returned` recording whether the physical device was actually recovered from the carcass.

Retirement is available only while the device is `APPLIED`. The tagged animal must be `ACTIVE` and must currently stand at the harvest premises — its `current_premises_id` must equal `harvest_premises_id`, else `ERR_ANIMAL_NOT_AT_PREMISES`. The harvest premises must have `operation_type` `HARVEST_FACILITY`, else `ERR_NOT_HARVEST_FACILITY`; it must be `ACTIVE` in registration; and it must not be `QUARANTINED`. The `harvested_at` instant may not be earlier than the `observed_at` of the animal's non-reversed sighting carrying the highest `sequence_no`, else `ERR_BACKDATED_EVENT`, and it may not be later than the logical clock's current instant.

Success runs Cascade B through four hops. Hop one retires the device itself: `retired_at` is stamped from the logical clock, `tag_returned` and `carcass_ref` are recorded, and `device_status` moves `APPLIED → RETIRED`. Hop two shifts the lot: because a device retired, the lot's `applied_count` falls by one and its `retired_count` rises by one, and if the lot's `unapplied_count` is now 0 the lot moves `ACTIVE → DEPLETED`. Hop three re-derives the supplier: because the lot counters changed, the supplier's `retired_tag_count` is recomputed, and its `retired_returned_count` is recomputed as well whenever `tag_returned` was true, which re-derives `conformance_score`, `probation` and `issuance_cap`. Hop four applies the discipline: a recomputed score below 70.00 moves an `APPROVED` supplier to `SUSPENDED`, recalling that supplier's `ISSUED` and `ACTIVE` lots as described in Section 3.2. Alongside the cascade, a `HARVEST` sighting is appended with `observed_at` equal to `harvested_at` and `premises_id` equal to the harvest premises, which re-projects the animal from `ACTIVE` to `HARVESTED`. A harvested animal keeps `current_premises_id` at the harvest premises but stops counting toward that premises' `animal_count`, so the head count falls by one and the owning producer's `enrolled_head_count` and `incentive_tier` are re-derived.

Periodically the physical devices on hand at a premises are counted and compared against what the books say should be there. A `Producer` opens a reconciliation for one of its own premises by naming the `premises_id`, the inclusive `period_start` and `period_end` dates in `YYYY-MM-DD` form with the end no earlier than the start, and the `expected_premises_version` guard. The run is created in `DRAFT` with `physical_unapplied_count`, `basis`, `book_unapplied_count`, `variance_count`, `variance_pct`, `submitted_by_user_id` and `approved_by_user_id` all null until the count is submitted, the premises' `last_reconciliation_run_at` is stamped from the logical clock, and the premises' `version` increments by one. Opening a run also supersedes stale work: any existing `DRAFT` run on the same premises whose period overlaps the new one transitions to `SUPERSEDED`, which is terminal — a superseded run can never be submitted, and the attempt returns `ERR_INVALID_STATE`.

Submitting the count is likewise the owning producer's act, available only from `DRAFT`, and supplies the `physical_unapplied_count` of devices actually found on hand, which must be at least 0. The run moves `DRAFT → SUBMITTED`, records `submitted_by_user_id`, and computes the book side of the comparison.

The **book balance** is computed under one of two bases, and the run's `basis` is selected from the premises' `operation_type` rather than from anything in the request. Cow-calf and feedlot operations record a `basis` of `AS_ISSUED`; markets and harvest facilities record a `basis` of `AS_SIGHTED`. Either way the lots considered are those whose `premises_id` is this premises and whose `status` is `ISSUED`, `ACTIVE` or `DEPLETED`; recalled and voided lots are excluded from both bases. Under `AS_ISSUED`, the book follows the devices: the `book_unapplied_count` is the sum, over those lots, of each lot's `quantity` less its `applied_count`, less its `retired_count`, less its `replaced_count`, less its `voided_count`. Under `AS_SIGHTED`, the book follows the events: the count of non-reversed `TAG_APPLIED` sightings whose `premises_id` is this premises and whose `observed_at` falls between the very start of `period_start` and the very end of `period_end` inclusive is subtracted from the plain sum of those lots' `quantity` values, ignoring the lot counters entirely. The two bases return different integers for the same underlying data whenever a device issued to one premises is applied at a sister premises inside the same producer's operation — `AS_ISSUED` still charges the device to the premises it was issued to, while `AS_SIGHTED` charges it only to the premises where the application was observed.

The **variance** follows immediately. The signed `variance_count` is `physical_unapplied_count` less `book_unapplied_count`. The `variance_pct` is 100.0 multiplied by the absolute value of `variance_count`, divided by the greater of 1 and `book_unapplied_count`, rounded half up to two decimal places. Only this run's own two counts feed the calculation; earlier runs are never netted in.

Adjudication belongs to the state and branches on a single boundary. Approving a run is available only from `SUBMITTED`, records `approved_by_user_id`, and then takes one of two disjoint paths. When `variance_pct` is at most 0.50 — a variance of exactly 0.50 percent is inside tolerance — the run moves `SUBMITTED → APPROVED`, and if the premises was sitting in `registration_status` `SUSPENDED` it is reinstated to `ACTIVE`, recomputing the owning producer's `enrolled_head_count` and `incentive_tier`. This is what makes the registration dimension cyclic: a clean count restores a suspended location. When `variance_pct` exceeds 0.50 the run moves `SUBMITTED → VARIANCE_FLAGGED` and Cascade D runs in three hops: hop one records `approved_by_user_id` and flags the run; hop two suspends the target premises, moving its `registration_status` from `ACTIVE` to `SUSPENDED`, which blocks tag application, movement reporting and lot issuance there with `ERR_PREMISES_NOT_ACTIVE`; hop three re-derives the owning producer, recomputing `enrolled_head_count` without the suspended premises' contribution and re-deriving `incentive_tier`, and lapsing the producer's `enrollment_status` from `ENROLLED` to `LAPSED` if no `ACTIVE` premises remains.

### 3.9. Earning, Paying and Reversing the Enrolment Incentive

The programme pays enrolled producers per head of identified livestock, scaled by herd size and by reporting discipline. A `ProgramAdmin` accrues one award per producer per period, naming the `producer_id` and the inclusive `period_start` and `period_end` dates in `YYYY-MM-DD` form with the end no earlier than the start. The producer's `enrollment_status` must be `ENROLLED` at that moment, else `ERR_PRODUCER_NOT_ENROLLED`; and at most one award in status `ACCRUED` or `PAID` may exist for a given producer and identical period pair, with a second refused as `ERR_AWARD_PERIOD_DUPLICATE`.

Accrual freezes three inputs onto the award so that later drift cannot change a settled amount. The `head_count_snapshot` copies the producer's `enrolled_head_count` at that instant. The `tier_factor` is the multiplier attached to the producer's `incentive_tier`, and the tier table is read directly from the head count: an enrolled head count of 0 is `TIER_0` with a factor of 0.00; from 1 through 99 head is `TIER_1` with a factor of 1.00; from 100 through 499 head is `TIER_2` with a factor of 1.15; and 500 head or more is `TIER_3` with a factor of 1.30. The `compliance_multiplier` is read from the producer's `on_time_movement_pct`: at least 95.00 percent earns 1.25; at least 80.00 but below 95.00 earns 1.00; at least 50.00 but below 80.00 earns 0.60; and below 50.00 earns 0.00, extinguishing the award entirely. Both frozen multipliers are recorded on the award as two-decimal numbers.

The award is then built as a list of per-premises lines. Only premises whose `registration_status` is `ACTIVE` at the accrual instant contribute, and they contribute in order of their own `created_at` ascending; a suspended or still-pending premises produces no line at all, rather than a zero-valued line. Each line records the `premises_id`, the premises' `animal_count`, and a `line_cents` amount computed as a base rate of 137 cents per head multiplied by that premises' `animal_count`, multiplied by the frozen `tier_factor`, multiplied by the frozen `compliance_multiplier`, and **rounded half up to whole cents at the line level**. Because `animal_count` is the live count of `ACTIVE` animals resident at that premises, in-transit, harvested and voided animals are excluded from it. The award's `awarded_cents` is the plain sum of the rounded `line_cents` values across `line_items`; the rounding is never deferred to the sum. A new award is created in `status` `ACCRUED` with `clawback_cents` at 0.

Payment is available only from `ACCRUED` and again requires the owning producer to be `ENROLLED`, else `ERR_PRODUCER_NOT_ENROLLED`. The award moves `ACCRUED → PAID` and the producer's `incentive_balance_cents` increases by the award's `awarded_cents`.

Reversal is the compensating action, available only from `PAID`. The award moves `PAID → CLAWED_BACK`, its `clawback_cents` is set equal to its `awarded_cents`, the producer's `incentive_balance_cents` decreases by that same amount — restoring the balance exactly to its pre-payment value — and the producer's `lifetime_clawback_cents` increases by that amount. Lifetime clawbacks only ever accumulate; the figure never decreases. Consistent with that, a producer's `incentive_balance_cents` is always the sum of `awarded_cents` across its `PAID` awards less the sum of `clawback_cents` across its `CLAWED_BACK` awards. `CLAWED_BACK` is terminal.
