## 71. SaaS Subscription Billing System

**Extended System Functional Description:**

SaaS subscription billing system: tenant management with tenant profile, contact information, billing entity (independent/parent-subsidiary), tenant-status (trial/active/suspended/terminated) management, and tenant data isolation; package definition (tiered/usage-based/hybrid) with package-type configuration, included quotas (seats/calls/storage), tiered-pricing brackets, overage rates, package add-ons, and package-version effective-date control; subscription upgrade/downgrade (pro-rated by day) with upgrade immediate effect and pro-rated charging, downgrade end-of-cycle effect or immediate refund, mid-cycle plan change rules, quota carry-forward and reset rules, and trial-to-paid conversion; usage data collection with multi-dimension metering (API call/storage/bandwidth/active user), real-time and batch ingestion, idempotent data processing, late-arrival data handling, and usage-data archiving; bill generation with billing cycle (monthly/quarterly/annual), pre-paid and post-paid combination, tax calculation by jurisdiction, multi-currency settlement, discount and credit application, and bill-issuance workflow; overdue handling with payment-due reminder, tiered overdue rules (read-only/feature-restriction/suspension/termination), late-fee calculation, payment-recovery workflow, dunning notification, and reactivation-on-payment; event-driven synchronization with payment, accounting, CRM, and tax-compliance systems; audit logging for subscription changes and billing adjustments.
