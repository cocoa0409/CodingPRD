# School Health Office API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

School nursing sits at the intersection of clinical practice, custodial duty and public-records law, and the paper systems it has historically run on cannot carry that weight. A single district nurse may cover three buildings across a week, hold delegation agreements with unlicensed aides, keep controlled medication in a locked cabinet whose unit count must reconcile to the dose, and be asked — often the same afternoon — to release a child's health file to an outside provider. When any one of those threads is tracked informally, the failure is not a paperwork failure: an epinephrine auto-injector expires unnoticed, a child is returned to class who should have been sent home, or a mental-health note leaves the building in a package nobody approved.

The School Health Office API is the district's system of record for that work. It is mounted under `/health-office` and models eleven peer record families — buildings, nurses, coverage blocks, students, chronic-condition action plans, medication orders, medication stock lots, office encounters, administration records, screenings and disclosure requests — as flat collections joined by identifier references rather than by nested URLs. Every clinically meaningful act is a state transition on one of those records, every quantity that can be miscounted is a conserved counter, and every act that ought to require two people genuinely requires two distinct operator identities.

Three design commitments run through everything that follows. First, the system is **deterministic**: it reads a server-held logical clock rather than wall-clock time, ranks candidate medication lots by an explicitly pinned total order, and rounds every derived figure at a pinned point in the arithmetic. Second, it is **defensively ordered**: when a request violates several rules at once, exactly one error is returned, and which one is fixed by a global rejection ladder rather than by implementation accident. Third, it is **conservative about release**: health information leaves the building only through a request that a guardian consented to, a physician decided, and a *different* operator handed over.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to the API must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role that principal is acting in. If either header is absent, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the six declared role names, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401) before anything else about the request is inspected. If the headers are well formed but the named role does not appear in the target endpoint's authorised-role list, the rejection is `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403).

The six roles are `DistrictHealthAdmin`, `SchoolNurse`, `HealthAide`, `Guardian`, `SchoolPhysician` and `RecordsOfficer`. They divide as follows.

- A **`DistrictHealthAdmin`** is the district health services operator. It owns buildings, nurse records, coverage blocks, medication inventory, the logical clock and the daily checkpoint, and it never touches a clinical encounter. It shares student enrolment and student amendment with `SchoolNurse`, and it may read every collection.
- A **`SchoolNurse`** is a licensed nurse — credential `RN` or `LPN` — who runs the office. Drafting and submitting action plans, creating medication orders, verifying an order clinically, reopening an encounter, voiding an administration record, and the whole screening programme are theirs alone. Encounter creation, assessment, disposition and closure, and the three administration steps, are shared with `HealthAide`. Only a nurse whose `credential` is `RN` may record a clinical verification.
- A **`HealthAide`** is unlicensed assistive personnel whose `credential` is `HEALTH_AIDE`. An aide may staff an office and give delegated, non-controlled medication, but only while the aide's `delegation_authorized` flag is true; every administration step by an aide without that flag, and every administration step by an aide on a controlled medication, is refused with `ERR_CREDENTIAL_NOT_DELEGATED`. An aide has no exclusive operations, cannot reopen an encounter, cannot verify a medication order, cannot void a record, and cannot touch action plans or screenings.
- A **`Guardian`** is a parent or guardian acting for exactly one student. Guardians grant or withdraw consent on action plans, on medication orders and on records-release requests, and may read that one child's student row, action plans, medication orders, encounters, screenings and disclosure requests.
- A **`SchoolPhysician`** is the medical director. Physicians decide and revoke action plans, suspend medication orders on clinical grounds, and approve or deny records releases.
- A **`RecordsOfficer`** is the records custodian: opening release requests, submitting them for consent, and handing over approved packages.

**Identity semantics differ by role, and the difference is load-bearing.** When `X-User-Role` is `Guardian`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Student`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `Student` compare the resource's `student_id` to `X-User-ID`. When `X-User-Role` is `SchoolNurse` or `HealthAide`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Nurse`; ownership checks compare `HealthVisit.nurse_id` to `X-User-ID` on the assess, dispose, close and reopen steps, and compare `AdministrationRecord.administering_nurse_id` to `X-User-ID` on the outcome and void steps. When `X-User-Role` is `DistrictHealthAdmin`, `SchoolPhysician` or `RecordsOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server may record it — a physician's identifier is stored as `decided_by_user_id` on a `DisclosureRequest` it approves, and a records officer's identifier is stored as `released_by_user_id` on a request it releases — but it never compares such an identifier to any entity's `id`. Any write by an entity-backed role against a row it does not own is refused with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403).
### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

All list endpoints paginate with two query parameters. `limit` defaults to 10, may be no smaller than 1 and no larger than 100. `offset` defaults to 0 and may be no smaller than 0. A `limit` outside the inclusive range 1 to 100, or a negative `offset`, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` (HTTP 422). Results are ordered by `created_at` ascending unless a subsection below states otherwise, and the `meta` object of a list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` (the number of rows matching the filter before the page window is applied) and `has_more` (true when rows remain beyond the current window). Filter parameters listed for a given collection combine conjunctively; an omitted filter constrains nothing.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Versioning and Timestamps

Every stored record carries a server-minted `id`, which is a UUID with no type prefix; clients never supply it. Every record also carries `created_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits recorded at insert time. All eleven record families in this system additionally carry `updated_at`, refreshed on every mutation to the record, and `version`, an integer that begins at 1 on creation and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation of that record. There is no entity in this system that omits `updated_at` or `version`.

A "mutation" for versioning purposes includes state transitions, field amendments, and cascade-driven changes written by the server on the operator's behalf. When one operation touches several records — and in this domain most operations do — each touched record's own `version` advances and each touched record's own `updated_at` is refreshed. Recomputation of a derived field counts as a mutation of the record that holds it.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock and the Daily Operational Checkpoint

Nothing in this system reads wall-clock time. A server-held **logical clock** supplies the instant used by every window check, expiry evaluation, coverage-block lookup and derived-field recomputation. Before any client sets it, the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and it never falls back to real time. A `DistrictHealthAdmin` moves it by supplying `to_iso8601`; the response reports `current_utc` and `previous_utc`. Advancing the clock does not itself rewrite stored rows, but it changes what the derived fields evaluate to: a coverage block's `status`, a medication order's `dispensable` flag and each building's `coverage_posture` all re-evaluate against the new instant on the next read.

Separately, an operational sweep — the **daily checkpoint** — advances the epoch and applies every deadline that has come due. It optionally narrows to one building via `school_id`, and it reports `epoch` together with seven identifier arrays: `expired_action_plan_ids`, `expired_authorization_ids`, `expired_lot_ids`, `expired_disclosure_ids`, `promoted_screening_ids`, `voided_administration_ids` and `recomputed_school_ids`. The sweep expires action plans whose review date has arrived, expires medication orders whose validity window has closed, expires stock lots whose expiration instant has arrived, expires approved but unreleased disclosure requests, promotes overdue rescreens to referral, and then re-runs the coverage ratio, the stock requirement, the days-of-supply figures, the stock protocol status, the building posture and each student's observed risk score. The precise preconditions and cascades of each of those sweeps are described alongside the record family they act on.

### 2.6. Optimistic Concurrency on Parent Records

Three write operations in this system are guarded by optimistic concurrency control against a *parent* record's `version`. In each case the version field is a non-optional input, not an optional hint, and a mismatch is a hard rejection rather than a retry-and-overwrite.

- A `POST /health-office/action-plans` request must carry `expected_student_version`, holding the client's last observed `version` of the target `Student`. If the student's current `version` differs, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_STUDENT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). A superseding plan may legitimately be drafted while another plan is already `ACTIVE`, so no state precondition excludes this write; the stale version is precisely what signals that the student's profile, alert flags or active-plan pointer moved underneath the author.
- A `POST /health-office/administration-records` request must carry `expected_authorization_version`, holding the client's last observed `version` of the target `MedicationAuthorization`. If the order's current `version` differs, the preparation is rejected with `ERR_STALE_AUTHORIZATION_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Two prepared doses on one order are legitimate under the daily cap, so nothing about the order's state excludes the write; the stale version marks the writer's view of consent, clinical status and today's dose count as out of date.
- A `POST /health-office/medication-stock-lots/{id}/inventory-action` request must carry `expected_lot_version`, holding the client's last observed `version` of the target `MedicationStockLot`. If the lot's current `version` differs, the action is rejected with `ERR_STALE_LOT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Repeated receipts and discards against one lot are legitimate, so no state precondition excludes the write; the stale version marks the caller's unit counts as out of date.

No other endpoint accepts a version field, and no other endpoint may reject on version grounds.

### 2.7. The Global Validation Ladder

When a request violates more than one rule, exactly one error is returned, and which one is fixed by a single global ladder. Every check in the system holds a position on that ladder; checks are evaluated in ascending ladder position and **the first failing check wins**. Text order in this document is not the ladder; the ordering below is.

The ladder runs in seven bands.

1. **Authentication (position 1).** Both headers present and `X-User-Role` one of the six declared roles, else `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. **Authorisation (positions 2 and 3).** The acting role appears in the endpoint's role list, else `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (position 2). Then, for entity-backed roles, `X-User-ID` matches the resource it claims — a `Guardian`'s identifier equals the target row's `student_id`; a nurse's or aide's identifier equals `HealthVisit.nurse_id` on assess, dispose, close and reopen, and equals `AdministrationRecord.administering_nurse_id` on outcome and void — else `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (position 3). Position 3 guards every `Guardian` endpoint, the four encounter steps just named, and the two administration-record steps just named.
3. **Existence (position 4).** Every referenced row exists. References are validated left to right: path parameters first, in URL order, then body foreign keys in the order they appear in the endpoint's input list. Any miss is `ERR_RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND`.
4. **Payload shape (positions 5 and 6).** Every non-optional input field is present and non-null, else `ERR_MISSING_REQUIRED_FIELD` (position 5, HTTP 400). Then every supplied field satisfies its declared type, enum membership and numeric range — including `limit` within 1 to 100 and `offset` at least 0 on list endpoints — else `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` (position 6, HTTP 422).
5. **State preconditions (positions 7 to 13).** Each record family has its own from-state check, and they sit in this relative order: `ActionPlan` (position 7, `ERR_PLAN_INVALID_STATE`), `MedicationAuthorization` (position 8, `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_INVALID_STATE`), `HealthVisit` (position 9, `ERR_VISIT_INVALID_STATE`), `AdministrationRecord` (position 10, `ERR_ADMINISTRATION_INVALID_STATE`), `MedicationStockLot` (position 11, `ERR_LOT_INVALID_STATE`), `Screening` (position 12, `ERR_SCREENING_INVALID_STATE`) and `DisclosureRequest` (position 13, `ERR_DISCLOSURE_INVALID_STATE`). The plan check guards the submit, guardian-consent, decision and revoke steps. The authorisation check guards the consent and clinical-review steps. The visit check guards assess, dispose, close and reopen, and it also guards both dose-preparation endpoints, because preparing a dose requires the encounter to be `IN_ASSESSMENT` or `DISPOSITIONED`. The administration check guards the outcome and void steps. The lot check guards the inventory action. The screening check guards record-result and follow-up. The disclosure check guards submit, guardian-consent, decide and release.
6. **Optimistic concurrency (positions 14 to 16).** `expected_student_version` on action-plan creation (position 14), `expected_authorization_version` on dose preparation (position 15), `expected_lot_version` on an inventory action (position 16). These sit *below* all state checks: an encounter in the wrong state fails at position 9 before the stale-order check at position 15 is ever reached.
7. **Business rules (positions 17 to 49).** Safety and compliance first, then temporal windows and interval geometry, then referential and uniqueness rules, then round caps and capacity or fiscal ceilings. The individual rules and their positions are given in the subsections that describe them, and the four cases where two plausible-looking checks collide are called out explicitly below.

Four collisions deserve naming, because in each the "obvious" reading is the wrong one.

- **Consent versus encounter state.** On dose preparation, the `HealthVisit` from-state check at position 9 outranks the consent check at position 23. Preparing a dose against an `OPEN` encounter for an order whose consent is still `CONSENT_PENDING` returns `ERR_VISIT_INVALID_STATE`, not `ERR_CONSENT_NOT_GRANTED`.
- **PRN interval versus daily cap.** On dose preparation, the PRN interval check at position 32 outranks the daily-cap check at position 35. A request that is both too soon after the last dose and over the day's cap returns `ERR_PRN_INTERVAL_NOT_ELAPSED`, not `ERR_DAILY_DOSE_CAP_REACHED`.
- **Building posture versus cot capacity.** On encounter creation, the coverage-posture check at position 18 outranks the cot-capacity check at position 34. A full office at an `UNCOVERED` building returns `ERR_SCHOOL_COVERAGE_UNAVAILABLE`, not `ERR_OFFICE_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`.
- **Coverage overlap versus nurse hours.** On coverage-block creation, the overlap check at position 33 outranks the contracted-hours check at position 47. A block that both collides with an existing block and would overdraw the nurse's week returns `ERR_COVERAGE_OVERLAP`, not `ERR_NURSE_HOURS_EXCEEDED`.

### 2.8. Numeric Precision, Rounding and Boundary Conventions

Unless a rule says otherwise, every monetary-style or ratio-style figure in this system is carried to **two decimal places and rounded half up**. Rounding is applied at pinned points in the arithmetic, never opportunistically: the coverage ratio divides unrounded minutes and rounds exactly once at the end, whereas the days-of-supply figure rounds each lot's own quotient first, sums the already-rounded terms, and then rounds the sum a second time. Scores are integers and are clamped, not rounded. Unit counts and staffing minutes are integers and never round at all.

Boundary conventions are equally pinned, and every threshold below is stated with its inclusivity. Recurring block intervals are half-open on the minute axis: a block covers `start_minute` inclusive through `end_minute` exclusive. Cot occupancy is a **closed** interval from arrival through closure, both ends inclusive. Validity windows on medication orders are inclusive at the opening instant and exclusive at the closing instant. A stock lot is eligible only while its expiration instant is strictly later than the logical clock, so a lot expiring exactly at the clock instant is out. The disclosure release window is half-open, so a release attempted exactly at the expiry instant is refused. Tier boundaries on risk scoring are inclusive on the lower edge; posture boundaries on the coverage ratio are inclusive on the upper edge.
### 2.9. Credentialled Practice and Segregation of Duties

Three acts in this system require two distinct people, and the system enforces that by comparing identifiers rather than by trusting workflow. On a controlled dose, the supplied `witness_nurse_id` must differ from the administering nurse. On closing a screening referral, the acting `X-User-ID` must differ from the `screened_by_nurse_id` stored when the result was recorded. On releasing a disclosure package, the acting `X-User-ID` must differ from the `decided_by_user_id` stored when the physician approved it. All three violations return the same code, `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403), and that check sits at ladder position 22 — guarding dose preparation, the dose outcome step, screening follow-up and disclosure release.

Credential gating is separate and sits one rung higher, at position 20. A nurse whose `credential` is `RN` may perform any administration step. A nurse whose `credential` is `LPN`, and any `HEALTH_AIDE`, may perform an administration step only when that person's `delegation_authorized` flag is true **and** the medication is not controlled. Anything else is `ERR_CREDENTIAL_NOT_DELEGATED` (HTTP 403), and it applies to dose preparation, emergency-stock preparation and the dose outcome step. A medication is controlled exactly when its `medication_code` is `METHYLPHENIDATE`; that fact is stored on each order as the derived boolean `is_controlled`. Where a controlled step is attempted with no witness named at all, the failure is the separate `ERR_WITNESS_REQUIRED` (HTTP 422) at position 21, which guards dose preparation and the dose outcome step — witness *absence* is a payload problem, witness *identity* is a duties problem, and the ladder reaches absence first.

### 2.10. Conserved Quantities

Two quantities in this system are conserved, meaning the sum of their parts is an invariant that must hold after **every single mutation, including each individual hop of a cascade** — not merely at rest.

The first is physical medication units on a lot. For every `MedicationStockLot`, `received_total` always equals the sum of `quantity_on_hand`, `quantity_reserved`, `quantity_administered` and `quantity_wasted`. `received_total` moves for exactly one reason: a receive action adds units to both `received_total` and `quantity_on_hand`. Every other movement redistributes between the four buckets without touching the total. A discard moves units from `quantity_on_hand` to `quantity_wasted`. Preparing a dose moves units from `quantity_on_hand` to `quantity_reserved`. Administering moves them from `quantity_reserved` to `quantity_administered`. A refusal returns them from `quantity_reserved` to `quantity_on_hand`. A waste outcome moves them from `quantity_reserved` to `quantity_wasted`. A void reverses whichever movement the source state represents: from a prepared record it returns units from `quantity_reserved` to `quantity_on_hand`; from an administered record it returns them from `quantity_administered` to `quantity_on_hand`; from a wasted record it returns them from `quantity_wasted` to `quantity_on_hand`. Preparing and then voiding, or preparing, administering and then voiding, both restore `quantity_on_hand` to exactly its prior value and leave `received_total` untouched. Zero drift is required; approximate restoration is a defect. Movements are in whole integer units and never round.

The second conserved quantity is staffing time. For every `Nurse`, `weekly_contracted_minutes` always equals `committed_weekly_minutes` plus `uncommitted_weekly_minutes`. Creating a coverage block adds that block's duration to `committed_weekly_minutes` and subtracts the same duration from `uncommitted_weekly_minutes`; ending a block reverses both moves exactly, so a create followed by an end leaves the nurse's week as it was found and no minutes leak. Commitment happens at create time, not at activation: the clock advancing a block from `SCHEDULED` to `ACTIVE` moves neither counter, and an `ENDED` block has already returned its minutes. The guard is applied to the post-move balance and permits equality with zero, so a block that consumes the nurse's last remaining minute exactly is accepted while one that would drive `uncommitted_weekly_minutes` below zero is refused with `ERR_NURSE_HOURS_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 409, ladder position 47) and moves neither counter.

### 2.11. Cascade Discipline and Derived-Field Refresh

Most write operations in this domain touch more than one record. The rule is that cascades are **hop-by-hop and causal**: each hop is triggered by the hop before it, and each hop's own side effects — including counter movements, version increments and derived-field recomputation — complete before the next hop begins. Two cascades in particular are four hops deep and are described in full where they originate: the supersede cascade that fires when a replacement action plan is approved, and the quarantine cascade that fires when a stock lot is pulled from service.

Derived fields are never stale. A student's risk scores, tier, alert flags, self-carry flag and active-plan pointer are recomputed on every mutation that could move them. A building's enrolled and eligible student counts, stock requirement, days-of-supply figures, protocol status, coverage ratios, posture and occupied-cot count are likewise recomputed whenever any input to them changes. Where a recomputation order matters — the stock requirement before the protocol status, the protocol status before the posture, the coverage ratio before the posture — that order is stated with the calculation.
## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Provisioning the District: Buildings, Nurses and Coverage Blocks

A district health administrator brings a building online by registering it with a display `name`, a short `building_code`, and the number of treatment cots in its health office. The building code is a two- to eight-character uppercase alphanumeric string and must be unique across every building on record; a collision is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_BUILDING_CODE` at ladder position 40. `cot_capacity` defaults to 2 and must be at least 1 and at most 20. The flag `emergency_protocol_enabled` defaults to true and governs whether undesignated stock epinephrine or albuterol may be given at that building at all. A freshly registered building starts with `enrolled_student_count` at 0, `occupied_cot_count` at 0 and `required_stock_units` at 0; the days-of-supply, protocol-status and posture calculations run immediately, which for an empty building yields a `stock_protocol_status` of `DEPLETED` and a `coverage_posture` of `UNCOVERED`.

Nurses are registered with a `full_name`, a `credential` of `RN`, `LPN` or `HEALTH_AIDE`, and `weekly_contracted_minutes` — the number of minutes per week the district buys from that person, which must be at least 0 and at most 3000. The optional `delegation_authorized` flag defaults to false and records whether an LPN or aide holds a signed delegation of nursing task. On registration, `employment_status` is `ACTIVE`, `committed_weekly_minutes` is 0, `uncommitted_weekly_minutes` equals `weekly_contracted_minutes`, and `assigned_school_count` is 0. Later, an administrator may amend a nurse's `employment_status` — the only other value is `INACTIVE` — or the `delegation_authorized` flag; either amendment increments the nurse's `version` and forces both coverage-ratio variants and the posture to be recomputed at every building where that nurse holds a block in `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE` status.

Coverage is expressed as recurring weekday blocks, each stored as a `CoverageAssignment`. A block names a `nurse_id` and a `school_id`, a `weekday` of `MON`, `TUE`, `WED`, `THU` or `FRI`, a `start_minute` and an `end_minute` measured as minutes after local midnight, an `effective_from` instant at which the arrangement begins, and an optional `effective_to` instant at which it stops. `start_minute` is at least 0 and less than 1440; `end_minute` is strictly greater than `start_minute` and at most 1440; `effective_to` defaults to null, meaning open-ended. The block's `duration_minutes` is simply its end minute less its start minute. A block's `status` is not stored by the client but derived from the logical clock: it is `SCHEDULED` while the clock is before `effective_from`, `ACTIVE` while the clock is at or after `effective_from` and either `effective_to` is null or the clock is before `effective_to`, and `ENDED` once `effective_to` is set and the clock has reached it. Blocks may be listed filtered by `nurse_id`, `school_id`, `weekday` and `status`.

**Interval rule 1 — the pairwise travel-buffer rule.** Before a new block is accepted, it is compared against every existing block held by the same nurse that is in `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE` status, falls on the same `weekday`, and whose effective-date range overlaps the new one. Two such blocks at the **same** building may touch exactly — an existing block ending on the very minute the new one starts is accepted — but may not overlap, because the minute interval is half-open. Two such blocks at **different** buildings must additionally be separated by at least 20 minutes of clear time on both sides, so that the nurse can physically travel. A violation of either arm is `ERR_COVERAGE_OVERLAP` at ladder position 33. Only once that check passes does the contracted-hours guard at position 47 apply, moving the nurse's two conservation counters and refusing the block with `ERR_NURSE_HOURS_EXCEEDED` if the post-move `uncommitted_weekly_minutes` would fall below zero. A successful create also refreshes `assigned_school_count`, which counts the distinct buildings at which the nurse holds a `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE` block, and re-runs both coverage-ratio variants, the protocol status and the posture for the building. Ending a block is the compensating half: the administrator supplies an `effective_to`, the block's minutes return to the nurse's uncommitted balance, `assigned_school_count` is refreshed, and the ratios and posture are recomputed.

**The nurse-to-student coverage ratio, in two variants.** Both variants divide a building's enrolled student count by a nurse-FTE figure, where one full-time equivalent is 2100 minutes per week; both round half up to two decimal places, dividing the unrounded minutes and rounding exactly once at the end. They differ entirely in what minutes they count. The **CONTRACTED** variant sums `duration_minutes` across every block at the building whose `status` is `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE`, at full weight, ignoring both the nurse's credential and the nurse's employment status; that sum is reported as `contracted_weekly_minutes`, and the resulting ratio as `contracted_students_per_nurse_fte`. The **DELIVERED** variant admits only blocks whose `status` is `ACTIVE` and whose nurse has `employment_status` of `ACTIVE`, and multiplies each block's duration by a credential weight before summing: an `RN` counts at 1.00, an `LPN` at 0.60, a `HEALTH_AIDE` with `delegation_authorized` true at 0.30, and a `HEALTH_AIDE` without that delegation at 0.00. That weighted sum is reported as `delivered_weekly_minutes` and the resulting ratio as `delivered_students_per_nurse_fte`. In both variants the numerator, `enrolled_student_count`, counts student rows at the building whose `enrollment_status` is `ENROLLED`. When the relevant minute total is zero, the corresponding ratio is null rather than any numeric value. Identical stored data therefore produces two different ratios whenever any block is still scheduled, any nurse is inactive, or any staffing minute belongs to someone other than an RN — which is exactly the point of reporting both: the contracted figure describes the district's paper commitment, the delivered figure describes what the building actually gets.

**Posture is the gate, and only the delivered figure feeds it.** Reading only `delivered_students_per_nurse_fte`, a base posture is determined: a ratio at or below 750.00 is `FULL`; a ratio strictly above 750.00 and at or below 1500.00 is `LIMITED`; a ratio strictly above 1500.00, or a null ratio, is `UNCOVERED`. One adjustment then applies: if the base posture is `FULL` but the building's `stock_protocol_status` is `DEPLETED`, the recorded `coverage_posture` is downgraded to `LIMITED`; an `UNCOVERED` base posture stays `UNCOVERED`, and a `LIMITED` base posture is unchanged. Exactly 750.00 is therefore `FULL` and exactly 1500.00 is `LIMITED`, while 1500.01 is `UNCOVERED`. The contracted ratio never gates any operation. A building with no active block at all, or one whose only blocks are held by inactive nurses or by undelegated aides, has zero delivered minutes, a null delivered ratio, and is `UNCOVERED` — which, as the encounter workflow explains, closes its office to new arrivals.
### 3.2. Enrolling a Student: Profile, Risk Scoring and Alert Flags

Enrolment is performed by a district administrator or a school nurse and records the child's building via `school_id`, a `full_name`, a `grade_level` between 0 and 12 inclusive, a `date_of_birth`, the highest documented `allergy_severity` of `NONE`, `MILD`, `MODERATE` or `ANAPHYLAXIS`, a list of `chronic_condition_codes` drawn from `ASTHMA`, `T1DM`, `SEIZURE`, `CARDIAC` and `MENTAL_HEALTH` (which may be empty), an `immunization_status` of `COMPLIANT`, `PROVISIONAL`, `EXEMPT` or `NONCOMPLIANT`, and an optional `privacy_hold` flag defaulting to false. On creation `enrollment_status` is `ENROLLED`; the only other values, reachable by amendment, are `TRANSFERRED` and `WITHDRAWN`. Amendment may change `allergy_severity`, `chronic_condition_codes`, `immunization_status`, `privacy_hold` and `enrollment_status`, and it increments the student's `version`. Students may be listed filtered by `school_id`, `enrollment_status`, `risk_tier` and a single `alert_flag`.

**Risk is scored twice, from two different worlds.** The **INTAKE** variant reads only what is declared on the student row and never touches the encounter tables. It awards 0 points for an `allergy_severity` of `NONE`, 5 for `MILD`, 15 for `MODERATE` and 40 for `ANAPHYLAXIS`; it adds 10 points for each entry in `chronic_condition_codes`; and it adds 0 points for a `COMPLIANT` immunisation status, 5 for `PROVISIONAL`, 5 for `EXEMPT` and 20 for `NONCOMPLIANT`. Those three contributions are summed and the total is capped at 100, giving `intake_risk_score`. It is computed at enrolment and recomputed on every profile amendment.

The **OBSERVED** variant reads a rolling window of the trailing 90 days, running from 90 days before the logical clock inclusive up to the logical clock exclusive. Within that window it counts two sets. The first is the student's encounters whose `status` is `DISPOSITIONED` or `CLOSED` and whose `arrival_at` falls in the window — encounters still `OPEN`, `IN_ASSESSMENT` or `REOPENED` are excluded — and among those it separately counts the subset whose `disposition` is `SEND_HOME` or `EMS_TRANSPORT`. The second is administration records still in `ADMINISTERED` status whose `protocol_mode` is `STOCK_EMERGENCY`, whose `administered_at` falls in the window, and whose encounter belongs to this student. The score awards 6 points for each qualifying encounter, a further 12 points for each encounter in the send-home-or-transport subset, and 8 points for each emergency-stock administration; the total is capped at 100 and stored as `observed_risk_score`. Because a voided record leaves the administered set, voiding a mis-entered emergency dose genuinely lowers the child's observed score again. At enrolment the observed score is 0; thereafter it is recomputed whenever an encounter is dispositioned to send-home or transport, whenever an encounter closes, whenever a prepared dose becomes administered, whenever any record is voided, and on the daily checkpoint.

Both scores are stored side by side. `effective_risk_score` is the larger of the two, and `risk_tier` reads that effective score: below 25 is `ROUTINE`, from 25 up to but not including 60 is `ELEVATED`, and 60 or above is `CRITICAL`. The lower edge of each tier is inclusive, so exactly 25 is `ELEVATED` and exactly 60 is `CRITICAL`.

**Alert flags**, stored as `alert_flags`, are a computed list of strings held in ascending sorted order and recomputed on every profile, plan and authorisation mutation. Five flags exist. `ALLERGY_ANAPHYLAXIS` is present when `allergy_severity` is `ANAPHYLAXIS`. `IMMUNIZATION_HOLD` is present when `immunization_status` is `NONCOMPLIANT`. `NO_ACTIVE_PLAN` is present whenever the student has no plan currently in force — it is always present at enrolment. `SCREENING_REFERRAL` is present while at least one of the student's screenings is awaiting referral closure. `SELF_CARRY` is present when the student's in-force plan requested self-carriage. Alongside the flags sit two more derived fields: `self_carry_authorized`, true exactly when some action plan for this student is `ACTIVE` and its `self_carry_requested` flag is true, and `active_action_plan_id`, a computed pointer — never a client-supplied reference — to the `ACTIVE` plan for this student with the latest effective date, or null when there is none.

Enrolment and amendment both ripple upward to the building. Enrolling a student increments `enrolled_student_count` and then re-runs, in that order, the stock requirement, the protocol status and the posture. An amendment re-runs the intake score, re-tiers the effective score, recomputes the alert flags, and then re-runs the enrolled count, the stock requirement, the protocol status, both coverage-ratio variants and the posture for the building. One amendment carries a further consequence: when `enrollment_status` moves out of `ENROLLED`, every screening for that student sitting in `SCHEDULED` or `RESCREEN_DUE` is moved to `DECLINED`. Records themselves survive withdrawal — a disclosure request may be opened for a student in any enrolment state — but clinical activity does not: creating an action plan, a medication order, an encounter or a screening for a student who is not `ENROLLED`, and likewise submitting a plan or recording guardian consent on a medication order for such a student, is refused with `ERR_STUDENT_NOT_ENROLLED` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 17.
### 3.3. Chronic-Condition Action Plans and the Supersede Cascade

A chronic-condition action plan is the clinical instruction sheet a building follows for one child and one condition. A school nurse drafts it, naming the `student_id`, a `condition_code` of `ASTHMA`, `ANAPHYLAXIS`, `SEIZURE` or `T1DM`, a clinician-assigned `severity_score` between 1 and 5 inclusive, whether the plan authorises undesignated stock epinephrine for this child via `requires_stock_epinephrine`, whether the child is to carry and self-administer via `self_carry_requested`, and the `effective_from` instant at which the plan becomes clinically in force. The draft also carries `expected_student_version`, and it may carry `supersedes_action_plan_id`, a self-referential pointer to the plan this one is intended to replace; that pointer defaults to null for a first plan. Plans may be listed filtered by `student_id`, `status`, `condition_code` and `supersedes_action_plan_id`.

Two admission rules apply at draft time beyond the enrolment and stale-version checks already described. No other plan for the same student and the same `condition_code` may currently be `ACTIVE`, **unless** `supersedes_action_plan_id` names exactly that plan; a conflict is refused with `ERR_ACTIVE_PLAN_CONFLICT` at ladder position 41. On acceptance the plan is created at `DRAFT` with `revision_round` 0, `guardian_consent_at` null and `provider_signed_at` null, the student's `version` is incremented by one, and the student's alert flags are recomputed — which adds `NO_ACTIVE_PLAN` when the student has no in-force plan pointer.

The plan then walks `DRAFT` → `PENDING_GUARDIAN` → `PENDING_PROVIDER` → `ACTIVE`. Submission by the drafting nurse moves a `DRAFT` plan to `PENDING_GUARDIAN` and requires the student to still be `ENROLLED`. The guardian, acting on their own child, records consent on a `PENDING_GUARDIAN` plan: `guardian_consent_at` is stamped with the logical clock and the plan moves to `PENDING_PROVIDER`. A physician then records a `decision` with an optional `note`.

**Approval.** A `decision` of `APPROVE` requires the plan to be `PENDING_PROVIDER`, requires `guardian_consent_at` to be non-null — a missing consent is `ERR_GUARDIAN_CONSENT_MISSING` at ladder position 25 — and requires the student to still be `ENROLLED`. On approval `provider_signed_at` is stamped with the clock, `review_due_at` is set to 365 days after the plan's `effective_from`, and the plan becomes `ACTIVE`. The student's `active_action_plan_id`, `self_carry_authorized` and alert flags are recomputed, dropping `NO_ACTIVE_PLAN` and adding `SELF_CARRY` when self-carriage was requested. The building's `eligible_stock_student_count`, `required_stock_units` and `stock_protocol_status` are then recomputed in that order.

**The four-hop supersede cascade.** If the approved plan named a `supersedes_action_plan_id` and that predecessor is `ACTIVE`, the predecessor moves `ACTIVE` → `SUPERSEDED`, and each subsequent hop is caused by the hop before it rather than by the approval itself. Hop 1 is that transition to `SUPERSEDED`. Hop 2 follows because the predecessor is now superseded: every medication order hanging off it — that is, every order whose `action_plan_id` names it — whose `clinical_status` is `VERIFIED` moves to `SUSPENDED`. Hop 3 follows because those orders are now suspended: every administration record referencing them that is still `PREPARED` moves to `VOIDED`, and each such record releases its reservation, returning its `units` from the lot's `quantity_reserved` to the lot's `quantity_on_hand`. Hop 4 follows because those reservations were released: any touched lot sitting at `DEPLETED` with a positive `quantity_on_hand` returns to `AVAILABLE`, and then the building's `epinephrine_days_of_supply`, `albuterol_days_of_supply` and `stock_protocol_status` are recomputed.

**Revision, and its ceiling.** A `decision` of `REQUEST_REVISION` on a `PENDING_PROVIDER` plan whose `revision_round` is below 2 increments `revision_round` by one, clears `guardian_consent_at` back to null so the guardian must consent afresh, and returns the plan to `DRAFT` — closing the cycle `DRAFT` → `PENDING_GUARDIAN` → `PENDING_PROVIDER` → `DRAFT`. A third such request, made when `revision_round` already stands at 2, is refused with `ERR_REVISION_LIMIT_REACHED` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 44; the plan remains `PENDING_PROVIDER` and the physician's remaining option is revocation.

**Revocation and expiry.** A physician may revoke a plan from `DRAFT`, `PENDING_GUARDIAN`, `PENDING_PROVIDER` or `ACTIVE`, optionally recording a `reason_code`. The plan becomes `REVOKED`; every medication order linked to it whose `clinical_status` is `UNVERIFIED` or `VERIFIED` moves to `SUSPENDED`; and the student's `active_action_plan_id`, `self_carry_authorized` and alert flags are recomputed. Expiry is the same shape but system-driven: during the daily checkpoint, an `ACTIVE` plan whose `review_due_at` is at or before the logical clock becomes `EXPIRED`, every linked order at `UNVERIFIED` or `VERIFIED` moves to `SUSPENDED`, and the student's plan pointer, self-carry flag and alert flags are recomputed — the flags regaining `NO_ACTIVE_PLAN`. `SUPERSEDED`, `EXPIRED` and `REVOKED` are terminal: nothing leaves them.

**The emergency-stock requirement this drives.** How many undesignated stock units a building must hold is computed in three pinned steps, and although the surge allowance appears to be keyed on the requirement it is added to, the evaluation is not iterated to a fixed point. Step one counts the eligible population: `eligible_stock_student_count` is the number of `ENROLLED` students at the building whose `allergy_severity` is `ANAPHYLAXIS` or whose `chronic_condition_codes` include `ASTHMA`, with a student meeting both criteria counted once. `base_required_units` is that count divided by 25 and rounded **up** to a whole number. Step two derives `surge_units` from `base_required_units` and nothing else: 0 when the base is 2 or fewer, 1 when the base is from 3 to 5 inclusive, and 2 when the base is 6 or more. Step three sets `required_stock_units` to the base plus the surge, and never re-tiers on that combined figure. A building with no eligible students has a base of 0, a surge of 0 and a requirement of 0. This requirement is recomputed on student enrolment, on student amendment, on the approval of an action plan, and at the daily checkpoint.
### 3.4. Medication Orders and Their Two Independent Dimensions

A medication order is created by a school nurse and records the `student_id` it authorises medication for, an optional `action_plan_id` naming the plan the order hangs off (null for a standalone order), a `medication_code` drawn from `EPINEPHRINE`, `ALBUTEROL`, `INSULIN`, `METHYLPHENIDATE` and `ACETAMINOPHEN`, a `route` of `IM`, `INHALED`, `ORAL` or `SUBQ`, a strictly positive `dose_amount` with a `dose_unit` of `MG`, `MCG`, `ML`, `PUFF` or `UNIT`, a `schedule_type` of `SCHEDULED` or `PRN`, a `max_doses_per_day` between 1 and 12 inclusive, and a validity window given by `valid_from` and a strictly later `valid_until`. The field `prn_min_interval_minutes` — between 1 and 1440 inclusive — must be supplied exactly when `schedule_type` is `PRN`, and must be null when the schedule is `SCHEDULED`. When an `action_plan_id` is given, that plan must belong to the same student. Orders may be listed filtered by `student_id`, `action_plan_id`, `medication_code`, `consent_status` and `clinical_status`.

On creation the derived flag `is_controlled` is set true exactly when the `medication_code` is `METHYLPHENIDATE`, `dispensable` is false, and the student's alert flags are recomputed.

**Two dimensions, advancing independently.** An order carries two state values on the same row, and neither gates the other's transitions. `consent_status` is the guardian-owned dimension, starting at `CONSENT_PENDING`. `clinical_status` is the nurse- and physician-owned dimension, starting at `UNVERIFIED`. A row may legitimately sit at `CONSENT_WITHDRAWN` paired with `VERIFIED`, or at `CONSENT_GRANTED` paired with `SUSPENDED`; both combinations are legal and both make the order undispensable. Only the derived flag `dispensable` reads both, and it is true only when `consent_status` is `CONSENT_GRANTED`, `clinical_status` is `VERIFIED`, and the logical clock is at or after `valid_from` and strictly before `valid_until`.

**The consent dimension is cyclic.** A guardian acting on their own child records a `decision`. `GRANT` moves `CONSENT_PENDING` to `CONSENT_GRANTED` — and it also moves `CONSENT_WITHDRAWN` back to `CONSENT_GRANTED`, so a withdrawn consent can be re-granted without touching the clinical dimension — and recomputes `dispensable`. Granting from `CONSENT_PENDING` additionally requires the student to still be `ENROLLED`. `WITHDRAW` moves `CONSENT_GRANTED` to `CONSENT_WITHDRAWN`, sets `dispensable` false, and cascades: every administration record on this order still in `PREPARED` moves to `VOIDED`, releasing its reservation by returning its `units` from `quantity_reserved` to `quantity_on_hand` on the lot, after which the `epinephrine_days_of_supply`, `albuterol_days_of_supply` and `stock_protocol_status` of each touched lot's building are recomputed.

**The clinical dimension is cyclic too.** A clinical review carries a `decision` and an optional `note`. A `decision` of `VERIFY` moves `UNVERIFIED` to `VERIFIED`, and equally moves `SUSPENDED` back to `VERIFIED`; either way the acting nurse's `credential` must be `RN`, and either the order's `action_plan_id` must be null or the referenced plan must be `ACTIVE`. Verification recomputes `dispensable`. A `decision` of `SUSPEND`, recorded by a physician, moves `VERIFIED` to `SUSPENDED`, sets `dispensable` false, voids every `PREPARED` administration record on the order — releasing each reservation — and recomputes the protocol status for each touched lot's building. The same `VERIFIED` → `SUSPENDED` edge is also driven by the system whenever a linked action plan leaves `ACTIVE`.

**Expiry closes the clinical dimension.** During the daily checkpoint, an order whose `clinical_status` is `UNVERIFIED`, `VERIFIED` or `SUSPENDED` and whose `valid_until` the logical clock has reached moves to `clinical_status` `EXPIRED`, `dispensable` is set false, and every `PREPARED` record on the order is voided with its reservation released. `EXPIRED` is terminal on that dimension.

Two derived counters live on the order and are read by the dose gate described later: `doses_administered_today`, the number of administration records on this order in `ADMINISTERED` status whose `administered_at` falls in the current UTC calendar day, and `last_administered_at`, the latest `administered_at` across the order's `ADMINISTERED` records, or null when there are none.

### 3.5. Stock Lots: Receipt, Inventory Actions, Selection and Readiness

Medication physically lives in lots. A district administrator receives a lot into a building by naming the `school_id`, the `medication_code`, the manufacturer's `lot_number`, whether this is undesignated emergency stock via `is_stock_protocol`, an `initial_quantity` of at least 1, and an `expiration_date`, which must be strictly later than the logical clock — a lot received already at or past its expiry is refused with `ERR_LOT_EXPIRED` at ladder position 39. On receipt, `received_total` and `quantity_on_hand` are both set to the initial quantity, `quantity_reserved`, `quantity_administered` and `quantity_wasted` are all 0, and the lot's `status` is `AVAILABLE`. The lot's `quantity_available` is an alias of `quantity_on_hand` and is the pool selection draws from. Lots may be listed filtered by `school_id`, `medication_code`, `status` and `is_stock_protocol`.

**Inventory actions.** An administrator applies an `action` of `RECEIVE`, `DISCARD`, `QUARANTINE` or `RESTORE`, always accompanied by `expected_lot_version`, with `units` where the action moves units and an optional `reason_code`. `RECEIVE` adds `units` to both `received_total` and `quantity_on_hand`, and returns a `DEPLETED` lot to `AVAILABLE`. `DISCARD` moves `units` from `quantity_on_hand` to `quantity_wasted` leaving `received_total` untouched, and a lot whose `quantity_on_hand` reaches exactly 0 moves `AVAILABLE` → `DEPLETED`; a discard that would drive `quantity_on_hand` below zero is refused with `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` at ladder position 49. `QUARANTINE` moves a lot from `AVAILABLE` or `DEPLETED` to `QUARANTINED`. `RESTORE` moves a `QUARANTINED` lot back to `AVAILABLE`, and requires the lot's `expiration_date` to still be strictly later than the clock — closing the cycle `AVAILABLE` → `QUARANTINED` → `AVAILABLE`. Every inventory action increments the lot's `version`, and every one re-runs the lot's own days of supply, the building's days-of-supply figures, the protocol status and the posture. A `QUARANTINE` or `RESTORE` attempted on a lot in a state that does not admit it, and any action at all on an `EXPIRED` lot, is refused with `ERR_LOT_INVALID_STATE` at ladder position 11; an inventory action targeting a lot whose expiration has passed is refused with `ERR_LOT_EXPIRED`.

**The four-hop quarantine cascade.** Pulling a lot from service is not a bookkeeping change. Hop 1 sets the lot to `QUARANTINED`. Hop 2 follows because the lot is quarantined: every administration record drawing on it that is still `PREPARED` moves to `VOIDED`, and consequently each of those reservations is released back to the lot — `units` moving from `quantity_reserved` to `quantity_on_hand` — while each parent encounter's `pending_administration_count` is decremented by one. Hop 3 follows because the lot has left `AVAILABLE` and therefore stops contributing to the building's days of supply: `epinephrine_days_of_supply` and `albuterol_days_of_supply` are recomputed and then `stock_protocol_status` is recomputed from them. Hop 4 follows because that status may now read `DEPLETED`: `coverage_posture` is recomputed and can drop from `FULL` to `LIMITED`, which in turn tightens the gate on new encounters at that building.

**Depletion and its reversal are system-driven.** A lot at `AVAILABLE` whose `quantity_on_hand` reaches exactly 0 — after an administered outcome, a wasted outcome, or a discard — moves to `DEPLETED` and thereby leaves the selection candidate pool. A lot at `DEPLETED` whose `quantity_on_hand` rises above 0, after a receipt or a void compensation, returns to `AVAILABLE`, provided its `expiration_date` is still strictly later than the clock. During the daily checkpoint, a lot at `AVAILABLE`, `QUARANTINED` or `DEPLETED` whose `expiration_date` the clock has reached moves to `EXPIRED` — a terminal state — voiding every `PREPARED` record drawing on it, releasing each reservation, decrementing each parent encounter's pending count, and then recomputing the building's two days-of-supply figures, protocol status and posture.

**Days of supply, rounded twice in a pinned order.** Each medication has an assumed average daily consumption: 0.30 units for `EPINEPHRINE`, 1.20 for `ALBUTEROL`, 3.00 for `INSULIN`, 1.00 for `METHYLPHENIDATE` and 2.50 for `ACETAMINOPHEN`. A lot's own `days_of_supply` is its `quantity_available` divided by that figure for its medication, rounded half up to two decimal places **first**. The building's figure for a medication is then the sum of those already-rounded per-lot terms across the building's lots of that medication that are `AVAILABLE` and whose `expiration_date` is strictly later than the clock, with that sum rounded half up to two decimal places once more. Quarantined, depleted and expired lots contribute nothing, and a lot expiring exactly at the clock instant is excluded. When no lot qualifies the result is 0.00, never null. The order of rounding is load-bearing and must not be reordered: two epinephrine lots holding one unit each contribute 3.33 and 3.33 for a building figure of 6.66, whereas summing the raw quotients first and rounding once would wrongly give 6.67. The results are stored as `epinephrine_days_of_supply` and `albuterol_days_of_supply`.

**Readiness of the emergency stock.** The building's `stock_protocol_status` looks only at undesignated stock: the epinephrine unit total is the sum of `quantity_available` over the building's epinephrine lots that are `AVAILABLE`, unexpired against the clock and flagged `is_stock_protocol`, and the albuterol unit total is the same sum for albuterol. Student-specific lots contribute to days of supply but never to this readiness view. If either unit total is zero, the status is `DEPLETED`. Otherwise, if the epinephrine unit total is strictly less than `required_stock_units`, or if the smaller of the building's two days-of-supply figures is strictly below 30.00, the status is `DEGRADED`. In every other case it is `READY`. Comparisons use the two-decimal rounded figures and the readiness edge is inclusive: exactly 30.00 days is `READY`, while 29.99 is `DEGRADED`. This status is recomputed immediately after either the days-of-supply calculation or the stock requirement runs, on every lot transition, and at the daily checkpoint — and the posture is recomputed immediately after it.

**Which lot a dose draws from.** When a dose is prepared, the candidate pool is every lot at the encounter's building whose `medication_code` equals the required medication, whose `status` is `AVAILABLE`, whose `expiration_date` is strictly later than the logical clock, whose `quantity_available` is at least the requested `units`, and whose `is_stock_protocol` flag matches the mode of the request — true for an emergency-stock dose, false for an order-backed dose. That equality makes the two pools disjoint: an authorised dose never draws on undesignated emergency stock, and an emergency dose never draws on a child's own supply. Candidates are ranked first-expiring-first-out, by `expiration_date` ascending. Ties are broken by `quantity_available` ascending, and any remaining tie by `created_at` ascending; the random `id` is never consulted at any level, including the final tie-break, so the order is a deterministic total order. If the candidate pool is empty the request is refused with `ERR_NO_ELIGIBLE_LOT` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 48; if a winning lot exists but its available units fall short of the request, the refusal is `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` (HTTP 409) at position 49. Should the winning lot's `medication_code` not match the required medication, the refusal is `ERR_LOT_MEDICATION_MISMATCH` at position 42.
### 3.6. The Office Encounter: Arrival, Assessment, Disposition, Closure and Reopening

A child arriving at the health office opens an encounter. A school nurse or health aide records the `student_id`, the `school_id` whose office is being used, the `nurse_id` who will own the record, a `complaint_code` of `INJURY`, `ILLNESS`, `MEDICATION`, `CHRONIC_CARE`, `MENTAL_HEALTH` or `SCREENING_FOLLOWUP`, and an `acuity` of `ROUTINE`, `URGENT` or `EMERGENT`. The student must be `ENROLLED` and their `school_id` must match the building supplied. The building's `coverage_posture` must be `FULL` or `LIMITED`; a building at `UNCOVERED` refuses new encounters with `ERR_SCHOOL_COVERAGE_UNAVAILABLE` at ladder position 18. Only then does the cot check at position 34 apply. On creation `arrival_at` is stamped with the logical clock, `status` is `OPEN`, `disposition` is null, `guardian_notified` is false, `reopen_count` is 0, and `occupied_cot_count` at the building is incremented by one. Encounters may be listed filtered by `student_id`, `school_id`, `nurse_id`, `status` and `disposition`.

**Interval rule 2 — aggregate cot capacity.** A cot is occupied for the closed interval running from `arrival_at` through `closed_at`, with **both** ends inclusive; adjacency therefore does not free a cot until the closing instant has passed. Unlike the pairwise coverage rule, overlap here is tolerated up to a ceiling rather than forbidden between pairs: the number of encounters at the building whose occupancy interval contains the new arrival instant — in practice, those with `status` of `OPEN`, `IN_ASSESSMENT` or `REOPENED`, which is exactly what `occupied_cot_count` reports — must be strictly less than the building's `cot_capacity`. Reaching the ceiling is refused with `ERR_OFFICE_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 409), and the same ceiling is re-checked when an encounter is reopened. There is no travel buffer here and the interval is closed, which is precisely how this rule differs from the nurse-scheduling rule described earlier.

**The triage score, frozen at arrival.** Four inputs interact and two clamps nest. The `acuity` supplies a base of 10 points for `ROUTINE`, 40 for `URGENT` and 70 for `EMERGENT`. The `complaint_code` adds 10 points for `INJURY`, 8 for `ILLNESS`, 12 for `MEDICATION`, 15 for `CHRONIC_CARE`, 18 for `MENTAL_HEALTH` and 2 for `SCREENING_FOLLOWUP`. The student's `risk_tier`, read from the student row at the instant of the transition, contributes 0 points at `ROUTINE`, 10 at `ELEVATED` and 20 at `CRITICAL`; an anaphylaxis bonus of a further 15 points applies when the student's `allergy_severity` is `ANAPHYLAXIS` **and** the `complaint_code` is `ILLNESS` or `MEDICATION`. Those two contributions are summed and then capped at 25 — the inner clamp — so a `CRITICAL` anaphylaxis student contributes 25 rather than 35. The building's `coverage_posture`, read at the same instant, adds a penalty of 0 points at `FULL`, 8 at `LIMITED` and 15 at `UNCOVERED`. The base, the complaint points, the capped risk uplift and the coverage penalty are summed, and the total is clamped to the range 0 through 100 — the outer clamp — giving `triage_score`. The derived flag `requires_ems_review` is true when `triage_score` is at least 85, so a score of exactly 85 sets it.

**Assessment.** Moving an encounter from `OPEN` to `IN_ASSESSMENT` — and equally from `REOPENED` to `IN_ASSESSMENT` — is done by the owning nurse, whose `X-User-ID` must equal the encounter's `nurse_id`. It additionally requires the acting nurse to be physically on coverage at that moment: a coverage block must exist for that nurse at that building whose `status` is `ACTIVE`, whose `weekday` matches the weekday of the logical clock, and whose minute range contains the clock's minute-of-day, counting from `start_minute` inclusive to `end_minute` exclusive. Failing that, the step is refused with `ERR_NURSE_NOT_ON_COVERAGE` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 19. The step stores the optional `assessment_note`; on a re-assessment after a reopen, the note is overwritten rather than appended.

**Four dispositions, four different consequences.** Each disposition requires the encounter to be `IN_ASSESSMENT` and moves it to `DISPOSITIONED`, and every branch decrements the building's `occupied_cot_count` by one — but there the similarity ends, and the request carries a `disposition` plus, for one branch, `guardian_notified`.

- **`RETURN_TO_CLASS`** is the quiet branch: it neither notifies a guardian nor touches any screening. It is barred in exactly one case — a student whose `immunization_status` is `NONCOMPLIANT` presenting with a `complaint_code` of `ILLNESS` may not be returned to class, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_IMMUNIZATION_HOLD` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 26.
- **`SEND_HOME`** requires the request body to carry `guardian_notified` as true; without it the disposition is refused with `ERR_GUARDIAN_NOTIFICATION_REQUIRED` (HTTP 409) at position 27. On success `guardian_notified` is recorded true, every screening for that student that is `SCHEDULED` with a `scheduled_for` falling on the same UTC calendar day as the clock is displaced to `RESCREEN_DUE` with `rescreen_due_at` set to 14 days after the clock, and the student's `observed_risk_score`, `effective_risk_score` and `risk_tier` are recomputed.
- **`EMS_TRANSPORT`** requires justification: either the encounter's `acuity` is `EMERGENT` or its `triage_score` is at least 85, failing which it is refused with `ERR_EMS_DISPOSITION_NOT_JUSTIFIED` (HTTP 409) at position 28. On success `guardian_notified` is forced to true regardless of what the request body says, the student's `observed_risk_score`, `effective_risk_score` and `risk_tier` are recomputed, and the building's `stock_protocol_status` is re-evaluated.
- **`REFERRED_EXTERNAL`** requires the encounter's `complaint_code` to be `SCREENING_FOLLOWUP`. Its only additional effect is a clock extension: every screening for that student currently `REFERRED` has its `referral_due_at` pushed out to 30 days after the logical clock.

**Closure.** A `DISPOSITIONED` encounter is closed once nothing is still reserved against it: `pending_administration_count`, which counts the encounter's administration records in `PREPARED` status, must be exactly 0, or the close is refused with `ERR_PENDING_ADMINISTRATION` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 43. On closure `closed_at` is stamped with the clock, `status` becomes `CLOSED`, `minutes_in_office` is set to the whole minutes between `arrival_at` and `closed_at` with any fraction discarded, and the student's observed score, effective score and tier are recomputed.

**Reopening, once and briefly.** Only a school nurse may reopen, and only within 120 minutes of closure: a reopen at or after that deadline is refused with `ERR_REOPEN_WINDOW_CLOSED` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 36. `reopen_count` must still be below 1, so a second reopen is refused with `ERR_REOPEN_LIMIT_REACHED` (HTTP 409) at position 45, and a cot must be free under the capacity rule. An optional `reason_code` may be recorded. On success the encounter moves `CLOSED` → `REOPENED`, `disposition` is cleared to null, `closed_at` and `minutes_in_office` are cleared to null, `reopen_count` and the building's `occupied_cot_count` are each incremented by one, and the triage score is recomputed from scratch against the student's *current* `risk_tier` and the building's *current* `coverage_posture` — which is why the same encounter can score differently the second time. That closes the cycle `CLOSED` → `REOPENED` → `IN_ASSESSMENT` → `DISPOSITIONED` → `CLOSED`.
### 3.7. Giving a Dose, and Undoing One

Every dose is a two-step affair: units are first reserved, then an outcome is recorded. Reservation creates an administration record at `PREPARED`; the outcome moves it to `ADMINISTERED`, `REFUSED` or `WASTED`; and a compensating void can reach `VOIDED` from any of `PREPARED`, `ADMINISTERED` or `WASTED`. `REFUSED` and `VOIDED` are terminal. Records may be listed filtered by `visit_id`, `authorization_id`, `lot_id`, `administering_nurse_id`, `witness_nurse_id`, `status` and `protocol_mode`.

**Order-backed reservation.** A nurse or aide reserves against an existing order by naming the `visit_id`, the `authorization_id`, the whole `units` to draw (at least 1 and at most 10), the `expected_authorization_version`, and, where the medication is controlled, a `witness_nurse_id`. The encounter must be `IN_ASSESSMENT` or `DISPOSITIONED`. The order's `consent_status` must be `CONSENT_GRANTED`, failing which the refusal is `ERR_CONSENT_NOT_GRANTED` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 23, and its `clinical_status` must be `VERIFIED`, failing which the refusal is `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_NOT_VERIFIED` (HTTP 409) at position 24. The order's `student_id` must equal the encounter's `student_id`, or the request is refused with `ERR_LOT_MEDICATION_MISMATCH`. The logical clock must sit at or after `valid_from` and strictly before `valid_until`, or the refusal is `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_WINDOW_CLOSED` (HTTP 409) at position 31. The acting nurse must clear the credential and delegation rule, and on a controlled medication a witness must be named and must differ from the acting nurse.

**The dose gate.** Two frequency limits guard an order-backed reservation, and their relative order matters. The daily count `doses_administered_today` is the number of records on this order in `ADMINISTERED` status whose `administered_at` falls inside the current UTC calendar day, measured from UTC midnight inclusive to the next UTC midnight exclusive; `last_administered_at` is the latest `administered_at` across the order's `ADMINISTERED` records, or null when there are none. Only records still in `ADMINISTERED` status count toward either figure — records that are `PREPARED`, `REFUSED` or `WASTED` never count, and a voided record leaves both the daily count and the maximum, which is exactly how a mis-entered dose is undone. For an order whose `schedule_type` is `PRN` and whose `last_administered_at` is not null, the elapsed time from that instant to the clock is floored to whole minutes and must be at least `prn_min_interval_minutes`; a dose at exactly the interval is allowed, and anything shorter is refused with `ERR_PRN_INTERVAL_NOT_ELAPSED` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 32. An order whose `schedule_type` is `SCHEDULED`, or a PRN order with no prior administered dose, passes the interval arm unconditionally. Separately, `doses_administered_today` must be strictly less than `max_doses_per_day`, so the dose that brings the count level with the cap is the last one allowed and the next is refused with `ERR_DAILY_DOSE_CAP_REACHED` (HTTP 409) at position 35. Because the interval check sits above the cap check, a request failing both reports the interval failure.

On success the lot is chosen by the selection policy, `medication_code` and `dose_amount` are copied from the order, `protocol_mode` is recorded as `AUTHORIZED`, `status` is `PREPARED`, `prepared_at` is stamped with the clock, and `administering_nurse_id` is copied from the acting `X-User-ID`. `units` move on the lot from `quantity_on_hand` to `quantity_reserved` with `received_total` unchanged; the encounter's `pending_administration_count` is incremented by one; the order's `version` is incremented by one; and the lot's own days of supply, the building's two days-of-supply figures, the protocol status and the posture are recomputed.

**Emergency stock reservation.** Undesignated stock is given with no order behind it. The request names the `visit_id`, the `medication_code`, the `units` and optionally a `witness_nurse_id`. The encounter must be `IN_ASSESSMENT` or `DISPOSITIONED`; the building's `emergency_protocol_enabled` must be true and the medication must be `EPINEPHRINE` or `ALBUTEROL`, failing either of which the request is refused with `ERR_STOCK_PROTOCOL_DISABLED` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 30; the acting nurse must clear the credential and delegation rule; and the selection policy, restricted to lots flagged `is_stock_protocol`, must yield a candidate. On success `authorization_id` is set to null, `protocol_mode` is `STOCK_EMERGENCY`, `status` is `PREPARED`, `prepared_at` is stamped, `administering_nurse_id` is the acting identifier, and `dose_amount` is taken from the standing protocol table rather than from any order: 0.3 for `EPINEPHRINE` and 2 for `ALBUTEROL`. Units move from `quantity_on_hand` to `quantity_reserved`, the encounter's `pending_administration_count` is incremented by one, and the lot's days of supply along with the building's protocol status are recomputed. Note that an emergency reservation carries no version field and no daily-cap or interval gate — there is no order to be stale about, and no order to count doses against.

**Recording the outcome.** The `outcome` step is taken by the nurse who prepared the dose — `X-User-ID` must equal `administering_nurse_id` — on a record still in `PREPARED`, with an optional `reason_code` and, on a controlled record, a `witness_nurse_id` that differs from the administering nurse. An `outcome` of `ADMINISTERED` stamps `administered_at` with the clock and moves `units` from `quantity_reserved` to `quantity_administered`; a lot whose `quantity_on_hand` now stands at 0 moves `AVAILABLE` → `DEPLETED`; the encounter's `pending_administration_count` is decremented by one; the order's `doses_administered_today` and `last_administered_at` are recomputed; the student's `observed_risk_score`, `effective_risk_score` and `risk_tier` are recomputed; and the lot's days of supply, the building's two days-of-supply figures, protocol status and posture are recomputed. An `outcome` of `REFUSED` stores the `reason_code`, returns `units` from `quantity_reserved` to `quantity_on_hand`, decrements the encounter's pending count by one, and recomputes the lot's days of supply and the building's protocol status. An `outcome` of `WASTED` stores the `reason_code`, moves `units` from `quantity_reserved` to `quantity_wasted`, moves a lot whose `quantity_on_hand` is now 0 from `AVAILABLE` to `DEPLETED`, decrements the pending count by one, and recomputes the lot's days of supply, both building days-of-supply figures, the protocol status and the posture.

**Voiding.** A school nurse — and only the nurse whose identifier equals `administering_nurse_id` — may void a record in `PREPARED`, `ADMINISTERED` or `WASTED`, supplying a `reason_code`. A manual void must happen within 1440 minutes of `prepared_at`; at or after that deadline it is refused with `ERR_VOID_WINDOW_CLOSED` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 37. On success `status` becomes `VOIDED` and `voided_at` is stamped with the clock. The lot is compensated exactly according to the state the record came from: from `PREPARED`, `units` move from `quantity_reserved` back to `quantity_on_hand`; from `ADMINISTERED`, from `quantity_administered` back to `quantity_on_hand`; from `WASTED`, from `quantity_wasted` back to `quantity_on_hand`. `received_total` never changes. Only when the source state was `PREPARED` is the encounter's `pending_administration_count` decremented. A lot sitting at `DEPLETED` with a positive `quantity_on_hand` returns to `AVAILABLE`. The order's `doses_administered_today` and `last_administered_at`, the student's observed and effective scores and tier, the lot's days of supply, the building's two days-of-supply figures, protocol status and posture are all recomputed. The same `VOIDED` state is also reached by system cascade, without any window check, whenever a linked order leaves `VERIFIED` or a lot leaves `AVAILABLE`.
### 3.8. Screening Programmes, Rescreen Rounds and Segregated Referral Closure

Screening is scheduled ahead of the event. A school nurse books a screening for an `ENROLLED` student whose `school_id` matches the building supplied, naming the `screening_type` — `VISION`, `HEARING` or `SCOLIOSIS` — and the `scheduled_for` instant. The screening opens at `SCHEDULED` with `cycle_number` 1 and `outcome` null. Screenings may be listed filtered by `student_id`, `school_id`, `screened_by_nurse_id`, `screening_type` and `status`.

Recording a result supplies a `result_value` and the `screened_by_nurse_id` of the nurse who performed it, and requires that nurse to hold an `ACTIVE` coverage block at the screening's building — failing which the step is refused with `ERR_NURSE_NOT_ON_COVERAGE`. The measurement is classified against thresholds specific to the programme, and each boundary is inclusive on the named edge. For `VISION`, where the value is the Snellen denominator at twenty feet, a value of 30 or below is `PASS`, a value strictly above 30 and strictly below 40 is `INCONCLUSIVE`, and a value of 40 or above is `REFER`. For `HEARING`, where the value is the pure-tone threshold in decibels hearing level, 20 or below is `PASS`, strictly above 20 and strictly below 30 is `INCONCLUSIVE`, and 30 or above is `REFER`. For `SCOLIOSIS`, where the value is the Adams forward-bend trunk rotation in degrees, 5 or below is `PASS`, strictly above 5 and strictly below 7 is `INCONCLUSIVE`, and 7 or above is `REFER`. A vision value of exactly 40 therefore refers, exactly 30 passes, and 35 is inconclusive. Only one measurement exists per cycle; the classification never reads prior cycles.

The three classifications fork the screening with equal ceremony. A `PASS` moves `SCHEDULED` → `COMPLETED`, storing `outcome` as `PASS` and the recording nurse. A `REFER` moves `SCHEDULED` → `REFERRED`, storing `outcome` as `REFER` and the recording nurse, setting `referral_due_at` to 30 days after the logical clock, and adding `SCREENING_REFERRAL` to the student's alert flags. An `INCONCLUSIVE` moves `SCHEDULED` → `RESCREEN_DUE`, storing `outcome` as `INCONCLUSIVE` and the recording nurse, and setting `rescreen_due_at` to 14 days after the clock.

Follow-up carries an `action`. `SCHEDULE_RESCREEN` applies to a screening at `RESCREEN_DUE` whose `cycle_number` is still below 3: it increments `cycle_number` by one, sets `scheduled_for` to the newly supplied instant, clears both `outcome` and `result_value` back to null, and returns the screening to `SCHEDULED`, closing the cycle `SCHEDULED` → `RESCREEN_DUE` → `SCHEDULED`. A fourth round, requested when `cycle_number` already stands at 3, is refused with `ERR_RESCREEN_LIMIT_REACHED` (HTTP 409) at ladder position 46. `CLOSE_REFERRAL` applies to a screening at `REFERRED`: it requires a supplied `referral_outcome` of `TREATED`, `NO_ACTION` or `LOST_TO_FOLLOWUP`, and it requires the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from the stored `screened_by_nurse_id` — the nurse who found the problem may not be the one who declares it resolved. On success `referral_outcome` is stored, `referral_completed_at` is stamped with the clock, the screening becomes `CLOSED`, and `SCREENING_REFERRAL` is dropped from the student's alert flags when no other screening for that student remains `REFERRED`.

Two further paths exist. Left unattended, a screening at `RESCREEN_DUE` whose `rescreen_due_at` the clock has reached is promoted at the daily checkpoint straight to `REFERRED`, with `outcome` set to `REFER`, `referral_due_at` set to 30 days after the clock, and `SCREENING_REFERRAL` added to the student's alert flags. And a screening at `SCHEDULED` or `RESCREEN_DUE` is moved by the system to `DECLINED` when the student's `enrollment_status` leaves `ENROLLED` for `TRANSFERRED` or `WITHDRAWN`. `CLOSED` and `DECLINED` are terminal.

### 3.9. Releasing Health Records: Consent, Decision, Redaction and Handover

A records officer opens a release request naming the `student_id`, a `requester_type` of `GUARDIAN`, `EXTERNAL_PROVIDER`, `SCHOOL_STAFF` or `AGENCY`, a `requester_reference` identifying the requesting party as written on the release form, a non-empty `scope` drawn from `VISITS`, `MEDICATIONS`, `SCREENINGS` and `ACTION_PLANS`, and a `purpose_code` of `TREATMENT`, `ENROLLMENT`, `LEGAL` or `RESEARCH`. Records survive withdrawal, so the student need not be enrolled. The request opens at `DRAFT` with `release_package_item_count` at 0 and a `redaction_level` computed immediately. Requests may be listed filtered by `student_id`, `status` and `requester_type`.

**Redaction is decided top-down, first match winning.** If the student's `privacy_hold` is true and the `requester_type` is anything other than `GUARDIAN`, the level is `FULL`. Otherwise, if the `purpose_code` is `ENROLLMENT` or `RESEARCH`, or if `MEDICATIONS` appears in the `scope` while the `requester_type` is `AGENCY`, the level is `PARTIAL`. In every other case the level is `NONE`. The level is recomputed on creation, on submission, on approval and on release.

The request then walks `DRAFT` → `CONSENT_PENDING` → `AWAITING_DECISION` → `APPROVED` → `RELEASED`. Submission by the records officer moves `DRAFT` to `CONSENT_PENDING` and recomputes the redaction level. The guardian of that child then records a `decision`: `GRANT` moves `CONSENT_PENDING` to `AWAITING_DECISION`, stamping `consent_granted_at` with the logical clock and setting `expires_at` to 30 days after that instant. `WITHDRAW` may be recorded from `CONSENT_PENDING`, `AWAITING_DECISION` or `APPROVED` and moves the request to `REVOKED`, clearing `consent_granted_at` and `expires_at` back to null and zeroing `release_package_item_count`.

The physician's `decision` on an `AWAITING_DECISION` request is `APPROVE` or `DENY`, and may carry a `redaction_override`. Approval requires the computed `redaction_level` not to be `FULL` — a fully redacted request cannot be approved, and the refusal is `ERR_PRIVACY_HOLD_BLOCKS_RELEASE` (HTTP 403) at ladder position 29 — and requires the clock to be strictly before `expires_at`, failing which the refusal is `ERR_DISCLOSURE_CONSENT_EXPIRED` (HTTP 409) at position 38. On approval, `decided_by_user_id` is set to the acting `X-User-ID`, the request becomes `APPROVED`, and the redaction level is recomputed; a supplied `redaction_override` may only tighten a level of `NONE` to `PARTIAL` and may never loosen an existing level. A `decision` of `DENY` sets `decided_by_user_id`, moves the request to `DENIED` and zeroes `release_package_item_count`.

**Handover is segregated and time-boxed.** Only a records officer whose `X-User-ID` differs from the stored `decided_by_user_id` may release an `APPROVED` request, and only while the clock is strictly before `expires_at` — a release attempted exactly at the expiry instant is refused with `ERR_DISCLOSURE_CONSENT_EXPIRED`. The student's `privacy_hold` must be false unless the `requester_type` is `GUARDIAN`; otherwise the release is refused with `ERR_PRIVACY_HOLD_BLOCKS_RELEASE`. On success `released_by_user_id` is set to the acting identifier, `released_at` is stamped with the clock, the request becomes `RELEASED`, and the package is finally counted.

**Counting the package.** Only terminal-quality records are releasable. The visits count is the student's encounters in `CLOSED` status — an encounter that is `OPEN`, `IN_ASSESSMENT`, `DISPOSITIONED` or `REOPENED` is never in a package. The medications count is the student's orders whose `consent_status` is `CONSENT_GRANTED`; pending and withdrawn orders are excluded. The screenings count is the student's screenings whose `status` is `COMPLETED`, `REFERRED` or `CLOSED`; `SCHEDULED`, `RESCREEN_DUE` and `DECLINED` are excluded. The plans count is the student's action plans whose `status` is `ACTIVE`, `SUPERSEDED` or `EXPIRED`; `DRAFT`, `PENDING_GUARDIAN`, `PENDING_PROVIDER` and `REVOKED` are excluded. Where the redaction level is `PARTIAL`, two withholdings then apply to those counts: encounters whose `complaint_code` is `MENTAL_HEALTH` are subtracted from the visits count, and orders whose `is_controlled` flag is true — that is, orders for `METHYLPHENIDATE` — are subtracted from the medications count. Where the level is `FULL`, `release_package_item_count` is 0 and the release is refused outright with `ERR_PRIVACY_HOLD_BLOCKS_RELEASE`. Finally, `release_package_item_count` is the sum of only those family counts whose family is named in the `scope`; a family absent from the scope contributes nothing. Until the request reaches `RELEASED` the count remains 0.

One last path closes the lifecycle. During the daily checkpoint, an `APPROVED` request whose `expires_at` the clock has reached moves to `EXPIRED` with `release_package_item_count` zeroed. `DENIED`, `RELEASED`, `EXPIRED` and `REVOKED` are all terminal.
