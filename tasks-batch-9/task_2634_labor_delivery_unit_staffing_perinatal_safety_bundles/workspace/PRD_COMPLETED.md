# Perinatal Safety Ops API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Every labor and delivery floor runs two books at once. One is the clinical record — who is in which room, how far along she is, what the strip showed at the last look. The other is a staffing ledger nobody writes down: which nurse still has room for another patient, whether the operating room could actually be opened in the next fifteen minutes, how many induction slots tomorrow can absorb before the unit starts turning people away. The Perinatal Safety Ops API is the system that makes the second book explicit, arithmetic and auditable, so that a charge nurse deciding whether to start an elective induction at 39 weeks is looking at the same numbers the quality committee will read three months later.

The product covers eight interlocking concerns for a single hospital obstetrics unit: staffing ratios that vary by labor phase and patient acuity and that sometimes collapse into an absolute one-to-one requirement; induction scheduling constrained at once by physical bed availability and by a daily bed-minute budget; hemorrhage and severe-hypertension bundles whose interventions are scored against timed checklists; emergency cesarean decision-to-incision readiness backed by periodically re-validated drills; fetal monitoring interpretation with an escalation chain that re-arms rather than restarts; structured review of unexpected newborn complications; doula and support-person visitation limited by room occupancy and by what is happening in the room; and quality measure reporting on primary cesarean and episiotomy rates.

Two design commitments run through the whole specification and should be read as requirements rather than as background. First, the unit's posture — whether it is open, ratio-strained, frozen to new inductions, or diverting — is never set by hand; it is derived, on every mutation, from the coverage deficit, the bed census, and the state of any active safety bundle. Second, nothing in this system consults a wall clock. Every deadline, freshness window, expiry and grace period is measured against a logical clock that an operator sets explicitly, and the consequences of time passing are realised only when a capacity sweep is run against the unit. Deadlines therefore do not fire spontaneously; they fire when someone sweeps.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting individual, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role that individual is acting in. A request missing either header, or carrying a role string that is not one of the six defined below, is rejected with `ERR_MISSING_OR_INVALID_USER_CONTEXT_HEADER` and HTTP 400. A request whose headers are well formed but whose role is not authorised for the requested operation is rejected with `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_PERMISSIONS` and HTTP 403. There are six roles: `ChargeNurse`, `BedsideNurse`, `ObstetricianAttending`, `AnesthesiaLead`, `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` and `PatientAccessCoordinator`.

When `X-User-Role` is `ChargeNurse`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `StaffMember`; ownership and authorship checks on any resource that references a `StaffMember` compare the resource's staff-reference field to `X-User-ID`. The charge nurse is the shift-by-shift owner of the unit's coverage: building, activating, relieving and cancelling shift assignments, attaching and blocking beds, creating and releasing care assignments, driving the labor phase ladder to discharge, admitting confirmed inductions, running readiness drills, and opening and scheduling newborn complication debriefs.

When `X-User-Role` is `BedsideNurse`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `StaffMember`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `StaffMember` compare the resource's staff-reference field to `X-User-ID`. This role holds no operation exclusively — by design, every capability it has is shared with at least one other role — and works at the bedside of one encounter: activating hemorrhage and hypertension bundles, completing timed checklist items, interpreting fetal monitoring strips and opening the escalation chain. Its writes carry an extra gate: the performer must be attached to the case, meaning the named staff member holds an `ACTIVE` `CareAssignment` on the encounter or an `ACTIVE` shift in the `CHARGE` or `OR_STANDBY` slot on the unit, failing which the request is refused with `ERR_PERFORMER_NOT_ON_CASE`.

When `X-User-Role` is `ObstetricianAttending`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `StaffMember`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `StaffMember` compare the resource's staff-reference field to `X-User-ID`. The physician of record owns the irreversible clinical decisions: escalating a bundle's stage, resolving a bundle, rescinding a bundle, standing down a fetal escalation chain, confirming an induction, transferring a patient out, and the entire emergency cesarean sequence from declaration through mobilisation, incision, delivery and abort.

When `X-User-Role` is `AnesthesiaLead`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `StaffMember`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `StaffMember` compare the resource's staff-reference field to `X-User-ID`. Like the bedside nurse this role holds nothing exclusively by design. It staffs operating-room standby coverage, sharing shift creation with the charge nurse, schedules and documents cesarean readiness drills, and reads the readiness and operating-room surfaces. It cannot touch labor phase, bundle or bed state.

When `X-User-Role` is `PerinatalSafetyOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `StaffMember`; ownership checks on any resource that references a `StaffMember` compare the resource's staff-reference field to `X-User-ID`. This is the quality and safety authority: it stands up and reconfigures units and beds and performs every retrospective sign-off — closing a bundle debrief, validating and invalidating drills, closing an emergency cesarean case, requesting rework on and closing a newborn review, and creating and certifying quality measure snapshots. It never documents primary care.

When `X-User-Role` is `PatientAccessCoordinator`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server may record it (for example, as `certified_by_user_id` or `closed_by_user_id` where a transition writes the acting identifier) but does not compare it to any entity's `id`. This non-clinical access and scheduling role requests induction bookings, holds and releases labor-delivery-recovery bed capacity, re-queues bumped bookings, cancels bookings, and admits and checks support persons in and out at the door. It holds no clinical write and appears in none of the six duty pairs described in section 2.9.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_` followed by exactly 20 characters drawn from the upper-case letters and the digits.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits. It is stamped from the host's real clock rather than from the logical clock of section 2.5, and it is the only value in the system read that way: the logical clock governs entity state, every deadline and every derived field, but never this envelope field.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints. They are also included by the coverage report of section 3.3, whose embedded encounter rows are windowed by `limit` and `offset` even though its `response_type` stays `object`; every other computed read returns an unpaginated object.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every collection read accepts `limit` and `offset` as query parameters. `limit` is an integer no smaller than 1 and no larger than 100, and defaults to 10 when the caller omits it; `offset` is an integer no smaller than 0 and defaults to 0. A `limit` below 1 or above 100, or a negative `offset`, is a field-value failure and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` and HTTP 400, and so is a `limit` or `offset` that is not a base-ten integer at all. A filter query parameter whose declared type is an enumeration, a boolean or a date fails the same way when its value is not a member of that enumeration or does not parse, while a query parameter the endpoint does not declare is ignored rather than rejected. The `meta` block of a list response reports the `limit` and `offset` actually applied, the `total_count` of rows matching the filter before the window was applied, and `has_more`, which is true when rows matching the filter remain beyond the returned window and false otherwise.

Each collection declares its own ordering, and that ordering is part of the contract because several algorithms in this specification consume the same order. Where no other ordering is stated, rows are returned by `created_at` ascending. The record's `id` is never an ordering key anywhere in this system; where two rows tie on their declared sort field, the tie is broken by `created_at` in the direction stated for that collection. Because a logical clock that only moves when an operator moves it makes identical `created_at` values ordinary rather than rare, rows that tie there as well are ordered by the sequence in which they were created, taken in the direction of that collection's last declared sort key; and a row whose sort field is null orders after every non-null value in an ascending direction and before all of them in a descending one.

### 2.4. Chart Identity and Revision Counters

Every persisted record in this system carries four common fields. `id` is a server-minted UUID with no type prefix, and clients never supply it. `created_at` and `updated_at` are ISO 8601 UTC timestamps with three fractional digits, set on creation and refreshed on every mutation respectively. `version` is an integer that starts at 1 when the record is created and increments by 1 on each mutation of that record; because this system cascades aggressively, a single request routinely increments the `version` of a dozen records, and every such increment is called out in the workflow descriptions in section 3. Two conventions bound that counting. A record written several times inside one request still advances its `version` by exactly 1, never once per internal step. And a cascade that re-derives a record but leaves every stored field at the value it already held does not by itself advance that record's `version` — an unchanged care-assignment debit, an encounter whose derived figures all recompute identically, a staff member whose `capacity_units` does not move; the `ObstetricUnit` is the deliberate exception, since every re-derivation of its posture increments its `version` whether or not the posture, the deficit or the buffer changed, as is a `DRAFT` quality measure snapshot recomputed because a delivery landed inside its window.

Four write operations additionally require the caller to echo the current revision of a parent record, so that a caller working from a chart it read earlier cannot silently overwrite decisions taken in between. Creating a `CareAssignment` requires `expected_encounter_version` to equal the current `version` of the named `LaborEncounter`; when the echoed value does not match, the write is refused with `ERR_STALE_ENCOUNTER_VERSION` and HTTP 412, and nothing is created or debited. Placing a capacity hold on an induction booking requires `expected_unit_version` to equal the current `version` of the owning `ObstetricUnit`; a mismatch is refused with `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION` and HTTP 412, leaving the bed and both bed-minute maps untouched. Creating a quality measure snapshot likewise requires `expected_unit_version` to equal the unit's current `version`, and a mismatch is refused with `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION` and HTTP 412 before any cohort is computed. Rescinding a safety bundle requires `expected_activation_version` to equal the current `version` of the `SafetyBundleActivation`, and a mismatch is refused with `ERR_STALE_ACTIVATION_VERSION` and HTTP 412 with no checklist item voided and no coverage restored.

The point of these four guards is that the parent record moves under the caller's feet for reasons the caller cannot see. A posture re-derivation triggered by somebody else's checklist completion increments the unit's `version`; an acuity re-price triggered by a phase advance increments the encounter's `version`. A caller holding a revision number that is no longer current is, by definition, reasoning about a chart state that has been superseded, and its write is refused rather than merged. The guard is evaluated before any state-machine or business rule for that operation, so a stale revision number is reported as a stale revision even when the request would also have failed a clinical precondition.

### 2.5. The Unit Clock and the Capacity Sweep

Time in this system is a value, not an observation. A single logical clock, referred to throughout this document as the current instant, supplies the answer to every question of the form "is this late", "has this expired", "is this fresh enough" and "is this due yet". At server startup the current instant is exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`. Any of the six roles may read the clock, and any of the six may set it by supplying `current_utc` as an ISO 8601 timestamp; the clock may be moved forward or backward, and setting it changes no entity state, recomputes no derived field and increments no `version`. Both the read and the set answer 200 OK with a data object whose only field is `current_utc`, the instant then in force. Every instant a caller supplies anywhere in this system is parsed the same way: a trailing `Z` or an explicit offset is converted to UTC, a value carrying no zone at all is read as already being UTC, a date-only value is read as midnight UTC, and a string that does not parse is an `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` field-value failure. The server never falls back to real wall-clock time under any circumstance for a question of state, deadline, expiry or freshness; the one value it does read from the host clock is the `api_request_timestamp` of the response envelope, which no rule consumes.

Because setting the clock changes nothing, the consequences of elapsed time are realised by an explicit operator action: the capacity sweep, run against one `ObstetricUnit` by a `ChargeNurse`, a `PatientAccessCoordinator` or a `PerinatalSafetyOfficer`. The sweep executes six steps in a pinned order and no other. First, it freezes the unit's `staffing_posture` as it stands at the start of the sweep, together with the `turnover_buffer_minutes` derived from that posture, and uses those frozen values for the whole sweep. Second, it clears beds in `TURNOVER` whose `turnover_clear_at` is at or before the current instant, returning them to `AVAILABLE`. Third, it expires bookings in `CAPACITY_HELD` whose `hold_expires_at` is at or before the current instant, releasing the held bed and returning the held bed-minutes. Fourth, it requeues bookings in `BUMPED` whose `requested_start_utc` is strictly after the current instant back to `REQUESTED`, recomputing their `priority_class`, and moves the remainder to `EXPIRED`. Fifth, it promotes bookings in `REQUESTED` one at a time, re-testing both the day's bed-minute budget and bed availability after each single promotion. Sixth, and only once, at the very end, it re-derives the unit's posture.

Alongside the booking waterfall the same sweep settles three other classes of overdue obligation, all three of them after the fourth booking step and before the fifth, so that the promotion which follows sees the capacity a breach-driven bump has just released, and in this order: checklist items still `PENDING` past their grace window become `MISSED`; fetal escalation chains sitting past a deadline become `TIMED_OUT_BREACH`, and chains sitting in `REASSESSMENT_PENDING` past their reassessment deadline re-arm to `ESCALATION_DUE`; and support passes in `ISSUED` or `CHECKED_OUT` whose validity has elapsed become `EXPIRED`. After promotion and before the closing posture re-derivation the sweep also re-ages drill currency for every staff member on the unit, since currency is a pure function of elapsed time: `drill_currency_state` becomes `CURRENT` where `last_validated_drill_at` is set and 90 days from it have not yet run out against the current instant, and `LAPSED` otherwise, with only the members whose state actually changes being touched and their `version` advanced. The sweep grants no currency of its own — the anchor it reads is written by drill validation alone — but it is what makes a lapse visible to the readiness derivation that runs moments later. Every encounter's `staffing_state` is then recomputed, in `created_at` ascending order, before the sixth step re-derives the unit. A sweep with nothing due is not an error: every counter it reports is 0, every array is empty, no record is touched, and the response is a normal 200 OK. The sweep records `last_capacity_sweep_at` on the unit, which is null until the first sweep runs. It answers 200 OK with an unpaginated object of exactly sixteen fields, in this order: `unit_id`; `swept_at`, the instant swept; `posture_at_sweep`, the posture frozen at the first step; `beds_cleared_count`; `holds_expired_booking_ids`; `bookings_requeued_booking_ids`; `bookings_expired_booking_ids`; `promoted_booking_ids`; `promoted_count`; `missed_checklist_item_ids`; `breached_assessment_ids`; `rearmed_assessment_ids`; `expired_pass_ids`; `breached_encounter_ids`; and finally `staffing_posture` and `coverage_deficit_units` as they stand after the closing re-derivation, which is why the first and the last posture field of one response may disagree. Each array lists only the records that step actually moved, in the order it processed them: expiring holds by `hold_expires_at` then `created_at` ascending, settled bumped bookings by `requested_start_utc` then `created_at` ascending, promotions in the promotion order of section 3.5, missed checklist items activation by activation in `activated_at` then `created_at` ascending and within an activation by `step_sequence` ascending, assessments encounter by encounter in `created_at` ascending and within an encounter by `assessed_at` then `created_at` ascending, and passes by `valid_until` then `created_at` ascending. `breached_encounter_ids` is narrower than the rest: it names only the encounters this sweep newly drove into `COVERAGE_BREACH`, not those that were already sitting there when it began.

### 2.6. Order of Refusal

When a request would fail more than one rule, the outcome is fully determined: rules are evaluated strictly in ascending priority order, and the first rule that fails produces the response. No later rule is consulted, so the presence of a second, third or fourth defect in the same request never changes the reported `error_id` or HTTP status. The ladder below is the complete evaluation order. Within the business band the ordering follows a deliberate domain principle: segregation of duties is checked before clinical safety, clinical safety before structural constraints, structural constraints before temporal windows, temporal windows before capacity and quota, and capacity and quota before reporting completeness.

**Priority 1** — authentication context, on every operation without exception: a missing or unrecognised `X-User-ID` or `X-User-Role` yields `ERR_MISSING_OR_INVALID_USER_CONTEXT_HEADER` at HTTP 400. **Priority 2** — role authorisation, on every operation without exception: a role not listed for the operation yields `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_PERMISSIONS` at HTTP 403. **Priority 3** — existence of every identifier taken from the request path, on every operation that carries one, resolved strictly left to right so that a unit identifier is resolved before a bed or snapshot identifier in the same path and an activation identifier before a checklist item identifier; a nested identifier that resolves to a real record which does not belong to the parent named earlier in the same path is reported as that nested record's own not-found outcome at HTTP 404, not as a scoping failure; the fifteen possible outcomes are `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_BED_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STAFF_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SHIFT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ENCOUNTER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CARE_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_BOOKING_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ACTIVATION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CHECKLIST_ITEM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ASSESSMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DRILL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REVIEW_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PASS_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_SNAPSHOT_NOT_FOUND`, each at HTTP 404.

**Priority 4** — existence of every identifier taken from the request body, resolved in the order the fields are declared for that operation, yielding `ERR_ENCOUNTER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SHIFT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STAFF_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_BED_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ASSESSMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ACTIVATION_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND` at HTTP 404. This priority applies to exactly these operations: care-assignment creation, shift-assignment creation, encounter bed attachment, bundle activation, checklist item completion, fetal assessment recording, fetal escalation, emergency cesarean declaration, cesarean team mobilisation, drill scheduling, newborn review opening, induction capacity holding, and shift relief. It also covers the `unit_id` named in the body of walk-in encounter creation and of induction booking creation, and the `encounter_id` named in the body of newborn review opening and of support pass issuance, where `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND` and `ERR_ENCOUNTER_NOT_FOUND` respectively are the outcomes. A body identifier that is absent, explicitly null or not a string is not resolved at this priority at all: it falls through to priority 5, where that field's own type and requiredness rules decide the outcome.

**Priority 5** — field types, enum membership, string lengths and numeric ranges, on every operation: `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` at HTTP 400. A request body that is absent or is not a JSON object is treated as an empty object; an explicit JSON `null` is treated exactly as an omission, so it takes the field's documented default and fails only where the field is required; a boolean is never accepted where an integer is declared; and a field the operation does not declare is ignored rather than rejected. **Priority 6** — the revision guard described in section 2.4, on care-assignment creation, induction capacity holding, snapshot creation and bundle rescission, yielding `ERR_STALE_ENCOUNTER_VERSION`, `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION` or `ERR_STALE_ACTIVATION_VERSION` at HTTP 412; it is checked before any state-machine or business rule for those four operations. The echoed integer is itself a required field, so omitting it or supplying a non-integer fails earlier, at priority 5, with `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` rather than at 412. **Priority 7** — cross-aggregate scoping, meaning that every referenced record must belong to the same `ObstetricUnit` as the operation's subject: `ERR_CROSS_UNIT_REFERENCE` at HTTP 422, on care-assignment creation, shift-assignment creation, encounter bed attachment, cesarean team mobilisation, fetal assessment recording, fetal escalation, drill scheduling, drill documentation, checklist item completion and newborn review opening. Emergency cesarean declaration is scoped at this same position by a narrower rule of its own: it takes no referenced record on another unit, but a `triggering_assessment_id` or `triggering_activation_id` naming a record that belongs to a different encounter is refused with the same `ERR_CROSS_UNIT_REFERENCE`.

**Priority 8** — the declared-transition guard, on every action operation: an attempt to move a record from a state for which the requested edge is not declared yields `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` at HTTP 409. Ten sub-bands follow, all at HTTP 409, in this exact order.

- **8.01** the subject encounter's `labor_phase` must not be `DISCHARGED` or `TRANSFERRED_OUT`, yielding `ERR_ENCOUNTER_PHASE_TERMINAL`, on care-assignment creation, bundle activation, checklist item completion, fetal assessment recording, emergency cesarean declaration, cesarean team mobilisation, support pass issuance, support pass check-in and newborn review opening.
- **8.02** the unit's posture must permit the operation, yielding `ERR_UNIT_POSTURE_BLOCKS_OPERATION`, on walk-in encounter creation, phase advance, induction capacity holding, induction confirmation, induction admission and drill start.
- **8.03** the parallel coverage axis must satisfy the target phase's floor, yielding `ERR_ENCOUNTER_COVERAGE_INSUFFICIENT` or `ERR_ONE_TO_ONE_COVERAGE_REQUIRED`, on phase advance.
- **8.04** blocking children, checked in exactly the order `ERR_NEWBORN_REVIEW_OPEN`, then `ERR_BUNDLE_NOT_CLOSED`, then `ERR_SUPPORT_PASS_STILL_PRESENT`, then `ERR_CARE_ASSIGNMENT_STILL_ACTIVE`, and for the transfer path `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_IN_PROGRESS` then `ERR_BUNDLE_STAGE_BLOCKS_TRANSFER`, on encounter discharge and encounter transfer-out. `ERR_NEWBORN_REVIEW_OPEN` alone also guards emergency cesarean closure, where it is decided after that path's declared-transition guard and before its segregation-of-duties test.
- **8.05** prerequisite children of a bundle step, yielding `ERR_CHECKLIST_STAGE_INCOMPLETE`, `ERR_CHECKLIST_STAGE_NOT_REACHED` or `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_IN_PROGRESS`, on bundle stage escalation, checklist item completion and bundle resolution.
- **8.06** at most one non-terminal activation per encounter and bundle type, yielding `ERR_BUNDLE_ALREADY_ACTIVE`, on bundle activation.
- **8.07** bed lifecycle guards, yielding `ERR_BED_TYPE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `ERR_OR_BED_UNAVAILABLE`, `ERR_BED_HOLD_MISMATCH`, `ERR_BED_NOT_RELEASABLE`, `ERR_BED_HAS_FUTURE_HOLDS` or `ERR_ENCOUNTER_ALREADY_IN_BED`, on induction admission, induction bump, encounter bed attachment, cesarean team mobilisation, bed status change and bed retirement. On induction admission and induction bump the guard is `ERR_BED_HOLD_MISMATCH`, that the bed the booking points at still records that booking as its holder. On cesarean team mobilisation this band decides `ERR_BED_TYPE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` for a nominated bed whose `bed_type` is not `OR`, and only then `ERR_OR_BED_UNAVAILABLE` for an `OR` bed that is not `AVAILABLE`.
- **8.08** shift and coverage guards, yielding `ERR_SHIFT_NOT_ACTIVE`, `ERR_SHIFT_HAS_ACTIVE_COVERAGE`, `ERR_RELIEVING_SHIFT_INELIGIBLE` or `ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_NOT_CURRENT`, on care-assignment creation, shift activation, shift relief and shift cancellation.
- **8.09** induction lifecycle guards, yielding `ERR_INDUCTION_HOLD_EXPIRED`, `ERR_INDUCTION_BUMP_LIMIT_REACHED`, `ERR_INDUCTION_NOT_BUMPABLE`, `ERR_INDUCTION_WINDOW_PASSED` or `ERR_INDUCTION_ALREADY_ADMITTED`, on induction confirmation, bump, requeue and cancellation.
- **8.10** escalation and attempt-limit guards, yielding `ERR_ESCALATION_TIER_SKIPPED`, `ERR_ESCALATION_NOT_STARTED`, `ERR_CESAREAN_ALREADY_INCISED`, `ERR_DRILL_NOT_ABANDONABLE`, `ERR_BUNDLE_STAGE_BLOCKS_RESCIND`, `ERR_REVIEW_REWORK_LIMIT_REACHED`, `ERR_DRILL_REVISION_LIMIT_REACHED` or `ERR_SUPPORT_REENTRY_LIMIT_REACHED`, on fetal escalation, fetal escalation stand-down, cesarean abort, drill abandonment, drill documentation, bundle rescission, review rework request and support pass check-in. Fetal escalation stand-down carries one further guard immediately behind `ERR_ESCALATION_NOT_STARTED` and still inside this band: `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_IN_PROGRESS`, if any emergency cesarean event this very assessment triggered has already reached theatre.

**Priority 9** — context-required fields and their length bounds, at HTTP 422: `ERR_RESCIND_REASON_REQUIRED`, `ERR_INVALIDATION_REASON_REQUIRED`, `ERR_DEBRIEF_NOTES_REQUIRED`, `ERR_CONTRIBUTING_FACTORS_REQUIRED` and `ERR_DELIVERY_MODE_REQUIRED`, on bundle rescission, bundle debrief closure, drill invalidation, review findings submission, review closure and phase advance. **Priority 10** — segregation of duties, `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION` at HTTP 403, on bundle debrief closure, drill validation, review debrief scheduling, review rework request, review closure, emergency cesarean closure and snapshot certification.

**Priority 11** covers clinical eligibility in eight ordered sub-bands, at HTTP 422 except for those few identifiers to which the table in section 5 assigns a different status.

- **11.1** staff credential and employment status, yielding `ERR_STAFF_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`, on care-assignment creation, shift-assignment creation, shift activation, shift relief, cesarean team mobilisation, drill scheduling, drill documentation, fetal assessment recording and newborn review opening. Wherever a whole roster of named staff is supplied at once — the mobilised cesarean team, a drill's participants — the test is applied to every member of it, in the order the identifiers appear in the payload, and one member who is not `ACTIVE` or whose `credential_expires_at` is not strictly after the current instant refuses the whole request.
- **11.2** competency and slot eligibility, yielding `ERR_ROLE_SLOT_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `ERR_MONITORING_CERT_INSUFFICIENT`, `ERR_STAFF_NOT_EXCLUSIVELY_AVAILABLE`, `ERR_OR_STANDBY_NOT_QUALIFIED`, `ERR_ESCALATION_TARGET_INELIGIBLE` or — where a shared row is offered to an encounter that has outgrown shared coverage, and at that identifier's own HTTP 409 rather than 422 — `ERR_ONE_TO_ONE_COVERAGE_REQUIRED`, on care-assignment creation, shift activation, fetal assessment recording and fetal escalation.
- **11.3** fetal monitoring gates, yielding `ERR_FETAL_ASSESSMENT_STALE`, `ERR_FETAL_ESCALATION_UNRESOLVED`, `ERR_FETAL_CATEGORY_BLOCKS_PHASE`, `ERR_FETAL_CATEGORY_BLOCKS_ABORT` or `ERR_ESCALATION_CATEGORY_INSUFFICIENT`, on phase advance, cesarean abort and fetal escalation.
- **11.4** cesarean readiness and team gates, yielding `ERR_CESAREAN_READINESS_NOT_MET`, `ERR_CESAREAN_TEAM_INCOMPLETE`, `ERR_OR_STANDBY_NOT_STAFFED`, `ERR_DRILL_PARTICIPANTS_INSUFFICIENT` or `ERR_DRILL_PERFORMANCE_FAILED`, on induction confirmation, phase advance, cesarean team mobilisation, bundle stage escalation, fetal escalation, drill documentation and drill validation.
- **11.5** bundle clinical criteria, yielding `ERR_ACTIVATION_CRITERIA_NOT_MET`, `ERR_BUNDLE_STAGE_CRITERIA_NOT_MET`, `ERR_BUNDLE_NOT_CONTROLLED` or `ERR_BUNDLE_SCORE_NOT_COMPUTED`, on bundle activation, stage escalation, resolution and debrief closure; `ERR_BUNDLE_NOT_CLOSED` also sits in this band on review findings submission, where it is decided before that path's `ERR_DEBRIEF_NOT_HELD`.
- **11.6** visitation safety, yielding `ERR_SUPPORT_PASS_EXPIRED`, `ERR_DOULA_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED` or `ERR_VISITATION_RESTRICTED_BY_PROCEDURE`, on support pass issuance and support pass check-in.
- **11.7** delivery mode coherence, yielding `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_NOT_DELIVERED`, `ERR_DELIVERY_MODE_CONFLICTS_HISTORY` or `ERR_EPISIOTOMY_MODE_CONFLICT`, on phase advance.
- **11.8** elective induction compliance, yielding `ERR_ELECTIVE_INDUCTION_GESTATIONAL_AGE`, on induction capacity holding and induction confirmation.

**Priority 12** — the performer must be attached to the case, `ERR_PERFORMER_NOT_ON_CASE` at HTTP 422, on checklist item completion, fetal assessment recording and drill documentation. **Priority 13** covers structural uniqueness and interval collision in three sub-bands, all at HTTP 409: **13.1** uniqueness, yielding `ERR_DUPLICATE_PATIENT_RECORD_CODE`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_BED_LABEL` or `ERR_DUPLICATE_UNIT_NAME`, on unit creation, bed creation, walk-in encounter creation and induction admission; **13.2** staff shift interval collision and maximum span, yielding `ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_TOO_LONG` or `ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_OVERLAP` in that order of precedence, on shift-assignment creation; **13.3** bed interval collision and bed eligibility, yielding `ERR_BED_TURNOVER_CONFLICT`, `ERR_BED_TYPE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` or `ERR_ENCOUNTER_BED_NOT_ASSIGNED`, on induction capacity holding, encounter bed attachment and phase advance. On induction capacity holding the eligibility test comes first: `ERR_BED_TYPE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` for a nominated bed whose `bed_type` is not `LDR`, and only then `ERR_BED_TURNOVER_CONFLICT`. Cesarean team mobilisation reaches neither identifier in this band; its bed-type and bed-availability refusals are decided earlier, in band 8.07.

**Priority 14** covers time in two sub-bands: **14.1** clinical timestamp ranges, yielding `ERR_CHECKLIST_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`, `ERR_CESAREAN_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`, `ERR_DRILL_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE` or `ERR_ASSESSMENT_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`, all at HTTP 400, on checklist item completion, incision recording, delivery recording, drill scheduling, drill documentation and fetal assessment recording; **14.2** windows and deadlines, yielding `ERR_ESCALATION_WINDOW_EXPIRED`, `ERR_DEBRIEF_WINDOW_VIOLATION`, `ERR_DEBRIEF_NOT_HELD`, `ERR_DRILL_NOT_DUE`, `ERR_INDUCTION_ADMIT_TOO_EARLY`, `ERR_BOOKING_START_IN_PAST` or `ERR_MEASURE_PERIOD_INVALID`, all at HTTP 422, on fetal escalation, review debrief scheduling, review findings submission, drill start, induction booking creation, induction admission and snapshot creation.

**Priority 15** — capacity and quota, checked after everything else and always with an inclusive boundary, so that landing exactly on a limit is allowed and only exceeding it fails: `ERR_STAFF_ACUITY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`, `ERR_INDUCTION_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` and `ERR_SUPPORT_OCCUPANCY_EXCEEDED`, all at HTTP 409, on care-assignment creation, shift relief, induction capacity holding, induction confirmation and support pass check-in. **Priority 16** — reporting completeness, the last band of all: `ERR_EMPTY_MEASURE_DENOMINATOR`, `ERR_MEASURE_PERIOD_NOT_CLOSED` and `ERR_BUNDLE_NOT_CLOSED`, all at HTTP 409, on snapshot certification.

Five combinations are worth stating explicitly because intuition tends to pick the wrong winner. A care-assignment creation that names an encounter already `DISCHARGED` and also names a staff member whose credential has elapsed fails at 8.01 with `ERR_ENCOUNTER_PHASE_TERMINAL` and HTTP 409, not at 11.1 with `ERR_STAFF_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`. An induction confirmation on a unit whose `cesarean_readiness_state` is `NOT_READY` and whose day is already at its bed-minute cap fails at 11.4 with `ERR_CESAREAN_READINESS_NOT_MET` and HTTP 422, not at 15 with `ERR_INDUCTION_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. A bundle debrief closure attempted by a clinician who performed one of that activation's checklist steps, on an activation whose `timeliness_score` is still null, fails at 10 with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION` and HTTP 403, not at 11.5 with `ERR_BUNDLE_SCORE_NOT_COMPUTED`. An induction capacity hold whose bed window touches an existing hold on the same bed and whose day is also at cap fails at 13.3 with `ERR_BED_TURNOVER_CONFLICT`, not at 15 with `ERR_INDUCTION_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`, even though both carry HTTP 409. A checklist completion whose performer is not attached to the case and whose `completed_at` also precedes the activation instant fails at 12 with `ERR_PERFORMER_NOT_ON_CASE` and HTTP 422, not at 14.1 with `ERR_CHECKLIST_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`.

### 2.7. Interval Arithmetic: Two Deliberately Different Conventions

Staff time and bed time are compared differently, and the difference is intentional. A `ShiftAssignment` occupies the half-open interval from `shift_start_utc` inclusive to `shift_end_utc` exclusive. Two shifts for the same staff member collide when their half-open intervals share any instant; a shift that ends at precisely the instant the next one starts does not collide, and both may exist. A shift's span may not exceed 720 minutes.

A bed reservation, by contrast, occupies a closed interval on both ends. For a new hold the window runs from `requested_start_utc` to that instant plus `expected_labor_minutes`. For an existing hold on the same bed the window runs from its own `requested_start_utc` to that instant plus its frozen `projected_occupancy_minutes` — the frozen figure, not a freshly computed one. An existing reservation here means any other booking pointing at that bed whose `status` is `CAPACITY_HELD`, `CONFIRMED` or `ADMITTED`; a booking in any other status reserves nothing and cannot collide. The two collide when the new window's start is at or before the existing window's end and the existing window's start is at or before the new window's end. Touching endpoints therefore do collide: a hold that would begin at the exact minute an earlier reservation is projected to release the bed is refused with `ERR_BED_TURNOVER_CONFLICT`. A caller who reasons about beds using the shift convention will place holds that this system rejects, and that is the intended behaviour.

### 2.8. Rounding, Precision and Boundary Conventions

Coverage arithmetic is carried at one decimal place, rounding half away from zero: every acuity figure, every capacity figure, every debit and every deficit total is a one-decimal quantity. Bundle timeliness scores are carried at two decimal places, rounding half away from zero, and the clamp into the range from 0.00 to 100.00 is applied to the unrounded value before that rounding, never after. Quality measure rates are carried at four decimal places, rounding half away from zero, and the composite index is carried at two, again rounding half away from zero. The rolling mean of decision-to-incision minutes is carried at two decimal places, rounding half away from zero.

Elapsed-time measurements are not rounded at all: an elapsed duration in minutes is the total number of whole seconds divided by 60 with the fraction discarded, so that a duration of thirty minutes and fifty-nine seconds measures 30. Every derived deadline is computed by adding a whole number of minutes, hours or days to an anchor instant, and is therefore expressed at whole-minute precision in UTC. Bed-minute budgets and support-person counts are integers throughout.

Boundaries are stated explicitly wherever they appear, because they differ from rule to rule. Freshness and eligibility windows are generally inclusive: an assessment exactly 30 minutes old is still fresh enough, an escalation notified at exactly the deadline instant is still in time, a drill measured at exactly the pass threshold passes, and every capacity test at priority 15 admits the value that lands exactly on the limit. Breach and expiry tests are generally strict: a chain breaches only once the current instant is strictly past its deadline, a checklist step is late only when completed strictly after its due instant, and a support pass is already expired at the instant its validity ends. Each rule in section 3 restates its own boundary so that no reader has to infer it.

### 2.9. Segregation of Duties

Six pairs of duties are mutually exclusive in this system. In each pair, the second act is refused when the acting `X-User-ID` was a party to the first, with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION` at HTTP 403. First, a clinician who completed any checklist item on an activation may not close that activation's debrief. Second, a clinician recorded as the documenter of a readiness drill may never validate that drill. Third, a staff member who has held a `CareAssignment` on an encounter — in any status whatsoever, active, released or superseded — may not be named as the facilitator of a newborn complication review on that encounter. Fourth, the facilitator of a review, and equally anyone who held a `CareAssignment` on the reviewed encounter, may not close that review, and the facilitator may not request rework on it. Fifth, anyone recorded among the mobilised team of an emergency cesarean may not close that case: the surgeons who ran it cannot sign it off. Sixth, anyone who facilitated a newborn complication review inside a snapshot's reporting window may not certify that snapshot.

These pairs bind identities, not job titles. The comparison is always against a `StaffMember` identifier held in a field such as `performed_by_staff_id`, `documented_by_staff_id`, `facilitator_staff_id`, `mobilized_staff_ids` or a care assignment's `staff_id`. A clinician who steps into the `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` role in order to perform a sign-off is still the same individual who performed the step, and is still refused. Because `PatientAccessCoordinator` is never compared to a `StaffMember` identifier, it appears in none of the six pairs.

### 2.10. Immutability, Terminal States and Frozen Records

Several values are captured once and then deliberately never recomputed. A shift's `capacity_units_effective` is snapshotted at activation from the staff member's capacity at that moment; later edits to that staff member's competency or employment status change the staff member's own `capacity_units` but never retro-change an already-snapshotted shift. A booking's `projected_occupancy_minutes` is frozen when its capacity hold is placed, and every later credit or debit of the day's bed-minute budget moves exactly that frozen figure, so that a change in the unit's turnover buffer between hold and release cannot leave the budget drifting. A certified quality measure snapshot freezes its counts and rates permanently and is excluded from all later recomputation, so a delivery recorded inside its window after certification changes nothing in it.

Terminal states are absolute. An encounter in `DISCHARGED` or `TRANSFERRED_OUT` accepts no further clinical writes. A bed in `CLOSED` is retired forever, and its `bed_label` becomes reusable. A booking in `ADMITTED`, `CANCELLED` or `EXPIRED` never moves again. A drill in `ABANDONED` never moves again, while `VALIDATED` and `INVALIDATED` are not terminal, because a drill may be re-documented after invalidation. An activation in `CLOSED` or `RESCINDED`, a pass in `REVOKED` or `EXPIRED`, and a snapshot in `SUPERSEDED` are all final. Creation-time identity fields are immutable once written: an encounter's `unit_id`, a bundle's `bundle_type`, an assessment's `category`, a cesarean's `urgency_class`, a drill's `drill_type` and a pass's `credential_type` can never be changed, and neither can a child's parent reference.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up a Unit: Licences, Rooms and the People Who Staff Them

A `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` brings a unit into existence by naming it and declaring its physical capacity. `unit_name` is a string of 1 to 120 characters and must be unique across all units; a repeat name is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_UNIT_NAME`. `licensed_ldr_beds` and `licensed_or_rooms` are integers no smaller than 1. `max_daily_induction_bed_minutes` is an integer no smaller than 0 and defaults to 2880 when omitted — two full bed-days of induction time per calendar day, which is the quota the whole scheduling half of this product spends. A newly created unit opens with `staffing_posture` at `OPEN`, `coverage_deficit_units` at 0, `cesarean_readiness_state` at `NOT_READY` because no drill has yet been validated, `readiness_reason` at `NO_CURRENT_DRILL` and `current_drill_id`, `current_drill_expires_at` and `mean_d2i_minutes` all null for the same reason, `escalation_breach_count` and `bundle_breach_count` at 0, `turnover_buffer_minutes` at 45, both of its bed-minute ledgers — `committed_bed_minutes_by_day` and `held_bed_minutes_by_day`, each a map keyed by UTC calendar date — empty, and `last_capacity_sweep_at` null. Only `max_daily_induction_bed_minutes` may later be adjusted, by the same role, and that integer is required on that request rather than optional; the name, the licensed counts and every derived field are immutable through that path, and although raising or lowering the budget never moves the posture by itself, it immediately changes the outcome of the priority-15 capacity test. Units may be listed filtered by `staffing_posture` and by `cesarean_readiness_state`, ordered by `created_at` ascending, and a single unit read exposes every derived figure together with the `version` an optimistic caller must echo as `expected_unit_version`.

Beds are created inside a unit by a `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` or a `ChargeNurse` and take their `unit_id` from the path, immutably. `bed_label` is a string of 1 to 40 characters, unique within the unit among beds whose `status` is not `CLOSED`; a collision is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_BED_LABEL`. `bed_type` is one of `LDR`, `OR`, `TRIAGE` or `POSTPARTUM`, and the type decides what the bed can be used for: only an `LDR` bed satisfies an induction hold or a walk-in admission, and only an `OR` bed satisfies a cesarean mobilisation, with a mismatch refused as `ERR_BED_TYPE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. A bed opens at `AVAILABLE` and moves through `HELD`, `OCCUPIED`, `TURNOVER`, `BLOCKED` and finally `CLOSED`, carrying `current_encounter_id` while a patient is in it, `held_booking_id` while a booking reserves it, and `turnover_clear_at` while it is being cleaned. The first `AVAILABLE` bed of type `LDR` in a unit can by itself lift a bed-exhaustion divert, so bed creation re-derives the unit's posture and increments the unit's `version`.

A `ChargeNurse` may toggle a bed between `AVAILABLE` and `BLOCKED` by supplying `status`; those are the only two values that path accepts, and a request naming the status the bed already holds, or naming either status for a bed that is already `CLOSED`, is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` before the release guard is reached; a bed currently `HELD`, `OCCUPIED` or `TURNOVER` cannot be blocked, which is refused with `ERR_BED_NOT_RELEASABLE`. Either direction of the toggle re-derives the unit's posture and increments the unit's `version`. Blocking the last `AVAILABLE` `LDR` bed drives the unit into `DIVERT` through the bed-exhaustion branch described in section 3.3. Retirement is a `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` act and moves a bed from `AVAILABLE` or `BLOCKED` to `CLOSED`. An already-`CLOSED` bed is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`; for every other source state two guards follow, and their order is pinned the other way round from what the source restriction suggests. First, the bed is refused with `ERR_BED_HAS_FUTURE_HOLDS` while any booking in `CAPACITY_HELD` or `CONFIRMED` still points at it — that guard has to answer first, because such a booking always leaves its bed `HELD` and the source restriction would otherwise make the future-holds refusal unreachable. Only then, for a bed that is neither `AVAILABLE` nor `BLOCKED` and carries no such booking, the attempt is refused with `ERR_BED_NOT_RELEASABLE`. A permitted retirement also clears `current_encounter_id`, `held_booking_id` and `turnover_clear_at` to null and re-derives the unit's posture. Beds may be listed within a unit ordered by `bed_label` ascending — the same lexicographic order the capacity sweep uses when it chooses among candidate beds — or across units through a flat collection in that same label order with `created_at` ascending as its tie-break; both forms filter on `bed_type`, `status`, `current_encounter_id` and `held_booking_id`, and the flat form additionally on `unit_id`, which is the only way to discover the bed a cascade attached to a particular encounter or booking.

Staff members are created inside a unit, again with `unit_id` taken from the path and immutable, by a `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` or a `ChargeNurse`. `full_name` is a string of 1 to 120 characters. `staff_role` is one of `RN_LABOR_DELIVERY`, `OB_ATTENDING`, `CERTIFIED_NURSE_MIDWIFE`, `ANESTHESIOLOGIST`, `SURGICAL_TECH` or `NEONATAL_RESUSCITATOR`. `fetal_monitoring_cert_level` is `BASIC`, `ADVANCED` or `EXPIRED` and defaults to `BASIC`. `credential_expires_at` is a required instant. `or_circulating_qualified` is a boolean defaulting to false. Three fields are not the caller's to set: `employment_status` opens at `ACTIVE` and may later be moved to `ON_LEAVE` or `INACTIVE`; `drill_currency_state` opens at `LAPSED`; and `last_validated_drill_at` opens null. Currency is granted by exactly one act in the entire system — the validation of a readiness drill — and by nothing else; the capacity sweep of section 2.5 re-ages an existing grant against its 90-day window, and drill invalidation re-derives the anchor from whatever validated drills remain, but neither of them can create a grant that no validated drill supports. The member's `capacity_units` is seeded on creation and evaluates to 0.0 at that point, because capacity is keyed by the slot a member is rostered into rather than by the member's clinical role, and a member holding no shift is rostered into nothing.

Availability and competency are edited afterwards by a `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` or `ChargeNurse` supplying any of `employment_status`, `fetal_monitoring_cert_level`, `credential_expires_at` and `or_circulating_qualified`. All four are optional and only the fields actually supplied are written; `employment_status` obeys no state machine on this path, so it may be set to any of `ACTIVE`, `ON_LEAVE` or `INACTIVE`, and a member who has been stood down is restored simply by naming `ACTIVE` again. An edit that supplies none of the four, or supplies exactly the values already stored, is still accepted and still advances the member's `version` by exactly 1. Each such edit recomputes the member's `capacity_units`, which is how a member is driven to zero capacity — by moving employment away from `ACTIVE`, by setting the certification to `EXPIRED`, or by letting `credential_expires_at` fall at or before the current instant. Already-snapshotted shift capacities are untouched by these edits, and so is everything outside the member's own row: the edit re-derives neither the unit's posture and readiness nor any encounter's coverage figures, even when it drops the member's capacity to 0.0. Staff may be listed within a unit or across the whole system, both forms filtered by `staff_role`, `employment_status`, `drill_currency_state` and `or_circulating_qualified` and the system-wide form additionally by `unit_id`, ordered by `created_at` ascending, and a single read exposes `capacity_units`, `last_validated_drill_at` and `drill_currency_state`, the three values that gate operating-room standby coverage and unit readiness.

### 3.2. Rostering the Floor: Slots, Capacity Units and Relief Handoffs

A roster entry is created by a `ChargeNurse` or an `AnesthesiaLead`, naming `unit_id`, `staff_id`, a `role_slot` of `CHARGE`, `BEDSIDE`, `ONE_TO_ONE_FLOAT`, `OR_STANDBY` or `TRIAGE`, and the half-open window `shift_start_utc` to `shift_end_utc`, where the end must be strictly after the start, refused otherwise as an `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` field-value failure. The named member must be employable already at this point — `employment_status` `ACTIVE` and `credential_expires_at` strictly after the current instant — failing which the creation is refused with `ERR_STAFF_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`, which answers ahead of both window refusals. A window longer than 720 minutes is refused with `ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_TOO_LONG`, and a window sharing any instant with another window already rostered for the same staff member — meaning another of that member's entries still in `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE`, since a `RELIEVED` or `CANCELLED` entry reserves nothing and can never collide — is refused with `ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_OVERLAP`; a window that is at once too long and overlapping reports the span refusal, because the span is tested first. The entry opens in `SCHEDULED` with `assigned_acuity_load_units` at 0 and `capacity_units_effective` null, and it may be listed filtered by `unit_id`, `staff_id`, `role_slot`, `status` and `relieved_by_shift_id`, ordered by `shift_start_utc` ascending with `created_at` ascending as the tie-break.

Activation, a `ChargeNurse` act, is where a roster entry becomes real: `SCHEDULED` becomes `ACTIVE` and `capacity_units_effective` is frozen. Three conditions gate it. The window must contain the current instant, failing which the attempt is refused with `ERR_SHIFT_WINDOW_NOT_CURRENT`. The staff member's `credential_expires_at` must be strictly after the current instant and `employment_status` must be `ACTIVE`, failing which the attempt is refused with `ERR_STAFF_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`. A slot of `OR_STANDBY` additionally demands that the member's `or_circulating_qualified` be true and `drill_currency_state` be `CURRENT`, failing which the attempt is refused with `ERR_OR_STANDBY_NOT_QUALIFIED`; satisfying it is the lever that lifts a unit's `cesarean_readiness_state` out of `DEGRADED`. Activation re-derives both unit readiness and unit posture, incrementing the shift's `version` and the unit's `version`.

The frozen capacity figure is computed as follows, at one decimal place with halves rounded away from zero. If the member's `employment_status` is anything other than `ACTIVE`, or `credential_expires_at` is at or before the current instant, or `fetal_monitoring_cert_level` is `EXPIRED`, the result is 0.0 and no further term is evaluated. Otherwise a raw figure is taken as the smaller of two ceilings: the base for the slot, which is 3.0 for `CHARGE`, 4.0 for `BEDSIDE`, 4.0 for `ONE_TO_ONE_FLOAT`, 2.0 for `OR_STANDBY` and 6.0 for `TRIAGE`; and the ceiling implied by certification, which is 4.0 for `ADVANCED`, 3.0 for `BASIC` and 0.0 for `EXPIRED`. From that raw figure an administrative offset of 1.0 is subtracted when the slot is `CHARGE`, and a focus penalty of 1.0 is subtracted when the member currently holds an `ACTIVE` care assignment on this shift covering an encounter whose `acuity_tier` is `TIER_IV`. The final capacity is the larger of 0.0 and the raw figure less those two subtractions, so the value never goes negative. A `BASIC`-certified nurse in the `BEDSIDE` slot therefore carries 3.0 units, and the same nurse in the `CHARGE` slot carries 2.0. That same formula also produces the member's own live `capacity_units`, taking the slot from whichever roster entry of theirs is currently `ACTIVE` and yielding 0.0 when none is; it is recomputed whenever a roster entry is activated, relieved or cancelled, whenever the member's availability or competency is edited, whenever a care assignment of theirs is created or released, whenever a drill is validated or invalidated, and when an encounter is transferred out.

Relief is the handoff mechanism, and it is a `ChargeNurse` act on an `ACTIVE` entry naming `relieved_by_shift_id`. The relieving entry must itself be `ACTIVE`, must belong to the same `unit_id`, must occupy the same `role_slot`, and must belong to a different `staff_id`; any of those failing is refused with `ERR_RELIEVING_SHIFT_INELIGIBLE`. The relieving entry's own member must also still be employable — `employment_status` `ACTIVE` and `credential_expires_at` strictly after the current instant — failing which the handoff is refused with `ERR_STAFF_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`; that test looks only at the incoming member, never at the outgoing one, and it answers after the eligibility refusal and before the capacity refusal below. Every `ACTIVE` care assignment on the outgoing entry is then re-pointed in place — its `status` stays `ACTIVE` while its `shift_assignment_id` and `staff_id` change — and the outgoing entry moves to `RELIEVED` with `relieved_by_shift_id` written. The arithmetic is exact: the outgoing entry is credited precisely each moved row's `acuity_units_consumed` and the relieving entry is debited precisely the same amount, so the sum of active debits on each entry continues to equal its `assigned_acuity_load_units` at every step. If the relieving entry cannot absorb the load within its own `capacity_units_effective`, the relief is refused with `ERR_STAFF_ACUITY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`; encounters that end up short after a permitted handoff fall to `COVERAGE_BREACH`. Relief also recomputes both members' live `capacity_units` — the incoming member's and the outgoing member's, since the handoff can lift a focus penalty off the entry being left behind — as well as unit readiness and unit posture, incrementing both entries' versions, each moved assignment's `version`, each affected encounter's `version` and the unit's `version`.

Cancellation is available only from `SCHEDULED`, again to a `ChargeNurse`, and is refused with `ERR_SHIFT_HAS_ACTIVE_COVERAGE` while any care assignment referencing the entry is still `ACTIVE`. That named guard is evaluated ahead of the source-state check, for the same reason as bed retirement: a care assignment can only be `ACTIVE` on an `ACTIVE` entry, so an entry still carrying live coverage reports `ERR_SHIFT_HAS_ACTIVE_COVERAGE` rather than `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`, and only once its rows have all been released or superseded does the same call fall back to the generic refusal. Cancelling the unit's only `OR_STANDBY` entry re-derives readiness downward. A single roster entry read exposes `capacity_units_effective`, the snapshot frozen at activation, alongside `assigned_acuity_load_units`, the conserved total that section 3.3 defines.

### 3.3. What Coverage Costs: Acuity Pricing, the Parallel Coverage Axis and Unit Posture

Coverage is a junction: a `ChargeNurse` joins one roster entry to one encounter by supplying `encounter_id`, `shift_assignment_id`, a `coverage_mode` of `RATIO_SHARED` or `ONE_TO_ONE`, and `expected_encounter_version`. The named roster entry must be `ACTIVE` and its window must contain the current instant, failing which the request is refused with `ERR_SHIFT_NOT_ACTIVE`. The new row opens in `ACTIVE` with `staff_id` denormalised from the roster entry, and carries `acuity_units_consumed`, `released_at` and `superseded_by_assignment_id` as its remaining state. Rows may be listed filtered by `encounter_id`, `shift_assignment_id`, `staff_id`, `coverage_mode`, `status` and `superseded_by_assignment_id`, ordered by `created_at` ascending — the same order a cascade uses when it has to choose which sibling encounter to breach first.

The price of a row is computed at one decimal place with halves rounded away from zero. A `ONE_TO_ONE` row debits the covering entry's entire `capacity_units_effective`, whatever that value is. A `RATIO_SHARED` row debits the product of two factors: the acuity weight, which is 1.0 for `TIER_I`, 1.5 for `TIER_II`, 2.0 for `TIER_III` and 4.0 for `TIER_IV`; and the phase multiplier, which is 0.5 for `ANTEPARTUM`, 1.0 for `LATENT`, 1.5 for `ACTIVE_FIRST_STAGE`, 2.0 for `SECOND_STAGE`, 1.5 for `THIRD_STAGE_DELIVERED`, 0.5 for `POSTPARTUM_RECOVERY`, 0.0 for `DISCHARGED` and 0.0 for `TRANSFERRED_OUT`. A `TIER_III` patient in `SECOND_STAGE` therefore costs 4.0 units of a shared nurse's capacity. Whenever a tier or a phase changes, every affected row is re-priced in place, and the order of the two ledger movements is fixed: the old value is credited back first and the new value debited second, never the reverse.

What a patient needs is derived, never entered, and it is derived in three steps. The first step establishes `acuity_tier` as the highest tier whose clause fires. `TIER_II` fires when `gestational_age_weeks` is below 37, or `fetus_count` is above 1, or `presentation` is anything other than `VERTEX`, or `prior_cesarean` is true, or `labor_phase` is `ACTIVE_FIRST_STAGE` or `THIRD_STAGE_DELIVERED`. `TIER_III` fires when `labor_phase` is `SECOND_STAGE`, or an activation on the encounter is in `STAGE_1_IN_PROGRESS`, or the latest assessment on the encounter is `CATEGORY_II` with an `escalation_status` of `ESCALATION_DUE`, `TIER_1_NOTIFIED`, `TIER_2_NOTIFIED` or `REASSESSMENT_PENDING`. `TIER_IV` fires when an activation is in `STAGE_2_ESCALATED` or `STAGE_3_MASSIVE_TRANSFUSION`, or `magnesium_infusion_active` is true, or the latest assessment is `CATEGORY_III` with an `escalation_status` other than `RESOLVED`, or a cesarean event on the encounter is in `DECISION_DECLARED`, `TEAM_MOBILIZED` or `INCISION_RECORDED`. When no clause fires the tier is `TIER_I`. The latest assessment throughout means the one with the greatest `assessed_at`, with the later `created_at` winning a tie; activations in `CLOSED` or `RESCINDED` and cesarean events in `CLOSED` or `ABORTED_TO_VAGINAL` are excluded from every clause above.

The second step sets `one_to_one_required` to true when `labor_phase` is `SECOND_STAGE`, or the tier resolved to `TIER_IV`, or `magnesium_infusion_active` is true, and to false otherwise. The third step produces `required_coverage_units`: 0.0 when `labor_phase` is `DISCHARGED` or `TRANSFERRED_OUT`; otherwise 4.0 when one-to-one coverage is required; otherwise the acuity weight multiplied by the phase multiplier from the table above. `assigned_coverage_units` starts at 0 and tracks what has actually been committed.

The coverage axis runs in parallel with the labor phase axis and has its own five states. An encounter starts `UNASSIGNED`. It reaches `RATIO_COVERED` only once `assigned_coverage_units` is at least `required_coverage_units`. An encounter whose `one_to_one_required` is true is never `RATIO_COVERED`: without an `ACTIVE` `ONE_TO_ONE` row it counts as short however many shared units it has accumulated, and an encounter one of whose covering roster entries is carrying more than its `capacity_units_effective` counts as short in the same way. Such an encounter may not even be offered a further shared row: creating a `RATIO_SHARED` care assignment against an encounter whose `one_to_one_required` is true is refused with `ERR_ONE_TO_ONE_COVERAGE_REQUIRED`, decided in the same competency band as the three `ONE_TO_ONE` gates below and therefore after the credential check and before the capacity check. A first row that leaves an `UNASSIGNED` encounter short is not a breach either: the axis stays at `UNASSIGNED` until it is genuinely covered. It reaches `ONE_TO_ONE_COVERED` from `UNASSIGNED`, `RATIO_COVERED` or `COVERAGE_BREACH` when a `ONE_TO_ONE` row is created, and that creation carries three extra gates: the covering entry's `role_slot` must be `BEDSIDE` or `ONE_TO_ONE_FLOAT`, refused otherwise with `ERR_ROLE_SLOT_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; the staff member must hold no other `ACTIVE` care assignment on a different encounter, refused otherwise with `ERR_STAFF_NOT_EXCLUSIVELY_AVAILABLE`; and the member's `fetal_monitoring_cert_level` must be `BASIC` or `ADVANCED`, and must be `ADVANCED` when the encounter's tier is `TIER_IV`, refused otherwise with `ERR_MONITORING_CERT_INSUFFICIENT`. A new `ONE_TO_ONE` row supersedes any prior one, writing `superseded_by_assignment_id` on the predecessor, moving it to `SUPERSEDED` and crediting back exactly its `acuity_units_consumed`; any `ACTIVE` `RATIO_SHARED` row displaced by the handoff moves to `RELEASED` with the same exact credit.

From `RATIO_COVERED` or `ONE_TO_ONE_COVERED` an encounter falls to `COVERAGE_BREACH`, and this edge is driven by the system rather than requested, may fire any number of times, and never produces an error. Remediation runs the other way, from `COVERAGE_BREACH` back to `RATIO_COVERED` or `ONE_TO_ONE_COVERED`. One consequence of that deserves emphasis: a remediating row that leaves the encounter still short of `required_coverage_units` is not a failure. It is created, the response is 201 Created, and the encounter simply remains in `COVERAGE_BREACH` — no error is raised, and bookings bumped by the earlier breach are not automatically restored. From any of `UNASSIGNED`, `RATIO_COVERED`, `ONE_TO_ONE_COVERED` and `COVERAGE_BREACH`, the axis ends at `RELEASED`.

Releasing a row is a `ChargeNurse` act available only from `ACTIVE`: a row already `RELEASED` or `SUPERSEDED` is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`, and no other guard applies, so a row may be released even from a terminal encounter. On a permitted release `status` becomes `RELEASED`, `released_at` becomes the current instant, and the covering entry is credited back exactly `acuity_units_consumed`. While any other `ACTIVE` row still covers the encounter the coverage axis is merely re-derived from the arithmetic, and may well stay where it was. Releasing the last `ACTIVE` row drives the encounter to `RELEASED` when its `labor_phase` is `POSTPARTUM_RECOVERY`, `DISCHARGED` or `TRANSFERRED_OUT`, and to `COVERAGE_BREACH` in any earlier phase — again recorded rather than refused. A release may also lift a `TIER_IV` focus penalty from the staff member's `capacity_units`.

Two conservation guarantees hold at all times, and both are exact after rounding to one decimal place. For every roster entry in `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE`, `assigned_acuity_load_units` equals the sum of `acuity_units_consumed` over that entry's `ACTIVE` rows. For every encounter, `assigned_coverage_units` equals the sum of `acuity_units_consumed` over its own `ACTIVE` rows. Creating a row and then releasing it, performing a one-to-one handoff, relieving a shift and rescinding a bundle each leave both sums, and the unit's `coverage_deficit_units`, identical to their values before the sequence began.

Capacity is the last thing checked when a row is created or a shift relieved: the covering entry's `assigned_acuity_load_units` plus the new debit must be no greater than its `capacity_units_effective`, with the boundary allowed, and an excess is refused with `ERR_STAFF_ACUITY_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. The projection anticipates the credits the write itself would perform: when a `ONE_TO_ONE` row is being created, the rows it is about to displace on that very same roster entry are credited back before the new debit is compared against the ceiling, so a nurse already holding this encounter's shared row can take it one-to-one even where the two debits together would overflow the ceiling. Displaced rows resting on some other entry earn no such allowance, because they were never charged to this one.

The unit's posture is derived from those figures on every mutation that can move them. First, `coverage_deficit_units` is recomputed as the sum, over every encounter in the unit whose `labor_phase` is one of the six non-terminal phases, of the larger of 0.0 and `required_coverage_units` less `assigned_coverage_units`, rounded to one decimal place with halves away from zero. Second, the number of beds of type `LDR` whose `status` is `AVAILABLE` is counted. Then the first matching branch of four decides `staffing_posture`, and no later branch is consulted. `DIVERT` when the deficit is 8.0 or greater, or no `LDR` bed is available, or any activation in the unit is in `STAGE_3_MASSIVE_TRANSFUSION`. `INDUCTION_FROZEN` when the deficit is 4.0 or greater, or `escalation_breach_count` is 2 or greater, or `bundle_breach_count` is 3 or greater, or `cesarean_readiness_state` is `NOT_READY`, or the deficit is greater than 0.0 while some activation is in `STAGE_2_ESCALATED`. `RATIO_STRAINED` when the deficit is greater than 0.0, or any encounter is in `COVERAGE_BREACH`. `OPEN` otherwise. The thresholds are exact: a deficit of 4.0 freezes inductions and a deficit of 3.9 does not; a deficit of 0.1 strains the unit and a deficit of exactly 0.0 does not.

After the posture is settled, and only then, `turnover_buffer_minutes` is set to 45 when the posture is `OPEN` and 75 otherwise. The new buffer is never fed back into the posture calculation within the same request, so a request cannot chase its own tail. A unit's coverage report exposes this whole derivation, and it recomputes both the deficit and the branch live from the unit's current rows rather than echoing the stored `staffing_posture`, so a unit whose rows have moved since its last re-derivation — a freshly created unit that has no `LDR` bed yet, for one — reports `DIVERT` in this read while its own record still says `OPEN`. It carries `unit_id`, the rolled-up `coverage_deficit_units`, `staffing_posture`, `posture_reason`, `escalation_breach_count`, `bundle_breach_count` and an `encounters` array of one row per non-terminal encounter, each row carrying `encounter_id`, `patient_record_code`, `labor_phase`, `staffing_state`, `acuity_tier`, `one_to_one_required`, `required_coverage_units`, `assigned_coverage_units` and that encounter's own `deficit_units` contribution, the array ordered by `created_at` ascending and paginated by `limit` and `offset`. `posture_reason` is the reason code for the branch that fired — `DIVERT_DEFICIT`, `NO_AVAILABLE_LDR_BED`, `STAGE_3_BUNDLE`, `FREEZE_DEFICIT`, `ESCALATION_BREACH`, `BUNDLE_BREACH`, `READINESS_NOT_READY`, `STAGE_2_WITH_DEFICIT`, `DEFICIT_POSITIVE`, `BREACHED_ENCOUNTER` or `NONE`. A per-encounter coverage summary complements it, naming which clause set the `acuity_tier` — one of `GESTATION_OR_PRESENTATION`, `PHASE_FLOOR`, `STAGE_1_BUNDLE`, `CATEGORY_II_UNRESOLVED`, `STAGE_2_OR_3_BUNDLE`, `MAGNESIUM`, `CATEGORY_III_UNRESOLVED`, `CESAREAN_IN_PROGRESS`, or `BASELINE` when no clause fired at all, with the first clause of the highest firing tier naming the reason where several fire — reporting that clause as `acuity_tier_reason` beside `encounter_id`, `labor_phase`, `staffing_state`, a freshly derived `acuity_tier`, `one_to_one_required` and `required_coverage_units`, the stored `assigned_coverage_units`, the `deficit_units` between those last two, `magnesium_infusion_active` and `review_required`, then an `active_assignments` array holding the individual debits behind `assigned_coverage_units` — one entry per `ACTIVE` row, ordered by `created_at` ascending, each carrying `care_assignment_id`, `shift_assignment_id`, `staff_id`, `coverage_mode` and `acuity_units_consumed` — then `latest_assessment_id`, `latest_assessment_category` and `latest_assessment_escalation_status`, all three null when the encounter has no assessment at all, and finally the current `support_person_allowance` beside `support_persons_checked_in`. Unlike the coverage report this summary is an unpaginated object.

### 3.4. The Labor Phase Ladder

A walk-in admission is created by a `ChargeNurse` or a `PatientAccessCoordinator` naming `unit_id`, `patient_record_code` — a string of 1 to 60 characters that must be unique within the unit among encounters in non-terminal phases, refused otherwise with `ERR_DUPLICATE_PATIENT_RECORD_CODE` — `parity` as an integer no smaller than 0, `gestational_age_weeks` as an integer from 20 to 44, and optionally `prior_cesarean` defaulting to false, `fetus_count` as an integer no smaller than 1 defaulting to 1, and `presentation` of `VERTEX`, `BREECH` or `OTHER` defaulting to `VERTEX`. The encounter opens with `labor_phase` at `ANTEPARTUM`, `staffing_state` at `UNASSIGNED`, `admitted_at` at the current instant, `bed_id` null, `source_booking_id` null, `magnesium_infusion_active` false, `episiotomy_performed` false, `review_required` false, and `delivery_mode` and `delivery_recorded_at` null. Creation is blocked by posture: a unit in `DIVERT` refuses new walk-ins with `ERR_UNIT_POSTURE_BLOCKS_OPERATION`, and that posture refusal is decided before the uniqueness refusal, so an admission that both repeats a live record code and arrives at a diverting unit reports the posture. A permitted creation immediately derives the new encounter's own requirement — an `ANTEPARTUM` `TIER_I` patient already requires 0.5 units — and then re-derives the unit, so the unit's `coverage_deficit_units`, posture, readiness figures and turnover buffer are all recomputed and the unit's `version` advances by 1, while the encounter's own `version` stays at 1 because a derivation that runs as part of a creation is not a mutation of its own subject.

One derived flag is decided at creation and matters enormously three months later. `is_ntsv` is true when all five of the following hold: `parity` is 0, `prior_cesarean` is false, `gestational_age_weeks` is 37 or greater, `fetus_count` is exactly 1, and `presentation` is `VERTEX`. It is false when any one of them fails. This flag is the denominator of the primary-cesarean quality measure in section 3.9.

Attaching a bed is a separate `ChargeNurse` act naming `bed_id`; the bed must be of type `LDR` and `AVAILABLE`, and a bed failing either half of that test — the wrong type, or the right type in any of `HELD`, `OCCUPIED`, `TURNOVER`, `BLOCKED` or `CLOSED` — is refused with the one identifier `ERR_BED_TYPE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. A permitted attachment moves the bed to `OCCUPIED` with `current_encounter_id` pointing at the encounter, and advances the bed's, the encounter's and the unit's `version` by 1 each. An encounter that already occupies a bed is refused with `ERR_ENCOUNTER_ALREADY_IN_BED`, which answers ahead of the eligibility refusal. Attachment carries no phase guard of its own: an encounter already `DISCHARGED` or `TRANSFERRED_OUT` has had its `bed_id` cleared, and so can be given a bed again. Consuming the last available `LDR` bed raises the unit into `DIVERT`. Encounters may be listed filtered by `unit_id`, `bed_id`, `source_booking_id`, `patient_record_code`, `labor_phase`, `staffing_state`, `acuity_tier` and `is_ntsv`, ordered by `admitted_at` ascending with `created_at` ascending as the tie-break; that flat list, or the booking itself, is the only way to discover an encounter minted by an induction admission.

A single operation, available to a `ChargeNurse` or an `ObstetricianAttending`, drives every forward step of the phase ladder, taking `target_phase` and, on the delivery step only, `delivery_mode` and `episiotomy_performed`. `target_phase` admits only the five forward targets `LATENT`, `ACTIVE_FIRST_STAGE`, `SECOND_STAGE`, `THIRD_STAGE_DELIVERED` and `POSTPARTUM_RECOVERY`; naming `ANTEPARTUM`, `DISCHARGED` or `TRANSFERRED_OUT` is not a rejected transition but a rejected value, failing at priority 5 with `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` and HTTP 400, since discharge and transfer are their own operations. A target drawn from that set which is nonetheless not the single successor of the encounter's present phase is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`, decided before every clinical gate below: the ladder permits no skipping, no repetition and no return. The other two fields are declared on every call — `delivery_mode` optional, `episiotomy_performed` defaulting to false — and are simply ignored on the four steps that are not the delivery step, where neither is validated against the target and neither is written to the encounter. Each target has its own preconditions.

Moving `ANTEPARTUM` to `LATENT` requires the coverage axis to be `RATIO_COVERED` or `ONE_TO_ONE_COVERED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_ENCOUNTER_COVERAGE_INSUFFICIENT`; requires `bed_id` to be non-null with that bed `OCCUPIED` and of type `LDR`, refused otherwise with `ERR_ENCOUNTER_BED_NOT_ASSIGNED`; and requires the unit's posture to be anything other than `DIVERT`, refused otherwise with `ERR_UNIT_POSTURE_BLOCKS_OPERATION`.

Moving `LATENT` to `ACTIVE_FIRST_STAGE` requires the same coverage floor, and adds two fetal gates. The latest assessment on the encounter must have been taken within 30 minutes of the current instant, with exactly 30 minutes still acceptable, refused otherwise with `ERR_FETAL_ASSESSMENT_STALE`. An encounter carrying no assessment at all fails that very test with that very identifier — the absence of a reading is treated as an unusably old one, not waved through for want of anything to measure — and the elapsed interval is counted in whole minutes, so a residual seconds fraction never carries a reading over the edge. That assessment's `escalation_status` must not be `ESCALATION_DUE`, `TIER_1_NOTIFIED`, `TIER_2_NOTIFIED`, `TIER_3_ACTIVATED` or `TIMED_OUT_BREACH`, refused otherwise with `ERR_FETAL_ESCALATION_UNRESOLVED`.

Moving `ACTIVE_FIRST_STAGE` to `SECOND_STAGE` is the most heavily gated step in the system. The coverage axis must be exactly `ONE_TO_ONE_COVERED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_ONE_TO_ONE_COVERAGE_REQUIRED`. The latest assessment must have been taken within 15 minutes of the current instant, with exactly 15 minutes still acceptable, refused otherwise with `ERR_FETAL_ASSESSMENT_STALE` — measured exactly as the 30-minute test is measured, and failing in the same way when the encounter has no assessment at all. That assessment must be `CATEGORY_I` or `CATEGORY_II`, or else `CATEGORY_III` accompanied by a cesarean event on the encounter in `DECISION_DECLARED` or `TEAM_MOBILIZED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_FETAL_CATEGORY_BLOCKS_PHASE`. And the unit's `cesarean_readiness_state` must be anything other than `NOT_READY`, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_READINESS_NOT_MET`.

That same step then fires five consequences in a fixed order. The encounter's `acuity_tier` is floored at `TIER_III` and `one_to_one_required` becomes true. The covering care assignment is re-priced, crediting the old debit and taking the new one. If the covering roster entry is now carrying more than its `capacity_units_effective`, the sibling encounters covered by that same entry and sitting in `RATIO_COVERED` or `ONE_TO_ONE_COVERED` are moved to `COVERAGE_BREACH`, taken in order of their covering assignment's `created_at` ascending — oldest first. Marking an encounter breached returns no capacity to the entry, so the over-capacity condition still holds at every step and every such sibling is breached; only a sibling still `UNASSIGNED` or already `RELEASED` is left where it is. The unit's posture is then re-derived and may reach `INDUCTION_FROZEN`. Finally, if the posture has reached `INDUCTION_FROZEN` or `DIVERT`, every booking in `CAPACITY_HELD` is moved to `BUMPED` — every one of them, whatever its priority class, so that this cascade alone reaches `MEDICALLY_INDICATED` holds as well and is the one exception to the rule stated in section 3.5 that an automatic cascade touches only elective and patient-request work; the bumping order is the same order section 3.5 fixes, and `CONFIRMED` bookings are still left alone. For each booking so bumped, its held bed returns from `HELD` to `AVAILABLE`, and the day's held bed-minutes are decremented by exactly that booking's frozen `projected_occupancy_minutes`.

Moving `SECOND_STAGE` to `THIRD_STAGE_DELIVERED` requires `delivery_mode` to be supplied, refused otherwise with `ERR_DELIVERY_MODE_REQUIRED`, and accepts `episiotomy_performed` only on this step. A `delivery_mode` of `CESAREAN_PRIMARY` or `CESAREAN_REPEAT` requires a cesarean event on the encounter in `DELIVERED`, `REVIEW_PENDING` or `CLOSED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_NOT_DELIVERED`. An encounter whose `prior_cesarean` is true may not be recorded as `CESAREAN_PRIMARY`, refused with `ERR_DELIVERY_MODE_CONFLICTS_HISTORY`. An `episiotomy_performed` of true is coherent only with `SPONTANEOUS_VAGINAL` or `OPERATIVE_VAGINAL`, refused otherwise with `ERR_EPISIOTOMY_MODE_CONFLICT`. The step writes `delivery_recorded_at` as the current instant and recomputes every snapshot in `DRAFT` whose reporting window contains that instant, leaving snapshots in `CERTIFIED` or `SUPERSEDED` untouched.

Moving `THIRD_STAGE_DELIVERED` to `POSTPARTUM_RECOVERY` requires every assessment on the encounter to sit in `NOT_REQUIRED`, `RESOLVED` or `TIMED_OUT_BREACH`, refused otherwise with `ERR_FETAL_ESCALATION_UNRESOLVED`, and requires no cesarean event on the encounter to be in `DECISION_DECLARED`, `TEAM_MOBILIZED` or `INCISION_RECORDED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_IN_PROGRESS`. The step sends the occupied bed to `TURNOVER` with `turnover_clear_at` set to the current instant plus the unit's `turnover_buffer_minutes`, and clears the encounter's `bed_id`.

Discharge is a `ChargeNurse` act from `POSTPARTUM_RECOVERY` to `DISCHARGED`, and its four blockers are checked in exactly this order: any newborn complication review on the encounter not yet `CLOSED` yields `ERR_NEWBORN_REVIEW_OPEN`; any activation not yet `CLOSED` or `RESCINDED` yields `ERR_BUNDLE_NOT_CLOSED`; any support pass still `ISSUED`, `CHECKED_IN` or `CHECKED_OUT` yields `ERR_SUPPORT_PASS_STILL_PRESENT`; and a `staffing_state` that is not yet `RELEASED` yields `ERR_CARE_ASSIGNMENT_STILL_ACTIVE`. Discharge sets `required_coverage_units` to 0.0, releases a still-attached bed to `TURNOVER` and re-derives posture. It is worth being explicit about what discharge does not do: even when it improves the posture, it never promotes a waiting booking. Promotion happens inside the capacity sweep and nowhere else.

Transfer-out is an `ObstetricianAttending` act from `ANTEPARTUM`, `LATENT` or `ACTIVE_FIRST_STAGE` to `TRANSFERRED_OUT`, and it is a compensating exit rather than a completion. It is blocked while a cesarean event on the encounter is in `TEAM_MOBILIZED` or `INCISION_RECORDED`, with `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_IN_PROGRESS`, and then blocked while an activation is in `STAGE_2_ESCALATED` or `STAGE_3_MASSIVE_TRANSFUSION`, with `ERR_BUNDLE_STAGE_BLOCKS_TRANSFER`, in that order. Once permitted it unwinds the case: every `ACTIVE` care assignment moves to `RELEASED` with `released_at` set and exactly its `acuity_units_consumed` credited back; the coverage axis moves to `RELEASED` and both `required_coverage_units` and `assigned_coverage_units` become 0.0; activations in `ACTIVATED` or `STAGE_1_IN_PROGRESS` move to `RESCINDED` with `rescind_reason` set to `TRANSFER_OUT`; their checklist items still `PENDING` become `NOT_APPLICABLE`; every non-terminal escalation chain becomes `RESOLVED`, keeping whatever `escalation_due_at`, `next_tier_due_at`, `reassessment_due_at` and `escalation_tier_high_water` it had recorded, since resolution here closes the chain without rewriting its history; every support pass in `ISSUED`, `CHECKED_IN` or `CHECKED_OUT` becomes `REVOKED`; and the bed goes to `TURNOVER`. One movement runs against intuition and is deliberate: for each assessment on the encounter sitting in `TIMED_OUT_BREACH`, the unit's `escalation_breach_count` is decremented by 1, though never below 0: the count is a running tally, not a signed one, so a transfer that would take it past zero simply leaves it at zero. The breach follows the patient out of the unit rather than staying on the unit's record.

### 3.5. Booking Inductions Against a Finite Bed-Minute Budget

An induction request is created by a `PatientAccessCoordinator` or an `ObstetricianAttending`, naming `unit_id`, `patient_record_code` as a string of 1 to 60 characters, `gestational_age_weeks` as an integer from 20 to 44, an `indication_code` of `PREECLAMPSIA`, `POST_DATES_41W`, `FGR`, `PROM`, `GDM_CONTROLLED` or `ELECTIVE_NO_INDICATION`, a `requested_start_utc` strictly after the current instant — refused otherwise with `ERR_BOOKING_START_IN_PAST` — and optionally `expected_labor_minutes` as an integer from 60 to 2160, defaulting to 720. The booking opens in `REQUESTED` with `bump_count` at 0, `bed_id`, `encounter_id`, `hold_expires_at` and `projected_occupancy_minutes` all null, and consumes no budget at all.

`priority_class` is derived immediately and re-derived on every requeue. It is `MEDICALLY_INDICATED` when `indication_code` is `PREECLAMPSIA`, `POST_DATES_41W`, `FGR` or `PROM`. It is `ELECTIVE_39_WEEK` when `indication_code` is `GDM_CONTROLLED` or `ELECTIVE_NO_INDICATION` and `gestational_age_weeks` is 39 or greater. It is `PATIENT_REQUEST` otherwise. After that assignment, a `bump_count` of 2 or greater overrides the result to `MEDICALLY_INDICATED`, which as a side effect also makes the booking unbumpable — a booking that has been pushed aside twice can no longer be pushed aside. For ordering purposes `MEDICALLY_INDICATED` ranks 0, `ELECTIVE_39_WEEK` ranks 1 and `PATIENT_REQUEST` ranks 2. An elective request at 39 weeks is `ELECTIVE_39_WEEK`; the same request at 38 weeks is `PATIENT_REQUEST`, and a hold or confirmation attempted on an `ELECTIVE_39_WEEK` booking whose `gestational_age_weeks` is below 39 is refused with `ERR_ELECTIVE_INDUCTION_GESTATIONAL_AGE`.

Placing a hold is a `PatientAccessCoordinator` act supplying `bed_id` and `expected_unit_version`. The booking moves `REQUESTED` to `CAPACITY_HELD`, the named `LDR` bed moves `AVAILABLE` to `HELD` with `held_booking_id` written, and `hold_expires_at` is set to the current instant plus 120 minutes. This is also the moment `projected_occupancy_minutes` is frozen, as `expected_labor_minutes` plus the unit's current `turnover_buffer_minutes`, and that frozen integer is the exact quantum every later credit and debit moves. The day's `held_bed_minutes_by_day` entry for the UTC date of `requested_start_utc` is increased by it. Two tests must pass first: the day's existing load, meaning that date's committed minutes plus its held minutes, plus the new projection, must be no greater than `max_daily_induction_bed_minutes`, with the boundary allowed and an excess refused as `ERR_INDUCTION_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`; and the bed's closed reservation window must not collide with an existing reservation on the same bed, refused otherwise with `ERR_BED_TURNOVER_CONFLICT`, which is also the outcome when the named bed is simply not `AVAILABLE`. A hold is additionally blocked when the unit's posture is `INDUCTION_FROZEN` or `DIVERT`, and a booking whose `priority_class` is `PATIENT_REQUEST` demands the stricter posture of `OPEN` outright, so a merely `RATIO_STRAINED` unit refuses it with the same `ERR_UNIT_POSTURE_BLOCKS_OPERATION`. The named bed must also be of `bed_type` `LDR`, refused otherwise with `ERR_BED_TYPE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. Where more than one of these could apply the ladder of section 2.6 settles the outcome: the posture test, then the elective gestational-age test, then bed eligibility, then the reservation collision, and the day's budget last of all.

Confirmation is an `ObstetricianAttending` act moving `CAPACITY_HELD` to `CONFIRMED`. It requires a hold that has not lapsed, refused otherwise with `ERR_INDUCTION_HOLD_EXPIRED` and with the boundary instant itself counting as lapsed, since the hold survives only while the current instant is strictly before `hold_expires_at`; a `cesarean_readiness_state` of `READY` or `DEGRADED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_READINESS_NOT_MET`; a posture of `OPEN` or `RATIO_STRAINED`, with no extra stricture for a `PATIENT_REQUEST` booking on this path; and, for an `ELECTIVE_39_WEEK` booking, a gestational age of at least 39 weeks. One further test closes the ladder, at the capacity band: that date's committed minutes alone, plus the frozen projection, must be no greater than `max_daily_induction_bed_minutes`, refused otherwise with `ERR_INDUCTION_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`. The held minutes this very booking is about to vacate are deliberately left out of that sum, so a confirmation of an already-held booking cannot be refused by its own reservation. The frozen projection moves from `held_bed_minutes_by_day` to `committed_bed_minutes_by_day` for the same date, a net-zero movement, `hold_expires_at` is cleared, and the bed stays `HELD`.

Admission is a `ChargeNurse` act moving `CONFIRMED` to `ADMITTED`, permitted from 30 minutes before `requested_start_utc` onwards, with that boundary instant itself allowed and anything earlier refused as `ERR_INDUCTION_ADMIT_TOO_EARLY`. Two further preconditions sit ahead of that window test. The unit's posture blocks an admission only when it is `DIVERT`, refused there with `ERR_UNIT_POSTURE_BLOCKS_OPERATION`; a unit in `INDUCTION_FROZEN` still admits a booking it has already confirmed, which is the one induction path a freeze does not stop. And the bed the booking points at must still be `HELD` with `held_booking_id` naming this very booking, refused otherwise with `ERR_BED_HOLD_MISMATCH`. The whole order is the declared transition, then the posture, then the bed hold, then the duplicate record code, and the admission window last of all. It mints the `LaborEncounter`: `labor_phase` `ANTEPARTUM`, `staffing_state` `UNASSIGNED`, `source_booking_id` pointing back at the booking, `admitted_at` the current instant, and `patient_record_code` and `gestational_age_weeks` copied across — a copy that can collide with an existing code and be refused as `ERR_DUPLICATE_PATIENT_RECORD_CODE`, the collision being sought only among encounters on this same unit whose `labor_phase` is neither `DISCHARGED` nor `TRANSFERRED_OUT`, so a code released by a discharge may be admitted again. The bed moves `HELD` to `OCCUPIED`, clearing `held_booking_id` and writing `current_encounter_id`, and the booking's own `encounter_id` is written back to point at the new row. The booking carries across no clinical detail beyond the gestational age, so the rest of the chart opens at the defaults of section 3.4: `parity` 0, `prior_cesarean` false, `fetus_count` 1, `presentation` `VERTEX`, `acuity_tier` `TIER_I`, `one_to_one_required` false, `magnesium_infusion_active` false, `episiotomy_performed` false, `review_required` false, and `delivery_mode` and `delivery_recorded_at` both null. Because four of the five NTSV conditions are satisfied by construction, `is_ntsv` on this path reduces to whether the copied `gestational_age_weeks` is 37 or greater. The committed minutes stay committed, since `CONFIRMED` and `ADMITTED` both count against the day. One detail separates this mint from the walk-in creation of section 3.4: there the creation-time coverage derivation is part of the creation and leaves the new encounter at `version` 1, whereas here it counts as a mutation of the freshly minted row, so an admission-minted encounter is readable at `version` 2.

Three exits release capacity. A bump, available to a `ChargeNurse` or `PatientAccessCoordinator` from `CAPACITY_HELD` or `CONFIRMED`, moves the booking to `BUMPED`, increments `bump_count`, clears `bed_id` and `hold_expires_at`, returns the bed from `HELD` to `AVAILABLE`, and decrements exactly `projected_occupancy_minutes` from the held map when the booking was held or from the committed map when it was confirmed. Ahead of either of the two counting tests, the bed the booking points at must still record that booking in `held_booking_id`, refused otherwise with `ERR_BED_HOLD_MISMATCH`; a `CONFIRMED` booking still satisfies this, because confirmation leaves its bed `HELD`. It is then refused with `ERR_INDUCTION_BUMP_LIMIT_REACHED` once `bump_count` has reached 2, and only after that refused with `ERR_INDUCTION_NOT_BUMPABLE` for a `MEDICALLY_INDICATED` booking, so a twice-bumped booking reports the limit rather than the class it was promoted into. A requeue, a `PatientAccessCoordinator` act, returns a `BUMPED` booking to `REQUESTED` and re-derives `priority_class`; it is refused with `ERR_INDUCTION_WINDOW_PASSED` once `requested_start_utc` is at or before the current instant, and moves no bed-minutes, because bookings in `REQUESTED` and `BUMPED` both contribute zero to both maps. A cancellation, also a `PatientAccessCoordinator` act, is available from `REQUESTED`, `CAPACITY_HELD` or `CONFIRMED`, releases any held bed and credits back exactly `projected_occupancy_minutes`; an `ADMITTED` booking can never be cancelled, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_INDUCTION_ALREADY_ADMITTED`. A hold that simply runs out of time moves to `EXPIRED` during a sweep once the current instant reaches `hold_expires_at`, the boundary instant itself expiring the hold. Bookings in `REQUESTED`, `BUMPED`, `CANCELLED` and `EXPIRED` all contribute zero minutes to both ledgers, while `CAPACITY_HELD` bookings are summed into the held map and `CONFIRMED` and `ADMITTED` bookings into the committed map, each keyed by the UTC date of `requested_start_utc`. A date whose running total in either map falls back to 0 is dropped from that map rather than kept as a zero entry, so both ledgers only ever list dates that are actually carrying minutes.

When an automatic cascade elsewhere in the system needs to bump bookings rather than being asked to bump one, it selects only bookings whose `priority_class` is `ELECTIVE_39_WEEK` or `PATIENT_REQUEST` — with the single exception noted in section 3.4, where the freeze that follows a second-stage advance sweeps up held bookings of every class — and it takes them in order of `priority_class` descending, so `PATIENT_REQUEST` goes first, then `requested_start_utc` descending, then `created_at` descending. Promotion inside the sweep runs the other way: candidates are ordered by priority rank ascending, then `requested_start_utc` ascending, then `created_at` ascending, and the record's `id` is never consulted. A booking is a promotion candidate only when its `status` is `REQUESTED`, its `requested_start_utc` is strictly after the current instant, the frozen sweep posture is `OPEN` or `RATIO_STRAINED`, its `priority_class` is not `PATIENT_REQUEST` unless that frozen posture is `OPEN`, and at least one `LDR` bed is `AVAILABLE` with no closed-interval collision; among eligible beds the one with the lowest `bed_label` in lexicographic order is chosen. Each promotion is tested on its own, so a budget that fits one booking but not two promotes exactly one. An empty candidate set promotes nothing, reports a promoted count of 0 and raises no error.

A per-day capacity summary read exposes the whole picture for one UTC date, taking `day` as a bare `YYYY-MM-DD` calendar date — anything else, a full timestamp included, is an `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` field-value failure — and defaulting to the date of the current instant. It reports that single date rather than either ledger in full: `unit_id`, the `day` actually applied, `committed_bed_minutes` and `held_bed_minutes` as that date's two frozen entries with 0 standing in for a date neither map carries, their sum as `day_load_minutes`, `max_daily_induction_bed_minutes`, `remaining_bed_minutes` as the budget less that load — a figure that is allowed to go negative once the budget has been lowered under an existing load — the unit's stored `turnover_buffer_minutes` and its stored `staffing_posture`, which this read echoes rather than recomputing, then the `LDR` bed census as `available_ldr_beds`, `held_ldr_beds`, `occupied_ldr_beds` and `turnover_ldr_beds`, each counting only beds of `bed_type` `LDR` in that one status so that `BLOCKED` and `CLOSED` beds enter no count, and finally `last_capacity_sweep_at`. Bookings may also be listed filtered by `unit_id`, `bed_id`, `encounter_id`, `status`, `priority_class` and `requested_day`, returned in the sweep's own promotion order so that a caller can read the queue before running it.

### 3.6. Hemorrhage and Hypertension Bundles: Timed Steps Measured Two Ways

An `ObstetricianAttending` or a `BedsideNurse` activates a bundle on an encounter by naming `encounter_id`, a `bundle_type` of `OBSTETRIC_HEMORRHAGE` or `SEVERE_HYPERTENSION`, and an `activation_trigger` of `QBL_THRESHOLD`, `BP_THRESHOLD` or `CLINICIAN_JUDGMENT`. The trigger decides which clinical figures must accompany it. `QBL_THRESHOLD` requires `quantitative_blood_loss_ml`, an integer no smaller than 0, to be 1000 or greater. `BP_THRESHOLD` requires `systolic_mmhg`, an integer from 60 to 300, to be 160 or greater, or `diastolic_mmhg`, an integer from 30 to 200, to be 110 or greater. `CLINICIAN_JUDGMENT` requires neither figure. A trigger whose threshold is not met is refused with `ERR_ACTIVATION_CRITERIA_NOT_MET`. The blood-loss figure must be null for a `SEVERE_HYPERTENSION` bundle and the pressure figures must be null for an `OBSTETRIC_HEMORRHAGE` bundle. All four of these coherence rules — a figure supplied where the bundle type forbids it, and a figure omitted where the trigger demands it — are field-value failures decided at priority 5, `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` at HTTP 400, and so are reported ahead of the terminal-phase, already-active and threshold tests; only a figure that is present, permitted and short of its threshold reaches `ERR_ACTIVATION_CRITERIA_NOT_MET`. At most one activation per encounter and `bundle_type` may sit in the six non-terminal statuses at a time; a second is refused with `ERR_BUNDLE_ALREADY_ACTIVE`. The activation opens in `ACTIVATED` with `activated_at` at the current instant, `unit_id` denormalised from the encounter, `highest_stage_reached` at 0, `on_time_step_count`, `late_step_count` and `missed_step_count` at 0, both scores null and `rescind_reason` null. Activation re-derives the encounter, and therefore the unit, before it answers; if the unit then stands in `INDUCTION_FROZEN` or `DIVERT` — whether this activation drove it there or it was already there — the unit's `CAPACITY_HELD` bookings of the elective and patient-request classes are bumped and their beds returned from `HELD` to `AVAILABLE`, and the unit is re-derived once more. `CONFIRMED` bookings are left alone on this path; only the stage escalations below reach them.

Activation also mints the checklist; items are never created by a client. An `OBSTETRIC_HEMORRHAGE` bundle receives four items: `step_sequence` 1, `step_code` `QBL_MEASURE`, `stage_gate` 1, `required_within_minutes` 10; sequence 2, `UTEROTONIC_GIVEN`, gate 1, 15 minutes; sequence 3, `TYPE_AND_SCREEN`, gate 2, 20 minutes; sequence 4, `MTP_ACTIVATED`, gate 3, 10 minutes. A `SEVERE_HYPERTENSION` bundle receives: sequence 1, `ANTIHYPERTENSIVE_GIVEN`, gate 1, 30 minutes; sequence 2, `MAGNESIUM_LOADED`, gate 1, 60 minutes; sequence 3, `REPEAT_BP`, gate 2, 15 minutes; sequence 4, `OB_AT_BEDSIDE`, gate 2, 20 minutes. Every item opens `PENDING` with `completed_at`, `performed_by_staff_id` and `minutes_from_activation` null.

Each item carries two due dates, and only one of them governs. `due_at_activation_anchored`, the authoritative date, is `activated_at` plus the sum of `required_within_minutes` across every item whose `step_sequence` is less than or equal to this item's. For a hemorrhage bundle that yields offsets of 10, 25, 45 and 55 minutes from activation. `due_at_sequence_anchored`, the advisory date, is instead the predecessor item's `completed_at` plus this item's own `required_within_minutes`; for the first item it is `activated_at` plus that item's own allowance, and for any item whose predecessor has not been completed it is null. At activation only the first item's sequence-anchored date can be computed. Both dates are expressed at whole-minute precision in UTC.

Completing an item is available to a `BedsideNurse`, `ChargeNurse` or `ObstetricianAttending`, supplying `completed_at` and `performed_by_staff_id`. The activation's `highest_stage_reached` must be at least the item's `stage_gate`, refused otherwise with `ERR_CHECKLIST_STAGE_NOT_REACHED` — with the deliberate exception that a gate-1 item may be completed while the activation is still `ACTIVATED`, since that first completion is what moves the activation forward. `completed_at` must be at or after `activated_at` and at or before the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_CHECKLIST_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`. The named performer must hold an `ACTIVE` care assignment on the encounter, or an `ACTIVE` roster entry on the unit in the `CHARGE` or `OR_STANDBY` slot, refused otherwise with `ERR_PERFORMER_NOT_ON_CASE`. The landing state is decided by the authoritative date alone: `completed_at` at or before `due_at_activation_anchored` yields `COMPLETED` and increments `on_time_step_count`, and the boundary instant counts as on time; `completed_at` strictly after it yields `LATE_COMPLETED` and increments `late_step_count`. Either way `minutes_from_activation` is recorded as the whole minutes elapsed from `activated_at`, and the successor item's `due_at_sequence_anchored` is recomputed from this `completed_at` — an advisory recomputation that never rewrites any authoritative date. The first completion of a gate-1 item also moves the activation from `ACTIVATED` to `STAGE_1_IN_PROGRESS` and raises `highest_stage_reached` from 0 to 1 — so an activation sitting in stage 1 reports 1, which is what lets its remaining gate-1 items pass the gate check on their own rather than through the exception above, and which still leaves it inside the "1 or less" window the rescission path requires — and that same edge floors the encounter at `TIER_III` and re-prices coverage; completing `MAGNESIUM_LOADED` on a hypertension bundle additionally sets `magnesium_infusion_active` to true, which forces one-to-one coverage and the whole cascade that follows from it. An item may also become `MISSED`, which happens only in a sweep, once the current instant is strictly past `due_at_activation_anchored` plus a 5-minute grace, provided the item's gate has been reached and the activation is still in a live stage — and the gate is read with exactly the same deliberate exception as completion, so a gate-1 item can be missed while its activation is still sitting in `ACTIVATED`, and a missed item raises `missed_step_count` by 1 as it lands; and it may become `NOT_APPLICABLE`, which happens only through the stage and compensation paths below. Items may be read through the activation or through a flat collection, filtered by `activation_id`, `performed_by_staff_id`, `status`, `stage_gate` and `step_code`, ordered by `step_sequence` ascending within an activation. A `stage_gate` filter outside the range 1 to 3 is an `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` field-value failure rather than a page with nothing on it. The flat collection, which can mix items minted by different activations, breaks ties on `created_at` ascending so that items sharing a `step_sequence` fall in mint order; the per-activation collection needs no tie-break, because `step_sequence` is unique inside one activation.

Only an `ObstetricianAttending` escalates a bundle, one stage at a time, naming `target_stage` as 2 or 3. `target_stage` 2 moves the activation from `STAGE_1_IN_PROGRESS` to `STAGE_2_ESCALATED`; `target_stage` 3 moves it from `STAGE_2_ESCALATED` to `STAGE_3_MASSIVE_TRANSFUSION`. Every checklist item of a lower gate must first have stopped moving — status `COMPLETED`, `LATE_COMPLETED`, `MISSED` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`, an item voided by an earlier compensation counting as settled exactly as a completed one does, so only a `PENDING` item blocks — meaning stage 2 requires every gate-1 item and stage 3 requires every item of gate 1 and gate 2; otherwise the escalation is refused with `ERR_CHECKLIST_STAGE_INCOMPLETE`. The stage threshold must also be objectively met. For an `OBSTETRIC_HEMORRHAGE` bundle the request must carry `quantitative_blood_loss_ml`, which must be 1500 or greater for stage 2 and 2500 or greater for stage 3; for a `SEVERE_HYPERTENSION` bundle stage 2 requires a second severe-range reading, supplied as `systolic_mmhg` or `diastolic_mmhg` — either one alone suffices — and stage 3 is unavailable to it entirely because massive transfusion belongs only to haemorrhage. A threshold that is unmet, or a stage 3 attempted on a hypertension bundle, is refused with `ERR_BUNDLE_STAGE_CRITERIA_NOT_MET`. A figure the stage demands but the request omits — `quantitative_blood_loss_ml` on any haemorrhage escalation, a severe-range reading on a hypertension escalation to stage 2 — is instead an `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` field-value failure at priority 5, so it is reported ahead of the state, gate and threshold tests alike. Stage 3 further requires the unit to hold an `ACTIVE` `OR_STANDBY` roster entry, refused otherwise with `ERR_OR_STANDBY_NOT_STAFFED`; that roster test is decided before the threshold test rather than after it, so a stage 3 attempted on a hypertension bundle whose unit has no standby entry reports `ERR_OR_STANDBY_NOT_STAFFED` rather than `ERR_BUNDLE_STAGE_CRITERIA_NOT_MET`. On success `highest_stage_reached` is set to `target_stage` and never decreases, which is what makes the newly gated items completable. Stage 2 forces the encounter's acuity to `TIER_IV` and mandatory one-to-one coverage; stage 3 additionally mints an `EmergencyCesareanEvent` of urgency `CATEGORY_1_CRASH` in `DECISION_DECLARED` with `triggering_activation_id` set to this activation, and drives the unit to `DIVERT`. Whichever stage was requested, the escalation writes the figures it carried onto the activation, overwriting the readings recorded at the original trigger, and a stage-2 escalation additionally keeps its `quantitative_blood_loss_ml` as the private ceiling against which the resolution below is measured. It then re-derives the encounter and the unit, and a unit left standing in `INDUCTION_FROZEN` or `DIVERT` — which stage 3 always produces and which a stage 2 can produce through the coverage cascade alone — has every `CAPACITY_HELD` and `CONFIRMED` booking of the elective and patient-request classes bumped and each of their beds returned from `HELD` to `AVAILABLE`, after which the unit is re-derived once more. The bump is therefore a consequence of where the posture lands, not of the stage number.

Resolution is where both stage branches join, and it too belongs to the `ObstetricianAttending`. From `STAGE_2_ESCALATED` the activation moves to `CONTROLLED` once every item of gate 1 and gate 2 is terminal; from `STAGE_3_MASSIVE_TRANSFUSION` it moves to `CONTROLLED` once every item of gate 1, 2 and 3 is terminal and the cesarean event minted at stage 3 has left the operating room, that is, its status is no longer `TEAM_MOBILIZED` or `INCISION_RECORDED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_IN_PROGRESS`. Here too an item voided to `NOT_APPLICABLE` counts as settled alongside the three terminal statuses, so only a `PENDING` item of a reached gate can hold a resolution back. A haemorrhage resolution must carry `quantitative_blood_loss_ml`, and that figure must not be greater than the reading supplied at stage 2 — the stage-2 reading specifically, not whatever a later stage-3 escalation overwrote it with — since bleeding that is still increasing is not controlled; a greater figure is refused with `ERR_BUNDLE_NOT_CONTROLLED`, and the accepted figure is written onto the activation. A hypertension resolution must leave the figure null. Omitting it where it is required, or supplying it where it is forbidden, is an `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` field-value failure at priority 5 and so precedes every other test on this path. Reaching `CONTROLLED` voids to `NOT_APPLICABLE` every still-`PENDING` item whose `stage_gate` was never reached, then computes both scores, then — in the very same request — moves the activation on from `CONTROLLED` to `DEBRIEF_PENDING` if `missed_step_count` is 1 or more, or `late_step_count` is 2 or more, or `highest_stage_reached` is 2 or more. Only the first of those three conditions, a `missed_step_count` of 1 or more, also increments the unit's `bundle_breach_count`, and it increments it by exactly 1 on that edge. The unit may leave `DIVERT` at this point as the posture is re-derived.

The two scores are computed by one procedure run twice, differing only in which due date it consults. Take the applicable items: every item of the activation whose `status` is not `NOT_APPLICABLE`. If that set is empty the score is null. Otherwise count the on-time items — those with a non-null `completed_at` at or before their governing due date — and the late items — those with a non-null `completed_at` strictly after it — and the missed items, being those whose `status` is `MISSED`. An item still `PENDING` counts as neither on-time nor late but does enter the denominator, which is the total number of applicable items. An item whose governing due date is null — which only the advisory anchoring can produce, for a step whose predecessor was never completed — is treated the same way even when it carries a `completed_at`: neither on-time nor late, but still in the denominator. Compute a raw figure as 100.0 multiplied by the sum of the on-time count and 0.5 times the late count, divided by that denominator, and then reduced by 10.0 points for each missed item. Clamp the raw figure so that a value below 0.0 becomes 0.0 and a value above 100.0 becomes 100.0, and only then round the clamped figure to 2 decimal places, half-up; clamping strictly precedes rounding. The authoritative run, whose result is stored in `timeliness_score`, uses `due_at_activation_anchored` as the governing date, so a step completed inside its own individual allowance but after the cumulative deadline is late; the counters `on_time_step_count` and `late_step_count` on the activation always follow this authoritative run, and it is the only figure any gate or report consumes. The advisory run, stored in `sequence_adjusted_score`, uses `due_at_sequence_anchored` and never gates anything. The same data yields different numbers: with an activation at instant T, item 1 completed at T plus 2 minutes and item 2 completed at T plus 20 minutes, the activation-anchored dates are T plus 10 and T plus 25 minutes, so both are on time and the authoritative score over 4 applicable items is 100.00, while the sequence-anchored dates are T plus 10 and T plus 17 minutes, making item 2 late for an advisory score of 87.50. Both scores stay null until the activation reaches `CONTROLLED`, and a timeliness report read, open to a `ChargeNurse`, an `ObstetricianAttending` or a `PerinatalSafetyOfficer`, exposes the two variants side by side. That report is an unpaginated object holding `activation_id`, `bundle_type`, `status`, `highest_stage_reached`, `activated_at`, `applicable_item_count` as the number of items whose `status` is not `NOT_APPLICABLE`, the stored `on_time_step_count`, `late_step_count` and `missed_step_count`, the stored `timeliness_score` and `sequence_adjusted_score`, and `items`. `items` carries one entry per checklist item of the activation in `step_sequence` ascending order, voided items included even though the count above excludes them, and each entry holds `checklist_item_id`, `step_code`, `step_sequence`, `stage_gate`, `status`, `completed_at`, `minutes_from_activation`, `due_at_activation_anchored`, `due_at_sequence_anchored`, and finally the two classifications `on_time_activation_anchored` and `on_time_sequence_anchored`. Each classification is three-valued rather than boolean: true where the item has a `completed_at` at or before that anchoring's due date, false where it has one strictly after it, and null wherever the item has no `completed_at` at all, or that anchoring yielded no due date, or the item was voided out of the applicable set — so a still-`PENDING` item is reported as null under both anchorings rather than as false. The scores and counters the report prints are the ones stored on the activation, while the per-item classifications are re-derived live at read time, which is why an activation short of `CONTROLLED` can show classifications against null scores.

A `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` closes the activation from either `CONTROLLED` or `DEBRIEF_PENDING`, landing on `CLOSED`. A close from `DEBRIEF_PENDING` requires `debrief_notes` of 40 to 2000 characters inclusive, refused otherwise with `ERR_DEBRIEF_NOTES_REQUIRED`; a close from `CONTROLLED` accepts the field but ignores it. The acting `X-User-ID` must not match any `performed_by_staff_id` recorded on this activation's checklist items — nobody signs off the review of steps they documented themselves, and a voided item still counts as one they documented — refused otherwise with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`. Only after that does the close require a non-null `timeliness_score`, refused otherwise with `ERR_BUNDLE_SCORE_NOT_COMPUTED`, so a close attempted by an implicated user on an unscored activation reports the segregation failure. Closing voids any item still `PENDING` to `NOT_APPLICABLE`, re-derives the encounter's `review_required`, and unblocks the encounter's discharge path.

Rescission is the compensating exit, available to an `ObstetricianAttending` only before stage 2, that is, while `highest_stage_reached` is 1 or less, moving the activation from `ACTIVATED` or `STAGE_1_IN_PROGRESS` to `RESCINDED`. A bundle that reached stage 2 or 3 is a real clinical event and must be closed rather than erased; an attempt is refused with `ERR_BUNDLE_STAGE_BLOCKS_RESCIND`. The request carries `rescind_reason` of 10 to 500 characters and the revision guard described in section 2.4. Rescission voids every checklist item to `NOT_APPLICABLE` irrespective of its prior status, resets `on_time_step_count`, `late_step_count` and `missed_step_count` to 0, leaves both scores null forever, clears `magnesium_infusion_active`, and restores the encounter's `required_coverage_units`, every affected roster entry's `assigned_acuity_load_units` and the unit's `coverage_deficit_units` to exactly their pre-activation values by crediting the new price and debiting the old, which may return the encounter from `COVERAGE_BREACH` to `RATIO_COVERED` or `ONE_TO_ONE_COVERED`. The unit's `bundle_breach_count` is left untouched by a rescission. Activations may be listed by `encounter_id`, `unit_id`, `bundle_type` and `status`, ordered by `activated_at` ascending with `created_at` ascending as the tie-break.

### 3.7. Reading the Strip: Fetal Monitoring and the Escalation Chain

A `BedsideNurse`, `ChargeNurse` or `ObstetricianAttending` records an interpretation of the fetal heart-rate tracing against an encounter, naming `encounter_id`, `interpreted_by_staff_id`, a `category` of `CATEGORY_I`, `CATEGORY_II` or `CATEGORY_III`, a `baseline_rate_bpm` from 50 to 220, a `variability` of `ABSENT`, `MINIMAL`, `MODERATE` or `MARKED`, an optional `recurrent_decelerations` flag defaulting to false, and an optional `assessed_at` which defaults to the current instant and, when supplied, must be at or after the encounter's `admitted_at` and at or before the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_ASSESSMENT_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`. The interpreter must belong to the encounter's own unit, refused otherwise with `ERR_CROSS_UNIT_REFERENCE`, must be `ACTIVE` with a `credential_expires_at` strictly after the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_STAFF_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`, must have a `fetal_monitoring_cert_level` of `BASIC` or `ADVANCED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_MONITORING_CERT_INSUFFICIENT`, and must be on the case in the sense described for checklist performers, refused otherwise with `ERR_PERFORMER_NOT_ON_CASE`. The cross-unit test sits ahead of the encounter's terminal-phase guard, and the other three follow that guard in exactly the order given, all of them ahead of the timestamp range check, so an expired credential is reported ahead of an insufficient certification level and both ahead of an out-of-range `assessed_at`. The timestamp range is checked only when `assessed_at` was actually supplied; the default never fails it. The `category` is immutable once the assessment exists: a changed impression is a new assessment, never an edit.

The new row's `escalation_status` starts at `NOT_REQUIRED` when the `category` is `CATEGORY_I` and at `ESCALATION_DUE` otherwise, and `supersedes_assessment_id` is set to the immediately prior assessment on the same encounter, chosen as the one with the greatest `assessed_at` and, where two share that instant, the later `created_at`. When the `category` is not `CATEGORY_I`, `escalation_due_at` is set to `assessed_at` plus 20 minutes for a `CATEGORY_II` reading and plus 5 minutes for a `CATEGORY_III` reading. An unresolved `CATEGORY_II` chain lifts the encounter's acuity floor to `TIER_III` and a `CATEGORY_III` chain to `TIER_IV`, with the coverage and posture consequences of section 3.3.

Recording a new assessment also settles a predecessor that is sitting in `REASSESSMENT_PENDING`, provided the new row's `assessed_at` is at or before the predecessor's `reassessment_due_at`, the boundary instant included. A newer `CATEGORY_I` inside that window moves the predecessor from `REASSESSMENT_PENDING` to `RESOLVED`, which is terminal, clearing both its `reassessment_due_at` and its `next_tier_due_at` to null, and the newer row itself opens at `NOT_REQUIRED`. A newer `CATEGORY_II` or `CATEGORY_III` inside that window instead re-arms the predecessor, moving it from `REASSESSMENT_PENDING` back to `ESCALATION_DUE` with `escalation_due_at` reset to the re-arm instant plus the same 20 or 5 minutes for its own immutable `category`, and both `reassessment_due_at` and `next_tier_due_at` cleared to null. The re-arm preserves `escalation_tier_high_water`, which is the whole point of it: a second lap around the chain must resume higher than the first. A chain may re-arm repeatedly until its high-water mark reaches 3, after which a newer `CATEGORY_I` reading inside the window can still resolve it but nothing can re-arm it: a newer non-reassuring reading leaves it exactly as it was, and the sweep, which only breaches chains that are awaiting a notification, cannot breach it either. A chain that has already run to tier 3 and then been stood down therefore waits in `REASSESSMENT_PENDING` until a reassuring reading inside its window resolves it, and its lapsed `reassessment_due_at` is left standing rather than cleared.

Escalation advances exactly one tier per call. The request names `target_tier` as 1, 2 or 3, and `notified_staff_id` which is required for tiers 1 and 2 and omitted for tier 3; omitting it where it is required, or naming it where it is forbidden, is an `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` field-value failure at priority 5 and so precedes every other test on this path. An assessment whose `escalation_status` is none of the three chain steps at all — `NOT_REQUIRED`, `REASSESSMENT_PENDING`, `RESOLVED` or `TIMED_OUT_BREACH` — has no escalation edge declared from it, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` rather than with a tier-skip. Where the status is a chain step, `target_tier` must equal `escalation_tier_high_water` plus 1 and that status must be the step that precedes the requested tier — `ESCALATION_DUE` for tier 1, `TIER_1_NOTIFIED` for tier 2, `TIER_2_NOTIFIED` for tier 3 — refused otherwise with `ERR_ESCALATION_TIER_SKIPPED`. A tier-1 call must arrive at or before `escalation_due_at`, the boundary instant still being in time, refused otherwise with `ERR_ESCALATION_WINDOW_EXPIRED`. A tier-1 target must be a staff member holding an `ACTIVE` roster entry in the `CHARGE` slot on the encounter's unit; a tier-2 target must have `staff_role` `OB_ATTENDING` and an `ACTIVE` roster entry on the unit; either failure is refused with `ERR_ESCALATION_TARGET_INELIGIBLE`. Tier 3 requires the assessment's `category` to be `CATEGORY_III`, refused otherwise with `ERR_ESCALATION_CATEGORY_INSUFFICIENT`, and requires an `ACTIVE` `OR_STANDBY` roster entry on the unit, refused otherwise with `ERR_OR_STANDBY_NOT_STAFFED`. A `notified_staff_id` naming a member of another unit is refused with `ERR_CROSS_UNIT_REFERENCE` before any of that. And the tier-1 window test, described first above for readability, is in fact the last of these to be decided: the tier-skip test, then target eligibility, then the tier-3 category test, then the tier-3 standby test, and only then the window — so a tier-1 call that is both out of time and aimed at an ineligible target reports `ERR_ESCALATION_TARGET_INELIGIBLE`.

A successful escalation sets `escalation_tier_high_water` to `target_tier` and moves `escalation_status` to `TIER_1_NOTIFIED`, `TIER_2_NOTIFIED` or `TIER_3_ACTIVATED`. For tiers 1 and 2 it sets `next_tier_due_at` to the instant of that notification plus 15 minutes for a `CATEGORY_II` assessment or 5 minutes for a `CATEGORY_III` assessment; at tier 3 `next_tier_due_at` becomes null, because there is no fourth tier. Tier 2 raises the encounter's acuity floor to `TIER_III`. Tier 3 fires a three-hop cascade: first an `EmergencyCesareanEvent` is minted on the encounter in `DECISION_DECLARED` with `urgency_class` `CATEGORY_1_CRASH`, `decision_at` at the current instant and `triggering_assessment_id` pointing at this assessment; second that event sets the encounter's `one_to_one_required` to true and its acuity to `TIER_IV`, re-pricing the covering care assignment; and third that re-pricing raises the covering roster entry's `assigned_acuity_load_units`, which can push sibling encounters on the same nurse from `RATIO_COVERED` or `ONE_TO_ONE_COVERED` into `COVERAGE_BREACH`, raise the unit's `coverage_deficit_units` and re-derive its posture.

An `ObstetricianAttending` stands a chain down from any notified tier — `TIER_1_NOTIFIED`, `TIER_2_NOTIFIED` or `TIER_3_ACTIVATED` — into `REASSESSMENT_PENDING`. This requires `escalation_tier_high_water` to be 1 or more, since nothing that was never escalated can be stood down, refused otherwise with `ERR_ESCALATION_NOT_STARTED`, and requires that no cesarean event minted from this assessment sits in `TEAM_MOBILIZED` or `INCISION_RECORDED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_IN_PROGRESS`. It sets `reassessment_due_at` to the current instant plus 30 minutes and clears `next_tier_due_at` to null.

The capacity sweep enforces the three deadlines, and every one of its tests is strict: the deadline instant itself is not yet a breach, only an instant after it is. From `ESCALATION_DUE` the sweep moves an assessment to `TIMED_OUT_BREACH` once the current instant is strictly after `escalation_due_at`; from `TIER_1_NOTIFIED` or `TIER_2_NOTIFIED` it does so once the current instant is strictly after `next_tier_due_at`. The breach clears `next_tier_due_at` to null and leaves `escalation_due_at` standing, as the record of the deadline that was missed. Each such breach increments the unit's `escalation_breach_count` by 1 and re-derives the posture, and because a count of 2 or more forces at least `INDUCTION_FROZEN`, the second breach on a unit bumps that unit's `CAPACITY_HELD` bookings of the elective and patient-request classes and releases the beds they were holding, leaving `MEDICALLY_INDICATED` holds and every `CONFIRMED` booking exactly where they were. That bump is applied once per sweep, after every chain on the unit has been settled and the count has taken all of its increments, rather than once per breached chain, and it is tested against the count as it then stands, so a unit already at 2 or more bumps again on any further breach. From `REASSESSMENT_PENDING`, with no newer assessment recorded at all, the sweep re-arms the chain once the current instant is strictly after `reassessment_due_at`, using the same re-arm semantics as a newer non-reassuring reading. Note the deliberate asymmetry between these strict breach tests and the inclusive test that lets a tier-1 call land exactly on `escalation_due_at`. Assessments may be listed by `encounter_id`, `interpreted_by_staff_id`, `supersedes_assessment_id`, `category` and `escalation_status`, ordered by `assessed_at` ascending with `created_at` ascending as the tie-break, which is the ascending mirror of the descending rule the phase gates use to identify the latest assessment.

### 3.8. Decision-to-Incision: Emergency Cesareans and Drill Currency

An emergency cesarean event may arrive on an encounter in two ways. It is minted automatically by a tier-3 fetal escalation and by a stage-3 bundle escalation, as described above; and an `ObstetricianAttending` may declare one directly, naming `encounter_id`, an `urgency_class` of `CATEGORY_1_CRASH` or `CATEGORY_2_URGENT`, and optionally `triggering_assessment_id` and `triggering_activation_id`, each of which must belong to the same encounter. Both routes produce an identical row: status `DECISION_DECLARED` with `decision_at` at the current instant, the two triggering references left null on a clinician declaration unless supplied. A triggering reference that names an assessment or an activation belonging to some other encounter is refused with `ERR_CROSS_UNIT_REFERENCE`, decided before the encounter's own terminal-phase guard. The `urgency_class` is immutable, because it fixes the decision-to-incision target at 15 minutes for `CATEGORY_1_CRASH` and 30 minutes for `CATEGORY_2_URGENT`. At most one event per encounter may sit in a non-terminal status; a second declaration while one is still live is refused with `ERR_CESAREAN_EVENT_IN_PROGRESS`, decided immediately after that terminal-phase guard. Declaring an event re-derives the encounter's acuity floor and the unit's posture, since a declared event counts as an encounter in theatre. Because cascade-minted events are never returned to any caller, the filtered event list is the only way to discover them; it filters on `encounter_id`, `unit_id` resolved through the parent encounter, `or_bed_id`, `triggering_assessment_id`, `triggering_activation_id`, `status` and `urgency_class`, ordered by `decision_at` descending with `created_at` descending as the tie-break.

Mobilising the team is an `ObstetricianAttending` action that moves the event from `DECISION_DECLARED` to `TEAM_MOBILIZED`. It names `or_bed_id`, which must be a bed of `bed_type` `OR` on the encounter's own unit whose `status` is `AVAILABLE`, and `staff_ids`, an array of 3 to 12 staff identifiers whose payload order is preserved verbatim in `mobilized_staff_ids`. The three ways that bed can be wrong are three different refusals rather than one: a bed on another unit is `ERR_CROSS_UNIT_REFERENCE`, a bed of any other `bed_type` is `ERR_BED_TYPE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, and only an `OR` bed on the right unit that is not `AVAILABLE` is `ERR_OR_BED_UNAVAILABLE` — in that order, and with the cross-unit test also covering the named staff, so a foreign bed or a foreign team member is reported before the event's own state and phase guards. An identifier in `staff_ids` that matches no staff member at all is `ERR_STAFF_NOT_FOUND`, resolved in payload order before even that. Every named member must additionally be `ACTIVE` with a `credential_expires_at` strictly after the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_STAFF_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`, which is decided after the bed tests and before the composition test below. That array must contain at least one staff member with `staff_role` `ANESTHESIOLOGIST`, at least one `SURGICAL_TECH` whose `or_circulating_qualified` is true, and at least one `NEONATAL_RESUSCITATOR`, each holding an `ACTIVE` roster entry on the unit, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_TEAM_INCOMPLETE`. For a `CATEGORY_2_URGENT` event the unit's `cesarean_readiness_state` must not be `NOT_READY`, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_READINESS_NOT_MET`; a `CATEGORY_1_CRASH` event bypasses that check entirely, because a crash section is never refused for a paperwork lapse. Mobilising takes the OR bed from `AVAILABLE` to `OCCUPIED`, sets the encounter's `one_to_one_required` to true with the usual re-pricing and posture consequences, and auto-checks-out every `CHECKED_IN` support pass on the encounter — moving it from `CHECKED_IN` to `CHECKED_OUT` — unless that pass has `credential_type` `DOULA_CERTIFIED` with `or_credentialed` true, which is the one pass allowed to follow the patient into theatre. The visitor allowance drops to 1 for the duration.

Recording the incision moves the event from `TEAM_MOBILIZED` to `INCISION_RECORDED` and produces the headline measurement. The request names `incision_at`, which must be strictly after `decision_at` and at or before the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`. `decision_to_incision_minutes` is the number of whole minutes in the interval from `decision_at` to `incision_at`, obtained by dividing the elapsed seconds by 60 and discarding any remainder — the figure always truncates downward and is never rounded up. `met_target` is true when that figure is less than or equal to the event's target, the boundary counting as met: a measurement of exactly 15 on a `CATEGORY_1_CRASH` meets its target, and an interval of 30 minutes and 1 second measures 30 whole minutes and still meets a 30-minute target, so only 31 whole minutes fails. Recording an incision re-derives the unit's readiness over its last three measured events, which can drop it to `DEGRADED` or `NOT_READY`; a *fresh* drop to `NOT_READY` — the state having been anything other than `NOT_READY` immediately before this re-derivation, so that a unit already `NOT_READY` when the incision is recorded fires no bump at all, unlike the drill invalidation described later in this section, which cascades on the resulting state alone — bumps every `CONFIRMED` booking of the `ELECTIVE_39_WEEK` class, moving each from `CONFIRMED` to `BUMPED`, returning its held bed from `HELD` to `AVAILABLE`, decrementing the day's committed bed-minutes by exactly the booking's frozen `projected_occupancy_minutes` and incrementing its `bump_count`.

Recording the delivery moves the event from `INCISION_RECORDED` to `DELIVERED`. The request names `delivery_at`, which must be at or after `incision_at` and at or before the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`. The system, not the caller, chooses the delivery mode: the encounter's `delivery_mode` becomes `CESAREAN_REPEAT` when its `prior_cesarean` is true and `CESAREAN_PRIMARY` otherwise, and `delivery_recorded_at` is set to `delivery_at`. The OR bed moves from `OCCUPIED` to `TURNOVER` with `turnover_clear_at` set to the current instant plus the unit's `turnover_buffer_minutes`. Every `DRAFT` quality snapshot whose window contains `delivery_recorded_at` is recomputed, in both its authoritative and its shadow variant; a `CERTIFIED` snapshot is never retro-mutated. Then, at the end of that same request, the event moves on from `DELIVERED` without a second call: to `REVIEW_PENDING` when `met_target` is false or the `urgency_class` is `CATEGORY_1_CRASH`, also setting the encounter's `review_required` to true; and straight to `CLOSED` when `met_target` is true and the `urgency_class` is `CATEGORY_2_URGENT`. That new status is then fed straight back into the derivations, as the closing act of the same request. Neither `REVIEW_PENDING` nor `CLOSED` is one of the three in-theatre statuses that fire the `TIER_IV` clause of section 3.3, so the encounter's `acuity_tier`, `one_to_one_required`, `required_coverage_units` and `review_required` are all recomputed from scratch, its `ACTIVE` coverage rows are re-priced, and the unit's `coverage_deficit_units`, `staffing_posture` and `turnover_buffer_minutes` are re-derived after them. An encounter whose only `TIER_IV` ground was the operation therefore falls back to the plain acuity-weight-times-phase-multiplier requirement the moment the delivery is recorded, and the unit's deficit and posture follow it down.

A `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` closes the event from either `DELIVERED` or `REVIEW_PENDING`. A close from `REVIEW_PENDING` requires a `CLOSED` newborn complication review on the encounter first, refused otherwise with `ERR_NEWBORN_REVIEW_OPEN`. The acting `X-User-ID` must not appear anywhere in `mobilized_staff_ids` — the team that ran the case cannot sign it off — refused otherwise with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`. Closing re-derives the encounter's `review_required`, which becomes false once no other event or review is open, and runs the same full re-derivation the delivery record ran — acuity, one-to-one requirement and coverage requirement on the encounter, then the unit's deficit, posture and turnover buffer. A `CLOSED` event is outside the in-theatre clause of the acuity derivation, exactly as `DELIVERED` and `REVIEW_PENDING` already were, so a close that follows a recorded delivery normally finds nothing left to move on that clause.

Aborting to a vaginal delivery is the other branch out of the machine, available to an `ObstetricianAttending` from `DECISION_DECLARED` or `TEAM_MOBILIZED`, landing on the terminal `ABORTED_TO_VAGINAL`. It requires `incision_at` to still be null, refused otherwise with `ERR_CESAREAN_ALREADY_INCISED`, and requires the latest assessment on the encounter to be `CATEGORY_I` or `CATEGORY_II`, since a `CATEGORY_III` tracing does not support abandoning the operation, refused otherwise with `ERR_FETAL_CATEGORY_BLOCKS_ABORT`. An encounter carrying no assessment at all passes that gate rather than failing it: the test is on the latest reading if there is one, and silence is not an objection. If an OR bed was held it moves from `OCCUPIED` to `TURNOVER` and `or_bed_id` is cleared to null. The encounter's `delivery_mode` is deliberately left untouched, so the later phase advance can record the vaginal mode, and `one_to_one_required` is re-derived. An aborted event never receives `decision_to_incision_minutes` or `met_target`, and so is permanently excluded from the rolling readiness mean.

Readiness drills are the other half of the same axis. A `ChargeNurse` or `AnesthesiaLead` schedules a drill on a unit, naming a `drill_type` of `DECISION_TO_INCISION`, `HEMORRHAGE_STAGE_2` or `ECLAMPSIA` which is immutable, a `scheduled_for_utc` which must be at or after the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_DRILL_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`, and `participant_staff_ids`, an array of 2 to 20 staff identifiers all belonging to that unit. An identifier matching no staff member is `ERR_STAFF_NOT_FOUND`, resolved in payload order; one belonging to another unit is `ERR_CROSS_UNIT_REFERENCE`; and every named participant must be `ACTIVE` with a `credential_expires_at` strictly after the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_STAFF_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`, which is decided before the `scheduled_for_utc` range test. The drill opens at `SCHEDULED`, and scheduling changes nothing about readiness, because only a validated drill grants currency — but it does advance the unit's own `version` by 1, so a caller holding a revision number for that unit must re-read after somebody else schedules a drill on it. A `ChargeNurse` starts the drill, moving it from `SCHEDULED` to `IN_PROGRESS`, once the current instant is at or after `scheduled_for_utc`, the boundary instant counting as due, refused otherwise with `ERR_DRILL_NOT_DUE`; a start is also blocked while the unit's `staffing_posture` is `DIVERT`, since a unit that is diverting real patients does not run simulations.

A `ChargeNurse` or `AnesthesiaLead` documents the drill, moving it to `DOCUMENTED` either from `IN_PROGRESS` or, as a re-documentation, from `INVALIDATED`. The request names a simulated `decision_at`, an `incision_at` strictly after it and at or before the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_DRILL_TIMESTAMP_OUT_OF_RANGE`, and `documented_by_staff_id`, which must appear in `participant_staff_ids`, refused otherwise with `ERR_PERFORMER_NOT_ON_CASE`. The participant roster must contain at least one staff member with `staff_role` `OB_ATTENDING` and at least one `ANESTHESIOLOGIST`, refused otherwise with `ERR_DRILL_PARTICIPANTS_INSUFFICIENT`. `measured_d2i_minutes` is computed by the same truncating rule as a real event: the elapsed seconds from `decision_at` to `incision_at` divided by 60 with the remainder discarded. A re-documentation entered from `INVALIDATED` requires `revision_count` to be less than 2, refused otherwise with `ERR_DRILL_REVISION_LIMIT_REACHED`, and increments `revision_count` by 1; a first documentation from `IN_PROGRESS` does not touch that counter. A `documented_by_staff_id` belonging to another unit is refused with `ERR_CROSS_UNIT_REFERENCE` ahead of everything else, and the documenter must itself be `ACTIVE` with a `credential_expires_at` strictly after the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_STAFF_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED` — a test applied to the documenter alone and never to the rest of the roster, so a drill may be documented while other participants have lapsed. These tests are decided in the ladder order of section 2.6 rather than in the order they are described here: the declared transition, then the revision limit, then the documenter's credential, then the participant composition, then the documenter's membership of the roster, and the timestamps last of all. Documentation alone still changes no readiness.

A `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` validates the drill, moving it from `DOCUMENTED` to `VALIDATED`. This requires `measured_d2i_minutes` to be less than or equal to 30, with exactly 30 passing, refused otherwise with `ERR_DRILL_PERFORMANCE_FAILED`, and requires the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from `documented_by_staff_id`, refused otherwise with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`. Validation sets `validated_at` to the current instant, `expires_at` to `validated_at` plus 90 days, and `validated_by_user_id` to the acting user; it stamps every participant's `last_validated_drill_at` with the drill's simulated `decision_at` and moves each participant's `drill_currency_state` from `LAPSED` to `CURRENT`. It then recomputes unit readiness, which can rise to `READY` and thereby unblock induction confirmations and second-stage advances, and makes `OR_STANDBY` eligibility satisfiable.

Invalidation is the deepest cascade in the system, and it too belongs to the `PerinatalSafetyOfficer`. It names an `invalidation_reason` of 10 to 500 characters and moves the drill from `VALIDATED` to `INVALIDATED`, nulling `expires_at`. First hop: each participant re-derives `last_validated_drill_at` from the drills that remain `VALIDATED` for them, ordered by `decision_at` descending with `created_at` descending as the tie-break, and any participant left with no other validated drill moves from `CURRENT` to `LAPSED`. Second hop: if this was the unit's only current drill, `cesarean_readiness_state` becomes `NOT_READY` with `readiness_reason` `NO_CURRENT_DRILL`. Third hop: a `NOT_READY` unit bumps every booking in `CAPACITY_HELD` or `CONFIRMED` whose `priority_class` is `ELECTIVE_39_WEEK` or `PATIENT_REQUEST`, moving each to `BUMPED` and incrementing its `bump_count`. Fourth hop: each bumped booking releases its bed from `HELD` to `AVAILABLE` and returns to the day's budget exactly the frozen `projected_occupancy_minutes` it had reserved, never a recomputed figure, after which the day's capacity projection is re-run. A `ChargeNurse` may instead abandon a drill, moving it from `SCHEDULED` or `IN_PROGRESS` to the terminal `ABANDONED`; a `DOCUMENTED`, `VALIDATED`, already-`INVALIDATED` or already-`ABANDONED` drill can never be abandoned, refused with `ERR_DRILL_NOT_ABANDONABLE` in every one of those four cases rather than with an undeclared-transition error, so a repeated abandonment reports that the drill is not abandonable. An abandoned drill changes no readiness, because it never granted currency. Drills are readable per unit, ordered by `scheduled_for_utc` descending with `created_at` descending as the tie-break, and at the collection root ordered by `scheduled_for_utc` ascending with `created_at` ascending; both forms filter on `status`, `drill_type`, `participant_staff_id` matching any entry of the participant array, and `documented_by_staff_id`, and the root form additionally on `unit_id`.

The unit's `cesarean_readiness_state` is derived, never set. Its three inputs are computed first. The current drill is the drill on this unit whose `status` is `VALIDATED` and whose `expires_at` is strictly after the current instant, ordered by `validated_at` descending with `created_at` descending as the tie-break, taking the first; if none qualifies the current drill is null, and `INVALIDATED`, `DOCUMENTED` and `ABANDONED` drills contribute nothing. The rolling set is the emergency cesarean events on this unit whose `decision_to_incision_minutes` is non-null and whose `status` is `INCISION_RECORDED`, `DELIVERED`, `REVIEW_PENDING` or `CLOSED`, ordered by `incision_at` descending with `created_at` descending as the tie-break, taking at most the first 3; aborted events, having no measurement, never enter it. `mean_d2i_minutes` is null when that set is empty, and otherwise the sum of their `decision_to_incision_minutes` divided by the count of them, rounded to 2 decimal places half-up. The standby test is satisfied when there exists an `ACTIVE` roster entry on the unit whose `role_slot` is `OR_STANDBY` and whose staff member's `drill_currency_state` is `CURRENT`. The state is then decided by the first matching branch: `NOT_READY` when the current drill is null, or when the mean is non-null and strictly greater than 45.00; otherwise `DEGRADED` when the standby test is not satisfied, or when the mean is non-null and strictly greater than 30.00; otherwise `READY`. Both ceilings are strict, so a mean of exactly 30.00 stays `READY` and a mean of exactly 45.00 stays `DEGRADED`. The empty rolling window is not a failure: a unit with a current validated drill, standby staffed and no measured emergency events at all is `READY`. The derivation re-runs on every request that re-derives the unit — drill validation and invalidation, roster activation, relief and cancellation, the recording of an incision, the capacity sweep, and equally every coverage cascade of section 3.3 — and always immediately before the posture derivation that consumes its result, and it publishes `cesarean_readiness_state`, `readiness_reason`, `current_drill_id`, `current_drill_expires_at` and `mean_d2i_minutes` on the unit itself, so that a plain unit read carries them alongside the posture figures. The readiness report re-runs that same derivation live rather than echoing the stored fields, and returns an unpaginated object holding `unit_id`, `cesarean_readiness_state`, `readiness_reason`, `current_drill_id`, `current_drill_expires_at` and `mean_d2i_minutes` together with `rolling_event_ids`, the identifiers of the events in the rolling set in that set's own descending order, and `or_standby_staffed`, the boolean outcome of the standby test. `readiness_reason` names the branch that produced the state and is one of `NO_CURRENT_DRILL`, `MEAN_D2I_ABOVE_DEGRADED_CEILING`, `NO_OR_STANDBY`, `MEAN_D2I_ABOVE_READY_CEILING` or `READY`, in that branch order.

### 3.9. Debriefs, Visitors and the Quality Ledger

A `ChargeNurse` or `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` opens a newborn complication review on an encounter, naming `encounter_id`, a `complication_code` of `UNEXPECTED_NICU_TERM`, `HIE_SUSPECTED`, `APGAR5_UNDER_7`, `BIRTH_INJURY` or `UNEXPECTED_TRANSFER`, and a `facilitator_staff_id` who must belong to the same unit as the encounter, refused otherwise with `ERR_CROSS_UNIT_REFERENCE`, and must never have held a care assignment on that encounter in any status whatsoever — not merely no active one — refused otherwise with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`. The facilitator must also be `ACTIVE` with a `credential_expires_at` strictly after the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_STAFF_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`; that credential test is decided after the segregation test, so a facilitator who is both implicated in the case and out of credential is reported as the segregation failure. The order over the whole path is the cross-unit test, then the encounter's terminal-phase guard, then segregation, then the credential. The review opens at `OPEN` with `opened_at` at the current instant and `debrief_due_at` set to `opened_at` plus 72 hours, and sets the encounter's `review_required` to true. An open review blocks discharge and blocks the close of a `REVIEW_PENDING` cesarean event.

A `ChargeNurse` books the debrief, moving the review from `OPEN` or — as the cycle re-entry — from `REWORK_REQUESTED` to `DEBRIEF_SCHEDULED`. The named `debrief_scheduled_for_utc` must be at or after the current instant and at or before `debrief_due_at`, both boundaries inclusive, refused otherwise with `ERR_DEBRIEF_WINDOW_VIOLATION`. On a reworked review the value compared against is the extended `debrief_due_at`, so a slot that was rejected before the rework can become legal after it. The facilitator segregation check is re-evaluated at this point. The same `ChargeNurse` role then submits findings, moving the review from `DEBRIEF_SCHEDULED` to `FINDINGS_SUBMITTED`, naming `findings_summary` of 40 to 2000 characters, refused otherwise with `ERR_DEBRIEF_NOTES_REQUIRED`, and `contributing_factor_codes`, a non-empty array of 1 to 10 entries. That array fails in two different ways: an array of more than 10 entries, or a value that is not an array at all, is an `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` field-value failure at priority 5, while an array that is simply absent or empty is `ERR_CONTRIBUTING_FACTORS_REQUIRED`, decided immediately after the `findings_summary` bound and well after the field-value band. Each entry must be a non-empty string, an empty string or a non-string element being a field-value failure in its own right, but the entries are otherwise unconstrained: no controlled vocabulary is enforced on them at any point. Submission then requires every bundle activation on the encounter to be `CLOSED` or `RESCINDED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_BUNDLE_NOT_CLOSED`, and only after that requires the current instant to be at or after `debrief_scheduled_for_utc`, the boundary instant counting as held, refused otherwise with `ERR_DEBRIEF_NOT_HELD` — so a submission attempted early on an encounter that still has a live bundle reports the bundle. It sets `debrief_held_at` to the current instant. Note the deliberate asymmetry with the scheduling gate: findings may be submitted after `debrief_due_at` has already passed, since only the scheduling was bounded and lateness is not blocked here.

A `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` may send the review back around the cycle, moving it from `FINDINGS_SUBMITTED` to `REWORK_REQUESTED` with a `rework_reason` of 10 to 500 characters. Unlike the other context reasons in this specification, that one is an ordinary declared field rather than a context-required field: omitting it or breaching its bounds is an `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` field-value failure at priority 5, and there is no rework-reason identifier of its own anywhere in the catalogue. This requires `rework_count` to be less than 2, refused otherwise with `ERR_REVIEW_REWORK_LIMIT_REACHED`, and the requester must not be the `facilitator_staff_id`, refused otherwise with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`. It increments `rework_count` by 1, clears `findings_summary`, `contributing_factor_codes`, `debrief_scheduled_for_utc` and `debrief_held_at`, and extends `debrief_due_at` to the current instant plus 24 hours. That extension is the only mutation of any deadline anywhere in this system; every other deadline in the specification is fixed at the moment it is derived. Finally a `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` closes the review from `FINDINGS_SUBMITTED` to the terminal `CLOSED`. The closer must be neither the `facilitator_staff_id` nor any staff member who ever held a care assignment on the encounter, refused otherwise with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`, and `contributing_factor_codes` must still be non-empty, refused otherwise with `ERR_CONTRIBUTING_FACTORS_REQUIRED` — which, sitting in the context-required band, is decided before either segregation test, so a close attempted by the facilitator on a review whose factors were cleared by a rework reports the missing factors. Closing records `closed_by_user_id` as the acting user, re-derives the encounter's `review_required` to false once this was the last review that was not `CLOSED` and no cesarean event is in `REVIEW_PENDING`, unblocks discharge and makes a `REVIEW_PENDING` cesarean event closable. Reviews are listed by `encounter_id`, `facilitator_staff_id`, `status` and `complication_code`, ordered by `opened_at` descending with `created_at` descending as the tie-break.

A `PatientAccessCoordinator` or `ChargeNurse` issues a support person pass on an encounter, naming `person_name` of 1 to 120 characters, an immutable `credential_type` of `PARTNER`, `DOULA_CERTIFIED`, `STUDENT_OBSERVER` or `INTERPRETER`, a `doula_certification_expires_at` which is required when the credential is `DOULA_CERTIFIED` and forbidden otherwise and which must be strictly after the current instant, and an optional `or_credentialed` flag defaulting to false. The two shapes of failure on that instant are different refusals: omitting it on a doula pass, or supplying it on any other credential type, is an `ERR_INVALID_FIELD_VALUE` field-value failure at priority 5, decided ahead of the encounter's terminal-phase guard, while only a value that is present, permitted and not strictly after the current instant is `ERR_DOULA_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`, decided after that guard. `or_credentialed` may be set true on any credential type, but it only ever means anything on a `DOULA_CERTIFIED` pass. The pass opens at `ISSUED` with `valid_until` set to the current instant plus 24 hours, and an issued pass occupies nothing until it is checked in.

A `PatientAccessCoordinator` checks the person in, moving the pass from `ISSUED` or — as the cycle re-entry — from `CHECKED_OUT` to `CHECKED_IN`. The current instant must be strictly before `valid_until`, the boundary instant already counting as expired, refused otherwise with `ERR_SUPPORT_PASS_EXPIRED`. A `DOULA_CERTIFIED` pass must have `doula_certification_expires_at` strictly after the current instant at this moment too, not merely at issuance, refused otherwise with `ERR_DOULA_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED`. If any cesarean event on the encounter sits in `TEAM_MOBILIZED` or `INCISION_RECORDED`, the check-in is refused with `ERR_VISITATION_RESTRICTED_BY_PROCEDURE` unless this pass is a `DOULA_CERTIFIED` pass whose `or_credentialed` is true. A re-entry from `CHECKED_OUT` requires `reentry_count` to be less than 3, refused otherwise with `ERR_SUPPORT_REENTRY_LIMIT_REACHED`, and increments `reentry_count` by 1; a first check-in from `ISSUED` does not touch that counter. These tests are not decided in the order they are described here: the declared transition comes first, then the encounter's terminal-phase guard, then the re-entry limit, then the pass's own expiry, then the doula credential, then the theatre restriction, and the occupancy allowance last of all — so a fourth re-entry attempted on a pass that has also expired reports `ERR_SUPPORT_REENTRY_LIMIT_REACHED`. Success sets `checked_in_at` to the current instant and clears `checked_out_at` to null. A `PatientAccessCoordinator` checks the person out again with no preconditions, moving the pass from `CHECKED_IN` to `CHECKED_OUT`, setting `checked_out_at` and freeing an occupancy slot — the manual counterpart of the automatic checkout inside team mobilisation. A `ChargeNurse` may revoke a pass from `ISSUED`, `CHECKED_IN` or `CHECKED_OUT` with no preconditions, the same edge the transfer-out cascade fires automatically; `REVOKED` is terminal, so unlike a checked-out pass a revoked one can never be checked in again, and it no longer blocks discharge. The sweep expires an `ISSUED` or `CHECKED_OUT` pass once `valid_until` has passed. Passes are listed by `encounter_id`, `status` and `credential_type`, ordered by `created_at` ascending.

How many support people may be in the room is derived, never stored as a limit. First count the staff in the room, being the care assignments on this encounter whose `status` is `ACTIVE`. Then take the base allowance for the encounter's `labor_phase`: 2 for `ANTEPARTUM`, 2 for `LATENT`, 2 for `ACTIVE_FIRST_STAGE`, 1 for `SECOND_STAGE`, 1 for `THIRD_STAGE_DELIVERED`, 2 for `POSTPARTUM_RECOVERY`, 0 for `DISCHARGED` and 0 for `TRANSFERRED_OUT`. Add a doula bonus of 1 when there exists a pass on this encounter whose `credential_type` is `DOULA_CERTIFIED`, whose `doula_certification_expires_at` is strictly after the current instant and whose `status` is `ISSUED`, `CHECKED_IN` or `CHECKED_OUT`, and the unit's `staffing_posture` is not `DIVERT`; otherwise add 0. The allowance is then the smaller of that sum and the figure obtained by subtracting the staff in the room from the maximum room occupancy of 4. If the unit's `staffing_posture` is `INDUCTION_FROZEN` or `DIVERT`, the allowance is reduced to 1 whenever it was larger than 1. If any cesarean event on the encounter is in `TEAM_MOBILIZED` or `INCISION_RECORDED`, the allowance is forced to exactly 1, and that operating-room override is what makes the OR-credentialed doula the only admissible visitor. Finally, if the allowance is below 0 it becomes 0. Only passes whose `status` is `CHECKED_IN` count toward occupancy; revoked and expired passes contribute nothing. A check-in is admitted while the current count of checked-in passes plus 1 is less than or equal to the allowance, so a check-in landing exactly on the allowance is admitted, and it is refused with `ERR_SUPPORT_OCCUPANCY_EXCEEDED` otherwise. That refusal is evaluated last of all, after every other check on the request. At the zero boundary — a maximum of 4 with 4 active care assignments — the allowance is 0 and every check-in fails, even for a certified doula.

A `PerinatalSafetyOfficer` computes a quality measure snapshot on a unit over the half-open window from `period_start_utc`, which is inclusive, to `period_end_utc`, which is exclusive and must be strictly greater than the start, refused otherwise with `ERR_MEASURE_PERIOD_INVALID`. The request also carries the revision guard described in section 2.4. The snapshot opens at `DRAFT`, and creating a snapshot for an identical window supersedes every existing row for that window, whether `DRAFT` or `CERTIFIED`, moving each to the terminal `SUPERSEDED` and writing the new snapshot's identifier into each predecessor's `superseded_by_snapshot_id`; a certified predecessor keeps its frozen counts for audit and is never recomputed, and a row already `SUPERSEDED` is passed over rather than superseded again. The new row's own figures are computed before that supersession runs, and creating a snapshot advances the unit's `version` by 1 as its closing act — which matters precisely because the request that creates it echoes that same `version` in its revision guard, so two snapshots in a row on one unit need two different echoed numbers.

The cohort is the encounters on this unit whose `delivery_mode` is non-null and whose attribution instant falls inside the half-open window, meaning at or after the start and strictly before the end. An encounter with no recorded delivery — including one transferred out before delivering — is excluded from both numerator and denominator. The `ntsv_cesarean_denominator` counts cohort encounters whose `is_ntsv` is true, and the `ntsv_cesarean_numerator` counts those same encounters whose `delivery_mode` is additionally `CESAREAN_PRIMARY`. The `episiotomy_denominator` counts cohort encounters whose `delivery_mode` is `SPONTANEOUS_VAGINAL` or `OPERATIVE_VAGINAL`, and the `episiotomy_numerator` counts those same encounters whose `episiotomy_performed` is additionally true. Each rate — `ntsv_cesarean_rate_pct` and `episiotomy_rate_pct` — is null when its own denominator is 0 — never 0.0 — and otherwise is 100.0 multiplied by that numerator and divided by that denominator, rounded to 4 decimal places half-up. The `composite_quality_index` is null when either rate is null, and otherwise is the sum of the two rates divided by 2.0, rounded to 2 decimal places half-up. The order here is pinned: the two component rates are rounded to 4 decimal places first and it is those already-rounded values that are averaged and then rounded to 2 places. Averaging the unrounded quotients and rounding once would yield a different value at the cent level, and that order is forbidden.

The snapshot is computed twice on the same call. The authoritative figures use `delivery_recorded_at` as the attribution instant; `attribution_basis` is always `DELIVERY_COHORT`, and these are the figures certification freezes and any external submission reports. The advisory figures use `admitted_at` instead and are written into `shadow_ntsv_rate_pct` for internal trending only; they are never certified and never gate anything. The two disagree whenever an encounter straddles a window boundary: a patient admitted at 2026-08-31T22:00:00Z who delivers by cesarean at 2026-09-01T03:00:00Z belongs to the August admission cohort and to the September delivery cohort, so the August snapshot's authoritative NTSV rate excludes her while its shadow rate includes her. A `DRAFT` snapshot keeps re-deriving both variants whenever a delivery is recorded inside its window, whether by a third-stage phase advance or by a cesarean delivery record.

Certification, again by a `PerinatalSafetyOfficer`, moves a snapshot from `DRAFT` to `CERTIFIED`. The certifier must not be the facilitator of any newborn complication review on this snapshot's own unit whose `opened_at` falls inside the reporting window — at or after `period_start_utc` and strictly before `period_end_utc`, the same half-open window the cohort uses — refused otherwise with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`. Reviews on other units, and reviews opened outside that window, are ignored whatever their status, and a review that has since closed still implicates its facilitator. That segregation test is decided immediately after the declared-transition guard and ahead of the three reporting tests that follow, which are themselves evaluated in the order given here. Both denominators must be 1 or more, since an empty cohort is readable as a draft but never certifiable, refused otherwise with `ERR_EMPTY_MEASURE_DENOMINATOR`. The period must be closed, meaning `period_end_utc` is at or before the current instant, refused otherwise with `ERR_MEASURE_PERIOD_NOT_CLOSED`. Every bundle activation belonging to the delivery cohort must be terminal, that is `CLOSED` or `RESCINDED`, refused otherwise with `ERR_BUNDLE_NOT_CLOSED`, and this is the last check evaluated on any request in the system. Certification stamps `certified_at` with the current instant and `certified_by_user_id` with the acting user and freezes the counts and rates permanently, so a delivery recorded inside the window afterwards changes nothing in that row. Snapshots are readable per unit, ordered by `period_start_utc` descending with `created_at` descending as the tie-break so that the live row for a window is the first entry that is not `SUPERSEDED`, and at the collection root ordered by `period_start_utc` ascending with `created_at` ascending; both forms filter on `status` and on `superseded_by_snapshot_id`, and the root form additionally on `unit_id`.

## 4. Endpoint Definitions

Every endpoint below is authenticated by the `X-User-ID` and `X-User-Role` headers described in section 2.1 and returns the envelope described in section 2.2. An endpoint that creates a new resource responds with 201 Created; an endpoint that modifies an existing resource, advances a state machine or reads data responds with 200 OK. Retirement of a bed responds with 200 OK and returns the updated resource, since the row is closed rather than erased. Error responses carry the status codes listed in section 5. Two paths sit outside that scheme: `GET /_health` is an unauthenticated liveness probe that answers 200 OK with the bare body `{"status": "ok"}` and no envelope at all, and a request to a path this document does not define is refused with `ERR_RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND` at HTTP 404 while a request using a method that path does not accept is refused with `ERR_METHOD_NOT_ALLOWED` at HTTP 405 — both in the standard error envelope, and neither identifier appearing in the catalogue of section 5 because neither is a domain rule.
