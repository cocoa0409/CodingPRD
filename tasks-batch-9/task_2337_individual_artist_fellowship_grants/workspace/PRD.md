# Artist Fellowship Grants API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

State arts councils award individual artist fellowships under a peculiar combination of pressures. The money is a legislative appropriation, so every cent committed must be traceable against a published budget and an administrative assessment. The judgement is peer judgement, so applications must reach panelists stripped of any identifying material, and a panelist who knows the applicant must be removed from that file without collapsing the panel. And because the decision denies far more artists than it funds, the whole procedure must be defensible afterwards: an applicant who believes the process — not the aesthetic verdict — went wrong is entitled to a remedy.

This document specifies the API that runs such a program end to end. It governs the construction of a **fellowship cycle** and its discipline sub-allocations; the registration of artists with their residency history, taxpayer status and program standing; the assembly of anonymous applications with work samples; the recruitment of discipline-expert, geographically distributed review panels and the management of their declared conflicts; criterion scoring across one or two deliberation rounds; the chair's ranked slate and the council board's rank-ordered disposition of it; the offer, acceptance, installment disbursement and tax reporting of each fellowship; the recipient's public-value reporting obligation; the appeal channel limited to procedural grounds; and a program evaluation that measures how concentrated the applicant pool was.

The API is mounted under `/grants`, with one additional test-driving capability mounted under `/_clock`. Everything the system decides — whether a residency is long enough, whether a panel may be chartered, how much an approved slot may be paid, whether an installment may leave the treasury — is derived from stored data and a logical clock, never from wall-clock time or operator discretion.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Council Identity and Role-Based Access Control

Every request other than the logical-clock advance must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, naming the acting user, and `X-User-Role`, naming the capacity in which they act. A request with a missing or empty `X-User-ID`, or with an `X-User-Role` that is missing or that names anything outside the six declared roles, is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401) — an unrecognized role is treated as a failure to establish identity, not as a permission problem. Once identity is established, the role must appear in the acting endpoint's authorized-role list; otherwise the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403). This role gate is evaluated before any body parsing and before any path lookup, so an unauthorized caller can never learn whether a resource exists or whether their payload was well-formed.

The six roles are `ProgramOfficer`, `Artist`, `Panelist`, `CouncilBoardMember`, `AppealsOfficer` and `FiscalOfficer`.

`ProgramOfficer` is council staff: they build cycles and discipline allocations, register artists, screen work samples for anonymity, record eligibility determinations, recruit and charter panels, open and close scoring rounds, screen conflicts, rescind awards and review final reports. When `X-User-Role` is `ProgramOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server records it where a transition demands an audit trail — as `eligibility_determined_by` on an application, `screened_by` on a work sample or a conflict declaration, `chartered_by` on a panel, `reviewed_by` on a final report — but never compares it to any entity's `id`.

`Artist` is the applicant and, once funded, the recipient. When `X-User-Role` is `Artist`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Artist` row; ownership checks on any resource that references an `Artist` compare the resource's artist reference to `X-User-ID`. An `X-User-ID` that resolves to no `Artist` row cannot act at all and is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`. An `Artist` caller acting on another artist's application, work sample, award, final report or appeal is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403); for an application the comparison runs through the application's `artist_id`, for a work sample and an appeal through the owning application, and for an award, an installment or a final report through the award's `artist_id`.

`Panelist` is a recruited reviewer. When `X-User-Role` is `Panelist`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Panelist` row; ownership checks compare a review's `panelist_id` to `X-User-ID`, and a panelist submitting another panelist's review is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. An `X-User-ID` that resolves to no `Panelist` row is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`. Panelists are additionally walled off from applicant identity: they may not read the artist register at all, and they receive `applicant_legal_name` and `applicant_region` as `null` on every application whose `anonymity_status` is anything other than `UNSEALED`. Only the panelist who is seated on a panel and carries `is_chair` set to true may transmit that panel's slate; any other panelist attempting it is rejected with `ERR_NOT_PANEL_CHAIR` (HTTP 403).

`CouncilBoardMember` disposes of slates and thereby commits the appropriation; `AppealsOfficer` screens and decides appeals; `FiscalOfficer` releases, pays and reverses installments and pulls tax reporting. For all three, as for `ProgramOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server records it — as `board_action_by` on a recommendation slot, `approved_by_user_id` on the resulting award, `screened_by` and `decided_by` on an appeal, `released_by` on an installment — but never resolves it against an entity `id`. Two of those recorded values are later compared against the acting user's `X-User-ID` to enforce segregation of duties, as described in §2.9.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination, Filtering and List Ordering

List endpoints accept two pagination query parameters. `limit` is the maximum number of rows returned; it defaults to 10, and any supplied value must be at least 1 and at most 100. `offset` is the number of rows skipped; it defaults to 0 and must not be negative. Out-of-band values are rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400) rather than silently clamped. Every list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more` inside the envelope's `meta` object, where `total_count` counts the rows matching the filters before pagination is applied.

Unless a specific collection says otherwise, list endpoints return rows sorted by `created_at` ascending. Five collections deviate, and their ordering is normative because downstream rules depend on it: the cycle collection is returned newest first, by `created_at` descending; work samples are ordered by `ordinal` ascending; recommendation slots by `rank` ascending and then `created_at` ascending; payment installments by `installment_number` ascending and then `created_at` ascending; and reviews by `created_at` ascending, which is exactly the tie-break order the score-trimming rule of §3.7 relies on.

Each collection also accepts equality filters, all of them optional and combinable. Cycles filter on `status`, `fiscal_year` and `posture`. Discipline categories filter on `cycle_id` and `code`. Artists filter on `region`, `standing` and `tin_status`. Applications filter on `cycle_id`, `discipline_category_id`, `artist_id`, `assigned_panel_id`, `status` and `anonymity_status`. Work samples filter on `application_id`, `status` and `medium`. Panels filter on `cycle_id`, `discipline_category_id` and `status`. Panelists filter on `panel_id`, `status`, `region` and `person_email`. Conflict declarations filter on `panelist_id`, `application_id`, `status` and `severity`. Reviews filter on `application_id`, `panelist_id`, `status` and `round_number`. Recommendation slots filter on `panel_id`, `application_id`, `cycle_id` and `status`. Awards filter on `application_id`, `artist_id`, `cycle_id`, `recommendation_slot_id`, `status`, `payment_status` and `reporting_status`. Payment installments filter on `award_id`, `replaces_installment_id` and `status`. Final reports filter on `award_id` and `status`. Appeals filter on `application_id`, `filed_by_artist_id`, `status` and `grounds_code`.

Several resources in this system are never created by a direct client request — recommendation slots are minted by the slate cascade, awards by board approval, and payment installments by award acceptance and by reversal. For those three, the filtered collection is the only discovery channel: a client learns the new identifiers by listing the collection filtered on the parent it just acted upon.

### 2.4. Common Resource Properties

Every resource in this system — fellowship cycles, discipline categories, artists, applications, work samples, panels, panelists, conflict declarations, reviews, recommendation slots, awards, payment installments, final reports and appeals — carries the same four system-managed properties:

- **`id`** (string): a server-minted UUID, with no type prefix and no embedded meaning. Clients never supply it.
- **`created_at`** (string): an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits, recording when the row was first created.
- **`updated_at`** (string): an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp in the same format, refreshed on every mutation of the row.
- **`version`** (integer): a counter that increments by one on every mutation of the row.

No entity in this system omits `updated_at` or `version`; a derived-field recomputation counts as a mutation, so a cycle whose `available_cents` changes because a distant award was declined has its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` incremented just as if an operator had edited it directly. This matters directly for the concurrency rules of §2.9: the cycle's `version` advances on cascades the client never issued.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock

Every temporal rule in this specification — submission windows, cycle closing, acceptance deadlines, installment due dates, reporting deadlines, appeal windows — is evaluated against a server-held **logical clock**, never against real wall-clock time. Before any client advances it, the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`; it never falls back to the host's real time. An administrative capability, available to callers in any of the six roles and exempt from the header requirements of §2.1, sets the clock to an arbitrary UTC instant supplied as `to_iso8601`. Its response reports the resulting `current_utc` together with three arrays naming everything the advance changed: `forfeited_award_ids`, `delinquent_award_ids` and `held_installment_ids`.

Advancing the clock runs an **operations checkpoint** that processes awards in `created_at` ascending order and applies three sweeps to each. First, an award still in `OFFERED` whose `acceptance_deadline_at` now lies in the past becomes `FORFEITED`: its application becomes `NOT_AWARDED`, its recommendation slot becomes `VOIDED`, the artist's `active_award_count` is decremented, and the owning cycle's `funded_total_cents`, `admin_assessment_rate`, `admin_assessment_cents`, `committed_cents`, `available_cents` and `posture` are all recomputed — the same fiscal unwinding a voluntary decline performs, so forfeiture and decline are exactly compensating. Second, an award in `ACCEPTED` or `ACTIVE` whose `grant_period_end` now lies in the past moves its `reporting_status` from `NOT_DUE` to `DUE`. Third, an award whose `reporting_status` is `DUE` and whose `final_report_due_at` now lies in the past with no final report on file moves to `DELINQUENT`, sets the artist's `standing` to `REPORT_DELINQUENT`, increments the artist's `delinquent_award_count`, and moves every `SCHEDULED` installment of that award to `HELD` with `hold_reason_code` set to `REPORT_DELINQUENT`.

### 2.6. Money, Scores and Rounding Discipline

All monetary amounts in this system are whole numbers of cents, stored and returned as integers; there are no fractional-cent fields anywhere. Wherever a monetary result is the product of an amount and a rate — the contingency reserve, the administrative assessment, per-installment tax withholding — the product is rounded to a whole cent using **round half up** (a value exactly on the half boundary rounds away from zero). Scores and rates are decimal numbers: each review's `weighted_score`, each application's `provisional_composite_score`, `final_composite_score` and `score_spread`, and each panel's `readiness_score` are carried to **two decimal places, round half up**. The program evaluation's `region_hhi` and `funded_rate` are carried to **four decimal places, round half up**, and its `geographic_distribution_score` to two.

Two rounding orders in this system are normative and must not be optimized away. First, aggregation always consumes already-rounded inputs: an application's composite score is the mean of the reviews' two-decimal `weighted_score` values, rounded again to two decimals — never the mean of the unrounded criterion sums. Second, an award's withholding total is the sum of the three per-installment withholding amounts, each rounded to a whole cent before summing — never the single rounded product of the whole gross and the rate. The two orders differ by a cent on realistic amounts, and the per-installment order is the correct one.

Ratios that feed a multiplicative score are capped before multiplication, not after, so surplus inputs cannot inflate a result: a panel with more seated members than it requires scores its seat component as exactly 1, and the same holds for its region component.

### 2.7. The Appropriation Ledger and Cycle Posture

A fellowship cycle is the fiscal container for everything beneath it, and it publishes its own financial position through six derived fields that are recomputed together whenever anything beneath them moves.

`funded_total_cents` is the sum of `gross_amount_cents` over the cycle's awards whose `status` is `OFFERED`, `ACCEPTED`, `ACTIVE` or `COMPLETED` — that is, over every award that has not been declined, rescinded or forfeited. That pre-assessment total is the sole basis for the administrative assessment the council retains. The rate is selected once from a three-band table: a basis of exactly zero selects a rate of 0, a basis of at most 2,000,000 cents selects 0.085, a basis above that but at most 8,000,000 cents selects 0.070, and a basis above 8,000,000 cents selects 0.055. The selected value is stored as `admin_assessment_rate`, and `admin_assessment_cents` is the basis multiplied by that rate, rounded half up to a whole cent. The rate is a flat average rate applied to the entire basis, not a marginal rate applied band by band, and — this is the trap worth naming explicitly — the band is **never** re-selected after the assessment is added. A cycle with a funded total of exactly 2,000,000 cents therefore sits in the first band, retains 170,000 cents and reports `committed_cents` of 2,170,000; re-selecting the band against that committed figure would wrongly drop the rate to 0.070, and that reading is forbidden.

`reserve_cents` is a contingency reserve withheld only while the cycle is still deciding: it equals five percent of `total_budget_cents`, rounded half up to a whole cent, while the cycle's `status` is `DRAFT`, `OPEN`, `CLOSED`, `PANEL_REVIEW` or `BOARD_REVIEW`, and it becomes 0 once the status reaches `AWARDED`, `CLOSED_OUT` or `CANCELLED`. `committed_cents` is `funded_total_cents` plus `admin_assessment_cents`. `available_cents` is `total_budget_cents` less `reserve_cents` and less `committed_cents`.

`posture` is the cycle's operational gate over its children, and it is decided by an **ordered first-match cascade** whose evaluation order is normative because a safety condition must always outrank a fiscal one. In order: if the cycle's `status` is `CANCELLED`, the posture is `FROZEN`; otherwise, if `unresolved_breach_count` is greater than zero, the posture is `FROZEN`; otherwise, if `available_cents` is less than `base_award_cents`, the posture is `RESTRICTED`; otherwise, if `blocking_conflict_count` is greater than zero, the posture is `RESTRICTED`; otherwise the posture is `OPEN`. The two counters feeding the cascade are themselves derived: `unresolved_breach_count` is the number of the cycle's applications whose `anonymity_status` is `BREACHED`, and `blocking_conflict_count` is the number of conflict declarations in status `BLOCKING` whose declaring panelist sits on a panel of this cycle.

A `FROZEN` posture blocks panel chartering, board action on slots, installment release and installment payment, each rejected with `ERR_CYCLE_POSTURE_FROZEN`. A `RESTRICTED` posture additionally blocks board approval of a slot, rejected with `ERR_CYCLE_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`. All six derived fields are computed at cycle creation, so a freshly created `DRAFT` cycle already reports its reserve, its availability and its posture, and they are recomputed after every cycle status change, every board action, every award acceptance, decline, rescission or forfeiture, every conflict screening, and every anonymity breach or redaction.

### 2.8. The Anonymous Review Protocol

Anonymity is tracked as a **second, independent state dimension** on the same application row. `status` records where the application stands in the review lifecycle; `anonymity_status` records whether the applicant's identity is still shielded, and it advances on its own triggers through `SEALED`, `BREACHED`, `REDACTED` and finally `UNSEALED`. The two dimensions never constrain each other's transitions directly — an application can be `UNDER_REVIEW` and `REDACTED` at the same time — but `anonymity_status` gates several operations, and the two are always reported together on the same resource.

Under the protocol, the only applicant handle a panelist ever sees is the `sealed_alias` minted at submission. The denormalized `applicant_legal_name` and `applicant_region` are stored on the application from the moment of submission, but they are served as `null` to any `Panelist` caller — on a single read and on every element of a list read alike — for as long as `anonymity_status` is not `UNSEALED`. Every other role sees the stored values at all times. A `Panelist` caller is also barred outright from the artist register, both the single read and the collection.

An unresolved breach is the most serious condition the system tracks: while any application of a cycle carries `anonymity_status` of `BREACHED`, the cycle's posture is `FROZEN`, no review may be submitted on that application, and no panel of that cycle may be chartered — the latter two rejected with `ERR_ANONYMITY_BREACH_UNRESOLVED`.

### 2.9. Segregation of Duties and Optimistic Concurrency Control

Two decisions in this program may not be made twice by the same person. The `FiscalOfficer` who releases an installment must have an `X-User-ID` different from the `approved_by_user_id` stored on that installment's award — the board member who committed the money may not also move it. The `AppealsOfficer` who decides an appeal must have an `X-User-ID` different from the `eligibility_determined_by` stored on the appealed application — the officer whose determination is under appeal may not rule on it. Either violation is rejected with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403), and the check runs after ownership but before any concurrency, state or business evaluation.

Two write operations carry optimistic concurrency control, and for both the version field is a **required** input, not an optional one.

A board action on a recommendation slot must carry `expected_cycle_version`, holding the `version` of the owning `FellowshipCycle` as the client last observed it. The server compares that value against the cycle's current `version` before any state, posture or fiscal evaluation. If the value does not match — because another board action, an award rescission, a decline, a forfeiture or a posture recomputation has advanced the cycle since the client read it — the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_CYCLE_VERSION` (HTTP 412), leaving the cycle, the slot and the budget untouched, and the stale caller never learns the fiscal outcome it would have produced. Every accepted board action increments the cycle's `version` by one, so a re-read is mandatory before the next action.

A slate submission must carry `expected_panel_version`, holding the `version` of the owning `Panel` as the client last observed it. The server compares that value against the panel's current `version` before it validates the ranked application list. A chair who read the panel before a round was reopened, before a panelist resigned or before a conflict was screened `BLOCKING` holds a stale value and is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PANEL_VERSION` (HTTP 412) with no recommendation slot created. An accepted slate submission increments the panel's `version` by one.

No other endpoint accepts a version field, and no other endpoint performs a concurrency check.

### 2.10. Rejection Precedence

When a request violates more than one rule, exactly one error is reported, and which one is normative. Checks are evaluated in this fixed order, and the first violation wins:

1. **Authentication** — headers present and the role recognized (`ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`).
2. **Role authorization** — the caller's role appears in the endpoint's list, and an entity-backed caller's `X-User-ID` resolves to a real `Artist` or `Panelist` row (`ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`).
3. **Request shape** — required fields present, correct JSON types, enum members inside their declared sets, integers inside their declared ranges, strings meeting their declared minimum lengths, datetimes in ISO 8601 UTC (`ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`).
4. **Path existence** — every identifier in the URL resolves to a row of the matching entity (the entity-specific 404).
5. **Body foreign-key existence** — every reference inside the request body resolves to a row, evaluated in the order the fields are declared for that endpoint, so a body with two bad references reports the first declared one (the entity-specific 404).
6. **Ownership** — `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`.
7. **Segregation of duties**, then the chair check — `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, then `ERR_NOT_PANEL_CHAIR`.
8. **Optimistic concurrency** — `ERR_STALE_CYCLE_VERSION`, `ERR_STALE_PANEL_VERSION`.
9. **Acceptance-deadline lapse on an award acceptance** — see the note below.
10. **Target-entity state** — the entity named in the path is in a state from which the requested transition is declared (the entity-specific `..._STATE_INVALID` code).
11. **Cross-entity state and cycle posture** — the owning cycle, panel, award or artist is in the state the operation requires, and the cycle's posture permits it.
12. **Business safety** — anonymity and conflict-management rules.
13. **Business temporal** — deadlines, windows and overlaps.
14. **Business constraint** — uniqueness, eligibility, quorum, slate completeness, appeal grounds.
15. **Business referential** — panel membership, slate membership, slot rank order, discipline panel capacity.
16. **Business fiscal** — allocation sufficiency (lowest priority of all).

Three orderings inside this ladder deserve to be called out because they decide which of two plausible codes a test will see. The acceptance-deadline check on an award acceptance deliberately outranks the generic target-state check, because the clock checkpoint will already have moved a lapsed offer to `FORFEITED`; the response therefore names the proximate cause, `ERR_ACCEPTANCE_DEADLINE_PASSED`, rather than the consequence. Accepting the same award twice while still inside the window is a different matter and reports the state error. Second, cycle posture outranks fiscal allocation, so a board approval that violates both the posture gate and the discipline remainder reports `ERR_CYCLE_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`, not `ERR_DISCIPLINE_ALLOCATION_EXHAUSTED`. Third, conflict safety outranks panel-membership referential integrity, so a recused panelist who tries to review an application belonging to some other panel reports `ERR_CONFLICT_BLOCKS_REVIEW`, not `ERR_PANELIST_NOT_ON_PANEL`.

Terminal states reject every trigger. Repeating a trigger that already succeeded is not idempotent — the second attempt is rejected with the target entity's state error.

### 2.11. Path Resolution and Reference Integrity

Path identifiers are resolved left to right, and the first identifier that names no existing row decides the response. Each entity has its own 404 code, and the mapping is fixed: `cycle_id` yields `ERR_CYCLE_NOT_FOUND`, `discipline_category_id` yields `ERR_DISCIPLINE_CATEGORY_NOT_FOUND`, `artist_id` yields `ERR_ARTIST_NOT_FOUND`, `application_id` yields `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND`, `work_sample_id` yields `ERR_WORK_SAMPLE_NOT_FOUND`, `panel_id` yields `ERR_PANEL_NOT_FOUND`, `panelist_id` yields `ERR_PANELIST_NOT_FOUND`, `conflict_declaration_id` yields `ERR_CONFLICT_NOT_FOUND`, `review_id` yields `ERR_REVIEW_NOT_FOUND`, `recommendation_slot_id` yields `ERR_SLOT_NOT_FOUND`, `award_id` yields `ERR_AWARD_NOT_FOUND`, `payment_installment_id` yields `ERR_INSTALLMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `final_report_id` yields `ERR_FINAL_REPORT_NOT_FOUND`, and `appeal_id` yields `ERR_APPEAL_NOT_FOUND`. The same codes serve references carried inside a request body, and body references are checked only after every path identifier has resolved.

All collections in this system are top-level: a work sample is addressed by its own identifier, not by a path that walks through its application. Parenthood is expressed through reference fields on the child row, and the server is responsible for confirming that a child's references are mutually consistent — an application's `discipline_category_id` must name a discipline category belonging to the cycle named by its `cycle_id`, and a panel's `discipline_category_id` must likewise belong to its `cycle_id`.

### 2.12. Cascade Discipline and Derived-Field Reconciliation

A single client request in this system routinely mutates four or five entities. Every such cascade is synchronous and complete before the response is returned; there is no deferred reconciliation and no eventual consistency. Each entity a cascade touches has its `updated_at` refreshed and its `version` incremented, including entities the client never named.

Where a cascade iterates over a set, the iteration order is normative because it determines which rows are processed and, in the fiscal cases, what the intermediate totals are. Applications are cascaded over in `created_at` ascending order — this governs the assignment of applications to a chartering panel, the withdrawal sweep when a cycle is cancelled, and the unsealing sweep when a cycle reaches `AWARDED`. Awards are cascaded over in `created_at` ascending order — this governs the clock checkpoint. Installments are cascaded over in `installment_number` ascending order within an award, and where a sweep spans several awards of one artist, awards are taken in `created_at` ascending order first. Board decisions are taken in `rank` ascending order and then `created_at` ascending order; entity identifiers are never used to order a business decision.

Derived fields are always recomputed from their sources rather than incrementally patched, so that a compensating operation restores the exact prior value. Declining, rescinding or forfeiting an award unwinds the cycle's fiscal fields to precisely the figures they held before the award existed; waiving a previously blocking conflict restores the affected application's review counts and provisional score to their exact pre-block values; and reversing a paid installment issues a replacement such that the award's `gross_amount_cents` still equals the sum of `gross_cents` over its installments in `SCHEDULED`, `HELD`, `RELEASED` and `PAID`.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up a Fellowship Cycle

A `ProgramOfficer` opens the program year by creating a **fellowship cycle**, which is born in `DRAFT`. The creation payload names the cycle publicly through `name`, ties it to a state fiscal year through `fiscal_year`, and fixes the submission window with `application_open_at` and `application_deadline_at` — the latter doing double duty as the date on which every applicant's residency duration is measured. `grant_period_start` and `grant_period_end` define the fellowship period that will later be copied onto every award of the cycle, and the payload is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` if the period end is not after the period start or if the deadline is not after the opening instant. Fiscally, `total_budget_cents` is the whole appropriation, `base_award_cents` the full stipend, and `min_prorated_award_cents` the floor below which a discipline remainder may not be awarded at all; each must be at least 1, and the prorated floor must not exceed the base award. Eligibility policy arrives as `residency_min_months`, the whole months of continuous state residency required, and `award_blackout_years`, the minimum fiscal-year gap between an artist's prior fellowship and this one.

Five further settings are optional and carry defaults. `proration_policy` defaults to `PRORATE` and may instead be `SKIP_TO_ALTERNATE`; it decides what happens when a discipline remainder falls short of a full stipend. `max_review_rounds` defaults to 2 and is the hard ceiling on how many scoring rounds a panel may open. `acceptance_window_days` defaults to 14 and is how long an offered award stays acceptable. `appeal_window_days` defaults to 30 and is how long after notification an appeal may be filed. `final_report_due_offset_days` defaults to 60 and is how long after the grant period ends the final narrative report is due.

Under the cycle sit **discipline categories**, each created by a `ProgramOfficer` with a `cycle_id` naming its cycle and a `code` drawn from `LITERARY_ARTS`, `VISUAL_ARTS`, `MEDIA_ARTS`, `PERFORMING_ARTS` and `TRADITIONAL_ARTS`; a code may appear at most once per cycle. `alias_prefix` is the short string that will lead every sealed alias in this discipline. `allocation_cents`, at least 1, is the sub-budget reserved to the discipline; whenever a category is added, the sum of `allocation_cents` across the cycle's categories must not exceed `total_budget_cents` less `reserve_cents`, and a violation is rejected with `ERR_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_BUDGET`. Submission requirements come next: `required_work_sample_count`, at least 1, is how many work samples an application must carry to be submittable, and `allowed_media` is a non-empty subset of `MANUSCRIPT_PAGES`, `AUDIO`, `VIDEO` and `IMAGE_SET`. `max_total_duration_seconds`, defaulting to 600 seconds — ten minutes of running time — is the inclusive ceiling on the summed duration of an application's time-based samples. Panel requirements are `required_panel_seats`, at least 3, and `required_regions`, a non-empty subset of `NORTH`, `CENTRAL`, `SOUTH`, `COASTAL` and `TRIBAL`. `max_concurrent_panels` defaults to 1 and caps how many panels of this discipline may hold overlapping deliberation windows. Each category also reports four bookkeeping figures that begin at zero: `committed_cents`, the sum of `gross_amount_cents` over awards on this discipline's applications whose status is `OFFERED`, `ACCEPTED`, `ACTIVE` or `COMPLETED`; `eligible_application_count`, the number of the discipline's applications whose status is `ELIGIBLE`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `SCORED`, `RECOMMENDED`, `NOT_RECOMMENDED`, `AWARDED` or `NOT_AWARDED`; `funded_application_count`, the number of the discipline's applications carrying an award in one of those same four award statuses; and `submitted_sequence_counter`, a monotonic counter that feeds alias minting.

The cycle advances through a status ladder, each step driven by a `ProgramOfficer` supplying the desired `to_status`. `DRAFT → OPEN` requires at least one discipline category to exist, failing which the request is rejected with `ERR_CYCLE_HAS_NO_DISCIPLINES`, and requires that the sum of the categories' `allocation_cents` be at most `total_budget_cents` less `reserve_cents`, failing which it is rejected with `ERR_ALLOCATION_EXCEEDS_BUDGET`; on success the reserve, availability and posture are recomputed. `OPEN → CLOSED` requires the logical clock to have reached `application_deadline_at`, and closing early is rejected with `ERR_DEADLINE_NOT_REACHED`; applications still sitting in `DRAFT` are left untouched but can no longer be submitted, because the submit window has closed with the cycle. `CLOSED → PANEL_REVIEW` requires that no application of the cycle remain in `SUBMITTED` — every submitted application must carry an eligibility determination — and a violation is rejected with `ERR_APPLICATION_STATE_INVALID`; the posture is recomputed. `PANEL_REVIEW → BOARD_REVIEW` requires every panel of the cycle to be in `DELIBERATING`, `SLATE_SUBMITTED` or `DISSOLVED`, failing which the request is rejected with `ERR_PANEL_STATE_INVALID`, and requires that no panel still have `reopen_required` set, failing which it is rejected with `ERR_SCORE_SPREAD_REQUIRES_ANOTHER_ROUND`; the posture is recomputed.

`BOARD_REVIEW → AWARDED` is the cycle's most consequential step. It requires that no recommendation slot of the cycle still be `PROPOSED`, so that the board has disposed of the whole slate; a pending slot is rejected with `ERR_SLOTS_PENDING_BOARD_ACTION`. On success three cascades fire together. Every application of the cycle whose `anonymity_status` is `SEALED` or `REDACTED` becomes `UNSEALED`, processed in application `created_at` ascending order, which makes `applicant_legal_name` and `applicant_region` visible to the `Panelist` role for the first time. `reserve_cents` becomes 0 — the contingency is released — and `available_cents` and `posture` are recomputed. And every application still in `SCORED` that carries no `BOARD_APPROVED` slot becomes `NOT_RECOMMENDED` with `notified_at` stamped from the logical clock.

`AWARDED → CLOSED_OUT` requires that every award of the cycle have reached `COMPLETED`, `DECLINED`, `RESCINDED` or `FORFEITED`, failing which it is rejected with `ERR_CYCLE_HAS_OPEN_AWARDS`, and that every appeal on the cycle's applications be `UPHELD` or `DENIED`, failing which it is rejected with `ERR_APPEAL_STATE_INVALID`; on success every panel of the cycle not already `DISSOLVED` becomes `DISSOLVED`, its seated panelists remaining `SEATED` but no further review being creatable. Cancellation is available from two states and has no preconditions. From `DRAFT`, `DRAFT → CANCELLED` simply forces the posture to `FROZEN`. From `OPEN`, `OPEN → CANCELLED` first sweeps every application of the cycle in `DRAFT` or `SUBMITTED` to `WITHDRAWN`, in application `created_at` ascending order, and then forces the posture to `FROZEN`. Both `CLOSED_OUT` and `CANCELLED` are terminal. Any `to_status` that is not declared from the cycle's current status is rejected with `ERR_CYCLE_STATE_INVALID`, and the same code is what a child operation receives when it requires a cycle status the cycle is not in.

### 3.2. The Artist Register and Program Standing

A `ProgramOfficer` registers each applicant as an **artist** before any application can exist, and the identifier the registration returns is the value that artist will supply as `X-User-ID` thereafter. The profile carries `legal_name`, an `email` that must be unique across artists, `residency_start_date` marking the instant continuous state residency began, `residency_county` as the county of record, and `region` drawn from `NORTH`, `CENTRAL`, `SOUTH`, `COASTAL` and `TRIBAL`. Two optional flags complete it: `is_enrolled_student` defaults to false, and degree-seeking enrollment makes an artist ineligible; `tin_status` defaults to `MISSING` and may be `ON_FILE` or `INVALID`, and anything other than `ON_FILE` triggers backup withholding on every installment. A `ProgramOfficer` may later update any of those seven fields — a taxpayer identification number that arrives after registration is the common case — but such an update never reaches backwards: an application's `residency_months_at_deadline`, `applicant_legal_name` and `applicant_region`, once frozen at submission, are immune to a subsequent profile edit.

The register also carries six derived or system-managed fields. `standing` begins at `GOOD_STANDING` and gates both submitting and accepting; it may become `REPORT_DELINQUENT` or `DEBARRED`. `last_award_fiscal_year` is the highest `fiscal_year` among the artist's recorded prior fellowships and is `null` when there are none; `last_award_period_end` is the latest grant-period end among them and is likewise `null` when there are none. `prior_award_count` counts recorded prior fellowships, both those entered historically and those this system itself issued, and begins at 0. `active_award_count` counts the artist's awards whose status is `OFFERED`, `ACCEPTED` or `ACTIVE`, and `delinquent_award_count` counts those whose `reporting_status` is `DELINQUENT`; both begin at 0. `debar_reason_code` is `null` until debarment and is then one of `FRAUD`, `REPORT_ABANDONED` or `FUNDS_MISUSE`.

Two administrative capabilities act on the register. Recording an out-of-system prior fellowship takes a `fiscal_year` and a `grant_period_end`; it increments `prior_award_count` by one, sets `last_award_fiscal_year` to the greater of its current value and the supplied fiscal year, and sets `last_award_period_end` to the later of its current value and the supplied period end. This is what makes the prior-award blackout and the grant-period cooling buffer of §3.8 bite on artists whose history predates the system. Debarment takes a `debar_reason_code`, sets `standing` to `DEBARRED`, and moves every `SCHEDULED` installment on every one of that artist's awards to `HELD` with `hold_reason_code` set to `ARTIST_DEBARRED`, processing awards in `created_at` ascending order and installments within each award in `installment_number` ascending order.

### 3.3. Assembling and Submitting an Application

An application begins as a draft, created either by the applicant themselves or by a `ProgramOfficer` acting on their behalf. The payload carries `cycle_id`, `discipline_category_id` — which must belong to that cycle — `artist_id`, and the two anonymous prose components the panel will read: `artist_statement` and `career_narrative`. An `Artist` caller may only name their own `artist_id` and is otherwise rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. The new row begins with `status` of `DRAFT` and `anonymity_status` of `SEALED`, `work_sample_count` and `valid_review_count` at 0, and `sealed_alias`, `submitted_at`, `notified_at`, `residency_months_at_deadline`, `blackout_gap_years`, `applicant_legal_name`, `applicant_region`, `assigned_panel_id`, `provisional_composite_score`, `final_composite_score` and `score_spread` all `null`, with `review_round_number` at 0.

**Work samples** are attached to a draft application by the applicant or a `ProgramOfficer`. Each sample names its `application_id`, a `medium` from `MANUSCRIPT_PAGES`, `AUDIO`, `VIDEO` and `IMAGE_SET` that must fall inside the discipline's `allowed_media`, and an `ordinal` of at least 1 that is unique within the application and fixes presentation order. `page_count` describes manuscript samples and defaults to `null`; `duration_seconds` gives running time for audio and video samples and defaults to 0. `contains_identifying_metadata` defaults to false and is the applicant's own declaration, which screening will confirm or refute. Each new sample begins in `PENDING_SCREEN` and increments the owning application's `work_sample_count`, which counts the application's samples in `PENDING_SCREEN`, `CLEARED` or `REDACTED`.

Submission is the applicant's own act and is the moment the eligibility inputs freeze. Six preconditions guard the `DRAFT → SUBMITTED` transition. The owning cycle must be `OPEN`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_STATE_INVALID`. The logical clock must be at or after `application_open_at`, otherwise `ERR_APPLICATION_WINDOW_NOT_OPEN`, and at or before `application_deadline_at`, otherwise `ERR_APPLICATION_DEADLINE_PASSED` — both boundaries inclusive. The artist's `standing` must be `GOOD_STANDING`, otherwise `ERR_ARTIST_NOT_IN_GOOD_STANDING`. The artist must have no other application in this cycle whose status lies outside `DRAFT` and `WITHDRAWN`, otherwise `ERR_DUPLICATE_APPLICATION`; one live application per artist per cycle, enforced at submission rather than at draft creation so that a withdrawn attempt can be replaced. And the work-sample package must satisfy the discipline: `work_sample_count` at least `required_work_sample_count`, every sample's `medium` inside `allowed_media`, and the summed `duration_seconds` of the `AUDIO` and `VIDEO` samples at or below `max_total_duration_seconds`, otherwise `ERR_WORK_SAMPLE_REQUIREMENTS_NOT_MET`. Note the ordering that follows from §2.10: a second submission attempted after the deadline reports the deadline error, not the duplicate error.

A successful submission does five things. `submitted_at` is stamped from the logical clock. The discipline's `submitted_sequence_counter` is incremented by one and the `sealed_alias` is then minted from that new value as the discipline's `alias_prefix`, a hyphen, the cycle's `fiscal_year`, a hyphen, and the counter written as four digits with leading zeros — the first literary-arts submission of fiscal year 2027 in a discipline whose prefix is LIT therefore receives the alias LIT-2027-0001. `residency_months_at_deadline` is frozen. `blackout_gap_years` is frozen as the cycle's `fiscal_year` less the artist's `last_award_fiscal_year`, or as `null` when the artist has no recorded prior award. And `applicant_legal_name` and `applicant_region` are denormalized from the artist row as of that instant; the latter is the basis of the geographic-distribution evaluation of §3.11. Submission does **not** touch the discipline's `eligible_application_count`, which counts only applications that have already received a determination.

Residency duration is measured in whole calendar months between the artist's `residency_start_date` and the UTC calendar date of the cycle's `application_deadline_at`, and the arithmetic is exact rather than approximate. Take the difference in years between the two dates, multiply by twelve, and add the difference in their month numbers. A month only counts once its day-of-month anniversary has been reached, so compare the deadline's day of month against the residency start's day of month, capped at the number of days in the deadline's month for the sake of start dates late in a long month; if the deadline's day of month falls short of that anniversary day, subtract one month. The result is then floored at zero, so a residency that has not yet begun measures 0 rather than a negative figure. With a deadline of 15 October 2026 and a residency minimum of 24 months, a residency beginning on 15 October 2024 measures exactly 24 months and passes, while one beginning a single day later measures 23 and fails.

Withdrawal is the applicant's escape hatch and is available from three states. From `DRAFT` it has no precondition. From `SUBMITTED` it requires the cycle to be `OPEN` or `CLOSED`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_STATE_INVALID`. From `ELIGIBLE` it requires `assigned_panel_id` to still be `null`, so an application already attached to a panel cannot be withdrawn and the attempt is rejected with `ERR_APPLICATION_STATE_INVALID`. Withdrawing from `DRAFT` or `SUBMITTED` rejects every work sample of the application still in `PENDING_SCREEN` or `FLAGGED`, moving them to `REJECTED`; withdrawing from `SUBMITTED` additionally recomputes the cycle's `unresolved_breach_count` and `posture`; and withdrawing from `ELIGIBLE` decrements the discipline's `eligible_application_count` by one. `WITHDRAWN` is terminal, as is `NOT_AWARDED`.

### 3.4. The Anonymity Screen and Breach Recovery

Before a panel ever sees a file, a `ProgramOfficer` screens each work sample by recording a `disposition`. The three dispositions are `CLEARED`, `FLAGGED_IDENTIFYING` and `REJECTED`, and all three require the owning application not to be `WITHDRAWN`, failing which the screening is rejected with `ERR_APPLICATION_STATE_INVALID`. Each stamps `screened_by` with the acting officer's `X-User-ID`, and a sample may only be screened once — a second attempt, or a redaction of a sample that is not `FLAGGED`, is rejected with `ERR_WORK_SAMPLE_STATE_INVALID`.

A `CLEARED` disposition moves the sample from `PENDING_SCREEN` to `CLEARED` and does nothing else. `REJECTED` moves it to `REJECTED` and decrements the application's `work_sample_count` by one. `FLAGGED_IDENTIFYING` moves the sample to `FLAGGED` and is the trigger for the breach cascade: the application's `anonymity_status` moves from `SEALED` to `BREACHED` (or from `REDACTED` back to `BREACHED`, when a later sample on an already-repaired application is flagged), every review of that application in `SUBMITTED` becomes `INVALIDATED` with `invalidation_reason` set to `ANONYMITY_BREACH`, the application's `valid_review_count`, `provisional_composite_score` and `score_spread` are recomputed from what remains, and the cycle's `unresolved_breach_count` is incremented and its `posture` recomputed — which, by the cascade of §2.7, means `FROZEN` for as long as the breach stands. `CLEARED` and `REJECTED` are terminal dispositions for a sample; `FLAGGED` is not.

Repair runs through redaction. A `ProgramOfficer` redacts a `FLAGGED` sample, which moves it to `REDACTED` and sets its `contains_identifying_metadata` to false. When and only when no `FLAGGED` sample remains anywhere on the application, the redaction bubbles up: the application's `anonymity_status` moves from `BREACHED` to `REDACTED`, the cycle's `unresolved_breach_count` is decremented, and the cycle's `posture` is recomputed — releasing the freeze if nothing else holds it. An application with two flagged samples therefore stays `BREACHED`, and the cycle stays frozen, until the second redaction lands. Reviews invalidated for `ANONYMITY_BREACH` are **not** restored by redaction; only a waived conflict restores reviews, as §3.6 describes. Both `SEALED` and `REDACTED` reach `UNSEALED` in the cycle-level cascade at `AWARDED`, and `UNSEALED` is terminal.

### 3.5. Eligibility Determination

Once the submission window has closed, a `ProgramOfficer` records a determination on each submitted application by supplying a `determination` of either `ELIGIBLE` or `INELIGIBLE`, with an `ineligibility_reason_code` where the determination is negative. Both branches require the owning cycle to be `CLOSED` or `PANEL_REVIEW`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_STATE_INVALID`, and both stamp `eligibility_determined_by` with the acting officer's `X-User-ID` — the value that will later bar that same officer from deciding this application's appeal.

An `ELIGIBLE` determination is only accepted when the server's own verdict agrees, and the verdict has three independent criteria. The frozen `residency_months_at_deadline` must be at least the cycle's `residency_min_months`. The frozen `blackout_gap_years` must either be `null`, meaning the artist has no recorded prior fellowship at all, or be at least the cycle's `award_blackout_years`. And the artist's `is_enrolled_student` must be false. If any of the three fails, the determination is rejected with `ERR_ELIGIBILITY_CRITERIA_NOT_MET`. On success the application moves `SUBMITTED → ELIGIBLE` and the discipline's `eligible_application_count` is incremented by one.

An `INELIGIBLE` determination is symmetric and just as strictly checked. The `ineligibility_reason_code` must be present, and its absence is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. The code must name a criterion that actually fails: `RESIDENCY_DURATION` requires `residency_months_at_deadline` to be strictly less than `residency_min_months`; `PRIOR_AWARD_BLACKOUT` requires `blackout_gap_years` to be non-null and strictly less than `award_blackout_years`; and `ENROLLED_STUDENT` requires `is_enrolled_student` to be true. An officer who cites a criterion the applicant in fact satisfies is rejected with `ERR_INELIGIBILITY_REASON_UNSUPPORTED`. On success the application moves `SUBMITTED → INELIGIBLE`, the `ineligibility_reason_code` is recorded, and `notified_at` is stamped from the logical clock — which opens the appeal window described in §3.11.

An `INELIGIBLE` application is not a dead end. An upheld appeal ordering the remedy `REINSTATE_ELIGIBILITY` moves it `INELIGIBLE → ELIGIBLE`, clears its `ineligibility_reason_code`, increments the discipline's `eligible_application_count`, and clears `assigned_panel_id` so that the next chartering picks it up.

### 3.6. Panel Recruitment, Conflict Management and Chartering

A **panel** is created by a `ProgramOfficer` in `FORMING`, naming its `cycle_id` and `discipline_category_id`, the `required_seats` it must fill — at least 3 — and the `required_regions` that must be represented among its seated members, a non-empty subset of the five council regions. Its `deliberation_window_start` and `deliberation_window_end` bound the period during which the panel occupies its panelists; the end must be strictly after the start, and the interval is **half-open**, including the start instant and excluding the end instant. At creation `round_number`, `seated_count`, `region_coverage_count`, `blocking_conflict_count` and `assigned_application_count` are all 0, `reopen_required` is false, `slate_submitted_at` and `chartered_by` are `null`, and the quorum and readiness figures are computed immediately.

`quorum_min_reviews` is the number of counted reviews each assigned application must accumulate before a round may close. It is the larger of 3 and sixty percent of `required_seats` rounded up to a whole number — a five-seat panel therefore needs 3, a six-seat panel needs 4, and an eight-seat panel needs 5. `quorum_met` is true when `seated_count` has reached `quorum_min_reviews`.

**Panelists** are nominated by a `ProgramOfficer` onto a forming panel. Each nomination carries the `panel_id`, a `person_email` that identifies the human stably across panels and is unique within a panel, a `full_name`, the `region` the panelist represents, and `expertise_codes`, a non-empty subset of the five discipline codes the panelist is credentialed in. `is_chair` defaults to false and at most one panelist per panel may carry it; the chair is the only panelist who may transmit the slate. A nomination begins in `NOMINATED` with `review_submitted_count` at 0, `recused_application_ids` an empty list and `seated_at` `null`.

Seating a nominated panelist requires the panel to still be `FORMING`, otherwise `ERR_PANEL_STATE_INVALID`, and requires at least one expertise code, the absence of which is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. It also enforces the double-booking rule: no other panelist with the same `person_email` may already be `SEATED` on another panel of the same cycle whose half-open deliberation window intersects this panel's window, and a collision is rejected with `ERR_PANELIST_WINDOW_CONFLICT`. Because the intervals are half-open, a panel that starts at the exact instant another ends is not a conflict. Seating stamps `seated_at` from the logical clock, moves the panelist `NOMINATED → SEATED`, increments the panel's `seated_count`, recomputes `region_coverage_count` as the number of distinct entries in the panel's `required_regions` that are covered by the regions of its seated panelists, and recomputes `quorum_min_reviews`, `quorum_met` and `readiness_score`. Seating the same panelist twice, or acting on one who has already resigned, is rejected with `ERR_PANELIST_STATE_INVALID`.

Resignation is available from `NOMINATED`, `SEATED` and `RECUSED`, and `RESIGNED` is terminal. From `NOMINATED` it has no precondition and no side effect. From `SEATED` and from `RECUSED` alike it requires the panel to be in `FORMING`, `CHARTERED`, `SCORING` or `DELIBERATING`, otherwise `ERR_PANEL_STATE_INVALID`. A seated panelist's resignation decrements the panel's `seated_count` by one, recomputes `region_coverage_count`, `quorum_min_reviews`, `quorum_met` and `readiness_score`, and invalidates every review that panelist authored in `SUBMITTED`, setting each `invalidation_reason` to `BLOCKING_CONFLICT` and recomputing each affected application's `valid_review_count` and `provisional_composite_score`. A recused panelist's resignation additionally decrements the panel's `blocking_conflict_count` by the number of that panelist's `BLOCKING` conflicts and recomputes the cycle's `blocking_conflict_count` and `posture`; and it too decrements `seated_count` by one, because recusal deliberately kept the seat — recusal is penalised through the conflict multiplier instead — so resignation is the only event that ever releases a seat.

A panelist declares a **conflict** with a single application, either personally or through a `ProgramOfficer`. The declaration names the `panelist_id` and `application_id` — the pair must be unique, and a repeat is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_CONFLICT` — a `relationship_type` from `TEACHER_STUDENT`, `IMMEDIATE_FAMILY`, `CO_AUTHOR`, `SAME_INSTITUTION`, `FINANCIAL_INTEREST` and `PRIOR_MENTOR`, and a written `disclosure_note`. `severity` is derived, never supplied: `TEACHER_STUDENT`, `IMMEDIATE_FAMILY` and `FINANCIAL_INTEREST` yield `MANDATORY_BLOCK`, and the remaining three yield `DISCRETIONARY`. The declaration begins in `DECLARED` with `screened_by` and `override_justification` `null`.

A `ProgramOfficer` screens a declaration with a `disposition` of either `BLOCKING` or `WAIVED`, and either way the panel must be in `FORMING`, `CHARTERED`, `SCORING`, `DELIBERATING` or `SLATE_SUBMITTED`, otherwise `ERR_PANEL_STATE_INVALID`; a second screening is rejected with `ERR_CONFLICT_STATE_INVALID`. A `WAIVED` screening requires `severity` to be `DISCRETIONARY`, and waiving a mandatory block is rejected with `ERR_MANDATORY_CONFLICT_NOT_WAIVABLE`; it also requires an `override_justification` of at least 20 characters, a shorter or absent one being rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. It stamps `screened_by` and has no further effect.

A `BLOCKING` screening is the widest cascade in the review phase, and every one of its six effects is required. `screened_by` is stamped. The panelist moves `SEATED → RECUSED` and the conflict's `application_id` is added to their `recused_application_ids`. Every review by that panelist on that application in `SUBMITTED` becomes `INVALIDATED` with `invalidation_reason` set to `BLOCKING_CONFLICT`. The application's `valid_review_count`, `provisional_composite_score` and `score_spread` are recomputed. The panel's `blocking_conflict_count`, `quorum_met` and `readiness_score` and the cycle's `blocking_conflict_count` and `posture` are all recomputed — which, since a positive blocking count forces `RESTRICTED` unless something worse applies, is what stops the board from approving further slots. And every recommendation slot for that application still in `PROPOSED` becomes `VOIDED`, with the owning panel's `reopen_required` set to true when that panel may still run another round.

Reversal is possible, but only for a discretionary conflict. A waive-override moves the declaration `BLOCKING → WAIVED` and requires `severity` to be `DISCRETIONARY` — a mandatory block is again rejected with `ERR_MANDATORY_CONFLICT_NOT_WAIVABLE` — an `override_justification` of at least 20 characters, and the panel to be in `CHARTERED`, `SCORING`, `DELIBERATING` or `SLATE_SUBMITTED`. Overriding a declaration that is not `BLOCKING` is rejected with `ERR_CONFLICT_STATE_INVALID`. The override is a true compensation: every review by that panelist on that application in `INVALIDATED` whose `invalidation_reason` is `BLOCKING_CONFLICT` returns to `SUBMITTED` with that reason cleared, the application's `valid_review_count`, `provisional_composite_score` and `score_spread` return to their exact pre-block values, the panel's `blocking_conflict_count` is decremented, the panelist moves `RECUSED → SEATED` provided no other `BLOCKING` conflict of theirs remains, and the panel's `readiness_score` and the cycle's `blocking_conflict_count` and `posture` are recomputed.

A panel's `readiness_score` is the composite gate on chartering, expressed on a 0-to-100 scale. Compute a seat ratio as `seated_count` divided by `required_seats`, capped at 1. Compute a region ratio as `region_coverage_count` divided by the number of entries in `required_regions`, also capped at 1. Compute a conflict multiplier as 1 less a penalty of 0.08 per blocking conflict, where that penalty is itself capped at 0.40 — so a panel carrying five or more blocking conflicts is penalised no more heavily than one carrying five. Apply a specialist bonus of 1.10 when at least one seated panelist carries `TRADITIONAL_ARTS` among their `expertise_codes`, and a bonus of 1.00 otherwise. Multiply 100 by the seat ratio, the region ratio, the conflict multiplier and the bonus, take the smaller of that product and 100, and round half up to two decimals. Worked out: five of five seats with full region coverage and no conflict scores 100.00 after the cap; three of five seats with full coverage scores 60.00, which fails the gate even though quorum is met; four of five with full coverage scores 80.00 and passes; that same four-of-five panel with one blocking conflict scores 73.60 and fails; and adding a `TRADITIONAL_ARTS` specialist to it lifts the score to 80.96 and passes.

Chartering is what attaches applications to a panel, and it is guarded by five preconditions. The cycle must be in `PANEL_REVIEW`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_STATE_INVALID`. The cycle's `posture` must not be `FROZEN`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_POSTURE_FROZEN`. The `readiness_score` must be at least 75.00, and at least one seated panelist must carry the panel's own discipline code among their `expertise_codes`; either failure is reported as `ERR_PANEL_NOT_READY`. And the discipline's concurrency ceiling must hold: among panels of the same `discipline_category_id` whose status is `CHARTERED`, `SCORING`, `DELIBERATING` or `SLATE_SUBMITTED`, the number whose half-open deliberation window intersects this panel's window must be strictly fewer than the discipline's `max_concurrent_panels`, and an excess is rejected with `ERR_DISCIPLINE_PANEL_CAPACITY`. A successful charter moves the panel `FORMING → CHARTERED`, stamps `chartered_by` with the acting officer's `X-User-ID`, and then sweeps the cycle in application `created_at` ascending order: every application of the cycle whose `discipline_category_id` matches the panel's, whose `status` is `ELIGIBLE`, whose `assigned_panel_id` is `null` and whose `anonymity_status` is `SEALED` or `REDACTED` moves `ELIGIBLE → UNDER_REVIEW` with `assigned_panel_id` set to this panel. An application whose anonymity is `BREACHED` is excluded from the sweep, and an attempt to charter over such an application is what `ERR_ANONYMITY_BREACH_UNRESOLVED` reports. `assigned_application_count` is set to the number of applications just assigned.

### 3.7. Scoring Rounds, Composite Scores and Deliberation

A chartered panel opens its first scoring round when a `ProgramOfficer` says so, moving `CHARTERED → SCORING`. Two preconditions apply, both reported as `ERR_PANEL_STATE_INVALID`: `assigned_application_count` must be at least 1, and `round_number` must be strictly below the cycle's `max_review_rounds`. The round opening increments `round_number` by one and resets `reopen_required` to false.

A seated panelist drafts a **review** of an assigned application, naming the `application_id` and their own `panelist_id` and supplying four criterion scores, each an integer from 0 to 10 inclusive: `artistic_excellence`, `work_sample_quality`, `career_impact` and `public_value`, plus optional `comments` that the applicant will be able to read once the file is unsealed. The draft's `round_number` is stamped from the panel's current `round_number`, its `status` begins at `DRAFT`, and `weighted_score`, `invalidation_reason` and `submitted_at` are `null`. Creation is refused in three ways: a panelist carrying a `BLOCKING` conflict on that application is rejected with `ERR_CONFLICT_BLOCKS_REVIEW`; a panelist who is not `SEATED` on the panel to which the application is assigned is rejected with `ERR_PANELIST_NOT_ON_PANEL`; and a second review by the same panelist on the same application in the same round is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_REVIEW`, the uniqueness being on the combination of application, panelist and round across reviews that are not `SUPERSEDED`. Per §2.10 the conflict check outranks the membership check.

Submitting a review is the author's own act, guarded by five preconditions. The panel must be in `SCORING` — a review that is perfectly legal in isolation is refused with `ERR_PANEL_STATE_INVALID` when its panel has not yet opened a round or has already closed one. The acting panelist must be the review's author, otherwise `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`. The application's `anonymity_status` must not be `BREACHED`, otherwise `ERR_ANONYMITY_BREACH_UNRESOLVED`. No conflict declaration for this panelist and this application may be in `BLOCKING`, otherwise `ERR_CONFLICT_BLOCKS_REVIEW`. And the review's own `round_number` must equal the panel's current `round_number`, otherwise `ERR_REVIEW_STATE_INVALID` — the same code a second submission attempt receives. Submission stamps `submitted_at` from the logical clock, computes `weighted_score`, increments the author's `review_submitted_count` by one, and recomputes the application's `valid_review_count` and `provisional_composite_score`.

`weighted_score` weights the four criteria unequally, because artistic merit dominates the council's rubric: forty percent on `artistic_excellence`, thirty percent on `work_sample_quality`, twenty percent on `career_impact` and ten percent on `public_value`. Multiply each criterion score by its weight, add the four products, and round half up to two decimal places; the weights sum to exactly one, so the result always lies between 0.00 and 10.00. Criterion scores of 9, 8, 7 and 6 in that order therefore give 8.00, and 7, 7, 6 and 5 give 6.60.

The **counted set** for an application is every review of that application whose `status` is `SUBMITTED` and whose `round_number` equals the assigned panel's current `round_number`, taken in `created_at` ascending order — the only sanctioned tie-break in the scoring rules. `valid_review_count` is the size of that set. When the set is empty, all three of `provisional_composite_score`, `final_composite_score` and `score_spread` are `null`.

Two composite variants are computed over that same set and stored in different fields, so their divergence is observable in a single response. The **provisional** variant is recomputed on every review lifecycle event while the panel is scoring — submission, invalidation, restoration, supersession — and is the plain mean of every counted review's `weighted_score`, rounded half up to two decimals. It never trims. The **final** variant is written once per round when the round closes, is exposed as `final_composite_score`, and is the figure the slate ranks on. It trims one highest and one lowest counted review before averaging, but only when the panel's `seated_count` is at least 5 **and** at least 3 reviews are counted; otherwise it is identical to the provisional figure. When trimming applies, the review to drop as the high one is the first in `created_at` ascending order among those holding the maximal `weighted_score`, and the review to drop as the low one is the first in that same order, excluding the already-dropped high review, among those holding the minimal `weighted_score`; the mean of the surviving reviews is then rounded half up to two decimals. `score_spread` is always measured over the whole counted set and never over the trimmed remainder: it is the maximal `weighted_score` less the minimal `weighted_score` among the counted reviews, rounded half up to two decimals.

The divergence is worth a worked case. A five-seat panel with five counted reviews scoring 8.00, 6.60, 7.30, 5.20 and 9.30 has a provisional composite of 7.28 — the five values sum to 36.40 — while the final composite trims 9.30 and 5.20, leaving 8.00, 6.60 and 7.30, which sum to 21.90 and average 7.30. Its `score_spread` is 4.10. By contrast a four-seat panel with three counted reviews of 8.00, 6.60 and 5.50 does not trim at all, because its `seated_count` is below 5, so provisional and final both read 6.70 and the spread is exactly 2.50.

Closing a round is a `ProgramOfficer` act that moves the panel `SCORING → DELIBERATING`. It requires the cycle to be in `PANEL_REVIEW`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_STATE_INVALID`, and requires every application assigned to the panel and still `UNDER_REVIEW` to have a `valid_review_count` of at least the panel's `quorum_min_reviews`; a shortfall on any one of them is rejected with `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_REVIEWS`, and reviews that are `DRAFT`, `INVALIDATED` or `SUPERSEDED` do not count towards it. On success each assigned application still `UNDER_REVIEW` receives its `final_composite_score` and `score_spread` from the final variant, with `review_round_number` set to the closing round. `reopen_required` is then set to true when at least one assigned application's `score_spread` is strictly greater than 2.50 **and** `round_number` is still below the cycle's `max_review_rounds`; otherwise it is set to false and every assigned `UNDER_REVIEW` application moves `UNDER_REVIEW → SCORED`. A spread of exactly 2.50 does not reopen a round; a spread of 4.10 does.

A panel that must run again returns `DELIBERATING → SCORING`. That requires `reopen_required` to be true and `round_number` to be strictly below `max_review_rounds`, either failure reported as `ERR_PANEL_STATE_INVALID`. Reopening increments `round_number` by one, resets `reopen_required` to false, and supersedes the past: every review on the panel's assigned applications in `SUBMITTED` or `INVALIDATED` whose `round_number` is below the new round becomes `SUPERSEDED`, after which each assigned application's `valid_review_count`, `provisional_composite_score` and `score_spread` are recomputed and read `null` where no counted review remains. `SUPERSEDED` is terminal for a review. The panel then scores afresh under the new `round_number`, and only reviews stamped with that round count.

### 3.8. The Slate, the Board's Disposition and the Award Offer

Once its deliberation is finished, the panel chair transmits the ranked funding recommendation. This is the only operation reserved to the chair, and it carries `ranked_application_ids` — the ordered list of applications, best first — together with `expected_panel_version`. Six preconditions guard it, and their order follows §2.10. The cycle must be in `BOARD_REVIEW`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_STATE_INVALID`. The acting panelist must be `SEATED` on this panel with `is_chair` true, otherwise `ERR_NOT_PANEL_CHAIR`. `expected_panel_version` must equal the panel's current `version`, otherwise `ERR_STALE_PANEL_VERSION` and no slot is created. `reopen_required` must be false, otherwise `ERR_SCORE_SPREAD_REQUIRES_ANOTHER_ROUND`. Every application assigned to this panel whose `status` is `SCORED` must appear exactly once in the list, and an omission or a repetition is rejected with `ERR_INCOMPLETE_SLATE`; a list containing literal duplicate entries is caught earlier still, as a shape error. Extraneous entries are deliberately **not** judged by the completeness rule: an application whose `assigned_panel_id` names a different panel is rejected with `ERR_APPLICATION_NOT_ON_PANEL`.

A successful transmission moves the panel `DELIBERATING → SLATE_SUBMITTED`, increments the panel's `version` by one, stamps `slate_submitted_at` from the logical clock, and mints one **recommendation slot** per listed application, in the order the chair listed them. Each slot begins in `PROPOSED` with its `panel_id`, its `application_id`, a `cycle_id` denormalized from the panel, a `rank` equal to its 1-based position in the list — unique per panel among slots that are not `VOIDED` — and `board_action_by`, `struck_reason_code` and `awarded_amount_cents` all `null`.

The council board then works each panel's slate strictly in rank order, supplying an `action` of either `APPROVE` or `STRIKE` together with `expected_cycle_version`, and a `struck_reason_code` of `BUDGET`, `PROCEDURAL_DEFECT` or `APPLICANT_INELIGIBLE` where the action is `STRIKE`. Both actions require the cycle to be in `BOARD_REVIEW`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_STATE_INVALID`, and require `expected_cycle_version` to equal the cycle's current `version`, otherwise `ERR_STALE_CYCLE_VERSION`. A board action on a slot that is not `PROPOSED` is rejected with `ERR_SLOT_STATE_INVALID`. Either accepted action increments the cycle's `version` by one and stamps `board_action_by` with the acting board member's `X-User-ID`.

A `STRIKE` needs its `struck_reason_code` — its absence is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` — and moves the slot `PROPOSED → BOARD_STRUCK`, records the reason, moves the application `SCORED → NOT_RECOMMENDED`, and stamps the application's `notified_at` from the logical clock, opening the appeal window.

An `APPROVE` carries the fiscal weight and adds four preconditions of its own. The cycle's `posture` must be `OPEN`: a `FROZEN` posture is rejected with `ERR_CYCLE_POSTURE_FROZEN` and a `RESTRICTED` one with `ERR_CYCLE_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`. Every recommendation slot of the same panel with a lower `rank` must already be `BOARD_APPROVED` or `BOARD_STRUCK`, so that the funding waterfall is deterministic; acting out of order is rejected with `ERR_SLOT_RANK_ORDER_VIOLATION`. The application must be in `SCORED`, otherwise `ERR_APPLICATION_STATE_INVALID`. And the artist must clear the grant-period cooling buffer: either the artist's `last_award_period_end` is `null`, or the cycle's `grant_period_start` falls at least 365 days after it, the boundary being inclusive so that a gap of exactly 365 days is permitted; a shorter gap is rejected with `ERR_AWARD_PERIOD_COOLING_VIOLATION`.

The amount an approved slot is worth comes from the **discipline funding waterfall**, which fills rank by rank out of the discipline's own allocation. Take the discipline's remaining allocation as its `allocation_cents` less its `committed_cents`. If that remainder is at least the cycle's `base_award_cents`, the award's `gross_amount_cents` is the full `base_award_cents` and `is_prorated` is false. Otherwise, if the remainder is at least the cycle's `min_prorated_award_cents` and the cycle's `proration_policy` is `PRORATE`, the `gross_amount_cents` is exactly the remainder and `is_prorated` is true. In every other case — a remainder below the prorated floor, or a policy of `SKIP_TO_ALTERNATE` — the approval is refused with `ERR_DISCIPLINE_ALLOCATION_EXHAUSTED`. There is no rounding anywhere in this rule; each branch yields an exact stored integer. A discipline holding 2,000,000 cents against a base award of 1,500,000 and a prorated floor of 500,000 under the `PRORATE` policy therefore funds its rank-1 slot in full at 1,500,000, funds its rank-2 slot at exactly the floor of 500,000 as a prorated award, and refuses its rank-3 slot outright; under `SKIP_TO_ALTERNATE` the rank-2 slot is refused instead of prorated. Note that a discipline's `committed_cents` accumulates gross amounts only — the cycle-level administrative assessment is held against the cycle appropriation, never against a discipline allocation.

An accepted approval fires the widest cascade in the program. The slot moves `PROPOSED → BOARD_APPROVED` with `awarded_amount_cents` recorded. The application moves `SCORED → RECOMMENDED` with `notified_at` stamped from the logical clock. An **award** is created carrying all three of its state dimensions at their initial values — `status` of `OFFERED`, `payment_status` of `UNPAID`, `reporting_status` of `NOT_DUE` — with `application_id`, `artist_id`, `cycle_id` and `recommendation_slot_id` all derived from the approved slot, `gross_amount_cents` and `is_prorated` from the waterfall, `grant_period_start` and `grant_period_end` copied from the cycle, `approved_by_user_id` set to the acting board member's `X-User-ID`, and `acceptance_deadline_at` set to the logical clock plus the cycle's `acceptance_window_days` days. Its `accept_agreement_version` is `null`, `tax_certification_signed` false, `final_report_due_at` and `rescind_reason_code` `null`, and its five ledger figures — `scheduled_withholding_cents`, `total_withholding_cents`, `disbursed_gross_cents`, `disbursed_net_cents` and `undisbursed_gross_cents` — all 0. The artist's `active_award_count` is incremented by one. The discipline's `committed_cents` and `funded_application_count` are updated. And the cycle's `funded_total_cents`, `admin_assessment_rate`, `admin_assessment_cents`, `committed_cents`, `available_cents` and `posture` are all recomputed by the rules of §2.7.

### 3.9. Acceptance, the Payment Schedule and Disbursement

An offered fellowship must be accepted by its recipient in person. Acceptance carries `accept_agreement_version`, which must equal exactly the string `FELLOWSHIP-AGREEMENT-2027.1` — any other value is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` — and `tax_certification_signed`, which must be true, a false value being rejected with `ERR_TAX_CERTIFICATION_REQUIRED` so that no schedule is generated for an incomplete agreement. The acting artist must be the award's own artist, otherwise `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`, and the logical clock must be at or before `acceptance_deadline_at`, otherwise `ERR_ACCEPTANCE_DEADLINE_PASSED`. The award moves `OFFERED → ACCEPTED`, the two agreement fields are recorded, `final_report_due_at` is set to `grant_period_end` plus the cycle's `final_report_due_offset_days` days, `undisbursed_gross_cents` is set to the full `gross_amount_cents`, the application moves `RECOMMENDED → AWARDED`, and the three-installment payment schedule is generated.

The **payment schedule** always has exactly three installments and always conserves both the gross and the withholding exactly. First fix the withholding rate: it is 0.2707 when the artist's `tin_status` is anything other than `ON_FILE` at this moment, and 0 when the status is `ON_FILE`; the rate that results is stored on each installment as `withholding_rate`. Next split the gross: installment one's `gross_cents` is forty percent of the award's `gross_amount_cents` rounded half up to a whole cent, installment two's is thirty-five percent rounded the same way, and installment three's is the award's `gross_amount_cents` less the first two — a subtraction, so the remainder absorbs any rounding drift and the three gross figures sum to the award gross exactly. Then, **for each installment separately**, multiply that installment's `gross_cents` by the rate and round half up to a whole cent to obtain its `withholding_cents`, and set its `net_cents` to its `gross_cents` less its `withholding_cents`. Each installment takes an `installment_number` of 1, 2 or 3 and a `scheduled_date` of the award's `grant_period_start` date plus an offset of 0, 120 and 240 days respectively. All three begin in `SCHEDULED` with `replaces_installment_id`, `released_by`, `released_at`, `paid_at`, `reversal_reason_code` and `hold_reason_code` `null`. Finally the award's `scheduled_withholding_cents` is set to the sum of the three rounded per-installment withholding amounts.

That per-installment rounding order is not incidental. On a gross of 1,500,000 cents with no taxpayer identification on file, the three gross figures are 600,000, 525,000 and 375,000; the three withholding figures are 162,420, 142,118 and 101,513, summing to 406,051; and the three nets are 437,580, 382,882 and 273,487, summing to 1,093,949. Rounding the whole gross against the rate just once would yield 406,050 — a one-cent divergence that the per-installment order is required to produce. With a `grant_period_start` of 1 January 2027 the three `scheduled_date` values are 1 January 2027, 1 May 2027 and 29 August 2027.

Money leaves the treasury in two steps, both reserved to a `FiscalOfficer`. **Release** moves an installment `SCHEDULED → RELEASED` and stamps `released_by` and `released_at`. It requires the cycle to be in `AWARDED`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_STATE_INVALID`; the cycle's `posture` not to be `FROZEN`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_POSTURE_FROZEN`; the award's `status` to be `ACCEPTED` or `ACTIVE`, otherwise `ERR_AWARD_STATE_INVALID`; the acting officer's `X-User-ID` to differ from the award's `approved_by_user_id`, otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`; the logical clock to be at or after the installment's `scheduled_date`; and every installment of the award with a lower `installment_number` to have already left `SCHEDULED` and `HELD` — that is, to be `PAID`, `RELEASED`, `VOIDED` or `REVERSED`. Either of the last two failures is reported as `ERR_INSTALLMENT_NOT_DUE`: installments are released in order and never early. Releasing something that is not `SCHEDULED` is rejected with `ERR_INSTALLMENT_STATE_INVALID`.

**Payment** moves an installment `RELEASED → PAID`, requires the cycle's `posture` not to be `FROZEN` and the award to be `ACCEPTED` or `ACTIVE`, and stamps `paid_at` from the logical clock — the instant that serves as the cash-basis tax-year key. It then re-derives the award's ledger from the installment rows: `disbursed_gross_cents` as the sum of `gross_cents` over installments that are `PAID`, `disbursed_net_cents` as the sum of `net_cents` over the same set, `total_withholding_cents` as the sum of `withholding_cents` over installments in `SCHEDULED`, `HELD`, `RELEASED` or `PAID`, and `undisbursed_gross_cents` as the sum of `gross_cents` over installments in `SCHEDULED`, `HELD` or `RELEASED`. The first payment also moves the award `ACCEPTED → ACTIVE`. The award's `payment_status` is re-derived on every installment write as its own independent dimension, and it moves in both directions. It goes from `UNPAID` to `PARTIALLY_PAID` when at least one but not all of the award's non-`VOIDED` installments are `PAID`. It goes from `PARTIALLY_PAID` to `FULLY_PAID` when every non-`VOIDED` installment is `PAID`, at which point `undisbursed_gross_cents` becomes 0. It goes back from `FULLY_PAID` to `PARTIALLY_PAID` when a reversal leaves at least one non-`VOIDED` installment no longer `PAID`. And it goes back from `PARTIALLY_PAID` to `UNPAID` when no installment of the award remains `PAID`, at which point `disbursed_gross_cents`, `disbursed_net_cents` and `total_withholding_cents` all return to 0.

**Reversal** handles a bank return, an overpayment or a fraud hold. It moves a `PAID` installment to `REVERSED`, requires a `reversal_reason_code` of `BANK_RETURN`, `OVERPAYMENT` or `FRAUD_HOLD` — its absence is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` — and requires the award to be `ACCEPTED` or `ACTIVE`. The reversed row **keeps** its `paid_at`, so the cash-basis tax year it fell into does not change. A replacement installment is then created in `SCHEDULED` with the same `installment_number`, `gross_cents`, `withholding_rate`, `withholding_cents` and `net_cents` as the reversed row, with `replaces_installment_id` pointing at the reversed row, and with `scheduled_date` set to the logical-clock date. The award's ledger figures and `payment_status` are re-derived, and the conservation identity holds: `gross_amount_cents` still equals the sum of `gross_cents` over the award's installments in `SCHEDULED`, `HELD`, `RELEASED` and `PAID`. `PAID`, `VOIDED` and `REVERSED` are terminal installment states; reversing something that is not `PAID` is rejected with `ERR_INSTALLMENT_STATE_INVALID`.

Holds interrupt the schedule without destroying it. A `SCHEDULED` installment moves to `HELD` — with `hold_reason_code` set to `REPORT_DELINQUENT` or `ARTIST_DEBARRED` — when the clock checkpoint finds its award's report overdue or when its artist is debarred. A `HELD` installment returns to `SCHEDULED`, with `hold_reason_code` cleared, when the reason is cleared and no other unresolved hold reason applies to the award's artist.

Three ways exist to end an award before completion. **Declining** is the recipient's own act, available only from `OFFERED` and only to the award's own artist: the award moves `OFFERED → DECLINED`, the application moves `RECOMMENDED → NOT_AWARDED` with `notified_at` re-stamped, the slot moves `BOARD_APPROVED → VOIDED` with its `awarded_amount_cents` reset to 0, the artist's `active_award_count` is decremented, the discipline's `committed_cents` and `funded_application_count` are decremented, and the cycle's `funded_total_cents`, `admin_assessment_rate`, `admin_assessment_cents`, `committed_cents`, `available_cents` and `posture` are recomputed. **Forfeiture** is the clock checkpoint's doing, described in §2.5, and performs exactly the same unwinding from `OFFERED → FORFEITED` — the two are compensating by design. **Rescission** is a `ProgramOfficer` act available from `OFFERED` and from `ACCEPTED`, requiring a `rescind_reason_code` of `APPEAL_REMEDY`, `INELIGIBILITY_DISCOVERED` or `APPLICANT_REQUEST` — its absence is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` — and, from `ACCEPTED`, requiring that no installment of the award be `RELEASED` or `PAID`, a violation being rejected with `ERR_AWARD_HAS_DISBURSEMENTS`: once money has left the council the award must be wound down through reversal and reporting, not rescission. Rescission from `ACCEPTED` additionally voids every installment in `SCHEDULED` or `HELD` and resets `scheduled_withholding_cents` and `undisbursed_gross_cents` to 0. In every rescission the application becomes `NOT_AWARDED` with `notified_at` re-stamped, the slot becomes `VOIDED`, the artist's `active_award_count` is decremented, and the discipline and cycle fiscal fields and posture are recomputed exactly as for a decline. Where the application was already `AWARDED` and the award was `ACCEPTED` or `ACTIVE`, the application moves `AWARDED → NOT_AWARDED`.

`COMPLETED`, `DECLINED`, `RESCINDED` and `FORFEITED` are the award's terminal states. An award reaches `ACTIVE → COMPLETED` only through the acceptance of its final report, and only when its `payment_status` is already `FULLY_PAID`; otherwise the completion is refused with `ERR_AWARD_STATE_INVALID`.

### 3.10. Public-Value Reporting, Tax Accounting and Close-Out

Every fellowship carries a public-value obligation, tracked by the award's third state dimension. `reporting_status` begins at `NOT_DUE`; the clock checkpoint moves it to `DUE` once `grant_period_end` has passed on an award that is `ACCEPTED` or `ACTIVE`, and on to `DELINQUENT` if `final_report_due_at` passes with nothing on file. Filing a report while the status is still `NOT_DUE` is rejected with `ERR_FINAL_REPORT_NOT_DUE` — a report cannot precede the end of the grant period.

The recipient files a **final report** naming its `award_id` and carrying four substantive fields: the `narrative` itself, the `public_value_statement` every recipient owes the council, an `audience_reached_count` of at least 0 recording the audience reached during the grant period, and an `expenditure_summary` accounting for how the stipend was spent. At most one report may exist per award, and a second attempt is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_FINAL_REPORT`; corrections travel through the revision loop, never through a second row. The new report begins in `SUBMITTED` with `revision_count` 0, `submitted_at` stamped from the logical clock, and `reviewed_by`, `revision_note` and `accepted_at` `null`. Filing moves the award's `reporting_status` from `DUE` or from `DELINQUENT` to `RECEIVED`; where the award had been `DELINQUENT`, filing also decrements the artist's `delinquent_award_count`, returns the artist's `standing` to `GOOD_STANDING` once that count reaches 0 and the artist is not `DEBARRED`, and returns every `HELD` installment of that award whose `hold_reason_code` is `REPORT_DELINQUENT` to `SCHEDULED`.

A `ProgramOfficer` reviews the report with a `decision` of either `ACCEPT` or `REQUEST_REVISION`. `ACCEPT` requires the award's `reporting_status` to be `RECEIVED`, otherwise `ERR_AWARD_STATE_INVALID`; it moves the report `SUBMITTED → ACCEPTED`, stamps `reviewed_by` and `accepted_at`, moves the award's `reporting_status` to `ACCEPTED`, and — when and only when the award's `payment_status` is `FULLY_PAID` — moves the award's `status` to `COMPLETED`. Completion is what finally writes the artist's award history: `active_award_count` is decremented, `prior_award_count` incremented, `last_award_fiscal_year` set to the cycle's `fiscal_year` and `last_award_period_end` set to the award's `grant_period_end`, which is what makes this fellowship count against the blackout and cooling rules of the next cycle. `REQUEST_REVISION` requires `revision_count` to be below 2 — a third revision request is refused with `ERR_MAX_REVISIONS_REACHED`, after which the report must be accepted or the award pursued as delinquent — and requires a `revision_note` of at least 20 characters, a shorter or absent one being rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`. It moves the report `SUBMITTED → REVISION_REQUESTED`, increments `revision_count` by one, and stamps `reviewed_by` and the `revision_note`. The recipient then resubmits, moving `REVISION_REQUESTED → SUBMITTED`, replacing `narrative`, `public_value_statement`, `audience_reached_count` and `expenditure_summary` wholesale and re-stamping `submitted_at`; only the award's own artist may resubmit, and reviewing a report that is not `SUBMITTED` or resubmitting one that is not `REVISION_REQUESTED` is rejected with `ERR_FINAL_REPORT_STATE_INVALID`. `ACCEPTED` is terminal for a report.

Tax reporting is a computed read over one award's installment ledger for one calendar year, available to the recipient, to council staff and to a `FiscalOfficer`. It takes an optional `tax_year`, defaulting to the calendar year of the logical clock, and an optional `basis`, defaulting to `ACCRUAL`. On the **accrual** basis — the council's obligation view — the rows counted are every installment of the award that is not `VOIDED` whose `scheduled_date` falls in the requested year, paid or not. On the **cash** basis — the recipient's constructive-receipt view — the rows counted are only installments that are `PAID` and whose `paid_at` is non-null and falls in the requested year; a scheduled-but-unpaid installment contributes nothing. Either way `reportable_gross_cents` is the sum of `gross_cents` over the counted rows, `reportable_withholding_cents` the sum of their `withholding_cents`, `reportable_net_cents` the gross figure less the withholding figure, and `installment_count` the number of rows counted; the response echoes the `award_id`, the resolved `tax_year` and the resolved `basis`. These are sums of already-rounded per-installment amounts and are never re-derived from the rate. Because a reversed installment keeps its `paid_at`, it stays in its original cash-basis year and only leaves that year's figures once its replacement is paid in a different one. Continuing the 1,500,000-cent example with all three installments scheduled during 2027 and installments one and two paid during 2027 while installment three is paid on 10 January 2028: the accrual view of 2027 reports 1,500,000 gross, 406,051 withholding, 1,093,949 net over 3 installments; the cash view of 2027 reports 1,125,000, 304,538, 820,462 over 2; the cash view of 2028 reports 375,000, 101,513, 273,487 over 1; and the accrual view of 2028 reports zeros over 0 installments.

### 3.11. Appeals on Procedural Grounds and Program Evaluation

An applicant who has been notified of an adverse outcome may **appeal**, but only on procedure. The filing names the `application_id`, cites a `grounds_code`, and carries the appellant's own `statement`; `filed_by_artist_id` is copied from the application rather than supplied, `status` begins at `FILED`, `filed_at` is stamped from the logical clock, and `remedy_code`, `screened_by` and `decided_by` begin `null`. The filing window is temporal: the application's `notified_at` must be set — the applicant must actually have been notified — and the logical clock must fall within the cycle's `appeal_window_days` of that instant, the window boundary itself being inclusive; a filing that is too early or too late is rejected with `ERR_APPEAL_WINDOW_CLOSED`. Only one live appeal may exist per application: a second filing while an existing appeal is anything other than `DENIED` is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_APPEAL`.

Six grounds codes may be cited. Five are procedural — `CONFLICT_NOT_MANAGED`, `ANONYMITY_BREACH`, `PANEL_COMPOSITION_DEFECT`, `SCORING_PROCEDURE_DEFECT` and `ELIGIBILITY_DETERMINATION_ERROR` — and the sixth, `SUBSTANTIVE_SCORE_DISAGREEMENT`, may be filed but can never be screened, because a disagreement with the panel's aesthetic judgement is not appealable. An `AppealsOfficer` screens a filed appeal for procedural jurisdiction, moving it `FILED → SCREENED` and stamping `screened_by`; an attempt to screen an appeal citing `SUBSTANTIVE_SCORE_DISAGREEMENT` is rejected with `ERR_NON_PROCEDURAL_APPEAL_GROUNDS`, and screening an appeal that is not `FILED` is rejected with `ERR_APPEAL_STATE_INVALID`.

Deciding an appeal takes a `decision` of `UPHOLD` or `DENY` plus a `remedy_code` where the decision is to uphold. Every decision path requires that the acting officer's `X-User-ID` differ from the appealed application's `eligibility_determined_by`, otherwise `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. A denial from `SCREENED` moves the appeal `SCREENED → DENIED`, stamps `decided_by`, and changes nothing about the application. There is exactly one path that skips screening: an appeal citing `SUBSTANTIVE_SCORE_DISAGREEMENT` may be denied directly from `FILED`, moving `FILED → DENIED` with `decided_by` stamped, `screened_by` left `null` and the application untouched. An appeal on any of the five procedural grounds that is decided before it has been screened is rejected with `ERR_APPEAL_STATE_INVALID`. `UPHELD` and `DENIED` are both terminal.

Upholding an appeal requires a `remedy_code` that is permitted for the cited `grounds_code`, and the mapping is exact: `ELIGIBILITY_DETERMINATION_ERROR` permits only `REINSTATE_ELIGIBILITY`; `CONFLICT_NOT_MANAGED`, `ANONYMITY_BREACH` and `SCORING_PROCEDURE_DEFECT` each permit only `RESCORE_BY_NEW_PANEL`; and `PANEL_COMPOSITION_DEFECT` permits either `RESCORE_BY_NEW_PANEL` or `REISSUE_SLATE`. A remedy outside the permitted set, or a missing one, is rejected with `ERR_REMEDY_NOT_PERMITTED_FOR_GROUNDS`. Upholding also requires the cycle to be in `PANEL_REVIEW` or `BOARD_REVIEW`, otherwise `ERR_CYCLE_STATE_INVALID`. Every upheld appeal moves `SCREENED → UPHELD`, stamps `decided_by` and records the `remedy_code`; each of the three remedies then runs its own distinct cascade.

`REINSTATE_ELIGIBILITY` moves the `INELIGIBLE` application to `ELIGIBLE`, clears its `ineligibility_reason_code`, clears its `assigned_panel_id` so a later charter can pick it up, and increments the discipline's `eligible_application_count` by one.

`RESCORE_BY_NEW_PANEL` unwinds the scoring. Every review of the application in `SUBMITTED` becomes `INVALIDATED` with `invalidation_reason` set to `APPEAL_REMEDY`; the application's `valid_review_count`, `provisional_composite_score`, `final_composite_score` and `score_spread` are recomputed, all reading `null` once no counted review remains; the application moves `SCORED → ELIGIBLE` with `assigned_panel_id` cleared; and the former panel's `assigned_application_count` is decremented. The former panel itself returns `DELIBERATING → SCORING` with its `round_number` incremented by one and its `reopen_required` reset to false — reached through this cascade rather than through an operator's round opening, and with the appellant application already detached. That panel return requires `round_number` to still be below the cycle's `max_review_rounds`, and a panel that has exhausted its rounds reports `ERR_SCORE_SPREAD_REQUIRES_ANOTHER_ROUND`.

`REISSUE_SLATE` reopens the board's decision. The application's recommendation slot, whether it sits in `VOIDED` or in `BOARD_STRUCK`, returns to `PROPOSED` with its `board_action_by` and `struck_reason_code` cleared, and the application returns `NOT_RECOMMENDED → SCORED`. This remedy requires the cycle to be in `BOARD_REVIEW`.

Program evaluation closes the loop. A computed read over a cycle, available to a `ProgramOfficer`, a `CouncilBoardMember` and an `AppealsOfficer`, measures how concentrated the applicant pool was and how the money landed. The **eligible pool** is every application of the cycle whose `status` is anything other than `DRAFT`, `WITHDRAWN` or `INELIGIBLE`, and `eligible_pool_size` is its size. When the pool is empty, `region_hhi`, `geographic_distribution_score` and `funded_rate` are all reported as `null`, with `region_breakdown` and `discipline_breakdown` empty — the evaluation never divides by zero. Otherwise, for each region appearing among the pool's `applicant_region` values, take that region's share as its applicant count divided by the pool size; `region_hhi` is the sum of the squares of those shares, a Herfindahl-Hirschman concentration index, rounded half up to four decimal places. `geographic_distribution_score` is then derived from the **rounded** index — the evaluation order is normative — as 125 multiplied by one less that rounded index, rounded half up to two decimal places. `funded_rate` is the number of the cycle's awards whose `status` is anything other than `DECLINED`, `RESCINDED` or `FORFEITED`, divided by the pool size, rounded half up to four decimal places. `region_breakdown` lists each region in ascending region-code order with its applicant count and its share, and `discipline_breakdown` lists each discipline in ascending `code` order with its `eligible_application_count`, its `funded_application_count` and its `committed_cents`. A pool of three applications from `NORTH`, `NORTH` and `SOUTH` gives shares of two-thirds and one-third, a raw index of 0.5555…, a `region_hhi` of 0.5556, and a `geographic_distribution_score` of 55.55; deriving the score from the unrounded index would give 55.56 instead, and that reading is wrong.
