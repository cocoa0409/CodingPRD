# Immigration Legal Services API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A nonprofit immigration legal services provider lives inside two hard constraints that pull against each other. On one side sits unbounded need: people arrive at the front door with asylum claims, removal proceedings, U visa petitions and naturalization applications, and a meaningful fraction of them are in detention with statutory clocks already running. On the other side sits a funded, countable capacity — a fixed number of cases the organization has money and staff to carry in a given grant period, part of which is deliberately walled off for detained clients who cannot wait. Every acceptance decision the program makes is simultaneously a legal judgment (does this person have relief available, and can we competently pursue it?) and an allocation judgment (does taking this case leave room for the person who walks in tomorrow?).

Layered on top is the authorization architecture peculiar to this sector. Only a small subset of the people doing the work are licensed attorneys. The rest are accredited representatives whose authority to appear is derived, not inherent: it flows from the organization's own recognition, through an individual accreditation limited to specific forums, and it evaporates the moment the recognition lapses. Pro bono volunteers and law graduates may never act alone. That means staffing is not a scheduling problem but a compliance problem, and it means a single administrative event — a lapsed recognition — must ripple deterministically through accreditations, assignments, matters, declarations and calendars without leaving a single case quietly unrepresented.

This document specifies the API that runs the program: intake screening with indigency and eligibility determination, urgency scoring, two-pool capacity allocation with a waitlist, the recognition and accreditation lifecycle, matter staffing with supervision requirements, evidence and declaration preparation with language-access guarantees, interpretation billing, hearing and biometrics calendaring with three distinct conflict geometries, detained-client facility access with expedited filing timelines, informed-decision documentation, and funder reporting under client confidentiality partitions. Two properties are treated as non-negotiable throughout: nothing in the system may silently lose a capacity slot or a caseload count, and no derived money figure may drift by a cent between two runs over the same data.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

Every collection in this API is mounted at the server root — there is no single owning aggregate, and resources such as clients, capacity cycles, representatives, matters and grant reports are top-level siblings that reference each other by identifier.

### 2.1. Actors, Identity and Role-Based Access Control

Every request must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role that principal is acting in. A request missing either header, or carrying a `X-User-Role` value that is not one of the six role names defined below, is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). This check runs before any identifier in the URL is looked up, so an unauthenticated request against a non-existent resource reports the authentication failure and never discloses whether the resource exists. When the role is recognized but is not among the roles permitted on the endpoint being called, the request is rejected with `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 403) — again before existence checks, so a caller who is not permitted on an endpoint learns nothing about the data behind it.

The six roles are `ProgramDirector`, `IntakeScreener`, `SupervisingAttorney`, `CaseRepresentative`, `GrantAnalyst`, and `ClientPortalUser`.

A `ProgramDirector` administers the program: the organization's recognition record, the capacity cycles that gate acceptance, the detention-facility registry, the representative roster, and the accreditation lifecycle. It holds the only two levers that can loosen a capacity freeze or advance the program's clock of record. When `X-User-Role` is `ProgramDirector`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity; the server may record it in an audit sense but never compares it to any entity's `id`. Because a `ProgramDirector` is not backed by a `Representative` row, it can never appear as an assignee, an interpreter, a declaration preparer or a supervisor, and therefore can never be either half of a segregation-of-duties pair.

A `GrantAnalyst` builds funder reports and the reimbursement rollup, and is read-only on case substance: it may never create, transition or staff a matter. When `X-User-Role` is `GrantAnalyst`, `X-User-ID` is likewise an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity, and is never compared to any entity's `id`.

Three roles are backed by the `Representative` roster. When `X-User-Role` is `IntakeScreener`, `SupervisingAttorney`, or `CaseRepresentative`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Representative`, and ownership checks on any resource that references a `Representative` compare that resource's representative-valued field to `X-User-ID`. This is how segregation of duties is enforced structurally: the identity recorded in `screener_representative_id` on a screening is compared against the identity presented at decision time, the identity behind a declaration's `preparer_assignment_id` is compared against the certifier, and no representative may approve or revoke its own accreditation. An `IntakeScreener` is front-door staff: it registers clients and runs the eligibility and urgency screen. A `SupervisingAttorney` is an authorized attorney who staffs matters, resolves supervision links, certifies declarations and countersigns records. A `CaseRepresentative` is any representative carrying a matter — staff attorney, accredited representative, pro bono volunteer or law graduate — doing the day-to-day evidence, declaration, interpretation, calendar, filing and hours work.

Finally, when `X-User-Role` is `ClientPortalUser`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Client`. Every read is filtered to rows whose `client_id` equals `X-User-ID`, and the role's writes are confined to the client's own acts: acknowledging an informed-decision record and withdrawing the client's own screening. A `ClientPortalUser` never sees another client's row and never sees internal capacity or grant aggregates.

Two further authorization checks apply to the entity-backed roles. Row-level scoping is enforced with `ERR_ACTOR_NOT_ON_CASE` (HTTP 403): a `CaseRepresentative` may act on a matter only when it holds a `CaseAssignment` on that matter whose status is `PROPOSED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`, and a `ClientPortalUser` may act only on rows whose `client_id` equals its own `Client` identifier. This runs after role membership, so a caller whose role is permitted on the endpoint but who is not attached to the target row still receives a 403 — with a distinct error id. Separately, a representative-backed actor whose backing `Representative` row does not have `status` equal to `ACTIVE` is rejected with `ERR_REPRESENTATIVE_INACTIVE` (HTTP 403): an `INACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` representative keeps its role name but loses every write authority, including on cases it still nominally holds.

When a two-person control is violated — deciding an intake the same identity screened, approving or revoking one's own accreditation, or certifying a declaration one prepared — the rejection is `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403).

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.


### 2.3. Pagination and List Ordering

Every list endpoint accepts two optional query parameters. `limit` controls page size: it defaults to 10, may not be lower than 1, and may not exceed 100. `offset` controls the starting position: it defaults to 0 and may not be negative. When a list endpoint returns, the `meta` object carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` (the number of rows matching the filter before pagination) and `has_more` (true when rows remain beyond the current page).

Unless a specific workflow states otherwise, list results are ordered by `created_at` ascending. Where the specification elsewhere speaks of "the earliest created row" — waitlist ranking, largest-remainder tie-breaks, supervisor tie-breaks — it means exactly this ordering, compared at millisecond resolution.

Filter parameters on list endpoints narrow the result set and combine conjunctively. They never change the ordering and never change the meaning of `total_count`, which always reflects the filtered set.

### 2.4. Common Resource Properties, Versioning and the Logical Clock

Every resource in this system carries a server-minted `id` in UUID form with no type prefix, and a `created_at` timestamp. Every resource also carries `updated_at` and an integer `version`. `created_at` and `updated_at` are ISO 8601 UTC timestamps rendered with exactly three fractional digits. `version` begins at 1 when the row is minted and increments by exactly 1 on every mutation of that row, including mutations the row suffers as a downstream consequence of a request aimed at a different resource. `updated_at` is refreshed on every such mutation as well. There are no entities in this domain that omit `updated_at` or `version`.

The `version` field is the observable trace of the cascade behaviour described throughout section 3. When a request touches five rows, five `version` counters advance, and a caller that had read one of those rows beforehand can tell that its view is stale. Sections 2.7 and 3 describe the three places where the server enforces that staleness check rather than leaving it to the caller.

All time-dependent behaviour in this system reads a **logical clock**, never the host wall clock. Deadline comparisons, accreditation expiry windows, facility visit lead times, waitlist expiry, posture overrides and every timestamp field the server writes all resolve against that logical clock. Before the clock has been explicitly set, it reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`; it must not fall back to real time. A `ProgramDirector` may move it to an explicit instant by supplying a `to_iso8601` value; doing so mutates no entity and fires no cascade, but every subsequent evaluation of `expiry_date` windows, calendar lead times, `waitlist_expires_at_utc` and `posture_override_until_utc` reads the new instant. Where this document says "the logical clock date", it means the UTC calendar date of that instant.

### 2.5. Numeric Precision and Rounding Discipline

Rounding in this domain is *half up* everywhere, and the number of decimal places is fixed per quantity rather than per endpoint.

Percentages of the federal poverty guideline (`fpl_percent`), urgency scores (`urgency_score`), and all hour quantities (`direct_hours_logged` and `supervision_hours_logged`, on both assignments and matters, and `weekly_capacity_hours`) carry two decimal places. Ratios that gate a decision — `utilization_ratio` on a capacity cycle and `readiness_index_weighted` on a matter — carry four decimal places. All money is held in whole cents as integers: `annual_household_income_cents`, `billable_cents`, `interpretation_cost_cents`, `base_reimbursable_cents`, `supervision_surcharge_cents`, `total_reimbursable_cents`, `allocated_line_total_cents` and `rounding_remainder_cents`.

Two consequences of this are load-bearing and are restated where they apply. First, **threshold comparisons use the rounded value, not the unrounded intermediate.** A utilization figure whose exact value is 0.79995 rounds to 0.8000 and therefore does trip the restricted posture; a weighted readiness figure that rounds to 0.7500 does clear the weighted gate. Second, **where a total is built from parts, the rounding point is pinned.** Interpretation cost is rounded once per session and then summed as exact integers; the reimbursement base is rounded once per matter and then summed; the supervision surcharge is rounded once from the already-summed base. Summing unrounded parts and rounding at the end produces different cents and is incorrect.

### 2.6. Path Resolution and Reference Integrity

Path parameters are resolved left to right, and identifier resolution precedes every business check. A URL segment that does not resolve to a row of the expected entity yields `ERR_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404) unless a more specific 404 applies to that identifier.

Several identifiers carry their own error ids because the caller needs to know which reference failed. A `matter_id`, whether it arrives in the path or in a request body, yields `ERR_MATTER_NOT_FOUND` when it does not resolve to a `CaseMatter`; this applies to creating a case assignment, an evidence item, a declaration, an interpretation session, a calendar event or an informed decision, and to every operation mounted under a matter. A `client_id` that does not resolve to a `Client` yields `ERR_CLIENT_NOT_FOUND` — this applies to creating an intake screening and to reading or patching a client. A `cycle_id` that does not resolve to a `ProgramCapacityCycle` yields `ERR_CYCLE_NOT_FOUND`, which guards intake-screening creation, grant-report creation, and reading a cycle. A non-null `facility_id` that does not resolve to a `DetentionFacility` yields `ERR_FACILITY_NOT_FOUND` on intake-screening creation, on attaching a facility to a matter, and on calendar-event creation; a null `facility_id` is legal for a non-detained client and skips the rule entirely. A `representative_id` that does not resolve yields `ERR_REPRESENTATIVE_NOT_FOUND`, and the same id is used for every field that names a representative — including `assigned_representative_id` on a calendar event, `interpreter_representative_id` on an interpretation session and `approved_by_representative_id` on an accreditation — on assignment creation, accreditation creation, calendar-event creation and declaration creation. A `recognition_id` that does not resolve, or the absence of any recognition row at all when a non-attorney representative or an accreditation needs one, yields `ERR_RECOGNITION_NOT_FOUND` on accreditation creation, accreditation renewal and assignment creation. A supplied `supervision_link_id` that does not resolve to a `CaseAssignment` row yields `ERR_SUPERVISION_LINK_NOT_FOUND` on assignment creation and on relinking supervision.

Once every identifier resolves, request shape is validated: required body fields must be present and non-null, and every enum-typed input value must be a member of its declared set. Failures here are `ERR_VALIDATION` (HTTP 422). The same id covers declared numeric bounds — a `weight_points` value at or below zero, a `reserved_detained_slots` value greater than `total_case_slots` — and intervals whose end instant is at or before their start instant.

Assignment references are additionally checked for matter agreement: a `preparer_assignment_id`, `reviewer_assignment_id`, `counseled_by_assignment_id` or `supervision_link_id` that resolves to an assignment belonging to a different matter is rejected with `ERR_ASSIGNMENT_MATTER_MISMATCH` (HTTP 409).

### 2.7. Rejection Ordering: Which Complaint the Server Reports

A single malformed request often violates several rules at once. The response is never a bag of complaints: exactly one error id is returned, and which one is fully determined by a fixed evaluation order. The server evaluates rule categories in this sequence, and **the first rule that fails determines the response — no later rule is evaluated.** Rules within the same tier can never both fail on the same request.

1. **Authentication and authorization.** Role resolvability (`ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`), then endpoint role membership (`ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED`), then row-level actor scoping (`ERR_ACTOR_NOT_ON_CASE`), then the acting representative's own employment status (`ERR_REPRESENTATIVE_INACTIVE`).
2. **Existence.** Generic path resolution (`ERR_NOT_FOUND`), then the entity-specific 404s in exactly this order: `ERR_MATTER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CLIENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REPRESENTATIVE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CYCLE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FACILITY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SUPERVISION_LINK_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_RECOGNITION_NOT_FOUND`. The entity-specific ids always take precedence over the generic one wherever they are defined, so a request naming both a dangling `matter_id` and a dangling `cycle_id` reports the matter.
3. **Shape.** Required-field presence and enum membership (`ERR_VALIDATION`). Deliberately *after* identifier resolution, so a request carrying both a bad enum value and a dangling identifier reports the 404.
4. **State.** Structural transition legality first (`ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`), then matter closure (`ERR_MATTER_CLOSED`), then intake acceptance state (`ERR_INTAKE_NOT_ACCEPTED`), then assignment activity (`ERR_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_ACTIVE`), then the two readiness gates (`ERR_MATTER_NOT_READY` before `ERR_BLOCKING_EVIDENCE_OUTSTANDING`), then recognition activity (`ERR_RECOGNITION_NOT_ACTIVE`), then report state (`ERR_REPORT_STATE_INVALID`).
5. **Business safety.** Supervision resolvability (`ERR_NO_ELIGIBLE_SUPERVISOR`), then explicit-link eligibility (`ERR_SUPERVISION_LINK_INELIGIBLE`), then forum authorization (`ERR_FORUM_NOT_AUTHORIZED`), then the supervisee cap (`ERR_SUPERVISEE_CAP_EXCEEDED`), then the caseload cap (`ERR_CASELOAD_CAP_EXCEEDED`), then lead uniqueness (`ERR_LEAD_ALREADY_ASSIGNED`), then declaration segregation of duties, then read-back completeness (`ERR_READBACK_NOT_COMPLETED`), then the calendar conflict scan, then facility lead time (`ERR_FACILITY_LEAD_TIME_NOT_MET`).
6. **Business fiscal.** Consent sufficiency (`ERR_CONSENT_SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT`), then slot availability (`ERR_NO_SLOT_AVAILABLE`), then capacity freeze (`ERR_CYCLE_CAPACITY_FROZEN`), then the indigency ceiling (`ERR_INDIGENCY_CEILING_EXCEEDED`), then the hours bound (`ERR_HOURS_OUT_OF_RANGE`), then the report period bound (`ERR_REPORT_PERIOD_INVALID`), then the stale-version guards of section 2.8.
7. **Post-write invariant assertion.** `ERR_INVARIANT_VIOLATION` (HTTP 500), described in section 2.9.

Four pairings deserve calling out explicitly, because each is a case where two plausible complaints compete and implementers routinely pick the wrong winner.

- A `ClientPortalUser` creating a case assignment against a `matter_id` that does not exist violates both role membership and matter existence. **Role membership wins:** the response is `ERR_ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED` with HTTP 403, and the server must not disclose whether the matter exists.
- Supplying a `supervision_link_id` that does not exist, on a matter whose `representation_status` is already `WITHDRAWN_FROM`, violates both link existence and matter closure. **Link existence wins:** `ERR_SUPERVISION_LINK_NOT_FOUND` with HTTP 404.
- Advancing a detained matter to `READY_TO_FILE` while its weighted readiness figure is below the gate *and* its lead representative's accreditation does not cover the matter's `forum` violates both the readiness gate and forum authorization. **The readiness gate wins:** `ERR_MATTER_NOT_READY` with HTTP 409.
- Activating an assignment for a representative already at its caseload ceiling, on a matter whose cycle posture is frozen, violates both the caseload cap and the freeze. **The caseload cap wins:** `ERR_CASELOAD_CAP_EXCEEDED` with HTTP 422.

### 2.8. Stale-Version Guards on Shared Parents

Three write operations mutate a child resource in a way that materially reinterprets a shared parent, and each therefore requires the caller to prove it has read the parent's current state. In each case the version token is a **required, non-optional** field of the request body, and a mismatch against the parent's current `version` is rejected before the write is attempted.

`POST /case-assignments` requires `expected_matter_version`, carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target `CaseMatter`. If it does not equal the matter's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_MATTER_VERSION` (HTTP 412). The reason this matters: activating a new lead assignment legitimately supersedes the previous lead, releasing it. A caller holding a stale view of the matter would otherwise displace staffing it never observed. On success the matter's `version` advances by one, so a second create holding the same token fails.

`POST /informed-decisions` also requires `expected_matter_version` against the same `CaseMatter.version`, and rejects a mismatch with `ERR_STALE_MATTER_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Here the hazard is supersession: a new decision of the same `decision_type` moves the previously documented decision to `SUPERSEDED`, so a stale writer would overwrite counseling it never read. On success the matter's `version` advances by one.

`POST /accreditations/{accreditation_id}/renew` requires `expected_recognition_version`, carrying the client's last observed `version` of the parent `OrganizationRecognition`. A mismatch is rejected with `ERR_STALE_RECOGNITION_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Renewal opens a new term against the recognition's current `authorized_forums`, so a stale view could put a round in flight for forums the organization is no longer recognized for. On success the recognition's `version` advances by one, so a second renewal attempt holding the same token fails.

These three are the complete set. No other endpoint accepts a version token, and no other endpoint may reject a request for staleness. The guards are evaluated **last** among the fiscal rules, so that every cheaper rejection is reported before the caller is told to refetch.

### 2.9. Conserved Quantities and Post-Write Assertions

Three identities must hold at the end of every request, and are asserted after the write completes. A failure indicates a server defect: the transaction is rolled back, nothing is persisted, and the response is `ERR_INVARIANT_VIOLATION` (HTTP 500). No partially applied cascade is ever left behind.

**Slot conservation.** For every capacity cycle, `general_slots_consumed` plus `detained_slots_consumed` plus `available_slot_count` equals `total_case_slots`, always. Furthermore, an acceptance followed by its compensating withdrawal of the same screening must restore the five-tuple of `general_slots_consumed`, `detained_slots_consumed`, `available_slot_count`, `utilization_ratio` and `capacity_posture` to exactly what it was before the acceptance — provided no waitlist promotion fired. When a promotion does fire, the consumed totals instead remain exactly as they stood immediately after the acceptance, because the released slot is re-consumed by the promoted screening in the same request.

**Caseload conservation.** Summed across all representatives, `active_case_load` equals the count of assignments whose `status` is `ACTIVE` and whose `role_on_case` is `LEAD` or `CO_COUNSEL`. Summed across all representatives, `supervisee_count` equals the count of assignments whose `status` is `ACTIVE` and whose `supervision_link_id` is non-null. An activation followed by its compensating release on the same assignment restores both counters, for every representative involved, to their pre-activation values.

**Grant allocation conservation.** On a generated report, `allocated_line_total_cents` equals `total_reimbursable_cents`; `total_reimbursable_cents` equals `base_reimbursable_cents` plus `supervision_surcharge_cents`; `suppressed_case_count` equals `cases_served_count` minus `consent_verified_count`; and the values in `outcomes_summary` sum to `cases_served_count`. Amending a report and regenerating it over an unchanged line set reproduces byte-identical money fields, because the allocation pass is fully deterministic.

### 2.10. Client Confidentiality Scopes

Every client carries a `confidentiality_consent_scope` describing how far client-identifying data may travel outside the program. It is one of `NONE`, `PROGRAM_ONLY`, `FUNDER_AGGREGATE`, or `FUNDER_IDENTIFIED`, and defaults to `PROGRAM_ONLY` at registration. The three non-`NONE` values are ordered — `PROGRAM_ONLY` is narrower than `FUNDER_AGGREGATE`, which is narrower than `FUNDER_IDENTIFIED` — while `NONE` sits below all three and is never sufficient for anything.

A scope of `NONE` blocks the recording of an informed decision outright, with `ERR_CONSENT_SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT` (HTTP 422). The same error id covers the two other consent-driven refusals: withdrawing representation without a documented withdrawal decision, and generating a funder report at an identified disclosure level over clients who have not consented to identified disclosure. Narrowing a client's scope after the fact is always permitted; it changes the suppression counts of the *next* report generation and leaves already-generated reports untouched.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Client Registration, Household Composition and the Indigency Test

Registration of a prospective client captures who they are and how the program will communicate with them. A `full_name` of between 1 and 160 characters, non-blank, is written as it will appear on filings. A `preferred_language_code` records the language the client is counseled and read back to, as a lowercase two-letter ISO 639-1 code, and a `country_of_birth_code` records country of birth as an uppercase two-letter ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code. A `date_of_birth` is supplied as a calendar date in `YYYY-MM-DD` form and must fall strictly before the logical clock date. An `a_number` — the client's alien registration number — is optional and defaults to absent; when present it must match a capital `A` followed by exactly nine digits, and it must be unique across all client rows. Registration is available to a `ProgramDirector` or an `IntakeScreener`, and returns HTTP 201 with the new client, whose `status` begins at `ACTIVE`, whose `open_matter_count` begins at 0, and whose `version` is 1.

Two household figures drive the financial half of eligibility. A `household_size` counts the persons in the client household and must be at least 1. An `annual_household_income_cents` records annual household income in whole cents and must be at least 0. From these the server derives `fpl_percent`, the client's income expressed as a percentage of the federal poverty guideline for a household of that size. The guideline is computed by taking a base of $15,080.00 for a single-person household and adding $5,320.00 for each additional household member — that is, the base plus the increment multiplied by one less than the household size. `fpl_percent` is the annual household income divided by that guideline figure, multiplied by 100, rounded half up to two decimal places. A client is **indigency-eligible** when the rounded `fpl_percent` is at most 200.00 percent; exactly 200.00 is inside the ceiling and 200.01 is outside it. Where an operation refuses service on financial grounds rather than merely recording a verdict — opening a screening, and deciding one — a `fpl_percent` above 200.00 on a case type that is not fee-exempt is rejected with `ERR_INDIGENCY_CEILING_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 422). Note that the comparison uses the *rounded* figure, so an exact quotient of 200.004 percent is eligible.

Language access is derived rather than declared: `interpreter_required` is true exactly when `preferred_language_code` is anything other than `en`. The registration and update payloads both accept an `interpreter_required` key for symmetry, but the server always recomputes the flag from the language code and never honours a contradicting value. The same holds for the client's `status`, which the update payload accepts but the server derives: it stays `ACTIVE` while the client has open matters and becomes `CLOSED` when `open_matter_count` falls to 0 after having been positive. That count itself tracks the number of the client's matters whose `representation_status` is anything other than `WITHDRAWN_FROM`.

Updating a client is available to a `ProgramDirector` or an `IntakeScreener` and may change `household_size`, `annual_household_income_cents`, `preferred_language_code`, `confidentiality_consent_scope`, `interpreter_required` or `status`. When either household figure changes, `fpl_percent` is recomputed immediately. Crucially, recomputation is forward-looking only: an already-decided screening's `legal_eligibility_verdict` is never retroactively rewritten by a later income correction. Every successful update advances the client's `version` by 1.

Clients may be listed with optional filters on `status`, `preferred_language_code` and `confidentiality_consent_scope`, and read individually — the single read exposes the computed `fpl_percent` and `open_matter_count`. A `ClientPortalUser` may read only its own row.

### 3.2. Capacity Cycles, the Two Slot Pools and Posture

The program commits to a finite number of cases per funding period, and a capacity cycle is the object that holds that commitment. A `ProgramDirector` creates one with a `cycle_label` between 1 and 60 characters that is unique across all cycles, a `period_start_date` and a `period_end_date` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form where the end date falls strictly after the start date, a `total_case_slots` of at least 1, and a `reserved_detained_slots` of at least 0 that must not exceed `total_case_slots`. Creation returns HTTP 201. A newly created cycle has `general_slots_consumed` and `detained_slots_consumed` at 0, `available_slot_count` equal to `total_case_slots`, `waitlisted_count` at 0, `utilization_ratio` at 0.0000, `capacity_posture` at `OPEN`, `posture_override_until_utc` absent, `checkpoint_epoch` at 1, and `last_checkpoint_utc` set to the logical clock.

The reservation splits the cycle into two pools that must be reasoned about separately. The **detained pool** holds `reserved_detained_slots` seats; its free count is the reservation minus `detained_slots_consumed`, floored at 0 so it never reports negative. The **general pool** holds everything not reserved — `total_case_slots` minus `reserved_detained_slots` — and its free count is that figure minus `general_slots_consumed`, likewise floored at 0. Both consumption counters count only matters whose `representation_status` is not `WITHDRAWN_FROM`; a withdrawn matter's slot is not consumed. The aggregate `available_slot_count` is `total_case_slots` minus `general_slots_consumed` minus `detained_slots_consumed`.

Utilization drives posture. `utilization_ratio` is the sum of the two consumption counters divided by `total_case_slots`, rounded half up to four decimal places. From the *rounded* ratio, the computed posture is `FROZEN` when the ratio is at least 0.9500, otherwise `RESTRICTED` when the ratio is at least 0.8000, otherwise `OPEN`. Both thresholds are inclusive lower bounds, so a ratio of exactly 0.9500 is frozen and exactly 0.8000 is restricted. Because the comparison consumes the rounded value, a true ratio of 0.94996 rounds to 0.9500 and does freeze the cycle.

A `ProgramDirector` may soften a freeze without changing the underlying arithmetic by setting a posture override. The override endpoint takes a `posture_override_until_utc` timestamp and a mandatory `override_reason_code` recording why the director intervened, and stores the timestamp on the cycle. While the logical clock is strictly before that timestamp, a computed posture of `FROZEN` is reported and enforced as `RESTRICTED` instead. The override is strictly a downgrade: it never turns `OPEN` into `RESTRICTED`, never turns `RESTRICTED` into `FROZEN`, and has no effect on a cycle whose computed posture is `OPEN` or `RESTRICTED`. Once the logical clock reaches or passes the stored timestamp the override stops applying and the computed `FROZEN` posture is reported again, with no further call required. Recomputation of `utilization_ratio` and `capacity_posture` happens on every acceptance, every withdrawal, every promotion, and every capacity-cycle update.

Updating a cycle is a `ProgramDirector` operation and may change `total_case_slots`, `reserved_detained_slots` or `cycle_label`. Enlarging `total_case_slots` can move a cycle from `FROZEN` back to `RESTRICTED` or `OPEN` in the same request, and doing so does not by itself promote anyone from the waitlist — promotion happens only through a withdrawal or a checkpoint sweep. Cycles may be listed with an optional filter on `capacity_posture`, and read individually; both reads are open to a `ProgramDirector`, a `GrantAnalyst`, a `SupervisingAttorney` and an `IntakeScreener`.

### 3.3. Screening: Legal Eligibility and the Two Urgency Geometries

A screening record is the program's assessment of one prospective case for one client against one cycle. An `IntakeScreener` or a `SupervisingAttorney` creates it in `DRAFT` with a `client_id`, a `cycle_id`, a `case_type` drawn from `ASYLUM_DEFENSIVE`, `ASYLUM_AFFIRMATIVE`, `U_VISA`, `VAWA_SELF_PETITION`, `SIJS`, `DACA_RENEWAL`, `NATURALIZATION`, `ADJUSTMENT_OF_STATUS`, `BOND_HEARING` or `REMOVAL_DEFENSE_OTHER`, and a set of case-posture flags. `detained` records whether the client is in immigration detention and defaults to false. A `facility_id` names the detention facility and is optional in general but **must** be present when `detained` is true; omitting it for a detained client is rejected with `ERR_FACILITY_REQUIRED_FOR_DETAINED` (HTTP 422). An optional `statutory_deadline_date` records a filing bar such as the asylum one-year deadline. Three further flags all default to false: `one_year_bar_applies`, `prior_removal_order`, and `unaccompanied_minor`. A newly created screening carries `legal_eligibility_verdict` of `PENDING`, no `urgency_score`, no `urgency_tier` and no `urgency_variant_applied`. Creation returns HTTP 201.

Screening a draft is the analytical step, available to an `IntakeScreener` or a `SupervisingAttorney`. It stamps `screener_representative_id` with the acting representative, sets `screened_at_utc` to the logical clock, moves `status` from `DRAFT` to `SCREENED`, and computes both the eligibility verdict and the urgency figures. A screening may be re-screened while it remains in `SCREENED`, which recomputes everything; it may not be screened once decided.

**The eligibility verdict** is resolved by testing four disqualifying clauses in a pinned order and taking the first that matches; if none matches the verdict is `ELIGIBLE`.

1. When `case_type` is `NATURALIZATION` and `prior_removal_order` is true, the verdict is `INELIGIBLE_NO_RELIEF`.
2. Otherwise, when `detained` is true and the named facility's `jurisdiction_code` is not one of `TX-ELP`, `AZ-ELO`, `CA-ADL`, `LA-JEN` or `GA-ATL`, the verdict is `INELIGIBLE_JURISDICTION`.
3. Otherwise, when the client is not indigency-eligible by the `fpl_percent` test of section 3.1 **and** `case_type` is not one of the fee-exempt types — `ASYLUM_DEFENSIVE`, `ASYLUM_AFFIRMATIVE`, `U_VISA`, `VAWA_SELF_PETITION`, `SIJS`, `BOND_HEARING` — the verdict is `INELIGIBLE_INCOME`.
4. Otherwise, when `case_type` is `ASYLUM_AFFIRMATIVE` and `one_year_bar_applies` is true and `unaccompanied_minor` is false, the verdict is `INELIGIBLE_NO_RELIEF`.

The ordering carries real consequences. A detained client outside the served jurisdictions who *also* has income above the ceiling on a non-exempt case type receives `INELIGIBLE_JURISDICTION`, not `INELIGIBLE_INCOME`, because clause 2 is tested first. An unaccompanied minor filing affirmatively past the one-year bar escapes clause 4 entirely and is `ELIGIBLE`. And a client above the income ceiling on a fee-exempt type such as `BOND_HEARING` passes clause 3 untouched.

**The urgency score** sums four contributions and caps the result. The base contribution comes from the case type: 42 points for `BOND_HEARING`, 40 for `ASYLUM_DEFENSIVE`, 38 for `REMOVAL_DEFENSE_OTHER`, 30 for `SIJS`, 26 for `ASYLUM_AFFIRMATIVE`, 24 for `VAWA_SELF_PETITION`, 22 for `U_VISA`, 14 for `DACA_RENEWAL`, 12 for `ADJUSTMENT_OF_STATUS`, and 8 for `NATURALIZATION`. Four flag bonuses are added when their conditions hold: 25 points when `detained` is true, 15 when `unaccompanied_minor` is true, 10 when `prior_removal_order` is true, and 8 when `one_year_bar_applies` is true. A fifth contribution, the horizon component, rewards imminence and is described below. The four contributions plus the horizon component are added, the sum is capped at 100.00, and the capped figure is rounded half up to two decimal places to yield `urgency_score`.

The horizon component depends on how many days separate the logical clock date from an anchor date, and it is here that the two geometries diverge. The server records which geometry it used in `urgency_variant_applied` and the anchor it chose in `urgency_anchor_date`.

- **The strict statutory geometry** applies when `detained` is false, and is recorded as `STRICT_STATUTORY`. Its only candidate anchor is `statutory_deadline_date`. Calendar events are ignored entirely, even when the client has an imminent hearing on another matter.
- **The calendar-inclusive geometry** applies when `detained` is true, and is recorded as `CALENDAR_INCLUSIVE`. Its anchor is the earlier of `statutory_deadline_date` and the earliest qualifying calendar event date across all of the client's matters. An event qualifies when its `status` is `SCHEDULED` or `CONFIRMED`, its `event_type` is `MASTER_CALENDAR_HEARING`, `INDIVIDUAL_HEARING`, `ASYLUM_INTERVIEW` or `BIOMETRICS`, and its matter's `filing_status` is neither `DECIDED` nor `CLOSED_NO_FILING`. Events of type `FILING_DEADLINE` never qualify as anchors. When either candidate is absent the other is used alone.

Given an anchor, the horizon in days is the anchor date minus the logical clock date. When there is no anchor at all the horizon component is 0 points. When the horizon is 0 days or negative — the anchor is today or already past — the component is its full ceiling of 30 points. Otherwise the component is 30 multiplied by one minus the ratio of horizon days to a span of 84 days, rounded half up to two decimal places, and floored at 0 so a horizon beyond 84 days contributes nothing. A horizon of exactly 84 days therefore contributes 0.00 points, a horizon of 42 days contributes 15.00, and a horizon of 21 days contributes 22.50.

The tier follows from the rounded score against three inclusive lower bounds: `EMERGENCY` at 85.00 and above, `URGENT` from 65.00 up to but not including 85.00, `ELEVATED` from 45.00 up to but not including 65.00, and `ROUTINE` below 45.00. A score of exactly 65.00 is `URGENT`, not `ELEVATED`. Because the cap is applied before rounding, a raw sum of 118 points and a raw sum of 100 points both produce a score of 100.00 and a tier of `EMERGENCY` — the tier cannot distinguish them, but `urgency_score` is nonetheless the capped value and never exceeds 100.00.

Screenings may be listed with optional filters on `client_id`, `cycle_id`, `screener_representative_id`, `decided_by_representative_id`, `facility_id`, `status` and `urgency_tier`, and read individually. A `ClientPortalUser` may read only screenings belonging to its own client row.

### 3.4. The Decision Fork, the Slot Waterfall and the Waitlist

Deciding a screened case is a four-way fork performed by a `SupervisingAttorney` or a `ProgramDirector`. The request carries a `decision` of `ACCEPT`, `WAITLIST`, `DECLINE` or `REFER_OUT`, a `decision_reason_code`, an optional `referral_partner_code`, and an optional `principal_matter_id`. Every branch records `decided_by_representative_id` as the acting representative and `decided_at_utc` as the logical clock, and every branch enforces segregation of duties: when the acting identifier equals the screening's `screener_representative_id`, the request is rejected with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). The person who assessed the case may not be the person who commits program resources to it. Deciding a screening that is not in `SCREENED` — with the single exception of promotion described below — is rejected with `ERR_INTAKE_NOT_ACCEPTED` (HTTP 409). The four branches diverge sharply, and the divergence is deliberate.

**The accept branch** is the only operation in the system that consumes a capacity slot. It requires `legal_eligibility_verdict` to be `ELIGIBLE`, requires `decision_reason_code` to be `ACCEPTED_ON_MERIT` or `ACCEPTED_DETAINED_PRIORITY`, and is subject to posture gating. When the cycle's effective posture is `FROZEN`, the accept is rejected with `ERR_CYCLE_CAPACITY_FROZEN` (HTTP 409) **unless** the screening has `detained` true and `urgency_tier` of `EMERGENCY`, which is the sole override. When the effective posture is `RESTRICTED`, the accept proceeds only when `urgency_tier` is `URGENT` or `EMERGENCY`; a `ROUTINE` or `ELEVATED` screening is refused. Remember that "effective posture" means the posture after any active override has downgraded a computed `FROZEN` to `RESTRICTED`, so an override converts an outright refusal into a tier test.

Slot resolution is a waterfall over an ordered pool list. For a detained screening the order is the detained reserved pool first, then the general pool; for a non-detained screening the list contains the general pool only. The server walks the list in order and takes the first pool whose free count is greater than 0, recording that pool on the new matter's `slot_pool_consumed`. If no pool in the list has a free seat, the accept is rejected with `ERR_NO_SLOT_AVAILABLE` (HTTP 409). Two consequences follow directly. A detained client can spill over into the general pool once the reservation is exhausted. A non-detained client can never touch the detained reservation, so a cycle with 10 total slots, 4 reserved and 6 general slots consumed refuses the next non-detained accept even though `available_slot_count` reports 4.

On success the accept creates exactly one case matter, copying `client_id`, `intake_id`, `cycle_id`, `facility_id`, `case_type` and `detained` from the screening, starting `representation_status` at `UNSTAFFED` and `filing_status` at `PRE_FILING`, and taking `principal_matter_id` from the request payload — absent when omitted. A supplied `principal_matter_id` links a derivative filing (a spouse's or child's petition) to its principal, and the named matter must belong to the same client as the accepted screening; a mismatch is rejected with `ERR_DERIVATIVE_CLIENT_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422), a check that runs *after* the acceptance-state gate, so a declined screening carrying a bad principal link still reports `ERR_INTAKE_NOT_ACCEPTED`. The new matter's `forum`, `expedited_deadline_date` and `effective_filing_deadline_date` are derived as described in section 3.9. The cycle's consumption counters, `available_slot_count`, `utilization_ratio` and `capacity_posture` are all recomputed, and the client's `open_matter_count` rises, restoring `status` to `ACTIVE` once it is at least 1. The screening, the cycle and the client each advance their `version` by 1. The created matter is not returned by the decide response — the response body is the updated screening — and is discoverable only by listing matters filtered on `intake_id`.

**The waitlist branch** requires `decision_reason_code` to be exactly `CAPACITY_FULL`. It assigns `waitlist_rank` as one more than the count of screenings of the same cycle already in `WAITLISTED`, making ranks 1-based and dense, and sets `waitlist_expires_at_utc` to the logical clock plus 30 days. It increments the cycle's `waitlisted_count`. It consumes **no** slot and leaves `capacity_posture` untouched — a crucial asymmetry against the accept branch, which is why a cycle can be `OPEN` and still have a long waitlist. The screening and the cycle each advance their `version` by 1.

**The decline branch** requires `decision_reason_code` to be one of `NO_LEGAL_RELIEF`, `INCOME_ABOVE_THRESHOLD`, `OUTSIDE_SERVICE_AREA` or `CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST`. It creates no matter, consumes no slot, touches no cycle field, and advances only the screening's `version`. **The refer-out branch** requires `decision_reason_code` to be one of `OUTSIDE_SERVICE_AREA`, `CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST` or `NO_LEGAL_RELIEF`, and additionally requires a non-null `referral_partner_code`; omitting the partner code is rejected with `ERR_VALIDATION` (HTTP 422). It too creates no matter and consumes no slot.

**Promotion** moves a screening from `WAITLISTED` to `ACCEPTED`. It happens in three ways: an explicit accept decision on a waitlisted row, the checkpoint sweep of section 3.10, or the slot-release hop of the withdrawal cascade. In every case a candidate is promotable only when all of the following hold: the logical clock is strictly before its `waitlist_expires_at_utc`; the recomputed `capacity_posture` is `OPEN` or `RESTRICTED`; when that posture is `RESTRICTED`, its `urgency_tier` is `URGENT` or `EMERGENCY`; and the slot waterfall resolves a pool with a free seat. Among promotable candidates of the cycle, the one with the lowest `waitlist_rank` is chosen, with earlier `created_at` breaking a tie. Promotion sets `decided_at_utc` to the logical clock and `decision_reason_code` to `ACCEPTED_ON_MERIT`, clears `waitlist_rank` and `waitlist_expires_at_utc`, creates a matter exactly as the accept branch does, recomputes the cycle's capacity fields, re-ranks the remaining waitlisted rows of the cycle by `created_at` ascending so ranks stay dense from 1, and recomputes the client's `open_matter_count`. The screening, the cycle and the client each advance their `version` by 1. Note that a row that is expired, or `ROUTINE` under a restricted posture, is simply skipped — the sweep moves on to the next candidate rather than stalling.

**Withdrawal** of a screening is available from `DRAFT`, `SCREENED`, `WAITLISTED` and `ACCEPTED`, to an `IntakeScreener` or a `ProgramDirector`; a `ClientPortalUser` may withdraw only a screening whose `client_id` matches its own identifier, and is otherwise refused with `ERR_ACTOR_NOT_ON_CASE` (HTTP 403). Every withdrawal sets `decision_reason_code` to `CLIENT_WITHDREW` and `decided_at_utc` to the logical clock. From `DRAFT` and from `SCREENED` nothing else happens. From `WAITLISTED` it clears `waitlist_rank` and `waitlist_expires_at_utc`, decrements the cycle's `waitlisted_count`, and re-ranks the remaining waitlisted rows by `created_at` ascending. From `ACCEPTED` it fires the four-hop compensation cascade of section 3.10, and is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` (HTTP 409) when the derived matter's `filing_status` is `FILED`, `DECIDED` or `APPEALED` — once a filing is on record with the adjudicator, the program cannot simply unwind the acceptance.

### 3.5. Recognition, Accreditation and Derived Authority

The program's right to appear before immigration forums flows from an organizational recognition, and each representative's right flows from that recognition. Neither is ever asserted directly; both are derived.

A `ProgramDirector` creates a recognition with a `recognition_number` between 1 and 40 characters that is unique across all recognitions, an `effective_date` and an `expiry_date` where the expiry falls strictly after the effective date, and an `authorized_forums` array that must be a non-empty subset of `USCIS`, `EOIR_IMMIGRATION_COURT`, `BIA` and `ASYLUM_OFFICE`. A duplicate `recognition_number` is rejected with `ERR_RECOGNITION_ALREADY_EXISTS` (HTTP 409). The new row starts at `status` of `ACTIVE` with `suspended_accreditation_count` at 0, no `conditional_reason_code` and no `lapsed_at_utc`; creation returns HTTP 201. Recognitions may be listed with an optional filter on `status`, and read individually by a `ProgramDirector`, a `SupervisingAttorney` or a `GrantAnalyst`.

Recognition transitions are driven by a single transition endpoint whose `action` is `CONDITION`, `REINSTATE`, `LAPSE` or `WITHDRAW`, with an optional `conditional_reason_code`. Conditioning an active recognition requires a `conditional_reason_code` of `ANNUAL_REPORT_LATE`, `STAFFING_DEFICIENCY` or `COMPLAINT_UNDER_REVIEW` and moves `status` to `CONDITIONAL` — a state in which accreditations continue to authorize, so no cascade fires, though every affected representative's `authorization_status` is re-derived. Reinstating serves two origins: from `CONDITIONAL` it clears `conditional_reason_code` and returns `status` to `ACTIVE`; from `LAPSED` it additionally clears `lapsed_at_utc` and partially undoes the lapse cascade. Either way it requires the logical clock date to be at or before `expiry_date`. Withdrawing is terminal and legal from `ACTIVE`, `CONDITIONAL` or `LAPSED`. Lapsing is the consequential one, and is described as cascade A in section 3.10.

A representative is registered by a `ProgramDirector` with a `full_name` between 1 and 120 characters, a `rep_type` of `STAFF_ATTORNEY`, `ACCREDITED_REP`, `PRO_BONO_VOLUNTEER` or `LAW_GRADUATE`, a non-empty `languages` array of lowercase two-letter ISO 639-1 codes, and three capacity settings: `max_case_load`, at least 0 and conventionally 25; `max_supervisees`, at least 0 and conventionally 10; and `weekly_capacity_hours`, carrying two decimal places, strictly greater than 0 and conventionally 40.00. An optional `bar_number` and an optional `facility_access_credential_expires_on` calendar date complete the payload. The `bar_number` obeys a conditional requirement in both directions: it must be non-null when `rep_type` is `STAFF_ATTORNEY` and must be null for every other `rep_type`. Violating either direction is rejected with `ERR_VALIDATION` (HTTP 422). Creation returns HTTP 201, with `active_case_load` and `supervisee_count` at 0 and `status` at `ACTIVE`. Updating a representative may change `status`, `max_case_load`, `max_supervisees`, `weekly_capacity_hours`, `languages` or `facility_access_credential_expires_on`; moving `status` to `INACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` does not retroactively release existing assignments, but it does immediately re-derive `authorization_status` and it blocks the representative from acquiring new ones.

Two representative fields are purely derived. `supervision_required` is true exactly when `rep_type` is `ACCREDITED_REP`, `PRO_BONO_VOLUNTEER` or `LAW_GRADUATE`, and false for a `STAFF_ATTORNEY`. `authorization_status` is resolved by taking the first matching clause of the following, so the ordering is normative:

1. When the representative's own `status` is `SUSPENDED`, the result is `AUTHORIZATION_SUSPENDED` regardless of accreditations.
2. When `rep_type` is `STAFF_ATTORNEY`, the result is `AUTHORIZED_ATTORNEY` when `status` is `ACTIVE` and `bar_number` is non-null, and `UNAUTHORIZED` otherwise.
3. When `rep_type` is `ACCREDITED_REP`: the result is `AUTHORIZED_ACCREDITED` when `status` is `ACTIVE` and the representative holds at least one qualifying accreditation — one whose own `status` is `APPROVED`, `EXPIRING` or `RENEWAL_PENDING`, whose parent recognition's `status` is `ACTIVE` or `CONDITIONAL`, and whose `expiry_date` is on or after the logical clock date. Otherwise, when the representative holds any accreditation in `SUSPENDED_BY_RECOGNITION`, the result is `AUTHORIZATION_SUSPENDED`. Otherwise the result is `UNAUTHORIZED`.
4. When `rep_type` is `PRO_BONO_VOLUNTEER` or `LAW_GRADUATE`, the result is `AUTHORIZED_SUPERVISED` when `status` is `ACTIVE` and `UNAUTHORIZED` otherwise — these types never reach `AUTHORIZED_ATTORNEY` or `AUTHORIZED_ACCREDITED` no matter what accreditations they hold.

Note carefully that clause 1 dominates: a suspended staff attorney with a valid bar number is `AUTHORIZATION_SUSPENDED`, not `AUTHORIZED_ATTORNEY`. And a recognition in `CONDITIONAL` still supports `AUTHORIZED_ACCREDITED`, whereas one in `LAPSED` or `WITHDRAWN` does not.

An accreditation binds one representative to one recognition. A `ProgramDirector` creates it with `representative_id`, `recognition_id`, an `accreditation_type` of `PARTIAL` or `FULL`, and a non-empty `requested_forums` subset of the four forums. The parent recognition must be in `ACTIVE` or `CONDITIONAL`; otherwise the create is rejected with `ERR_RECOGNITION_NOT_ACTIVE` (HTTP 409). A `rep_type` of `STAFF_ATTORNEY` cannot hold an accreditation at all, and the attempt is rejected with `ERR_ACCREDITATION_NOT_APPLICABLE` (HTTP 409) — a staff attorney's authority comes from the bar, not from accreditation. The new row begins at `status` of `PENDING`, with `authorized_forums` empty, `effective_date` and `expiry_date` absent, `renewal_round` at 0, and returns HTTP 201.

The forum grant is computed at approval and again at renewal, and it is a three-way intersection. The accreditation type sets a ceiling: a `PARTIAL` accreditation may reach at most `USCIS` and `ASYLUM_OFFICE`; a `FULL` accreditation may reach those two plus `EOIR_IMMIGRATION_COURT` and `BIA`. The granted `authorized_forums` is the intersection of `requested_forums`, the parent recognition's `authorized_forums`, and that ceiling, sorted alphabetically for stable output. When the intersection is empty the operation is rejected with `ERR_FORUM_GRANT_EMPTY` (HTTP 422) and no term is issued — for instance, a `PARTIAL` accreditation requesting only `BIA` can never be granted, because `BIA` lies above the partial ceiling. The `effective_date` is set to the logical clock date and the `expiry_date` to that date plus 1095 days. An accreditation is treated as expiring once the logical clock date falls within 90 days of its `expiry_date`.

Accreditation decisions run through a transition endpoint whose `action` is `APPROVE`, `DENY` or `REVOKE`, with an optional `decision_reason_code`. Approval is legal from `PENDING` and from `RENEWAL_PENDING`, which is what closes the approved-expiring-renewal-approved cycle. It stamps `approved_by_representative_id` with the acting supervising attorney — who must be a different person from the accredited representative, or the request is rejected with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403) — computes `authorized_forums`, `effective_date` and `expiry_date`, sets `status` to `APPROVED`, and when the origin was `RENEWAL_PENDING` also increments `renewal_round` by 1. Denial requires a `decision_reason_code` of `TRAINING_INCOMPLETE` or `BACKGROUND_ADVERSE` and is terminal. Revocation requires a `decision_reason_code` of `CONDUCT_VIOLATION` or `VOLUNTARY_SURRENDER`, is terminal, and reaches further than the accreditation itself: every assignment of that representative whose `status` is `PROPOSED` or `ACTIVE` moves to `RELEASED` with `release_reason_code` of `AUTHORIZATION_REVOKED`, and the affected matters fall into `COVERAGE_GAP`. Every action re-derives the representative's `authorization_status` and advances both the accreditation's and the representative's `version` by 1.

The remaining accreditation states are never reached by a caller. `EXPIRING` and `EXPIRED` are produced by the checkpoint sweep of section 3.10 as the term ages, and `SUSPENDED_BY_RECOGNITION` is produced and undone by the recognition cascade.

Renewal is a separate endpoint that opens a renewal round rather than completing one. It takes the `expected_recognition_version` token of section 2.8 together with a fresh `requested_forums` array, requires the accreditation to be `EXPIRING`, requires the parent recognition's `status` to be `ACTIVE` or `CONDITIONAL` — otherwise `ERR_RECOGNITION_NOT_ACTIVE` (HTTP 409) — and refuses a logical clock date already past the accreditation's `expiry_date` with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` (HTTP 409). On success it records the new `requested_forums` for the round and moves `status` to `RENEWAL_PENDING`. The grant itself is **not** computed here: it is computed at the subsequent approval, against the recognition's `authorized_forums` as they stand at that moment. A recognition narrowed between the renewal and the approval therefore narrows the renewed term, and the previous grant is never carried forward. Accreditations may be listed with optional filters on `representative_id`, `recognition_id`, `approved_by_representative_id`, `status` and `accreditation_type`, and read individually.

### 3.6. Staffing a Matter: Roles, Supervision Selection and Logged Hours

Staffing attaches representatives to a matter. A `SupervisingAttorney` or a `ProgramDirector` creates an assignment with a `matter_id`, a `representative_id`, a `role_on_case` of `LEAD`, `CO_COUNSEL`, `SUPERVISOR` or `INTERPRETER_COORDINATOR`, the mandatory `expected_matter_version` token of section 2.8, and an optional `supervision_link_id`. The new row begins at `status` of `PROPOSED`, with `assigned_at_utc` at the logical clock and both `direct_hours_logged` and `supervision_hours_logged` at 0.00; creation returns HTTP 201. A matter may carry at most one assignment with `role_on_case` of `LEAD` whose `status` is `PROPOSED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED`; a second one is rejected with `ERR_LEAD_ALREADY_ASSIGNED` (HTTP 409). Creation deliberately does **not** touch the representative's `active_case_load` — proposing a name costs nothing, and the counters move only on activation.

Supervision is resolved at creation time whenever the named representative has `supervision_required` true, and it is recorded in `supervision_source`. When the payload omits `supervision_link_id`, the server selects a supervisor itself and records `AUTO_SELECTED`; when the payload supplies one, the server validates it and records `EXPLICIT_LINK`; when the representative needs no supervision, the field stays `NOT_REQUIRED`.

Automatic selection filters candidate assignments to those on the **same matter** whose `status` is `ACTIVE` and whose `role_on_case` is `SUPERVISOR`, whose representative has `rep_type` of `STAFF_ATTORNEY`, own `status` of `ACTIVE` and `authorization_status` of `AUTHORIZED_ATTORNEY`, whose `supervisee_count` is strictly less than its `max_supervisees`, and whose `languages` array contains the client's `preferred_language_code`. Every clause is mandatory; a supervisor who is at capacity, or who does not speak the client's language, is not a candidate. Surviving candidates are ordered by a pinned four-key chain: ascending `supervisee_count` first; then descending remaining headroom, meaning `max_supervisees` minus `supervisee_count`; then ascending `active_case_load`; then ascending `created_at` as the final deterministic tie-break. The first candidate in that order is selected. When the candidate pool is empty, the create is rejected with `ERR_NO_ELIGIBLE_SUPERVISOR` (HTTP 422). The second key is not redundant with the first: two supervisors each carrying 2 supervisees but with ceilings of 10 and 4 tie on the first key, and the one with the ceiling of 10 wins on headroom. An explicitly supplied `supervision_link_id` is tested against the identical predicate; failing it yields `ERR_SUPERVISION_LINK_INELIGIBLE` (HTTP 422), while naming a link that does not exist at all yields `ERR_SUPERVISION_LINK_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404).

The assignment lifecycle runs through a transition endpoint whose `action` is `ACTIVATE`, `SUSPEND` or `RELEASE`, with an optional `release_reason_code`. Activation is legal from `PROPOSED` and from `SUSPENDED`, which makes the active-suspended-active path a genuine cycle whose re-entry re-checks every ceiling from scratch. Activation requires the representative's `status` to be `ACTIVE` and its `authorization_status` to be neither `AUTHORIZATION_SUSPENDED` nor `UNAUTHORIZED`; an inactive representative is refused with `ERR_REPRESENTATIVE_INACTIVE` (HTTP 403). For a `LEAD` or `CO_COUNSEL` role, `active_case_load` must be strictly less than `max_case_load`, otherwise `ERR_CASELOAD_CAP_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 422) — a representative with `max_case_load` of 25 and `active_case_load` of 25 is refused, while one at 24 succeeds and lands exactly at the ceiling. When the representative has `supervision_required` true, a resolvable supervision link is required, and the supervisor's `supervisee_count` must be strictly below its `max_supervisees`, otherwise `ERR_SUPERVISEE_CAP_EXCEEDED` (HTTP 422). Activating a `LEAD` on a matter whose `forum` is `EOIR_IMMIGRATION_COURT` or `BIA` additionally requires either an `AUTHORIZED_ATTORNEY` or a `FULL` accreditation whose `authorized_forums` contains that forum, otherwise `ERR_FORUM_NOT_AUTHORIZED` (HTTP 422) — a `PARTIAL` accreditation can never lead a court case.

Activation sets `activated_at_utc` to the logical clock, releases any other `LEAD` assignment of the matter with `release_reason_code` of `SUPERSEDED_BY_NEW_LEAD`, increments the representative's `active_case_load` and the supervisor's `supervisee_count`, and advances the matter's `representation_status`: to `STAFFED` when the activated lead needs no supervision, or to `SUPERVISED_STAFFED` when it does, clearing `coverage_gap_flag` in both cases. Suspension and release decrement exactly the counters activation incremented. Both require a `release_reason_code` when the actor is a `SupervisingAttorney`, drawn from `CLIENT_WITHDREW`, `MATTER_CLOSED`, `RECOGNITION_LAPSE`, `CASELOAD_REBALANCE`, `AUTHORIZATION_REVOKED`, `SUPERSEDED_BY_NEW_LEAD` or `VOLUNTEER_UNAVAILABLE`, and both stamp `released_at_utc` on release. Suspending or releasing the only `ACTIVE` `LEAD` moves the matter's `representation_status` to `COVERAGE_GAP` and sets `coverage_gap_flag` true; the only way back is to activate a replacement lead. Releasing a `SUPERVISOR` assignment is refused while any assignment whose `supervision_link_id` points at it still has `status` of `PROPOSED` or `ACTIVE` — the supervisees must be relinked or released first. Attempting a transition against an assignment whose `status` does not permit it is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` (HTTP 409); operations that require an active assignment refuse others with `ERR_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_ACTIVE` (HTTP 409). Each transition advances the assignment's, the representative's and the matter's `version` by 1.

Relinking supervision is the escape hatch that makes a supervisor assignment releasable. It takes a new `supervision_link_id`, requires the assignment's own `status` to be `ACTIVE`, validates the new link against the *full* selection predicate including the client-language clause, decrements the old supervisor's `supervisee_count` and increments the new one's, and sets `supervision_source` to `EXPLICIT_LINK`. The assignment's `version` and both representatives' `version` values each advance by 1.

Logging time is available to a `CaseRepresentative`, a `SupervisingAttorney` or a `ProgramDirector` against an active assignment. The request carries `hours` with two decimal places, a `hours_kind` of `DIRECT` or `SUPERVISION`, and a `service_date`. Hours at or below 0.00, or above 12.00, are rejected with `ERR_HOURS_OUT_OF_RANGE` (HTTP 422); exactly 12.00 is accepted. The kind must match the role: `SUPERVISION` hours are legal only against a `SUPERVISOR` assignment, and `DIRECT` hours only against a `LEAD`, `CO_COUNSEL` or `INTERPRETER_COORDINATOR` assignment. A mismatch in either direction is also `ERR_HOURS_OUT_OF_RANGE`. On success the value is added to the assignment's `direct_hours_logged` or `supervision_hours_logged`, and the matter's corresponding rollup is recomputed as the sum over all of its assignments. Rounding is half up to two decimal places at each accumulation and at each rollup — the intermediate rounding is normative, so 0.005 hours logged twice yields 0.02 on the assignment, not 0.01. Assignments that are later released continue to contribute to the matter's rollups: service already delivered is never un-reported, which is what makes the grant reimbursement of section 3.10 stable across staffing churn. The assignment's and the matter's `version` each advance by 1.

Assignments may be listed with optional filters on `matter_id`, `representative_id`, `supervision_link_id`, `role_on_case` and `status`, and read individually. Listing filtered on `matter_id` is the only way to discover the supervisor row that automatic selection chose. Representatives may be listed with optional filters on `rep_type`, `status`, `authorization_status` and `language_code` — the last matching against the `languages` array — and read individually — the single read exposes the derived `active_case_load`, `supervisee_count`, `supervision_required` and `authorization_status`.

### 3.7. Evidence, Declarations and Interpretation

**Evidence.** A `CaseRepresentative`, a `SupervisingAttorney` or a `ProgramDirector` adds an evidence item to a matter's checklist with a `matter_id`, an `item_type` of `IDENTITY_DOCUMENT`, `COUNTRY_CONDITIONS`, `MEDICAL_RECORD`, `POLICE_REPORT`, `WITNESS_STATEMENT`, `TAX_RECORD` or `CERTIFICATION_LETTER`, a `tier` of `REQUIRED`, `SUPPORTING` or `OPTIONAL`, a `weight_points` between 1 and 10 inclusive, an optional `source_language_code`, an optional `requires_translation`, and an optional `due_date`. A `weight_points` at or below 0 is rejected with `ERR_VALIDATION` (HTTP 422). The item begins at `status` of `REQUESTED` with `rejection_round` at 0 and no `received_at_utc` or `verified_at_utc`; creation returns HTTP 201. As with the client's interpreter flag, `requires_translation` is accepted in the payload but always derived: it is true exactly when `source_language_code` is anything other than `en`.

Evidence moves through a transition endpoint whose `action` is `RECEIVE`, `TRANSLATE`, `VERIFY`, `REJECT`, `REREQUEST` or `WAIVE`, with an optional `disposition_reason_code` and an optional `new_due_date`. Receiving stamps `received_at_utc` and requires the matter's `representation_status` to be `STAFFED` or `SUPERVISED_STAFFED` — an unstaffed matter cannot take custody of documents. Translating is legal only from `RECEIVED` and only when `requires_translation` is true; conversely, verifying directly from `RECEIVED` is legal only when `requires_translation` is false, so a foreign-language document must pass through `TRANSLATED` before it can be verified. Verifying stamps `verified_at_utc`. Rejecting requires a `disposition_reason_code` from `ILLEGIBLE`, `UNTRANSLATED`, `INCONSISTENT_WITH_DECLARATION`, `UNOBTAINABLE_COUNTRY_CONDITIONS`, `CLIENT_SAFETY_RISK` or `DUPLICATE`, increments `rejection_round` by 1, and leaves the row blocking. Re-requesting a rejected item requires a `new_due_date` at or after the logical clock date, writes it into `due_date`, clears `received_at_utc` and `disposition_reason_code`, and returns the row to `REQUESTED` — so the received-rejected-requested-received path is an unbounded cycle in which `rejection_round` is the only monotonic trace of how many times it turned. Waiving is legal from `REQUESTED`, `RECEIVED`, `TRANSLATED` and `REJECTED`, requires a `disposition_reason_code` of `UNOBTAINABLE_COUNTRY_CONDITIONS` or `CLIENT_SAFETY_RISK`, and is terminal. A rejection carrying `INCONSISTENT_WITH_DECLARATION` additionally blocks certification of the matter's declaration until the item leaves `REJECTED`. Every action recomputes the matter's readiness figures and advances both the item's and the matter's `version` by 1.

The readiness computation produces three values and is the source of the two filing gates. The counted set is every evidence item of the matter whose `status` is not `WAIVED` — a waived item leaves **both** the numerator and the denominator, so waiving a low-weight unverified item raises the index while waiving a high-weight verified item lowers it. `readiness_index_weighted` is the total `weight_points` of counted items whose `status` is `VERIFIED`, divided by the total `weight_points` of all counted items, rounded half up to four decimal places; when the denominator is 0 the index is exactly 0.0000 rather than an error. Separately, `readiness_ready_strict` is true when the matter has at least one `REQUIRED` item and no `REQUIRED` item whose `status` is outside `VERIFIED` and `WAIVED` — a matter with zero required items is never strictly ready. And `evidence_blocking_count` is the number of `REQUIRED` items not yet `VERIFIED` or `WAIVED`, plus the number of items in `REJECTED` at any tier, so a rejected supporting document raises the blocking count without affecting strict readiness. Evidence items may be listed with optional filters on `matter_id`, `tier`, `status` and `requires_translation`; there is no single-item read, so the list is the only read channel. Note that `item_type` is recorded on the row but is not a list filter.

**Declarations.** The client's sworn narrative is a declaration, created against a matter by a `CaseRepresentative`, a `SupervisingAttorney` or a `ProgramDirector` with a `matter_id`, a `preparer_assignment_id` naming the assignment that drafted it, a `language_code`, and an optional `word_count`. The preparer assignment must belong to the same matter, or the create is rejected with `ERR_ASSIGNMENT_MATTER_MISMATCH` (HTTP 409), and it must be `ACTIVE`, or `ERR_ASSIGNMENT_NOT_ACTIVE` (HTTP 409). A `word_count`, when supplied, must be at least 1. The row begins at `status` of `DRAFT` with `revision_round` at 0, `readback_completed` false, and no `reviewer_assignment_id` or `readback_session_id`; creation returns HTTP 201.

The declaration transition endpoint takes an `action` of `SUBMIT_FOR_REVIEW`, `REQUEST_REVISION`, `RESUME_DRAFT`, `CERTIFY` or `WITHDRAW`, with an optional `reviewer_assignment_id`, an optional `word_count` and an optional `revision_reason_code`.

Submitting for review moves `DRAFT` to `IN_REVIEW` and may be performed only by the preparer assignment's own representative. It also enforces language access: either the declaration's `language_code` equals the client's `preferred_language_code`, or the matter must already have a completed interpretation session whose `purpose` is `DECLARATION_READBACK` in the client's language. When neither holds the request is rejected with `ERR_READBACK_NOT_COMPLETED` (HTTP 409) — a narrative cannot go to review until it has been read back to the client in a language they understand. On success the server binds `readback_session_id` to the qualifying session with the latest `scheduled_start_utc`, breaking a tie by earlier `created_at`, and sets `readback_completed` true.

Requesting a revision is a `SupervisingAttorney` act, requires a `revision_reason_code` from `FACTUAL_GAP`, `LANGUAGE_MISMATCH`, `MISSING_READBACK`, `EVIDENCE_INCONSISTENCY` or `SUPERVISION_REQUIRED`, increments `revision_round` by 1, and moves the declaration to `REVISION_REQUESTED`. Resuming the draft from there is the preparer's act, requires a `word_count` in the payload, clears `revision_reason_code`, and returns the row to `DRAFT` — another unbounded cycle traced only by `revision_round`.

Certification is the gate that matters. It is legal only from `IN_REVIEW` and requires a `reviewer_assignment_id` pointing at an `ACTIVE` assignment on the same matter whose representative is **both** different from the preparer's representative **and** a `STAFF_ATTORNEY` whose `authorization_status` is `AUTHORIZED_ATTORNEY`; failing either half is `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403), because the same person may not draft and certify a sworn statement and a non-attorney may not certify one at all. Certification additionally requires `readback_completed` to be true whenever the client's `interpreter_required` is true, otherwise `ERR_READBACK_NOT_COMPLETED` (HTTP 409), and requires that no evidence item of the matter sits in `REJECTED` with `disposition_reason_code` of `INCONSISTENT_WITH_DECLARATION`. On success `certified_at_utc` is stamped, `status` becomes `CERTIFIED`, and both the declaration's and the matter's `version` advance by 1. Withdrawal is legal from `DRAFT`, `IN_REVIEW` and `REVISION_REQUESTED`, clears `readback_session_id` and `readback_completed`, and is terminal. Declarations may be listed with optional filters on `matter_id`, `preparer_assignment_id`, `reviewer_assignment_id`, `readback_session_id`, `status` and `language_code`; as with evidence, the list is the only read channel.

**Interpretation.** A session is booked against a matter with a `matter_id`, a `language_code`, an `interpreter_source` of `STAFF`, `VOLUNTEER`, `CONTRACT` or `TELEPHONIC`, a `purpose` of `INTAKE`, `DECLARATION_READBACK`, `RIGHTS_COUNSELING`, `HEARING_PREP` or `FACILITY_VISIT`, a `scheduled_start_utc`, a `scheduled_end_utc` strictly after it, and an optional `interpreter_representative_id`. The interpreter identifier obeys a two-directional conditional: it must be non-null when `interpreter_source` is `STAFF` or `VOLUNTEER`, and must be null when `interpreter_source` is `CONTRACT` or `TELEPHONIC`. Violating either direction is rejected with `ERR_INTERPRETER_SOURCE_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422). When an interpreter is named, that representative's `languages` array must contain the session's `language_code`, or the booking is rejected with `ERR_INTERPRETER_LANGUAGE_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422). The row begins at `status` of `SCHEDULED` with `billed_minutes` at 0 and `billable_cents` at 0; creation returns HTTP 201.

The session transition endpoint takes an `action` of `COMPLETE`, `MARK_NO_SHOW` or `CANCEL`, with an optional `actual_minutes` and an optional `closure_reason_code`. All three are terminal, and each requires a different position of the logical clock relative to the booked window. Completing requires `actual_minutes` of at least 1 and the logical clock at or after `scheduled_start_utc`, and computes the billing described next. Marking a no-show requires the logical clock at or after `scheduled_end_utc`. Cancelling requires the logical clock strictly before `scheduled_start_utc`. The consequence is worth stating plainly: a session whose window has begun but not ended can be completed, but can be neither no-showed nor cancelled. A no-show or a cancellation requires a `closure_reason_code` from `CLIENT_UNAVAILABLE`, `INTERPRETER_UNAVAILABLE`, `FACILITY_DENIED_ACCESS`, `HEARING_CONTINUED` or `LANGUAGE_MISMATCH`, leaves `billed_minutes` and `billable_cents` at 0, and leaves the matter's `interpretation_cost_cents` unchanged — only completed sessions ever bill.

Completion of a session whose `purpose` is `DECLARATION_READBACK` has a further reach: it sets `readback_session_id` and `readback_completed` true on every `IN_REVIEW` declaration of the matter that shares its `language_code`. Cancellation runs that backwards, clearing `readback_session_id` and setting `readback_completed` false on every declaration that had bound the cancelled session, which can retroactively block a certification that was previously reachable.

Billing runs in a fixed order. First, `billed_minutes` is the larger of a 30-minute minimum and `actual_minutes` rounded **up** to the next whole multiple of 15 minutes: 1 actual minute bills 30, 30 bills 30, 31 bills 45, 45 bills 45, and 46 bills 60. Second, the hourly rate follows `interpreter_source`: a `STAFF` or `VOLUNTEER` interpreter bills 0 cents per hour, a `CONTRACT` interpreter 9,500 cents per hour, and a `TELEPHONIC` interpreter 6,200 cents per hour. Third, a rare-language surcharge of 15 percent applies unless `language_code` is one of `es`, `fr`, `pt`, `zh` or `ar`, for which the surcharge is 0 percent. Fourth, `billable_cents` is the rate multiplied by `billed_minutes` divided by 60, multiplied by one plus the surcharge fraction, rounded half up to a whole number of cents. A staff or volunteer session therefore bills 0 cents no matter how long it ran or how rare the language. The matter's `interpretation_cost_cents` is the exact integer sum of the already-rounded `billable_cents` of its completed sessions — each session is rounded first and the rounded values are then summed, never the reverse. Sessions may be listed with optional filters on `matter_id`, `interpreter_representative_id`, `status`, `purpose` and `language_code`; the list is the only read channel.

### 3.8. Detention Facilities and the Three Calendar Geometries

A `ProgramDirector` registers a detention facility with a `name` between 1 and 120 characters that is unique across all facilities, a `jurisdiction_code` of 2 to 8 uppercase characters, a `visit_lead_time_hours` of at least 0 defaulting to 48, a `max_daily_visit_slots` of at least 0, an `expedited_filing_days` of at least 1 defaulting to 21, and an optional `access_credential_required` defaulting to true. The row is created at `status` of `OPEN` and returns HTTP 201; `status` may also be `RESTRICTED_ACCESS` or `CLOSED`, and it is settable only at creation. A `max_daily_visit_slots` of 0 is legal and makes every facility visit at that facility fail the aggregate geometry below no matter when it is booked. Facilities may be listed with optional filters on `jurisdiction_code` and `status`, and read individually.

An event is booked against a matter by a `CaseRepresentative`, a `SupervisingAttorney` or a `ProgramDirector` with a `matter_id`, an `event_type` of `BIOMETRICS`, `MASTER_CALENDAR_HEARING`, `INDIVIDUAL_HEARING`, `ASYLUM_INTERVIEW`, `FILING_DEADLINE` or `FACILITY_VISIT`, a `starts_at_utc`, an `ends_at_utc` strictly after it, a `location_code` of 2 to 12 uppercase characters, an optional `assigned_representative_id`, an optional `facility_id`, and an optional `client_conflict_waived` defaulting to false. A `facility_id` is required when and only when `event_type` is `FACILITY_VISIT`. An `assigned_representative_id` may be absent only for a `FILING_DEADLINE`, which is a date on a docket rather than a person's appointment. The row is created at `status` of `SCHEDULED` with `reschedule_count` at 0, `coverage_gap_flag` false, no `continued_from_event_id`, and `travel_buffer_minutes` set from the event type: 90 minutes for a `FACILITY_VISIT`, 60 for an `INDIVIDUAL_HEARING`, 45 for a `MASTER_CALENDAR_HEARING`, 45 for an `ASYLUM_INTERVIEW`, 30 for `BIOMETRICS`, and 0 for a `FILING_DEADLINE`. Creation returns HTTP 201.

Two facility-access rules apply before the conflict scan. A `FACILITY_VISIT` must start at least `visit_lead_time_hours` after the logical clock; a start exactly at the boundary is accepted, and anything earlier is rejected with `ERR_FACILITY_LEAD_TIME_NOT_MET` (HTTP 422). And when the facility's `access_credential_required` is true, the assigned representative's `facility_access_credential_expires_on` must be present and on or after the event's UTC calendar date, otherwise `ERR_FACILITY_CREDENTIAL_EXPIRED` (HTTP 422) — a credential expiring on the event date is still valid on that date.

The conflict scan then runs three geometries **in a fixed order** — the representative geometry, then the client geometry, then the facility geometry — and reports the first that trips. A request violating two geometries always reports the representative conflict. The three use deliberately different interval arithmetic, and conflating them is the most common implementation error in this area.

**The representative geometry** examines the other events sharing the candidate's `assigned_representative_id`, across every matter. Its interval comparison is half-open, and its travel buffer is applied **asymmetrically**: the buffer extends the existing event's end only when that event's `location_code` differs from the candidate's, because a representative already at the same location needs no travel time. Concretely, a conflict exists when the candidate's start is strictly before the existing event's end plus the applicable buffer, **and** the existing event's start is strictly before the candidate's end. Half-open means that a candidate starting exactly when a same-location event ends does not conflict. When the locations differ, the buffer used is the buffer of the **existing** event's type. A violation is `ERR_REPRESENTATIVE_CALENDAR_CONFLICT` (HTTP 409).

**The client geometry** examines the other events across all matters of the same client, regardless of which representative is assigned. Its comparison is **fully closed** and carries **no buffer**: a conflict exists when the candidate's start is at or before the existing event's end and the existing event's start is at or before the candidate's end. Because the endpoints are inclusive, a candidate starting exactly when an existing client event ends **does** conflict here, whereas the same pair would pass the representative geometry. Two exemptions apply: the entire client scan is skipped when the candidate carries `client_conflict_waived` true, and an event of type `FILING_DEADLINE` is exempt on either side of the comparison, so a filing deadline can share a window with a hearing for the same client. A violation is `ERR_CLIENT_CALENDAR_CONFLICT` (HTTP 409).

**The facility geometry** is not an interval comparison at all: it is a per-date aggregate. For a `FACILITY_VISIT`, the server counts the existing facility visits at the same `facility_id` whose `starts_at_utc` falls on the same UTC calendar date, and rejects the booking when that count is already at or above the facility's `max_daily_visit_slots`. Two visits at the same facility on the same date at completely non-overlapping times therefore conflict when the ceiling is 1, even though neither interval geometry would object. A violation is `ERR_FACILITY_VISIT_SLOTS_EXHAUSTED` (HTTP 409).

Across all three geometries only events whose `status` is `SCHEDULED` or `CONFIRMED` occupy time or consume a facility slot. An event in `CONTINUED`, `COMPLETED`, `MISSED` or `CANCELLED` is invisible to the scan, which is why cancelling a facility visit immediately frees its per-date slot and makes a previously rejected same-date visit bookable.

Rescheduling moves a `SCHEDULED` event to a new window. It takes a new `starts_at_utc` and `ends_at_utc`, an optional `location_code`, an optional `assigned_representative_id` and an optional `client_conflict_waived`, requires the event's `status` to be `SCHEDULED` — a confirmed event must be transitioned rather than rescheduled, and the attempt is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` (HTTP 409) — and re-runs the full scan with the event's **own row excluded from every geometry**, so an event may always be moved onto a window that overlaps only itself. It re-derives `travel_buffer_minutes` and re-applies the lead-time and credential checks against the new interval. `reschedule_count` increments by 1 on each success, and a request is rejected with `ERR_RESCHEDULE_LIMIT_REACHED` (HTTP 409) once the increment would carry `reschedule_count` above 3. Three reschedules therefore succeed, leaving the counter at 3, and the fourth attempt is refused.

The event transition endpoint takes an `action` of `CONFIRM`, `COMPLETE`, `MARK_MISSED`, `CONTINUE` or `CANCEL`, with optional `successor_starts_at_utc`, `successor_ends_at_utc` and `reason_code`. Confirming requires a non-null `assigned_representative_id` whose `authorization_status` is neither `AUTHORIZATION_SUSPENDED` nor `UNAUTHORIZED`, plus a valid facility credential for a facility visit. Completing and marking missed both require the logical clock to be at or after `ends_at_utc` — the system will not record attendance at an event that has not finished. Marking a `MASTER_CALENDAR_HEARING` or an `INDIVIDUAL_HEARING` as missed additionally sets the matter's `coverage_gap_flag` true, because a missed hearing is a representation failure. Continuing is legal only for `MASTER_CALENDAR_HEARING`, `INDIVIDUAL_HEARING` and `ASYLUM_INTERVIEW`, requires `successor_starts_at_utc` strictly after the predecessor's `ends_at_utc`, and creates a successor event whose `continued_from_event_id` points at the predecessor, inheriting `matter_id`, `facility_id`, `assigned_representative_id`, `event_type`, `location_code` and `client_conflict_waived`, beginning at `status` of `SCHEDULED` with `version` 1. The successor is validated against the full three-geometry scan, and because the response body returns the *predecessor*, the successor is discoverable only by listing events filtered on `continued_from_event_id`. Cancelling is legal from `SCHEDULED` and `CONFIRMED`. Each transition advances the event's `version` by 1, plus the matter's `version` when the matter was flagged. Events may be listed with optional filters on `matter_id`, `facility_id`, `assigned_representative_id`, `continued_from_event_id`, `event_type`, `status` and `coverage_gap_flag`; the list is the only read channel.

### 3.9. Informed Decisions and the Filing Dimension

A matter carries two independent status dimensions on the same row. `representation_status` is driven only by assignment transitions and authorization cascades; `filing_status` is driven only by the filing-advance endpoint. Neither dimension constrains the other except where a precondition explicitly says so. A matter is never created directly — it exists only as a product of an accepted screening — and is discoverable by listing matters filtered on `intake_id` or read individually. The single read exposes both dimensions, the readiness figures, the hours rollups, `interpretation_cost_cents`, and the `version` that serves as the concurrency token for assignment and informed-decision creation. Listing supports optional filters on `client_id`, `intake_id`, `cycle_id`, `principal_matter_id`, `facility_id`, `representation_status`, `filing_status`, `detained` and `coverage_gap_flag`.

The matter's `forum` is derived from `case_type` at creation and is not supplied: `ASYLUM_DEFENSIVE`, `REMOVAL_DEFENSE_OTHER` and `BOND_HEARING` map to `EOIR_IMMIGRATION_COURT`; `ASYLUM_AFFIRMATIVE` maps to `ASYLUM_OFFICE`; and `U_VISA`, `VAWA_SELF_PETITION`, `SIJS`, `DACA_RENEWAL`, `NATURALIZATION` and `ADJUSTMENT_OF_STATUS` all map to `USCIS`. The two deadline fields follow. `statutory_deadline_date` is copied from the screening. `expedited_deadline_date` is set only for a detained matter with a facility, as the decision date plus that facility's `expedited_filing_days`; for a non-detained matter it is absent. And `effective_filing_deadline_date` is the earlier of the two whenever both are present, whichever one is present when only one is, and absent when neither is — a detained matter can therefore have an effective deadline that is *earlier* than any statutory bar.

Attaching, moving or clearing a matter's facility is a `ProgramDirector` or `SupervisingAttorney` operation taking a `detained` flag and an optional `facility_id`. Setting `detained` true with no `facility_id` is rejected with `ERR_FACILITY_REQUIRED_FOR_DETAINED` (HTTP 422). Any change while `filing_status` is `FILED` or `RFE_PENDING` is rejected with `ERR_FACILITY_CHANGE_AFTER_FILING` (HTTP 409), because it would retroactively move the expedited deadline of an already-filed matter. On success both derived deadlines are recomputed against the new facility's `expedited_filing_days`, and the readiness figures and `readiness_gate_applied` are recomputed under the newly applicable gate variant. That last consequence is subtle and intended: releasing a client from detention flips the gate, so a matter that reached `READY_TO_FILE` under one gate can fail its *next* transition with a different error id than it would have failed with before.

Recording counseling is how the program documents that the client understood the choice. A `CaseRepresentative`, a `SupervisingAttorney` or a `ProgramDirector` creates an informed decision with a `matter_id`, a `counseled_by_assignment_id`, a `decision_type` of `FILE_AFFIRMATIVE`, `PURSUE_RELIEF_IN_COURT`, `ACCEPT_VOLUNTARY_DEPARTURE`, `APPEAL_DECISION`, `WITHDRAW_CASE` or `DECLINE_RELIEF`, a non-empty `risks_disclosed` subset of `REMOVAL_RISK`, `DETENTION_RISK`, `BAR_TO_FUTURE_RELIEF`, `FEE_LOSS`, `CRIMINAL_EXPOSURE`, `FAMILY_SEPARATION` and `DATA_DISCLOSURE`, a non-empty `alternatives_disclosed` subset of `DO_NOTHING`, `SEEK_OTHER_COUNSEL`, `ALTERNATIVE_RELIEF_PATH`, `VOLUNTARY_DEPARTURE`, `ADMINISTRATIVE_CLOSURE` and `APPEAL`, a `counseling_language_code`, the mandatory `expected_matter_version` token, and an optional `readback_session_id`. The matter must already carry at least one `CERTIFIED` declaration, otherwise `ERR_DECLARATION_NOT_CERTIFIED` (HTTP 409). The `counseled_by_assignment_id` must be an `ACTIVE` assignment on the same matter. The client's `confidentiality_consent_scope` must not be `NONE`, otherwise `ERR_CONSENT_SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT` (HTTP 422).

Language access is verified rather than asserted. `language_match_verified` is set true only when `counseling_language_code` equals the client's `preferred_language_code`, **or** the payload supplies a `readback_session_id` naming a completed interpretation session of the same matter whose `language_code` equals `counseling_language_code`. When neither holds, the create is rejected with `ERR_COUNSELING_LANGUAGE_UNVERIFIED` (HTTP 422): counseling delivered in a language the client does not speak, without an interpreter on record, is not informed consent. An empty `risks_disclosed` or an empty `alternatives_disclosed` array is likewise rejected with `ERR_CONSENT_SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT` (HTTP 422). The row begins at `status` of `PENDING` with no `superseded_by_decision_id`; creation returns HTTP 201. Decisions may be listed with optional filters on `matter_id`, `counseled_by_assignment_id`, `readback_session_id`, `superseded_by_decision_id`, `decision_type` and `status`; the list is the only read channel.

Supersession is not an action a caller invokes: creating a decision automatically supersedes the existing `DOCUMENTED` decision of the same `matter_id` and `decision_type`, setting that older row's `superseded_by_decision_id` to the new row and moving it to `SUPERSEDED`. This is precisely why the operation carries a concurrency token.

The informed-decision transition endpoint takes an `action` of `ACKNOWLEDGE` or `REVOKE`, with an optional `revocation_reason_code`. Acknowledgement is the client's own act and the only way a decision becomes `DOCUMENTED`: it is available solely to a `ClientPortalUser` whose identifier equals the matter's `client_id`, requires `language_match_verified` to be true and the matter's `representation_status` to be `STAFFED` or `SUPERVISED_STAFFED`, and stamps `acknowledged_at_utc` with the logical clock. Counseling that the client has not acknowledged satisfies no filing gate. Revocation is a `SupervisingAttorney` act, requires a `revocation_reason_code` from `CLIENT_CHANGED_MIND`, `NEW_FACTS`, `COUNSEL_ERROR` or `LANGUAGE_ACCESS_DEFECT`, and is refused when the matter is already `FILED` and the `decision_type` is `FILE_AFFIRMATIVE` or `PURSUE_RELIEF_IN_COURT` — a filing already lodged cannot have its consent withdrawn out from under it. A revoked or superseded decision no longer satisfies any filing gate, so a matter whose withdrawal decision was revoked can no longer be withdrawn until a new one is acknowledged.

The filing dimension advances through its own endpoint, taking a `target_filing_status` of `EVIDENCE_GATHERING`, `READY_TO_FILE`, `FILED`, `RFE_PENDING`, `DECIDED` or `APPEALED`, with an optional `outcome`, an optional `rfe_response_due_date` and an optional `appeal_due_date`. The same target means different things from different origins, and the preconditions differ accordingly.

- Moving from `PRE_FILING` to `EVIDENCE_GATHERING` requires `representation_status` to be `STAFFED` or `SUPERVISED_STAFFED` — the one genuine cross-dimension precondition — and at least one `REQUIRED` evidence item on the matter.
- Moving from `EVIDENCE_GATHERING` to `READY_TO_FILE` requires a `CERTIFIED` declaration and evaluates the readiness gate selected by `detained`. For a **detained** matter the weighted gate applies: it passes when `readiness_index_weighted` is at least 0.7500, **even when required items remain open**, and failure is `ERR_MATTER_NOT_READY` (HTTP 409). For a **non-detained** matter the strict checklist gate applies: it passes only when `readiness_ready_strict` is true, and failure is `ERR_BLOCKING_EVIDENCE_OUTSTANDING` (HTTP 409). The two gates can disagree on identical evidence — a matter at 0.8000 weighted readiness with one unverified required item passes while detained and fails while not detained — and `readiness_gate_applied` records which one ran, as `EVIDENCE_WEIGHTED_GATE` or `CHECKLIST_STRICT_GATE`.
- Moving from `READY_TO_FILE` back to `EVIDENCE_GATHERING` is the revert edge and always succeeds from `READY_TO_FILE`.
- Moving from `READY_TO_FILE` to `FILED` is the initial filing. It requires a `DOCUMENTED` informed decision whose `decision_type` is `FILE_AFFIRMATIVE` or `PURSUE_RELIEF_IN_COURT`, and requires the logical clock date to be at or before `effective_filing_deadline_date`. It completes every `SCHEDULED` or `CONFIRMED` `FILING_DEADLINE` event of the matter.
- Moving from `FILED` to `RFE_PENDING` is the receipt of a request for evidence. It requires `rfe_response_due_date`, writes that date into `statutory_deadline_date`, recomputes `effective_filing_deadline_date`, and creates one `FILING_DEADLINE` event at midnight UTC on that date with `location_code` of `MAIL` — discoverable only by listing events filtered on `matter_id`.
- Moving from `RFE_PENDING` back to `FILED` is the RFE response, and requires `evidence_blocking_count` to be 0 after recomputation. This cycle may repeat without bound.
- Moving from `FILED` or `RFE_PENDING` to `DECIDED` requires an `outcome` of `GRANTED`, `DENIED`, `ADMINISTRATIVELY_CLOSED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `TERMINATED` or `REFERRED_TO_COURT`, stamps `outcome_recorded_at_utc`, and cancels every `SCHEDULED` or `CONFIRMED` event of the matter whose `event_type` is not `FACILITY_VISIT` — visits continue because a detained client still needs counsel.
- Moving from `DECIDED` to `APPEALED` requires the recorded `outcome` to be `DENIED`, a `DOCUMENTED` informed decision of type `APPEAL_DECISION`, and an `appeal_due_date`. It sets `forum` to `BIA` and **clears** `outcome`, so the matter must be decided again. This cycle may also repeat without bound.

The forum-authorization rule of section 3.6 also applies to filing advances, and it presupposes a lead to examine. A matter sitting in `COVERAGE_GAP`, whose `LEAD` assignment was released or suspended by an authorization cascade, has no lead to authorize at all, and the same advance is refused with `ERR_LEAD_NOT_ASSIGNED` (HTTP 409) before any forum grant is computed — a distinct id from `ERR_FORUM_NOT_AUTHORIZED`, and reported instead of it.

Every advance leaves `representation_status` untouched and advances the matter's `version` by 1, which invalidates any outstanding concurrency token a client was holding. An advance that is not one of the listed edges is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION` (HTTP 409), and any advance against a matter whose `representation_status` is already `WITHDRAWN_FROM` is rejected with `ERR_MATTER_CLOSED` (HTTP 409).

Withdrawing the organization from a matter is the other way a matter closes, available to a `SupervisingAttorney` or a `ProgramDirector` with an optional `withdrawal_motion_filed` and an optional `reason_code`. It requires a `DOCUMENTED` informed decision of this matter whose `decision_type` is `WITHDRAW_CASE`, otherwise `ERR_CONSENT_SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT` (HTTP 422), and it requires `filing_status` to be anything other than `FILED` unless `withdrawal_motion_filed` is true — a court filing cannot be abandoned without a motion. On success `representation_status` becomes `WITHDRAWN_FROM` and `closed_at_utc` is stamped; `filing_status` becomes `CLOSED_NO_FILING` unless it is already `DECIDED`, which is preserved. Every assignment in `PROPOSED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` is released with `release_reason_code` of `MATTER_CLOSED` and each affected representative's `active_case_load` and `supervisee_count` are recomputed. The slot recorded in `slot_pool_consumed` is returned to its pool and the cycle's counters and `capacity_posture` are recomputed. The client's `open_matter_count` is recomputed and its `status` becomes `CLOSED` when the count reaches 0. The matter, the cycle and the client each advance their `version` by 1. Unlike screening withdrawal, this endpoint does not promote anyone from the waitlist and does not touch the underlying screening.

### 3.10. Cascades, the Checkpoint Sweep and Funder Reporting

**The logical clock.** Every deadline, expiry, lead time and window in this system reads a server-held logical clock, never the host wall clock. A `ProgramDirector` sets it with a `to_iso8601` instant. Before any such call the clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and it must not fall back to real time. Setting the clock mutates no entity and fires no cascade; it merely changes what subsequent evaluations of accreditation expiry windows, event lead times, waitlist expiry timestamps and posture overrides see. The endpoint returns HTTP 200 with an empty data object.

**The recognition-lapse cascade.** Lapsing an organizational recognition is the single most consequential operation in the system, and it propagates through four hops in a fixed order. Hop 0 sets the recognition's `status` to `LAPSED`, stamps `lapsed_at_utc`, and clears `conditional_reason_code`. Hop 1, caused by hop 0, moves every accreditation of that recognition whose `status` is `APPROVED`, `EXPIRING` or `RENEWAL_PENDING` to `SUSPENDED_BY_RECOGNITION` with `decision_reason_code` of `RECOGNITION_LAPSE`, and sets `suspended_accreditation_count` to the resulting count. Hop 2, caused by hop 1, re-derives every affected representative's `authorization_status`; for each one that now yields `AUTHORIZATION_SUSPENDED`, every assignment whose `status` is `ACTIVE` moves to `SUSPENDED` with `release_reason_code` of `RECOGNITION_LAPSE`, and `active_case_load` and `supervisee_count` are recomputed for each affected representative. Hop 3, caused by hop 2, moves every matter that lost its only `ACTIVE` `LEAD` to `representation_status` of `COVERAGE_GAP` with `coverage_gap_flag` true. Hop 4, caused by hop 3, moves every `IN_REVIEW` declaration of those matters to `REVISION_REQUESTED` with `revision_reason_code` of `SUPERVISION_REQUIRED` and `revision_round` incremented by 1, and sets `coverage_gap_flag` true on every event of those matters whose `status` is `SCHEDULED` or `CONFIRMED` and whose `starts_at_utc` falls within 14 days after the logical clock. Every row touched at every hop has its `version` advanced by 1. Note that staff attorneys are untouched: their authority comes from the bar, so a lapse leaves an attorney-led matter fully staffed.

Reinstating a lapsed recognition **partially** compensates that cascade, and the incompleteness is deliberate. Reinstatement requires the logical clock date to be at or before the recognition's `expiry_date`. Accreditations still inside their own `expiry_date` return to `APPROVED`, while those now past it move to `EXPIRED` instead. Suspended assignments whose representative is again authorized return to `ACTIVE`, and `active_case_load` and `supervisee_count` are recomputed for each affected representative — including the supervisor named by a revived assignment's `supervision_link_id`, whose `supervisee_count` counts that assignment again. Matters that thereby regain an `ACTIVE` `LEAD` leave `COVERAGE_GAP`. But declarations pushed to `REVISION_REQUESTED` are **not** restored to `IN_REVIEW`, and `revision_round` is not decremented — a revision request is a fact about the record that reinstatement cannot unsay. Nor are the `coverage_gap_flag` values on individual calendar events cleared.

**The accepted-screening withdrawal cascade.** Withdrawing an accepted screening propagates through four hops. Hop 1 moves the derived matter to `representation_status` of `WITHDRAWN_FROM`, `filing_status` of `CLOSED_NO_FILING`, `outcome` of `WITHDRAWN`, with `outcome_recorded_at_utc` and `closed_at_utc` at the logical clock. Hop 2, caused by hop 1, moves every assignment of that matter whose `status` is `PROPOSED`, `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` to `RELEASED` with `release_reason_code` of `CLIENT_WITHDREW` and `released_at_utc` at the logical clock, recomputing each affected representative's `active_case_load` and `supervisee_count` and re-deriving its `authorization_status`. Hop 3, also caused by hop 1, returns the slot recorded in `slot_pool_consumed` to its pool: the matter no longer counts against `general_slots_consumed` or `detained_slots_consumed`, and `available_slot_count`, `utilization_ratio` and `capacity_posture` are recomputed. Hop 4, caused by hop 3, promotes the promotable waitlisted screening of the same cycle with the lowest `waitlist_rank` — tie-broken by earlier `created_at` — to `ACCEPTED`, which itself creates a matter and re-consumes a pool seat. When no promotable row exists, the freed slot simply stays available. The client's `open_matter_count` is recomputed and its `status` becomes `CLOSED` when the count reaches 0.

The interaction between hops 3 and 4 is where the conservation identity of section 2.9 earns its keep. When no promotion fires, the cycle's five capacity values return exactly to their pre-acceptance state. When a promotion does fire, the consumed counters instead stay where the acceptance had left them, because one matter left the pool and another entered it in the same request — and the promoted screening may consume a *different* pool than the withdrawn one did, if one was detained and the other was not.

**The checkpoint sweep.** A `ProgramDirector` runs the cycle's time-driven sweep, which is the only endpoint that performs the system-actor transitions and which is idempotent for a fixed logical clock. It runs three phases in a normative, observable order.

- Phase 1, expiry: every waitlisted screening of the cycle whose `waitlist_expires_at_utc` is at or before the logical clock moves to `WAITLIST_EXPIRED`, its `waitlist_rank` is cleared while its `waitlist_expires_at_utc` is left in place as the record of the window that closed, and the remaining waitlisted rows are re-ranked by `created_at` ascending.
- Phase 2, promotion, evaluated strictly after phase 1: while the waterfall still resolves a pool with a free seat and the recomputed `capacity_posture` is `OPEN` or `RESTRICTED`, the promotable waitlisted row with the lowest `waitlist_rank` — tie-broken by `created_at` ascending — moves to `ACCEPTED`, creating a matter and consuming a slot. Under a `RESTRICTED` posture only rows whose `urgency_tier` is `URGENT` or `EMERGENCY` are promotable. The loop continues until no free pool remains or no promotable row remains.
- Phase 3, credential aging: every `APPROVED` accreditation now inside its 90-day expiry window moves to `EXPIRING`, and every accreditation in `APPROVED`, `EXPIRING`, `RENEWAL_PENDING` or `SUSPENDED_BY_RECOGNITION` whose `expiry_date` is past moves to `EXPIRED`, re-deriving that representative's `authorization_status` and suspending its `ACTIVE` assignments.

The sweep then recomputes `utilization_ratio` and `capacity_posture`, increments `checkpoint_epoch` by 1, and sets `last_checkpoint_utc` to the logical clock. The cycle's `version` advances by 1, as does the `version` of every row the sweep mutated. The phase ordering is directly observable: because expiry runs first, a screening whose waitlist window closed at the same instant the sweep runs is **never** promoted, even when a slot is free. Reversing the phases would promote it.

**Funder reporting.** A `GrantAnalyst` or a `ProgramDirector` opens a report with a `cycle_id`, a `grant_code` of 2 to 24 uppercase characters, a `period_start_date`, a `period_end_date` and a `disclosure_level` of `AGGREGATE_ONLY`, `DEIDENTIFIED_CASE_LEVEL` or `IDENTIFIED_WITH_CONSENT`. A `period_start_date` after `period_end_date`, or a period falling outside the cycle's own inclusive window, is rejected with `ERR_REPORT_PERIOD_INVALID` (HTTP 422); a one-day period whose start equals its end is legal. The row begins at `status` of `DRAFT` with `amendment_round` at 0, an empty `outcomes_summary`, and every count and money field at 0; creation returns HTTP 201. Reports may be listed with optional filters on `cycle_id`, `grant_code`, `disclosure_level` and `status`, and read individually.

The report transition endpoint takes an `action` of `GENERATE`, `SUBMIT` or `AMEND`, with an optional `amendment_reason_code`. Generation is legal from `DRAFT` and from `AMENDED`, which closes the generated-submitted-amended-generated cycle; attempting it from an inapplicable state is rejected with `ERR_REPORT_STATE_INVALID` (HTTP 409).

Generation first fixes the reported population. The line set is every matter of the cycle whose screening's `decided_at_utc` date falls within `period_start_date` through `period_end_date` **inclusive at both ends** and whose `representation_status` is not `WITHDRAWN_FROM`, ordered by `created_at` ascending. `cases_served_count` is the size of that set. `outcomes_summary` is a histogram keyed by each `outcome` value plus the additional key `NOT_YET_DECIDED` for matters with no recorded outcome, and its values sum to `cases_served_count`. Consent is then partitioned by disclosure level: at `AGGREGATE_ONLY` a client's `confidentiality_consent_scope` of `PROGRAM_ONLY`, `FUNDER_AGGREGATE` or `FUNDER_IDENTIFIED` all suffice; at `DEIDENTIFIED_CASE_LEVEL` only `FUNDER_AGGREGATE` or `FUNDER_IDENTIFIED` suffice; at `IDENTIFIED_WITH_CONSENT` only `FUNDER_IDENTIFIED` suffices. A scope of `NONE` is always suppressed. `consent_verified_count` counts the matters whose client's scope suffices, and `suppressed_case_count` is `cases_served_count` minus `consent_verified_count`. Generation at `IDENTIFIED_WITH_CONSENT` is rejected outright with `ERR_CONSENT_SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT` (HTTP 422) unless **every** reported client holds `FUNDER_IDENTIFIED`.

Generation then computes the money. For each reported matter, its reimbursable amount is `direct_hours_logged` multiplied by 12,500 cents per hour, rounded half up to whole cents **per matter**; `base_reimbursable_cents` is the integer sum of those per-matter figures. The supervision surcharge rate is selected from `base_reimbursable_cents` **alone** — not from any hours figure and not from the total: the rate is 0.00 when the base is below 500,000 cents, 0.06 when the base is at least 500,000 and below 2,000,000 cents, and 0.09 when the base is at least 2,000,000 cents. Both thresholds are inclusive lower bounds, so a base of exactly 500,000 cents takes the 0.06 rate. `supervision_surcharge_cents` is the base multiplied by that rate, rounded half up to whole cents **once**. `total_reimbursable_cents` is `base_reimbursable_cents` plus `supervision_surcharge_cents`.

That total is then distributed back across the reported matters by largest-remainder allocation, so the per-line amounts sum exactly to the total with no drift. Each matter's ideal share is the total multiplied by its `direct_hours_logged` divided by the sum of all reported `direct_hours_logged`. Each line first receives the whole-cent floor of its ideal share. The leftover cents — the total minus the sum of those floors — are then handed out one cent each to the lines with the largest fractional remainders, and where two remainders are equal the line with the earlier `created_at` receives the cent first. `allocated_line_total_cents` is the sum of the allocated lines and must equal `total_reimbursable_cents`; `rounding_remainder_cents` records the number of leftover cents that were distributed. When every reported matter has 0.00 logged hours, all money fields are 0 and the allocation is trivially satisfied. When the line set is empty, generation sets every count and money field to 0 and **must not** raise an error. On success `generated_at_utc` is stamped and `status` becomes `GENERATED`.

Submission requires `allocated_line_total_cents` to equal `total_reimbursable_cents` and, at `IDENTIFIED_WITH_CONSENT`, requires `suppressed_case_count` to be 0; it stamps `submitted_at_utc` and moves `status` to `SUBMITTED`. Amending increments `amendment_round` by 1, clears `submitted_at_utc`, and moves `status` to `AMENDED`, from which a regeneration over an unchanged line set reproduces byte-identical money fields because every step of the allocation is deterministic. Each transition advances the report's `version` by 1.
