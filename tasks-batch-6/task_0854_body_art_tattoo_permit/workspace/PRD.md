# Body-Art & Tattoo Establishment Permit API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

County health departments carry statutory responsibility for every premises where ink, needles and pigment meet human skin. Today that responsibility is discharged through a patchwork of paper plan submissions, clipboard inspection forms, filing-cabinet consent binders and spreadsheet fee schedules, with the result that nobody in the department can answer the only question that actually matters on any given morning: *is this shop safe to operate right now?* The Body-Art & Tattoo Establishment Permit service exists to answer that question continuously, for every permitted premises and every permitted practitioner in the jurisdiction, from a single authoritative record.

The service models the full regulatory arc. A prospective operator submits construction plans for review; an examiner approves them or sends them back for correction. A field inspector conducts a pre-opening visit; only a passed pre-opening visit unlocks permit issuance. Once open, the premises accumulates evidence — autoclave cycle records with biological indicator results, guardian consent forms for minor clients, procedure session logs, routine inspection scores, public complaints and reportable infection reports. All of that evidence collapses into two derived figures: a numeric **risk score** and an enum **operating posture**. The posture is the gate: an unrestricted premises may serve anyone, a restricted premises may serve adults only, and a closed premises may serve nobody. When the risk score crosses the department's suspension breakpoint, the system issues an enforcement action of its own accord rather than waiting for an officer to notice.

Three cross-cutting design commitments shape the specification that follows. First, **every derived figure is recomputed at the moment its inputs change**, never on a schedule and never lazily at read time; a client that reads an establishment immediately after citing a violation sees the new counters, the new score and the new posture. Second, **segregation of duties is structural, not advisory** — the officer whose identifier issued an enforcement action is barred from lifting it. Third, the server runs on a **logical clock** that tests advance explicitly, so that a permit term, an abatement deadline or a training expiry can be crossed deterministically rather than by waiting.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

Every route in this specification is mounted beneath the `/body-art` prefix. All request and response bodies are JSON.

### 2.1. Departmental Users and Role-Based Access Control

Authentication is header-borne. Every request must present both an `X-User-ID` header and an `X-User-Role` header. If either header is absent, or if `X-User-Role` carries a string that is not one of the seven declared roles, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). If the headers are well formed but the named role does not appear on the role list of the route being called, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403). Authentication is always evaluated before authorization, and both are evaluated before anything else in the request is examined.

The seven roles are `HealthOfficer`, `Inspector`, `PlanReviewer`, `RecordsClerk`, `EstablishmentOperator`, `Artist` and `PublicUser`.

A `HealthOfficer` is a county health department officer: they register premises, issue establishment and artist permits, drive complaint and infection-report workflows, audit procedure logs, issue and lift enforcement actions, and decide renewals. An `Inspector` is a field inspector who conducts on-site visits, cites violations and rules on submitted corrections. A `PlanReviewer` is a plan examiner who rules on construction-plan submissions before any permit can be issued. A `RecordsClerk` is a departmental clerk who verifies guardian consent forms, takes renewal payments and issues refunds. An `EstablishmentOperator` is the owner-operator of a permitted premises. An `Artist` is an individual practitioner. A `PublicUser` is a member of the public with no read access whatsoever to departmental records — their only capabilities are filing a complaint and filing a reportable infection report.

Identity semantics differ by role, and this distinction governs every ownership check in the system. When `X-User-Role` is `HealthOfficer`, `Inspector`, `PlanReviewer`, `RecordsClerk` or `PublicUser`, the `X-User-ID` value is an opaque operator identifier that is not tied to any persisted entity; the server may record it (for example as `inspector_user_id` on an inspection, as `decided_by_user_id` on a plan review or renewal, as `verified_by_user_id` on a consent, or as `issued_by_user_id` and `lifted_by_user_id` on an enforcement action) but never compares it to any entity's `id`. When `X-User-Role` is `EstablishmentOperator`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting establishment; ownership checks on any resource that references an establishment compare the resource's establishment reference to `X-User-ID`. When `X-User-Role` is `Artist`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting artist; ownership checks compare either the target artist's own `id` or the `artist_id` carried in the request body to `X-User-ID`. Whenever an entity-backed caller acts on a resource that is not theirs, the request is rejected with `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER` (HTTP 403). This ownership rule applies to every write route whose role list includes `EstablishmentOperator` or `Artist`.

Because segregation of duties is enforced against `issued_by_user_id`, the department must operate with at least two distinct `HealthOfficer` identifiers; a single shared officer identifier would make suspensions permanently unliftable.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every collection route accepts two optional query parameters. `limit` controls page size: it defaults to 10, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` controls the starting position: it defaults to 0 and its minimum accepted value is 0. A `limit` outside the inclusive range from 1 to 100, a non-integer `limit`, or a negative `offset` is a request-shape failure and is rejected with `ERR_VALIDATION` (HTTP 400).

Collections are always returned sorted by `created_at` in ascending order, so that page boundaries are stable as new rows arrive. For list responses the `meta` object carries `limit` and `offset` echoing the effective values, `total_count` giving the number of rows matching the filters before pagination is applied, and `has_more` indicating whether any further rows remain beyond the current page.

### 2.4. Record Identity, Timestamps and Versioning

Every stored record carries an `id` that is a server-minted UUID. Identifiers carry no type prefix, no check digit and no embedded meaning; a client must never parse one. Every record also carries a `created_at` timestamp, an ISO 8601 UTC instant with three fractional digits, stamped from the logical clock at the moment the row is written.

Most records additionally carry an `updated_at` timestamp and an integer `version`. The `version` of a fresh record is 1, and every mutation that touches the record increments it by exactly one and refreshes `updated_at` to the current logical-clock instant. This applies to establishments, artists, plan reviews, inspections, violations, minor consents, procedure logs, complaints, infection reports, enforcement actions and renewal applications.

Sterilization records are the single exception. An autoclave cycle record is immutable once filed: it carries only `id` and `created_at`. It has no `updated_at` field and no `version` field, and no route in this specification mutates one.

A crucial consequence of the recompute-on-change principle is that an establishment's `version` advances not only when the establishment itself is edited but whenever any derived field on it moves — its counters, its risk figure, its posture, its money totals or its permit dates. Clients that hold an establishment version for concurrency purposes must therefore expect it to advance as a side effect of activity elsewhere in the aggregate.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock

The server does not read wall-clock time for any domain decision. Instead it maintains a single logical clock which, before any client moves it, reads exactly `2026-05-01T00:00:00Z`. Every domain timestamp the server generates — issuance instants, finalization instants, decision instants, `created_at` and `updated_at` — is taken from this clock, and every temporal comparison in this document (deadlines, expiries, windows, overlaps) is evaluated against it.

An administrative test-driving capability moves the clock forward to a supplied `to_iso8601` instant. The clock is strictly monotonic: a target instant that is at or before the current logical-clock instant is rejected with `ERR_CLOCK_NOT_MONOTONIC` (HTTP 409). Every role may exercise this capability. Advancing the clock also runs a two-step sweep, described in §3.3 and §3.4, and the response reports the resulting `current_utc` along with the arrays `expired_establishment_ids` and `escalated_violation_ids`.

### 2.6. Path Resolution and Referential Integrity

Nested routes are resolved from left to right. Each `{..._id}` segment in the path is looked up in turn, and the first segment that fails to resolve determines the error returned, regardless of whether a later segment would also have failed. An unresolved establishment segment yields `ERR_ESTABLISHMENT_NOT_FOUND`, an artist segment yields `ERR_ARTIST_NOT_FOUND`, a plan-review segment yields `ERR_PLAN_REVIEW_NOT_FOUND`, an inspection segment yields `ERR_INSPECTION_NOT_FOUND`, a violation segment yields `ERR_VIOLATION_NOT_FOUND`, a sterilization-record segment yields `ERR_STERILIZATION_RECORD_NOT_FOUND`, a minor-consent segment yields `ERR_MINOR_CONSENT_NOT_FOUND`, a procedure-log segment yields `ERR_PROCEDURE_LOG_NOT_FOUND`, a complaint segment yields `ERR_COMPLAINT_NOT_FOUND`, an infection-report segment yields `ERR_INFECTION_REPORT_NOT_FOUND`, an enforcement-action segment yields `ERR_ENFORCEMENT_ACTION_NOT_FOUND` and a renewal-application segment yields `ERR_RENEWAL_APPLICATION_NOT_FOUND`. All of these carry HTTP 404. The path variables in play are `establishment_id`, `artist_id`, `plan_review_id`, `inspection_id`, `violation_id`, `sterilization_record_id`, `minor_consent_id`, `procedure_log_id`, `complaint_id`, `infection_report_id`, `enforcement_action_id` and `renewal_application_id`.

Foreign keys supplied inside a request body are resolved with the same error vocabulary but at a later point in the precedence order. On creation routes that accept a referenced identifier — artist registration, plan-review submission, inspection scheduling, sterilization-record filing, minor-consent filing, procedure-log filing, complaint filing, infection-report filing and renewal-application creation — each of `establishment_id`, `artist_id`, `minor_consent_id` and `supersedes_record_id` must resolve to a stored row, and a failure yields the corresponding not-found code with HTTP 404.

### 2.7. Order of Evaluation

When several checks could reject the same request, the outcome is fully determined by a fixed precedence order. Implementations must evaluate in exactly this sequence, returning the first failure encountered:

1. Header authentication — `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
2. Role authorization against the route's role list — `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`.
3. Path-identifier resolution, left to right, as described in §2.6.
4. Request-body shape: every required input field present, every enum field holding a declared member, every numeric field inside its declared range, every datetime field a well-formed ISO 8601 UTC instant, `scheduled_end_at` strictly after `scheduled_start_at`, `ended_at` strictly after `started_at`, and pagination parameters within their declared bounds — all failures yield `ERR_VALIDATION` (HTTP 400).
5. Body foreign-key resolution, as described in §2.6.
6. Ownership for entity-backed callers — `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`.
7. Artist-to-establishment consistency on procedure-log filing — `ERR_ARTIST_ESTABLISHMENT_MISMATCH` (HTTP 409).
8. Segregation of duties on an enforcement lift — `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403).
9. Stale-version rejection, as described in §2.8.
10. State-machine admissibility of the requested transition, as described in §2.9.
11. Onward, the domain-specific safety, temporal, referential and fiscal rules described throughout §3, in the order stated there.

The ordering matters in practice. An unauthorized caller aiming at a nonexistent record learns only that they are unauthorized. A stale version is reported before the target's state is examined, so a client holding an outdated version never discovers whether the transition would have been admissible. And on procedure-log filing, an artist who belongs to a different establishment is reported as a mismatch before any posture, training or consent rule is consulted.

### 2.8. Stale-Version Protection

Two write routes carry optimistic concurrency control, and on both the version field is a required input rather than an optional one.

Creating a renewal application requires `expected_establishment_version` carrying the client's last observed `version` of the target establishment. If that value does not equal the establishment's current `version` at the moment the write is applied, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_ESTABLISHMENT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). This protection exists because a newly created renewal application legitimately supersedes any prior application for the same premises that is still `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `DEFICIENT`; without the check, a client working from an outdated read would silently discard renewal work it had never seen.

Issuing an enforcement action against an individual artist requires `expected_artist_version` carrying the client's last observed `version` of that artist. A mismatch against the artist's current `version` is rejected with `ERR_STALE_ARTIST_VERSION` (HTTP 412). The artist may legitimately have moved to `PROBATION`, or had fresh bloodborne-pathogen training recorded, between the officer's read and their write — either of which changes whether the action is warranted — so the officer must re-read before writing.

No other route in this specification accepts a version field, and supplying one elsewhere has no effect.

### 2.9. State Admissibility and Error Attribution

Every action route that drives a state machine first checks that its target sits in a state the requested transition declares as its origin. When it does not, the error identifier is chosen by the entity that owns the state machine, not by the action: establishments yield `ERR_ESTABLISHMENT_STATE_INVALID`, artists `ERR_ARTIST_STATE_INVALID`, plan reviews `ERR_PLAN_REVIEW_STATE_INVALID`, inspections `ERR_INSPECTION_STATE_INVALID`, violations `ERR_VIOLATION_STATE_INVALID`, minor consents `ERR_CONSENT_STATE_INVALID`, procedure logs `ERR_PROCEDURE_LOG_STATE_INVALID`, complaints `ERR_COMPLAINT_STATE_INVALID`, infection reports `ERR_INFECTION_STATE_INVALID`, enforcement actions `ERR_ENFORCEMENT_STATE_INVALID` and renewal applications `ERR_RENEWAL_STATE_INVALID`. All eleven carry HTTP 409.

Inspections attach one extra condition to the same code: an inspector who did not start a given visit may not finalize it and may not cite violations on it, and that attempt is also reported as `ERR_INSPECTION_STATE_INVALID`.

### 2.10. Precision, Rounding and Money

All monetary amounts in this system are integers denominated in cents; there are no fractional-cent values anywhere in a stored record or a response. Wherever a monetary computation produces a fractional cent, the result is rounded half up to a whole cent — a value exactly halfway between two cents rounds away from zero. Inspection deduction points and inspection scores are likewise whole integers, rounded half up wherever a fractional point arises. The single non-integer figure the system exposes is the minor-consent audit rate, which is rounded half up to four decimal places.

Rounding order is never left to the implementation's discretion. Where a computation involves several lines and a multiplier, §3.9 pins whether rounding happens per line or once at the end; the two orders produce different totals and the pinned order is the specification.

### 2.11. Operating Posture: the Master Gate

Every establishment carries a derived `operating_posture` that takes one of three values — `OPEN`, `RESTRICTED` or `CLOSED` — and is recomputed on every change to the establishment's `permit_status`, `risk_score`, `open_critical_violation_count` or `sterilization_compliance_status`. No other data feeds it directly.

The computation is short. If the establishment's `permit_status` is any of `PENDING_PLAN_REVIEW`, `PENDING_INSPECTION`, `SUSPENDED`, `EXPIRED` or `REVOKED`, the posture is `CLOSED`. Otherwise the permit is `ACTIVE`, and the posture is `RESTRICTED` when either the `risk_score` has reached 60 or higher, or the `open_critical_violation_count` is greater than zero. When neither of those conditions holds, the posture is `OPEN`.

Posture is what gates day-to-day operations at the premises. A `CLOSED` establishment may not record any procedure session at all. A `RESTRICTED` establishment may keep serving adult clients but may not serve minors. An `OPEN` establishment operates without posture-derived restriction. A second, higher breakpoint at a risk score of 80 does not appear in the posture computation at all; it is the threshold at which the violation-issuance cascade auto-generates a suspension, and its consequences reach posture only indirectly, through the resulting change of `permit_status`.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Standing Up a Premises: Registration and the Plan-Review Cycle

A premises enters the system when a `HealthOfficer` registers it. The registration payload carries the legal business `name` of the shop, which must be between 1 and 120 characters and non-empty once surrounding whitespace is trimmed; the `street_address` of the permitted premises, between 1 and 200 characters; the `county_code` asserting jurisdiction, which must be exactly three uppercase letters A through Z; and the `establishment_type`, which is one of `TATTOO`, `PIERCING`, `PERMANENT_COSMETICS` or `COMBINED`. The record is born in `permit_status` `PENDING_PLAN_REVIEW` with `permit_issued_on` and `permit_expires_on` both null, `active_artist_count`, `open_violation_count`, `open_critical_violation_count`, `fees_paid_cents` and `fees_refunded_cents` all zero, and `plan_review_cycles_used` at 1.

Registration is not inert, however. Two derived figures are evaluated immediately. The sterilization verdict runs over an empty record set and therefore lands on `NONCOMPLIANT` — the empty set is explicitly non-compliant, never vacuously compliant — writing `sterilization_compliance_status`. The provisional risk figure then runs and, with no violations, no complaints and no infection reports on file, consists solely of the non-compliance penalty, so a brand-new establishment opens its life with a `risk_score` of 25. Its `operating_posture` is `CLOSED`, because the permit is not yet `ACTIVE`.

The identifier minted for the new establishment is operationally significant: it is the exact value that the shop's owner-operator will present as `X-User-ID` when acting in the `EstablishmentOperator` role. Departmental staff can read any establishment individually, and `HealthOfficer`, `Inspector`, `PlanReviewer` and `RecordsClerk` users can browse the whole roster with optional filters on `county_code`, `permit_status` and `operating_posture` alongside the usual `limit` and `offset`.

Before a permit can be issued, construction plans must clear examination. The operator submits a plan review carrying the `establishment_id` of their premises, a `floor_plan_reference` of 1 to 80 characters identifying the submitted drawing, the declared `sterilization_area_sqft` of the dedicated sterilization area, which must be greater than zero, the `procedure_station_count` shown on the plan, which must be a whole number of at least 1, and the boolean `has_handwash_sink` recording whether a dedicated handwash sink appears in the procedure area. The new review is born `SUBMITTED` with `cycle_count` at 1 and `decision_notes` and `decided_by_user_id` both null. A premises may hold only one review in flight at a time: if any prior plan review for the same establishment is still `SUBMITTED` or `CORRECTIONS_REQUIRED`, a further submission is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_OPEN_PLAN_REVIEW` (HTTP 409).

Examination is the `PlanReviewer`'s exclusive prerogative. The reviewer records one of three decisions — `APPROVE`, `REQUEST_CORRECTIONS` or `DENY` — supplied in the `decision` field, optionally accompanied by free-text `decision_notes`. Every decision stamps `decided_by_user_id` with the reviewer's identifier and stores the supplied note. Approval is admissible only from `SUBMITTED`, and it additionally demands that the plan actually meet the code: `has_handwash_sink` must be true and `sterilization_area_sqft` must be at least 40 square feet. A plan that fails either requirement is rejected with `ERR_PLAN_REVIEW_REQUIREMENTS_NOT_MET` (HTTP 422) rather than being approved. A correction request is admissible only from `SUBMITTED` and moves the review `SUBMITTED → CORRECTIONS_REQUIRED`. A denial is admissible from either `SUBMITTED` or `CORRECTIONS_REQUIRED`, and both `APPROVED` and `DENIED` are terminal.

An approval does not stop at the plan review. It cascades: the parent establishment advances `PENDING_PLAN_REVIEW → PENDING_INSPECTION`, its `version` increments by one, its `updated_at` refreshes, and its posture is recomputed — which leaves it `CLOSED`, since the permit is still not `ACTIVE`.

When corrections are demanded, the operator revises and resubmits, moving the review `CORRECTIONS_REQUIRED → SUBMITTED`. A resubmission carries fresh values for `floor_plan_reference`, `sterilization_area_sqft`, `procedure_station_count` and `has_handwash_sink`, which overwrite the stored values outright; it increments `cycle_count` by one and copies that new count onto the establishment's `plan_review_cycles_used`; and it clears `decision_notes` back to null. This revision loop may repeat, but not indefinitely — a resubmission is admissible only while `cycle_count` remains strictly below 3, so the third submission of a plan is the last one the department will look at. An attempt beyond that ceiling is rejected with `ERR_PLAN_REVIEW_CYCLE_LIMIT` (HTTP 409). Plan reviews can be read individually and browsed as a filtered collection using `establishment_id` and `status`.

### 3.2. Permit Issuance and the Artist Roster

Issuance is a `HealthOfficer` act. It advances the establishment `PENDING_INSPECTION → ACTIVE` and is admissible only from `PENDING_INSPECTION`; the premises must additionally have at least one inspection of `inspection_type` `PREOPENING` sitting in status `PASSED`, and without one the attempt is rejected with `ERR_PREOPENING_INSPECTION_REQUIRED` (HTTP 409). Upon issuance the server stamps `permit_issued_on` with the current logical-clock instant and sets `permit_expires_on` to exactly 365 days later — a one-year permit term. It then recomputes `sterilization_compliance_status`, the provisional `risk_score` and `operating_posture`, and increments the establishment's `version` by one. Note the sequencing consequence: a shop that has passed its pre-opening visit but has filed no autoclave records is still carrying a non-compliance penalty, so it may well open in `RESTRICTED` posture rather than `OPEN`.

Individual practitioners are registered separately, either by a `HealthOfficer` or by the operator themselves. The registration payload names the `establishment_id` of the premises at which the practitioner will work and the practitioner's `legal_name`, between 1 and 120 characters. A newly registered artist sits in `permit_status` `PENDING` with no training on file: `bbp_training_completed_on` and `bbp_training_expires_on` are null, `bbp_recert_required` is false, `permit_expires_on` is null, and `confirmed_infection_count` and `suspension_count` are both zero. As with establishments, the identifier returned is the value the practitioner will present as `X-User-ID` when acting in the `Artist` role. The artist roster can be read one record at a time or browsed with filters on `establishment_id` and `permit_status`.

Bloodborne-pathogen training is recorded against the artist by the practitioner themselves or by their operator, supplying the `completed_on` date of the course. The recording sets `bbp_training_completed_on` to that date, sets `bbp_training_expires_on` to exactly 730 days after it — a two-year certification — and clears `bbp_recert_required` to false. The effect on `permit_status` depends on where the artist stands. An artist in `PENDING` or `ACTIVE` simply has the dates written; the permit state does not move. An artist in `PROBATION`, by contrast, is rehabilitated: they advance `PROBATION → ACTIVE`, which requires that the submitted `completed_on` be at or before the current logical-clock instant, and the transition increments the parent establishment's `active_artist_count` by one, recomputes that establishment's `operating_posture`, and increments both the artist's `version` and the establishment's `version` by one.

Permitting an artist is a `HealthOfficer` act that drives `PENDING → ACTIVE`. Two gates guard it, evaluated in this order. First, the parent establishment must itself hold `permit_status` `ACTIVE`; a premises still awaiting plan review, awaiting inspection, suspended, expired or revoked cannot host a newly permitted practitioner, and the attempt is rejected with `ERR_ESTABLISHMENT_PERMIT_NOT_ACTIVE` (HTTP 409). Second, the artist must hold live training: `bbp_training_expires_on` must be non-null and strictly after the current logical-clock instant, failing which the attempt is rejected with `ERR_ARTIST_BBP_MISSING` (HTTP 422). On success the artist's `permit_expires_on` is copied from the parent establishment's `permit_expires_on`, the establishment's `active_artist_count` rises by one and the establishment's `version` increments.

The artist permit state space is `PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, `PROBATION`, `SUSPENDED`, `INACTIVE` and `REVOKED`, with `REVOKED` terminal. Beyond activation and rehabilitation, every remaining artist transition is driven by something happening elsewhere. A confirmed infection report moves an `ACTIVE` artist `ACTIVE → PROBATION`. An enforcement suspension moves an `ACTIVE` artist `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` and equally moves a `PROBATION` artist `PROBATION → SUSPENDED`; the first of those two also decrements the establishment's `active_artist_count` and recomputes its posture and bumps both versions, whereas the probation branch only increments the artist's `version`, because a probationary artist was already excluded from the active count. Lifting that suspension moves the artist `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`, provided the parent establishment is itself `ACTIVE`, and restores the count and posture with both versions bumped. A revocation moves an `ACTIVE` artist `ACTIVE → REVOKED`, decrementing `active_artist_count` and bumping both versions. Finally, when the parent establishment leaves `ACTIVE` — whether it becomes `SUSPENDED`, `REVOKED` or `EXPIRED` — every artist there who is `ACTIVE` moves `ACTIVE → INACTIVE` and every artist who is `PROBATION` moves `PROBATION → INACTIVE`, each bumping only the artist's own `version`; and when the establishment later returns to `ACTIVE` from `SUSPENDED` or from `EXPIRED`, every `INACTIVE` artist there moves `INACTIVE → ACTIVE` with `permit_expires_on` refreshed from the establishment's current `permit_expires_on`. All of these roster cascades are processed in ascending `created_at` order of the artist rows.

Only `ACTIVE` artists count toward `active_artist_count`. Practitioners in `PENDING`, `PROBATION`, `SUSPENDED`, `INACTIVE` or `REVOKED` are excluded, which matters because that count feeds the renewal fee schedule.

### 3.3. Scheduling, Conducting and Scoring an Inspection

Both `HealthOfficer` and `Inspector` users may book a visit window. The booking names the `establishment_id`, an `inspection_type` of `PREOPENING`, `ROUTINE`, `COMPLAINT` or `REINSPECTION`, and the window itself as `scheduled_start_at` and `scheduled_end_at`, the latter strictly after the former. The new visit is born `SCHEDULED` with `inspector_user_id` null, `score` null, `finalized_at` null, `open_violation_count_at_start` at zero and `source_complaint_id` null.

A single premises cannot host two overlapping visits, and the overlap test is generous on purpose: inspectors need travel and setup time. Each window is expanded by one hour on both sides, giving a closed interval running from sixty minutes before `scheduled_start_at` to sixty minutes after `scheduled_end_at`. The proposed expanded interval is compared against the identically expanded interval of every other inspection at the same establishment whose status is `SCHEDULED` or `IN_PROGRESS`; inspections that have already reached `PASSED`, `FAILED` or `CANCELLED` are ignored. Because the intervals are closed, expanded windows that merely touch at an endpoint do conflict — a proposed visit whose expanded interval begins at exactly the instant another expanded interval ends is a collision, not an adjacency. Any intersection is rejected with `ERR_INSPECTION_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (HTTP 409). This same buffered test governs the visit that complaint triage generates, and inspections created by the failed-finalize follow-up cascade are equally visible to it as scheduling candidates.

Beginning the visit is the starting `Inspector`'s act and drives `SCHEDULED → IN_PROGRESS`. It is admissible only from `SCHEDULED` and only once the current logical-clock instant has reached `scheduled_start_at`; an attempt made before the window opens is rejected with `ERR_INSPECTION_STATE_INVALID` (HTTP 409). Two things are written at that moment: `inspector_user_id` takes the calling `X-User-ID`, and the establishment's current `open_violation_count` is snapshotted into `open_violation_count_at_start`. That snapshot is deliberate — the carryover penalty in the score reflects the shop's backlog as the inspector walked in, not as they walked out, so violations cited during this very visit do not double-count through the carryover term.

Closing the visit is the same inspector's act, and only theirs: an inspector whose `X-User-ID` differs from the stored `inspector_user_id` is rejected with `ERR_INSPECTION_STATE_INVALID`. Closing computes the score, writes it to `score`, stamps `finalized_at` with the current logical-clock instant, and branches the visit to either `IN_PROGRESS → PASSED` or `IN_PROGRESS → FAILED`.

The score is built in four steps and one branch. **Step one** walks every violation cited on this inspection, in ascending `created_at` order of the violation rows, and assigns each a line deduction. A citation of `severity` `CRITICAL` starts from a base of 12 points and a citation of `severity` `NONCRITICAL` starts from a base of 6 points. Each line is then tested for repetition: the line counts as a repeat when some other violation exists at the same establishment carrying an identical `violation_code`, having a different identifier, sitting in status `VERIFIED` or `ESCALATED`, and whose `issued_at` falls at or after the instant 365 days before this inspection's `scheduled_start_at` and strictly before that `scheduled_start_at` itself. A repeat line records `repeat_multiplier_applied` of 1.5; a first-time line records 1.0. The line's `deduction_points` is the base multiplied by that factor, rounded half up to a whole point. The raw deduction is the sum of the `deduction_points` across all lines, and it is zero when the visit cited nothing at all.

**Step two** adds the carryover term: five points for every violation counted in `open_violation_count_at_start`, capped so that the carryover contribution can never exceed 20 points no matter how large the backlog. **Step three** selects the inspection-type factor, which is 1.25 when this visit's own `inspection_type` is `REINSPECTION` and 1.00 for every other type — a re-visit is scored more harshly because the shop has already had its chance. **Step four** performs the one and only final rounding: the raw deduction and the carryover are added together, that sum is multiplied by the type factor, the product is rounded half up to a whole point, and the result is subtracted from a perfect score of 100. If that subtraction would go below zero the score is clamped at zero. Note carefully that the type factor is applied once, to the combined sum, and not per line.

The **branch** follows from the finished score and the citation set. The visit passes when the score is 70 or higher *and* no violation cited on this inspection carries `severity` `CRITICAL`. It fails when the score is strictly below 70, or when at least one cited violation is `CRITICAL` — a single critical finding fails the visit regardless of how good the arithmetic looks. Both branches write `score` and `finalized_at`, and both recompute the establishment's `risk_score` and `operating_posture`.

The two branches part company on one further effect. A failure spawns a follow-up: a fresh inspection is created with `inspection_type` `REINSPECTION`, status `SCHEDULED`, `scheduled_start_at` set to exactly seven days after this visit's `finalized_at` and `scheduled_end_at` set to 120 minutes after that start. This follow-up is generated only when the failing visit's own `inspection_type` is `ROUTINE`, `COMPLAINT` or `REINSPECTION`. A failed `PREOPENING` visit spawns nothing, because there is no permit yet to re-inspect. A passing visit spawns nothing either.

Scheduled visits can also die without ever being conducted. When an establishment is suspended out of `ACTIVE`, every one of its inspections that is still `SCHEDULED` and whose `inspection_type` is `ROUTINE` moves `SCHEDULED → CANCELLED`, in ascending `created_at` order, each bumping its own `version` by one. Visits of other types are left standing. `PASSED`, `FAILED` and `CANCELLED` are all terminal.

Inspections are readable individually, and the collection accepts filters on `establishment_id`, `source_complaint_id`, `status` and `inspection_type`.

### 3.4. Citing Violations, the Abatement Cycle and the Auto-Suspension Cascade

Citations are recorded during the visit, not after it. Only the inspector who started a given visit may cite on it, and only while that visit is `IN_PROGRESS`; any other combination is rejected with `ERR_INSPECTION_STATE_INVALID`. Each citation carries a `violation_code` — the health-code citation identifier, 1 to 16 characters drawn only from uppercase letters, digits and underscores, such as `BBP_001` or `STER_004` — a `severity` of `CRITICAL` or `NONCRITICAL`, and a `narrative` of 1 to 500 characters describing what the inspector observed. The new row records its owning `inspection_id`, opens in status `ISSUED`, stamps `issued_at` with the current logical-clock instant, and starts with `repeat_multiplier_applied` at 1.0, `deduction_points` at zero, and `correction_note` and `correction_submitted_at` both null. Those first two figures stay at their defaults until the visit is finalized and the scoring pass writes them.

The abatement deadline is computed at creation and written to `abatement_due_at`. A `CRITICAL` citation must be abated within 3 days of `issued_at`; a `NONCRITICAL` citation gets 14 days. Comparisons against this deadline are consistent throughout the system: an instant at or after `abatement_due_at` is overdue, and an instant strictly before it is still within the abatement period.

Issuing a citation sets off the system's deepest cascade, and every hop is a consequence of the hop before it. **Hop one:** the establishment's `open_violation_count` and `open_critical_violation_count` are recomputed. The open set is defined across every violation reachable through that establishment's inspections whose status is `ISSUED`, `CORRECTION_SUBMITTED` or `ESCALATED`, with the critical counter restricted further to rows whose `severity` is `CRITICAL`; no time window applies to either count. **Hop two:** because those counters moved, the establishment's provisional `risk_score` is recomputed. **Hop three:** because the risk figure moved, the suspension breakpoint is tested — when `risk_score` has reached 80 or higher and no enforcement action against that establishment is currently `ACTIVE`, the system creates one of its own: `subject_type` `ESTABLISHMENT`, `action_type` `SUSPENSION`, `reason_code` `RISK_THRESHOLD`, `auto_generated` true and `issued_by_user_id` set to the literal string `SYSTEM`. **Hop four:** because that action is `ACTIVE`, the establishment transitions `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` and its `operating_posture` becomes `CLOSED`. **Hop five:** because the establishment became `SUSPENDED`, every artist there holding `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION` drops to `INACTIVE` and every `SCHEDULED` `ROUTINE` inspection there is `CANCELLED`, with `active_artist_count` recomputed and the establishment's `version` incremented. When the recomputed `risk_score` stays strictly below 80, hops three through five simply do not fire and the only remaining effect is a recomputation of `operating_posture`.

Abatement is the operator's move. The owning `EstablishmentOperator` submits a `correction_note` describing the corrective action taken, which drives the citation `ISSUED → CORRECTION_SUBMITTED` and stamps `correction_submitted_at` with the current logical-clock instant. Submitting a correction changes nothing about the establishment's counters, because `CORRECTION_SUBMITTED` is still an open status — the shop does not get credit for claiming a fix, only for having one verified. The submission is admissible only from `ISSUED`, and only from the operator whose `X-User-ID` matches the establishment reached through the citation's owning inspection.

Adjudicating the correction belongs to an `Inspector`, who supplies a `decision` of either `VERIFY` or `REJECT`. Both are admissible only from `CORRECTION_SUBMITTED`. A verification drives `CORRECTION_SUBMITTED → VERIFIED`, which removes the row from the open set: `open_violation_count` and `open_critical_violation_count` are recomputed, `risk_score` and `operating_posture` follow, and the establishment's `version` increments by one. A rejection branches on the clock. Rejected strictly before `abatement_due_at`, the citation returns `CORRECTION_SUBMITTED → ISSUED` with `correction_note` and `correction_submitted_at` cleared back to null and the establishment's counters untouched — this is the revision loop, and it may repeat as many times as the operator can manage while the deadline holds. Rejected at or after `abatement_due_at`, the citation instead moves `CORRECTION_SUBMITTED → ESCALATED`, and because `ESCALATED` remains inside the open set the counters do not move, though `risk_score` and `operating_posture` are still recomputed. `VERIFIED` and `ESCALATED` are the two terminal outcomes.

Deadlines also expire on their own. When the logical clock is advanced, the second step of the sweep visits every violation still sitting in `ISSUED` whose `abatement_due_at` is at or before the new instant and moves it `ISSUED → ESCALATED`, processing rows in ascending `created_at` order, recomputing the owning establishment's `risk_score` and `operating_posture` for each, and appending each identifier to the `escalated_violation_ids` array in the sweep response. The first step of the same sweep handles permits: every establishment sitting in `ACTIVE` whose `permit_expires_on` is at or before the new instant moves `ACTIVE → EXPIRED`, processed in ascending `created_at` order of the establishment rows; each such expiry drops every `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION` artist at that premises to `INACTIVE`, forces `operating_posture` to `CLOSED`, recomputes `active_artist_count`, increments the establishment's `version` by one, and appends the establishment identifier to the `expired_establishment_ids` array.

Individual citations are readable, and the collection accepts filters on `inspection_id`, `status` and `severity`.

### 3.5. Sterilization Evidence and the Rolling Compliance Verdict

Every autoclave cycle run at a permitted premises is documented by its operator. The filing names the `establishment_id`, the `device_serial` of the sterilizer that ran the cycle (1 to 40 characters), the `cycle_completed_at` instant at which the cycle finished, and the biological indicator outcome in `spore_test_result`, which is one of `PASS`, `FAIL` or `PENDING`. The server stamps `recorded_by_user_id` with the filing operator's `X-User-ID`. A cycle cannot have completed in the future: a `cycle_completed_at` after the current logical-clock instant is rejected with `ERR_CYCLE_TIMESTAMP_FUTURE` (HTTP 422).

When a failed or still-pending cycle is retested, the retest record names the earlier record in `supersedes_record_id`. That reference must point at a record belonging to the same establishment and must point at a record that no other record already supersedes; a cross-premises reference or a double-supersede attempt is rejected with `ERR_STERILIZATION_SUPERSEDE_INVALID` (HTTP 409), while a reference that resolves to nothing at all yields `ERR_STERILIZATION_RECORD_NOT_FOUND` (HTTP 404). These records are write-once — nothing in the system edits one after it is filed, which is why they carry no `updated_at` and no `version`.

Filing a record immediately recomputes the establishment's `sterilization_compliance_status`, then its `risk_score`, then its `operating_posture`, and increments the establishment's `version` by one. The verdict is also refreshed whenever the logical clock advances (the trailing window has moved, so yesterday's compliance can lapse without anybody filing anything) and whenever the risk figure is recomputed for any other reason.

The verdict itself is a four-way decision over a trailing 30-day window, closed at both ends, running from the instant 30 days before the current logical-clock instant up to and including that instant. The candidate set contains every sterilization record of this establishment whose `cycle_completed_at` falls inside that window *and* which no later record supersedes. Superseded records are removed from the candidate set entirely — including their failures, which is the whole point of a retest. From that candidate set: if the set is empty, the verdict is `NONCOMPLIANT`, since no evidence is not the same as clean evidence; if any candidate carries `spore_test_result` of `FAIL` or `PENDING`, the verdict is `NONCOMPLIANT`, because an unresolved indicator is treated exactly as harshly as a failed one; if the number of candidates carrying `PASS` has reached 4 or more, the verdict is `COMPLIANT`; and otherwise — one, two or three clean passes and nothing outstanding — the verdict is `DEFICIENT`. Exactly four passes is enough for compliance; three is not.

The verdict then feeds the risk figure through a sterilization penalty: 25 points when the verdict is `NONCOMPLIANT`, 10 points when it is `DEFICIENT`, and nothing at all when it is `COMPLIANT`. That penalty alone is what puts a freshly registered premises at a risk score of 25 before any inspector has set foot in it. Sterilization records are readable individually and as a collection filterable by `establishment_id`, `supersedes_record_id` and `spore_test_result`.

### 3.6. Guardian Consent, Procedure Logs and the Audit Trail

Where the jurisdiction permits body art on a minor, the department requires a guardian consent form on file before the needle touches skin, and the filing is the operator's responsibility. The consent names the `establishment_id` holding the signed form, the `minor_full_name` of the client (1 to 120 characters), the `minor_age_at_signing` as a whole number of years from 0 through 17 inclusive, the `guardian_full_name` (1 to 120 characters), the `guardian_id_document_type` presented, which is one of `DRIVERS_LICENSE`, `PASSPORT`, `STATE_ID` or `MILITARY_ID`, the `guardian_id_document_number` printed on it (1 to 40 characters), the single `authorized_procedure_type` the form authorizes, which is one of `TATTOO`, `PIERCING` or `PERMANENT_COSMETICS`, and the `consent_signed_at` instant at which the guardian signed. The record opens with `verification_status` `PENDING`, `verified_by_user_id` and `rejection_reason` null, and a computed `expires_at` set to exactly 30 days after `consent_signed_at`. A consent is usable only for a session that starts strictly before that expiry.

Verification is the `RecordsClerk`'s exclusive act. The clerk supplies a `decision` of `VERIFY` or `REJECT`, and on a rejection may attach a `rejection_reason`. Both decisions are admissible only from `PENDING`, and both stamp `verified_by_user_id` with the clerk's identifier; the rejection additionally stores the supplied reason. A verification drives `PENDING → VERIFIED` and additionally requires that `expires_at` still be strictly after the current logical-clock instant — a form that has already lapsed cannot be verified into usefulness. A rejection drives `PENDING → REJECTED` and carries no such temporal condition. Both `VERIFIED` and `REJECTED` are terminal. Consents are readable individually and as a collection filterable by `establishment_id` and `verification_status`.

Procedure sessions are logged by the practitioner who performed them, and only by them. The log names the `establishment_id` where the work happened, the `artist_id` who performed it, the `client_age` as a whole number of years from 0 through 120 inclusive, the `procedure_type` performed (`TATTOO`, `PIERCING` or `PERMANENT_COSMETICS`), the session boundaries `started_at` and `ended_at` with the end strictly after the start, the boolean `aftercare_provided` recording whether written aftercare instructions were handed over, and, for minor clients only, the `minor_consent_id` of the authorizing form. The log opens in `audit_status` `RECORDED` with `audit_finding_code` null.

Filing a log runs the longest validation chain in the system, and the order is fixed. After the generic checks of §2.7 have passed, the artist named by `artist_id` must belong to the establishment named by `establishment_id`, failing which the filing is rejected with `ERR_ARTIST_ESTABLISHMENT_MISMATCH` (HTTP 409). Then the posture gate: an establishment whose `operating_posture` is `CLOSED` accepts no sessions at all, and the filing is rejected with `ERR_ESTABLISHMENT_NOT_OPERATING` (HTTP 409). Then the artist's own fitness, in two parts — their `bbp_training_expires_on` must be strictly after the session's `started_at`, failing which the filing is rejected with `ERR_ARTIST_BBP_EXPIRED` (HTTP 422), and their `permit_status` must be `ACTIVE`, failing which it is rejected with `ERR_ARTIST_NOT_PERMITTED` (HTTP 409). Then the restricted-posture minor block: when the establishment's `operating_posture` is `RESTRICTED`, `client_age` must be at least 18, because a restricted premises may keep serving adults but not minors, and a younger client is rejected with `ERR_RESTRICTED_POSTURE_MINOR_BLOCK` (HTTP 409).

The consent chain follows, in five further steps. A `client_age` strictly below 18 requires a `minor_consent_id`; its absence is rejected with `ERR_MINOR_CONSENT_REQUIRED` (HTTP 422). The referenced consent must already carry `verification_status` `VERIFIED`; one still `PENDING` or already `REJECTED` is rejected with `ERR_MINOR_CONSENT_NOT_VERIFIED` (HTTP 409). The session's `started_at` must be strictly before the consent's `expires_at`; a start at or after that instant is rejected with `ERR_MINOR_CONSENT_EXPIRED` (HTTP 409). The consent's `authorized_procedure_type` must equal the log's `procedure_type` and the consent's `establishment_id` must equal the log's `establishment_id`; either mismatch is rejected with `ERR_MINOR_CONSENT_MISMATCH` (HTTP 409). And the symmetric rule closes the chain: a `client_age` of 18 or above must arrive with `minor_consent_id` null, and supplying a consent for an adult is rejected with `ERR_MINOR_CONSENT_NOT_APPLICABLE` (HTTP 422).

Last in the chain comes session exclusivity. A practitioner cannot be in two places at once, so the new session's interval — half-open, running from `started_at` up to but not including `ended_at`, with no buffer of any kind — must not intersect the identically half-open interval of any existing log for the same `artist_id`, in any `audit_status` whatsoever. Because the intervals are half-open, back-to-back work is legitimate: an existing session ending at exactly the new session's `started_at`, or beginning at exactly the new session's `ended_at`, is adjacency rather than conflict. A true intersection is rejected with `ERR_ARTIST_SESSION_OVERLAP` (HTTP 409). Note the contrast with inspection scheduling, which uses closed intervals padded by an hour on each side — the two overlap rules in this system deliberately differ.

Departmental audit of the logs belongs to a `HealthOfficer`, who supplies a `decision` of `FLAG` or `CLEAR`. Flagging is admissible only from `RECORDED`, drives `RECORDED → FLAGGED` and writes the supplied `finding_code` into `audit_finding_code`, which is one of `CONSENT_EXPIRED`, `CONSENT_TYPE_MISMATCH`, `AFTERCARE_MISSING` or `ARTIST_NOT_PERMITTED`. Clearing is admissible only from `FLAGGED`, drives `FLAGGED → CLEARED` and resets `audit_finding_code` back to null; `CLEARED` is terminal. Either decision increments the establishment's `version` by one, and either decision additionally recomputes the establishment's `risk_score` and `operating_posture` — but only when the audited log's `client_age` is strictly below 18, since only minor sessions carry weight in the risk figure. Logs are readable individually, and the collection accepts filters on `establishment_id`, `artist_id`, `minor_consent_id` and `audit_status`.

### 3.7. Public Complaints, Reportable Infections and the Risk Figure

Anyone may complain. A `PublicUser` files against a premises by naming the `establishment_id`, supplying a `complainant_contact` string of 1 to 120 characters, choosing a `category` from `SANITATION`, `UNLICENSED_ARTIST`, `MINOR_PROCEDURE`, `INJURY` or `OTHER`, and describing the alleged condition in a `narrative` of 1 to 500 characters. The complaint opens in status `RECEIVED` with `received_at` stamped from the logical clock, `reopen_count` at zero, `triage_overdue` false, and `triage_due_at` computed as exactly 5 days after `received_at` — the department's self-imposed triage service level.

A `HealthOfficer` walks the complaint forward by supplying an `action`. `TRIAGE` is admissible only from `RECEIVED` and drives `RECEIVED → TRIAGED`. It sets `triage_overdue` to true when the current logical-clock instant has reached `triage_due_at` or passed it, and to false otherwise — the department records its own lateness rather than hiding it. Triage also books the field response: an inspection is created for the same establishment with `inspection_type` `COMPLAINT`, status `SCHEDULED`, `source_complaint_id` pointing back at this complaint, `scheduled_start_at` set to 24 hours after the current logical-clock instant and `scheduled_end_at` set 120 minutes after that start. That derived window is subject to the same buffered closed-interval exclusivity test described in §3.3, so a triage whose generated visit would collide with an existing `SCHEDULED` or `IN_PROGRESS` visit at the premises is rejected with `ERR_INSPECTION_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (HTTP 409) and the complaint stays in `RECEIVED`.

`INVESTIGATE` is admissible only from `TRIAGED`, drives `TRIAGED → INVESTIGATING` and increments the complaint's `version` by one. `SUBSTANTIATE` is admissible only from `INVESTIGATING`, drives `INVESTIGATING → SUBSTANTIATED`, recomputes the establishment's `risk_score` — the substantiated-complaint weight now applies — and its `operating_posture`, and increments the establishment's `version` by one. `UNSUBSTANTIATE` is equally admissible only from `INVESTIGATING`, drives `INVESTIGATING → UNSUBSTANTIATED`, and likewise recomputes `risk_score` and `operating_posture`, this time because the complaint stops contributing its in-flight weight to the audit figure. `REOPEN` is admissible only from `UNSUBSTANTIATED`, drives `UNSUBSTANTIATED → INVESTIGATING`, increments `reopen_count` by one and recomputes `risk_score` and `operating_posture`; it is permitted only while `reopen_count` is strictly below 2, so a dismissed complaint can be revived at most twice before the file is closed for good, and a further attempt is rejected with `ERR_COMPLAINT_REOPEN_LIMIT` (HTTP 409). `SUBSTANTIATED` is the machine's only terminal state — an unsubstantiated complaint is never truly finished. Complaints are readable individually and as a collection filterable by `establishment_id` and `status`.

Reportable infections arrive from either the public or the operator. The report names the `establishment_id` implicated, optionally the `artist_id` of the implicated practitioner where the reporter can identify one, a `reporter_contact` string of 1 to 120 characters, an `infection_type` drawn from `BACTERIAL`, `VIRAL_HEPATITIS`, `MRSA`, `MYCOBACTERIAL` or `OTHER`, the `procedure_date` on which the implicated work was performed, and the `symptom_onset_at` instant at which symptoms began, which must be at or after `procedure_date`; an onset before the procedure is a request-shape failure yielding `ERR_VALIDATION`. The report opens with `investigation_status` `RECEIVED`, `notification_status` `PENDING`, `notification_late` false, `reopen_count` at zero, and `notification_due_at` computed as 24 hours after `created_at`.

An infection report advances along two entirely independent axes, and this independence is a hard requirement: an action on one axis leaves the other axis untouched. The **investigation axis** takes values `RECEIVED`, `SCREENING`, `CONFIRMED` and `RULED_OUT`, and is driven by a `HealthOfficer` supplying an `action`. `SCREEN` is admissible only from `RECEIVED` and drives `RECEIVED → SCREENING`. `RULE_OUT` is admissible only from `SCREENING`, drives `SCREENING → RULED_OUT` and recomputes the establishment's `risk_score`, since the report stops contributing its in-flight screening weight to the audit figure. `REOPEN` is admissible only from `RULED_OUT`, drives `RULED_OUT → SCREENING`, increments `reopen_count` by one and recomputes `risk_score`; it is permitted only while `reopen_count` is strictly below 2, and a further attempt is rejected with `ERR_INFECTION_REOPEN_LIMIT` (HTTP 409).

`CONFIRM` is admissible only from `SCREENING` and sets off a four-hop cascade. **Hop one:** the report's `investigation_status` becomes `CONFIRMED`, which is terminal on that axis. **Hop two:** because the report was confirmed, the practitioner named by `artist_id`, when one was named, moves `ACTIVE → PROBATION` and has `bbp_recert_required` set to true, with `confirmed_infection_count` recomputed on that artist. **Hop three:** because that practitioner left `ACTIVE`, the establishment's `active_artist_count` is decremented and its `risk_score` is recomputed, now carrying the confirmed-infection weight. **Hop four:** because the risk figure moved, `operating_posture` is recomputed, and the premises drops to `RESTRICTED` once the score reaches 60 or higher. Both the artist's `version` and the establishment's `version` increment. Throughout all four hops the notification axis is untouched.

The **notification axis** takes values `PENDING`, `CDC_NOTIFIED`, `NOT_REQUIRED` and `CLOSED`, and is driven by the same officer through a separate `action`. `NOTIFY` is admissible only from `PENDING`, drives `PENDING → CDC_NOTIFIED` and sets `notification_late` to true when the current logical-clock instant has reached `notification_due_at` or passed it, and to false otherwise. `MARK_NOT_REQUIRED` is also admissible only from `PENDING` and drives `PENDING → NOT_REQUIRED`, but only for a report whose `infection_type` is `OTHER`; every other clinical category is registry-reportable and cannot be waived, so the attempt is rejected with `ERR_NOTIFICATION_NOT_WAIVABLE` (HTTP 409). `CLOSE` drives `CDC_NOTIFIED → CLOSED` and equally drives `NOT_REQUIRED → CLOSED`, and `CLOSED` is terminal on this axis. Reports are readable individually and as a collection filterable by `establishment_id`, `artist_id`, `investigation_status` and `notification_status`.

All of this evidence collapses into the establishment risk figure, which is recomputed on every mutation that changes an open violation set, a complaint status, an infection-report investigation status, a sterilization record set, or the audit status of a minor procedure log at the premises. The figure is assembled over a lookback window of 365 days, closed at both ends, running from the instant 365 days before the current logical-clock instant up to and including that instant. Six terms are summed. The open-critical term contributes 15 points for each violation counted in `open_critical_violation_count`. The open-non-critical term contributes 5 points for each violation in the open set that is not critical — that is, for the difference between `open_violation_count` and `open_critical_violation_count`. Note that neither violation term is windowed; the open set is the open set, however old. The complaint term contributes 10 points for each complaint of this establishment whose status is `SUBSTANTIATED` and whose `received_at` falls inside the window. The infection term contributes 20 points for each infection report of this establishment whose `investigation_status` is `CONFIRMED` and whose `created_at` falls inside the window. The minor-log term contributes 12 points for each procedure log of this establishment with `client_age` strictly below 18, `audit_status` `FLAGGED` and `started_at` inside the window. The sixth term is the sterilization penalty described in §3.5. Every weight and every count is a whole number, so no rounding arises; the sum is finally capped at 100, and a score can never exceed that ceiling however bad the premises.

That figure has two variants, and only one of them is real. The **provisional** variant adds nothing further: complaints under triage or investigation and infection reports under screening are excluded entirely, so only `SUBSTANTIATED` complaints and `CONFIRMED` reports contribute. The provisional variant is the one persisted to `risk_score`, the one that drives `operating_posture`, and the one tested against the suspension breakpoint of 80. The **audit** variant adds an in-flight term to the same six: 5 points for each complaint of this establishment sitting in `TRIAGED` or `INVESTIGATING`, plus 10 points for each infection report sitting in `investigation_status` `SCREENING`, both counted over the same closed 365-day window, with the total still capped at 100. The audit variant is never persisted and never gates any transition; it exists only as a departmental early-warning read. Identical stored data therefore yields a strictly larger audit figure than provisional figure whenever any complaint or report is still in flight.

### 3.8. Enforcement, Reinstatement and Segregation of Duties

Enforcement instruments are written against one subject at a time, and the subject is decided by which record the officer aims at rather than by anything in the payload. An action aimed at a premises records `subject_type` `ESTABLISHMENT` with `establishment_id` populated and `artist_id` null; an action aimed at a practitioner records `subject_type` `ARTIST` with `artist_id` populated and `establishment_id` null. Either way the payload carries an `action_type` of `SUSPENSION` or `REVOCATION` and a `reason_code` citing the statutory basis, drawn from `IMMINENT_HEALTH_HAZARD`, `RISK_THRESHOLD`, `CONFIRMED_INFECTION`, `UNLICENSED_PRACTICE` or `FEE_DELINQUENCY`. The action opens in status `ACTIVE`, stamps `issued_at` from the logical clock, records `issued_by_user_id` as the acting officer's `X-User-ID`, sets `auto_generated` to false, and leaves `lifted_by_user_id`, `lifted_at`, `suspension_days`, `reinstatement_base_cents`, `reinstatement_admin_fee_cents` and `reinstatement_total_cents` all null.

A subject may be under at most one live instrument: if any enforcement action against the same establishment, or against the same artist, is already `ACTIVE`, a second issuance is rejected with `ERR_ACTIVE_ENFORCEMENT_EXISTS` (HTTP 409). This is the same condition the auto-suspension cascade of §3.4 consults before generating its own action, which is why a shop already under suspension does not accumulate duplicates as further violations pile up. An action issued against a practitioner additionally travels under stale-version protection: the officer must present `expected_artist_version`, as described in §2.8.

Issuing against a premises drives it out of `ACTIVE`: a suspension moves it `ACTIVE → SUSPENDED` and a revocation moves it `ACTIVE → REVOKED`. Both set `operating_posture` to `CLOSED`, both drop every `ACTIVE` or `PROBATION` artist at the premises to `INACTIVE` in ascending artist `created_at` order, and both increment the establishment's `version` by one; the suspension branch additionally cancels every `SCHEDULED` `ROUTINE` inspection there and recomputes `active_artist_count`. `REVOKED` is terminal for an establishment — no lift, no renewal and no clock advance ever brings it back. Issuing against a practitioner drives the artist transitions catalogued in §3.2.

Lifting is a `HealthOfficer` act, and it is where segregation of duties bites. The checks run in a fixed order. First, the calling `X-User-ID` must differ from the action's `issued_by_user_id`; an officer attempting to lift their own instrument is rejected with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403). Second, the action must still be `ACTIVE`; a lift aimed at one already `LIFTED` is rejected with `ERR_ENFORCEMENT_STATE_INVALID` (HTTP 409). Third, the action's `action_type` must be `SUSPENSION`; revocations are permanent instruments with no exit path at all, and a lift aimed at one is rejected with `ERR_ENFORCEMENT_NOT_LIFTABLE` (HTTP 409). An automatically generated action carries `issued_by_user_id` of `SYSTEM`, so any human officer may lift it.

A successful lift drives `ACTIVE → LIFTED`, the machine's only terminal state, stamping `lifted_by_user_id` with the lifting officer and `lifted_at` with the current logical-clock instant, and writing the reinstatement penalty breakdown. It then reinstates the subject. For an establishment action, the premises moves `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE` provided that no other enforcement action against it remains `ACTIVE` and that its `permit_expires_on` is strictly after the current logical-clock instant; the reinstatement restores every `INACTIVE` artist there to `ACTIVE` in ascending `created_at` order, refreshing each artist's `permit_expires_on` from the establishment's, and recomputes `active_artist_count`, `risk_score` and `operating_posture` with the establishment's `version` incremented. For an artist action, the practitioner moves `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE` provided their parent establishment is itself `ACTIVE`, with `active_artist_count`, `operating_posture` and both versions updated accordingly.

The reinstatement penalty is computed in four steps, evaluated before the transition is written. **Step one** measures the length of the suspension: `suspension_days` is the number of whole 24-hour days between `issued_at` and the lift instant, with any partial day discarded — a lift 19 days and 23 hours after issuance yields 19, not 20. **Step two** builds the base figure into `reinstatement_base_cents`: a flat 500 dollars, expressed as 50000 cents, plus 2600 cents for every whole suspension day. **Step three** selects the administrative percentage, and the tier is keyed on the base figure and never on the total: a base strictly below 100000 cents draws 5 percent, a base at or above 100000 cents but strictly below 200000 cents draws 8 percent, and a base of 200000 cents or more draws 12 percent. The administrative fee, written to `reinstatement_admin_fee_cents`, is the base figure multiplied by that percentage and rounded half up to a whole cent — this is the only rounding in the whole computation. **Step four** writes `reinstatement_total_cents` as the base figure plus the administrative fee. The apparent circularity — an administrative fee that is a percentage of a total that includes the administrative fee — is resolved by that pinned order: the tier lookup reads the base, so a case whose base sits in one tier but whose total would sit in the next tier is charged at the base's tier.

Every suspension ever written against a practitioner, whether still `ACTIVE` or already `LIFTED`, is counted in that artist's `suspension_count`. Enforcement actions are readable individually and as a collection filterable by `establishment_id`, `artist_id`, `subject_type` and `status`.

### 3.9. Renewal, the Tiered Fee Schedule and Departmental Money

Permits run for a year and must be renewed. The operator opens an application by naming their `establishment_id`, the `requested_term_start_on` instant they would like the renewed term to begin, the two attestations `attested_bbp_current` (that every `ACTIVE` practitioner on the roster holds unexpired bloodborne-pathogen training) and `attested_sterilization_logs_complete` (that the trailing sterilization record set is complete), an optional `expedite_requested` flag defaulting to false, and the `expected_establishment_version` that guards the create as described in §2.8. The application is born `DRAFT` with `cycle_count` at zero, `payment_status` `UNPAID`, `late_fee_applied` false, `fee_paid_cents`, `fee_refunded_cents` and `fee_balance_due_cents` all zero, and `submitted_at`, `artist_count_at_submit`, `fee_estimate_cents`, `fee_final_cents`, `deficiency_code`, `decided_by_user_id` and `decided_at` all null.

Creation is also a housekeeping act. Every prior renewal application for the same premises sitting in `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` or `DEFICIENT` is moved to `SUPERSEDED`, each bumping its own `version` by one, and the establishment's own `version` increments by one as well. `SUPERSEDED`, `APPROVED` and `DENIED` are the three terminal states of this machine.

Submission is the owning operator's act — the caller's `X-User-ID` must equal the application's `establishment_id`. It drives `DRAFT → SUBMITTED` from a fresh application and `DEFICIENT → SUBMITTED` from one the department sent back, and the second of those is capped: a resubmission is admissible only while `cycle_count` is strictly below 3, and beyond that ceiling it is rejected with `ERR_RENEWAL_CYCLE_LIMIT` (HTTP 409). Either way, submission stamps `submitted_at` with the current logical-clock instant, refreshes `artist_count_at_submit` from the establishment's current `active_artist_count`, increments `cycle_count` by one, clears `deficiency_code` back to null, and writes `fee_estimate_cents` and `late_fee_applied` from the estimate variant of the fee schedule.

The fee schedule is a four-line tiered computation, and its rounding order is pinned. Let the artist count be the number of practitioners at the premises holding `permit_status` `ACTIVE`; practitioners in `PENDING`, `PROBATION`, `SUSPENDED`, `INACTIVE` or `REVOKED` do not count. The **base line** is a flat 25000 cents. The **artist line** is tiered: the first three artists are charged 7500 cents each, artists four through eight are charged 6000 cents each, and every artist beyond the eighth is charged 4500 cents each — so a roster of five produces three charges at the first rate and two at the second, and a roster of ten produces three, five and two charges at the three rates respectively. The **technology line** is 1050 cents for every artist in the count, untiered. The **expedite line** is 10000 cents when `expedite_requested` is true and nothing when it is false.

Each of those four raw lines is then multiplied by the county administrative multiplier of 1.075 and that product is rounded half up to a whole cent **individually**, and only then are the four rounded lines added together to form the subtotal. Summing the raw lines first and rounding once at the end is explicitly not the rule and produces a different subtotal in the general case. The late line is computed afterwards, on the already-rounded subtotal: the application is late when the establishment's `permit_expires_on` is non-null and the evaluation instant is strictly after it, in which case the late fee is 15 percent of the subtotal rounded half up to a whole cent, and `late_fee_applied` records whether that line was included. The total is the subtotal plus the late fee.

That computation runs twice against the same application, and the two runs can legitimately disagree. The **estimate** variant is written to `fee_estimate_cents` at submission, using `artist_count_at_submit` as the artist count and `submitted_at` as the evaluation instant. The **final** variant is written to `fee_final_cents` at approval, using the establishment's `active_artist_count` re-read at that moment as the artist count and `decided_at` as the evaluation instant. Artists activated or deactivated between submission and approval move the count, and an approval recorded after `permit_expires_on` adds the late line even when the submission itself was on time, so the two figures differ for identical stored inputs. The interaction with expiry is worth spelling out: once the clock sweep has moved a permit to `EXPIRED`, every artist on that roster is `INACTIVE`, so a late final evaluation legitimately sees an artist count of zero and charges base plus late only, until the roster is reinstated.

Money is recorded against the application by either the operator or a `RecordsClerk`, who supplies `amount_cents`. The payment must settle the outstanding amount exactly: while the application is `SUBMITTED` or `DEFICIENT` the outstanding amount is `fee_estimate_cents` less `fee_paid_cents`, and once the application is `APPROVED` it is `fee_balance_due_cents`. Any other figure is rejected with `ERR_FEE_AMOUNT_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422), and a payment against an application in any other status is rejected with `ERR_RENEWAL_STATE_INVALID` (HTTP 409). A successful payment adds `amount_cents` to `fee_paid_cents`, sets `payment_status` to `PAID`, recomputes `fee_balance_due_cents` as `fee_final_cents` less `fee_paid_cents` floored at zero — a figure that stays at zero for as long as `fee_final_cents` is null — refreshes the establishment's `fees_paid_cents`, and increments the establishment's `version` by one. The establishment's `fees_paid_cents` and `fees_refunded_cents` are simple sums of `fee_paid_cents` and `fee_refunded_cents` across every renewal application of that premises in any status.

Review belongs to a `HealthOfficer`, who supplies a `decision` of `APPROVE`, `MARK_DEFICIENT` or `DENY`, all three admissible only from `SUBMITTED`. Approval is guarded by five conditions evaluated in this order: the establishment's `open_critical_violation_count` must be zero, failing which the approval is rejected with `ERR_OPEN_CRITICAL_VIOLATION_BLOCKS_RENEWAL` (HTTP 409); the establishment's `sterilization_compliance_status` must be `COMPLIANT` or `DEFICIENT`, with `NONCOMPLIANT` rejected as `ERR_STERILIZATION_NONCOMPLIANT` (HTTP 409); no enforcement action against that establishment may still be `ACTIVE`, failing which the rejection is `ERR_ACTIVE_ENFORCEMENT_BLOCKS_RENEWAL` (HTTP 409); both `attested_bbp_current` and `attested_sterilization_logs_complete` must be true, failing which the rejection is `ERR_RENEWAL_ATTESTATION_MISSING` (HTTP 422); and `payment_status` must be `PAID`, failing which the rejection is `ERR_RENEWAL_FEE_UNPAID` (HTTP 402). Note that a `DEFICIENT` sterilization verdict blocks nothing here — only outright non-compliance does.

An approval drives `SUBMITTED → APPROVED`, writes `fee_final_cents` and sets `fee_balance_due_cents` to `fee_final_cents` less `fee_paid_cents` floored at zero, and stamps `decided_by_user_id` and `decided_at`. It then extends the permit. An establishment sitting in `EXPIRED` moves `EXPIRED → ACTIVE`; one still in `ACTIVE` re-enters `ACTIVE` from `ACTIVE`. In both cases `permit_issued_on` is set to the decision instant and `permit_expires_on` to exactly 365 days after that instant — the unused remainder of the old term is not carried forward, so an early renewal costs the operator the leftover days. The revival branch additionally restores every `INACTIVE` artist at the premises to `ACTIVE` in ascending `created_at` order with each artist's `permit_expires_on` refreshed from the new establishment expiry, then recomputes `active_artist_count`, `risk_score` and `operating_posture`; the still-active branch refreshes `permit_expires_on` on every `ACTIVE` artist there. Both increment the establishment's `version` by one.

A deficiency decision drives `SUBMITTED → DEFICIENT` and records the supplied `deficiency_code`, which is one of `MISSING_BBP_ATTESTATION`, `MISSING_STERILIZATION_ATTESTATION`, `OPEN_CRITICAL_VIOLATION` or `ARTIST_ROSTER_MISMATCH`, alongside `decided_by_user_id` and `decided_at`. A denial drives `SUBMITTED → DENIED` and stamps the same two decision fields. Both leave the money alone.

Returning money is the `RecordsClerk`'s exclusive act, and it is deliberately narrow: a refund requires `payment_status` of `PAID` and an application status of either `DENIED` or `SUPERSEDED`, and any other combination is rejected with `ERR_REFUND_NOT_ALLOWED` (HTTP 409). The refund sets `fee_refunded_cents` equal to `fee_paid_cents` and `payment_status` to `REFUNDED`, then refreshes the establishment's `fees_refunded_cents` so that the difference between the establishment's `fees_paid_cents` and `fees_refunded_cents` returns to its pre-payment figure, and increments the establishment's `version` by one. Renewal applications are readable individually and as a collection filterable by `establishment_id`, `status` and `payment_status`.

Finally, the department needs one place to look. A computed compliance read over a single establishment returns, without persisting anything, a payload containing the `establishment_id` and current `permit_status` and `operating_posture`; both risk variants as `risk_score_provisional` and `risk_score_audit`; the `sterilization_compliance_status` verdict together with `sterilization_candidate_count` (the number of records in the trailing 30-day candidate set after superseded records are removed) and `sterilization_pass_count` (how many of those candidates carry a `PASS`); the `open_violation_count`, `open_critical_violation_count` and `active_artist_count` counters; and three minor-consent audit figures — `minor_procedure_count_in_window`, `flagged_minor_procedure_count` and the derived `minor_consent_audit_rate`.

That last rate is computed over a trailing 90-day window, closed at both ends, running from the instant 90 days before the current logical-clock instant up to and including that instant. Its denominator, reported as `minor_procedure_count_in_window`, counts every procedure log of this establishment whose `client_age` is strictly below 18 and whose `started_at` falls inside the window; adult sessions are excluded from both the denominator and the numerator. Its numerator, reported as `flagged_minor_procedure_count`, counts how many of those same logs carry `audit_status` `FLAGGED`. The rate is the numerator divided by the denominator, rounded half up to four decimal places. When the denominator is zero the rate is the literal value 0.0 — never null, and never an error.
