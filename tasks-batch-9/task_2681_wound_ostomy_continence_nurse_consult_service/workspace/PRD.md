# Woc Consult Service API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Wound, ostomy and continence (WOC) nursing is one of the few hospital services where a documentation error is itself a reportable clinical harm. A pressure injury that was already present when the patient arrived on the ward is a quality-of-care fact about somebody else's institution; the identical wound, first documented thirty hours after admission, is a hospital-acquired pressure injury that triggers a root cause analysis, moves the unit's public reporting numbers, and — in this service's design — freezes the unit's ability to spend rental money on specialty beds. The difference between those two outcomes is a timestamp and a second nurse's signature. This API exists to make that difference auditable.

The service covers the whole span of a hospital WOC consult team's work: bedside consult requests raised by charge nurses and triaged by acuity into service-level bands; bedside skin assessment with staging, measurement and present-on-admission adjudication under a strict two-person control; the pressure injury case file and its root cause analysis; hospital-wide prevalence and incidence survey rounds that report per-unit rates from the same observation data using deliberately different inclusion sets; a rental fleet of support surfaces with a tiered per-day cost model, a vendor service fee, and a per-unit encumbrance ledger that must never be overspent; formulary-governed standardized dressing change protocols; ostomy education with teach-back scoring, outpatient supply transition and follow-up booking; and the internal certification pipeline that decides which nurses may carry which clinical workload.

Two design themes recur throughout and should be read as requirements rather than as commentary. First, **the same clinical fact is scored twice**: a wound's acuity is computed once from what the requesting unit claimed at intake and again from what the WOC nurse actually staged and measured at the bedside, and because those two scores can land in different priority bands, the SLA clock moves under the consult. Second, **money and logistics are separate dimensions of the same rental order**: a bed can be delivered without any money moving, and an invoice can be reconciled without the bed moving, so the two state dimensions must never be coupled. All timing rules — SLA deadlines, present-on-admission windows, reclean buffers, RCA due dates, certification recency decay — are evaluated against a server-held logical clock rather than wall-clock time, so that every deadline in the system is deterministically reproducible.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. Clinical Principals and Role-Based Access Control

Every request to this API must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the acting principal, and `X-User-Role`, naming the role that principal is acting in. If either header is absent, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the five role names below, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401) before any other processing occurs. If both headers are well-formed but the named role is not among the roles authorized for the target operation (see the table in §4), the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (HTTP 403).

The five roles are `WocNurse`, `UnitCharge`, `WocProgramDirector`, `MaterialsCoordinator` and `SystemOperator`, spelled exactly that way.

A `WocNurse` is a wound/ostomy/continence nurse, certified or in training, who performs the bedside clinical work: staging wounds, validating another nurse's staging, authoring dressing protocols, delivering ostomy education, running root cause analyses and walking survey rounds. When `X-User-Role` is `WocNurse`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting WOC nurse record; ownership and segregation-of-duties checks on any resource that references a WOC nurse compare that resource's nurse-reference field to `X-User-ID`. Concretely, the nurse who files a skin assessment has their own `id` recorded as `staged_by_nurse_id` on that assessment — the value is taken from the header and any client-supplied value in the payload is ignored — and later checks compare `X-User-ID` against `staged_by_nurse_id` and against a case's `rca_lead_nurse_id`.

A `UnitCharge` is the charge nurse of an inpatient unit. This role admits and discharges patients, raises consult requests for the patients on its own unit, resubmits consults that were returned for more information, and reads unit-level survey reporting. When `X-User-Role` is `UnitCharge`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting unit; ownership checks on any resource that references a unit compare the resource's unit-reference field to `X-User-ID`.

The remaining three roles are operator identities not tied to any persisted business row. When `X-User-Role` is `WocProgramDirector`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier: the director owns the formulary, the survey programme, the certification pipeline, unit budget caps, consult assignment, consult cancellation, staging disputes and case closure. The server may record that identifier where a rule says so — for example as `validated_by_user_id` on a skin assessment the director validates — but it never compares it to any entity's `id`. When `X-User-Role` is `MaterialsCoordinator`, `X-User-ID` is likewise an opaque operator identifier; the coordinator owns the rental fleet, the rental approval decision, the unit rental encumbrance ledger and vendor reconciliation, and holds no clinical authority. When `X-User-Role` is `SystemOperator`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier as well; this principal exists solely to position the server's logical clock and holds neither clinical nor fiscal authority.

Note that validation of a skin assessment is open to both `WocNurse` and `WocProgramDirector`, and the self-validation prohibition described in §2.7 is conditioned on the role: it bites when the validator acts as `WocNurse`, because only then is `X-User-ID` comparable to a nurse record.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every collection-listing operation is paginated with two query parameters. `limit` controls page size: it defaults to 10 when omitted, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` controls the starting position: it defaults to 0 when omitted and its minimum accepted value is 0, with no upper bound. A `limit` below 1 or above 100, or a negative `offset`, is a schema-level failure and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400) rather than being silently clamped.

Unless a specific listing says otherwise, results are ordered by `created_at` ascending. The `meta` block of a list response carries `limit` and `offset` as they were resolved for that request, `total_count` as the number of rows matching the supplied filters before pagination is applied, and `has_more` as a boolean that is true exactly when `offset` plus the number of returned rows is less than `total_count`.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and the Logical Clock

Every resource carries an `id` that is a server-minted UUID string with no type prefix of any kind; clients never supply it. Every resource also carries a `created_at` timestamp, formatted as an ISO 8601 UTC instant with exactly three fractional-second digits (for example `2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z`), stamped at insertion.

Most resources additionally carry an `updated_at` timestamp in the same format and an integer `version`. `version` starts at 1 when the row is created and increases by exactly 1 on every accepted mutation of that row, whether the mutation was requested directly or arrived as a cascade from a different entity's operation. `updated_at` is refreshed to the logical clock on every such mutation. The one exception is the survey observation: a survey observation is an immutable audit record and carries only `id` and `created_at` — it has neither `updated_at` nor `version`, and there is no operation that mutates one after it is filed.

All deadline, window, expiry and buffer arithmetic in this system reads a server-held **logical clock** rather than the host's wall clock. Before the clock has ever been positioned, it reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`, and the server must not fall back to real time. A `SystemOperator`-only administrative capability repositions the clock to a supplied instant, taking a single `to_iso8601` field in its payload and answering with an object whose sole member is `current_utc`, the instant the clock now reads. Repositioning the clock writes no business row and increments no `version`.

### 2.5. Path Resolution, Reference Resolution and Referential Coherence

Identifiers embedded in a URL path are resolved strictly left to right, and the first one that fails to resolve determines the error. Each identifier has its own dedicated not-found code, all at HTTP 404: `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND` for a `unit_id` path segment, `ERR_PATIENT_NOT_FOUND` for `patient_id`, `ERR_NURSE_NOT_FOUND` for `nurse_id`, `ERR_CONSULT_NOT_FOUND` for `consult_id`, `ERR_ASSESSMENT_NOT_FOUND` for `assessment_id`, `ERR_CASE_NOT_FOUND` for `case_id`, `ERR_SURVEY_ROUND_NOT_FOUND` for `round_id`, `ERR_OBSERVATION_NOT_FOUND` for `observation_id`, `ERR_SURFACE_NOT_FOUND` for `surface_id`, `ERR_ORDER_NOT_FOUND` for `order_id`, `ERR_PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND` for `product_id`, `ERR_PROTOCOL_NOT_FOUND` for `protocol_id` and `ERR_PLAN_NOT_FOUND` for `plan_id`.

Foreign-key identifiers supplied inside a request body are resolved after every path identifier has resolved, and they are resolved in the order in which the endpoint declares them. The same not-found codes apply, keyed by what the reference points at rather than by the field name: a payload `patient_id`, `unit_id`, `home_unit_id`, `requesting_unit_id`, `assigned_nurse_id`, `rca_lead_nurse_id`, `observer_nurse_id`, `consult_request_id`, `support_surface_id`, `formulary_product_id`, `survey_round_id` or `skin_assessment_id` that matches no row yields the not-found code for that row's type.

Once all references resolve individually, the server checks that they belong together, rejecting any disagreement with `ERR_ENTITY_LINK_MISMATCH` (HTTP 422). Five agreements are enforced: a consult request's `requesting_unit_id` must equal the `unit_id` of its patient; a skin assessment's `patient_id` must equal the `patient_id` of the consult request it answers; a rental order's `unit_id` must equal the `unit_id` of its patient; a survey observation's `unit_id` must equal the `unit_id` of its patient *and* must be one of the elements of its round's `participating_unit_ids`; and an ostomy education plan's `patient_id` must equal the `patient_id` of the consult request it hangs from.

### 2.6. Optimistic Concurrency on Patient and Unit Parents

Exactly two write operations in this API are guarded by optimistic concurrency control, and in both cases the guarded quantity is the `version` of a *parent* row rather than of the row being written.

Filing a bedside skin assessment requires a non-optional `expected_patient_version` field in the payload, carrying the `version` the client last observed on that patient. If it does not equal the patient's current `version`, the assessment is not created and the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_PATIENT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). This guard exists because a second assessment for the same patient and body site legitimately supersedes the first, so no state precondition would stop the write; the stale-version check is what stops a writer whose view of the patient predates that supersession from silently discarding it. A client seeds the field by reading the patient record, whose response includes `version`.

Approving a support surface rental order requires a non-optional `expected_unit_version` field in the payload, carrying the `version` the client last observed on the unit whose rental ledger will be charged. If it does not equal that unit's current `version`, the approval is rejected with `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Two distinct requested orders on the same unit each satisfy every state precondition on their own and each would appear affordable against the ledger the client read; this check forces the second approver to re-read `rental_available_cents` before committing more of the same budget.

No other operation in this API accepts a version field, and none may be made to require one.

### 2.7. Two-Person Clinical Controls

The clinical controls in this domain are two-person controls by design, and all three of them share a single rejection code, `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 422). First, when a skin assessment is validated by a caller acting as `WocNurse`, `X-User-ID` must not equal that assessment's `staged_by_nurse_id` — a nurse may not confirm their own staging. Second, when a root cause analysis is opened on a pressure injury case, the nominated `rca_lead_nurse_id` must not equal the `staged_by_nurse_id` of the skin assessment that originated that case — the nurse who documented the injury may not lead the investigation into it. Third, and in the opposite direction, only the nurse who staged an assessment may re-stage it after a dispute: on that operation `X-User-ID` must equal `staged_by_nurse_id`, and any other caller is refused with the same code.

### 2.8. Precision, Rounding and the Money Discipline

All monetary amounts in this system are integer counts of cents; there are no fractional-cent fields and no floating-point money in any payload. Percentages, rates and scores are decimal numbers with a pinned scale.

Four precision rules govern the calculations described in §3, and each names both a scale and a rounding method. Consult acuity is carried to **1 decimal place**, rounded half up. Survey prevalence and incidence rates, and the nurse certification eligibility score, are carried to **2 decimal places**, rounded half up. Rental cost projections are whole cents, rounded half up **once per billable day and never on the total**. Vendor service fees are whole cents, rounded half up on the fee amount only, after which the addition that produces the authorized cost is exact integer arithmetic.

Two evaluation orders are pinned and must not be reversed, because reversing either produces a different legal-looking answer. In the acuity calculation, the weighted raw figure is rounded to one decimal place **first** and only then clamped to the ceiling of 100; clamping before rounding is forbidden. In the rental projection, each day's charge is rounded to whole cents **first** and the rounded per-day figures are then summed; summing unrounded per-day rates and rounding the total once is forbidden.

### 2.9. Cascade Discipline and Derived-Counter Consistency

Several operations in this service mutate rows belonging to entities other than the one addressed. Those secondary mutations are not optional, are applied inside the same request, and each of them bumps the `version` and refreshes the `updated_at` of every row it touches — not merely of the row the caller addressed. A caller who reads a unit immediately after validating a skin assessment on one of its patients must see the unit's counters and `version` already moved.

Derived counters are recomputed rather than remembered. A unit's `rental_available_cents` is always its `rental_budget_cap_cents` minus its `rental_encumbered_cents` minus its `rental_committed_cents`, recomputed on every ledger mutation. A unit's `survey_posture` is always re-derived from its `open_hapi_rca_count` on every change to that count, using a three-rung ladder: `FROZEN` when the open-RCA count is at least 4, otherwise `RESTRICTED` when it is at least 2, otherwise `OPEN`. A nurse's `max_consult_load` is always re-derived from their `certification_status`. Compensating operations reverse their originating cascade exactly: closing a hospital-acquired case decrements the very counter its classification incremented and lifts the very posture holds that increment caused.

### 2.10. Validation Order

When more than one rule could reject a request, the rules are evaluated in the fixed ascending order below, the first failing rule wins, and no later rule is evaluated. This ordering is normative: a request that violates two rules must surface the earlier one's code. The ladder is intentionally shaped so that authentication precedes shape, shape precedes existence, existence precedes cross-reference coherence, coherence precedes state, state precedes optimistic concurrency, concurrency precedes clinical safety, safety precedes temporal and capacity contention, contention precedes ordinary business constraints, and fiscal checks come last.

1. Headers present and `X-User-Role` one of the five declared roles — `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (401), on every endpoint.
2. The caller's role appears in the target endpoint's authorized-role list — `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED` (403), on every endpoint.
3. Request-body and query-string schema conformance: every required field present, every type correct, every enum member legal, every numeric range and string length honored, `limit` within 1 through 100 and `offset` at least 0 — `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (400), on every endpoint.
4. Every identifier in the URL path resolves, left to right — the per-type 404 codes listed in §2.5, on every path-parameterised endpoint.
5. Every foreign-key identifier in the request body resolves, in the endpoint's declared field order — the per-type 404 codes, on `POST /patients`, `POST /woc-nurses`, `POST /consult-requests`, `POST /consult-requests/{consult_id}/assign`, `POST /skin-assessments`, `POST /pressure-injury-cases/{case_id}/rca-start`, `POST /survey-rounds`, `POST /survey-observations`, `POST /support-surface-orders`, `POST /dressing-protocols` and `POST /ostomy-education-plans`.
6. Cross-entity field agreement as enumerated in §2.5 — `ERR_ENTITY_LINK_MISMATCH` (422), on `POST /consult-requests`, `POST /skin-assessments`, `POST /support-surface-orders`, `POST /survey-observations` and `POST /ostomy-education-plans`.
10. The consult request's `status` is a legal starting state for the requested transition — `ERR_CONSULT_INVALID_STATE` (409), on `POST /consult-requests/{consult_id}/triage`, `/return`, `/resubmit`, `/assign`, `/escalate`, `/close` and `/cancel`, and also on `POST /skin-assessments`, `POST /dressing-protocols` and `POST /ostomy-education-plans`, each of which requires its referenced consult to be in a permitted state.
11. The skin assessment's `validation_status` is a legal starting state for the requested transition — `ERR_ASSESSMENT_INVALID_STATE` (409), on `POST /skin-assessments/{assessment_id}/validate`, `/dispute` and `/restage`.
12. The rental order's `fulfillment_status` is a legal starting state: approve and deny require `REQUESTED`, deliver requires `APPROVED`, return requires `DELIVERED` — `ERR_ORDER_NOT_PENDING` (409), on `POST /support-surface-orders/{order_id}/approve`, `/deny`, `/deliver` and `/return`.
13. The rental order's `billing_status` is a legal starting state for the requested billing transition: reconcile requires `COMMITTED` — `ERR_ORDER_INVALID_BILLING_STATE` (409), on `POST /support-surface-orders/{order_id}/reconcile`.
14. The pressure injury case's `status` is a legal starting state: rca-start requires `RCA_REQUIRED`, rca-complete requires `RCA_IN_PROGRESS`, close requires either `RCA_COMPLETE` or `CLASSIFIED_POA` — `ERR_CASE_INVALID_STATE` (409), on `POST /pressure-injury-cases/{case_id}/rca-start`, `/rca-complete` and `/close`.
15. The survey round's `status` is a legal starting state for the requested transition, and filing an observation additionally requires the round to be `OPEN` — `ERR_SURVEY_ROUND_NOT_OPEN` (409), on `POST /survey-rounds/{round_id}/open`, `/lock`, `/publish`, `/cancel` and on `POST /survey-observations`.
16. The patient's `status` is `ADMITTED` wherever a clinical write is attempted against them — `ERR_PATIENT_DISCHARGED` (409), on `POST /consult-requests`, `POST /consult-requests/{consult_id}/triage`, `POST /consult-requests/{consult_id}/assign`, `POST /skin-assessments`, `POST /support-surface-orders`, `POST /survey-observations` and `POST /patients/{patient_id}/discharge`.
17. Catalogue rows are live: the referenced support surface's `status` is not `RETIRED`, the referenced formulary product's `status` is `ACTIVE`, and a surface being retired has no order on it whose `fulfillment_status` is `REQUESTED`, `APPROVED` or `DELIVERED` — `ERR_RESOURCE_NOT_ACTIVE` (409), on `POST /support-surface-orders`, `POST /support-surfaces/{surface_id}/retire`, `POST /dressing-protocols` and `POST /formulary-products/{product_id}/archive`.
20. The supplied parent version matches the live parent version — `expected_patient_version` against the patient's `version` yielding `ERR_STALE_PATIENT_VERSION`, `expected_unit_version` against the unit's `version` yielding `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION`, both HTTP 412, on `POST /skin-assessments` and `POST /support-surface-orders/{order_id}/approve` respectively.
30. Segregation of duties as described in §2.7 — `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (422), on `POST /skin-assessments/{assessment_id}/validate`, `POST /skin-assessments/{assessment_id}/restage` and `POST /pressure-injury-cases/{case_id}/rca-start`.
32. Certification gating of nurse references: an assignment target's `certification_status` must be `CANDIDATE` or `CERTIFIED`, an RCA lead's must be exactly `CERTIFIED`, and a survey observer's must be `CANDIDATE` or `CERTIFIED` — `ERR_NURSE_NOT_CERTIFIED` (422), on `POST /consult-requests/{consult_id}/assign`, `POST /pressure-injury-cases/{case_id}/rca-start` and `POST /survey-observations`.
34. A dressing protocol may only be filed when its consult's `protocol_hold` is false and the consult's most recently created skin assessment has `validation_status` equal to `VALIDATED` — `ERR_STAGE_NOT_VALIDATED` (422), on `POST /dressing-protocols`.
36. A consult may only close when every pressure injury case originating from its assessments has a `status` of `CLASSIFIED_POA`, `RCA_COMPLETE`, `CLOSED` or `RECLASSIFIED` — `ERR_RCA_NOT_COMPLETE` (409), on `POST /consult-requests/{consult_id}/close`.
40. `present_on_admission_confirmed` may only be accepted as true when the assessment's `observed_at` is at or before the patient's `admitted_at` plus 24 hours — `ERR_POA_WINDOW_CLOSED` (422), on `POST /skin-assessments/{assessment_id}/validate`.
42. A survey observation's `observed_at` must lie inside the closed interval from its round's `window_start` through its round's `window_end`, and a supplied `onset_at` must be at or before that `observed_at` — `ERR_OBSERVATION_OUTSIDE_WINDOW` (422), on `POST /survey-observations`.
44. Survey window contention on a shared unit — `ERR_SURVEY_WINDOW_CONFLICT` (409), on `POST /survey-rounds/{round_id}/open`; the arithmetic is given in §3.7.
46. Support surface occupation contention — `ERR_SURFACE_UNAVAILABLE` (409), on `POST /support-surface-orders/{order_id}/approve`; the arithmetic is given in §3.8.
50. The consult's `revision_count` is strictly below 2 — `ERR_REVISION_LIMIT_REACHED` (409), on both `POST /consult-requests/{consult_id}/return` and `POST /consult-requests/{consult_id}/resubmit`.
52. The assignment target's `active_consult_load` is strictly below their `max_consult_load`, evaluated before any increment; re-assigning the nurse who already holds the consult skips this check entirely — `ERR_NURSE_LOAD_EXCEEDED` (422), on `POST /consult-requests/{consult_id}/assign`.
54. At most one survey observation exists per combination of `survey_round_id` and `patient_id` — `ERR_DUPLICATE_OBSERVATION` (409), on `POST /survey-observations`.
55. Natural keys are unique: a unit's `unit_code`, a patient's `mrn`, a support surface's `asset_tag` and a formulary product's `sku` — `ERR_DUPLICATE_NATURAL_KEY` (409), on `POST /units`, `POST /patients`, `POST /support-surfaces` and `POST /formulary-products`.
56. A non-blank `justification` of 1 to 500 characters is present whenever the selected product's `formulary_tier` is `NON_FORMULARY` — `ERR_NON_FORMULARY_JUSTIFICATION_REQUIRED` (422), on `POST /dressing-protocols`.
58. The unit's `survey_posture` is not `FROZEN` when a rental order is created, and an order whose `requires_director_override` is true is only approved when `director_override` is supplied as true — `ERR_UNIT_POSTURE_RESTRICTED` (409), on `POST /support-surface-orders` and `POST /support-surface-orders/{order_id}/approve`.
60. Every unit id in the round's `participating_unit_ids` has at least one observation for that round before the round may be locked — `ERR_SURVEY_UNIT_COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE` (409), on `POST /survey-rounds/{round_id}/lock`.
62. The certification pipeline gate: certifying requires a `certification_eligibility_score` of at least 55.00, `ce_credits` of at least 30 and `competency_signoff_count` of at least 2, while advancing to candidate requires `competency_signoff_count` of at least 1 — `ERR_CERTIFICATION_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (422), on `POST /woc-nurses/{nurse_id}/certify` and `POST /woc-nurses/{nurse_id}/advance`.
64. Ostomy plan eligibility: the patient's `ostomy_present` is true, the consult's `consult_type` is `OSTOMY`, and on supply transition the supplied `followup_appointment_at` is strictly after the logical clock — `ERR_OSTOMY_PLAN_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (422), on `POST /ostomy-education-plans` and `POST /ostomy-education-plans/{plan_id}/transition-supplies`.
70. The order's computed `billable_days` does not exceed its `authorized_days` — `ERR_RENTAL_DAYS_EXCEED_AUTHORIZATION` (422), on `POST /support-surface-orders/{order_id}/approve`.
72. The order's `authorized_cost_cents` does not exceed the unit's `rental_available_cents`, and a budget adjustment may not drive `rental_available_cents` below zero — `ERR_RENTAL_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` (402), on `POST /support-surface-orders/{order_id}/approve` and `POST /units/{unit_id}/budget-adjustments`.

Within a single rung, sub-checks are evaluated in the order they are written above. Note in particular rung 12 versus rung 20 on rental approval: an order that is no longer `REQUESTED` reports `ERR_ORDER_NOT_PENDING` even when the supplied `expected_unit_version` is also stale, and rung 58's override requirement is reached only after the version check has passed.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Units, Bed Capacity and the Rental Budget Ledger

The inpatient unit is the accounting and reporting anchor of the whole service. A `WocProgramDirector` registers one by supplying a `unit_code`, a `name` and a `bed_count`, and may optionally supply an opening `rental_budget_cap_cents`. The `unit_code` is the short identifier that appears on survey reports: it accepts only uppercase letters, digits and the hyphen, must be between 2 and 12 characters long, and must not already be in use by another unit — a collision is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_NATURAL_KEY`. The `name` is free text of 1 to 120 characters. The `bed_count` is the licensed bed count, at least 1 and at most 400, and it functions as the ceiling on how large an at-risk census a survey round may plausibly report for the unit. When `rental_budget_cap_cents` is omitted it defaults to 0, and it may never be negative.

Registration initialises the unit's rental ledger to a known-empty state: `rental_encumbered_cents`, `rental_committed_cents` and `rental_released_cents` all start at 0, `rental_available_cents` starts equal to `rental_budget_cap_cents`, `open_hapi_rca_count` and `disputed_staging_count` start at 0, and `survey_posture` starts at `OPEN`. Both reporting rates start as null: `last_prevalence_rate` and `last_incidence_rate` remain null until a survey round of the matching type has published for that unit.

Four of those ledger figures have distinct meanings and must not be conflated. `rental_encumbered_cents` is the sum of `authorized_cost_cents` across the unit's rental orders whose `billing_status` is `ENCUMBERED` — money promised but not yet spent. `rental_committed_cents` is the sum of `actual_cost_cents` across the unit's orders whose `billing_status` is either `COMMITTED` or `RECONCILED` — money actually spent, and it does not move again when an order is reconciled, because the committed figure already carries the realised cost in both of those states. `rental_released_cents` is a lifetime, monotonically non-decreasing audit counter of money handed back to the unit by a denial, a consult cancellation, or a downward revision at return time; nothing is ever subtracted from it. `rental_available_cents` is spendable headroom, recomputed on every ledger mutation as `rental_budget_cap_cents` less `rental_encumbered_cents` less `rental_committed_cents`; it may be exactly 0 but must never be negative, and every path that encumbers money checks it first.

The budget ceiling itself is mutable only through a director-only budget-adjustment capability, which takes a signed `delta_cents` and a mandatory `reason` and answers with the updated unit. The adjustment adds `delta_cents` to `rental_budget_cap_cents` — a negative delta lowers the cap — and then recomputes `rental_available_cents`. An adjustment that would drive `rental_available_cents` below zero, because the unit has already encumbered or committed more than the reduced cap, is refused with `ERR_RENTAL_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` and leaves the cap untouched.

`survey_posture` is not settable by anyone. It is a derived gate, re-derived on every change to `open_hapi_rca_count` — which itself counts the unit's pressure injury cases whose `status` is `RCA_REQUIRED`, `RCA_IN_PROGRESS` or `RCA_COMPLETE` — and it reads `FROZEN` when that count is 4 or more, `RESTRICTED` when it is 2 or 3, and `OPEN` when it is 0 or 1. A unit's `disputed_staging_count` is likewise derived and monotonically non-decreasing: it counts, over the unit's lifetime, every skin assessment for one of its patients that has entered a disputed staging state. Units can be listed with a `survey_posture` filter, which is the fastest way for a coordinator to find the wards whose rental spending is currently gated, and a single unit can be read to see its live ledger, its posture and its `version`.

### 3.2. Patient Admission, the Record, and Discharge

A `UnitCharge` or a `WocProgramDirector` admits a patient by supplying an `mrn`, the `unit_id` of the receiving unit, and an `admitted_at` instant. The `mrn` is the medical record number, 6 to 16 characters, unique across all patients — a reuse is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_NATURAL_KEY`. The `admitted_at` value is an ISO 8601 UTC instant at second precision (fractional seconds are accepted but not required) and must not be later than the logical clock at the moment of admission; it anchors the present-on-admission documentation window that §3.6 depends on. The `unit_id` is fixed at admission and is not changed afterwards.

Three optional clinical flags round out the record and all three feed the acuity calculation. `comorbidity_count` counts acuity-relevant comorbidities, defaults to 0, and must be between 0 and 10 inclusive. `device_related_risk` defaults to false and is set true when a medical device — a tracheostomy collar, a brace, a length of tubing — sits over an at-risk skin site. `ostomy_present` defaults to false and is set true when the patient has a stoma; it is a hard precondition for opening an ostomy education plan.

Two further fields on the patient record are computed and never supplied. `poa_skin_assessment_documented` starts false and is flipped to true the first time any skin assessment for that patient reaches a validated staging with a present-on-admission determination. `pending_outpatient_followup_count` starts at 0 and counts the patient's ostomy education plans whose `status` is `FOLLOWUP_SCHEDULED`.

A patient's `status` begins at `ADMITTED`. Discharge moves it `ADMITTED → DISCHARGED`, stamping the supplied `discharged_at` onto the record. The supplied `discharged_at` must be at or after `admitted_at`. Discharging a patient who is already `DISCHARGED` is refused with `ERR_PATIENT_DISCHARGED`, which is also the code raised whenever any clinical write — a new consult, a triage, an assignment, a bedside assessment, a survey observation or a rental order — is attempted against a discharged patient. Discharge is additionally blocked while the patient still has a rental order whose `fulfillment_status` is `APPROVED` or `DELIVERED`: a specialty bed cannot be left in the field against a discharged record, and the attempt is refused as a state conflict carrying `ERR_ORDER_NOT_PENDING`.

Patients can be listed with `unit_id` and `status` filters, which is how a caller discovers who is on a ward. Reading a single patient returns the full record including `version`, and that read is how a client seeds the `expected_patient_version` value described in §2.6.

### 3.3. The WOC Nurse Certification Pipeline

Nurses enter the service at the bottom of a four-rung certification ladder and may cycle back down and up again. A `WocProgramDirector` registers a nurse with a `full_name` of 1 to 120 characters, a `home_unit_id` naming the unit the nurse is administratively based on, and optionally an opening `ce_credits` figure. Being based on a unit does not restrict which units the nurse may consult on — `home_unit_id` is an administrative attribute only. `ce_credits` records continuing-education credits, defaults to 0, and must be between 0 and 200 inclusive. Registration sets `certification_status` to `TRAINEE`, `max_consult_load` to 0 and `active_consult_load` to 0.

`certification_status` takes exactly one of `TRAINEE`, `CANDIDATE`, `CERTIFIED` and `LAPSED`, and it is the sole determinant of `max_consult_load`: a `CERTIFIED` nurse may hold 8 concurrent consults, a `CANDIDATE` 3, and a `TRAINEE` or `LAPSED` nurse none at all. `active_consult_load` counts the nurse's consults whose `status` is `ASSIGNED`, `ASSESSED`, `PLAN_ACTIVE` or `ESCALATED` — note that an escalated consult still occupies load — and it is a conserved quantity: every operation that adds one to it somewhere must take one away somewhere else or be an outright assignment, and it may never exceed `max_consult_load` or fall below 0. `mentored_consult_count` counts the nurse's consults whose `status` is `CLOSED`.

Competency signoffs are recorded one at a time by the director against a named nurse, supplying a `signed_off_at` instant and a `competency_domain` label. Each accepted signoff adds 1 to `competency_signoff_count`, overwrites `last_competency_signoff_at` with the supplied `signed_off_at`, and recomputes `certification_eligibility_score`. Where several signoffs exist, `last_competency_signoff_at` is the `signed_off_at` of the most recent one, ordering by `signed_off_at` descending and breaking ties by `created_at` descending.

`certification_eligibility_score` is a weighted score on a 0.00-to-100.00 scale, carried to two decimal places, and it is null until the first recompute. It is recomputed on every competency signoff and again every time one of the nurse's consults is closed. It is built from three components. The **recency component** starts from a base of 100 and loses 5 points for each whole 30-day month elapsed since the last signoff: the elapsed time from `last_competency_signoff_at` to the logical clock is measured in whole hours and divided by 720, discarding any remainder, to give a month count; the component is 100 less 5 times that month count, floored at 0 so it never goes negative; and when `last_competency_signoff_at` is null the month count is treated as 0, which leaves the recency component at its full 100. The **credit component** is 2 points for each unit of `ce_credits`, capped at 60 points, so credits beyond 30 add nothing to this component. The **mentored component** is 10 points for each closed consult the nurse has mentored, counting at most 4 of them, so it saturates at 40 points. The score is then the recency component weighted at 0.4, plus the credit component weighted at 0.4, plus the mentored component weighted at 0.2, and that weighted sum is rounded half up to two decimal places once, at the end. The three components are themselves never individually rounded.

Advancement from `TRAINEE → CANDIDATE` requires at least 1 recorded competency signoff and sets `max_consult_load` to 3. Certification is reachable from either `CANDIDATE` or `LAPSED`, moving the nurse to `CERTIFIED`, stamping the supplied `cert_expires_at` onto the record and setting `max_consult_load` to 8; it demands all three gates simultaneously — a `certification_eligibility_score` of at least 55.00, `ce_credits` of at least 30, and `competency_signoff_count` of at least 2. Any of those gates failing, or an advancement attempted on a nurse who is not `TRAINEE`, or a certification attempted on a nurse who is neither `CANDIDATE` nor `LAPSED`, is refused with `ERR_CERTIFICATION_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. Lapsing takes a `CERTIFIED` nurse to `LAPSED` with a recorded `reason` and drops `max_consult_load` to 0; crucially, `active_consult_load` is left exactly as it was, because a lapse does not tear a nurse away from patients already in their care — it only blocks new assignments. Renewal closes the cycle: a `LAPSED` nurse who satisfies the same three gates returns `LAPSED → CERTIFIED` with a fresh `cert_expires_at` and a `max_consult_load` of 8 once more. Attempting to lapse a nurse who is not `CERTIFIED` is refused with the same eligibility code.

Nurses can be listed with `home_unit_id` and `certification_status` filters, and a single nurse can be read to inspect `certification_eligibility_score`, `active_consult_load`, `max_consult_load`, `competency_signoff_count`, `last_competency_signoff_at`, `mentored_consult_count` and `cert_expires_at`.

### 3.4. The Consult Lifecycle: Intake, Triage, Routing and Termination

A consult request is raised by a `UnitCharge` on behalf of a patient on their own ward. The payload names the `patient_id`, the `requesting_unit_id`, a `consult_type` of `WOUND`, `OSTOMY` or `INCONTINENCE`, a `reported_stage`, and a `clinical_note` of 1 to 2000 characters carrying the intake narrative. The `requesting_unit_id` must be the unit the patient is actually on, and a mismatch is an `ERR_ENTITY_LINK_MISMATCH`. The patient must be `ADMITTED`. The `reported_stage` is what the requesting unit *claims* to be seeing and is drawn from a wider vocabulary than a WOC nurse's own staging, because a bedside unit is allowed to say there is nothing to stage: it takes one of `NONE`, `MUCOSAL`, `STAGE_1`, `STAGE_2`, `STAGE_3`, `STAGE_4`, `DTI` or `UNSTAGEABLE`. A new consult starts with `status` `REQUESTED`, `revision_count` 0, `escalation_count` 0 and `protocol_hold` false; `acuity_score`, `acuity_variant`, `priority_band`, `triaged_at`, `sla_due_at`, `assigned_nurse_id` and `closing_summary` are all null.

The consult's `status` moves through `REQUESTED`, `TRIAGED`, `RETURNED_FOR_INFO`, `ASSIGNED`, `ASSESSED`, `PLAN_ACTIVE`, `ESCALATED`, and terminates in either `CLOSED` or `CANCELLED`.

**Triage.** A `WocNurse` triages a `REQUESTED` consult whose patient is still `ADMITTED`, moving it `REQUESTED → TRIAGED`. Triage takes no payload at all: everything it needs is already on the consult and the patient. It computes the **intake-provisional acuity**, the first of the two acuity variants this service maintains, and records `acuity_variant` as `INTAKE_PROVISIONAL`. The computation starts from a stage weight read off the claimed `reported_stage`: `NONE` contributes 0 points, `MUCOSAL` 15, `STAGE_1` 20, `STAGE_2` 35, `DTI` 45, `STAGE_3` 60, `UNSTAGEABLE` 70 and `STAGE_4` 80. To that is added a comorbidity contribution of 5 points for each of the patient's `comorbidity_count`, capped at 25 points, so a patient with 5 or more comorbidities contributes the same 25. A further 10 points are added when the patient's `device_related_risk` is true, and nothing when it is false. Under this variant the wound-area contribution is exactly 0, because the requesting unit supplies no measurement. Those four contributions are summed and the sum is multiplied by a consult-type multiplier: 1.00 for a `WOUND` consult, 1.15 for an `OSTOMY` consult and 0.90 for an `INCONTINENCE` consult. The product is then rounded half up to one decimal place, and only after that rounding is the result capped at 100 — rounding first and capping second is mandatory, and doing it the other way round is a defect.

The resulting `acuity_score` selects the `priority_band`: `P1` when the score is 70.0 or higher, otherwise `P2` when it is 40.0 or higher, otherwise `P3`. Triage sets `triaged_at` to the logical clock and derives `sla_due_at` as `triaged_at` plus the band's response allowance — 4 hours for `P1`, a full day for `P2`, and three days for `P3`. Whenever `priority_band` later changes, `sla_due_at` is re-derived from the same `triaged_at` with the new band's allowance; `triaged_at` itself is not moved by a recompute.

**Return and resubmission.** A triaged consult whose narrative is too thin to act on is sent back by the triaging `WocNurse`, moving it `TRIAGED → RETURNED_FOR_INFO` with a `reason`. The return deliberately leaves `clinical_note`, `acuity_score` and `priority_band` intact so the history is legible, but it clears `sla_due_at` to null, because the service is no longer on the clock while the ward is gathering information. The requesting `UnitCharge` then resubmits, moving the consult `RETURNED_FOR_INFO → REQUESTED` with a revised `clinical_note` and a revised `reported_stage`; resubmission adds 1 to `revision_count` and wipes the whole triage output — `acuity_score`, `acuity_variant`, `priority_band`, `triaged_at` and `sla_due_at` all return to null — so that triage runs again from scratch against the revised claim. This is a genuine cycle: `TRIAGED → RETURNED_FOR_INFO → REQUESTED → TRIAGED` may be walked twice. Both legs are gated on `revision_count` being strictly below 2, and once two revisions have been consumed both the return and the resubmit are refused with `ERR_REVISION_LIMIT_REACHED`.

**Assignment.** A `WocProgramDirector` routes a triaged consult to a nurse, moving it `TRIAGED → ASSIGNED` and writing the payload `assigned_nurse_id` onto the consult. The nominated nurse's `certification_status` must be `CANDIDATE` or `CERTIFIED` — a `TRAINEE` or `LAPSED` nurse is refused with `ERR_NURSE_NOT_CERTIFIED` — and their `active_consult_load` must be strictly below their `max_consult_load`, otherwise `ERR_NURSE_LOAD_EXCEEDED`. The patient must still be `ADMITTED`. On success the nominated nurse's `active_consult_load` rises by 1 and that nurse's row is versioned.

The same assignment capability also closes the escalation loop. An escalated consult is re-routed `ESCALATED → ASSIGNED`, and here the load arithmetic is conditional on whether the nurse actually changes. When the payload names a different nurse from the one currently holding the consult, the displaced nurse's `active_consult_load` falls by 1, the incoming nurse's rises by 1, and both nurse rows are versioned; the incoming nurse must satisfy the load headroom check. When the payload names the *same* nurse who already holds the consult, no load moves at all and the headroom check is skipped entirely — otherwise a nurse sitting exactly at their ceiling could never have their own escalated consult handed back to them. The certification check applies in both cases.

**Escalation.** When the clock runs out on an assigned consult, a `WocNurse` or a `WocProgramDirector` escalates it with a `reason`, moving it `ASSIGNED → ESCALATED` and adding 1 to `escalation_count`. Escalation is only permitted once the logical clock has passed `sla_due_at` strictly; escalating a consult that is still inside its allowance is not a legal transition from that state. The assigned nurse's `active_consult_load` is deliberately *not* reduced — the consult is late, not abandoned, and it continues to occupy the nurse's capacity.

**Assessment and planning.** Two further advances happen as automatic consequences of work described elsewhere rather than as calls against the consult itself. Filing a bedside skin assessment moves the consult `ASSIGNED → ASSESSED` and recomputes acuity under the assessed-final variant (see §3.5); a second assessment on an already-assessed consult holds it at `ASSESSED` in a self-transition and recomputes acuity again from the new row alone. Filing a dressing protocol moves the consult `ASSESSED → PLAN_ACTIVE`, and filing a revised protocol on an already-planned consult holds it at `PLAN_ACTIVE` in a self-transition (see §3.9). Both of those protocol legs additionally require that `protocol_hold` is false and that the consult's most recently created skin assessment is in a validated state.

**Closure.** Only a `PLAN_ACTIVE` consult may be closed, and only by a `WocNurse`, supplying a `closing_summary` narrative that is stored on the record. Closure is gated on adjudication: every pressure injury case originating from any of this consult's skin assessments must have a `status` of `CLASSIFIED_POA`, `RCA_COMPLETE`, `CLOSED` or `RECLASSIFIED`, and any case still `OPEN`, `CLASSIFIED_HAPI`, `RCA_REQUIRED` or `RCA_IN_PROGRESS` blocks the close with `ERR_RCA_NOT_COMPLETE`. Closing moves the consult `PLAN_ACTIVE → CLOSED` and reaches into the assigned nurse's record in three ways at once: `active_consult_load` falls by 1, `mentored_consult_count` rises by 1, and `certification_eligibility_score` is recomputed from the new mentored count, with the nurse row versioned for all three.

**Cancellation and its compensating cascade.** A `UnitCharge` or a `WocProgramDirector` may cancel a consult from any non-terminal state — `REQUESTED`, `TRIAGED`, `RETURNED_FOR_INFO`, `ASSIGNED`, `ASSESSED`, `ESCALATED` or `PLAN_ACTIVE` — supplying a `reason`. A consult already `CLOSED` or `CANCELLED` cannot be cancelled again, and the attempt is an `ERR_CONSULT_INVALID_STATE`. Cancellation is the single largest compensating operation in the service, and its effects are applied in a fixed sequence of hops, each caused by the one before it rather than by the original request.

The first hop sets the consult's `status` to `CANCELLED` and, when an `assigned_nurse_id` is present, reduces that nurse's `active_consult_load` by 1 and versions the nurse row. The second hop follows from the cancellation: every rental order that names this consult in its `consult_request_id` and whose `billing_status` is `ENCUMBERED` has its `billing_status` moved `ENCUMBERED → RELEASED`, and every such order whose `fulfillment_status` is still `REQUESTED` additionally moves `REQUESTED → DENIED` with its `denial_reason` recorded as the literal string `CONSULT_CANCELLED`; each affected order row is versioned. The third hop follows from the second: for every order whose billing moved to `RELEASED`, that order's unit has its `rental_encumbered_cents` reduced by the order's `authorized_cost_cents`, its `rental_released_cents` raised by the same `authorized_cost_cents`, its `rental_available_cents` recomputed, and its row versioned — the money is returned to headroom and simultaneously written into the lifetime release counter.

Two further branches hang off the first hop. Every dressing protocol naming this consult whose `status` is `ACTIVE` moves `ACTIVE → VOIDED` and is versioned. Every ostomy education plan naming this consult whose `status` is `ACTIVE`, `TEACH_BACK_PASSED`, `SUPPLIES_TRANSITIONED` or `FOLLOWUP_SCHEDULED` moves to `CLOSED_INCOMPLETE` with its `followup_appointment_at` cleared to null, and is versioned. Finally, following from that plan branch, each plan that was actually sitting in `FOLLOWUP_SCHEDULED` when it was closed reduces its patient's `pending_outpatient_followup_count` by 1 and versions the patient row; a plan that never reached `FOLLOWUP_SCHEDULED` leaves that counter alone, because it was never counted in the first place. The hops are applied in this order: consult and nurse first, then rental orders, then the unit ledger, then dressing protocols, then education plans, then the patient's follow-up counter.

Consults can be listed with `patient_id`, `requesting_unit_id`, `assigned_nurse_id`, `status` and `priority_band` filters, and a single consult can be read to inspect `acuity_score`, `acuity_variant`, `priority_band`, `sla_due_at`, `triaged_at`, `revision_count`, `escalation_count`, `protocol_hold` and `closing_summary`.

### 3.5. Bedside Staging, Validation and the Dispute Cycle

A skin assessment is the WOC nurse's own observation of a wound, and it is the document that turns a claim into a fact. Only a `WocNurse` may file one, and the filing names the `consult_request_id` it answers, the `patient_id`, the `anatomic_location`, the `observed_stage`, the `measured_area_cm2`, the `observed_at` instant and the `expected_patient_version` described in §2.6; a `present_on_admission_claimed` boolean may also be supplied and defaults to false. The referenced consult must be in `ASSIGNED` or `ASSESSED` — the latter because re-assessing an already-assessed consult is a legitimate and expected act — and the assessment's `patient_id` must be the same patient the consult is about.

`anatomic_location` is one of `SACRUM`, `HEEL_LEFT`, `HEEL_RIGHT`, `ISCHIUM`, `TROCHANTER` or `OTHER`, and it matters far beyond description: supersession, present-on-admission adjudication and case succession are all scoped to the pair of patient and body site. `observed_stage` is the nurse's own staging and, unlike the requesting unit's `reported_stage`, has no "nothing to see" member — it is one of `MUCOSAL`, `STAGE_1`, `STAGE_2`, `STAGE_3`, `STAGE_4`, `DTI` or `UNSTAGEABLE`. `measured_area_cm2` is the wound surface area in square centimetres, at least 0 and at most 500, with at most one decimal place. `observed_at` must be at or after the patient's `admitted_at` and at or before the logical clock. `present_on_admission_claimed` records what the filing nurse believes about present-on-admission status and is advisory only — nothing is decided by it until a validator acts.

The server, not the client, writes `staged_by_nurse_id`: it is copied from `X-User-ID`, and any value a client puts in the payload for it is ignored. A new assessment begins with `validation_status` `PENDING_VALIDATION`, with `poa_determination`, `confirmed_stage`, `validated_by_user_id` and `dispute_reason` all null and `restage_count` at 0. `validation_status` takes one of `PENDING_VALIDATION`, `VALIDATED`, `DISPUTED` or `SUPERSEDED`, and `SUPERSEDED` is terminal.

Filing an assessment does four things beyond inserting the row. It supersedes history: any earlier assessment for the same patient and the same `anatomic_location` whose `validation_status` is `PENDING_VALIDATION` or `VALIDATED` moves to `SUPERSEDED` and is versioned. It opens a case file: a pressure injury case is created with `classification` `UNDETERMINED`, `status` `OPEN`, its `skin_assessment_id` pointing at the new assessment, its `patient_id` copied from the assessment and its `unit_id` copied from the patient's `unit_id`. It advances the consult, `ASSIGNED → ASSESSED` on the first assessment and holding at `ASSESSED` on a later one. And it versions the patient row, which is precisely why a client's `expected_patient_version` goes stale after each filing.

**The assessed-final acuity variant.** Filing an assessment recomputes the consult's `acuity_score` under the second variant, setting `acuity_variant` to `ASSESSED_FINAL`. The structure is identical to the intake-provisional computation of §3.4 — the same stage weights, the same 5-points-per-comorbidity contribution capped at 25, the same 10 points for `device_related_risk`, the same consult-type multiplier, the same round-half-up-to-one-decimal-then-cap-at-100 order, and the same 70.0 and 40.0 band thresholds — with two differences. The stage weight is read from the WOC nurse's own `observed_stage` rather than from the ward's `reported_stage`. And the wound-area contribution is no longer zero: it awards 5 points for each whole 10 square centimetres of `measured_area_cm2`, discarding any remainder, capped at 15 points. So a wound of 9.9 square centimetres earns nothing, 10.0 earns 5, 29.9 earns 10, and anything from 30.0 upward earns the full 15. Because the two variants can differ substantially, the same wound routinely lands in a different `priority_band` after assessment than it did at triage, and `sla_due_at` is re-derived from the unchanged `triaged_at` accordingly. Only the single assessment row that drove the recompute participates: no other assessment for that patient enters the formula, so a row that has become `SUPERSEDED` never influences `acuity_score` again.

**Validation.** Either a `WocNurse` or a `WocProgramDirector` validates a pending assessment, supplying the `confirmed_stage` they are willing to stand behind and a `present_on_admission_confirmed` boolean. The `confirmed_stage` may legitimately differ from the `observed_stage`. When the validator acts as `WocNurse`, `X-User-ID` must not be the assessment's `staged_by_nurse_id`. When `present_on_admission_confirmed` is supplied as true, the assessment's `observed_at` must be at or before the patient's `admitted_at` plus 24 hours; a later observation cannot be confirmed as present on admission and the attempt is refused with `ERR_POA_WINDOW_CLOSED`. Validation moves the assessment `PENDING_VALIDATION → VALIDATED`, writes `confirmed_stage`, records `validated_by_user_id` as the acting `X-User-ID`, and computes `poa_determination`.

`poa_determination` is a chronological verdict, not a copy of the checkbox. Its candidate set is every skin assessment for the same patient and the same `anatomic_location` whose `validation_status` is `PENDING_VALIDATION`, `VALIDATED`, `DISPUTED` or `SUPERSEDED` — that is, every row that has ever documented that site. Those rows are ordered by `observed_at` ascending with `created_at` ascending as the sole tie-break; the row `id` is never consulted. The `observed_at` of the first row in that ordering is the earliest documentation of the site. The verdict is then `POA` whenever `present_on_admission_confirmed` was supplied as true; otherwise it is `HAPI` when `present_on_admission_confirmed` was false *and* that earliest documentation instant falls strictly later than the patient's `admitted_at` plus 24 hours; otherwise it is `INDETERMINATE`. The 24-hour bound is inclusive: an observation landing exactly on `admitted_at` plus 24 hours is inside the window. Including superseded rows in the candidate set is deliberate — a later re-documentation of the same site cannot retroactively convert an injury first seen on admission into a hospital-acquired one.

Validation then runs a five-deep cascade, each hop caused by the hop before it. The first hop is the assessment's own mutation just described, plus, when `poa_determination` came out `POA`, setting the patient's `poa_skin_assessment_documented` to true and versioning the patient. Alongside it, the consult's `acuity_score` is recomputed under the assessed-final variant using the newly pinned `confirmed_stage` — not `observed_stage` — together with `measured_area_cm2`, and `priority_band` and `sla_due_at` are re-derived and the consult versioned. The second hop reaches the case file: the pressure injury case whose `skin_assessment_id` is this assessment takes `classification` from the verdict and moves `OPEN → CLASSIFIED_POA` when the verdict was `POA`, `OPEN → CLASSIFIED_HAPI` when it was `HAPI`, and stays at `OPEN` with `classification` left `UNDETERMINED` when it was `INDETERMINATE`. The third hop fires only when the second hop reached `CLASSIFIED_HAPI`: that case immediately moves `CLASSIFIED_HAPI → RCA_REQUIRED` and its `rca_due_at` is set to the logical clock plus 72 hours. The fourth hop follows from the third: the case's unit has `open_hapi_rca_count` raised by 1 and `survey_posture` re-derived on the four-or-more-`FROZEN`, two-or-more-`RESTRICTED`, otherwise-`OPEN` ladder, and the unit is versioned. The fifth hop follows from the fourth, but only when that re-derivation actually changed `survey_posture` to `RESTRICTED` or `FROZEN`: every rental order on that unit whose `fulfillment_status` is still `REQUESTED` has `requires_director_override` set to true and `posture_hold_applied_at` stamped with the logical clock, and each such order is versioned. A single staging validation therefore ends four hops away, holding rental orders that have nothing to do with the patient assessed.

**Dispute — the divergent branch.** A `WocProgramDirector` who does not accept a pending staging disputes it instead, supplying a `dispute_reason`, and the assessment moves `PENDING_VALIDATION → DISPUTED`. The side-effect set here diverges sharply from validation and the difference is the point of the branch. `poa_determination` stays null and `confirmed_stage` stays null. No pressure injury case field is touched at all: the case remains `classification` `UNDETERMINED` and `status` `OPEN`, no root cause analysis is opened, and no unit posture moves. What *does* happen is a hold and a counter that validation never touches: the consult's `protocol_hold` is set to true and the consult versioned, and the unit of the assessment's patient has `disputed_staging_count` raised by 1 and is versioned. While `protocol_hold` is true, no dressing protocol may be filed on that consult.

**Restaging — closing the cycle.** Only the nurse who staged the assessment may re-stage it, and only from `DISPUTED`. The re-staging supplies a fresh `observed_stage` and a fresh `measured_area_cm2`, which overwrite the originals; `restage_count` rises by 1, `dispute_reason` is cleared to null, the assessment returns `DISPUTED → PENDING_VALIDATION`, and the consult's `protocol_hold` is released back to false with the consult versioned. The assessment can then be validated or disputed again, so `PENDING_VALIDATION → DISPUTED → PENDING_VALIDATION` is a genuine loop with no cap on the number of turns.

**Supersession of a validated row.** When a new assessment supersedes an earlier row that was already `VALIDATED`, the consequences run further than the plain `PENDING_VALIDATION → SUPERSEDED` case. The earlier row moves `VALIDATED → SUPERSEDED`; its pressure injury case, if that case's `status` is `OPEN`, `CLASSIFIED_POA`, `CLASSIFIED_HAPI` or `RCA_REQUIRED`, moves to `RECLASSIFIED`; and a successor case is created carrying `predecessor_case_id` pointing at the reclassified case, `skin_assessment_id` pointing at the *new* assessment, `classification` `UNDETERMINED` and `status` `OPEN`. If the reclassified case had been sitting in `CLASSIFIED_HAPI` or `RCA_REQUIRED`, its unit's `open_hapi_rca_count` falls by 1, `survey_posture` is re-derived and the unit versioned — a reversal that can lift a `FROZEN` or `RESTRICTED` posture as readily as a case closure can.

Assessments can be listed with `consult_request_id`, `patient_id`, `staged_by_nurse_id` and `validation_status` filters, and a single assessment can be read to inspect `poa_determination`, `confirmed_stage`, `validated_by_user_id`, `dispute_reason` and `restage_count`.

### 3.6. Pressure Injury Cases and Root Cause Analysis

Nobody creates a pressure injury case directly; every case is opened by the filing of a skin assessment and every field on it that is not a copied reference is written by a state transition. A case carries `patient_id` and `unit_id` copied at creation, `skin_assessment_id` naming the assessment whose filing opened it, and a nullable `predecessor_case_id` that is set only on a successor case born of a superseding assessment — the one self-referential link in this domain and the audit trail through a reversed determination. Its `classification` is one of `UNDETERMINED`, `POA` or `HAPI`, and its `status` is one of `OPEN`, `CLASSIFIED_POA`, `CLASSIFIED_HAPI`, `RCA_REQUIRED`, `RCA_IN_PROGRESS`, `RCA_COMPLETE`, `CLOSED` or `RECLASSIFIED`, of which `CLOSED` and `RECLASSIFIED` are terminal.

The fork at classification is where the two clinical stories separate. A case classified present-on-admission takes `classification` `POA` and lands in `CLASSIFIED_POA`; its unit's `open_hapi_rca_count` is explicitly *not* touched and no investigation is opened, because a wound the patient arrived with is not this hospital's incident. A case classified hospital-acquired takes `classification` `HAPI`, lands in `CLASSIFIED_HAPI`, and immediately advances again to `RCA_REQUIRED` with `rca_due_at` set to the logical clock plus 72 hours, at which point the unit's `open_hapi_rca_count` rises and its `survey_posture` is re-derived.

**Opening the investigation.** A `WocNurse` or a `WocProgramDirector` starts the analysis on a case in `RCA_REQUIRED`, nominating an `rca_lead_nurse_id`. The lead must be fully `CERTIFIED` — `CANDIDATE` is not sufficient here, unlike consult assignment — and must not be the nurse recorded as `staged_by_nurse_id` on the case's originating assessment. On success the case moves `RCA_REQUIRED → RCA_IN_PROGRESS` and the nominated lead is written to `rca_lead_nurse_id`.

**Recording the finding.** The nominated lead, and only the nominated lead, completes the analysis: the acting `X-User-ID` must equal the case's `rca_lead_nurse_id`. The payload carries a `root_cause_category` drawn from `REPOSITIONING`, `SUPPORT_SURFACE`, `NUTRITION`, `DEVICE`, `MOISTURE` and `DOCUMENTATION`, plus a `contributing_factors` list of between 1 and 6 strings, each 1 to 120 characters long. An empty list is not acceptable. The case moves `RCA_IN_PROGRESS → RCA_COMPLETE` with both fields stored.

**Closure and the exact reversal.** A `WocProgramDirector` closes an adjudicated case with a `closure_note`, and closure is reachable from exactly two states. From `RCA_COMPLETE`, the case moves `RCA_COMPLETE → CLOSED` with `closed_at` stamped from the logical clock, and it then reverses the validation cascade precisely: the unit's `open_hapi_rca_count` falls by 1, `survey_posture` is re-derived on the same three-rung ladder, the unit is versioned, and — only when that re-derivation returns `survey_posture` all the way to `OPEN` — every rental order on that unit whose `fulfillment_status` is still `REQUESTED` has `requires_director_override` reset to false and `posture_hold_applied_at` cleared to null, with each such order versioned. From `CLASSIFIED_POA`, the case moves `CLASSIFIED_POA → CLOSED` with `closed_at` stamped, and no unit counter is touched at all, because a present-on-admission case never incremented `open_hapi_rca_count` in the first place. Closing a case from any other state is an `ERR_CASE_INVALID_STATE`, as is starting an analysis on a case that is not `RCA_REQUIRED` or completing one on a case that is not `RCA_IN_PROGRESS`.

Cases can be listed with `patient_id`, `unit_id`, `skin_assessment_id`, `predecessor_case_id`, `rca_lead_nurse_id`, `status` and `classification` filters, and a single case can be read to inspect `classification`, `rca_due_at`, `root_cause_category`, `contributing_factors` and `closed_at`.

### 3.7. The Prevalence and Incidence Survey Programme

Twice-yearly skin prevalence rounds and continuous incidence surveillance are the WOC service's public numbers, and the two are computed from the very same walk of the wards. A `WocProgramDirector` plans a round by supplying a `round_type` of either `PREVALENCE` or `INCIDENCE`, a `window_start`, a `window_end` and a `participating_unit_ids` list. The window is a **closed** interval running from `window_start` through `window_end` inclusive, and `window_end` must be strictly later than `window_start`. `participating_unit_ids` is a list of between 1 and 20 unique unit identifiers, each of which must resolve to an existing unit; it is a plain list rather than a foreign-key column, but every element is checked at creation. A planned round starts with `status` `PLANNED`, `observation_count` 0 and `published_at` null. `status` runs through `PLANNED`, `OPEN`, `LOCKED` and terminates at either `PUBLISHED` or `CANCELLED`.

**Opening a round and window contention.** The director opens a planned round, moving it `PLANNED → OPEN`, and this is the only point at which window contention is checked. Two rounds collide when their closed windows touch at all: collision holds exactly when the first round's `window_start` is at or before the second round's `window_end` and the second round's `window_start` is at or before the first round's `window_end`. No buffer is added on either side. Contention is then assessed per `round_type` and only against rounds that share at least one unit identifier with this round's `participating_unit_ids`. A `PREVALENCE` round may not open when any other `PREVALENCE` round whose `status` is `OPEN`, `LOCKED` or `PUBLISHED` collides on a shared unit — the aggregate capacity for prevalence work on a ward is exactly one round. An `INCIDENCE` round may not open when two or more other `INCIDENCE` rounds whose `status` is `OPEN` or `LOCKED` already collide on a shared unit — the aggregate capacity for incidence surveillance is two concurrent rounds, so a third is refused. Note the asymmetry in the status sets: a published prevalence round still blocks, while a published incidence round does not. Either refusal carries `ERR_SURVEY_WINDOW_CONFLICT`.

**Filing observations.** While a round is `OPEN`, a `WocNurse` records one immutable observation per patient per round. The payload names the `survey_round_id`, the `unit_id` being walked, the `patient_id`, the `observer_nurse_id` doing the walking, an `observed_at` instant, an `injury_present` boolean and a `classification`; it may also carry `observed_stage`, `onset_at` and `at_risk`. The round must be `OPEN` — filing against a `PLANNED`, `LOCKED`, `PUBLISHED` or `CANCELLED` round is refused with `ERR_SURVEY_ROUND_NOT_OPEN`. The `unit_id` must be one of the round's `participating_unit_ids` and must be the unit the patient is actually on; the patient must be `ADMITTED`; and the `observer_nurse_id` must name a nurse whose `certification_status` is `CANDIDATE` or `CERTIFIED`. The `observed_at` instant must lie inside the round's closed window, and a supplied `onset_at` must be at or before `observed_at`; either failure is an `ERR_OBSERVATION_OUTSIDE_WINDOW`. At most one observation may exist for a given combination of `survey_round_id` and `patient_id`, and a second attempt is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_OBSERVATION`.

The optional fields are conditionally required rather than freely optional. `observed_stage` — one of `MUCOSAL`, `STAGE_1`, `STAGE_2`, `STAGE_3`, `STAGE_4`, `DTI` or `UNSTAGEABLE` — is required when `injury_present` is true and must be omitted or null when `injury_present` is false. `classification` is one of `NONE`, `POA` or `HAPI` and must be `NONE` whenever `injury_present` is false. `onset_at` is the documented onset instant of the injury and is required when `classification` is `HAPI` and must be null otherwise. `at_risk` records whether the patient was on the ward's at-risk register during the pass and defaults to true when omitted. Filing an observation raises the round's `observation_count` by 1 and versions the round. The observation itself is an audit record: as noted in §2.4 it carries only `id` and `created_at`, has neither `updated_at` nor `version`, and no operation modifies it afterwards. Observations can be listed with `survey_round_id`, `unit_id`, `patient_id` and `observer_nurse_id` filters, and read individually.

**Locking.** The director locks an open round, moving it `OPEN → LOCKED`, and this is the compound guard of the survey machine: the round may only lock when *every* unit identifier in `participating_unit_ids` has at least one observation attached to this round. A single unwalked ward blocks the lock with `ERR_SURVEY_UNIT_COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE`. Once locked, no further observation may be created against the round, which is what freezes the input set for the metric computation.

**Publishing and the two divergent metrics.** The director publishes a locked round, moving it `LOCKED → PUBLISHED` and stamping `published_at` from the logical clock. Publication computes one metric per unit identifier in `participating_unit_ids`, and the scope for each computation is precisely the observations attached to *this* round and to *that* unit — observations attached to other rounds covering the same ward are excluded, and ordering is irrelevant because both metrics are pure counts. A `PREVALENCE` round writes each unit's `last_prevalence_rate`; an `INCIDENCE` round writes each unit's `last_incidence_rate`; each affected unit is versioned.

The two metrics deliberately disagree on identical data because their inclusion sets differ. For **prevalence**, the numerator is the count of that unit's observations in the round whose `injury_present` is true, regardless of `classification`, so injuries the patient arrived with are counted; the denominator is the count of all of that unit's observations in the round, regardless of `at_risk`. For **incidence**, the numerator is the count of that unit's observations in the round where `injury_present` is true *and* `classification` is `HAPI` *and* `onset_at` falls inside the round's closed window from `window_start` through `window_end` inclusive, so present-on-admission injuries are excluded; and the denominator is the count only of that unit's observations whose `at_risk` is true. In both cases the rate is the numerator divided by the denominator, multiplied by 100, and rounded half up to two decimal places. When the denominator is 0 the rate is exactly 0.00 and no error is raised.

A computed per-unit report is also readable for any round, and it is the diagnostic surface for that divergence: it answers with the round's `round_id`, its `round_type`, an `observation_total` across the whole round, and a `unit_rows` array. Each element of `unit_rows` carries that unit's `unit_id` and `unit_code`, its `observation_count` within this round, its `injury_present_count`, its `hapi_in_window_count`, its `at_risk_count`, and both computed figures side by side as `prevalence_rate` and `incidence_rate` — so a caller can see both metrics for the same observations regardless of which one the round's `round_type` will actually publish.

**Cancellation.** A round may be cancelled from either `PLANNED` or `OPEN` with a `reason`. Cancelling an open round retains the observations already filed against it as audit records but writes no unit metric whatsoever, so `last_prevalence_rate` and `last_incidence_rate` are left exactly as the last published round left them. A `LOCKED` or `PUBLISHED` round cannot be cancelled. Rounds can be listed with `status` and `round_type` filters and read individually to inspect `observation_count` and `published_at`.

### 3.8. Support Surfaces, Rental Orders and the Two Dimensions of a Rental

**The fleet.** A `MaterialsCoordinator` adds a rental asset by supplying an `asset_tag`, a `surface_class`, a `daily_rate_cents` and a `vendor_name`. The `asset_tag` is the vendor's tag on the physical bed or overlay, 3 to 24 characters, unique across the fleet — a reuse is an `ERR_DUPLICATE_NATURAL_KEY`. `surface_class` is the therapy class and is one of `FOAM_OVERLAY`, `ALTERNATING_PRESSURE`, `LOW_AIR_LOSS` or `BARIATRIC_BED`. `daily_rate_cents` is the vendor's list rate per billable day before any tier discount, at least 1 and at most 2000000. `vendor_name` is 1 to 120 characters. A new asset starts with `status` `AVAILABLE` and with `occupied_until` and `available_from` both null.

An asset's `status` is one of `AVAILABLE`, `IN_USE`, `CLEANING` or `RETIRED`, and the first three form a cycle. Approving an order on the asset moves it `AVAILABLE → IN_USE` (or `CLEANING → IN_USE`, since a freshly recleaned bed may be booked straight out again) and writes the occupation high-water mark: `occupied_until` becomes the approved order's `rental_end` and `available_from` becomes that same instant plus 12 hours of reclean time. Returning the asset moves it `IN_USE → CLEANING` and rewrites the marks from reality: `occupied_until` becomes the order's `actual_return_at` and `available_from` becomes that instant plus the same 12 hours. Reconciling the order's invoice moves the asset `CLEANING → AVAILABLE`, but only when the logical clock has reached or passed `available_from`; if the reclean buffer has not yet elapsed the asset stays `CLEANING`. Where several orders have driven the same asset, the marks reflect the driving order chosen by the latest `created_at`. Both marks stay null until the asset's first approval, and `available_from` is null exactly while `occupied_until` is null. Those two fields serve two purposes: they are the earliest `rental_start` a new order on the asset may sensibly carry, and they are the gate on returning a cleaning asset to service.

Retirement is the terminal state. A coordinator retires an asset from either `AVAILABLE` or `CLEANING` with a `reason`, and only when no order on that asset has a `fulfillment_status` of `REQUESTED`, `APPROVED` or `DELIVERED`; an attempt while any such order is live, like an attempt to order a `RETIRED` asset, is refused with `ERR_RESOURCE_NOT_ACTIVE`. Assets can be listed with `surface_class` and `status` filters and read individually to inspect `occupied_until` and `available_from`.

**Requesting a rental.** Either a `MaterialsCoordinator` or a `WocNurse` may raise a rental order, naming the `patient_id`, the `unit_id` whose ledger will be charged, the `support_surface_id` of the physical asset, a `rental_start`, a `rental_end` and an `authorized_days` figure; a `consult_request_id` may optionally be supplied to tie the rental to the consult that justifies it. The `unit_id` must be the unit the patient is on, the patient must be `ADMITTED`, and the asset must not be `RETIRED`. The occupation interval is **half-open**: `rental_start` is included and `rental_end` is excluded, and `rental_end` must be strictly later than `rental_start`. `authorized_days` is what the unit has agreed to pay for, at least 1 and at most 120. The order starts with `fulfillment_status` `REQUESTED` and `billing_status` `UNBILLED`, and no money moves at creation — creation is a costing exercise, not a ledger event. Creation is nevertheless refused outright with `ERR_UNIT_POSTURE_RESTRICTED` when the unit's `survey_posture` is `FROZEN`, and when the posture is `RESTRICTED` at creation time the order is created with `requires_director_override` already true.

**Costing the rental.** Three figures are computed at creation, in this order. First, `billable_days`: the whole hours between `rental_start` and `rental_end` divided by 24, with any partial day rounded **up**, so a 25-hour rental is 2 billable days. Second, `projected_cost_cents`, using a tiered per-day rate. Each billable day is numbered from 1, and the rate for a day is the asset's `daily_rate_cents` at full price for days 1 through 7, at 90 percent of it for days 8 through 21, and at 75 percent of it for every day from 22 onward. Each day's charge is rounded half up to whole cents **individually**, and the rounded per-day figures are then added together. Summing unrounded rates and rounding the total once is forbidden and gives a different answer: 14 days at a `daily_rate_cents` of 12345 comes to 164192 cents when each day is rounded before summing, versus 164189 if the total were rounded once.

Third, `vendor_service_fee_cents`, which looks circular but is not. The fee percentage is keyed on the **pre-fee** `projected_cost_cents`, never on the post-fee total: the percentage is 8 percent when `projected_cost_cents` is below 50000 cents, 5 percent when it is at least 50000 and below 200000, and 3 percent when it is 200000 or more. The fee is that percentage of `projected_cost_cents`, rounded half up to whole cents. `authorized_cost_cents` is then exactly `projected_cost_cents` plus `vendor_service_fee_cents`, by integer addition. Worked through: a `projected_cost_cents` of 199000 falls in the middle tier at 5 percent, giving a fee of 9950 and an `authorized_cost_cents` of 208950. Had the tier been keyed on the 208950 total it would have fallen in the 3 percent tier, giving 5970 and 204970 — that reading is wrong.

**Two independent dimensions.** Every rental order carries two state dimensions on the same row and they must never be coupled. `fulfillment_status` tracks logistics and runs `REQUESTED`, `APPROVED`, `DELIVERED`, terminating at `RETURNED` or `DENIED`. `billing_status` tracks money and runs `UNBILLED`, `ENCUMBERED`, `COMMITTED`, terminating at `RECONCILED` or `RELEASED`. Marking a delivery moves only the fulfillment dimension; reconciling an invoice moves only the billing dimension. Only two operations move both at once, and both are described below.

**Approval.** A `MaterialsCoordinator` approves a `REQUESTED` order, supplying the `expected_unit_version` of §2.6 and optionally a `director_override` boolean. Five substantive gates apply beyond the version check. The unit's `survey_posture` must not be `FROZEN`, and an order whose `requires_director_override` is true may only be approved when `director_override` is supplied as true — either failure is `ERR_UNIT_POSTURE_RESTRICTED`. The order's computed `billable_days` must not exceed its `authorized_days`, or the approval is refused with `ERR_RENTAL_DAYS_EXCEED_AUTHORIZATION`. The order's `authorized_cost_cents` must not exceed the unit's `rental_available_cents`, or the approval is refused with `ERR_RENTAL_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` at HTTP 402 — this is the one payment-required status in the catalogue. And the asset must be free for the whole requested span.

Occupation contention uses half-open arithmetic with a trailing buffer, and it is asymmetric on purpose. The candidate span is the order's own `[rental_start, rental_end)`. The blocked span of an existing order on the same `support_surface_id` runs from that order's `rental_start` up to but not including its `rental_end` plus 12 hours of reclean time. Approval is refused with `ERR_SURFACE_UNAVAILABLE` when the candidate span intersects the blocked span of any other order on that asset whose `fulfillment_status` is `APPROVED` or `DELIVERED`. Aggregate capacity is exactly one, because the asset is a single physical bed. Orders in `REQUESTED`, `RETURNED` or `DENIED` do not block anything. This check is computed from the live order rows rather than from the asset's `occupied_until` marker, so it remains correct when several orders have been written against the same asset.

Approval moves `fulfillment_status` `REQUESTED → APPROVED` and, in the same breath, `billing_status` `UNBILLED → ENCUMBERED`. The unit's `rental_encumbered_cents` rises by the order's `authorized_cost_cents`, `rental_available_cents` is recomputed and the unit is versioned. The asset moves to `IN_USE` with its `occupied_until` and `available_from` written as described above and is versioned. The order is versioned.

**Denial.** A coordinator denies a `REQUESTED` order with a `denial_reason`, moving `fulfillment_status` `REQUESTED → DENIED`. The `billing_status` is left at `UNBILLED`, because nothing was ever encumbered — this is not a release, and `rental_released_cents` does not move.

**Delivery.** A coordinator marks an `APPROVED` order delivered by supplying a `delivered_at` instant that is at or after `rental_start`. The order moves `fulfillment_status` `APPROVED → DELIVERED` and stores `delivered_at`. The `billing_status` is not touched and remains `ENCUMBERED`. This transition is the clearest demonstration that the dimensions are independent.

**Return.** A coordinator returns a `DELIVERED` order by supplying an `actual_return_at` instant strictly later than `rental_start`. The order moves `fulfillment_status` `DELIVERED → RETURNED` and `billing_status` `ENCUMBERED → COMMITTED`, and this is the second operation that moves both dimensions. `actual_cost_cents` is recomputed over the realised half-open interval from `rental_start` up to `actual_return_at` using exactly the same machinery as the projection — whole hours divided by 24 and rounded up to a day count, the same 1.00/0.90/0.75 day-tier factors with per-day rounding, and then the same pre-fee-keyed vendor service fee added on top of that recomputed projection. The unit ledger then settles in one movement: `rental_encumbered_cents` falls by the order's `authorized_cost_cents`, `rental_committed_cents` rises by the order's `actual_cost_cents`, `rental_released_cents` rises by the amount by which `authorized_cost_cents` exceeded `actual_cost_cents` — or by nothing at all when the realised cost met or exceeded the authorization, since that release figure is never negative — and `rental_available_cents` is recomputed, with the unit versioned. The asset moves to `CLEANING` with `occupied_until` set to `actual_return_at` and `available_from` to 12 hours later, and is versioned.

**Reconciliation.** A coordinator reconciles an order against the vendor invoice. The order's `billing_status` must be `COMMITTED` — anything else, whether still `UNBILLED` or `ENCUMBERED`, or already terminal at `RELEASED` or `RECONCILED`, is refused with `ERR_ORDER_INVALID_BILLING_STATE` — and its `fulfillment_status` must be `RETURNED`. The order moves `billing_status` `COMMITTED → RECONCILED`, `fulfillment_status` is untouched, and no ledger amount moves at all, because `rental_committed_cents` already carries `actual_cost_cents` in both of those billing states. The asset returns to `AVAILABLE` if and only if the logical clock has reached `available_from`.

**Release.** The fifth billing state is reached only by compensation, never by a direct call: an order sitting at `ENCUMBERED` whose consult is cancelled moves `billing_status` `ENCUMBERED → RELEASED` and hands its `authorized_cost_cents` back to the unit's headroom while adding the same figure to `rental_released_cents`, exactly as §3.4 describes. Together the encumber, commit, release and reconcile movements conserve the unit's money: what leaves `rental_encumbered_cents` always arrives either in `rental_committed_cents` or in `rental_released_cents`, and `rental_available_cents` always equals the cap less the encumbered and committed sums.

Orders can be listed with `patient_id`, `unit_id`, `support_surface_id`, `consult_request_id`, `fulfillment_status` and `billing_status` filters, and a single order can be read to inspect `billable_days`, `projected_cost_cents`, `vendor_service_fee_cents`, `authorized_cost_cents`, `actual_return_at`, `actual_cost_cents`, `delivered_at`, `denial_reason`, `requires_director_override` and `posture_hold_applied_at`.

### 3.9. Formulary Governance, Dressing Protocols and Ostomy Education

**The formulary.** A `WocProgramDirector` curates the product catalogue, adding a product with a `sku`, a `name`, a `category`, a `unit_cost_cents` and a `formulary_tier`. The `sku` is 3 to 24 characters and unique across the catalogue, with a reuse refused as `ERR_DUPLICATE_NATURAL_KEY`; `name` is 1 to 120 characters. `category` names the dressing or ostomy product family and is one of `FOAM`, `HYDROCOLLOID`, `ALGINATE`, `SILVER_ANTIMICROBIAL`, `OSTOMY_BARRIER` or `SKIN_PROTECTANT`. `unit_cost_cents` is the cost of a single unit, at least 0 and at most 1000000. `formulary_tier` is one of `PREFERRED`, `RESTRICTED` or `NON_FORMULARY`: the first two are on formulary and carry no penalty, while `NON_FORMULARY` both demands a written justification and attracts a cost surcharge. A product starts `ACTIVE` and may be archived by the director with a `reason`, moving it `ACTIVE → ARCHIVED`. Archiving is forward-looking only: dressing protocols already written against the product keep their status and their computed cost untouched, and only *new* protocol authorship is blocked — naming an `ARCHIVED` product on a new protocol is refused with `ERR_RESOURCE_NOT_ACTIVE`. Products can be listed with `category`, `formulary_tier` and `status` filters and read individually.

**Standardized dressing change protocols.** A `WocNurse` files the standing protocol for an assessed consult, naming the `consult_request_id`, the `formulary_product_id` selected, a `change_frequency_hours` and a `units_per_change`, plus a `justification` where one is required. The consult must be in `ASSESSED` or `PLAN_ACTIVE` — the latter because revising a live protocol is normal practice. `change_frequency_hours` is the interval between scheduled dressing changes, at least 8 and at most 168. `units_per_change` is how many units of product each change consumes, at least 1 and at most 20.

Two clinical gates guard authorship, and both surface through `ERR_STAGE_NOT_VALIDATED`: the consult's `protocol_hold` must be false, and the consult's most recently created skin assessment — the one with the latest `created_at` — must have `validation_status` equal to `VALIDATED`. A consult whose staging is still `PENDING_VALIDATION`, or is under dispute, cannot carry a protocol. Separately, when the selected product's `formulary_tier` is `NON_FORMULARY`, a `justification` of 1 to 500 non-blank characters is mandatory, and its absence, nullity or blankness is refused with `ERR_NON_FORMULARY_JUSTIFICATION_REQUIRED`. On an on-formulary product the `justification` remains optional, and where supplied it is still bounded at 1 to 500 characters.

The weekly cost is derived in two steps. `changes_per_week` is 168 hours — one week — divided by `change_frequency_hours`, discarding any remainder, so a 12-hour interval yields 14 changes and a 5-day interval yields 1. `weekly_cost_cents` is then `changes_per_week` multiplied by the product's `unit_cost_cents` multiplied by `units_per_change`. On a `PREFERRED` or `RESTRICTED` product that product is the answer exactly, with no rounding needed. On a `NON_FORMULARY` product a 20 percent surcharge is applied: the base figure is multiplied by 1.20 and the result rounded half up to whole cents. Rounding therefore happens once, and only on the non-formulary branch.

Filing a protocol has three consequences beyond the insert. The protocol starts with `status` `ACTIVE`. Any protocol already `ACTIVE` on the same consult moves `ACTIVE → SUPERSEDED` and is versioned, so a consult never carries two live protocols. And the consult advances, `ASSESSED → PLAN_ACTIVE` on a first protocol or holding at `PLAN_ACTIVE` on a revision, and is versioned. A protocol's third state, `VOIDED`, is reached only by compensation, when the consult behind it is cancelled. Protocols can be listed with `consult_request_id`, `formulary_product_id` and `status` filters and read individually to inspect `changes_per_week` and `weekly_cost_cents`.

**Ostomy education, supply transition and follow-up.** A `WocNurse` opens an education plan for an ostomy consult, naming the `consult_request_id` and the `patient_id`. Three eligibility conditions apply and all three surface as `ERR_OSTOMY_PLAN_NOT_ELIGIBLE`: the patient's `ostomy_present` must be true, the consult's `consult_type` must be `OSTOMY`, and — on the later supply transition — the booked appointment must be in the future. The consult must additionally be in `ASSESSED` or `PLAN_ACTIVE`, and the plan's `patient_id` must be the consult's own patient. A new plan starts with `status` `ACTIVE`, `sessions_completed` 0, `teach_back_score` null, `supply_transition_status` `PENDING` and `followup_appointment_at` null.

Education sessions are recorded one at a time, each supplying a `teach_back_score` between 0 and 100. Every accepted session raises `sessions_completed` by 1 and overwrites the plan's `teach_back_score` with the newest figure, which is always the score of the most recent session ordered by `created_at` descending. Whether the plan advances depends on the score: a score of 80 or higher moves the plan `ACTIVE → TEACH_BACK_PASSED`, while a score below 80 leaves the plan at `ACTIVE` — the session still counts and the score is still recorded, but the teaching is not yet done, and further sessions may be recorded until the patient passes.

Supply transition is the plan's second step and is available only from `TEACH_BACK_PASSED`. The nurse supplies a `followup_appointment_at` instant that must be strictly later than the logical clock; booking a follow-up in the past or exactly at the current instant is refused. The transition sets `supply_transition_status` to `TRANSITIONED` and stores `followup_appointment_at`, moving the plan `TEACH_BACK_PASSED → SUPPLIES_TRANSITIONED`; and because `followup_appointment_at` has become non-null, the plan immediately advances again `SUPPLIES_TRANSITIONED → FOLLOWUP_SCHEDULED` and the patient's `pending_outpatient_followup_count` rises by 1 with the patient versioned. That counter increment is a consequence of the appointment being booked and is applied exactly once per plan — never twice, even though two state advances occur in the one request.

`FOLLOWUP_SCHEDULED` is a terminal state and the successful end of the education pathway. The other terminal state, `CLOSED_INCOMPLETE`, is reached only by compensation when the consult behind the plan is cancelled: a plan in any of `ACTIVE`, `TEACH_BACK_PASSED`, `SUPPLIES_TRANSITIONED` or `FOLLOWUP_SCHEDULED` is closed incomplete with `followup_appointment_at` cleared to null, and the patient's `pending_outpatient_followup_count` is reduced by 1 only when the plan was actually in `FOLLOWUP_SCHEDULED` at that moment — a plan closed out of `ACTIVE`, `TEACH_BACK_PASSED` or `SUPPLIES_TRANSITIONED` leaves the counter alone, because it was never counted. Plans can be listed with `consult_request_id`, `patient_id` and `status` filters and read individually to inspect `sessions_completed`, `teach_back_score`, `supply_transition_status` and `followup_appointment_at`.

## 4. Endpoint Definitions

All collections in this API are peers at the root; sub-paths carry action verbs and computed reads only. An operation that brings a new resource into existence answers with HTTP 201 and the created resource in `data`; every other successful operation answers with HTTP 200 and, unless noted otherwise, the affected resource in `data`. Failures carry the HTTP status attached to their error code in §5. The request and response payloads of each operation are not repeated here — they are specified in the §3 narratives that describe the corresponding business step.
