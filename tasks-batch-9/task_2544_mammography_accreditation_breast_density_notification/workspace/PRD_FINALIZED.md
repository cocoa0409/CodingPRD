# Mammography Accreditation & Breast Density Notification API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

A breast imaging center lives inside three regulatory clocks at once. The Mammography Quality Standards Act requires that every mammography unit hold a current accreditation certificate, that a medical physicist survey each unit annually and again after certain equipment repairs, and that every interpreting physician and technologist keep accumulating continuing-education hours and reading volume. Layered on top of that, federal density-notification rules require the center to send each patient a plain-language lay summary that names her breast density, while individual states impose their own notice codes, reading-grade ceilings, delivery deadlines, and supplemental-screening coverage mandates. Nobody at the front desk can hold all of this in their head, and the failure modes are expensive: a unit that keeps imaging after its accreditation lapses, a patient who is never told her breasts are dense, a recalled woman who is never biopsied and quietly becomes lost to follow-up.

This document specifies the backend service that carries that burden. The service is the system of record for state notification policies, facilities, mammography units, MQSA personnel and their credits, physicist surveys, FDA annual inspections, clinical image review packets submitted for reaccreditation, exams and their interpretations, patient notification letters, supplemental screening orders, equipment failures, repeat-imaging documentation, and per-radiologist audit cycles that score recall rate and positive predictive value. It does not render letter PDFs, talk to a PACS, or adjudicate claims with a real payer; it computes the obligations, tracks the states, holds the money in escrow, and refuses the operations that regulation forbids.

Two design commitments run through everything that follows. First, **time is explicit**: the service never reads wall-clock time, only a logical clock that tests and operators advance deliberately, so that a deadline sweep is a reproducible event rather than a background surprise. Second, **compliance outranks convenience and outranks money**: when several rules could reject the same request, the service reports the patient-safety or MQSA-compliance failure before it reports a referential problem, a scheduling collision, or an insufficient accreditation-fee balance. Engineers implementing this specification should read the validation ladder in §2.8 as normative, not advisory.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

### 2.1. User and Role-Based Access Control

Every request — reads included — must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the caller, and `X-User-Role`, naming the caller's role. If either header is missing or empty, or if `X-User-Role` is not one of the seven role names below spelled exactly, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` (HTTP 401). When the headers are well formed but the role is not among the roles the target endpoint admits, the rejection is `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE` (HTTP 403).

The seven roles are `AccreditationCoordinator`, `AccreditationBodyReviewer`, `FdaInspector`, `QualityAssuranceOfficer`, `InterpretingPhysician`, `Technologist`, and `MedicalPhysicist`.

- `AccreditationCoordinator` is the facility-side accreditation and compliance operator. This role registers facilities, units and staff, funds the accreditation-fee account, files and withdraws clinical image packets, records post-remediation survey re-measurements, restores repaired units to service, generates and dispatches patient letters, drives supplemental orders through coverage determination, scheduling and completion, and fires the facility compliance run.
- `AccreditationBodyReviewer` is the ACR or state accreditation-body image reviewer. It is the only role that may decide a clinical image review packet, and it is deliberately never the role that files one.
- `FdaInspector` is the federal or state MQSA inspector. It records annual inspection findings and closes corrective actions; facility-side roles can neither record nor close their own inspections.
- `QualityAssuranceOfficer` is the independent quality assurance officer. It records pathology outcomes, retracts mis-documented repeat records, and opens and closes radiologist audit cycles.
- `InterpretingPhysician` is the MQSA-qualified interpreting radiologist. It is the only role that may interpret or amend an exam, and it is the clinical origin of a supplemental screening recommendation.
- `Technologist` is the MQSA-qualified radiologic technologist. It acquires exams and documents repeated imaging.
- `MedicalPhysicist` is the MQSA-qualified medical physicist. It opens survey records on a unit and submits its own survey decisions, but may not record the coordinator-side remediation of a failed survey.

**Identity semantics.** The three clinical roles are entity-backed. When `X-User-Role` is `InterpretingPhysician`, `Technologist`, or `MedicalPhysicist`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Personnel` row, and ownership checks on any resource that references a `Personnel` row compare that resource's personnel-bearing field to `X-User-ID` — the exam's `technologist_id` and `interpreting_physician_id`, the survey's `physicist_id`, the audit cycle's `radiologist_id`. For those three roles the service additionally requires that the referenced `Personnel` row belong to the facility that owns the target resource and that its `role_type` match the acting role (`INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN` for `InterpretingPhysician`, `TECHNOLOGIST` for `Technologist`, `MEDICAL_PHYSICIST` for `MedicalPhysicist`); a mismatch on either count is `ERR_ACTOR_NOT_AT_FACILITY` (HTTP 403). That facility-membership check is applied to exactly five write operations: opening a physicist survey on a unit (`POST /units/{id}/physicist-surveys`), submitting a survey decision (`POST /physicist-surveys/{id}/submit`), recording an acquired exam (`POST /exams`), interpreting an exam (`POST /exams/{id}/interpret`), and amending an exam (`POST /exams/{id}/amend`).

The four operational roles are opaque. When `X-User-Role` is `AccreditationCoordinator`, `AccreditationBodyReviewer`, `FdaInspector`, or `QualityAssuranceOfficer`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier not tied to any persisted entity. The server may compare such an identifier against a stored personnel identifier for the segregation-of-duties rules described below — for example against an exam's `interpreting_physician_id` or an audit cycle's `radiologist_id` — but it never requires that the identifier itself resolve to a `Personnel` row, and it never writes it onto an entity as an owner.

**Segregation of duties.** Three independent separations are enforced. A `QualityAssuranceOfficer` recording a pathology outcome on an exam must not be that exam's `interpreting_physician_id`, and a `QualityAssuranceOfficer` closing an audit cycle must not be that cycle's `radiologist_id`; both violations are reported as `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES` (HTTP 403), and both apply to exactly two operations — `POST /exams/{id}/outcome` and `POST /audit-cycles/{id}/close`. On the survey side, the `MedicalPhysicist` submitting a survey decision must be the survey's own `physicist_id`, while a remediation re-measurement must be recorded by an `AccreditationCoordinator` and never by the physicist who authored the survey; both violations also surface as `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, on `POST /physicist-surveys/{id}/submit` and `POST /physicist-surveys/{id}/remediate`. Finally, amendment ownership: when an exam already carries an `interpreting_physician_id`, the `InterpretingPhysician` amending it must be that person, or the request is rejected with `ERR_NOT_INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN` (HTTP 403). An exam that has never been interpreted carries no owner, so this ownership check passes and the request falls through to the state check, which rejects it as not amendable.

All seven roles share every read endpoint. Write endpoints are partitioned as described above and enumerated per endpoint in §4.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination

Every list endpoint accepts two optional query parameters. `limit` controls page size: it defaults to 10 when omitted, its minimum accepted value is 1 and its maximum accepted value is 100. `offset` controls the starting position: it defaults to 0 and its minimum accepted value is 0. A `limit` or `offset` outside those bounds is a request-schema violation and is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST` (HTTP 400).

List results are ordered by `created_at` ascending unless a subsection below pins a different order for a specific computation. The `meta` block of a list response carries `limit` (the effective page size actually applied), `offset` (the effective starting position), `total_count` (the number of rows matching the filters before pagination), and `has_more` (true when rows remain beyond this page). Single-resource responses carry no pagination fields in `meta`.

Where a list endpoint also accepts filter parameters — `status`, `survey_type`, `role_type`, `qualification_status`, `ce_status`, `accreditation_status`, `operational_state`, `inspector_findings_level`, `severity`, `credit_type`, `exam_type`, `report_status`, `notification_status`, `follow_up_status`, `letter_type`, `coverage_outcome`, `reason_code`, `retracted`, `modality`, `unit_id`, `exam_id`, `technologist_id`, `interpreting_physician_id`, `physicist_id`, `equipment_failure_id`, `supersedes_letter_id` — every filter is optional, and filters combine conjunctively: a row must match all supplied filters to appear.

### 2.4. Resource Identity, Timestamps and Mutation Counters

Every resource carries a server-minted `id` that is a UUID string with no type prefix; clients never supply an `id`. Every resource also carries `created_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits recording the logical-clock instant of creation.

Most resources additionally carry `updated_at` — the same format, refreshed on every accepted mutation — and `version`, an integer that starts at 1 on creation and increases by exactly 1 on every accepted mutation of that resource. Cascaded changes count: when a survey decision drives a unit out of service, the unit's own `version` advances even though the request addressed the survey.

One entity is deliberately immutable. A `QualificationCredit` row carries only `id` and `created_at` — it has no `updated_at` and no `version`, there is no endpoint that modifies or deletes it, and no field on it can ever change after the row is written. Every other entity in this specification — `StatePolicy`, `Facility`, `MammographyUnit`, `Personnel`, `PhysicistSurvey`, `AnnualInspection`, `ClinicalImageReviewSubmission`, `Exam`, `NotificationLetter`, `SupplementalScreeningOrder`, `EquipmentFailureEvent`, `RepeatImagingRecord`, and `RadiologistAuditCycle` — carries the full set of `id`, `created_at`, `updated_at` and `version`.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock

Nothing in this service reads wall-clock time. Every deadline, lookback window, expiry, sweep and stamped instant reads a single logical clock whose mandatory startup value is `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`. Before any client sets the clock, reading it returns exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`; the implementation must not fall back to real time.

Any of the seven roles may read the clock or move it. Setting the clock takes a single `current_utc` instant and answers with both the new `current_utc` and the `previous_utc` it replaced. The clock may only move forward or be held at its present value: a requested `current_utc` strictly earlier than the current value is rejected with `ERR_CLOCK_REWIND` (HTTP 422). Advancing the clock mutates no entity and fires no sweep — sweeps only run inside a facility compliance run, described in §3.9.

### 2.6. Resolving the Governing State Policy

Notification content, coverage treatment, report turnaround, follow-up grace and continuing-education grace are all read from a `StatePolicy` row, and which row governs is itself a rule. For any exam-scoped decision the service first looks for the `StatePolicy` whose `state_code` equals the exam's `patient_state_code`; `state_code` is globally unique, so at most one row can match. If such a row exists it is the governing policy. If no row exists for the patient's state, the governing policy is the `StatePolicy` referenced by `state_policy_id` on the facility that owns the exam's unit — the facility's home-state fallback. Because `state_policy_id` is mandatory when a facility is registered, this resolution never yields nothing.

The resolved policy's `state_code` is what gets recorded as `governing_state_code` on a notification letter and on a supplemental screening order, so a letter for an out-of-state patient whose state has no registered policy records the facility's home state, not the patient's. For personnel-scoped decisions there is no patient involved, so the facility's home-state policy is always the governing policy.

Reaching the facility from an exam is a three-hop traversal: the exam names a `unit_id`, the unit names a `facility_id`, and the facility names a `state_policy_id`. A supplemental screening order reaches the facility one hop further out, through its `exam_id`.

### 2.7. Precision, Rounding and Conserved Quantities

All percentages in this service — the two unit repeat rates, the recall rate and the positive predictive value — are held to 2 decimal places and rounded half up. Each percentage is formed once, from aggregated integer counts, and rounded once at the end; percentages are never produced by averaging per-exam, per-unit or per-modality rates.

All money is integer cents. The accreditation application fee, funding deposits, and every field of a coverage determination are whole numbers of cents, and the only rounding step anywhere in the money path is the payer's covered share, which is computed by multiplying the allowed amount by the tier rate and rounding half up to whole cents. The patient's residual share is then obtained by subtraction, so the covered share and the patient share always sum back to the allowed amount exactly.

Two conserved quantities must hold after every accepted write. First, the facility fee ledger: `total_funded_cents` always equals `prepaid_credit_cents` plus `fee_held_cents` plus `fee_consumed_cents`, with `total_funded_cents` and `fee_consumed_cents` both monotonically non-decreasing — neither ever goes down. Second, the unit image ledger: `total_images_acquired` always equals the sum of `original_image_count` across the unit's exams plus `repeat_images_acquired`. Retracting a repeat record restores `total_images_acquired` to exactly the value it held before that record was documented, with no drift; withdrawing a submission restores `prepaid_credit_cents` to exactly the value it held before the escrow, with no drift.

Survey measurements are compared without rounding: `phantom_score` is a one-decimal number and `mean_glandular_dose_mgy` is a two-decimal number, and each is compared against its threshold at that precision with no pre-comparison rounding. Continuing-education hours are summed at two decimals; credited exam counts are summed as integers. Grade levels and day counts are integers, and a deadline computed from the clock is an exact whole-day offset.

### 2.8. The Validation Ladder

When more than one rule could reject a request, the service evaluates the checks in the fixed order below and reports the first failure it finds. Lower numbers are evaluated first. The ladder exists because masking matters: a facility must be told its unit is out of service before it is told its prepaid accreditation credit is short, and a caller whose attempt budget is exhausted must learn that rather than learning about the suspension that exhausting the budget causes.

1. **Authentication (priority 1)** — both headers present and non-empty, `X-User-Role` one of the seven declared roles: `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`, 401. Applies to every endpoint.
2. **Role authorization (priority 2)** — the acting role appears in the endpoint's declared role list: `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE`, 403. Applies to every endpoint.
3. **Actor facility membership (priority 3)** — for the three entity-backed roles, `X-User-ID` resolves to a `Personnel` row whose `facility_id` matches the facility owning the target resource and whose `role_type` matches the acting role: `ERR_ACTOR_NOT_AT_FACILITY`, 403. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/physicist-surveys`, `POST /physicist-surveys/{id}/submit`, `POST /exams`, `POST /exams/{id}/interpret`, `POST /exams/{id}/amend`.
4. **Segregation of duties, outcome and audit (priority 4)** — the officer recording a pathology outcome is not the exam's `interpreting_physician_id`, and the officer closing an audit cycle is not the cycle's `radiologist_id`: `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, 403. Applies to `POST /exams/{id}/outcome`, `POST /audit-cycles/{id}/close`.
5. **Segregation of duties, surveys (priority 5)** — the physicist submitting a survey decision is the survey's `physicist_id`, and a remediation decision comes from an `AccreditationCoordinator` rather than the authoring physicist: `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`, 403. Applies to `POST /physicist-surveys/{id}/submit`, `POST /physicist-surveys/{id}/remediate`.
6. **Amendment ownership (priority 6)** — when the exam carries an `interpreting_physician_id`, the amender is that person: `ERR_NOT_INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN`, 403. Applies to `POST /exams/{id}/amend`. An exam never interpreted has no owner, so this check passes and the state check at step 9 rejects the request instead.
7. **Path existence (priority 10)** — every id embedded in the request path exists, validated left to right: 404 with the code for the entity that was not found — `ERR_STATE_POLICY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FACILITY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PERSONNEL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SURVEY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_INSPECTION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_SUBMISSION_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EXAM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_LETTER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_ORDER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FAILURE_EVENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REPEAT_RECORD_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AUDIT_CYCLE_NOT_FOUND`. Applies to every endpoint with a path parameter. Left-to-right means the leftmost missing segment decides the code: addressing a unit under a non-existent facility yields `ERR_FACILITY_NOT_FOUND`, never `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND`.
8. **Request schema (priority 20)** — required body fields present, enum values in range, numeric ranges honored, state codes exactly two uppercase letters, and conditional field pairings satisfied: `ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`, 400. Applies to every write endpoint. The enforced numeric ranges are: `birads_assessment` from 0 through 6, `phantom_score` from 0.0 through 12.0, `mean_glandular_dose_mgy` from 0.00 through 10.00, `lay_summary_max_reading_grade` from 4 through 12, `lay_summary_deadline_days` from 1 through 90, `report_turnaround_days` from 1 through 90, `urgent_report_turnaround_days` from 1 through 30 and no greater than `report_turnaround_days`, `followup_grace_days` from 1 through 365, `ce_grace_days` from 1 through 365, `supplemental_cost_share_waiver_threshold_cents` at least 0, `original_image_count` at least 1, `repeat_image_count` at least 1, `supplemental_concurrent_capacity` from 1 through 10, `followup_window_days` from 30 through 730, `initial_training_hours` at least 0, `initial_read_volume` at least 0, and `ce_hours` and `exam_count` strictly greater than 0 whenever present. The conditional pairings are: `ce_hours` may be supplied only when `credit_type` is `CATEGORY_1_CE` and must be absent otherwise; `exam_count` may be supplied only when `credit_type` is `READ_VOLUME` and must be absent otherwise; `equipment_failure_id` may be supplied only when `reason_code` is `EQUIPMENT_FAILURE` and must be absent otherwise.
9. **Uniqueness (priority 21)** — `state_code` is globally unique across `StatePolicy`, and `model_serial` is unique within a facility: `ERR_DUPLICATE_RESOURCE`, 409. Applies to `POST /state-policies`, `POST /facilities/{id}/units`.
10. **Packet size (priority 22)** — a clinical image packet carries at least 2 distinct exam ids with no duplicates: `ERR_PACKET_TOO_SMALL`, 422. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`, `POST /clinical-image-reviews/{id}/resubmit`.
11. **Body-reference existence (priority 25)** — every id named in the request body exists: `state_policy_id`, `technologist_id`, `unit_id`, every element of `exam_ids`, and `equipment_failure_id`, reported as `ERR_STATE_POLICY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_PERSONNEL_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_UNIT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EXAM_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_FAILURE_EVENT_NOT_FOUND`, all 404. Applies to `POST /facilities`, `POST /exams`, `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`, `POST /clinical-image-reviews/{id}/resubmit`, `POST /exams/{id}/repeat-records`.
12. **Optimistic concurrency (priority 30)** — the supplied `expected_unit_version` or `expected_exam_version` equals the parent's current `version`: `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION` or `ERR_STALE_EXAM_VERSION`, 412. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`, `POST /clinical-image-reviews/{id}/resubmit`, `POST /clinical-image-reviews/{id}/withdraw`, `POST /exams/{id}/notification-letters`. See §2.9.
13. **State precondition (priority 40)** — the target resource sits in a state from which the requested transition is declared: 409 with the code for the operation — `ERR_SURVEY_NOT_DECIDABLE`, `ERR_SUBMISSION_NOT_REVIEWABLE`, `ERR_SUBMISSION_NOT_REVISABLE`, `ERR_SUBMISSION_NOT_WITHDRAWABLE`, `ERR_INSPECTION_ALREADY_CLOSED`, `ERR_LETTER_NOT_SENDABLE`, `ERR_LETTER_NOT_SENT`, `ERR_ORDER_NOT_DETERMINABLE`, `ERR_ORDER_NOT_SCHEDULABLE`, `ERR_ORDER_NOT_COMPLETABLE`, `ERR_EXAM_NOT_INTERPRETABLE`, `ERR_EXAM_NOT_AMENDABLE`, `ERR_OUTCOME_ALREADY_RECORDED`, `ERR_AUDIT_CYCLE_NOT_CLOSABLE`, `ERR_FAILURE_ALREADY_RESOLVED`, `ERR_REPEAT_RECORD_ALREADY_RETRACTED`. Applies to every action endpoint.
14. **Unit in service (priority 41)** — the unit that acquired the images is `IN_SERVICE`: `ERR_UNIT_NOT_IN_SERVICE`, 409. Applies to `POST /exams`.
15. **Facility not suspended (priority 42)** — a supplemental order cannot be scheduled while the owning facility's `compliance_posture` is `SUSPENDED`: `ERR_FACILITY_SUSPENDED`, 409. Applies to `POST /supplemental-orders/{id}/schedule`.
16. **Attempt budget (priority 43)** — the unit has at least 1 remaining `reaccreditation_attempts_remaining` before a submission or resubmission consumes one: `ERR_REACCREDITATION_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`, 409. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`, `POST /clinical-image-reviews/{id}/resubmit`. This guard is deliberately ordered ahead of the accreditation-suspension check at step 20, because exhausting the attempt budget is precisely what suspends the unit — if the suspension check ran first, the exhaustion error would be permanently masked.
17. **Technologist qualified (priority 50)** — the technologist named on an exam has `qualification_status` exactly `QUALIFIED`, not `PROVISIONAL`, `LAPSED` or `INACTIVE`: `ERR_TECHNOLOGIST_NOT_QUALIFIED`, 422. Applies to `POST /exams`.
18. **Physician qualified (priority 51)** — the interpreting physician has `qualification_status` `QUALIFIED`: `ERR_PHYSICIAN_NOT_QUALIFIED`, 422. Applies to `POST /exams/{id}/interpret` and `POST /exams/{id}/amend`.
19. **Modality certification (priority 52)** — the acting person's `modality_certifications` contains the unit's `modality`: `ERR_MODALITY_NOT_CERTIFIED`, 422. Applies to `POST /exams` and `POST /exams/{id}/interpret`.
20. **Unit accreditation not suspended (priority 53)** — a unit whose `accreditation_status` is `SUSPENDED` cannot acquire exams, accept a new clinical image review submission, or be restored to service: `ERR_UNIT_ACCREDITATION_SUSPENDED`, 409. Applies to `POST /exams`, `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`, `POST /equipment-failures/{id}/restore`.
21. **No open Level 1 finding (priority 54)** — a facility with a `LEVEL_1` inspection finding still in `CORRECTIVE_ACTION_REQUIRED` cannot submit or resubmit a clinical image packet: `ERR_OPEN_LEVEL_1_FINDING`, 409. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`, `POST /clinical-image-reviews/{id}/resubmit`.
22. **Post-repair survey present (priority 55)** — a unit may not return to service until a `PASSED` survey of `survey_type` `POST_REPAIR` exists on it whose `effective_start` is at or after the failure's `detected_at`: `ERR_POST_REPAIR_SURVEY_MISSING`, 409. Applies to `POST /equipment-failures/{id}/restore`.
23. **Packet unit match (priority 56)** — every exam in a packet belongs to the same unit as the submission: `ERR_EXAM_UNIT_MISMATCH`, 422. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`, `POST /clinical-image-reviews/{id}/resubmit`.
24. **Packet exams final (priority 57)** — every exam in a packet has `report_status` `INTERPRETED_FINAL` or `AMENDED`: `ERR_PACKET_EXAM_NOT_FINAL`, 422. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`, `POST /clinical-image-reviews/{id}/resubmit`.
25. **Failure unit match (priority 58)** — the referenced `EquipmentFailureEvent` belongs to the same unit as the exam being repeated: `ERR_FAILURE_UNIT_MISMATCH`, 422. Applies to `POST /exams/{id}/repeat-records`.
26. **Audit subject is a radiologist (priority 59)** — the `Personnel` row an audit cycle is opened on has `role_type` `INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN`: `ERR_NOT_AN_INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN`, 422. Applies to `POST /personnel/{id}/audit-cycles`.
27. **No future backdating (priority 60)** — a client-supplied historical instant is not after the logical clock; this covers `performed_at`, `detected_at`, `inspection_date`, `awarded_on` and `initial_qualification_on`: `ERR_FUTURE_TIMESTAMP`, 422. Applies to `POST /exams`, `POST /units/{id}/equipment-failures`, `POST /facilities/{id}/inspections`, `POST /personnel/{id}/credits`, `POST /facilities/{id}/personnel`.
28. **Survey window well formed (priority 61)** — `effective_end` is strictly after `effective_start` and the window spans at most 396 days: `ERR_SURVEY_WINDOW_INVALID`, 422. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/physicist-surveys`.
29. **Survey window exclusivity (priority 62)** — the new window does not overlap a live same-type window on the same unit; touching endpoints are adjacent rather than overlapping: `ERR_SURVEY_WINDOW_OVERLAP`, 409. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/physicist-surveys`.
30. **Reaccreditation window open (priority 63)** — a packet may only be submitted or resubmitted while `accreditation_expires_on` is at or after the logical clock, or while the unit has never been `ACCREDITED`: `ERR_REACCREDITATION_WINDOW_CLOSED`, 409. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`, `POST /clinical-image-reviews/{id}/resubmit`.
31. **Audit period elapsed (priority 64)** — a cycle may only be closed once the logical clock has reached `period_end`: `ERR_AUDIT_PERIOD_NOT_ELAPSED`, 409. Applies to `POST /audit-cycles/{id}/close`.
32. **Appointment not in the past (priority 65)** — the requested `appointment_start` is at or after the logical clock: `ERR_APPOINTMENT_IN_PAST`, 422. Applies to `POST /supplemental-orders/{id}/schedule`.
33. **Clock monotonicity (priority 66)** — the requested `current_utc` is at or after the clock's present value: `ERR_CLOCK_REWIND`, 422. Applies to `POST /_clock/set_current_utc`.
34. **One open submission per unit (priority 71)** — at most one non-terminal `ClinicalImageReviewSubmission`, meaning one in `SUBMITTED` or `REVISION_REQUIRED`, may exist on a unit: `ERR_SUBMISSION_ALREADY_OPEN`, 409. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`.
35. **Audit cycle exclusivity (priority 72)** — at most one `OPEN` cycle may exist per radiologist, and a new cycle's period must not overlap an existing cycle's period: `ERR_AUDIT_CYCLE_OVERLAP`, 409. Applies to `POST /personnel/{id}/audit-cycles`.
36. **Amendment changes something (priority 73)** — at least one of `birads_assessment` or `density_category` differs from the stored value: `ERR_AMENDMENT_NO_CHANGE`, 422. Applies to `POST /exams/{id}/amend`.
37. **Density category required (priority 74)** — a `SCREENING` exam must carry a `density_category` at interpretation: `ERR_DENSITY_CATEGORY_REQUIRED`, 422. Applies to `POST /exams/{id}/interpret`.
38. **Supplemental eligibility (priority 75)** — a supplemental order may only be created for an exam whose `report_status` is `INTERPRETED_FINAL` or `AMENDED` and whose `density_category` is present: `ERR_EXAM_NOT_ELIGIBLE_FOR_SUPPLEMENTAL`, 422. Applies to `POST /exams/{id}/supplemental-orders`.
39. **Coverage determined before scheduling (priority 76)** — an order may only be scheduled once its `coverage_outcome` is something other than `UNDETERMINED`: `ERR_COVERAGE_NOT_DETERMINED`, 409. Applies to `POST /supplemental-orders/{id}/schedule`.
40. **Supplemental capacity (priority 77)** — the buffered occupancy interval of the requested appointment collides with fewer than `supplemental_concurrent_capacity` already-`SCHEDULED` orders at the same facility: `ERR_SUPPLEMENTAL_CAPACITY_CONFLICT`, 409. Applies to `POST /supplemental-orders/{id}/schedule`.
41. **Deposit amount (priority 80)** — a funding deposit `amount_cents` is a positive integer number of cents: `ERR_INVALID_DEPOSIT_AMOUNT`, 422. Applies to `POST /facilities/{id}/funding-deposits`.
42. **Prepaid credit sufficiency (priority 81)** — the facility holds at least 250000 cents of `prepaid_credit_cents` to escrow against a new submission or resubmission: `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_PREPAID_CREDIT`, 402. Applies to `POST /units/{id}/clinical-image-reviews`, `POST /clinical-image-reviews/{id}/resubmit`.

Note the resulting ordering on a packet submission, where seven business rules can all fire: the attempt budget (step 16) is checked before the unit's accreditation suspension (step 20), which precedes the open Level 1 finding (step 21), which precedes packet unit membership (step 23) and packet finality (step 24), which precede the reaccreditation window (step 30), the one-open-submission rule (step 34) and, last of all, the prepaid credit balance (step 42). Money is always the final word.

### 2.9. Optimistic Concurrency on Units and Exams

Three write operations require the client to declare which version of a parent resource it was looking at.

Creating a clinical image review submission on a unit requires a non-optional `expected_unit_version` field carrying the client's last observed `version` of that `MammographyUnit`. If the value does not match the unit's current `version`, the request is rejected with `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). This matters because creating a submission legitimately consumes one of the unit's three reaccreditation attempts and escrows the 250000-cent application fee, so a create carrying an older observed version would silently spend a second attempt against a stale view of the accreditation cycle.

Resubmitting a revision-required packet likewise requires `expected_unit_version` matching the unit's current `version`, and a mismatch is rejected with `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION` (HTTP 412). A resubmit consumes another attempt and re-escrows the fee, and the attempt budget it spends may already have been consumed by an intervening submission or restored by a withdrawal — both of which advance the unit's `version`.

Withdrawing a submission also requires `expected_unit_version` matching the unit's current `version`, rejected on mismatch with `ERR_STALE_UNIT_VERSION` (HTTP 412), because a withdrawal restores an attempt and compensates the escrow against a view of the unit the client must still hold.

Generating a notification letter on an exam requires a non-optional `expected_exam_version` field carrying the client's last observed `version` of that `Exam`. A mismatch with the exam's current `version` is rejected with `ERR_STALE_EXAM_VERSION` (HTTP 412). Generation legitimately supersedes the exam's prior letter, so without this guard the operation would succeed twice and mail a second patient letter whose density statement and notice codes were selected from a superseded interpretation.

No other endpoint accepts a version field. Callers of any other write operation must not send one, and the server must not require one.

### 2.10. Facility Compliance Posture and Cascade Discipline

`compliance_posture` on a facility is never set by a client. It is recomputed from scratch — from the current state of every mammography unit, every annual inspection and every personnel row at that facility — at the end of any accepted write that changes a unit state field, records a physicist survey decision, changes an annual inspection's status, changes a personnel qualification or continuing-education field, or records a clinical image review decision, and once at the end of every compliance run.

The recomputation is **first match wins**, over three ordered levels. A `SUSPENDED` trigger outranks every `CONDITIONAL` trigger, even when several `CONDITIONAL` predicates hold in the same snapshot.

Level one yields `SUSPENDED` if any of these four conditions holds: at least one unit at the facility has `accreditation_status` `SUSPENDED`; or at least one unit has `operational_state` `OUT_OF_SERVICE` while its `accreditation_status` is `PROBATION` or `SUSPENDED`; or at least one annual inspection has `inspector_findings_level` `LEVEL_1` while its `status` is `CORRECTIVE_ACTION_REQUIRED`; or the facility has at least one unit and every one of its units has `operational_state` `OUT_OF_SERVICE`.

Level two, reached only when no level-one condition holds, yields `CONDITIONAL` if any of these five conditions holds: at least one unit has `accreditation_status` `PROBATION` or `REACCREDITATION_DUE`; or at least one unit has `survey_compliance` `OVERDUE` or `FAILED`; or at least one unit has `operational_state` `OUT_OF_SERVICE`; or at least one annual inspection has `inspector_findings_level` `LEVEL_2` while its `status` is `CORRECTIVE_ACTION_REQUIRED`; or at least one personnel row has `ce_status` `DEFICIENT` or `qualification_status` `LAPSED`.

Level three, reached when nothing above matched, yields `COMPLIANT`. A facility with no units at all is therefore `COMPLIANT`.

Every posture change refreshes the facility's `updated_at` and increases its `version` by exactly 1. All six observable jumps are reachable: `COMPLIANT` to `CONDITIONAL` when a unit enters `PROBATION` or its `survey_compliance` becomes `OVERDUE` or `FAILED` while at least one unit is still in service and no Level 1 corrective action is open; `COMPLIANT` to `SUSPENDED` and `CONDITIONAL` to `SUSPENDED` under the same level-one predicate; `CONDITIONAL` to `COMPLIANT` and `SUSPENDED` to `COMPLIANT` when no unit is on probation, every unit's `survey_compliance` is `CURRENT`, at least one unit is in service, and no `LEVEL_1` finding remains in `CORRECTIVE_ACTION_REQUIRED`; and `SUSPENDED` to `CONDITIONAL` when a unit is back in service with `accreditation_status` among `PROVISIONAL`, `ACCREDITED` or `PROBATION`, no `LEVEL_1` finding remains open, but some unit is still on `PROBATION` or still has `survey_compliance` `OVERDUE` or `FAILED`.

**The referral cascade.** When a recomputation produces `SUSPENDED` and the previous value was not `SUSPENDED`, the service immediately refers out the facility's open supplemental screening work. Every `SupplementalScreeningOrder` reachable from the facility — through a unit of the facility, to an exam acquired on that unit, to that exam's orders — whose `status` is `RECOMMENDED` or `COVERAGE_DETERMINED` moves to `REFERRED_OUT` with `external_referral_note` stamped, and each such order in turn moves its exam's `follow_up_status` from `PENDING` to `PENDING_EXTERNAL`. Orders are processed in a pinned order: ascending exam `performed_at` first, then ascending order `created_at` as the tie-break, never by `id`. Referral is one-way — a later posture recovery to `CONDITIONAL` or `COMPLIANT` does **not** restore referred-out orders.

Cascades are transactional and ordered. A single accepted request may traverse five levels: a failing survey decision sets the unit's `survey_compliance` (level 1), which drives `accreditation_status` and possibly `operational_state` (level 2), which triggers the posture recomputation (level 3), which may refer out open orders (level 4), which flips those orders' exams to `PENDING_EXTERNAL` (level 5). Every entity touched anywhere in that chain has its own `updated_at` refreshed and its own `version` increased by 1, even though the request addressed only the survey.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Policy Foundations, Facility Registration and the Accreditation-Fee Account

Nothing else in the system can be created until at least one state policy exists, because a facility must name its home state's rules at registration. Registering a state policy is reserved to the two regulator-side roles, the `AccreditationBodyReviewer` and the `FdaInspector`; facility staff never author the rules they are measured against. The payload carries a `state_code` of exactly two uppercase letters, unique across all registered policies — a second attempt on the same state is rejected with `ERR_DUPLICATE_RESOURCE`. It carries a `density_notice_rule` naming which density-notification regime the state layers on top of the federal lay-summary rule, one of `FEDERAL_ONLY`, `STATE_DENSE_ONLY` or `STATE_ENHANCED_ALL`. It carries a `lay_summary_max_reading_grade`, the state's ceiling on the plain-language reading grade of the summary, from 4 through 12; a `lay_summary_deadline_days`, the state's delivery deadline counted in days from letter generation, from 1 through 90; and a `supplemental_coverage_mandate`, one of `NONE`, `COVERAGE_WITH_COST_SHARING` or `COVERAGE_NO_COST_SHARING`, governing how supplemental screening ordered off a dense-breast finding is paid for.

Five further policy fields are optional and each has a declared default. `required_notice_codes` is an ordered list of state-specific notice codes, empty when omitted, and its declared order is preserved verbatim wherever it is used. `supplemental_cost_share_waiver_threshold_cents` is the de-minimis patient-responsibility amount the facility absorbs under a cost-sharing mandate; it defaults to 0 and may not be negative. `followup_grace_days` is how many days a recommended supplemental order may sit before it becomes lost to follow-up, defaulting to 60 and constrained from 1 through 365. `report_turnaround_days` is how many days after acquisition a routine mammogram report is due, defaulting to 30 and constrained from 1 through 90. `urgent_report_turnaround_days` is the tighter deadline for an urgent finding, defaulting to 7, constrained from 1 through 30, and additionally never permitted to exceed `report_turnaround_days`. `ce_grace_days` is the grace period granted when a continuing-education or volume shortfall is first detected, defaulting to 90 and constrained from 1 through 365. Any policy can be read back individually, and the facilities that name a given policy as their home-state fallback can be listed from the policy itself.

An `AccreditationCoordinator` registers the breast imaging center. The payload names the center's `name`, between 1 and 120 characters; the `state_policy_id` of its home-state policy, which must exist; the `accreditation_body` that will review its clinical image packets, either `ACR` or `STATE_CERTIFIED`; and optionally `supplemental_concurrent_capacity`, the number of supplemental screening appointments the center can occupy at one instant, defaulting to 2 and constrained from 1 through 10. A freshly registered facility starts with an empty fee ledger — `total_funded_cents`, `prepaid_credit_cents`, `fee_held_cents` and `fee_consumed_cents` all 0 — a `compliance_posture` of `COMPLIANT`, `open_notice_obligations` of 0, and a null `last_compliance_run_at`, since no compliance run has happened yet.

Accreditation review is not free, and the center pre-funds it. A coordinator records a funding deposit against the facility with an `amount_cents` and an optional `memo`; the deposit adds that amount to both `total_funded_cents` and `prepaid_credit_cents` and answers with the refreshed facility, not with a deposit receipt. A deposit whose `amount_cents` is not a positive integer number of cents is refused with `ERR_INVALID_DEPOSIT_AMOUNT`. Because `total_funded_cents` and `fee_consumed_cents` never decrease, and because `prepaid_credit_cents` and `fee_held_cents` only ever move by exact multiples of the 250000-cent application fee, the facility ledger reads as an auditable history rather than a running balance.

Adding a mammography unit to the facility is also a coordinator action. The unit's `facility_id` is taken from the creation path and never from the body. The payload names the equipment `manufacturer`, the `model_serial` — which must be unique among that facility's units, or the create is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_RESOURCE` — the `modality` the unit is accredited for, either `DM_2D` for two-dimensional digital mammography or `DBT_3D` for digital breast tomosynthesis, and the `accreditation_expires_on` instant at which the current certificate lapses. A new unit lands with `accreditation_status` `PROVISIONAL` and `operational_state` `IN_SERVICE` — two dimensions that evolve entirely independently of one another — with `survey_compliance` `OVERDUE` because no physicist survey has passed yet, a null `last_passed_survey_end`, and `reaccreditation_attempts_remaining` of 3. Its image ledger starts empty: `total_images_acquired` and `repeat_images_acquired` at 0, both `image_based_repeat_rate_pct` and `patient_based_repeat_rate_pct` null, and `repeat_rate_flag` false. Because the new unit's `survey_compliance` is `OVERDUE`, the facility posture recomputation that follows the create will move a previously `COMPLIANT` facility to `CONDITIONAL`.

Units can be listed under their facility, filtered by `accreditation_status` and `operational_state`, and read individually — the individual read is where both state dimensions, the conserved image ledger and both repeat-rate variants surface together.

### 3.2. Staffing the Facility: Qualification Thresholds, Credits and Continuing Education

Personnel are enrolled by an `AccreditationCoordinator` under the facility, which supplies the `facility_id`. Each person declares a `full_name`, a `role_type` — `INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN`, `TECHNOLOGIST` or `MEDICAL_PHYSICIST`, the MQSA personnel category that fixes every threshold applied to them — the `initial_qualification_on` instant at which initial qualification was attested, the attested `initial_training_hours` of category-1 training, and the attested `initial_read_volume` of supervised work, which means interpretations for a physician, exams performed for a technologist, and surveys assisted for a physicist. Neither attested figure may be negative, and `initial_qualification_on` may not be after the logical clock. Optionally the payload carries `modality_certifications`, a list of modality codes drawn from `DM_2D` and `DBT_3D`, empty when omitted.

The initial thresholds are evaluated at enrollment and are role-specific. An `INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN` needs at least 60 initial training hours and at least 240 initial read volume. A `TECHNOLOGIST` needs at least 40 hours and at least 25. A `MEDICAL_PHYSICIST` needs at least 20 hours and at least 1. A value exactly equal to a threshold satisfies it. When both of a role's initial thresholds are met the person is created with `qualification_status` `QUALIFIED`; otherwise they are created `PROVISIONAL`. Either way `ce_status` starts `COMPLIANT`, `ce_hours_36mo` and `volume_credit_24mo` start at 0, and `ce_deadline_at` and `last_qualification_review_at` start null. `qualification_status` has four possible values in all — `PROVISIONAL`, `QUALIFIED`, `LAPSED` and `INACTIVE` — and it is a parallel dimension to `ce_status`, whose values are `COMPLIANT`, `GRACE` and `DEFICIENT`. A person can sit in `GRACE` for an entire grace period while remaining `QUALIFIED`.

Continuing education and reading volume accumulate through credit rows, posted by a coordinator against the person, whose `personnel_id` comes from the creation path. A credit declares a `credit_type` of either `CATEGORY_1_CE` or `READ_VOLUME`, and an `awarded_on` instant that may not be after the logical clock. A `CATEGORY_1_CE` credit carries `ce_hours`, which is required for that type, must be strictly greater than zero, and must be absent for the other type. A `READ_VOLUME` credit carries `exam_count`, likewise required for its own type, strictly positive, and absent otherwise. A credit may optionally name the `modality` it applies to — `DM_2D`, `DBT_3D`, or null meaning all modalities — and may carry a free-text `source_note` describing its provenance. Credit rows are immutable and, on their own, change nothing about the person: posting a credit never moves `qualification_status` or `ce_status`. A qualification review must be run to convert credits into state. Credits can be listed under the person and filtered by `credit_type`.

Running a qualification review is a coordinator action carrying only an optional `review_note`, and it answers with the refreshed personnel row. The review recomputes two lookback aggregates against the logical clock. `ce_hours_36mo` is the sum of `ce_hours` across the person's `CATEGORY_1_CE` credits whose `awarded_on` falls in the window that begins 1095 days before the clock and ends at the clock, with both edges included. `volume_credit_24mo` is the sum of `exam_count` across the person's `READ_VOLUME` credits whose `awarded_on` falls in the window that begins 730 days before the clock and ends at the clock, again with both edges included. Every role carries the same continuing requirement of at least 15 category-1 hours inside the 36-month window, but the volume requirements differ sharply: an `INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN` must show at least 960 interpretations inside the 24-month window, a `TECHNOLOGIST` at least 200, and a `MEDICAL_PHYSICIST` at least 2. A shortfall exists when the accumulated hours fall below the role's hour requirement, or the accumulated volume falls below the role's volume requirement, or both. Whatever the outcome, `last_qualification_review_at` is stamped with the logical clock.

The review's branches are mutually exclusive and evaluated in order. When no shortfall remains, `ce_status` becomes `COMPLIANT` and `ce_deadline_at` is cleared; a person who was `LAPSED` returns to `QUALIFIED`, and a person who was `PROVISIONAL` becomes `QUALIFIED` provided their original initial thresholds were met. Otherwise, when a shortfall is detected and `ce_status` was still `COMPLIANT`, this is a first detection: `ce_status` moves `COMPLIANT` to `GRACE` and `ce_deadline_at` is set to the logical clock plus the governing policy's `ce_grace_days`, while `qualification_status` is deliberately left untouched because the two dimensions are parallel. Otherwise, when `ce_status` is `GRACE` and the logical clock has passed `ce_deadline_at` while a shortfall still exists, the grace period has expired: `ce_status` moves `GRACE` to `DEFICIENT`, and a person who was `QUALIFIED` moves to `LAPSED`. Otherwise, when `ce_status` is `GRACE` and the clock is still inside the granted period, nothing changes at all. And when `ce_status` is already `DEFICIENT` and the shortfall persists, a `QUALIFIED` person is moved to `LAPSED`. The `DEFICIENT` to `COMPLIANT` recovery is the mirror of the first branch: make-up credits landing inside both windows return `ce_status` to `COMPLIANT`, clear `ce_deadline_at`, and lift `qualification_status` from `LAPSED` back to `QUALIFIED`.

A lapse has no retroactive effect. Exams already recorded by a technologist who later goes `LAPSED` stay valid; the consequence is forward-looking only, in that a `LAPSED` technologist can no longer be named on a new exam and a `LAPSED` physician can no longer interpret or amend. Because the posture recomputation counts a person with `ce_status` `DEFICIENT` or `qualification_status` `LAPSED` as a `CONDITIONAL` trigger, a single deficient staff member degrades the whole facility's posture. Personnel can be listed under their facility with filters on `role_type`, `qualification_status` and `ce_status`, and an individual read exposes both parallel dimensions together with the two lookback aggregates.

### 3.3. Physicist Surveys, Equipment Failures and the Service Dimension

A `MedicalPhysicist` opens a survey record on a unit; the survey's `unit_id` comes from the creation path and its `physicist_id` is taken from the caller's `X-User-ID`, never from the body. The payload declares a `survey_type` — `ANNUAL` for the yearly MQSA survey, `POST_REPAIR` for the survey required before a unit returns to service after a qualifying failure, or `MOBILE` for a mobile-unit relocation check — together with the validity window's inclusive `effective_start` and exclusive `effective_end`. Measurements may be supplied at creation but need not be: `phantom_score`, `mean_glandular_dose_mgy`, `critical_deficiency_codes` and `outcome_note` are all optional here, and any values captured at creation are overwritten by whatever the decision call later supplies. A new survey lands in `status` `DRAFT` with `remediation_attempts` at 0, a null `decided_at`, and `critical_deficiency_codes` defaulting to an empty list.

Window hygiene is enforced at creation in two steps. First the window must be well formed: `effective_end` strictly after `effective_start`, and the span between them at most 396 days, or the create is refused with `ERR_SURVEY_WINDOW_INVALID`. Then the window must not overlap a live window of the same `survey_type` on the same unit, where "live" means `status` `DRAFT`, `PASSED` or `FAILED_ACTION_REQUIRED` — a `FAILED_FINAL` survey releases its window and is ignored. The window is closed on the left and open on the right, so two windows overlap only when the existing window's `effective_start` is strictly before the new `effective_end` **and** the new `effective_start` is strictly before the existing `effective_end`. An incoming window whose `effective_start` exactly equals an existing window's `effective_end` is therefore adjacent, not overlapping, and is allowed; a genuine overlap is refused with `ERR_SURVEY_WINDOW_OVERLAP`. Surveys can be read individually, listed under their unit filtered by `status` and `survey_type`, and listed globally with filters on `unit_id`, `physicist_id`, `status` and `survey_type`.

The survey decision is where the branching happens. Only the authoring physicist may record it, and only on a survey still in `DRAFT`; a decision on any other state is `ERR_SURVEY_NOT_DECIDABLE`. The decision supplies `phantom_score` and `mean_glandular_dose_mgy` as required fields plus optional `critical_deficiency_codes` and `outcome_note`, and those supplied measurements overwrite whatever the survey was carrying. The survey passes when all three of the following hold simultaneously: the `phantom_score` is at least 6.0; the `mean_glandular_dose_mgy` is at most 3.00; and `critical_deficiency_codes` is empty. The phantom score is a lower bound and the dose is an upper bound, and neither comparison is preceded by rounding. All three conjuncts are evaluated together — a perfect phantom score does not excuse an over-dose unit, and neither excuses a recorded critical deficiency.

On a pass, the survey moves `DRAFT` to `PASSED` with `decided_at` stamped at the logical clock. The unit's `last_passed_survey_end` is set to the survey's `effective_end`, and the unit's `survey_compliance` becomes `CURRENT` when the logical clock falls inside the window — at or after `effective_start` and strictly before `effective_end` — and is otherwise left unchanged, which for a window entirely in the past means the unit remains `OVERDUE`. The facility posture is then recomputed, and the unit's `version` advances.

On a failure, the survey moves `DRAFT` to `FAILED_ACTION_REQUIRED` with `decided_at` stamped, and the five-level cascade described in §2.10 opens. Level one sets the unit's `survey_compliance` to `FAILED`. Level two, caused by that, moves the unit's `accreditation_status` to `PROBATION` — this fires from `ACCREDITED`, from `PROVISIONAL` and from `REACCREDITATION_DUE` alike — and, only when the deciding survey's `critical_deficiency_codes` is non-empty, additionally moves the unit's `operational_state` from `IN_SERVICE` to `OUT_OF_SERVICE`. Level three recomputes the facility posture from the changed unit fields. Level four, when that posture recomputation newly produces `SUSPENDED`, refers out the facility's open supplemental orders. Level five flips each referred order's exam `follow_up_status` from `PENDING` to `PENDING_EXTERNAL`. The unit's `version` advances by 1.

Remediation is the coordinator's side of the split, and it is only available on a survey in `FAILED_ACTION_REQUIRED`. It carries the same four measurement fields and re-runs the same three-part pass test against the re-measured values. Each remediation increments `remediation_attempts` and stamps `decided_at`; `remediation_attempts` is bounded at 2. A first remediation that still fails — meaning `remediation_attempts` was 0 and becomes 1 — is a genuine self-loop: the survey stays in `FAILED_ACTION_REQUIRED`, the measurements are overwritten, the unit's `survey_compliance` stays `FAILED`, and no further unit transition fires. A remediation that passes moves the survey `FAILED_ACTION_REQUIRED` to `PASSED`, still incrementing `remediation_attempts`, sets `last_passed_survey_end` to the survey's `effective_end`, restores `survey_compliance` to `CURRENT` when the window covers the clock, and recomputes the facility posture — but a unit already on `PROBATION` stays on probation until a clinical image review passes; survey recovery alone does not lift probation. A second remediation that still fails — meaning `remediation_attempts` was already 1 — is terminal: the survey moves `FAILED_ACTION_REQUIRED` to `FAILED_FINAL` with `remediation_attempts` at 2, and the unit's `operational_state` becomes `OUT_OF_SERVICE` unconditionally, whether or not any critical deficiency code was recorded. The facility posture is recomputed and the unit's `version` advances.

Equipment failures arrive from the shop floor. A `Technologist`, a `MedicalPhysicist` or an `AccreditationCoordinator` may report one against a unit, supplying the failed subsystem as a `failure_code` from `DETECTOR`, `AEC`, `COLLIMATION`, `COMPRESSION`, `DISPLAY` or `SOFTWARE`; an operational `severity` of `MINOR`, `MAJOR` or `CRITICAL`; and the `detected_at` instant, which may not be after the logical clock. The event's `unit_id` comes from the path. The event lands in `status` `OPEN` with a null `resolved_at`, and the service derives `requires_post_repair_survey`: it is true exactly when the `severity` is `CRITICAL` **or** the `failure_code` is one of `DETECTOR`, `AEC` or `COLLIMATION`, and false otherwise. A `MINOR` display fault is therefore merely logged, while a `MINOR` detector fault is not. When `requires_post_repair_survey` is true the unit's `operational_state` moves `IN_SERVICE` to `OUT_OF_SERVICE`, the facility posture is recomputed off that change, and the unit's `version` advances.

Returning a unit to service is a coordinator action on the failure event, carrying a required `repair_note`. It is available only while the event is `OPEN` — a second attempt is `ERR_FAILURE_ALREADY_RESOLVED` — and it is gated by three further conditions. When the event required a post-repair survey, a survey must exist on the same unit with `survey_type` `POST_REPAIR`, `status` `PASSED`, and an `effective_start` at or after the event's `detected_at`; otherwise the restore is refused with `ERR_POST_REPAIR_SURVEY_MISSING`, which is deliberately precise about ordering — a post-repair survey whose window opened before the failure was even detected cannot certify the repair. The unit's `accreditation_status` must not be `SUSPENDED`, or the restore is refused with `ERR_UNIT_ACCREDITATION_SUSPENDED`. And the unit only actually returns to service when no *other* `OPEN` failure event on it still has `requires_post_repair_survey` true — a unit with two qualifying failures needs both repairs verified before it images again. On success the event moves `OPEN` to `REPAIR_VERIFIED` with `resolved_at` stamped and `repair_note` stored, the unit's `operational_state` returns to `IN_SERVICE` when nothing else holds it down, the facility posture is recomputed, and the versions of the event and the unit advance. Failure events can be read individually and listed under their unit with filters on `status` and `severity`.

### 3.4. The Accreditation Cycle: Clinical Image Packets, the Attempt Budget and the Fee Escrow

Reaccreditation is decided on images, not paperwork. The facility assembles a clinical image packet — a set of already-interpreted exams from one unit — and files it with its accreditation body, which either passes it or sends it back for revision. The whole cycle is metered by two scarce resources: the unit's `reaccreditation_attempts_remaining`, which starts at 3 and is bounded from 0 to 3, and the facility's `prepaid_credit_cents`, from which each filing escrows an application fee of 250000 cents — two thousand five hundred dollars.

Filing a packet is an `AccreditationCoordinator` action against the unit, which supplies the submission's `unit_id`. The payload carries `exam_ids`, the list of exams forming the packet; `expected_unit_version`, the mandatory concurrency token described in §2.9; and an optional `reviewer_note`. `exam_ids` is a many-to-many packet membership rather than a scalar reference: it must contain at least 2 distinct ids with no duplicates (`ERR_PACKET_TOO_SMALL`), every id must resolve to an existing exam (`ERR_EXAM_NOT_FOUND`), every one of those exams must have been acquired on this same unit (`ERR_EXAM_UNIT_MISMATCH`), and every one must be in `report_status` `INTERPRETED_FINAL` or `AMENDED` (`ERR_PACKET_EXAM_NOT_FINAL`) — a packet of un-read images certifies nothing. Beyond packet shape, the filing requires that the unit still have at least one attempt left (`ERR_REACCREDITATION_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`), that the unit's `accreditation_status` not already be `SUSPENDED` (`ERR_UNIT_ACCREDITATION_SUSPENDED`), that the facility carry no `LEVEL_1` inspection finding still in `CORRECTIVE_ACTION_REQUIRED` (`ERR_OPEN_LEVEL_1_FINDING`), that the unit still be inside its reaccreditation window — meaning `accreditation_expires_on` is at or after the logical clock, or the unit has never been `ACCREDITED` at all (`ERR_REACCREDITATION_WINDOW_CLOSED`) — that no other non-terminal submission in `SUBMITTED` or `REVISION_REQUIRED` already exist on the unit (`ERR_SUBMISSION_ALREADY_OPEN`), and finally that `prepaid_credit_cents` be at least 250000 (`ERR_INSUFFICIENT_PREPAID_CREDIT`).

An accepted filing lands the submission in `status` `SUBMITTED` with `attempt_number` 1, `submitted_at` stamped at the logical clock, a null `reviewed_at`, a null `review_decision`, an empty `deficiency_categories`, and `fee_held_cents` of 250000. The escrow step moves exactly 250000 cents out of `prepaid_credit_cents` and into `fee_held_cents` on the facility. The unit's `reaccreditation_attempts_remaining` decreases by 1 and its `version` advances, and the facility posture is recomputed. The facility's `fee_held_cents` must at all times equal 250000 multiplied by the number of that facility's submissions currently in `SUBMITTED` — that identity is the ledger's self-check.

The accreditation body's decision is the `AccreditationBodyReviewer`'s exclusive act, available only on a submission in `SUBMITTED` (otherwise `ERR_SUBMISSION_NOT_REVIEWABLE`). It carries a `decision` of either `PASS` or `REVISION_REQUIRED`, optional `deficiency_categories` cited on a revision, and an optional `reviewer_note`. Every decision, whatever its verdict, consumes the escrow: 250000 cents leave `fee_held_cents` and enter `fee_consumed_cents`, the submission's own `fee_held_cents` drops to 0, and `prepaid_credit_cents` is untouched — once images have been reviewed the fee is non-refundable. `reviewed_at` is stamped at the logical clock and `review_decision` records the verdict.

A `PASS` moves the submission `SUBMITTED` to `PASSED` and re-accredits the unit: `accreditation_status` becomes `ACCREDITED`, `accreditation_expires_on` is set to the logical clock plus 1095 days — three years — and `reaccreditation_attempts_remaining` is restored to 3 for the new cycle. This same passing path lifts a unit out of `PROVISIONAL`, out of `REACCREDITATION_DUE`, and out of `PROBATION`, with one extra condition on the probation route: probation is only lifted when the unit's `survey_compliance` is `CURRENT`, that is, when a `PASSED` physicist survey window covers the logical clock. A unit still carrying failed or overdue survey currency stays on `PROBATION` even though its packet passed, because the `PROBATION` to `ACCREDITED` transition is simply not available to it. In all cases the facility posture is recomputed and the unit's `version` advances.

A `REVISION_REQUIRED` decision forks on the attempt budget. While the unit still has at least 1 attempt remaining, the submission moves `SUBMITTED` to `REVISION_REQUIRED`, the cited `deficiency_categories` are stored on the row, and the unit's `accreditation_status` moves to `PROBATION` when it was `ACCREDITED` or `REACCREDITATION_DUE` — a unit that was merely `PROVISIONAL` stays `PROVISIONAL`, since it has no accreditation to demote. When instead the unit's `reaccreditation_attempts_remaining` has reached 0, the same `REVISION_REQUIRED` verdict is terminal: the submission moves `SUBMITTED` to `DENIED_FINAL`, and a three-level cascade follows. Level one sets the unit's `accreditation_status` to `SUSPENDED` — a terminal accreditation state. Level two, caused by that suspension, forces the unit's `operational_state` to `OUT_OF_SERVICE`. Level three, caused by the unit leaving service, recomputes the facility posture, which will read `SUSPENDED` and therefore fire the referral cascade of §2.10. Both the `PROBATION` and the `PROVISIONAL` route into `SUSPENDED` behave identically here; a unit that was never `ACCREDITED` reaches `SUSPENDED` straight from `PROVISIONAL` because no `REVISION_REQUIRED` review ever put it on probation.

Revising and refiling is the coordinator's move, available only on a submission in `REVISION_REQUIRED` (otherwise `ERR_SUBMISSION_NOT_REVISABLE`). It carries a fresh `exam_ids` list, an `expected_unit_version`, and an optional `reviewer_note`, and it re-runs every packet check the original filing ran: minimum size, existence, unit membership, and report finality. It also requires an attempt still remaining, no open `LEVEL_1` finding, an open reaccreditation window, and at least 250000 cents of `prepaid_credit_cents`. On success the submission returns `REVISION_REQUIRED` to `SUBMITTED`, `attempt_number` increases by 1 — bounded at 3 — the packet's `exam_ids` are replaced wholesale, `submitted_at` is re-stamped at the logical clock, the fee is escrowed again, the unit's `reaccreditation_attempts_remaining` decreases by 1, and the unit's `version` advances. Three passes through the revision loop therefore exhaust the budget, and the fourth `REVISION_REQUIRED` verdict is the one that suspends the unit.

Withdrawal is the compensating move, available to the coordinator only while the submission is still `SUBMITTED` (otherwise `ERR_SUBMISSION_NOT_WITHDRAWABLE`), and it also requires `expected_unit_version`; it carries an optional `withdrawal_reason`. Withdrawal moves the submission `SUBMITTED` to `WITHDRAWN` and reverses the escrow exactly: 250000 cents return from `fee_held_cents` to `prepaid_credit_cents`, the submission's `fee_held_cents` drops to 0, and `fee_consumed_cents` and `total_funded_cents` are left completely untouched — a withdrawal costs the facility nothing. The unit's `reaccreditation_attempts_remaining` increases by 1, restoring the attempt the filing spent, while the unit's `accreditation_status` is left exactly as it was. The unit's `version` advances. `PASSED`, `DENIED_FINAL` and `WITHDRAWN` are the three terminal submission states; only `SUBMITTED` and `REVISION_REQUIRED` are live. Submissions can be read individually and listed under their unit filtered by `status`.

### 3.5. Annual Inspections and Corrective Actions

An `FdaInspector` records the annual MQSA inspection against the facility, which supplies the record's `facility_id`. The payload names the `inspection_date` on which the inspection was conducted, which may not be after the logical clock; the `inspector_findings_level`, the most severe finding recorded, one of `NONE`, `LEVEL_3`, `LEVEL_2` or `LEVEL_1` in ascending order of severity; and optionally `finding_codes`, the list of citation codes, empty when omitted.

The finding level branches the record at creation, before the row is ever observable. A `NONE` or `LEVEL_3` finding lands the inspection directly in `status` `RECORDED` with a null `corrective_action_due_at`. A `LEVEL_2` or `LEVEL_1` finding instead lands it in `CORRECTIVE_ACTION_REQUIRED` and stamps `corrective_action_due_at`: the `inspection_date` plus 15 days for a `LEVEL_1` finding, or the `inspection_date` plus 30 days for a `LEVEL_2` finding. `closed_at` is null until the corrective action is accepted.

The posture consequence is sharply asymmetric. An open `LEVEL_2` corrective action is a `CONDITIONAL` trigger, but an open `LEVEL_1` corrective action is a level-one `SUSPENDED` trigger — recording one drops the facility straight to `SUSPENDED` and fires the referral cascade, moving every open supplemental order at the facility to `REFERRED_OUT` and every such order's exam `follow_up_status` from `PENDING` to `PENDING_EXTERNAL`. While that `LEVEL_1` finding remains open the facility also cannot file or refile a clinical image packet.

Closing an inspection is likewise the inspector's act, carrying an optional `closure_note`. It is available from both live states. From `CORRECTIVE_ACTION_REQUIRED` it moves the inspection to `CLOSED`, stamps `closed_at` at the logical clock, and recomputes the facility posture — a closed `LEVEL_1` finding no longer forces `SUSPENDED`, so the facility can climb back to `CONDITIONAL` or `COMPLIANT` depending on what else is wrong, though any orders already referred out stay referred out. From `RECORDED` it moves equally to `CLOSED` and stamps `closed_at`; there was no corrective action to accept, so the posture is unaffected by the closure itself. Either way the inspection's `version` advances. `CLOSED` is terminal: a second closure attempt is refused with `ERR_INSPECTION_ALREADY_CLOSED`. Inspections can be read individually and listed under their facility with filters on `status` and `inspector_findings_level`.

### 3.6. The Exam Record: Acquisition, Interpretation, Amendment, Outcome and Repeat Documentation

An exam carries three completely independent state dimensions — `report_status` for the reporting workflow, `notification_status` for the patient-notification obligation, and `follow_up_status` for downstream follow-up — and it is worth stating plainly that a single exam can be `AMENDED`, `PENDING` and `LOST_TO_FOLLOW_UP` at the same time, because each dimension answers a different regulator.

Acquisition is the `Technologist`'s exclusive act, and it is the only exam-creating operation. The payload names the `unit_id` of the unit that acquired the images; the `technologist_id` of the acquiring technologist, which must equal the caller's own `X-User-ID`; a pseudonymous `patient_ref` that repeats across that patient's exams; the `patient_state_code`, exactly two uppercase letters, naming the patient's state of residence and thereby driving policy resolution; the `exam_type`, either `SCREENING` or `DIAGNOSTIC`; the `performed_at` acquisition instant, which may not be after the logical clock; and `original_image_count`, the number of images acquired on the first pass, at least 1. Acquisition is blocked when the unit's `operational_state` is `OUT_OF_SERVICE` (`ERR_UNIT_NOT_IN_SERVICE`), when the unit's `accreditation_status` is `SUSPENDED` (`ERR_UNIT_ACCREDITATION_SUSPENDED`), when the named technologist's `qualification_status` is anything other than `QUALIFIED` (`ERR_TECHNOLOGIST_NOT_QUALIFIED`), and when the technologist's `modality_certifications` does not contain the unit's `modality` (`ERR_MODALITY_NOT_CERTIFIED`).

A new exam lands with `report_status` `AWAITING_INTERPRETATION`, `notification_status` `NOT_REQUIRED` and `follow_up_status` `NOT_REQUIRED`; with `recall_flag` false, `biopsy_performed` false, `pathology_result` null, `repeat_image_count` 0, `amendment_count` 0 and `interpreted_late` false; and with `interpreting_physician_id`, `birads_assessment`, `density_category`, `interpreted_at`, `outcome_recorded_at` and `notification_deadline_at` all null. Its `report_due_at` is stamped immediately as the `performed_at` instant plus the governing policy's `report_turnaround_days`, counted in whole days — the routine turnaround, because at acquisition time nobody yet knows how urgent the finding is. The acquisition also refreshes the unit's image ledger: `total_images_acquired` is recomputed, both repeat-rate variants are recomputed, and the unit's `version` advances. Exams can be read individually and listed with filters on `unit_id`, `technologist_id`, `interpreting_physician_id`, `exam_type`, `report_status`, `notification_status` and `follow_up_status`.

Interpretation is the `InterpretingPhysician`'s exclusive act and is available from `AWAITING_INTERPRETATION` and from `DELAYED`; from any other reporting state it is `ERR_EXAM_NOT_INTERPRETABLE`. The acting physician must be a `Personnel` row at the exam's unit's facility with `role_type` `INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN`, must hold `qualification_status` `QUALIFIED` (`ERR_PHYSICIAN_NOT_QUALIFIED`), and must be certified for the unit's `modality` (`ERR_MODALITY_NOT_CERTIFIED`). The payload carries a required `birads_assessment`, the BI-RADS final assessment category from 0 through 6, and an optional `density_category`, the BI-RADS breast density category `A`, `B`, `C` or `D`. For a `SCREENING` exam the density category is not optional in practice: interpreting a screening exam without one is refused with `ERR_DENSITY_CATEGORY_REQUIRED`, because the federal lay summary cannot be composed without it.

A successful interpretation does six things. It moves `report_status` to `INTERPRETED_FINAL`, stamps `interpreted_at` at the logical clock, and writes `interpreting_physician_id` from the caller's `X-User-ID` — this is the moment the exam acquires an owner. It re-derives the reporting deadline: when the recorded `birads_assessment` is one of 0, 4 or 5, the urgency of the finding retroactively tightens `report_due_at` to the `performed_at` instant plus the governing policy's `urgent_report_turnaround_days`, replacing the routine deadline the exam has been carrying since acquisition; for any other assessment the routine `report_due_at` stands. It then sets `interpreted_late` to true exactly when `interpreted_at` is strictly after the resulting `report_due_at`, which means an exam interpreted precisely at its deadline is on time — and note that an exam reached through the `DELAYED` state is late by construction, so `interpreted_late` is true on that route. It recomputes `recall_flag`, which is true exactly when the `exam_type` is `SCREENING` **and** the `birads_assessment` is 0; a diagnostic BI-RADS 0 is not a recall, and a screening BI-RADS 4 is not a recall either. It opens the notification obligation by moving `notification_status` from `NOT_REQUIRED` to `PENDING`, and it opens the follow-up dimension by moving `follow_up_status` to `PENDING` when `recall_flag` came out true, leaving it `NOT_REQUIRED` otherwise. Finally the owning facility's `open_notice_obligations` is recomputed as the count of that facility's exams whose `notification_status` is `PENDING`, `GENERATED` or `SENT`, which on this path means it increases by 1, and the exam's `version` advances.

Amendment revisits a finished read. It is the `InterpretingPhysician`'s exclusive act, restricted by ownership to the exam's own `interpreting_physician_id`, and available from `INTERPRETED_FINAL` and from `AMENDED` — the latter is a real self-loop, so a report can be amended repeatedly, with `amendment_count` climbing each time. From any other reporting state it is `ERR_EXAM_NOT_AMENDABLE`. The payload carries an optional `birads_assessment`, an optional `density_category` and an optional `amendment_reason`, and at least one of the two clinical values must actually differ from what is stored, or the request is refused with `ERR_AMENDMENT_NO_CHANGE`. The amending physician must still hold `qualification_status` `QUALIFIED`.

An accepted amendment moves `report_status` to `AMENDED`, increments `amendment_count` by 1, and recomputes `recall_flag` from the new values — an amendment that changes a screening read from BI-RADS 2 to BI-RADS 0 turns the exam into a recall retroactively. Then a three-level cascade unwinds the notification obligation. Level two supersedes the exam's current letter: whichever `NotificationLetter` of this exam is in `QUEUED`, `SENT` or `DELIVERED_ASSUMED` moves to `SUPERSEDED`. Level three, caused by that supersession, moves the exam's `notification_status` back to `PENDING` and clears `notification_deadline_at`, because the letter the patient received described a finding that no longer stands. The effect on the facility's `open_notice_obligations` depends on where the notification dimension was: a letter that was `QUEUED` or `SENT` was already counted as an open obligation, so the count is unchanged, whereas a `COMPLETE` obligation reopening from a `DELIVERED_ASSUMED` letter increases the count by 1. Separately from that cascade chain, the amendment fans out to the audit ledger: every `LOCKED` `RadiologistAuditCycle` belonging to the exam's `interpreting_physician_id` whose period contains the exam's `performed_at` moves to `AMENDED`. The exam's `version` advances.

Pathology outcomes are recorded by the `QualityAssuranceOfficer`, never by the physician who signed the read. The payload carries `biopsy_performed` and `pathology_result`, where the result is `PENDING`, `BENIGN` or `MALIGNANT`, and the service stamps `outcome_recorded_at` at the logical clock. An outcome may be recorded once and refined while it is still open, but an exam whose `pathology_result` is already `BENIGN` or `MALIGNANT` is settled: a further attempt is refused with `ERR_OUTCOME_ALREADY_RECORDED`. Both positive-predictive-value variants read `biopsy_performed`, `pathology_result` and `outcome_recorded_at`, so this is the single input that decides whether a biopsied exam can be scored at all.

Repeated imaging must be documented, and the documentation is what MQSA audits. A `Technologist` or an `AccreditationCoordinator` records a repeat against the exam, which supplies the record's `exam_id`. The payload names the `reason_code` — `POSITIONING`, `MOTION`, `EXPOSURE`, `ARTIFACT` or `EQUIPMENT_FAILURE` — and the `repeat_image_count` of images re-acquired, at least 1. When and only when the reason is `EQUIPMENT_FAILURE`, the payload must also name the `equipment_failure_id` of the responsible failure event, and that event must belong to the same unit as the exam being repeated, or the record is refused with `ERR_FAILURE_UNIT_MISMATCH`. A new record stamps `documented_at` at the logical clock, lands with `retracted` false and a null `retraction_reason`, and immediately propagates into the ledger: the exam's own `repeat_image_count` is recomputed as the sum of `repeat_image_count` over that exam's non-retracted repeat records, then the unit's `repeat_images_acquired`, `total_images_acquired`, both repeat-rate variants and `repeat_rate_flag` are recomputed, and the unit's `version` advances.

The two repeat rates are deliberately different measures of the same behaviour, and both are always computed and always exposed on the unit. `repeat_images_acquired` is the sum of `repeat_image_count` across the unit's exams, and `total_images_acquired` is the sum, across the same exams, of `original_image_count` plus that exam's `repeat_image_count`; every exam of the unit is included regardless of its `report_status`. The image-based rate, `image_based_repeat_rate_pct`, is 100 multiplied by `repeat_images_acquired`, divided by `total_images_acquired`, rounded half up to two decimal places — and it is null when `total_images_acquired` is 0. The patient-based rate, `patient_based_repeat_rate_pct`, is 100 multiplied by the number of the unit's exams that have at least one non-retracted repeat, divided by the total number of the unit's exams, rounded half up to two decimal places — and it is null when the unit has no exams at all. The distinction matters: one exam repeated ten times moves the image-based rate hard but contributes exactly as much to the patient-based rate as an exam repeated once. Both ratios are formed from the aggregated integer counts and rounded once at the end, never by averaging per-exam rates. Only the image-based figure drives the regulatory flag: `repeat_rate_flag` is true exactly when `image_based_repeat_rate_pct` is non-null and strictly greater than 5.00 percent, so a unit sitting at exactly 5.00 is not flagged.

Retraction is the `QualityAssuranceOfficer`'s remedy for a repeat documented in error. It carries a required `retraction_reason`, sets `retracted` to true, and is available only once — a second attempt is refused with `ERR_REPEAT_RECORD_ALREADY_RETRACTED`. Retraction then reverses the ledger exactly: the exam's `repeat_image_count` and the unit's `repeat_images_acquired`, `total_images_acquired`, both rate variants and `repeat_rate_flag` are recomputed with the retracted record excluded, returning `total_images_acquired` to precisely the value it held before the record was ever documented. Repeat records can be read individually, listed under their exam filtered by `retracted`, and listed globally with filters on `exam_id`, `equipment_failure_id`, `reason_code` and `retracted`.

### 3.7. Patient Notification: Lay Summaries and Density Letters

Every interpreted mammogram owes the patient a plain-language letter, and composing it is the single most rule-dense operation in the service. An `AccreditationCoordinator` generates the letter against the exam, which supplies the letter's `exam_id`. The payload carries the mandatory `expected_exam_version` concurrency token from §2.9 and an optional `dispatch_channel`, either `MAIL` or `PORTAL`, defaulting to `MAIL`. Generation requires the exam to be in `report_status` `INTERPRETED_FINAL` or `AMENDED` and its `notification_status` to be `PENDING`.

Composition begins by resolving the governing policy for the exam as described in §2.6 and recording that policy's `state_code` as the letter's `governing_state_code`. Two derived predicates drive everything after that. The exam counts as **dense** when its `density_category` is `C` or `D`; category `C` is the first dense category, so `A` and `B` are not dense. The exam counts as **urgent** when its `birads_assessment` is 0, 4 or 5.

The federal density statement follows directly: `density_statement_code` is `FDA_DENSE` for a dense exam and `FDA_NOT_DENSE` otherwise, and this field is set on every letter regardless of what the state requires.

Whether a separate state notice applies depends on the policy's `density_notice_rule`. Under `STATE_ENHANCED_ALL` the state notice applies to every patient, dense or not. Under `STATE_DENSE_ONLY` it applies only when the exam is dense. Under `FEDERAL_ONLY` it never applies at all. The letter's `letter_type` is `COMBINED` when the state notice applies and `LAY_SUMMARY` when it does not. Two named regimes are worth naming explicitly because they behave differently over identical exam data. Under the **federal-floor** regime — a `FEDERAL_ONLY` policy, or a `STATE_DENSE_ONLY` policy meeting a non-dense exam — the letter is a `LAY_SUMMARY` and none of the state's declared `required_notice_codes` are appended, even though the policy declares them. Under the **state-layered** regime — a `STATE_ENHANCED_ALL` policy, or a `STATE_DENSE_ONLY` policy meeting a dense exam — the letter is `COMBINED` and the state's `required_notice_codes` are appended after the federal codes in exactly their declared order.

Two ceilings are then taken as minima, because a state may only tighten the federal floor and never loosen it. The letter's `reading_grade_level` is the lower of the policy's `lay_summary_max_reading_grade` and the federal ceiling of grade 8 — a state declaring grade 12 still yields grade 8, while a state declaring grade 6 yields grade 6. The deadline in days is the lower of the policy's `lay_summary_deadline_days` and the federal ceiling of 30 days; and then, when the exam is urgent, that result is lowered again to at most 7 days. A state permitting 90 days therefore produces 30 days for a routine finding and 7 days for a BI-RADS 0, 4 or 5 finding, while a state permitting 5 days produces 5 days in both cases because 5 is already below both ceilings. The letter's `deadline_at` is the generation instant — the logical clock — plus the resulting number of days, and the same instant is copied onto the exam as `notification_deadline_at`. `generated_at` is likewise stamped at the logical clock.

The letter's `required_notice_codes` is an ordered list, assembled by concatenation and then de-duplicated keeping each code's first occurrence. The first element is always the federal lay-summary code `FDA-LAY-SUMMARY`. Next comes the density code: `FDA-DENSE` for a dense exam or `FDA-NOT-DENSE` otherwise. Next, only when the exam is urgent, comes `FDA-URGENT-FINDING`. Finally, only when the state notice applies, the policy's own `required_notice_codes` are appended in their declared order. If a state happens to declare a code that a federal code already occupies, the duplicate is dropped and the earlier position is what survives.

Generation also supersedes history. Whichever letter of this exam is not already `SUPERSEDED` moves to `SUPERSEDED`, and the new letter's `supersedes_letter_id` points at the letter it replaced; on the first letter of an exam that field is null. The new letter lands in `status` `QUEUED` with null `sent_at` and null `undeliverable_reason`, the exam's `notification_status` moves `PENDING` to `GENERATED`, and the exam's `version` advances.

Dispatch is a coordinator action on the letter, taking no payload at all. It is available only from `QUEUED` — any other state is `ERR_LETTER_NOT_SENDABLE` — and it additionally requires the letter's exam still to be in `report_status` `INTERPRETED_FINAL` or `AMENDED`. Dispatch moves the letter `QUEUED` to `SENT`, stamps `sent_at` at the logical clock, moves the exam's `notification_status` to `SENT`, and advances both the letter's and the exam's `version`.

A letter that comes back is recorded by the coordinator with a required `undeliverable_reason`, and only from `SENT` — any other state is `ERR_LETTER_NOT_SENT`. The letter moves `SENT` to `UNDELIVERABLE` with the reason stored, the exam's `notification_status` returns to `PENDING`, and the exam's `notification_deadline_at` is cleared. The facility's `open_notice_obligations` is unchanged by this, because a `PENDING` obligation counts as open exactly as a `SENT` one did. Reopening the obligation is what lets the coordinator generate a replacement letter; when that replacement is generated, the `UNDELIVERABLE` letter moves to `SUPERSEDED` and the replacement's `supersedes_letter_id` points at it.

Delivery is never confirmed by the postal service, so it is assumed by the clock. During a compliance run, any letter still in `SENT` whose `sent_at` is at least 14 days before the logical clock — the comparison is inclusive, so exactly 14 days elapsed is enough — moves `SENT` to `DELIVERED_ASSUMED`, the exam's `notification_status` moves `SENT` to `COMPLETE`, and the facility's `open_notice_obligations` decreases by 1. `DELIVERED_ASSUMED` and `SUPERSEDED` are the two terminal letter states, and the notification dimension is cyclic precisely because an amendment can drag a `COMPLETE` exam back to `PENDING`, and an undeliverable return can drag a `SENT` exam back to `PENDING`. Letters can be read individually, listed under their exam filtered by `status`, and listed globally with filters on `exam_id`, `supersedes_letter_id`, `status` and `letter_type` — the `supersedes_letter_id` filter is how a reviewer walks a supersession chain.

### 3.8. Supplemental Screening: Coverage Determination, Scheduling and Follow-Up

A dense-breast finding often warrants additional screening, and whether the patient pays for it varies wildly by state. Either an `InterpretingPhysician` or an `AccreditationCoordinator` may recommend supplemental screening against the exam, which supplies the order's `exam_id`. The payload names the `modality` recommended — `ULTRASOUND`, `MRI` or `CEM` for contrast-enhanced mammography — and may carry a free-text `recommendation_note`. The exam must be in `report_status` `INTERPRETED_FINAL` or `AMENDED` and must carry a non-null `density_category`, or the recommendation is refused with `ERR_EXAM_NOT_ELIGIBLE_FOR_SUPPLEMENTAL`.

A new order lands in `status` `RECOMMENDED` with `coverage_outcome` `UNDETERMINED`, all five money and tier fields — `allowed_amount_cents`, `covered_amount_cents`, `patient_responsibility_cents`, `coverage_tier` and `governing_state_code` — still null, and null `appointment_start`, `appointment_end`, `completed_at` and `external_referral_note`. `recommended_at` is stamped at the logical clock, and `followup_due_at` is stamped as `recommended_at` plus the governing policy's `followup_grace_days`, in whole days. Recommending an order also moves the exam's `follow_up_status` to `PENDING`.

Coverage determination is a coordinator action available only from `RECOMMENDED` — any other state is `ERR_ORDER_NOT_DETERMINABLE` — and it takes no payload; the exam must carry a non-null `density_category`. It executes ten numbered steps in a fixed order, and the order is observable, because the tier is selected and recorded before the mandate's cost-sharing treatment is applied. An order whose patient share is entirely waived therefore still carries a non-null `coverage_tier`.

Step one resolves the governing policy and records its `state_code` as the order's `governing_state_code`. Step two derives whether the exam is dense, again meaning `density_category` `C` or `D`. Step three short-circuits on mandate: when the policy's `supplemental_coverage_mandate` is `NONE`, the `allowed_amount_cents` is the bare fee-schedule amount for the ordered modality with no administration fee added, the `coverage_outcome` is `PATIENT_RESPONSIBILITY`, `covered_amount_cents` is 0, `patient_responsibility_cents` equals the allowed amount, and `coverage_tier` stays null. Step four short-circuits on density: when a mandate does exist but the exam is not dense, the `allowed_amount_cents` is likewise the bare fee-schedule amount with no administration fee, the `coverage_outcome` is `DENIED`, `covered_amount_cents` is 0, `patient_responsibility_cents` equals the allowed amount, and `coverage_tier` stays null. The fee schedule is 28500 cents for `ULTRASOUND`, 120000 cents for `MRI`, and 60000 cents for `CEM`.

Step five applies only when a mandate exists and the exam is dense, and this is where the facility's 3000-cent administration fee enters: the `allowed_amount_cents` becomes the modality's fee-schedule amount plus 3000 cents. Crucially the administration fee is inside the tier decision, not bolted on afterwards. Step six selects the tier from that fee-inclusive amount, with both ceilings inclusive: an allowed amount of at most 31500 cents is `TIER_1`, an allowed amount above that but at most 63000 cents is `TIER_2`, and anything above 63000 cents is `TIER_3`. So an ultrasound at 28500 plus 3000 lands at 31500 cents and is exactly on the `TIER_1` ceiling; a contrast-enhanced study at 60000 plus 3000 lands at 63000 and is exactly on the `TIER_2` ceiling; an MRI at 120000 plus 3000 lands in `TIER_3`. Step seven prices it: the payer's share is the allowed amount multiplied by the tier rate — 0.615 for `TIER_1`, 0.775 for `TIER_2`, 0.90 for `TIER_3` — rounded half up to whole cents, and the residual patient share is the allowed amount minus that covered figure, so the two always sum back to the allowed amount exactly.

Step eight branches on the mandate for the no-cost-sharing case: when the policy's `supplemental_coverage_mandate` is `COVERAGE_NO_COST_SHARING`, the `coverage_outcome` is `COVERED_NO_COST_SHARE`, `covered_amount_cents` equals the whole `allowed_amount_cents`, `patient_responsibility_cents` is 0, and the `coverage_tier` selected in step six is still recorded on the row even though its rate was not billed. Step nine handles the de-minimis waiver under `COVERAGE_WITH_COST_SHARING`: when the residual patient share computed in step seven is at or below the policy's `supplemental_cost_share_waiver_threshold_cents`, the facility absorbs it — the `coverage_outcome` is `COVERED_NO_COST_SHARE`, `covered_amount_cents` becomes the whole `allowed_amount_cents`, `patient_responsibility_cents` is 0, and the tier is retained. Step ten is the ordinary cost-sharing result: the residual share survives only when it strictly exceeds the waiver threshold, in which case the `coverage_outcome` is `COVERED_WITH_COST_SHARE`, `covered_amount_cents` is the tier-rated figure from step seven, and `patient_responsibility_cents` is the residual. Whatever the branch, the order moves `RECOMMENDED` to `COVERAGE_DETERMINED` and its `version` advances.

Scheduling is a coordinator action, available from `COVERAGE_DETERMINED` and from `LOST_TO_FOLLOW_UP` — the latter is how a re-engaged patient rejoins the pathway — and from any other state it is `ERR_ORDER_NOT_SCHEDULABLE`. It carries a required `appointment_start`, which must be at or after the logical clock (`ERR_APPOINTMENT_IN_PAST`). The order's `coverage_outcome` must no longer be `UNDETERMINED` (`ERR_COVERAGE_NOT_DETERMINED`), and the owning facility's `compliance_posture` must not be `SUSPENDED` (`ERR_FACILITY_SUSPENDED`). The `appointment_end` is derived from the modality's duration: 30 minutes for `ULTRASOUND`, 45 minutes for `MRI`, and 30 minutes for `CEM`.

Room capacity is then enforced by interval overlap. Each scheduled order occupies an interval running from its `appointment_start` to its `appointment_end` plus 15 minutes of room turnover, and that occupancy interval is **closed on both ends**, so two intervals that merely touch at an endpoint do collide — a patient booked to start exactly when the previous slot's turnover ends is a collision, not a fit. The candidate order's occupancy is compared against every other order in `status` `SCHEDULED` whose exam's unit belongs to the same facility; orders in `RECOMMENDED`, `COVERAGE_DETERMINED`, `COMPLETED`, `REFERRED_OUT` or `LOST_TO_FOLLOW_UP` occupy nothing and are ignored. When the number of colliding peers reaches or exceeds the facility's `supplemental_concurrent_capacity`, the request is refused with `ERR_SUPPLEMENTAL_CAPACITY_CONFLICT`; a facility with the default capacity of 2 therefore admits a third overlapping booking only after one of the two existing ones leaves `SCHEDULED`. On success the order moves to `SCHEDULED`, `appointment_start` is stored and `appointment_end` computed, the exam's `follow_up_status` becomes `PENDING` — which for a re-engaged patient means moving it out of `LOST_TO_FOLLOW_UP` — and the order's `version` advances.

Completion is a coordinator action available only from `SCHEDULED`; from any other state it is `ERR_ORDER_NOT_COMPLETABLE`. It carries an optional `completion_note`, moves the order `SCHEDULED` to `COMPLETED`, stamps `completed_at` at the logical clock, and advances the order's `version`. The exam's `follow_up_status` becomes `COMPLETED` only when no other non-terminal order remains on that exam — a patient owed both an ultrasound and an MRI is not done until both are done.

Two involuntary exits also exist. During a compliance run, any order still in `RECOMMENDED` or `COVERAGE_DETERMINED` whose `followup_due_at` is strictly before the logical clock moves to `LOST_TO_FOLLOW_UP`, and its exam's `follow_up_status` moves to `LOST_TO_FOLLOW_UP` too. And when the owning facility's posture newly drops to `SUSPENDED`, any order in `RECOMMENDED` or `COVERAGE_DETERMINED` moves to `REFERRED_OUT` with `external_referral_note` stamped, carrying its exam's `follow_up_status` from `PENDING` to `PENDING_EXTERNAL`. `COMPLETED` and `REFERRED_OUT` are the two terminal order states; `LOST_TO_FOLLOW_UP` is not terminal, which is exactly what makes the follow-up dimension cyclic. Orders can be read individually, listed under their exam filtered by `status`, and listed globally with filters on `exam_id`, `status`, `modality` and `coverage_outcome`.

### 3.9. The Medical Outcomes Audit and the Compliance-Run Checkpoint

MQSA obliges each facility to audit its interpreting physicians against national benchmarks, and the service models that as an explicit audit cycle owned by a radiologist. A `QualityAssuranceOfficer` opens the cycle against a `Personnel` row, which supplies the cycle's `radiologist_id`; that person's `role_type` must be `INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN` or the request is refused with `ERR_NOT_INTERPRETING_PHYSICIAN`. The payload carries a required `period_start`, a required `period_end` that must be strictly after `period_start` (`ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`), and an optional `followup_window_days` defaulting to 365 and constrained to the inclusive range 30 through 730. The period is **half-open**: `period_start` is inclusive and `period_end` is exclusive. A new cycle whose period overlaps any existing cycle of the same radiologist is refused with `ERR_AUDIT_CYCLE_OVERLAP`, as is a second cycle for a radiologist who already has one in `OPEN`. A fresh cycle lands in `status` `OPEN` with `variant_at_close`, `locked_at` and every metric field — `screening_exam_count`, `recall_count`, `biopsy_count`, `cancer_count`, `excluded_exam_count`, `recall_rate_pct`, `ppv_pct` and `abnormal_interpretation_flag` — all null. Nothing is computed until the cycle is closed.

The in-scope exam set is identical for both metric variants. An exam is in scope when its `interpreting_physician_id` equals the cycle's `radiologist_id`, its `exam_type` is `SCREENING`, its `report_status` is `INTERPRETED_FINAL` or `AMENDED`, and its `performed_at` is at or after `period_start` and strictly before `period_end`. `DIAGNOSTIC` exams, exams still `AWAITING_INTERPRETATION` or `DELAYED`, exams read by another radiologist, and exams performed exactly at `period_end` are all outside the set. `screening_exam_count` is the size of that set and `recall_count` is the number of in-scope exams whose `recall_flag` is true — which, per §3.6, means BI-RADS 0 screening reads only, never BI-RADS 4 or 5.

The `recall_rate_pct` is null when `screening_exam_count` is zero; otherwise it is 100 times `recall_count` divided by `screening_exam_count`, pooled first and rounded half up to 2 decimals exactly once. It is never the mean of per-unit or per-modality rates: one recall out of one `DM_2D` exam together with zero recalls out of two `DBT_3D` exams pools to 33.33, whereas averaging the two sub-rates would wrongly give 50.00.

The two variants differ only in the denominator, and the difference is chosen by the closing call. Under `PPV1_SCREENING` — the MQSA and BCSC screening measure — the candidate set is the in-scope exams that were **both** recalled (`recall_flag` true) **and** have `biopsy_performed` true, and a candidate is scored only when its `pathology_result` is `BENIGN` or `MALIGNANT` **and** its `outcome_recorded_at` is strictly earlier than its `performed_at` plus the cycle's `followup_window_days`. A biopsy attributable to a BI-RADS 4 screening read is therefore excluded from PPV1 entirely, because such an exam has no `recall_flag`. Under `PPV3_BIOPSY_ALL` — the ACR biopsy-yield measure — the candidate set is every in-scope exam with `biopsy_performed` true regardless of `recall_flag`, and a candidate is scored purely on its `pathology_result` being `BENIGN` or `MALIGNANT`; the `followup_window_days` window is deliberately not applied. Over identical data the PPV3 denominator can therefore be strictly larger than the PPV1 denominator.

From the applied variant, `biopsy_count` is the number of scored exams, `cancer_count` is the number of scored exams whose `pathology_result` is `MALIGNANT`, and `excluded_exam_count` is the number of candidates the variant could not score — outcome never recorded, `pathology_result` still `PENDING`, or, under `PPV1_SCREENING` only, an outcome recorded at or beyond the follow-up window. The `ppv_pct` is null when `biopsy_count` is zero; otherwise it is 100 times `cancer_count` divided by `biopsy_count`, rounded half up to 2 decimals. Finally, `abnormal_interpretation_flag` is true only when `recall_rate_pct` is non-null **and** falls outside the benchmark band whose lower bound is 5.00 percent and upper bound is 12.00 percent. That band is closed on both edges, so a recall rate of exactly 5.00 or exactly 12.00 is in-band and the flag stays false; a null `recall_rate_pct` also leaves the flag false rather than null.

A read-only preview of these metrics is available on the cycle without mutating anything. It accepts an optional `variant` query parameter, defaults to `PPV1_SCREENING` when omitted, and returns the nine-field shape `variant`, `screening_exam_count`, `recall_count`, `biopsy_count`, `cancer_count`, `excluded_exam_count`, `recall_rate_pct`, `ppv_pct` and `abnormal_interpretation_flag`. Reading the preview never changes the cycle's `status`, `locked_at`, `variant_at_close` or `version`, so the same cycle can be previewed under both variants before a decision is made. All seven roles may read it, and all seven may read a cycle directly or list a radiologist's cycles filtered by `status`.

Closing is a `QualityAssuranceOfficer` action carrying a required `variant` of either `PPV1_SCREENING` or `PPV3_BIOPSY_ALL`. The closer must not be the audited radiologist: an `X-User-ID` equal to the cycle's `radiologist_id` is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES`. The cycle must be in `OPEN` or `AMENDED`; any other state is `ERR_AUDIT_CYCLE_NOT_CLOSABLE`. Closing from `OPEN` additionally requires the logical clock to have reached `period_end` — the comparison is inclusive, so a clock exactly at `period_end` may close — and an earlier clock is refused with `ERR_AUDIT_PERIOD_NOT_ELAPSED`. On success the metrics run under the requested variant, every metric field plus `variant_at_close` is frozen onto the row, the `status` becomes `LOCKED`, `locked_at` is stamped at the logical clock, and the `version` advances.

The cycle is cyclic because a late amendment invalidates a closed audit. As part of the amendment fan-out described in §3.6, amending an exam whose `interpreting_physician_id` matches a `LOCKED` cycle's `radiologist_id` and whose `performed_at` lies inside that cycle's half-open period moves the cycle `LOCKED` to `AMENDED` and advances its `version`, while leaving the stored metrics frozen at their previously locked values — the numbers stay visibly stale, which is what signals that a re-close is owed. Re-closing from `AMENDED` recomputes every metric from the amended exam set under the variant supplied by that second call, which need not be the variant used the first time, and returns the cycle to `LOCKED` with a new `locked_at`. Re-closing from `AMENDED` does not re-check the period, because the period has necessarily already elapsed. Neither `LOCKED` nor `AMENDED` is terminal.

#### The Compliance Run: One Checkpoint, Five Sweeps

Nothing in this service expires on its own. Deadlines only take effect when an `AccreditationCoordinator` or a `QualityAssuranceOfficer` runs a compliance run against a facility, and that run is the only operation that reconciles stored deadlines against the logical clock. It takes no payload. It executes five sweeps in a fixed, observable order, all against the same clock reading, and then recomputes the facility's posture exactly once at the end.

Sweep one handles equipment and certificates. Every unit of the facility has its `survey_compliance` recomputed against the clock: a unit whose most recent `PASSED` survey window still covers the clock is `CURRENT`, and a unit whose window has lapsed becomes `OVERDUE`. A unit already in `FAILED` from a failing survey decision is not rehabilitated by a sweep. Then accreditation certificates are aged. A unit in `ACCREDITED` whose clock has reached `accreditation_expires_on` minus 180 days moves to `REACCREDITATION_DUE` — the comparison is inclusive, so exactly 180 days of lead time triggers it, and this transition alone does not degrade the facility's posture, it only opens the reaccreditation window. A unit in `REACCREDITATION_DUE` whose clock has passed `accreditation_expires_on` plus 30 days — a strict comparison, so exactly 30 days of certificate grace still survives — moves to `SUSPENDED`, which cascades its `operational_state` to `OUT_OF_SERVICE`.

Sweep two handles delayed reports. Every exam of the facility still in `report_status` `AWAITING_INTERPRETATION` whose `report_due_at` is strictly before the clock moves to `DELAYED`. These are processed in ascending `performed_at` with ascending `created_at` as the tie-break, never by `id`.

Sweep three handles assumed delivery, exactly as described in §3.7: letters in `SENT` whose `sent_at` is 14 or more days before the clock move to `DELIVERED_ASSUMED`, carry their exam's `notification_status` to `COMPLETE`, and decrement the facility's `open_notice_obligations`.

Sweep four handles abandoned follow-up, exactly as described in §3.8: supplemental orders of the facility in `RECOMMENDED` or `COVERAGE_DETERMINED` whose `followup_due_at` is strictly before the clock move to `LOST_TO_FOLLOW_UP` and carry their exam's `follow_up_status` to `LOST_TO_FOLLOW_UP`. These are processed in ascending `recommended_at` with ascending `created_at` as the tie-break, never by `id`.

Sweep five re-evaluates staffing, running the continuing-education and read-volume evaluation of §3.2 for every one of the facility's personnel against the same clock. A person newly short of either threshold moves `ce_status` from `COMPLIANT` to `GRACE` with a fresh `ce_deadline_at`, leaving `qualification_status` untouched. A person already in `GRACE` whose `ce_deadline_at` the clock has strictly passed moves to `DEFICIENT` and, if still `QUALIFIED`, has `qualification_status` moved to `LAPSED`. A person who has come back into compliance returns `ce_status` to `COMPLIANT`, clears `ce_deadline_at`, and is restored from `LAPSED` to `QUALIFIED`.

Only after all five sweeps have finished is the facility's `compliance_posture` recomputed, once, by the first-match-wins ladder of §2.10 — so a single run can suspend a unit in sweep one and then observe the resulting posture drop at the end, rather than recomputing the posture five times as it goes. The facility's `last_compliance_run_at` is stamped at the logical clock. The run returns nine fields: `ran_at`, the clock instant the run executed; `units_marked_reaccreditation_due` and `units_survey_overdue` from sweep one — `units_survey_overdue` is a movement count rather than a census: it counts only those units whose `survey_compliance` this sweep actually changed to `OVERDUE`, so a unit that was already `OVERDUE` when the run began and a unit that sweep one leaves in `FAILED` are both uncounted; `exams_marked_delayed` from sweep two; `letters_marked_delivered` from sweep three; `orders_marked_lost` from sweep four; `personnel_moved_to_grace` and `personnel_moved_to_deficient` from sweep five; and `compliance_posture`, the facility's posture as recomputed at the end. Running the same checkpoint twice at the same clock reading is safe: the second run finds nothing left to move and reports zeros across all seven counters while still restamping `ran_at`, `last_compliance_run_at` and the recomputed `compliance_posture`.
