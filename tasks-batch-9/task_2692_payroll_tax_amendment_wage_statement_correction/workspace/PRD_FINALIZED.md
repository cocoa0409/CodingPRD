# Payroll Tax Amendment & Wage Statement Correction API — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction

Payroll tax errors are discovered late, in the gap between three records that are supposed to agree: the payroll register that says what was actually paid and withheld, the deposit trail that says what money actually reached the taxing agency, and the quarterly return that says what the employer told the agency. When those three disagree, an employer does not have one problem — it has a chain of them. Somebody must categorise the variance, decide whether it is a taxable-wage error, a withholding error, a jurisdiction misassignment or a plain deposit shortfall, prepare an amended return for the right agency and the right quarter, compute statutory interest and penalty on the shortfall, reissue corrected wage statements to affected employees, chase repayment of wages that were overpaid, answer the agency notice that arrived in the meantime, argue for a penalty waiver, fix the retroactive state and local registrations nobody opened in time, and finally prove that the control which failed has been remediated.

This document specifies the API for a service that runs that whole chain as a single auditable workflow. The system is deliberately built as a **reconciliation ledger with a compliance overlay**: every monetary figure it publishes is a derived rollup that can be recomputed from primary records, and every consequential act — signing an amendment, granting a waiver, verifying a remediation — is gated by an explicit segregation-of-duties rule so that no single operator can both create and bless the same exposure. Correctness here is not a matter of taste. An interest figure that is one cent off is a filing defect; a penalty floor applied in the wrong step order produces a different number and a different assessment; a wage statement reissued twice against the same predecessor double-counts an employee's reportable wages for the rest of the tax year.

Two design consequences run through the whole specification and should be read before anything else. First, **time is a server-owned logical value, never wall-clock time**: every deadline, accrual window, cooling-off buffer, prior-year test and audit stamp reads a logical UTC clock that clients can set explicitly, because the correctness of an amendment depends on when it is deemed to have happened. Second, **state is multi-dimensional**: a correction case carries four independent lifecycle dimensions on one row (overall case status, reconciliation status, filing status, statement status) and an agency notice carries two (response status and abatement status), because filing and employee-statement work proceed in parallel and neither one waits for the other. Readers building this system should expect to hold several state machines in mind at once, and should expect cascades that reach five entities deep from a single call.

## 2. Global Specifications & System-Wide Principles

Everything in this section is a contract. Where §3 narrates business intent and may be read for meaning, §2 fixes shapes, names, defaults, orderings and rejection codes that must match exactly.

### 2.1. User Identity and Role-Based Access Control

Every request other than the logical-clock setter must carry two headers: `X-User-ID`, identifying the caller, and `X-User-Role`, naming the caller's role. If either header is missing or present but empty, the request is rejected with `ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED` and HTTP 401. If `X-User-Role` is not one of the seven role names declared below, or is a declared role that the addressed endpoint does not authorise, the request is rejected with `ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE` and HTTP 403. The seven declared roles are `PlatformAdmin`, `PayrollAnalyst`, `TaxPreparer`, `TaxOfficer`, `ControlsAuditor`, `EmployerRep` and `EmployeeSelfService`.

The roles divide the work along the fault lines of a payroll tax function. `PlatformAdmin` is the operations superuser and may perform every declared operation, but it earns no exemption from the segregation-of-duties rules described in §2.8 — an admin who prepared an amendment still cannot sign it. `PayrollAnalyst` owns the payroll evidence and the detection side: posting register lines and deposit records, opening correction cases, running reconciliation, categorising and dismissing discrepancies, and proposing, renegotiating and collecting wage repayments. `TaxPreparer` prepares amendments and corrected wage statements — drafting amended returns, preparing them, drafting statements, issuing them, reporting them undeliverable and reissuing them — and may never sign, submit or record an agency outcome. `TaxOfficer` is the independent signing and agency-facing authority: it signs, submits, dispositions and voids amendments, records and works agency notices through their response and abatement rounds, writes off repayments, and closes cases. `ControlsAuditor` owns root-cause remediation and its independent verification, creating remediations, assigning owners and verifying evidence, and is otherwise read-only over the tax filing surface. Both `PayrollAnalyst` and `ControlsAuditor` may submit remediation evidence for verification.

Five of the seven roles carry an **opaque** identity. When `X-User-Role` is `PlatformAdmin`, `PayrollAnalyst`, `TaxPreparer`, `TaxOfficer` or `ControlsAuditor`, `X-User-ID` is an opaque operator identifier that is not tied to any persisted entity; the server never compares it to any row's `id`. It is, however, recorded where a transition demands accountability — as `prepared_by_user_id` and `signed_by_user_id` on an amended return, as `owner_user_id` and `verified_by_user_id` on a control remediation — and those recorded values are what the segregation-of-duties comparisons use.

The remaining two roles are **entity-backed**. When `X-User-Role` is `EmployerRep`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Employer`; ownership checks on any resource that references an `Employer` compare the resource's employer foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. When `X-User-Role` is `EmployeeSelfService`, `X-User-ID` is the `id` of the acting `Employee`; ownership checks on any resource that references an `Employee` compare the resource's employee foreign-key field to `X-User-ID`. For both entity-backed roles, if `X-User-ID` does not name an existing `Employer` row (for `EmployerRep`) or an existing `Employee` row (for `EmployeeSelfService`), the request is rejected with `ERR_IDENTITY_NOT_RESOLVABLE` and HTTP 403. If the identity resolves but the addressed row lies outside the caller's own scope — an employer reading another employer's chain, or an employee touching another employee's statement or repayment — the rejection is `ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`, also HTTP 403. An `EmployerRep` may add employees to its own roster and read within its own chain; an `EmployeeSelfService` caller may acknowledge its own corrected statements, agree to its own repayment arrangements, and read its own employee row, statements and repayments.

The logical-clock setter is the single exception to header enforcement: it is available to all seven roles and it does not require the two headers to be present at all.

### 2.2. API Response Structure

#### Standard Success Response Format

All successful API responses (those with 2xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format in their JSON body:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z",
    "limit": 10,
    "offset": 0,
    "total_count": 100,
    "has_more": true
  },
  "response_type": "object" | "array",
  "data": { }
}
```

- The `api_request_id` is a server-generated unique identifier for the request that starts with `req_`.
- The `api_request_timestamp` is a server-generated ISO 8601 UTC timestamp with three fractional digits.
- The `response_type` is either "object" for a single resource or "array" for a list of resources.
- The `data` field contains the resource object or an array of resource objects. For 2xx responses with no data, it contains an empty object. Unless otherwise noted, operations that create or modify a resource will return the created or updated resource object in the data field.
- Pagination-related fields in `meta` (`limit`, `offset`, `total_count`, `has_more`) are included for list endpoints.

#### Standard Error Response Format

All error API responses (4xx status codes) follow this standard envelope format:

```json
{
  "meta": {
    "api_request_id": "req_RANDOMALPHANUM123456",
    "api_request_timestamp": "2025-05-24T10:30:00.123Z"
  },
  "response_type": "error",
  "data": {
    "error_id": "UNIQUE_ERROR_IDENTIFIER_STRING",
    "message": "A human-readable explanation of the error."
  }
}
```

- The `meta` object for error responses contains only `api_request_id` and `api_request_timestamp`.
- The `error_id` is a unique, machine-readable string that identifies the specific error.
- The `message` provides a human-readable explanation of what went wrong.

### 2.3. Pagination and List Ordering

Every list endpoint accepts two optional query parameters, `limit` and `offset`. `limit` defaults to 10 when omitted, may not be smaller than 1, and may not be larger than 100. `offset` defaults to 0 and may not be negative. A `limit` outside the inclusive range of 1 through 100, or a negative `offset`, or either value supplied as something other than an integer, is rejected with `ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION` and HTTP 400.

List results are ordered by `created_at` ascending unless a specific narrative in §3 pins a different order for a computation. The `meta` object of a list response carries `limit`, `offset`, `total_count` and `has_more`, where `total_count` is the number of rows matching the filters before the window is applied and `has_more` indicates whether rows remain beyond the current window. Single-resource reads carry no pagination fields in `meta`.

Filters on list endpoints are conjunctive: supplying several of them narrows the result to rows matching all of them. Because this API addresses every entity at its own top-level collection and never nests one resource inside another's URL, filtered lists are the only way to discover child rows — the discrepancies emitted by a reconciliation run, the successor statement created inside a reissue cascade, and the remediation auto-created by the amendment acceptance cascade are all discoverable exclusively through their collection's filtered list.

### 2.4. Record Identity, Audit Stamps and Row Versioning

Every persisted row carries an `id` that is a server-minted UUID. Identifiers never carry a type prefix and are never supplied by the client. Every row also carries a `created_at` timestamp: an ISO 8601 UTC instant with exactly three fractional-second digits, stamped from the logical clock described in §2.5.

Most entities additionally carry `updated_at`, with the same format as `created_at` and refreshed on every mutation, and `version`, an integer that begins at 1 when the row is created and increases by exactly 1 on every mutation of that row. `Employer`, `TaxAgency`, `AgencyAccount`, `Employee`, `FiledReturn`, `CorrectionCase`, `Discrepancy`, `AmendedReturn`, `CorrectedWageStatement`, `WageRepayment`, `AgencyNotice` and `ControlRemediation` all carry `updated_at` and `version`.

Two entities are deliberate exceptions. `PayrollRegisterLine` and `DepositRecord` are immutable evidence rows: once posted they are never modified, and consequently they carry only `id` and `created_at`. Neither `updated_at` nor `version` exists on those two entities at all, and no endpoint accepts a version field for them.

### 2.5. The Logical Clock and Period Arithmetic

The server owns a logical UTC clock, and it is the only source of "now" anywhere in the system. Before any client sets it, the logical clock reads exactly `2026-08-01T00:00:00Z`; it must never fall back to real wall-clock time. Setting the clock takes a single field, `to_iso8601`, and responds with the resulting instant as `current_utc`. No business row is modified when the clock moves. Every deadline test, every accrual window, every cooling-off buffer, every prior-year determination and every `*_at` stamp — `activated_at`, `submitted_at`, `accepted_at`, `issued_at`, `acknowledged_at`, `responded_at`, `verified_at` and `closed_at` — reads this clock.

Quarters are compared through a single integer coordinate called a **period ordinal**, computed as the tax year multiplied by four plus the quarter minus one. So the first quarter of a year has ordinal four times that year, and the fourth quarter has that plus three. This coordinate is what makes registration coverage and adjacency testable with plain integer comparison.

Statutory due dates are derived, never supplied. For a return covering the first quarter the original due date is the thirtieth of April of the same tax year at midnight UTC; for the second quarter, the thirty-first of July of the same tax year; for the third quarter, the thirty-first of October of the same tax year; and for the fourth quarter, the thirty-first of January of the *following* calendar year. All four are expressed as ISO 8601 UTC instants at midnight with three fractional digits.

Wherever this specification counts days, it counts **whole elapsed days** between two instants — the number of complete twenty-four-hour spans, discarding any partial remainder. Where a rule speaks of a count of days being "at least" a threshold, equality satisfies the rule.

### 2.6. Money, Basis Points and Rounding Discipline

All monetary quantities are integer cents. Every field whose name ends in `_cents` holds cents, and every such field supplied in a request body must be a non-negative integer unless a narrative explicitly declares the field signed. The only signed monetary quantities the system publishes are variance and delta figures: a discrepancy's `amount_cents`, an amended return's `wage_delta_cents`, and a corrected statement's `wage_delta_cents`, `fica_wage_delta_cents` and `withholding_delta_cents`.

Rates and proportions are expressed in **basis points**, where ten thousand basis points is one hundred percent. Interest rates, penalty multipliers, workday apportionment shares and waiver percentages all use this convention, and every conversion divides by ten thousand.

Wherever a computation must convert a basis-point proportion into cents, the result is rounded to the nearest whole cent with ties rounded upward — that is, two decimal places at the currency level, rounded half up. The *number of times* rounding occurs and the *point* at which it occurs are part of each formula's contract and are pinned in §3; rounding a sum is not interchangeable with summing rounded terms, and the specification always says which one applies. Rollups that merely add already-integral cents perform no rounding at all.

### 2.7. Optimistic Concurrency Control on Parent Rows

Two write operations reach into a parent row that the client must have observed first, and both therefore demand an explicit version assertion. There is no other version field anywhere in the API: endpoints not named below accept no version input at all.

Creating an amended return requires a field named `expected_correction_case_version`, carrying the `version` of the `CorrectionCase` the client last read. It is a required input, not an optional one. If the value does not equal the case's current `version`, the create is rejected with `ERR_STALE_CASE_VERSION` and HTTP 412. This exists because a reconciliation re-run re-derives the case's entire discrepancy set; an amendment built against a discrepancy set that has since been superseded would settle variances that no longer exist, so the client is required to prove it is looking at the case as it stands now.

Reissuing a corrected wage statement requires a field named `expected_statement_version`, carrying the `version` of the `CorrectedWageStatement` being reissued. It too is required. If the value does not equal that statement's current `version`, the reissue is rejected with `ERR_STALE_STATEMENT_VERSION` and HTTP 412. A reissue supersedes the statement it names, so a client acting on a stale view would attach a second successor to the same predecessor and count that predecessor's wage delta twice in the case's corrected wage base.

### 2.8. Segregation of Duties

Two acts in this system must be performed by someone other than the person who set them up, and both comparisons are made on `X-User-ID`.

Signing an amended return requires the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from the `prepared_by_user_id` recorded on that return. Verifying a control remediation requires the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from the `owner_user_id` recorded on that remediation. Both violations are reported as `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION` with HTTP 403. `PlatformAdmin` is not exempt from either rule.

The signing rule is reinforced structurally: re-preparing an already-signed amendment clears its signature, so a preparer cannot launder a signature by preparing a return after someone else has signed it and then leaving the old signature in place.

### 2.9. Flat Addressing, Referential Scope and Existence Checks

Entities are peers. Each is addressed at its own top-level collection and parentage is expressed only through foreign-key fields in request bodies, never through nested URLs. Consequently there is no left-to-right path hierarchy to validate: each endpoint carries at most one path parameter, and that parameter must name an existing row of its own entity or the request fails with HTTP 404 carrying the entity-specific code — `ERR_EMPLOYER_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_TAX_AGENCY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AGENCY_ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_EMPLOYEE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_REGISTER_LINE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DEPOSIT_RECORD_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_FILED_RETURN_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_CORRECTION_CASE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_DISCREPANCY_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AMENDED_RETURN_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_STATEMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_WAGE_REPAYMENT_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AGENCY_NOTICE_NOT_FOUND` or `ERR_CONTROL_REMEDIATION_NOT_FOUND`.

The path parameter is always named after the entity it addresses. Employer-scoped routes take `employer_id`; agency routes take `tax_agency_id`; registration routes take `agency_account_id`; employee routes take `employee_id`; register-line routes take `register_line_id`; deposit routes take `deposit_record_id`; original-return routes take `filed_return_id`; case routes take `correction_case_id`; discrepancy routes take `discrepancy_id`; amendment routes take `amended_return_id`; corrected-statement routes take `statement_id`; repayment routes take `wage_repayment_id`; notice routes take `agency_notice_id`; and remediation routes take `control_remediation_id`.

The same existence discipline applies to foreign keys supplied in a request body. Every such reference must name an existing row of the entity it points at, and a dangling reference produces the same 404 code family: an unknown employer reference yields `ERR_EMPLOYER_NOT_FOUND`, an unknown agency reference `ERR_TAX_AGENCY_NOT_FOUND`, an unknown employee reference `ERR_EMPLOYEE_NOT_FOUND`, an unknown case reference `ERR_CORRECTION_CASE_NOT_FOUND`, an unknown predecessor amendment reference `ERR_AMENDED_RETURN_NOT_FOUND`, and an unknown predecessor statement reference `ERR_STATEMENT_NOT_FOUND`.

Existence is necessary but not sufficient. Rows joined into one operation must share a scope, and a violation is reported as `ERR_SCOPE_MISMATCH` with HTTP 422. Concretely: a discrepancy folded into an amendment must belong to the same correction case as that amendment; a corrected wage statement's employee must belong to the case's employer; an agency notice attached to a case must share that case's employer, agency, tax year and quarter; and a wage repayment's employee must be the same employee as the statement it affects. This scope rule guards five operations — creating an amended return, creating a corrected wage statement, creating an agency notice, creating a wage repayment, and categorising a discrepancy.

### 2.10. Rejection Precedence

When a single request violates several rules, exactly one error is returned, and which one is fixed by a global precedence order. Checks are evaluated in this sequence, and the first failure wins.

Authentication and authorization come first, in four steps: header presence (`ERR_UNAUTHENTICATED`), then role recognition and endpoint authorisation (`ERR_FORBIDDEN_ROLE`), then entity-backed identity resolution (`ERR_IDENTITY_NOT_RESOLVABLE`), then ownership scope for entity-backed callers (`ERR_NOT_RESOURCE_OWNER`).

Existence comes next: path-parameter existence is checked before body foreign-key existence, both yielding the 404 family described in §2.9.

Schema validation follows, in three steps: general body validation (`ERR_INVALID_REQUEST`), then pagination bounds on list endpoints (`ERR_INVALID_PAGINATION`), then the repayment-specific arithmetic and year-ordering rule (`ERR_INVALID_RECOVERY_YEAR`). General body validation requires the body to be a JSON object, every required input field to be present and of its declared type, every enumerated field to hold one of its declared values, every `_cents` field to be a non-negative integer unless the field is explicitly signed, `fein` to consist of exactly two digits, a hyphen, and exactly seven digits, every `quarter` to lie in the inclusive range 1 through 4, every `tax_year` to lie in the inclusive range 2000 through 2100, and `workday_ratio_bps` to lie in the inclusive range 0 through 10000. Unknown fields in a request body are ignored rather than rejected.

Optimistic concurrency is checked after schema and before state: the case version assertion (`ERR_STALE_CASE_VERSION`) then the statement version assertion (`ERR_STALE_STATEMENT_VERSION`).

State checks come next, in three steps: whether the addressed entity's relevant state field permits the requested transition at all (`ERR_INVALID_STATE`, or `ERR_AMENDED_RETURN_NOT_SIGNED` when the attempted act is submitting an amendment that is not in `SIGNED`), then whether the entity is already in a terminal state (`ERR_ALREADY_TERMINAL`), then the amendment-specific discrepancy readiness check (`ERR_UNCATEGORIZED_DISCREPANCIES`).

Business referential checks follow in this order: scope agreement (`ERR_SCOPE_MISMATCH`), registration coverage (`ERR_AGENCY_ACCOUNT_NOT_ACTIVE`), remediation completeness at closure (`ERR_REMEDIATION_NOT_VERIFIED`), the empty-case guard on amendment creation (`ERR_CASE_HAS_NO_DISCREPANCIES`), and repayment settlement at closure (`ERR_REPAYMENT_NOT_SETTLED`). Segregation of duties is evaluated immediately after those, first for signing and then for remediation verification, both as `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`.

Temporal checks come next in this order: the amendment window (`ERR_AMENDMENT_WINDOW_CLOSED`), the cooling-off buffer between amendments (`ERR_AMENDMENT_COOLING_OFF_ACTIVE`), the accrual date bounds (`ERR_INVALID_ACCRUAL_DATE`), the notice response deadline (`ERR_NOTICE_RESPONSE_OVERDUE`), and the prior-year statement window (`ERR_PRIOR_YEAR_WINDOW_CLOSED`).

Business constraints follow, in this order: the amendment slot rule (`ERR_AMENDMENT_SLOT_OCCUPIED`), the registration period overlap rule (`ERR_ACCOUNT_PERIOD_OVERLAP`), the uniqueness family (`ERR_DUPLICATE_FILED_RETURN`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_EMPLOYER_FEIN`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_NOTICE_NUMBER`, `ERR_DUPLICATE_AGENCY_CODE`), the zero-delta statement rule (`ERR_NO_REPORTABLE_DELTA`), the filed-return prerequisite for reconciliation (`ERR_NO_FILED_RETURN_FOR_PERIOD`), and the delivery-channel rule for undeliverable reports (`ERR_CHANNEL_NOT_UNDELIVERABLE_CAPABLE`).

Fiscal checks are evaluated last, in this order: the signing authorization limit (`ERR_AUTHORIZATION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`), the exact-collection rule (`ERR_OVERCOLLECTION`), and the requested-waiver ceiling (`ERR_WAIVER_EXCEEDS_ASSESSMENT`).

### 2.11. Derived Rollups, Cascades and Recomputation

Nothing monetary in this system is stored twice as an independent truth. Six values are **derived rollups**, recomputed from primary rows on every write that could change them, never accepted as input: an employer's `open_exposure_cents`, `open_remediation_count` and `granted_abatement_count`; a case's `total_exposure_cents`, `corrected_wage_base_cents`, `open_discrepancy_count`, `repayment_recovered_cents` and `dominant_root_cause_code`; an employee's `active_statement_count`; and a notice's `net_exposure_cents`.

Recomputation is immediate and transitive. When an operation changes a case's exposure, the owning employer's open exposure is recomputed in the same request. When a statement changes state, both the employee's active statement count and the case's corrected wage base are recomputed in the same request. A cascade that reaches five entities deep completes entirely before the response is returned; there is no eventual consistency anywhere in this API.

Every row a cascade mutates has its `version` increased by 1 and its `updated_at` refreshed, not just the row the request addressed. That is a load-bearing consequence rather than an incidental one: a client that read a correction case, then watched an unrelated writer prepare an amendment against it, will find its `expected_correction_case_version` assertion failing on the next create, exactly as §2.7 intends.

Compensating actions conserve the ledger. Voiding a prepared amendment removes that amendment's whole contribution from the case exposure and therefore from the employer's open exposure; reopening a case that had advanced to remediation re-admits the case's entire exposure to the employer's open total; closing a case removes it. Two derived counters — an employer's `open_exposure_cents` and its `open_remediation_count` — are therefore only ever as stale as the last write, and a sequence of prepare-then-void, or issue-then-void-then-reissue, must return them to where they started.

## 3. Business Logic & Workflows

### 3.1. Reference Data, Registration Coverage and Payroll Evidence

Nothing can be corrected before the world it lives in has been described, and the description starts with the filing entity. Registering an employer captures its `legal_name`, which must be between 1 and 120 characters, and its `fein` — the federal employer identification number, written as exactly two digits, a hyphen, then exactly seven digits. That number is globally unique across all employers; a second employer claiming a `fein` already in use is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_EMPLOYER_FEIN`. Registration also fixes the employer's `deposit_schedule`, which is either `MONTHLY` or `SEMIWEEKLY` and determines the cadence of its deposit obligations, and its `authorization_limit_cents`, a non-negative ceiling on how much exposure a single correction case may carry at the moment an amendment is signed. Three derived counters are seeded to zero at registration and are never writable thereafter: `open_exposure_cents`, `open_remediation_count` and `granted_abatement_count`. Employers can later be adjusted along exactly two axes — `deposit_schedule` and `authorization_limit_cents` — and any such adjustment increments the employer's `version`. Employer lists can be narrowed by `fein` or by `deposit_schedule`.

A taxing agency is described by the regime it imposes. Its `agency_code` is a globally unique short identifier of between 3 and 16 characters drawn from upper-case letters, digits and hyphens; a duplicate is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_AGENCY_CODE`. Its `level` is `FEDERAL`, `STATE` or `LOCAL`, and its `jurisdiction_code` — 2 to 8 upper-case characters — names the jurisdiction it taxes and is the value against which work locations are compared during detection. Four numbers define its arithmetic: `interest_rate_bps_annual` is the annual statutory interest rate in basis points, between 0 and 10000; `interest_method` is either `STATUTORY_DAILY` or `DEPOSIT_WEIGHTED` and selects which interest variant every amendment for this agency uses; `penalty_multiplier_bps` is an agency-specific scaling between 0 and 20000 applied to the floored gross penalty; and `min_days_between_amendments`, a non-negative whole-day count, is the cooling-off buffer measured from the previous amendment's transmission for the same employer, agency, tax year and quarter slot. `amendment_window_days`, which must be at least 1, is the number of whole days after the original statutory due date during which an amendment may still be transmitted. Finally, `requires_registration` — false unless supplied — declares whether this agency insists on a live account registration before an amendment for a period may be prepared. Agency lists accept `level`, `jurisdiction_code` and `interest_method` as filters.

State and local agencies routinely require registration, and registrations are the part of payroll tax operations that is most often discovered late. A registration is opened against a specific `employer_id` and `agency_id` and covers a closed-closed range of quarters: `effective_from_tax_year` with `effective_from_quarter` names the first covered quarter, and `effective_to_tax_year` with `effective_to_quarter` name the last. Both closing fields may be omitted, in which case the coverage is open-ended. The system derives `from_period_ordinal` from the opening pair using the period-ordinal arithmetic of §2.5, and derives `to_period_ordinal` from the closing pair the same way — except that when no closing tax year was supplied, `to_period_ordinal` is set to the sentinel value 999999, which is what "open-ended" means in integer terms. An `account_number` may be supplied at creation or omitted and provided later. Two registrations for the same employer and agency may not cover overlapping quarter ranges: if the closed-closed ordinal interval of a new registration intersects the interval of any sibling registration for that employer-agency pair whose `registration_status` is `PENDING`, `RETRO_PENDING`, `ACTIVE` or `RETRO_ACTIVE`, the creation is refused with `ERR_ACCOUNT_PERIOD_OVERLAP`. Ranges that merely touch — where the new registration's opening ordinal is exactly one greater than a sibling's closing ordinal — are adjacent, not overlapping, and are explicitly permitted.

Registration creation branches on one boolean. When `retroactive` is omitted or false, the row is written in `PENDING`. When `retroactive` is true — meaning the registration is being opened for quarters that have already closed — the row is written directly in `RETRO_PENDING`, and the overlap check above applies identically on that branch. Activation is what turns either pending form into live coverage, and it requires an `account_number` in the request body: without it the activation cannot proceed. On the ordinary branch, `PENDING → ACTIVE` records the supplied `account_number`, stamps `activated_at` with the current logical instant, and increments the registration's `version` by 1. On the retroactive branch, `RETRO_PENDING → RETRO_ACTIVE` does all of that and one thing more: it back-dates coverage, so that every correction case for the same employer and agency whose quarter ordinal falls within the registration's closed-closed ordinal range becomes eligible for amendment preparation immediately, with no further action required. `ACTIVE` and `RETRO_ACTIVE` satisfy the registration gate equally; the back-dating side effect is the only difference between them.

Retiring a registration happens two ways, and both land in `CLOSED`. An administrator may close an active registration explicitly by naming a final quarter through `effective_to_tax_year` and `effective_to_quarter`, which shrinks the coverage range: `registration_status` becomes `CLOSED`, `to_period_ordinal` is recomputed from the newly supplied pair, and `version` is incremented by 1. Separately, `ACTIVE → CLOSED` occurs as a consequence of succession — activating a successor registration for the same employer and agency whose `from_period_ordinal` is exactly one greater than this registration's `to_period_ordinal` retires this one, incrementing its `version` by 1. That succession route requires this registration's `to_period_ordinal` to be a real closing ordinal rather than the open-ended sentinel 999999; an open-ended registration cannot be retired by adjacency because nothing can be adjacent to it. Note that merely *creating* a non-overlapping successor is not sufficient to retire the predecessor — the successor must be activated. Registration lists can be filtered by `employer_id`, `agency_id`, `registration_status` and `retroactive`.

Employees are added to an employer's roster with an `employer_id`, a `full_name` of 1 to 120 characters, `national_id_last4` written as exactly four digits, and a `mailing_state_code` of exactly two upper-case characters that governs where paper statements are delivered. An employee's `active_statement_count` is derived, seeded to zero, and equal at all times to the number of corrected wage statements for that employee whose status is `ISSUED` or `ACKNOWLEDGED`. Employee lists accept `employer_id` and `mailing_state_code` as filters. An `EmployerRep` may add employees to its own roster; the tax-operations roles may add them to any.

The payroll register is the first of the three records reconciliation compares, and its lines are immutable evidence. A line names an `employer_id`, an `employee_id` and an `agency_id`, the `tax_year` and `quarter` of the pay period, the `taxable_wage_cents` assigned to that agency for that employee and quarter, the `withheld_cents` remitted to that agency, and the `work_jurisdiction_code` — 2 to 8 upper-case characters naming where the work was actually performed. When that jurisdiction differs from the agency's own `jurisdiction_code`, the line is misassigned and reconciliation will say so. A line also carries `workday_ratio_bps`, between 0 and 10000, giving the share of the line's wages that genuinely belong to the correct jurisdiction; it defaults to 10000, meaning every workday of that line fell inside the agency's own jurisdiction. Every line is written with `posting_status` fixed at `POSTED`, the only value that field ever takes, and only `POSTED` lines enter reconciliation. Register lines are never modified after posting: they carry no `version` field and no `updated_at`. Register-line lists can be filtered by `employer_id`, `employee_id`, `agency_id`, `tax_year` and `quarter`.

The deposit trail is the second record. A deposit is posted against an `employer_id`, an `agency_id`, a `tax_year` and a `quarter`, and it carries two dates and two amounts. `deposit_due_date` is the statutory due date of the obligation the deposit was meant to satisfy, and `obligation_amount_cents` is the amount that was due on that date; `deposit_date` is when the money actually moved, and `amount_cents` is how much of it moved. `clearing_status` is `CLEARED` or `RETURNED` and defaults to `CLEARED`; only cleared deposits enter the reconciliation waterfall, and a returned deposit contributes neither cash nor obligation to it. Like register lines, deposit records are immutable and carry neither `updated_at` nor `version`. Deposit lists accept `employer_id`, `agency_id`, `tax_year`, `quarter` and `clearing_status` as filters.

The originally filed return is the third record. Filing captures the `employer_id`, `agency_id`, `tax_year` and `quarter` it covers, the `form_code` of 1 to 16 characters as originally filed, `filed_at` recording when the return was transmitted, and the two figures the employer told the agency: `reported_taxable_wage_cents` and `reported_withheld_cents`. The statutory `original_due_date` is derived from the tax year and quarter using the four-quarter calendar of §2.5 and is never supplied by the client, and `status` is written as `FILED`. At most one return may exist for a given employer, agency, tax year, quarter and `form_code` combination; a duplicate is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_FILED_RETURN`. Filed-return lists accept `employer_id`, `agency_id`, `tax_year`, `quarter` and `status` as filters. A filed return has exactly one further state to reach: when an amendment for the same employer, agency, tax year and quarter is accepted by the agency, the return transitions `FILED → AMENDED` and its `version` is incremented by 1 — a transition available only from `FILED`, and only when the return's employer, agency, tax year and quarter all equal those of the accepted amendment's case.

### 3.2. Opening a Correction Case and the Three-Way Reconciliation

A correction case is the unit of work. Opening one names an `employer_id`, an `agency_id`, a `tax_year` and a `quarter`, and that four-part key is the case's scope for the rest of its life. All four state dimensions begin at their initial values: `status` is `OPEN`, `detection_status` is `NOT_RUN`, `filing_status` is `FILING_NOT_REQUIRED`, and `statement_status` is `STMT_NOT_REQUIRED`. The derived fields begin correspondingly: `open_discrepancy_count` at 0, `total_exposure_cents` at 0, `repayment_recovered_cents` at 0, `dominant_root_cause_code` at null, and `closed_at` at null. One derived field is seeded with real content at creation — `corrected_wage_base_cents` is set from the posted register lines on the case's scope — and the owning employer's `open_exposure_cents` is recomputed in the same request, because a new case in `OPEN` joins the employer's open set.

The corrected wage base is defined once and recomputed identically everywhere it appears. Its base term is the sum of `taxable_wage_cents` over every payroll register line whose employer, agency, tax year and quarter equal the case's and whose `posting_status` is `POSTED`. Its adjustment term is the sum of `wage_delta_cents` over every corrected wage statement belonging to the case whose status is `ISSUED` or `ACKNOWLEDGED`. The case's `corrected_wage_base_cents` is the base plus the adjustment, in whole cents, with no rounding. Statements in `DRAFT`, `RETURNED_UNDELIVERABLE` or `VOIDED` contribute nothing to the adjustment term — which is precisely what allows a void paired with a successor issuance carrying the same wage delta to leave the corrected wage base exactly where it was. The companion counter is defined the same way: an employee's `active_statement_count` is the number of corrected wage statements for that employee whose status is `ISSUED` or `ACKNOWLEDGED`. Both are recomputed whenever a statement is issued, acknowledged, reported undeliverable or voided, and both are recomputed when a reconciliation re-run re-derives the case's discrepancy set.

Cases can be listed and narrowed along every axis that matters operationally: `employer_id`, `agency_id`, `tax_year`, `quarter`, and any of the four state dimensions independently — `status`, `detection_status`, `filing_status` and `statement_status`.

Reconciliation is the detection act, and it is available to the payroll side. Before it may run, the case must be in `status` `OPEN`, and exactly one filed return whose `status` is `FILED` or `AMENDED` must exist on the case's scope. If no such return exists, the run is refused with `ERR_NO_FILED_RETURN_FOR_PERIOD`. The run compares three bodies of evidence and produces a set of discrepancies whose order is pinned, because the position of each discrepancy inside its run is recorded and later relied upon.

The comparison proceeds as follows. First, the run gathers every payroll register line whose employer, agency, tax year and quarter match the case's scope and whose `posting_status` is `POSTED`, ordered by `created_at` ascending; that ordering is pinned and no row identifier ever participates in it. The register wage total is the sum of `taxable_wage_cents` over those lines and the register withholding total is the sum of `withheld_cents` over the same lines. Second, the run reads the single filed return described above, taking `reported_taxable_wage_cents` and `reported_withheld_cents` from it. Third, the run gathers every deposit record on the same scope whose `clearing_status` is `CLEARED`; deposits whose `clearing_status` is `RETURNED` are excluded entirely and contribute neither cash nor obligation. Those cleared deposits are read twice, in two different orders: in **obligation fill order**, sorted by `deposit_due_date` ascending and then by `created_at` ascending, and in **chronological cash order**, sorted by `deposit_date` ascending and then by `created_at` ascending. The total obligation is the sum of `obligation_amount_cents` over the cleared deposits. Discrepancy rows emitted by any previous run of this case are not inputs to a new run.

The deposit comparison is a **chronological waterfall**. Each obligation starts out needing its own `obligation_amount_cents` and no more. The run then walks the cleared deposits in chronological cash order; for each one it takes that deposit's `amount_cents` as available cash and walks the obligations in obligation fill order, applying to each obligation the smaller of the cash still in hand and the amount that obligation still needs, reducing both by the amount applied, and adding the amount applied to a running applied-cash total. When a deposit's cash is exhausted the run moves on to the next deposit; when every obligation has been filled, any cash still in hand from the current or later deposits is discarded as over-deposit and is never carried anywhere. The applied-cash total that survives this walk is what gets compared to the total obligation.

Discrepancies are then emitted in a **pinned category order**: taxable wage first, withholding second, jurisdiction assignment third, and deposit shortfall last. If the register wage total differs from `reported_taxable_wage_cents`, a discrepancy with `detected_category` `TAXABLE_WAGE` is emitted whose `amount_cents` is the register wage total minus the reported taxable wage, with `employee_id` and `register_line_id` both left null. If the register withholding total differs from `reported_withheld_cents`, a discrepancy with `detected_category` `WITHHOLDING` is emitted whose `amount_cents` is the register withholding total minus the reported withholding, again with both optional references null. Next, the run walks the register lines once more, in the same pinned `created_at` ascending order, and for every line whose `work_jurisdiction_code` differs from the agency's `jurisdiction_code` it emits a discrepancy with `detected_category` `JURISDICTION_ASSIGNMENT` whose `amount_cents` is that line's apportioned removal, whose `employee_id` is the line's employee and whose `register_line_id` is the line itself — this is the only category that is employee-scoped, and every other category leaves both of those references null. Finally, if the applied-cash total is strictly less than the total obligation, a discrepancy with `detected_category` `DEPOSIT_SHORTFALL` is emitted whose `amount_cents` is the total obligation minus the applied-cash total. Because a positive `amount_cents` always means the register exceeds what was filed or deposited, these values are signed. Each emitted discrepancy receives an `emission_index` equal to its zero-based position in the emitted sequence, and its `detected_category` is fixed at emission and never mutated afterwards.

The apportioned removal for a jurisdiction-misassigned line is computed with a rounding discipline that must not be reorganised. For each line individually, multiply that line's `taxable_wage_cents` by that line's `workday_ratio_bps`, divide by ten thousand, and round the result to the nearest whole cent with ties rounded upward. Rounding happens **once per line, before any summation**. Wherever several lines are aggregated — and the amendment rollup of §3.4 does exactly that — the already-rounded per-line amounts are added together. The alternative of summing the raw wage amounts first and rounding the aggregate once is forbidden, because it produces a different number of cents. To make the difference concrete: three lines of 100001 cents each at a workday share of 5000 basis points round to 50001 cents per line and sum to 150003 cents, whereas rounding the aggregate would give 150002 cents. Only the first result is correct.

Nothing found is a legitimate and final answer. When the run emits no discrepancy at all, the case transitions `NOT_RUN → NO_VARIANCE`: `filing_status` stays at `FILING_NOT_REQUIRED`, `statement_status` stays at `STMT_NOT_REQUIRED`, `open_discrepancy_count` is recomputed to 0, and the case's `version` is incremented by 1. `NO_VARIANCE` is terminal for the reconciliation dimension — a case that reconciled cleanly can never be reconciled again, and an attempt to do so is refused with `ERR_ALREADY_TERMINAL`.

When the run emits at least one discrepancy, the case transitions `NOT_RUN → VARIANCE_FOUND`. One discrepancy row is created per emitted variance, each in status `OPEN` with its `emission_index` assigned in the pinned order. Two further dimensions may move in the same request, and they move on different triggers: `filing_status` is set to `FILING_PENDING` when any emitted discrepancy carries a `detected_category` of `TAXABLE_WAGE`, `WITHHOLDING` or `DEPOSIT_SHORTFALL`, and `statement_status` is set to `STMT_PENDING` when any emitted discrepancy carries a `detected_category` of `JURISDICTION_ASSIGNMENT`. An employee-level reportable-wage reassignment is the only kind of variance that forces a corrected wage statement, which is why only that category moves the statement dimension. The run also recomputes `open_discrepancy_count` — at all times the number of discrepancies on the case whose status is `OPEN` or `CATEGORIZED` — and increments the case's `version` by 1.

Source records change, and reconciliation is therefore re-enterable. A case already in `VARIANCE_FOUND` may be reconciled again, transitioning `VARIANCE_FOUND → VARIANCE_FOUND`, provided the case `status` is still `OPEN` and no amendment for the case has reached `SIGNED`, `SUBMITTED` or `ACCEPTED` — once a signature exists, the discrepancy set it was built on is frozen. A re-run first supersedes the previous answer: every discrepancy on the case whose status is `OPEN` or `CATEGORIZED` transitions to `SUPERSEDED`, and each of those rows has its own `version` incremented by 1. A fresh discrepancy set is then derived from the current source records with `emission_index` restarting at 0. The re-run recomputes `open_discrepancy_count`, `dominant_root_cause_code` and `corrected_wage_base_cents`, and increments the case's `version` by 1. `SUPERSEDED` is terminal for a discrepancy; so is `DISMISSED`. Because the case's `version` moves, any amendment creation still holding the pre-run version will fail its concurrency assertion, which is the intended serialisation point between re-detection and amendment drafting.

### 3.3. Discrepancy Triage and Root-Cause Attribution

Discrepancies are never created by hand. Reconciliation is their only origin, which makes the filtered discrepancy list their sole discovery channel; it can be narrowed by `correction_case_id`, by `employee_id`, by `register_line_id`, by `resolved_amended_return_id`, by `category` and by `status`. A single discrepancy is fetched by its `discrepancy_id`, and an unknown identifier yields `ERR_DISCREPANCY_NOT_FOUND`.

Detection guesses; an analyst decides. Categorising a discrepancy is available from `OPEN` only, and only while the owning case is still in `status` `OPEN`; an attempt from any other discrepancy state is refused with `ERR_INVALID_STATE`, and an attempt on a discrepancy already in `DISMISSED` or `SUPERSEDED` — both terminal — is refused with `ERR_ALREADY_TERMINAL`. Categorisation supplies two values. `category` is the analyst-confirmed classification, drawn from `TAXABLE_WAGE`, `WITHHOLDING`, `JURISDICTION_ASSIGNMENT` and `DEPOSIT_SHORTFALL`, and it is permitted to differ from the `detected_category` the algorithm assigned — the detection value is a machine opinion recorded for audit and is never overwritten, while the analyst value is what every downstream computation actually reads. `root_cause_code` is the analyst's attribution of *why* it happened, drawn from `RATE_TABLE_STALE`, `JURISDICTION_MAPPING_ERROR`, `THIRD_PARTY_FEED_ERROR`, `MANUAL_ENTRY_ERROR` and `DEPOSIT_SCHEDULING_ERROR`. On success the discrepancy transitions `OPEN → CATEGORIZED`, both supplied values are stored, the owning case's `dominant_root_cause_code` and `open_discrepancy_count` are recomputed, and the discrepancy's `version` is incremented by 1. Categorisation is also one of the five operations guarded by the scope rule of §2.9, so a discrepancy must belong to the case it is being triaged under or the request is refused with `ERR_SCOPE_MISMATCH`.

The dominant root cause is a single derived label with an explicit tie-break. Among the discrepancies belonging to the case whose status is `CATEGORIZED` or `RESOLVED`, take the one whose `amount_cents` has the largest absolute value, and publish that row's `root_cause_code` as the case's `dominant_root_cause_code`. Where two such rows have equally large absolute amounts, the one with the earlier `created_at` wins. When that set of discrepancies is empty, `dominant_root_cause_code` is null. Discrepancies in `OPEN`, `DISMISSED` or `SUPERSEDED` never contribute to the label.

Not every variance deserves an amendment. Dismissing a discrepancy is available from `CATEGORIZED` only — a discrepancy must be classified before it can be waved away — and again only while the owning case is in `status` `OPEN`. The request supplies a free-text `dismissal_reason`, which is recorded. On success the discrepancy transitions `CATEGORIZED → DISMISSED`, the case's `open_discrepancy_count` and `dominant_root_cause_code` are both recomputed, and the discrepancy's `version` is incremented by 1. Dismissal carries one further consequence that is easy to miss: if the recomputed `open_discrepancy_count` reaches 0 while the case's `filing_status` is `FILING_IN_PROGRESS`, the case's filing dimension advances `FILING_IN_PROGRESS → FILING_COMPLETE` in the same request, exactly as a resolution would have done. Dismissing the last outstanding discrepancy therefore completes the filing dimension.

Two more fields on a discrepancy exist only to record the amendment that settled it. `resolved_amended_return_id` names the accepted amendment that resolved the row, and `resolved_amount_cents` freezes the row's `amount_cents` at the instant it entered `RESOLVED`. Both are written by the acceptance cascade of §3.5 and both are cleared back to null if that amendment is later voided.

### 3.4. Amendment Drafting, the Delta Rollup and Statutory Charges

An amendment begins as a draft against a case. Drafting supplies the `correction_case_id`, the `form_code` of 1 to 16 characters that will be transmitted to the agency, an `accrual_through_date` marking the end of the interest accrual window, and the `expected_correction_case_version` assertion described in §2.7. When the draft replaces a predecessor that the agency rejected or that was voided, `supersedes_amended_return_id` names that predecessor; for a first attempt it is omitted. The new row is written in `status` `DRAFT`, with `wage_delta_cents`, `tax_delta_cents`, `deposit_shortfall_cents`, `days_late`, `interest_cents` and `penalty_cents` all at 0, `interest_variant` at null, `voluntary_reduction_applied` at false, and `prepared_by_user_id`, `signed_by_user_id`, `submitted_at`, `accepted_at`, `rejection_reason` and `void_reason` all at null. Creating the draft increments the *case's* `version` by 1, which is why two drafts cannot be created against one observed case version in succession.

Two guards apply at drafting time beyond the concurrency assertion. First, an amendment may not be opened on a case that reconciliation has found nothing to correct: if the case has no discrepancy rows at all — which is exactly the situation when `detection_status` is `NOT_RUN` or `NO_VARIANCE` — the creation is refused with `ERR_CASE_HAS_NO_DISCREPANCIES`. Second, at most one amendment per case and `form_code` combination may occupy a live slot, where "live" means a `status` of `DRAFT`, `PREPARED`, `SIGNED` or `SUBMITTED`; a second draft for the same case and form while one of those is outstanding is refused with `ERR_AMENDMENT_SLOT_OCCUPIED`. Returns in `ACCEPTED`, `AGENCY_REJECTED` or `VOID` do not occupy the slot, which is what lets a rejected attempt be replaced. Amendment lists can be narrowed by `correction_case_id`, by `supersedes_amended_return_id`, by `form_code`, by `status` and by `interest_variant`.

Preparation is where all the arithmetic happens, and it is the preparer's act. It may optionally re-supply `accrual_through_date`, overriding the value carried on the draft. Four preconditions gate it. The amendment must be in a state that permits preparation. Every discrepancy on the case must have been triaged: the count of discrepancies on the case whose status is `OPEN` must be zero, and the count whose status is `CATEGORIZED` must be greater than zero — a case with nothing categorised has nothing to amend. Failing either half of that condition is refused with `ERR_UNCATEGORIZED_DISCREPANCIES`. When the case's agency has `requires_registration` set to true, a registration for the case's employer and agency must exist whose `registration_status` is `ACTIVE` or `RETRO_ACTIVE` and whose coverage brackets the case's period: the registration's `from_period_ordinal` must be no greater than the case's period ordinal and its `to_period_ordinal` no smaller. Absent such coverage the preparation is refused with `ERR_AGENCY_ACCOUNT_NOT_ACTIVE` — and note that this is precisely the gap a retroactive registration fills, since activating one back-dates coverage over its whole ordinal range. Finally, `accrual_through_date` must be no earlier than the case's filed return's `original_due_date` and no later than the current logical instant; either violation is refused with `ERR_INVALID_ACCRUAL_DATE`.

The **delta rollup** reads exactly one set of rows: the discrepancies belonging to the amendment's case whose status is `CATEGORIZED`, iterated in `emission_index` ascending order. Rows in `DISMISSED`, `SUPERSEDED` and `RESOLVED` are excluded, and the analyst-confirmed `category` is what selects rows — `detected_category` never participates. From that set, three sums and one difference are formed. The taxable-wage delta is the sum of `amount_cents` over the rows whose `category` is `TAXABLE_WAGE`. The jurisdiction removal is the sum, over the rows whose `category` is `JURISDICTION_ASSIGNMENT`, of the per-line apportioned amount of the register line each such row names — computed with the per-line rounding of §3.2, meaning each line's amount is rounded to a whole cent before the addition. The amendment's `wage_delta_cents` is the taxable-wage delta minus the jurisdiction removal. Its `tax_delta_cents` is the sum of `amount_cents` over the rows whose `category` is `WITHHOLDING`, and its `deposit_shortfall_cents` is the sum of `amount_cents` over the rows whose `category` is `DEPOSIT_SHORTFALL`. Its `days_late` is the count of whole days from the case's filed return's `original_due_date` to the amendment's `accrual_through_date`, floored at zero so that an early accrual date yields zero rather than a negative count. No rounding occurs anywhere in the rollup other than the per-line rounding just described.

Interest is agency-specific, and the agency chooses the variant rather than the preparer. The amendment's `interest_variant` is copied from the case agency's `interest_method` and recorded on the row, so a reader can always tell which formula produced the number. The annual rate is the agency's `interest_rate_bps_annual` divided by ten thousand, and every accrual divides by a year of 365 days.

Under the `STATUTORY_DAILY` variant — the federal-style regime — one simple-interest accrual runs on the whole tax delta from the filed return's statutory due date. Take the amendment's `tax_delta_cents`, multiply by the annual rate, multiply by `days_late` (the whole-day count from the filed return's `original_due_date` to `accrual_through_date`, floored at zero), divide by 365, and round the result to the nearest whole cent with ties rounded upward. That single figure is `interest_cents`.

Under the `DEPOSIT_WEIGHTED` variant — the state-style regime — the tax delta is split across the quarter's deposit obligations and each piece accrues from its own due date, which is earlier than the return's statutory due date and therefore accrues for more days. The run gathers every deposit record on the case's employer, agency, tax year and quarter whose `clearing_status` is `CLEARED`, ordered by `deposit_due_date` ascending and then by `created_at` ascending, and forms the total obligation as the sum of `obligation_amount_cents` over them. If there are no such deposits, or if that total obligation is zero, the variant falls back to the `STATUTORY_DAILY` computation exactly as written above. Otherwise, walking the obligations in that pinned order, every obligation except the last receives a tranche of the tax delta equal to `tax_delta_cents` multiplied by that obligation's `obligation_amount_cents`, divided by the total obligation, rounded to the nearest whole cent with ties rounded upward; those tranches are accumulated as they are formed. The final obligation's tranche is `tax_delta_cents` minus the accumulated total of all earlier tranches, so the last tranche absorbs the rounding remainder and the tranches always sum back to the tax delta exactly. For each obligation, the elapsed days are the whole days from that obligation's own `deposit_due_date` to `accrual_through_date`, floored at zero. Each obligation's interest contribution is its tranche multiplied by the annual rate, multiplied by its own elapsed days, divided by 365, and rounded to the nearest whole cent with ties rounded upward — **each tranche's interest is rounded before the additions**, not after. `interest_cents` is the sum of those rounded contributions. The two variants genuinely differ: for a tax delta of 250000 cents, an annual rate of 1200 basis points, an accrual-through date of 30 April 2026 and two cleared obligations of 150000 cents due 19 February 2025 and 100000 cents due 19 March 2025, `DEPOSIT_WEIGHTED` yields 34833 cents while `STATUTORY_DAILY` on identical inputs yields 30000 cents.

Penalty is a five-step tiered assessment, and the order of the last three steps is pinned because reordering them changes the number of cents. **Step one** selects a failure-to-deposit rate from a days-late ladder: when `days_late` is 5 or fewer the rate is 200 basis points; when it is 6 through 15 the rate is 500 basis points; when it is 16 or more the rate is 1000 basis points. The failure-to-deposit charge is `deposit_shortfall_cents` multiplied by that rate, divided by ten thousand, rounded half up to a whole cent. **Step two** computes the failure-to-file charge at 500 basis points for each whole or partial thirty-day month of lateness, capped at five such months: when `days_late` is greater than zero, the month count is `days_late` divided by 30 and rounded *up* to the next whole number, then limited to at most 5; when `days_late` is zero the month count is zero. The uncapped charge is `tax_delta_cents` multiplied by 500 times the month count, divided by ten thousand, rounded half up. A separate ceiling is computed as `tax_delta_cents` multiplied by 2500, divided by ten thousand, rounded half up — that is, a quarter of the tax delta. The failure-to-file charge is the smaller of the uncapped charge and that ceiling. **Step three** adds the two charges into a gross penalty and then, if and only if that gross is greater than zero, raises it to the statutory minimum of 5000 cents — that is, the gross becomes the larger of itself and 5000 cents. A gross of exactly zero stays zero and the floor does not fire. **Step four** applies the agency multiplier: the floored gross multiplied by the agency's `penalty_multiplier_bps`, divided by ten thousand, rounded half up. **Step five**, last of all, applies the voluntary self-correction reduction. The reduction applies when no agency notice exists whose employer, agency, tax year and quarter match the case's four-part key; existence alone suppresses it, in *any* `response_status` and *any* `abatement_status`. When the reduction applies, `penalty_cents` is the scaled amount multiplied by 7500 (that is, ten thousand less the 2500-basis-point reduction), divided by ten thousand, rounded half up, and `voluntary_reduction_applied` is recorded as true; when a matching notice exists, `penalty_cents` is the scaled amount unchanged and `voluntary_reduction_applied` is recorded as false. Applying the statutory floor after the multiplier, or after the voluntary reduction, is forbidden.

Everything preparation touches is written in one request. The amendment transitions `DRAFT → PREPARED`; `wage_delta_cents`, `tax_delta_cents`, `deposit_shortfall_cents`, `days_late`, `interest_variant`, `interest_cents`, `penalty_cents` and `voluntary_reduction_applied` are all written; `prepared_by_user_id` is set to the acting `X-User-ID`; the case's `filing_status` advances `FILING_PENDING → FILING_IN_PROGRESS`, which itself recomputes the case's `total_exposure_cents` from the newly prepared amendment and then the employer's `open_exposure_cents`, and increments the case's `version` by 1; and the amendment's own `version` is incremented by 1.

Preparation is also the recall lever, and it works from two other states. A `PREPARED` amendment that has been signed may be prepared again, transitioning `SIGNED → DRAFT`: the signature is recalled by clearing `signed_by_user_id` back to null, and `wage_delta_cents`, `tax_delta_cents`, `deposit_shortfall_cents`, `interest_cents` and `penalty_cents` are all cleared back to 0 *before* the recomputation runs, after which the case's `total_exposure_cents` and the employer's `open_exposure_cents` are recomputed and the amendment's `version` is incremented by 1. A re-preparation always voids the prior signature, so segregation of duties cannot be laundered by preparing after someone else has signed. Similarly, an amendment the agency rejected may be prepared again, transitioning `AGENCY_REJECTED → DRAFT`, which clears `signed_by_user_id`, `submitted_at` and `rejection_reason` back to null and increments `version` by 1. Both routes rejoin the ordinary `DRAFT → PREPARED → SIGNED` path, giving the amendment lifecycle two genuine cycles.

### 3.5. Signature, Transmission and Agency Disposition

A prepared amendment carries a number that somebody has to stand behind, and the person who stands behind it may not be the person who computed it. Signing is available only from `PREPARED` and takes no request body. Two conditions gate it. The acting `X-User-ID` must differ from the `prepared_by_user_id` recorded on the amendment; if they are equal the request is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`. And the case's `total_exposure_cents`, as it stands with this amendment counted, must be no greater than the employer's `authorization_limit_cents`; if the exposure would exceed the limit the signature is refused with `ERR_AUTHORIZATION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, on the principle that no officer may bind the employer beyond the authority the employer granted. On success the amendment transitions `PREPARED → SIGNED`, `signed_by_user_id` is set to the acting `X-User-ID`, and the amendment's `version` is incremented by 1.

Transmission is likewise bodyless, and it is where the two statutory clocks are enforced. The amendment must be in `SIGNED`; attempting transmission on an amendment in any other state is refused with `ERR_AMENDED_RETURN_NOT_SIGNED`. The **amendment window** requires the current logical instant to fall on or before the filed return's `original_due_date` advanced by the agency's `amendment_window_days` whole days — the boundary instant itself is inside the window — and a later attempt is refused with `ERR_AMENDMENT_WINDOW_CLOSED`. The **cooling-off buffer** looks for a previous amendment on the same employer, agency, tax year and quarter whose `status` is `SUBMITTED`, `ACCEPTED` or `AGENCY_REJECTED`. If no such previous amendment exists, the buffer is satisfied trivially. If one or more exist, take the most recent of them and count the whole days from its `submitted_at` to the current logical instant; that count must be at least the agency's `min_days_between_amendments`. The buffer is half-open, so a count exactly equal to the configured minimum passes. A shorter interval is refused with `ERR_AMENDMENT_COOLING_OFF_ACTIVE`. On success the amendment transitions `SIGNED → SUBMITTED`, `submitted_at` is stamped from the logical clock, and `version` is incremented by 1.

The agency then decides, and the two decisions could not diverge more sharply. Recording the disposition supplies an `outcome` of either `ACCEPTED` or `REJECTED`, together with a `rejection_reason` on the rejecting branch. On `REJECTED` the amendment transitions `SUBMITTED → AGENCY_REJECTED`, the supplied `rejection_reason` is stored, and **nothing else in the system moves**: no discrepancy is resolved, the case's `filing_status` remains at `FILING_IN_PROGRESS`, and the only version that changes is the amendment's own, incremented by 1. A `rejection_reason` is required on this branch.

On `ACCEPTED` the amendment transitions `SUBMITTED → ACCEPTED` and a five-link cascade runs to completion inside the same request. **Link one:** the amendment's `accepted_at` is stamped from the logical clock and its `interest_cents` and `penalty_cents` are frozen at their prepared values, and the case's filed return transitions `FILED → AMENDED` with its own `version` incremented by 1 — a transition available only from `FILED`, and only when that return's employer, agency, tax year and quarter all equal the accepted amendment's case keys. **Link two:** every discrepancy on the case whose status is `CATEGORIZED` transitions `CATEGORIZED → RESOLVED`, each one recording `resolved_amended_return_id` as the accepted amendment and freezing `resolved_amount_cents` at its own `amount_cents`, with each such discrepancy's `version` incremented by 1. **Link three:** those discrepancy transitions recompute the case's `open_discrepancy_count`, and when that count reaches 0 while `filing_status` is `FILING_IN_PROGRESS`, the case's filing dimension advances `FILING_IN_PROGRESS → FILING_COMPLETE` and the case's `version` is incremented by 1. The filing dimension is driven by the discrepancy transitions, not by the disposition call itself — which is exactly why dismissing the last discrepancy has the same effect. **Link four:** reaching `FILING_COMPLETE` while `statement_status` is `STMT_NOT_REQUIRED`, `STMT_ISSUED` or `STMT_COMPLETE` advances the case's overall `status` `OPEN → REMEDIATION_PENDING` — the join point of the filing and statement dimensions — and, provided no control remediation for this case already exists, auto-creates exactly one control remediation in `status` `OPEN` whose `correction_case_id` is this case, whose `root_cause_code` is the case's `dominant_root_cause_code`, whose `control_gap_class` is `PROCESS_CONTROL`, whose `owner_user_id` is null and whose `target_date` is the current logical instant advanced by 30 days. **Link five:** when that remediation was in fact created, the employer's `open_remediation_count` is incremented by 1. `ACCEPTED` is terminal for an amendment, as is `VOID`; a further transition attempt out of either is refused with `ERR_ALREADY_TERMINAL`.

The same join rule applies from the other direction. Whenever the statement dimension reaches `STMT_ISSUED` or `STMT_COMPLETE` while `filing_status` is `FILING_NOT_REQUIRED` or `FILING_COMPLETE`, the case advances `OPEN → REMEDIATION_PENDING` with the identical remediation auto-creation and the identical employer counter increment. In both directions the transition requires `filing_status` to be one of `FILING_NOT_REQUIRED` or `FILING_COMPLETE` **and** `statement_status` to be one of `STMT_NOT_REQUIRED`, `STMT_ISSUED` or `STMT_COMPLETE`; neither dimension alone is sufficient.

Voiding is the compensating action, and it exists so that a mistaken preparation costs nothing permanent. A void supplies a `void_reason`, which is required, and is available from `DRAFT`, `PREPARED` and `AGENCY_REJECTED`. The three branches are deliberately unequal in what they reverse, because they contributed unequally in the first place. Voiding a `DRAFT` amendment transitions `DRAFT → VOID`, records the `void_reason`, and increments the amendment's `version` by 1 — nothing else changes, because a draft never contributed to any rollup. Voiding a `PREPARED` amendment transitions `PREPARED → VOID`, records the `void_reason`, transitions every discrepancy whose `resolved_amended_return_id` names this amendment back `RESOLVED → CATEGORIZED` (clearing both `resolved_amended_return_id` and `resolved_amount_cents` to null, recomputing the case's `open_discrepancy_count`, and incrementing each such discrepancy's `version` by 1), recomputes the case's `total_exposure_cents` now that this amendment has left the summed set, recomputes the employer's `open_exposure_cents`, returns the case's `filing_status` `FILING_IN_PROGRESS → FILING_PENDING` provided no other amendment for the case sits in `PREPARED`, `SIGNED`, `SUBMITTED` or `ACCEPTED`, and increments the amendment's `version` by 1. Voiding an `AGENCY_REJECTED` amendment transitions `AGENCY_REJECTED → VOID`, records the `void_reason`, recomputes the case's `total_exposure_cents` and the employer's `open_exposure_cents`, and increments the amendment's `version` by 1. Across all three branches the invariant is the same: prepare-then-void restores the employer's `open_exposure_cents` to exactly the value it held before the preparation.

### 3.6. Corrected Wage Statements and the Reissue Chain

When a jurisdiction misassignment moves an employee's reportable wages, that employee is owed a corrected wage statement. Drafting one names the `correction_case_id`, the `employee_id` receiving it, the `tax_year` being corrected, and the `delivery_channel` — either `MAIL` or `PORTAL`. A `supersedes_statement_id` may be supplied when the draft is being created as a known successor. The employee must belong to the case's employer or the request is refused with `ERR_SCOPE_MISMATCH`. The three box deltas are never supplied by the client: they are computed at draft time and recomputed at issue time and again whenever a successor is spawned. Drafting writes the row in `status` `DRAFT`, derives `is_prior_year` by comparing the statement's `tax_year` against the calendar year of the current logical instant — true when the statement's tax year is strictly earlier — and assigns `sequence_no` as one plus the number of statements already existing for the same case and employee, so the first statement in a chain carries `sequence_no` 1 and a null `supersedes_statement_id`.

The **box deltas** are a projection of what the case discovered about this one employee. Start with the employee's jurisdiction removal: the sum of `amount_cents` over the discrepancies belonging to this case whose `employee_id` is this statement's employee, whose `category` is `JURISDICTION_ASSIGNMENT`, and whose status is `CATEGORIZED` or `RESOLVED`, iterated in `emission_index` ascending order. Discrepancies in `OPEN`, `DISMISSED` or `SUPERSEDED` are excluded, as are discrepancies for other employees. Then look for a matching collected repayment: the wage repayment belonging to this case whose `employee_id` is this statement's employee, whose `status` is `COLLECTED`, and whose `overpayment_tax_year` equals this statement's `tax_year`; where several match, order them by `created_at` descending and take the first. Repayments in `PROPOSED`, `AGREED`, `WRITTEN_OFF` or `CANCELLED` never move a box.

Three outcomes follow. If no such collected repayment exists, `wage_delta_cents` is the negation of the jurisdiction removal, `fica_wage_delta_cents` is likewise the negation of the jurisdiction removal, and `withholding_delta_cents` is 0. If a collected repayment exists and its `recovery_basis` is `SAME_YEAR_NET` — the current-year box treatment, in which all three boxes are adjusted — then `wage_delta_cents` is the negation of the jurisdiction removal reduced further by the repayment's `overpaid_gross_cents`, `fica_wage_delta_cents` is that same figure, and `withholding_delta_cents` is the negation of the repayment's `overpaid_income_tax_withheld_cents`. If instead the collected repayment's `recovery_basis` is `PRIOR_YEAR_GROSS` — the prior-year box treatment, in which box 1 taxable wages and box 2 withholding are left untouched and only the social-insurance wages move — then `wage_delta_cents` is the negation of the jurisdiction removal alone, `fica_wage_delta_cents` is the negation of the jurisdiction removal reduced further by the repayment's `overpaid_gross_cents`, and `withholding_delta_cents` is 0. All three figures are signed whole cents; the only rounding involved already happened per-line when the jurisdiction apportionment was computed.

A statement with nothing to say may not exist. If the computation yields 0 for all three of `wage_delta_cents`, `fica_wage_delta_cents` and `withholding_delta_cents`, the draft is refused with `ERR_NO_REPORTABLE_DELTA`. Age also bounds the work: a statement whose `tax_year` is more than three calendar years before the current logical year may neither be drafted nor issued, and either attempt is refused with `ERR_PRIOR_YEAR_WINDOW_CLOSED`. Statement lists can be narrowed by `correction_case_id`, `employee_id`, `supersedes_statement_id`, `tax_year`, `is_prior_year` and `status`; successor statements born inside a cascade are discoverable only there.

Issuance is available from `DRAFT` and takes no request body. Beyond being in `DRAFT`, three conditions gate it. At least one of `wage_delta_cents`, `fica_wage_delta_cents` and `withholding_delta_cents` must be non-zero. Every wage repayment belonging to the statement's case must have settled — its `status` must be `COLLECTED`, `WRITTEN_OFF` or `CANCELLED` — because an unsettled repayment can still change the boxes, and issuing before it settles would put a figure in front of an employee that the system is about to contradict. And when `is_prior_year` is true, the current logical instant must fall on or before the agency's `amendment_window_days` boundary measured from the case's filed return `original_due_date`; past that boundary the prior-year statement may not be issued. Issuance stamps `issued_at`, records the `delivery_channel`, and transitions the statement `DRAFT → ISSUED`. In the same request the case's statement dimension advances — `STMT_PENDING → STMT_ISSUED` for a first statement, or `STMT_REISSUE_PENDING → STMT_ISSUED` for a successor — the employee's `active_statement_count` and the case's `corrected_wage_base_cents` are recomputed, the case's `version` is incremented by 1, and the statement's own `version` is incremented by 1.

The employee closes the loop themselves. Acknowledgement is available from `ISSUED` and is performed by the employee whose `X-User-ID` equals the statement's `employee_id`; any other caller in that role is refused as a non-owner. It stamps `acknowledged_at` and transitions the statement `ISSUED → ACKNOWLEDGED`, which is terminal, and increments the statement's `version` by 1. Acknowledgement also carries the case's statement dimension forward, but only conditionally: when no statement belonging to the case remains in `DRAFT`, `ISSUED` or `RETURNED_UNDELIVERABLE` — that is, when the count of statements on the case in those three states reaches zero — the case advances `STMT_ISSUED → STMT_COMPLETE` and its `version` is incremented by 1. And reaching `STMT_COMPLETE` while the case's overall `status` is `OPEN` and its `filing_status` is `FILING_NOT_REQUIRED` or `FILING_COMPLETE` advances the case `OPEN → REMEDIATION_PENDING`, with the same remediation auto-creation and employer counter increment described in §3.5.

Mail comes back. Reporting a statement undeliverable is available from `ISSUED` and requires an `undeliverable_reason`; it is available only when the statement's `delivery_channel` is `MAIL`, and an attempt on a `PORTAL` statement is refused with `ERR_CHANNEL_NOT_UNDELIVERABLE_CAPABLE`. The statement transitions `ISSUED → RETURNED_UNDELIVERABLE`, the reason is recorded, the employee's `active_statement_count` is recomputed because this statement has left the counted set, the case's `corrected_wage_base_cents` is recomputed because the statement has stopped contributing its wage delta, and the statement's `version` is incremented by 1. Note that `RETURNED_UNDELIVERABLE` still counts as an outstanding statement for the purposes of the acknowledgement completion test above — it blocks `STMT_COMPLETE` until it is dealt with.

Reissue is how a statement is superseded, and it does two things in one operation: it retires the predecessor and it creates the successor. It is available from `ISSUED` and from `RETURNED_UNDELIVERABLE`, and it requires the `expected_statement_version` assertion of §2.7 carrying the predecessor's current `version`. Optional inputs shape the successor and its delivery: a replacement `delivery_channel`, a `reissue_reason` recorded with the act, and a `corrected_mailing_state_code` which, when supplied, updates the employee's `mailing_state_code` so future paper reaches them. The predecessor transitions to `VOIDED`, which is terminal, and a successor is created in `status` `DRAFT` whose `supersedes_statement_id` names the predecessor, whose `sequence_no` is one greater than the predecessor's, and whose `correction_case_id`, `employee_id` and `tax_year` are copied from the predecessor; the successor's own box deltas are computed afresh. The case's statement dimension moves `STMT_ISSUED → STMT_REISSUE_PENDING`, the case's `corrected_wage_base_cents` and the employee's `active_statement_count` are recomputed, and the predecessor's `version` is incremented by 1. Because the response reports a newly created resource, reissue answers with HTTP 201. The full cycle — `ISSUED → RETURNED_UNDELIVERABLE → VOIDED`, then a successor `DRAFT → ISSUED` — is traceable through the chain of `supersedes_statement_id` links.

One consequence of `STMT_ISSUED → STMT_REISSUE_PENDING` reaches back into the case's overall lifecycle. The transition requires that the predecessor statement has in fact reached `VOIDED`. When it fires while the case's overall `status` is `REMEDIATION_PENDING`, the case is reopened `REMEDIATION_PENDING → OPEN` and the employer's `open_exposure_cents` is recomputed, because the case re-enters the employer's open set and re-admits its whole `total_exposure_cents`. The case's `corrected_wage_base_cents` is recomputed and its `version` is incremented by 1. A case that had reached the remediation stage can therefore be pulled back to `OPEN` by a statement reissue, and it must be walked forward again.

### 3.7. Wage Repayment Recovery and Its Statement Cascade

Sometimes the error ran the other way and the employee was paid too much. Proposing recovery names the `correction_case_id` that discovered the overpayment and the `employee_id` who must repay, and states four numbers plus two years: `overpaid_gross_cents`, which must be strictly greater than zero; `overpaid_income_tax_withheld_cents` and `overpaid_fica_withheld_cents`, the income-tax and social-insurance amounts withheld on those overpaid wages, both of which must be non-negative; `overpayment_tax_year`, the tax year in which the overpaid wages were paid; and `recovery_tax_year`, the tax year in which the money is being recovered. An `installment_count` between 1 and 24 may be supplied. Two arithmetic conditions are checked together: `recovery_tax_year` may not be earlier than `overpayment_tax_year`, and the two withheld components added together may not exceed `overpaid_gross_cents`. Either violation is refused with `ERR_INVALID_RECOVERY_YEAR`. The employee must also belong to the case's scope or the proposal is refused with `ERR_SCOPE_MISMATCH`. The row is written in `status` `PROPOSED` with `collected_cents` at 0 and `write_off_reason` at null.

Which year the money comes back in changes what the employee owes, and that is the whole point of the **recovery basis**. When `recovery_tax_year` equals `overpayment_tax_year`, the wage statement for the year is still open and the employer can still reverse income-tax withholding inside the same reporting year; the basis recorded is `SAME_YEAR_NET`, `employee_due_cents` is `overpaid_gross_cents` reduced by `overpaid_income_tax_withheld_cents` and further reduced by `overpaid_fica_withheld_cents` — the net the employee actually received — and `agency_recoverable_cents` is `overpaid_income_tax_withheld_cents` plus `overpaid_fica_withheld_cents`, since both are reclaimable from the agencies. When `recovery_tax_year` is later than `overpayment_tax_year`, the prior year's statement has already been filed and the income tax withheld was already credited to the employee, so the employer cannot reclaim it; the basis recorded is `PRIOR_YEAR_GROSS`, `employee_due_cents` is the full `overpaid_gross_cents`, and `agency_recoverable_cents` is `overpaid_fica_withheld_cents` alone. The computation reads only those four amounts and two years on the repayment row itself; no other entity participates, and no rounding occurs because every input is already integral cents. The divergence is substantial: for an overpaid gross of 400000 cents with 60000 cents of income tax and 30600 cents of social-insurance tax withheld, `SAME_YEAR_NET` yields an amount due of 309400 cents and an agency-recoverable amount of 90600 cents, while `PRIOR_YEAR_GROSS` on identical inputs yields 400000 cents and 30600 cents. Repayment lists can be narrowed by `correction_case_id`, `employee_id`, `recovery_basis` and `status`.

The employee agrees to the arrangement themselves, from `PROPOSED`. The acting `X-User-ID` must equal the repayment's `employee_id`, and the owning case's `status` must be `OPEN` or `REMEDIATION_PENDING` — a closed case cannot acquire new agreements. Agreement transitions the row `PROPOSED → AGREED`, records the `installment_count` the employee has agreed to, and increments the repayment's `version` by 1.

Terms can be renegotiated, but only before any money has moved. Renegotiation is available from `AGREED` and only while `collected_cents` is still 0. It may revise `overpaid_gross_cents`, `overpaid_income_tax_withheld_cents`, `overpaid_fica_withheld_cents`, `recovery_tax_year` and `installment_count`, and it re-runs the recovery-basis computation over the revised inputs — so a changed recovery year genuinely flips the basis and rewrites both `employee_due_cents` and `agency_recoverable_cents`. The same two arithmetic conditions apply to the revised values, again reported as `ERR_INVALID_RECOVERY_YEAR`. The row transitions `AGREED → PROPOSED`, `installment_count` is cleared back to null, and the repayment's `version` is incremented by 1. This is a genuine cycle: `PROPOSED → AGREED → PROPOSED`.

Collection is exact or it is nothing. Available from `AGREED`, it posts a `collected_cents` figure that must equal `employee_due_cents` precisely; anything larger is an over-collection and anything smaller is an under-collection, and either is refused with `ERR_OVERCOLLECTION`. On success the repayment transitions `AGREED → COLLECTED`, the posted amount is stored in `collected_cents`, the case's `repayment_recovered_cents` — defined as the sum of `agency_recoverable_cents` over every repayment on the case whose `status` is `COLLECTED` — is recomputed, and the repayment's `version` is incremented by 1. Collection then heads a five-link cascade. **Link one** is the transition itself and the recovery rollup just described. **Link two:** every corrected wage statement belonging to the case whose `tax_year` equals this repayment's `overpayment_tax_year` and whose status is `ISSUED` or `ACKNOWLEDGED` transitions to `VOIDED` and spawns a successor in `DRAFT`, each successor carrying `supersedes_statement_id` pointing at its predecessor and a `sequence_no` one greater, with box deltas recomputed — and because a collected repayment is now in play, those recomputed deltas take the current-year or prior-year box treatment of §3.6 according to the repayment's `recovery_basis`. **Link three:** each successor creation recomputes the employee's `active_statement_count` and the case's `corrected_wage_base_cents`. **Link four:** the case's statement dimension flips `STMT_ISSUED → STMT_REISSUE_PENDING`. **Link five:** that flip reverts the case's overall `status` `REMEDIATION_PENDING → OPEN` and recomputes the employer's `open_exposure_cents`, re-admitting the case's full exposure. A collection late in a case's life therefore reopens it, and the statement work has to be redone with the repayment folded in.

Writing off is the divergent branch, and it is the officer's call rather than the analyst's. From `AGREED`, a write-off supplies a required `write_off_reason` and transitions the row `AGREED → WRITTEN_OFF`: the reason is recorded, `collected_cents` stays at whatever value it already held, **no** statement is voided, the case's `corrected_wage_base_cents` is left unchanged, and the repayment's `version` is incremented by 1. None of the five cascade links fires. The same act performed earlier means something different: from `PROPOSED`, where the employee never agreed to anything, the write-off cancels the proposal instead of writing off a balance, transitioning `PROPOSED → CANCELLED`, recording the `write_off_reason`, and incrementing `version` by 1. `COLLECTED`, `WRITTEN_OFF` and `CANCELLED` are all terminal, and a further transition attempt out of any of them is refused with `ERR_ALREADY_TERMINAL`.

### 3.8. Agency Notices, Response Rounds and Penalty Abatement

Agencies do not wait to be corrected. A notice records what the agency asserts: the `employer_id` that received it, the `agency_id` that issued it, the `tax_year` and `quarter` it assesses, the `notice_number` of 1 to 32 characters that identifies it, the three assessed amounts `assessed_tax_cents`, `assessed_penalty_cents` and `assessed_interest_cents`, and the `response_due_date` by which the employer must answer. A `correction_case_id` may be supplied to attach the notice to an open case, or omitted to leave it unattached; when supplied, the notice's employer, agency, tax year and quarter must match the case's, or the request is refused with `ERR_SCOPE_MISMATCH`. `notice_number` must be unique within its issuing agency, and a duplicate is refused with `ERR_DUPLICATE_NOTICE_NUMBER`. A new notice starts with `response_status` at `RECEIVED`, `abatement_status` at `NONE`, `abatement_round` at 0, `waiver_pct_bps`, `waived_penalty_cents`, `waived_interest_cents` and `net_exposure_cents` all at 0, and `response_narrative`, `abatement_grounds`, `requested_penalty_waiver_cents`, `responded_at` and `agency_decision_reference` all at null. Recording a notice has a consequence elsewhere that is easy to overlook: because a notice now exists on that employer, agency, tax year and quarter, the voluntary self-correction reduction described in §3.4 no longer applies to penalties computed for that period. Notice lists can be narrowed by `employer_id`, `agency_id`, `correction_case_id`, `tax_year`, `quarter`, `response_status` and `abatement_status`.

The two dimensions of a notice move independently. The response dimension tracks the correspondence; the abatement dimension tracks the waiver argument. Drafting a response is available when `response_status` is `RECEIVED` or `INFO_REQUESTED`, and only while the current logical instant is at or before `response_due_date`. It supplies a required `response_narrative` and, optionally, the abatement argument: `abatement_grounds`, drawn from `FIRST_TIME`, `REASONABLE_CAUSE`, `STATUTORY_EXCEPTION` and `AGENCY_ERROR`, together with `requested_penalty_waiver_cents` naming the penalty amount the employer asks to have waived. When `requested_penalty_waiver_cents` is supplied it may not exceed `assessed_penalty_cents`. On success the notice transitions to `RESPONSE_DRAFTED` — from `RECEIVED` on a first pass, from `INFO_REQUESTED` on a supplemental pass — the narrative, grounds and requested waiver are stored (a supplemental pass overwrites the previously drafted `response_narrative`), `abatement_round` is incremented by 1 on every draft including supplemental rounds, and the notice's `version` is incremented by 1. When `abatement_grounds` was supplied and the abatement dimension is still at `NONE`, that dimension advances `NONE → REQUESTED` in the same request.

Transmitting the response is available from `RESPONSE_DRAFTED` and takes no body. It requires the current logical instant to be on or before `response_due_date`; a later attempt is refused with `ERR_NOTICE_RESPONSE_OVERDUE`. The notice transitions `RESPONSE_DRAFTED → RESPONDED`, `responded_at` is stamped from the logical clock, and `version` is incremented by 1.

Agencies come back asking for more. From `RESPONDED`, and only while the abatement dimension sits at `REQUESTED`, the agency's request for further information can be recorded with an optional `info_request_note`; the notice transitions `RESPONDED → INFO_REQUESTED`, `responded_at` is cleared back to null, and `version` is incremented by 1. From `INFO_REQUESTED` the notice returns to drafting, giving the cycle `RESPONDED → INFO_REQUESTED → RESPONSE_DRAFTED → RESPONDED` with `abatement_round` counting how many times the argument has been made.

The decision closes the correspondence and computes the waiver. Recording an outcome is available from `RESPONDED`; it supplies an `outcome` of `ABATED_FULL`, `ABATED_PARTIAL` or `UPHELD`, and optionally an `agency_decision_reference` recorded alongside. If `requested_penalty_waiver_cents` exceeds `assessed_penalty_cents`, the outcome is refused with `ERR_WAIVER_EXCEEDS_ASSESSMENT`.

The **waiver computation** proceeds in four steps, and its most important property is that the tier key is fixed before anything is waived. **Step one** forms the pre-waiver gross exposure as `assessed_tax_cents` plus `assessed_penalty_cents` plus `assessed_interest_cents`. That figure is evaluated exactly once and is never re-evaluated against the post-waiver net; pinning it this way is what breaks the otherwise circular dependency between net exposure, tier, waiver and net exposure again. **Step two** selects the waiver percentage in basis points from the grounds argued, but only when the outcome is not `UPHELD`. When the outcome *is* `UPHELD`, the computation stops immediately with `waiver_pct_bps`, `waived_penalty_cents` and `waived_interest_cents` all 0 and `net_exposure_cents` equal to the full gross exposure. Otherwise: for `FIRST_TIME` grounds, the percentage is 10000 basis points when the employer's `granted_abatement_count` is 0 **and** the gross exposure is no greater than 5000000 cents, and 5000 basis points in every other case. For `REASONABLE_CAUSE` grounds, the base is 6000 basis points, to which a bonus of 2000 basis points is added when a control remediation exists on the notice's own `correction_case_id` whose `control_gap_class` is `THIRD_PARTY_FAILURE` and whose `status` is already `VERIFIED` at decision time; the sum is then capped at 10000 basis points. Remediations in `SUBMITTED_FOR_VERIFICATION` or `VERIFICATION_FAILED` contribute nothing to that bonus. For `AGENCY_ERROR` grounds the percentage is 10000 basis points, and the assessed interest is fully waived as well. For `STATUTORY_EXCEPTION` grounds the percentage is also 10000 basis points, but it applies to the penalty only and the assessed interest is untouched. The employer's `granted_abatement_count` is read **before** this notice's own `abatement_status` is updated, so a first-ever abatement always sees a count of zero.

**Step three** turns the percentage into cents. `waived_penalty_cents` is `assessed_penalty_cents` multiplied by `waiver_pct_bps`, divided by ten thousand, rounded to the nearest whole cent with ties rounded upward — this is the only rounding in the whole computation. `waived_interest_cents` is the full `assessed_interest_cents` when the grounds are `AGENCY_ERROR`, and 0 for every other grounds value. When the outcome is `ABATED_PARTIAL`, `waived_penalty_cents` is then reduced to the smaller of itself and `requested_penalty_waiver_cents` — the agency cannot be recorded as having waived more than was asked for.

**Step four** computes `net_exposure_cents` as the gross exposure reduced by `waived_penalty_cents` and further reduced by `waived_interest_cents`, and then applies the **de-minimis sweep** — executed exactly once, and only on the `ABATED_FULL` branch. If the outcome is `ABATED_FULL` and the resulting net exposure is greater than 0 and no greater than 2500 cents, the sweep replaces `waived_penalty_cents` with the whole `assessed_penalty_cents`, replaces `waived_interest_cents` with the whole `assessed_interest_cents`, and recomputes `net_exposure_cents` as the gross exposure reduced by both of those full amounts. The sweep never re-runs the percentage selection and never re-tiers. On the `ABATED_PARTIAL` branch the sweep is not applied at all, and on the `UPHELD` branch there is nothing to sweep.

The three outcomes therefore write three different rows, and the abatement dimension records which. `ABATED_FULL` advances `REQUESTED → GRANTED` with the de-minimis sweep in force and increments the employer's `granted_abatement_count` by 1. `ABATED_PARTIAL` advances `REQUESTED → PARTIALLY_GRANTED` with the waiver capped at the requested amount and the sweep skipped, and also increments the employer's `granted_abatement_count` by 1. `UPHELD` advances `REQUESTED → DENIED`, leaves `waiver_pct_bps`, `waived_penalty_cents` and `waived_interest_cents` all at 0 with `net_exposure_cents` equal to the full assessment, and leaves the employer's `granted_abatement_count` unchanged. All three of `GRANTED`, `PARTIALLY_GRANTED` and `DENIED` are terminal for the abatement dimension, and all three require the abatement dimension to be at `REQUESTED` and the response dimension to be at `RESPONDED`. Where no abatement was ever requested, the abatement dimension simply stays at `NONE`. In every case the response dimension advances `RESPONDED → CLOSED`, which is terminal for that dimension; when the notice carries a non-null `correction_case_id`, the case's `total_exposure_cents` is recomputed and then the employer's `open_exposure_cents`; and the notice's `version` is incremented by 1. A granted or partially granted waiver therefore reduces the case exposure that governs future signing authority, while a denial leaves it exactly where it was.

### 3.9. Control Remediation, Case Closure and the Exposure Ledger

Filing an amendment fixes the number; it does not fix whatever produced the number. A remediation record is the commitment that the failing control will be repaired. Recording one names the `correction_case_id` whose root cause it addresses, the `root_cause_code` it targets — one of `RATE_TABLE_STALE`, `JURISDICTION_MAPPING_ERROR`, `THIRD_PARTY_FEED_ERROR`, `MANUAL_ENTRY_ERROR` or `DEPOSIT_SCHEDULING_ERROR` — the `control_gap_class` that classifies the failing control as `PROCESS_CONTROL`, `SYSTEM_CONTROL` or `THIRD_PARTY_FAILURE`, the `owner_user_id` accountable for the work, and the `target_date` by which it is committed. The row is written in `status` `OPEN` with `evidence_reference`, `verified_by_user_id` and `verified_at` at null and `verification_round` at 0, and the employer's `open_remediation_count` — the number of remediations belonging to any case of that employer whose `status` is `OPEN`, `SUBMITTED_FOR_VERIFICATION` or `VERIFICATION_FAILED` — is recomputed. Remediation lists can be narrowed by `correction_case_id`, `root_cause_code`, `control_gap_class` and `status`; a remediation that the acceptance cascade created rather than a human is discoverable only through that filtered list, because nothing in the acceptance response names it.

That cascade-created remediation is deliberately incomplete: it carries the case's `dominant_root_cause_code` as its `root_cause_code`, a `control_gap_class` of `PROCESS_CONTROL`, a `target_date` thirty days after the current logical instant, and an `owner_user_id` of null. A remediation with no owner cannot be submitted for verification, so assignment is the act that unlocks it. Assignment is available while the remediation's `status` is `OPEN` or `VERIFICATION_FAILED`; it sets `owner_user_id` from the request, replaces `target_date` when a new one is supplied, and increments the remediation's `version` by 1. Applied to an `OPEN` remediation this is a self-loop — the state does not change, only the fields — and it is the sole route by which an auto-created remediation acquires the owner its next transition requires.

Submitting evidence is available from `OPEN` and from `VERIFICATION_FAILED`, and it requires an `evidence_reference` in the request body. From `OPEN` it additionally requires `owner_user_id` to be non-null. On success the remediation transitions to `SUBMITTED_FOR_VERIFICATION`, the supplied `evidence_reference` is stored, `verification_round` is incremented by 1 — so it counts every attempt, not just the successful one — and `version` is incremented by 1.

Verification is the second control point, and like signing an amendment it may not be self-service. It is available from `SUBMITTED_FOR_VERIFICATION`, takes a boolean `passed` and an optional `verification_note`, and requires the acting `X-User-ID` to differ from the remediation's `owner_user_id`; an owner verifying their own work is refused with `ERR_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`. When `passed` is true the remediation transitions `SUBMITTED_FOR_VERIFICATION → VERIFIED`, `verified_by_user_id` is set from the acting `X-User-ID`, `verified_at` is stamped from the logical clock, the employer's `open_remediation_count` is recomputed because this remediation has left the counted set, and `version` is incremented by 1; `VERIFIED` is terminal, and a further transition attempt is refused with `ERR_ALREADY_TERMINAL`. When `passed` is false the remediation transitions `SUBMITTED_FOR_VERIFICATION → VERIFICATION_FAILED`, `verified_by_user_id` and `verified_at` are both left at null, the employer's `open_remediation_count` is deliberately **unchanged** because the remediation is still outstanding, and `version` is incremented by 1. A failed verification returns to evidence submission, giving the cycle `OPEN → SUBMITTED_FOR_VERIFICATION → VERIFICATION_FAILED → SUBMITTED_FOR_VERIFICATION → VERIFIED` with `verification_round` recording how many attempts it took. Only a `VERIFIED` remediation whose `control_gap_class` is `THIRD_PARTY_FAILURE` contributes the reasonable-cause bonus of §3.8; one still sitting in `SUBMITTED_FOR_VERIFICATION` or `VERIFICATION_FAILED` contributes nothing.

Closing a case is the last act, and it is gated on all four dimensions plus everything hanging off them. Closure is available only from `REMEDIATION_PENDING`, accepts an optional `closure_note`, and requires three things beyond that state: the case's `open_discrepancy_count` must be 0; every remediation on the case must be in `status` `VERIFIED`, or the request is refused with `ERR_REMEDIATION_NOT_VERIFIED`; and every wage repayment on the case must be in a terminal status of `COLLECTED`, `WRITTEN_OFF` or `CANCELLED`, or the request is refused with `ERR_REPAYMENT_NOT_SETTLED`. A single `PROPOSED` or `AGREED` repayment therefore holds a case open indefinitely. On success the case transitions `REMEDIATION_PENDING → CLOSED`, `closed_at` is stamped from the logical clock, the employer's `open_exposure_cents` is recomputed so that the case's whole exposure drops out, the employer's `open_remediation_count` is decremented, and the case's `version` is incremented by 1. `CLOSED` is terminal.

The **case exposure rollup** is what all of that arithmetic feeds. A case's `total_exposure_cents` is computed by first summing, over every amended return on the case whose `status` is `PREPARED`, `SIGNED`, `SUBMITTED` or `ACCEPTED`, that return's `tax_delta_cents` plus `interest_cents` plus `penalty_cents`; then summing, over every agency notice on the case whose `abatement_status` is `GRANTED` or `PARTIALLY_GRANTED`, that notice's `waived_penalty_cents` plus `waived_interest_cents`; and then taking the larger of zero and the first sum reduced by the second. Amended returns in `DRAFT`, `AGENCY_REJECTED` or `VOID` are excluded from the first sum entirely, which is exactly why voiding a return removes its whole contribution and why a rejected return leaves exposure untouched. Notices whose `abatement_status` is `NONE`, `REQUESTED` or `DENIED` contribute nothing to the second sum. Both sums are order-insensitive, so no tie-break arises, and the result is floored at zero so that waivers exceeding the summed amendment exposure can never produce a negative figure. The rollup is recomputed whenever an amended return is prepared, reverted from signature, or voided from either the prepared or the agency-rejected state; whenever a notice's correspondence closes; and whenever a case closes or reopens.

The **employer open exposure rollup** sits one level above it. An employer's `open_exposure_cents` is the sum of `total_exposure_cents` over every case belonging to that employer whose `status` is `OPEN` or `REMEDIATION_PENDING`. Closed cases are excluded entirely, and a reopen driven by a repayment collection re-admits the reopened case's full `total_exposure_cents`. It is recomputed on every write that changes any of that employer's cases' `total_exposure_cents` or `status`, which is what makes signing authority responsive: a waiver granted on one case immediately widens the headroom available to a signature on another, and a void immediately restores it.

Finally, a case exposes a computed read that assembles the whole ledger in one place: `gross_exposure_cents` for the summed amendment exposure before waivers, `waived_cents` for the summed granted and partially granted waivers, `total_exposure_cents` for the floored net, `corrected_wage_base_cents` for the wage base as the issued statements now report it, `repayment_recovered_cents` for the agency-recoverable amounts actually collected back from employees, and `dominant_root_cause_code`, which is null when no discrepancy on the case has been categorized. Together these six figures reconcile the case: what the amendments assessed, what the agencies forgave, what remains owed, what the statements now say, what came back from employees, and why it happened in the first place.
